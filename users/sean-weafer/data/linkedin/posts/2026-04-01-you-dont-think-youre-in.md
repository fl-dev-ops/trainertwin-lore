You don’t think you’re in sales... But your customers might disagree.

If you’re a:
Pre-sales consultant
Solution architect
Project manager
Programme leader etc

You are often the person who:
• Hears the real problems
• Sees the hidden risks
• Understands what success actually looks like

Which means…

You are already influencing buying decisions.

The only difference is…

You’re doing it unconsciously.

And here’s the problem: Most non-sales professionals avoid “selling” because of what they think it is:

Pushy
Transactional
Self-serving

So they stay silent.

But silence doesn’t protect the relationship.

It limits the value of it.

If you can see a risk your customer cannot see…

And you choose not to raise it…

You are not being helpful. You are holding back.

This is the shift:
Selling is not being pushy.
Selling is helping a customer make a better decision.
