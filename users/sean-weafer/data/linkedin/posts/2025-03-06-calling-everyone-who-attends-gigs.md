Calling everyone who attends gigs…

In addition to relaunching my music podcast this month, I’m also starting a new one dedicated to small independent music and entertainment venues. For some time I’ve been concerned about the disappearance of these vital community hubs, so I plan to put the word out about what we might lose. I want to interview owners, bookers and performers at these venues. And promote them too. 

So here’s how you can help.

- suggestions for venues to feature. (Like The Sound Lounge, The Green Note, King Tut’s Wah Wah Hut, The Prince Charles Cinema etc) 
- suggestions for a name for the show
- suggestions for guests or co-hosts

Thanks! All ideas welcome. Reposting welcome. And no, I have no idea what chord I was playing in 1967.
