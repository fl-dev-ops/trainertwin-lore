Thanks for the shout out on the show Simon, always a pleasure to be name checked by you, Anthony and Graham on your show! 

I think you and the crew did a great job on unpacking this critical subject of sales leadership. For me selling is the first of all the business disciplines. There's no need for any other part of the business unless a sale is first made. 

That said, I believe it is the least respected of all the business disciplines as is evidenced by the woeful lack of training for sales managers and sales leaders. I'm coaching 3 sales leaders at the moment and none of them (and one has 26 years of being a leader) has ever had any form of coaching, training or support to lead to his potential. 

There is another reason why sales leaders can be quite dictatorial. Many sales people who make it to manager and director do so because they live with the understandable perception that just 'being a sales rep' somehow lacks any status. So there can be a huge desire to make it to 'manager' and 'director' level for the perceived kudos, but that's where the lack of support and training can kick in. 

Anyway great job and looking forward to more of your excellent shows.
