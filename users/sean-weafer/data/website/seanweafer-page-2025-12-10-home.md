Coaching

I work individually with leaders and senior executives.

At this level, technical ability is a given but what moves the leadership dial is the ability to win trust, lead teams, build colleague and client relationships to manage stakeholders and present with confidence and impact.

My coaching sessions for individual leadership and management professionals are a practical, structured approach with proven frameworks and measurable outcomes. The focus is on real behaviour change, not theory.

The result: professionals and experts who move from being valued experts to trusted advisors - and who look, sound and act like highly trusted leaders, advisors and partners.

Workshops

Turn Expertise into Influence and Trust With Colleagues and Clients

I deliver in-house or webinar-based Highly Trusted Advisor workshops for consultancy, advisory and technically expert teams who need to develop inter-team communications and performance OR win work without selling their soul.

These are practical, senior-level sessions in communications, social intelligence, networking, presenting and colleague and client conversations - designed for experts who already know their craft but want to build compelling levels of trust and influence with colleagues, stakeholders and clients.

The goal - to build professional leaders and advisors who can communicate with trust, influence and impact.

Built on my Highly Trusted Advisor framework, each workshop sharpens how your people think, speak and show up with colleagues or clients - so relationships deepen, opportunities expand and building trust and influence becomes a natural extension of professional credibility, not an awkward add-on.

No hype. Just better conversations, stronger colleague and client relationships and measurable commercial impact.
