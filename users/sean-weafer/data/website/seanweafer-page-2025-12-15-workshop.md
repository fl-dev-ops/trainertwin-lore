Highly Trusted Advisor Workshops

Turn Expertise into Trust and Turn Conversations into Outcomes

I deliver in-house Highly Trusted Advisor workshops for leadership and sales teams who want to transform their technical, consulting or advisory expertise into trust, influence and better professional outcomes.

These are practical, senior-level sessions in communications psychology, trust-building, networking and influence - designed for consultants, advisors, technical experts in leadership and sales positions who already know their craft but want to be trusted leaders or rainmakers.

Built on my Highly Trusted Advisor framework, each workshop sharpens how people think, speak and show up with colleagues and clients - so relationships deepen, opportunities expand and engagement becomes a natural extension of professional credibility, not an awkward add-on.

You can book a single module for your team or tailor a training program to meet your specific needs based on selected modules.

No scripts. No hype. Just better conversations, stronger relationships and measurable impact. Learn More

The Highly Trusted Advisor Workshops

Explore real-world scenarios to boost performance and become a highly trusted advisor in leadership or client development.

Module 1: MINDSET, MOTIVATION & MESSAGING How to develop a powerful growth mindset and motivation techniques and how to create compelling new team and client messages.

Module 2: STOP HESITATING; START NETWORKING! How to master the skills of professional networking, brand-building and referral success.

Module 3: CONNECTING IN COLOUR - How to build high levels of trust with colleagues, team members and clients.

Module 4: MANAGING MEETINGS: ADVANCED QUESTIONING & INFLUENCING SKILLS - How to deploy advanced questioning, objection handling skills in meetings and lead conversations that drive team performance or stakeholder agreement.

Module 5: THE PSYCHOLOGY OF WINNING PRESENTATIONS - How to use winning presentation psychology, structure and skills to successfully influence any audience.

Module 6: MANAGING YOUR PROFESSIONAL TIME: How to master focus, decision-making and professional productivity.
