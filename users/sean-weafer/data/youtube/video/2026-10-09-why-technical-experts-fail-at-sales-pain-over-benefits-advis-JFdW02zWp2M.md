  In this episode of FailCraft, founder of http://SalesMentor.pro, qualified psychotherapist, NLP master, and Hall of Fame professional speaker Sean Weafer joins Ezekiel to dismantle why technically brilliant professionals constantly struggle to close deals and win business. Transitioning from marine engineering and the merchant navy into high-stakes IT sales leadership, Sean uncovered a costly truth: encyclopedic technical knowledge is virtually useless unless you understand how to navigate the psychology of the buyer.

Drawing on Harvard research showing that 95% of buying decisions are emotional, he explains why remote screen selling extends the sales cycle, how automating customer touchpoints destroys brand equity, and how technical specialists can use "soft language" to shift from pushy order-takers into highly trusted advisors.

  * The Cost of Chasing New Leads: Maximizing Lifetime Client Value (LTCV) through upsells rather than burning resources on cold pipelines.

   

  * The Danger of Automated Bots: Why substituting human relationships with automated ticketing systems destroys customer trust [09:01 / 10:49].

   

  * The 3D vs. 2D Selling Disconnect: Why selling through a screen lengthens sales cycles by cutting off physical micro-signals and safety cues [11:17 / 13:24].
