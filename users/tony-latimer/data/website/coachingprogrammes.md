Executive coaching programmes

Engagements are not time bound or charged by the hour. Assignments are scoped so that you get the amount of coaching a specific situation requires, discussed with the individual and, where appropriate, with their senior stakeholder and their HR partner. From there the right programme is selected and the engagement is planned around the demands of the role.

Two terms appear throughout. Shadow coaching means I attend a leadership team meeting or strategy session as an observer, then coach the leader immediately afterwards on what transpired. Calibration means testing what has actually changed in the eyes of the people around the leader, starting from a baseline of stakeholder interviews and continuing through the engagement.
