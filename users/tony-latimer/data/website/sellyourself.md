#### Let me be blunt.

If you cannot sell yourself directly to corporates, you will always earn less than your coaching is worth. Not because you lack skill or credibility, but because no one ever showed you how corporate buying actually works, or how to have those conversations in a way that feels natural as a coach.

Most coaches end up relying on platforms, intermediaries, or referrals that never quite turn into a sustainable corporate practice. This book was written to change that.

#### **What this book gives you**

**Sell Yourself to Corporates** is not a sales textbook.

It is a story that teaches you how selling really works, without asking you to become someone you are not.

Through the journey of Arthur King, you quietly absorb the core principles of the Nine Actions® and begin to see:

- How to uncover real needs you can genuinely serve
- How to have confident conversations with senior people
- How selling can feel as natural as coaching

#### Why this approach works

If traditional sales advice worked for coaches, you probably wouldn't be reading this page.

This book takes a different path. Instead of scripts, tactics, or techniques, it uses story to gently rearrange how you think. As you read, selling stops feeling like selling and starts to feel like a natural extension of how you already coach.
