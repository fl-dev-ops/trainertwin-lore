## Personal Coaching

Coaching is helping someone get absolute clarity on where they are NOW; absolute clarity on where they need to be in the FUTURE; and absolute clarity on how to get there.

Stepping into a senior role starts a clock most people never mention. For the first 90 days or so, few people are really looking at how you are getting on. You have perhaps six months before they start to seriously question what they see. That window is your chance to get up to speed and establish yourself before opinions set.

Some people read this as they are about to step in, and want to be ready from the first week. Others are already a few months into the role, far enough to know the job is bigger than the brief, and that the right support would make the difference. Either way, you are still inside the window.

There are also times when a coach is exactly what you need, but the organisation is not the right place to get one. The role may be too new, the politics too delicate, or you may simply want this to be yours, private and outside the system you are working to establish yourself in. When that is the case, you can work with me directly. No sponsorship, no third party, no internal visibility. The coaching is the same. The arrangement is simply more straightforward.
