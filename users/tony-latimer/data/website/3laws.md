# Tony Latimer's 3 Laws of Coaching

### Tony Latimer's

## Three Laws of Coaching

The Three Laws of Coaching™ © 2025 Tony Latimer. All rights reserved.

Use, teaching, or distribution not permitted without written licence.

**First Law**Everything the coach does in a coaching conversation must have the intention of triggering fresh thinking in the client; whether in establishing or resolving the agreed outcome for the coaching, addressing issues they need to resolve to achieve that outcome, or deepening their understanding of themselves.

**Second Law**Everything the coach does must be responsive to what the client has communicated and must not conflict with the First Law.

**Third Law**The coach may bring silence, questions, insights, observations, metaphors, analogies, knowledge, or information to the coaching conversation, provided this does not conflict with the First or Second Laws.
