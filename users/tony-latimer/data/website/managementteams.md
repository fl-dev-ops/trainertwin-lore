## The layer that makes or breaks execution

A CEO can have the right strategy and the right leadership team. It still will not deliver if the managers beneath them cannot execute. The people who actually run the business day to day are the people managers. They set the tone, manage performance, develop the talent, and hold the whole thing together. And in most organisations, they are the people who get the least support. Our **corporate management training programmes** and tactical masterclasses close this execution gap across Singapore, APAC, and EMEA.

When I ask CEOs what they need to see different in the leadership beneath them, the answer is always the same three things. "I need to see my managers step up out of the detail. I need to see them proactively develop their people. And I need you to teach them to operate at a more strategic level."

The managers themselves are not the problem. The problem is that nobody has given them absolute clarity on what the job actually entails at that level, the skills to do it, or helped them find the time to do it differently.

That is the part I can help you with.
