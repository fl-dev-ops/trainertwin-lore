## Caption
One of the members made an observation this week that's stuck with me. We'd gone around the room, each answering the same question in our own words, and instead of judging who was "right," she was tracking what happened in her own body as she listened. Some answers made her nervous system spike. Others didn't, even when every answer was technically accurate. It's a good reminder for any of us explaining what coaching is, to a stakeholder, a prospect, or a client. The goal isn't the perfect definition. It's noticing whether your words are landing for the person in front of you. We dig into moments exactly like this live, four times a month, at Simplicity of Mastery.
