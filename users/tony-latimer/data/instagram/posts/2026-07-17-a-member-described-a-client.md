## Caption
A member described a client who arrived exhausted and asked to keep things light. It's a completely normal moment, and how you respond to it matters more than most coaches realise. Swapping "want" for "need" in your opening question changes what the client's subconscious offers up. It's a small shift with a genuinely different result, and it's exactly the kind of detail we work on live, four times a month, at Simplicity of Mastery.
