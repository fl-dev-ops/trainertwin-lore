A member asked me this week whether it's okay to teach sometimes, given she's a certified coach. I get this question constantly, and I think it comes from a good place, but it's built on a myth. Coaching is a skill you deploy, not a club membership you have to keep pure. The credentialed coaches who get the best results are the ones who know exactly which tool the moment calls for, and aren't afraid to use it. We work through exactly this kind of judgment call live, four times a month, inside Simplicity of Mastery.
