Most coaches don’t consciously choose to earn less than they’re worth.

What they usually choose is comfort. Comfort in letting platforms find clients for them, handle the contracting, and skim the fee. It feels easier, cleaner, and safer.

The book begins by quietly questioning that trade-off. Not morally, but practically. Because comfort has a cost, and for many coaches that cost shows up every time they look at their income.
