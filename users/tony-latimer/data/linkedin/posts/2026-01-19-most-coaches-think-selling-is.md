Most coaches think selling is what happens when you stop being a coach and start being something else.

That belief is the problem. It’s not just that it makes selling feel awkward, it makes you abandon the very skills that would make the conversation easy. Listening, curiosity, staying with what the other person actually means, those are sales skills when you apply them in a commercial room.

Selling Yourself to Corporates is a story that invites you to notice that shift in yourself, and then watch what changes when you stop trying to “sell coaching” and start trying to understand what someone genuinely needs.
