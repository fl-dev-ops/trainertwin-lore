Your ears and your mouth don’t work at the same time. 
And that applies to your internal mouth (thinking). 

Taking notes requires conscious thought which pulls the coach back up to their conscious awareness. Deepest listening happens when you listen from your subconscious. So your conscious mind must be still and clear. 

You will never coach at the highest levels if you are doing the clients job for them and taking notes. 

If your coaching approach means you find it helpful to have notes for yourself, do the after the session finishes. Trust your brain. You can recall the stuff you need to write down.
