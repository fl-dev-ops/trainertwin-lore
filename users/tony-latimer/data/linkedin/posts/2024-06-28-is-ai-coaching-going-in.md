Is AI coaching going in the wrong direction? 
Think about it. An ai chatbot by design is created to ANSWER questions from a knowledge-base, a coach by design(training) is created to ASK questions [from a body of knowledge and expereince]. 
Are we trying to force it to do something against its very nature?

Solution 1: Completely redesign to a new style of chatbot. 

Expensive. 

Solution 2: Let go of the insistence on “pure” coaching (as defined by current professional bodies and their competency models) and create expert mentors. 

The best mentors bring coaching skills, the ability to trigger thinking, into how they work. 

Working from an expert database prevents most of the hallucinations that come from a bot with complete access to all the rubbish in the internet that initially trained its communication ability. 

By ceasing to call it coaching you free yourself from the “need” to have compliance to competencies, thus removing the need for privacy invading compliance oversight (by human or another ai tool). 

Clients often need a combination of coaching and learning. An expert mentor bot designed for purpose could provide that more easily. 

Thoughts?
