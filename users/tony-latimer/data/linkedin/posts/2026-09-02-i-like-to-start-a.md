"I like to start a session with . . . " When I hear a coach say that, I know we have a wall to break through. 

Very few coaches bother using a mentor coach after getting their PCC credential; until its time for the next level credential. And then its too late. 

5 to 7 years of building the hours actually means 5 to 7 years of building the habits. 
And then you expect 10 hours of mentor coaching to elevate your skills up to mastery. That is why the first time pass rate is so low. 

You're attempting the biggest jump in coaching skills, using the smallest amount of effort.
You haven't had anyone really hold the mirror up to the habits you are forming.
