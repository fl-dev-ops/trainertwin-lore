“Sometimes the most powerful move in coaching isn’t a question — it’s the courage to name the pattern the client can’t see.”

As coaches, do we sometimes forget we can say the thing that changes everything?

In our latest Simplicity of Mastery tutorials, one member realised her client kept circling the same issue — and the only thing stopping her from naming it was… forgetting she could.

We explored the courage to hold up the mirror, even to say, “I’m bored,” when it serves the client’s breakthrough. We dug into the discipline of letting the client do the thinking, resisting the urge to problem-solve for them. And we challenged the myth that coaching must always “follow the rules” — because mastery isn’t about ticking boxes, it’s about the client getting the result they came for.

For PCC coaches, this is the heart of moving to mastery:

Trusting your instincts.
Naming patterns without fear.
Working beyond rigid models when the client’s growth demands it.

If you’re a PCC ready to step beyond safe habits and learn the skills that really change lives — long before you face your MCC exam — join us in Simplicity of Mastery. It’s where we practise the courage, precision, and artistry that make coaching at the highest level possible.
