When Failure Isn’t an Option, You Discover Who You Really Are as a Coach

In my early days, coaching wasn’t a “nice to have.” It wasn’t something I could dabble in until it took off.

It was rent to pay. Kids to feed. Failure simply wasn’t an option.

And when failure isn’t an available option, something shifts. You find a whole new level inside yourself. You do whatever it takes. You discover resilience you didn’t know you had, resourcefulness you didn’t think you possessed, and courage you didn’t realise was there all along.

This short conversation with Stan is for any coach who’s struggling to get their business established. If it feels like a constant uphill battle, ask yourself: is this a hobby… or is it something you’re committed to making work?

Because the moment you decide it must work, everything changes.

🎥 Watch the clip — then tell me: what was your moment of “failure is not an option”?
