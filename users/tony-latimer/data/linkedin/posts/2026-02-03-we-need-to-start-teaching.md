We need to start teaching mastery to beginners. 

We already have a two tier system. Those who coach at the “Newtonian” level using two of the five senses; and those who coach at “quantum” physics level bringing energy signals into the work. AI will eventually be able to do all of the newton coaching when it is given the right sensors and trained at a higher level than Grow and similar limiting models. 

AI will probably trigger a huge increase in the adoption of coaching, and will absorb most of that growth. 

I think relatively few people currently using a human coaches would switch to ai, but some will. 

Coach training needs to accelerate as the coaches at risk are those in the early development stages of their practice (ACC and PCC in ICF terms. Though basic MCC can still be achieved with “Newtonian” coaching. 

We need to start teaching mastery to beginners.
