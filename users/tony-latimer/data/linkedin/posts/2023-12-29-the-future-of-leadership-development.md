The Future of Leadership Development
“Ain’t nobody got time for that!” 

In the realm of leadership development, the winds of change are blowing. 
The pandemic killed off in person training and sent everyone rushing to virtual classes.

With mixed results.

We also saw a huge growth in online, on-demand e-learning. Companies rushed in a bought subscriptions to libraries of learning content. Yet in all the conversations I am having with L&D this year, the overwhelming realisation is that no-one is using it!

So that money was pretty much wasted.

Here is the real issue: When learning was live and in person, it was more enjoyable, but pretty much most of it was also wasted. 

Why? Because people only learn what they believe they need to learn.

So you had to maximise learning by doing pre-work to help them figure out what they needed to learn, and then post-work to ensure implementation of learning. If you did that, you at least ensured they would learn the bit they needed, and use it to some effect. 

But the majority of what was covered in the class was stuff they didn’t NEED right now. So it was lost. And the money was wasted.

E-Learning doesn’t solve the problem. It just means people have to take the initiative to search through content to find the answer they wanted.
