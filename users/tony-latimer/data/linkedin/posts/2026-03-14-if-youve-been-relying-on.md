If you’ve been relying on platforms for corporate work, it can feel like the safest route.

And it is safe, in the sense that you don’t have to initiate anything. But the price of that safety is that you’re not building your own relationships, and you’re rarely being paid in line with the value you create.

The book doesn’t attack platforms. It simply invites you to stop making them your ceiling.
