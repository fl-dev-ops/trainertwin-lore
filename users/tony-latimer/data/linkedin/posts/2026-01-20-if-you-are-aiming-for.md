If you are aiming for MCC, it’s worth being clear about what the credential actually reflects.

It doesn’t reward complexity. It doesn’t reward eloquence. It rewards the consistent ability to trigger fresh thinking in the client, with precision and restraint, in service of their agenda rather than the coach’s performance.

This programme is designed around that reality. It focuses on how coaching actually lands, rather than how it looks or sounds on the surface.
