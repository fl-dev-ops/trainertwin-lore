One of the most powerful things a professional coach can do for their credibility… is give their work away.

Last night, I had the absolute pleasure of gathering with fellow coaches from the ICF Singapore Coach Bench—a group driven by passion, purpose, and the incredible leadership of Barbara Noonan, a true powerhouse. 

What started as a bold vision has turned into a remarkable reality. Early last year, Barbara and I were chatting about her setting an ambitious goal: to grow the Coach Bench from 20 coaches to 100, all committed to delivering pro bono coaching to deserving charities and NGOs. Not only has she achieved this goal in less than a year, but she has also surpassed it, creating a profound impact on Singapore’s society. 

The statistics are nothing short of inspiring. The work done by this bench of coaches has provided invaluable support to leaders in charities and NGOs, enabling them to better serve their communities. It’s a ripple effect: when leaders are supported, the people they serve benefit too. 

For the coaches involved, this initiative is equally transformative. It offers an opportunity to refine their skills, work with real-world challenges, and contribute to a cause larger than themselves. As part of this community, I’m reminded of the power of collaboration—like the frame of a pyramid, where every side leans in to support the others. 

With over 1,000 members in our ICF Singapore chapter, I believe we can dream even bigger. Imagine the impact if every credentialed coach joined this movement. 

So, here’s my call to action: If you’re a credentialed coach, come join us. Be part of this incredible journey. Together, we can make a difference—one coaching conversation at a time.
