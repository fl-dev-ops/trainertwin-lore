“The future of coaching is already here—and it’s energetic.”

Recent research by Rebecca Rutschmann, David Tee and Jonathan Passmore shows AI bots are now performing at ACC level and closing in on PCC. That should be a wake-up call.

If you’re still coaching at the surface—just listening for goals, values, or problems—you’ll soon be replaced. But there’s one thing AI cannot touch: the energetic level of human presence.

In this article I talk about the difference and what you need to do
