🎯 Coaching at Mastery Level means letting go of control.
In this week’s Simplicity of Mastery tutorials, one coach described it perfectly:
“I didn’t feel like I was doing anything special… but the client said, ‘I could feel you pulling my energy and understanding me.’”

That’s the paradox of mastery. The more you practise the core framework—the “now to future” flow, the energy work, the subroutines—the less you do, and the more it simply is.

You move beyond competence. Beyond MCC. You become.

💡 But let’s be clear: this isn’t something you can learn from someone who only teaches coaching. Mastery needs to be passed on by a long-term, and current, practitioner. Someone who’s spent decades in the field, coaching live with CEOs, boards, and cross-cultural teams under real pressure.

👉 If you’re a PCC holder and serious about coaching senior executives at the top level—this is your sign. Don’t wait until you “feel ready.” Mastery takes time. Start now. Get around people doing the work. Learn from someone who’s been there for decades.
