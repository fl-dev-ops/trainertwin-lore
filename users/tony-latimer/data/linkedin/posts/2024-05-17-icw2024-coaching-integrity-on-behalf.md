#ICW2024 #coaching #integrity On behalf of someone who does so much to promote coaching . . .
But probably won't promote themselves enough . . .

Georgy Simonyan messaged me quietly last night to share he has got his PCC; and was shy because "it took so long to get the coaching hours after passing the rest of the exam 1.5 years ago".

Why did it take so long?

Because he believed that only coaching real clients, not just making up hours by doing vast amounts of reciprocal coaching with other coaches was the right thing to do.

The right way to enhance his skills and deliver value to clients.

Congratulations Georgy; You make me proud; A great way to end International Coaching Week.
