“AI will probably not replace the human coach, but the human coach using AI responsibly will replace the human coach without AI.” 🤔 I’m not convinced totally but what I believe will happen is

“AI coaching bots will absorb a large part of the growth in coaching”

It will also generate additional coaching for humans to do; but they will need to be able to coach at a higher level than the bots. And that bar is raising all the time.
