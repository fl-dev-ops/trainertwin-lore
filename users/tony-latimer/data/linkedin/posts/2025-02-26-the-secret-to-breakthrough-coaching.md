The Secret to Breakthrough Coaching (Without Asking a Single Question!)

Most coaches think their job is to ask powerful questions. But last week in Simplicity of Mastery, we proved that’s not always the case.

During our coaching practice session, we did something radical: we got a client to a major breakthrough—without asking a single question.

How? By getting absolute clarity on where they are NOW.
🔹 Just deep, empty-minded listening.
🔹 Just reflecting back what we heard.
🔹 Just holding space without filling it with words.

And then—it happened. The client heard their own thoughts reflected back with such clarity that the shift happened instantly.

🚀 No “powerful” questions.
🚀 No long exploration of solutions.
🚀 Just pure coaching mastery.

This is what separates good coaching from masterful coaching. It’s not about what you ask—it’s about how you listen. If you’re a PCC-level coach ready to level up, join us in Simplicity of Mastery and start your journey to true coaching mastery.
