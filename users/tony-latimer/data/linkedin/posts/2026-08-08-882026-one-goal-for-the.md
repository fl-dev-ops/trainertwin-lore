8.8.2026 one goal for the rest of my working life is to help create 1,000 more MCCs. 

Three years ago Fran Fisher motivated and inspired me to create Simplicity of Mastery Professional Membership, a unique low-cost monthly membership program that allows coaches at any level to bring mastery into their coaching .

So far, just over 500 coaches have experienced the program and what becomes possible when they move beyond dependence on models, techniques, and prepared questions and begin coaching at the speed of thought, leveraging simplicity and energetic presence. They're not all MCC yet. Just a few of them have got to that level so far, but the others are coaching at a mastery level. By the time they're ready to apply for MCC, it will be simply a confirmation of the way they've been working for a number of years. 

You can bring mastery into your coaching long before you're ready for the MCC credential. Mastery is not a title awarded at the end of your development. It's something you progressively bring into the way you coach, as early as possible. 

Now, for the first time, I am taking that work into an intensive four-month ICF Level 3 program.

The founding Simplicity Mastery Level 3 cohort will work directly with me, with a focus on practical application. 

Training based on competencies tells you what to do. Simplicity of Mastery shows you how to do it. 

There will only ever be one founding cohort. 

It begins on the 28th of October 2026. 

There are 12 places. 

If you are currently a PCC, actively coaching, and serious about developing coaching worthy of MCC standards, applications are now open.
