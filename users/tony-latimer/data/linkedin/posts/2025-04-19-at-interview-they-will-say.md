A client recently asked me:
🧠 “How do we make sure new hires truly align with our company values?”

The answer? You can’t fake it.
Not in interviews. Not in onboarding.
You need a system for identifying values-fit before the offer goes out.

That’s exactly what TonyLatimer.ai is built for.

So we asked the question and here is a sample of the answer:

"1. Understand and Define the Updated Company Values
Spend time clearly defining the updated company values and what they mean in terms of behaviors. As noted, values are best understood and measured through behaviors, as they are observable and can be described and evaluated effectively.
Ensure that everyone involved in the hiring process understands these values and how they translate into day-to-day actions within the company.

2. Clarify the Evidence You Need
Identify the specific evidence you need to see or hear during the interview process that indicates a candidate's personal values align with the company values. For example:
- What behaviors or actions would demonstrate alignment with your values?
- What past experiences or decisions might reflect these values in action?

3. Incorporate Values into Job Descriptions and Adverts
Update job descriptions and adverts to reflect the importance of values alignment. Avoid focusing solely on tasks and activities; instead, include language that highlights the values and behaviors you are looking for in candidates.
