Forget the Household Names: Where the Real Corporate Coaching Work Is

At the tail end of my corporate career leading international business development, our strategy was not to go head-to-head against household-name vendors. We targeted the B-class multinationals—big organisations that Joe Public has never heard of, but are too small to attract the giant platform sales reps. In the coaching world, these B-class organisations are the exact sweet spot for coaches to build direct, full-fee relationships.
