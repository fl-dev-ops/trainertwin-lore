If you want to earn serious money as a coach, you eventually have to be able to initiate conversations directly with the people who can say yes.

Not dozens of conversations, and not endless pitching, just the ability to step into a clean discovery dialogue with a decision-maker and uncover what they actually need.

Arthur’s story is, in many ways, a practical demonstration of how those conversations work when you stop performing and start listening.
