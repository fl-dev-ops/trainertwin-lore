The fear of selling often hides behind a more respectable story.

“I’m too busy.”
“I’m not sure what to offer.”
“I don’t want to be pushy.”

Arthur has his own versions of those stories. And the book quietly shows how those stories keep you safe, but also keep you stuck.

The antidote is not force. It’s clarity, and clarity comes from conversation.
