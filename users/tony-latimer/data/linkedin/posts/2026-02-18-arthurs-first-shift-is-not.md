Arthur’s first shift is not strategy. It’s orientation.

He starts to ask: who needs what I’ve got, how do I find them, how do I learn what matters to them, how do I structure the conversation so it leads somewhere. Those are business development questions, but they’re also coaching questions.

The book keeps bringing you back to that simple truth, selling is a conversation that becomes easier when you know what you’re listening for.
