2023 was the year I finally got persuaded to share my approach to coaching with external coaches.

I started the year with a mission to democratise coach training; supported by a belief (proven) that you can teach mastery to anyone; and with a goal of helping bring the power of mastery to the results of coaching clients of many more coaches around the world.

It has been a thrill developing the programme this year, which many of you are going to be hearing about as we expand our reach globally in 2024.

The year is ending with our first member to achieve MCC. Huge congratulations to Winston Chue  (ICF - MCC, CMCS , FCD-I, GCDF-I)

His willingness and dedication to self examination and reflection has been an inspiration. Everyone at the Simplicity of Mastery tutorials has been in awe of his visible growth into mastery.
