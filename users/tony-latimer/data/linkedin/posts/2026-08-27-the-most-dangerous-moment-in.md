It’s that 4-second delay where the client hasn't answered yet, and your brain starts screaming to add a follow-up or rephrase it.

Great coaching rarely breaks down because of bad questions. 

It breaks down because we interrupt the client's internal process.

Holding true silence takes massive emotional discipline. It means letting the space feel heavy without rescuing the client.
