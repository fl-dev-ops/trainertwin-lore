Barbara Noonan and her social impact team at ICF Singapore make giving back easy for our members. You just have to join the coach bench and Barbara and her team do all the work to bring significant, well planned programmes for you to just sign up and deliver. 

Great for newer coaches (credential required) to get more experience coaching real people with real problems instead of relying on reciprocal coaching to log hours. And great for experienced busy coaches to give back to society without the hassle of seeking out opportunities.
