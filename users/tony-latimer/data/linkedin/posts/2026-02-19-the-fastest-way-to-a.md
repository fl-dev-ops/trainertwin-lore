The fastest way to a breakthrough is to slow down at the Now.
Most coaches believe they are working at depth on Competency 3. In reality, they move on to coaching for a solution far too quickly.

Last week inside Simplicity of Mastery we spent a third of the tutorial on deep practice of my playback, stretch and test approach. The focus is on disciplined listening from the subconscious and establishing absolute clarity on the Now. What began as a surface concern for the client about whether offering tools was appropriate, gradually revealed something much deeper — a long-held belief about value, worth and the idea that only manual labour is “real work.” There was a visible shift in the room. Long silence. You could almost see the internal reorganisation taking place within the client.

This is what happens when you truly leverage Competency 3 at the deepest level. When the Now is explored properly, the underlying belief shows up. Once that belief is seen clearly, the solution emerges. Most coaching never reaches this point because the coach becomes uncomfortable with silence or feels pressure to be useful. Mastery requires you to stay longer, go deeper, and trust that disciplined exploration of what is actually going on will do the heavy lifting.
