A very few companies know the secret to measuring “soft skills”
The majority struggle at appraisal time because they don’t
Here is the secret . . . 👇🏼 

In this article I’m going to focus specifically on how we measure the soft skills relevant to managers.

But first let me make a clear distinction; I’m going to talk about management, not leadership.

Why? 

Because Manager is the role, Leadership is the style.

Every manager should exhibit good leadership, but it is possible to exhibit leadership without being in a management role.

Today I am focussing on how we develop effective managers in the organisation. The majority of the work gets done, and the business results delivered, by the teams working for a people manager. 

If you don’t see excellence in the people management, no amount of great leadership displayed elsewhere will help. Results will not be delivered, engagement will be low, and people will leave.

Everything is measurable.

I believe everything is measurable. The hard part is deciding WHAT to measure.

Managers have to deliver two outcomes:
1. The business results they are measured against
2. The growth of the people who report to them

I believe the manager has the right, and the responsibility to ensure their direct reports perform on the current job, and are prepared for the next. The responsibility of longer term career development for the organisation should sit elsewhere. (Partly in ownership by the individual, partly in the relevant HR function, and ideally supported by an internal mentoring program).
