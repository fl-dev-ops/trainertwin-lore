To stay ahead, leadership and people management needs to be at a higher level than just being the "go-to" problem solver. 
The leader needs to focus their time on just three interlinked areas:
1. Pro-active people development. Doing the coaching, training, mentoring etc that is needed to ensure your people are constantly growing.
2. Linking your unit across the organisation, and beyond, for effective global matrix impact.
3. Spend time to "smell the horizon", see what is coming and how the competency and composition of your team needs to change to handle the future.
