Last month in Simplicity of Mastery we did something that even seasoned MCCs rarely experience. We coached in total silence. No models, no questions, no talking tricks. Just clean energetic connection. Five coaches, eyes closed, fully empty minded, focusing their intention on one colleague who was sitting with a real dilemma.

Within minutes, the whole group felt it.
A physical pulse. A shift in the field.
Candida opened her eyes, nodded, and said one word that changed everything: “Dignity.”

Her entire decision became clear in that moment. Not because someone asked a clever question, but because when you remove the noise and return to empty minded presence, the client’s own system reorganises. This is the part of mastery that no competency document can capture. It is beyond technique. It is beyond analysis. It is the work behind the work.

As Amber said during the session, “Mastery through this work is worth more than a sticker.” And she is right. When you can coach at this level, you stop trying to perform coaching and instead become the coach.
