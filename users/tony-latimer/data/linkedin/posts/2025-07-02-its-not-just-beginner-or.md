It's not just beginner or incompetent coaches who can be replaced by AI; it's anyone who is not coaching at the level only humans can. 

AND many coaches overestimate what level that is. For example, much of what we do when we say we are using our intuition is just pattern matching of data we have in our human brain.

Pattern matching is something computers are very good at; and AI now has the patterns of personal, emotional and professional experiences of everyone who has ever used social media in the past 10 years; so a far bigger data set than any human has.
