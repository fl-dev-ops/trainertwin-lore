Trends don’t define the next chapter; they only influence the next sentence. 

Everyone’s debating which trend will shape coaching in 2026. I’m more interested in what coaching will do to how humans communicate.

We need to look beyond surface-level trends. AI will dramatically expand awareness of coaching and accelerate its adoption, but that is not the end point. The real shift will come afterwards.

As coaching becomes mainstream, the core skills that underpin it; suspending judgement, deep listening, and triggering fresh thinking, will stop being viewed as coaching techniques and become the baseline of effective human communication.
