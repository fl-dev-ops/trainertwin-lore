One of the most satisfying moments in the story is when Arthur stops being intimidated by “the process”.

He realises most of what people call sales process is just a series of conversations, with someone keeping track of what matters, what’s been agreed, and what needs to happen next.

Coaches are often excellent at the conversation, they just haven’t treated the conversation as business development.
