Scale is often treated as a virtue in learning programmes. In mastery work, it’s usually a liability.

When numbers grow, precision drops. Feedback becomes generic. The work shifts towards explanation rather than refinement.

That’s why Level 3 is deliberately small. The quality of attention matters more here than the size of the cohort.

Simplicity of Mastery™ Level 3 expressions of interest are open.
