The reason selling feels frightening for coaches is not because it’s hard.

It feels frightening because it threatens identity. Coaches want to be helpful, clean, and non-manipulative. Selling, in their imagination, is pushy, performative, and slightly grubby. So they either avoid it, or they do it with their shoulders up around their ears.

Arthur’s journey is basically a slow unravelling of that tension, not in coach-training language, but in lived moments where he realises, again and again, that the answers are in the conversation.
