Most coaches talk too much. The masters say almost nothing.

If you want to coach at mastery level, here is the truth nobody tells you.
It is not about techniques. It is about who you are when the client walks into the room.

Last week’s Simplicity of Mastery - Foundations tutorial was a perfect reminder of this.

A coach brought a case where the client had been diagnosed with ADHD. The moment she read the client’s previous session notes on the platform, her presence collapsed. Her mind filled with doubt. She shifted from “meeting the human in front of me” to “trying to reconcile a label, a story, and a contradiction.”

And this is where mastery begins.

Coaching at mastery level is the ability to stay clean, stay empty, stay connected, even when your mind wants to run off with assumptions, labels or prior data. Anyone can ask questions. Anyone can follow a model. Very few can drop all of that and coach the human being who shows up right now.

When we stripped the situation back, the reality became simple.
Labels do not coach.
History does not coach.
Only presence coaches.

What mattered were the behaviours the client wanted to change, the impact those behaviours were having, and the future she wanted to create. That is the heart of coaching. That is mastery.
