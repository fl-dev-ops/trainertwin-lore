Next week I’ll be speaking to International Coaching Federation  coaches across America, courtesy of ICF Colorado on a topic that always raises eyebrows.
“Competency 5, the One You Can Ignore.”

The title is provocative for a reason.
Most coaches treat the ICF Core Competencies as a checklist. But when you step into master-level coaching, something different happens. The competencies stop behaving like separate boxes. They become a single, interwoven field of listening, presence, intention and energetic awareness.

That is where the real work happens.
And that is where Competency 5 quietly sits at the centre of everything.

In this session, I’ll reveal how the competencies fuse together. Why silent coaching works. And how deep, clean intention triggers fresh thinking in the client without techniques, tricks or tools.

If you want to coach at the level where the conversation feels effortless, spacious and profoundly human, join us next week.

Mastery is not more. It is less.
Less noise. Less technique. More thinking from the client.

Looking forward to seeing many of you there.
