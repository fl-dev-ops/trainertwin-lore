Listening is the pre-eminent coaching skill. 
And you don’t need video for that. 

If we go back just ten or fifteen years all coaching was either in person or by phone. 

In training coaches I used platforms like GoToMeeting for remote sessions but we always turned video off for coaching practice. 

With audio only you just have your listening and your connection. 

It is different from face to face but both work equally well. 

And we have to remember that video can introduce false information as you can’t  see the context of all physical change. 

A key step in elevating coaching skills to mastery is waaaay deeper listening, and video inhibits that.

If you want to bring mastery into your coaching look at simplicityofmastery.com
