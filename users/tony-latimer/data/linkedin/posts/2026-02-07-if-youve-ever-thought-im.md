If you’ve ever thought, “I’m great once a client is in front of me, I just can’t get them in front of me,” you’ll recognise Arthur.

He’s not incompetent. He’s stuck in dread, avoidance, and the strange rituals we all develop when we’re trying not to face something. Sunday afternoons, pizza, beer, the long dark teatime of the soul.

Then he gets a glimpse of a different way, and the story becomes about reclaiming agency through better conversations.
