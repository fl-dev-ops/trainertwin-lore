Selling Yourself to Corporates is not a textbook. It’s a story.

It follows Arthur, a tired, cynical salesperson in the mid-90s, who slowly stumbles into something that should have been obvious all along; selling works when you learn how to uncover what people want, what they care about, and what problem they’re actually trying to solve. In other words, it works when you stop pushing and start listening properly.

The point of the story is not to give you a script. It’s to let you recognise that your coaching skills already contain the core of what makes selling work.
