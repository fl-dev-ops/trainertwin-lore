Most leadership failures don’t come from incompetence.
They come from promotion.

We promote people for being brilliant individual contributors, then act surprised when they struggle in a role that requires a completely different way of thinking.

It’s a bit like rewarding someone for being an excellent violinist and then handing them a conductor’s baton on Monday morning.

When someone steps into a bigger leadership role, or manages people for the first time, there are two things they almost never get, and almost always need:

Fast, practical learning on how to lead at the next level, not theory, not personality tests, but the core thinking and behaviours that make leadership work.
Short, sharp coaching support to help them translate that learning into real decisions, real conversations, and real authority in the role.

Most organisations provide neither.
They provide congratulations, a job title, and best wishes.

That’s why I’ve created TonyLatimer.ai. It exists to support exactly these moments of transition, when the cost of getting it wrong is high, and the upside of getting it right is enormous. (And it's free)

If you’re promoting someone into a critical role, the smart question isn’t “Are they ready?”
It’s “What would a focused 90-day leadership coaching runway do for their chances of success?”

Often, that small intervention makes all the difference.
