One of the strangest things about mastery is that it often feels like doing less, not more.

Many PCC coaches are already capable of MCC-level work in moments, but they don’t yet trust those moments enough to stay with them. They add questions, explanations, or frameworks that dilute the impact.

Level 3 is about learning to recognise when less is not only sufficient, but essential.
