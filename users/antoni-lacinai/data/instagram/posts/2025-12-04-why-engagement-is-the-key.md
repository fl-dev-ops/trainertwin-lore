speaker_1: Sometime ago, I was asked to give a keynote to 200 executives at a company in Eastern Europe. And I wanted to talk about employee engagement because I know that engagement is the key to unlock performance. But they said, we already did this engagement stuff. Now it's about getting shit done. And of course, you get shit done if people are engaged, at least on a sustainable level. You can make people perform really well by making them afraid. Sure, you can do that. It works for a while until it doesn't. We are at this crossroads right now where some managers who are insecure, who are afraid of losing control, will do everything they can to go back into leading with a clenched fist instead of an open hand. I believe that's wrong. I believe that will backfire and they will have big trouble when people are leaving and going to places where they actually want to perform because they adhere to the vision, they adhere to the purpose, and they like their boss who likes them back. So, if you can, make people work because they want to, not because they have to. And that means that you as a leader will work more on how you can inspire, how you can listen, how you can be present, and how you can get and have their back.
