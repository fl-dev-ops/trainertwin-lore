speaker_1: Are you using management by seagulls or management by duck moms? I was keynoting in Rovinj, Croatia a few years ago at an HR event and Rovinj is a beautiful little medieval town. And the guide said to us when we went around there, watch out for the seagulls. They are screaming, noisy, they fly down, steal your food and fly away. And I thought to myself, that's a perfect example of an asshole boss. Barging into a meeting, yelling at everyone, stealing your ideas and dash off to the next one. Don't be a seagull, be a duck mom, caring for all the ducklings, making sure that you all reach the same destination together.
