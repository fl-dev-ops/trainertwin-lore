The management team is dead. That's what one of my clients said when I talked to him and coached him. He said, I have a management team, I have people that report to me, and I put them in the same meeting, and they have nothing in common because they have different business areas, and they just report, and it's a waste of time. I realized it, they realized it, so I skipped these meetings. Those management teams meetings are gone. And now I just meet them one by one and everyone is just more happy. Why is that a problem for so many companies? It's because it's not really a management team. It's a management group. A group of people that has nothing in common, that doesn't help each other, that doesn't use the collective intelligence of the group to sort out issues and problems and challenges. If you do want to be a management team, work together. Then you will have that strength of the collective intelligence and you can help each other out. Those management teams are very much alive.
