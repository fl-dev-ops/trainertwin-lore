speaker_1: I was recently giving a keynote in Vilnius, Lithuania for about 1500 or so HR professionals. And I talked about my favorite topic, how we communicate with each other. And the three communication superpowers, empathy, clarity, energy. But after me came Dr. Martin Gutman, and he talked about attitude and mindset, which I absolutely love because otherwise it would be too technical if it's only about how you act and how you do things. The superpower that he talked about, the hidden superpower is humility, to be humble, to realize that you need everyone to pitch in, you need the help of everyone to make things happen. It takes a village and that you don't have all the answers yourself. So yes, keep going with the three superpowers if you want to influence people, if you want to build relationships, if you want to get along, if you want to make an impact. But never forget, be humble as well. Make sure that you use the collective intelligence of your team and not only being a bottleneck trying to figure it out by yourself.
