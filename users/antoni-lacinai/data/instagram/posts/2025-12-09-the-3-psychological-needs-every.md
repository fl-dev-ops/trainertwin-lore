What does the individual person in your team want? Apart from a salary, roof over their heads, food on their table and so on. Well, they also want the ABC of psychological needs. They want autonomy, the mandate to decide on things without you breathing down their neck. They want a sense of belonging so that they feel that they contribute to their tribe and they want to show and grow their competence so that they're always significant and can help out. Give them the ABC. I have found when I've been coaching leaders and teams around the world that the best teams, they have this figured out. They give their people autonomy, they create a sense of belonging and they also grow their competence. If you do the same thing, you will have a high performing team and that counts for something, doesn't it?
