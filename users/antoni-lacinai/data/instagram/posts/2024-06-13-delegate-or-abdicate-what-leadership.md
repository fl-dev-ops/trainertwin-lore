### Speaker 1 · Speaker 1

There's a fine balance between delegating and abdicating if you're a leader. Today, I will spend some two hours with 60 executives in Finland for a company. We will talk about this. How to create a culture of innovation? Meaning that you delegate, you coach, you trust, but you don't abdicate. If you say to them, you have this responsibility, it doesn't mean that you can take away your own responsibility. If you say, you have this accountability, it doesn't mean that you can take away your accountability. You are the boss, and 70% of the reasons for them to be engaged is because of you. So, find that ground where you delegate, where you coach, where you trust, without abdicating, saying you're on your own. That's a good sweet spot.
