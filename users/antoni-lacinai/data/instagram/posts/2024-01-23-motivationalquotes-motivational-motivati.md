speaker_1: If you're stuck when it comes to a task of some time and you go to your manager and the manager is not there, what do you do then? How would you solve the problem if he or she wasn't there? Sometimes I myself had that question from from people when I was a manager back in the days when I still had a normal job. And I asked sometimes, what would you do if I wasn't here? And you would be surprised how many people came up with really good solutions to it. Because sometimes they just needed to bounce the ideas back and forth. And that's fine. But if we have enough trusted people, we could ask this question. What would you do if I wasn't here? And probably at least 50% of the time, the answer is right there. So don't be too quick if you're the manager to just provide and serve a solution, because then they will never grow. You need to help them sometimes also think by themselves. Not all the time, but many times. So think about that question. What would you do if I wasn't here?
