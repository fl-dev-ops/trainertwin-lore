## Transcript

speaker_1: What do you do if somebody in a meeting for instance has spoken at length, presenting long, boring slides, and you don't really know what the message is. And they expect you perhaps to take a decision. I've found a number of ways to deal with that, especially when I was a moderator at a number of events. One way is to say, what do you mean with what you just said? This forces them to focus and give you a proper answer. A version of that is to say, if you would have summarized everything you said into one or two sentences, what would you say? Again, forces them to be clear in their head and deliver a coherent, clear message. Try this one. I promise you, it will help you and the other person as well.
