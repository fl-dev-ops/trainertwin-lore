If you know your WHY, then you can articulate and explain your “BECAUSE”

It feels really good to have figured out your Why. It is so much easier to decide, to choose, to say No - or Yes. And even to explain your Why to others. 

Purpose gives you motivation, forward-thinking, hope, and perseverance. 

I remember not too many years ago when I felt low. Having spent my adult life finding clues to human communication, especially at the workplace, and then realizing that it is not the most important thing you can do... it was tough. But then I decided to stay true to my higher purpose and make sure I help clients who are good and kind and most of all sustainable. To act as a sherpa to the right companies, the ones who aren't ruthless to our nature and us living on this planet. It was comforting to bring clarity and hopefully be part of the solution instead of the problem. 

What is your Why?
