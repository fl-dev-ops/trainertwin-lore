When I speak about the three communicative superpowers, energy, empathy and clarity, and I explain that empathy is number one out of those three. I often get questions from the audience saying, okay, empathy is number one, but what about psychopaths? And what do you do with them? And how can they work with empathy and so on? Are there different kinds of empathy? And my simple explanation to this is, the way I explain it is that you have two kinds of empathy here. You have empathy by calculation and empathy by compassion. Empathy by calculation is when you just anticipate, you anticipate people's reactions. And it's good, you need that of course, in order to survive as a species and so on. Unless you have that type of calculation, you might be very gullible and very naive and then perhaps you get killed, you know, in ancient times at least. Empathy by calculation can be done by computers today. You have different systems who kind of look into the way, the tonality of how you say things and they have something called effective computing. So they can anticipate you. But empathy with compassion is something else. That's deeply human. Now you're really trying to understand another person and you want them well. So when you have those two different ones, empathy by calculation, that might be the psychopath or the robot way, but empathy with compassion, that's the human way. So if you want to be great leaders, you might want to have some calculation going on, but you should put your emphasis on compassion.
