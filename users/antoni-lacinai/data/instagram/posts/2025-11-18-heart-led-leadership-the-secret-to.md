## Caption
Heart-Led Leadership: The Secret to True Influence 

Or:
Skip the clenched fists and offer a helping hand instead. 

What are your thoughts on this?

## Transcript

speaker_1: How about moving your leadership from hands to head to heart? We used to lead hands. If one person got broken down or got sick, replace them with a new pair of hands. If you want to increase productivity, more hands. If somebody protested, fire them, new hands. That doesn't work anymore. Go from hands all the way via head, which is where the strategic thinking is going, all the way to the heart. People will perform much better if they feel that you care. Care for them, care for the value, the mission, the vision, and so on. Care for the people. If they feel that you got their back, if they feel that you care, they will care too. So stop leading with a clenched fist and start leading with a helping hand, using your heart as your compass.
