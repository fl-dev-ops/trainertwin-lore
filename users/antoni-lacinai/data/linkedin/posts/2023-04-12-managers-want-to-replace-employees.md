### Mats Lewan (Responsible E-mobility at Power Circle AB)
> A part from being an example of lousy leadership when replacing people with AI, here's a reason for being careful even if you're a cold blooded capitalist: When you automate, you always risk losing competence. If you're automating limited manual tasks the consequences are manageable. With the current AI-driven automation it is different. ChatGPT has certainly proven impressively competent, but it doesn't have the human capability of relating to other people and to understand how to put new knowledge into a new human context. This means that you will become vulnerable to change. For this reason, underestimating the need for humans could turn out to be an unpleasant and very costly mistake in the long run, from a pure business and revenue perspective.
