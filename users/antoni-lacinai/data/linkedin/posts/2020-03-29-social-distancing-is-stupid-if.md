SOCIAL DISTANCING IS STUPID!

If you are like me, and believe that the WORDS WE USE MATTER, then why do we call it social distancing? We need to be socially closer than ever, with our older relatives for instance who we can't meet in person right now. We should rename the whole thing to physical distancing instead. Do you agree?

PHYSICAL DISTANCING IS SMART!

Let's keep it that way, to flatten the curve. Stay close socially - Stay apart physically

Thank you fellow speaker Hasse Carlsson who brought this to my attention
