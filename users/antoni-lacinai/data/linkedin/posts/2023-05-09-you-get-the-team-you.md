You get the team you deSERVE

Your team is not there for you. You are there for your team.
The customers are not there for you. You are there for the customers. 

Do you have a servant mentality? How does it show? An example:
Recently I drove my car to give a keynote at an international company's kickoff. I GPS'd my way through the pouring rain and drove to the reception where there were parking spaces next to it. But - those spaces were only reserved for the executives within the company. I had to keep searching and finally found a parking lot some distance from the building, then ran back and got soaking wet when I arrived. It felt like this fine company missed out on something important...

Many of you know that I often return to the superpower of Empathy and how important it is. Empathic managers create more engagement, which leads to better results. But empathy is also the most fragile of all superpowers. Especially for a manager who can suddenly feel superior to his employees. Unfortunately, it happens that the promoted manager demands a nice corner room, fresh fruit baskets, business class on everything, and of course the nearest parking lot. Not good.

Everything we do sends signals. Everything communicates.

As soon as that manager becomes arrogant and superior in his (yes, often a man) attitude, employee engagement erodes. Dissatisfaction spreads and performance deteriorates. Quiet quitting becomes a fact, or the boss ends up moving on, unaware of what just happened.

Remember dear boss: 
Your team is not there for you. You are there for your team.
The customers are not there for you. You are there for the customers.

It may be wise to look at your role with new eyes and ask yourself who you are there to serve. If you can find a sense of service then you will be a much better manager and a happier person. 

You get the team you deSERVE.

Thanks for reading my mind
