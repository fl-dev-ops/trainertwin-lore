We'd rather be "above the line" than below the line.

Our thoughts and attitudes matter. 
Here is the theory behind the expression:

There's a transformative concept within leadership that can elevate your team's achievements and drive your personal growth. It's known as "Above the Line" thinking, and it's a catalyst for success.

"Above the Line" thinkers are proactive, accountable, and solution-oriented. They view challenges as opportunities and flourish in a culture of responsibility and progress.

Conversely, "Below the Line" thinking leads to stagnation, characterized by blame, excuses, and resistance to change. We have all met people who are constantly negative. It drains the energy from the group. 

It's like having a growth mindset (there is always room for development and new learnings) vs a fixed mindset (I am what I am and nothing will ever change)

Ask yourself these 5 questions:
1. 𝗪𝗵𝗮𝘁 𝗺𝗼𝘁𝗶𝘃𝗮𝘁𝗲𝘀 𝗺𝗲?
2. 𝗗𝗼 𝗜 𝗵𝗮𝘃𝗲 𝗺𝗲𝗮𝗻𝗶𝗻𝗴𝗳𝘂𝗹 𝗴𝗼𝗮𝗹𝘀?
3. 𝗗𝗼 𝗜 𝗵𝗮𝘃𝗲 𝗮𝗻 𝗼𝗽𝘁𝗶𝗺𝗶𝘀𝘁𝗶𝗰 𝗼𝘂𝘁𝗹𝗼𝗼𝗸?
4. 𝗪𝗵𝗮𝘁 𝗰𝗮𝗻 𝗜 𝗱𝗼 𝘁𝗼𝗱𝗮𝘆 𝘁𝗼 𝗴𝗲𝘁 𝗺𝗼𝗺𝗲𝗻𝘁𝘂𝗺?
5. 𝗜𝗳 𝘁𝗵𝗲 𝘀𝗵𝗶𝘁 𝗵𝗶𝘁𝘀 𝘁𝗵𝗲 𝗳𝗮𝗻, 𝘄𝗵𝗮𝘁 𝗶𝘀 𝗺𝘆 𝗽𝗹𝗮𝗻 𝘁𝗼 𝗼𝘃𝗲𝗿𝗰𝗼𝗺𝗲 𝗶𝘁?

The worst thing that can happen if you anticipate success and you fail, is that you have been unnecessarily happy for a longer time!
