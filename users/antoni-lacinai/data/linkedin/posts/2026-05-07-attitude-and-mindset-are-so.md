Attitude and mindset are so important for your well-being when you lead, serve, and collaborate—the ability to believe in people—and yourself.

In one of the Hotel Marigold movies, one character says, "Don't worry, it will all be good at the end. And if it isn't good, it isn't the end."

A great line, but not necessarily true. However, it sure helps if you believe in your goals and purpose, and in people, and why not the whole planet? 

What do you think? Or - what do you believe? 
How much of your success is your skill level, and how much is your attitude?
