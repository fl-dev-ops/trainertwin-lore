Hybrid = Digital - with a twist 

Many of the organizations I support are taking their company culture very seriously. I like that. Defining who you are or who you want to be is valuable. The risk I see during 2022 is how to foster a common culture in a hybrid workplace. 

So, it's about culture - even in a hybrid setting:
- Communication culture
- Organization culture
- Leadership culture

How do you foster a great culture regardless of where your team is located?

It's about making sure that you include everyone, you praise everyone, you don't have a proximity bias and promote only the people next to you, you keep using digital tools.
