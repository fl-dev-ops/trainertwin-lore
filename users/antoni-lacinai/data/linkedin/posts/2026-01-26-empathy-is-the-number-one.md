Empathy is the number one superpower — but also the most fragile.

When people feel understood, they open up. When they feel seen, they engage. Empathy narrows the distance between people.

In my experience, you win both loyalty from the team and business from your clients by showing real interest and care. The impact is significant.

But empathy is fragile. It is the first thing that disappears the moment you feel entitled. 
• A better title
• The corner office
• The best parking space
• A sense that you “deserve” more

The moment you put yourself above others, people sense it. And they step back.
If you want loyalty, trust, and true engagement—keep your empathy close. 

Empathy: Guard it. Show it. Share it. 


The three communication superpowers are:
Empathy - so that people feel that you care
Clarity - so that they understand and remember
Energy, so that they believe that you believe
