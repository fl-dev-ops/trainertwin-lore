Do you have mismatched people in your team?
Probably...

More than 90% of workers seek employers who care for their well-being. By forcing everyone back to the office five days a week, some organizations send a signal that they don't care. On the other hand, by being so clear, it is much easier to turn these employers down directly. Muddy communication is worse since it can easily create false expectations.

Caring doesn't mean that one can accommodate every whim and wish from everyone, all the time. It's more about listening, involving, and respecting differences and always aiming for a win/win solution. It's about defining the company values and the culture you want to have. 

1. Explain your goal and your purpose, for instance on how you view flexible work
2. listen with the intent to understand
3. Communicate clearly what you can or cannot do,  will or won't do, accept and don't accept
4. Show that you care and respect people even if you disagree. Try to find common ground.
5. Implement changes.
6. Follow up and communicate progress
7. Handle people who refuse to follow

did I miss anything?
