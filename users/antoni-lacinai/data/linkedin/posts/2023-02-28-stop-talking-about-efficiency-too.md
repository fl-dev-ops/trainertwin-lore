STOP TALKING ABOUT EFFICIENCY 

Too many leaders put too much focus on efficiency. This creates one-dimensional leadership which can lead to stress, anxiety, and burnout. 

So I developed this simple formula called E=ExE for leaders and teams who are fed up with not getting the real impact they deserve but instead want to have more fun at work and still get good things done.
