The way leaders communicate will impact the whole organization

When leaders distrust, people will prove them right 
When leaders trust, people will prove them right too

The article shows what happens when leaders are scared of losing control. I feel for them. And their employees. 

It is not easy being a manager or an executive. Anyone who tried knows this. The burden of hitting targets is there all the time. And they are accountable.

At the same time, these "leaders" feel that they are losing control when employees want a better work-life balance. They lack trust in people doing their job if they are not physically in an office, or at their desk.
