Is your communication affecting your mood? Or…
…Is your mood affecting your communication?

Imagine a traffic light in your brain. 
Red light is danger
Yellow light is drive
Green light is decompression 

Depending on which color is dominant, you will have very different communication styles and culture. You will have a different state of mind. 

Here’s a snippet from a virtual keynote I recently did for business leaders in India. But they apply to anyone since they are deeply human. 

What color is your brain? 
Your colleagues?
Your customers?
