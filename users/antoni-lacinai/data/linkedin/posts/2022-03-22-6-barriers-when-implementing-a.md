6 BARRIERS when implementing a STRATEGY. Which are they?

My latest column to 80,000 HR professionals and managers. In Swedish but here is the gist when it comes to the 6 barriers I have identified:
1. Poor communication
2. Poor motivation
3. Unclear goals
4. Unclear roles
5. Bad meetings
6. Bad greetings

I could have settled with the first bullet since it contains all of the rest...

Anything to add to the list?
