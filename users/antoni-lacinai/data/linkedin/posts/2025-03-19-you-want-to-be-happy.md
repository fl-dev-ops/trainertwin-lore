You want to be happy?
Hang out with happy people ^_^

I get energy from positive, happy people, but I get drained when I spend too much time with constant complainers. Do you feel the same way?

𝗘𝗺𝗼𝘁𝗶𝗼𝗻𝘀 𝗮𝗿𝗲 𝗰𝗼𝗻𝘁𝗮𝗴𝗶𝗼𝘂𝘀. 

Research by Christakis and Fowler found that happy people tend to be at the center of their social networks, which consist of large clusters of other happy people. 

𝗘𝗮𝗰𝗵 𝗮𝗱𝗱𝗶𝘁𝗶𝗼𝗻𝗮𝗹 𝗵𝗮𝗽𝗽𝘆 𝗳𝗿𝗶𝗲𝗻𝗱 𝗶𝗻𝗰𝗿𝗲𝗮𝘀𝗲𝘀 𝘆𝗼𝘂𝗿 𝗽𝗿𝗼𝗯𝗮𝗯𝗶𝗹𝗶𝘁𝘆 𝗼𝗳 𝗵𝗮𝗽𝗽𝗶𝗻𝗲𝘀𝘀 𝗯𝘆 𝗮𝗯𝗼𝘂𝘁 𝟵 𝗽𝗲𝗿𝗰𝗲𝗻𝘁.

So your happiness is related to the happiness of your friends, their friends, and their friends’ friends, etc.
