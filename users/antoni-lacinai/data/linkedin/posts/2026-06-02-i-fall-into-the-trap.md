I fall into the trap of using fancy words. But it is not a smart thing to do.  

Recently, I spoke with a client, and we discussed a lost business deal. I asked if they had done a 𝘱𝘰𝘴𝘵-𝘮𝘰𝘳𝘵𝘦𝘮. "What does that mean?" they asked, and I cursed myself. 
"I mean, have you analyzed what went wrong?" I said. 
Even more embarrassing is that this happened right after my lecture to them on communicating with clarity... 

I see an acronym popping up: KISS. Keep It Simple, Stupid. 

Let's digest Stupid. It could be two things
1. 𝗖𝗼𝗻𝗱𝗲𝘀𝗮𝗻𝗱𝗶𝗻𝗴𝗹𝘆 𝘀𝗶𝗺𝗽𝗹𝗲. Don't treat me like I am five years old. 
2. 𝗢𝘃𝗲𝗿𝗹𝘆 𝗰𝗼𝗺𝗽𝗹𝗶𝗰𝗮𝘁𝗲𝗱. Don't hide your own confusion behind corporate BS. 

And now, Cornell has proven that people who use, or are impressed by, corporate jargon make worse decisions. Yes, worse. I call this The Clarity Gap. 

This article will make me redouble my own efforts. What about you? Do you also have a soft spot for words like 'optimize,' 'monetize,' 'proactive,' 'synergetic,' 'holistic,' 'efficiency,'...?

Then you know what to do. Simplify. Or go full KITNS (Keep It Simple, Not Stupid) 😄 I am here to help you if you want. Just say the word.
