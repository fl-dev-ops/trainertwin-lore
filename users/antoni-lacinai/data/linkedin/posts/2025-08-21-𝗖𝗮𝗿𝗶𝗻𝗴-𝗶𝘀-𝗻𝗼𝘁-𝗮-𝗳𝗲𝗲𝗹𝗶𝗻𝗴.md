I have found that much of my motivation is about taking action. To do. It creates energy. When it comes to caring for others, it is not enough to think about it; we have to act on it to make a positive difference. 

As a leader, what if you:

• Allocate time for real connections, not just checklists?
• Celebrate acts of kindness, not just wins?
• Make people expect kindness? 

This is not about adding one more thing to the to-do list. 
This 𝘪𝘴 the to-do list.

If you want teams that are engaged, creative, and loyal, start by making kindness the norm, not the exception. My best managers I had (when I still had a normal job) were all caring and kind. It didn't mean soft, and it didn't mean weak. They are not synonyms. 

And as a communication nerd like me, please remember: 
Trust is built when caring is in your actions, not in your PowerPoint slides

Do you care? How do people notice?
