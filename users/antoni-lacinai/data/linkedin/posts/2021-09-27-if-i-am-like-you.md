If I am like you I tend to like you

If I am not like you I tend to not like you

Going into a flexible hybrid work-situation we might risk a new kind of Us vs them i.e WE in the office, and They out there... somewhere. 

We all need a sense of belonging, to be part of something bigger than ourselves, like an idea, and a tribe. To feel isolated and excluded is a hurtful experience. I have felt it. I am sure you have too.

How do we expand the WE and US in order to minimize the "them"?
