How good are you at leading engaging meetings?
And do you get the effect you want?

Together with gr8meetings, I created a self-assessment to determine the effect you get from your meetings. 

10 Yes/No questions on Efficiency
10 Yes/No questions on Energy
Maximum effect-score = 100

This will be your subjective perception. If you are like most meeting leaders, you will think that you are much better than you really are. But it's a good start. 

We spend an lot of time in meetings…

Let's prepare them well
Let's run them well
Feel the joy!

You got this!
