Will you choose to lose money or to lose your soul?

In other words:  Are your company's CORE VALUES for real or for show?

Back in February I was asked if I could give a keynote to a Russian bank later this spring. I said yes. Then you-know-what happened. So I said no. Then a friend asked me if my new decision would made me lose money. I answered that "Yes, I will lose money. But I won't lose my soul."  

Right now we see  many large companies closing shop in Russia. 
Will they lose money? Yes. 
Will they lose their soul? No. 
Is it smart in the long run? Yes. 
I applaud those companies who follow their core values* when put to the test. 

We see how the world of sports is uniting against Russia and their puppet state Belarus. I am guessing they will lose some sponsor money, but again, not their soul

I understand that it's harder to say no - if you can't afford it. I really do. If you need the dough to feed your family, then you make decisions based on short term survival. But if you do have the cash and you still go for the short term wins (while selling your soul) then you're just mean and greedy. 

When have you acted in line with your values? When have you not? How did it feel? True leadership is here and now. 

#leadership #communication #engagement

*I see core values as a business "Identity goal" (Who do we want to be?) The only way for those values to mean something is when they are paired with a wanted behavior. "If X happens, we do Y" or "When A occurs, we will go for B"

One more thing. The opposite side have the same reasoning. I am sure they feel that yes, they will lose money, admiration and respect but not their soul i.e. the nostalgic vision of a  former Soviet union. They just act according to a totally different view of reality
