Another year is soon in the books. But what about 2023?

As a guest columnist at the event magazine Eventeffect, I look into my crystal ball and see six possible trends for both the event industry and businesses in general. In short:
1. Continous trend of shorter time from booking a conference, to having it.
2. Downturns in  the economy will make some organizations hesitant of having physical events
3. Quiet quitting will keep puzzling leaders
4. Engaged employees will be even more important in tough times
5. Hybrid workplaces and meetings will still be a thing next year. 
6. Best service and tech skills at conference providers will be winners.

What do you predict?
