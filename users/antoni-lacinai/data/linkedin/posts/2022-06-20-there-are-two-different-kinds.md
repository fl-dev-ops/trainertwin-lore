THERE ARE TWO DIFFERENT KINDS OF EMPATHY – AND HERE IS WHY THEY MATTER.

”If empathy is so important, why are there so many psychopaths in high leadership roles?”
I got that question from the audience after one of my keynotes. Here is my response:

There are two kinds of empathy:

First: you have Empathy paired with Calculation. That’s when a person* can detect and anticipate other’s state of mind and their reactions, in a logical, non-emotional way. Some psychopaths excel in this and become master manipulators. Sooner or later, they get exposed for what they are, but then it might be too late. Look at some dictators for instance. 

Second: you have Empathy paired with Compassion. This is when a person can tune in to your true emotions with an attitude of care and even love. I would put Nelson Mandela in that corner. A psychopath, on the other hand, lacks this ability. 

When I talk about empathy being a communicative superpower, I actually talk about both. The first is for your survival. If you can anticipate an opponent’s next move, you have a bigger chance of avoiding death (or humiliation, deception, being fooled, etc.) The second is for you who want to be a good-hearted, wise, and kind leader who genuinely care for your people and hopefully for the whole planet’s ecosystem. 

So the questions are: 
-	Does the leader really care? About you, your colleagues, clients, etc. 
-	How can you tell? 
-	How does he/she treat you when things go right or wrong? 
-	Is there something “off” about them? 
-	Do they feel authentic? 
-	Are they kind? 
-	Do they listen to understand instead of respond?

If you are a leader, ask yourself the same questions. 

Thanks for reading my mind
