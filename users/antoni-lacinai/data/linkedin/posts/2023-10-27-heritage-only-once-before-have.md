Heritage…

Only once before have I visited my father’s home country. That was about 30 years ago. They had just abandoned the political doctrine to be a democracy. It was a poor country but I loved it and all the warm, generous people I met. 

Now, finally, I will go back. This time to speak at at great event in Tirana. 
It’s emotional. It’s good. It’s a heritage coming back to me.
