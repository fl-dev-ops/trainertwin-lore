Debate - Discussion - Dialogue
Why bother?

Knowing the difference between debate, discussion, and dialogue can improve your interactions. 

• 𝗗𝗲𝗯𝗮𝘁𝗲: A competitive exchange where the goal is to win. The focus is on proving a point.
• 𝗗𝗶𝘀𝗰𝘂𝘀𝘀𝗶𝗼𝗻: A cooperative exchange of ideas and information. The aim is to reach a common understanding or solution.
• 𝗗𝗶𝗮𝗹𝗼𝗴𝘂𝗲: A collaborative conversation focused on mutual understanding. This involves deep listening, empathy, and openness without the need to agree.

𝟯 𝘁𝗶𝗽𝘀 𝘁𝗼 𝗸𝗲𝗲𝗽 𝗰𝗼𝗺𝗺𝘂𝗻𝗶𝗰𝗮𝘁𝗶𝗼𝗻 𝗺𝗼𝗿𝗲 𝗰𝗶𝘃𝗶𝗹

➔ 𝗣𝗿𝗮𝗰𝘁𝗶𝗰𝗲 𝗔𝗰𝘁𝗶𝘃𝗲 𝗟𝗶𝘀𝘁𝗲𝗻𝗶𝗻𝗴: Show genuine interest by focusing on the speaker. Reflect and clarify to ensure understanding. Avoid distractions like phones and other devices.

➔ 𝗖𝘂𝗹𝘁𝗶𝘃𝗮𝘁𝗲 𝗘𝗺𝗽𝗮𝘁𝗵𝘆: Acknowledge the emotions behind the words. Put yourself in their shoes to understand their perspective. Respond with kindness even in disagreement.

➔ 𝗦𝗲𝘁 𝗖𝗹𝗲𝗮𝗿 𝗜𝗻𝘁𝗲𝗻𝘁𝗶𝗼𝗻𝘀: Aim for understanding rather than persuading. Be transparent about your goals for the conversation. Respect boundaries and revisit topics if needed.
