I’m traveling to refuel and recharge. And it feels strange…

How often du you set aside time to reflect? Or sharpen you skills?

Athletes compete perhaps 10% and train 90%. 

Office workers “compete” 99% and train maybe 1%.  I wonder what would happen if you would change that to 5%. That would be one day per month, or about 10 days per year (take away vacations, potential illness and unavoidable deadlines)

Would that decrease or increase wellness, productivity, meaning?

I am on my way to a five day business retreat.  Not as a facilitator but as a participant. It’s not something I would normally prioritize. I have this feeling of both anticipation and a guilty conscience. 

Weird… especially since I know about the power of recharging, refueling, rebooting. I see it in others every time I am on a stage or a conference room, or in a coaching situation. But when it’s my turn…

What are your thoughts? How do you upskill yourself?
