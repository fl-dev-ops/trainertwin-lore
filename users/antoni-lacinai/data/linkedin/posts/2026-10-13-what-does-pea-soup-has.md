What does pea soup has to with a job as a global speaker? Stay with me on this one. 

I am a global speaker who don’t like travelling…

I am not afraid of flying. Not at all. But I am afraid of missing connecting flights… the stress of running between terminals! I have recurring nightmares about it. 

But I love to arrive! 
I love to meet people
So the anxiety I feel when traveling is worth it. 

Its like pea soup and pancakes. In Sweden there is an old tradition that lunch on thursdays used to be pea soup and then pancakes. I don’t like pea soup. I love pancakes. 

The traveling is the pea soup. I endure

The audience are the pancakes. I devour 😄

So if you are in the audience on the 14th of October at HR week in Riga, know that when I give you my broadest smile, you are my pancakes. 

Dainius Baltrušaitis and Arturs C.    thanks for bringing me in and making the travel part a smooth experience. You rock!
