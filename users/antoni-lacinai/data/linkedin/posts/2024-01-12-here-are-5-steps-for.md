Here are 5 steps for a better workshop

and it is not until step 4 you actually run it

Great workshops are great!

→ You share ideas
→ You are aligned
→ You create energy and enthusiasm
→ You use the team's collective intelligence

But too many workshops are not workshops at all

❌ Poorly designed with too many PowerPoints
❌ Boring setup with too little involvement

✔️ Use these 5 steps as your framework
✔️ Then craft your step-by-step agenda
✔️ Then lead an amazing workshop

Your turn ^_^
