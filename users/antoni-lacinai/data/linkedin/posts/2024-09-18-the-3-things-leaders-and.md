The 3 things leaders and teams can learn from Balinese culture 

Greeting from Bali where I will soon speak at a conference. I am overwhelmed with impressions. Everything has (to me) hidden meanings; every statue, temple, color…

Here are three things i believe that leaders and teams can learn from Bali:

1. The energy you send will reflect back to you. Look at the statue in the picture. It is a guard and the saying is: if you enter the temple with negative energy, you’ll get an immediate headache. 
- How do you enter a meeting? 
- How do you greet your team in the morning?

2. You are always in luck (called Agad). For instance: If you miss a train, you were lucky that you could use your body to run in the first place. 
- What good can come out of a missed opportunity at work?
- How do you react to a coworker missing a deadline?

3. May all good be with you. In Balinese: ”Om swasti astu”. There is almost always a positive outlook and intention in Bali towards humans, gods and nature. 
- Do you believe in your team? 
- How do you show it? 

There we go. Something to reflect on 😃
