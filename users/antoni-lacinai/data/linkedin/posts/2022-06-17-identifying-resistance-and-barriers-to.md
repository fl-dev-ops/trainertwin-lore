IDENTIFYING RESISTANCE and BARRIERS TO DO YOUR JOB. Not easy but really useful. 

I remember when I was a deputy manager for a group of great marketers. It was a temp job for about a year since our manager was working on a special project. I told the group that I was aware of the time limit, but that I would tackle this as a ten-year assignment, just to make sure I didn't idle. 

Then I created a workshop that I called "The steeplechase course"
The whole point was for us to identify anything that stopped us from doing our jobs, or at least slowed us down. Very problem-focused and not something I recommend you to do if your group isn't in a safe place mentally. 
We listed the problems and voted on what was most urgent, important etc.
Then we decided who amongst us that could take on the different barriers. Some fell back on me, others they took responsibility for. 

It was a real wow experience and especially during the months to come when we every single week could report progress or problems. Very action-oriented.
