Times are ALWAYS uncertain

Ask people 100 years ago, after surviving a first world war, the Spanish flu, and entering the mother of all economic depressions if times were uncertain.

Yes, we live in times of uncertainty
Yes,  we also live in times of possibilities

In this Swedish column, I share 5 advice that can ease the worry and pain and hopefully bring some optimism back. We sure need it.  

In short:
1. Acknowledge the worry
2. Be transparent and clear
3. involve more than you inform
4. Care for your customers
5. lead by example

What would you add to the list?
