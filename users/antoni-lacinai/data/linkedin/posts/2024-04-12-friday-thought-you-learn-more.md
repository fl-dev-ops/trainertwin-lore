Friday thought: 
You learn more when you listen than when you speak...

Do you want to understand another person?

• Ask them questions
• Check if you understood
• Still that inner voice of yours
• Let them do most of the talking 

I feel that this is often a challenge. I want to jump in, take over, talk over... Not good. 
Better to listen - to understand, not to respond

This is my belief. What about you?
