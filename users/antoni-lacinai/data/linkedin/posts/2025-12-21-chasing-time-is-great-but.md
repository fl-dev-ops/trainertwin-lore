Chasing time is great. But not always. And not with your family over the holidays.

My dad lives a few hours' drive from me. When I visit him, I do this: I pick him up, take him to a shopping mall where I can charge my car's battery while we're there. Now we have an hour to drink coffee and chat away. Then I drive him home. 

Very efficient

But why? He is 84 years old. I can't calculate years with him anymore. I should calculate the few times I can still meet him... It is becoming very real. And here I am, chasing time...

At work, it is a different story. I read that Grammarly surveyed managers, claiming that misunderstandings cost a full day per week in lost productivity. That is huge! Another study showed that clear communication made us some 25-30% more productive. These two go hand in hand. 

Of course we should be effective and efficient. We want to be productive and get good results. But I wonder: Must we bring this constant pressure and stress into our private lives?

We should work hard and smart to save time on work processes. 
Not at home with friends and family.  

I wish all of you who will take some time off, a restful, time-spending holiday with your loved ones. I'll take two weeks off myself. See you next year.

^_^
//Antoni
