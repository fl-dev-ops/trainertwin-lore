Friday thought
“It is much easier to be complicated than to be simple. 

It’s also lazy”

Level 1: You are simple because you don't know better

Level 2: You complicate the shit out of things because you know more

Level 3: You are simple, but now with substance. 

It is harder, but it is worth it. I promise.
