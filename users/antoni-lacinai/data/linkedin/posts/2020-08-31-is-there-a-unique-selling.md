Is there a Unique Selling Point if you want to create an OUTSTANDING CUSTOMER EXPERIENCE?

It is. It is so obvious, yet so many people miss out on it...

DO WHAT YOU SAY YOU WILL DO!

I’ll share two examples from car dealers, when I was the customer. Or at least tried to be …

A few years ago, different car dealers called me on the phone (they saw that my lease for the car I had was about to end). They asked if I was interested in a proposal. I said yes. They said that they would return with proposals within short.
I am still waiting…

Meanwhile I went to another car dealer. After twenty minutes, a sales man finally acknowledged my existence. I told him iI was thinking about changing my car to a newer model since my lease was about to end. He said he would return with a proposal the following day. I sent clear buying signals.
I am still waiting...

These examples was from three years ago. I have now had the pleasure of meeting some new sales reps at car dealers. These new people were way better at this and now I have ordered a new car. 

DO WHAT YOU SAY YOU WILL DO

This will put you ahead of the crowd. It shouldn't, but it will.

Do you agree? What else should be in place?

// Antoni Lacinai
