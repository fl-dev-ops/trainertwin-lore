Employee engagement is not about happiness... only.

Just to clarify: Happiness is amazing and something to strive for. I mean, what's the point if you don't feel good? But employee engagement is much more. It is about  determination,  motivation and  perseverance. 

Back in the days when I was employed, I was working for a global company that had the word "Perseverance" as one the their core values. 
When I look at that company I must say that I am proud to have worked there and they really adhered to that word. 

How do you create engagement at your workplace?
