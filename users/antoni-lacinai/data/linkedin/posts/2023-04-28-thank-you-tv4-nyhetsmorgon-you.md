Thank you TV4 Nyhetsmorgon. You are incredibly service minded! Always a pleasure!

Do you feel manipulated? Here are some techniques that people use...
They give to get
They aim big by asking for small things
They start big to get what they really wanted
They lie
They make you say Yes until you can't say no
And so on

By being able to spot this you can preempt your response. Personally, I am a sucker and just buy anyway - with a smile on my face many times ^_^, knowing that they are playing me. 

In Swedish: Ett nöje som vanligt att hänga med vännerna på Nyhetsmorgon. Denna gång om manipulation. 
Om en månad är jag tillbaka och pratar om hur man ger dåliga nyheter.
