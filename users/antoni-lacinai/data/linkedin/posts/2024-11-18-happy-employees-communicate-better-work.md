Happy employees communicate better, work harder, and stay longer. So why aren't they happy?

We know that happy people will perform better. Joy and satisfaction are directly tied to productivity in the workplace. All numbers point in this direction.

What if we focus on greater job satisfaction and lower turnover rates? Just look around you. Can you see happy employees? They are the ones who are smiling, walking with a bounce, making others feel good, etc. They are super-spreaders of joy. 

here are three things to do
