What am I missing? 
What can’t I see?

Got my pizza delivered by this cute bot. But not until the waitress moved it from the counter to the bot and typed my table number. She was 10 steps from me. Instead of being served by a human, I got this bot. 

So tell me what I am missing. 
Was it just a gimmick? Are they trying to be more efficient? Is the human staff being treated poorly by the guests? 

I feel that I lack the intelligence and foresight to understand. 

What do you think?
