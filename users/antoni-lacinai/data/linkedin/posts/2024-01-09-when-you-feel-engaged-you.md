When you feel engaged you will act!

And communication plays a big part:

✔️ What you say
✔️ How you sound
✔️ How you look
✔️ How you listen
✔️ What you do

Everything about you communicates!

Imagine going to a store where no one greets you.
Are they communicating with you?
Yes, in abundance

They are telling you that you are not important. We've all been there. When I train people, it's the first thing they learn. See people! Acknowledge them. 

When they do see you
When they really listen
When they inspire you

That's when magic happens

→ Communication leads to engagement
→ Engagement leads to performance
→ Performance leads to results

Communication in itself is pointless. 
But without communication everything is pointless
