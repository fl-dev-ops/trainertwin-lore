Do you want better results in your organization? 
Play more, laugh more, have more fun

Today, we celebrate Playfulness Day. 

Studies show that this will increase your creativity, productivity, team spirit, trust, and employee satisfaction.  

If you're a hardcore, result-oriented boss, Playfulness gets you sustainable and great results.

There are two ways to go:
1. pinball machines and ping pong. Non-work related. This is a good way to quickly recharge and reset. 
2. Fun exercises within work, like ice-breakers and energizers, at otherwise boring meetings. They will boost team spirit and open of for new ideas.
