Whoowhoop! Celebrate with me. 
My TEDx is now officially on the ted.com website. A milestone indeed!

My message: in this ever more digital world, analog communication is  still superior. 

#Communication #leadership #engagement #antonilacinai #keynote

With that said: I don’t want to go back to slow modems, screeching faxes and dumb phones. Both have a place. Digital communication shows it’s strength in times like this when a little bug from China hinders people to meet. As long as we get that they are much poorer than face2face meetings, they can be the second best thing.
