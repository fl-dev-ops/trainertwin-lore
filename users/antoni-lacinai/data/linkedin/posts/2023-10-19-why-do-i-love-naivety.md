Why do I love naivety so much? Because it can move mountains!

I was visiting a mid-size city in Sweden on a warm summer day. Right before I entered the hotel a massive rain soaked me to the bones so I went to their relaxation department and submerged myself in the hot tub. A Finnish young man started talking to me. He told me he was out cycling. He had five weeks of vacation and set himself the goal of cycling 2000 km. 

I asked him how long it had been since he decided to undertake his ordeal. - It was a few years ago. I asked how much he had cycled since then. - Almost nothing. But he ran marathons and played soccer, so it should work out fine. I then asked him if he didn't have any pain - yes he did. I asked if he would do anything differently if given the chance. - He said that maybe he should have prepared a little better. For example, a wheel had broken and he had never taken a wheel off before. Which he had to do for the first time, with the mosquitoes buzzing around him. His face and arms were pink/red from the sun which contrasted with the white skin he otherwise had.

I laughed. He laughed. We got ourselves a beer and toasted. 

One might think that Jaako, as he is called, was not really wise. He got on a bicycle and started pedaling. He took the problems when they came and solved them. An older person probably wouldn't have done it that way. They would have planned more and trained more. If they did at all. We seem to "stiffen" with age and don't dare as much. We call it experience.

I think there is a huge... charm to what Jaako did. He showed the great youthful energy that can move mountains. He decided and then he did. He was pretty naive - in a good way.

Imagine if we could pair experience with that naive attitude that anything is possible. Talk about energy, focus, action, and opportunities! 

I always have new projects going on. Sometimes I'm insecure and a little scared. But what is really the worst that can happen? I guess I'll have to learn to repair my own metaphorical wheels among buzzing mosquitoes. I mean - how hard can it be?

Thank you for reading my mind
