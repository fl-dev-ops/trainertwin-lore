I believe that a key to maximizing your performance is to set clear, concrete, precise, specific, measurable goals.* 

I have found that too many good people at too many good organizations lack an understanding of how to set proper goals. This is too bad. The right kind of goals will increase their productivity by 15-30%. (I don't care if there are bad companies who disrespect the planet and future humans that fail, but I really want the good ones to succeed)

Here is a simple question you can ask. If you don't have an answer, you don't have a goal. Simple. 

#leadership #communication #engagement

* Oh, they need to be meaningful too. And attractive. And challenging.
