Your stress might not be workload-related. It could be your reaction to your boss's behavior...

I used to have a normal job. The best managers made me feel seen and appreciated. The worst was micromanaging, they looked over your shoulder to find more interesting people (if you couldn't help them advance their career) and didn't care about me at all...  

According to Gallup, 2/3 of all reasons for you to be engaged are due to your nearest manager's behavior and communication. If you are that manager, the burden falls on you. When employees feel unsupported or micromanaged, their stress levels rise, while engagement drops. 

Here are three questions for you and if your answer is NO in any of them, you have work to do:

• 𝗛𝗮𝘃𝗲 𝘆𝗼𝘂 𝗜𝗺𝗽𝗹𝗲𝗺𝗲𝗻𝘁𝗲𝗱 𝗹𝗲𝗮𝗱𝗲𝗿𝘀𝗵𝗶𝗽 𝘁𝗿𝗮𝗶𝗻𝗶𝗻𝗴?
  - focused on emotional intelligence.
  
• 𝗗𝗼 𝘆𝗼𝘂 𝗳𝗼𝘀𝘁𝗲𝗿 𝗮 𝘀𝘂𝗽𝗽𝗼𝗿𝘁𝗶𝘃𝗲, 𝗰𝗮𝗿𝗶𝗻𝗴 𝗰𝘂𝗹𝘁𝘂𝗿𝗲?
  - where employees feel listened to and appreciated.

• 𝗗𝗼 𝘆𝗼𝘂 𝗲𝗻𝗰𝗼𝘂𝗿𝗮𝗴𝗲 𝘁𝗿𝗮𝗻𝘀𝗽𝗮𝗿𝗲𝗻𝘁, 𝗿𝗲𝗴𝘂𝗹𝗮𝗿 𝗰𝗼𝗺𝗺𝘂𝗻𝗶𝗰𝗮𝘁𝗶𝗼𝗻?
  - within and between teams.
