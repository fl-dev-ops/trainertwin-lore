Communication in large organisations is hard. Really hard. 

Being self employed, like I am, is a piece of cake from a communication perspective. Working in a small team is pretty easy. But what if you have larger teams?

The complexity of communication increases dramatically with the group size.

The formula below shows you how to calculate the number of 1-1 contacts you have.
5 people = 15 contacts
10 people = 55 contacts
50 people = 1225 contacts
100 people = 5050 contacts

think about it if you ever say: "How hard can it be?"
Pretty hard.
