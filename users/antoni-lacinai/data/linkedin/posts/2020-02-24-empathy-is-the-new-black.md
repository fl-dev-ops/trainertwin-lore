EMPATHY IS THE NEW BLACK

I remember, a long time ago, trying to talk to a manager. I wanted her advice on something. She interrupted me halfway through my question and answered. I tried again. Same result. I criticized her for not listening. Her frustration was great. Mine too.

Unfortunately, I also remember, roughly in the same time frame, how a colleague asked me for advice, and I did the exact same thing. I was a lousy listener who just wanted to rush to a solution before I had enough information.

To listen is perhaps the most important trait of a manager. To confirm, see, involve and respect. This does not mean that the manager has to agree with everything. Instead, it is about being INTERESTED rather than interesting. To have an empathic ability. Empathy is for sure the new black.

The three communicative superpowers in my opinion are Energy, Empathy and Clarity. Today I salute Empathy; the art of listening with the intention to understand, not to answer, and certainly not to answer before you even know what the question is.

Communication, employee engagement and results go hand in hand. Communicate as a leader. Listen like a leader.
