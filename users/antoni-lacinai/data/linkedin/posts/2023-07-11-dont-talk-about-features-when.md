### Jeremy Foster (Symply: Discovering Better Recovery)
> Excellent!!  Actually Antoni, I wonder about the incentive here.. 
> 
> I don't think businesses or people move towards something good, they only steer that way, after moving away from something bad. 
> 
> I think we need to start with the bad. Ie What problem are we solving? Bad is like the fuel in a gas tank. A good future state is the destination. We (our proposition, people, process, tools, products etc) are the vehicle.. 
> 
> In your example, of taking a taxi, the 'bad' could be:
> - Are you under time pressure? 
> - Do you have a lot of heavy luggage to move?
> - Are you lost?
> 
> If none of those are true, there is no 'fuel' to choose a taxi over a bus. If all are true, then probably $100 is a bargain. 
> 
> Why start with the bad? People and businesses move away from bad immediately. They move towards good only after deliberation. 
> 
> Trying the positive on the taxi scenario.. 
> 
> - Would you like a nice plush ride to the Airport?
> - Would you like plenty of space?
> - Would you like to have the vehicle all to yourself?
> - Would you like help with your luggage?
> 
> All positive, but is it compelling? Not really.  I reckon, start with the bad.
> 
> Anyway, great to see the creative discussion. All the best from NZ in the meantime. 
> 
> MVH.. Jeremy
