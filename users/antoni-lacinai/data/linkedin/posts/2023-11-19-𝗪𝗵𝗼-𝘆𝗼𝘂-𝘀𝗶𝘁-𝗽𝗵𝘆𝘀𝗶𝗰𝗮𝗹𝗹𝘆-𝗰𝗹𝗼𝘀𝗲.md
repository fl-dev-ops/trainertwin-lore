But the opposite is also true... 

This is why you as a leader should care:

If you place people close to a high performer, they will increase their productivity by about 15%? 
If you place them next to a slacker you will decrease their productivity by about 30%. 

This is called The Spillover Effect and will silently shape productivity and engagement. 

What can you as a leader do? Here are 5 Tips:

1. 𝗟𝗲𝗮𝗱 𝗯𝘆 𝗘𝘅𝗮𝗺𝗽𝗹𝗲: Your actions set the tone for the entire team. 
2. 𝗖𝘂𝗹𝘁𝗶𝘃𝗮𝘁𝗲 𝗮 𝗣𝗼𝘀𝗶𝘁𝗶𝘃𝗲 𝗖𝘂𝗹𝘁𝘂𝗿𝗲: Encourage collaboration and accountability. 
3. 𝗦𝘄𝗶𝗳𝘁𝗹𝘆 𝗔𝗱𝗱𝗿𝗲𝘀𝘀 𝗡𝗲𝗴𝗮𝘁𝗶𝘃𝗶𝘁𝘆: Don't allow negative behaviors to fester. 
4. 𝗙𝗼𝗰𝘂𝘀 𝗼𝗻 𝘁𝗵𝗲 𝗽𝗼𝘀𝗶𝘁𝗶𝘃𝗲: Amplify the positive and mitigate the negative.
5. 𝗙𝗼𝘀𝘁𝗲𝗿 𝗜𝗻𝗰𝗹𝘂𝘀𝗶𝘃𝗶𝘁𝘆: This breeds engagement.
