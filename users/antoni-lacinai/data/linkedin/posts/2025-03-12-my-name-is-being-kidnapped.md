My name is being "kidnapped". I guess I should be flattered ^_^

Is my brand so strong that event organizers now pretend I am speaking at their event???

For the third time in a year, I am discovering that I am listed as a speaker at a conference. People are adding me on LinkedIn and telling me that they are excited to hear me speak at this or that event. 

My 1st reaction is "Sh-t! Have I missed this?"
My 2nd reaction is "Why are they doing this?"
My 3rd reaction is: "Why don't they respond?"

I am sorry to say that I won't be speaking at this event. I could have if they had just called me back so that we could agree on the terms. 

Have you ever experienced similar things? Is your name being used without your knowledge?
