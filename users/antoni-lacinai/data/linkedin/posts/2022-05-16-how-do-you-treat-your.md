How do you treat your people?
How do they treat your customers?

How you meet, greet and treat each other will have a direct effect on how you meet, greet and treat your partners and clients.

Sure, you can have an amazing customer service rep in a toxic company and you can have a bitter demoralizing person at customer care in a great company. But normally they are affecting each other with scary accuracy. 

How do you meet, greet and treat your colleagues and customers?
