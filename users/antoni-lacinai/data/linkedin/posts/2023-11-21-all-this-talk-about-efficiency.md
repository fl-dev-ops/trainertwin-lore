All this talk about EFFICIENCY is far from EFFICIENT

So why all this talk about efficiency?

Because it feels like a logical conclusion. 

I have coached leaders and teams across the world for the last 17 years
I have found that many people struggle with too low engagement and too many misunderstandings. The confusion levels are high. They crave clarity and think that Efficiency is the solution

Efficient leadership
Efficient processed
Efficient meetings

This creates one-dimensional leadership which can lead to stress, anxiety, and burnout. 

So I developed this simple formula called E=ExE for leaders and teams who are fed up with not getting the real impact they deserve but instead want to have more fun at work and still get good things done. 

Be Efficient. Add Energy. Get an outstanding EFFECT
