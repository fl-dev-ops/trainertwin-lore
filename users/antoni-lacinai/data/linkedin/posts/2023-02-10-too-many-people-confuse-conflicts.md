Too many people confuse conflicts with disagreements. 
Conflicts are not constructive, nor developing. They are destructive. 

A healthy, respectful disagreement on the other hand can be very fruitful

Remember: Words matter. So, when people say that they love conflicts, they are either talking about disagreements or they are combative and power-hungry.
