Why is it called Monday meetings?
Or Management meetings
Or Project meetings

Can anybody explain the value of knowing that we have a meeting because it’s Monday? Or that it is more important to know who will come than what we want to achieve?

Why not call them
- Exploring-new-segments meeting
- If-the-shit-hits-the-fan meeting
- delighting-our-loyal-customers meeting
- what-we-are-grateful-for-this-week meeting
- How-to-attract-new-employees meeting 
- Create-our-future-the-coming-month meeting


Just to mention a few much better names.

What would you call a meeting in your workplace if you could decide?

Words matter. Choose them wisely
