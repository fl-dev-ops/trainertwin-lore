When do you feel inferior? Why?

For me:
Put me in front of a video camera and I have very low stress levels (unless it’s on national TV where the puls is slightly elevated)

But in front of a regular camera… that’s when I feel small and out of my league. 

Yesterday was such a day. Even though I had an amazing photographer and my great team at a PR firm editK who wanted me to shoot press photos, I got performance anxiety. 

Now, they all told me that it all went very well and I am sure they got some good photos. It’s like when I train people in presentation skills. I tell them that their nerves doesn’t show nearly as much as they think. 

The team did a good job calming me down. Keep your fingers crossed for me and for a good outcome 😃
