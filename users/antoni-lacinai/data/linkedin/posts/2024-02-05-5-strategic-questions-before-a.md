5 strategic questions before a reorganization:

I remember from my last employment. 
During a period of about 8 years:
• We had 16 reorganizations
• I had 16 different titles
• for 16 managers
• in 8 buildings

...doing the same job. 

Some of those reorgs were probably justified
Some were because a new boss came in
Some were because of a lack of clarity

We managed to collaborate despite the organizational structures that constantly shifted. It was a learning experience. 

So what can you do?

If you are thinking about a reorganization, take some time to reflect on these 5 strategic questions first. Preferably with your colleagues in a workshop. 

Management by constant reorganizations 
- the height of confusion...
