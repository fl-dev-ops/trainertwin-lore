You are most probably awesome!!... 
...So wouldn't it be great if warm words were coming your way a bit more often?

Self-leadership is great, but that doesn't remove the need to be seen, heard, respected and appreciated by others. I have read (can't remember where) that 50% of all the praise you get in your lifetime, you get before you turn three years old...

What does it do to your self-confidence?
What does it do to your self worth?

Perhaps it is time to add more praise, compassion, encouragement...

What do you do to boost people?
