How do you serve a demotivated team?

I remember being in a perfect storm of layoffs. 

In the 1st round, 80% of my peers- gone
In the 2nd round, 50% - gone
In the 3rd round, 30% - gone

It was brutal and necessary at the same time

But during this process as well as afterward, too many employees felt hurt, wounded, demotivated...

How do you pick them up?
What happens if you can recharge and restore?

Here are 7 ideas on how you serve a demotivated team. Discuss them with your peers if you can.

Motivation leads to performance which leads to results.
