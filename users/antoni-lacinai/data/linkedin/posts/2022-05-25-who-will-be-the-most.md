Who will be the MOST productive people in a hybrid world?
In-Office employees or Remote employees

I did LinkedIn poll, and this is what the participants said:

6% believed that In-office employees would be more productive
39% said that Remote employees would be more productive
55% said that there was no difference

I am a strong believer in the power of analog, face2face communication (even if I wrote a book about virtual meetings with Micke Darmell already in 2016). I love the richness of those meetings. I still don’t think, even after we have taken a giant digital leap lately, that we are born to cooperate in 2D. 

But I have also come to love some indirect benefits of digital communication, like the enormous time savings on travel, the fact that I can address participants in my keynotes with names which is much more personal than in a physical conference, and this instant move to breakout rooms which is awesome (not very instant in Teams where it takes longer to open those virtual rooms then to put on a cup of coffee…)

But if those numbers are close to some sort of truth (and different studies show different results), this is a sign that productivity itself isn’t the real issue. Or, is it just wishful thinking from people who don’t want to go back full time to the office – ever?

What do you think?
