Don't forget the moderator!

When you run a 3-hour virtual masterclass for hundreds of managers at the same time, one thing is especially valuable: a sidekick to moderate the session. 

There are just too many things to handle when so many people have free access to the chat function (which I always encourage), when breaks and breakout sessions are planned, and when people appear in the waiting room. 

Today I am running two of those sessions. It's gonna be a long day. Fun too. And my mind will be focused on them, the messages, and the methods. Luckily, I'll have a moderator helping me. 

OK, time to put the plan into action. 

Communication shapes behavior. Behavior drives results
