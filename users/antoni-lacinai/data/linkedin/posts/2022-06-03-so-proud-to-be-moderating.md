So proud to be moderating this important event for the Latvian government. 

We will leave day-to-day operations behind and look way into the future of education together with the minister of education, MIT, Harvard, etc., and to yet again work with thought-provoking futurist Gerd Leonhard. 

I remember when I was a young adult, studying marketing. The headteacher of business economics praised us for being motivated and wanting to learn. I took that to heart and see myself as a learning person, always reading books, attending courses, interviewing leaders, creating surveys and concepts, etc. I don't think I will ever stop learning. It's a way of life. 

What we decide today will have an impact on people way after we have left this earth, so we might as well make that future for them as good as possible. 

This will be very exciting! Thank you Dita Traidas for bringing me onboard 🙏🏻
