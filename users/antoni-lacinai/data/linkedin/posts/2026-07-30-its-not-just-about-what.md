It’s not just about what you know. It’s about how you make others feel.

A Harvard study shows that people with strong social skills lift their team’s performance almost as much as task skills do.

They didn't talk more. They weren't smarter. 
They just made others feel more engaged, thus making them perform better. How?
• They were better at reading emotions.
• They noticed people being confused or holding back.
• They adjusted how they spoke and listened.

That’s what I mean when I say: communicate with empathy, clarity, and energy.

It’s how good teams become great.
