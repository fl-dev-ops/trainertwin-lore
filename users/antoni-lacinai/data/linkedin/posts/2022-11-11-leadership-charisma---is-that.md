Leadership Charisma - is that even a thing?

One could say that charisma is the experience people have of your communication and behavior. Leaders "send on a higher volume" so whatever is good or bad will be amplified. 

In my latest column for the Swedish premium leadership magazine Chefstidningen, I give five tips on how you can develop your charisma according to science.
In short:
- Be brave
- Be empathetic
- Use anecdotes
- Use meaningful gestures
- Vary your voice

And while you work on these skills: Be present. That will compensate for lack of charisma. 

What would you add to this list?
