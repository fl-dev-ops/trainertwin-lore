Friday thought: “If you can’t explain it on the back of a napkin, you probably haven’t understood it yourself…”

Is that why we complicate the sh-t out of things? Because we hide our ignorance and insecurity? 

Or do we know so much that we cannot, or will not, degrade this superior knowledge into any kind of simple language?

Just saying.
