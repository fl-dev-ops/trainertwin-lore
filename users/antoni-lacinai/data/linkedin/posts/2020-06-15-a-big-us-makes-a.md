A BIG "US" makes a smaller "them"

It's so common to limit the tribe to the closest department, instead of including the whole company. WE in sales, and those guys in marketing, WE in construction and those in production.

This isn't smart thinking. Look at sport teams. They have different positions on the field, but they are still ONE team. 

Let's go for unity instead of dividing
Let's believe in the strength of communities instead of dictatorship
Let's celebrate togetherness instead of helplessness
