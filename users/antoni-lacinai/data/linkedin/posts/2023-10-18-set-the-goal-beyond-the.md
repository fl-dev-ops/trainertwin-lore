SET THE GOAL BEYOND THE GOAL

English below

Vad händer om du ska springa hundra meter och närmar dig mållinjen? Kommer du att omedvetet bromsa in eller gasar du förbi linjen innan du saktar ner? Läs mina tankar hos eminenta HRnytt

Set the goal beyond the goal. This way you won't slow down until after you cross the finish line. It is so valid in businesses too. It's not until your client has received their products, or even installed them and claimed that they are happy that you can say that you have reached your goal.
