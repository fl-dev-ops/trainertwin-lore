A meeting without a goal is not a meeting
It is a coffee break!!

Always has a goal for your meetings. Ask yourself:
- When the meeting is over - what should have happened then?
Or
- How do I know that the meeting was a success?

If you can answer these questions you have a goal.
 If you can't... don't waste people's time. Cancel the meeting. Or put your brain to work and figure it out. 

Clarity brings value. Clear goals will make your team perform 15-30 % better. 

There. First tips this week about meeting competence and how to maximize performance in meetings.
