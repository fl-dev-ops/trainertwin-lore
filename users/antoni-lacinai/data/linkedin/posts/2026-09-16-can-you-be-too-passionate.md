Can you be too passionate about the products you sell?

Today I will speak to 80 retailers from the Nordics and Baltics, selling the coolest bikes to the most initiated buyers. We’ll talk about world-class customer communication. 

And it made me think about a time where I worked in retail, selling stuff like office equipment and supplies. Unsexy to most but being the person I am I went all-in on learning as much as I could. At one point a customer wanted to buy a new office chair. I didn’t give him a chance. Before he knew what happened, he had bought the chair that I liked the best. Not what he needed. Or wanted. 

My passion got in the way of listening. This is an error that is easily fixed but so hard to do when you forget that you are there to serve, more than to sell. 

So I listened to smart people, learnt their models and processes and the result is this:

First you need to Know
Then you can Show
And then you Go for the close. 

Know - Show - Go =  Flow

I have trained retailers, trade show staff and global account managers, using this simple process. It works. 

Have you experienced sales people being too passionate to listen?

I don’t know if this group today is too passionate, but passionate they are. I like that. Passion is their engine filled with energy that make them move. The rest is about steering their conversations in the right direction.
