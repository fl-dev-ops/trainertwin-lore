Distress kills communication. Euphoric stress stimulates it - up to a point

A Hong Kong study showed that 77% of workers are stressed out and 82% are thinking about disengaging from work. Ouch!

People who are too stressed are impatient and sometimes rude. 
Whenever I am overwhelmed with stress I change my communication style:
• More direct
• Less empathy
• Negative energy

I am not alone. When I instead feel calm, rested, at ease, and safe, I am a much better me. 

Do you recognize this yourself?

As a leader, you often face the challenge of creating a performance-based culture while at the same time creating a safe space of happiness and fun. It's not easy when the pressure is on. 

The thing is: if you want sustainable growth these two forces are what you must balance. 

The core of it all is to CARE while being CALM

You got this ^_^
