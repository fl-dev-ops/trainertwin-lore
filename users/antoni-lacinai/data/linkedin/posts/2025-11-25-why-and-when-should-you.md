Why and When should you script a speech?

Here is my take:

• 𝗜𝗳 𝗶𝘁 𝗶𝘀 𝘀𝗵𝗼𝗿𝘁, 𝘀𝗰𝗿𝗶𝗽𝘁 𝗶𝘁
• 𝗜𝗳 𝗶𝘁 𝗶𝘀 𝗮 𝗵𝗶𝗴𝗵-𝘀𝘁𝗮𝗸𝗲 𝗽𝗿𝗲𝘀𝗲𝗻𝘁𝗮𝘁𝗶𝗼𝗻, 𝘀𝗰𝗿𝗶𝗽𝘁 𝗶𝘁

• If you script it, write it as you would say it, not as you would read it. 
• Put intonations in bold. Add spaces for pauses.
• Read it out loud, then edit the text
• Practice, practice, and practice some more

When I prepared for my TEDx, I did all the above, and I believe what I then said is about 95% the same as what I wrote.

Last weekend I attended a wedding. I had the shortest speech, just under 2,5 minutes. It went well, mainly because I prepared and scripted it. And had room to improvise. If I hadn't written it down and practiced it, I might have gotten away with it, but purely by chance (well, in my case, years of experience)
  
Today I am visiting a client in Vancouver, where we'll talk about how we talk. Wish us luck. ^_^
