3 closing techniques 
For 3 decision styles 

Do you want a commitment from your boss or client? Check out this video.

Here you will get the 3 common decision styles

→ I decide
→ I need time
→ I need reassurance

Then you will get 3 ways to get that commitment

Call it manipulation or influence. They will increase your chances of getting a Yes to that proposal, or idea of yours. 

→ I decide - Let them
→ I need time - Ask why and when to meet again.
→ I need reassurance - summarize and propose
