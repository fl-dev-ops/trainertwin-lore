PLACE OR PEOPLE? Which is most important

I strongly believe that, in business, people outweighs location any day of the week. The right team can overcome most obstacles by cooperating and help each other with respect, empathy and positivity. I am not saying that location doesn't matter. If you have both, even better. But perhaps location will be less important as we move into digital and hybrid...

Watch this video and ask yourself: 
- If we have to choose - what is most important to us? Why?
- If we can have both, how would be achieve it?
