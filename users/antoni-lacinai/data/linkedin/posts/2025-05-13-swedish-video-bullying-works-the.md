Swedish video: Bullying works…

The uncomfortable truth is that bullying works… if you want to get rid of people by making them quit on their own. According to a UK study I read about, 24% of bullied employees leave. And - 20% watching it happen, leave too! 

It is also an awful, despicable way of leading an organization. 

Have you ever been bullied? If you have been, you will know it. It sticks to you forever. Let’s be kind instead and lead with care!
