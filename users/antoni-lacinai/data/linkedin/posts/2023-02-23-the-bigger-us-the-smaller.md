The bigger “US” the smaller “them”

Today’s keynote takes place in Budapest. And it’s funny how I view Hungary as a part of me, even if it is unknown territory. My mothers father’s last name was Toth; a common name in Hungary. Even if I have no idea if he really was from Hungary, I have created a WE that includes them. 
We all have that power. We can all decide who is in and who is not. 
How do you expand your WE and US?
