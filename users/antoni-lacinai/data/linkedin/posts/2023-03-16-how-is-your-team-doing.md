How is your team doing - I mean really?

I have looked into an area of psychology called Compassion Focused theory, and I got this epiphany: 
What if the culture in a company is fueled by something deeper? 

According to this theory, you have three different systems in the brain:

- The Threat system - this helps you survive right now and if you feel threatened you will probably run och fight for your life. (think bullying, regorgs, downsizing etc)

- The Exploration system - this is what make you search for food or friends or knowledge etc. If you feel lost you get frustrated, until you reach a goal and then you feel rewarded. 

- The Soothing/comfort system - this is where you are at ease, satisfied, happy. 

The thing is that you should lead people differently depending what system is dominant at that time and here is where I can merge my three communication superpowers. 
Empathy for the threatened
Clarity for for the frustrated
Energy for the comfortable

And a good mix of all three for the high performers.
