Do you have a goal or just wishful thinking?

There are 3 reasons why so many people hate goals

1. They feel like failures if they can't reach them
2. They get irritated by being micro-managed
3. They get confused about what a goal is

Confusion is the opposite of clarity

Don't say "I want to lose weight"
Say "I will lose 3 kg"

Don't say "We will have a little meeting and chat"
Say "We will meet for 30 minutes and decide"

Don't say "We will see who will buy it"
Say "We will sell x units to segment Y"

Set goals- Get goals. Have fun. 

PS - don't hate goals. They can increase your performance by 15-30%.
