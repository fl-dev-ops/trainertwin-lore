Did you know that there are 3 levels of listening?
And the first is hardly listening at all...

→  Listen to respond
→  Listen to understand the content
→  Listen to understand the whole person

But to really listen you need to let go of your ego
You need to tell that inner voice to shut up
"Which inner voice?" you ask
That inner voice

I know this and still, I often find it hard to let go and be truly present. 

It's more like: "Enough talk about me. Let's talk about you. What do you think about me?"

That's what I think.
