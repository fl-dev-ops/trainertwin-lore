2 conferences. 1 speaker slot. How?
By getting them all in the same room

Greetings from Kuala Lumpur where I have just delivered a keynote on communication, motivation, engagement, and having a "greenlight mindset"

The organizer CWI is running two parallel conferences at the same venue. During a half-day, they combine them to get insights and inspiration from the main stage.  I like that!

Here are some stats I shared: 
• High-performing teams have 6x more positive interactions than the average team. 
• Only 22% of the global workforce is super motivated and highly engaged. 
• Creativity goes down 38% in a rude and uncivil environment

We can do better.
You can do better
You got this!

PS - Who made you feel happy last week? That person is your hero!
