4 Cornerstones for your Hybrid Meetings.

In my latest column in the Swedish HRnytt (to 60,000 HR professionals), I share 4 success factors for leading a great hybrid meeting.

As a summary:
* Hybrid = Digital. Always think "remote first"
* Let everyone be involved in how you will meet, greet and treat each other
* Skip the buns and cakes in the physical conference room
* Have some pure on-site meetings
