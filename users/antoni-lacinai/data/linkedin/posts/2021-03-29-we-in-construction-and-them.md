WE in construction and THEM in production
WE in the factory and THEM in the office
WE in marketing and THEM in sales

Way too often I find organizations having a toxic Us vs Them - environment. It creates friction, conflicts and a loss of motivation. I say that these companies are "in the basement" and that they would be happy just to get above ground, the "entrance level" where things seems ok but engagement is unstable. 

And then you have the "Top floor" firms, the penthouse people where they feel safe and can be constructive, productive and creative. This is the level few are at but where most people want to be .

Here are some of my thoughts around Us vs Them. 

How is your team spirit?
