Thoughts of the day
Leaders vs managers - a different perspective

→ A great leader serves a cause, a quest, a mission
→ A great manager serves their team

This means that a team is never there for the manager. It is always the other way around. When I was young I told my staff "You do what I say because I am your boss!"... OMG that was so embarrassingly bad...

This doesn't mean that the manager will accommodate every wish and whim of everyone. There is still a cause, quest, and mission to strive for. 

Can you get people on board with your vision of the future? Congrats! you are a leader. 

Can you get people engaged and peak-perform? Congrats. You are a great manager. 

You got this!
