Are leaders giving up communicating with remote team members? 

I have worked from a home office since 2006. I feared that I would be isolated but I found that I am often alone, but very seldom lonely. I meet so many people both in the virtual world and also when I am traveling to train and speak. I have friends, new and old, and I feel blessed. 

But not everyone shares that feeling. 𝟮𝟯% 𝗼𝗳 𝗴𝗹𝗼𝗯𝗮𝗹 𝗲𝗺𝗽𝗹𝗼𝘆𝗲𝗲𝘀 𝗳𝗲𝗲𝗹 𝗹𝗼𝗻𝗲𝗹𝘆, 𝗲𝘀𝗽𝗲𝗰𝗶𝗮𝗹𝗹𝘆 𝗿𝗲𝗺𝗼𝘁𝗲 𝘄𝗼𝗿𝗸𝗲𝗿𝘀. 

Remote work offers flexibility but often leads to isolation, especially if the team members are only focusing on 𝘁𝗮𝘀𝗸𝘀 and not 𝗿𝗲𝗹𝗮𝘁𝗶𝗼𝗻𝘀𝗵𝗶𝗽𝘀. 

𝗪𝗵𝗮𝘁 𝗶𝗳 𝘆𝗼𝘂 𝗮𝘀 𝗮 𝗹𝗲𝗮𝗱𝗲𝗿:
