The more Respect - the better Effect!

Do you remember what Google's mantra used to be: 
"Don't be evil". 
I liked that. 

A disrespectful work environment may be costing your business more than you realize. Research by Dan Hasson, and TED speaker Christine Porath has both shed light on the significant impact of workplace incivility:

• 48% put in less effort due to incivility.
• 47% cut back on work hours intentionally. 
• 25% of affected employees take out their frustration on customers

𝗕𝘂𝘁 𝘄𝗮𝗶𝘁. 𝗧𝗵𝗲𝗿𝗲 𝗶𝘀 𝗺𝗼𝗿𝗲:
• Employees' working memory dips by 17%.
• Creative solutions decline by 38%.
• Calculation errors rise by 43%, and careless mistakes increase by 25%.

𝗕𝘂𝘁 𝘄𝗮𝗶𝘁, 𝘁𝗵𝗲𝗿𝗲 𝗶𝘀 𝗲𝘃𝗲𝗻 𝗺𝗼𝗿𝗲:
• 80% lose time worrying about what happened
• 63% lose time avoiding the uncivil person
• 66% feel that their performance has deteriorated
