5 TIPS FOR SUCCESSFUL HYBRID MEETINGS

I was recently interviewed by AKAVIA, a Swedish union. The article is in Swedish, so for my international friends: Here is the short version:

1. Hybrid is virtual. Always go digital when you have some people onsite and some off-site.
2. Keep 'em short. Remember zoom-fatigue. It's a thing.
3. Split the meeting in two parts. One off-line where you get info. One online where you discuss and decide.
4. Skip the cookies and buns in the physical conference room. You will only create a bad feeling from the remote people.
5. have some 100% physical meetings to build and strengthen personal relationships.

What would you add to this list?
