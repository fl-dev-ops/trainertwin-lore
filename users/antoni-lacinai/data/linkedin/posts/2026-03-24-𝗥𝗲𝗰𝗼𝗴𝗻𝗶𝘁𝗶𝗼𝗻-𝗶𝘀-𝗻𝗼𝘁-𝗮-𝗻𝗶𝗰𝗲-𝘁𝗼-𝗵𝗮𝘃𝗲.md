𝗥𝗲𝗰𝗼𝗴𝗻𝗶𝘁𝗶𝗼𝗻 𝗶𝘀 𝗻𝗼𝘁 𝗮 𝗻𝗶𝗰𝗲-𝘁𝗼-𝗵𝗮𝘃𝗲

I once read that half of all the praise you receive in your life comes before the age of three. The rest is spread over the rest of your life...

Say what?

That should make every leader pause. Or everyone else, for that matter. 

Recognition is not about complimenting someone's dress or hairstyle (by all means, do that too if it is appropriate). It is about creating a sense of belonging and positive energy. It is about letting people know, “I see you, and I appreciate you.”

High-performing teams give more positive recognition (6x more than average), not because they are nicer (they generally are), but because it helps them collaborate better and get things done.

People work harder when they feel valued. They show courage when they feel safe. They trust leaders who acknowledge them.

The bar is low. A “thank you,” a slight nod, a simple “good morning.” It costs nothing. It means everything. You know who is the absolute worst at this? Stressed people. And car dealers...

Who did you greet today with a smile?
