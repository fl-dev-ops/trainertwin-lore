"Why aren't they as passionate as I am?"
"Where is the engagement?"
"Why do we have such high absence and burnouts?"

Sometimes  we complicate things too much. Like how to set and reach certain goals in the organization. We complain that we are misunderstood and we misunderstand others. 

Communication, motivation, and performance go hand in hand. Excellent communication leads to high engagement, which leads to high performance and ultimately great results.

Here are 4 cornerstones to get more engagement:
- Involve people more than you inform. 
- Trust that you hired good people and leverage their collective intelligence. 
- Praise them. 
- Have fun.
