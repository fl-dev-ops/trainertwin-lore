I just realized my company is 20 years old!
Celebrate with me 😃

I haven't given it much thought since I waited until 2006 to do this full-time. However, I did register the company in 2004, the same year my youngest son was born. 20 years... wow. 

After my wife had been home with him for almost a year, I took parental leave for 11 more months (yes, we can do that in Sweden). Whenever he was sleeping, I got to work, creating training programs, business plans, a first home page, etc. 

I took on bookings as a moderator, trainer, and facilitator. 𝗜 𝗹𝗼𝘃𝗲𝗱 𝗶𝘁! 
I decided that I wouldn't return to my employment. 

Many people said: "Why don't you keep your safe job and salary, and do it as a side hustle?"
"No way," I said. "It's all or nothing"

So here I am, 20 years from when I registered my company. Talking on stages across the world on CARE-centric workplace communication... What's not to love?


My company is not a teenager anymore. Just like my sons are not. Cool!
