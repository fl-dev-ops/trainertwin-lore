TV4 today at 0943. In Sweden.

Topic: how to wind down when vacation is knocking on the door.

Energy is a finite resource. If we don’t make sure that we wind down or even switch off, we will not regain our energy, thus making us weaker until we burn out. 

It seems that very few of us will switch off completely. Phone will be on. Emails will be read. If it floats your boat, then fine. If it consumes you when you are supposed to regain your strength, then watch out. 
If your boss pushes you to work on your vacation, then switch boss.
