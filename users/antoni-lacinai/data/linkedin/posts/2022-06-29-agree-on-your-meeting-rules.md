Agree on your meeting rules. 

This is one of my best tips for having engaging and effective meetings.  Every time I run a meeting rule workshop for a team, the productivity goes up. 

Ask everyone in the team:

• Is it okay to read mail during a meeting?
• Is it okay to be late for a meeting?
• Is it okay for the leader to stop conversations that aren’t leading anywhere?
• Is it okay to show emotions or not to show them?
• Is it okay to leave the meeting for a while, rejoining later?
• Is it okay not to have a goal for every meeting?
• Is it okay to abort a meeting?
• Is it okay to speak in a phone meeting without saying your name? 
• Is it okay to express complaints without offering a solution?
• Is it okay to try to address one item meeting after meeting?
• Is it okay to always have the meeting when it suits the leader, even if some participants are in time zones which means late night/early morning?
• Is it okay to run over the planned time if you don’t get everything sorted?
• Is it okay to break (or bend) the meeting rules? What happens if you do?

Let everyone has a say on how much they agree or disagree. Then let everyone explain. Then agree as a team on how you will meet, greet and treat each other. Finally, hold each other accountable!
