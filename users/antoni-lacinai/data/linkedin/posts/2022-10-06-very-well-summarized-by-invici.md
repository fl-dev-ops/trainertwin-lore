Very well summarized by Invici on my thoughts on Hybrid leadership! Thanks!
In English:
DO
+ Involve more than you inform
+ Gather ALL people at least once a week (if possible)
+ Let the task decide the workplace
+ Hybrid = Digital
+ Watch out for "proximity bias"

DON'T
- Force people back full-time
- Abdicate and let anyone do anything anywhere
- Reduce office space
- Go back to useless PowerPoint conferences on-site
- Treat in-person meeting members with buns and cake
