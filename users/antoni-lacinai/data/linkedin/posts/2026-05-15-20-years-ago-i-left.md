20 years ago, I left the corporate life to build something of my own. 

We, the whole family, moved to another city.  New house, new schools, new everything.

People called it brave.  I didn’t see it that way. I simply could not ignore the pull to help leaders communicate better and create workplaces where people want to contribute. 

A magnet on our fridge said: “𝘓𝘦𝘢𝘱 — 𝘢𝘯𝘥 𝘵𝘩𝘦 𝘯𝘦𝘵 𝘸𝘪𝘭𝘭 𝘢𝘱𝘱𝘦𝘢𝘳.”  I did.
 
I haven't changed my offering all that much
• More books
• More keynotes
• More global work
• More leadership coaching
  
Experience helps.  A global perspective helps.  Clients benefit. And my mission is still the same: To help leaders communicate with clarity, empathy, and energy —  so people collaborate better, take action, and create real impact.
 
I am grateful for everyone who has been part of the journey so far.  And we’re just getting started.
