Are you “allvarlig” or “seriös”?

In Sweden, we have two different words for serious:

• 𝗔𝗹𝗹𝘃𝗮𝗿𝗹𝗶𝗴 = grave (and grey). Here you claim that what you do can't include fun. In a business context, a boss might say: "𝘞𝘩𝘺 𝘢𝘳𝘦 𝘺𝘰𝘶 𝘭𝘢𝘶𝘨𝘩𝘪𝘯𝘨? 𝘎𝘦𝘵 𝘣𝘢𝘤𝘬 𝘵𝘰 𝘸𝘰𝘳𝘬." 
  
• 𝗦𝗲𝗿𝗶𝗼̈𝘀 = professional but not grave. Here, you can play and laugh while being very professional. (side-note: Many stand-up comedians are super serious and have amazing work ethics. )

At work, you can divide play into 

𝟭. 𝗹𝗲𝘁𝘁𝗶𝗻𝗴 𝗼𝗳𝗳 𝘀𝘁𝗲𝗮𝗺 (non-work related things, where a ping-pong table can act as a symbol. I remember working for a global company, and I felt the enthusiasm diminish on our floor. So I brought a ping pong ball and every Friday, for ten minutes, we turned a conference room into a Round-the-table ping pong. Great fun. And then back to work with new energy. 

𝟮. 𝗪𝗼𝗿𝗸-𝗿𝗲𝗹𝗮𝘁𝗲𝗱 𝗽𝗹𝗮𝘆 like ice-breakers, energizers, etc. This is what I, and people like me, do every time I conduct  a training or a workshop for a client. 

Adding playfulness at work is not just fun – it’s smart. Studies show that teams that engage in playful activities can boost performance and see a 20% rise in productivity. Not bad. 

Playfulness builds trust, creativity, and better collaboration. Done right, we won't have anyone feeling lonely and isolated, which are motivational killers.

So what can you do? Here are three ideas you can try:

• Workshop on how you can do things 𝗿𝗲𝗮𝗹𝗹𝘆 𝗯𝗮𝗱𝗹𝘆
  
• Ping Pong, Pinball, AW, Trivia quizzes...
  
• 2 truths and 1 lie. Share three things about yourself and let the group figure out which one is a lie. 


Work is serious. Play is serious, too. Laugh. Have fun. Play.
– and watch your team grow
