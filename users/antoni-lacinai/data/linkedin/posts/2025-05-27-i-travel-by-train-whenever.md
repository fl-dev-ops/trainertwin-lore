I travel by train whenever I can
But I mostly stand still 

I travel by train whenever I can
Or, I want to travel by train 
I mostly stand still
Like now

Traveling by train in Sweden 
is a complete nightmare
But I am prepared!
I took an earlier train
Now I can sit and wait
and hopefully make it in time 

It’s sustainable
It’s just not on time
Too bad that this tradeoff exists 
Imagine the productivity loss
or the gain if it ever works
I travel by train 
Like now
Or do I?
