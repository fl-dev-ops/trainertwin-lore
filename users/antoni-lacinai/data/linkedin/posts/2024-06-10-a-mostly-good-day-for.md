A mostly good day for Sweden 
A mostly sad day for EU

The EU election is over. 51% voted. (Such a low number in itself is alarming for democracy.)

The good: In Sweden, and most Nordic countries, many people have realized that climate change is the Only important question we have, in the long term. 

The sad: In many other countries, extremist parties gained ground. They basically want to use democracy to implement the opposite. And too many people seem to happily want to give up their democratic influence to them…

The really worrying: our collective memory is around 50-80 years. Go back 80 years. What happened then? Are we heading into that darkness in Europe again? Well, ask Ukraine…

Our ability to look forward is even less. Most people have a time horizon of 3 months to 2 years…
We are balancing on a very scary and thin line right now. 

How will our leaders act? 
How will leaders communicate? 

Who will stand tall morally? 
Who will lie and divide? 

What actions will we see?
What collaborations will happen?

Time will tell

This is a different post from me, I know. But not all different. It is still about human communication and motivation. Just on a larger scale.
