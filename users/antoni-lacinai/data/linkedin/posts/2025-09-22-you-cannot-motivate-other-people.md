You cannot motivate other people

Well, you can, if you work with external motivation:
- threats of punishment
- promises of rewards

None of them are sustainable. 
None of them come from within. 

What can you do then? 
Inspire, clarify, educate. 
Energize, care, amplify.

But motivate? No

My thoughts. Yours?
