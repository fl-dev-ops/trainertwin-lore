 Here is an example: Yesterday, as we were preparing for my youngest son to leave for a year of studies abroad, we went to a mobile repair shop to get a new battery installed. 

The repairman took his time, and when we finally approached him, he confessed that he had accidentally damaged a cable inside the phone. A new cable would come within three days. Were we ok to wait?

We will leave tonight. 
He can't wait. 
So, No.

I immediately got angry, and I could feel my blood pumping and my eyebrows narrowing, but before I could say much, my son talked to the repairman in a calm voice, saying that anybody can make mistakes, that he was not upset, but that he couldn't wait. How could they solve this? 

After a discussion and some phone calls, they were able to get a cable an hour later, and yet another hour later, everything was fixed. 

Had it helped if I started to yell at the repairman? No
Was my son's communication style top-notch? Yes

🙂 is better than 😡 almost always. 

Note that I am supposed to be a communication expert, but time and again I realize that I am proud when my sons exceed me in ... well, everything!

How do you react shen things don't go your way?
