Many people fear speaking because they focus on themselves: How do I look? What if I fail? What will they think?

But your message is not about you. It is about them.
A mentor once told me, “There is 𝘆𝗼𝘂. There is 𝗺𝗲. And there is 𝗶𝘁.” 
“It” is the message. The purpose. The value you bring.

When you speak for someone else’s benefit, fear shrinks. You become a vessel. A carrier of something useful. Your voice matters most when it helps others. 

And you cannot help them if you stay silent.

When did you last speak up, even though you were afraid of what people might say or think?
