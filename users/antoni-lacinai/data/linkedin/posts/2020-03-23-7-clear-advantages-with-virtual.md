7 CLEAR ADVANTAGES WITH VIRTUAL MEETINGS - from my book Virtual meetings, that you can find on Amazon and other internet bookstores:

1. It’s better for the ENVIRONMENT because we save energy, produce less
CO2/SO2, burn fewer worn-out tyres.
2. We save TIME. Imagine travelling for a couple of hours or even days to
participate in a 30-minute meeting.
3. We save money on air fares, taxis, hotels, rounds of drinks...
4.  We become more PRODUCTIVE. That travelling time – even with a laptop
or tablet on the go – is surely less fruitful than staying where you really
belong and doing what you’re best at.
5. The RIGHT PEOPLE can participate. The brightest and best in the organization are usually in demand; they don’t find it easy to leave base for a couple of days to join a buzz-group 1000 miles away. But to close the office door for an hour and log on to the real-time meeting you have organised? No problem!
6. We can ACT QUICKER. If there is an emergency we can’t always wait for all the people to travel all the way to HQ/command centre/conference plaza.
7. NEW: We minimize the risk of VIRUS infections

Best of luck with your virtual, travel free meetings!
