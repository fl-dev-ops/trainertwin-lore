"What’s clear, however, is that we probably don’t need to be connected to each other all the time. Whether we are working remotely or in the office, it’s good to talk – but sometimes our interactions with others are best taken in short doses."

I have been an advocate of MORE communication when you lead a remote team, simply because you don't meet people by the coffee machine. On the other hand, you won't get into flow if you are constantly interrupted, and you won't get time to form your own opinion or come up with new ideas if you are all about teamwork and constant communication.

What's your opinion? What is the optimal balance of communication when you work remotely?
