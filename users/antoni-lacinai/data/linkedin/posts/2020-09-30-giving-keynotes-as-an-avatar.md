Giving keynotes as an Avatar!

Today I will give a keynote about World class (online) customer meetings. But I will not be there in person. I will not even be there in camera. I will be an Avatar! And so will the audience members, who will have a two-day conference in this format. 

Here's a sneak preview of how it will look. 

I am excited and I have to admit, a bit tense. I will move my avatar on the stage with my mouse, to get the experience for the audience that I am addressing everyone, while showing slides, igniting chat discussions and delivering with high energy.

Have you ever tried it?

EDIT: I did it, and left some impressions in the comments below ^_^
