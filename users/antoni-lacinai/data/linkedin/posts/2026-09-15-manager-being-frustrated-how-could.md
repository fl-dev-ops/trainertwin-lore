Manager, being frustrated: "How could they misunderstand me???"

Me after asking and listening: "I want to introduce you to the three communication superpowers."

Manager afterward: "Aha..."

It took a good 25 years to distill my learnings around human communication into these three: Energy, Empathy, Clarity. 

Perhaps I am a slow learner. Perhaps it takes time to be simple. But here we are, and here you are. I hope this is helpful. It is one of the core frameworks I share on stages and in conference rooms. 

Communication shapes behavior. Behavior drives results.
