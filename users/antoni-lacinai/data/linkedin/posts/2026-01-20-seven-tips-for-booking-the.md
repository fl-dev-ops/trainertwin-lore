Seven tips for booking the right speaker.
Designed, not improvised.

Not everyone who books speakers does so for a living. You can be a manager, an assistant, a marketing or HR professional, and so on. 

Here are some of my best tips for you if you're booking speakers for your internal kickoff or conference. And remember that many speaker bureaus have a good database and deep knowledge too, like PepTalk Inspirational Speakers A-Speakers Premium Speakers Agency Unique Speaker Bureau International The Closer Agency and MySpeaker just to mention a few. 

I hope this guide helps.
Stay awesome!
^_^

𝟭. 𝗦𝘁𝗮𝗿𝘁 𝘄𝗶𝘁𝗵 𝘁𝗵𝗲 𝗼𝘂𝘁𝗰𝗼𝗺𝗲
Sets direction. Without this, everything else is guesswork.
𝟮. 𝗕𝗼𝗼𝗸 𝗳𝗼𝗿 𝘆𝗼𝘂𝗿 𝗽𝗲𝗼𝗽𝗹𝗲
Clarifies relevance. This narrows the field quickly.
𝟯. 𝗔𝘀𝘀𝗲𝘀𝘀 𝘁𝗵𝗲 𝘀𝗽𝗲𝗮𝗸𝗲𝗿
This is where risk shows up. “Can we trust this person? Will this work?”
𝟰. 𝗜𝗻𝘃𝗼𝗹𝘃𝗲 𝘁𝗵𝗲 𝘀𝗽𝗲𝗮𝗸𝗲𝗿 𝗲𝗮𝗿𝗹𝘆
Once trust exists, collaboration becomes possible.
𝟱. 𝗟𝗲𝘁 𝘁𝗵𝗲 𝗼𝘂𝘁𝗰𝗼𝗺𝗲 𝗴𝘂𝗶𝗱𝗲 𝗱𝗲𝗽𝘁𝗵 𝗮𝗻𝗱 𝗲𝗻𝗲𝗿𝗴𝘆
Now you fine-tune the format, tone, and level.
𝟲. 𝗕𝗲 𝗼𝗽𝗲𝗻 𝗮𝗯𝗼𝘂𝘁 𝘁𝗵𝗲 𝗯𝘂𝗱𝗴𝗲𝘁
At this stage, constraints can be discussed without tension.
𝟳. 𝗘𝘅𝘁𝗲𝗻𝗱 𝘁𝗵𝗲 𝘃𝗮𝗹𝘂𝗲 𝗯𝗲𝘆𝗼𝗻𝗱 𝘁𝗵𝗲 𝘀𝘁𝗮𝗴𝗲
This closes the loop and lifts the thinking from event to impact.
