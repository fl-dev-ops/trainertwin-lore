In 1996, I thought I had most things figured out. Now, I am just glad that I have been around long enough to realize how little I know...

At that point in my life, I was on my second job, working in sales for a global datacom distributor. My life was work, playing football, and going out with friends. 

Not a bad life. 

Fast forward 30 years: new jobs. climbing the ladder, starting my own business, traveling the world as a keynote speaker, being blessed with a family of a wife and two boys who are now men, and meeting amazing people all over the world.

Not a bad life either.

But the biggest insight is that your own development is never done - unless you decide it is. I thought I had it figured out as a young adult. I hadn't. But here's the thing. I still haven't. I don't think anyone has. We are ALL thinking that everyone else knows their sh-t.  

At this point, I believe that I am a little less stupid than I was 30 years ago. That counts for something and helps me share insights with my clients and my network. The journey doesn't have a set destination (at least for as long as we're alive). I hope to be around for many years to come, and who knows? One day I might even be considered wise ^_^

How about you? Have you figured it all out?
