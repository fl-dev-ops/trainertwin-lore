If everybody talks, then who listens?

Imagine going to a dinner party. You share a story and get... zero questions. Instead,  someone else will top that story with their own... This might work at a friendly dinner, but not in a work-life context. 

To truly listen is to practice the number one superpower Empathy and with empathy comes trust, loyalty,  and engagement. 

   How do you listen?
