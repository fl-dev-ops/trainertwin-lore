We all bring "invisible" people into meetings. Especially the ones who made us feel bad. The driver who cut us off. The colleague who annoyed us. The stress we carried from home.

Unless we “check them in,” they follow us into the room and negatively affect our communication. In a recent podcast, I used the IKEA playroom as a metaphor. Put those people there before going into your meetings. Deal with them later.

Mood and mindset shape everything: Your tone. Your patience. Your ability to listen. Your ability to lead. If you feel safe, you communicate safety. If you feel stressed, you communicate tension—even when you try to hide it.

Start with mindset. Your communication will follow. And communication is the key that unlocks performance!

Who did you bring to work today?
