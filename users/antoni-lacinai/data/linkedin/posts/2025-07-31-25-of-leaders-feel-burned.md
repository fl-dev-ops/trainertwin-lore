25% of leaders feel burned out, and it creates a ripple effect of negativity

I've seen this so many times. A manager walks by, barks some orders, and has no time to listen or explain before they dash off to their next meeting. Stress is a motivational killer. 

Burnout isn’t limited to employees—leaders are feeling the heat too, and this affects their ability to communicate well and motivate and engage their teams. 

What about you?

When you are overwhelmed, team engagement suffers. 

Investing in your well-being—by getting support, coaching, tips, and stress management strategies—can help prevent burnout and maintain a healthy, productive work environment.
