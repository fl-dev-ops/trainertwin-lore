A humbling milestone: more than 400,000 people have watched my TEDx on 6 communication truths that everyone should know. 

The fact that universities around the world incorporate this talk into their communication classes is equally humbling. 

It's a celebration of analog communication in an ever-more digital world. For instance: just because you add more communication channels doesn't mean you automatically improve communication quality.

I gave this talk this already in 2017 at TEDx Vasa . Let's just say that the importance of communication and collaboration is as important today as it was then.
