The power of having a network with amazing people!

A few days ago I was in Munich, Germany and I met up with Katja Schaefer. We first met very briefly in Bali in September when we attended the same conference. 

Katja is a leadership coach and fellow speaker, and we had so much to discuss. We talked about business models, resilience, coaching, etc. And not in a drab building or a beige conference room. No no. We spent six hours walking the city and she was a great guide too. 

Katja embodied the generosity that I so often feel when I meet other speakers. 

Why am I sharing this? Because a network of good people is invaluable.

• Find your tribe
• Add value to them
• Ask for help and support

And who knows, You might end up in a Walk&Talk meeting in the streets of a beautiful city
