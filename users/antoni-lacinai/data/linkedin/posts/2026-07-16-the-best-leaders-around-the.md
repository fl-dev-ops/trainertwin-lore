The best leaders around the world fulfill their teams' ABC of needs.
Autonomy
Belonging
Competence

There is a lot of talk about cultural differences globally, but my perspective is that these three needs are universal. 
We all want to feel that we have a say in what we do, that we are part of a tribe, and that we contribute to this tribe.
