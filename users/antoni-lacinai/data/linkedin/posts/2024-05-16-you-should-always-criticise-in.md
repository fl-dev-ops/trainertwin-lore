You should always criticise in private
Or should you...?

Praise in public. Criticise Praise in private. That's what most of us have learned over the years. And it makes sense. 

You don't want to embarrass people who did something wrong
You want to display good behavior to the group

Ok, but what if someone is bullying a colleague at a meeting?
Should you really let that pass and then later talk to the person?
Or should you address it immediately so everyone hears that this behavior is unacceptable?

What do you think? Share your thoughts
