One of the biggest reasons customers leave you isn’t price. It’s not even product quality.

It’s the feeling that you don’t care.

Years ago, a receptionist named Linn greeted me at a hotel in Helsingborg with a smile that reached her eyes. When I told her I had no fresh shirt for tomorrow’s keynote, she said: “You're the same size as my colleague. Wait here.” Five minutes later, she handed me a freshly ironed white shirt. No fuss. Just kindness.

Compare that to my last car-buying experience. No one answered the phone or called back. No one responded to my emails. I even went to the store, and the salesperson just waved toward the car I was interested in and stayed in his glass office. I was there for 15 minutes. He never came out. They lost a 70,000€ deal that day - and they don't even know it.

If you’re not Apple or Harley-Davidson, you need someone like Linn. Someone who 𝗰𝗮𝗿𝗲𝘀.
Otherwise, you’re left with discounts, lock-ins, and bitter customers.

But with a Linn? You get loyal ambassadors. Like me. I’ve told her story on at least 100 stages to over 10,000 people.

CARE. For your clients, colleagues, and why not the whole planet. It's gonna show on your bottom line.
