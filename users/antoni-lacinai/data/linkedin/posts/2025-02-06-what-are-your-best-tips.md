What are your best tips to keep good people?

I read an article claiming that 48% of employees are ready to leave when the economy improves. It's a leadership challenge, if any. 

Supply and demand come in waves. Due to the recession, employers have the upper hand right now. 

But soon enough, the tide will turn. The "Great Resignation 2.0" might appear, and with nearly half of your employees considering leaving their jobs when economic conditions improve, the challenge is huge. I don't know about you, but isn't that a scary high number? Most probably, we won't see that much movement, but still...

You don't want to lose your best people—it's too costly. Can you take proactive steps to engage and motivate your workforce 𝗻𝗼𝘄 to avoid high turnover (and a hangover) later?

𝗗𝗼 𝘆𝗼𝘂 𝗣𝗿𝗮𝗶𝘀𝗲 𝘁𝗵𝗲𝗺?
Employees stay where they feel valued.

𝗗𝗼 𝘆𝗼𝘂 𝗽𝗿𝗼𝗺𝗼𝘁𝗲 𝗙𝗹𝗲𝘅𝗶𝗯𝗶𝗹𝗶𝘁𝘆?
A work-life balance is key to retention.

𝗗𝗼 𝘆𝗼𝘂 𝗰𝗼𝗺𝗺𝘂𝗻𝗶𝗰𝗮𝘁𝗲 𝗧𝗿𝘂𝘀𝘁? 
Transparency from leadership builds loyalty.
