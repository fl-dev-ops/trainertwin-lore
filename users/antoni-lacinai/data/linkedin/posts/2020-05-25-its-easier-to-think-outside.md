It's easier to THINK OUTSIDE THE BOX
if you feel safe INSIDE THE BOX

If you are criticized, bullied or ridiculed,  your stress levels goes up the roof, and you will not get access to more advanced cognitive functions where you are constructive and creative.

If you are praised and included, the opposite happens and you will be able to really contribute.

How is it where you work? Do you make each other feel good?
