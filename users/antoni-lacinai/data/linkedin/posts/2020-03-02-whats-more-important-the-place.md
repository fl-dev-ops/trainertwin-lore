What's more important: The place or the people?

I believe that:

IT'S NOT THE PLACE, IT'S THE PEOPLE
If you surround yourself with the right people, good things will happen. And sometimes the right person simply makes other people feel good.

IT'S NOT THE PLACE, IT'S THE PLACEMENT - OF THE PEOPLE 
Put the right person in the right position and good things will happen.

IT'S NOT EVEN THE PEOPLE. IT'S THE BOSS
Of all the factors that affect an employee's engagement, most are about the manager's behavior. (You go to a job and you leave a boss)

But hey, you might say, Of course a good workplace matters? Sure it does. But if I had to choose between a good place and the right people, I choose the right people every day of the week. A venue is much easier to find or replace than a key individual.

Be best!

// Antoni

3 more insights:

- Create a really good service - internally. There is a strong correlation between how you communicate internally and how you communicate with the outside.

- Find the right people (both managers and colleagues) who are passionate about the same things and share the same vision and values.

- Make the location optimal. Be inspired by the best offices, the best conference facilities, and then steal with pride.
