Do you want to be likable?
Match people

If I am like you I tend to like you
If I am not like you I tend to not like you

Find common ground
Match words, tempo, body language

Do it with a genuine interest

I remember failing miserably at a customer meeting. 
I spoke fast, my client was s l o w
I chose expressions my client couldn't relate to

I failed
I didn't get the business

And rightfully so
It is still an embarrassing moment
