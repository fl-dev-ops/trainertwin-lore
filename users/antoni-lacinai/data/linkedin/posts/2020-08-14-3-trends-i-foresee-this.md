3 TRENDS I FORESEE THIS FALL IN THE EVENT INDUSTRY

Every time I have looked into my home office this summer I have sighed and turned away. It's been a mess. Cords, microphones, light, web cameras, tripods, switches... I needed a break after a steep learning curve in new tech for online keynotes. I thought I was good at this before Corona. I wasn't.

 But now I am back in business, starting with these three trends I see. Do you agree?

1. The demand for digital deliveries with high quality will go up.
A laptop and it's built-in mic and camera will not cut it. These demands will create new winners and losers

2. We will stop comparing digital events with analog. We will instead compare them with TV-productions. Every event pros I have talked to say the same thing. Short clips, program leaders instead of moderators, jingles, different cameras etc. 

3. We'll see plan B -preparations all the time. All physical events will have a plan B for digital deliveries in case restrictions put a stop to them. 

And longer term? I believe that at least 50% of all events the coming year will be digital. At least 30%  after that will be digital. We've gotten used to them and see the benefits. 

What's your opinion?
