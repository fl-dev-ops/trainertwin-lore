Can you be simple - but with substance?
Congratulations: You are a super communicator!

Many people, leaders and team members alike, have what I call a Clarity Gap. It's the difference between what they actually mean and what they say on the one hand, and what people understand and remember on the other hand. 

Your mantra should be: 
- Make people feel smart. 
- Make my messages stick

Don't overcomplicate. Don't oversimplify. Find the sweetspot between those two. 

I am here for you if you need me, as your 1:1 personal trainer. Just like in the gym, but for communication.
