As a leader, you cannot motivate anyone!
Tough insight? Maybe. 

What I claimed isn't completely correct. You can work with external motivation, like rewards and punishments. These are shallow and unsustainable. 

Inner motivation is a different story, but this is where you cannot tread. You can inspire and educate, but you cannot motivate. 

Thoughts?
