Today is often called 𝘉𝘭𝘶𝘦 𝘔𝘰𝘯𝘥𝘢𝘺. “The saddest day of the year.”

There is no scientific basis for that claim. Still, January can feel heavy. In the Nordic countries where I live, we deal with darkness, cold, tight finances after the holidays, and energy that simply runs low.

But instead of asking “Are people sad today?” better leaders ask:
• How are we doing right now? 
• Where is energy leaking? 
• Who needs more clarity and less pressure? 
• Who needs to feel seen?

That is caring leadership to me. Care creates energy. Energy creates engagement.

On a personal note: I am consistent with my health goals. Same goals for nearly 18 years. So today is not blue for me. It is the color of determination.

What color is your Monday?
