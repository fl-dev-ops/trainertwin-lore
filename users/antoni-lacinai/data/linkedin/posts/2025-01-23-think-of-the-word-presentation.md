Think of the word "presentation" as PRESENT-ation
It's a gift. Treat it as such.

Imagine it's your birthday. When you are getting a present, you appreciate a nice gift wrapping; when you open it, you also appreciate something inside. Something that makes you happy. 

What if you got the best gift wrapping but there was nothing inside?

It's the same with a presentation. Make sure you have memorable content, packaged in meaningful gestures, and the right tonality.
