Is your message easy to remember? Are you clear and concrete, and does your message stick?

I remember once when a person crossed the street and said: 
"I don't remember anything you said, but you were awesome!!!" At that point, I realized why I didn't see any changes. How could they?

You might have the best insights, but if you miss out on how to communicate them, chances are you will end up in a situation where they say: 
"I can't remember what you said, but I am sure it was something important..."

And people cannot act on what they don't remember.

For your convenience, here are five ways to make your messages stick.
