Communication can sometimes go in circles 
And it's not always bad. 

At a trade show, you will have a high number of interactions. Let's call it a meeting wheel - The simplest and easiest process for booth staff:

𝗛𝗜 - Follow up with a question 
𝗪𝗵𝗼 𝗮𝗿𝗲 𝘆𝗼𝘂? - Qualify the visitor
𝗪𝗵𝗮𝘁 𝗮𝗿𝗲 𝘆𝗼𝘂𝗿 𝗻𝗲𝗲𝗱𝘀? - Analyze with interest
𝗧𝗵𝗶𝘀 𝗶𝘀 𝘄𝗵𝗮𝘁 𝗜 𝗰𝗮𝗻 𝗱𝗼 𝗳𝗼𝗿 𝘆𝗼𝘂 - Keep it short
𝗛𝗼𝘄 𝗱𝗼 𝘄𝗲 𝗺𝗼𝘃𝗲 𝗼𝗻? - Get a commitment
𝗚𝗼𝗼𝗱𝗯𝘆𝗲 - End it on a good note

And then repeat as many times as you can. 

You got this!
