Never trust a Swedish train
To ever be on time
Come snow or sun or a little rain,
hear the wagons whine
“Don’t push us, we are delicate
We’re sensitive as hell 
And that is why we’re always late
How much? No one can tell…
There is no past. 
There is no future 
There is only now
So be more zen
Use mindfulness 
Accept the fate and bow”
