7 Leadership Traits in Times of Uncertainty and Change.

I meet many L&D people, managers, and executives who struggle right now. What can you do in your organization to cope with the impending recession, climate crisis, inflation, war, etc.? 

Here are 7 tips that can help you:

1. Lead by example. 
   ✔️ Skip the perks if your people don't get them.
   
2. Be transparent and clear. 
   ✔️ You gain more trust and commitment. 
   
3. Involve more than you inform. 
   ✔️ You don't have to have all the answers yourself. 
   
4. Be responsive. 
   ✔️ There will be a lot of questions.
   
5. Be empathetic.
   ✔️Acknowledge the concerns among all of you.
   
6. Care about your customers. 
   ✔️Best customer service wins in the long run.
   
7. Have fun.
   ✔️Find reasons to laugh and celebrate.

Communication, motivation, and performance are connected. In good times and in bad.
