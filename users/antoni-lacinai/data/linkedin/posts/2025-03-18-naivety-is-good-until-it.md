Naivety is good. Until it isn't

I got an email from a Finnish startup. Two young entrepreneurs developed a high-priced portfolio of sunglasses. Would I be interested in MC'ing their red-carpet event with 150 influencers? 

The work seemed like fun. The fee was good. I had the time. I said "Yes, let's do it"

I paid for the travel
I bought a tuxedo
I did the gig
It was easy
It was fun!

Two weeks later, they went bust, and I would never get the money I invoiced. 

What can we learn from this? Not trusting people? Getting paid in advance? Saying no when in doubt? Do your research? What are your thoughts?

My own thoughts: I will always be naive. I believe in people. But I have to minimize being gullible. 
---------

I did get a pair of sunglasses though. They costed me more than 7000€ in missed fees and expenses. And I also have a story to tell and a tuxedo to wear ^_^
