How do you reset at work after a break or a quieter summer period? 

LinkedIn asked me. I will interpret it as "How do you restart" rather than reset. I have spoken about this on Swedish national TV so here are my five best tips/questions:

1. If it works, could you start slow? You will have plenty of time to be overwhelmed and stressed again.
2. Could you set goals for the fall? It is a new beginning and worthwhile looking forward to.
3. Can you start on a Thursday? Be sneaky and set your out-of-office on the following Monday.  This gives you time to catch up. 
4. Do you have something to look forward to? If you do, you won't feel that you only grind.
5. Do you have a to-do list? Or a not-to-do list? Both will help you create momentum. 

I started two weeks ago. I took it slow (because I could) Now I am almost back to speed with some webinars and tomorrow I am traveling to Finland for my first in-person keynote this fall. I feel good ^_^

PS - what do you look forward to?
