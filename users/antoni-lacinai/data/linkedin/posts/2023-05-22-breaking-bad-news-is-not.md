Breaking bad news is not easy...

...and the more that is at stake, the harder it can be.

THANK YOU TV4 and excellent TV hosts Martin and Soraya for a great conversation on this delicate topic.

What are the three typical mistakes that people do?
- They make it about them instead of the recipient of your bad news.
- They beat around the bush instead of stating the news
- They don't respect people's feelings and instead say things like "Cheer up"

In this interview, I also share the right steps to take. It's not fun, or easy, but will help you both come out of it with dignity. If you want the steps, DM me or write YES in the comment.
