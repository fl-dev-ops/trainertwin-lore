A imaginary dialog on communication, leadership styles and incongruence:
 
- Daddy daddy, is it wrong to lie?
- Of course not my son. 
- So it's ok if I tell my class mates and teachers in school that I won't bully the skinny guy, but then I hit him anyway and steal his lunch money?
- It exactly how you should do it. Lie to their faces and then punch him. 

- Daddy I don't think the teachers believed me. They said that they would be very upset if I did it and that I won't be allowed to sit at their tables during lunch. 
- So what did you do then my son?
- I punched the thin guy in the face and stole his lunch money. And I said it was really my lunch money anyway, so I was justified. Now then don't let me even eat in the school restaurant but I can eat elsewhere for now. They will soon forget. 
- I am so proud of you my son. You will grow up to be an important man, knowing that if you want something you just take it. 
- Can I keep lying?
- Absolutely. If someone protests, you say that you never hit anybody.
- Even if they see me do it?
- Even if they see you do it. 
- Thanks dad. See you later, I m going after another skinny guy now. He won't know what hit him...
