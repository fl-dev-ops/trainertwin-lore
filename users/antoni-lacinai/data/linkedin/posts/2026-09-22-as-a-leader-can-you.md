As a leader, can you do the work without your team? 

Can they do it without you?

These are questions I often ask in leadership summits and HR conferences. And I always get the same response

Q1: 100% No
Q2: 50% Yes. 50% No

Interesting...

Can you reach your goals, fulfill your strategies, and meet your KPIs without them? Most likely not. But they cannot do it without you for any longer period. 

You are important. You matter. Or at least: Your role matters. Your function is important. What you do with it is often another question. 

Communication shapes behavior. Behavior drives results. If you are not around, communication can break down quickly

My view. Yours?
