There is a lot of US vs THEM going on in the world today. Not good. 

To have that mentality in an organisation is just stupid and far from productive. We can do better. 
Expand the WE because the bigger WE then smaller Them, which leads to better cooperation, better results and more fun at work. 

How is it like where you work? Can you hear it in your communication? If you hear a lot of Us vs Them, then you know that there is no harmony in your workplace.
