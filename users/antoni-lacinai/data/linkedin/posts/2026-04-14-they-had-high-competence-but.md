They had high competence but low influence, until communication changed...
 
I worked with a global retail procurement team that was doing everything “right.” They had strong expertise and a big budget responsibility. 
 
Yet their ideas didn’t land. They felt insecure, and decision-making slowed.  They finally realized that in key moments, how they showed up didn’t match what they knew.
 
They were too detailed in leadership settings and too hesitant under pressure.  
They were also too focused on content and too little on connection. 
 
So we didn’t add more knowledge. Well, we actually did, but only when it came to communication. 
 
And here’s the shift: +29% non-verbal presence,  +18% emotional connection,  +15% communication under pressure,  +8% strategic clarity 
 
All in half a day.
 
That’s when the team moved from functional contributors to strategic voices. Now they can show up with more confidence and clarity, and build trust. 
 
And that’s when things started to move.

pretty cool if you ask me ^_^

Upskilling is within your grasp, too. 
You won’t get a different result without changing people's behavior.  
And you won’t change behavior unless you change your communication.
