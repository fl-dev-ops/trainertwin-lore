Why focusing on your Why can be bad for your business…

I meet many companies who are going through workshops and processes to find their core values. I am sometimes facilitating these journeys myself. They want to express their Why and their deep beliefs. Often, the end result are three power-words that the employees can find on their intranet or on roll-ups in a conference room. Words like “Open, Generous, Creative” or “Respect, Professionalism, Perseverance” etc. 

The problem is that focusing on your Why isn’t enough. Having three words on a poster isn’t enough. Those deep beliefs will quickly become shallow and unsatisfying unless they are followed up with wanted behaviors.

Tens of millions have watched the leadership guru #SimonSinek's TEDx talk “Start with Why”. Very few people think about the two outer circles; How and What. Or actually most organizations focus on those two solely - but they lack a genuine connection to their Why. 

That disconnect will create frustration among employees, being disappointed that all those fancy words were just fluff without any good examples in their day-to-day business. Instead they might witness the opposite behavior compared to the core values, which at large goes under the radar. 

Frustration then leads to either conflicts or general disengagement. Multiple studies have shown that the bottom line for a company is greatly affected by the staffs engagement levels. 

My advice then? Before you kick off a project or a workshop finding your Why, and your deep values: Make sure that you in that process also include a correlating How and What and that you then follow-up on all three. “Are what we do, and how we do it, in line with our core values, vision and mission?” How do we measure it? What do we do if they aren’t? What do we do when they are?

Thanks for reading my mind.
