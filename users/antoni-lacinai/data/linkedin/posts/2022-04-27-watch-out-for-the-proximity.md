Watch out for the Proximity bias, dear hybrid leader!

Brian Kropp  from Gartner explains this when Fast Company recently wrote an article about the "Command & Control" managers who want to force people back to the office or otherwise treat remote workers like second class employees. 

Scary stuff...

How is it at your workplace?

#leadership #hybrid #engagement #proximitybias 

Yesterday I gave a keynote about hybrid leadership, saying that it has the potential of bringing the best of two worlds - if you do it right. 

One of the many risks hybrid leaders face, is that they favor team member who are physically closer to them, regardless of wether they are the best match for the project or promotion etc.
