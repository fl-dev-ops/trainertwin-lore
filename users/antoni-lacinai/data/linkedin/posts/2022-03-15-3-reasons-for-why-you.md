3 REASONS for why you should COMMUNICATE YOUR GOALS

Some experts claim that it is bad to share your goals because then your motivation goes down. Even if this is the case for some, it is not the case for many. 

Share your goals. Because when you do, you will gain three benefits that will help you reach them. At least one of them is especially good for large matrix organizations where you depend on each other to get the job done, but they will help you both professionally and in your private life.
