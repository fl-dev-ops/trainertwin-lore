Clarity is subtraction

I recently coached a CFO to improve his communication skills. He wasn't the typical finance dude. He had tattoos and earrings, and looked more like a heavy metal fan  (yes, I know you can be both, but I am painting a picture here ^_^)

His energy was high! His empathy too. He struggled because he used too many words when he could have said it with half as many. The problem was clarity.

Clarity is subtraction. Getting to the point with 1. fewer, better words, and 2. better descriptions and stories.

So, on the one hand, you want to get to the point. On the other hand, you want to color your message so it is memorable. Not an easy task. 

How do you bring clarity to your messages?
