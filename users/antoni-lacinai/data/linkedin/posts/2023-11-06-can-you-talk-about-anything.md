Can you talk about anything in your team?

Or should you avoid certain topics?

The answers are No and Yes. Based on my experience of coaching leaders and teams across the globe, there are topics that make people uncomfortable or even combative. Avoid them if you can. This was the topic on TV4 where I yet again was invited to share insights on how to boost motivation and communication. 

If somebody persists, try this:
- Be nice
- Set boundaries 
- Stay away from conflicts
- Agree on what is ok or not
- Be interested, not interesting

#keynotespeaker #communication #motivation

Let’s make our time here on earth count.
