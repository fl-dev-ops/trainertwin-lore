WHO do you want to get STUCK WITH at an airport?

“My wife says that who you hang out with is important.” a friend of mine declared. “She says that you should have people around you who "you want to get stuck with at an airport."”

What a great expression! 

Ask yourself:
•	When you have been through something difficult - who do you want to call?
•	When you've done something fun - who do you want to call?
•	When you just want to talk to someone - who do you want to call?

Is it a colleague you're thinking of? A family member? A friend? 

The people you think of are the people that you should cherish and nurture and love. They can help you become the best version of yourself. 

Communicate with them as often as you can. Those people make you feel good, and when you feel good, it's so much easier to spread the joy further and do good things for your business, for mankind, and for the whole planet. 

Thank you for reading my mind!
