Outcome goals are great ...

...but are they enough?

Most organizations I coach focus on outcome goals, or result goals, as they are also called. 

The same goes for athletes and sports teams. 
Results are everything!

Why? Because these goals are easy to measure. 

✔️ Turnover
✔️ Gold medal
✔️ Market share

So what's the problem?

→ Your competitors want that turnover
→ Your competitors want that gold medal
→ Your competitors want your market share

And you can't control them. 

You are often less motivated by what is outside your control

• Own your goals
• Define your steps
• Enjoy your journey

If it isn't concrete - it isn't a goal


PS - have you set your goals for 2024 yet?
