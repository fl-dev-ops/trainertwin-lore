Communication is so much more than a person presenting. 

It is how you look, how you sound, how you listen, what you wear... and perhaps most of all - what you DO. 

Say that you claim to be friendly and then skip asking one single colleague when you go to lunch...
Say that you are generous and then withhold useful information to your peers in order to get ahead...

What are you communicating today?
