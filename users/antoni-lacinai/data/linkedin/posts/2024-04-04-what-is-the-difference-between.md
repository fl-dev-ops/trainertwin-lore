"What is the difference between hearing and listening?"

I asked my son this question when he was only six years old. His answer was priceless!

"To be able to hear is simply to not be deaf... but to listen, that is when you are trying to understand what the other person is saying Dad, and not only saying but also what they really mean."

I looked at my boy for three silent seconds. Then I said:
"Welcome to the company my son!"

In my leadership training as well as my sales training, the art of listening seems to be the hardest to master for most people...

Why?
1. People are stressed
2. People want to connect dots too fast
3. People have an inner voice, constantly judging
