”Why do some psychopaths become leaders?” 
I got that question after a keynote in Tallinn. My answer:
There are two kinds of empathy. 
Empathy by calculation and empathy by compassion. 
A psychopath can only manage the first, but a compassionate human can 
master both!
Which of these that will dominate a workplace depends on the CEO and the culture of the organization, but there is only one way that is sustainable. Guess which?
