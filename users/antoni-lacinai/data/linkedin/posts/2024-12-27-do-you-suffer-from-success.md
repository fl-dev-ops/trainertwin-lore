Do you suffer from Success Syndrome?
Yep, it's a thing

So, we turn the page from 24 to 25 and you are doing great. You are successful - and therefore happy and grateful, right?

Wrong. 

If you suffer from success syndrome you might instead feel high pressure, isolation, stress, and a constant need to perform even better (boy, do I relate), which can potentially lead to burnout. 

Ouch!

You may feel failure more intensely, become overly focused on work at the expense of other areas of life, or feel pressured by external expectations. Where is the fun in that?

Your success becomes a trap and a constant dissatisfaction. 

So, dear high-achieving human being, if  you want to feel a higher sense of well-being instead of stress-being, start to communicate with yourself and say

• I am grateful
• I am doing well
• I am not my performance
• I can still fail and that is ok
• It’s not how many likes I get
• it’s not about the money I make
• I am the sum of the people around me
• I am best when I act on my values
• I grow without burning out
• I am good enough
• I appreciate me

I am writing this both for myself and for you. People around me claim that I am successful. I have a really hard time agreeing since I know how much farther I want to go and I am not even halfway there.  It's like getting high praise from 99 people but you still focus on that one who says "naah"

Do you suffer from Success Syndrome? How are you coping? Share.
