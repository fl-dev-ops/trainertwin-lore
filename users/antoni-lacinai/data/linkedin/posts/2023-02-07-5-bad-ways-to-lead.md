5 BAD WAYS TO LEAD A HYBRID TEAM:

1.  FORCE EVERYONE BACK INTO THE OFFICE FULL-TIME. 
Nobody wants to feel distrusted and such a decision makes employees feel forced into something they don't want. The feeling of micromanagement is present and the autonomy is gone. It's a motivational killer.

2. LEAVE EVERYTHING FREE TO EACH INDIVIDUAL TO DECIDE. 
It is equally wrong to abdicate one's leadership. It will create frustration, difficulties with cooperation,  and misunderstandings. All this leads to lower engagement.

3. REDUCE THE NUMBER OF WORKPLACES. 
Very few people like insecurity. By removing all fixed seats and also having fewer of them, you set the stage for more stress and frustration for those who come in later and find that they are "not wanted" and may not even have anywhere to sit.

4. CONTINUE WITH POWERPOINT CONFERENCES. 
Not using the team's collective intelligence with the help of co-creation, discussions,  and reflections was stupid before the pandemic. The difference is that people are now used to working digitally, so once you have a physical meeting, the demands are high for the physical meetings to be better. Otherwise, the result will be reduced engagement.

5. OFFER BUNS TO THOSE PHYSICALLY PRESENT AT MEETINGS. 
A sure way to create an us-and-them feeling is for those in the office to receive benefits that the home workers cannot take part in. Those remote will feel like second-class employees and quickly lose motivation.

What other good advice can you share, if you want a really bad hybrid workplace? Mia Liljeberg Anna Bellman Daniela Landherr
