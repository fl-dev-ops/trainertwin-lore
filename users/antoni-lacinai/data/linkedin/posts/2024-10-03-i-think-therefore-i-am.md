“I think, therefore I am”
With AI, I wonder if that is true?

Cogito ergo sum
Famous words from Descartes 
With AI doing a lot of “thinking” I think we should focus on our human side. 

How about “Sentio ergo sum”
I feel, therefore I am. 

Or “Curo ergo sum”
I CARE, therefore I am

I believe in a more care-centric leadership and communication
