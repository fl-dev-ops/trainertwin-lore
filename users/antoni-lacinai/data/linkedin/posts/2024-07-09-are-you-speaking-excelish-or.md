Are you speaking Excelish? Or PowerPointish? Or Acronymish? 
Don't!

I get that it is hard to simplify things, especially if you are well-read and intelligent. 

You don't want to patronize people
You don't want to dumb it down
You want to be correct

But this behavior is not useful if the recipient feels stupid. 

There is an acronym (since I just mentioned the problem with them):
KISS = Keep It Simple Stupid

I prefer: Keep it simple - not stupid

In this video, I explain the 3 step maturity process of your communication skills. Most people reach level 2. Very few reach level 3. 

What level are you at? 1-3
