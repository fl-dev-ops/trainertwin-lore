We need 3-11 positive interactions  for every negative to get a high-performing team. 

High-performing  teams have fewer accidents, higher engagement, better customer service,  and make fewer errors. And it starts with the simplest of things: Be nice!

We like to be around people who spread joy. (when they come, not when they leave) and there are numbers to how many positive interactions you need to get a high-performing team. 3-11 for every negative. 

I have pondered about the cap on 11. Why not more? My conclusion is that if the number is higher, the team risk becoming a cult. But so far, in my decades in the corporate world, coaching leaders and teams around the globe, I have yet to find any such organization...
