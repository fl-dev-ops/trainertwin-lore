If you want to reach far - together - then share your goals. Let people know what you aim for. You will be surprised how much help you can get. 

This works really well in the business environment, and perhaps especially in large matrix organizations. I have been working in those. It's not easy. Also, in some workplaces I see how people don't want to share. Like it's a powerplay going on. Yak.

But - If all departments were transparent with their goals, misunderstandings will be fewer and productivity will go up. 

Organizations who share knowledge and goals outperforms those who don't. 

What goals can you share with your colleagues (you don't have to answer that here ^_^)
