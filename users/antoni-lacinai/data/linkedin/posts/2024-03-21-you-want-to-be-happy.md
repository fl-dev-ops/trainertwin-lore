You want to be happy?
Hang out with happy people!

Research found that for each additional happy friend you have you increase your probability of being happy by about 9 percent! 𝗜𝘀𝗻'𝘁 𝘁𝗵𝗮𝘁 𝗮𝗺𝗮𝘇𝗶𝗻𝗴?

Just think about your workplace. Positive energy is like a magnet of laughter, collaboration, fun, productivity, and great results. 

Negative energy is unfortunately also a magnet. Often stronger. There you will find trash-talk, bullying, racism, sabotage, low customer satisfaction, and poor results.

Negative emotions stick to you like gum on a sole. 

Leave all the whiners, energy-draining complainers, saboteurs, and pseudo-victims behind you when you enter your workplace. Don't entertain them. They are vampires, sucking the joy out of you. Instead:

Smile
Be happy
Be friendly
Be dependable
Be that 9 percent 
Make a positive difference
Be the person you want to have as a friend

That's what I believe. 
What about you?
