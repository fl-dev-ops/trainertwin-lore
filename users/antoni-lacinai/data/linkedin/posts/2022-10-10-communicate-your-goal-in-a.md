COMMUNICATE YOUR GOAL

In a business context, clear communication is key. If you communicate your goals, you get three benefits and that's what I share in this video. Enjoy ^_^ 

#leadership #productivity #keynote #goalsetting #communication

Today is my 24th wedding anniversary! 
I remember how I clearly communicated my goal about where to live: 
I resided in Gothenburg. She lived in Stockholm. So she came to my home town for a weekend and I showed her every beautiful place in the city explaining that she should move there. Shortly thereafter I packed my stuff and moved to Stockholm... I lost the round but I won the game.
