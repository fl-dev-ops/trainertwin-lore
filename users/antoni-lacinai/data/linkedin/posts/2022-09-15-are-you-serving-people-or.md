Are you serving people or are you serving a cause?

Welcome to a Servant leadership podcast and a lovely talk.

I was recently a guest at dr. Lena Holmes podcast where we had a great time, talking about servant leadership, employee engagement,  and how to act and communicate as a leader. 

One thought for you all: Is servant leadership about serving your team or serving a higher purpose, a cause, and then inviting people to join you on the quest to fulfill that purpose?

Thank you Lena for the inspiring meeting!
