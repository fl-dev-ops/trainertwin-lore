LEADERS: DON'T BLOCK THE SUN!

Is anyone able to motivate anyone else? Can any manager eat you full? Can any leader drink you un-thirsty? The answer is simply – No, they can’t.

Motivation comes from the latin word movere which is about moving, mentally or physically. Motivation comes from within. If you are motivated, you will move. 

A simple formula for motivation is: MOTIVATION = GOALS - RESISTANCE

If you have a strong purpose, a great drive, a clear goal, the right knowledge, courage and resources, you put them all in the Goal-basket

If you have only a fuzzy idea, no commitment, fear, laziness, and incompetence, you put it in the Resistance-basket.

Which ever is biggest will determine if you move or not i.e. if you are motivated or not.

Can you really grow a plant? I would say no to that question as well. The plant grows if you nourish it. And that’s what you should focus on as a manager and a leader. Nourish your people and then stay out of the way so you don't block the sun. Let them grow.

Have a motivating, meaningful day ^_^
