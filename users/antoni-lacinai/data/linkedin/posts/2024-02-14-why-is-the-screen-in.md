Why is the screen in the middle when you present?

Everywhere I go it is the same.
Projector lights in your face.
A screen behind you. 
Why is that?

I had an awesome training yesterday in Riga. The group was engaged, active, motivated. We had fun and I shared my communication insights with them.

There was only one thing that struck me. As it so often does. Hotels don't understand meetings. 

The staff were super nice. 
The food was excellent. 
We had a great time!

Perhaps it could be a USP (unique selling point) for a hotel to show the clients that they really get meetings and not only food and logistics. 

I think so. Do you?

PS - a concrete tip: Use a black background instead. It will at least take away the blinding effect and your shadow too
