Friday thought: To show your speaker notes on slides, is like showing your used toilet paper at a restaurant...

Getting images in your head? Very few people will be inspired, motivated, and engaged by this abuse of a visual tool. Just stop doing it. 

Keep that in mind the next time you are preparing your slides. 

Here are five simple tips for your PowerPoints

1. Use a dark background
2. One message per slide
3. Use LARGE font sizes
4. Use a dark background   
5. Combine 1-3 words with a relevant picture      
6. Do 2 versions. One simple for you to present. On "richer" for people to read. 

Are you going out to dinner this weekend? Think about this and bon apetit ^_^

PS - which of the tips will you do more of? 1-6
