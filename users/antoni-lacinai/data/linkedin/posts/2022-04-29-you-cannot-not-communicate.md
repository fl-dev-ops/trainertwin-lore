you cannot NOT communicate - Everything about you communicates.

Have you ever gone into a store where two clerks are talking to each other and ignoring you completely? How did that make you feel? Did they communicate with you? Of course they did! They told you that you’re not important. 

Communication is not only what you say. It is:
• What you think
• What you say
• What you DO (how you look, sound, listen, act)

You cannot choose whether or not you communicate. You can’t even decide what you are communicating. that's up to everyone else...

Imagine you’re the boss and you are in a meeting. You find it boring and decide to be efficient, so you pick up you phone and respond to some emails. This will not go unnoticed. Your people will think “Hm, we’re obviously less important than whatever is on that screen...”
