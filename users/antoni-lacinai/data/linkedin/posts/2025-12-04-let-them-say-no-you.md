Let them say "No"

You can help a lonely person at work by inviting them and letting them decide. It is empowering, engaging, and makes them feel included. 

• Ask them to join your table when you have lunch. 
  Let them say No
  
• Ask if they have an opinion or idea to share in the meeting. 
  Let them say No
  
• Ask them to tag along for an AW.
  Let them say No
  
Loneliness doesn't always show on the outside. 
They might sit at another table because they feel left out and don't want to intrude. The happiness they will feel upon being invited can make their day. 

They might agree in the meeting, but you don't know until you ask. Or they have an excellent contribution that you will never hear because they are shy. 

They might be happy that you asked, even if they can't go with you. Or they will come,  but they don't want to show their vulnerability. 

Don't say no on their behalf. And don't stop asking. You can be the best thing that happens to them that day, just by letting them say no. Or yes.
