Clarity without empathy is like a dentist fixing your cavity without anesthesia - while being irritated that you scream in pain. 

I recently spoke at an HR convention in Finland, arranged by Alma Media and HENRY-ry. Then I wrote this article for them. It's about the three communication superpowers, with a focus on Empathy. 

The reason? You increase clarity when you know who you're talking to and care that they understand. Any leader who understands this is much better off, and so are their teams. 

Communication shapes behavior. Behavior drives results.
