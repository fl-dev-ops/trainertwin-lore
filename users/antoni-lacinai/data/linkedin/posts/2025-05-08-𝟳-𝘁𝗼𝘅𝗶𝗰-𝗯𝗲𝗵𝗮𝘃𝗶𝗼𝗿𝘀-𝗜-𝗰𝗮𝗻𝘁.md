Communication, engagement, and results go hand in hand. But some behaviors kill motivation and performance.

Here are 7 toxic behaviors I can't stand:

1. 𝗚𝗿𝗲𝗲𝗱𝘆 𝗹𝗲𝗮𝗱𝗲𝗿𝘀 – flying business class while cutting the team’s fruit baskets. Taking bonuses after layoffs. Everyone sees through it. 
2. 𝗕𝘂𝗹𝗹𝘆𝗶𝗻𝗴 – masked as “tough love” or "I was just joking". It’s not funny. It’s just mean.
3. 𝗦𝗮𝗯𝗼𝘁𝗲𝘂𝗿𝘀 – who show up to fight rather than contribute. Don’t like your job? Fix it or leave.
4. 𝗭𝗼𝗺𝗯𝗶𝗲 𝗺𝗲𝗲𝘁𝗶𝗻𝗴𝘀 – Endless PowerPoints, zero passion, zero engagement. Stop draining your team.
5. 𝗞𝗻𝗼𝘄-𝗶𝘁-𝗮𝗹𝗹𝘀 – who correct others to look smart, not to help. If you're not here to lift others, zip it.
6. 𝗕𝗮𝗰𝗸𝘀𝘁𝗮𝗯𝗯𝗲𝗿𝘀 – who smile in your face and then twist the knife. Yak!
7. 𝗠𝗲-𝗺𝗲-𝗺𝗲 𝗽𝗲𝗼𝗽𝗹𝗲 – who think only their voice matters and that stealing a colleague's ideas is good behavior. Spoiler: It is not!.

My advice: Do the opposite. Communicate with energy, empathy, and clarity. That moves people and teams forward, so you can finally implement your strategies and have a motivated workplace.
