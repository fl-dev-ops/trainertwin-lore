### Eliot Freed (Live Marketing - Digital Events and Experiences at Ericsson)
> Thanks for sharing this. This was a thought-provoking conversation. The topic that is forefront in my mind on a daily basis. I agree that companies should probably focus more on the carrot as opposed to the stick approach when it comes to forcing employees to work in the office. I do think though that the past few years has created an inflection point into how work and collaboration can be done. We’ve left behind the one-size-fits-all approach where everyone has to be in the office all the time. We have the digital tools and online mindset to accomplish quite a lot remote these days. However in the end it really depends on the person and their situation and the type of work that they do. I do believe though that communities can be built both virtually, in person, and then hybrid. To say that a company can only innovate face-to-face is in my opinion not true and the companies that learn to embrace innovation through all means, regardless of where people physically are, will be the ones who succeed.
