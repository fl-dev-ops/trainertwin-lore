Very seldom do I see people so desperate as I have seen this time when I'm trying to get to Macedonia for a keynote tomorrow. 

The airline company is a low-budget company, and they allow only one piece of luggage on the plane. The rest has to be checked in. And the way people have squeezed their backpacks to fit that little compartment that they have as, a measuring device has been incredible, and I don't blame them. They try their best, taking out tennis shoes, putting stuff in their pockets instead, and so on. 

But I wonder what, what the idea is behind that from the airline company's perspective. Why are they trying so hard to piss off people? I don't know. 

Anyway, I'm looking forward to going to Skopje, and deliver a keynote about employee experience and customer experience at the same time. and a following masterclass on world-class communication 😃
