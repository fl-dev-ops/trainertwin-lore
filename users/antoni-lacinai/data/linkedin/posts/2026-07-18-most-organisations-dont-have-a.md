Most organisations don't have a strategy problem. They have a communication problem dressed in a strategy suit.

That line belongs to Antoni Lacinai. I heard it sitting across from him at The Black Friar in London.

The pub was built in 1875 on the site of a medieval Dominican friary. The Dominicans were known as the Order of Preachers. Their job was to communicate ideas so clearly that people would understand, believe, and act.

It seemed like the right place to meet someone who's built his career on exactly that.

Antoni calls it the Clarity Gap. It's the disconnect between what leaders mean and what their people truly grasp. He's delivered keynotes in 30 countries working with organisations like Ericsson, IKEA, and Mitsubishi. His TEDx talk has over 400,000 views. He was nominated International Speaker of the Year in Finland and voted one of Sweden's most popular speakers six years running.

But what struck me sitting in that pub was how he talked about empathy, clarity, and energy as disciplines. Not soft skills. Superpowers that can be trained.

I've been doing this for 30 years. The speakers who move a room are the ones who make complex ideas land. Antoni does that.

I'm delighted to say he's joined my inner circle of key speakers.

Where have you seen the Clarity Gap hit hardest?
