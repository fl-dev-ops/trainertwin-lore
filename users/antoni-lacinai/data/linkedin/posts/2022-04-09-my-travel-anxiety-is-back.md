My travel anxiety is back!

Last week I was flying for the first time in 18 months. I was going to the Swedish Lappland. It takes me two flights. 
The first flight was running late. Not good. 
Up in the air I realize I will only have 15-20 minutes to change flights. I asked the stewardess what my chances were. 
-	You will make it. Your connecting flight leaves from gate 8. You will land on gate F35.
-	F35? But that’s on terminal 5. The international terminal. I am flying domestic here. So what you’re saying is that I will make it to Terminal 4 in 20 minutes?
-	Yes
-	But don’t I have to go through a new security check then?
-	Yes
-	Hmm, ok thanks.

When the plane is at gate 35, I have 17 minutes before takeoff. I run. I get tired pretty quickly, but I am giving it everything I have. This is when I realize that it isn’t the first time. It’s a common sprint that I have done at so many occasions before. 

When I finally come to terminal 4 it is an abandoned terminal. Dark. Empty. Apocalyptic. My anxiety level is through the roof. I ask a group of people coming my way where to go now.
All flights are now on terminal 5, they said…

I make a new run for it. I cut the line of the check-in. I cut the line of the security check, although it still takes time. I run to gate 8. It is of course as far away it can be on the other side of terminal five. I am breathing hard and heavy. People hear me from 5 meters and move aside. I get to gate 8 and read the sign. “Departing to Munich”. What the …???? The stewardess helps me. My flight is moved to terminal 9, only 40 meters away. I run there. The steward behind the desk sees me and says:
-	I am sorry, the gate is closed. 
I see the plane on the other side of the window, still at the gate. Then I see the steward picking up a com-radio. He says
-	You’ll have to pick out a suitcase, the passenger is a no-show. 

I am that passenger…The plane sits there for another 5-10 minutes while they locate my luggage and remove it. 

At this point I am thinking. “I want to go home. I want to go to my studio and deliver my content online. This is not worth it.”

On-site appearances are rich, awesome, and really good. But the hassle to travel there… haha, I am not so sure that it beats virtual. Or am I just out of shape? What is your experience and attitude towards traveling to business meetings? 

Pre-pandemic I had a recurring nightmare where I missed my flight. Or boat, bus, train…
During the pandemic it shifted into a stressful dream that I couldn’t get my computer up and running. 
But now – I am back to my missed flights. And this time, not only in my dreams.
