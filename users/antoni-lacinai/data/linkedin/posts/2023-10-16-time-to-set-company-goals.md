Time to set company goals for 2024?

For me, there are three kinds of goals that I work with, from a time perspective:
Identity goals. Who are we? Who do we want to be?  They are long-lasting.
Outcome goals. What shall we achieve? They are medium-term  goals
Performance goals. How shall we get there?  These are activity-based, short-term goals. 

All three are needed if you want to go in the right direction. 

What are your reflections?
