How do you create the right atmosphere for a workshop?

Workshop time and this is the layout of the room when the 15 participants enter. So how do I create a good feeling?

First, I will ask them to “Grab a chair and sit so you can see all the others “ (great advice from Micke Darmell ) as they will naturally form a circle. 

Then I will ask them to turn to a neighbor and “tell them who made you feel good the last week”
This will make them more positive and open to new ideas. 

From then on it is all about cooperation 

How do you create an optimal atmosphere for a workshop?
