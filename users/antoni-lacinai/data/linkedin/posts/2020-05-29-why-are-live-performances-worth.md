Why are Live performances worth more than pre-recorded sessions?
The answer lies in our emotions

Google 110 seconds and David JP Phillips  where he explains the research about eye contact online, where live session elicited a stronger emotional response compared to recorded ones.

Why does this matter?

If we invest in something emotionally we will have a heightened experience and we will remember and learn more. That's value. We pay for the value of elevated emotions.
