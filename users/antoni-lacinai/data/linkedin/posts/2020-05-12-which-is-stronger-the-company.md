Which is stronger: the company culture or the regional culture? Or is it the individuals' personalities?

Later today, I will give an online keynote about "3 superpowers of Workplace Communication" (the ones on my t-shirt). There will be some 80 people from 15 countries and one question I ask myself, after all my travels around the world, is whether company culture is stronger than the location of where you were growing up. 

- Will the Chinese participants applaud when I explain the value of Energy, or will that be left to the Australian colleague?
- Will the bloke from Russia nod when I say that Empathy and compassion is a superpower or will that be more interesting to the Dane?
- Will the attendee from France agree that Clarity is vastly important or is that only for the German dude?

What do you think?
