Have you ever felt lonely?
You are not alone

According to Gallup. 20 % of all employees feel lonely at work. That hurts. I started to look at this when I read an article about a woman in Arizona who died in her office, and it took a full four days before anyone discovered this. 

Can you imagine? Nobody thought to seek her out. 
The employer claimed it was because of the increasingly common hybrid workplace situation. Ok, I can see the risk of this happening going down if everybody is in an office constantly. I can also see that it will not combat loneliness since many people also feel lonely in a crowd. 

Loneliness comes with a cost for both individuals and organizations.  
Engagement goes down
Absenteeism goes up
Churn goes up

I've been on TV and HR magazines talking about this and I saw an interesting YouTube podcast with Oprah talking about it. 

But - most of the questions were about what the lonely person can do. I suggest that the biggest responsibility lies with all the people around them. It's tough enough to suffer from loneliness. 

So look around. Say hello to people. Invite them to contribute. Invite them to join you. Praise them when they do something good. Noone should feel lonely. 

That's what I think? What about you?
