Be Simple but with Substance

Clarity matters for any leader today. In this 2-minute video, we first go back to 1504 and Michelangelo's take on clarity, then to the present day, where we seem to complicate the sh*t out of things. For no good reason at all. 

I see communication as a 3-step process:

1. You are simple because you don't know things
2. You are complicated because everyone else is
3. You are simple but with substance

Hands on your heart: Which one is you?
