3 values on a poster isn’t enough. Love them and live them. 

In the papers today I read about leaders who fail and make their employees feel either miserable or right out angry. 

In the first case there’s this young Swedish influencer who’s staff feel like shit. 
The value words are great: Kindness. Inspiration. Respect. 
The problem is that they don’t seem to translate into the right behavior and leadership. In communication, this is called being “incongruent” (what you say and what you do doesn’t add up)

I don’t know this woman but I am sure she is really driven and perhaps lost herself in her success at a young age. I hope she quickly get external help from mentors or other wise people so that she’ll be back on track and use that drive for good purposes. I am optimistic that she will. 

The second case is the rise and fall of failed battery maker Northvolt, also in Sweden. 
Their value words are:
Boldness. Passion. Excellence. 
Strong words. And as it seems; misinterpreted. 

While in a chapter 11 process the leaders are now working hard - to get high bonuses paid out to them… say what?

The greed is astounding. The staff is furious. The investors should be equally baffled and angry at the same time. 

A question arises. Should leaders get bonuses for trying (like kids getting medals for participating) or for getting results?

This behavior is nothing new. Bonuses while laying off people is common practice. Ethics is falling further back while ruthlessness is on the rise. 

As a leader you should have a North Star. Values that resonates with staff, customers as well as investors. But don’t stop with those words. It is when you love them and live them that true leaders are shining and leading the way for others. 

These two cases are shining too. Just not the way they should. 

That’s what I think. What’s your opinion?
