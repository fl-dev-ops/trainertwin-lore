You are awesome!
You influence people.

Or more accurately, you could rock. So many people decide to play it small and safe. I don't mean that everyone need to strive for the impact of Greta, Malala, Mandela or Gandhi. But every helping gesture sends a positive ripple in the ocean of people. 

At work, what can you do to create a better, more fun and productive environment? What will you foster? What will you not tolerate?
