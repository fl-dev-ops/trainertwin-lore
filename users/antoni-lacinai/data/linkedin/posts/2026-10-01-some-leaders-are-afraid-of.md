Some leaders are afraid of being viewed as people-pleasers when they give ”too much” praise. That is rarely the case. 

Leaders who praise are not people-pleasers. They are a force of energy, inspiration and enthusiasm. They are superspreaders of good. And the organization will outperform those that focus on negativity. Research shows this clearly. 

Here is a banal example. You come into a meeting room a few minutes before it starts, and the first thing you hear is music from a loudspeaker. You smile and say, "What a nice feeling having the music on. And you have great taste in music"

What are the chances that your colleague will put on music the next time? And what about other people who heard the conversation too?

Compare that with saying nothing, or saying "I don't like reggae!" or "This is a workplace, not a kindergarten..."

What is your praise ratio compared to negative criticism?
What is the consequence?
