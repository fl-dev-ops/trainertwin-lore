Why do I care? Why do you?

I travel to all kind of places, speaking about employee  engagement, leadership (and workplace) communication and customer service and experience? 
Why? Easy!
- I want people to get along.
- I want people to look forward to another day at work, rather than feeling anxiety. 
- I want leaders and colleagues to treat each other kindly, with compassion and respect.. 
It's that simple, because if we do, life will be more fun and we will perform better as well. Everybody wins.

What do you care about? And why?
