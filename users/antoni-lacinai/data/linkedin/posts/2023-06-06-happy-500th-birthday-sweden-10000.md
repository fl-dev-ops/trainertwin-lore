Happy 500th birthday Sweden!

10,000 years ago, people started to come back to this region as the latest ice age withdrew and made it liveable again. 

500 years ago Gustav Vasa became the first king of Sweden. 

60 years ago my father moved here from Albania, a country as closed as North Korea is today. A few years later, my mother also migrated here from former Yugoslavia, unwillingly, as a 16-year young girl. 

I am a second generation immigrant. I consider myself immensely fortunate to have grown up in this country. Having the freedom to go through a great school system. Having the possibility to be home with my children their first year. Having health care without worrying if my insurance will cover the bill. 

I am writing this, sitting on my patio, listening to birds chirping instead of blasting bombs or sounds of gunfire . Free to vote and disagree and find ways forward instead of being tortured or imprisoned. Democracy is fragile, young and hard to obtain. 

Now, let’s keep innovating, collaborating and grow in every sustainable way. And why not celebrate? 

Happy 500 years Sweden. 
Thank you!
