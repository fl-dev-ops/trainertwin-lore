I remember being contacted by a company where toxicity rules. The three departments didn't speak to each other, and the boss said, "Help me!!" 
I asked the boss what they would do after my speech. "I'll take it from there," he said. 

I got two hours at their kickoff (an emotionally cold kickoff), so I warmed them up and even got them to smile, laugh, and feel better. Then I went home...

After a few months, I reached out to the boss to ask how things were going. "Worse!" he said. I haven't had any time to follow up on the day..."

That's when I realized that I was nothing but a sparkle, shining bright for a few minutes before things went dark again. 

You know, I can live with that. It's fun to get applause. A bit like candy. Lots of empty calories. But it leads to exactly ZERO change, so from a client's perspective, I would want something deeper. And yet, companies keep booking “motivational” speakers without following up or connecting it to real transformation.
