HYBRID - THE BEST OF BOTH WORLDS
(if done right)

If you want your hybrid team to feel truly engaged, no matter where they are located, it is important that you treat them as one team so that you don't get remote workers to feel like second-class employees. 

When I give keynotes and training in hybrid leadership and hybrid workplaces, I stress the importance of building a strong togetherness and always think digital first. This way we all meet on the same terms. 

Engaged people are 30% more productive than average and way more productive than disengaged teams. 

I did a survey in 2021 where I asked some 500-600 leaders around the world what they worried about in the hybrid era. Let me know if you are interested in the result.
