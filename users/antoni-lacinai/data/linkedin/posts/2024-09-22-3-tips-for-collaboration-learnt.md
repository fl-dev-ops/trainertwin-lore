3 tips for collaboration learnt from the INSANE Balinese traffic

The Bali traffic is insane. Cars, lorries, people, scooters and dogs. All on small, narrows roads, where potential lanes are seen as recommendations at best. Still the traffic flows. How come? And what can we learn from it?

First: Accept what is. The Balinese people don’t spend anytime being irritated. They go with the flow. 
- how do you collaborate within a constant stream of  changes in your workplace?

Second: Honk the horn. They don’t use it angrily. It is a communication device alerting people to where you are. 
- do you share your goals and intentions with your colleagues?

Third: Forget tight deadlines. It could take you 30 minutes or 90 minutes for the same distance. 
- do you add time for unforeseen events when you plan your projects?

And as a bonus: sacrifice to the gods. That’s what the little basket with the flowers is for 😃
