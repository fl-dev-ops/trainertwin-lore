A transformational leader - what is that?

One of my clients asked me to do a keynote for 200 executives and middle managers. 

The HR department said: "Share ideas of what it means to be a transformational leader"

I asked: "What do you mean with that term?"

They couldn't say. 

So I thought long and hard, and I came up with these 5 traits of a transformational leader. I presented them and the attendees took pictures and agreed. 

Check out the list. Talk about it at your next management team meeting. 

• Are you a transformational leader? 
• Do you move mountains by yourself?  
• What happens if you invite others to join you?
