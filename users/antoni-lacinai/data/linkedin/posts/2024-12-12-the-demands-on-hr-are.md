The Demands on HR are Increasing – and Almost Everything Seems to be About AI (but not from me)

Recently, I gave a lecture at an HR conference in beautiful Singapore. During the first two days, one topic dominated completely: 𝗔𝗜. There was so much AI talk that, when I came in on the second day and spoke about people, many felt relieved and happy that this part still matters.

In a way, I can understand that AI is important within HR, which, to be honest, hasn’t exactly been an early adopter in technology and IT. The topic is needed. But almost all of these “experts” lacked concrete tools and tips for HR. They talked about why AI was important, but almost no one discussed how to use AI to improve HR – for example, 
• 𝗶𝗺𝗽𝗿𝗼𝘃𝗶𝗻𝗴 𝗿𝗲𝗰𝗿𝘂𝗶𝘁𝗺𝗲𝗻𝘁, 
• 𝗺𝗲𝗮𝘀𝘂𝗿𝗶𝗻𝗴 𝗲𝗻𝗴𝗮𝗴𝗲𝗺𝗲𝗻𝘁, 
• 𝗵𝗮𝗻𝗱𝗹𝗶𝗻𝗴 𝗽𝗲𝗿𝘀𝗼𝗻𝗻𝗲𝗹 𝗱𝗲𝘃𝗲𝗹𝗼𝗽𝗺𝗲𝗻𝘁
• 𝘀𝘁𝗿𝗲𝗮𝗺𝗹𝗶𝗻𝗲 𝗮𝗱𝗺𝗶𝗻𝗶𝘀𝘁𝗿𝗮𝘁𝗶𝘃𝗲 𝗽𝗿𝗼𝗰𝗲𝘀𝘀𝗲𝘀, etc. 

HR needs 𝗰𝗼𝗻𝗰𝗿𝗲𝘁𝗲 𝘁𝗶𝗽𝘀 and examples to improve their processes and those of others. The demands on you in HR are increasing, and you are expected to do more with less.

It’s perhaps useful to know that over half of all managers in Southeast Asia, according to a survey, actually expect HR to drive the digital culture. Who knows when we will see a CIO reporting to the CHRO...

Ok, so you in HR need to step up. I recently read a horrendous example where recruitment for a new position was going to be handled by AI, which would do all the screening before a human got involved. Strangely enough, only men passed through the filter. The recruiting manager thought this was odd. After investigating why, it turned out that the prompt (the instruction to the AI) was so skewed that no woman had a chance. People got fired, and the process had to be redone.
