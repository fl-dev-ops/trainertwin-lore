Many companies say: "Digital first!!
Really?

I am all for technology.  I am old enough to have listened to screeching modems and telefaxes, using only landline phones, and even pagers. I don't want to go back. 

I am amazed that I can communicate with people worldwide, with a DM, a video call, or a simple phone conversation. But come on! Analog communication rules! Period!
