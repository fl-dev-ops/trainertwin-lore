Is courage the same as naivety?

It's amazing what you can learn from people, if you let go of your digital tools and digital toys, and be really present. That's what happened when I met Jaako, a young Finn who decided to take his bike for a 2000km vacation tour, without any real preparation...

I told this story to 1500 people at a recent online event, where the theme was Courage. Thank you Lauri Sallantaus for bringing me in 😃

What is better in your book? Being brave or naive?
