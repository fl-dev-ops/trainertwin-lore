Don't talk about Features when you sell. 
But don't talk about Benefits either...

I am not a very practical person. In fact, I am pretty useless when anything needs to be repaired or assembled. Not the best, when you grew up in a typical working-class environment where all parents worked at the Volvo assembly line... "You should be in sales," people said. And so, after military service, I went into sales. I learned pretty quickly that it was good that I could talk about features, but it was almost never enough. I needed to get Why they wanted to buy.

Here's a short video where I explain why you should go beyond benefits if you really want to help your customer to buy your product. 

What is your experience? Are salespeople good at this or not?
