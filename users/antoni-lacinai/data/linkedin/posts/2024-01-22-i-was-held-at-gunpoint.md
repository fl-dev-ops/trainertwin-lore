I was held at gunpoint...

I was 24 years old
2 am a warm summer night
Walking home from a night of partying

The streets were empty apart from two young men

"HEY YOU!" one said. "You got a cigarette?"
"No"
"Then give me your wallet," the other said
"No"
"Are you sure about that?" a gun in his hand
"No"

I gave them my wallet. I looked away for a while and then back. They handed my wallet back to me. 

"Why?" I asked
"Because you had such good answers to our questions." 

After I came back home I asked myself...
What just happened?

Communication can be a matter of life and death. Sometimes with only a single word like Yes, or No.
