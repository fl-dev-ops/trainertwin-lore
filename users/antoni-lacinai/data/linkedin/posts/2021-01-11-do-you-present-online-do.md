Do you present online? !
Do you want some tips?

I train people almost daily in digital presentations, digital meetings and virtual leadership. Below is a simple top-10 list. It starts with gadgets  and end with you:

1. Get an external camera. The one on the laptop is rarely good enough.

2. Use an external microphone. Preferably with "cardioid" function so that it directs its attention to your mouth and filters out the rest.

3. Have a light directed at you. We want to see you clearly.

4. Use a neutral background. We don't want to see messy backgrounds any more.

5. Place the camera at eye level. No more nostrils and ceiling lights please.

6. Look into the camera while talking. Then it feels like we have eye contact.

7. Be yourself plus 10-20 percent. You need to turn it up extra a notch when presenting online.

8. Stand up. Then you get more energy which you can forward to the audience..

9. Keep your gestures closer to your head. What we see of you is limited.

10. Mirror your gestures. If you want to show that you go from A to B, do it from right to left. Then it will be right for the recipient.

If you want more tips, I made a version with 33 tips for a customer recently. DM me.
