1. Wear comfortable shoes. 
2. Practice standing up before the expo.
3. Change your socks and get refreshed. 
4. Make sure to take breaks and recharge. 
5. Breathe slower  when you are stressed
6. Take good care of your voice 
7. Less alcohol. More water
8. Think of what you eat. 
9. Have fun.
