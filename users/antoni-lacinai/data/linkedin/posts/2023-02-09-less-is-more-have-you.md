LESS is MORE

Have you heard about a term called Flash Fiction?

The legend has it that Ernest Hemingway made a bet with his friends that he could write a whole novel using only six words. 
He wrote:

FOR SALE
BABY SHOES
NEVER WORN

He won the bet.

When you present, do you cram in as many slides as you possibly can? Or do you use fewer better words instead?

Contenplate on that...

What do you say about that Steve Lowell - Past President Global Speakers Federation, Steve Bustin, Katja Schleicher
