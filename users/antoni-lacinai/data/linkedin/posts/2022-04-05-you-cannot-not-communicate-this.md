You cannot NOT communicate

This is the first of my 20 communication insights. 
It means that EVERYTHING about you communicates. What you say, think, eat, do etc. 

Have you been to a store where two sales people talked to each other without giving you a glance? Have you seen a colleague walking into your office, looking down at his/her phone instead of saying "good morning"

What did they communicate? 
Think about it.
