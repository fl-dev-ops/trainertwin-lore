Question: What is the one thing you can never get back? 

The answer is:

...Time

If you give your time - and attention - you give your most valuable asset. That is a real gift. 

Do this:
1. Tell your inner voice to shut up!
2. Listen to understand, not to answer
3. Pay attention to body language and tonality
4. Ask open questions more often than closed ones
5. Listen to the whole sentences, not just the first half
6. Ask follow-up questions for a deeper understanding

In my keynotes and training sessions, I spend a large amount on empathy. Even in presentation skills training. It's about connecting with people. 

To truly listen is to use the superpower Empathy and with empathy comes trust, loyalty, and engagement. 

We need more empathy in our workplaces.It is an antidote to stress.

Empathy is the new black!
