If everybody talks, then who listens?


What is the one thing you can never get back?

Answer: Time.

If you give your time - and attention - you just give your most valuable asset. That is a real gift. 

Imagine going to a dinner party. You share a story and get... zero questions. Instead,  someone else will top that story with their own... This might work at a friendly dinner, but not in a work-life context. 

To truly listen is to practice the number one superpower Empathy and with empathy comes trust, loyalty,  and engagement. We need more empathy in our workplaces. It is an antidote to stress.

Empathy is the new black!

#leadership #workplacecommunication #engagement

PS - How do you listen? And is there a difference between hearing and listening?
