3 questions about Quiet Quitting

and all of them are to you, the boss...

→What makes people do only minimum effort?
→Why won't they walk the extra mile?
→What's wrong with them?

It might be that nothing is wrong with them.
They could have other priorities.
Who set the bar for them?

I meet so many managers and business owners who can't understand why their employees aren't as passionate as they are. 

I have some hard questions for them...

PS - there is a clear Command&Control trend right now where people are being forced back to drag offices 5 days per week. I can promise right here and now that you will get more quiet quitters by adopting that authoritarian bossy style.
