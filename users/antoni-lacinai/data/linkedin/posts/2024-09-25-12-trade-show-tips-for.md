12 trade show tips for great communication at a trade show. 
Directly from the book Speed Selling

1. 𝗙𝗼𝗰𝘂𝘀 𝗼𝗻 𝗩𝗮𝗹𝘂𝗲, 𝗡𝗼𝘁 𝗙𝗲𝗮𝘁𝘂𝗿𝗲𝘀
Talk value and people will be interested. 
Talk features, and most people switch off or get confused. Your choice.

2. 𝗨𝗻𝗱𝗲𝗿𝘀𝘁𝗮𝗻𝗱 𝘁𝗵𝗲 𝗖𝘂𝘀𝘁𝗼𝗺𝗲𝗿'𝘀 𝗣𝗲𝗿𝘀𝗽𝗲𝗰𝘁𝗶𝘃𝗲
Find out what is important to the customer and why. 
Then focus on explaining the value of your solution according to what the customer has told you.

3. 𝗕𝗲 𝗮𝗻 𝗔𝗰𝘁𝗶𝘃𝗲 𝗟𝗶𝘀𝘁𝗲𝗻𝗲𝗿
Listen actively by showing genuine interest, nodding, and summarizing the customer’s points to confirm understanding.

4. 𝗕𝘂𝗶𝗹𝗱 𝗘𝗺𝗽𝗮𝘁𝗵𝘆
Tip: Empathy in sales builds stronger connections. Understand your customer's perspective to tailor your solutions effectively.

5. 𝗛𝗮𝗻𝗱𝗹𝗲 𝗢𝗯𝗷𝗲𝗰𝘁𝗶𝗼𝗻𝘀 𝘄𝗶𝘁𝗵 𝗚𝗿𝗮𝗰𝗲
Prepare and handle objections gracefully to turn potential "no" into a "yes."

6. 𝗨𝘀𝗲 𝗦𝘁𝗼𝗿𝘆𝘁𝗲𝗹𝗹𝗶𝗻𝗴
Use compelling stories to highlight the benefits of your product and connect emotionally with your customers.

7. 𝗥𝗲𝗰𝗼𝗴𝗻𝗶𝘇𝗲 𝗕𝘂𝘆𝗶𝗻𝗴 𝗦𝗶𝗴𝗻𝗮𝗹𝘀
Recognize buying signals to close the deal. Look for engagement cues and specific product questions.

8. 𝗣𝗿𝗮𝗰𝘁𝗶𝗰𝗲 𝗔𝗰𝘁𝗶𝘃𝗲 𝗟𝗶𝘀𝘁𝗲𝗻𝗶𝗻𝗴
Show that you are listening by nodding, maintaining eye contact, and responding appropriately. Reflect back on what you've heard to confirm your understanding.

9. 𝗣𝗲𝗿𝘀𝗼𝗻𝗮𝗹𝗶𝘇𝗲 𝗬𝗼𝘂𝗿 𝗔𝗽𝗽𝗿𝗼𝗮𝗰𝗵
Tailor your sales pitch to meet the specific needs and preferences of each customer. Use their name, mention their company, and address their unique challenges.

10. 𝗙𝗼𝗹𝗹𝗼𝘄 𝗨𝗽 𝗣𝗿𝗼𝗺𝗽𝘁𝗹𝘆
Always follow up after a meeting or a call. Send a thank-you email summarizing key points discussed and the next steps. This shows your commitment and keeps the conversation going.

11. 𝗨𝘁𝗶𝗹𝗶𝘇𝗲 𝗦𝗼𝗰𝗶𝗮𝗹 𝗣𝗿𝗼𝗼𝗳
Use testimonials, reviews, and case studies to build credibility. Share how other customers have benefited from your product or service.

12. 𝗕𝗲 𝗣𝗲𝗿𝘀𝗶𝘀𝘁𝗲𝗻𝘁, 𝗡𝗼𝘁 𝗣𝘂𝘀𝗵𝘆
Follow up consistently but respectfully. Show that you are genuinely interested in helping the customer without overwhelming them with pressure.
