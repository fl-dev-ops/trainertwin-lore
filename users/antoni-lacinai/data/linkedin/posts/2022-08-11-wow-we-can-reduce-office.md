### Ian Hatton (I liberate leaders to be powerful AND human | Author: ‘EGG Based Leadership’ and ‘Lead Like Morpheus’ | EGG3 Leadership Assessment Creator | International Keynote Speaker | SA Educator Hall of Fame | MA (OL))
> Thank you for sounding the alarm, Antoni Lacinai! Even pre-pandemic, research from Grenny & Maxfield showed that remote employees felt "left out and ganged up on". Add to that post-pandemic angst and it becomes a lethal cocktail for employee engagement. 
> 
> Conscious leaders can mitigate this through frequently connecting with their team members in ways that help them to feel aligned and engaged with their leader, their teammates and the organisation. 
> 
> How frequently? The same study suggested weekly one-on-one check-ins with each team member. This combats feelings of isolation and even paranoia. It also ensures that remote employees have regular opportunities to address professional (and personal) concerns. Especially vital at a time when mental health issues are at unprecedented levels.
> 
> When my corporate leadership clients object that they don't have the time to check in with each team member, I have one response. 
> 
> Do you have the time (and resources) not to? What is the cost of disengagement, poor productivity, mistakes, mental health issues, and staff turnover? 
> 
> Let's make employees feel welcome at work. Wherever they are.
