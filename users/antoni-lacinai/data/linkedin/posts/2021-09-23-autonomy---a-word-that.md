Autonomy - A word that has never been so important as now. Especially in a hybrid workplace. 
Surveys are showing (and people I know in the recruitment business) that oh so many employees will actively search for a new job if they are forced to work at the office full time. 

How will it be in your workplace?

#hybridleadership #rengagement #communication

My take: If your team lives close to the office, be there together at least a few days per week. But involve the team as well of when and how.
