Are you a predictable manager? Good!

If you are predictable and kind, people will stay and contribute to their best abilities.

If you are predictable and evil, people know that they should leave - fast.

The problem is if you are a mood swinger or if your behavior changes every time somebody suggest something new, just because of the novelty. It's very tiresome.

There are some additional thoughts in the article. Do you agree?
