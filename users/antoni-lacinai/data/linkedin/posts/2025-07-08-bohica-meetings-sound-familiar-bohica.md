BOHICA meetings – sound familiar?

BOHICA stands for "Bend Over, Here It Comes Again." And that’s exactly how many employees feel.
But meetings don’t have to be painful.

I have co-written three books on how to lead engaging and effective meetings. I believe in the power of great meetings where ideas and decisions are produced. 

I am allergic to bad meetings, though. They suck big time.

Three things you can do today to stop wasting time in meetings:

1. Define the purpose and goals BEFORE the meeting. Communicate them in the invitation and at the beginning of the meeting
   
2. No more PowerPoint marathons. Information overload creates zero engagement.
   
3. End every meeting with evaluating the progress vs the goal and purpose, and set a concrete action plan
