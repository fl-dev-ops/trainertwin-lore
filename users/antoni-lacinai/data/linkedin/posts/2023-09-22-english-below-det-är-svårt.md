Det är svårt att bryta vanor och ändra kulturen i en organisation. Som ledare kan det ibland kännas som att man trampar vatten. Här är fem saker framgångsrika förändringcshefer gör.

It's not easy to transform a team or an organization. Habits run deep. Cultures are strong and don't want to be changed. 

As a leader, it's important to lead with purpose and manage through goals, to separate conflicts from disagreements, and to care deeply for your quest, your people, and the planet. Sharing ideas, knowledge, and progress is key to success, but it also takes courage to be vulnerable and trust your team.
