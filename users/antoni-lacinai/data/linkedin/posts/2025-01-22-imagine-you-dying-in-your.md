Imagine you dying in your office
and no one notice it for four days...

This article from the US is maybe the best example of why we should be more in the office. But at the same time - she was in the office! And where were the colleagues and the manager in all this? 

In  another article, I read that 40,000 Japanese died alone. 4000 of them weren't found within a month from when they died...

In yet another article, I read that loneliness makes people 7x more likely to be disengaged, 5x more likely to be home, sick, and 2x more likely to look for a new job. 

What can you as a leader or colleague do if you want to reduce loneliness in your workplace?

• Say hello to people in the morning
• Ask them how they have been after a weekend
• Make sure people are not eating alone (unless they want to)

Let's CARE more. Our fellow human beings need it.
