How do you handle people who talk and talk until the original message is buried under words and words?

I learnt this as a moderator. You make them summarize and distill their content. 

Have you tried it in any conversations yourself?

## Comments (5)

### Amy Jacobson (Emotional Intelligence | Speaker | Published Author | Workshop | Programs | amy@amyjacobson.com.au)
> I like to ask the question “in the end, what do you want to feel and what do you want them to do?”
> This has them creating summaries and gives me very clear direction on exactly what the focus areas are.

### Dixie Maria Carlton - CSP (Empowering & Educating authors and professional speakers From Pages to Stages. Some call me ‘The Word Witch’, because I help create practical magic for those with wisdom worth sharing.)
> Excellent questions... and you can guarantee if you're confused, you won't be the only person in the room who is.  Summarise in 2 sentences - yes please!
