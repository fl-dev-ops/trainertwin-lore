3 is the magic number

If you want people to remember anything you said, placing your content in triads is the key.

• Faith hope and glory
• I came, I saw, I conquered
• Sex and drugs and rock-n-roll

Then you have the father, son and the holy ghost. Or Brahman, Vishnu and Shiva...

I always speak about these 3 communicative superpowers:
❤️ Empathy
🏆Clarity 
🧲 Energy

3 is the magic number
