Can you disagree?
Are you allowed to?

Here's one thing I didn't know...
We should all have a fool in our castles

What do I mean by that?
We need somebody who can challenge our egos - and - is allowed to express that without fear of punishment. 

Think about the organization you work for:

→ Is this a safe workplace?
→ Is this a place where you can admit faults?
→ Is this a place to tell your boss they are wrong?

Is it?

How do you maintain honest communication while still respecting each other? Share your best tips.
