As AI becomes more present in the workplace, what happens to our human skills?

I just read about a study from Workday. According to them, 82% of employees believe the need for human interaction will increase with more AI.

But only 65% of leaders agree. Eeehh?

If you are a leader who hasn't figured this out, just realize that:

• AI won’t build trust
• AI won't care about you
• AI won't care about your people

That’s still your job. And the better you get at it, the more valuable you become. AI won’t replace real conversations. That's still on you. 

Sure, use AI and other tech. I do, every day. It's pretty awesome. But don't pretend that your culture doesn't need social interactions. 

I help leaders and teams get better at what only humans can do: Empathy. Clarity. Energy. So that you all move your strategies towards reality

Let’s not forget – the more tech we add, the more human we need to be.
