"TRUST is the password"

In leadership as well as parenting

My good friend Henrik Pallin is an expert in young kids' screen habits. Worrying parents ask him: "Do you think I should get hold of my kid's password?"
Henrik says: "I already know your kid's password. It is spelled T.R.U.S.T"

Trust is the password in all organizations too. I see it so often. Some teams don't trust each other. They trash-talk colleagues behind their backs. They don't share intel. It's an energy-draining place. 

But I also meet teams with positive energy, and a sharing culture where they help each other. They have a servant mentality. They have way more fun too. 

→ Trust brings loyalty
→ Trust brings motivation
→ Trust brings engagement

Where there is trust, there is momentum, performance, and ultimately great results

PS - Who do you trust? And: Who trusts you?
