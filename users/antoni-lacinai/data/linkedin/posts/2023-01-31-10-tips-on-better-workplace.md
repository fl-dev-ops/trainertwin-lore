10 tips on better workplace communication

I was interviewed before Christmas about the life of a speaker and workplace communication expert, by an industry association. The link gives you ten tips in Swedish, on how to lead people, but in short:

1. Show Empathy
2. Be Clear
3. Create a Safe space
4. Be afraid of Conflicts
5. Don't be Yourself...
6. Communicate your higher Purpose
7. Set goals and give autonomy
8. Praise people
9. Have Fun Together 
10. Let your team members grow their competence

Anything that stands out?
