                        Level: Foundational

COURSE DESCRIPTION
This course gives you the essential skills to handle sales objections with confidence and clarity. Learn the most common objections, why they arise, and proven techniques to overcome them without pressure or defensiveness. You’ll also uncover the mistakes sellers often make and how to turn moments of hesitation into opportunities for stronger buyer alignment.
