The Human Side of Selling
- 
                        Level: Foundational
- 
                        Study time: 1 hour
COURSE DESCRIPTION
This course explores the psychology behind successful selling. Learn why people buy, how to sell effectively across different personality types, and how to communicate with confidence using body language. You’ll also build resilience by managing rejection and stress, and discover how introverts can thrive in a sales environment.
