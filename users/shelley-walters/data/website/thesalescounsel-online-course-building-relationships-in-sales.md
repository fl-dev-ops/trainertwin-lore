COURSE DESCRIPTION
This course equips you with the core skills of consultative selling. Learn how to qualify leads using the BANT framework, understand what potential buyers truly need, and build trust through transparency and emotional intelligence. You’ll also discover how to work effectively with decision-makers, influencers, and gatekeepers, enabling you to create deeper relationships and drive higher-quality opportunities.
