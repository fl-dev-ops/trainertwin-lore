Core Principles of Modern Selling
    Course overview
This course gives you a solid grounding in modern sales. Learn how the sales pipeline works, why CRM matters, how to use essential sales tools, and how to align your approach with both marketing and the buyer’s journey. Build the communication and foundational skills you need to start selling with confidence and clarity.
