COURSE DESCRIPTION
This course gives you a complete foundation in sales negotiation. Learn how to prepare for negotiations, apply essential techniques, counter common buyer tactics, and confidently close the sale. You’ll also discover how to transition new customers into a positive post-sale experience that sets the stage for long-term success and growth.
