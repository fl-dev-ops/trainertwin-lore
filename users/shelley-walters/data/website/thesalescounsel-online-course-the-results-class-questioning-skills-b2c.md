COURSE DESCRIPTION
Questioning is the cornerstone of every successful sales conversation. This Intermediate Questioning Skills course equips professional salespeople with advanced tools to engage deeply, uncover client needs, and confidently address objections. Learn strategic techniques like Socratic questioning, active listening, and the funneling method to create trust and foster meaningful dialogues. Gain actionable insights into crafting impactful questions and leveraging psychology to guide decision-making. Designed for sales professionals ready to transform their questioning techniques into a decisive competitive advantage.
