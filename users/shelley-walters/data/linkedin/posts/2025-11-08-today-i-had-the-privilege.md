Today I had the privilege of addressing around 250–300 attendees at the Plumblink Conference at the River Sun in Vanderbijl Park on the topic: Improving Performance, Increasing Sales.

The message was incredibly well received,  and it’s easy to see why when you look at the business behind the event.

🔧 Why Plumblink stands out

Plumblink operates over 120 branches across South Africa and Namibia.

They’re one of the country’s largest and most trusted plumbing, bathroom, and kitchenware merchants.

Their contribution to job creation and skills development in South Africa is remarkable, and their commitment to transformation is genuine — not just a line on a corporate report.

Having worked with the Plumblink team, I can say their culture is exceptional,  high-performance, values-driven, and people-centred. The management team in particular is world-class: grounded, strategic, and genuinely invested in their people and customers alike.

If you’re in the market for anything plumbing related, I cannot recommend supporting Plumblink highly enough. They’re proof that when you build a strong culture, commercial success follows naturally.

Thank you to the Plumblink team for the opportunity to contribute, and Duncan Hesketh from Conference Speakers International for the booking.
