The best sales leaders rarely apply. They get called.

So this is a call, and it is a good one.

The Sales Counsel is running a search for a Head of Sales: Africa for a market-leading fleet safety and telematics business. Before the criteria, the reason it matters: this company sells technology that keeps drivers, fleets and people safe. Its purpose, to get everyone safely home, is not a slogan. It is the outcome customers buy. You would be leading the sale of something that genuinely protects lives, assets and livelihoods.

The South African business is established and strong. There is already a footprint across the rest of Africa, and the plans for growth are clear. That is the exciting part. This is the appointment that owns the African commercial number and builds the organisation to capture the growth ahead.

It is a continental commercial leadership role, reporting into a global Chief Revenue Officer, accountable for the full African number across new business, key account growth and retention. You would shape the sales organisation for scale: separating new business hunting from key account management, installing real pipeline and forecast discipline, running 

Salesforce as the operating system, and leading a capable team across markets.
I am looking for a commercial leader who has:
•   Carried a national, regional or multi-country revenue number, and delivered against it
•   Led a sales team of 10 or more across both new business and key accounts
•   Sold complex enterprise B2B solutions with long cycles and real service dependency after the sale
•   Built and led a team through growth, and taken it into new markets or scale

Sector experience in telematics, fleet, logistics, B2B SaaS or managed services shortens the ramp. Experience selling across more than one African market is an advantage. The calibre of the leadership, and the appetite for the mission, matter more than the logo on the CV. Johannesburg-based, Africa-wide mandate, executive-level appointment.

If this is you, or you know exactly who it should be, message me directly or email talent@thesalescounsel.co.za. Every conversation is confidential
