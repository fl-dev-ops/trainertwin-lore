After years of wrestling with commas, side-eyeing the Oxford comma, and questioning my use of full stops, I’ve now discovered the truth.

It was the hyphen.
The hyphen had been there all along.

Not drawing attention to itself. Not demanding a pause. Just quietly over-connecting words that probably didn’t need to be linked in the first place.

AI, in all its wisdom, has taught me many things, but none so humbling as this: I have clearly been underestimating the hyphen.

Now, if only someone could explain to me why AI insists on using it every. single. time. Is this grammatical efficiency, or some kind of secret code among language models?

I’ll be over here rethinking my punctuation choices—and questioning everything I thought I knew about sentence structure.
