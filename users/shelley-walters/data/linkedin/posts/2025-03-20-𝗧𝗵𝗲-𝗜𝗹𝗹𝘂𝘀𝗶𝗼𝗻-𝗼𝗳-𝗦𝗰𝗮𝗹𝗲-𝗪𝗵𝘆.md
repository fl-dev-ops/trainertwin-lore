Scaling isn’t just about adding skills—it’s about creating capacity.

Many organisations believe that upskilling their existing team will lead to growth. But when that team is already stretched to the limit of delivering client work, where will sales fit in?

The reality is that even the best training will struggle to drive results without additional capacity.

Imagine pouring more water into a full glass. It doesn’t expand the glass—it just overflows. To truly scale, you must first expand your organisation’s ability to absorb new responsibilities. Only then can skills development deliver a meaningful impact.

In my experience working with listed companies, large sales teams, and growing organisations, I’ve seen time and time again that capacity must come before skills.

This thinking has crystallised further as I’ve been reading Jason Goldberg's The Art of Scale. It’s helped me clarify many of these thoughts, and I can’t recommend it highly enough.
