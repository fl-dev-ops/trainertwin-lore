There have been some really big things happening at The Sales Counsel — and bigger things to come. 🚧

In the middle of the dust, decisions, building work, paperwork and planning, I have found myself pausing for perspective.

When I started The Sales Counsel, it was just me, working from my office at home.

Then it became me and a virtual assistant.

Then we created Africa’s first Remote Selling School, built a remote team, served clients online and in person, and grew a business that, for a long time, could operate from almost anywhere.

At no point did I think we would ever take offices.

Ever.

Then, on 1 June last year, we moved into our first office. It was small, professional, and exactly what we needed at the time. It was also, if I’m honest, a very tight fit for all of us.

And now we are preparing to move again.

This time into a bigger space, with a longer lease, room to grow the team, and room to grow the work.

The Sales Counsel is turning 10 — and growing up.

Maybe someone needs to hear this:

Do not despise the day of small beginnings.

You never know what can happen when a small seed is met with diligence, focus, faith, good people, loyal clients, and a deeply hedgehog-minded commitment to doing the work well.

We do not take any of this for granted.

Our clients have made this possible. Their trust, partnership, referrals, repeat business, and willingness to keep learning and growing with us have helped us get here.

Still under construction.
Still becoming.
Still grateful.

More soon.
