Do you rate yourself as a great Sales Manager?

And are you looking for a challenge where you can actually prove it?

I’m working with an exceptional client in a fascinating, demanding industry, and we’re looking for a Sales Manager who knows how to build a sales team, build a pipeline and deliver a number. 

You understand the relationship between sales process, activity, pipeline and conversion. You know how to build and sustain a healthy pipeline, and when performance starts slipping, you can get underneath the numbers quickly enough to work out where the problem is.

Then you do something about it.

You coach reps individually. You know where they’re losing deals and why. You can help improve conversion at different stages of the pipeline. You’ll get into the weeds, get on the road, sit alongside your salespeople, join customer conversations and help move real opportunities forward.

You’re not afraid of accountability either. When there’s a gap between the pipeline and the target, your job isn’t simply to explain the gap. It’s to help close it.

You’ll work closely with an excellent Head of Sales, lead and develop the team, and play a meaningful role in the growth of a business with serious ambition.

So, if you genuinely rate yourself as a great Sales Manager and you’re ready for a role that will ask something of you, I want to hear from you.
