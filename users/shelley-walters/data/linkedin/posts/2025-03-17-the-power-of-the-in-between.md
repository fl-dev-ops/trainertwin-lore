The Power of the In-Between Moments

This past week, my husband and I took a road trip to attend the BizNews Conference, a gathering of sharp minds and bold ideas. But as much as I was looking forward to the event itself, I’ve always believed that the journey offers just as much insight as the destination.

Over the coming days I will share insights and reflections from our road trip, I hope you enjoy them 

En route to Gariep Dam, we crossed this striking metal bridge—symmetrical, repetitive, and seemingly endless in its patterns. It made me pause and reflect: how often do we find ourselves in these in-between spaces in life and business? Not quite where we were, not yet where we’re going. And yet, these moments are where transformation happens.

Growth rarely comes in the comfort of the past or the clarity of the finish line. It happens here—in the middle of the bridge—where we’re forced to embrace the uncertainty of transition and the perspective that comes with it.

The challenge is to lean into these spaces, rather than rush through them. Because often, this is where the real shifts happen.
