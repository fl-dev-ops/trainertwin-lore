I loved reading this reflection from Uthandile Samela after our Full Throttle Training work with Mercedes-Benz.

What she captures so beautifully is that meaningful learning is never only about the content in the room. It is also about the preparation, the relationships, the moments between sessions, the questions asked, the silences held, and the care taken to make sure people feel seen and supported.

Her takeaways on fluidity, upstream thinking, silence, and human connection speak directly to what we believe at The Sales Counsel: growth is not just information transfer. It is identity, behaviour, courage, and intention in motion.

It was a privilege to be part of this work with the Mercedes-Benz team, and even more meaningful to see the learning land in ways that extend beyond the workshop itself. Thank you, Uthandile, for such a thoughtful and generous reflection.
