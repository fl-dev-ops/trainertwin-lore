Here's the question I'd ask your sales leadership team today. How many big deals did your team close this year? And what share of your pipeline do they make up?

If the honest answer is "I'd have to check", keep reading. Big deals aren't just bigger. They behave differently. The cycle drags. More people get involved, each with their own priorities and their own idea of value. Procurement digs in. By the time you reach the sharp end, every competitor left standing is credible.

And here's the uncomfortable bit: a deal that looks healthy in your CRM can be far less advanced inside the customer's organisation than your salesperson believes.

A healthy and sustainable pipeline that exceeds target needs big deals and a good relationship and a persuasive proposal won't get you there on their own.

That's why I've built Landing the Whale, one of three new keynotes for 2027.

No hypothetical deals. Your team brings a real one into the room and puts it under scrutiny:
→ Who have we really got around the table?
→ Who are we missing?
→ Where does the influence sit?
→ Have we built consensus, or just a relationship?

They leave knowing where the deal is strong, where it's vulnerable, and the two moves to make next.
