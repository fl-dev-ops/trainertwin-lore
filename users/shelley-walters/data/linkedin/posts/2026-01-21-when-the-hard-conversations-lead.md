When the hard conversations lead to the strongest partnerships

Over the past year, we’ve been quietly doing some of the most important work in the life of The Sales Counsel.

Not just new logos.
Not new marketing.

But the work that actually determines whether a business is built to scale or just survive.

A big part of that has been our partnership with Anne 💡 Gray.

Anne came into our world as an operational and strategic thinking partner, and what she brought was not surface-level advice, but depth:

• A disciplined way to interrogate our revenue models
• Clear thinking around pricing, resourcing and margin
• A sharper lens on what we say yes to and why
• And the operational backbone required to support more complex, more demanding client engagements

We have been in business for nine years, but the level of rigour we now apply to how we design, price and deliver our work is meaningfully different because of this partnership.

What I value most, though, is not just the mechanics.

It’s the thinking.

Anne has been: 
• A sound listening ear
• A critical thinking partner
• A challenger of lazy assumptions
• And a powerful ally in client rooms, helping us sharpen both our propositions and the commercial logic behind them.

That combination, strategic, commercial and human, is rare.

As The Sales Counsel evolves into new products, new channels and new ways of going to market, we fully expect Anne to remain close as a trusted advisor and sparring partner in that journey.

She goes deep.
She does not waste time.

And she genuinely cares about helping businesses build something that actually works.

If you are navigating complexity, growth, or the uncomfortable middle between vision and execution, she is someone I would strongly encourage you to speak to.
