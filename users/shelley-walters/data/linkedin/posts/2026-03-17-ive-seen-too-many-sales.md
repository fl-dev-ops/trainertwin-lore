I’ve seen too many sales pipelines turn into fiction because of one hidden culprit: decision fatigue.

When every deal requires interpretation, judgment, and guesswork, teams naturally default to optimism. The result? Close dates slip, next steps blur, and the people we think are "decision-makers" turn out not to be.It’s not that sales teams are incapable. It’s that no one has reduced the number of decisions required to understand what’s actually real in the pipeline.

This short video captures why pipeline inspection changes everything. By anchoring every deal to three simple things: who the decision-maker is, what the next committed step is, and when it was last validated with the client—clarity returns. And with clarity comes movement.
