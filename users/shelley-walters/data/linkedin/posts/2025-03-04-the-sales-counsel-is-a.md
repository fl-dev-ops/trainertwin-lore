The Sales Counsel is a rapidly growing, full-service sales enablement firm. 

As we expand, we seek top-tier sales professionals to join us as associates and work alongside our high-calibre, predominantly B2B clients, including listed organisations and select SMEs.

We are looking for experienced sales leaders, consultants, and specialists with a proven track record in B2B sales, whether as C-level executives, senior or mid-level sales managers, or top-performing individual contributors. Our associates must be able to engage strategically with business leaders, bringing both deep expertise and strong communication skills.

Who We Are Looking For
We are seeking a variety of experienced professionals, all of whom must have a proven track record in sales and strong consulting skills. Relevant expertise may include:

Sales Leadership & Strategy – Professionals with experience leading sales teams, setting strategy, and engaging at the C-suite level.
Sales Operations & Analytics – Experts in sales forecasting, pipeline management, and performance analysis.

Compensation & Remuneration Consultants – Specialists in designing and implementing sales incentives, commission structures, and pay-for-performance models.

Sales Subject Matter Experts & Facilitators – Individuals who can train and upskill sales teams but only if they also have strong, hands-on experience in sales leadership or individual contribution.

Sales Coaches & Mentors – Experienced sales leaders who can guide, challenge, and develop sales teams effectively.

This is not a role for training facilitators without in-field sales experience. Subject matter expertise is critical, and facilitation alone is not sufficient. If you are applying for a facilitation role, you must also have real-world sales leadership or individual sales success.

Why Join The Sales Counsel?
Work with premium, high-calibre clients on project-based engagements.
Be part of an exclusive network of top-tier sales professionals.
Opportunities for ongoing collaboration and long-term engagements.
Engage in meaningful, high-impact sales transformation initiatives.

What You Need to Know
This is an associate opportunity, not a permanent role.
We are seeking Johannesburg-based professionals only.
We value ethical, self-starting professionals who can be trusted to represent The Sales Counsel with integrity.
Exceptional communication skills are required, as associates will engage with business leaders at a strategic level.

Candidates must provide contactable references, and we will follow up with references for shortlisted applicants.

If you meet these criteria and are interested in working with The Sales Counsel, please email hello@thesalescounsel.co.za with your profile and area of expertise.
