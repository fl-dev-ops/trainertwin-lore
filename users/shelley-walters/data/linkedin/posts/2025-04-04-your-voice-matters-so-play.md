Your voice matters. So play your instrument and speak with impact.

Today, I watched Ursula Botha do exactly that. She stood on stage, not just to speak, but to move, to connect, to resonate.

What struck me most was not only her message, but the way she delivered it — with clarity, conviction and an authenticity that reminded us all that presence is not about volume, it’s about intention.

Ursula, thank you for reminding us that our voice is an instrument, and when we use it purposefully, it can stir hearts, change minds and make people feel seen.

If you ever get the chance to hear her speak, take it. You’ll leave reminded of your own power to show up, stand tall, and speak with impact.

Ursula Botha
