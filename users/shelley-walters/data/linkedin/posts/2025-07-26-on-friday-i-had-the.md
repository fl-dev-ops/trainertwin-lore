On Friday, I had the privilege of delivering the keynote at Metrofile’s Annual Sales Conference.

The theme? Hunt. Win. Expand.

We drew inspiration from the animal kingdom — the strategic patience of lions and the relentless persistence of African wild dogs. Together, we explored how these hunting behaviours map directly onto high-performance sales teams. We closed with a spoken word piece I wrote called “The Hunt for the Deserving Salesperson”.

Then the roar began.

To the sound of Katy Perry’s “Roar,” the room rose to its feet — fired up, united, and ready to pursue their "big buffalo". The energy was electric. This morning, I received word that the Cape Town team is still singing it at the airport.

Moments like these remind me how powerful internal conferences can be. When done right, they spark something that lasts far beyond the closing slide.

A huge thank you to Metrofile — what an incredible client to work with. Their team is dynamic, passionate, and fully committed to growth. It’s always a privilege to partner with businesses who value real momentum and make space for moments that matter.

Thank you Nicola McGowan Shivan Mansingh Sameshan Reddy for yet another wonderful conference and the opportunity to participate again this year. 

We love working alongside our clients to co-create meaningful in-house events — the kind that ignite energy, foster alignment, and set the tone for the year ahead.
