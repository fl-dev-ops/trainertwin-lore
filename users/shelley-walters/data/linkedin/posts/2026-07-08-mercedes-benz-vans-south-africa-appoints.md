Mercedes-Benz Vans South Africa Appoints Shelley Walters as First B2B Friend of the Brand

Mercedes-Benz Vans South Africa has appointed Shelley Walters, Founder and CEO of The Sales Counsel, as its newest Friend of the Brand — the first appointment in the programme from the B2B commercial leadership space.

Walters took delivery of a Mercedes-Benz Vito 119 Mixto Select today, which will accompany her to executive boardrooms, keynote stages, and commercial events across South Africa over the next 12 months.
"This partnership extends the Friend of the Brand programme into a new and commercially significant space," says Reandren Thulkanam, General Manager at Mercedes-Benz Vans South Africa. "Shelley Walters brings our brand directly into South Africa's corporate boardrooms and fleet-facing environments. By aligning with The Sales Counsel, we are underscoring our commitment to getting behind South African small businesses and supporting the economic growth they drive."

"When Mercedes-Benz Vans South Africa approached me, I was surprised and deeply honoured," says Walters. "We are both in the business of precision, performance, and forward momentum. At The Sales Counsel, we turn pipeline into revenue, revenue into jobs, and jobs into a stronger economy. Now we get to drive that commercial success literally, in a Mercedes-Benz."

The partnership launches as The Sales Counsel approaches its tenth anniversary in September 2026. Since founding in 2016, the firm has grown by over 700%, having worked with enterprises including the JSE, Mercedes-Benz, Bidvest McCarthy, Waltons Coca-Cola, PPS, ABSA, Optix and many more.
