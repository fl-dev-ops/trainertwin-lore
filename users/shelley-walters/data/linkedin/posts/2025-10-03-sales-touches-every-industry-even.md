Sales touches every industry — even the ones you’d never expect.

Today I’m en route to deliver a keynote at the Annual ATOI (Agricultural Tour Operators International) Conference, proudly hosted in South Africa 🇿🇦, with delegates from across the globe.

I recently discovered this fascinating world of agricultural tourism, where farmers travel to other farms to learn from one another. They share insights, exchange best practices, and explore how others face challenges like water scarcity, soil erosion, and shifting climates. It is an entire ecosystem of curiosity and innovation.

In my session, I’ll be sharing a three-part, nine-step framework that explores how we:
1️⃣ Win the work through purposeful prospecting.
2️⃣ Convert the work through influence, empathy, and evidence.
3️⃣ Repeat the work by creating customers who return, refer, and rave.

Over the next few weeks, I’ll unpack each of these nine steps — from prospecting to persuasion and post-sale engagement — with practical insights for anyone working in an industry that relies on relationships and revenue.

Because sales isn’t just a function; it’s the energy that fuels growth, innovation, and impact.
