The true measure of impact isn’t what happens in the room—it’s what lingers long after.

Last week, I had the privilege of guiding the DA's provincial leadership team through a transformative experience. What struck me most was the depth of the discussions and how they continued well beyond our time together.
Vicky Knoetze, a Member of the Eastern Cape Provincial Legislature, shared this with me:

"People did not stop talking about you all week! You were referenced around dinner tables, drinks, and casual discussions in questions and opinions. The team that arrived on Monday was not the same team that left on Wednesday."

This speaks to something more profound than just ‘training’—it’s about unlocking conversations that wouldn’t have happened otherwise.

Leadership development isn’t about surface-level change. It’s about creating shifts that are felt, discussed, and acted upon. And when that happens, the ripple effect is undeniable.

What’s a conversation that has stuck with you long after the event? I’d love to hear about the moments that truly changed your thinking.
