Long weekends are meant for rest. And yet, this long weekend my team are spending it it neck-deep in detail, preparing for an international rollout.

It’s a strange duality, to crave rest and still feel deeply alive in the rush of readiness.
I don’t always get the balance right. But I am learning to honour both: the need to pause, and the call to build.

It's liberating.
