Bob Bradley from MD2MD is speaking truth today.

Building your business? It starts with clarity of purpose and ends with bold, consistent action.

Here are some of the standout insights from his list of top tips:

Know your "why" – It influences everything else you do.

Be prepared to sacrifice – Growth takes effort, investment, and intention.

Narrow your focus – Clear, attractive positioning beats trying to please everyone.

Stand out – Remarkability is your edge. Own your uniqueness.

Don’t fear polarisation – Be Marmite. The right people will stick.

Test, refine, repeat – Lead generation is a process, not a perfect science.

Be helpful and visible – Communities build businesses. Relationships grow revenue.

Competence matters, but marketing wins – Being brilliant is not enough if no one knows.


A timely reminder that success is a mix of courage, clarity, and community.
