Joey Evans is the closing speaker of the #PSASA2025 conference. 

“The only way to ride a bike on sand is to ride it fast.”

While racing the Dakar, he hit a camel and was seriously injured. While he was getting medical attention, his team was rebuilding his bike. When Joey was awake and clear headed, he realised that he could still get back into the race, the race wasn't over. 

He was on his bike, body battered, one hand swollen, wrist broken, shoulder barely holding up. In front of him: a long, brutal 22kms needed to finish the race. 

One catch, the 22kms was on the beach! Riding on soft sand is dangerous. Slow down, and you’re guaranteed to crash. Go fast, and you just might make it through.

There are four rules, he says:

1. Stand up

2. Look up

3. Gear up

4. Open up

And then—you commit.

Because on the sand (on the track, or in life), hesitation is what hurts you.
Life is the same. You can’t tiptoe through adversity.
You stand up, hold your vision, shift into gear, and give it everything you’ve got.

That’s how Joey Evans rides.
That’s how he leads.
And that’s why he moves teams—not with theory, but with real, lived grit.
