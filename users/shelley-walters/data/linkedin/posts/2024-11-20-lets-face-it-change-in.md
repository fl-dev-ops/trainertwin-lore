Let’s face it: change in sales is like trying to keep up with Gen Z slang. One day you’re “fire” 🔥, the next, you’re scrambling to figure out what “sus” means. (Pro tip: it’s not about your suspension system.)

Change can be frustrating, overwhelming, and yes, even demoralising at times. The Kübler-Ross Change Model won’t magically fix everything, but it can help you make sense of where you are in the journey. It’s not about avoiding the discomfort—it’s about recognising it and finding your footing within the chaos.
