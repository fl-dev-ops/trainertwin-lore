A really important perspective from Barbara Arnold on where market data is heading.

We are no longer operating in a world where access to data alone creates advantage. The advantage increasingly sits in interpretation, usability, relevance and trust. As AI accelerates the shift from scarcity to abundance, the winners will be the organisations that stay close to what users actually need and translate data into decisions, not just volume.

Looking forward to listening to the full conversation. This is a timely lens for anyone thinking seriously about the future of market data, AI-ready datasets and value creation.
