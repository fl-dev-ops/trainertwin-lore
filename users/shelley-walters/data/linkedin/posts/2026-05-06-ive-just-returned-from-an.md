I've just returned from an incredibly insightful experience at the Mercedes-Benz Vans 'Full Throttle' Sales Conference, and it's left me reflecting deeply on the true essence of commercial performance.

It was a genuine privilege to spend time with the Mercedes-Benz Vans team, where our work across the keynote and two workshop sessions wasn't just about what we sell, but how we truly understand the buyer in front of us.The 'Vito Select' conversation, in particular, served as a powerful lens for this. It highlighted that our role isn't simply to present a product; it's to uncover the person, the context, the pressures, the aspirations, and the underlying decisions behind every enquiry.

Speaking of the Vito, I also had the privilege of test-driving the Vito Mixto for a week, and what an absolute pleasure it was! It's an incredible vehicle, holding an exciting place in the market. This firsthand experience reinforced for me why we truly want to speak to our clients' outcomes, not just product features. 

This shift, from merely providing product information to gaining profound buyer insight, from features to meaning, and from a standard opener to a diagnostic conversation, was the heart of our session.

In today's market, buyers arrive more informed than ever. The real differentiator often isn't how much a salesperson knows, but what they are able to uncover. It's about moving from asking "What are you looking for?" to genuinely inquiring, "Tell me about you."

Thank you to Mercedes-Benz Vans for fostering such an environment at the 'Full Throttle' Sales Conference 2026, and for trusting The Sales Counsel to contribute to such a pivotal commercial moment. It was truly inspiring to be part of a team so committed to elevating the sales conversation.
