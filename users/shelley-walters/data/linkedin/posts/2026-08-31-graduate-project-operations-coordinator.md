Graduate Project & Operations Coordinator
Full-time | In-office | Cresta, Johannesburg

Do you get more satisfaction from designing the plan, or from making sure all the small actions in the plan actually happen?

If it’s the second one, this job and our team may be for you. 

The Sales Counsel has an opportunity for a smart, early-career Project & Operations Coordinator to join our small, high-performing team. You’ll help us keep client programmes, communicate with our associates, prepare workshops and keep internal projects moving. 

That means things like:
- tracking actions, deadlines and deliverables
- coordinating meetings, workshops and logistics
- following up with colleagues, clients and suppliers
- keeping project systems and documentation accurate
- turning meetings into clear actions and next steps
 - spotting what is missing or at risk before it becomes a problem
- using AI and digital tools to work faster and smarter

We’re looking for someone who is naturally organised, proactive, technologically confident and very good at follow-through. Excellent spoken and written English is essential.

You’ll probably have a relevant qualification in business, operations, project management, logistics, administration, or a related field, with around 1–2 years of workplace experience.

This is a full-time, predominantly in-office role based in Cresta, Johannesburg.
To apply, send your CV together with a short response to these two questions:
1. Tell us about something you noticed needed to be done, even though nobody had specifically asked you to do it. What did you do?
2. Which gives you more satisfaction: designing the plan, or making sure all the small actions in the plan actually happen? Why?

We’re looking for someone who wants to learn quickly, take ownership and grow with the business. If you think this might be for you, please email talent@thesalescounsel.co.za
