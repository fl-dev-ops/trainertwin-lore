I somehow missed this tag last year, but seeing it now made me stop and reflect.

I have been part of Civitas for several years now, and when I look back over that period, I can see very clearly how much this network has contributed to my thinking, my leadership and the growth of The Sales Counsel.

Civitas has given me access to a broad network of talented, driven and ambitious entrepreneurs. People who are building serious businesses, asking better questions, challenging each other, sharing hard-won lessons and refusing to stay comfortable.

There is a line from Proverbs that says, “As iron sharpens iron, so one person sharpens another.”

That has been my experience of Civitas.

It has sharpened my thinking. It has exposed me to different business models, different levels of commercial maturity, different leadership challenges and different ways of seeing the world. It has also given me access to mentoring, thought leadership and relationships that have mattered.

In the years that I have been a member, The Sales Counsel has grown by around 700%. Of course, no single network can claim responsibility for that. But Civitas has, in no small way, formed part of the ecosystem that helped make that growth possible.

I’m grateful for the challenge.

I’m grateful for the support.

And I’m grateful for the quality of people I’ve had the privilege of being around through this network.

Thank you to the Civitas Network team and to the members who continue to make it such a valuable space.
