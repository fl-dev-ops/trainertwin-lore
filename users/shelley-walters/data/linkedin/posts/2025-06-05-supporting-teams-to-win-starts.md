Supporting Teams to Win Starts with Understanding How Teams Form

Today we had the privilege of welcoming Anne 💡 Gray  to our offices. Anne is a trusted partner with whom we work closely on client projects. 

Today we were the beneficiaries of her vast knowledge, her skill and her insight as Anne facilitated a powerful and highly engaging workshop on the Forming, Storming, Norming, and Performing framework by psychologist Bruce Tuckman.

This framework is essential for any team in its early stages, helping us navigate the natural phases of team development with greater awareness, empathy, and intention.

Anne is a master facilitator, and our team walked away feeling energised, connected, and aligned. The session was as rewarding as it was meaningful l, reminding us that high-performing teams are never accidental. They’re created, nurtured, and supported.

At The Sales Counsel, we are doing everything we can to position our people to win, just like we do for our clients. Today was a beautiful step forward in that direction.

Thank you, Anne. You Rock!
