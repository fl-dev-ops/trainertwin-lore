You can’t be brave if you’re not well.

That was one of the standout moments from Helen Nicholson session on Personal Branding—a talk that hit home on so many levels. Helen  also invited us to reflect on how we show up and express our brand. Are we talking too much? Too little? Are we clear? She shared a useful guide: 60% listening. 40% speaking.
It’s not just smart communication—it’s smart presence.

She also asked us to look honestly at our personal brand weaknesses, and reminded us that you can’t be brave if you’re not well. That landed.

When you're at a networking event, she said, don’t just speak to the people you know. Don’t stay comfortable. Look for a group of three.
Not two (you’re intruding).
Not four (too many).
Three is the magic number.
