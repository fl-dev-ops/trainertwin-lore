We're officially in our new offices, and I can't help but stand still and marvel at the journey the last ten years has been.

They say if you want to go fast, go alone. If you want to go far, go together. And that, more than anything, is the truth of it. The Sales Counsel would not be here, not in this form, not growing the way we are, not with this team, had it not been for every client, every partner, every associate, and every team member who chose to walk this road with us.

Moments like these are hard to move through without reflecting, on who we are, on how far we've come, and on the people who shaped both.
Yes, we've moved into grown-up offices. And if you look closely, you'll notice there are still empty desks and open seats. That's deliberate. We built in capacity on purpose, because we're on a mission to create jobs, and we look forward to seeing this space fill with new talent as we grow into our next decade.

Ten years this September. To everyone who helped us get here: thank you. The best is still ahead.

We win it. We don't wing it.
