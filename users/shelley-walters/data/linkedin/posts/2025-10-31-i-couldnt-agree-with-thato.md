I couldn’t agree with Thato Schermer more.

Why isn’t there a BCom in Sales?

Imagine what it would mean for South African graduates if we taught sales as a serious business discipline?

If there were a BCom in Sales, this is what I believe the foundation curriculum should include:

Pipeline management and diagnosis

Prospecting at foundational, intermediate, and mastery levels

Consultative conversations and needs analysis

Presenting a value proposition

High-stakes pitch delivery

Closing Skills, including how to solicit and test for buying signals

Negotiation skills

Account management and client growth

Influence mapping and stakeholder management

Productivity, focus, and revenue-generating behaviours

Emotional intelligence and conflict resolution

Personal Branding and influence 

Imagine the edge this would give BCom or accounting graduates entering the market,  they’d not only be employable, but valuable from day one, because they could contribute to revenue-generating activity.

Interestingly, research suggests that roughly one in five global CEOs began their careers in sales or marketing. Many believe the only path to the top runs through finance, yet sales builds leaders too -  resilient, commercially literate, emotionally intelligent ones!

If you could design a BCom in Sales, what would you include in the curriculum?
