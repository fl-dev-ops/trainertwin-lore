I’ve been a member of Civitas since 2018, and it remains one of the best decisions I’ve made as a business owner.

There are some rooms that change the quality of your thinking.

Civitas has been one of those rooms for me.

It gives business owners and CEOs access to people who understand the weight of building, leading, deciding, employing, growing, carrying risk, and staying commercially sharp through different seasons of business.

I’ll be honest: I have not attended as consistently as I would have liked.

Like many founders, I have often put the immediate demands of the business first.

Client work.
Delivery.
Team needs.
Pipeline.
Cashflow.
Deadlines.

All important.

But I am reminding myself that investing in the right peer environment is also an investment in the business.

Tonight I’m attending a Civitas CEO Advisory event, and I’m genuinely looking forward to being back in the room.

For business owners and CEOs who are building, scaling, recalibrating, or carrying the real weight of leadership, the right community matters.

Not performative networking, but a room where business owners can think properly, be challenged intelligently, and learn from others who understand the terrain.

That has been my experience of Civitas.

And I’m grateful to be part of it.
