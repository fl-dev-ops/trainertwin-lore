Work has gone digital crazy. But people? People are still human.

At #PSASA2025, keynote speaker Brad Shorkend reminded us of something vital: In a world that is increasingly dominated by technology, algorithms, and automation, it is our ability to better human that will define whether we succeed or burn out.

Brad, CEO of Still Human, has spent nearly two decades helping businesses, leaders, and everyday people create workplaces where humans can actually thrive—not just survive. His talk challenged us to look beyond productivity hacks and digital tools and focus instead on the messy, complex, beautiful task of being human at work.

Leadership. Innovation. Culture. Strategy. None of it matters if we forget the people who bring it to life.

If you’ve ever felt overwhelmed by the pace of change, or underwhelmed by the humanity in your workplace, Brad’s message is for you.

Let’s not just adapt to a digital world. Let’s lead with what makes us human.

Brad Shorkend you delivered an incredible session!
