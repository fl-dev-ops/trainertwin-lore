Really? 

Everyone loves this Thich Nhat Hanh quote:

“When you plant lettuce, if it does not grow well, you don’t blame the lettuce.”

It is a beautiful metaphor for leadership, but it is incomplete.

Lettuce only grows when it is planted in the right soil, at the right time, and in the right conditions. Too much sun, too little water, or the wrong environment, and even the healthiest seed will struggle to thrive.

Sales is no different.

When we plant the right salesperson in the right environment, and we nourish them with teaching, coaching, stretch goals, encouragement, and accountability, they flourish. But not every type of salesperson thrives in every type of sales environment.

As Forrester describes in The 21st Century Salesperson, there is a world of difference between:

The Expediter, who succeeds in low-complexity, high-velocity environments.

The Engager, who bridges product knowledge and buyer understanding.

The Consultant, who adapts solutions and relationships for mid-complex sales.

The Orchestrator, who leads complex, multi-stakeholder, insight-driven engagements.

Before we blame the lettuce, we should ask: have we matched the seed to the soil?

That is what real sales leadership is about, creating the right conditions for the right people to grow.
