When does your English feel heaviest?

Over the last few days I’ve been talking about 𝗰𝗼𝗴𝗻𝗶𝘁𝗶𝘃𝗲 𝗹𝗼𝗮𝗱, what happens when your brain is solving the problem, translating, structuring, and speaking at the same time.

Most senior professionals I talk to can handle English when the situation is easy.
But when the stakes go up, things get complicated.

When you need to be clear.
When you need to be fast.
When you need to sound confident.
When people are listening carefully.

That’s when the load increases.

You might think it’s purely a language problem.
But in reality, your brain is doing too many things at once.

In my experience, this shows up in very specific situations:

• meetings where you want to sound decisive
• presentations where you need to be structured
• interviews where every word feels important
• client calls where you can’t hesitate
• explaining complex ideas in real time
• “small talk” when you need to connect

Some contexts feel light.
Some feel heavy.

So I’m curious.
