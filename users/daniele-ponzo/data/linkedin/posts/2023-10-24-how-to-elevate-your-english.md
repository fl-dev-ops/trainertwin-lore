How to elevate your English with a simple strategy

Last month we spoke about a quick trick to uplift your English:

Change a common verb ➡️ to a professional sounding one.

Here is “𝐏𝐚𝐫𝐭 2” (I wanted to do it more “Hollywood Style”, but I couldn’t).

So, depending on the situation (a formal meeting with a client or an informal catch up with a colleague) we might want to use the “simple” or the “professional” option.

And we should be ready to switch.

This is why I think it is useful to have them both in your toolbox.

So, next time you’re in a call.

Or you want to write an important email.

You can pick the most appropriate verb.

For example: “check” ➡️ “Review”.
 
 "I will review the project documentation for accuracy."
