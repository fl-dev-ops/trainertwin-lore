You told me what’s holding you back — and I listened.

A couple of weeks ago, I asked a simple question:
👉 𝗪𝗵𝗮𝘁 𝗽𝗮𝗿𝘁 𝗼𝗳 𝘆𝗼𝘂𝗿 𝗘𝗻𝗴𝗹𝗶𝘀𝗵 𝘄𝗼𝘂𝗹𝗱 𝘆𝗼𝘂 𝗹𝗶𝗸𝗲 𝘁𝗼 𝗶𝗺𝗽𝗿𝗼𝘃𝗲?

Some people were kind enough to share their perspective.

And what they said matched exactly what I hear every day in my work:
🧠 “I know the vocabulary in my head — but I can’t find it when I speak.”
🧱 “I second-guess every sentence I write.”
🎧 “Fast speech? I miss half of what’s said.”
🗣 “I’m not sure I’m even pronouncing common words right.”

These aren’t small issues.
They’re the reason so many smart professionals feel stuck, even after years of study.

So this week, I want to offer something practical.

Each day, I’ll post a short free masterclass to help you work through one of these four blocks:
📘 Vocabulary
🔤 Grammar
🔊 Pronunciation
👂 Listening

No theory. No overwhelm.
Just useful, focused strategies you can use right away
to speak, listen, and lead more confidently in English.

Follow along here on LinkedIn. I’ll post one link each day this week.
Let’s move past these blocks together. One step at a time.

See you tomorrow with the first masterclass!
