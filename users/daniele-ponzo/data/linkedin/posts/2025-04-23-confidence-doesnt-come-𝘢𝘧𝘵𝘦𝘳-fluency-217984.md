But in reality, it’s a trap.

You don’t wait until you’re strong to start training.

And I say this not just as a coach, but from experience.
I fell into that trap myself.

I studied hard to get certified.
I thought I needed to be perfect before I could show up and speak with confidence.

I waited. I over-prepared. I stayed quiet.
But nothing really changed until I reversed the approach. 🔁 

When I started speaking first (even when it wasn’t perfect) everything shifted.

That’s when I started improving.

Not because I knew more, but because I practiced in real conversations.

That’s when fluency stopped feeling like a finish line and started feeling possible.

Confidence comes first. 🔆 
Not the perfect grammar.
Not the advanced vocabulary.

Just the courage to try. To show up. To speak. Even if it’s messy.
