That awkward moment when…


…you smile and nod, even though you understood absolutely nothing. 😅

We’ve all been there.

You’re talking to a native speaker.
They say something quickly…
or with an accent you’re still decoding…
or they crack a joke you didn’t quite catch…

And suddenly you’re stuck in the most universal language-learning reaction:

The “𝘀𝗺𝗶𝗹𝗲, 𝗻𝗼𝗱, 𝗮𝗻𝗱 𝗽𝗿𝗮𝘆 𝘁𝗵𝗲𝘆 𝗱𝗼𝗻’𝘁 𝗮𝘀𝗸 𝗮 𝗳𝗼𝗹𝗹𝗼𝘄-𝘂𝗽 𝗾𝘂𝗲𝘀𝘁𝗶𝗼𝗻” face. 😬

Why do we do it?
Because we don’t want to:
❌ say “Sorry, can you repeat?” for the 5th time
❌ look rude
❌ kill the flow of the conversation
❌ ruin their joke by asking them to explain it
❌ admit we got lost after word number three

So we freeze…
we smile…
we nod…
and we hope our face doesn’t scream “I am panicking inside”.

I've done it many times during my years in Ireland.
And the funny thing?
It happens to EVERYONE, even famous people.

Imagine this: 
- Ed Sheeran excitedly talking to Messi praising him, telling him how incredible he is for nearly 5 minutes.
- Messi nodding along and smiling politely, 
- A representative coming in and telling Ed that Messi “doesn’t speak English”

So  we're in very, very good company.

If I could go back to those awkward moments, I’d simply:
✔ ask people to repeat
✔ ask them to slow down
✔ tell them “I didn’t catch that, can you say it again?”
✔ or even laugh and say, “Sorry, that went over my head!”

One thing I learned:
𝗡𝗼𝗯𝗼𝗱𝘆 𝘄𝗮𝘀 𝗷𝘂𝗱𝗴𝗶𝗻𝗴 𝗺𝗲.
It wasn’t my first language and that’s perfectly fine.

Most of the time, people are more than happy to repeat.
We’re the ones who make it awkward by pretending everything is fine.

Your turn! 
What’s your funniest “I’ll just smile and nod” language moment?
I'm just looking forward to hear your funny stories (so I don't feel alone)
