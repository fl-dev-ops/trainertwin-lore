The most effective way to improve your career.

I know I'm a bit biased, but this is based on research.

When you improve your English you get instant access to:

✓ A larger job market (+1,000s more job posts)
✓ Better positions (management, senior)
✓ Salary increase opportunities (up to +19%)
✓ Promotions (faster career growth)

If you don't believe me, I'll leave some interesting articles in the first comment below. They talk about Italy, Spain and France (I picked them because these are the languages I can read).

Some surprising facts?

1. Nobody is safe

English is now required in sectors across the market: admin, sales rep, technicians, engineers.
➡️ The market is global and so is the language.

2. Far from perfect 

You don't need to be King Charles to be hired. A medium level opens up great opportunities.
➡️ Put yourself out there and then start improving.

3. The average is low

Some big countries show a very low level of English. In many cases, people give up job applications because of their (perceived?) poor level.
➡️ So, it's not that hard to stand out and get noticed.

4. Juniors and Seniors 

English is required also in intermediate and senior roles. So, this is not only for people starting now. But also for seasoned pros.
➡️ Want to look for a better job? There will be an interview in English. It’s a fact.

Now, the question is: are you in admin, sales, technical support, project management, software or marketing?🤔

❌ If the answer is “no” you can still postpone your English improvement (for now…beware of AI).

✅ If the answer is “Yes”, then you can't wait any longer. The global market is catching up ⏱ .

You MUST take action, improve your English and stay competitive.

Remember: 
Investment today -> NO regrets tomorrow.
