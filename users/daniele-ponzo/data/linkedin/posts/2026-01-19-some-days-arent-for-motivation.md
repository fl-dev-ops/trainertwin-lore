Some days aren’t for motivation.
They’re for control.

The weather is grey, the sense of celebration (Christmas, where are you?) has faded and most New Year’s resolutions have disappeared.

The result? Low motivation and poor energy levels.
And we paint the world blue 🔵.

But wait a minute, there is a way to bounce back the blues!

First, get over the things you can’t control: the weather is bad, the holidays have gone, past setbacks are…well, they’re past.

And 𝗳𝗼𝗰𝘂𝘀 𝗼𝗻 𝘁𝗵𝗲 𝘁𝗵𝗶𝗻𝗴𝘀 𝘆𝗼𝘂 𝗖𝗔𝗡 𝗰𝗼𝗻𝘁𝗿𝗼𝗹💫:
Regaining motivation, 
Retargeting Resolutions, 
Finding new Energy, 
Revamping your Goals.

✅ Move your body!: Even a 10-minute stretch or a quick walk can boost your mood.

✅ Revisit Your Why: Reflect on your career goals and how today's work connects to your long-term success.

✅ Pick One Resolution: Identify one manageable aspect of a resolution you abandoned and restart with small steps.

✅ Set a Micro-Goal: Choose one small, achievable goal for today, such as completing a task you've been procrastinating.

✅ Use a Habit Tracker: Commit to a single action you can repeat daily for the rest of the month.

✅ Reward yourself for the effort to stay motivated and reinforce positive behaviour.

So, it’s about using your energy wisely.
And drafting a strategy to take action and fuel your progress.

This is 𝗵𝗼𝘄 𝘆𝗼𝘂 𝘁𝗮𝗸𝗲 𝗯𝗮𝗰𝗸 𝗰𝗼𝗻𝘁𝗿𝗼𝗹.
And start to repaint your day, your week, your month with a brighter range of colours.

What’s one small thing you can control today?
