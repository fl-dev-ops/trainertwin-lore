Here are some highlights from my clients in the last couple of weeks:

🙂 A Contract Manager volunteered to show his London colleagues around the office and be their host for the day

↳ From “I can’t speak English with native British, they will laugh 𝗮𝘁 me”
↳ To “I was even able to make a joke, and they laughed 𝘄𝗶𝘁𝗵 me”

🙂 A Team Leader started building up his confidence in speaking by sending daily voice messages in English

↳ From “English is not for me; I’ve tried many times, and I just can’t speak”
↳ To practicing in a safe space and speaking naturally.

🙂 A Senior Finance Manager travelled abroad to present in front of the board of directors

↳ From “I feel left out in the calls, I can’t share my ideas”
↳ To speaking in front of a real audience
