An IT PRO guide to visibility in the workplace.


I’ve recently read a great article in the harvard business review.

“An Introvert’s Guide to Visibility in the Workplace”.

Being an introvert myself, this deeply resonates.

And I believe this is the same for non-native English speakers working in an international environment.

Remember, being unseen can lead to being overlooked for promotions or misunderstood as lacking passion 😕 .

So, let’s change this by following this strategy!

How?

Here's a good strategy:

⭕ Speaking up early in meetings, so you engage thoughtfully, 
⭕ Using assertive language, so you avoid self-deprecation
⭕ making the most of asynchronous communication.
⭕ Showing gratitude.
