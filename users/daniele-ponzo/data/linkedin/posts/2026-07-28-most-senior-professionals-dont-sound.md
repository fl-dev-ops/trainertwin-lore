Most senior professionals don't sound more intelligent.

They sound easier to follow.

If you work in tech and communicate in English, these are five communication habits that will instantly make you sound more senior.

SPOILER: You don’t need to use fancier or bigger words.

But you need to make your thinking easier to understand.

Here's the framework I come back to most often with the engineers and tech leaders I coach.

1️⃣ 𝗦𝘁𝗮𝗿𝘁 𝘄𝗶𝘁𝗵 𝗱𝗶𝗿𝗲𝗰𝘁𝗶𝗼𝗻

Don't make people guess where you're going.

❌ Instead of:
"I was just thinking that maybe we could..."

✅ Try:
"My recommendation is..."
or
"The point I'd like to raise is..."

The first sentence should reduce uncertainty, not create it.

2️⃣ 𝗟𝗲𝗮𝗱 𝘄𝗶𝘁𝗵 𝘁𝗵𝗲 𝗽𝗼𝗶𝗻𝘁

Many professionals explain everything...
before saying what they actually mean.

History.
Background.
Exceptions.
Technical details.

By the time they reach the point...
the room has already moved on.

A much better structure is:

Point → Reason → Detail

For example:

"I recommend delaying the release by one week. The main reason is quality risk. We still have two unresolved issues in the payment flow."

Simple.
Logical.
Easy to follow.

3️⃣ 𝗦𝘁𝗿𝘂𝗰𝘁𝘂𝗿𝗲 𝗯𝗲𝗮𝘁𝘀 𝗰𝗲𝗿𝘁𝗮𝗶𝗻𝘁𝘆

You don't need to sound 100% certain.
You do need to sound organised.

❌ "Maybe... I'm not sure... perhaps..."

✅ "I see two possible options."
or
"My initial view is..."

People don't expect you to know everything.
They do expect you to think clearly.

4️⃣ 𝗙𝗶𝗻𝗶𝘀𝗵 𝘄𝗶𝘁𝗵 𝗮 𝗿𝗲𝗰𝗼𝗺𝗺𝗲𝗻𝗱𝗮𝘁𝗶𝗼𝗻

Senior communication doesn't stop at information.
It ends with direction.

Instead of finishing with:
"So... that's the situation."

Finish with:
✅ "My recommendation is..."
✅ "The next step is..."
✅ "What I'd suggest is..."

Never leave people wondering:
"So... what do we do now?"

5️⃣ 𝗥𝗲𝗽𝗹𝗮𝗰𝗲 𝗳𝗶𝗹𝗹𝗲𝗿 𝘄𝗼𝗿𝗱𝘀 𝘄𝗶𝘁𝗵 𝗽𝗮𝘂𝘀𝗲𝘀

When your brain needs a second...
don't fill the silence.

❌
"Uhm..."
"Basically..."
"You know..."
"How can I say..."

Pause.
Think.
Continue.

A short pause sounds far more confident than a sentence full of filler words.

It also reduces cognitive load
for you and for the people listening.

Here's the interesting part.

None of these habits require better English.

They require better communication.

At senior level, people are evaluating your judgement.
Your clarity.
Your ability to guide a conversation.

That's what people remember.
