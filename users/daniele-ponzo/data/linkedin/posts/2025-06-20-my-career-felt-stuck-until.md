My career felt stuck — until I found my voice.

There was a point in my IT career where everything felt… off.

I was showing up. Doing the work. Hitting deadlines.

But something wasn’t moving.

I watched colleagues get promoted.
Take on exciting projects.
Be seen.

And me? Still waiting.
Still wondering why I felt invisible.
Still blaming “the system,” “corporate politics,” “bad luck.”

But the truth hit me eventually. And it wasn’t easy to admit:

My work was solid.
But my communication wasn’t.

I didn’t sound confident.
I didn’t sound convincing.
I didn’t sound like someone ready to lead.

So I started working on that.
📣 I stopped chasing “perfect English” and started building presence.
🎤 I learned how to speak with clarity and calm, even when I felt unsure.
🧠 I worked on mindset, not just vocabulary.

And slowly, things shifted.

I began leading client meetings across Europe.
Presenting solutions. Handling tough questions without fear.
Then I started managing two teams and coaching service delivery pros to grow in their roles. Just like I had.

And that gave me the courage to do something I never expected:

👨‍🏫 Restart my career.
As a language coach.
Bringing together IT, communication, and training in one offer.

A career that finally feels like mine.

You don’t need to change your personality to lead.
But you do need to be heard.

And being heard starts with finding a way to speak that feels both clear and true to you.
