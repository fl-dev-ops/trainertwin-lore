LinkedIn? In summer? Shouldn’t you be off right now?

I mean, really… what are we all doing here? 😅

Shouldn’t we all be lying on a beach with a book in one hand and an ice cream in the other?

And yet… here you are. 

On LinkedIn. 
The work network. 
The place for professionals, goals and growth.

So, either you’re still working…
…or you're like me.

Yes, I am still working, but 
I believe that summer is also that sweet spot where work slows just enough to finally look inward.

Time to rest, recharge, maybe finally read that novel gathering dust or clear out that one drawer we keep pretending doesn’t exist.

But it’s the season of paradoxes: naps and notebooks. Ice cream and inbox zero (…maybe).

Personally, I’m trying to enjoy the slower pace while still keeping a couple gears turning.

Summer doesn’t have to be all-or-nothing. It can be both rest and growth, just on your own terms.

It’s a chance to relax and disconnect
But also, an opportunity to learn while we’re “less busy”

Sandcastles, podcasts, ice creams, articles, swimming pool, courses, sunbathing, studying… 🌅 🤓 

What a mix!

P.S. What’s something you’re making time for this summer (big or small)? I’d love to hear 👇
A course?
More time offline?
Tackling something you've been putting off?

(and if you’re planning to do absolutely nothing, that’s fine)

Let’s chat!
