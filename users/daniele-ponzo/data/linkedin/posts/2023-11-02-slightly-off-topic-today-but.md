Slightly off topic today, but very relevant, I think.

Is the 9-5 office schedule still the best way to work?

I’ve been following the online debate this viral video started a few days ago.

This American (freshly graduated) girl is complaining on TikTok about the fact that her job does not leave time for anything else.

Her points have been broadly dismissed (she’s immature, Gen Z don’t know anything about work, welcome to the real world, etc.).

She’s very emotional, but I do think she raised important points:

🔵 𝐂𝐨𝐦𝐦𝐮𝐭𝐢𝐧𝐠: more than 2 hours a day “wasted” on public transport to get to work and back;

🔵 𝐖𝐨𝐫𝐤-𝐋𝐢𝐟𝐞 𝐛𝐚𝐥𝐚𝐧𝐜𝐞: 8 hours in the office and 2 hours of commuting don’t leave much energy for anything else.

🔵 The fact that she accepted the job only to repay her tens of thousands of dollars’ 𝐝𝐞𝐛𝐭 contracted to complete her 𝐜𝐨𝐥𝐥𝐞𝐠𝐞 𝐝𝐞𝐠𝐫𝐞𝐞.

🔵 The concept itself of 𝐛𝐞𝐢𝐧𝐠 𝐢𝐧 𝐚𝐧 𝐨𝐟𝐟𝐢𝐜𝐞 𝐟𝐨𝐫 8 𝐡𝐨𝐮𝐫𝐬, “pushing the same rock up and down a hill every day”.
 
Ultimately, all these points are about quality of life, mental health, and one’s dream. 

Is this not something we always talk about?

Sure, the 9-5 schedule can secure stability. But what is the cost?

And why do we have to be inside the office when the same tasks can often be completed remotely?

So, I’m glad this “40 𝘩𝘰𝘶𝘳𝘴 𝘪𝘯 𝘵𝘩𝘦 𝘰𝘧𝘧𝘪𝘤𝘦” work culture is being questioned.

And if a girl does it in an emotional video, it is no reason to dismiss her or ignore the point she (and more people of her generation) is trying to make.

I’d love to know your opinion on this. What are the pros and cons of the 9-5 work culture? Let’s chat in the comments below 👇 .
