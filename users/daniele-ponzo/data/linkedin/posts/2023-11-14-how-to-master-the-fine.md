How to master the fine art of persuasion with effective language.


Working in IT means putting together strong solutions, interesting proposals, and innovative developments.

But when it comes to selling them, we must rely on different skills:

💪 Positive Attitude
👩‍💼 Confidence
🤝 Negotiation Skills
📈 Customer Focus
🗣 Communication Skills

Convincing our boss our idea is effective can take more than showing them the project plan.

Explaining the solution to our client needs clear, effective and professional language.

Especially if we want them to buy our product.

We can work on our confidence and develop a positive attitude.

And we can refine our language to increase the possibility of getting that “yes”.
