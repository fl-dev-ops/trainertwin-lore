Is it possible for Introverted IT leaders to feel confident and make an impact?
  
I have always been an introvert.
 
Dreading that phone call📞.
 
Collecting excuses to avoid meetings 🏃‍♂️.
 
Steering clear of social events 😬 .
 
But it DOESN’T have to be that way.

So, How do you break free from hesitation and embrace confident English speaking as an introvert?
 
How do you make sure you’re ready for your meetings, presentations, calls…
 
Well, here’s a strategy that has worked for me.
 
First of all, you should stop doing these things:

❌ Overthinking: 
That voice in your head…Sometimes, the most impactful words come naturally.

❌ Rushing: 
Speak at your pace. Remember, It's about clarity, not speed.

❌ Fearing Mistakes: 
When you interact, you are going to make mistakes. They are stepping stones to mastery. Learn from them.

❌ Multitasking:
Give conversations your full attention. It's a sign of respect and helps comprehension.

❌ Comparing Yourself: 
Your introvert strengths are your unique advantage. Embrace them!
 
 And then, you should focus on the following:
 
✅ Prepare Thoroughly: 
Boost your confidence by arming yourself with knowledge and questions in advance.

✅ Listen Actively: 
According to some studies, when we communicate, we spend 45% of our time listening (compared to 30% speaking). So, Listening can be your secret weapon in understanding and responding effectively.

✅Take Small Steps: 
Confidence grows with each contribution. Start small, and you'll find your voice.

✅ Practice Deep Breathing: 
When you feel nervous, remember to take a moment to breathe deeply and centre yourself.
 
✅ Speaking is Better than Texting: 
I know, this can be hard. Make an effort, call, meet and talk to other people. This helps clarify opinions and avoid misunderstandings. I promise it will become natural…
 
Remember, it's not about transforming into an extrovert but using your introvert strengths to communicate effectively in the business environment.
