Demystifying Business Jargon #19: "It is what it is".

A few years ago, I was in a heated service improvement meeting.

One of our clients (a large French multinational company) was complaining as, according to them, we hadn’t respected our contract deliverables.

When illustrating the improvement plan, my manager stated: “it is what it is, let’s now win back our customer’s trust”.

So, what does it mean 🤔 ?

"It is what it is" is a phrase used to accept a situation that cannot be changed. 

It’s a way of acknowledging reality without complaining or resisting. 

In corporations, Business and IT, this phrase can be particularly useful when dealing with challenges or limitations that are beyond our control.

Here are a couple of examples:

Example 1: Dealing with Legacy Systems

Scenario: Your team is working with outdated legacy systems that are difficult to update or replace.

Usage: “We have to integrate our new software with this old system. It is what it is, so let’s focus on finding the best solution given the constraints.”

Example 2: Handling Tight Deadlines

Scenario: You’re handed a project with an unreasonably tight deadline and limited resources.

Usage: “The deadline is non-negotiable, and we have to work with what we have. It is what it is. Let’s prioritize the key features and make it work.”

How do you use it effectively?

🔵 Acknowledge the Situation: Recognize the reality of the challenge you’re facing.
🔵 Stay Positive: Use the phrase to shift focus from complaining to problem-solving.
🔵 Move Forward: Encourage your team to accept the situation and concentrate on actionable steps.

According to the New York Times, the phrase “it is what it is” appeared as early as an 1949 article by J.E. Lawrence in The Nebraska State Journal.

“𝘕𝘦𝘸 𝘭𝘢𝘯𝘥 𝘪𝘴 𝘩𝘢𝘳𝘴𝘩, 𝘢𝘯𝘥 𝘷𝘪𝘨𝘰𝘳𝘰𝘶𝘴, 𝘢𝘯𝘥 𝘴𝘵𝘶𝘳𝘥𝘺. 𝘐𝘵 𝘴𝘤𝘰𝘳𝘯𝘴 𝘦𝘷𝘪𝘥𝘦𝘯𝘤𝘦 𝘰𝘧 𝘸𝘦𝘢𝘬𝘯𝘦𝘴𝘴. 𝘛𝘩𝘦𝘳𝘦 𝘪𝘴 𝘯𝘰𝘵𝘩𝘪𝘯𝘨 𝘰𝘧 𝘴𝘩𝘢𝘮 𝘰𝘳 𝘩𝘺𝘱𝘰𝘤𝘳𝘪𝘴𝘺 𝘪𝘯 𝘪𝘵. 𝘐𝘵 𝘪𝘴 𝘸𝘩𝘢𝘵 𝘪𝘵 𝘪𝘴, 𝘸𝘪𝘵𝘩𝘰𝘶𝘵 𝘢𝘱𝘰𝘭𝘰𝘨𝘺.”

As we saw in previous episodes, this phrase too became popular in sports about losses, and was later transferred to the world of business.

Source: Dictionary ‘dot’ com.

So, next time you face a frustrating situation….”it is what it is” and move on!
