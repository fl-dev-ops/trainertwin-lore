We all spend hours in meetings.
But are they helping us move forward — or just draining our time?

Research shows that when meetings are done well, they boost team success, trust, and even personal growth. 

But when they’re poorly run (or when we don’t feel confident enough to speak up), we miss opportunities — for our teams and our careers.

That’s why I wrote a new article on:
 👉 Why meetings matter more than we think
 👉 The unique challenges IT professionals face in international settings
 👉 5 practical strategies to communicate clearly and build visibility in meetings

Here’s my question for you:
What’s the hardest part of international meetings for you: understanding, speaking up, or being heard?

And if you want to dive deeper, you’ll find more strategies in my new book Easy English for IT, created to help tech professionals not just “survive” meetings, but lead them with confidence.
