Ever had an English hangover?

You know the feeling.

The meeting ends. You shut your laptop.

And then it hits you — hard.
💥 Suddenly, you remember what you wanted to say.
💥 The perfect sentence appears in your head.
💥 The words come back… 10 minutes too late.

Welcome to The English Hangover🥴:

No wild parties.
No tigers in the bathroom.
Just the sharp pain of missed opportunities.

You understood everything.
You had ideas.
But when it was your turn to speak, your mind went blank… again.

This happens to incredibly smart professionals every day

People who can code, build, present, manage, lead…
but freeze the moment English is required.

It’s not about intelligence.
It’s not about effort.

It’s about a skill no one teaches:

💬 how to think and speak in real time, even when you’re nervous.

If you’ve been stuck in this loop
replaying conversations in your head and rewriting your lines after the meeting ends
you’re not alone.

🎯 I’m curious:
When was your last “English hangover”?
What triggered it, and what did you wish you’d said?
