Do you ever get that feeling that your computer is talking to you? 💻 


Well, I got it now.

This actually reminded me of something which happened to me a long time ago (I was still in customer support). 📞 

I answered the phone, and this slightly annoyed British customer was on the other side.

(He had some issues with one of his devices, so he had a reason to be annoyed).  

I asked his name, and I invited him to describe the problem.

He gave me a brief description of the issue and I took some mental notes on how I could help him.

Then I went:

“Can I have your serial number, please?”

That was the end of my support.😬

He replied (very irritated):

“Do you mean my machine’s serial number? I haven’t got a serial number!” 😠 

And that was it. I tried to bend over backwards to make up for my “mistake”, but there was just no way I could please him.

Talking about language barriers…

Anyway, I think my computer is correct, now.

It’s my battery that needs recharging! 😅 

I wish you all a great weekend!
