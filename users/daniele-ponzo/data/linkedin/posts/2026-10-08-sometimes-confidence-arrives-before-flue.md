Sometimes confidence arrives before fluency.

And that's exactly how it's supposed to happen.

A person who would have hesitated before

decides to say 𝘆𝗲𝘀.

A couple of days ago, I received this message from a client after an English work call:

“La call è andata bene.”
“It was my first English work call.”

And honestly, I think this says a lot.

For many professionals, the biggest challenge is accepting the opportunity in the first place.

The invitation arrives.
You’re not sure
And that's how opportunities quietly disappear.

But then one day you find the confidence to:

• accept the invitation
• speak first
• present your work
• represent your team
• say yes to opportunities

The first time is hard...
the next time becomes easier.

That’s why I care so much about helping people build 𝗽𝗿𝗮𝗰𝘁𝗶𝗰𝗮𝗹 𝘀𝗽𝗲𝗮𝗸𝗶𝗻𝗴 𝗰𝗼𝗻𝗳𝗶𝗱𝗲𝗻𝗰𝗲, not just language knowledge.

There are two kinds of confidence.

The first says:

"Now my English is perfect."

Very few people ever feel that.

The second says:

"𝗜'𝗺 𝘀𝘁𝗶𝗹𝗹 𝗹𝗲𝗮𝗿𝗻𝗶𝗻𝗴... 𝗯𝘂𝘁 𝗜'𝗺 𝗴𝗼𝗶𝗻𝗴 𝗮𝗻𝘆𝘄𝗮𝘆."

That's the confidence that changes careers.

Obviously, that doesn’t mean you stop making mistakes.
It means that English stops being the reason you hold yourself back.

The first English meeting rarely changes your career.

But saying 𝘆𝗲𝘀 to the first one often does.

Because after the first...

there's a second.

Then a presentation.

Then an international project.

Then leadership starts seeing you differently.

Because you finally became visible.
