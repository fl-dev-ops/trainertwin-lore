The way you see yourself is costing you confidence.

And it’s why so many professionals struggle in international meetings. ❌

𝗬𝗼𝘂’𝗿𝗲 𝗹𝗼𝗼𝗸𝗶𝗻𝗴 𝗶𝗻𝘁𝗼 𝘁𝗵𝗲 𝘄𝗿𝗼𝗻𝗴 𝗺𝗶𝗿𝗿𝗼𝗿.

Imagine walking into an important meeting.

You’re an expert. You have the knowledge. You’ve solved these problems before.

But when it’s time to speak, your confidence wavers.
🔹 “How do they see me?”
🔹 “Do they think I sound professional?”
🔹 “Do they respect my opinion?”

This is 𝗧𝗵𝗲 𝗟𝗼𝗼𝗸𝗶𝗻𝗴-𝗚𝗹𝗮𝘀𝘀 𝗘𝗳𝗳𝗲𝗰𝘁. 🪞

We see ourselves through the reactions of others 
(or the reaction we 𝗶𝗺𝗮𝗴𝗶𝗻𝗲 others will have).
And when speaking in a second language, those reflections can distort reality.

If someone seems confused, you might think:
 ❌ "My English isn’t good enough."
 ❌ "They don’t take me seriously."
 ❌ "I should say less to avoid mistakes."

So, you hold back. You speak less. Others lead the conversation.

But here’s the truth:
Their reaction doesn’t define your expertise. 
Your communication does.

A clear, confident speaker 𝘀𝗵𝗮𝗽𝗲𝘀 the reflection others see.
A hesitant speaker 𝘄𝗮𝗶𝘁𝘀 for validation from others.

So, what’s the fix?

Start by changing your own mirror. 🪞
✅ Focus on delivering your ideas, not just on how you sound.
✅ Own your expertise. Fluency is not the same as intelligence.
✅ Look for real feedback, not imagined judgment.

Are you ready to go through the looking glass?

P.S. What’s one way you can start shaping your reflection today?
