'I want to be perfect'.

I need to know all the grammar and the vocabulary BEFORE I can speak.
Otherwise, I will not be confident enough.

If I could get a €🪙 for every time I’ve heard this, I would be rich.

Perfectionism is the best way to get stuck.

You keep looking for the perfect language and pronunciation. 

You study a lot, go through courses, complete Duolingo tasks, watch movies.

BUT

You keep postponing the essential part:

ACTION.

You can sit there, contemplating all the amount of knowledge you’re piling up.

And keeping still, striving for perfection.

But action is what really makes a difference.

Repetition -> Practice -> Experience -> Confidence.

🔹 Do you need your English to advance at work?

👉 Start practicing those essential skills (meetings, presentations, calls).

🔹 Are you looking for a new, international job?

👉 Start working on interview skills, personal brand, self-presentation.

How?

Start speaking today:

➡ Record yourself and listen back.
➡ Send voice messages to your friends or colleagues. 
➡ Video-record yourself to combine words and body language.
➡ Simulate interviews, presentations, calls and repeat, repeat, repeat.

Remember: if you want to keep still and not progress, then keep looking for perfection.

But if you want to advance, move, excel, then put yourself out there and level up later.

You won’t regret it.
