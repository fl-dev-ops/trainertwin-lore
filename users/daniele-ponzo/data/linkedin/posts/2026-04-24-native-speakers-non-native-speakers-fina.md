Native speakers 🤝 Non-native speakers

Finally agreeing on something:

Culture shock.

You travel abroad.

At first, everything feels… off.

Different food
New language
Never-experienced-before habits

And you feel awkward

“There is only one way to cook Carbonara”
“Why are you standing so close to me? Personal space invasion!”
“Can you speak clearer English (French, Italian, Spanish, Portuguese…)!”

But eventually you adapt

Your identity shifts
Your communication changes

And you start sounding… a bit more local.

Yet, deep inside you, you keep questioning yourself

Do I actually belong here…
or am I just trying to fit in?

Like you’re there, but you’re not really there

You’re a local and a foreigner at the same time.

And just when you think you’ve figured it out…

You go back home.

(what is home, anyway?)

And a nice surprise is waiting for you

Reverse culture shock

Coming back home should feel nice, safe, familiar
Well, it is…. and it is not

You start questioning everything:

“There should be more variety in food”
“Why is the city so dirty?”
“Is that how you do [insert any habit here] here? This doesn’t make sense”

And, ta dah! 

You start questioning yourself again:

Do I actually belong here…
or am I just trying to fit in?
or somewhere in between?

So, travellers of the World, unite!

It doesn’t matter if you’re native English speakers or non-native English speakers (or no English speakers at all).

The minute you leave the comfort of your home

You’re going to experience culture shock

And that’s the beauty of it!

What’s your best (or worst…) experience with culture shock?

Let’s chat in the comments!
