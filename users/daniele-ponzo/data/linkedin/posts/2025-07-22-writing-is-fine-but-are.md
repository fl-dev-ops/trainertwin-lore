Writing is fine... but are you ready for the real thing?

You spend time drafting the perfect message.
You double-check the spelling.
You even use AI, Grammarly, or DeepL to make sure everything flows just right.

We all do it, and that’s ok

But here’s the catch…

You sound great on paper but then someone calls you for a meeting.
And suddenly, your mind goes blank.

What happened?
It’s simple: writing and speaking are different skills.

Writing lets you edit, rethink, and polish.
Speaking is real-time. Unfiltered. Human.

I’ve heard this story so many times from the professionals I work with:
“In my mind, I speak great English. But when it’s time to talk, I freeze.”
“I use AI and translations for emails, but in meetings… I panic.”
“I’m confident on LinkedIn. But in real life, I don’t sound like ‘me’ anymore.”

Of course, it’s not about perfection.
It’s about alignment  between your written self and your spoken self. 

Between what you post and what you say in a room full of people.

Here’s the good news:
You can work on this and get better at it.

✅ Practice speaking even when no one is listening.
✅ Record yourself. Even just once a week.
✅ Speak to a friend, a coach, a mentor — regularly.
✅ Don’t let your English voice become a stranger to your own ears.

Use the tools. Get support. But don’t outsource your confidence.

We don’t want two versions of ourselves:
The polished, confident writer and the scared, apologetic speaker.

Let’s bring those two together. Let’s make your English real.
