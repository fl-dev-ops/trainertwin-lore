Imagine: you are in a high-stakes meeting 👨🏻‍💼 with colleagues from different countries.

The meeting is conducted entirely in English, which is not your first language. As the discussion progresses, your anxiety starts to mount. Your mind races with thoughts of making mistakes, not being able to express yourself clearly, or being misunderstood.

As the pressure builds, your confidence begins to waver, and you start to doubt your ability to actively contribute to the conversation. You feel self-conscious and withdrawn. 🙇‍♀️

The good news is it DOESN’T have to be that way. ✊

Take a deep breath and remind yourself of your accomplishments and expertise. Here are a few strategies to alleviate your anxiety and boost your confidence:

👨‍🎓  Preparation: dedicate time to prepare for the meeting. Review the agenda and anticipate potential discussion points. This preparation helps you feel more knowledgeable and ready to contribute.

🔊  Active Listening and Observing: Instead of solely focusing on your own language skills, actively listen to others and observe their non-verbal cues to gain a deeper understanding of the conversation. 

🙋‍♀️  Asking for Clarification: it's perfectly acceptable to ask for clarification if you don't understand something or needs further explanation. Seeking clarification demonstrates your commitment to understanding and contributing effectively.

🗣  Speaking in Short Sentences: Recognize that simplicity can sometimes be more effective than complexity. Choose to communicate your ideas in shorter, concise sentences. This approach helps you express yourself more clearly and reduces the pressure to deliver long, elaborate responses.

☺️  Positive Self-Talk: consciously replace negative thoughts with positive affirmations. Remind yourself of your technical expertise, and dedication to continuous improvement. This positive self-talk helps you regain confidence.

👨‍💻  Seeking Support: remember, you don’t have to do this alone! Reach out to a trusted colleague or a language coach to practice speaking in English before the meeting.

By implementing these strategies, you will manage to navigate the meeting despite your initial anxiety. With the right mindset, preparation, support, and a commitment to continuous improvement, you can overcome your anxieties and confidently contribute your valuable insights to the global IT community. 🚀
