Avoid the summer slide… or risk the “Baby Shark” effect 🎵😅

The summer slide isn’t just for kids.

Professionals feel it too 
that slow fade in vocabulary, fluency, and confidence when we take a long break from using English.

Psychologist Hermann Ebbinghaus called this the Forgetting Curve:
Memory starts to drop immediately after learning
Without recall or repetition, it plummets fast 📉 

The fix?

Consistent, light practice 
and yes, a little overlearning. 
(Think of why you could sing “Baby Shark” in your sleep… 
10 years from now. 🎵)
