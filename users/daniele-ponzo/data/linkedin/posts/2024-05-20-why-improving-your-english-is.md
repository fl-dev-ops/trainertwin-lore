Why improving your English is hard.

(and what you can do to fix it).

The one thing you should know about improving your English is that it is mostly down to building new habits. 
But new habits are difficult to build for four reasons.

⚠ First, finding the motivation to change your habits is not easy (what we usually refer to when we say “step out of your comfort zone”).

⚠Second, new habits can take up to 30 days (21 is the minimum) to form, and that’s a long-time investment for many people.

⚠Third, new habits don’t always show rewards immediately, so it is easier to slow down or quit.

⚠Fourth, habits tend to be a reflection of your group of friends and unless you are in an English-speaking country, it’s hard to change this variable.

I know what you’re thinking. This is just impossible.

Well, let me tell you, this isn’t.

It’s all about breaking old habits and creating new, positive ones.

Like going to the gym or starting a diet 💪 .

It takes motivation, consistency, patience, and smart strategies.

So, here’s what you can do, instead:

🔵 Why. To start, find your "why." Career advancement? Better communication with colleagues? Personal growth? Why? Why? (and why!)
Write down your reasons and keep them visible.

🔵 Consistency (oh, yes!). Create a routine. Set specific times each day dedicated to practicing English. Use apps, online courses, or join English-speaking groups.

🔵 Rewards (that little cherry on top 🍒 ). Celebrate small wins by treating yourself. After a week of consistent practice, reward yourself with something you enjoy, like having a favourite treat.

🔵 Swim in the English sea (might need a wetsuit). Create your own opportunities. Engage in online forums, watch English media, join online communities related to IT where English is the primary language. 

Or follow English-speaking tech & Business influencers on social media. 
Here are some great examples:
Nicola Ballotta, Petar Ivanov, Anemari Fiser, Omar Halabieh, Irina Stanescu, Dora Vanourek, Tiger Abrodi, Stuart Todd, Jade Wilson, Catherine McDonald (and many more).

P.S.: want to know one thing that has worked for me every time I needed to change habits? 
The “complaint bracelet”⭕. 
You put the bracelet on your wrist (Day 1) and every time you complain, you must switch arm (back to Day 1).

The target is to go 21 days complaint-free.

Use this every time you want to build a new habit.

You won’t regret it.
