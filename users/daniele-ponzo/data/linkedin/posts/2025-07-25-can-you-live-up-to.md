“Can you live up to their expectations?”

That’s the question that used to haunt me.

At the start of my corporate career, I relied 𝘩𝘦𝘢𝘷𝘪𝘭𝘺 on email.

Writing was my comfort zone.
It felt safe. Polished. Controlled.
And honestly? It worked… until it didn’t.

Because sooner or later, there will be a follow up to the emails you write.

You will have to walk into a room.
You will need to meet the client.
You will need to speak out loud.

I still remember the first time I had to face that test.
A client visit in Germany.
We had to explain some issues with our service and propose a solution.

My first instinct? Panic and run.

But, of course, that wasn’t an option.

So, I built a ritual to carry me through:
☑️ I woke up early to ease into the day
☑️ Put on a suit & tie to “step into” the confident version of me
☑️ Checked my slides again (my safety net)
☑️ Did voice warm-ups and rehearsed my opening

Then something unexpected helped even more:
They gave me a full tour of the factory.

Walking, chatting, observing it loosened me up, calmed my nerves, and helped me connect.

That experience taught me something big:

I could live up to the expectations I had created with my writing.

But only if I was willing to show up, in person, with presence.

And if I used the opportunity to go “beyond the script”
And connect
Human to human.

In retrospect, I realized there was something else that helped me a lot

I said I relied heavily on email at the beginning.

But I didn’t have AI, so they were my emails with my style, my language (and my mistakes)

They helped shape my communication approach and build up some confidence without even realizing it.

So when the time came to speak up
I was readier than I thought!

🗣️ If you’re relying on perfect messages, AI writing tools, or rehearsed scripts...
Just ask yourself: “𝘊𝘢𝘯 𝘐 𝘭𝘪𝘷𝘦 𝘶𝘱 𝘵𝘰 𝘸𝘩𝘢𝘵 𝘐 𝘴𝘰𝘶𝘯𝘥 𝘭𝘪𝘬𝘦 𝘰𝘯 𝘱𝘢𝘱𝘦𝘳?”
You might be closer than you think.

🔁 Have you ever had a similar moment? What helped you prepare — or show up — when it really mattered?
