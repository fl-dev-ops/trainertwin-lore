My English is good, but I have problems when I need to speak.

Sound familiar?🤔

This is one of the most common problems I hear.

We have some amazing ideas, but sometimes we are not confident enough to give our contribution in:

-       Meetings
-       Conference calls
-       Social situations

And we have great skills but feel we can’t get the message across.😒

So, how can we boost our confidence and trust ourselves in our next meeting?🚀

How can we make sure we can explain properly what our "Start-up" does?

Or our job role?

Here are some actionable ideas to improve confidence when speaking English at work➡️🚀:

𝐏𝐫𝐚𝐜𝐭𝐢𝐜𝐞 𝐒𝐩𝐞𝐚𝐤𝐢𝐧𝐠 𝐑𝐞𝐠𝐮𝐥𝐚𝐫𝐥𝐲: Engage in daily conversations in English, both inside and outside the workplace, so it becomes natural.

𝐄𝐱𝐩𝐚𝐧𝐝 𝐕𝐨𝐜𝐚𝐛𝐮𝐥𝐚𝐫𝐲: Make a habit of noting down new vocabulary encountered during meetings, presentations, or discussions, so you will make them yours.

𝐋𝐢𝐬𝐭𝐞𝐧 𝐭𝐨 𝐚𝐧𝐝 𝐌𝐢𝐦𝐢𝐜 𝐍𝐚𝐭𝐢𝐯𝐞 𝐒𝐩𝐞𝐚𝐤𝐞𝐫𝐬: podcasts, TED Talks, English IT Professionals. Try to replicate accents, intonation and pronunciation so you can unlock the essential elements of the language.

𝐑𝐞𝐜𝐨𝐫𝐝 𝐚𝐧𝐝 𝐑𝐞𝐯𝐢𝐞𝐰 𝐘𝐨𝐮𝐫𝐬𝐞𝐥𝐟: online meetings, presentations, or live practice sessions. Review the recordings to identify areas of improvement (pronunciation, fluency, or grammar), so you don’t make the same mistake twice.

𝐑𝐨𝐥𝐞-𝐏𝐥𝐚𝐲 𝐖𝐨𝐫𝐤 𝐒𝐜𝐞𝐧𝐚𝐫𝐢𝐨𝐬: Practice client meetings, presentations, or negotiations through role-play. 
This is where coaching can make a difference. Role-Play is crucial to gain confidence and receive immediate feedback on your language skills.

P.S. mastering a language is a journey, so remember to set achievable goals 📌(delivering a presentation in English or actively participating in meetings), celebrate each milestone achieved🌟, and embrace mistakes as they are a natural part of the learning process.🌈
