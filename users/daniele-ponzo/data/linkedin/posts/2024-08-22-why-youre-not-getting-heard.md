Why you're not getting heard in your meetings + what to do to fix it.

81% of employers use manager team meetings as the primary collaboration channels.

However, most of the time, Professionals are left with the feeling that they weren’t productive, or they didn’t contribute effectively. 

Not surprisingly, according to one study, 65% of people feel that meetings are a waste of time.

So, here are some tips to promote your ideas more effectively during team meetings and make them more successful.

1. Effective communication starts with giving advice. Avoid being too direct, especially if you are new to the team or if you have just started managing one.

Here are some Effective Expressions for Advice:

𝐌𝐚𝐤𝐞 𝐒𝐮𝐫𝐞 𝐘𝐨𝐮:
❌ You need to check the code thoroughly.
✅ Make sure you thoroughly review the code for any potential issues.

𝐈𝐟 𝐈 𝐖𝐞𝐫𝐞 𝐘𝐨𝐮 𝐈'𝐝:
❌ You should update the system.
✅ If I were you, I'd consider updating the system for enhanced security.

𝐓𝐡𝐞 𝐌𝐨𝐬𝐭 𝐈𝐦𝐩𝐨𝐫𝐭𝐚𝐧𝐭 𝐓𝐡𝐢𝐧𝐠 𝐈𝐬 𝐓𝐨:
❌ Just fix the bug quickly.
✅ The most important thing is to address the bug promptly to ensure a seamless user experience.

𝐃𝐨𝐧'𝐭 𝐅𝐨𝐫𝐠𝐞𝐭 𝐓𝐨:
❌ You need to install the security patch.
✅ Don't forget to install the latest security patch to fortify our system against potential threats.

2. When you spot failures or process gaps, it’s crucial to flag them right away. This will ensure you fix them before they escalate into bigger problems.

BONUS: you will get noticed if your contribution helps to “save the day”. And if you’re managing an international team, employees will appreciate if you’re not too direct.

Effective Expressions for Warnings:

𝐖𝐚𝐭𝐜𝐡 𝐎𝐮𝐭 𝐅𝐨𝐫:
❌ Be cautious when deploying the update.
✅ Watch out for any unforeseen issues during the deployment of the update.

𝐘𝐨𝐮'𝐝 𝐁𝐞𝐭𝐭𝐞𝐫:
❌ Finish the project on time.
✅ You'd better ensure the project is completed on time to meet our client's expectations.

𝐖𝐡𝐚𝐭𝐞𝐯𝐞𝐫 𝐘𝐨𝐮 𝐃𝐨, 𝐃𝐨𝐧'𝐭:
❌ You should avoid making mistakes.
✅ Whatever you do, don't overlook the details to prevent potential errors.

𝐂𝐚𝐮𝐭𝐢𝐨𝐧𝐚𝐫𝐲 𝐓𝐨𝐧𝐞:
❌ Don’t mess up the presentation.
✅ Approach the presentation with precision; the success of the project relies on it.
