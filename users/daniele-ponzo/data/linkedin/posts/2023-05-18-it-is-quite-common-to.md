It is quite common to hear native English speakers in IT use this expression:

"To get down to business"

Have you come across it before?
It means “to start getting serious about something”.
 
It can be a great idiom to use when you want to introduce an important topic in a meeting or maybe after a break, to start a serious conversation.
 
To help you, below are some examples of using this idiom.
 
“Wow, it’s getting late…maybe we’d better get down to business”

“Ok, thanks for the coffee…now, let’s get down to business”

So, do not postpone your English improvement, get down to business! Let’s try to use this idiom when you can.
