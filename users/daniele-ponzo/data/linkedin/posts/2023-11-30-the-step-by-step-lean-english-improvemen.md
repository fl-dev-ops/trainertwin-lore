The step-by-step Lean English Improvement roadmap for IT Professionals

Are you tired of spending hours learning the “general” language?

What if we had an effective method, instead?

The good news is we don’t need to reinvent the wheel.

Practical methodologies like 𝐋𝐞𝐚𝐧 𝐒𝐢𝐱 𝐒𝐢𝐠𝐦𝐚 help us devise the perfect strategy.

Let me show you how we can use the 𝐃𝐌𝐀𝐈𝐂 (Define—Measure—Analyze—Improve—Control) improvement process to master English fluency.

🧩 𝐃𝐞𝐟𝐢𝐧𝐞 𝐘𝐨𝐮𝐫 𝐆𝐨𝐚𝐥𝐬 (𝐃𝐞𝐟𝐢𝐧𝐞):
When it comes to improving your English, it all begins with setting clear, achievable goals. 
Define what you want to achieve - is it more fluent communication, better email writing, or enhanced presentation skills?
🔍 Example:
Goal: "𝘐 𝘸𝘢𝘯𝘵 𝘵𝘰 𝘪𝘮𝘱𝘳𝘰𝘷𝘦 𝘮𝘺 𝘧𝘭𝘶𝘦𝘯𝘤𝘺 𝘵𝘰 𝘤𝘰𝘯𝘧𝘪𝘥𝘦𝘯𝘵𝘭𝘺 𝘭𝘦𝘢𝘥 𝘮𝘦𝘦𝘵𝘪𝘯𝘨𝘴 𝘢𝘯𝘥 𝘤𝘰𝘮𝘮𝘶𝘯𝘪𝘤𝘢𝘵𝘦 𝘸𝘪𝘵𝘩 𝘨𝘭𝘰𝘣𝘢𝘭 𝘤𝘭𝘪𝘦𝘯𝘵𝘴."

📊 𝐌𝐞𝐚𝐬𝐮𝐫𝐞 𝐘𝐨𝐮𝐫 𝐏𝐫𝐨𝐠𝐫𝐞𝐬𝐬 (𝐌𝐞𝐚𝐬𝐮𝐫𝐞):
Regularly assess your current English skills to identify strengths and areas for improvement. 
Tools like language assessments and self-evaluations are your best friends here.
🔍 Example:
Strength: "𝘐'𝘮 𝘱𝘳𝘰𝘧𝘪𝘤𝘪𝘦𝘯𝘵 𝘪𝘯 𝘵𝘦𝘤𝘩𝘯𝘪𝘤𝘢𝘭 𝘵𝘦𝘳𝘮𝘴."
Area for Improvement: "𝘐 𝘴𝘵𝘳𝘶𝘨𝘨𝘭𝘦 𝘸𝘪𝘵𝘩 𝘯𝘢𝘵𝘶𝘳𝘢𝘭 𝘤𝘰𝘯𝘷𝘦𝘳𝘴𝘢𝘵𝘪𝘰𝘯 𝘧𝘭𝘰𝘸."

🧰 𝐀𝐧𝐚𝐥𝐲𝐳𝐞 𝐘𝐨𝐮𝐫 𝐀𝐩𝐩𝐫𝐨𝐚𝐜𝐡 (𝐀𝐧𝐚𝐥𝐲𝐳𝐞):
Check your learning methods. What's working, and what isn't? Identify and eliminate waste. 
In language learning, this means focusing on the most effective strategies.
🔍 Example:
Effective Method: "𝘋𝘢𝘪𝘭𝘺 15-𝘮𝘪𝘯𝘶𝘵𝘦 𝘌𝘯𝘨𝘭𝘪𝘴𝘩 𝘤𝘰𝘯𝘷𝘦𝘳𝘴𝘢𝘵𝘪𝘰𝘯 𝘱𝘳𝘢𝘤𝘵𝘪𝘤𝘦."
Ineffective Method: "𝘚𝘱𝘦𝘯𝘥𝘪𝘯𝘨 𝘩𝘰𝘶𝘳𝘴 𝘰𝘯 𝘨𝘳𝘢𝘮𝘮𝘢𝘳 𝘳𝘶𝘭𝘦𝘴."

🚀 𝐈𝐦𝐩𝐫𝐨𝐯𝐞 𝐂𝐨𝐧𝐭𝐢𝐧𝐮𝐨𝐮𝐬𝐥𝐲 (𝐈𝐦𝐩𝐫𝐨𝐯𝐞):
Constantly refine your approach based on your findings. 
Seek feedback, adopt new techniques, and evolve your learning strategy.
🔍 Example: "𝘐𝘯𝘤𝘰𝘳𝘱𝘰𝘳𝘢𝘵𝘪𝘯𝘨 𝘮𝘰𝘳𝘦 𝘭𝘪𝘴𝘵𝘦𝘯𝘪𝘯𝘨 𝘦𝘹𝘦𝘳𝘤𝘪𝘴𝘦𝘴 𝘪𝘯𝘵𝘰 𝘮𝘺 𝘳𝘰𝘶𝘵𝘪𝘯𝘦."

📋 𝐂𝐨𝐧𝐭𝐫𝐨𝐥 𝐘𝐨𝐮𝐫 𝐏𝐫𝐨𝐠𝐫𝐞𝐬𝐬 (𝐂𝐨𝐧𝐭𝐫𝐨𝐥):
Monitor your goals, measure your advancements, and ensure that your approach remains effective.
🔍 Example:
Control: "𝘙𝘦𝘨𝘶𝘭𝘢𝘳𝘭𝘺 𝘵𝘳𝘢𝘤𝘬 𝘮𝘺 𝘧𝘭𝘶𝘦𝘯𝘤𝘺 𝘶𝘴𝘪𝘯𝘨 𝘤𝘰𝘯𝘷𝘦𝘳𝘴𝘢𝘵𝘪𝘰𝘯 𝘮𝘦𝘵𝘳𝘪𝘤𝘴. 𝘊𝘰𝘯𝘥𝘶𝘤𝘵 𝘱𝘦𝘳𝘪𝘰𝘥𝘪𝘤 𝘭𝘢𝘯𝘨𝘶𝘢𝘨𝘦 𝘢𝘴𝘴𝘦𝘴𝘴𝘮𝘦𝘯𝘵𝘴 𝘵𝘰 𝘰𝘣𝘫𝘦𝘤𝘵𝘪𝘷𝘦𝘭𝘺 𝘮𝘦𝘢𝘴𝘶𝘳𝘦 𝘮𝘺 𝘭𝘢𝘯𝘨𝘶𝘢𝘨𝘦 𝘱𝘳𝘰𝘧𝘪𝘤𝘪𝘦𝘯𝘤𝘺."

Remember: make it measurable and achievable so, you get tangible proof of your progress, and you keep your motivation up.

Step up and start working effectively on your English improvement plan.

Establish a clear path to achieving your objectives.
