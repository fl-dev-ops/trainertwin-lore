Halfway through 2025. How’s it going?

Six months in.
Half the year behind us. Half still ahead.

So let me ask:
📌 Did you set big goals back in January?
📌 Are you still working on them?
📌 Or have they quietly drifted into the “maybe next year” zone?

This is how I (sometimes) feel:
I start strong, get pulled in a thousand directions, and forget to check back in with myself.

But June is a perfect time to hit pause.
👉 What do I want the second half of my year to look like?
👉 Where can I simplify, refocus, or restart?

Perfection?
Of course not (as I say, it doesn't exist)

But small moves? Yes 
With real meaning!
→ One new habit
→ One conversation you’ve been avoiding.
→ One shift in mindset.

There’s still time. To grow, to improve, to feel ready.
Not just for summer.

But for the season that comes after it
When things start to move again.

So tell me:
How’s your year going so far?
What’s felt good? What’s still unfinished?
And what do you want to create space for in the second half?
I’d love to hear it 👇
