Here’s how you can become a better English communicator WITHOUT improving your English

There’s an often overlooked part of communication and it has NOTHING to do with your words

It’s about using your body🕴.

Mastering body language is a skill that empowers us to communicate authentically, connect deeply, and make a lasting impact.

🤩 Our body language is a powerful vehicle for expressing enthusiasm and passion💫. 

Smiling, using animated facial expressions, and employing dynamic gestures can ignite energy and captivate the audience's attention.

𝐐𝐮𝐢𝐜𝐤, 𝐚𝐜𝐭𝐢𝐨𝐧𝐚𝐛𝐥𝐞 𝐭𝐢𝐩: remember to look at each member of the audience in the eye for just a few seconds

💻 Even in the digital realm, body language remains vital🌐. 

During online interactions, maintaining good eye contact through the camera, sitting upright, and using hand gestures within the frame convey attentiveness and professionalism.

𝐐𝐮𝐢𝐜𝐤, 𝐚𝐜𝐭𝐢𝐨𝐧𝐚𝐛𝐥𝐞 𝐭𝐢𝐩: resize your videocalling app (Zoom, Skype) and move the icon just under the camera, so you'll be looking directly at your interlocutor

Remember that Body Language transcends cultural and linguistic barriers, enabling meaningful connections across diverse backgrounds

and it can support you when you are not 100% confident with your language
