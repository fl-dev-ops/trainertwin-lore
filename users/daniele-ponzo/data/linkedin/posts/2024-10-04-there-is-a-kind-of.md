There is a kind of energy which goes above and beyond time.

It's called music.

Generations, age, taste, technique...these become pointless.

Literally.

When a teenager and an 80 year old sing the same song,

dance to the same tune,

celebrate the same moment...

it means something magical is happening.

Something you don't witness every day.

Sure.

The grass was greener...

The light was brighter,

The taste was sweeter...

But the nights are still of wonder!

Thanks Mr.Gilmour, it was an honour to be there.
