Yesterday, someone called me in panic.

He had one hour.
  
Fast.
Tense.
Desperate.
  
A professional I had spoken to before.

Great profile.
Strong technically.
Good with people.  

His company had just offered him a new role:

→ client representative
→ international exposure
→ direct contact with stakeholders

A real step forward.
  
Just one problem:

English.
  
He said:
“I don’t feel ready.”
  
But the company insisted.

They wanted him.

So they proposed:

“A quick test call in English.”
  
That’s when he called me.

Panic.
  
One hour.

To prepare for something that could change his career.
  
But the truth is…

You can’t fix years of hesitation, structure, and confidence
 in one hour.
(As much as I’d love to 😅)
  
This is what many professionals don’t realize:

Opportunities don’t wait for you to feel ready.

They appear.

Unexpectedly.
  
And when they do, you either:

→ step in with clarity
 or
→ step back with doubt
  
So here’s the real question:

𝗔𝗿𝗲 𝘆𝗼𝘂 𝗴𝗼𝗶𝗻𝗴 𝘁𝗼 𝗺𝗮𝗸𝗲 𝘁𝗵𝗮𝘁 𝗰𝗮𝗹𝗹 𝗼𝗻𝗲 𝗱𝗮𝘆…
𝗼𝗿 𝗮𝗿𝗲 𝘆𝗼𝘂 𝗽𝗿𝗲𝗽𝗮𝗿𝗶𝗻𝗴 𝗯𝗲𝗳𝗼𝗿𝗲 𝗶𝘁 𝗵𝗮𝗽𝗽𝗲𝗻𝘀?
  
Preparing to:
• handle conversations under pressure
• explain ideas clearly
• keep your structure
• sound confident when it matters

I don’t know yet if he passed.

I’ll speak to him later today.
  
But one thing is certain:

That situation is becoming more and more common.
  
If you don’t want that call to be yours one day…

start preparing before it happens.
