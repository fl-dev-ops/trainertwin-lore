Want to boost your English faster? Don’t do it alone.

Back when I worked in IT, a few of us in the office made a pact: every day at lunch we’d speak a different language.

🇫🇷 Monday was French day (way out of my comfort zone 😅)
🇮🇹 Tuesday was Italian day (it was my turn to guide the conversation)
🇪🇸 Wednesday was Spanish day (slightly easier for me)
🇬🇧 Thursday and Friday were back to English (phew!)

Was it awkward? Yep.

Did we get stuck, mix up words, and laugh a lot? Absolutely.

But did we improve? So much. And not just our language skills. 
Our confidence, teamwork, and empathy too.

And that’s what the C in my HACK strategy stands for: 𝗖𝗼𝗹𝗹𝗮𝗯𝗼𝗿𝗮𝘁𝗶𝗼𝗻.
 
Learning a language doesn't have to be a lonely grind. 

In fact, research shows it's way more effective when you're not alone:
✅ You hear new vocabulary in real conversations
✅ You get to make mistakes in a safe space
✅ You learn to express your thoughts with real people
✅ You stay motivated because others are cheering you on

According to several studies, people working in groups not only learn more but also enjoy it more. And that goes for adult professionals too.

👥 So find your people.
Lunch partners. Online communities. A study buddy. A coach.
Language is social and your learning should be too.

So, I'd like to know: If you had to choose one person to practise your English with every week, who would it be, and why?
What would you talk about? What would you learn from each other?
