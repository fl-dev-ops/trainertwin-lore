Non-native leaders

People often ask me why I have a podcast.

It's a fair question, as this is something extra, so there isn't a practical reason really.

I do it for myself.

➡️ To learn
➡️ To grow 
➡️ To have fun

And I'm even happier when I get a chance to meet my guest in person.

Yesterday I had the opportunity to spend some time with my friend Francesco Petroni.

He was just back from a business trip to Holland, ready to fly to the States to spend time with his team. 

What did I learn from him?

✅The art of networking.

✅How powerful is it to put yourself out there.

✅A genuine interest and love for people.

✅The importance of learning lessons from all your experiences in life.

A few months ago we chatted about the importance of building a strong network to excel in your career.

You can catch up our chat in the first comment 👇.

Have a great Sunday, everyone!
