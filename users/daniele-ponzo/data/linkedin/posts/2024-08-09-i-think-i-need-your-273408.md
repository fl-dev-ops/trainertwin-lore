I was recently chatting with a friend about software development methods.

We couldn’t quite agree on the best.

But I think it’s not that complicated. Let’s start with a quick description:

💠 Waterfall: 𝘙𝘪𝘨𝘪𝘥. "Because nothing ever changes halfway through a project, right?"

💠Agile: 𝘊𝘩𝘢𝘰𝘵𝘪𝘤. "Who needs a plan when you can just keep guessing?"

💠Kanban: 𝘍𝘭𝘰𝘸. "Where work flows so smoothly... if only everyone could stop adding tasks."

💠Scrum: 𝘚𝘱𝘳𝘪𝘯𝘵𝘴. "Perfect for when you enjoy sprinting in circles."

💠Lean Development: 𝘛𝘳𝘪𝘮. "Because cutting corners is practically a fitness regime."
