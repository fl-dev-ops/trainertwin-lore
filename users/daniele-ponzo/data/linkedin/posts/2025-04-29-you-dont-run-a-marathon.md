You don’t run a marathon on your first day 🏃‍♂️ .

If you decided to start running.
Would you lace up your shoes for the first time... and immediately try to run a marathon?

Of course not.

You’d start with a few small runs.

You’d be out of breath at first.
Your legs would ache.
You might want to quit 😓 .

But if you kept going

Running a little more each time,
Showing up even when it felt messy

💪 You’d get stronger.
💪 Your breathing would smooth out.
💪 Your body would adapt.

And what felt impossible at the start would feel natural over time.

Speaking English works the same way.

You don’t build fluency by waiting until you feel "ready."
You build it by moving, a little at a time.

Small steps.
Consistent practice.
Permission to be imperfect.

That’s how you build the strength and confidence to speak clearly in real conversations
In meetings, on calls, with clients.
