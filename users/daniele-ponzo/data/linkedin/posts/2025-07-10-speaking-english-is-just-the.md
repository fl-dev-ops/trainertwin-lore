Speaking English is just the start. Real understanding is harder.

You’ve got international clients.
Or a remote team spread across time zones.
Maybe you’ve led a meeting where everyone technically spoke English…
 …but no one seemed truly aligned.

🌍 Working across borders means navigating more than language.
It’s about culture, clarity, assumptions, and how we build trust.

I’ve been talking about this all week, and now I’d love to hear from you:

1️⃣ Understanding communication styles
Some people are direct... some take their time. Others say yes but truly mean no. On top of this we have pronunciation, intonation...

2️⃣ Expressing myself clearly in meetings or emails
You think you nailed it, after all we all speak English! But then people look at you and you realize something is missing...

3️⃣ Building trust and relationships remotely
Network, they said... build relationships, they said... but how are you supposed to do it? Face to face is already hard, imagine when you can't physically meet people!

4️⃣ Managing misunderstandings caused by language or tone
You want to be assertive, people think you're rude. You want to sound ironic, you get awkward silence in return. You want to confirm your point and your colleagues think you're asking a question...
