Tech was a lot about information, but not so much about communication. 

Throughout my years as a CTO, developer, and founder, I encountered this problem frequently. 

It wasn't the actual technical issues that were holding us back; it was the things in between the people that created Resistance. And communication is the alpha & omega of the process to fix it and, of course, turn it into a stable environment worth being a part of.

For this episode, I invited a person worth following: Daniele Ponzo. After I read his book "Easy English for IT" I wanted to talk with Daniele on stream so that we can share thoughts and learnings with you, the audience.
