𝗥𝗲𝘀𝗲𝗮𝗿𝗰𝗵 𝗮𝗻𝗱 𝗣𝗲𝗿𝘀𝗼𝗻𝗮𝗹𝗶𝘇𝗮𝘁𝗶𝗼𝗻
Understand the company's culture, the requirements, and how your skills align with their goals.
↳Tips to Improve: Review the job description, study the company’s website, prepare specific examples from your experience to address the job’s needs.
Language Examples:"I noticed your company recently launched [project/initiative]. My experience in [specific skill] could contribute to its success by [specific action]."

𝗔𝗻𝘀𝘄𝗲𝗿 𝗹𝗶𝗸𝗲 𝗮 𝗦𝗧𝗔𝗥
Use the STAR method (Situation, Task, Action, Result) to answer.
↳Tips to Improve: Write down a few scenarios for common interview topics (teamwork, conflict resolution, leadership) and practice storytelling, focusing on results.
↳ Language Examples: "In my previous role at [company], I faced [situation]. My task was to [task]. I took the following actions: [action]. As a result, [result]."

𝗔𝗰𝘁𝗶𝗼𝗻!
Prepare responses to common questions using action-oriented language.
↳Tips to Improve: List past projects, challenges, and successes and focus on how you added value and solved problems.
↳Language Examples:
"During [project], we faced a tight deadline. I coordinated daily stand-ups, identified bottlenecks, and delegated tasks strategically. We delivered the project on time, exceeding client expectations."

𝗗𝗲𝗺𝗼𝗻𝘀𝘁𝗿𝗮𝘁𝗲 𝗦𝗼𝗳𝘁 𝗦𝗸𝗶𝗹𝗹𝘀
Highlight your communication and problem-solving abilities.
↳Tips to Improve: Practice discussing soft skills with concrete examples.
↳Language Examples: "I believe strong communication is key to project success. For example, I implemented a streamlined reporting system that improved transparency among stakeholders."

𝗔𝘀𝗸 𝗧𝗵𝗼𝘀𝗲 𝗤𝘂𝗲𝘀𝘁𝗶𝗼𝗻𝘀
Asking thoughtful questions shows genuine interest and preparation.
↳Tips to Improve: Think of questions that reflect your research and enthusiasm for the role.
↳Language Examples: "How does this role contribute to the company’s overall strategy?"

𝗠𝗮𝗻𝗮𝗴𝗲 𝗡𝗲𝗿𝘃𝗲𝘀
↳ Focus on confident, clear communication and positive body language.
↳Tips to Improve: Practice speaking slowly and clearly and use power poses before the interview to boost confidence.

𝗣𝗿𝗲𝗽𝗮𝗿𝗲 𝗳𝗼𝗿 𝗧𝗲𝗰𝗵𝗻𝗶𝗰𝗮𝗹 𝗤𝘂𝗲𝘀𝘁𝗶𝗼𝗻𝘀
Showcase both your technical knowledge and how you approach problem-solving.
↳Tips to Improve: Review the technical skills listed in the job description and practice explaining technical concepts in simple terms.
↳Language Examples: "In my previous role, I optimized [technical process], which improved efficiency by [metric]."
