The ultimate checklist for your English presentation

Do you have to be on stage soon?

Selling, convincing and explaining?

Whether you are leading a team, working on a proposal or preparing a solution, you will have to present (sooner or later).

And if you’re not doing it today, you will have to.
If you want to improve your career.

But what do you do when you can’t find the right words, or your intonation is as exciting as watching paint dry🖌️🪣?

There is one strategy.

Simple BUT powerful:

BE PREPARED

📙  Know your material -> You’re the expert!
🗣  Practice -> then do it again. And again. And again.
👀  Engage with your audience -> They’re there for you.
🎯  Focus on key messages -> No wishy-washy approach.
😨  Manage nervousness -> You’re not fighting gladiators here.

How?

Check my carousel below 👇 for some tips.

P.S. what is the hardest part for you? Building a relationship with my audience was the scariest for me.
