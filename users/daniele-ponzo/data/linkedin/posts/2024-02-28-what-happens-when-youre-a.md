What happens when you’re a great professional and you work in a multinational company?

Chances are you will need to use English (sooner or later).

BayWa r.e. EMEA is a large company based in 33 countries. They think energy - how it is produced, stored and can be best used to enable the global renewable energy transition that is essential to the future of our planet. 🌍 

They are a leading global developer, service supplier, distributor and solutions provider and have brought over 6 GW of energy online and manage over 10.5 GW of assets. 
They are also an Independent Power Producer with an expanding energy trading business. ⚡ 

With offices in Germany, Italy, UK, Ireland, Sweden, Thailand…they really act on a truly global stage. 🌐 

I had the honour of working with part of their Italian team on a project to boost their Comprehension and Interaction skills.

They were all amazing professionals with a great experience in project management, contract management and on-field solutions for their clients.

And they all had one thing in common:

They felt anxious when they needed to express themselves in English in important situations at work.

So, here’s a summary of what we covered together:

🗣 Understanding FAST SPEECH.
💬 Dealing with different ACCENTS in English.
👩‍💼 The language and vocabulary to SOUND MORE PROFESSIONAL.
📈 Dealing with MEETINGS, PRESENTATIONS, and job INTERVIEWS.
💼 Role-playing how to handle the most common situations at work.

In one sentence: equipping them with the tools to navigate international interactions.

I’d like to thank everybody who was part of this journey.

It was quite intense, but the energy and engagement were amazing.

Also, a heartfelt thanks to Forma-Tec Srl-tec for coming to me and asking me to organize the training.

Here’s to taking your English confidence to the next level!

P.S. if you think your team needs extra support on their communication skills, feel free to reach out. 📩
