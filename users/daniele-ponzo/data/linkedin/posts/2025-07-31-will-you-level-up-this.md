Will you level up this summer… or switch off? 🌞

In the last few years, I have observed myself

Every summer, I start with the best intentions:

- First, I sort through all the 📚 books I didn’t have time to read

- Then, I review my Udemy profile to check the courses I started but never finished

- Next, I open my YouTube “Watch Later” list… so many videos waiting

- Then my Kindle… full of unread downloads

- Finally, my podcast list, where a few episodes haven’t even been started

I begin motivated and excited… but somewhere along the way, I get lost juggling too many things.

That’s why I have often ended up in the 𝗗𝗿𝗲𝗮𝗺𝗲𝗿-𝗝𝘂𝗴𝗴𝗹𝗲𝗿 trap, full of ideas but lacking the structure to keep momentum.

So, I’d like to ask you:
When it comes to learning during the summer, which is your identity?

1️⃣ The Sunbather: I switch off completely
☀️ I rest, recharge, and don’t plan to learn a thing.

2️⃣ The Dreamer: I want to, but don’t know where to start
🤔 Motivation is there, but I get stuck in “someday mode.”

3️⃣ The Juggler: I try, but struggle with consistency
🤹 I start with plans and resources, but life and distractions win.

4️⃣ The Strategist: I have a clear plan and stick to it
📅 My summer learning is structured, focused, and realistic.

You know, my goal is to become “The Strategist”

It sounds like a Liam Neeson movie, but it could actually help you stay focused
And start off on the right foot in September

So, which summer learning mode are you in right now? Vote and let’s see which team wins! 👇
(and if you're somewhere in between, let me know in the comments)
