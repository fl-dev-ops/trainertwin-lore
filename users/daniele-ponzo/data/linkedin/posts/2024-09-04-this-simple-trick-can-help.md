This simple trick can help you with your English meetings.

You’ve probably experienced this a million times.

You go into a meeting, and you know you will have to contribute.

You prepare your speech, your lines, your phrases.

But the reality is different.

Once they start talking, everything seems faster, you start sweating, panicking, dreading the moment you will have to speak 😱 .

They go around, everybody is giving their contribution and then it’s your turn.

Suddenly you can’t find your words, you get confused, you’re thinking what to say next and lose track of your message.

You feel uncomfortable, hesitant, shy 😞 .

This is how I used to feel in my meetings.

BUT it doesn’t have to be like that.

The first two points of my H.A.C.K. strategy are:

HONE -> refine your vocabulary
ACTION. -> put yourself out there

In the video below I’m sharing a simple trick to approach this logically.

📌 Hone: use the “bullet points” approach. Break your speech down into manageable bits and sequence them (First, then, finally).

🗣 Action: practice your speech using the new approach until you get clear on what you want to say and you feel confident.

And don’t forget to COLLABORATE (seeking feedback) and KUDOS (give yourself a pat on the shoulder for doing it).
