If I were to start learning English today, here’s what I’d do differently.

Back in 2004 I got my first job in a large IT American multinational company.
I had mixed feelings about that.

I was excited, I was afraid and I would fluctuate from days of confidence to days where imposter syndrome was driving me. 😬 

And I was convinced I needed perfect English before I could apply for interesting positions.

So I felt a bit shy when new opportunities were available.

I concentrated on getting Language certifications before focusing on my career.

Fast forward to 2014, I was leading an international Team of experienced IT professionals. 📈 

I was training new starters and coaching them on the job.

And I was meeting clients regularly to design and implement new solutions.
So, what happened in the meantime?

➡ I started learning from the great people who surrounded me.

➡I developed my speaking and presentation skills, so I could talk confidently in those business situations.

➡I started to listen more, so I could learn from my managers and my peers.

➡And I put myself out there, took a more active role in meetings and social events to make my work more visible.

Today I’m applying the same principles to my language coaching.

I’ve seen them working for me.

I believe everybody can benefit from a method focused on skills rather than general language.
