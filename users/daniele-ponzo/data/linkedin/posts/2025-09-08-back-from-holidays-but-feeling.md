Back from holidays… but feeling the blues?

It’s Monday, the first full week back after the summer break.

Yes, I’m talking about the adults…

For many, this shift from beaches and mountains back to screens and meetings comes with a heavy feeling: the post-holiday blues.

Sometimes, it’s normal. Just the shock of routine changing from freedom to deadlines. 
A few days in, and you’re back in the flow.

But other times, that “blues” is a signal worth listening to.
👉 Is this really where I want to be?
👉 Am I passionate about what I’m doing?
👉 Can I still talk about mission when I describe my work?

These aren’t easy questions. But they matter, especially as the world of work keeps evolving. 

So, just check... is this you?
🔺 You feel a constant heaviness or dread on Sunday evening.
🔺 You’re more drained than energized after a typical workday.
🔺 You find yourself counting hours instead of losing track in flow.
🔺 You hesitate when someone asks: “Do you love what you do?”
🔺 You catch yourself daydreaming about a different path.

If it is, you might be feeling like Frank in the video below...

For previous generations, finding meaning, purpose, and passion at work often felt like a luxury. 
But I believe there’s been a shift.

And, more and more people are simply not willing to give their passions up.

And finding purpose at work is becoming essential.
(A 2024 Deloitte survey found that more than 85% of Millennials and Gen Z think having a sense of purpose at work is key for job happiness and wellbeing).

So let me ask you: what would change in your life if your work truly felt like your mission?
