Confidence doesn’t sound “perfect.” It sounds like you.

A lot of professionals think confidence means speaking fast, using big words, or sounding more “native.”

But here’s what I’ve seen (and lived):

The more you try to sound impressive, the less connected you feel to your words.
You overthink. You translate. You lose yourself in the performance.

And guess what? People feel that:
❌ Lack of trust
❌ Weak connections
❌ Missed career opportunities

And you feel that:
❌ Fear
❌ Frustration
❌ Uncertainty

Real confidence isn’t about how you sound.
It’s about how present you are when you speak.

Even a simple sentence, spoken with calm and clarity, has more power than five jargon-filled paragraphs delivered with doubt.

So if you’ve been waiting to sound a certain way before you speak up…
What if the goal isn’t to sound perfect, but to sound like yourself?

Spotless accent, flawless grammar, perfect vocabulary don’t give you confidence
(spoiler: they don’t even exist) ⬅️
