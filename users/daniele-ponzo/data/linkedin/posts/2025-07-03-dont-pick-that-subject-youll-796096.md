"Don't pick that subject, you'll never find a job".

Sound familiar?

As a kid, as a teenager and as a young adult, every time I showed an interest in something, someone would come up and repeat that phrase to me.

"I love drawing, I want to study comics art"
"Don't pick that subject, you'll never find a job".

"I'm really keen on languages, I might get into language studies"
"Don't pick that subject, you'll never find a job".

"Communication. Systems, feedback loops, virtual communication".
"Don't pick that subject, you'll never find a job".

"People! Social Psychology, Sociology, how group and teams work".
"Don't pick that subject, you'll never find a job".

Can you feel the negative mantra in action?

But you know what? I actually ended up studying them all (talking about being stubborn...).

It isn't easy when people do recognise the value in what you do.

But I used all of them throughout my career. 

And I still use those skills in the job I do.

🔹Languages: the learning mechanism 
🔹Communication: because there's more than words
🔹Sociology: we don't live in a bubble and we need to get out there.
🔹Drawing: creativity, for my notes and for my content.

Over to you, now: 

did you pick your studies for the job opportunities? 

Are you working in the same field you studied?

Let me know below 👇 .
