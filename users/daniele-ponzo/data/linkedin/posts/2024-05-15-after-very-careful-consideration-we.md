“After very careful consideration, we regret that, on this occasion you have not been successful with your application”.

What’s your turning point?

For me, it was rejection feedback after a job interview.

Many years ago.

Sometimes we are convinced our career is linear⬆️.

Entry, Junior, senior, manager, director…

But the reality is different.

That was an eye opener for me👀.

It gave me an idea of some of my weaknesses.

But more than anything, it was an opportunity to look inside myself and see where I really wanted to go.

And I discovered that I wasn’t so good at “sales and commercial”.

(the reason why I was rejected).

But I had a knack for communicating and helping people🤝.

So, that negative feedback allowed me to reshape my career.

Get new responsibilities in Learning and Development.

Obtain new roles in Team management.

Coordinate projects with senior professionals.

And eventually take the leap and become a full-time language coach.

So, how do I feel today?

Recently, one of my former managers told me:

“Daniele - you were always a good communicator- and have become a great communicator with practice and experience”.

And this is exactly how I feel.

Proud.

That I could learn from my failures.

That I got past my mistakes

That I’m doing something I truly love.

And that I get to share it all with amazing professionals who want to find their voice and excel in their career.

Curious to know, what was your turning point?
