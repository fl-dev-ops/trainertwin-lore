The quickest way to sound more professional in English.

 
Do you want to have an effective and impactful communication?

I bet you do.

A quick strategy is to use professional-sounding verbs.

For example: Instead of saying "We did the project," say "We executed the project".

Professional-sounding verbs help us on:

👌Clarity and Precision, so you reduce the risk of misinterpretation.

👩‍💼Credibility and Authority, so your statements are more convincing.

🌟Engagement and Impact, so you emphasize actions, and create a sense of urgency.

👏Memorability, so your audience will remember you.

📈Professional Image, so you show your commitment to effective communication.
