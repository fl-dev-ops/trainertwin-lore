"Do you know how smart I am in my native language?"

Every international professional has had this moment.

You know exactly what you want to say.

In your head, it's clear.

Logical.

Even persuasive.

Then the meeting starts.

And suddenly your brain is trying to:

• solve the technical problem
• translate your thoughts into English
• remember the right vocabulary
• sound professional

...all at the same time.

No wonder your mind goes blank.

It isn't because your English is bad.

It's because your brain is overloaded.

I see this all the time with software engineers, architects and tech leaders.

They spend too much mental energy building the perfect sentence.

Instead, try this.

Don't start with the sentence.

Start with one idea.

Then add one reason.

If you need to, finish with one example.

That's enough.

Simple doesn't make you sound less intelligent.

It frees up your brain to think.

Ironically, that's what usually makes you sound more senior.

So the next time you catch yourself translating an entire paragraph in your head...
Remember Gloria. 😄

She wasn't struggling because she wasn't smart.

She was trying to run too many processes at once.

And that's a bug you can fix.

💬 Have you ever finished a meeting thinking:

"I knew exactly what I wanted to say... just not in English."
