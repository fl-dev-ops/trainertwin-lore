The ultimate guide for international travellers

Are you travelling around this summer ⛱️?

Do you fear you're missing the key phrases to communicate with the locals?

Don't worry, I've got you covered.

Here is what to say to impress your hosts:

Italy: I'd like some fruit on my pizza, please.

England: It's coming home.

Portugal: Congratulations, you speak very good Brazilian.

Ireland: I like it here in the UK.

Germany: Tell me again, why does your language sound so harsh?

Spain: Can we have a party? Let's have a party. Yeah, I know you want to party with me right now, let's party. Fiesta!

Greece: Hola, hola I gusta el mediterraneo. I want ketchup on my fish!

Holland: Your language is just a dialect of German, correct?

Sweden: I know your country, I go to IKEA every week.

Norway: Hey guys, I love black metal, listen to me.

Denmark: Yeah, sure, everything is great here, but where is the sun?

Switzerland: My chocolate watch is not working anymore. Give me my money back!

Poland: I actually prefer German beer.

Austria: I've heard kangaroos live here. Can you show me kangaroos?

USA: this is not the real pizza!

Czech Republic: without 2 meters of snow covering everything, your country is not really romantic enough.

India: Can you make chow mein? I love your chow mein!

Romania: I know why you said that, it's because of Dracula, right?

France: Anything in English.

P.S. they all come (more or less) from real life experiences, so feel free to continue the list.
