Working in IT?

Don’t make my mistake ⬇ .

When we start in a new role it’s common to end up in the “work-work-work” vicious circle:

☀  I’ll work on my tasks and demonstrate I have what it takes.
⛅  I’ll work some more to impress my boss and my colleagues.
🌧  If I keep doing it, opportunities will come.

It’s plain to see that there’s something wrong here.

It’s a highway to "burnout" ⛈ .

And more than that.

You are not in control of your professional life and your career.

You’re always waiting for someone to notice you.

And to tell you how good you are.

So, what can we do?

Have a look at the video below for an alternative approach which has worked for me (and has helped me shape my career) 🌞 .
