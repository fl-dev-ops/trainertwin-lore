Working with a group is surely different than working 1 on 1.


This is why I particularly appreciate this feedback from one of my clients.

Ms G. was part of one of my mini groups (3 people).

She had a good level of English, but she was asked to step up and be more active in international calls.

Working in a group, she was afraid she would not receive 100% focus and dedication.

But her company opted for group lessons rather than 1 on 1. 

So, we worked together to:

✅ Strengthen business vocabulary.
✅ Role-play typical work-related situations (meetings, calls…).
✅ Refine the useful grammar to sound more professional.

At the end of the Program, she was much more confident and actually enjoyed the group dynamics and the customized activities we did together.

And she had overcome her fears and uncertainties.

So, I believe working with a group is about empathizing and connecting with the members.

And try to give each one of them a unique experience.

Even though they might have slightly different needs and challenges.
