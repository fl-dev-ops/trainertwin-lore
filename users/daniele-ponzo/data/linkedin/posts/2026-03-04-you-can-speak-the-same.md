You can speak the same language…
and still misunderstand each other.

Not because of grammar.
But because of culture.
Bias.
Different assumptions about what things mean.

In this episode of The Non-Native Leader - Podcast, I speak with Gülbin Özdemir, award-winning educator and international speaker.

She shares something powerful:

How small communication gaps can turn into offence, distance... even hostility.

And how, in one unexpected moment, people from different countries simply…

laughed.
danced.
shared food.
connected.

That moment changed everything for her.

She realized that communication is the most powerful way to bring people together.

In international environments (especially in tech) we often focus on:

• perfection
• hierarchy
• authority
• performance

But connection is what sustains collaboration.

And connection requires awareness.

In this conversation, we talk about:

– Culture shock (the real kind, not the textbook definition)
– Feeling “other” in international spaces
– The courage it takes to keep showing up
– Why colourful environments expand who we become

If you work internationally, this will likely resonate.

Because at some point, we’ve all felt:

misunderstood
out of place
or silently judged

And yet, we keep choosing to try.

🎙️ The full episode is in the comments.

I’m curious:
Have you ever experienced a moment where cultural misunderstanding shifted into connection?
