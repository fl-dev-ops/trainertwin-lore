“You’ll never go back and say: we had a fantastic Excel spreadsheet”

Thanks everyone for joining us live yesterday

Liam (Tara Tours Ireland) and I had a great time in our live event: 

“𝗧𝗵𝗲 𝗟𝗮𝗻𝗴𝘂𝗮𝗴𝗲 𝗼𝗳 𝗕𝘂𝘀𝗶𝗻𝗲𝘀𝘀: 𝗜𝗿𝗲𝗹𝗮𝗻𝗱 & 𝗧𝗲𝗰𝗵”

🔅 We shared some funny stories about idioms and misunderstandings in the office
“you’re just learning the ropes, so don’t worry about that”

🔅We went through the challenges non-native speakers (including the Irish) have when adapting to corporate jargon:
“we’ll have to circle back on this. Yeah, we might need to pivot”

🔅We talked about the unique Irish approach to small talk
“How are you?” “howya?” “What’s the story?”

And finally, we described what people can expect when visiting one of the top destinations for IT, Tech and culture.

In one sentence?
It was good ‘craic’!

And here's the conclusion:

Tech, IT, corporate jargon… you’ll never go back and say: "we had a fantastic Excel spreadsheet".
What you will remember are the experiences, the people and the connections you made ☘️ .
