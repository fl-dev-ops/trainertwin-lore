The easiest way to sound more professional in English⬇️

As an IT Team leader I experienced ups and downs with report writing, emails and client presentations.

There is one simple way to instantly make your English more formal and professional👩‍💼:

Use the Passive.

Here are 3 examples👇:

"𝑻𝒉𝒆 𝒓𝒆𝒑𝒐𝒓𝒕 𝒘𝒂𝒔 𝒑𝒓𝒆𝒑𝒂𝒓𝒆𝒅 𝒃𝒚 𝒐𝒖𝒓 𝒕𝒆𝒂𝒎 𝒐𝒇 𝒆𝒙𝒑𝒆𝒓𝒕𝒔."

✅the emphasis is placed on the action of preparing the report.

This creates a more formal tone, allowing the focus to be on the report itself. 

"𝑻𝒉𝒆 𝒅𝒆𝒄𝒊𝒔𝒊𝒐𝒏 𝒘𝒂𝒔 𝒎𝒂𝒅𝒆 𝒃𝒂𝒔𝒆𝒅 𝒐𝒏 𝒄𝒂𝒓𝒆𝒇𝒖𝒍 𝒂𝒏𝒂𝒍𝒚𝒔𝒊𝒔."

✅We highlight the decision itself rather than specifying who made the decision. 

"𝑻𝒉𝒆 𝒑𝒓𝒐𝒋𝒆𝒄𝒕 𝒘𝒊𝒍𝒍 𝒃𝒆 𝒄𝒐𝒎𝒑𝒍𝒆𝒕𝒆𝒅 𝒃𝒚 𝒕𝒉𝒆 𝒆𝒏𝒅 𝒐𝒇 𝒕𝒉𝒆 𝒎𝒐𝒏𝒕𝒉."

✅The attention is placed on the completion of the project and not on who will be responsible for completing it.

In these examples we use the passive to maintain a professional and formal tone by emphasizing the action, object, or result.

And we don't mention the subject performing the action.

This can lend an air of objectivity, formality, and professionalism to written and spoken communication.

P.S.  Remember⚠️

Use the passive sensibly: always remember to consider the context and the desired effect.
