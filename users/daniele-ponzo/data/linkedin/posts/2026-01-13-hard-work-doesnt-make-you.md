Hard work doesn’t make you visible.
Communication does.

Talking more? No.
Being intentional? Yes!

𝟭. 𝗙𝗼𝗰𝘂𝘀 𝗼𝗻 𝘆𝗼𝘂𝗿 𝘀𝘁𝗿𝗲𝗻𝗴𝘁𝗵𝘀.
➜ Reflect on what makes you unique (not what the others expect of you). Use your style. At the beginning, prioritize quality over quantity (and speed).
👉 "I believe in taking the time to ensure accuracy and precision in my work, which ultimately leads to better outcomes."

𝟮. 𝗥𝗲𝗳𝗶𝗻𝗲 𝘆𝗼𝘂𝗿 𝗹𝗮𝗻𝗴𝘂𝗮𝗴𝗲.
➜ Learn how to be effective but professional at the same time. Be patient.
👉 “FIRST we’ll discuss the upgrade, THEN we’ll submit it for approval, FINALLY, we’ll implement it”
“Could you 𝗽𝗹𝗲𝗮𝘀𝗲 help me with my report?”

𝟯. 𝗙𝗶𝗻𝗱 𝗮 𝗺𝗲𝗻𝘁𝗼𝗿.
➜ Seek guidance, support and valuable feedback. Learn from the best.
👉 “I'm really impressed by your expertise. Could I ask you a few questions to clarify this process ands maybe get a few extra ideas on my personal development?”

𝟰. 𝗕𝘂𝗶𝗹𝗱 𝗮 𝗻𝗲𝘁𝘄𝗼𝗿𝗸.
➜ Be open with your peers, colleagues and management. Join virtual (social network) and real networks. Make the most of company events and use “small talk”.
👉 "Hi there! How are you finding the event so far? I noticed your name badge - are you part of the Finance department? Have you been to similar events before?"

𝟱. 𝗕𝗲 𝗮𝗰𝘁𝗶𝘃𝗲 𝗶𝗻 𝘁𝗲𝗮𝗺 𝗺𝗲𝗲𝘁𝗶𝗻𝗴𝘀.
➜ Ask questions, volunteer to present part of the agenda, offer to take notes, contribute with well-thought questions.
👉 “I've been thinking about our current project, and I believe we could improve the UX integrating the new layout. What are your thoughts on this?"

𝟲. 𝗠𝗮𝗸𝗲 𝘁𝗵𝗲 𝗺𝗼𝘀𝘁 𝗼𝗳 𝘆𝗼𝘂𝗿 𝟭 𝘁𝗼 𝟭’𝘀
➜ Listen to and use the feedback you receive. Make sure you share your expectations and growth plan with your leader. If you keep silent, they won’t know.
👉 “Could we schedule some time to discuss potential opportunities for development and how I can best align my goals with the team's objectives?"

𝟳. 𝗠𝗮𝗸𝗲 𝘆𝗼𝘂𝗿 𝘄𝗼𝗿𝗸 𝘃𝗶𝘀𝗶𝗯𝗹𝗲.
➜ It’s not only about doing the work. But also making sure people know. Talk & write about it. Ask for 5 minutes in your next team meeting, write a newsletter, email your leader asking for advice.
👉 "I wanted to share an update on the project that I've been working on. The first test was successful, and I believe we’re on the right track."

𝟴. 𝗧𝗮𝗸𝗲 𝗰𝗮𝗿𝗲 𝗼𝗳 𝘆𝗼𝘂𝗿𝘀𝗲𝗹𝗳.
➜ Stay active both mentally and physically. Mind your own wellbeing so it reflects at work.
👉 “I've been implementing deep breathing and short breaks to help manage stress levels during busy periods, and I've found it really beneficial."

Which one of these 8 is hardest for you right now? 👇
