Perfect English didn’t get me promoted.

Speaking up did.

For years, I was obsessed with perfection.
Perfect grammar.
Perfect pronunciation.
Perfect sentences.
Perfect everything.

I thought that to be respected in an international environment, my English had to match the level of my professional preparation.

I had studied.
I worked hard.
I knew I was good at my job.

So my thinking was simple:
👉 “If my English isn’t perfect, people will think I’m not good enough.”

And because of that belief, I held back.
I stayed quiet.
I waited for the “right moment.”
I prepared obsessively… but spoke rarely.

Meanwhile?
People around me (with much less perfect English) were:
✔ getting interesting projects
✔ being invited to international meetings
✔ presenting to clients
✔ moving into leadership roles

Not because they were better than me.
But because they communicated.

They weren’t held hostage by ego.
They didn’t care if a sentence wasn’t perfect.
They focused on clarity, presence, and visibility, not perfection.

And that’s when it hit me:
✨ My ego was holding my career back more than my English ever did.
I wanted to sound as “perfect” as I believed my professional skills were.

But opportunities don’t go to the people who sound perfect.
They go to the people who show up.

So I let go of my ego.
I re-learned how to communicate… mistakes, flaws, imperfect sentences included.

I spoke more.
I presented more.
I engaged more.
And everything changed.

More responsibility.
More trust.
More visibility.
More opportunities.

Your English doesn’t need to be perfect.
Your communication needs to be present.

If you want to grow, influence, lead, or go international…
Start speaking up, exactly as you are today.
That’s the only path to where you want to be.
