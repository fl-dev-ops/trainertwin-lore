Your LinkedIn profile is working harder than your CV

Let’s face it: your next job opportunity might come from halfway around the world, not just your own city.

Why LinkedIn matters:
𝗚𝗹𝗼𝗯𝗮𝗹 𝗿𝗲𝗮𝗰𝗵: LinkedIn has over 1 billion members across 200+ countries, making it the prime stage for international visibility. 

𝗥𝗲𝗰𝗿𝘂𝗶𝘁𝗲𝗿 𝗵𝗲𝗮𝘃𝘆 𝗵𝗶𝘁𝘁𝗲𝗿: A stunning 72% of recruiters use LinkedIn to find talent. 

𝗝𝗼𝗯 𝗺𝗮𝗴𝗻𝗲𝘁: Every week, about 65 million people search for jobs on the platform. 

So what can you do to stand out, especially as a tech professional looking to attract global opportunities?

Best practices for an international-ready LinkedIn profile:

▶️ Headline & Summary – Go beyond your position. Highlight your expertise, your comfort with English communication, your international mindset.

▶️ Featured Section – Showcase your work: GitHub repos, article links, presentations… anything that speaks globally.

▶️ Skills & Endorsements – Keep them current. Profiles with multiple skill endorsements attract 17× more recruiter views. 

▶️ Post & Engage – Share insights, projects or commentary in English regularly. Engagement on LinkedIn is up by 44% YoY, especially through meaningful comments. 

▶️ Network Smartly – Actively connect and interact with peers and influencers internationally. Stronger networks lead to more recruiter outreach and greater career progression. 

Leadership, visibility, and global opportunity start with a strong LinkedIn presence, even more so if English isn’t your first language.
