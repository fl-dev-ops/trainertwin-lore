We are all superheroes🦸

And as you know, “𝗪𝗶𝘁𝗵 𝗴𝗿𝗲𝗮𝘁 𝗽𝗼𝘄𝗲𝗿 𝗰𝗼𝗺𝗲𝘀 𝗴𝗿𝗲𝗮𝘁 𝗿𝗲𝘀𝗽𝗼𝗻𝘀𝗶𝗯𝗶𝗹𝗶𝘁𝘆”.

But then, bugs come our way🐞.
1, 10, 100 of them…

So, how do you fight back?

You have 2 options:
Fight or flight.

We are fighters, right? Well, here is how to deal with bugs:

👨‍💻 JavaScript Developer: Opens Google → "Ah yes, Stack Overflow... Wait, why are there 17 different solutions?" 

⚙️ Backend Developer: "It works fine on my local. Must be a frontend problem. Or a database issue. Or... DevOps' fault!" 

🌐 Web Developer: " It's fine in Chrome. Just tell the user to switch browsers."

📞 Technical Support: "Have you tried turning it off and on again? No? Alright, let me remotely break it further." 

🔍 Quality Assurance: "I clicked one button and found 12 critical bugs. What do you mean 'we're releasing tomorrow'?" 

💾 C++ Developer: "Why does this bug only appear when I compile with optimizations? Also, why is my debugger crashing?" 

🚀 Project Manager: "So... can we fix it AND add a new feature before the deadline? Shouldn't take long, right?" 

🔐 Cybersecurity Manager: "WHO OPENED THIS EMAIL ATTACHMENT? EVERYONE, UNPLUG YOUR MACHINES. WE'RE GOING OFFLINE!"
 
📢 Me: "No, it’s 'MAN-age-ment,' not 'mehnage-ment.' Yes, I know English is weird. No, I don’t make the rules." 

Remember: "This Is The Fight Of Our Lives. And We're Going To Win. Whatever It Takes."

I’m curious: who are you in this story? 👇
