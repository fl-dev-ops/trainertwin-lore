You don’t speak better by knowing more.
You speak better by speaking more.

A lot of people wait.

They wait until their grammar is perfect.
Until they find the “right” word.
Until they feel ready.

They wait… and wait… and the conversation moves on without them.

And the painful part?

They often know more English than they think.

They just don’t use it.

Because 𝗽𝗲𝗿𝗳𝗲𝗰𝘁𝗶𝗼𝗻𝗶𝘀𝗺 is sneaky.

It tells you:
🧠 “Wait a bit longer.”
🧠 “You need more practice first.”
🧠 “You’re not ready yet.”

But the truth is different:

There is no perfect English speaker.
Not even among natives.
Not even the most important native👑

People make mistakes, restart sentences, forget words… and keep going.

Do you think confidence comes from mastering every grammar rule?
Think again.

Confidence comes from showing up and using what you already know.

Repetition → Practice → Experience → 𝗖𝗼𝗻𝗳𝗶𝗱𝗲𝗻𝗰𝗲

In one word?

Action!

So, if you've been stuck in preparation mode, just know:

The “perfect moment” isn’t coming.
But your next opportunity is already here.
Will you take it?
