Is it possible for Introvert IT leaders to feel confident and make an impact?
 
 
Some years ago, I had my answer.
 
I have always been an introvert.
 
But my goal was simple ⬇️

Coach and inspire people.

And do the job I love.

So, today I feel thankful I pushed on,

And thankful I followed my passion,

But how do you break free from hesitation and embrace confident English speaking as an introvert?
 
First of all, you should stop doing these things:

❌ Overthinking: 
Sometimes, the most impactful words come naturally.

❌ Rushing: 
Speak at your pace. It's about clarity, not speed.

❌ Fearing Mistakes: 
Mistakes are stepping stones to mastery. Learn from them.

❌ Multitasking:
Give conversations your full attention. It's a sign of respect and aids comprehension.

❌ Comparing Yourself: 
Your introvert strengths are your unique advantage. Embrace them!
 
Instead, you should focus on the following:
 
👉 Prepare Thoroughly: 
Boost your confidence by arming yourself with knowledge and questions in advance.

👉 Listen Actively: 
Embrace your knack for attentive listening, it's your secret weapon in understanding and responding effectively.

👉 Take Small Steps: 
Confidence grows with each contribution. Start small, and you'll find your voice.

👉 Use Visual Aids: 
Leverage visuals to complement your message, allowing you to shine even brighter.

👉 Practice Deep Breathing: 
When nerves strike, remember to take a moment to breathe deeply and centre yourself.
 
P.S. Remember, it's not about transforming into an extrovert but harnessing your introvert strengths to communicate effectively in the IT leadership realm.
