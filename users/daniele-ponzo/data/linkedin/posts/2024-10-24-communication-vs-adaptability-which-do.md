⁉️ Communication vs. Adaptability: Which do you struggle with?

Mastering soft skills (superhero skills) in IT can be tough. You have to collaborate with team members, and present technical concepts to non-technical stakeholders.

And at the same time, you must be flexible and open to learning and adapting to new programming languages, tools, and methodologies.

All in a different language.

Not an easy task, if you ask me 😬 .

But without communication, collaboration and adaptability, you’ll hit the global wall🌐🧱 .

Missed opportunities, career stagnation, isolation.

Luckily, it DOESN’T have to be that way.

Like all skills, superhero skills can be learned.

With dedication and commitment.

So, I’m curious. What’s the biggest challenge for you?
 
📊 Vote in the poll and let’s chat in the comments.

I’ll be happy to share some ideas or just offer a shoulder to cry on.

Ok, maybe we don’t share tears on LinkedIn…but you know what I mean, don’t you?  🤜🤛

P.S. I always give myself small goals when it comes to communication, like “go to an event and talk to 2 new people before leaving” .
