The quickest way to sound more professional in English.

Spoiler: it's not about dark-coloured suits and tie or button-down shirts.👩‍💼

I’m talking about PRONUNCIATION

If you are working for a global team, chances are you have to interact with people coming from different parts of the world.

And you’re using English to talk to them.

One essential thing?

A clear and neutral pronunciation, to make sure everybody understands you.

It doesn’t matter if you have different accents.

Pronunciation is about clear articulation. Two people with different accents can still pronounce words clearly and be understood globally.

Here's how you can practice pronunciation:

𝗦𝗵𝗮𝗱𝗼𝘄 𝗧𝗲𝗰𝗵 𝗧𝗮𝗹𝗸 𝗩𝗶𝗱𝗲𝗼𝘀 🖥
↳Find tech talks, pause after each sentence, and repeat in the exact tone and speed. 
↳This is powerful for mimicking the rhythm and cadence of technical conversations.

𝗨𝘀𝗲 𝗧𝗼𝗻𝗴𝘂𝗲 𝗧𝘄𝗶𝘀𝘁𝗲𝗿𝘀 👅
↳Start a standup meeting with a quick, tech-focused tongue twister like, “She sells C++ shell scripts by the server.” 
↳Warm up your mouth muscles and catch common pronunciation slips.

𝗟𝗶𝘀𝘁𝗲𝗻 𝗶𝗻 𝗦𝗹𝗼𝘄 𝗠𝗼𝘁𝗶𝗼𝗻 👂 
↳Use YouTube to slow down technical videos. Practice imitating the slow speech before gradually increasing speed, 
↳Helps you catch nuances in pronunciation.

𝗥𝗲𝗰𝗼𝗿𝗱 𝗮𝗻𝗱 𝗥𝗲-𝗹𝗶𝘀𝘁𝗲𝗻 ⏺️ 
↳Record yourself reading some technical content and play it back. 
↳Hearing your own voice is surprising, and it helps identify mispronunciations you might not notice otherwise.

𝗔𝗱𝗼𝗽𝘁 𝘁𝗵𝗲 ‘𝗜𝗻𝘁𝗲𝗻𝘁𝗶𝗼𝗻𝗮𝗹 𝗠𝗶𝘀𝗽𝗿𝗼𝗻𝘂𝗻𝗰𝗶𝗮𝘁𝗶𝗼𝗻’ 𝗧𝗲𝗰𝗵𝗻𝗶𝗾𝘂𝗲 😮 
↳Mispronounce difficult words to exaggerate certain sounds (like saying "pro-NUN-see-A-shun") and then pronounce them correctly. 
↳“Resets” your brain, making the accurate pronunciation stand out more.
