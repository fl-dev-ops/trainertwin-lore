As I walked past the building on this lovely summer morning, I thought about my personal #LinkedIn journey.

Showing up regularly, learning, sharing my view.

But what has really made a difference for me, is the new connections I’ve made.

Chats, comments, conversations, laughs, advice…I’ve had them all. It’s not only about professional relationships, but also about sharing our journeys, our challenges and our rewards.

The relationships you build can feel so strong, that sometimes I forget it’s all virtual. It’s like we regularly meet for a coffee or a drink ☕ .

This is how strong a community can be.

So, thanks to LinkedIn I could meet some amazing professionals (and great human beings):

Francesco Mandolini. Music, copywriting and humour. Do I need to add anything else?

Robbie Kane. His passion in helping teachers get what they deserve is truly inspiring.

Lorena Ignazzi. She’s an amazing business consultant. I love how she goes the extra mile and invites her connections to have a “virtual” coffee together.

Maryia Tuleika. She has true passion for learning, for sharing and for keeping the tech world “human” in every possible sense.

Catherine McDonald. If you are looking for advice on career, leadership and emotional intelligence, look no further. 

Pavel Polcr. A software engineer who likes to get out of his comfort zone and share his personal stories online.

David Cabezas. A teacher. A colleague. Always a source of great inspiration.

Jonni Albertin. A true supporter, I can’t thank him enough (and I love his regular surveys).

So, thank you all for being part of my journey here!
