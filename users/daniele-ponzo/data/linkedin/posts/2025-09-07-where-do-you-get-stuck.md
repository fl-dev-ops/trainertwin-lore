Where do you get stuck when speaking English? 🤔

You have good grammar (you’ve studied English back in school).
Your vocabulary is ok (you can use the technical terms no problem).

But you still hesitate when you have to speak in your team meetings.
And you think you’re not making an impact when presenting. 

This is about the way you 𝗱𝗲𝗹𝗶𝘃𝗲𝗿 𝘆𝗼𝘂𝗿 𝗺𝗲𝘀𝘀𝗮𝗴𝗲 
and that’s where many IT & Tech pros feel stuck.

I’m curious… which of these challenges feels most true for you?

🧩 𝗟𝗼𝗴𝗶𝗰𝗮𝗹 𝗳𝗹𝗼𝘄 — You start translating in your head, lose your train of thought, and end up speaking in short, broken sentences. 
The result? People focus on your struggle, not your ideas.

🎵 𝗜𝗻𝘁𝗼𝗻𝗮𝘁𝗶𝗼𝗻 — You speak with the same rhythm all the time, sounding robotic or flat. Without natural pauses or emphasis, your message loses power and people tune out.

📖 𝗦𝘁𝗼𝗿𝘆𝘁𝗲𝗹𝗹𝗶𝗻𝗴 — You only share the facts, without details or emphasis. Colleagues don’t remember your points, and your presentations lack the spark that makes people listen.

💁 𝗕𝗼𝗱𝘆 𝗹𝗮𝗻𝗴𝘂𝗮𝗴𝗲 — You avoid eye contact, keep your hands frozen, or look nervous. Instead of owning the room, you look unsure… even if your ideas are great.

So, where do you want to grow most?
