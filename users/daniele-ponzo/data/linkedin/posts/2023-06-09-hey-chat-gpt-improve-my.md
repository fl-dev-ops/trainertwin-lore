Hey Chat GPT, improve my English...

 
I am exploiting the hype around this viral ad to share my opinion.

I believe language learning is very similar to putting up a building.

👷‍♂️You start with the foundations.

🏗You build up on your foundations and erect your structure.

🏚Once you have a structure, you’re ready to put the walls and close the gaps.

🏠Finally, you perfect your building with details, colours and you customize it.
 
Can AI do this for us? Well, I don’t think so. It can surely help and support us in the planning stage or in the revision phase…
 
But ultimately our skills are irreplaceable.
 
We need dedication, constancy, and human support to progress in language learning.

My 2 cents on the “competition” between Humans and AI. 👩🤖
