“I can’t find the words, I don’t feel confident, so I will keep my ideas for myself”.

“I’m always thinking about what to say next, so I get lost in the conversation”.

I can feel the pain and the frustration when I speak to my clients.

One lady I was working with had recently been promoted and she was sitting in international meetings.

Director level 👩‍💼 .

Her English was good, but she felt she couldn’t speak up in her meetings and so she was missing the opportunity to contribute and get noticed.

The truth?

I believe this had not much to do with her language level. It was more a mental block.

I mean, she was new in the role, the other participants came from all over Europe, she had not been formally introduced…it was intimidating.

How did we work together?

𝐅𝐢𝐫𝐬𝐭, we had to recognize the real issue: senior people, speaking in English with different accents and different intonation, at the same time. 
It feels hard even when I write it.
➡ Identify the problem

𝐓𝐡𝐞𝐧, we broke down the big task into manageable bits. I suggested contacting these people individually for a quick chat or virtual coffee. 
She had the role, so they couldn’t say no. 
➡ Remove the noise and speak more freely

𝐀𝐟𝐭𝐞𝐫 𝐭𝐡𝐚𝐭, I asked her to write some notes and summarize the individual calls to highlight her contributions and her feelings.
➡ Feedback loops

𝐅𝐢𝐧𝐚𝐥𝐥𝐲, we used her notes to highlight her progress and her wins to build up confidence and prepare for the next general meetings, when these people would be there together again.
➡ Celebrate your milestones

The result?

She was way more relaxed, and she kept one important thing in mind all the time:

These people were human too and all they were trying to do was communicate in a common language.

And these meetings were no longer an issue for her.

There is so much “implicit” knowledge within us. And most of the times we are not aware of how much we already know.

It’s about tapping into that knowledge to bring out the best version of ourselves.

And shine 🌟 !
