English idioms are not important for an IT professional.


They are actually vital!

Knowing idioms can make or break your career.

Why am I saying this?

How can I be so sure?

This reflection came from a personal story, which I’d like to share.

I was working in Service Delivery and this interesting position as Team leader 👨‍💼 became available, so I decided to apply.

I had everything I needed, I knew the job and I was ready for it.

So, the day of the interview arrived.

I was nervous, but I had the right amount of confidence.

And the interview was going well, I went through some usual and not so usual questions but all in all, no issues.

Until the last question came:

The hiring manager asked me:

“Daniele, if I give you this job, I won’t have time to train you, so you will have to 𝗵𝗶𝘁 𝘁𝗵𝗲 𝗴𝗿𝗼𝘂𝗻𝗱 𝗿𝘂𝗻𝗻𝗶𝗻𝗴. Tell me how you will do it”.

*suspense* 😲 

Did I know the meaning of this idiom?

Was I able to reply and demonstrate my knowledge?

Luckily, I was. 

I went through my strategy, and I explained how I would independently organize 1:1 meetings, how I would carry on the team strategy, and how I would support and motivate Team members.

So, in a nutshell, as per Cambridge online dictionary: how I would “𝘪𝘮𝘮𝘦𝘥𝘪𝘢𝘵𝘦𝘭𝘺 𝘸𝘰𝘳𝘬 𝘩𝘢𝘳𝘥 𝘢𝘯𝘥 𝘴𝘶𝘤𝘤𝘦𝘴𝘴𝘧𝘶𝘭𝘭𝘺 𝘢𝘵 𝘢 𝘮𝘺 𝘯𝘦𝘸 𝘢𝘤𝘵𝘪𝘷𝘪𝘵𝘺”.

Experience, knowledge, confidence. We surely need all of them.

But office English, buzzwords and idioms are equally important.

To leave a great impression and showcase our expertise.

P.S. Eventually, I got the job then and that allowed me to refine my skills as a coach, mentor and leader. And I will always be grateful for that. 🙏
