As #worldwellbeingweek drives to an end, I would like to reflect on a fundamental aspect.
 
A #wellbeingeconomy 
 
I’ve had the luck of working with people who have helped shape the concept of Wellbeing Economy, and this really resonates with me.
 
A Wellbeing Economy goes beyond "green" and "sustainable". Its solutions are people-centered, geared towards environmental protection and regeneration, and long-term.
 
𝐏𝐞𝐨𝐩𝐥𝐞 𝐜𝐞𝐧𝐭𝐞𝐫𝐞𝐝. We have heard this many times: We don't live to work, we work to live. Humans need to be at the core of economy planning (urban design, education, company organization)

𝐄𝐧𝐯𝐢𝐫𝐨𝐧𝐦𝐞𝐧𝐭𝐚𝐥 𝐩𝐫𝐨𝐭𝐞𝐜𝐭𝐢𝐨𝐧. A regenerative economy is not only sustainable, but it also helps managing resources (Energy, soil) more effectively

𝐋𝐨𝐧𝐠 𝐭𝐞𝐫𝐦. This ensures financial stability (Think about Companies and their strategic planning), but also the compatibility of present needs with those of future generations 
 
How do we transition to a Wellbeing economy?

💹I believe Governments need to change how they measure economic performance and implement new indicators, alternative to GDP;

💵Also, some reforms in the Tax and funding systems are required 

👩‍🎓Finally, we need to approach education in a different way: equip learners with the real tools for a changing world (Technology, mindset, sustainability)
 
Some countries have already started implementing these changes (Scotland, New Zealand), but a lot needs to be done.
