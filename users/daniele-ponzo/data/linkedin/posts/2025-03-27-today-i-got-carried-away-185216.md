Today I got carried away.

And decided to do a little experiment

I started testing “𝘚𝘦𝘴𝘢𝘮𝘦” an open-source app that promised interactive conversations.
And I ended up getting hooked.

Why? Because it explains things, reacts to your input, and even reads the emotional context.

Of course, I wanted to see if we can use it for learning and practicing.

So I had one conversation where I pretended to struggle to follow, to see if I could:
 ✅ Ask it to repeat things differently
 ✅ Slow it down when needed
 ✅ Get real-time explanations

And guess what? It kind of worked.

They want to achieve “voice presence”. And in a way, it really feels like you’re having a conversation (check the video below 👇 ).

This tool has been optimized for friendliness and expressivity.

So, I think it can help boost confidence, sharpen listening skills, and make English practice truly interactive.

The downside? There is no security and no limitation.

🤔 Good or bad? I still don't know. Try it yourself and let me know.
