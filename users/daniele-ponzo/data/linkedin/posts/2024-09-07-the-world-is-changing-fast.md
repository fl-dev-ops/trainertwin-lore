The world is changing fast 🌏 . 

And so is the structure of companies that operate in the IT business. 

Many countries are working nonstop to manage this transition and thrive in it. 

#Uzbekistan 🇺🇿 is amongst them: the current government is investing massively in educating its workforce towards this international dimension, to encourage multinational companies to invest in their country. 

The English language is, of course, a crucial aspect of this, along with cultural nuances that people have to be aware of when dealing with colleagues or clients from different backgrounds.

As an expert in this field, I had the privilege to be contacted by Asel Mamaeva, manager at IT PARK Uzbekistan.

We had a mutually beneficial exchange and I wish them the very best in expanding their #nonnative frontier and embrace the evolution of the #ITbusiness.
