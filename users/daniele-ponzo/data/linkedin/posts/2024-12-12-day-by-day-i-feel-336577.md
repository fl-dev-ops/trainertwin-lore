Who said the goal was perfection?

I’ve worked with a lot of professionals.

They often come to me with a similar pain point:

“I can understand but I can’t speak. I can’t explain complex ideas…I tried everything, maybe English is not for me” 😕 

And they miss chances and opportunities.

So, there are a few things we always do in our session:

✳️ Restore their confidence
✳️ Speak (a lot) and practice
✳️ Try the tools they’re going to use in the real world

The result?

You can read it above.

It’s not perfection.

It’s the idea of going out there and try.

They feel they can speak now, they send me recordings, videos. They go to international meetings, they want to communicate.

In one sentence?

They enjoy speaking English

They don’t see it as the enemy anymore. 🤝 

If you also want to make peace with English, you don’t need to look for perfection and you don’t need to overthink.

You just need the right plan and a safe environment to practice.
