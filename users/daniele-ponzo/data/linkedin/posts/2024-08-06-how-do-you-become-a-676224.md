How do you become a True International Leader?

Some things are important:

Language, Curiosity Mentorship…

BUT
One thing is crucial:

The willingness to learn and to put effort into it.

I’ve discussed these points with Marek Sowa, Global Head of GenAI CoE, Offering & Innovation Director at Capgemini in the latest episode of “The Non-Native Leader” Video Podcast 🎤 .

There are no shortcuts.

There is no magic wand.

Only a proactive attitude will get you closer to your target.

And Marek has the best possible mindset:

🔆Working in a Hotel -> learn customer support
🔆Being in an international team -> learn cultural nuances
🔆Mentorship -> actively look for a mentor and ask the right questions
🔆Finding gap in your knowledge -> learn, improve, put yourself out there 
🔆Joining a large company -> opportunity to learn the financial aspect of the business.

If you’re feeling insecure, anxious, uncertain…well, we’ve all been there. 

But if you’re interested in becoming more confident (Global Director Confident), then you don’t want to miss our chat.
