Welcome to another day in international tech.

Working in tech is brilliant.

Interesting problems.

Great people.

Endless opportunities.

But some days look more like this:

💻 You fix one bug...
...and discover three new ones.

📅 Every meeting could have been an email.
Except the emails also become meetings.

❓ "Quick question..."
It was never quick.

🤖 AI writes half the code...
...you spend the other half explaining why it shouldn't.

☕ You finally make a coffee.

Production goes down.

Coincidence?
Probably not.

After 13 years working in international IT, I've realised something.

The hardest problems were rarely technical.

They were human.

Explaining complex ideas.

Managing expectations.

Leading meetings.

Communicating clearly under pressure.

Because eventually...
every great technical solution needs someone who can explain it.

Maybe that's why I still enjoy working with tech professionals today.

The technology changes.

The communication challenges never really disappear.
