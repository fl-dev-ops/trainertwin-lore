I disagree!
 
Sound harsh? 

Well, it is!

Choose your words wisely when expressing disagreement.

Here are a few key strategies to keep in mind:
 
1️⃣ Be Respectful.
Try to empathise with the other person.
2️⃣ Offer Alternatives or Suggestions
You have an alternative idea? Suggest the best course of action.
3️⃣ Provide Supporting Evidence
Ok, you disagree. Can you explain why?
 
And how can we do this?
 
By using Softening Language.
 
Softening language can help to be less direct when we disagree. 
 
For example: here are some useful phrases to use when we don't fully agree.
 
·     I think I disagree.
·     I’m not so sure about that.
·     Maybe you’ve got a point there.
·     Don’t get me wrong, but I don’t think so.
·     I’m not convinced.
 
And here are some useful examples:
𝐈𝐧 𝐚 𝐌𝐞𝐞𝐭𝐢𝐧𝐠:
👉 "I think I disagree with the proposed approach to network security. It might leave some vulnerabilities."
 
𝐃𝐮𝐫𝐢𝐧𝐠 𝐚 𝐃𝐢𝐬𝐜𝐮𝐬𝐬𝐢𝐨𝐧:
👉 "I’m not so sure about that new software implementation plan. Have we considered potential compatibility issues?"
Acknowledging a Valid Point:
 
𝐈𝐧 𝐚 𝐁𝐫𝐚𝐢𝐧𝐬𝐭𝐨𝐫𝐦𝐢𝐧𝐠 𝐒𝐞𝐬𝐬𝐢𝐨𝐧:
👉 "Maybe you’ve got a point there regarding the use of cloud services. It could enhance scalability."
 
𝐈𝐧 𝐚 𝐏𝐫𝐨𝐣𝐞𝐜𝐭 𝐑𝐞𝐯𝐢𝐞𝐰:
👉 "Don’t get me wrong, but I don’t think expanding the scope at this stage is a good idea. It might affect the timeline."
 
𝐃𝐮𝐫𝐢𝐧𝐠 𝐚 𝐒𝐭𝐫𝐚𝐭𝐞𝐠𝐲 𝐌𝐞𝐞𝐭𝐢𝐧𝐠:
👉 "I’m not convinced about adopting this new framework. Can we explore potential risks before making a decision?"
 
Respect, understanding, and collaboration are crucial when we work in teams.

Remember, most of the times we want to build bridges, not walls 🌉
