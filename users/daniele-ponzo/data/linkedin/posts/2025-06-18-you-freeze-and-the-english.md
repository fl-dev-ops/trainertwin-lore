You freeze. And the English just… disappears.

You’re in a meeting.
You’ve prepared. You’ve practiced.
And you even know what to say.

But when it’s your turn to speak…
💬 You overthink.
💭 You worry about sounding “wrong.”
😬 You hesitate — and someone else jumps in.

Later, you replay the moment in your head — fluently, of course.

If this has ever happened to you, I want you to know:
It’s not just about your grammar.
It’s not about your vocabulary.
And it’s definitely not about needing “perfect English.”

👉 Swipe through this carousel for a different perspective on what’s really holding you back...
...and what it actually takes to feel more confident when you speak.

Because sometimes, it’s not your English that needs fixing.
It’s the pressure we put on ourselves to speak a certain way.
