Is it only about the language?

Well, not really.

English is about language, culture, stories, history, vibes.

And, most important, English is not only about England.

(even though the have just gained access to the Euro 2024 final ⚽ , fair play to them).

Here in Ireland, storytelling is a serious thing.

So, when I was asked by Passaparola Italia to show a group of students the city of Dublin, I decided to team up with my good friend Liam from Tara Tours Ireland to plan a memorable day of discoveries around the city.

Liam took care of the stories, the insights, the characters that make Irish history unique. ☘ 

And I took care of the language, helping the students with the nuances of the Irish “lingo” 🗣 .

Expressions, pronunciation, colours, intonations.

We all had great fun and we tried to offer a true 360-degree experience for the students who are visiting Ireland this month.

To share some of the fun (or ‘craic’, as Liam would say here), here are some of my favourite Irish expressions ever:

-       What’s the story? = How are you?
-       I will yeah = Definitely not.
          o  As in: “𝘞𝘪𝘭𝘭 𝘺𝘰𝘶 𝘱𝘳𝘦𝘱𝘢𝘳𝘦 𝘥𝘪𝘯𝘯𝘦𝘳? 𝘐 𝘸𝘪𝘭𝘭 𝘺𝘦𝘢𝘩”.
-       Fair play = Well done
-       Knackered= exhausted
-       Fella = man (any age)
-       Oul fella= father
-       Manky= disgusting, dirty
-       Yoke= thing (when you don’t remember the name)
         o  As in: “𝘊𝘢𝘯 𝘺𝘰𝘶 𝘱𝘢𝘴𝘴 𝘮𝘦 𝘵𝘩𝘢𝘵 𝘺𝘰𝘬𝘦?”
