The dangers of generative AI

I get it, most of us have a great relationship with generative AI:

❤️ consumers love it
❤️ company executives are excited about the benefits

and they lived happily ever after...

or not?

There is a downside to this, I believe.

When consumers are prepared to entrust AI with medical advice and personal interactions, AI is no longer a tool. It's an authoritative figure.

When Leaders delegate the creation of engaging customer experiences to AI, then AI becomes an influencer.

So, how can we learn to live with AI and make sure this is a TOOL for  a HUMAN to use (rather than the other way round)?

✅ Build ethical foundations to limit the "Emotional" and "Influencing" side of AI
✅ Implement "Guardrails" to ensure a safe development of new tools
✅ Modernize Rules & Regulations in place at all levels (Government, Business, Public bodies)

and we need these to make sure HUMANS can thrive with AI.
