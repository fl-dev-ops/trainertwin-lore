Your English isn’t broken. Your approach might be. 🎯

This week, I've had the chance to join GK TALKS and share something close to my heart:
👉 My personal story
👉 The real challenges non-native professionals face when speaking English
👉 And the method I now teach, the 𝗛𝗔𝗖𝗞 framework to finally unlock clarity and confidence.

There is one thing that I've learned:
➝ Your grammar might be “correct”
➝ You may have taken multiple courses
➝ You even think in English

But when it’s time to speak in a meeting, your mind goes blank.
You hesitate. You overthink.
And your real ideas… never make it out.

That used to be me too. Until I changed everything.

It’s not about learning more English…
It’s about learning how to use the English you already know.

That’s what the HACK method is for:
𝗛one – Focus on what you actually need
𝗔ction – Use it actively (no more waiting!)
𝗖ollaboration – Get support and feedback
𝗞udos – Track progress and celebrate small wins

In this talk, I walk through the full framework and share how my clients (and I!) use it to speak English clearly, confidently, even under pressure.
