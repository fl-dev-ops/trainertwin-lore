How Alex found his dream job in an international company.

By applying my H.A.C.K. strategy to his English.

Before Alex and I started working together, he felt he could not work confidently in English. Despite being a senior project manager, with more than 10 years’ experience in Fintech, E-Commerce and Startups, he was struggling to speak up in his English meetings

But he knew exactly what he wanted:

Get a job in a multinational company and boost his career.

So, we teamed up and worked on a practical and effective strategy to:

|          Identify the critical work situations he needed to face.
||           Get enough practice on those situations.
|||           Keep the motivation up.

And this is how we did it:

👨‍💼 Focused on the essential work vocabulary to have the right expressions in the right context (H).

💪 Practised interviews, meetings, presentations to get enough exposure and be ready for the REAL thing (A).

🔄 Stayed in touch during the whole process so we could exchange feedback and keep up the momentum (C).

🥂 Celebrated together whenever he reached his goals (K).

And now?

From feeling like an imposter to being a leader in an American company with native English speakers.

Through a practical, hands-on approach to communication in English.
