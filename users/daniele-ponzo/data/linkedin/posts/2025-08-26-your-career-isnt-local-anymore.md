Your career isn’t local anymore. It’s global. 🌍

One big shift we don’t talk about enough:
Your competition is no longer just the person in your city.

The developer might be in Bangalore or Barcelona.
The designer in São Paulo, Stockholm or Lagos.
The marketer in Berlin, Sydney or Beijing.

And especially in tech, the language that connects us all is English.

That means:
✅ Opportunities are bigger than ever, across borders, cultures, and industries.
✅ Networking in English opens doors you might not even know exist.
✅ It’s not enough to have great skills if you’re not able to share them with the world.

Remember:

𝗧𝗵𝗲 𝗺𝗮𝗿𝗸𝗲𝘁 𝗶𝘀 𝗴𝗹𝗼𝗯𝗮𝗹
According to the World Economic Forum, by 2030, the number of digital jobs that can be performed remotely is expected to grow by 25%, reaching around 92 million roles, making location less of a barrier than ever.

𝗖𝗮𝗿𝗲𝗲𝗿𝘀 𝗮𝗿𝗲 𝗻𝗼𝗻𝗹𝗶𝗻𝗲𝗮𝗿, 𝗳𝗹𝘂𝗶𝗱, 𝗮𝗻𝗱 𝗮𝗱𝗮𝗽𝘁𝗮𝗯𝗹𝗲.
In the U.S., the median tenure at a job has dropped to 3.9 years as of 2024, down from 4.6 years in 2014. 
Surveys also show that many people change jobs every 2.7 years on average, especially within the 25–34 age group

So what does this mean for you?

Get ready for the future of work:

▶️ Invest in languages. English is the gateway, but multilingual professionals always stand out.

▶️ Build your personal brand. Your LinkedIn profile, online presence, and thought leadership are as important as your CV.

▶️ Network globally. Join international events, communities, and online forums where conversations happen in English.

▶️ Stay adaptable. Continuous learning: not just hard skills, but also cross-cultural communication, agility, and collaboration.

▶️ Think portfolio, not ladder. Embrace hybrid paths: freelancing, side projects, entrepreneurship.

The world is borderless, and so should be your career strategy.
