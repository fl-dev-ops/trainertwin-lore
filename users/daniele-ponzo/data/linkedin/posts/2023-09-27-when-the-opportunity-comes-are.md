When the opportunity comes, are you going to grab it?


Mr P. came to me with his job interview in mind.

It was his real possibility for a career switch.

He has great skills in his field.

He has a degree in Economics & Management.

And he had worked hard to earn the interview.

But his problem was that only the first part of the interview was in Italian.

Then he had to switch to English and make sure he could talk about his expertise.

His English was good, but he wasn't confident he could pass the interview in English.

So, we worked together on:

✅ Equipping him with the right vocabulary to use in international situations.

✅ Role-playing the interview process and some typical questions.

✅ Refining expressions so he could present himself with confidence.

And today he has been offered a rewarding job in an international environment.

I'm thrilled for Mr P as this is an amazing opportunity for him.

Well done, Mr P for stepping up and taking action!
