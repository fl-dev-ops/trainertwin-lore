You had the idea.

But it didn’t come out.
  
You’re in the meeting.

You understand everything.
You even know the solution.

And then your brain goes:

“Ok… say it…”
“Wait… how do I start…”
“No… that sounds wrong…”
“Is it this word or… another one…”
“Ok just say something…”

You start:

“Yeah so… I mean… the problem is… eh…”
  
And then…

you lose it.
  
Because your brain is overloaded.
  
In that moment, you’re doing too much:

• thinking the idea
• translating it
• fixing the sentence
• checking if it sounds “ok”

So it comes out like this:

“Maybe… we could… I think… the… configuration… is… not… correct…”
  
And inside your head?

💭  𝘛𝘩𝘪𝘴 𝘴𝘰𝘶𝘯𝘥𝘦𝘥 𝘴𝘰 𝘮𝘶𝘤𝘩 𝘣𝘦𝘵𝘵𝘦𝘳 5 𝘴𝘦𝘤𝘰𝘯𝘥𝘴 𝘢𝘨𝘰.
  
So you stop.

Or you keep going… but:

🔴  the sentence gets longer
🔴 the structure disappears
🔴 the point gets weaker
  
Meanwhile?

Someone else jumps in:

“The issue is the configuration.”

Done.
  
Same idea.

Different delivery.
  
And now it looks like:

🟢 they’re clearer
🟢 they’re more confident
🟢 they know more
  
Even if they don’t.
  
This is the part people don’t say out loud:

“I know what I want to say…
 I just can’t say it fast enough.”

“I’m not sure if it’s correct…
 so I wait.”

“My mind goes blank…
 and I start searching for words…”
  
So what do you do?

You hold back.

You let others speak.

You tell yourself:

“I’ll say it later.”
“I’ll write it better.”
  
But later never has the same impact.
  
And slowly:

• you speak less
• you doubt more
• others expect less from you
  
Something you need to know:

You’re not less capable.
  
You’re just trying to do too many things at once…
in real time.
  
That’s where things break.
  
“learn more, study more, think more...”

nope

• say it simpler
• say it earlier
• stop trying to make it perfect
  
Because clarity beats perfection.

Every time.
  
▶️  When this happens to you… what does it sound like in your head?

“Wait… how do I say this…”
 “I lost the word…”
 Or… nothing at all?
