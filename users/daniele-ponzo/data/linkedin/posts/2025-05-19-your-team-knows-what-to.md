Your team knows what to say, but not how.

You can have the smartest team in the room…
But when English gets in the way, everything slows down.

I’ve seen it all:
🔺 A local team suddenly becoming international after a merger
🔺 A company with HQ abroad where English is the default
🔺 Teams expanding into foreign markets, needing to pitch and close in English

In every case, the same challenge shows up.

Brilliant professionals who know what to say, but hold back because they’re not confident speaking English:

🔴 Silence in meetings because people aren’t confident
🔴 Clients confused after a call, even though the message was right
🔴 Emails that need three rounds of clarification
🔴 Talented professionals holding back, afraid to sound unprofessional

This surely is a language issue.
But it’s also a communication issue.
And communication drives performance.

The cost?
 
Lost deals. Misunderstandings. Missed promotions...💰.
 
And a team that doesn’t sound as smart as it really is.

In cases like these, a generic grammar course is not enough.

Employees need relevant, real-world English training, built around how they work and who they talk to.

Is your team is growing globally but struggling to keep up in English?
DM me “CONFIDENCE” and I’ll ask a couple of questions to see if I can help.

P.S. Have you ever had to deal with managers, colleagues or clients in a different country? How was that for you?
