The real reason why you’re not progressing in English.

This is not easy, but it needs to be said 🤷‍♂️ .

If you are like me and have an “intellectual” approach, you might have experienced this.

You get your book, you study the grammar, the words, the meaning the insights of a language 📖 .

Don’t get me wrong, this is all ok.

It will get up to a good level.

But confidence?

That’s a different story.

I was lucky enough to have a meaningful conversation with my friend Martin Oetting, a multilingual artist and filmmaker.

He learned English, French and Italian and he summarized the process well.

⚠ WARNING: Becoming comfortable with a language might hurt (at the beginning):

1️⃣ Immerse yourself in the language.
2️⃣ Feel like an idiot, as you don’t understand much.
3️⃣ Put yourself out there and make mistakes.
4️⃣ Repeat it a thousand times.
🔅Finally get more confident.
