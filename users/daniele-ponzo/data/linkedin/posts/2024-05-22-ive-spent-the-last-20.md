I’ve spent the last 20 years working with people.

Here’s what I learned:

► Customer support: Building rapport and trust. Patience, patience, patience.
↳“We’re here to help. Shouting at me won’t speed up the process, sir. Yes, without ticket number there’s nothing I can do”. 

► Service Delivery: Getting closer to the Heart of Darkness: presenting in front of the customer. Panic kicks in. Rewards & Recognition.
↳“Our solution can reduce your costs by 15%, help you streamline your processes while leveraging your current strengths. Deal done”.

► L&D: New people, new faces, teaching the job, the insights, help them locate the coffee machine, test. Repeat.
↳“Without you, the process book is useless. Without the process book, you are useless”.

►Team Management: Finally, a chance to be the Team Leader I wish I had.
↳“Hey everyone, new company strategy, let’s implement the CHANGE. Stop, you can’t run away!”

► Robotic Process Automation: Workflows, processes, and the language of software engineers.
↳“First, solve the problem. Then write the code. Ok, I will solve the problem and you will write the code, fair enough”.

► Teaching English to kids: Fear, terror, and apocalyptic vibes: entering a classroom full of screaming bambinos.
↳“3, 2, 1: jump up and down for no reason, jump up and down for no reason…”

► Teaching English to Teens: Grammar. Storytelling. Instagram, Tik Tok, Twich. Yes, their English is different.
↳“I’m here to teach you English, bro. That ain’t gonna be easy. Yeah, you can use “ship” as a verb, different meaning, though.”

► Teaching Adults: Those good old days at school, do you remember? Well, now they’re back!
↳“Homework for you. Yes, homework. Yes, you might need to read and write, sorry”.

► Working for a Minister: How to deal with high-profile people while trying to smooth Byzantine working processes.
↳“Ok, we shall start the meeting at 1PM. And we will, as usual, finish around dinner time”.

► Coaching IT Professionals: all the above.
↳“I’ve got your back”.

👉 And you? What have you learned in your journey?

P.S. If you need an extra hand getting more visible at work and navigating cultural nuances, then reach out and let’s chat 📩 .
