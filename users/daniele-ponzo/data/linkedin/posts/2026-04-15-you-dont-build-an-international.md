You don’t build an international career by waiting until your English is “ready.”

Sometimes…

you just get dropped into Japan.

No plan.
Limited English.
No way back.

And suddenly… you have no choice but to speak.

In Episode 36 of The Non-Native Leader - Podcast,
Mario Crea (Head of Digital Transformation Philip Morris International) shares exactly this.

He didn’t start with perfect English.

He found himself in an international environment in Japan 
surrounded by people speaking different languages,
forced to communicate just to function.

That was his turning point.

What he calls a “𝗚𝗮𝗻𝗱𝗮𝗹𝗳 𝗺𝗼𝗺𝗲𝗻𝘁” 🧙‍♂️ 
That moment where something (or someone) pushes you and says:

“Go.”

Because here’s the reality most professionals miss:

You don’t improve first… and then act.

You act → and that’s how you improve.

That’s how you build:
• confidence
• clarity
• real communication skills

And even when he learned Japanese, 
he had to start with exposure, pressure, and repetition.
to get comfortable with the language.

🎙️ In this episode, we talk about:
• what really accelerates growth abroad
• why language is not the starting point
• how to create your own “Gandalf moments”
• and why discomfort is the real teacher

If you’ve ever thought:

“I’m not ready yet…”

This might change your perspective.
