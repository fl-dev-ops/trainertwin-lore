I wanna be like you!

How do you leave a great impression in your meetings, calls and presentations?

Learn from Baloo: preparation, engagement, and adaptability 🐻 .
And you can walk into the room with confidence and ease.

So, what’s the secret? I’ll lay it on you 🔥 :

𝗣𝗿𝗲𝗽𝗮𝗿𝗮𝘁𝗶𝗼𝗻 𝗶𝘀 𝗣𝗼𝘄𝗲𝗿
Great communicators don’t just show up—they prepare.
↳How to Improve: list the top three points you need to make. Rehearse saying them out loud. 
↳Language Example: “I’ve identified three key issues we need to address today. First, our timeline is off. Second, the budget needs adjustment. And third, we need to improve team communication.”

𝗖𝗹𝗮𝗿𝗶𝘁𝘆 𝗮𝗻𝗱 𝗕𝗿𝗲𝘃𝗶𝘁𝘆
keep your message simple and easy to understand.
↳How to Improve: Explain your points in short, clear sentences. One idea per sentence or slide.
↳Language Example: 
❌“Our objective is to enhance the consumer experience by optimizing the interface and incorporating real-time feedback loops,” 
✅ “We need to make our website easier to use and get feedback from customers quickly.”

𝗔𝗰𝘁𝗶𝘃𝗲 𝗟𝗶𝘀𝘁𝗲𝗻𝗶𝗻𝗴 𝗮𝗻𝗱 𝗘𝗻𝗴𝗮𝗴𝗲𝗺𝗲𝗻𝘁
Communication isn’t just about talking—it’s about listening.
↳How to Improve: Focus on the person speaking, nod to show you’re following, and ask questions to dig deeper.
↳Language Example: “That’s an interesting point—can you tell me more about how you see that working in practice?”

𝗕𝗼𝗱𝘆 𝗟𝗮𝗻𝗴𝘂𝗮𝗴𝗲
Confident stance, eye contact, and using your hands for emphasis.
↳How to Improve: Practice standing tall, making eye contact with your audience, and using natural hand gestures.

𝗔𝗱𝗮𝗽𝘁𝗮𝗯𝗶𝗹𝗶𝘁𝘆
Adapt your message to the audience and the situation.
↳How to Improve: Check your audience. If they look confused, explain in simpler terms. If they seem impatient, speed up or ask for their input.
↳Language Example: “I see some of you are wondering how this applies to our project—let me give you a quick example that might clarify things.”

𝗣𝘂𝗿𝗽𝗼𝘀𝗲
Have a clear goal for every meeting, presentation, or call.
↳How to Improve: Before you start, ask yourself, “What do I want the audience to do or understand by the end of this?”
↳Language Example: “By the end of this meeting, I hope we can all agree on the next steps for launching the project.”

Remember: if you want to improve, seek feedback, record yourself and use techniques to keep calm. 

And be reasonably crazy 😉 .
