Most people try to become confident before they speak.

That’s why nothing changes.

They study more.
They prepare more.
They wait to feel “ready”.

And then the moment comes:

“You’re an experienced developer, so what do you recommend?”

Everyone's eyes are on you.

And this is what happens:

“Wait… how do I say this…”
“Is this the right word…”
“Let me think…”

How do we break the pattern?

You don’t need to “improve your English”. 
It’s too generic and it won’t help you

You need to change how you speak under pressure

When you look at this “before vs after”… where do you recognize yourself the most?
