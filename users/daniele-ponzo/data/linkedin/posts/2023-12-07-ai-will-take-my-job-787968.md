“AI will take my job”

How many times have we heard this?

I believe this is not coming any time soon.

Take the ”Ursula Burns experiment”

I sometimes brainstorm ideas on ChatGPT.

I believe it is useful when you have something in your mind and you’re looking for a second thought.

However, I’m aware that “𝘊𝘩𝘢𝘵𝘎𝘗𝘛 𝘤𝘢𝘯 𝘮𝘢𝘬𝘦 𝘮𝘪𝘴𝘵𝘢𝘬𝘦𝘴. 𝘊𝘰𝘯𝘴𝘪𝘥𝘦𝘳 𝘤𝘩𝘦𝘤𝘬𝘪𝘯𝘨 𝘪𝘮𝘱𝘰𝘳𝘵𝘢𝘯𝘵 𝘪𝘯𝘧𝘰𝘳𝘮𝘢𝘵𝘪𝘰𝘯.” (as OpenAI declare).
 
And here there is a plain mistake, right in front of our eyes.
 
ChatGPT lists Ursula Burns as a “non-native” English speaker.
 
I wonder. 🤔 
 
Is this an AI generated mistake? 🤖 
 
Or is it the result of a human generated bias? 😕 
 
Regardless, we should always be careful when we get info from generative AI.

P.S. one thing I appreciated was the in-built politeness ChatGPT is showing here…is it trying to make up for its mistake? 

P.P.S. Any funny stories with AI? I'd love to hear them in the comments below. 👇
