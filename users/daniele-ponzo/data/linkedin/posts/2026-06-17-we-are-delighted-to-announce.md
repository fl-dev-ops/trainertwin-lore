We are delighted to announce Daniele Ponzo – Hack Your English as the winner of the Business Language Coach of the Year 2026 award at the Corporate LiveWire Innovation & Excellence Awards.

Daniele has been recognised for his innovative approach to helping international professionals transform the way they communicate in English. Drawing on his extensive background in the technology sector, he understands the real challenges faced by global teams and provides practical coaching that goes beyond language learning — building confidence, clarity, and the communication skills needed to thrive in today’s business world.

Through Hack Your English, Daniele empowers professionals to succeed in meetings, presentations, interviews, and cross-cultural workplace conversations by focusing on authentic and effective communication.
The judges were particularly impressed by Daniele’s ability to bridge technical expertise with business language coaching, creating a service that delivers meaningful impact for professionals and organisations worldwide.

👏 Congratulations to Daniele Ponzo on this outstanding achievement.
