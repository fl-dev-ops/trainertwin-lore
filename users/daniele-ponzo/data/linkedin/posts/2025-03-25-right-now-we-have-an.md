> 𝗘𝗺𝗲𝗿𝗴𝗲𝗻𝗰𝗲 𝗼𝗳 𝗮 𝗡𝗲𝘄 𝗘𝗻𝗴𝗹𝗶𝘀𝗵 𝗠𝗼𝗱𝗲𝗹: The traditional concept of a "native speaker" is being challenged. Instead of aiming to sound like a native speaker, non-native speakers are encouraged to retain their national identity while developing fluency and intercultural communication skills​
> 𝗖𝗵𝗮𝗻𝗴𝗶𝗻𝗴 𝗔𝘂𝘁𝗵𝗼𝗿𝗶𝘁𝘆 𝗶𝗻 𝗘𝗻𝗴𝗹𝗶𝘀𝗵 𝗨𝘀𝗮𝗴𝗲: The idea of native speakers as the "gold standard" is being questioned. Native-speaker norms are seen as an obstacle to the free development of global English, and many learners prefer models that reflect their own communication needs​
> 𝗧𝗵𝗿𝗲𝗲 𝗖𝗶𝗿𝗰𝗹𝗲𝘀 𝗼𝗳 𝗘𝗻𝗴𝗹𝗶𝘀𝗵 𝗨𝘀𝗲𝗿𝘀: The traditional model divides English users into three groups:
> The "Inner Circle" (native speakers, e.g., UK, USA).
> The "Outer Circle" (countries where English is a second language, e.g., India).
> The "Expanding Circle" (countries where English is learned as a foreign language). However, this model is evolving, with high-proficiency non-native speakers often functioning as the new standard​
