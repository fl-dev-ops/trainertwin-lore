Your brain is running at 100%.
But your English feels like it’s buffering. 🧠⏳

You’re in a global meeting.
You know the solution.
You’ve already refactored it mentally.
You can see exactly where the bottleneck is.

Then someone says:

“So… what do you think?”

And suddenly:
– your vocabulary logs out
– your sentence starts with “So basically…”
– you’re translating while speaking
 
System Overload!

You’re:
• solving the problem
• translating it
• structuring it
• checking grammar
• monitoring how you sound
All at once.

That’s not speaking.
That’s running five applications on low battery mode.

Now let’s add the second pressure.

AI. 🤖

AI writes functions in seconds.
AI suggests improvements.
AI even comments your code.

So the real fear isn’t:
“Can I work with AI?”

It’s:
“Am I still valuable?”

Here’s the good news.

AI can generate code.

It can’t:

– handle political meetings
– read the room
– explain trade-offs clearly
– defend architectural decisions
– build trust

That’s still human territory.
But only if you can communicate it.
  
The new Tech reality:

Your code gets reviewed.
Your English gets judged.
Your clarity gets remembered.

And clarity wins.
