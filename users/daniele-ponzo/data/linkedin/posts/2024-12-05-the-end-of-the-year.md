The end of the year is coming

ok, maybe it sounds a bit gloomy 😨 …

But it’s that time of the year.

Promises, resolutions, dreams, …all while trying to close current projects.

The truth?

It’s hard to turn promises into dreams 

Dreams into resolutions.

And Resolutions into a solid plan.

Job opportunities, growth, working for an important company, starting an international career…

They are all great dreams.💭

But they often only stay dreams.

Because you’re not sure.

You don’t know where to start.

You’ve tried to improve your English before.

Courses, apps, certifications, conversation classes didn’t help you much.

You promise: “I will do it one day”.

“When I have more time, when I’m ready, when I finish this project…”

But what if you could break that negative cycle? 🔁 

Turn the promise into a dream. 

The dream into a resolution

And the resolution into confidence…

…to start the new year with new energy 🌈 .

How would that feel?

Vote in the poll and let me know. 📊 

P.S. sometimes it is not easy to frame our goals. 

But it's the first step to making a solid plan.
