Demystifying Business Jargon #16: "It's Not Rocket Science"🚀

I’m sure you’ve already heard this phrase "It's not rocket science", especially if you’re working in tech.

It’s probably because in IT we often encounter complex challenges and tasks that may seem intimidating at first glance 🤔 . 

However, when someone says, "It's not rocket science," they're essentially suggesting that the problem at hand is not as difficult or as complicated as it may appear.

Here's are a couple of examples in context:

During a Team Meeting:

🔹 Team Leader: "Alright everyone, we need to streamline our project management process to improve efficiency. Let's brainstorm some ideas."

🔹Team Member: "I think we can simplify the workflow by integrating automation tools. It's not rocket science; we just need to identify the key steps and automate them."

In a Casual Conversation:

🔹Colleague 1: "I'm having trouble setting up this new software. It seems so complicated."

🔹Colleague 2: "Don't worry, configuring the software is straightforward once you get the hang of it. It's not rocket science; you'll figure it out in no time."

The American rocket science (aerospace engineering) program started in the 1940s and it was always seen as something overly complicated. Ultimately, it helped send man to the moon 🌕 .

As expected, the idiom went first through the world of sport (football) before transitioning into business. In the 1980s it was used to describe the degree of difficulty in coaching. (source: “the Grammarist”).

At the end of the day, it’s all about tackling challenges with confidence.

And remind us that most of the times we got what it takes to overcome them.

It’s not rocket science, after all!
