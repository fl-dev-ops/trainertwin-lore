PRESENT PERFECT vs. PAST SIMPLE. The eternal struggle!

When do we use them? I get this question a LOT!

The key here is "WHEN"🕑.

If I specify the time, it's PAST SIMPLE (yesterday, last year, 3 minutes ago): 
👉Yesterday I delivered my presentation. 
👉Last week we met our President
👉1 year ago I was still in Accounting

If I don't specify the time, it's PRESENT PERFECT (I talk about my EXPERIENCE):
👉I've worked in Service Delivery, RPA and L&D
👉I've met the CEO 3 times
👉We've been to Spain on various business trips

The PRESENT PERFECT is particularly useful when you want to explain your expertise (for example, in a job interview). I've been..., I've done..., I've worked...
