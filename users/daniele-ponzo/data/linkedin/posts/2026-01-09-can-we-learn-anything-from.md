Can we learn anything from the Lobster🦞?

Yes, we can. Actually we should.

Want to know why?

Well, here’s the story:

The Lobster is a soft animal inside a hard shell.

The animal wants to grow, but the shell does not expand.

As the animal grows, it goes through pain and discomfort because the shell is confining.

So, the Lobster has no choice. If it wants to grow, it needs a new shell.

And if it wants a new shell, it needs to get rid of the old one.

What does it do, then?

It goes under a rock, protects itself, eliminates its confining shell and goes through positive change😌.

This is a wonderful analogy from Rabbi Abraham Twerski.

My favourite part? 

If the Lobster had painkillers, it wouldn’t feel the stress and discomfort.

But then, it would never grow.

Sometimes we can't avoid stress and adversity if we want to get out of the comfort zone.

When I transitioned from IT to teaching, I had a big Lobster moment.

Learning new skills, working in a completely different environment.

I was experiencing stress and discomfort before forming a new, larger shell.

But now I’m happy I’m doing one of the most rewarding jobs in the World.

So, what can you learn from the Lobster 🤔 ?

Well, if you want to become confident when speaking English, you have to go through some pain and discomfort.

Stress, fear can be signals.

You need to find somewhere safe, go through pain and get rid of your confining shell.

The journey is not easy, but it’s the only way if you want to break free and grow stronger.

And become a better communicator 💪 .

I can’t remove the discomfort.

And I can’t avoid the stress for you.

But I can surely offer that protective environment to help you grow and support you during your journey.
