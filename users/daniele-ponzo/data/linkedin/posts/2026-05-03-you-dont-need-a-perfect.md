You don’t need a “perfect accent” to sound professional in English.

Because here’s the truth most people don’t tell you:

👉 A “perfect British” or “perfect American” accent doesn’t exist.

What actually matters is something else.

Pronunciation.

Let’s make it simple:

Accent = how you sound (influenced by your background)
Pronunciation = how clearly people understand you

And in international environments?

Clarity beats perfection. Every time.

I’ve worked with hundreds of IT professionals who believed their accent was the problem.

It wasn’t.

The real issue was:

unclear sounds
flat intonation
speaking too fast under pressure

Once we fixed those?

They didn’t sound “more native.”

They sounded more clear, more confident, more credible.

That’s what gets you:
✔ trust in meetings
✔ impact in presentations
✔ better opportunities
