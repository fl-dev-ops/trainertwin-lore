What defines a confident English speaker?

Most people would answer:

⇢ Perfect grammar
⇢ Flawless accent
⇢ Sophisticated vocabulary

The reality?
Different story.

I had the opportunity to discuss this with Georgios Christou, PhD, former University professor and senior Tech consultant.

And here’s what we uncovered:
💡 Confidence in English isn’t about perfection.
💡 It’s about 𝗰𝗼𝗺𝗺𝘂𝗻𝗶𝗰𝗮𝘁𝗶𝗼𝗻, 𝗰𝗹𝗮𝗿𝗶𝘁𝘆, 𝗮𝗻𝗱 𝗺𝗶𝗻𝗱𝘀𝗲𝘁.

Most non-native tech professionals think they struggle with English because of grammar gaps or pronunciation mistakes.

But that’s not the real issue.

At first, they believe:
 ❌ "I need a bigger vocabulary."
 ❌ "I should sound more like a native speaker."
 ❌ "I must avoid all mistakes to be taken seriously."

But when they start working internationally, they realize:
 ✅ Clarity beats complexity.
 ✅ Mistakes don’t stop great communication—hesitation does.
 ✅ A strong mindset matters more than a perfect accent.

This is exactly what we explored in 𝗘𝗽𝗶𝘀𝗼𝗱𝗲 𝟮𝟰 of The Non-Native Leader Podcast.

Georgios shares his journey from academia to the tech industry, his experience as a non-native professional, and the surprising truth about what really makes you a great communicator.
