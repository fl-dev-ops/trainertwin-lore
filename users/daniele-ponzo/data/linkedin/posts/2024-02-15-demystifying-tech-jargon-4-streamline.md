Demystifying Tech Jargon #4: "Streamline" 

Ever felt puzzled when your manager mentioned "streamlining
processes"? Let's look at this executive lingo together!

🧩 Problem: Lost in Buzzwords. Tech, IT, Business World… understanding phrases like "streamline" is pivotal. 

It's not about rivers or playlists; it's about making things more efficient.

🛠️ Solution: Deciphering the Jargon
When your boss says, "Let's streamline the development pipeline,"
they mean optimizing it for better efficiency. 

Here's how you can put it in
your language.

🚀 Example 1:
👨‍💼 Manager:
"We need to streamline our IT support. Any ideas?"
👩‍💻 Team Member: 
"Absolutely, integrating a ticketing system will help
us manage requests more efficiently."

🚀 Example 2:
👨‍💼 Manager:
"Let's streamline the onboarding process for new employees."
👨‍💻 Team Member: 
"Sure, automating the paperwork will make it faster and smoother for everyone."

So, where does it come from?

Streamline is a:"line drawn from point to point, so that its direction is everywhere that of the motion of the fluid" [Lamb, "Hydrodynamics," 1906], from stream (n.) + line (n.) as in line of flow. (Etymonline)

🔧 Tools for Efficiency!
"Streamlining" isn't just a buzzword; it's about finding tools and methods to work smarter, not harder.
