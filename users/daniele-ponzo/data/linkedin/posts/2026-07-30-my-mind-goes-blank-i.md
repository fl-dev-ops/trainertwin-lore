"My mind goes blank."

"I start searching for words."

"The only thing I express is... uncertainty."

None of those are my words.

They're real messages I've received from software engineers, architects and tech leads over the years. 

And they all point to the same problem.

Not a lack of technical expertise.
Not even a lack of English.

But what happens inside your brain when you're put on the spot in a meeting.

When you're writing in Slack, Jira or email, you have time.

You can think.
Edit.
Rewrite.
Hit backspace.

Live meetings are different.

Your brain is suddenly trying to:

• solve the technical problem
• translate your thoughts into English
• search for the "right" words
• think about how you sound

All at the same time.

That's when cognitive load takes over.

Communication latency increases.

Your ideas are still there...

they're just harder to access.

So instead of speaking clearly, many professionals:

• freeze halfway through a sentence
• overthink every word
• start panic explaining
• sound far less confident than they actually are

✅ what's really happening in the brain
✅ why it feels that way
✅ one practical solution that immediately reduces the pressure
