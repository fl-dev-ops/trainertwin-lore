This is the story of an email.

Going back and forth, trying to find its purpose.
Until something magical happened.

The story started like this:

📨 “Dear client,
The proposal is ready; technical specifications, details and features are available at this address.
All the best”
The address: ‘𝘝𝘦𝘳𝘺𝘐𝘯𝘵𝘦𝘳𝘯𝘢𝘭𝘕𝘦𝘷𝘦𝘳𝘚𝘦𝘦𝘯𝘉𝘦𝘧𝘰𝘳𝘦𝘉𝘺𝘛𝘩𝘦𝘊𝘭𝘪𝘦𝘯𝘵𝘚𝘩𝘢𝘳𝘦𝘥𝘋𝘳𝘪𝘷𝘦”

A puzzled client, trying to open the folder replies:

🤔 “Well, I’ve tried but I can’t open the folder”
Screenshot proof follows.

But the email wasn’t tired and went for another spin:

📨“Dear client
yes, I have sent some additional info on a separate mail.
We just need to check if the login works correctly through the "account".
Once you enter the main page, the folder is called "Proposal"
Please let me know if you manage to access the site”

And so, the poor client now felt like a victim of “Jigsaw”:

🤔“what do you mean with: 
 ‘if you manage to access the site’

But the email wasn’t done yet and decided to hurt more:

📨"Dear Client
I just wanted to make sure that the login was working correctly.
I then realize that you didn't receive the login yet, so now I have sent it on a separate mail.
With that login and password, you should be able to access the site”.

That might have been just too much for our ‘dear client’. He wasn’t ready for another enigma.

But the email didn’t want to give up and started screaming, it wanted to be right at all costs:

📨“I've sent him all the info, instructions and link on a separate mail, I don't know why he's still replying to all".

And that was it. 

We needed something magical to happen to break through the dilemma.

And the words came, as strong and powerful as a ’abracadabra’🧙‍♂️.

✨“Can you contact him, you will find that this method of communication overcomes barriers, ensures that the problem/problems are understood and generally helps getting things resolved.
So perhaps he is replying to all, as he is slightly confused / fed up with the process, his day job is not the same as yours and he probably isn’t familiar with our internal shared drive, specs and technicalities”✨.

What a revelation!

The email was touched by the magic wand and turned into a phone call.🪄

More stunning than Cinderella’s “Pumpkin Coach”.

The moral?

Email, if you strongly believe in yourself, give up your ego and open up to the world, one day you can become a phone call and help communication flow better.

And that was a crucial lesson for me back then:

Less writing ⬇️ More talking ⬆️ .

Talk (write, actually…) to me below: how easy is for you to grab the phone and talk to people instead of sending emails?

P.S. No emails (and Thom Yorke) were harmed during the making of this post.
