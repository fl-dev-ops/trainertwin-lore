The more AI writes...

the more your communication matters.

For years, technical excellence was enough to stand out.

Today, that's changing.

AI is removing friction from writing code.

Which means the real differentiator is moving somewhere else.

Towards:

• better judgement
• better context
• better collaboration
• better decisions
• better communication

That's exactly why Emiliano Pisu and I decided to bring this conversation to Rome Future Week.

We can't predict the future.

But we can surely explore what makes developers valuable when AI becomes part of everyone's toolkit.

🤖 What changes when AI writes more of the code?
