"USED TO", "BE USED TO", "GET USED TO" can be really confusing.

I've had this point raised many times by my clients as they don't really know  which one to use and in which situation. 
However, they are essential as they can elevate your speech and make you look and sound more professional.

They all have to do with routines, habits and familiar actions.

➡️ USED TO + INFINITIVE: this is about the PAST. We use it for a past routine:
✔️I used to work in Accounting, but now I am a Service Delivery Manager;
✔️Michael used to lead meetings every day when he was in Project Management.

➡️ BE USED TO + GERUND: this is the same as BE FAMILIAR. We can use it for a NEW situation which is now common:
✔️I have just changed job and now I am used to working in an international environment.
✔️John is a new developer and he isn't used to dealing with customers.

➡️ GET USED TO + GERUND: this is a routine IN PROGRESS. We use it for a new situation which is becoming more familiar:
✔️I have just moved to a new department and I am getting used to dealing with foreign customers.
✔️Don't worry, you will soon get used to operating the new system.

Just remember to add INFINITIVE or GERUND as appropriate. 

So, next time you're in a conversation, don't say:
🚫 I worked as a senior developer
but say:
✅ I USED TO WORK as a senior developer

I'm sure you will soon get used to this;)
