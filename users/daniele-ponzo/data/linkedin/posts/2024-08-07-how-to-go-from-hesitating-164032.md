How to go from hesitating to fluent English speaker

Spoiler: this has not much to do with grammar.

Sure, we need to know the rules. But there’s more to it.

When you work with Business Professionals, it’s about experiencing the situations, living them and getting ready to think in English.

It’s about practicing, role-playing and becoming more confident at work.

And this is what I do in my English Accelerator program.

Sometimes I work with a team. Like the Italian CCS Team below.

A group of experienced IT Professionals who needed to level up and become comfortable in international situations.

We teamed up and worked on:

➡ Expanding business vocabulary
➡ How to talk effectively in calls
➡ Speaking up in meetings
➡ Strategies to communicate in real life
➡ Presenting in front of the Client

Now they are no longer scared when they receive an international call or need to talk to their boss in England.

As a result, they’re getting praised by their managers and their colleagues.

Remember: Practice makes perfect. Guided practice makes perfect faster.
