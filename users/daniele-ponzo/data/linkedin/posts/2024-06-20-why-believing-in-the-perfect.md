Why believing in the “perfect British accent” is wrong.

And what you should focus on, instead.

I was a professional in an American multinational and I’ve been teaching English for a number of years now. People keep telling me they’re looking for the perfect English accent. Here is why I believe we need to shift perspective and concentrate on something different.

➡ Is it accent or pronunciation?

When we speak English, we all have an accent, both native and non-native: Spanish, French, German, Italian, Irish, Scottish, South African…you name it. Accents are part of our identity; they shape our culture and personality. You should treasure your accent and work to improve your pronunciation, instead.

➡ How is pronunciation different from accent?

Pronunciation is about sounds, phonemes, word stress and intonation. It is about getting the right sound for the right word (like in the cartoon below, “ʌ” instead of “ɔː”), the right stress (𝐜𝐨𝐦fortable instead of comfort𝐚𝐛𝐥𝐞) and a good tone (rising tone in a polite request: “Could you tell me the time, please? ↗). 

➡ How can you improve your pronunciation?

You focus on pronunciation to make sure everybody understands you when you speak. The best ways to improve include using the phonetic alphabet (the weird symbols you saw above), breaking the words down into individual sounds, reading aloud (I love tongue twisters: “She sells seashells by the seashore”).

➡ Remember: if you want to avoid the 3rd World war, you don’t need a perfect British accent.

You need a clear and good pronunciation.

P.S. have you ever had any funny experiences with pronunciation? The first time I went to Ireland it took me 15 minutes to explain my destination to the Taxi driver… 🤦‍♂️
