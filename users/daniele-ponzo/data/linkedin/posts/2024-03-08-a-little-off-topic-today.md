A little off topic today

(it’s Friday, after all 😊)

I’d like to talk about a passion of mine. Hiking.

I’ve always done it (as far as I can remember). The first time in the baby carrier with my dad.

Hiking can be tiring, hard and, at times, frustrating.

So, why do I like it?

I just love the concept of the upward climb. It forces me to break the long journey into small, manageable pieces.

Every single stone takes you a bit higher.

Every obstacle you overcome gets you closer to the summit.

I often go off the beaten track. I love to explore and get lost in uncharted territories.

I use my senses and whatever surrounds me to find my way back. I remember once I followed some horses in the wild to get back to the main path.

Another time I had to do like in children’s stories. The place was so foggy I had to break twigs and branches to leave on the path to find my way back.

The feeling of being lost, but not really astray (we belong to nature, after all).

You stop at a plateau, and you see the peak. Still far, but more reachable.

I treasure the challenge, the reflection, the pauses, the air. 

You breathe and you talk to your inner self.

And you know you can do it.

So, this is my “because”.

Facing the challenge.

Planning the route.

Climbing up.

Quite metaphorical, I think.
