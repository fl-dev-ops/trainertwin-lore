Is your accent making you second-guess your worth?

You walk into a meeting prepared.
With ideas, 
With knowledge
With rehearsed sentences.
 
Clean. Clear. “Neutral.”

You speak up.
Someone squints.
“Sorry, could you repeat that?”
And just like that, your confidence dips.

Did you make a mistake?
No, you didn’t.
But this happens too often.

You know your English is correct.
But the pause, the confusion, the slow nods…
It’s enough to make you speak less next time.

Maybe you’ve thought:
 👉 “Is it my accent?”
 👉 “Did they not get me… or not listen?”
 👉 “Would this happen if I spoke like them?”

This is what it means to carry the invisible tax of being multilingual in a monolingual workspace.

You have to manage tasks, 
But also, you’re managing how you sound, how you’re perceived, how you’re received.

That tension? That second-guessing?
You think it’s about your English.
But in reality, it’s about belonging.

Your accent isn’t a flaw, it’s a flag.
And it shouldn’t cost you your confidence.
