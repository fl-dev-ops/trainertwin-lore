What happens when we can’t speak?

Ok, I don’t mean like Neo in Matrix (the interrogation scene!) 😶 

I mean English

You’ve studied for years.
You can read, you can listen, you can write emails just fine.

But the moment you have to speak

🔴 Your mind goes blank.
🔴 You overthink every word.
🔴 You feel like your voice disappears.

Sound familiar?
Well, it is.

Many professionals feel exactly the same.
But it doesn’t mean you’re bad at English.

Speaking is like a muscle: if you only train it in your head, it never grows.

So what can you do instead?
Start with small, intentional practice every day:
✅ 2 minutes describing what you did this morning.
✅ 3 sentences about a recent project.
✅ 1 short recording of yourself explaining something simple.

That’s how real confidence starts. 
Not by learning more, but by building momentum.
