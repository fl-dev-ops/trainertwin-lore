From Argentina to Denmark, from HR leaders to marketing directors, startup founders, engineers, and NGO professionals, this podcast has become a space for real stories of career growth, identity, and confidence beyond borders.

In every episode, I sit down with professionals who built their careers internationally, often in English, but not as native speakers.

We talk about:
▶️ Overcoming impostor syndrome
▶️ Switching industries and roles
▶️ Leading global teams
▶️ Embracing accents and authentic communication
▶️ Building careers in tech, pharma, consulting, education, and beyond

🌐 Guests have joined me from Spain, Ireland, Argentina, Germany, Italy, Denmark, and many more corners of the world. What connects them? 

Courage, clarity, and the will to grow without waiting for “perfect” English.
