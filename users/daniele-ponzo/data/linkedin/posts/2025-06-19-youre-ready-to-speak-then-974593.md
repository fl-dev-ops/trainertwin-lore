You’re ready to speak… then your mind goes silent.

You’re in a meeting.
Your idea is clear (in your head)
But when it’s time to speak… your mouth stalls.

You freeze. Overthink. Panic a little. Say nothing.

Then later, you replay the moment in perfect English (classic).

If this has happened to you, let me tell you:  you’re not alone.

It happens to brilliant professionals all the time.

And most people try something — anything — to push through it.

So let me ask you this📊

👉 When you froze or got stuck in English… what did you do to deal with it?

➡️ Tried to memorize full scripts
Learning by heart should help... shouldn't it?

➡️ Practiced alone (in my head or out loud)
Pacing your room, speaking to an invisible audience

➡️ Overprepared (and still felt unsure)
Spent hours going through every single detail and yet...

➡️ Said nothing and replayed it later
If... I should... why didn't I say...

Remember, your vote is anonymous.
