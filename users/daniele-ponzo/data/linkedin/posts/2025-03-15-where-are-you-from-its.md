Where are you from?

It's an easy question, right?

But the answer is not always straightforward.

I mean, if you've lived in different countries and different cities...

It is sometimes difficult to answer.

The first time you leave your home Country and you somewhere else

Everything seems strange, different, odd.

But then you go back and things feel strange there too.

Until one day you stop paying attention to what's different and what's the same.

Everyone does everything in a different way.

Different culture 
Different language 
Different habits 

There's no right or wrong 

And you won't find the same solutions around the world 

But it's a learning curve 

One that inspires
And leaves you richer

So where is home?

Where are you from?

Now it's hard to answer.

P.S. Today I'm going to support the underdog 

Come on Italy 💪
