Do you need to push harder?
Well, first just notice how far you’ve come.

The K in my HACK strategy stands for 𝗞𝘂𝗱𝗼𝘀. 
No, it’s not just a nice word. It’s a strategy.

Because effort deserves recognition.

And when we celebrate our small wins, we build motivation, consistency, and self-trust.

This week alone, I’ve seen moments worth cheering for:

▶️ A senior Project Manager who recorded herself on audio for the first time just to hear how she sounds in English.

▶️ A client who sent me her very first video and finally saw how confident she actually looks when speaking.

▶️ And a former client who sends me regular WhatsApp audio messages in English, just to keep the habit going. We’ve been having full-on voice chats!

So, do we need to be perfect?
Well, of course not
(if you've been following me, you know I believe perfection doesn't exist)

What's the magic word, then?
CONSISTENCY

Show up, put yourself out there, talk, write, communicate... consistently.

And stay motivated!
✅ Create simple habits
✅ Track your progress
✅ And reward yourself when you hit those micro-milestones.

To help you do that, I’ve created some free resources to plan your learning journey, your tasks and  your milestones

📍 Find them in the first comment. No sign-up, no email. Just tools to help you stay on track and celebrate your wins 👇 .

You need to notice what you’re already doing right.
