Tomorrow, we won't be talking about the future of developers.

We'll be debugging it.

This is what you usually get:
Two speakers.
One audience.
One hour of listening.

We wanted something different.

Tomorrow, the audience becomes part of the discussion.

We'll ask you to take a position.
🟢 Agree.
🔴 Disagree.

Then we'll challenge the room with questions like:

Can a great developer build a great career without becoming a great communicator?

When AI writes more of the code... what becomes your real value?

Would you deploy AI-generated code you can't explain?

Is communication now part of engineering?

We'll put those ideas under pressure.

Real developer scenarios.

Real stakeholder conversations.

Real trade-offs.

Together, we'll debug the developer.

Turn technical explanations into clear business conversations.

And explore one question that I believe every developer should be asking:

If producing code becomes easier...
what becomes more valuable?

That's where Emiliano Pisu and I come in from two different perspectives.

👨‍💻 Engineering, AI and developer experience.
💬 Communication, clarity and influence.

Two perspectives.

One conversation. 

And an audience that's just as important as the speakers.

📍 Developers in the Age of AI: Code & Communication part of Rome Future Week 
📅 Tomorrow | 17:30
📍 dotcampus Roma
🎟️ Free (registration required: https://lnkd.in/env-UQzs)

🍸 And yes...

we'll continue the discussion over an aperitivo afterwards.

If you work in tech...
come ready to challenge ideas.
Maybe even your own.

Because the best events don't give you all the answers.
They change the questions you're asking.
