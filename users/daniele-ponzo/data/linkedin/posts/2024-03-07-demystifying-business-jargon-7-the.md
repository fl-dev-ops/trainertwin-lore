Demystifying business jargon #7: the Elevator Pitch, A Must-Have Skill for IT Leaders! 📈

How easy is for you to pitch a project or an idea?

How fast can you do it?

Enter the "Elevator Pitch" – a brief, compelling summary of your project, idea, or yourself that can be delivered within the time it takes to ride an elevator🛗. 

Essentially, you should be able to deliver an effective and convincing presentation in just a few seconds.

Let's break it down:

⭕ Project Pitch:
Effective: We're implementing a cutting-edge software upgrade that will boost system efficiency by 30%, saving both time and resources.
 
⭕ Self-Presentation:
Effective: As an IT manager with over a decade of experience, I specialize in creating robust, scalable solutions that drive operational excellence.
 
⭕ Cost-Saving Crusader:
Crafted: Thanks to our cost-saving initiative, we've implemented innovative solutions that slash operational expenses by 25%, boosting the company's financial resilience.

⭕ Cybersecurity Sentinel:
Polished: I lead a dynamic cybersecurity team, fortifying our digital fortress against evolving threats. Our proactive approach ensures data integrity, and client trust remains strong.

⭕ Service Delivery Dynamo:
Articulated: Our tailored solutions ensure seamless product deployment, enhancing user experience and elevating customer satisfaction to unprecedented levels.
 
The origin is unclear, but my favourite story is the one reported by “Pitcherific”.

Elisha Otis made an emergency brake system for elevators. Since he had some problems selling his idea, he thought of staging a live demonstration. 

First he had an elevator built in the middle of a conference room.

Then, while he was riding the elevator, he had people cut the main cable just to show how safe his brakes were.

He was unarmed and showed off his idea.

The first elevator pitch!

Your elevator pitch is your chance to make a lasting impression. Craft it carefully to showcase the value you bring to the table, whether it's a project, an idea, or your professional expertise.

Elevate your communication skills!
