Public speaking in a second language can be intimidating

Especially when you don’t have a clear strategy

If you are a Non-Native IT professionals 
You might be struggling with one of these challenges:

𝗟𝗼𝗴𝗶𝗰𝗮𝗹 𝗙𝗹𝗼𝘄 – 𝗚𝘂𝗶𝗱𝗶𝗻𝗴 𝗬𝗼𝘂𝗿 𝗔𝘂𝗱𝗶𝗲𝗻𝗰𝗲 🕺
Sequence and “Magic Words” to move through the slides (like a dancer)

𝗜𝗻𝘁𝗼𝗻𝗮𝘁𝗶𝗼𝗻 𝗮𝗻𝗱 𝗘𝗺𝗽𝗵𝗮𝘀𝗶𝘀 – 𝗕𝗿𝗶𝗻𝗴𝗶𝗻𝗴 𝗬𝗼𝘂𝗿 𝗪𝗼𝗿𝗱𝘀 𝘁𝗼 𝗟𝗶𝗳𝗲 🗣️ 
Natural flow, pauses, rhythm to avoid sounding flat (and boring)

𝗗𝗲𝗹𝗶𝘃𝗲𝗿𝗶𝗻𝗴 𝘄𝗶𝘁𝗵 𝗖𝗼𝗻𝗳𝗶𝗱𝗲𝗻𝗰𝗲 𝗮𝗻𝗱 𝗔𝘂𝘁𝗵𝗼𝗿𝗶𝘁𝘆 😎
Body, gestures, volume to engage your audience (and thrill them)
