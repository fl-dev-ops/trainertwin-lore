Many years ago, I was in a maze without a map ꡙ‍

I thought I knew English.
I had taken a standard English course: grammar, vocabulary.

But when I arrived in 𝗗𝘂𝗯𝗹𝗶𝗻… everything changed.

People used a “different” type of English.

The Irish had their own expressions, rhythm, and way of speaking.
And they kept telling me I would be “𝘨𝘳𝘢𝘯𝘥”

At work, in an American multinational, the English was even stranger: business jargon, buzzwords, idioms.

“𝘏𝘦𝘺 𝘋𝘢𝘯𝘪𝘦𝘭𝘦, 𝘐’𝘭𝘭 𝘱𝘪𝘯𝘨 𝘺𝘰𝘶 𝘭𝘢𝘵𝘦𝘳 𝘵𝘰 𝘢𝘴𝘴𝘦𝘴𝘴 𝘵𝘩𝘦 𝘨𝘰-𝘭𝘪𝘷𝘦 𝘴𝘵𝘳𝘢𝘵𝘦𝘨𝘺 𝘢𝘯𝘥 𝘷𝘢𝘭𝘪𝘥𝘢𝘵𝘦 𝘵𝘩𝘦 𝘣𝘢𝘭𝘭𝘱𝘢𝘳𝘬 𝘧𝘪𝘨𝘶𝘳𝘦”

It felt like I had to learn three languages at the same time.
 
And honestly? I struggled.

𝗜 𝘄𝗮𝘀 𝗳𝗿𝘂𝘀𝘁𝗿𝗮𝘁𝗲𝗱.

And of course I was getting nowhere.

But once I learned to let go? Everything changed.

I took a step back and realized that real English isn’t just grammar and vocabulary.
