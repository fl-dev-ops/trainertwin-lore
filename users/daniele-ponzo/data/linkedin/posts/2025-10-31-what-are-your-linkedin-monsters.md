What are your LinkedIn monsters?

Halloween 🎃 is here, time to talk about our horror stories.

Let's make it very relevant and talk about "LinkedIn Monsters".

It's a journey into horror and fear...sinister people and spooky relationships 😱 

Here are my top 4 monsters:

👻 𝕿𝖍𝖊 𝖌𝖍𝖔𝖘𝖙: Have you ever seen one? They send you a message, so you think you've seen them, but then they disappear again.

🤡 𝕿𝖍𝖊 𝕮𝖑𝖔𝖜𝖓: Do you know "fake it until you make it?" Well, I'm afraid it doesn't always work. Creating a parallel reality here on LinkedIn might be ok for a while, but eventually the make up will fade...

Don't leave me alone with my monsters.😈 Tell me: what's your worst nightmare here on LinkedIn?
