Cognitive load theory helps us communicate complex ideas with ease! 🤯💭

As a coach, understanding cognitive load theory can be beneficial in several ways:

- Tailoring instruction to the learner and avoid overloading them with too much information.

- Designing effective training materials by reducing irrelevant information.

- Providing constant feedback to improve performance, and enhance learning.
