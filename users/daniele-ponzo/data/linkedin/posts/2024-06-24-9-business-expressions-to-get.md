9 Business Expressions to get you started in an international job.
 
Have you just started working for a multinational company?

Or maybe you’re being promoted to a global role?

There are some work-related words that are essential for you to know.

Why?

Because they are used daily.

And because nobody is going to explain or translate them for you in the office.

So, here’s a quick list (with examples) of some of the most common words:

1. Unemployed
Definition: Not having a job. 
Example: "After the company downsized, several IT professionals found themselves unemployed and started looking for new opportunities."

2. Self-Employed
Definition: Working for oneself as a freelancer or the owner of a business rather than for an employer. 
Example: "After years in corporate IT, Sarah decided to become self-employed and started her own cybersecurity consulting firm."

3. Part-Time
Definition: Working fewer hours than a full-time job, often less than 35 hours a week. 
Example: "To balance his studies and work, John took a part-time position as a junior developer at a tech startup."

4. Temporary
Definition: Working for a limited period. 
Example: "During the project implementation phase, we hired a temporary IT specialist to help manage the increased workload."

5. Work For
Definition: To be employed by someone or an organization. 
Example: "I work for a leading software development company, focusing on project management and team coordination."

6. To Be in Charge of
Definition: To have control or responsibility for something. 
Example: "As the IT manager, I am in charge of overseeing the network infrastructure and ensuring system security."

7. To Be Responsible For
Definition: To have a duty to take care of something or someone. 
Example: "I am responsible for the performance and development of my team, making sure they meet our project deadlines."

8. To Be Sacked (or Fired)
Definition: To be dismissed from a job. 
Example: "Unfortunately, due to repeated security breaches, the IT director was sacked for failing to protect company data."

9. Set Up a Company
Definition: To start a new business. 
Example: "After gaining extensive experience in software development, I decided to set up a company specializing in custom application solutions for businesses."
