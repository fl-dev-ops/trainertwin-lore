Why I stopped focusing on formal education and you should too.
 
Exactly 20 years ago I started working in a multinational company.
 
In a new field (IT)
 
In a new country (Ireland)
 
Back then, I was obsessed with degrees, certifications, diplomas.
 
This meant that I didn’t have the mental freedom to speak:
 
✖  Speak up in meetings (I didn’t have my Cambridge diploma).
✖  Speak and present in front of the client (I didn’t have a degree in sales).
✖  Speak out in my 1:1 and be clear on my growth plan (I didn’t have a formal MBA)
 
In one word?
 
“Stuck”
 
When I get my certification, I’ll start speaking...
 
If I get my diploma, I’ll go for a job interview...
 
So, how did I get myself “unstuck”✌️?
 
By applying a simple, yet powerful strategy:
 
Reverse the order🔄
 
✅ First start speaking, then level up.
✅ Work closer to experienced leaders and learn while doing (hands-on).
✅ Get a mentor, get the exposure, apply for senior positions, fail, learn, apply again.
 
And, don’t forget: celebrate your wins! Treat yourself when you reach your targets🎉.
 
If you want to have a global role.
 
You might experience the same.
 
But the right strategy can make the difference.
 
The result? I got the Leadership position I wanted, I coached IT pros in my company, and I have now made this my own job.
 
So, here is one piece of advice:
 
Practice, practice and… practice.
 
I’d love to have your opinion: formal education or learn by doing (or a blend)? 
 
P.S. yeah, that’s me cycling…I haven’t read a manual and I haven’t followed a course on “how to cycle”. I was lucky enough to have a mentor (my dad) to help me and then…practice, practice, practice.
