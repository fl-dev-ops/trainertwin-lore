  👉 I used to believe I needed perfect English before I could speak up, especially in meetings.
  So I stayed quiet.

  I thought I was playing it safe… but really, I was holding myself back.
  I didn’t get noticed.
  I had no visibility.

  And I missed out on the more interesting projects. Not because I lacked skills, but because I wasn’t showing up with my voice.

It’s not that you don’t know English.

You read it. You understand it.
Sometimes you even rehearse conversations in your mind
In perfect English!

But when it’s time to actually speak… things fall apart.🥀

You freeze.
Your mind goes blank.
You start searching for the right word.
You try to sound formal or correct… and then feel disconnected or unsure.

The moment passes. You stay silent. Again.
