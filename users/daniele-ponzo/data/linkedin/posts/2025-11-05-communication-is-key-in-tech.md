Communication is key in tech teams!


It's a foundation, not a may-be-topic. Let's discuss how we can improve communication in tech teams, encompassing all levels from junior to senior.

Our tech industry is still too focused on managing as tech lead, PM, or PO, especially when it comes to tickets. 

What happens if we focus again on talking to each other instead of top-down? 
It's not just a great way to work as an IC, but a great strategy as a real leader.
