The best developers don't always become the most influential ones.

Of course, traditional technical skills still matter.

But they're no longer the only thing that makes you valuable.

AI is changing how software is built.

Global teams are changing how software is delivered.

And that means communication is becoming a bigger competitive advantage than ever.

Think about code.

Good code doesn't just compile.

It's easy to read.

Easy to maintain.

Easy to build upon.

Communication works the same way.

It's not enough to know the grammar.

Or have a large vocabulary.

Your ideas need to be clear.

Structured.

Easy for colleagues, stakeholders and clients to understand.

Especially when you're working across countries, cultures and time zones.

That's when something interesting happens.

You stop being the quiet developer in the meeting.

And you become the person people trust to explain the solution.

That's exactly what Emiliano Pisu and I will be discussing this Thursday.

Not whether AI will replace developers.

But what makes developers even more valuable in the age of AI.

Because the future belongs to professionals who combine technical expertise with clear communication.
