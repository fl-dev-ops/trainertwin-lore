You need to have perfect English to get an international career.

Well, no, you don’t.

So, let’s rephrase it:

You need a good level of English.

And then you need training.

Very much like an athlete:

Consistent, purposeful practice. 🏋️‍♀️

This has been a beacon in my journey.

Because I’ve been there.

Self-doubts, missing words, fear…

The truth is:

You don't have to endlessly search for the "perfect" moment to start. 🚀

Focus on the skills you need at work, instead:
 
 🗣️ Speaking up in a meeting.
 📈 Presenting with confidence.
 ☎️ Contributing to a conference call.
 🧑‍🎓 Studying for your IT certificate.

Find a bit of bravery.

And put yourself out there.

The right opportunities will come for you.
