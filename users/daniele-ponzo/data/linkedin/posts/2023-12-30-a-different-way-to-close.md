A different way to close 2023...

This year my holidays have been a bit unconventional.

No Christmas tree.

No travelling,

No big party.

But a lot of joy and excitement...in a different way!

Why?

Well, I'm moving house!🏡

Packing, wrapping, sorting and (occasionally 😅) breaking.

With my little boy around (helping in his own way)

These have been my last few days.

So, surely this is a stressful time.

But also one filled with happiness and gratitude.

I'm lucky to live in a place I love.

Sorrounded by people who love me.

And who help me every day!

So, this is the best way to say goodbye to 2023.

And welcome 2024.🎇

Lots of things to celebrate already!

So, wherever you are and whatever you're doing,  I wish you an exciting start.

Take good care!

P.S. talking about "random acts of kindness" my neighbour lent me his "vintage" FIAT van to move some of my stuff. 🚐
Loved the thrilling driving experience 😄
