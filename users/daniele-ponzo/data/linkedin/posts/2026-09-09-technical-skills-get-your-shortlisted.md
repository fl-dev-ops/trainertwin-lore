Technical skills get your shortlisted

Communication gets you hired

For years, professionals believed that improving their career meant learning another programming language, earning another certification, or mastering another framework.
  
Those things still matter.
  
But the rules have changed.
  
Today, your next opportunity might come from another country.
  
Your manager could be in the UK.
  
Your customer in France.
  
Your teammates in India, Poland or Nigeria.
  
The market is no longer local.
  
Your communication can't be either.
  
Most people think the answer is simply:
  
"Improve your English."
  
I think that's only half the story.
  
English opens the door.
  
Communication moves your career forward.
  
Because global companies don't only look for people who can solve problems.
  
→ They look for people who can explain those solutions.
  
→ Present them with confidence.
  
→ Influence decisions.
  
→ Build trust across cultures.
  
The professionals who progress fastest aren't always the ones with the highest English level.
  
They're the ones who can make complex ideas easy to understand.
  
That's what creates visibility.
  
That's what builds leadership.
  
That's what opens international opportunities.
  
Technical expertise gets you noticed.
  
Communication makes you unforgettable.
  
I'm curious...
  
If an international opportunity landed in your inbox tomorrow...
  
what would worry you more:
  
the technical interview... or communicating confidently in English?
