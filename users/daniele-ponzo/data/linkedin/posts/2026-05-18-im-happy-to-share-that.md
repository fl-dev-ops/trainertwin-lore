A beautifully curated and genuinely insightful edition.

In the article, I reflect on what my years in corporate Learning & Development taught me about how people really grow.

Not just through training sessions.

But through:

• conversations
• mentoring
• feedback
• reflection
• confidence-building
• real-world application

One idea sits at the centre of the article:

People don’t learn when you tell them what to do.
They learn when they reflect on what they’ve done.

That lesson has shaped the way I teach, coach, and design learning experiences today, especially with professionals who need to communicate clearly and confidently in English at work.

Corporate L&D taught me structure.

Education taught me empathy.

Coaching taught me confidence.

And modern learning needs all three.
