When I came across Daniele Ponzo’s profile, I was curious about his story. What struck me wasn’t only his 13+ years in IT, but how his struggles became the foundation of his mission — and later his company.

Born in Rome, Daniele left Italy to work for an American company in Ireland. Determined to grow, he mastered grammar and earned certifications. His skills improved, but something was missing.

Two moments gave him clarity:
🔹 At a corporate party, he overheard managers say, “He works really well, but should be more extroverted.” They meant him.
🔹 Later, in a job interview, an unexpected question broke his flow — polished preparation wasn’t enough.

Both moments cut deep but also lit the path forward. Daniele realized communication is about connection: presence, culture, and showing the person behind the skills. Soon, his company asked him to mentor others — because he had made the journey himself.

The word communication comes from the Latin communicare — “to share, to make common.” That is exactly what Daniele does: helping people move from isolation to connection, from being technically correct to being truly heard.

Through his HACK strategy — Hone, Action, Collaboration, Kudos — Daniele has guided more than 500 students (including 80+ IT & Tech professionals) to turn the fear of being misunderstood into clarity and confidence. The “Action” part is key: you can’t learn to swim by reading about swimming, and you can’t improve your English without speaking it.

💬 “Your technical skills may open the door, but it’s your voice that gets you a seat at the table.”

What makes him so effective is empathy. Colleagues often told him he had a superpower: making complex things simple. That ability comes from putting himself in other people’s shoes — turning communication into trust, presence, and courage.

Daniele also shares these lessons through The Non-Native Leader - Podcast, where non-native leaders open up about their journeys, proving that perfection doesn’t exist — and isn’t needed. And he has just published his first book offering strategies, tools, and exercises for professionals who need to speak English at work. 

🙏 Thank you, Daniele, for talking values with me and showing that empowerment through communication isn’t abstract — it’s the value that turns language from a barrier into a bridge.
