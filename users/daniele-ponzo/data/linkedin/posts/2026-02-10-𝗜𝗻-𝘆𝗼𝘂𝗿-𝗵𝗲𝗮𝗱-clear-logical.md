𝗜𝗻 𝘆𝗼𝘂𝗿 𝗵𝗲𝗮𝗱:
🧠 Clear. Logical. Well-structured. Senior.

𝗢𝘂𝘁 𝗹𝗼𝘂𝗱 𝗶𝗻 𝘁𝗵𝗲 𝗺𝗲𝗲𝘁𝗶𝗻𝗴:
😐 “So… maybe… what I was thinking is… we could possibly…”

Sound familiar?

Most IT & Tech professionals don’t struggle with ideas.
They struggle with how those ideas come out under pressure.

Nerves?
Lack of English?
Impostor Syndrome?
 
I think this is what happens when 𝗲𝘅𝗽𝗲𝗿𝘁 𝘁𝗵𝗶𝗻𝗸𝗶𝗻𝗴 meets 𝗿𝗲𝗮𝗹-𝘁𝗶𝗺𝗲 𝘀𝗽𝗲𝗮𝗸𝗶𝗻𝗴…
without structure.

In your head, everything is connected.
Out loud, it comes out all at once.

That’s why technically strong people sometimes sound less senior than they actually are, especially in high-stakes workplace English.

Same expertise.
Same experience.
Different delivery.

And delivery is what people respond to.

This is where 𝗹𝗲𝗮𝗱𝗲𝗿𝘀𝗵𝗶𝗽-𝗹𝗲𝘃𝗲𝗹 𝗰𝗼𝗺𝗺𝘂𝗻𝗶𝗰𝗮𝘁𝗶𝗼𝗻 makes the difference.
Not bigger words.
Not perfect grammar.

Because in meetings, people don’t hear what you know.
They hear what you can 𝗹𝗮𝗻𝗱.

And the irony?

The moment you finally stop talking…
someone else says your idea (in simpler words)
and the room goes: “𝘠𝘦𝘴. 𝘛𝘩𝘢𝘵’𝘴 𝘪𝘵.” 😅

Same idea.
Different delivery.

So here’s the real question:
👉 In your next meeting, will your ideas stay senior in your head...
 or sound senior out loud?
