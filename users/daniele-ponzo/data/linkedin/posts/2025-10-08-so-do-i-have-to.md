So, do I have to become a writer if I want to do business?

Well, not really.

But you need to tell stories.
Yes, storytelling isn’t only for writers. It’s a business skill.

Think about it. Every time you:
💬 Present a new product or idea to a client,
💬 Share your story in a job interview,
💬 Or explain a project update to your manager…

You’re telling a story.
And stories are how we connect.

They help people remember us, trust us, and believe in what we do.

For non-native English speakers, this is even more important.
Because when you tell your story well, with emotion, structure, and clarity
your language stops being a limitation, and becomes your bridge to others.

Here’s how to start improving your storytelling today 👇

1️⃣ 𝗗𝗲𝘀𝗰𝗿𝗶𝗯𝗲 𝗮 𝗰𝗵𝗮𝗹𝗹𝗲𝗻𝗴𝗶𝗻𝗴 𝘀𝗶𝘁𝘂𝗮𝘁𝗶𝗼𝗻.
 Set the stage. What happened? What was at stake? Who was involved?
 People relate to struggle more than perfection.

2️⃣ 𝗦𝗵𝗼𝘄 𝘁𝗵𝗲 𝗰𝗼𝗻𝗳𝗹𝗶𝗰𝘁 𝗮𝗻𝗱 𝗿𝗲𝘀𝗼𝗹𝘂𝘁𝗶𝗼𝗻.
 Every good story has tension: a problem, a challenge, a decision.
 Don’t just say “it was difficult.” Say why and what you did to overcome it.

3️⃣ 𝗨𝘀𝗲 𝘀𝗲𝗻𝘀𝗼𝗿𝘆 𝗱𝗲𝘁𝗮𝗶𝗹𝘀 𝗮𝗻𝗱 𝗲𝗺𝗼𝘁𝗶𝗼𝗻 𝗮𝗱𝗷𝗲𝗰𝘁𝗶𝘃𝗲𝘀.
Don’t be afraid to say: “I felt frustrated,” “the atmosphere was tense,” “the deadline was fast approaching.”
It helps your listener feel your story. And we connect through emotions.

4️⃣ 𝗣𝗿𝗮𝗰𝘁𝗶𝗰𝗲 𝘁𝗵𝗲 𝗹𝗼𝗴𝗶𝗰𝗮𝗹 𝗳𝗹𝗼𝘄.
A beginning (context), a middle (the challenge), and an end (the result or lesson).
You can use real experiences or even imagined situations to practice the flow.
You don’t need to be overly dramatic. But you need to guide your audience and made them feel your story and make it memorable.
It’s how you move from sharing information to inspiring people.
