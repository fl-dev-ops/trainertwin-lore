She read her notes in daily meetings.

Now English feels like freedom.

Meet Alina Kulish.

A software engineer from Ukraine who has built a career working with international teams.

But when she first started working in English, even daily meetings felt intimidating.

She knew her work.

But speaking without prepared notes felt risky.

So she wrote things down.
Prepared her sentences.
And read from paper during meetings.

Then she joined a truly international team.

Different accents.
Different cultures.
Different senses of humour.
And conversations that didn't always follow a script.

Suddenly, being prepared wasn't enough.

She needed to become comfortable with the unexpected.

And that's when she started changing her approach.

Instead of treating English as another obligation, she found ways to enjoy using it.

She joined speaking clubs.
Watched documentaries.
Listened to different accents.
Made English part of her everyday life.

Not just another subject to study.
Something to experience.

And slowly, things started to change.

Not overnight.
Not magically.
