Fluency doesn’t come from theory. It comes from doing.

You can study for years.
Know all the rules.
Even get a certificate that says “Advanced.”

But if you don’t use the language…

If you don’t speak, write, or put yourself out there it’s hard to build real confidence.

That’s why A = 𝗔𝗰𝘁𝗶𝗼𝗻 is the second step in my HACK strategy.

Because English is a skill and skills grow through practice, not perfection.

Here’s what taking action looks like:

▶️ Speaking up in your next meeting, even just to ask a question
Give yourself a small objective. Raise your hand and speak!

▶️ Volunteering to explain one slide in your team presentation
In your next 1:1 ask your manager if you can present in your next team meeting. Something simple.

▶️ Writing and posting something in English here on LinkedIn
Start with short comments. Repost someone's post with your note. Interact and reach out to people to start building up your confidence.

▶️ Recording a short voice message and listening back
Recording yourself (audio or video) is the best way to get more confident when you don't have many opportunities to speak.

No one’s asking you to be perfect.
But you do have to 𝘀𝗵𝗼𝘄 𝘂𝗽.

Even small actions — repeated consistently — change everything.

Remember:
✅ Theory = potential
✅ Action = progress
