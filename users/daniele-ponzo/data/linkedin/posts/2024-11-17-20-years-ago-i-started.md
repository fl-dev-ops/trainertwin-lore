20 years ago I started working in an American Multinational Company.
 
This is how I built my visibility – as a Non-Native English speaker.
 
🔹 Be ok with ok: I started speaking even though my English level was far from perfect. 
 ↳ I gave myself weekly objectives, like preparing a quick update to share in a team meeting. 
 
🔹 Mimic: I took the habit of observing “influencers” at work: managers, mentors, senior leaders, great communicators. 
 ↳ My goal was to get the best intonation, posture, body language.
 
🔹 Polish: I consciously decided to refine my language. Learned the differences between “common” English and “Professional” English. 
 ↳ I wanted to be ready to communicate in every context.
 
🔹 Listen: we all know listening plays a big part in every interaction. Listen carefully to other people, absorb the message and learn how to paraphrase. 
 ↳ My objective was to connect with empathy.
 
🔹Cultural awareness: Different cultures communicate in a different way. I used my observation skills to figure out how people interact. 
 ↳ My goal was to make sure I could get my message across without being misunderstood. 
 
🔹Repetition: I “trained” my communication skills by repeating the phrases and the language I needed in the office. 
 ↳ I wanted to become confident in my meetings, and comfortable when presenting in front of the customer.
 
🔹Pronunciation: nothing to do with accent. We all have an accent; it talks about our history and our culture. Focusing on pronunciation means syllable stress, connected speech, intonation. 
 ↳ I wanted to be understood by everybody.
 
You might think it’s too hard or it’s too late to change now.
 
Remember - it’s NEVER too late to change direction.
 
Start putting yourself out there and give yourself more opportunities.
 
Start with 1% today, 1% tomorrow, 1% the day after… one day things start to build up.
 
So, my question to you is: what are you going to START? I'd love to know your stories.

P.S. If you’re a great professional but struggling with your English, I can help.
