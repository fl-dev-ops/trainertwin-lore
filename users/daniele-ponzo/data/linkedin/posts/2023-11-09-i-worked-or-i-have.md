I WORKED or I HAVE WORKED? I CALLED HER or I HAVE CALLED HER?

Which is correct?

I often get this question from my clients.

Actually, they are both correct…but in different situations.

It’s the “PRESENT PERFECT vs. PAST SIMPLE”. The eternal struggle 👊 !
 
The key here is "WHEN"🕑.
 
If I specify the time, it's PAST SIMPLE (yesterday, last year, 3 minutes ago – SPECIFIC, COMPLETED ACTION): 

👉Yesterday I delivered my presentation. 
👉Last week we met our President.
👉1 year ago I was still in Accounting.
 
If I don't specify the time, it's PRESENT PERFECT (in my life, Never, Recently, so far - I talk about my EXPERIENCE, GENERIC and RELEVANT TO THE PRESENT):

 👉I've worked in Service Delivery, RPA and L&D.
 👉I've never met the CEO.
 👉We've been to Spain on various business trips.
  
The PRESENT PERFECT is particularly useful when you want to explain your expertise. 

For example, in a job interview you can say: 

➡ I have spoken in public many times.
➡I have completed various projects on customer experience.
➡I have successfully planned and delivered customized solutions.

I've always loved this song from “Creedence Clearwater Revival". Can you find the past simple and the present perfect here?
