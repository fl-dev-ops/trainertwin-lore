The most effective way to reach success with your English. ⬇️ 
 
 
Teaming up.
 
Cambridge says 'To Team up': “form a group with other people, in order to do something together”.
 
If you want to make real progress, this is the best strategy.
 
✔ Commitment
 
✔ Dedication
 
✔ The right mindset.
 
This why my support does not stop at the end of our session.
 
And it is not confined to sharing the material and the recordings.
 
I like to establish a meaningful relationship with my clients.
 
I am there for you.
 
I can guide you and provide feedback.
 
I want to make sure you’re progressing towards your goals.
 
One good thing I like doing with my clients is exchanging audio messages.
 
Maybe you don’t have many opportunities to speak English at work but want to practise.
 
Or you have an important meeting coming up and you need to go through your speech and repeat it.
 
Or you simply want to work deeper on your assignments.
 
Whatever the case, I believe this is a productive exchange. This is when we can really work as a team to get you closer to your goal.
 
Remember, there is no magic wand🪄.
 
So, here is the deal:
 
Coaching, Guidance and Support from my part.
 
Commitment and Dedication from yours.
 
This is when the true magic happens 💫 
And you start seeing real progress. ↗
