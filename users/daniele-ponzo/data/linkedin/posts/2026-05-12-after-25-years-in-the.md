After 25 years in the same career…

she walked away.

Same profession.
Same environment.
A strong comfort zone.

And then:

New country.
New direction.
New identity.

That’s what came out of my conversation with Tuba Kizilkan, an expert in Digital Leadership in the latest episode of The Non-Native Leader - Podcast.

At one point, she said something that stayed with me:

“Learning is not only about vocabulary or grammar.
It’s about how people structure ideas and how they think.”

And that’s where everything shifts.

Most professionals focus on language.

But what really changes your career is:

• how you think
• how you structure ideas
• how you communicate
• how you adapt across cultures

And then we touched on something uncomfortable:

The comfort zone.

Is it really comfort…
or is it a cage?

Because staying in the same place for too long feels safe.

But it can also quietly limit your growth.

Another line from the conversation:

“The perfect time will never come.”

No perfect English.
No perfect conditions.
No perfect moment.

Just a decision to move…

and figure things out along the way.

If you’re working in English (or planning to)…

this episode will give you a different perspective.

Not on how to “improve your English”

but on how to think, adapt, and communicate in international environments.
