You know this moment.

You’re in the meeting.
You have the idea.
A good one.

You start building the sentence in your head.

“Ok… how do I say this in English…
Maybe I should phrase it better…
What’s the right word…”

You hesitate.

You change it again.

You try to make it perfect.

And while you’re still structuring it…

Someone else says it.

Same idea.
Cleaner sentence.
Everyone nods.

And you think:

“I knew that.”

This doesn’t happen because you’re not competent.

It happens because your brain is running too many processes at once:

• solving the problem
• translating
• structuring
• checking grammar
• managing how you sound

That’s cognitive load.

And when the load is high, hesitation wins.
