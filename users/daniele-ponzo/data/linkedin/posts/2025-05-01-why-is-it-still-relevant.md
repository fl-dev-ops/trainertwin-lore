Why is it still relevant today to celebrate international worker’s day?

After all, this is an old story.

Well, let’s see where this comes from.

Back in the late 1800’s, people were requesting:

🔸 Shorter workday (no more than 8 hours).
🔸 A fair wage for workers.
🔸 Safe work conditions.
🔸 A stop to child labour.
🔸 Health benefits.
🔸 Support for workers who were injured or retired.

When we read this list, it doesn’t look that old anymore.

For many people around the world, this is still a daily struggle.

🔴 Child labour is still happening today (ILO and UNICEF).
🔴 “Workaholism” is a reality in Europe, UK, USA, where at least 10% of workers are unable to switch off.
🔴 Death from overwork (Karoshi) is not only confined to Japan, but it is now global (WHO).
🔴 Workplace fatalities still happens regularly in Europe 
🔴 Illegal workers are still exploited worldwide (OECD)

And let's not forget the impact AI is going to have on the job market.

So, I believe this is still very relevant today.

I think we should celebrate the success we have achieved. 

All we have accomplished.

And the privilege we have.
