So well prepared…

…that you become spontaneous

Yes, that’s exactly it.

When you want to move to another country, look for another job, get promoted…

The best strategy is to do your homework.

My friend Luca Primavera has changed various jobs in his career.

From hospitality to real estate to Finance in large corporations.

And when you talk to him (and I was lucky enough to talk to him a few weeks ago for my video podcast series 🎤 ), everything seems really easy.

✔️ You go to an interview
✔️ You enjoy the conversation
✔️ You talk nicely, pretending you’re just there for a chat.
✔️ You get the job

But obviously there is much more behind.

In this short video he actually reveals his secret.

Which gets us back right to the beginning of this post:

You can be so well prepared…
…that you become spontaneous.

Being prepared requires energy, time, effort. And strategy.

➡️ to understand the culture
➡️ to Identify the skills and experiences they are looking for
➡️ to practice common questions 

🌟 And ultimately to learn the language of confidence.

(yes, learning English is not enough)

So, get into action 🎬 . Study, practice, speak.

And become spontaneous.

P.S. I’m putting together my new year resolutions, already. 

Maybe it’s a bit too early.

Curious to know: are you already working on your dreams for 2025? 🤔
