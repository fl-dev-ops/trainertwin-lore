How do you communicate your true self?

Take a step back to move forward.

Real growth is uncomfortable. We've heard it many times.

But what does it really mean?

My friend Dariusz Sadowski helped me answer this question.
He shared his experience as a software engineer turned into leader and coach.

Limiting beliefs, stereotypes, roles, labels.

They all contribute to keeping us stuck.🚧

Like a super comfy sofa, we get absorbed in our cocoon.

“I’m an introvert, so I don’t do social events”
“Languages? They are not for me”
“I prefer computers”

And we keep communicating the same linear message:

“I am not going to move from here”

Monochrome.⚫

The reality?

It’s polychrome.🎨

But in order to shine and reach our full potential
We need to take a step back:

Face our limiting beliefs

Face our “comfortable self”

And question it:

“What are my objectives?”
“Where do I want to be?”

Reframe our communication and get rid of ‘labels’.

To discover that there’s more than meets the eye.👀

Over to you now: do you feel monochrome or polychrome? I would love to hear your take on this.
