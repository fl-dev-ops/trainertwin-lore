Did you know there’s a simple trick to getting people’s attention when speaking English?
 
Yes, exactly…be polite! 🙏
 
When you use courteous language, especially in a business environment, you can see English speakers are more willing to listen to you, help you out with enquiries and engage with you. It can be a simple favour from a colleague or a serious question to one of your clients. It doesn’t matter: being polite always does the trick.  

When we begin our conversation, we want to start off on the right foot. 👍
