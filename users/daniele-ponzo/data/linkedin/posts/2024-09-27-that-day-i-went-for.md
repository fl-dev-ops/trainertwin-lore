That day I went for a walk, and I was transported into a nightmare.

A proper one💀.

It was a few years ago, I was walking around in Rome as a tourist and I wanted to see a new place. I had already seen all the standard landmarks and I was looking for a new experience. In retrospect, I can say that was definitely something different 😬 .

 “Saint Chrysogonus Church and crypt. I’ve never been here, let’s go!”

One and a half thousand years old, dedicated to an early martyr and full of art. And for a mere €2 you could visit the excavations too. It sounded like the perfect deal, so I went in🏛️.

It started getting weird when I walked in.

The church itself was beautiful full of baroque paintings and monuments. Relics and tombs added to the fashion. But then, I met this interesting character, straight from “the name of the Rose”🤔.

He gave me a funny look, I paid him €2 and went downstairs.

“I’ll switch on the lights for you”, he said. Down I went, magnificent old church, vivid medieval frescoes and ancient sarcophagi. I wanted to take photos, but I realized I had not battery. Climbed up and down, walked around the crypt and then…panic⚫.

Lights off, pitch black, absolutely alone.

Tried to turn on the phone, no luck. Started screaming, nobody answered. I had more than 6 meters of solid rock above me, so banging on the wall was useless too🧱.

Fear became solid, I could almost touch it.

Tried the phone again, I got a quick flash before it died again. I used that to confirm my mental map of the place. And I started walking in the sheer darkness😧.

It really was the only thing to do.

Feeling walls with my hands, waiting for that sarcophagus (I knew it had a different texture). “Found it! Ok, I’m halfway there! Where are the stairs now?” Beyond the ancient altar✝.

I knew the altar was round and that’s how I found it!

I got over it, but I forgot there was a gap, so I fell on the ground. Centuries of dust on my clothes and on my hands. I got back up and I carefully threaded my way along the wall. I was closer!🤞

And there it was, a single drop of light!

Coming from above, the main door above was ajar and I could now see it! It guided me back to the entrance and to the land of the living. It was like being born again✨.

Adrenaline out. I was alive!

A few bruises, dirty clothes and a ghostlike face…and obviously, no explanation on why the lights had simply disappeared🤕.

Resilience? Persistence? Survival instinct?

Talk to me in the comments... What would you have done in this situation?
 
P.S. I have never been back to that church and I’m not sure if should go again…😰
