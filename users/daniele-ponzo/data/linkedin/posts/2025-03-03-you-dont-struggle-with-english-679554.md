You don’t struggle with English for the reasons you think 🤔

Here’s why (and how to fix it).

A few weeks ago I met a professional who studied English for years.

Grammar? 
Vocabulary? 
Certifications? 

But every time he had to speak in a meeting, his mind went blank.

He told me:
"𝘐𝘵'𝘴 𝘯𝘰𝘵 𝘭𝘪𝘬𝘦 𝘐 𝘥𝘰𝘯'𝘵 𝘬𝘯𝘰𝘸 𝘌𝘯𝘨𝘭𝘪𝘴𝘩 𝘢𝘵 𝘢𝘭𝘭... 𝘐 𝘳𝘦𝘢𝘥 𝘪𝘵, 𝘐 𝘵𝘩𝘪𝘯𝘬 𝘪𝘯 𝘌𝘯𝘨𝘭𝘪𝘴𝘩... 𝘣𝘶𝘵 𝘸𝘩𝘦𝘯 𝘐 𝘵𝘳𝘺 𝘵𝘰 𝘴𝘱𝘦𝘢𝘬, 𝘐 𝘴𝘵𝘢𝘳𝘵 𝘴𝘦𝘢𝘳𝘤𝘩𝘪𝘯𝘨 𝘧𝘰𝘳 𝘸𝘰𝘳𝘥𝘴, 𝘢𝘯𝘥 𝘮𝘺 𝘮𝘪𝘯𝘥 𝘫𝘶𝘴𝘵 𝘧𝘳𝘦𝘦𝘻𝘦𝘴."

Sound familiar? 😱 

At first, he thought his problem was lack of knowledge.

But the real issue? 𝗙𝗲𝗮𝗿.

➜ Fear of being judged by colleagues.
➜ Fear of making mistakes.
➜ Fear of not sounding “perfect.”

And this is what keeps so many IT professionals stuck.

You don’t need more grammar books or another vocabulary list.
You need mindset training.

The fastest way to improve your spoken English is to break the perfectionism cycle.

✅ Accept that mistakes are part of learning.
✅ Stop waiting for the “right moment” to start speaking.
✅ Focus on confidence-building, not just language rules.

As a friend recently told me:
"𝘐𝘧 𝘐 𝘩𝘢𝘥 𝘵𝘰 𝘸𝘢𝘪𝘵 𝘧𝘰𝘳 𝘮𝘺 𝘌𝘯𝘨𝘭𝘪𝘴𝘩 𝘵𝘰 𝘣𝘦 𝘱𝘦𝘳𝘧𝘦𝘤𝘵, 𝘐 𝘸𝘰𝘶𝘭𝘥 𝘴𝘵𝘪𝘭𝘭 𝘣𝘦 𝘸𝘢𝘪𝘵𝘪𝘯𝘨 𝘵𝘰 𝘰𝘱𝘦𝘯 𝘮𝘺 𝘮𝘰𝘶𝘵𝘩."

So, let me ask you:

👉 Do you need to improve English as a whole?
Or do you first need to overcome your fear of speaking?
