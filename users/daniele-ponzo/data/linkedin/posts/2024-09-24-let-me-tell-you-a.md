Let me tell you a story about telling a story.

Job interviews, presentations, client meetings. They all have a common ingredient to be successful.

A great story.

Here is how you can build it:

1️⃣  Think of a nice story (conflict resolution, problem)💭
2️⃣  Structure Your Story.🧱
3️⃣  Connect your Ideas.🔗
4️⃣  Add Emotions.🩷

1️⃣ 𝐓𝐡𝐢𝐧𝐤 𝐨𝐟 𝐚 𝐧𝐢𝐜𝐞 𝐬𝐭𝐨𝐫𝐲 𝐚𝐧𝐝 𝐛𝐫𝐞𝐚𝐤 𝐢𝐭 𝐝𝐨𝐰𝐧 𝐢𝐧 4 𝐨𝐫 5 𝐬𝐭𝐞𝐩𝐬

Beginning: something I need to do -> problem -> my solution (description) -> implementation (success)

A customer needed to use my app -> the customer was not happy, he contacted me -> I listened and offered a solution -> the customer was happy.

2️⃣ 𝐬𝐭𝐫𝐮𝐜𝐭𝐮𝐫𝐞 𝐲𝐨𝐮𝐫 𝐬𝐭𝐨𝐫𝐲. 𝐔𝐬𝐞 𝐞𝐟𝐟𝐞𝐜𝐭𝐢𝐯𝐞 𝐬𝐞𝐪𝐮𝐞𝐧𝐜𝐞𝐫𝐬 (𝐅𝐈𝐑𝐒𝐓, 𝐈𝐍𝐈𝐓𝐈𝐀𝐋𝐋𝐘, 𝐓𝐇𝐄𝐍, 𝐀𝐅𝐓𝐄𝐑 𝐓𝐇𝐀𝐓, 𝐅𝐈𝐍𝐀𝐋𝐋𝐘, 𝐀𝐒 𝐀 𝐑𝐄𝐒𝐔𝐋𝐓)

INITIALLY, a customer needed to use my App -> they were not happy with the service, they contacted me -> THEN, I listened carefully to the complaint. -> AFTER THAT I offered a solution -> FINALLY the customer was satisfied with the solution.

3️⃣ 𝐜𝐨𝐧𝐧𝐞𝐜𝐭 𝐲𝐨𝐮𝐫 𝐢𝐝𝐞𝐚𝐬 𝐮𝐬𝐢𝐧𝐠 𝐥𝐢𝐧𝐤𝐢𝐧𝐠 𝐰𝐨𝐫𝐝𝐬 (SO; BUT; AND; OR; BECAUSE)

INITIALLY, a customer needed to use my App, SO they downloaded it from our website BUT there was something wrong with it -> they were not happy with the service, SO they contacted me -> THEN, I listened carefully to the complaint, BECAUSE I wanted to get more details. -> AFTER THAT I offered a solution AND apologized for the inconvenience -> FINALLY the customer was satisfied with the solution.

4️⃣ 𝐀𝐝𝐝 𝐄𝐦𝐨𝐭𝐢𝐨𝐧𝐬 𝐮𝐬𝐢𝐧𝐠 𝐞𝐟𝐟𝐞𝐜𝐭𝐢𝐯𝐞 𝐰𝐨𝐫𝐝𝐬 𝐭𝐨 𝐝𝐞𝐬𝐜𝐫𝐢𝐛𝐞 𝐭𝐡𝐞𝐦. Extra TIP: don’t always use good, bad, happy…

INITIALLY, a customer needed to use my App, so they downloaded it from our website BUT there was something wrong with it and they felt 𝐟𝐫𝐮𝐬𝐭𝐫𝐚𝐭𝐞𝐝 -> they were not happy with the service, SO they contacted me. I was 𝐰𝐨𝐫𝐫𝐢𝐞𝐝 as I wanted to provide excellent service -> THEN, I listened carefully to the complaint, BECAUSE I wanted to get more details. Now I felt 𝐜𝐨𝐧𝐟𝐢𝐝𝐞𝐧𝐭 I could provide some help -> AFTER THAT I offered a solution AND apologized for the inconvenience. I was 𝐞𝐱𝐜𝐢𝐭𝐞𝐝 because the customer seemed to appreciate my plan -> FINALLY the customer was satisfied with the solution and I felt 𝐫𝐞𝐥𝐢𝐞𝐯𝐞𝐝: I solved a tricky problem!.

𝐁𝐎𝐍𝐔𝐒 𝐓𝐈𝐏𝐒:

Practice using tenses: Use the 𝐩𝐚𝐬𝐭 𝐬𝐢𝐦𝐩𝐥𝐞 for completed actions, the 𝐩𝐚𝐬𝐭 𝐜𝐨𝐧𝐭𝐢𝐧𝐮𝐨𝐮𝐬 for ongoing actions, and the 𝐩𝐫𝐞𝐬𝐞𝐧𝐭 𝐬𝐢𝐦𝐩𝐥𝐞 for facts.

“I worked hard on the solution. While I was listening to the customer, I wrote down the key points. I am pleased with my approach”
