How to Make Absolutely No Progress with Your English (A Friendly Guide)

If you’re truly committed to staying stuck with your English, don’t worry,  you’re not alone.

Here’s a foolproof plan to ensure you don’t improve, don’t feel confident, and definitely never speak up in meetings.

Reading this will guarantee that you:

✔️ Don’t join that meeting.
✔️ Don’t apply for that job.
✔️ Definitely don’t speak up in front of other people

Remember: it’s much safer to talk to yourself in your head where no one can judge you. 
(And where, conveniently, you always sound fluent.)

Bonus tip: If you want to remain extra-stuck, remind yourself daily that your English “still isn’t good enough.” That's the best way to avoid progress 😉 

P.S. make sure you follow me for more tips!
