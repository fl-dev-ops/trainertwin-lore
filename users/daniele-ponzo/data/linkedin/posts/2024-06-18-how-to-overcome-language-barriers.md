How to overcome language barriers and become a successful entrepreneur.

In a world which is faster, quicker and more immediate than ever, people often forget the value of patience.

Patience can be a true game-changer.

It helps you:

👉 Overcome language barriers.

👉Build true and meaningful connections.

👉Learn the skills you need to succeed at work.

👉Have a great work-life balance enjoying family and entrepreneurship.

I’ve discussed the value of patience with Sandra Stynes, Co-Founder & Executive Director at Stellar Corporate Housing, in my latest episode of “The Non-Native Leader” video podcast 🎤 .

Her career history is non-linear, she’s a career switcher and this deeply resonates with me.

She’s been in tourism and IT and she’s now founded her own company. All of this while building a wonderful family.

She started small, moving from Spain to Ireland she had to learn the ropes.

🔸 Language
🔸 Culture
🔸 Work

But she's never lost her spirit and her positive approach (and her smile) 🙂 .

So, if you want to know on how to deal with “strange” English accents, how to overcome language barriers and how to successfully switch career and build your own business, watch the full episode.
