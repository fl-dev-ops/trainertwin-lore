If you translate in your head,
you’ll always freeze when speaking..

Here’s how to start thinking in English instead.

One of the biggest reasons people freeze when speaking English isn’t grammar or vocabulary.

It’s this 👇

They translate every sentence in their head before speaking.

The result?
• you lose momentum
• you forget what you wanted to say
• the sentence collapses halfway
• silence takes over

So what’s the alternative?

👉 Simplify. Structure. Connect.
Let me show you a practical way to do it.

𝗦𝘁𝗲𝗽 𝟭: 𝗦𝘁𝗮𝗿𝘁 𝗳𝗿𝗼𝗺 𝗶𝗱𝗲𝗮𝘀, 𝗻𝗼𝘁 𝘀𝗲𝗻𝘁𝗲𝗻𝗰𝗲𝘀
Before worrying about “correct English”, focus on ideas.

pick a topic and write down 𝟯 𝘀𝗶𝗺𝗽𝗹𝗲 𝗶𝗱𝗲𝗮𝘀
(single words or short phrases are enough)

They can be sequenced logically (first, then, after that, finally)

No grammar. No full sentences. Just meaning.

𝗦𝘁𝗲𝗽 𝟮: 𝗔𝗱𝗱 𝗦𝘂𝗯-𝗶𝗱𝗲𝗮𝘀 (𝗰𝗮𝘂𝘀𝗲–𝗲𝗳𝗳𝗲𝗰𝘁 𝗼𝗿 𝗯𝗮𝗰𝗸𝗴𝗿𝗼𝘂𝗻𝗱)
Next, expand each main idea with sub-ideas:
 • explanations
 • examples
 • consequences

Think in terms of:
 • main idea (1)
 • sub-ideas (1.1, 1.2)

𝗦𝘁𝗲𝗽 𝟯: 𝗖𝗼𝗻𝗻𝗲𝗰𝘁 𝗶𝗱𝗲𝗮𝘀 𝘄𝗶𝘁𝗵 𝘀𝗶𝗺𝗽𝗹𝗲 𝗹𝗶𝗻𝗸𝗲𝗿𝘀
Now connect those ideas using basic linking words:
➡️ and
➡️ but
➡️ because
➡️ so
➡️ to

For example:
• Problem → because of X
• Delay → so Y happened
• Solution → to avoid Z
This creates a mini 𝗺𝗶𝗻𝗱 𝗺𝗮𝗽 for your speech.

𝗦𝘁𝗲𝗽 𝟰: 𝗨𝘀𝗲 𝗮 𝘀𝗶𝗺𝗽𝗹𝗲 𝗴𝗿𝗶𝗱
Use a table like this:
• Main idea
• Linker
• Extra idea

You don’t memorize sentences.
You follow a 𝗹𝗼𝗴𝗶𝗰.
And that logic becomes your speaking notes.

The result?
✔ shorter sentences
✔ clearer message
✔ less panic
✔ more control
✔ no mental translation

At first, this is a conscious process.
But with practice, it becomes 𝗮𝘂𝘁𝗼𝗺𝗮𝘁𝗶𝗰.

That’s how you rewire your brain to start thinking directly in English.
