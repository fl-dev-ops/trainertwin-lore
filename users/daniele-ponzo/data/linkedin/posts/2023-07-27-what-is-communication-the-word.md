What is communication?🗣

The word itself comes from the Latin word “𝐜𝐨𝐦𝐦ū𝐧𝐢𝐜ā𝐫𝐞” which means: “to share”.

So, it is not a one-way system.➡️ ⬅️

Communication is a shared process where (at least) two people exchange information (oral or written).

And the Encoding part of the message is fundamental.

Here the sender uses words or non-verbal methods such as symbols, signs, body gestures.🙆‍♂️

The sender chooses the medium to transmit the message.

And then the receiver interprets the message through the “decoding” phase.

So, when we communicate, it is not only the words we use, but how we encode our message (body, symbols) and how the receiver decodes it.

Decoding is linked to the receiver personal experience.

It is pivotal that the receiver understands the message in exactly the same way as it was intended by the sender.

We must remember that noise (environment, disturbance) can influence the message we want to convey.

And here we get to the final step.

Feedback.

This tells us what the receiver has understood.

Feedback helps shape the communication, brings new ideas and completes the message.

So, it is critical that we keep this in mind every time we communicate:

✔️It’s not only the words we use that shape our message.
✔️Non-verbal communication is equally important for our message.
✔️We need to be able to listen actively to the feedback received.
✔️We close the loop by incorporating the feedback in our message.

Only when we go through the full loop, we can achieve real communication.🔁

Let’s keep this in mind whenever we work on communication, language improvement, confidence building.

And also when we post on social media.
