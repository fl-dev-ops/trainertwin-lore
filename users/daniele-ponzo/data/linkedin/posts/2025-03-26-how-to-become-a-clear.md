  If your message feels complicated, chances are you’re trying to say too much at once.
  𝗙𝗶𝘅: Break long sentences into short, clear ideas.

  🔹 Instead of this:
   "Since we’ve identified several security vulnerabilities in the system, we should prioritize fixing them to prevent potential breaches and ensure compliance with industry standards."

  ✅ Say this:
   "We found security vulnerabilities. We need to fix them now to prevent breaches. This will also help us stay compliant."

  Why it works:
  Each sentence has one clear idea.
  Listeners process information faster.
  You sound confident and direct.

  Try this in your next meeting or email!
