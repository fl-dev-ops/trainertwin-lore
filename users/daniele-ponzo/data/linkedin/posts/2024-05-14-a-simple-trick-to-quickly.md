A simple trick to quickly uplift your English:

It’s easier than we think 😉.

You want to make sure you leave a great impression.

It could be a meeting with a potential client.

A call with a colleague.

Or a zoom meeting with your new boss.

It doesn’t matter. 

A simple language shift will do the trick.

Change a common verb ➡️ to a professional sounding one.

For example: “help” ➡️ “assist”.

"I will assist the team in troubleshooting the network issue."
