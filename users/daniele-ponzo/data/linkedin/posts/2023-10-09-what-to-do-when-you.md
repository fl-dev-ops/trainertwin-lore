What to do when you are in an awkward situation at work

We have all had those tough moments. 😬 

Delivering bad news.

Dealing with a difficult team member.

Handling a complaint.

Sometimes we just want to disappear. 👻 

And be somewhere else.

But it’s our job. 

And there are times we can’t avoid those awkward situations.

So, if you need to deal with them in English, use the examples in the carousel below 👇 .

You might be with very important customer.

Or with a long-known team member.

That’s why I’ve included an “informal”🙍‍♀️and a “formal”👩‍💼version, so you can adapt the phrases to the context.  

P.S. what kind of situation is the worst for you? I’d love to hear some of your “awkward” experiences.
