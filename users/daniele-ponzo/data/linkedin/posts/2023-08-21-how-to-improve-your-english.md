How to improve your English even if you’re too busy

 
“I don’t have time to study English”;

“I’m too busy with all my tasks at work”;

“As soon as I finish this project, I will work on my English”.

I understand you have a busy life.

So you keep postponing English improvement.

What if I told you it only takes 10 minutes a day?

And that you can work with your best buddy?

Wondering who that is?

It’s YOU, of course.

There are a number of exercises you can do alone.

Here are some ideas:

✅𝐒𝐨𝐥𝐨 𝐑𝐞𝐚𝐝-𝐀𝐥𝐨𝐮𝐝 𝐏𝐫𝐚𝐜𝐭𝐢𝐜𝐞:
Choose a book, article, or any text in English that interests you. 
Read it aloud to yourself. 
Focus on enunciating words clearly, maintaining a steady pace. 
👉Improve your fluency and get comfortable with the rhythm of spoken English.
 
✅𝐑𝐞𝐜𝐨𝐫𝐝𝐞𝐝 𝐌𝐨𝐧𝐨𝐥𝐨𝐠𝐮𝐞𝐬:
Pick a topic you're passionate about. 
Record yourself speaking on that topic for a few minutes. 
Listen to the recording and evaluate your tone, clarity, and fluency. 
👉Identify areas for improvement and track your progress over time.
 
✅𝐃𝐞𝐬𝐜𝐫𝐢𝐛𝐞 𝐈𝐦𝐚𝐠𝐞𝐬 𝐨𝐫 𝐎𝐛𝐣𝐞𝐜𝐭𝐬:
Take a picture or choose an object from your surroundings. 
Describe it, covering its appearance, purpose, and relevant details. 
👉Enhance your ability to think on your feet, expand your vocabulary, and improve your descriptive skills.
 
✅𝐍𝐚𝐫𝐫𝐚𝐭𝐞 𝐏𝐞𝐫𝐬𝐨𝐧𝐚𝐥 𝐒𝐭𝐨𝐫𝐢𝐞𝐬:
Recall a personal experience or story from your life. 
Practice narrating it, focusing on the sequence of events, emotions, and key details. 
👉Connect with the language on a more personal level and boost your storytelling skills.

So, no more excuses!

Grab that mirror and start working with your lovely self.
