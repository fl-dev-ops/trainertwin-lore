What is leadership?

What is a leader?

Is it someone who’s responsible? Or accountable?

Do they have be authoritative? Open? Rigid?

These are all interesting questions, and they can be answered in many different ways…

In the last few days, I’ve had the pleasure of discussing this with Dario Pugliese. He comes from a “project management” perspective. You will find his point of view in his post (link in the first comment). ⬇

Here is my take on the concept of “Leadership”:

I am passionate about etymology as it allows me to go to the “root” of a word. 🌱

According to the “online etymology dictionary”, the word comes from the Old English "𝘭æ𝘥𝘦𝘳𝘦":

first or most prominent, from the verb “𝘭æ𝘥𝘢𝘯"to guide, conduct".

This confirms the meaning of leader as one who is responsible for guiding a team, a reference point. Team members look at the leader as the most prominent figure. 👩‍💼

Something I found really interesting is that the verb “to lead” has a close connection with the old German "𝘭𝘪ð𝘢𝘯", "to travel”.🗺️

I believe this contributes to shaping the idea of leader. Guiding, being responsible, but with a clear goal!

A leader is someone who can show the way, very much like an explorer. A leader can also find new ways and accompany the rest of the team.🧭

In this sense, a leader must be open-minded, get feedback from people around him, but also from the environment if they are to discover new ways of doing things.

So, an effective leader must be able to inspire people, must be flexible and ready to adapt. And they should always remember to keep the focus on the journey.

While travelling there are obstacles, delays, detours, unexpected issues. Very much like our everyday life.

This is why we need strong, flexible and adaptive leaders to help shape organizations and, ultimately the future world we want.
