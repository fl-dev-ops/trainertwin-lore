The 4 biggest mistakes people make when speaking English

Have you ever found yourself stuck mid-sentence, feeling like your brain suddenly switched off?
Yes, it's a common feeling!

Most people don’t struggle because they “don’t know English.”
They struggle because they’re using it the 𝘸𝘳𝘰𝘯𝘨 𝘸𝘢𝘺.

Here are 4 common traps (and how to avoid them 👇):

❌ 𝗢𝘃𝗲𝗿𝘁𝗵𝗶𝗻𝗸𝗶𝗻𝗴 𝗲𝘃𝗲𝗿𝘆 𝘄𝗼𝗿𝗱
You start speaking… and suddenly your brain becomes a live coding debugger.
“Wait, is that the correct tense? Should I use 𝘩𝘢𝘷𝘦 𝘣𝘦𝘦𝘯 or 𝘩𝘢𝘥 𝘣𝘦𝘦𝘯?”
By the time you decide, the conversation has moved on… twice.
▶️  Tip: Focus on clarity, not grammar perfection. Say it simply and keep the flow.

❌ 𝗧𝗿𝗮𝗻𝘀𝗹𝗮𝘁𝗶𝗻𝗴 𝘄𝗼𝗿𝗱 𝗯𝘆 𝘄𝗼𝗿𝗱
Ever tried to speak English while mentally translating from your native language?
It’s like running Windows inside a Mac. Everything slows down.
▶️  Tip: Train your brain to think in English. Start small. Describe your morning or your lunch without translating first.

❌ 𝗨𝘀𝗶𝗻𝗴 “𝗳𝗮𝗻𝗰𝘆” 𝘄𝗼𝗿𝗱𝘀 𝘁𝗼 𝘀𝗼𝘂𝗻𝗱 𝘀𝗺𝗮𝗿𝘁
“I am extremely delighted to be in the possession of this magnificent opportunity to address you…”
Meanwhile, your audience is thinking: “Wait, what?”
▶️  Tip: Simple language is powerful language. You don’t need fireworks. Just clarity.

❌ 𝗖𝗼𝗽𝘆𝗶𝗻𝗴 𝘆𝗼𝘂𝗿 𝗻𝗮𝘁𝗶𝘃𝗲 𝗹𝗮𝗻𝗴𝘂𝗮𝗴𝗲 𝗴𝗿𝗮𝗺𝗺𝗮𝗿
In Italian, Spanish, or French, long sentences full of connectors sound elegant.
In English? It can sound like a never-ending maze.
By the time you reach the verb, your listener has already gone for coffee. ☕
▶️  Tip: Keep sentences short. One idea per sentence. Your message will land stronger.

The result of all these mistakes?
👉 You lose your audience.
👉 Your message gets blurry.
👉 And you end up frustrated. 
Not because of your English, but because of how you use it.

So, this week, try this:

Speak more simply.
(Rember: “KISS”)
Think less.
And connect more.
