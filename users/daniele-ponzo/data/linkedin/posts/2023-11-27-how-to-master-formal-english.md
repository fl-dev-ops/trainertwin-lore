How to master formal English in 5 steps
 
Picture this:

You’ve spent hours preparing your solution offer.

And you are sure your client will like it.

You’ve covered all the functionalities they’ve requested.

So you’re ready to send it and jump into a meeting with them to present it.

One question, now.

Do you have the right language to sound professional and impress your customer?

What if you use wrong expressions and you sound clumsy or impolite?

Fear no more! I’m coming to the rescue!✊ Here are 5 strategies to instantly sound more professional:

1.𝐀𝐯𝐨𝐢𝐝 𝐜𝐨𝐧𝐭𝐫𝐚𝐜𝐭𝐢𝐨𝐧𝐬. 
Keep words separate, and opt for more elaborate sentences. For example:

❌ "We can't meet the deadline because of some issues with the software."
✅ "We cannot meet the deadline due to certain issues with the software."

2. 𝐂𝐡𝐨𝐨𝐬𝐞 𝐦𝐨𝐫𝐞 𝐬𝐨𝐩𝐡𝐢𝐬𝐭𝐢𝐜𝐚𝐭𝐞𝐝 𝐕𝐨𝐜𝐚𝐛𝐮𝐥𝐚𝐫𝐲:

❌Informal: "Start"
✅Formal: "Commence"
❌Informal: " stick to the rules"
✅Formal: " adhere to the established protocols"
❌Informal: "Help"
✅Formal: "Assist"

3. 𝐔𝐬𝐞 𝐏𝐫𝐨𝐟𝐞𝐬𝐬𝐢𝐨𝐧𝐚𝐥 𝐆𝐫𝐞𝐞𝐭𝐢𝐧𝐠𝐬 𝐚𝐧𝐝 𝐂𝐥𝐨𝐬𝐢𝐧𝐠𝐬 (especially in emails or letters):

❌"Hi there,"
✅"Dear [Name],"
❌"Thanks,"
✅"Yours sincerely,"

4. 𝐔𝐬𝐞 “𝐖𝐨𝐮𝐥𝐝” 𝐚𝐧𝐝 “𝐂𝐨𝐮𝐥𝐝”: 

❌Let's talk about the changes in the next meeting.
✅I would like to discuss the proposed changes during our upcoming meeting.

5. 𝐁𝐞 𝐌𝐢𝐧𝐝𝐟𝐮𝐥 𝐨𝐟 𝐓𝐨𝐧𝐞 𝐚𝐧𝐝 𝐏𝐢𝐭𝐜𝐡: 

Maintain a moderate and controlled tone. Pay attention to your pitch, ensuring it is neither too high nor too low.
❌"Yeah, so I think this approach is not gonna work. It's like, too complicated, you know?"
✅"I believe this approach may present challenges due to its complexity. I recommend exploring alternative solutions."

Now you are well equipped to impress your clients! 💪
