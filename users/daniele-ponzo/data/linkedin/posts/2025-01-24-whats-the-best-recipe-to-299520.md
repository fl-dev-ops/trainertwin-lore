What’s the best recipe to 𝗳𝗮𝗶𝗹 to reach your dreams?

▶️ Do you hate working for global companies?
▶️ Do you want to avoid that promotion?
▶️ Do you jump scared when you think of an international role?

I’ve got your back!

Follow my advice and you’re guaranteed to bomb your job interviews, be ignored for promotions and keep doing the same (comfortable) old:

👉  Keep putting things off: "I’ll start improving next week..."

👉  Get paralysed with fear: You're scared. Seriously. You can’t do this. Just give up.

👉  Wait for the perfect moment to start. Venus, Mars, Jupiter and Saturn need to be aligned.

👉  Never set specific language goals. “Thou shalt speak like Shakespeare. No compromise”.

👉  Encourage your moods: yes, today you want to practice. Tomorrow? Nah… maybe next week.

👉  Wait! First, you need a certification. How dare you! Get a diploma before doing anything else.

👉  Skip networking: Who needs other people, anyway?

👉  Ignore cultural differences. Remember: YOU are the centre of the world. Like the Earth, everything rotates around you. 🌍 

So, are you ready?

Remember: you can be defined by your failures 🤷‍♂️ . Definitely!

You don’t need to communicate effectively. 

And you don’t need to work on your dreams. 

It’s enough when you wait and one day… puff! Something magical will happen. The genie will appear, and you can ask him everything you want🧞.

In the meantime, keep doing… more of the same!
