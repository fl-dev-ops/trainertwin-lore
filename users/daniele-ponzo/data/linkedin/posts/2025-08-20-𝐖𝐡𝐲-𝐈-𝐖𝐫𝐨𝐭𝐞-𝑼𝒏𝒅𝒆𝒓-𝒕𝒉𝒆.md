Last week, Daniele Ponzo’s post made me reflect on why I decided to write my book in both English and Bahasa Indonesia.
See his post here https://lnkd.in/gSciK-Xx

Originally, I planned to write only in English. After living half my life outside #indonesia, #english allowed me to write as an 𝘰𝘶𝘵𝘴𝘪𝘥𝘦𝘳—freely, without fear, telling family stories as history.

Then my Dad (Rudy, the character in my book) said:
"𝘞𝘳𝘪𝘵𝘦 𝘪𝘯 𝘉𝘢𝘩𝘢𝘴𝘢 𝘐𝘯𝘥𝘰𝘯𝘦𝘴𝘪𝘢. 𝘐 𝘸𝘢𝘯𝘵 𝘰𝘶𝘳 𝘵𝘳𝘪𝘣𝘦 𝘵𝘰 𝘳𝘦𝘢𝘥 𝘪𝘵 𝘵𝘰𝘰."

That’s when I got stuck. Writing in Bahasa turned me into an 𝘪𝘯𝘴𝘪𝘥𝘦𝘳. My words became sharp, personal—sometimes uncomfortably so. Readers even told me the book was “too honest,” suggesting that I should focus instead on filial piety or how to achieve success.

But I decided not to tone it down. Because this book isn’t just a translation. It’s a portrait of my two selves: the diaspora outsider and the Indonesian insider.

👉 Have you ever found that one language lets you say things another cannot?
👉 Or that your personal truth clashes with cultural expectations?
I’d love to hear your thoughts.
