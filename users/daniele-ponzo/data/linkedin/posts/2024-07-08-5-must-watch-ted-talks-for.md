5 Must-watch TED talks for Non-Native IT Pros to boost their confidence

Are you going to level up this summer? Save these to watch later:

1️⃣ "Your Body Language May Shape Who You Are" by Amy Cuddy

https://lnkd.in/dK9QY99A

-> Discover the power of body language in boosting confidence and enhancing your presentation skills.

2️⃣ "How to Speak So That People Want to Listen" by Julian Treasure

https://lnkd.in/dhbbqD7f

-> Learn vocal techniques and language tips to make your speech more engaging and impactful.

3️⃣ "The Secret Structure of Great Talks" by Nancy Duarte

https://lnkd.in/d9EpujP8

-> Understand the storytelling framework that makes presentations memorable and compelling.

4️⃣ "The Power of Vulnerability" by Brené Brown

https://lnkd.in/dZpdPww7

-> Gain insights on how embracing vulnerability can enhance your authenticity and connection with the audience.

5️⃣ "What Makes a Good Life? Lessons from the Longest Study on Happiness" by Robert Waldinger

https://lnkd.in/dq8DjhMw

-> Improve your listening and comprehension skills while learning about the importance of personal and professional well-being.

Which are you going to watch first?
