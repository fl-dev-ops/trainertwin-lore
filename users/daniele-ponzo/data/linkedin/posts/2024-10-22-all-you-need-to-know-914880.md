All you need to know about soft skills.

(and why you need to level up your English to make an impact).

If you work in IT, or are a Tech professional, you might want to check this out.

First, don’t call them soft skills. Call them human skills. Or SUPERHERO skills🦸‍♀️🦸‍♀️.
There is nothing ‘soft’ about them.

Second, if you want to feel more comfortable in your position or grow into leadership roles, human skills and English language should be your best friends🤝.

Here is why:

1. 𝗘𝗳𝗳𝗲𝗰𝘁𝗶𝘃𝗲 𝗖𝗼𝗺𝗺𝘂𝗻𝗶𝗰𝗮𝘁𝗶𝗼𝗻
↳If you have an ‘ok’ level, can you explain complex technical issues to non-technical stakeholders?
↳Skill: Active listening and clear articulation to ensure that your team understands expectations and goals. 

2. 𝗘𝗺𝗼𝘁𝗶𝗼𝗻𝗮𝗹 𝗜𝗻𝘁𝗲𝗹𝗹𝗶𝗴𝗲𝗻𝗰𝗲 (𝗘𝗤)
↳Empathy, persuasion, conflict resolution. In English! Tone, politeness, and cultural nuances can be tricky.
↳Skill: Fluency in English to better express understanding, manage emotions, and resolve conflicts to lead teams more effectively.

3. 𝗟𝗲𝗮𝗱𝗲𝗿𝘀𝗵𝗶𝗽 & 𝗧𝗲𝗮𝗺𝘄𝗼𝗿𝗸
↳Can you motivate others in English? Do you know how to be assertive?
↳Skill: English proficiency in specific contexts (e.g., giving feedback, negotiation) to help develop leadership and collaborative abilities. 

4. 𝗖𝘂𝗹𝘁𝘂𝗿𝗮𝗹 𝗦𝗲𝗻𝘀𝗶𝘁𝗶𝘃𝗶𝘁𝘆
↳You need to adapt your language to the multicultural context. (Yeas, jokes and humour too).
↳Skill: Improving English skills helps you navigate these cultural nuances, building stronger relationships with international colleagues and clients.

5. 𝗣𝗿𝗲𝘀𝗲𝗻𝘁𝗮𝘁𝗶𝗼𝗻 𝗮𝗻𝗱 𝗣𝘂𝗯𝗹𝗶𝗰 𝗦𝗽𝗲𝗮𝗸𝗶𝗻𝗴
↳You have the technical knowledge but struggle with presenting your ideas to management or clients (again, in English!). This can limit your career progression.
↳Skill: Improving your English presentation skills enables you to communicate confidently in meetings and pitch ideas.

6. 𝗣𝗿𝗼𝗯𝗹𝗲𝗺-𝗦𝗼𝗹𝘃𝗶𝗻𝗴 𝗮𝗻𝗱 𝗖𝗿𝗶𝘁𝗶𝗰𝗮𝗹 𝗧𝗵𝗶𝗻𝗸𝗶𝗻𝗴
↳You often need to discuss problems, brainstorm solutions, and work through complex issues. This needs precise language skills to avoid misunderstandings.
↳Skill: Proficiency in English allows you to better express logical reasoning and propose solutions in a way that is understandable to all.

7. 𝗡𝗲𝘁𝘄𝗼𝗿𝗸𝗶𝗻𝗴 𝗮𝗻𝗱 𝗣𝗿𝗼𝗳𝗲𝘀𝘀𝗶𝗼𝗻𝗮𝗹 𝗥𝗲𝗹𝗮𝘁𝗶𝗼𝗻𝘀𝗵𝗶𝗽𝘀
↳Networking often means that you need to talk to people. Chats, emails are not enough. You might find yourself limited if you aren’t comfortable with informal conversation in English.
↳Skill: Small talk! This will help you be more confident in networking situations, contributing to better professional relationships and opportunities.

This is an ongoing journey. It needs training and dedication.

Focus on English for work scenarios, for giving feedback or negotiating and role-play the situations.

Practice makes perfect.

P.S. Am I missing anything? Let me know below 👇 .
