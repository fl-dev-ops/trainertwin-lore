These 5 remarkable people have 1 thing in common.

You might be wondering why they are in the same picture 🤔 :

a Nobel Prize-winning physicist, 

a former U.S. Secretary of State, 

a Nobel laureate and advocate for girls' education, 

a prominent journalist,

an award-winning actress.

Yes, they have all overcome language barriers.

English was not their native tongue.

And they have demonstrated that learning a new language is a powerful tool that can open doors to success, even in highly competitive fields.

Was it easy for them?

Let’s hear their own voice 🗣 :

𝐌𝐚𝐝𝐞𝐥𝐞𝐢𝐧𝐞 𝐀𝐥𝐛𝐫𝐢𝐠𝐡𝐭 (First female U.S. Secretary of State)
"𝘐 𝘸𝘢𝘴 𝘢 𝘳𝘦𝘧𝘶𝘨𝘦𝘦. 𝘐 𝘥𝘪𝘥𝘯'𝘵 𝘴𝘱𝘦𝘢𝘬 𝘌𝘯𝘨𝘭𝘪𝘴𝘩 𝘸𝘩𝘦𝘯 𝘐 𝘨𝘰𝘵 𝘩𝘦𝘳𝘦, 𝘴𝘰 𝘐 𝘬𝘯𝘰𝘸 𝘸𝘩𝘢𝘵 𝘪𝘵'𝘴 𝘭𝘪𝘬𝘦."

𝐌𝐚𝐥𝐚𝐥𝐚 𝐘𝐨𝐮𝐬𝐚𝐟𝐳𝐚𝐢 (Nobel laureate for advocating girls' education)
"𝘐 𝘴𝘱𝘦𝘢𝘬 𝘯𝘰𝘵 𝘧𝘰𝘳 𝘮𝘺𝘴𝘦𝘭𝘧 𝘣𝘶𝘵 𝘧𝘰𝘳 𝘵𝘩𝘰𝘴𝘦 𝘸𝘪𝘵𝘩𝘰𝘶𝘵 𝘷𝘰𝘪𝘤𝘦."

𝐀𝐫𝐢𝐚𝐧𝐧𝐚 𝐇𝐮𝐟𝐟𝐢𝐧𝐠𝐭𝐨𝐧 (Co-founder of The Huffington Post).
"𝘍𝘢𝘪𝘭𝘶𝘳𝘦 𝘪𝘴 𝘯𝘰𝘵 𝘵𝘩𝘦 𝘰𝘱𝘱𝘰𝘴𝘪𝘵𝘦 𝘰𝘧 𝘴𝘶𝘤𝘤𝘦𝘴𝘴; 𝘪𝘵'𝘴 𝘱𝘢𝘳𝘵 𝘰𝘧 𝘴𝘶𝘤𝘤𝘦𝘴𝘴."

𝐀𝐥𝐛𝐞𝐫𝐭 𝐄𝐢𝐧𝐬𝐭𝐞𝐢𝐧 (One of the most renowned physicists in history).
"𝘛𝘩𝘦 𝘰𝘯𝘭𝘺 𝘴𝘰𝘶𝘳𝘤𝘦 𝘰𝘧 𝘬𝘯𝘰𝘸𝘭𝘦𝘥𝘨𝘦 𝘪𝘴 𝘦𝘹𝘱𝘦𝘳𝘪𝘦𝘯𝘤𝘦."

𝐒𝐚𝐧𝐝𝐫𝐚 𝐎𝐡 (Award-winning actress).
"𝘐 𝘧𝘦𝘭𝘵 𝘷𝘦𝘳𝘺 𝘪𝘴𝘰𝘭𝘢𝘵𝘦𝘥. 𝘐 𝘥𝘪𝘥𝘯'𝘵 𝘴𝘱𝘦𝘢𝘬 𝘵𝘩𝘦 𝘭𝘢𝘯𝘨𝘶𝘢𝘨𝘦, 𝘢𝘯𝘥 𝘐 𝘩𝘢𝘥 𝘵𝘩𝘪𝘴 𝘰𝘷𝘦𝘳𝘸𝘩𝘦𝘭𝘮𝘪𝘯𝘨 𝘴𝘦𝘯𝘴𝘦 𝘰𝘧 𝘯𝘰𝘵 𝘬𝘯𝘰𝘸𝘪𝘯𝘨 𝘸𝘩𝘢𝘵 𝘸𝘢𝘴 𝘨𝘰𝘪𝘯𝘨 𝘰𝘯."

So no, it was not easy.

Determination, resilience, and the pursuit of your goals.

This is what makes the difference.

And if you look at these remarkable people, they had to learn the language as they had no choice. But it didn’t stop them from achieving their success.

It doesn’t matter if you are learning a new language as a hobby.

If you’re perfecting it to get better opportunities.

Or if you must improve it as your professional career demands it.

Focus on your objective.

Train the skills you need.

Embrace fear and keep on reading inspiring stories.

Do you feel you need extra support?

Drop me a message 📩  and we can figure it out together.

Here is to all language learners and to everybody who’s helping overcome communication barriers! 🌟
