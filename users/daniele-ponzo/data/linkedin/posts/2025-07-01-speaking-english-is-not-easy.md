Speaking English is NOT easy.


As a matter of fact, speaking itself is a complex process.

It's not only about using your mouth to produce sounds.

At least 5 areas of your left brain are involved in speaking🧠.

They take care of:

🧠 Speaking 
🧠 Words Comprehension
🧠 Reading
🧠 Understanding sounds
🧠 Vocabulary

With so much work involved, this is not straight forward.

Children learn to coordinate these parts naturally. I see it with my son every day. It's amazing to see his progress.

It's mostly spontaneous, but we can help it, for example by reading to kids.

So, what can we do, as adults, to improve our speaking capabilities in English?

Here are some tips:

🔘 Speaking: mimic and voice over videos in English. 
This helps with pronunciation, rhythm and intonation. Record yourself and get feedback on your recordings. Use every opportunity to speak. Seriously.

🔘 Words Comprehension: focus on the individual sounds and learn how to match them with written patterns. 
Copy how confident speakers position their mouth when pronouncing certain words.

🔘 Reading: read a lot. Posts, books, magazines, websites to expand your knowledge of the language. 
Reading is powerful, it has an impact on how you speak, how you articulate and the words you use.

🔘 Understanding sounds: listen to podcasts, slow the audio down to get used to the sound of the language. 
Study the pronunciation specifics (vowels, consonants, connected speech) so you know what to expect.

🔘 Vocabulary: expand your vocabulary by immersing yourself in the language. Switch your phone and laptop to English. 
Get into the habit of describing situations around you. And when you don't know a word say: “it's a kind of…” “it’s similar to…” rather than translating.

This is what kids do. They never study grammar, and they don't look up new words in a dictionary.

They live the language, and they mimic the adults.

They learn how to pronounce, how to position their mouth.

And learn more than 50 new words a day 🔤 .

You can work with your brain and radically improve the way you speak.

The secret is in the method.
