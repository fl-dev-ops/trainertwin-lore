Stop aiming for perfect English. Aim for present English.

In the latest episode of The Non-Native Leader - Podcast, I spoke with Adrian Stanek — a tech leader and non-native speaker who creates content, leads teams, and coaches others… all in English.

And we kept coming back to one big idea:
📌 The goal is not 𝘱𝘦𝘳𝘧𝘦𝘤𝘵 English.
📌 The goal is 𝘱𝘳𝘦𝘴𝘦𝘯𝘵 English.

Adrian shared how, for years, he avoided speaking on video because he didn’t feel “native enough.” He was afraid of mistakes. Afraid of being judged.

Sound familiar?

But here’s what changed everything:

He stopped trying to erase his accent or hide his mistakes… and started focusing on connecting. 
On being present. 
On showing up — fully, flaws and all.

And that’s when confidence clicked.

You don’t build confidence by removing every mistake.
You build it by showing up anyway.

Not scripted. Not spotless.
Just honest, human, and present.

That’s the kind of English that creates real leadership impact.
