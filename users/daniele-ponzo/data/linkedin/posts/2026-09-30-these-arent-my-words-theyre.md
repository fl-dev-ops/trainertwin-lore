These aren't my words.

They're yours.

Over the last few months, I've asked hundreds of tech professionals one simple question:

"What's the hardest part about communicating in English at work?"

Some of the answers stopped me in my tracks.

"I know what I want to say, but I need too much time to find the right words." 

"I pause to find the right word. Someone jumps in. The moment is gone." 

"I understand everything... but when it's my turn to speak, I lose momentum." 

"I overthink how to phrase things the right way." 

"My biggest challenge is explaining technical topics clearly under pressure." 

Notice something?

None of these people said:

"I need to learn the third conditional."

They aren't struggling with grammar.

They're struggling with speed.
With clarity.
With confidence.

With turning good technical ideas into conversations that move projects forward.

That's why I don't teach "Business English."

I help tech professionals communicate when the pressure is real:

• stakeholder meetings
• technical discussions
• presentations
• interviews
• leadership conversations

Because that's where careers change.

And if you've ever thought:

"I know the answer... I just couldn't say it quickly enough."

You're definitely not the only one.

💬 Which of these quotes felt most familiar?

Or what sentence would you add to the list?
