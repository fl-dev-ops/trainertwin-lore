What is the most effective strategy to online visibility? 

Networking, Personal Branding, and Growth.

In the latest episode of "𝐓𝐡𝐞 𝐍𝐨𝐧-𝐍𝐚𝐭𝐢𝐯𝐞 𝐥𝐞𝐚𝐝𝐞𝐫” video podcast🎤, I talked to Francesco Mandolini, a freelance Senior Copywriter.

He shared with me the challenges and rewards of his journey, from the London School of Journalism to his current job as communication experts for personal clients, CEO’s and multinational companies.

He revealed his magic formula when it comes to building your online personal brand:

🔵 Curiosity
🔵 Personality
🔵 Practical experience

Without forgetting networking and mentorship (and music!).

Of course, this is not a short hop. 

It feels more like a journey across the sea 🌊 .

Sometimes it’s calm, and you move ahead at full speed.

Sometimes you face a storm, and you slow down or even walk backwards.

As with every journey, it starts with the first, small step.

So, start now and level up later!
