The meeting didn't need your perfect words

It needed your ideas...

Every week I speak to Tech professionals
  
And I hear things like:
  
"I know exactly what I want to say..."
  
"I just can't find the right word."
  
The instinct is always the same.
  
Study more vocabulary.
  
Memorize longer lists.
  
Learn more advanced expressions.
  
But that's rarely the real problem.
  
While you're searching for the perfect word...
  
your brain is also:
  
• solving the technical problem
• translating
• organizing the sentence
• managing the pressure of speaking in front of other people
  
Eventually...
  
your mental CPU runs out of capacity.
  
And that's when you freeze.
  
Here's something that surprises many of my clients:
  
The most effective communicators don't always use the most advanced vocabulary.
  
They use the 𝗳𝗶𝗿𝘀𝘁 𝗰𝗹𝗲𝗮𝗿 𝘄𝗼𝗿𝗱 that comes to mind.
  
Then they keep moving.
  
That's what keeps the conversation flowing.
  
And beats your worst enemies: silence and filler words (uhm, ehm…).
  
If you want to find words faster under pressure, try these three habits.
  
1️⃣ 𝗕𝘂𝗶𝗹𝗱 𝗽𝗵𝗿𝗮𝘀𝗲 𝗹𝗶𝗯𝗿𝗮𝗿𝗶𝗲𝘀, 𝗻𝗼𝘁 𝘃𝗼𝗰𝗮𝗯𝘂𝗹𝗮𝗿𝘆 𝗹𝗶𝘀𝘁𝘀.
  
Don't memorize:
  
"mitigate"
  
Memorise:
  
"One way to reduce the risk is..."
  
Your brain retrieves chunks much faster than isolated words.
  
 2️⃣ 𝗣𝗿𝗮𝗰𝘁𝗶𝗰𝗲 𝘄𝗶𝘁𝗵 𝗿𝗲𝗮𝗹 𝘀𝗰𝗲𝗻𝗮𝗿𝗶𝗼𝘀.
  
Don't learn words in alphabetical order.
  
Practise the words you'll actually need in:
  
• stand-ups
• sprint reviews
• stakeholder meetings
• customer calls
  
Context beats memorization.
  
3️⃣ 𝗦𝘁𝗼𝗽 𝗰𝗵𝗮𝘀𝗶𝗻𝗴 𝘁𝗵𝗲 𝗽𝗲𝗿𝗳𝗲𝗰𝘁 𝘄𝗼𝗿𝗱.
  
The simple word you already know...
  
spoken confidently...
  
is almost always better than the sophisticated word you can't remember.

  
Remember:
  
The goal isn't impressive English.
  
The goal is clear communication.

  
💬 I'm curious.
  
Have you ever stopped mid-sentence...
 
because you were searching for a "better" word?
