Time flies...

I used to buy disks to listen to music.

I used to exchange cassette tapes with my mates.

The bigger the speakers the cooler the effect.

I used to record my favourite movies on VHS.

Ready with the remote to skip the ads.

I had a videocamera to shoot my "films".

I wanted to be Fellini.

I would spend hours in second hand book shops and record stores.

If I wanted to meet a friend or a girl again, I had to memorize their phone number.

10-number landline.

When I look at my maps in the bookshelf I get a weird feeling. New bridges, new monuments. Why aren't they updated?

Yellow pages, white pages...to use on a public phone to find a restaurant or a dog sitter.

I used to stop random people around. They had a portable radio and I wanted to know about the football matches.

One day I went to the other side of the city to buy a memory expansion for my brand new computer.

1MB memory expansion.

I felt like a king.

Am I the only one who can remember how this feels like?

P.S. sure, you can't fit the man in your pocket...but wait for the next AI development😀!
