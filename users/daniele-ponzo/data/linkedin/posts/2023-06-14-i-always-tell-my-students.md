I always tell my students that there is only one way to improve speaking skills: you have to speak.

 
Exactly, it’s a simple as this.
 
Our worst enemy here is fear:
Fear of not being confident, fear of making mistakes, fear of getting confused.
 
Speaking English needs training. It’s like an athlete preparing for a high jump. 

Sure, our athlete needs to be able to run and to be fit. 

But ultimately, repetition and focus on the main skill will make the difference.
 
So high jumpers practice high jump day in, day out until they become masters.
 
And what about speaking?

Here is an actionable plan to start improving today:

Step 1: 📝🔍 Assess Your Current Skills and Identify Areas of Focus, so you identify the specific areas you want to improve, such as confidence, vocabulary, and real-life IT scenarios. 
 
Step 2: 🏋️‍♂️📚 Build a Strong Foundation so you can focus on grammar, vocabulary, and pronunciation specific to the IT industry
 
Step 3: 🗣️💡Practice in Real-Life Situations so you can You can express your ideas effectively, engage in discussions, and present with confidence
 
Step 4: 🔄📈 Continuous Learning and Feedback Loop so you get constructive feedback, enabling you to refine your skills further.
