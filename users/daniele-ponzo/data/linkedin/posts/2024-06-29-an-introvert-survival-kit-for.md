An Introvert survival kit for social events.

If you're like me, you're probably not 100% comfortable in social events.

Especially when the situation is unexpected.

Yesterday I met a friend of mine for a quiet chat and I ended up sitting at a table with people from Tennessee, Dublin, Dundalk.

“I'm a master of small talk, I got this”.

👌Part 1: what small talk is usually about:

Irish food, Irish weather, American food, Italian food, Italian way of life, travel arrangements, pretty good, I'm doing well! Small talk at its best.

👌Part 2: all the things you should never mention:

Weapons, death penalty, Nigel Farage, the children’s hospital, George W, the Troubles, the Godfather, the Pope, Religion. Not so small anymore.

In fairness, it wasn't me who brought the topics on the table, so for once I can't blame myself.

👌Part 3: the ultimate small talk killer:

I believe pineapple on pizza is great, I would eat Hawaiian every day.

That did it.

I survived the night, ready to write a manual on small talk: what you should do and how you should and should not answer.

Have a great weekend, everyone!
