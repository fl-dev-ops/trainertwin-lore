Do we really need New Year’s resolutions?

January arrives and suddenly everything feels possible.
✨ New beginnings
✨ Fresh starts
✨ Big declarations

Promises, promises everywhere:
 • get fit
 • eat better
 • learn something new
 • save more money
 • change career

They look great on paper.
They sound convincing in our head.
And yet… most of them quietly fade by February.

Why?

Because many of these goals are driven by what I’d call 𝘄𝗲𝗮𝗸 𝗺𝗼𝘁𝗶𝘃𝗮𝘁𝗶𝗼𝗻.

The kind that comes from:
• peer pressure
• social expectations
• “this is what I should want”
• comparison with others

It’s the motivation that looks good from the outside
but doesn’t hold when things get uncomfortable.

There’s another kind of motivation though.

A 𝘀𝘁𝗿𝗼𝗻𝗴 𝗺𝗼𝘁𝗶𝘃𝗮𝘁𝗶𝗼𝗻.
The one that comes from inside:
• a desire for a better role
• more meaningful work
• better pay
• more confidence
• feeling proud of yourself
• building a career that actually fits you

Strong motivation doesn’t need January.
People who reached out to work with me didn’t wait for a new year.
They did it in August, September, October, November.

Why?

Because their goals were clear.
And because procrastinating hurt more than taking action.

That’s the difference.

A new notebook, a new app, a new year look great.
But 𝗮𝗹𝗶𝗴𝗻𝗺𝗲𝗻𝘁 between what you want and what you’re doing helps you find your true motivation and your consistency.

𝗦𝗼 𝗹𝗲𝘁 𝗺𝗲 𝗮𝘀𝗸 𝘆𝗼𝘂 𝘁𝗵𝗶𝘀, 𝘁𝗼 𝗿𝗲𝗮𝗹𝗹𝘆 𝘀𝘁𝗮𝗿𝘁 𝘁𝗵𝗲 𝘆𝗲𝗮𝗿:
Are you trying to reinforce a weak motivation…
or are you ready to stop procrastinating on the strong one?
