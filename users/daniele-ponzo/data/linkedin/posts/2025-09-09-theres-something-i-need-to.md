There’s something I need to tell you about getting confident with English 👂 

I used to work in an IT Service Delivery team with a lot of senior professionals.

In an American multinational company, as a non-native English speaker.

At the beginning, I remember I got some compliments on my work, and I was excited, and I thought: "if I keep my standards high, I will get noticed and I will have some great opportunities".

So, I worked a lot, I studied on my own to improve my Service delivery skills and my English.

I thought I was doing everything perfectly. “I’m doing great, I got this”.

A few months went by.

You know what happened next?

I started seeing people around me being assigned interesting projects and getting promoted.

An Important business trip to meet a client.

A role in a management position.

And me?

Nothing. Nada. Niente 👎 .

As you do, I started blaming others: my manager, the company, the system, the universe…

And that was exactly the reason why I couldn’t get anywhere.

I needed a change of mentality.

A powerful switch.

An epiphany 💫 .

I needed to understand I was in charge of my own future.

It was ME who had to get back in control.

The skills, the language, the visibility.

So, you know what I did?

→ I started following influencing people around me. Managers, executives, peers.

→ How they spoke, how they handled calls, how they presented, how they behaved in front of the customer.

→ I found myself a mentor, to ask all the questions and learn all the secrets.

→ I started being more open. Instead of learning on my own, I shared my roadblocks with my colleagues, got their perspective and their support.

And I slowly built my own strategy.

The technical skills were not enough.

A good English level was not enough.

I needed the presence, the language, the exposure to become a better professional and a leader.

And I eventually became a leader.

Team Leader, Project leader.

That allowed me to do what I really loved (and still love) doing:

Work with professionals like you.

Support, enable, coach them.

It’s invaluable.

P.S. Do you feel stuck in your career journey?

Feel free to DM me, I’d be glad to share some ideas with you ✌ .
