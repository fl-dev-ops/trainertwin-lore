How to become a global leader even if you feel your English isn’t perfect.

By leveraging your existing knowledge, you can:

🔹 Showcase your expertise
🔹 Become part of meaningful conversations
🔹 Get noticed (and trusted) by decision makers

I had the privilege of discussing this with Marek Sowa, a Global Director at Capgemini as part of my video podcast series.

At the beginning of his career in IT, he felt he couldn’t communicate properly, and he was missing out on good opportunities.

Did he weep and complain?

No, he didn’t.

He applied a simple, yet effective strategy:

He used the language of technology to overcome language barriers.

JavaScript, Python and AI gave him the opportunity to take part in great projects.

And he used the opportunity to practice daily.

In the projects he had to collaborate with other people.

So, he multiplied the interaction opportunities.

And this helped him get more and more confident.

You see?

Repetition -> Practice -> Experience -> Confidence

And he’s now a leader on the global stage.
