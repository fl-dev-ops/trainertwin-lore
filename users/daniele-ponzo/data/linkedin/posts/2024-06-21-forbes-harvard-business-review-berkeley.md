Forbes, Harvard Business Review, Berkeley Professors and BBC Business Journalists suggested a crucial mindset shift to advance in your career.

They’ve highlighted the importance of VISIBILITY in the workplace 👀 .

These days, doing your work is not enough. Your day-to-day work, your results, your performance are important, sure.

But the real question is: are you REALLY getting noticed? Does your boss know what you do? Does their boss know you and what you do?

🔹If you want your name mentioned where decisions are being made.

🔹If you want to be part of exciting projects.

🔹If you want to have promotion opportunities.

Then you need to make sure decision makers know what you're doing.

Low visibility can seriously impact your career opportunities. No matter how good you are.

Imagine how much you could achieve with better visibility at work.

And imagine the opportunities you would have with a clear visibility strategy.

So, are you really visible at work?
