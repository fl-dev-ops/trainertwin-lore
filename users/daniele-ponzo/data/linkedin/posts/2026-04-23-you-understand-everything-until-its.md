You understand everything.
Until it’s your turn to speak.

When:

you’re put on the spot in a meeting
you get an unexpected question
you need to explain something complex… fast
you disagree, but don’t want to sound rude

That’s where things break.

Not as “bad English”…

But as:

→ hesitation
→ overthinking
→ running around in circles
→ silence
  
I see this all the time with developers.

They know exactly what to say.

But they don’t say it fast enough.
  
And over time, that creates a gap between:

→ how good they actually are
→ how they’re perceived
  
In the carousel, I break down 5 specific moments where communication fails
(and what it actually sounds like when it happens)
  
If you’ve ever thought:

“I know this… I just can’t say it properly”

You’ll recognise yourself in at least one.
