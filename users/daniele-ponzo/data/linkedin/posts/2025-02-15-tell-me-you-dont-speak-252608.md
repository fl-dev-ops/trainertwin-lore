Tell me you don’t speak English…
… without telling me you don’t speak English.

I believe this song accurately reflects the English learner’s experience across the world.

Italian singer Adriano Celentano said: the intent was to demonstrate how English sounds to people who do not understand the language 🧱 .

The result is astonishing.

I bet you will find yourself singing along… even though there is absolutely no meaning here.

I also love the reflection on communication barriers:

We live in a global world, we are influenced by a global language… yet we struggle to communicate and to understand each other.

So, “Prisencolinensinainciusol” was born.

Meaningless gibberish

Sung in a meaningful way.

Regardless of the absolute absence of meaning, you can still use the song to practice pronunciation, intonation, rhythm.
