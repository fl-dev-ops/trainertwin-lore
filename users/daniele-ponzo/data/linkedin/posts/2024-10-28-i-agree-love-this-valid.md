How to sound 100%... boring! 😴 

When it comes to suggesting ideas or commenting on other people’s ideas, you have a great opportunity to use engaging language to:

✅ Explain your point
✅ Articulate the logical flow
✅ Sound professional.

I understand, this is not easy.

You concentrate on the right word to use, on the right grammar and you miss the expressions to connect your thoughts.

Let me tell you, this is absolutely normal.

I’ve been there too, with my broken English, my doubts, my mistakes. 😱 

But it’s essential to slowly refine your language to sound like a pro.

So, here you have 2 options:

1️⃣  In a fast-paced world we need to foster improvement to leverage our language. In the dynamic realm of IT, it is important to delve into the… AI, AI, AI! 🤖 
2️⃣  Check my carousel below to get you started. Try to incorporate some of these expressions in your emails, conversations, comments. 👇 

Please, don’t go (only) for option 1. 

AI is not there (yet) when you need to speak. You need to get familiar with simple but effective language which sounds natural.

Are you ready to level up? 💪 

P.S. delve, leverage, dynamic, embrace, embark, dive…ChatGPT loves these words. Are you still using them?
