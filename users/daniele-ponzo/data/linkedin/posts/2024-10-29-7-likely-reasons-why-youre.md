7 likely reasons why you’re not progressing in English

(And how you can fix it)

𝟭: "𝗜 𝗮𝗺 𝘁𝗼𝗼 𝗼𝗹𝗱 𝘁𝗼 𝗹𝗲𝗮𝗿𝗻 𝗮 𝗻𝗲𝘄 𝗹𝗮𝗻𝗴𝘂𝗮𝗴𝗲."

🔴 You think you’ve missed your opportunity. You believe the ship has sailed. 
↳Studies show this is about motivation and passion. And the right learning strategies. There’s no such thing as “too late”.

𝟮: "𝗜 𝗻𝗲𝗲𝗱 𝘁𝗼 𝗯𝗲 𝗽𝗲𝗿𝗳𝗲𝗰𝘁 𝗯𝗲𝗳𝗼𝗿𝗲 𝗜 𝘀𝗽𝗲𝗮𝗸."

🔴You’re afraid of making mistakes and being judged by others. The reality? Perfectionism stops progress. 
↳Smart people love making mistakes (you are not born smart, you become smart).

𝟯: "𝗜 𝗱𝗼𝗻'𝘁 𝗵𝗮𝘃𝗲 𝗲𝗻𝗼𝘂𝗴𝗵 𝘁𝗶𝗺𝗲."

🔴You have a busy schedule. You believe you cannot dedicate enough time to English. But Language learning can be flexible and integrated into daily routines. 
↳Consistency is the game changer.

𝟰: "𝗜 𝗮𝗺 𝗻𝗼𝘁 𝗴𝗼𝗼𝗱 𝗮𝘁 𝗹𝗮𝗻𝗴𝘂𝗮𝗴𝗲𝘀."

🔴You’ve had a terrible past experience with language learning. You now lack self-confidence. 
↳The truth is everyone can improve with practice and the right strategies.

𝟱: "𝗠𝘆 𝗮𝗰𝗰𝗲𝗻𝘁 𝗶𝘀 𝘁𝗼𝗼 𝘀𝘁𝗿𝗼𝗻𝗴; 𝗽𝗲𝗼𝗽𝗹𝗲 𝘄𝗼𝗻'𝘁 𝘂𝗻𝗱𝗲𝗿𝘀𝘁𝗮𝗻𝗱 𝗺𝗲."

🔴You’re convinced people won’t understand you. Because of your accent. But everybody has an accent. Including native speakers. 
↳Intonation, pronunciation and clarity are more important than having a perfect accent.

𝟲: "𝗜 𝗰𝗮𝗻'𝘁 𝗮𝗳𝗳𝗼𝗿𝗱 𝗲𝘅𝗽𝗲𝗻𝘀𝗶𝘃𝗲 𝗹𝗮𝗻𝗴𝘂𝗮𝗴𝗲 𝗰𝗼𝘂𝗿𝘀𝗲𝘀."

🔴You think you can’t invest money right now. But what’s the cost of missing opportunities? How much does your dream job cost? 
↳The real question is: can you afford to invest in your future?

𝟳: "𝗜𝘁'𝘀 𝘁𝗼𝗼 𝗲𝗺𝗯𝗮𝗿𝗿𝗮𝘀𝘀𝗶𝗻𝗴 𝘁𝗼 𝘀𝗽𝗲𝗮𝗸 𝗶𝗻 𝗳𝗿𝗼𝗻𝘁 𝗼𝗳 𝗼𝘁𝗵𝗲𝗿𝘀."

🔴Real life can be stressful. Pressure, anxiety, self-consciousness stop you from finding your voice. 
↳Practice in safe environment before moving on to Incremental exposure and increase the difficulty.

Personal development is an ongoing practice. 

It’s your ability to question yourself, change your habits and slowly - but steadily - improve over time 📈 .

It’s about energy and consistency (think about a river carving its bed🌌).

And where you want to be (dreams, motivation, goal) 🎯 .

Over to you. What’s the number 1 life-changing habit you have implemented? I’d love to know your story 👇.
