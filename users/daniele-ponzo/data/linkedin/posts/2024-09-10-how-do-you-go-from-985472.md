How do you go from hesitant to fluent speaker?

It’s a million-dollar question.

I’ve tried to answer this (and other questions) in my latest episode of “the non-native leader” video Podcast.

And I had the valuable help of Martin Oetting, an artist, activist and filmmaker, who went from hesitant to fluent not only once, but 3 times (he speaks German, English, French and Italian).

When we learn or improve a language as adults, we have one thing which holds us back:

Pride

We are used to being the experts, we know our world, we took years to build our personality. 
Except this doesn’t work anymore when you have to speak a new language.

Martin has applied this philosophy also to his job.

He has just launched a new documentary called “𝐏𝐮𝐫𝐩𝐨𝐬𝐞”.  

The film is about the Economy, the Climate, the Society. When we can’t understand the world around us, we should stop, take a step back and question it.

This is exactly what he has done in his latest film: he asked some prominent figures in the #wellbeingeconomy, who are working on a solution.
