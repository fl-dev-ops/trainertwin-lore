Your message is getting 𝗟𝗢𝗦𝗧
and so are your opportunities.

When your English isn't clear, you risk more than just a few awkward moments.

❌ You hesitate, searching for the right words.
❌ You get stuck in your head, overthinking every sentence.
❌ You sound disconnected, even uninterested.
❌ You fail to convey your ideas with impact.

💬 "in my native language, I try to be as precise as possible and find the right words. In English, it’s worse. I don’t even know how to express myself."
💬 "When I speak, my mind goes blank… I start searching for words, unsure how to finish my sentence."

Sound familiar? You’re not alone.

Lack of clarity costs you:
⚠️ Missed job opportunities.
⚠️ Lost confidence in meetings.
⚠️ Miscommunication that leads to costly mistakes.

Clarity isn’t about knowing more English. 
It’s about making your message simple, structured, and natural.

✅ Do this instead:
 🔹 Use straightforward words, not complex ones.
 🔹 Speak in short, structured sentences.
 🔹 Focus on getting your point across, not sounding perfect.

If your ideas are clear, people will listen. 
If they’re vague, they’ll move on.
