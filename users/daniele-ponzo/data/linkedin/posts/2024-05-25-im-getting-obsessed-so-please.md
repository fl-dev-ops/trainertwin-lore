I’m getting obsessed, so please, help me!

#LinkedIn has recently introduced “pre-packed”, “ready-made” comments under people’s posts.

And I’m loving them ❤️ .

Seriously, they are served on a silver plate for you to pick. It’s like a commenting buffet with free food. 

Good to know! Can you resist that?

And the best thing is that they change according to your reaction. Amazing!

When you support a post, you can choose an astonishing “keep it up!”. If you find it insightful 💡 , you can reply with a deep and life changing “Very Helpful!”.

I got so much into them that I have started my own private collection.
 
It’s like Pokémon: I want to catch them all before they are gone.🎮🕹️👾

And here is where I need your help:

I want to make this the best collection ever.

A combination of reactions and languages. 

Please, send me a copy of pre-packed comments in your language for every reaction. I want to make the most comprehensive catalogue of human interaction at its deepest level.

It’s social research, machine learning and communication studies at their best.
