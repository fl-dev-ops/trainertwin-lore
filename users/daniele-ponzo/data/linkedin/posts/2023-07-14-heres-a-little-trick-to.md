Let me know if this sounds familiar...

Now you’re exhausted.😒

And worse, you start losing focus and concentration.

Why?

Because of Eye dryness and strain.👀

So what's the solution? It's actually really simple.
 
Simple but not easy.
 
Meaning everybody CAN do it, but most won't.

It's called Eye stretching. 👀🤸‍♀️

An athlete wouldn't run a marathon without stretching their legs.
 
A swimmer wouldn't set on without stretching their legs.
 
And you shouldn't have a full morning of eyes exercise without stretching them.

Here are some simple eye stretching exercises that you can do daily:

👉Blinking: blink your eyes rapidly for a few seconds to moisturize and refresh the eyes, preventing dryness and relieving strain.

👉Palming: Rub your palms together to generate heat and then gently place them over your closed eyes to relax the eye muscles.

👉Eye rolling: Slowly roll your eyes in a circular motion clockwise and then anticlockwise to improve eye flexibility

👉Focusing near and far: Focus on an object that is close to you, then shift your gaze to something distant to exercise the eye muscles.

👉Figure-eight eye movement: Imagine a large figure-eight shape in front of you and follow it with your eyes to strengthen eye muscles.

👉Eye massages: Gently massage the area around your eyes using your fingertips to stimulates blood circulation.

One final tip…

☕️take regular breaks from the computer screen, practice good posture, and ensure proper lighting conditions to further reduce eye strain.
