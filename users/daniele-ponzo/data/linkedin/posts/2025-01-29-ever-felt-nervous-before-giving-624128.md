Ever felt nervous before giving a presentation?
 
Pitching a project, explaining technical concepts, or leading a team meeting can be scary 😨.
Especially when English is not your first language
 
And when you get nervous, you end up:

⇢ Speaking in a Monotone (boring and harder to follow)
⇢ Reading Directly from Slides (disconnected from the audience)
⇢ Rushing Through the Presentation (you talk too fast)
⇢ Using Poor Structure (you confuse the audience)

I’ve been there myself:

🔴 Team members falling asleep
🔴 Fidgeting and using filler words
🔴 Making the audience impatient

What was I missing?

CLARITY

✅ Structure your presentation
✅ Use your words logically
