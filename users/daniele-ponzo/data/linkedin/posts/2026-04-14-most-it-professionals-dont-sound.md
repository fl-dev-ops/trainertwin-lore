Most IT professionals don’t sound less senior because of their English.

They sound less senior because of how they communicate.

I see the same mistakes in global teams every day:

𝗦𝘁𝗮𝗿𝘁𝗶𝗻𝗴 𝘄𝗶𝘁𝗵𝗼𝘂𝘁 𝗮 𝗰𝗹𝗲𝗮𝗿 𝗽𝗼𝗶𝗻𝘁
You begin with context, details, background…
and lose people halfway through.
👉 Say the conclusion first. Then explain.

𝗧𝗿𝘆𝗶𝗻𝗴 𝘁𝗼 𝘀𝗮𝘆 𝗲𝘃𝗲𝗿𝘆𝘁𝗵𝗶𝗻𝗴 𝗽𝗲𝗿𝗳𝗲𝗰𝘁𝗹𝘆
You search for the “right” words… and slow yourself down.
👉 Clarity beats perfection.

𝗢𝘃𝗲𝗿𝗲𝘅𝗽𝗹𝗮𝗶𝗻𝗶𝗻𝗴 𝘁𝗼 𝘀𝗼𝘂𝗻𝗱 𝘀𝗺𝗮𝗿𝘁
Long sentences. Too many details. No direction.
👉 Break ideas into simple, structured points.

𝗦𝗼𝗳𝘁𝗲𝗻𝗶𝗻𝗴 𝘆𝗼𝘂𝗿 𝗼𝗽𝗶𝗻𝗶𝗼𝗻 𝘁𝗼𝗼 𝗺𝘂𝗰𝗵
“Maybe… I think… I’m not sure…”
👉 You sound less confident than you are.
Be direct. Then support your point.

𝗧𝗿𝗮𝗻𝘀𝗹𝗮𝘁𝗶𝗻𝗴 𝘄𝗵𝗶𝗹𝗲 𝘀𝗽𝗲𝗮𝗸𝗶𝗻𝗴
You build the sentence in your head… then say it.
👉 Train patterns so you can think and speak at the same time.

Your English is not your problem.

Your communication is your signal.

And that signal determines how senior you sound, especially under pressure.

Think about your last meeting...

Which of these do you recognize in yourself?
