Today I have some Bad News and some Good News.

As it is customary, let’s start with the bad news. 😕 

It is impossible to get perfect English in a month.

In 2 months

In 6 months.

I can’t help you.

Actually, nobody can.

That’s a fact. Anybody who’s telling you otherwise, is not being honest.

So, when you see a “get fluent in 10 days method”, accept one piece of advice:

Stay away from it. Seriously. The risk is that you waste your time.
 
And you waste your money (which you are not getting back).

Now, for the good news. 🙂 

You don’t need perfect English to:

👩‍💼 Get a good job.
📈 Advance in your career
🌎 Work in an international environment.

Instead, you need to concentrate on:

✅ Getting more confident, so you find the right words to use in your meetings or in a job interview.
✅ Building the skills to communicate effectively at work, so you’ll get noticed by your colleagues and your boss.
✅ Identifying common mistakes so you can avoid embarrassing situations.

Want to know even better news? 😃 

Here is where I can actually help you.

My program is designed to equip you with exactly those skills.

To help you get comfortable in the typical business interactions.

Get you through those challenging moments when you need to interact in English.

And support you as you grow in your career.

Now, this is realistic.

Focus on the skills, not on the “perfection”. 

Interested in knowing more?

Feel free to reach out for a chat and we can discuss the best ways to take your English confidence to the next level 🚀
