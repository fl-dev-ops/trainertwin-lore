The meeting starts.

And your brain stops working.

You’re a developer.

You’ve already solved the problem.

So, you simply need to explain it.

And then your brain goes:

“Is this the right word…”
“Wait… how do I say this…”
“No… that sounds wrong…”

Someone asks:

“So what do you recommend?”

You start:

“Uhm… I think… maybe we could…”
Halfway through:


you lose the structure
you start searching
the sentence gets messy

And inside your head:

“What am I saying…”

And the moment is gone.
The worst part?

Not during the meeting.

After.

Because later…

the perfect sentence arrives.

Clear. Structured. Simple.

That’s 𝘭’𝘦𝘴𝘱𝘳𝘪𝘵 𝘥𝘦 𝘭’𝘦𝘴𝘤𝘢𝘭𝘪𝘦𝘳 (pardon my French). 

When the perfect reply arrives too late.

“I knew this…
 why couldn’t I say it like that…”

I hear this all the time:

💬 “My mind goes blank when I speak”
💬 “I know what I want to say… but I don’t know how to say it”
💬 “I spend too much time trying to find the right words”

It’s not that you don’t know.

It’s that your brain is doing two things at once:

solving the problem
and trying to say it perfectly

And under pressure…

That’s where things break.

So over time:

• you speak less
• you hesitate more
• you sound less confident than you are

And it starts to look like

you don’t have the answer.

Even when you do.

Most tech people think they need better English.

When they actually need a different way to speak when it matters.

▶️ If you’ve worked through this: what helped you stay clear when the pressure kicks in?
