"It's never going to work for me"

That voice in your head. Know it?
Most of us do.

Especially if we’re building something new.
Especially if we’re non-native professionals trying to show up, speak up, or sell what we do.

In the latest episode of The Non-Native Leader - Podcast, Language Consultant Gydion Kummer shares a story I think many of us need to hear.

🚫 Not the “fake it till you make it” version.
✅ The real process of going from self-doubt to self-belief.

Many times we think we're special
In the wrong way.

We see people succeed
We see happy entrepreneurs
We see confident speakers

And we think: "Me? This is never going to work for me".

But then things change.

You start talking to people
You take action
You gather evidence

And you think: wait, people trust me. 
They want to listen to me, they want to work with me
That voice in my head? It wasn’t the truth. It was just fear.

So, how do you defeat impostor syndrome?
You defeat it by doing anyway.

By collecting evidence that says:
📌 Yes, I can.
📌 Yes, people see value in what I do.
📌 Yes, I belong.

If you're sitting on an idea, a project, a shift you want to make… but that voice is holding you back, this episode is your reminder to do it anyway.
