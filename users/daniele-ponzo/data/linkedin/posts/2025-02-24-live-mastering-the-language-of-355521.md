LIVE: Mastering The Language of Business: Ireland & TECH

Can you guess what Google, Meta, Microsoft and LinkedIn have in common?
Yes, they have their European headquarters in Ireland

You might have heard of the “Silicon Docks”. 
If not, let me tell you: it’s the area in Dublin where Global tech giants are concentrated.

We all know that Tech speaks English. But more than ever, Irish-English.
