Of authority, nightmares, and twisted reality.

This is a story of as man who’s brainwashed and believes in a different reality.

And does things he wouldn’t normally do.

He is conditioned to behave in an irrational way and consume the life of the people he meets.

A rude, manipulative authority can create a parallel universe where people are no longer themselves.

Ah, this is a story from 1920.

I’m nerdy about black and white and silent films 🎞 .

I love the poetry, the techniques, and the way they were using a newly created medium.

“𝐓𝐡𝐞 𝐂𝐚𝐛𝐢𝐧𝐞𝐭 𝐨𝐟 𝐃𝐫. 𝐂𝐚𝐥𝐢𝐠𝐚𝐫𝐢“ by Robert Wiene is one of my favourite films of all times.

It has been defined as "Arguably the first true horror film”.

With its passionate acting, grotesque makeup and expressionist setting, it is truly disturbing.

The pointed forms and oblique lines reflect the alternative reality you see when you’re brainwashed.

A reality made of strong lights and shadows, not at all similar to the ones you see in the real world.

In this film, shadows and lights were painted directly onto the sets, to create a stronger contrast.

And immerse the viewer in a reality where nothing is real and the perspective changes all the time.

So, what happens when we are caught in an artificial reality, and we no longer control our will?

Can we escape the seduction?

And how do we deal with this “different” version of the self we have created?

Of course, I’m not going to spoil it.

But do yourself a favour: next time you have 74 free minutes, watch “The Cabinet of Dr. Caligari “.

And see for yourself.
