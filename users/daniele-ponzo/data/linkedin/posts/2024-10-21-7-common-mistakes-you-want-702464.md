7 Common Mistakes You Want to Avoid when You Use English.

To become confident with any language, we need to master soft skills and hard skills.

🛠️Hard skills are: grammar, pronunciation, vocabulary.
💬Soft skills include: cultural awareness, context, tone.

They all have a key role to play when we want to get our message across.

Some mistakes are mostly ok (articles, minor spelling mistakes).
But others can impact the effectiveness of your communication.

Imagine this: you take weeks to prepare your presentation, and then you sound:

❌ Rude
❌ Shabby
❌ Impolite
❌ unprofessional

because of your language, your intonation o the lack of cultural awareness.

I believe this is the last thing you want to do.

Make sure you check my carousel for some common mistakes and tips to avoid them.

So, next time you present you sound:

✅ Tidy 
✅ Polite
✅ Confident
✅ Professional

The result?

🔅CLARITY 🔅
