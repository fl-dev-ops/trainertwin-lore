Ever felt overwhelmed by Grammar?

I mean, “F” overwhelmed.

A few days ago, a professional reached out to me for some advice.

She is preparing for a B2 English exam (requested by her company).

She bought an English Grammar book, and she started studying everything she could find there.

Past, past perfect, mixed conditionals, inversion with negative adverbials, future in the past… 📖 

Too much already

But one thing struck me.

She told me. “Since I started studying for the exam, I’m afraid to speak. There is so much grammar and so many rules that I always think I’m making a mistake”.

As you can guess, her English was good, we could have a conversation, nothing wrong there.

But when you focus too much on one specific thing (grammar, in this case)

❌ You become too self-conscious.
❌ You start comparing yourself with the Grammar book
❌ And you create the perfect setting for Impostor Syndrome

The result?

We’ve seen it above: 

𝗙rustration
𝗙ragility
𝗙ear

Triple “𝗙” to make a horrific combination

So, is there a recipe to get rid of these “F” words?

I had my answer many years ago, when I was in a similar situation.

I showed my grammar exercises to my flatmate (a native English speaker) and he told me: “man, I’m not sure I would know how to solve them. But luckily, I don’t need them”.

So, I told myself: “why should I let grammar stop me?”

And I replaced “F” with “𝗦”:

✅ Frustration -> 𝗦𝗮𝘁𝗶𝘀𝗳𝗮𝗰𝘁𝗶𝗼𝗻: I can have a conversation in English!
✅ Fragility -> 𝗦𝘁𝗿𝗲𝗻𝗴𝘁𝗵: I’m getting better every day
✅ Fear -> 𝗦𝗲𝗹𝗳-𝗰𝗼𝗻𝗳𝗶𝗱𝗲𝗻𝗰𝗲: I’m not perfect, but I want to communicate

And this is exactly what I told her.

Speaking English has nothing to do with perfection.

It’s more about overcoming mental barriers
And putting yourself out there.

The more you do you, the easier it becomes.
