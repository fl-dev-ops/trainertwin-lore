Why do you like teaching?

I get this question a lot.

To be honest, it’s not an easy one.

I love the process: Every question I get forces me to think, to dig deep, to articulate my knowledge in a way that's understandable and actionable.

So, teaching allows me to learn at the same time.

This has been a vital part of my journey.

First, learning the ropes in a big multinational company, getting used to the language, the role, the culture 👩‍💼 .

Then, training people, helping them grow and grab career opportunities 📈 .

And then, starting anew, teaching and coaching people, learning to work with children, teenagers and adult professionals 👨‍🎓 .

This happens when you take continuous improvement literally

So, why do I like teaching, then? Because of the true connection you can build with people 🤝 .

For the everlasting memories, the effort, the journey and the joy you get when you help people.

Every challenge you face, every success you achieve, provides valuable insights about the learning process.

And all the lessons I learn from you, help make a difference in my life.

It’s a privilege to be there with you.

P.S. if you’d like to know more about my program, send me a message. I’ll be happy to chat. And maybe become your favourite teacher too 😉 .
