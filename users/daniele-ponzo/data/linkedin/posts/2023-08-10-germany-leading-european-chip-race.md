"Germany leading European chip race"



I firmly believe in diversification:

Chips, semiconductors are the "new oil" in many ways.

They are used to fuel our ever growing tech market.📈

So, I think it is a smart choice to move away from the reliance on a single country (notably China).

However, I'm surprised (ironic) that there hasn't been a joint EU strategy here.🤔

I mean, the German government is investing +$20bn. 

And mostly to subsidize the new factory.

I would have expected a similar investment at a European level, rather than seeing a "chip race".🇪🇺

This is one area where Europe should act as a single entity, I believe.

What are your thoughts? I would love to get some experts opinions on this.👇

P.S. I'm curious to see how China will respond ...
