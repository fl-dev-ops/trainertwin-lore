High-performing teams are diverse.

Different thinking styles.
Different cultural backgrounds.
Different communication patterns.

And yet…

In many global meetings, the smartest people speak the least.

Why?

Because cognitive diversity without structure becomes friction.

Some professionals:

– Process internally
– Translate in real time
– Structure before speaking
– Assess political risk
– Monitor tone and perception

By the time they’re ready…

The meeting has moved on.

Silence is often misinterpreted as lack of ideas.

When in reality, it’s overload.

Confidence isn’t personality-based.
It’s contextual.

English fluency doesn’t automatically create leadership presence.
And psychological safety must include linguistic safety.
