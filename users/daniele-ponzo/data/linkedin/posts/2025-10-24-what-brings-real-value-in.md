What brings real value in our work and leadership?

This question has been echoing in my mind for a while.

Not the kind of “value” we put in spreadsheets or KPIs.
But the kind that defines how we show up, connect, and lead.

I’m incredibly proud to be part of the “Let’s Talk Values” series hosted The Value Based Institute and curated by Eva Andersson, alongside other amazing professionals who are sharing powerful perspectives on leadership, culture, and purpose.

Each week, Eva introduces a new voice. 

And each week we get inspired by a new story. 

Stories of professionals who “walk the talk” and live by their values of authenticity, commitment and recognition.  

Here are just a few insights from the series that resonated deeply with me:

💡 “Conflict is inevitable, but how we handle it defines the culture we create.”
 💡 “Strategy must have soul. Without it, growth is just numbers. With it, growth becomes legacy.”
 💡 “If your people can’t see themselves in your strategy, you don’t have one.”
 💡 “Leadership is about opening doors for others and building bridges where others see walls.”

Being part of this initiative has reminded me that 𝗹𝗮𝗻𝗴𝘂𝗮𝗴𝗲, 𝗹𝗲𝗮𝗱𝗲𝗿𝘀𝗵𝗶𝗽, 𝗮𝗻𝗱 𝘃𝗮𝗹𝘂𝗲𝘀 are all deeply connected.

The way we speak (and listen) shapes the culture we build together.

If you want a weekly dose of inspiration and reflection, follow The Value Based Institute and meet all the guests: Jameson Jones-Doyle • Jean-Christian Rindoni • Ramla R. • Niklas Österberg 🇸🇪🇹🇿🇮🇳🇵🇱🇱🇻 • Harriet C. • Kathy Eastwood • Ines Bosnjak • Abbe Alzarzour • Saphie Nefer ALIM • Marc Lawn • Bobby Bakshi • Daniele Ponzo • Shekhar Kohli • Siam Hossain
