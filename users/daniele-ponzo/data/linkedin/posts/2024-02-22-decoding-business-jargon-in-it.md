Decoding Business Jargon in IT #5 

🐘 Unveiling the Elephant in the Room: 🚀

Today, let's talk about a commonly used phrase in the corporate world: "The Elephant in the Room."

What's the Elephant in the Room? 🤔🐘

In the business world, the "elephant in the room" symbolizes an obvious problem or issue that everyone is aware of but chooses not to address. 

Imagine a large, looming elephant representing a challenge that's silently present during meetings and discussions.

Why is This Jargon Relevant in IT?

Example: Picture a major software upgrade on the horizon, but the persistent issue of resource allocation remains the unspoken "elephant in the room."
