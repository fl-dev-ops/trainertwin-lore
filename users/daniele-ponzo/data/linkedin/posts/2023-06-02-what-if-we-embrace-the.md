What if we embrace the fortune cookie philosophy?

 

Ok, I don’t particularly love fortune cookies. However, one thing I love is the messages. They are utterly positive and, more important, they never expire (like the biscuits).

 

So, when you get a message saying: “An exciting week awaits you in all areas of your life”, well, I want to believe it!

I am going to put it on my wall, so I can read this over and over and I will get exciting weeks over and over 😉

 

This is the #fortunecookiephylosophy

 

Have a great weekend, everybody!
