LIVE: How to speak up with Confidence with Teodora Juduc

You know the answer, but your brain goes blank.

You’re in the meeting.
The client asks a question.
You literally just talked about this in Slack.
You know the answer. Easy.

But now it’s your turn to speak…
…and your brain goes on holiday.

“Wait, how do I say this clearly?”
“What if I sound unprepared?”
“Is this the right word? Maybe I should wait…”

So you smile.

You let someone else take it.
And later, you replay the whole moment in your head. 
In perfect English, of course.

Sound familiar?

You’re not alone. This happens to so many smart professionals
Especially if you’re new to leading or you’re speaking English as a second language.

And that’s exactly why I’ve teamed up with Ex-Engineering Leader and Communication Coach Teodora Juduc for a LIVE conversation this Friday:
