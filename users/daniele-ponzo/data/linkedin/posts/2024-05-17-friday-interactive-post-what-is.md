FRIDAY INTERACTIVE POST

What is the shortest job you’ve ever had?

I’ll start…

I once was a hotel porter.

For 2 hours. (two).

It was long time ago; I had recently graduated and was looking for a job.

Either a summer job or the job of my life, I didn’t know.

As you do, I was leaving my CV nearly everywhere in Dublin town.

Companies, shops, pubs…you name it.

Then one day I got a call from a hotel manager, he said he wanted to see me for an interview.

I went there and he started describing the job and everything.

Then he started asking me questions.

“So, Jimmy, tell me can you do this and that?”

“Jimmy, this is a 3-start hotel, and this is what we do…”

I stopped him and said I was no Jimmy…

And then he had a puzzled look on his face 🤔 .

He had mixed two CV’s, so he was interviewing the wrong person.

And then he went: ”ok, fine…the job is yours anyway”.

So, he got me started (had no idea what I was supposed to do).

And what happened next?

Sweeping the street, polishing knobs, cleaning rooms, hoovering stairs.

Setting up the function room, putting all the pens in the same direction.

EXACTLY the same direction 🖋 .

I wasn’t ready for that.

(And I hadn’t really applied for that job anyway).

So, after 2 hours I called it a day.

And I quit.

Over to you now: What is the shortest job you’ve ever had?
