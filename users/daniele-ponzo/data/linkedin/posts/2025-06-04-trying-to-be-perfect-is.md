Trying to be perfect is exactly what’s making you sound unsure.

Here’s something I hear all the time:

“I don’t want to speak because I’m scared I’ll mess up the grammar.”
Let’s be honest: 
grammar has been used more to silence people than to help them speak.

And the truth is, most professionals aren’t making huge grammar mistakes.

They’re just overthinking every sentence, afraid of getting it slightly wrong.

But here's the thing:
🧠 Native speakers make grammar mistakes every day.
🧠 Managers and executives don't pause mid-sentence to check if it's past perfect.
🧠 No one is counting your verb tenses. They’re listening for clarity, confidence, and presence.

Grammar isn’t about being perfect.

It’s about knowing what structures help you explain your ideas clearly and sound professional in real situations.

That’s what today’s mini-masterclass is all about: 
Grammar: 𝗦𝗼𝘂𝗻𝗱 𝗣𝗿𝗼𝗳𝗲𝘀𝘀𝗶𝗼𝗻𝗮𝗹 (𝗪𝗶𝘁𝗵𝗼𝘂𝘁 𝗢𝘃𝗲𝗿𝘁𝗵𝗶𝗻𝗸𝗶𝗻𝗴 𝗘𝘃𝗲𝗿𝘆 𝗦𝗲𝗻𝘁𝗲𝗻𝗰𝗲)

We’ll cover:
 ✅ The structures you actually need in interviews, meetings, and negotiations
 ✅ How to stop second-guessing yourself mid-sentence
 ✅ What "good grammar" sounds like when you're leading a conversation
