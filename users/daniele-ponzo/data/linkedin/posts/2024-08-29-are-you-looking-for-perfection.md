Are you looking for perfection 🤓 ?

When it comes to English speaking, many people are obsessed with perfection.

Perfect grammar, perfect pronunciation, perfect writing.

BUT this can be a limiting belief ❌ .

Preventing you from making REAL progress.

In my experience, focusing on the learning rather than the final “perfect” result made a huge difference.

And that is exactly what I did.

I decided I wanted to HACK my English👌.

🅗 𝐇𝐨𝐧𝐞: Refine your vocabulary so you sound more professional
🅐 𝐀𝐜𝐭𝐢𝐨𝐧: Put yourself out there, interact so you learn and grow.
🅒 𝐂𝐨𝐥𝐥𝐚𝐛𝐨𝐫𝐚𝐭𝐢𝐨𝐧: Create a strong network with your peers so you exchange ideas and feedback.
🅚 𝐊𝐮𝐝𝐨𝐬: Celebrate milestones along the way so you boost your morale and motivation.

This simple, yet effective strategy gave me the energy and the confidence I needed in my career.

I focused on the practical learning process.
