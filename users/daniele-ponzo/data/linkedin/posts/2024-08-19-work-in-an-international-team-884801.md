Work in an international team? Then this might be you…

Sometimes you just want to mute your microphone and disappear.

Or hide at the back of the room and keep still, hoping they don’t notice you in the meeting, like the T-Rex scene in Jurassic Park🦖.

And what about getting that phone call from abroad? You run to your English-speaking colleague as if they had found the Holy Grail🌟.

Everything you want to do is run away🏃‍♂️.

But you’re also running away from opportunities.

Opportunities to speak, to contribute, to be noticed.

Does it have to stay this way?

Not really.

Now, imagine offering support in your next meeting.

Or sharing your personal view in your next conference call.

Or picking up that phone with confidence when someone calls.

How does it feel?

Better, I hope.

And the great thing is that there is no need to become “Barack-Obama-confident” overnight.

The truth is, it all starts with a very small step.

Raising your hand in your next meeting✋.

Turning on your microphone to introduce yourself🎙.

Pressing the green button on your phone to answer the call📞.

And when your colleagues are listening to you.

When your manager thanks you for your contribution

Or when you can understand your international client…

It feels great!

So, next time you’re in meeting, give yourself a small objective.

I'll leave here some phrases you could use:

🔘Initiating the Conversation:
"Hi everyone, I hope you're all doing well. I'd like to share my thoughts on..."
"Good morning/afternoon, everyone. I'm excited to be here and contribute to the discussion."

🔘Asking Open-Ended Questions:
"Could you share your experiences with similar challenges in the past?"
Expressing Curiosity or Interest:
"I'm really interested in learning more about how we can improve our processes."

🔘Sharing Personal Experiences or Insights:
"In my previous role, I encountered a similar issue and found that..."

🔘Offering Support or Agreement:
"I agree with what John mentioned earlier. I think that's a valid point."
"I'm fully supportive of the proposed solution and am willing to assist in any way I can."

🔘Acknowledging Others' Contributions:
"I appreciate the deep analysis Sarah provided in her presentation."
"Thanks to everyone for their valuable input so far. It's been enlightening."

🔘Expressing Confidence with Humility:
"I'm still learning, but I believe my perspective might offer a different angle."
"I'm not entirely sure, but I'd like to propose..."

🔘Seeking Clarification or Verification:
"Could you repeat that last point, please? I want to make sure I understand correctly."
"Just to clarify, are we discussing the budget for Q4 or Q1 next year?"

🔘Expressing Appreciation for Feedback:
"Thank you for your insights. I'll definitely take them into consideration."
"I appreciate the feedback and will work on incorporating it into my approach."

Are you ready to face the T-Rex🦖?
