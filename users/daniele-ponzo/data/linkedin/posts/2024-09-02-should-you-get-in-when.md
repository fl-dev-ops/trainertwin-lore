Should you get in?

When you decide you want to improve your career, scary things start to happen 😱 .

First, you think you haven’t got what it takes (imposter syndrome).

Then, you build up your credibility, which means speaking up and making sure your managers notice you.

Finally, you start handling tricky situations, like customer presentations, sales calls.

The more you go up, the scarier it gets😨.

This is exactly what happened to me.
The first time I was asked to present in front of an international client.

“I’m still a junior in my role, the customer will notice this”
“What if I make a grammar mistake and I sound like a joke?”
“I will just read the slides, so I don’t get lost”.

And I was getting restless.

So, I opted for a different strategy. Practice.

↳ I had a quick look at my slides and described them.
↳ I started speaking to myself, rehearsing my speech without reading.
↳ I visualized the situation and rehearsed a few times more.

And I decided to get in🔑🚪:

In the room, in the situation, in the role.

It felt scary at first but got better as I was speaking and eventually became easier.

And I started to feel confident with myself.

The right strategy can get you there.
Just trust yourself and try.

Over to you, now. Do you feel ready to get in? let me know in the comments.
P.S. I actually went in and visited the castle🏰…not scary at all, it was empty and uninhabited
