Why I Stopped Obsessing Over Mistakes (And You Should Too!)

I think we all know that feeling:

❌ Sending an email and realizing there was a mistake.
❌ Holding back in meetings because we feared saying something wrong.
❌ Feeling like we’d never sound professional enough.

I’ve been there. And so has Luther Lebtag, my guest in the latest episode of The Non-Native Leader podcast. 🎤 

Luther built a successful international career in IT, leading global projects for Lenovo. But when he started, he faced the same struggles that many non-native professionals do.

The turning point? He stopped obsessing over mistakes and focused on communication.

🔹 He learned that fluency matters more than perfect grammar.
🔹 He embraced feedback—even when it was hard to hear.
🔹 He realized that confidence comes from practice, not from avoiding errors.

If you’ve ever held yourself back because of language doubts, this episode is for you.
