People often ask me what the most rewarding part of running a podcast is.
And I'm not obsessed with downloads or metrics.

But I'm obsessed with connection.

➡️ With stories
➡️ With people
➡️ With the parts of ourselves we forget to celebrate

This week I had the chance to reconnect with my friends Luca Primavera who also happens to be the guest of Episode 20 of The Non-Native Leader - Podcast 

It had been a while since we last sat down in person, and yet, as always, his story reminded me of something essential.

What did I learn from him?
✅ That building a career abroad takes more than courage. It takes vision
✅ That adapting to new cultures starts with curiosity, not certainty
✅ That it's OK to re-invent yourself, even if it means changing your name (!)
✅ That mindset is a strategy, especially in job interviews

All of this while having fun...

We spoke in the episode about music, interviews, moving abroad, and becoming your own definition of success.

You’ll find the episode in the first comment 👇

Wishing you all a great weekend!
