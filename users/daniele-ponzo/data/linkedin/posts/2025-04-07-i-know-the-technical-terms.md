“I know the technical terms. But when I have to explain my ideas, I struggle.”

That’s what an experienced project manager told me yesterday.

And I know you might feel the same.

After years in tech, you’ve nailed the vocabulary.

You understand the docs.
You follow the meetings.
You get the technical language.

But when it’s time to speak, present, or lead a discussion
💬 The words don’t come out right.
🧩 Your message gets lost.
😶‍🌫️ Your confidence disappears.

Here’s the truth:

It’s not your technical vocabulary.

It’s everything around it.
 → The connectors
 → The structure
 → The flow
 → The storytelling

And that gap?

It’s not just frustrating.
It’s limiting your 𝘃𝗶𝘀𝗶𝗯𝗶𝗹𝗶𝘁𝘆, your 𝗼𝗽𝗽𝗼𝗿𝘁𝘂𝗻𝗶𝘁𝗶𝗲𝘀, and your 𝗰𝗮𝗿𝗲𝗲𝗿 𝗴𝗿𝗼𝘄𝘁𝗵.

Because no matter how good you are at your job

If you can’t communicate your value,
Others won’t see it either.

So what’s the fix?

Don’t just work on what you say.
Start working on how you say it.

That’s how you move from:
❌ Silent contributor → ✅ Visible expert
❌ Missed chances → ✅ Clear influence
❌ “Good job” → ✅ “We want you leading this”

Let’s talk about how to close that gap.
