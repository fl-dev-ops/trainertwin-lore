AI can write your emails

It can't speak for you in meetings.
  
You spend time polishing the perfect message.
  
AI improves the wording.
  
Grammarly fixes the grammar.
  
DeepL suggests a better expression.
  
You press 𝗦𝗲𝗻𝗱 feeling confident.
  
Then someone replies:
  
"𝗖𝗮𝗻 𝘄𝗲 𝗷𝘂𝗺𝗽 𝗼𝗻 𝗮 𝗾𝘂𝗶𝗰𝗸 𝗰𝗮𝗹𝗹?"
  
And suddenly...
  
everything changes.
  
Your mind speeds up.
  
You start translating.
  
You search for the "perfect" word.
  
You worry about making mistakes.
  
Your mental CPU is now running several processes at once.
  
That's because writing and speaking are completely different skills.
  
Writing lets you:
  
✔ pause
  
✔ edit
  
✔ rewrite
  
Speaking removes all three.
  
It's real time.
  
That's why so many international professionals tell me:
  
"Sometimes we try to take async communication to real life. When we receive a message, we have time to overthink and answer later. But in real life if we do this, we look disconnected"
  
The problem isn't your English.
  
It's the extra cognitive load that only live conversations create.
  
The good news?
  
Speaking is trainable.
  
By practising communication under real pressure.
  
Because we don't want two different versions of you.
  
The confident professional who writes brilliant emails...
  
and the hesitant professional who disappears in meetings.
  
We want those two people to become one.
  
AI is an incredible writing assistant.
  
But confidence still has to come from you.
  
💬 I'm curious.
  
Which feels more challenging today?
  
✍️ Writing an email
  
or
  
🎤 Speaking in a meeting?
