“Spero che non mi chiedano niente”

“espero que no me pregunten nada“

"J'espère qu'ils ne me demandent rien"

"Ich hoffe, sie fragen mich nichts"

Sound familiar?

This was me (and a lot of my colleagues) a few years ago.

Every single time there was a meeting or a call.
With English speaking people.

The fear of being asked for updates.

The sheer terror of making mistakes. 

The daunting sensation of not having the right words.

And falling into an embarrassing silence 😱 .

In front of your colleagues, your clients, and your boss.

Can you see the issue here?

Not only you’re not taking active part in meetings.

But you’re missing opportunities (are they going to put you forward for a promotion if they don’t know your voice?)

And, even worst, you’re not in control.

You’re always hoping to remain a spectator.

So, what’s the solution?

A mindset shift.

▶ Start with small steps.
▶Get out of your comfort zone.
▶And put together all your small wins.

Repeat this every day.

“Practice makes perfect“ they say.

And it’s true.

You become skilled with practice.
And practice will give you experience.
And eventually confidence.

So, next time you are in a:

⮕  Video call
⮕  Meeting
⮕  "1 to 1"
⮕  Presentation

Change your perspective.

Stop being invisible.

And become a protagonist.

So you can stand up and say:

“’I’m here to talk, to contribute and be heard. Out of my shell I come”.
