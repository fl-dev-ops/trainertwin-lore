Yesterday, my son built my replacement. 🤖
 
A little LEGO robot...
 
holding my book.
 
I have to admit, I smiled.
 
It's probably the most "tech" version of me anyone has ever seen.
 
Then I started wondering...
 
With AI moving so fast...
 
and now my son building robots...
 
should I be worried? 😄
 
Luckily, I think this little guy still needs a few communication lessons before he can coach software engineers.
 
So I think my job is safe.
 
For now.
 
Have a wonderful weekend, everyone!
