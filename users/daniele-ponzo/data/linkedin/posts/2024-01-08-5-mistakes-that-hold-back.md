5 mistakes that hold back IT Pros confidence in English.

(Point #4 is very relevant – it can make or break your career)

1. Avoiding Informal Conversations:

👉 Those casual conversation at work are gold. They help you with exposure to everyday English usage, support the development of natural conversational skills and help you get noticed at work.
✅ So, instead of waiting for others to ask you questions, take the lead., Introduce yourself, open up and try to make “small talk”.

2. Overemphasis on Technical Jargon:

👉I get it, technical words feel comfortable. You know them and you use them every day. But if you get a blank face or an embarrassed silence? In multicultural settings not everyone may be familiar with complex technical terms.
✅Find the right balance between technical jargon and plain language, and make sure your communication is clear.

3. Not Participating in Meetings:

👉Instead of dreading the next team meeting, take control. Prepare some questions, reflect on your contribution and talk about what you do.
✅This is the best way to build confidence and overcome that fear of making mistakes. 

4. Lack of Proactive Learning:

👉Company courses are good, and they are usually free. But what if they never come? Or if they are too late? Waiting for formal language training can actually slow you down.
✅Take ownership of your language skills and start improving them independently. Be proactive. 

5. Fear of Feedback:

👉Feedback is a powerful tool for growth. Let’s use it effectively to boost our confidence. How? 
↳ Be specific.
↳ Choose the right person.
↳ Set a positive tone.
✅Once you get some feedback, create a practical action plan. For instance, if pronunciation is highlighted, practice those specific sounds regularly.

Remember, confidence is a skill, and it can be developed and improved. You have more control over it than you think.

Am I missing anything? Let me know in the comments.
