Take a step back, so you can move forward➡️.

This is the best advice when it comes to language confidence.

When you play chess, your ego, your pride, your “adulthood” want you go forward♟️.

Attack

Push

Conquer

Win.

But the reality is different.

Sometimes you are in an unfamiliar situation.

You don’t understand what’s going on around you.

Everybody seems stronger, more expert, more at ease.

Your pieces are surrounded, your king is in danger, you might fail.

And here you have 2 options.

🔴 Keep pushing, head down. You win all or you lose all. No compromise.

🟢 Pause, accept to go back, embrace reality. You are in a weak position, you need a new strategy.

In a game of chess, you would pick the second option.

Move backwards to advance.🔙➡️

And get closer to your target.🎯

When it comes to language, I picked the second option too.

I decided to let go of my ego.

I swallowed my pride.

And embraced ignorance.

When you want to improve as an adult, this is the only viable option.

We have to accept that we are NOT experts anymore.

And we often don’t understand what’s going on around us.

And this powerful mental switch
Gets us closer to our goal.

Checkmate👑!
