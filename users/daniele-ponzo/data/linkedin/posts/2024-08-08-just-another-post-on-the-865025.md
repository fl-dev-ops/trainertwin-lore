Just another post on the Olympics.

What do Miltiadis Tentoglou and the guy on the right have in common 🤔 ?

(I’m not talking about the hair colour)

Yeah, I’m talking about “practice”.

How did Miltiadis become the best long jumper two days ago🥇? 

He repeated the long jump countless times, until he could master it to perfection. 

Sure, he needs to know how to run and he has to be fit.

But ultimately, repetition and focus on the main skill have made the difference.

And the guy on the right?

For sure, he needs to know the language, the rules, the grammar.

But they are not enough to become a confident communicator.

Role play, repetition, rehearsal. 

In one sentence?

Live and experience the situation before facing it in real life. 

Don’t focus on the language as a whole, but on the skills you need to progress in your career.

P.S. one thing I love about Miltiadis story is that he’s an athlete by “accident”. He built up his expertise by doing parkour as a teenager, until he was noticed by a coach and started doing athletics🏃🏻.

So, your current situation and your level don’t matter much.

There isn’t just one way of learning.

It doesn’t have to be linear.

It’s never too late!
