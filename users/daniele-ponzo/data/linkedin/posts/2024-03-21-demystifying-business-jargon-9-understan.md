Demystifying Business jargon #9: Understanding "Cutting-Edge" 
 
Ok, this might look a bit scary🔪… but it comes up very often in business discussions.
 
What does it really mean? 
 
Let's break it down together!
 
When talking about Technology, being "cutting-edge" means being at the forefront of innovation and advancement📱🖥️. 
 
It's about pushing boundaries, pioneering new ideas, and staying ahead of the curve. 
  
It can be used as a noun or, more often as an adjective.
 
Here are a couple of examples to illustrate:
 
🔹 Example 1: "Our company prides itself on delivering cutting-edge  solutions to our clients. With our team's expertise, we're constantly exploring emerging technologies to stay ahead of the competition."
 
🔹 Example 2: "As an IT manager, it's crucial to keep our systems and processes cutting-edge to meet the evolving needs of our organization. We're always on the lookout for innovative tools and strategies to optimize efficiency and drive growth."
 
Origin: In the literal sense a cutting-edge is the “cutting surface of a blade or tool”, especially referred to ploughs (used in farming to turn the soil over)🚜.
Its figurative usage started in the mid ‘60s.
Source: Etymonline
 
Embracing the cutting-edge mindset is essential for IT managers and team leaders looking to drive innovation and lead their teams to success.
