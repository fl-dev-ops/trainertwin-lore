Struggle to speak English fluently under pressure? Try this ⬇️ .

You know your work.
You know English.
But when you’re suddenly asked to speak in a meeting, introduce yourself, or answer a question on the spot…

Your mind goes blank
Words come out slowly, with lots of pauses
You feel nervous or afraid of being judged

It’s frustrating because it feels like all your study disappears the moment the spotlight is on you.

And if you're like me, you start telling yourself
1️⃣ “Maybe English just isn’t for me.”
2️⃣ “I’m too shy to ever speak under pressure.”
3️⃣ “People will judge me if I make mistakes.”

Here’s the truth: speaking under pressure is a skill, and like any skill, it can be trained, if you practice differently.

Instead of waiting for a “big moment” and hoping for the best, practice with small, time‑boxed challenges that gradually train your brain to think and speak faster.

Here’s a 4‑step routine you can try this week 👇

1️⃣ Describe Your Room (90 sec)
Look around and describe what you see
Explain what each object is used for
🎯 Goal: Speak without long pauses

2️⃣ Describe the View (90 sec)
Open your window and describe everything you see
Imagine explaining it to someone who can’t see it
🎯 Goal: Keep talking continuously for 90 seconds

3️⃣ Describe Your Day (2 min)
Talk through your routine, actions, and the people you meet
Prepare bullet points — no script
🎯 Goal: 2 minutes, minimal “uhms” or hesitations

4️⃣ Explain Your Job (2 min)
Describe your role to someone outside your field
Give examples and explain simply
🎯 Goal: 2 minutes, clear and confident flow

Extra Tip:
Record yourself and listen back (or ask for feedback)
Drop 50 cents in a piggy bank for every “uhm” or pause... make it fun!🪙

Fluency under pressure is built one micro‑target at a time.
Start small, practice daily, and watch your confidence grow.

Which of these 4 exercises will you try first?
Give yourself 90 seconds today — and see how it feels! 🎯👇
