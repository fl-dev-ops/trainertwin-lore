How to Fail to Speak English in the Real World

Are you planning to be as ineffective as possible with your spoken English this season? 👍 

Do you want to keep skipping international conversations?

Then follow my list to make sure your real-world English stays unsuccessful:

𝗚𝗿𝗮𝗺𝗺𝗮𝗿-𝗙𝗶𝗿𝘀𝘁 𝗔𝗽𝗽𝗿𝗼𝗮𝗰𝗵
Focus on perfecting grammar rules through repetition and worksheets before attempting to speak.
🌟 Perfect for those who are looking for perfection and overthinking.

𝗠𝗲𝗺𝗼𝗿𝗶𝘇𝗲 𝗩𝗼𝗰𝗮𝗯𝘂𝗹𝗮𝗿𝘆 𝗟𝗶𝘀𝘁𝘀
Learn words in isolation, unrelated to actual contexts (e.g., lists of fruits, animals, or random adjectives).
🌟As exciting as going through the yellow pages (boomer reference here).

𝗧𝗿𝗮𝗻𝘀𝗹𝗮𝘁𝗶𝗼𝗻 𝗘𝘅𝗲𝗿𝗰𝗶𝘀𝗲𝘀
Translate sentences or paragraphs from your native language into English and vice versa.
🌟Ideal if you want to reinforce your dependency on thinking in the native language and make spontaneous English speech harder.

𝗧𝗲𝗮𝗰𝗵𝗲𝗿-𝗖𝗲𝗻𝘁𝗲𝗿𝗲𝗱 𝗖𝗹𝗮𝘀𝘀𝗿𝗼𝗼𝗺𝘀
Attend classes where teachers lecture in the native language about English rules, with minimal participation from students.
🌟This is the best if you’re looking for passive understanding of English. This often comes with the bonus zero speaking practice.

𝗥𝗲𝗽𝗲𝗮𝘁 𝗦𝗲𝘁 𝗣𝗵𝗿𝗮𝘀𝗲𝘀
Memorize polite expressions like “How are you?” or “I’m fine, thank you” without learning how to adapt these to actual conversations.
🌟In the age of AI it’s great to sound like a Robot: avoid understanding of real-life variations.

𝗙𝗼𝗰𝘂𝘀 𝗼𝗻 𝗡𝗮𝘁𝗶𝘃𝗲-𝗟𝗶𝗸𝗲 𝗣𝗿𝗼𝗻𝘂𝗻𝗰𝗶𝗮𝘁𝗶𝗼𝗻
You need to get a “perfect” accent, a native-like diction and enunciation.
🌟Great if you want to avoid speaking for fear of sounding “wrong”!

𝗠𝗲𝗺𝗼𝗿𝗶𝘇𝗲 𝗜𝗿𝗿𝗲𝗴𝘂𝗹𝗮𝗿 𝗩𝗲𝗿𝗯𝘀
Repeat lists of irregular verb conjugations (e.g., go-went-gone) without practice in sentences or dialogue.
🌟I think I’ll go back to the yellow pages above…

𝗢𝗻𝗹𝘆 𝗥𝗲𝗹𝘆 𝗼𝗻 𝗔𝗽𝗽𝘀
Only use apps like Duolingo or Babbel for learning but never engage in face-to-face speaking practice.
🌟Who needs spontaneous speech when you can interact with your phone!

Speaking English in the real world has never been so hard.

Ready to turn off your camera, sit at the back of the room and pass that international call to your colleague? 👌 

Then, keep doing…
