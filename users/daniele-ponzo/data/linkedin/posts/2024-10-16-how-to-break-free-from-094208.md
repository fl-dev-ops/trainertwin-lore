How to break free from hesitation and become a confident English speaker.

At the beginning of your international journey, FEAR is there with you.

😬  You don’t understand others
😨  Others don’t understand you
😱  You feel left out

Have you ever experienced this?

Your self esteem is affected because you can’t express yourself.

And imposter syndrome kicks in.

It’s like you bought a ticket to the wrong rollercoaster and all you want is to get out 🎢 .

BUT it doesn’t have to be like that

Recently, I had the opportunity to chat with Reanne Besa for my video podcast series.

She has 1 clear recipe for overcoming fear and imposter syndrome:

‘Put yourself out there’ 🔅 .

How?

✅ Challenge yourself
✅ Have small yet effective goals
✅ Make it fun

Watch some highlights of our conversation below and tell me:

How are you challenging yourself?
