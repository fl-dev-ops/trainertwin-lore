Have Courage and be Kind

Is this the perfect recipe for teaching a language?

I’ve discussed this in Episode 21 of my Video podcast series 🎤 : 
𝗧𝗵𝗲 𝗡𝗼𝗻-𝗡𝗮𝘁𝗶𝘃𝗲 𝗟𝗲𝗮𝗱𝗲𝗿.

A special episode this time, as I talked to a colleague.

Donna Maravillas has been teaching English for more than 10 years. And she is a language learner herself (first English, then Italian).

So she knows challenges, pains, wins and rewards.

And like many teachers, she started by helping someone close.
Her daughter, in her case.

Then, she decided to study, practice and get certified to start teaching other children🧒.

Really showing Kindness & Courage❤️.

So, there isn’t one “best method” to teach a language.

And surely a unique secret for learning a language hasn’t been found yet.

But I believe there is a great approach, one that uses Courage and Kindness.

The same approach that allows you to embrace new cultures, languages, and professional growth.
