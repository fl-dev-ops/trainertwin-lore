Slow down to get there faster.

A few years ago, I was travelling around Senegal 🇸🇳.

A van, 10 friends, 2 local guides.

It was the trip of a lifetime. Villages, sandy roads, amazing hospitality, breathtaking landscapes🌅.

We were flowing seamlessly in our little van🚐.

Until

The sandy road played a nice trick on us.

Yeah, the van wasn’t moving anymore.

Picture this:

In the middle of nowhere. No villages nearby. Wheels biting into the sand with no result.

The driver went on accelerating, trying to get the van out of the sandpit.

It was all useless, we weren’t moving an inch.

Excitement quickly turned into panic 😱 .

We’re going nowhere!

BUT then.

We did the only sensible thing, given the situation.

Took a deep breath, slowed down for a minute and approached the situation from a different angle.

And we started pushing🫸.

It wasn’t easy: an old van, stuck in the sand in the tropical heat🥵.

Not the ideal situation for a workout.

But we did it!

Centimetre after centimetre, we slowly got out van out of the sand.

And when we saw it moving again…

…we celebrated with a loud “hurray”🎉.

In a moment when speed wasn’t getting us anywhere.

Slowness changed the game.

There are times when to reach your goal, to hit your target, you have to slow down.

To keep track of your thoughts, to avoid panic and to find a solution.

P.S. Have you ever been in a similar situation😮? Let me know in the comments below  .
