Bye-bye Silicon Valley 👋 

The leaves are brown, and the sky is grey… what are you dreaming of?

In the last couple of weeks, I had the opportunity to chat with two great professionals.

A Senior UI Designer and a Project Engineer.

Pretty much the same age.
Similar stage of their career. 
Same ambition.

And pretty good at their job.

So good that they got noticed by recruiters from tech giants in California.

▶️ "Hey, I saw your profile and the work you're doing. Why don't you apply for our open position here?"
▶️"We feel you have the skills we're looking for; we would like to put you forward for a job interview".

But unfortunately, they also have another thing in common:

𝗟𝗮𝗰𝗸 𝗼𝗳 𝗰𝗼𝗻𝗳𝗶𝗱𝗲𝗻𝗰𝗲 𝘄𝗶𝘁𝗵 𝘁𝗵𝗲𝗶𝗿 𝗘𝗻𝗴𝗹𝗶𝘀𝗵.

So, they both declined the offers.

❌ "I can't work for an American company; it would be impossible for me to understand them"
❌"The American market? A job interview with an English-speaking manager? I can't do that, sorry".

When things like these happen, you always have 2 choices:

➡️ Complain, blame it on the bad moment, bad timing, bad situation... And keep doing more of the same.
➡️ Realize this was a great opportunity, but it's not the last, so you take action to solve your problem.

What’s the best way to after your dream?

The second, of course.

𝗗𝗿𝗲𝗮𝗺𝘀...
✅ Need careful planning.
✅ A solid strategy.
✅ And action.

So, next time California comes knocking, will you be in and ready to talk?

Or will you hide in a corner?

California dreamin' (California dreamin')
On such a winter's day...
