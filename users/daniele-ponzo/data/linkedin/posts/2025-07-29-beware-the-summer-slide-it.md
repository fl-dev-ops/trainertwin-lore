Beware the “summer slide”… it hits adults too! 

We usually talk about the summer slide in schools
that quiet loss of skills and knowledge that happens when students check out for a couple of months.

But here’s the secret no one tells you: it happens to professionals too.

When we take a long break from using English 
(or any skill we care about),
things start to slip:

𝗙𝗹𝘂𝗲𝗻𝗰𝘆 feels slower, and words don’t come as easily. We find ourselves “mumbling” more

𝗖𝗼𝗺𝗽𝗿𝗲𝗵𝗲𝗻𝘀𝗶𝗼𝗻. Listening and reading take a hit because of lack of exposure, and nuance feels harder to catch

𝗩𝗼𝗰𝗮𝗯𝘂𝗹𝗮𝗿𝘆. we forget words, we forget phrases, and the vocabulary starts melting… yes, like an ice cream in the sun 

𝗚𝗿𝗮𝗺𝗺𝗮𝗿 is generally more resistant, but mistakes creep in as rules get fuzzy…like your grammar is experiencing sunstroke

𝗣𝗿𝗼𝗻𝘂𝗻𝗰𝗶𝗮𝘁𝗶𝗼𝗻 and confidence suffer without regular practice… where are the silent letters? Lost them…

This slide is sneaky. 
You don’t notice it day to day, 
but by September… it shows.

The antidote?

One word: 𝗖𝗼𝗻𝘀𝗶𝘀𝘁𝗲𝗻𝗰𝘆.

A little practice, even 10 minutes a day, beats the all-or-nothing approach.

Without sacrificing your summer fun. 🌴
