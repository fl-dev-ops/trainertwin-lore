Those 8 confusing pairs can make or break your English communication.



Do you want to communicate with precision 👌 ?

How about clarifying intensity or frequency?

Or maybe you want to showcase your professionalism.

Then you need adverbs:

Efficiently, occasionally, successfully

"The system works 𝗲𝗳𝗳𝗶𝗰𝗶𝗲𝗻𝘁𝗹𝘆."
 
"We 𝗼𝗰𝗰𝗮𝘀𝗶𝗼𝗻𝗮𝗹𝗹𝘆 encountered issues."
 
"We 𝘀𝘂𝗰𝗰𝗲𝘀𝘀𝗳𝘂𝗹𝗹𝘆 completed the project."
 
And they lived happily ever after ❤️ …
 
Or did they 💔 ?
 
Unfortunately, things are rarely straightforward in English.
 
Some adverbs can be really confusing, and they can truly impact the effectiveness of your message.
 
Hardly, hard, lately, late, nearly, near, especially, specially, actually 🤔 …

So, if you want to use your adverbs confidently, I strongly recommend reviewing the carousel below 👇 for some examples.

I promise you will clearly understand the difference between those confusing adverbs.

And your doubts will disappear completely 🌟 !
