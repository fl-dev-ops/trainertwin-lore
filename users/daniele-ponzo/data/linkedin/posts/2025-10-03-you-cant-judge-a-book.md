You can't judge a book by its cover…

Over the last few weeks, I’ve shared stories about the struggles IT professionals face when English is part of the job.

Real struggles I hear all the time:
 👉 “My mind goes blank in meetings.”
 👉 “I spend so much time searching for the right words that I end up sounding insecure.”
 👉 “I’ve lost opportunities because my English wasn’t strong enough.”

And these struggles come from people who have a good level of English. 
They can read, write and watch videos.
But they feel they can’t speak.

So, these don’t look like grammar problems. 
They’re confidence, communication, and visibility problems.
