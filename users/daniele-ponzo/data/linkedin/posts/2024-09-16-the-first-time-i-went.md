The first time I went through the looking glass.

Didn’t meet strange animals there, though 🐰 .

It was a good few years ago.

I had just been promoted as Team Leader.

Of a high-profile Service Delivery Team.

So, when the next team meeting came, I was no longer sitting at my desk, as a spectator.

This time I was on stage. The meeting was mine. I had to present, lead, explain, engage 👨‍💼 .

And to make things worst.

I had to introduce and “sell” the new company strategy. Which was based on the scary word:

CHANGE 😱 

Change processes, change workflows, change priorities.

Needless to say, I felt like the biggest impostor ever.

“My slides will be boring”
“I won’t find the right words”
“People won’t trust me”

A Mantra coming straight from Mr. Insecurity Guru 😈 .

The days before the meeting, as I worked on my slides, I felt panic, anxiety and stress kicking in.

Standing on the other side of the room? No way!

But, as we all do in times of distress, I tapped into my inner strength, and something clicked.

What do I have to offer, I asked myself?

Empathy, Experience, Humour (EEH, in short) ✨ .

So, I decided to use them to prepare my slides, my presentation and the meeting.

🔹 Empathy: “I know how you guys are feeling, we’ve been doing things in the same way for years now, so change is hard”.

🔹Experience: “However, most of us have worked overtime, we’ve failed a few deliveries and we’re working in silos. Let’s put a stop to that”.

🔹Humour: “After all, I don’t think this is the first time in history that a group of humans has to change strategy”.

Before the meeting I kept thinking ‘EEH’.

And it sounded like a relief (it made a funny sound).

This helped me to relax, get a bit more comfortable and ready to face the challenge.

So, the day of the meeting I opened the door, walked in, plugged my laptop, checked the screen, reviewed my slides and rehearsed my presentation one final time. And then…

…EEH…

Yes! The meeting started, everyone came in, I delivered my presentation, asked some questions, got some questions back.

I could feel the ice melting (I promise it wasn’t me sweating) 😅 .

And that was it, the meeting was a success, I survived, and my team was ready to face the challenge.

The best part?

Unlike Alice and her looking glass, this was not a dream!🪞
