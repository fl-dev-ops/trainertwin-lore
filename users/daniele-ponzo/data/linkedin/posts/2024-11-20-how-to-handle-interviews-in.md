How to handle interviews in English like a pro

And get your dream job...

We’ve all been there.

Job interviews feel scary (imposter syndrome, missing words, hesitation, language barriers…) 😨 

Especially so when they’re not in your first language.

But the mindset you have, plays an essential role.

I’ve discussed this (and many other) interesting point with Luca Primavera, a Finance specialist at DeepL SE, as part of my Video podcast “The non-native leader” 🎤 .

Luca is a career switcher. 

Like many expats, he started in an Italian restaurant and slowly but surely built his expertise in Finance and he managed to work for top companies like PwC.

Here are some key takeaways from our chat:

✅ Understanding cultural differences.
↳ As important as the language itself.

✅ Adaptability, curiosity, and willingness to learn.
↳ Play a crucial role in career progression.

✅ Job interviews: self-awareness and a clear vision.
↳ Study the company culture before interviews.

✅ Strategic self-presentation.
↳ Self-confidence, awareness of one’s abilities, and a relaxed approach.

All of this with dragons in the background 🐲 .
