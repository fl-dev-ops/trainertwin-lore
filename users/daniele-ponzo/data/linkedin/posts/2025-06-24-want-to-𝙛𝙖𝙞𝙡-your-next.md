Looking to waste time, budget, and goodwill on your next Business English program?

Here’s a simple list to guarantee failure:

𝗛𝗼𝘄 𝘁𝗼 𝗙𝗮𝗶𝗹 𝗮 𝗖𝗼𝗿𝗽𝗼𝗿𝗮𝘁𝗲 𝗕𝘂𝘀𝗶𝗻𝗲𝘀𝘀 𝗘𝗻𝗴𝗹𝗶𝘀𝗵 𝗧𝗿𝗮𝗶𝗻𝗶𝗻𝗴 (𝗶𝗻 𝟱 𝗘𝗮𝘀𝘆 𝗦𝘁𝗲𝗽𝘀):

1️⃣ Make it all about grammar.
Forget communication goals. Just hammer tenses and conditionals into everyone’s brain.

2️⃣ Ignore what your employees actually do.
Team meetings? Client presentation? Project update? Irrelevant. Give them textbook dialogues about ordering coffee in London.

3️⃣ Squeeze 12 people with 12 levels into one group.
Confidence killer and logistical nightmare. Two birds, one stone!

4️⃣ Assume no one needs to speak.
Let them stay silent. That’s how fluency works, right?

5️⃣ End the course with no follow-up.
Close the folder. Check the box. Hope they “keep practicing.” 🙃

🌟 Bonus point: All cameras off and all mics on mute? That guarantees success!

Real impact in Business English training comes from:
✅ Relevant content
✅ Small-group confidence
✅ Sessions tied to actual work situations
✅ A safe, shame-free learning space
✅ Follow-through that sticks

If you’ve ever run (or been involved in) a training that looked good on paper but didn’t translate into real progress, you’re not alone.
