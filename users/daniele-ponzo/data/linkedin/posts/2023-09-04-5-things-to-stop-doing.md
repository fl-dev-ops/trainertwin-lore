5 things to stop doing to communicate confidently in English.

I know this might be unpopular.

But I believe you should stop obsessing on these right away:

❌ Focusing on a perfect British pronunciation.
❌ Having a 100,000-word vocabulary.
❌ Eliminating all the mistakes.
❌ Trying to understand every single word people say.
❌ Waiting until you are 100% perfect before speaking.
 
Of course improving your vocabulary and grammar is important.

You are studying and memorizing words.

But it is not all.

If your aim is to communicate more effectively remember:
 
You don’t need to have PERFECT English to speak confidently and effectively on calls and meetings.
 
Here is what you should do, instead:
 
✔️Focus on clear pronunciation, so you avoid misunderstandings
✔️Learn useful sentences so you can use them comfortably in your meetings
✔️Embrace your mistakes and learn from them, so you become a stronger speaker
✔️Concentrate on the relevant words people use so you get the overall meaning
✔️Speak, speak and speak, so you become more confident.

That’s it.

When you switch from: "learning English as a language".

To: "developing the skills you need to communicate",
 
then you feel you become more and more confident.

Once you've mastered this strategy, you'll find much of the stress and uncertainty around speaking English disappears 🎯.
