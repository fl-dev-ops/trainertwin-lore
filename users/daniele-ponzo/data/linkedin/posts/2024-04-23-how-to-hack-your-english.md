How to H.A.C.K. your English as an IT Professional.

It’s a little change, yet very powerful.

First, a chicken and egg question 🐔 🥚.

🔸 Do we perfect our English so we can get more confident when we speak?

🔹 Or we start speaking so we get confident (and improve our English as a consequence)?

We learn by doing.

If you want to become a confident and effective speaker, start by incorporating your learning in the workflow.

The best way to do it, is a Practical, Effective, Actionable

Strategy.

So, to hack your English, Start applying the H.A.C.K. strategy today:
 
 🅗 𝐇𝐨𝐧𝐞: refine your skills by practicing daily (at work, with friends or by interacting through voice messages)
 
 🅐 𝐀𝐜𝐭𝐢𝐨𝐧: Presentations, meetings, calls… every interaction is a chance to learn and grow.
 
 🅒 𝐂𝐨𝐥𝐥𝐚𝐛𝐨𝐫𝐚𝐭𝐢𝐨𝐧: Create a strong network with your peers, exchange ideas and feedback.
 
 🅚 𝐊𝐮𝐝𝐨𝐬: Recognize and celebrate milestones along the way to boost morale and motivate continued growth.
