If you're still Googling how to say what you do, we need to talk.

Here’s what I see all the time:

Smart, skilled professionals, great at what they do
still feel blocked in conversations because they can’t find the words.

Not because they don’t know English.
But because they don’t speak the language of their industry.

👉 You know the ideas.
👉 You know your work.

But when it’s time to explain it?
You go blank. Or use vague phrases that don’t match your expertise.

This doesn’t mean your vocabulary is weak.
It means it’s not active yet, especially under pressure.

The truth is, business and tech have their own way of speaking.

And if you don’t learn to speak that language with clarity and confidence, it’s easy to feel overlooked or underestimated.
