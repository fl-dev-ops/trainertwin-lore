'The longest night'

10 PM: Daddy goes to bed; Son wakes up. "I'm Hungry!"

10:30 PM: Daddy heats milk. Son drinks milk and eats biscuits. Mommy helps Son. 

11 PM: Son wants to play. Daddy wants to sleep. Mommy eats one biscuit.

11:30 PM: Change of scenery: we go to a different room. This looks perfect. Son goes to bed. Daddy is hopeful. Mommy tries to sleep.

12 AM: T-Rex is ready to go for an adventure. Son is excited. Daddy wants to sleep.

12:30 AM: Mommy sings lullaby. Daddy cuddles son. Son gets more excited and wants to play Jurassic Park.

1 AM: Another change of scenery. Another room, another bed. Maybe this is the right one. Son not sure. Daddy looks at T-Rex, hopeless. Mommy is hopeful.

1:30 AM: Son is listing names and surnames of his classmates. Daddy is  pacing up and down on the corridor. Mommy stares at an empty glass.

2 AM: Son suddenly remembers a lovely song he learned at school. He wants daddy to sing it. Daddy wants to sing death metal. Mommy is more into Wagner.

2:30 AM: Back to room number 1 and bed number 1. Son wakes Mommy up. Daddy tries to get some sleep.

3 AM: Daddy is sleeping in room number 1. Mummy is sleeping in room number 2. Son is pacing up and down on the corridor

3:30 AM: T-Rex goes to sleep. Son is singing lullaby to T-Rex. Daddy is sing ning lullaby to Son. Mommy is singing lullaby to Daddy.

4 AM: all goes quiet. Daddy hugs T-Rex. Son hugs Daddy. T-Rex hugs Mommy. Family sleeps

6 AM: alarm clock goes off. Time to start working. Son wakes up. Go back to the first line. Story repeats. 🔄
