Do you really have what it takes?

If you’ve ever stayed quiet in a meeting because you thought your English wasn’t “good enough”, this one’s for you.

I’ve had some interesting conversations in the last weeks, and the same story keeps coming up.

💬 A Managing Director told me:
“We work in a multinational environment, so I’d like my employees to be quick and interact with their colleagues in Germany, the UK, and France. It’s frustrating when I always have to step in and act as a translator.”

💬 A Director in cybersecurity shared:
“I can read and follow videos in English. But when I need to present or be part of a conversation, I lose momentum… I feel too slow.”

💬 And a manager in a large company admitted:
“Many people simply give up on job offers because there’s a language requirement. I’d like my employees to be more confident so they can apply for international positions.”

These are not grammar issues.
They’re communication issues. Confidence issues. Visibility issues.

But waiting for your English to be “perfect” won’t fix them.

SPOILER: You don’t need to sound like Shakespeare.

You just need to sound like yourself: clear, confident, and human.

Think of it like a system update 💻:
Communication isn’t something you fix once and forget.
It’s something you improve over time:small patches, regular updates, continuous optimization.

Every meeting, every email, every conversation is a new version release.
Each one makes your system (and your confidence) more stable.

Forget perfection. Focus on progress.

Speak up, make mistakes, update, and repeat.

👉 If you could “update” one aspect of your English communication this week, what would it be?
