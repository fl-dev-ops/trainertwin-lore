😘 KISS your… 

… English!

How many times have you started a phrase in English
Only to get lost halfway through?

“Yes, but in my [insert native language] I can use passive, conditionals, relative clauses, diplomacy, I can make long sentences, digressions, reverse, somersault, various twists, etc. etc.”

The result?

You create an amazingly complex sentence in your head.

And you try to translate it in English.

“I am attending to what you say, it is an argument very interesting that is growing importance in me…”

Wow!😕

Unfortunately, this simply doesn’t work.

We need something different:

𝗞eep
𝗜t 
𝗦imple
(𝗦tupid!) – I swear it’s the acronym, no bad intention here 🙂 

English is not your first language, so try to start small:

1️⃣ Build simple sentences (subject, verb, object)
 ⇢ It makes it easier to think in English.

2️⃣ Connect your sentences with simple linkers (so, but, and, because) 
⇢ Do it on paper first to get familiar with the process.

3️⃣ Use this scheme: idea ⇢reason⇢ example
⇢ You gradually make more complex sentences 

4️⃣ Expand as you get more confident
⇢ Experiment with relative pronouns, past forms etc.

Here is an example:

𝗦𝘁𝗮𝗴𝗲 𝟭: 𝗕𝗮𝘀𝗶𝗰 𝗦𝗲𝗻𝘁𝗲𝗻𝗰𝗲𝘀
Sentence 1: I went to the store.
Sentence 2: I bought apples.
Sentence 3: I felt happy.

𝗦𝘁𝗮𝗴𝗲 𝟮: 𝗔𝗱𝗱𝗶𝗻𝗴 𝗦𝗶𝗺𝗽𝗹𝗲 𝗟𝗶𝗻𝗸𝗲𝗿𝘀
I went to the store, and I bought apples. I felt happy.

𝗦𝘁𝗮𝗴𝗲 𝟯: 𝗨𝘀𝗶𝗻𝗴 𝗟𝗶𝗻𝗸𝗲𝗿𝘀 𝗮𝗻𝗱 𝗜𝗻𝘁𝗿𝗼𝗱𝘂𝗰𝗶𝗻𝗴 𝗮 𝗥𝗲𝗮𝘀𝗼𝗻
I went to the store, and I bought apples because I needed them. I felt happy because they were fresh.

𝗦𝘁𝗮𝗴𝗲 𝟰: 𝗜𝗻𝘁𝗿𝗼𝗱𝘂𝗰𝗶𝗻𝗴 𝗠𝗼𝗿𝗲 𝗖𝗼𝗺𝗽𝗹𝗲𝘅 𝗦𝘁𝗿𝘂𝗰𝘁𝘂𝗿𝗲𝘀
After I went to the store, where I found fresh apples that I needed, I felt happy with my purchase, because they were fresh.

The most important thing?

Do not blame yourself. Never. Reward yourself for your effort, instead.

Concentrate on what you want to say
not on crafting perfect sentences. 

The goal is 𝗲𝗳𝗳𝗲𝗰𝘁𝗶𝘃𝗲 𝗰𝗼𝗺𝗺𝘂𝗻𝗶𝗰𝗮𝘁𝗶𝗼𝗻, not perfection.
