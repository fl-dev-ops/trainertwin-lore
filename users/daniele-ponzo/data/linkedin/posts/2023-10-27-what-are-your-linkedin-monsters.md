What are your Linkedin monsters?

With Halloween 🎃  fast approaching, I wanted to brush up my passion for horror stories.

Since we're on LinkedIn, I though I would make it very relevant.

And so I've reflected on my 4 "LinkedIn Monsters".

Are you ready for this journey into horror and fear? 😱 

👻 The ghost: have you ever seen one? Sometimes you think you've seen them, but then they disappear again. 

🤢 The Frankenstein Monster: They think they have it! "I can use AI for my posts, my comments and my content. IT COULD WORK!" And then the monster escapes and they can't control it anymore.

🦇 The Vampire: they seduce you, show all their charm, but in reality they're only after...well, hopefully not your blood, but just your money. And unfortunately, I think they're immortal too.

🤡 The Clown: do you know "fake it until you make it?" Well, I'm afraid it doesn't always work. Creating a parallel reality here on LinkedIn might be ok for a while, but eventually the make up will fade...

What's your worst nightmare here on LinkedIn?

I would like to thank Maria Letizia Russo for inspiring me with her "#LinkedinHorror" series

P.S. Happy halloween everybody!
