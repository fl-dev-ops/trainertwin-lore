Why I stopped focusing on mistakes (and you should too)

I guess we all remember our days back in school:

Mistake = irritated teacher = red (or blue) pen✍️

The result?

❌ Discouraged learners
❌Loss of confidence
❌No more initiative

I still have this clear picture in my mind. 
The day I said: “I will stop speaking, so I lower the chance of making mistakes and embarrassing myself”.

And the sad truth is that this applies to all subjects.

Languages
Maths
Science
The list goes on.

Focusing on mistakes?

It’s like feeding your inner critic with caviar and champagne every day🧐.

What if you flipped this approach?

Take English, for example.

I spoke to a lot of people who told me they’re afraid of speaking because they don’t want to make mistakes.

They don’t want to look ridiculous.

But there’s nothing ridiculous there.

👉 When you shift the focus from perfection to progress, you are more willing to take risks in communication.

👉By normalizing mistakes, you reduce fear and can engage more freely in conversations, meetings, and presentations.

👉When you let go of the need to be perfect, you create more authentic and human connections with colleagues. People appreciate when you are genuine and open.

👉When you embrace mistakes, you become more focused on conveying your message clearly and effectively, rather than on how perfect your grammar or accent is.

And you find your voice!
