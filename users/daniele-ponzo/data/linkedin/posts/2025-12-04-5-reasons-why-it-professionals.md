5 reasons why IT professionals need to be good communicators

Do you think you can work on your own?

And what about networking?

Do you feel you don’t need that either?

Well, everything will be fine for a while (a short while).

But what if I told you that you DO need to improve your communication skills…
… even though you’re working in software, cybersecurity or coding?

"Why?" You might ask.

Well, here are 5 good opportunities you don't want to miss. 

𝗖𝗼𝗹𝗹𝗮𝗯𝗼𝗿𝗮𝘁𝗶𝗼𝗻 𝘄𝗶𝘁𝗵𝗶𝗻 𝗧𝗲𝗮𝗺𝘀
↳ Scenario: Agile environments, DevOps settings, cross-functional projects
↳Why: we need interpersonal skills to make sure everyone is aligned on goals, timelines, and task distribution.
↳ Result: Higher productivity and less stress. 🔅 

𝗨𝗻𝗱𝗲𝗿𝘀𝘁𝗮𝗻𝗱𝗶𝗻𝗴 𝗖𝗹𝗶𝗲𝗻𝘁/𝗦𝘁𝗮𝗸𝗲𝗵𝗼𝗹𝗱𝗲𝗿 𝗡𝗲𝗲𝗱𝘀
↳ Scenario: Clients may not have technical background.
↳ Why: Miscommunication can lead to incorrect product specifications.
↳Result: Better project’s outcome.🔅

𝗕𝘂𝗶𝗹𝗱𝗶𝗻𝗴 𝗧𝗿𝘂𝘀𝘁
↳Scenario: Complex projects that require high collaboration
↳Why: If you want collaboration you need to know your colleagues, talk and engage with them.
↳Result: Better teams, better work environment (and some smiles). 🔅

𝗖𝗼𝗻𝗳𝗹𝗶𝗰𝘁 𝗥𝗲𝘀𝗼𝗹𝘂𝘁𝗶𝗼𝗻
↳Scenario: Disagreements about the direction of a project, tool usage, or implementation.
↳Why: Less rants and complains to navigate conflicts productively (build bridges, not walls)
↳Result: We can solve complex problems.🔅

𝗖𝗮𝗿𝗲𝗲𝗿 𝗚𝗿𝗼𝘄𝘁𝗵 𝗮𝗻𝗱 𝗟𝗲𝗮𝗱𝗲𝗿𝘀𝗵𝗶𝗽
↳Scenario: You want to take on managerial or leadership roles.
↳Why: Mentoring, guiding teams, and presenting to executives will become key responsibilities.
↳Result: You get promoted and improve your career.🔅
