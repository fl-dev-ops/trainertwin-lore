1 BIG mistake IT pros make when they’re trying to become leaders.

And 1 powerful fix.

When they want to become Tech leaders, people forget how important communication is. They're often either too passive or too aggressive and they don’t get the results they want. I made this mistake the first time I became Team Leader and I had to learn it the hard way.

❌ “Yeah, of course. No problem at all.”

At the beginning, I thought I needed to be a “people pleaser”, putting other people’s needs first. If I do that, I thought, people will love me. As a result, I was helping more than necessary and I was getting swamped with work, as people tended to rely on me too much.

❌“Absolutely not. You always ask for favours at the last minute!”

And there was when I made my second mistake. I wanted to to build this “aggressive” persona to be respected and “feared” –  authoritarian instead of authoritative. As a result, I wasn’t getting much support from the team, which meant delays in the delivery of our solutions.

So, if you’re afraid of making the same mistake, there is a small but incredibly powerful communication shift that can help you change perspective, get people to work with you and support you.

✅ “Please don’t walk away from me while we’re having a conversation.”

One day I was having a 1:1 with a senior member of the team about his performance and how that was impacting the team strategy. He was getting frustrated, and he didn’t want to listen to me, as he perceived himself as more “expert”. At one point, he stood up and went towards the door.

In that moment, I knew passive and aggressive would not help me.

So, I stood up, looked him in the eye and, with a steady tone of voice I told him: “Please don’t walk away from me while we’re having a conversation. I see your view, change is hard – after all, we’ve always done things in the same way. But, I need your support to implement our strategy”.

He stopped, sat down again, ready to continue our conversation.

Something clicked when I started believing in myself and being more assertive in my communication. Being transparent, showing empathy with clear and direct statements got me where I wanted to. I was showing some respect not only for him, but for me too.

Assertive communication can truly change your relationships at work and, eventually, help your career progression.

You can start working on this today. Here are some ideas:

✅ Use of “I” statements: “I understand...”, “I need...”, “I see...”, “I believe...”
✅ Use clear verbs: “need” instead of “want”; “will need” instead of “could”
✅ Avoid extreme language: “always” or “never”
✅ Listen actively: gather feedback and paraphrase what you’re hearing
✅ Non-Verbal Cues: body language, eye contact, and tone of voice.
