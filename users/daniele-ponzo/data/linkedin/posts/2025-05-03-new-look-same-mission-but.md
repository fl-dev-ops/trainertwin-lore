New look, same mission (but bolder)

A few months ago, I started feeling it.

Like a jacket that used to fit but now felt too tight.

The words, the visuals, the vibe — I needed to find my voice.

Fully. Now.

So I did what I ask my clients to do every day: Get uncomfortable and start communicating from the inside out.

I teamed up with the brilliant Thomas Cocco to rethink everything
from the visuals to the concept behind them🔧 .

The result?

A visual identity that communicates.
🗨 The double balloon in the logo is about dialogue🗨.
🧠 The new colours blend tech clarity with human warmth.
🎯 The core message? I help tech professionals with English not just for grammar, but for growth.

For me, this is a reminder:
You’re allowed to evolve.
To outgrow what once fit.
To speak louder and more clearly.

And if you're in that space — feeling that itch to shift — this is your sign.
