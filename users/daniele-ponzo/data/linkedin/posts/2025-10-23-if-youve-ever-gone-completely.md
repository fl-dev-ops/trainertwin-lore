If you’ve ever gone completely blank in an English meeting, this article is for you.

You might have studied English for years, passed exams, memorized grammar rules, even watched entire seasons of your favorite shows without subtitles.

You can follow meetings, understand your colleagues, and read complex documents just fine.

But when it’s your turn to speak… nothing happens.
 
Your mind goes blank, your heart races, and suddenly the words you know so well vanish into thin air.

You’re not alone.

It’s one of the most common challenges I see among professionals working in international environments.
And it has little to do with grammar or vocabulary.

In this article, I’ll walk you through the six “characters” that might be silently holding you back… and how you can finally find your voice and start speaking English with confidence.
