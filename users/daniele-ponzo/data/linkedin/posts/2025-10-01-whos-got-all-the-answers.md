Who’s got all the answers?

A couple of weeks ago I met a giant robot, and we had an interesting chat. It went (pretty much) like this:

🤖 Robot: Why should anyone work with you?
 They already have me: AI, free online courses, language apps.

👤 Me: True. Those tools are useful.

🤖 Robot: And I look perfect, right?

👤 Me: Kind of…

🤖 Robot: Yes, and I can help people feel perfect. But you can’t!
You can’t remove their accent.
You can’t teach them all the grammar in a week.
You can’t write their emails in 10 seconds or less.
 
👤 Me: You’re right, I can’t. And I don’t promise that.

🤖 Robot: You see! Then what can you actually do?

👤 Me: I can help professionals in multinational companies:
✔ Use the right business vocabulary, so they sound professional.
✔ Master expressions for meetings and presentations, so they become visible at work.
✔ Navigate cultural nuances, so they avoid embarrassing mistakes.

🤖 Robot: And why you? Why not just another course?

👤 Me: Because I’ve lived it.
I was a non-native English speaker in an American multinational.
I had to figure out a strategy to get noticed, or I’d watch opportunities fly past like butterflies in spring 🦋.

🤖 Robot: So you don’t sell perfection?

👤 Me: No. I help people communicate with confidence.
 To be noticed. Heard. Understood.

🤖 Robot: Hm. Sounds like something even AI can’t replace. Yet. 😉

👤 Me: Exactly, that’s’ the point.

🤖 Robot: But I think people want to feel perfect with little effort. So they will still choose me.

👤 Me: They might… and that’s ok. I haven’t got all the answers. But just in case they’re ready to put some effort in and build true connection through their communication, then it’s different.

🤖 Robot: Right.

👤 Me: So, who would they choose in that case?

🤖 Robot: I’m pointing at him.

👤 Me: Thank you, Goldrake!

P.S. If you're a motivated, hard-working Tech or IT professional and you’re ready to excel in your career, send me a DM, and we'll see if we're a good fit to work together. Or I can always refer you back to my new friend🤖…
