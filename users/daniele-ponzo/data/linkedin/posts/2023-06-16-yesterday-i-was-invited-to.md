Yesterday I was invited to this event at the Little Genius International - Società Benefit near where I live.

It’s great to see an international school for young children and to see such an inspiring environment.

I had the opportunity to reflect on the importance of teaching STEM subject (science, technology, engineering and mathematics) with a Humanistic approach, which is what they are doing there.

When children learn about #blockchain, #AI and robotics through augmented learning, the use of a FabLab, an “open mind” approach and cross-curricular activities, then they can have an impact on shaping the use of these tools in the future.

More so if this is combined with a multi-lingual approach, a close connection with local research institutes (#INFN, #ESA) and an innovative approach to sustainability (the school is in an organic building which which promotes harmony between human habitation and the natural surroundings).

So, I believe this should be the way forward for education: Technology, Sustainability and a Human approach.

Any thoughts?
