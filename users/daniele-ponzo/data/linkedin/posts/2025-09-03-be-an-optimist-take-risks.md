Be an optimist.
Take risks.
That’s the mindset that can change your career... and your life.

But what does it really mean to take risks as a non-native English speaker building an international career?

In Episode 30 of The Non-Native Leader - Podcast, I sat down with Anas Riad, a Data Scientist from Morocco who moved to the UK to grow his career and embrace new challenges.

Here is the heart of the conversation:
 🌍 The risks of moving abroad vs. staying in your comfort zone
 🌍 Using optimism to face cultural and professional challenges
 🌍 Building confidence in English communication — even when it feels hard
 🌍 How self-help books, immersion, and community fuel personal growth

There is something that Anas said that truly resonated with me:

“𝘐𝘧 𝘺𝘰𝘶 𝘸𝘢𝘯𝘵 𝘵𝘰 𝘨𝘳𝘰𝘸, 𝘺𝘰𝘶 𝘩𝘢𝘷𝘦 𝘵𝘰 𝘵𝘢𝘬𝘦 𝘳𝘪𝘴𝘬𝘴. 𝘌𝘷𝘦𝘯 𝘪𝘧 𝘪𝘵 𝘮𝘦𝘢𝘯𝘴 𝘧𝘢𝘪𝘭𝘪𝘯𝘨, 𝘪𝘵’𝘴 𝘣𝘦𝘵𝘵𝘦𝘳 𝘵𝘩𝘢𝘯 𝘯𝘰𝘵 𝘵𝘳𝘺𝘪𝘯𝘨 𝘢𝘵 𝘢𝘭𝘭.”

If you’ve ever:
 👉 Wondered if moving abroad is worth the risk
 👉 Felt fear holding you back from using English at work
 👉 Questioned whether you have what it takes to succeed internationally

 …this episode will inspire you.

What I loved most about our chat?

Anas doesn’t just share career tips.
He shares honesty, courage, and a reminder that optimism and risk-taking are part of every growth story.

English is a tool, yes. It opens doors.
But more than that, it’s about the mindset that helps you walk through them.
