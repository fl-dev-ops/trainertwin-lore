If you're working in IT, this simple tip will help you excel in English👇


Focus on the skills, not on English as a language.

Similar to many IT professionals I’ve worked with, you probably have worked very hard to improve your English hoping to excel in your career.

You’ve probably tried to memorize vocabulary, take tests, use language apps, but you still don’t find it enough to help you with your communication.

Why is that the case?

It is because you focus on the wrong thing.

You have focused on learning English as a language, not developing the skills you need to excel at work.

Think about it for a second.

Even if your English is fluent and perfect, can you for certain do well at meetings, presentations, networking?

Probably not, because we all need to develop these skills to boost our confidence.

If you agree, let’s stop trying to improve your English as a language.
You need to work on the following, instead:

👉 expand your Business and IT vocabulary, so you can express yourself naturally at your next business meeting.
 
👉 focus on improving your pronunciation, so you can avoid embarrassing misunderstandings when speaking with your clients.

👉 get your intonation right, so you can convey your message effectively.
 
👉 role-play real business situations, so you can feel sure before your next presentation or conference call.

👉 write professional-sounding emails, so you can feel confident before hitting send.

When you effectively focus on the skills you need, your English speaking will skyrocket 🚀
