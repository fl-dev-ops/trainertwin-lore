Would you like to impress clients, colleagues, recruiters, investors?

Have you got a good story to do it?
 
I get it, it sounds like another buzzword.
 
BUT whatever you do in business, you are going to tell a story📖
 
-> You apply for your dream job? Tell your professional story.
 
-> You need to introduce your startup on stage? Do it through a story.
 
-> And what about presenting in your meeting? Yes, I guess you know it…you need a story to engage people.
 
It’s normal to struggle a bit when we want to tell a story in English. Finding a good story, giving it a structure, using the right intonation and rhythm.
 
Effective storytelling is already part of my program, but I would like to refine it to help you get there faster.
