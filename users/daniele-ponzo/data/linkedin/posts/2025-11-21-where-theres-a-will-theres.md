“Where there’s a will, there’s a way”

My Grandfather was born in a small village up the mountains
In 1925

He always loved learning, drawing and building things

When he was 6, his family moved to the big city
He had a chance to go to school. Technical drawing.

But then life took over.

The family got larger
The need to make ends meet
Poverty
War

So, he couldn’t complete his school.

After the war he built his own family.

And one day he found a job as a typographer
In the university
Printing reference books, non-fiction, encyclopaedias, dictionaries

And he could finally combine his passion for building things
With his thirst for learning.

He would study through encyclopaedia pages while printing them
Reading through pages of literature while selecting the colours
Scanning dictionary entries while arranging the fonts

So he finally built his own expertise.

It was a bit of a “rigid” one
Words acted as a trigger to him

He would recite entries by heart
Quote famous novels
Name rivers, borders, mountain ranges
Capital cities, poems, historical figures

It sounds crazy these days as we live in the age of information
We can access the entire World knowledge through our phones

But the pride he had

And the joy when we would take one of the books back home
to spend hours with me going through facts, pictures, stories.

So, I learned very soon to love reading, studying, “continuous improvement”

And that if you really want something, you can get it

“Where there’s a will, there’s a way”.

He would have been 100 this month
But decided to leave us earlier.

But every time I open a book, look for some information online, even brainstorm with ChatGPT.

I think of him.

What’s your relationship with knowledge?
