Laugh, and the world laughs with you; weep, and you weep alone...
 
Smiling and laughing are really powerful, on so many levels:

🙂 Improve learning: studies show that humour activates the brain’s dopamine system, which stimulates goal-oriented motivation and long-term memory.

🙂 Help to relax: laughing reduces stress and anxiety, creating a better learning environment, especially when it comes to languages.

🙂 Enhance confidence: when you make people laugh (using a different language), it means you’re connecting on a higher level and you’re using your full personality.
