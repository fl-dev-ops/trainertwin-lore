This day many years ago I entered the world of IT.
 
A new job.

A new City.

A new Country.

How was I feeling?

One word: overwhelmed😓.

The interview process.

The induction.

Hours and hours of training.

Buddying up with my colleagues.

All in English🗣.

So, I wasn’t exactly in my comfort zone.

But I made a decision.

To get further away from my comfort zone.

Luckily, my manager at the time offered me 2 extra tasks:
-      Support only British customers;
-      Train and develop new starters.

And I went for it.

I have never regretted that day because it allowed me to:
-      Practise English on a deeper level.
-      Find my love for training, coaching and teaching.

This is why I’m doing this today.

To help IT professional overcome that sense of pain and embarrassment when it comes to English.

Send me a message📩 if you’d like to know more.
