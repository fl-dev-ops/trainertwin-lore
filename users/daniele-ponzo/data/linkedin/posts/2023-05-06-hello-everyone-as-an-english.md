Hello everyone, as an English language coach, I want to emphasize the importance of self-motivation when working with a coach.

Learning a new language requires a lot of effort, time, and dedication, and it's easy to feel discouraged or overwhelmed along the way. However, when we're self-motivated, we're more likely to stay focused, engaged, and committed to our language learning goals.

As a coach, my role is to provide you with the guidance, support, and resources you need to improve your English language skills. When you combine this with your own motivation and drive, then the learning process skyrockets!

So, how can you stay self-motivated when learning English? One way is to set specific and measurable goals that align with your personal or professional aspirations. 

You can also find inspiration and motivation from within yourself by focusing on the progress you've made and celebrating your successes, no matter how small they may be.
