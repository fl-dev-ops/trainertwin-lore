Another off-topic Friday.

This is another thing I love doing when I’m not working.

Get the two wheels rolling.

Put some hard work in

The wind in my face.

Uphill: the struggle.

Downhill: the reward.

Push, push, on the pedals.

Seeing the endless road ahead of me gives me energy.

And then there is Nature.

The amazing silence.

Colours passing by.

Like a painter’s stroke.

High Clouds in the Sky.

Suggesting what’s to come.

The wheels, the pedals and my heartbeat are together.

Time

Becomes

Meaningless

This is why I love:
