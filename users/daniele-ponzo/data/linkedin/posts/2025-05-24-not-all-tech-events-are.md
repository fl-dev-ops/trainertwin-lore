Not all tech events are cold and robotic.

Last month, I went to Codemotion in Rome.
 
Not your average Tech event.

Big screens. Demos. AI buzzwords?

Not really.

I connected with incredible people.
I listened to talks about the unexpected bond between arts, mathematics, and coding.
I explored how AI is reshaping the business world and the job market and what that means for communication, human skills, and the way we work.

And I left feeling not just smarter, but inspired.

Oh — and I finally fulfilled a childhood dream:

📸 I got a photo with a dinosaur🦖.

Yes, that was part of the event too.

So, here’s a certificate I’m genuinely proud of.

Because learning doesn’t always happen in classrooms.

Sometimes it happens in the weirdest, most wonderful places, surrounded by code, curiosity… and prehistoric creatures.

So, thanks Codemotion for welcoming me!
