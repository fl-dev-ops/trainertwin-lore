I’ve spent the last 21 years working with people.

Here’s what I learned 👇 :

▶️ 𝗖𝘂𝘀𝘁𝗼𝗺𝗲𝗿 𝘀𝘂𝗽𝗽𝗼𝗿𝘁: Building rapport and trust. Patience, patience, patience.
↳“We’re here to help. Shouting at me won’t speed up the process, sir. Yes, without ticket number there’s nothing I can do”. 

▶️ 𝗦𝗲𝗿𝘃𝗶𝗰𝗲 𝗗𝗲𝗹𝗶𝘃𝗲𝗿𝘆: Getting closer to the Heart of Darkness: presenting in front of the customer. Panic kicks in. Rewards & Recognition.
↳“Our solution can reduce your costs by 15%, help you streamline your processes while leveraging your current strengths. Deal done”.

▶️ 𝗟&𝗗: New people, new faces, teaching the job, the insights, help them locate the coffee machine, test. Repeat.
↳“Without you, the process book is useless. Without the process book, you are useless”.

▶️ 𝗧𝗲𝗮𝗺 𝗠𝗮𝗻𝗮𝗴𝗲𝗺𝗲𝗻𝘁: Finally, a chance to be the Team Leader I wish I had.
↳“Hey everyone, new company strategy, let’s implement the CHANGE. Stop, you can’t run away!”

▶️ 𝗥𝗼𝗯𝗼𝘁𝗶𝗰 𝗣𝗿𝗼𝗰𝗲𝘀𝘀 𝗔𝘂𝘁𝗼𝗺𝗮𝘁𝗶𝗼𝗻: Workflows, processes, and the language of software engineers.
↳“First, solve the problem. Then write the code. Ok, I will solve the problem and you will write the code, fair enough”.

▶️ 𝗧𝗲𝗮𝗰𝗵𝗶𝗻𝗴 𝗘𝗻𝗴𝗹𝗶𝘀𝗵 𝘁𝗼 𝗸𝗶𝗱𝘀: Fear, terror, and apocalyptic vibes: entering a classroom full of screaming bambinos.
↳“3, 2, 1: jump up and down for no reason, jump up and down for no reason…”

▶️ 𝗧𝗲𝗮𝗰𝗵𝗶𝗻𝗴 𝗘𝗻𝗴𝗹𝗶𝘀𝗵 𝘁𝗼 𝗧𝗲𝗲𝗻𝘀: Grammar. Storytelling. Instagram, Tik Tok, Twitch. Yes, their English is different.
↳I was like: hey I'm here to teach you English, bro". They were like: "That ain’t gonna be easy". And then I was like: "let's 'slay' it".

▶️ 𝗧𝗲𝗮𝗰𝗵𝗶𝗻𝗴 𝗔𝗱𝘂𝗹𝘁𝘀: Those good old days at school, do you remember? Well, now they’re back!
↳“Homework for you. Yes, homework. Yes, you might need to read and write, sorry”.

▶️ 𝗪𝗼𝗿𝗸𝗶𝗻𝗴 𝗳𝗼𝗿 𝗮 𝗠𝗶𝗻𝗶𝘀𝘁𝗲𝗿: How to deal with high-profile people while trying to smooth out Byzantine working processes. And politics...
↳“Ok, we shall start the meeting at 1PM. And we will, as usual, finish around dinner time”.

▶️ 𝗖𝗼𝗮𝗰𝗵𝗶𝗻𝗴 𝗜𝗧 𝗣𝗿𝗼𝗳𝗲𝘀𝘀𝗶𝗼𝗻𝗮𝗹𝘀: all the above.
