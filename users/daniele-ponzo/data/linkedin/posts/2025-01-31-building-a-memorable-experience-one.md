Building a Memorable Experience

One that will be with you in your professional life

When it comes to public speaking, there is much more than words.

To get fluency and confidence.

Pronunciation, Intonation, Approach.

But the most important thing is:

Inspiration.

Working with E. was a great experience.

And I love the way he summarized it.

“Break some patterns”

When we’re used to leaning English at school

We simply don’t get a chance 

-> To speak freely
-> To make mistakes and learn from them
-> To put ourselves out there.

Patterns

This is why we worked together on:

✅ Creating a solid structure to navigate public speaking
✅ Building a natural and engaging intonation
✅ Practicing confident delivery

In a safe (and non-judgmental) space.

So, it was not only about a language course and English training.
It was about creating an experience made of tips, exchanges, practice… and fun.

Because if we’re not happy, we simply don’t want to do it.

And I’m especially proud of E.’s feedback. Because this experience will stay with him. And because it will support him as he grows in his role in IT.
