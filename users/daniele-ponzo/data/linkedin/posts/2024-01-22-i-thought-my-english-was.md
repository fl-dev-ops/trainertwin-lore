“I thought my English was good, but I can’t understand when people speak”.

Sound familiar?

Why is this challenging?

Essentially, it comes down to 3 factors:

🔴 Accents
🔴 Speed of Speech
🔴 Vocabulary

And what can we do to improve?

Can we do it in a short time?

Here is an action plan. It’s immersive, intensive (and tiring). 

But I promise it will be rewarding. And it can be done over 3 days:

𝐃𝐚𝐲 1: 𝐋𝐢𝐬𝐭𝐞𝐧𝐢𝐧𝐠 𝐌𝐚𝐫𝐚𝐭𝐡𝐨𝐧
1.     Morning (2 hours): Podcast Immersion
·       Choose a podcast series with clear speech.
·       Listen for 30 mins, take notes on key points.
·       Repeat if necessary.

2.     Afternoon (2 hours): TED Talks Challenge
·       Select TED Talks on topics of interest.
·       Listen actively, note main ideas.
·       Replay sections to improve understanding.

3.     Evening (2 hours): Movie Night
·       Opt for movies with English subtitles.
·       Focus on dialogue, nuances, and accents.
·       Pause, rewind, and replay scenes for clarity.

4.     Night (1 hour): Audiobook Bedtime
·       Pick an audiobook with a captivating narrator.
·       Follow along with the written version.
·       Listen for pleasure and understanding.

𝐃𝐚𝐲 2: 𝐑𝐞𝐚𝐝𝐢𝐧𝐠 𝐅𝐫𝐞𝐧𝐳𝐲
1.     Morning (2 hours): News Articles Dive
·       Explore reputable news websites.
·       Read articles on various topics.
·       Summarize each article in writing.

2.     Afternoon (2 hours): Blog Browsing
·       Find blogs related to hobbies or work interests.
·       Analyze language nuances and expressions.
·       Note down new phrases for later use.

3.     Evening (2 hours): Book Expedition
·       Choose a story you like.
·       Read for comprehension, focusing on context.
·       Use a dictionary sparingly.

4.     Night (1 hour): Grammar in Context
·       Pick a short story or article.
·       Identify and understand grammar structures.
·       Create your sentences using similar structures.

𝐃𝐚𝐲 3: 𝐑𝐞𝐢𝐧𝐟𝐨𝐫𝐜𝐞𝐦𝐞𝐧𝐭 𝐚𝐧𝐝 𝐑𝐞𝐟𝐥𝐞𝐜𝐭𝐢𝐨𝐧
1.     Morning (2 hours): Recap and Review
·       Go through your notes from listening and reading activities.
·       Identify challenging areas and revisit them.

2.     Afternoon (2 hours): Language Exchange
·       Engage in conversations with language exchange partners.
·       Practice using newly acquired phrases.

3.     Evening (2 hours): Writing Practice
·       Write a short essay or summary of a podcast.
·       Focus on clear expression and coherence.

4.     Night (1 hour): Goal Setting
·       Reflect on improvements.
·       Set realistic goals for ongoing language development.

Obviously, this can be extreme. But it is useful if you want to see quick progress.

Or if you have an important event coming up.

Remember, you are in control and improvement is a journey.
Celebrate small victories, and enjoy the process of mastering English comprehension! 🚀

So, are you up for the challenge?
