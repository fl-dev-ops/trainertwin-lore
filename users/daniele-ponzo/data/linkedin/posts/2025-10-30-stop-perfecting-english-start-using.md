Stop perfecting English. Start using it.

I had the pleasure of joining Hadas Yehezkely on the Eduvation-podcast to talk about something I’m deeply passionate about: 
the 𝗣𝗮𝗿𝗮𝗱𝗼𝘅 of English learning.

So many professionals study for years, collect certificates, master grammar…
Yet when it’s time to speak in a meeting... silence.

They freeze.
They overthink.
They feel “less than”, even when they’re brilliant at what they do.

I know that feeling.
I lived it.
That’s exactly why I created my unique approach, 
combining language + communication + mindset.

We discussed:
▶️ The paradox of knowing English but not using it
▶️ The fear of judgment and perfectionism at work
▶️ The ego trap that holds experienced professionals back
▶️ Practical strategies to build real confidence
▶️ My book 𝘌𝘢𝘴𝘺 𝘌𝘯𝘨𝘭𝘪𝘴𝘩 𝘧𝘰𝘳 𝘐𝘛 and how it blends mindset, language, and career growth

It was such a powerful conversation, thank you, Hadas, for the great questions and the space to explore what truly matters when it comes to communication.

👉 You can listen to the full episode in 𝘁𝗵𝗲 𝗳𝗶𝗿𝘀𝘁 𝗰𝗼𝗺𝗺𝗲𝗻𝘁 𝗯𝗲𝗹𝗼𝘄.

I might have said it before... but you shouldn't look for “perfect” English.
You should find our own voice
You should start sounding like you, just in another language.
