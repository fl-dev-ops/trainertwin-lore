Back from holiday… and the struggle is real!

Coming back after a holiday always feels like an identity crisis.

On one side, there’s 𝗧𝗵𝗲 𝗛𝗼𝗹𝗶𝗱𝗮𝘆 𝗛𝗮𝗿𝗱𝗰𝗼𝗿𝗲 😎:
Wants to keep doing absolutely nothing.
Still dreaming of sunsets, naps, and gelato.
Secretly Googling “cheap flights back” during the first meeting.

On the other side, there’s 𝗧𝗵𝗲 𝗘𝗮𝗴𝗲𝗿 𝗕𝗲𝗮𝘃𝗲𝗿 🚀:
Bursting with ideas, ready to dive back in.
Excited to open the laptop again.
Can’t wait to start fresh with new projects.

I guess both live inside us...

And the tug-of-war between “take me back” and “let’s gooo” makes the first week after a break… interesting, to say the least.

No matter which one you are, there’s a common struggle:
👉 Getting back into the routine.

Getting back into it is tough because your brain is still on “holiday mode”: slower mornings, no deadlines, less structure.

What can we do to help? Start with one small win.
Clear your inbox. Tidy your desk. Finish one easy task.

Momentum builds from there
And suddenly the routine feels less overwhelming.

As for me? I’m somewhere in between the two: sipping coffee while catching myself scrolling holiday photos.

How about you?
Did you feel (or are you feeling) the same?
Who are you today: Holiday Hardcore or Eager Beaver? 👇
