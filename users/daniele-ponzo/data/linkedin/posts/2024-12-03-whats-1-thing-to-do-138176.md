What’s 1 thing to do to get your dream job?

You have to study.

But don’t just take my word for it  .

Career experts and top consultants agree🧑‍🎓:

Researching a company before applying and interviewing can truly change your game. 
You demonstrate your interest, tailor your application, and make informed decisions.

So, what should you research?

1️⃣ Company Values and Culture
↳ Simon Sinek, "Start with Why"

2️⃣ Industry Position and Competitors
↳  Harvard Business Review "Strategic Management for Competitive Advantage"

3️⃣ Recent Achievements and Projects
↳  Steve Dalton, "The 2-Hour Job Search" 

4️⃣  Leadership Team
↳  Forbes, "7 Interview Questions That Provide Insight Into A Company’s Culture"

5️⃣  Role Expectations
↳ LinkedIn, "tailor your skills to a job description"

6️⃣  Challenges and Pain Points
↳ James Reed, "101 Interview Questions You’ll Never Fear Again" 

7️⃣  Employee Experience
↳ Adam Grant, "Give and Take"

8️⃣  Diversity and Inclusion Policies
↳  McKinsey’s "Diversity Wins report"
