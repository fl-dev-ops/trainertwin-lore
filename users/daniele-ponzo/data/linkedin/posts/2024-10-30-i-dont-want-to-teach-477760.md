❌ I don’t want to teach you the rules.
✅ I want you to speak English

❌I don’t want you to learn grammar by heart.
✅I want you to use it.

❌I don’t want you to learn a long list of words.
✅I want you to put them in the right context.

❌I don’t want you to wait until you feel perfect.
✅I want you to take risks and speak up.

❌I don’t want you to turn off your camera.
✅I want you to show your face and communicate.

❌I don’t want you to mute your mic.
✅I want you to find your voice.

❌I don’t want you to only rely on me.
✅I want you to practice daily.

❌I don’t want you to study from page 7 to page 10.
✅I want you to speak, record yourself, speak again.

❌I don’t want you to feel timid because you miss the language.
✅I want you to shine.

❌I don’t want you to hide at the back of the room.
✅I want you to raise your hand and contribute.

❌I don’t want to teach you English.
✅I want you to find your true self in English. 

Remember: improving a language is not a course, a book, homework.

It’s a journey, where pilot and co-pilot cooperate. ✈️ 

To get to your destination.

So, are you just a passenger or do you want to be the co-pilot?

Send me a message and if you are a good fit we can team up to get you where you want to be 🤝 .

I’m rooting for you!
