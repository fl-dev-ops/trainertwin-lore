1 Little-Known way to boost your speaking confidence

A few years ago, I thought I had it:

- Moved to another Country
- Signed up for an English course to get my certificate 
- Had a job in a multinational company.

So, my idea was: “I have everything I need to impress people when I speak and get a brilliant career”.

Except….that didn’t happen 😨 .

Living in Ireland was only helping me with casual conversations
My English Certificate didn’t make me a confident speaker
My career wasn’t taking off.

The 1 thing that really helped me was…. volunteering

I chose to volunteer and put myself out there.

I’m passionate about communities, nature and ‘green’ initiatives, so it was easy for me to pick 🌱 :

➡️ I joined a local Community Garden
↳ This helped me to learn how to interact within a small group
↳ I later became President and got the opportunity to present our initiative to the public
↳ I was invited to gatherings, events and parties organized by growers and public authorities -> 𝗦𝗼𝗰𝗶𝗮𝗹 𝗦𝗸𝗶𝗹𝗹𝘀 𝗶𝗻 𝗜𝗻𝗳𝗼𝗿𝗺𝗮𝗹 & 𝗙𝗼𝗿𝗺𝗮𝗹 𝘀𝗲𝘁𝘁𝗶𝗻𝗴𝘀
-           
➡️I helped found a CSA (Community Supported Agriculture) in Dublin
↳ I had to introduce the initiative to new members
↳ We organized public events, and I lead the Q&A sessions 
↳ I travelled to an international conference to present the community to a group of academics -> 𝗦𝗽𝗲𝗮𝗸𝗶𝗻𝗴 𝗶𝗻 𝗣𝘂𝗯𝗹𝗶𝗰

➡️I put myself forward to become “Sustainability Champion” at work
↳ Regular meetings with HR, Learning & Development, Facilities.
↳ But also presenting and explaining to colleagues and senior managers (nobody had heard of that before)
↳ We then included sustainability metrics in our offers, giving me more talking points with our customers -> 𝗣𝗿𝗲𝘀𝗲𝗻𝘁𝗶𝗻𝗴 𝘁𝗼 𝗦𝗲𝗻𝗶𝗼𝗿 𝗦𝘁𝗮𝗸𝗲𝗵𝗼𝗹𝗱𝗲𝗿𝘀 & 𝗖𝗹𝗶𝗲𝗻𝘁𝘀

Volunteering was my real turning point

✳️ It helped me realize I wanted to work with people
✳️ It made me more confident when speaking in public
✳️ It gave me hours and hours of extra practice for free.

Have you found your turning point yet?

Let me know below 👇

P.S. a certificate and a job won’t give you visibility. A strategy and effective practice will.
