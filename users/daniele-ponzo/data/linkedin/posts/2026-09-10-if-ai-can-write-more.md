If AI can write more and more of the code… what becomes more valuable for the developer?

That's one of the questions I've been thinking about a lot lately.

And on September 24, I'll explore it together with someone who sees this transformation from right inside software development: Emiliano Pisu.

We're bringing two different perspectives to the same question.

Emiliano: code, AI, engineering and the developer experience.

Me: communication, clarity and what happens when technical expertise has to move between people, teams and stakeholders.

Because perhaps the future of software development isn't simply about writing code faster.

Perhaps the bottleneck is moving...

From writing → to reviewing.
From syntax → to specification.
From producing code → to making good decisions.
From simply knowing → to being able to explain why.

That's what we'll explore at:
