Not progressing fast enough?

It’s because you need to become... unconscious

Just to get this straight, it doesn’t involve violence.

So I’m not going to hit you in the head.😵

I’m talking about the Conscious Competence Learning Model, which explains how people progress through four learning stages.

From unconscious incompetence (you don’t know what you don’t know) to unconscious competence (performing with ease).

Here is how you can apply the method to improve your English:

1️⃣  𝗨𝗻𝗰𝗼𝗻𝘀𝗰𝗶𝗼𝘂𝘀 𝗜𝗻𝗰𝗼𝗺𝗽𝗲𝘁𝗲𝗻𝗰𝗲: the ‘awakening’ stage. 
↳Identify key communication weaknesses in English.
↳Record yourself while talking, then identify misused terms, unclear phrasing, or pronunciation issues.

2️⃣  𝗖𝗼𝗻𝘀𝗰𝗶𝗼𝘂𝘀 𝗜𝗻𝗰𝗼𝗺𝗽𝗲𝘁𝗲𝗻𝗰𝗲: the ‘battle’ stage. 
↳Face challenges directly.
↳Practice your weak areas using role-plays of common IT scenarios, like project meetings to get used to applying new vocabulary or correct grammar structures in a guided setting.

3️⃣  𝗖𝗼𝗻𝘀𝗰𝗶𝗼𝘂𝘀 𝗖𝗼𝗺𝗽𝗲𝘁𝗲𝗻𝗰𝗲: the ‘reinforcement’ stage. 
↳Apply your new skills deliberately.
↳Conduct mock client presentations with deliberate focus on clarity, pacing, and technical terms. Feedback helps reinforce good habits and make corrections more automatic.

4️⃣  𝗨𝗻𝗰𝗼𝗻𝘀𝗰𝗶𝗼𝘂𝘀 𝗖𝗼𝗺𝗽𝗲𝘁𝗲𝗻𝗰𝗲: the ‘second nature’ stage. 
↳Fluent, automatic language use in professional settings
↳You can handle real-time Q&A or complex negotiations in simulations. This level of comfort in English translates to smooth, natural communication at work.

One key takeaway? 

🌟 Practice -> Experience -> Confidence🌟 

There is no way around it.

A skill will become natural only if have a chance to use it regularly.

So, once you are aware of your gaps (Conscious Incompetence), you have 2 options:

⬇️  You get discouraged and say: “I’ve tried everything, English is not for me”. “I’m too old, I’m never going to improve”.
⬆️  You get motivated and say: “this is my current state. How do I get to the future state?” and realize that challenge is an indicator of growth.

The choice is yours.

Over to you, now. What do you feel ‘unconscious’ about? 🤔
