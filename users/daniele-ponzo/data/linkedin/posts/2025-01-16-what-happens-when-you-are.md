What happens when you are NOT confident about your English?

Here is what I learned 👇 

🔴 You see an interesting post, but you don’t comment as you don’t feel you can express yourself clearly.

🔴 You don’t network as effectively as you would like to.

🔴 You’re in a meeting sitting silently hoping they won’t ask you anything.

🔴 You watch the others speak and give their contribution, but you’re lacking the language to give your opinion.

🔴 You find a good position, but don’t apply as you can’t go through the interview.

🔴 And if you apply, insecurity doesn’t help you through the interview process.

🔴 You have a great startup, but you can’t work on the international stage as you can’t talk to potential clients or sponsors.

In one phrase?

YOU MISS OPPORTUNITIES.

Why am I so sure?

Because I have been there.

Feeling like a spectator in my own life.

Seeing people around me being assigned interesting projects and getting promoted.

I had to go through the pain of missed opportunities before realizing I needed to work on my communication skills.

So, let’s stop.

Rewind ⏪ .

And turn the negative into positive.

What happens when you are confident about your English?

✅ You now feel you have the language and comment under interesting post, so you expand your network and meet great people internationally.

✅ You become and active member in your meetings, which means your colleagues and your boss notice you.

✅ And you are an asset to your team.

✅ You now have the courage to apply for that position and you prepare yourself effectively, which means you have better chances to succeed.

✅ You are now confident you can communicate the value of your startup so you can talk to clients and sponsors worldwide and expand your market. 

See the difference?

You can become the protagonist of your life. 💪 

Just by changing your approach to language.
