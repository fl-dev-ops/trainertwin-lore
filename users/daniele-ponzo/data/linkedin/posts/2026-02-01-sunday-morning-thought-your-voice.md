Sunday morning thought, your voice is part of your leadership. ☕️

On Worth Following, I asked Daniele a question that keeps coming up for founders, managers, and anyone leading in public, what does "voice modulation" actually mean when the room has drifted off?

And people often drift away because they are distracted, stressed or have another deadline in mind.

He put it simply, it is intonation, emphasis, and strategic pauses.

Intonation is the melody of your speech, the rise and fall that signals meaning beyond the words.

Modulation is your ability to change that melody on purpose, pace, volume, rhythm, and silence, so your message lands.

Because leadership is not just what you say.
It is how you make people feel while you say it.

"We went a little monotone, attention dropped, now you need everyone back.
Same words, different delivery, suddenly the room is listening again."

If you lead meetings, give updates, sell the vision, or host a stream, your voice is a tool, not a "nice to have".

What’s one moment this week where your voice could have used a deliberate pause, or a stronger emphasis?

—Adrian
