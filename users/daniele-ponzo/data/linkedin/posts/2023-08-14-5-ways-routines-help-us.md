5 ways routines help us with our tasks👇


We have heard of the power of routine:

Repeating a task

Constant practice.

How can they help us improve our English?

Here are 5 ways:

✅ Commitment: once you start, make it a routine.

✅ Training: like an athlete, you repeat until you perfect it.

✅ Habit: something new slowly becomes normal.

✅ Accountability: hold yourself responsible for following your routine through.

✅ Daily wins: every time you build a new routine, stick to it and celebrate your daily wins.✨

They can be long term (like a new diet, new lifestyle) or short term, when we want to practise specific vocabulary or new English phrases.

What about you? Are you implementing any new routines over the summer?
