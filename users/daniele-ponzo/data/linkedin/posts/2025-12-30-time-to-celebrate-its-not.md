Time to celebrate! ✨ 

It's not the new year yet... but it's a personal milestone.
The Non-Native Leader - Podcast is an official winner of Corporate Vision Magazine Media Innovator Awards 2025. 

In particular, it has been awarded for:

✨ Best Global Leadership & Diversity Podcast 2025
✨ Inclusive Communication & Cultural Empowerment Excellence Award 2025

This makes me immensely proud, my aim with the podcast was exactly this: inspire people from all over the world with stories of international struggles, challenges and success.

I would like to thank all the watchers, listeners and followers. Your support means a lot to me.

Also, I want to take this opportunity to thank the Nomination team at AI Global Media and Corporate Vision Magazine. I'm truly honoured!
