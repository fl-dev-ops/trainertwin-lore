📢We are excited to feature Daniele Ponzo from Italy 🎉, an inspiring English Fluency and Public Speaking Coach for tech professionals around the world.

With over 13 years of experience in the IT industry and more than 8 years of coaching, Daniele deeply understands the challenges that non-native English speakers face in global work environments, especially when it comes to speaking up in meetings, giving presentations, or expressing ideas clearly.

To solve this, Daniele created the HACK Framework, a simple, actionable, and transformational method designed to help professionals become more confident, fluent, and impactful communicators. Through his coaching, he’s helped hundreds of engineers, coders, and consultants move from silent contributors to strong, vocal leaders in their fields.

His mission? To amplify the voices of non-native professionals, empower them to own their message, and help them speak with clarity, courage, and authenticity in any setting.

Daniele’s story and work align beautifully with GK Talks' vision of sharing real stories that inspire real change, and we’re honored to welcome him to the community.

Let’s give a heartfelt thanks to💐 Daniele Ponzo 💐 for sharing his invaluable insights and empowering non-native professionals to speak up with confidence through his HACK framework.
