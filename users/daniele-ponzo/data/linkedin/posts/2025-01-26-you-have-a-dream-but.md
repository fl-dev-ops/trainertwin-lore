“You have a dream”. But what about realizing it?

When it comes to language improvement, It can get complicated

🔵 Defining a clear objective
🔵 Making the tasks enjoyable
🔵 Building up a new routine

You may hit roadblocks.

Start running in circles 🔄 .

And your language, your job, your career and your desires are affected.

Why?

You might be experiencing one of these:

🔴 Procrastination (I’ll start next week, next month, next year…)
🔴 Fear (new habits, what if they’re too hard?)
🔴 Disillusion (I’ve failed too many times, this is not for me)
🔴 Lack of strategy (where do I start? Will I be able to keep up?)

So, this is your chance to reflect.

And to identify the #1 issue that is stopping you from realizing your dream.

If you want, share your idea in the (anonymous) poll below📊.

Take this opportunity to start facing your obstacles.
And begin to work on the solution.
