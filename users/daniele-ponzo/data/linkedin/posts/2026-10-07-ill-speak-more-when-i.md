"I'll speak more when I feel confident"

This comes up quite often in chats, conversations and meetings.

And I understand the logic.

You want to improve your vocabulary first.
Fix your pronunciation.
Make fewer mistakes.
Feel more prepared

𝗧𝗵𝗲𝗻 you'll start speaking more.

The problem?

Speaking confidence usually doesn't develop in that order.

You build it by gradually putting yourself in situations where you have to speak, think, react, recover and continue.

First in a safer environment.

Then with a little more pressure.

Then in situations that increasingly resemble the ones you face at work.

Most likely, that nervousness will never fully disappear.

And to be honest, it's not even the goal. 

I believe the real goal is to reach a point where nervousness 𝗱𝗼𝗲𝘀𝗻'𝘁 𝗱𝗲𝗰𝗶𝗱𝗲 𝘄𝗵𝗲𝘁𝗵𝗲𝗿 𝘆𝗼𝘂 𝗽𝗮𝗿𝘁𝗶𝗰𝗶𝗽𝗮𝘁𝗲.

That difference matters.

Especially when the next opportunity is a meeting, a presentation or an international call.

What makes you hesitate most when you have to speak English at work?
