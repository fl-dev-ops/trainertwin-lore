“We have problems with our client in Germany. Daniele, get on a plane, meet the customer and solve the problem”.

“No pressure” I added😬.

Ever felt like this?

This used to be the typical pattern in my mind:

❌  Self-doubt: Why me? There are other colleagues, much more experienced. Surely, I will mess this up.
❌  Fear: What if my presentation is not good? What if I’m not reassuring but at the same time assertive enough? 
❌  Panic: I will say something wrong; I will look ridiculous or worse – I will say something completely inappropriate.

The next thing?

Looking for a good hiding place and hoping everyone would forget about me.

This is what I call “The Impostor Vicious Circle”👺🔄:

Overthinking -> Hesitation -> Impostor Syndrome -> More Overthinking

You’re guaranteed to make mistakes.

So, what was the turning point for me?

I started receiving more and more of these requests. Fixing problems, building relationships, presenting solutions.

I was getting very anxious, until a conversation with a more experienced colleague helped me shift perspective:

“If they keep asking it’s because they believe you have what it takes”

Me?
What...
It...
Takes…

And he was right!

I had it all, but I couldn’t see it.

✳️  Assurance: Why me? I’m experienced enough. There are other colleagues, but I can be more diplomatic in a hard situation.
✳️  Courage: Work on my presentation, practice it with friends, colleagues and make sure I have the right intonation to be polite but assertive. 
✳️  Confidence: I’m multilingual and culture aware. This is why they picked me; I know how to behave in a global context.

So, from that day I started looking at “The Wizard Virtuous Circle”🧙‍♂️🔄:

Qualities -> Practice -> Experience -> Confidence

And that became my new mantra.

Has fear disappeared?

Of course not.

But we are in good terms now🤝. 

We listen to each other.

And came to a common agreement:

“I am more important than my fear”.

Over to you, now. How do you stay friends with your fear?👈
