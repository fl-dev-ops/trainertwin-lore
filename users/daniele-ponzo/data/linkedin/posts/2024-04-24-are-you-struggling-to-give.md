Are you struggling to give your contribution in meetings, calls, presentations?

Is finding your voice at work hard?

Then this simple tip might help you ✌ .

I used to think that I had to overcomplicate my language to be heard.

Create intricate sentence constructions which felt like exploring the Amazon forest without a map🌳.

The result?

I ended being silent. 

Or worst.

I started talking about a process update, but I could never get to the end of my super-duper overcomplicated stream of thoughts.

And that has an impact on confidence and visibility.

My tip below will help you:

👩‍💼 Speak more effectively.
🔤 Keep your ideas in order.
🗣 Be heard.

Remember: “Less is more”.
