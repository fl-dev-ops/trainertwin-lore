The first time I worked in English…
I realised I wasn’t ready.

First meetings.
International colleagues.
Fast conversations.

I understood (almost) everything.

But when it was my turn to speak?

I hesitated.
I softened my ideas.
I didn’t sound like myself.
  
At the same time…

David H. Stirling, a Career Communication Coach from England, had a similar experience.

First in France during his Erasmus.
Then later in Thailand.
(Where he currently lives)

Different language.
Different culture.

Same feeling:

👉 “I know what I want to say…
but it’s not landing the way I want.”
  
Because in international environments, the challenge is not vocabulary.
It’s:

• sounding confident in real conversations
• adapting to different cultures
• explaining ideas clearly under pressure
• creating connection, not just “correct sentences”
  
That’s the shift most people miss.

Language is just one layer.

What really matters is:
👉 how you communicate
👉 how you adapt
👉 how you connect
