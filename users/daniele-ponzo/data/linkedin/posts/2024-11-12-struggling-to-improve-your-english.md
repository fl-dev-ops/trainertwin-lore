Struggling to improve your English?

Here is how to fix it.

You’ve been studying English for many years.

You took language courses

And you’re probably on a great Duolingo streak. 

❌ But when you’re in a meeting, you hide at the back of the room.

❌ Or you turn off your camera.

And what about that international call?

❌ You always try to delegate it to someone else.

The result?

You miss important opportunities.

Now, imagine this:

🔆 You raise your hand in a meeting and explain your ideas with clarity

🔆You get an international call, and you say “let me handle this”

🔆You prepare your presentation, and you deliver it with confidence.

How?

It all starts with ACTION.

Put yourself out there.

Use any available opportunity to speak.

Mistakes, …sure, we all make them.

But I’d always choose communication over perfection

✅ To get visible at work
✅ To tell your personal story
✅ To start speaking with confidence
