Do you trust generative AI?

A revealing report from #capgemini  Research Institute has come up with some interesting numbers.

Some key findings:

✔️ 86% of consumers are aware of the latest trends in the generative AI 
✔️ 73% of consumers trust content written by generative AI
✔️ 43% of consumers would like to see a broad implementation of generative AI across their interactions with organizations 
✔️ Generative AI tools such as #chatgpt are becoming the new go-to for 70% of consumers when it comes to seeking product or service recommendations

What strikes me a "revolutionary" though, is the level of trust in AI in specific fields:

💲 53% of consumers trust generative-AI-assisted financial planning.
👩‍🔬 67% of consumers believe they could benefit from receiving medical advice from generative AI.
❤️ A striking 66% of consumers would seek advice from a generative AI tool on personal interactions or relationships.

I believe this is really surprising. I was aware of the general trust and use of AI as a toll for content and research.

However, it seems AI tools are becoming the new "opinion leader" for a lot of people.

They can tell you how to use your money wisely, they can replace your local doctor and your best buddy when it comes to talking about your loved ones.

And that's the real change, in my opinion. What do you think? I would love to hear your ideas on this.

The research was conducted on 10,000 consumers over the age of 18 in 13 countries across the US, Europe, and Asia-Pacific. You can find all the links and the methodology in the first comment below👇.
