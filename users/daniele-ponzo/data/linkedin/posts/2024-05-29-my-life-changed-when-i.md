“My life changed when I believed I could work in an English environment with native speakers”.

This is what Aleksandr told me. And now he is closer to his dream.

He is a senior project manager, with more than 10 years’ experience in Fintech, E-Commerce and Startups.

He came to me because he wanted, one day, to work for an American Company.

His English was good, but he wasn't confident he could work as a Product Manager in an English-speaking environment.

So he joined my 10-week accelerator program for IT Professionals. And now he can:

🔹Describe the product in more depth so he can focus on the business outcome.
🔹Present more confidently in English so he can showcase the results and UI improvements.
🔹Build rapport with clients and colleagues so he can develop stronger, more trusting relationships.

And on week 9 of 10 (before we've even finished the programme), he has already been offered a new job, a BETTER job for a US company!

I'm so proud of Aleksandr for stepping up and taking this big step in his career and getting closer to his dream!

Dreams can happen when we take action!
