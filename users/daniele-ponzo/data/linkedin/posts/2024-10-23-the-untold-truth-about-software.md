The untold truth about Software Engineers.

I bet you’ve all seen the stereotypes.
Software engineers are introverted, socially awkward and only interested in coding 🤓 .

As if you only work in isolation and prefer computers over people.

But the story is quite different.

I had the opportunity to talk to Dariusz Sadowski in my latest episode of my video podcast series: ‘The Non-Native Leader’ 🎤 🖥️ .

Dariusz is a software engineer, but also a leader and a coach.

His approach?

“Stop pushing on tech
 Start working with people”

Soft skills can make or break your career. 

In this episode, we explored:

▶️ Communication (technical and non-technical) 
▶️ Collaboration: the power of teamwork.
▶️ Transition from Engineering to Leadership and Coaching.
▶️ The importance of interaction.

And also some tips and ideas on how to stay motivated and keep shining at work and in social situations 🔅 .
