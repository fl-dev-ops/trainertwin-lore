How can you increase your impact as an engineer?

By sharing your knowledge.

And, of course, dedication, perseverance, and a lot of creativity.

I’ve discussed this and more in the latest episode of "𝐓𝐡𝐞 𝐍𝐨𝐧-𝐍𝐚𝐭𝐢𝐯𝐞 𝐥𝐞𝐚𝐝𝐞𝐫” video podcast🎤.

In this episode, I talked to Maryia Tuleika, Test automation lead at the Stockholm traffic administration (Trafikförvaltningen, Region Stockholm) and technical trainer Nackademin.

Here are some key takeaways:

➡ The best way to learn is by teaching. It forces us to research and be open minded.

➡ Technical training needs a lot of creativity, from language to concepts.

➡ Learning a new language requires consistency.

➡ It is great to see the impact of what you do in your everyday life.

➡ Sharing really IS caring.

One great thing about Maryia? She’s not afraid of putting herself out there.

Whether is exploring technicalities, teaching a new class in a new language, or speaking in public at a conference, she takes on new challenges with a smile on her face.
