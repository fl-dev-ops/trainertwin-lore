More grammar? Seriously?.

Let’s be honest.

If you’re like most IT professionals I work with, you don’t need someone to explain modal verbs or the present perfect.

You already know English. You can read it. You understand it.

But when it’s time to speak up in a meeting or present in front of others…
❌ Your mind goes blank.
❌ You overthink every word.
❌ You hear your own voice and don’t recognize yourself.

And after the meeting, that frustrating thought comes back:
“I’m better than this. I just don’t know how to show it.”

I know that feeling.

Years ago, I was rejected at a job interview.
Not because I said something wrong. Not because of grammar mistakes.
In fact, my grammar was spotless.

The problem?

My language wasn’t powerful enough.
They wanted a convincing answer 𝘳𝘪𝘨𝘩𝘵 𝘵𝘩𝘦𝘳𝘦, 𝘪𝘯 𝘵𝘩𝘦 𝘮𝘰𝘮𝘦𝘯𝘵.
I hadn’t rehearsed for the unexpected. I didn’t sound confident. And I didn’t get the job.

But I learned something important:

Studying grammar alone won’t get you there.
To sound convincing, to lead, to sell your ideas — you need to work on 𝗰𝗼𝗺𝗺𝘂𝗻𝗶𝗰𝗮𝘁𝗶𝗼𝗻 𝗮𝘀 𝗮 𝘄𝗵𝗼𝗹𝗲.

That realization changed my path.

I became a leader of 2 international teams, trained dozens of IT pros, and coached hundreds of students to use English effectively.

So, here’s what I would like to tell you today:
📌 It’s not about your grammar.
📌 It’s not because you’re not smart enough.
📌 It’s not because you’re too old, too late, or too slow.

The real issue?
You’ve been trained to wait until you’re “𝘱𝘦𝘳𝘧𝘦𝘤𝘵” before you speak.
And perfection never comes.

So you focus on the wrong thing. 
And the opportunities — jobs, promotions, projects — pass you by.

But it doesn’t have to be this way.

In a few days, I’ll share something I’ve created specifically for IT professionals like you.
People who are tired of feeling stuck, tired of missing out, and ready to finally speak with clarity and confidence.

No boring grammar drills. No fake dialogues. No shame.
Just practical tools to help you use English as a bridge, not a barrier.
Give me a follow 🔔 if you would like to know more.
