So good that I wanted more.

I’ve recently had the pleasure of working on a Business English course for Topnetwork S.p.A., a forward-thinking IT company.

Their approach is clear: “we are strongly focused on enhancing the potential of people, basing our success on the professional excellence of the team.”

So, we worked together on the main goal:

Equip their sales team with the skills to handle conversations with international clients.
Clearly, confidently, and effectively.

Throughout the course, we focused on real-world scenarios:
→  presenting solutions,
→  negotiating with clients,
→  and communicating with clarity — even when things got complex.

By the end of the program, I was proud to hear this feedback from the L&D team:
✅ “An increase in the level of confidence and linguistic competence.”
✅ “Improvement in communication skills in real situations.”
✅ “Helped participants feel increasingly comfortable speaking in English.”

But one note stood out to me the most:

➡️ “At the end of the course I achieved remarkable improvements.
 I was so pleased that I decided to continue the lessons privately.” ⬅️ 

That’s when I knew something really clicked.

Learning, sure. But also trust.
Language, but also momentum.
We’d built something solid.

And that relationship gave us the space to go even deeper in a 1:1 coaching journey.
Something that feels both personal and powerful.

When corporate training works, it doesn’t end when the course does.
It creates a shift.
It sparks curiosity.
It opens the door to long-term growth.
