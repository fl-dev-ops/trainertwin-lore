What IT Professionals need to know about Corporate English.

No, it’s not more grammar.

If you’re working in an international company, you might have experienced it.

I call it the “Jargon Overkill”💀.

You’re in your meetings, ready to have a meaningful conversation.

Or you’re in a Zoom call and want to talk about process updates.

Forget it.

You’re instantly swamped with data-driven solutions, compelling "outside the box" thinking, continually leveraging existing channels, dynamic scenarios, KPI, BID, COB, LMK, OMG, IMHO, BS🤔.

So, you spend years studying English.

You go through hours of grammar, reading, listening.

You prepare for certification, exams, diplomas to master the language.

Only to feel like a Rugby player at a ballroom dance competition🏉💃🏻🕺🏽.

Out of place?

Yes, this is what I mean.

I believe you have two possibilities here:

🏃‍♂️ Leave the room (or the call) screaming and randomly threatening people.
🧘🏻 Stay calm, breath in, breath out and get a bit more familiar with corporate jargon.

Number 1 is liberating but won’t get you far.

Number 2 probably goes against your ethics, but hey, you need to survive there!

Guess which one I went for?

Well, I can’t say I’m the “Ultimate Jargon Master” (or UJM) or the “Corporate Knowledge Wizard” (CKW), but I did find a way to stay alive.

And I am still mentally sane🧠.

So, there is hope!

Just be careful:

Corporate jargon is always evolving, you need to study it like a “parallel language”.

Buzzwords, acronyms, expressions need to be in your toolbox.

Talk to me, what’s the best experience you’ve ever had with corporate jargon? Don’t leave me alone, please 😅 !
