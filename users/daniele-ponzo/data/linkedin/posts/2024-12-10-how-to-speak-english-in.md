How to Speak English in the Real World (and not fail)

Are you planning to be as effective as possible with your spoken English this season?

Do you want to master international conversations?

Then follow my list to make sure your real-world English becomes successful:

𝗪𝗿𝗶𝘁𝗲 𝗮𝗯𝗼𝘂𝘁 𝘆𝗼𝘂𝗿 𝘄𝗼𝗿𝗸 𝗶𝗻 𝗘𝗻𝗴𝗹𝗶𝘀𝗵
Write about topics you’re passionate about (code snippets, tools). 
↳Think deeply and expand your vocabulary. Publish on platforms like Medium, LinkedIn, or GitHub.

𝗧𝗮𝗹𝗸 𝗮𝗯𝗼𝘂𝘁 𝘆𝗼𝘂𝗿 𝘄𝗼𝗿𝗸 𝗶𝗻 𝗘𝗻𝗴𝗹𝗶𝘀𝗵
As you code or troubleshoot, explain your process out loud in English as if you’re recording a tutorial. 
↳It might sound weird (talking alone), but it builds fluency and increases confidence in speaking.

𝗝𝗼𝗶𝗻 𝗢𝗻𝗹𝗶𝗻𝗲 𝗧𝗲𝗰𝗵 𝗠𝗲𝗲𝘁𝘂𝗽𝘀
Join a virtual meetup on platforms like Meetup, Discord, or Reddit. 
↳Dariusz Sadowski has a great community on Discord for tech enthusiasts.

𝗥𝗲𝘃𝗲𝗿𝘀𝗲 𝗘𝗻𝗴𝗶𝗻𝗲𝗲𝗿 𝗧𝗲𝗰𝗵𝗻𝗶𝗰𝗮𝗹 𝗗𝗼𝗰𝘂𝗺𝗲𝗻𝘁𝗮𝘁𝗶𝗼𝗻
Pick technical documents in English, ask ChatGPT to summarize and simplify them (in bullet points), and explain them as if teaching a junior developer. ↳Bonus: Record your explanation as practice for clarity and pronunciation.

𝗚𝗮𝗺𝗶𝗳𝘆 𝘄𝗶𝘁𝗵 𝗖𝗼𝗱𝗲 𝗖𝗵𝗮𝗹𝗹𝗲𝗻𝗴𝗲𝘀 𝗶𝗻 𝗘𝗻𝗴𝗹𝗶𝘀𝗵
Join coding challenge platforms like #HackerRank or #LeetCode and participate in discussion forums. 
↳Explain your solutions in English or answer others’ questions.

𝗟𝗶𝘀𝘁𝗲𝗻 𝘁𝗼 𝗧𝗲𝗰𝗵 𝗣𝗼𝗱𝗰𝗮𝘀𝘁𝘀 𝗮𝗻𝗱 𝗦𝗵𝗮𝗱𝗼𝘄 𝗦𝗽𝗲𝗮𝗸𝗲𝗿𝘀
Find podcasts like Software Engineering Daily, Coding Blocks or Software Engineering Tales by Pavel Polcr. 
↳Listen and repeat key phrases aloud to improve fluency.

𝗨𝘀𝗲 “𝗥𝘂𝗯𝗯𝗲𝗿 𝗗𝘂𝗰𝗸 𝗗𝗲𝗯𝘂𝗴𝗴𝗶𝗻𝗴” 𝗶𝗻 𝗘𝗻𝗴𝗹𝗶𝘀𝗵
Obtain a rubber duck🐤. Place rubber duck on desk. Explain to the duck what your code is supposed to do. 
↳This helps improve logical expression and vocabulary.

𝗔𝗻𝗮𝗹𝘆𝘇𝗲 𝗧𝗲𝗰𝗵 𝗔𝗿𝘁𝗶𝗰𝗹𝗲𝘀 𝗢𝘂𝘁 𝗟𝗼𝘂𝗱
Summarize articles (TechCrunch, The Verge, or Wired) or newsletters (like “Getting Better” by Giacomo Falcone). Use your own words. 
↳This helps you to think and speak logically.

𝗥𝗼𝗹𝗲-𝗽𝗹𝗮𝘆 𝗥𝗲𝗮𝗹 𝗜𝗧 𝗦𝗰𝗲𝗻𝗮𝗿𝗶𝗼𝘀
Simulate job interviews, client calls, presentations or team meetings with ChatGPT or with a language coach (the good old humans). 
↳Use IT-related scenarios to make the practice more realistic.

Speaking English in the real world has never been so easy.

Ready to turn on your camera, sit at the front of the room and take that international call?

Then, start doing…

SOMETHING DIFFERENT!
