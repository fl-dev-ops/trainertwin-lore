Tech runs on code
but it breaks on communication.

We often say “Tech speaks English.”
And it’s true: we read documentation, follow tutorials, join global communities.

But when it comes to real communication (small talk, meetings, presentations, 1:1s) many professionals suddenly hit a wall.

👉 The paradox?
We can explain APIs but struggle to explain ourselves.
We can debug systems but not misunderstandings.
And that gap between technical clarity and human connection
is often what limits careers in tech.

We’ll discuss how communication impacts team dynamics, leadership, and growth
and how you can train it just like any other skill.

▶️ collaborate better across cultures,
