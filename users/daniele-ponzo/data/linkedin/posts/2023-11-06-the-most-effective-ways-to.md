The most effective ways to sound more professional in English.

When it comes to being polite and less direct, English is quite different from other languages.

For example, in Italian you can use “𝘓𝘦𝘪” (3rd person) to keep your distance form the interlocutor:
🇮🇹  𝘐𝘭 𝘳𝘦𝘱𝘰𝘳𝘵 𝘤𝘩𝘦 𝘓𝘦𝘪 𝘩𝘢 𝘱𝘳𝘦𝘱𝘢𝘳𝘢𝘵𝘰 𝘯𝘦𝘤𝘦𝘴𝘴𝘪𝘵𝘢 𝘥𝘪 𝘢𝘭𝘤𝘶𝘯𝘦 𝘮𝘰𝘥𝘪𝘧𝘪𝘤𝘩𝘦.

We can use a similar strategy in Spanish with “𝘶𝘴𝘵𝘦𝘥”:
🇪🇸  𝘜𝘴𝘵𝘦𝘥 𝘥𝘦𝘣𝘦𝘳í𝘢 𝘮𝘰𝘥𝘪𝘧𝘪𝘤𝘢𝘳 𝘦𝘭 á𝘮𝘣𝘪𝘵𝘰 𝘥𝘦𝘭 𝘱𝘳𝘰𝘺𝘦𝘤𝘵𝘰.

And in French with “𝘝𝘰𝘶𝘴”:
🇫🇷  𝘗𝘰𝘶𝘳𝘳𝘪𝘦𝘻-𝘷𝘰𝘶𝘴 𝘤𝘩𝘢𝘯𝘨𝘦𝘳 𝘭𝘦 𝘯𝘰𝘮 𝘥𝘶 𝘱𝘳𝘰𝘫𝘦𝘵?

Similar possibilities exist in German (𝘚𝘪𝘦), Polish (𝘗𝘢𝘯/𝘗𝘢𝘯𝘪) and other languages.

And what about English?

We don’t have that option, we only have “𝐘𝐨𝐮”. 👈 

When we speak to a colleague, we say “you”; 

When we speak to our boss, we say “you”. 

And what about a client? “You”.

And the King of England? “You” (or, more appropriate “Your Majesty”). 👑 

So we need to find different strategies to keep our distance and be polite.

Here are 3 ways we can use in English to be more professional, more polite and less direct:

✅ Introduce our question with “𝐂𝐨𝐮𝐥𝐝”:
👉 "Could you please change this part of your project?"

✅ Avoid using the word “you” (so, using the 𝐏𝐚𝐬𝐬𝐢𝐯𝐞):
👉 "Can the report be changed, please? We would need to include our customer’s requirements".

✅ Start with a 𝐩𝐨𝐬𝐢𝐭𝐢𝐯𝐞 note before introducing a negative feedback:
👉 "You project plan addresses many good points. However, would you mind rewriting the second part, please?"

And let’s not forget the magic word “please”!🪄

Hopefully, these strategies will avoid awkward situations and misunderstandings.

And they will help us get more support from the key people who need to help us in Business.

So, remember, Less direct ⬇️ More polite ⬆️ 👌
