That time of the year🎅!
 
Talk to all our clients and arrange everything for the new year.
Make sure there’ll be no surprises after the holidays.
We’re under pressure to complete our projects.
Meetings and meetings and meetings.
Write “to do” lists before the break.
Run like a headless chicken.
Deliver one more thing.
Read, write, plan.

Or

Prioritize.
Use some help.
Postpone less urgent stuff.
Use an effective planning method.
Project-manage your important tasks.
Set clear expectations with your customers.
Steer away from crazy last minute client requests.
Switch to a “holiday” mood, relax, take it easy, chill out.
 
What’s your approach to the winter break deadlines? Easy or painful? Share your point of view below.👇
