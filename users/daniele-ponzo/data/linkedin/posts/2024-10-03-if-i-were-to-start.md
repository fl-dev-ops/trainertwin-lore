If I were to start learning English today, there’s one thing I would change.

I would make it more fun 🙂 !

We’re obsessed with formal learning, grades, tests certifications…and we forget one essential thing:

If It's not fun, you're not doing it right.

I had the great opportunity to discuss this with Reanne Besa, a visual artist, IT student (and much more) as part of my “𝐍𝐨𝐧-𝐍𝐚𝐭𝐢𝐯𝐞 𝐋𝐞𝐚𝐝𝐞𝐫” Video Podcast series🎤.

Reanne is helping professionals to simplify ideas and clarify concepts with the support of her incredible visuals.

With her hand-drawn visuals people can enhance their brand and convey their message in a more effective way✍.

She moved to Australia as a kid and had to go through the challenge of feeling left out, learning a new language and a new culture.

Luckily, she had some valuable support:

Cartoons, comics and the library💬.

They helped her to get more confident with English.

And to connect with people going through the same struggle.

What I love about her approach? She constantly challenges herself and she does it with smile on her face🙂.

Not bad!
