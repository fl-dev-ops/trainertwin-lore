Want to boost your confidence in English? Hit “record.”

We all carry a powerful language learning tool in our pocket.
No subscriptions. No complicated setup.
Just your phone’s camera. 😮 

Recording yourself on video might feel awkward at first, I get it.
It's the same for everyone

But it’s one of the most effective ways to:

𝗕𝘂𝗶𝗹𝗱 𝗿𝗲𝗮𝗹-𝘄𝗼𝗿𝗹𝗱 𝘀𝗽𝗲𝗮𝗸𝗶𝗻𝗴 𝗰𝗼𝗻𝗳𝗶𝗱𝗲𝗻𝗰𝗲
You practise with the same pressure as a live call — but without the risk.
No one’s watching. No one’s judging.
You’re just training your voice to show up.

𝗛𝗲𝗮𝗿 𝘄𝗵𝗮𝘁 𝘆𝗼𝘂 𝘀𝗼𝘂𝗻𝗱 𝗹𝗶𝗸𝗲 (𝗮𝗻𝗱 𝗮𝗱𝗷𝘂𝘀𝘁)
Most of us never hear ourselves speak.
When you rewatch, you catch what others hear:
Are you speaking clearly?
Are you pausing too much?
Are you mumbling key words?

𝗡𝗼𝘁𝗶𝗰𝗲 𝗶𝗺𝗽𝗿𝗼𝘃𝗲𝗺𝗲𝗻𝘁 𝗼𝘃𝗲𝗿 𝘁𝗶𝗺𝗲
Keep a few recordings. Watch the first one a month later.
You’ll be amazed at the growth.

𝗣𝗿𝗮𝗰𝘁𝗶𝗰𝗲 𝗳𝗼𝗿 𝘀𝗽𝗲𝗰𝗶𝗳𝗶𝗰 𝘁𝗮𝘀𝗸𝘀
Want to:
Sound better in meetings?
Introduce yourself with confidence?
Pitch an idea or present your project?
➡️ Record it first. Then refine.

𝗔𝘀𝗸 𝗳𝗼𝗿 𝗳𝗲𝗲𝗱𝗯𝗮𝗰𝗸 (𝗼𝗽𝘁𝗶𝗼𝗻𝗮𝗹 — 𝗯𝘂𝘁 𝗽𝗼𝘄𝗲𝗿𝗳𝘂𝗹!)
You can share your video with a friend, a mentor, or even a trusted colleague.
Say: “I’m practising this. Would you mind sharing what works and what I could improve?”

✨ Start small. Talk about your favourite object.
✨ Describe your role at work.
✨ Share a win from your week.

And remember: The goal isn’t to go viral.
(unless you want to be the next influencer)
It’s to find your voice and use it with confidence.

Have you ever tried this? What would you talk about in your first video?
