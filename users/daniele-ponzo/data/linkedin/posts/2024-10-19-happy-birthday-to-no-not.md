Happy birthday to…

No, not to me 😊

To my Youtube channel. ▶️ 

It’s been 1 year (where has the time gone?)

This is something I started because I couldn’t do otherwise.

I had had this idea for years. I wanted to reconnect with old friends, talk to amazing people and meet professionals online.

And thanks to my video podcast I’ve managed to do exactly that.

So, this has been a great journey so far.

I’ve been lucky enough to talk about career challenges, soft skills, the power of communication, the amazing surprises we get when we put ourselves out there…

And I’ve been able to share a thing or two as well.

I haven’t become a youtuber🙂.
My numbers are far from breathtaking.

But I still want to share them, because this is all about passion.

And I really enjoy what I do.

So, I want to send a (virtual) hug to everyone who’s been part of my journey and who’s supported me in the last year. Thank you! 💙 

P.S. Now into year 2. More podcast episodes, more tutorials, more videos. 
I can’t wait!

See you next week with “The Non-Native Leader” 🎤 🖥️
