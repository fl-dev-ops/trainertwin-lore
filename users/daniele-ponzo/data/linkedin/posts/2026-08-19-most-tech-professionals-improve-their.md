Most tech professionals improve their English...

the opposite way they improve everything else.

When a production system slows down, engineers don't panic.

They don't randomly rewrite the code.

They don't read a 500-page manual from cover to cover.

They follow a process.
They identify the bottleneck.

Measure it.
Analyse it.
Improve it.
Monitor it.

But when it comes to communication...
many of us abandon that mindset completely.

We study more grammar.
Memorise more vocabulary.
Hope confidence will eventually arrive.

What if we applied Lean thinking to communication instead?

That's exactly what I explore in my latest LinkedIn article.

I take one of the most widely used continuous improvement frameworks

DMAIC

and show how it can help international tech professionals:

✔️ identify communication bottlenecks
✔️ reduce cognitive load
✔️ improve performance in meetings
✔️ build repeatable communication systems instead of relying on "perfect English"

Because communication is a system.
And systems can be improved.
