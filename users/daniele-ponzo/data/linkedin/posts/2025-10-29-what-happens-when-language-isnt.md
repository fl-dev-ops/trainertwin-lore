What happens when language isn’t just a tool for work… but a piece of your identity? 🌍

Many of us spend years trying to fit in: 

to sound more “native,” 
to hide our accent, 
to speak in a way that feels safe… but not really us

This week on The Non-Native Leader - Podcast, I talk to Anna Aleksenko, an inspiring language trainer and communication coach, about the deep connection between language, identity, and confidence.

We talked about:

▶️ The moment you stop trying to fit in and 𝘀𝘁𝗮𝗿𝘁 𝘁𝗿𝘂𝗹𝘆 𝗯𝗲𝗹𝗼𝗻𝗴𝗶𝗻𝗴 in your team, in your company, in your own voice.

▶️ Why 𝗶𝗺𝗽𝗼𝘀𝘁𝗼𝗿 𝘀𝘆𝗻𝗱𝗿𝗼𝗺𝗲 hits non-native speakers so hard.

▶️ The 𝗲𝗺𝗼𝘁𝗶𝗼𝗻𝗮𝗹 side of working internationally and finding your place.

▶️ The 𝗹𝗼𝗻𝗲𝗹𝗶𝗻𝗲𝘀𝘀 that often comes with moving to a new country, and how to rebuild 𝗰𝗼𝗺𝗺𝘂𝗻𝗶𝘁𝘆 through language.

Anna’s journey is powerful and deeply relatable, especially if you’ve ever felt like you were “pretending” when speaking English or doubted your right to take up space in an international environment.

One key takeaway?
Let’s stop chasing perfection. Really, it’s not the goal (SPOILER: it doesn’t exist).
Let’s start 𝗯𝗲𝗹𝗼𝗻𝗴𝗶𝗻𝗴 to our own story.
