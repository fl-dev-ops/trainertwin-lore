Where have you been?

I’m going to tell you in style!

Also, a great opportunity to review some English vocabulary...
So here we go:

𝗛𝗲𝗮𝗱𝗮𝗰𝗵𝗲. Pronunciation: Head – /eɪk/. It rhymes with “cake”

𝗧𝗲𝗺𝗽𝗲𝗿𝗮𝘁𝘂𝗿𝗲. As in “I had a temperature and I wasn’t feeling ok”

𝗙𝗲𝘃𝗲𝗿. Same as above (synonym)

𝗖𝗼𝗹𝗱. As in “to catch a cold”: to become ill with a common infection that causes you to cough and sneeze and your nose to run (= drip liquid) or feel blocked

𝗥𝘂𝗻𝗻𝘆 𝗻𝗼𝘀𝗲 (when your nose drips liquid…)

𝗖𝗼𝘂𝗴𝗵. It’s onomatopoeia, so the word sounds exactly like the problem you have. It rhymes with ‘off’: “I tried to play it cool… then a cough set me off.”

𝗦𝗻𝗲𝗲𝘇𝗲 (regular verb). when air suddenly comes out of your nose and mouth in a way you cannot control

𝗕𝗹𝗼𝘄 (your) nose: force air out of your nose to clean it. As in: “I took a handkerchief and blew my nose” (yes, blow is irregular)

𝗛𝗮𝗻𝗱𝗸𝗲𝗿𝗰𝗵𝗶𝗲𝗳. Pronunciation: hank-er-chief. a square piece of cloth or paper used for cleaning the nose.

𝗩𝗶𝗿𝘂𝘀. Yes, the doctor got involved.

𝗦𝗶𝗻𝘂𝘀𝗶𝘁𝗶𝘀: inflammation of the sinus cavities, often after a cold, causing facial pain/pressure,  congestion, and sometimes fever, headache, cough

𝗠𝗲𝗱𝗶𝗰𝗶𝗻𝗲. Countable, in this case. As in “the doctor prescribed various medicines”

𝗧𝗮𝗯𝗹𝗲𝘁𝘀. Nothing to do with IT. They can be defined as “the solid unit dosage form of medication”. As in “the doctor prescribed various tablets”

𝗡𝗲𝗯𝘂𝗹𝗶𝘇𝗲𝗿: a medical device that turns liquid medicine into a fine mist (aerosol). I love the way in Italian this device is simply called “the aerosol”

What would you add? (I hope nothing, as that was already enough...)

Anyway, I’m back now. The real question is “have you missed me 😅 ?”
