How to sound more professional without the need for an extensive vocabulary.⬇️

Why do we need to sound more professional anyway?

You’re right…

Most of the times we get away with “I don’t know”, “it’s a problem”, “I think”, “I’m not sure”.

But what if…

We are meeting a new client 👩‍💼.
We have a conference call with our new boss 👩‍💻 (and they speak English).
We are in a meeting with our new international team 🗣.

Then we need to:

✅Sound more diplomatic when expressing our opinion.
✅Convey a more professional sense of caution.
✅Express our need for more information.
✅Sound more formal and confident.

This is why I have created the carousel below 👇.

It offers 8 expressions you can easily swap with a more formal, professional, and diplomatic equivalent.

They bridge the gap when we seem to miss the right words and help us communicate effectively in various settings.

P.S. Remember, it's all about adapting to the context, and these expressions will help you strike the right chord:

Informal 👉 common expression
