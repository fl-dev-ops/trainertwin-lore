I don’t want to teach you a language.

I would like to help you interact in a complex system🔁.

Communicating doesn’t mean (only) learning new words or new grammar.

Just like the dots on the video below, We use our words, our bodies, our chemicals and we bump into each other in a constant interactive feedback loop 🔃

👥Human Interaction: Communication or engagement between people, which can be verbal, non-verbal, physical, or digital.

⚗️Chemical/Biological Interaction: The way substances or organisms influence each other, like interactions between species in an ecosystem.

🤝Social Interaction: The ways in which individuals or groups relate to and influence one another in a social setting.

So you see, there is more to it.

In the “Life orchestra” each person is like a musician, playing their instrument: their unique skills, thoughts, and actions🎼. 

The larger system is the orchestra itself, a complex arrangement of individuals working together. 

Every conversation, gesture, or shared experience is a note, contributing to the music of daily life🎶.

If one part of the orchestra plays out of sync or ignores the cues from others, the music falters, and the system becomes chaotic🌀.

Each musician must listen, adapt, and respond to the conductor and other players. 

In our daily life we humans must continually communicate, collaborate, and adapt to our environment and the people around us.

And we can create harmony or discord ♡𖡎.

We play and experiment with our social space every day.

So, only learning words and grammar will not help.

We need to get out there, interact, collide with other people, play with our personal space🔁.

There is not other way, I’m afraid.

How’s your communication game going? Let me know in the comments 👇 .
