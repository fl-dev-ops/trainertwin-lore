Being understood and being heard are not the same thing.

That idea has shaped my entire career.

And this week, it received an unexpected validation.

I’m honoured to share that I have been named:

🏆 Business Language Coach of the Year 2026
at the Corporate LiveWire Innovation & Excellence Awards.

What means the most to me is not the award itself.

It’s the reason it was awarded.

The judges recognised my work for helping international professionals: communicate with more clarity, confidence, and credibility in meetings, presentations, interviews, and cross-cultural workplace conversations. 

They also highlighted the way I bridge technical expertise with business communication.

That matters because I didn’t start this work from a position of ease.

I started it from frustration.

When I first began working in international corporate environments, I often knew exactly what I wanted to say.

The idea was there.
The solution was there.

But when the meeting started...
something changed.

I would overthink the sentence.
Add too much context.
Try to sound more professional.
Sometimes speak less than I should have.

But communication is not just about knowing something.

It’s about helping other people understand it.

That experience became the foundation of my work.

Helping professionalscommunicate more effectively when it matters most.

Because in global teams, English is rarely judged as English.

It becomes the lens through which people assess:

how clearly you think,
how confidently you lead,
how well you explain complexity,
and how much they can trust your recommendation.

That’s why I believe communication coaching needs to go beyond vocabulary and grammar.

It needs to address:

🔹how professionals structure ideas under pressure,
🔹how they reduce cognitive load,
🔹how they make technical expertise easier to understand,
🔹and how they communicate at the level they’ve already reached professionally.

That is the work I care about.

Because I know what it feels like to have the right idea...
and struggle to make it land.

And I know what changes when people finally do.

Thank you to Corporate LiveWire for this recognition.

And thank you to every client, colleague, learner, and professional who has trusted me with their growth over the years.

This award is a milestone.

But the mission remains the same:

Helping international professionals stop sounding less capable than they are.

When did you realise communication mattered as much as expertise in your career?
