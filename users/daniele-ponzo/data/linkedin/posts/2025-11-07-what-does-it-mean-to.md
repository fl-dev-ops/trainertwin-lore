What does it mean to H.A.C.K. your English?

Let me tell you through one of my favourite stories from one of my clients.

She came to me with a clear goal:
“I want to feel confident speaking English in interviews and meetings.”

Like many professionals, she already had a good level of English
But she felt insecure about using English in professional situations
And she wanted clarity, structure, and confidence.

So we decided to go for “𝗧𝗵𝗲 𝗚𝗿𝗲𝗮𝘁 𝗛𝗮𝗰𝗸”

Let’s hear from her words:

𝗛 – 𝗛𝗼𝗻𝗲 𝘆𝗼𝘂𝗿 𝘀𝗸𝗶𝗹𝗹𝘀: "concrete examples and effective tools that I could immediately apply to real situations"
𝗔 – 𝗔𝗰𝘁𝗶𝗼𝗻: "gave me the confidence to handle interviews and meetings successfully"
𝗖 – 𝗖𝗼𝗹𝗹𝗮𝗯𝗼𝗿𝗮𝘁𝗶𝗼𝗻: "a supportive environment where I could grow, practise, and make mistakes without feeling judged"
𝗞 – 𝗞𝘂𝗱𝗼𝘀: "I could clearly see my progress"

She brought incredible energy to every session

And her transformation didn’t stop there.
She got so passionate about communicating in English that she decided to join an improv theatre group in English.

When confidence meets curiosity, there are no limits.
That’s what happens when you decide to put yourself out there.

These are the stories that make me so proud!
People that decide to stop waiting
Take action
Invest in themselves
And become protagonists on the international stage

And I’m absolutely delighted to see my client’s transformation

Time for some extra Kudos!
