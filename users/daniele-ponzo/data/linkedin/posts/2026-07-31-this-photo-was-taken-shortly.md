This photo was taken shortly after I moved to Ireland.

I'd already quit one job.

After 2 hours.

I'd recently graduated and was leaving my CV almost everywhere in Dublin.

Shops.
Restaurants.
Pubs.
Hotels.
Offices.

Anywhere that might give me a chance.

One day, a hotel manager called me for an interview.

He welcomed me in and started explaining the role.

Then he looked at me and said:

"So, Jimmy..."

He carried on for a few minutes.

"Jimmy, this is a 3-star hotel..."

"Jimmy, can you do this...?"

Eventually, I interrupted him.

"Sorry... I'm not Jimmy."

He looked at me.

Looked at the CV.

Looked back at me.

He'd mixed up two applications and had been interviewing the wrong person.

There was a long pause.

Then he smiled and said:

"𝗪𝗲𝗹𝗹... 𝘆𝗼𝘂'𝗿𝗲 𝗵𝗲𝗿𝗲 𝗻𝗼𝘄. 𝗧𝗵𝗲 𝗷𝗼𝗯'𝘀 𝘆𝗼𝘂𝗿𝘀."

I thought, why not?

So I started.

Sweeping the street.
Polishing door handles.
Vacuuming stairs.
Cleaning rooms.
Setting up the function room.

And, apparently, making sure every pen pointed in exactly the same direction.

𝗘𝘅𝗮𝗰𝘁𝗹𝘆. 𝗧𝗵𝗲. 𝗦𝗮𝗺𝗲. 𝗗𝗶𝗿𝗲𝗰𝘁𝗶𝗼𝗻.

Two hours later, I realised two things:

- This wasn't the job I'd imagined.

- It probably wasn't the job I'd applied for either.

So I thanked them...

and quit.

To this day, it's still the shortest job I've ever had.

Looking back, I'm glad I walked away.

It simply wasn't my job.

Funny enough, that accidental interview became one of my favourite stories from my early days in Ireland.

Now I'm curious...
