People often assume I've always been confident speaking English.

They couldn't be more wrong.

If you only know me from LinkedIn, you probably know three things.

I help tech professionals communicate with confidence in international environments.

I spend a worrying amount of time talking about meetings, presentations and difficult conversations.

And I genuinely believe communication can change a career.

All true.

But none of those things explain why I do this.

Long before I started coaching, I was the person hiding in the back of the room during meetings, because I wasn't sure I could explain things well enough in English.

Rehearsing a sentence in my head a million times before speaking.

I know what it feels like to leave a meeting thinking:

"I should have said that".

And I also know what it feels like when something finally changes.

One day, you stop monitoring every word.

You stop wondering whether your grammar is correct.

You stop translating in your head.

Instead...

you start thinking about the conversation.

English stops being the thing you're focused on.

It simply becomes the tool you use.

That changed everything for me.

Maybe that's why I coach the way I do today.

People often think I teach English.

I don't.

I help people communicate clearly, think under pressure and stay in the conversation even when English isn't their first language.

Because I'm not interested in helping someone sound "perfect."

I'm much more interested in helping them:

• say what they really mean
• recover when they lose their train of thought
• explain complex ideas simply
• contribute with confidence
• feel like themselves when they speak

This is real communication.

There's one more thing you probably wouldn't guess from my LinkedIn headline.

I'm endlessly curious about people.

I love hearing about the projects they're building.

The strange career paths that brought them here.

The conversations they're avoiding.

The opportunities they're afraid to accept.

The goals they're quietly working towards.

Maybe that's why coaching never feels repetitive.

Every conversation teaches me something too.

So, if we've recently connected...

Hi, I'm Daniele. 👋

Now you know a little more about the person behind the posts.

I'm curious...

What's one experience that shaped your career but doesn't appear anywhere on your LinkedIn profile?
