Teaching English is a serious business.

But it doesn’t mean you can’t adopt a relaxed and easy-going approach in your classes.

I often talk to great professionals who feel:

⚠Anxious
⚠Nervous
⚠Embarrassed
⚠Uncomfortable

When it comes to using English at work.

So, one of my main goals is putting people at ease.

When people feel calm and relaxed, then you create a productive atmosphere.

And the exchange becomes more meaningful.

I have to say it feels great when this is recognized.

Ms D was extremely nervous when we first met.

She told me she hated the idea of having to use English at work, as she had never done it.

And her confidence level was very low.

So, I really focused on releasing some of the tension around speaking English.

And use that bit of humour to smooth things down. 😉 

The whole learning process benefitted from this.

And today Ms D can face more confidently her challenges at work.

She can sit more comfortably in her conference calls.

And she can even smile while using English! 😊 

👉 If you feel you need some support to overcome the stress around speaking English at work, then reach out.
I’d be honoured to team up and beat that anxiety together. 👊
