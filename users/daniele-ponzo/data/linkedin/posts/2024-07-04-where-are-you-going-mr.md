Where are you going, Mr D.?

Every time I am in a train station, I ask myself this question.🚆

It’s not that I don’t know my direction.

But I always wonder.

What if I change.

What if I take an unexpected road?

I’ve already done it a few times in my life.

And I have never regretted it.

I’ve missed trains, I’ve arrived at stations too late or too early, I’ve taken the wrong trains, I’ve spent minutes and hours on trains, I’ve slept on a train, I’ve met amazing people on trains, and I’ve been irritated by horrible people.

I’ve worked in Research, IT, Politics, Language teaching, coaching, I’ve changed countries, houses, habits.

I’ve changed direction, explored new fields, new lands, new environments.

I’ve felt like an impostor many times on the wrong train.

But I learned something new in every single experience.

So, really, do you know where you are going?

Is your journey a straight line, like a railway track?🛤️

Or it feels more like a rollercoaster?🎢

Do you sometimes feel like an impostor too?

With the right ticket on the wrong train?

Well, know that you’re not alone.

I’ve been there.

And you don’t need to miss that train.

So, if you really want to have a meaningful journey.

And

you want to get to the right place at the right time.

Then, DM me the word ‘TRAIN’.

And take your language journey to the next level.

Mr D., the “Train manager”🚃.
