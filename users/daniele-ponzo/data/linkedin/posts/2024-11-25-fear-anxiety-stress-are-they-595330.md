Fear, Anxiety, stress. Are they holding you back when you want to speak English?

Well, let me give you… a hand 🖐 !

Actually, I’m going to ask Dr Daniel Siegel to give us all a hand.

The hand model of the brain can help you overcome stress and anxiety 🧠 .

In Siegel's model, our brain is represented by a hand, 

The thumb – the “downstairs” brain symbolizes the lower parts responsible for our basic survival instincts ⬇ .

The fingers – the “upstairs” brain represent the higher cognitive functions ⬆ 

When we feel scared or "blocked" in language learning or speaking, we often find ourselves stuck in the "thumb" – the reactive and instinctual part of the brain.

Our hand is open, our fingers are lifted, our fist is released. This is when we lose connection, we can't control our emotions and we can’t effectively communicate with others.

It’s the “fight or flight” mode, where fear takes over and we are unable to speak effectively.

Survival instinct.

How do we overcome this status?

CONNECTION!

It’s as simple as curling our fingers over our thumb. Now our upstairs and downstairs brain are connected ✊ !

We activate the higher brain regions associated with rationalization, learning, and creativity. 

By engaging these upper parts of the brain, we can overcome our fears and limitations.
And we communicate more effectively and confidently.
 
So, next time you feel nervous or want to run away, think about the hand:

Break down the task into manageable steps. Close one finger at the time.

👉  Self Control: Stop Overthinking
👉 Problem Solving: Practice
👉 Regulation: Repeat it
👉 Logic: Confidence

Remind yourself to shift your focus from nervousness (the "thumb") to rationality and creativity (the "fingers" of the brain). 

And visualize your fist closing and the upper part of your brain embracing the lower part, creating real connection.

Give yourself a hand!
