High-stakes meetings don’t test your English.
They test your structure.

Your English is only as strong as it is under pressure.

Not when you’re chatting.
Not when you’re writing tickets.
Not when you’re explaining something familiar.

But when you have to:
• push back
• disagree
• say no
• make a recommendation under pressure

This is where many IT & Tech professionals feel their English “disappears”.

They hesitate.
They soften.
They over-explain.

And suddenly, they don’t sound as senior as they actually are.

Not because they don’t know what to say
but because 𝗹𝗲𝗮𝗱𝗲𝗿𝘀𝗵𝗶𝗽-𝗹𝗲𝘃𝗲𝗹 𝗰𝗼𝗺𝗺𝘂𝗻𝗶𝗰𝗮𝘁𝗶𝗼𝗻 𝗶𝗻 𝗘𝗻𝗴𝗹𝗶𝘀𝗵 𝗿𝗲𝗾𝘂𝗶𝗿𝗲𝘀 𝘀𝘁𝗿𝘂𝗰𝘁𝘂𝗿𝗲.

High-stakes workplace English is a different skill.

It’s the ability to stay clear and decisive when the room is watching.
That’s what signals authority.

👉 When the pressure rises, does your authority rise with it?
