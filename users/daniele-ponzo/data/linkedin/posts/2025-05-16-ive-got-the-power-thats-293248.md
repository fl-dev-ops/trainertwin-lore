🎶 "I've got the power!"

That’s exactly how 𝘩𝘦 felt

A senior developer took part in my  5-day challenge
And recorded himself speaking English on video for the very first time.

Until recently, he believed fluency meant speaking fast, with no mistakes.
He thought the goal was to be perfect.

And like many others, he was stuck

Not because of lack of knowledge, 
but because of pressure and fear.

This week, something shifted.

“𝘉𝘦𝘧𝘰𝘳𝘦 𝘵𝘩𝘪𝘴 𝘸𝘦𝘦𝘬'𝘴 𝘤𝘩𝘢𝘭𝘭𝘦𝘯𝘨𝘦, 𝘐 𝘸𝘢𝘴 𝘢𝘭𝘸𝘢𝘺𝘴 𝘯𝘦𝘳𝘷𝘰𝘶𝘴 𝘵𝘰 𝘴𝘱𝘦𝘢𝘬 𝘪𝘯 𝘌𝘯𝘨𝘭𝘪𝘴𝘩… 𝘐 𝘶𝘴𝘦𝘥 𝘵𝘰 𝘵𝘩𝘪𝘯𝘬 𝘵𝘩𝘦 𝘮𝘢𝘪𝘯 𝘨𝘰𝘢𝘭 𝘸𝘢𝘴 𝘧𝘭𝘶𝘦𝘯𝘤𝘺 𝘸𝘪𝘵𝘩𝘰𝘶𝘵 𝘦𝘳𝘳𝘰𝘳𝘴. 𝘉𝘶𝘵 𝘸𝘪𝘵𝘩 𝘺𝘰𝘶𝘳 𝘴𝘪𝘮𝘱𝘭𝘦 𝘴𝘵𝘦𝘱𝘴 𝘢𝘯𝘥 𝘴𝘵𝘳𝘢𝘵𝘦𝘨𝘪𝘦𝘴, 𝘐 𝘭𝘦𝘢𝘳𝘯𝘦𝘥 𝘩𝘰𝘸 𝘵𝘰 𝘴𝘭𝘰𝘸 𝘥𝘰𝘸𝘯, 𝘵𝘩𝘪𝘯𝘬 𝘤𝘭𝘦𝘢𝘳𝘭𝘺, 𝘢𝘯𝘥 𝘧𝘦𝘦𝘭 𝘮𝘰𝘳𝘦 𝘧𝘭𝘶𝘦𝘯𝘵.”

He realized something powerful:
Speaking with intention beats rushing through your words.

We sometimes consider slow speaking a weakness
But I think it’s a strength.

It helps you
🔹Think
🔹Catch your breath
🔹Be clear

💬 Finding your voice in another language can feel like unlocking a superpower.

Sure, you're not going to be 'almighty'. 
But you'll feel you're finally being heard.
In English.

So… have you found your power yet?
