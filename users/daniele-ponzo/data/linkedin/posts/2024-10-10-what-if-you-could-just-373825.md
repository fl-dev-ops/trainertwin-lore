What if you could just HACK your English?

You want to get more confident so you can speak freely.

But you need to start speaking to get more confident.

I used to feel exactly the same 🔄 .

The result? I was not moving forward.

Not heard, not seen, not noticed.
Like a lamp post in daylight ⚫ .

So, how do you break this vicious circle?

➡️Start learning by doing.
And incorporate your learning in the workflow.

Here is what worked for me:

A practical and effective strategy.

‘H.A.C.K’
 
🅗 𝐇𝐨𝐧𝐞: Refine your vocabulary so you sound more professional (Phrasal verbs, idioms, business expressions).
 
🅐 𝐀𝐜𝐭𝐢𝐨𝐧: Presentations, meetings, calls… every interaction is a chance to learn and grow.
 
🅒 𝐂𝐨𝐥𝐥𝐚𝐛𝐨𝐫𝐚𝐭𝐢𝐨𝐧: Create a strong network with your peers, exchange ideas and feedback.
 
🅚 𝐊𝐮𝐝𝐨𝐬: Recognize and celebrate milestones along the way to boost morale and motivate continued growth.

Without this I will still be living with Mr. Insecurity.
