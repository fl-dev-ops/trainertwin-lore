Different language, different emotion.

When you're multilingual, each language shapes your identity. Often in ways that you don't expect.

One language is more suitable for "feelings", another is more "professional"... it depends on your history and your relationship with them.

How you learned them and in which phase of your life.

For me, Italian is the language of family, English is the language of emotions and my first choice for professional interactions. And Spanish for friends and fun.

I think it's a true blessing and I will be forever grateful.

Thanks Mariska T. for following up on this great topic!
