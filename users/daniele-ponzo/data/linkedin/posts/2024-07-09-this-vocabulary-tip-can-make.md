This vocabulary tip can make or break your English CV

According to one study from TLNT.com, backed by a great article by Dr. John Sullivan, ACTION verbs in your CV can increase the chances of an interview by 140%.

Yes, 140%

So, if you want to make your CV stand out, highlight your achievements and your abilities, you MUST use action verbs in your CV.

Here is how you can use the verbs:

➡ Be direct and clear. Use verbs which describe exactly what you did and go straight to the point: for example: lead, manage.

➡ Highlight your achievement. Show your role and your contribution in the projects: increase, improve, develop.

➡ Show initiative and results: launch, start, create, optimize put the accent on your achievement and your initiative.

➡ Underline ownership. Don’t just show that you “follow”. Explain what you owned, managed or directed to make sure you are noticed.

➡ Demonstrate your results: use numbers, %, +. You could say: “I improved the margin by 17% thanks to my project…”

Remember: networking, testimonials and recommendations are still very important to get a job interview. BUT putting the right content in your CV can boost your chances to be interviewed🚀.
