Demystifying Business Jargon in IT #13: "Hold the Fort"🏰

Ever heard the phrase "hold the fort" in a business setting? 

What does it really mean?

"Hold the fort" is a common expression used in the business world to indicate the act of maintaining control or responsibility for a situation or task while someone else is temporarily absent. 

Essentially, it means to maintain stability and keep things under control until others return.

Here are a couple of examples of how this phrase is used in the IT business world:

👉 "While our project manager is attending a conference, I'll need you to hold the fort and ensure that our team continues to meet project deadlines."

👉"As the senior developer, you'll be asked to hold the fort during the CEO's absence, overseeing the day-to-day operations of our software development team."

The origins are a bit controversial as they date back to the American Civil War era and the Wild West🏜️. 

The idea was to protect the fort against the enemy.

Luckily, we don’t have to use guns today.

So, next time you hear this expression, remember people are only talking about backup plans and business continuity 🙂 .
