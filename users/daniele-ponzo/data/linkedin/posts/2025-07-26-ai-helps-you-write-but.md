AI helps you write. But what happens when it’s time to speak?

You’ve crafted the perfect email, message, or even LinkedIn post.
You used ChatGPT, Grammarly, DeepL... whatever works.

It sounds professional. Clear. Maybe even impressive. 

But then… someone calls.
Or a meeting starts.

And suddenly your words disappear.

This is something I see all the time with my clients:
✅ Clear writing (with help)
❌ Uncertainty when it’s time to speak

There’s nothing wrong with using support.

The problem is when we rely on tools to sound like “us”
and forget to train our real voice.

If your writing and speaking feel like two different people, you're not alone.
