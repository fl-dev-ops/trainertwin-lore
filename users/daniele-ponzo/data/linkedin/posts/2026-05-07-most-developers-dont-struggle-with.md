Most developers don’t struggle with English.

Until it actually matters.

They read documentation.
They write emails.
They follow technical discussions.

No problem.

But then:

A meeting with leadership.
A client call.
A presentation.
An interview.

And suddenly:

“Ok… how do I say this…”
“Wait… what’s the right word…”
“Let me think…”

Same knowledge.

Different performance.

That’s where most careers quietly slow down.

Communication under pressure.

I’ve just published a new article where I break down:

→ the 5 biggest communication problems I see in developers
→ what actually happens in high-stakes situations
→ and how to fix it (without “studying more English”)

If you’ve ever felt like:

“I know this… I just can’t say it properly”

You’ll recognise yourself in this.
