When your message doesn’t land…

You knew exactly what you wanted to say.
It was clear in your head.
But in the meeting, it just… didn’t land.

I recently asked senior IT professionals what’s really happening in those moments.

Most didn’t blame vocabulary.

They mentioned

Overthinking.
Mental load.
Positioning.
structure.
Pressure.
Stress.

But not everyone agrees.

So I’m curious.

𝗪𝗵𝗲𝗻 𝘆𝗼𝘂𝗿 𝗺𝗲𝘀𝘀𝗮𝗴𝗲 𝗱𝗼𝗲𝘀𝗻’𝘁 𝗹𝗮𝗻𝗱 𝗶𝗻 𝗘𝗻𝗴𝗹𝗶𝘀𝗵, 𝗶𝘁’𝘀 𝗺𝗮𝗶𝗻𝗹𝘆 𝗯𝗲𝗰𝗮𝘂𝘀𝗲:

1️⃣ My English isn’t strong enough
→ I don’t always have the vocabulary or fluency I need in the moment.

2️⃣ I lose structure under pressure
→ My ideas are clear in my head, but they come out disorganized.

3️⃣ I don’t adapt to the audience well
→ I say the right things, but not in the way this person needs to hear them.

4️⃣ I overthink and soften my message
→ I hesitate, qualify too much, or dilute my recommendation.
