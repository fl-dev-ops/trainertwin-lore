For two weeks...

No Notifications.

No Meet.

No email.

No "quick questions."

Just fresh air, long walks, family time, and a chance to completely switch off.

If you work in tech, you already know what happens when a production system runs at 100% CPU utilisation for too long.

Performance drops.
Latency increases.

Eventually...

something breaks.

Our brains aren't much different.

Over the last two weeks I gave mine the reboot it needed.

Stepping away from work didn't just recharge my batteries.

It gave me something even more valuable:

perspective.

One thing became even clearer to me.

The professionals I work with struggle because they're expected to solve complex problems while communicating under pressure.

And that takes mental bandwidth.

You communicate better when your brain isn't busy juggling twenty background processes.

That's exactly what I'll be focusing on over the next few months.

More ideas.
More practical frameworks.
More client success stories.

It's good to be back!

💬 One question...

Did you manage to completely disconnect this summer...

or did Slack eventually find you?
