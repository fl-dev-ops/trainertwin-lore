My program is full of mistakes.

Literally.

All the mistakes I’ve made in my professional life.

All the lessons I’ve learned.

(sometimes the hard way).

Why?

So you don’t have to make the same mistakes.

How did I fail?

🔴 That time I was afraid of making a phone call.

And I kept sending emails hoping the problem would vanish.

Wrong. When you can, a conversation is always better than an email.

👉 What I’ve learned: Pick up the phone and have some good language ready to use, so this will become natural.

🔴 Or when I failed a job interview.

I spent hours preparing a shiny presentation.

I was ready to go in front of senior management.

All was going well, until they interrupted me to ask a “customer style” question.

I wasn’t able to answer it on the spot.

Lost confidence.

And didn’t get the job. 

👉 What I’ve learned: have a strategy to answer unexpected questions. Always expect the unexpected.

🔴 What about my first meeting as a Team Leader?

I wanted to impress my team and make sure my manager would trust me to implement the new strategy.

I ended up scaring the team and everybody felt I was not in control.

👉 What I’ve learned: meetings have to be planned carefully. You have to be clear on the different stages and what to say, so you leave a great impression.

The truth us, we never stop learning.

If you’ve been in those situations, you know what I’m talking about.

So, yeah, my program contains 20 years of mistakes and lessons learned, which means you'll know exactly what to say in those important moments.

To impress your customers.

Get your dream job.

And be a confident leader.
