“How can I be fluent in English?”

“How do I learn English in 6 months?”

”I want to improve the English language quickly, but how?”

”How can I improve my English skills if they are very weak?”

”I can speak English, but hesitation comes to my way. Why?”

”Is it possible to become fluent in speaking English if someone is willing to put the time and effort into learning it?”

”I can understand what someone is saying in English but I can't respond in English.”

”How can a non-native speaker of English learn and equip himself with the skills to communicate?”

These are just some of the questions I’ve received from clients and on social media.

The reality is:

There is no magic wand. ✨ 

You can’t fully learn a language in 6 months.

But…

You can get more confident.

You can improve the skills you need.

You can focus on business communication.

It takes dedication.

Consistency.

And willingness to put yourself out there.

So, here is a quick, 3-step plan to see a quick improvement in speaking:

✔ Dedicate a few minutes each day to review and learn new vocabulary. 
✔ Practice IT-related scenarios like
      👉 explaining a technical issue or 
      👉discussing a project with colleagues or clients. 
✔ Record short audios or videos where you explain technical concepts or give presentations in English.
