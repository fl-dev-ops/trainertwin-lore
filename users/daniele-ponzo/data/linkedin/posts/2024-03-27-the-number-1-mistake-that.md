The number 1 mistake that keeps IT Professionals from advancing in their career.
 
Many years ago I was working in a large IT company.

I had a good role and I was happy with my work.

But after a while, I felt I was ready for new challenges.

So I sat back, continued working hard, wating for my chance to get promoted. 📈 

Except, my chance never arrived. 📉 

I couldn’t work out why. 🤔 

I was doing all the work.

I was always spot on, no mistakes on my reports and on data control.

Until one day it hit me😶.

It was during one of the company’s social nights.

Being an introvert by nature, I had never really taken part in those events.

But that night I overheard this conversation (more or less):

'…yes, he’s good, but he has to come forward. Sure, he’s doing the work, but how come he’s not speaking up…?'

It took me a couple of minutes to realize they were talking about me👈.

I guess you can imagine the mixture of pride (he’s good) and shame (he’s not speaking up).

And I saw that no matter how good I was, it was useless if nobody knew it.

So, from that day, I started working on getting more visible!

I put together my plan and I started working on:

🔵Engaging in casual conversations (small talk)
🔵Showing my achievements in performance reviews.
🔵Sharing my wins with the other team members.
🔵Becoming an active participant in meetings

My number 1 recommendation?

Make yourself visible 👀 .

Don’t be afraid to speak up, have your say, and show how good you are.

P.S. If you are working in an international company or would like to one day, you might need to make yourself more visible.

If you feel you need support, send me a message.
I’ll be happy to chat and give you some tips. 🤝
