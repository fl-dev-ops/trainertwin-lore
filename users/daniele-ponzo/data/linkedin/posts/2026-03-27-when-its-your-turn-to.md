When it’s your turn to speak in the meeting…

…and suddenly your brain opens 27 tabs at once.

You know this moment.

You understand everything.
You even have a strong point to add.

Then it’s your turn.

And your brain goes:

“Ok… say it in English… structure it… don’t make mistakes… sound confident… what’s the word… how do I start…”

😐

Instead of:

clear → direct → structured

You get:

“Uhm… yes… what I mean is… eh… maybe…”

Or worse… silence.

The frustrating part?

Yes, you know English.
You've studied it, you understand it, you can read it.

But when you speak under pressure, your brain is trying to:

• translate
• structure
• choose words
• check grammar
• manage how you sound

All at the same time.

Too many tabs open.
