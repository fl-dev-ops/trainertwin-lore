First day leading the team.

He’s pacing up and down the corridor.
Slowly moving towards the meeting room.

Everything is in order.
The slide deck is solid.
A couple of lighter moments built in.

The team is there, waiting.

New organization.
New strategy.
New direction.

He’s ready. Rehearsed. Prepared.

After all, this time he’s on stage.

But… what if?

What if the message isn’t clear?
What if he stumbles?
What if he sounds hesitant? Defensive?

Some team members are more senior.
One never fully accepted his promotion.
Some juniors are afraid of what the new strategy will bring.

Half the team will connect remotely.
New members, he will have to introduce them.

Too hard.

He shouldn’t have done this.

He remembers all the years studying English.
They didn’t prepare him for this.

“Mr Impostor, you were right.”

But then… he smiles.

He’s done this before.
Just not officially.

People came to him for advice.
Managers relied on him to handle difficult conversations.
His boss asked him to train new starters.

Why?

Because they trusted him.

His communication.
His empathy.
His attitude.

That’s how he built his expertise.

So he walks into the room.
Opens the slides.
And delivers.

After the meeting, one of the senior team members approaches him.

“Thank you, Daniele. The strategy is clear. It’s hard, but I understand why we’re doing it.”

Clarity.
Not perfection.
To beat Mr impostor.
