  Scroll through any social media network and you’re highly likely to encounter content created by artificial intelligence. Some of it, like the ubiquitous cat videos, will be cute and harmless. However, the same platforms that bind us together and create moments of communal joy and connection can also divide us through algorithmic radicalization.
