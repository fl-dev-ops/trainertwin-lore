Why this topic matters
As agents get more capable, the bottleneck shifts from building them to improving them. Observability and evals surface what an agent is doing, but a human still reads the dashboards and combs the traces to fix what broke. Aparna walks through how observability is moving from human-driven investigation to agent-driven execution, and the loop that turns a production break into a verified fix.
