⛳ An open challenge: find me a better AI community of thoughtful people who are continuously learning, sharing what they’re seeing, and shipping useful, non BS work this quickly.

One of the best kept secrets of taking our Maven courses is the option to join Chai & AI after they end, a community made up of people across our network, including VCs, managers, builders, and students from every cohort. 

I’m revealing it today because I’m incredibly proud of how far it has come!

The community stays available long after the course ends, and we host two kinds of sessions for the community every week. 
- Our Chai & AI conversations are open, informal discussions about the state of AI, changing jobs, new models, what’s actually working, and everything people are trying to make sense of. Everyone participates, and we usually have far too much fun.
- We also run weekly community sessions across enterprise AI, AI research, and AI productivity. So far, we’ve covered everything from Claude Code 101 and building an AI chief of staff to memory systems, harness engineering, voice AI agents, FDEs, and several much deeper technical topics.

Members often bring us ideas based on what they’re hearing at work or seeing across the industry. They ask us to run sessions because they trust that the conversation won’t be generic or full of fluff. They know there’ll be no BS.

This is an example: A community member asked for a session on loop engineering on July 6. We’re delivering it on July 15. That’s 10 days from request to session.

Most online communities slowly turn into self promotion, random messages, and channels nobody checks. Ours  hasn’t!

People from our very first cohort are still actively learning, contributing ideas, and upskilling through these sessions.

I’m incredibly proud of what we’ve built, and even more proud of the people who’ve built it with us!!
