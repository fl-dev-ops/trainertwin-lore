🫠 Kind of funny how people thought AI would completely automate outreach and marketing, but it’s becoming quite the opposite.

I’ve become so averse to generic cold outreach messages, even when they look like some effort was put into researching me. Most of them still feel like a template that was used across a bunch of people with a few fields filled in.
The bar for getting a reply has gone up significantly.

Now, I think people want some kind of organic and authentic touchpoint before they buy something, instead of outreach that only appears heavily researched. Everyone knows that doing that research from publicly available sources has also become much easier now.

Jevons paradox keeps showing up again and again.

When something becomes easy to produce, you have to work much harder to make it worth responding to.
