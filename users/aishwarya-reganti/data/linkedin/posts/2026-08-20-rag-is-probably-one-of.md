RAG is probably one of the most evolved applied AI concepts since its inception. It’s still incredibly important, but what it means to build RAG in 2026 has changed dramatically.

A lot of the content I still see online is stuck on papers published years ago, architectures that are no longer representative of what strong teams are building, and assumptions that just don’t hold anymore.

I did a deep dive in this video on what it actually means to build RAG in 2026, how companies are approaching it in production, and the million dollar question: what do people actually mean when they say “RAG is dead”?

A lot of what people call dead hasn’t disappeared. It’s evolved into much richer retrieval and knowledge systems.

This, IMO, is one of the deepest videos on RAG you'll find on YouTube, going well beyond the typical 101s and into what teams are actually building today, with insights straight from the field.
