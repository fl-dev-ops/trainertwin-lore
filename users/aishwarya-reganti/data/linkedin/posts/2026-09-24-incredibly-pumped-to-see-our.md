💯 Incredibly pumped to see our Harness Engineering course being received so well!

There’s enough evidence now that the harness can matter more than the model for complex AI applications. There’s still very little practical material on how to design one well.

Kiriti Badam and I decided to put together our advanced Maven course on Harness Engineering fairly last-minute because we wanted to create something rigorous and hands-on.

A lot of the course came directly from Kiriti’s experience building and operating these systems at the frontier, so it was pretty much learning from the horse’s mouth.

The course covers:
- How to read a harness as a set of design decisions
- Building an agent harness from scratch
- Context management and compaction
- Tools, skills, subagents, and browser use
- Permissions, sandboxing, and human approvals
- Verification, observability, and failure recovery
- Customizing an open-source harness for a specific use case

The assignments had students build their own harnesses and implement things like compaction and browser use themselves.

I’m super happy to see the outcomes and how excited students are about the course. It’s also rated 4.9/5, which speaks volumes about the quality and the impact it’s having.
It’s been so impactful that we’ll be teaching it at the Andreessen Horowitz Academy next year too!

We’re not running another live cohort right now. We’ll probably have another iteration in 2027. 
Until then, the self-paced course is included in our Full Stack AI Bundle.
