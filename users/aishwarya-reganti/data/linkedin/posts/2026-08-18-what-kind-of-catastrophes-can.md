What kind of catastrophes can AI generated videos create in the future? This paper is a pretty good taste of it. And also a bit of an scary 😅 

The paper basically studies how convincing AI generated videos can become when they’re anchored to real events, like wars, disasters, protests, or political incidents, rather than generated from scratch.

Approach:
- Instead of generating arbitrary fake scenes, they start with footage from real wars, disasters, political events, public health crises, accidents, and other consequential events. They then take a real frame and generate what supposedly happens next. The dataset has 17,886 videos in all
- They test both humans and AI detectors on the dataset, and finds that both struggle, especially with videos from stronger closed models and videos conditioned on real frames.
- Detector models include open-source and frontier models  like GPT 5.5, Gemini 3.1, Qwen 3.5 and more!
- They then simulate how those videos would actually spread online (think social media viral clips), with compression, lower resolution, lower frame rates, and news style overlays, and finds that detection performance drops even more dramatically. 

Deepfakes are going to get a *hell* lot better 🫠
