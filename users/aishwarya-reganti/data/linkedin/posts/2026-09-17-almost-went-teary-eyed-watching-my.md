Almost went teary-eyed watching my TEDx talk on AI Generated Misinformation.

My TEDx talk was about misinformation in the age of AI: how easy it is to shape people’s thoughts and emotions with synthetic data, and make people see what they *want to see*. 

I gave the talk almost 2 years ago now and so much of it has aged the way I predicted.  I’m incredibly optimistic about the progress AI has made, but I can also see the impact it will have on our minds.

This snippet (below video) is something that will stay with me forever: the choice is still ours, but it’s getting harder and harder to trust what we see. 

I think we’ll probably sail through this, but we're way far in the AI generation era now and it'll only get harder from here.
