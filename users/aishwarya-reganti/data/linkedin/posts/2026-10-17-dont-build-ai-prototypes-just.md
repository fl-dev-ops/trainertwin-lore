"Don’t build AI prototypes just for the heck of it. Build thoughtfully, starting with the problem you’re trying to solve." has never been more true!

Kiriti Badam & I have been saying this for the past two years, and I think 2026 has made it even more important. 

There’s so much more we can build now, and it’s easy to get pulled into trying every new tool without being clear on why you’re using it.

So pumped that we are bringing those lessons into our last Problem First AI cohort of 2026 (starting tomorrow)!

We’ve updated a big part of the syllabus, with more on multimodal AI, designing deep agents and building harnesses. These updates are grounded in our own experience building these systems. We’ll work through when these approaches are useful, what problems they solve and how to build with them thoughtfully.

Teaching this course has given me so much joy over the past two years. We’re so pumped to get back into it and welcome our newest members into the community. It’s that time again!
