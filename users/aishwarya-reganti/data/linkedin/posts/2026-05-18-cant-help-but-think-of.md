😇 Can’t help but think of my favorite quote from 3 Idiots when I read notes like this. If you’re an Indian millennial, I’m sure you remember it:

“Excellence ka peecha karo, success jhak marke tumhare peeche aayegi.”

For anyone who doesn’t understand Hindi, it roughly means:
Pursue excellence in what you do, and success will eventually follow.

This was a submission one of our students made for the mid course takeaways in our Maven course.

For us, the goal will always be to deliver excellence and give it our 100%. Hopefully, success follows, but that’ll always be the side effect, not the main goal.

When someone sees that effort and reflects it back like this, it genuinely makes my entire week!!
