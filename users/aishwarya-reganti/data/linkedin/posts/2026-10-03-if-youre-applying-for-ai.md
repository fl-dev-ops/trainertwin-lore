If you’re applying for AI roles in 2026, memorizing RAG, agents, and evals is not enough to get you through the system design round.

You have to frame the problem, make tradeoffs, and explain why you made each decision.

After several months of hiring and watching candidates struggle with this, we created a completely free AI System Design Interview handbook with 10 real-world cases.

Each case includes:

- a PM/strategist version, focused on user needs, product decisions, risks, and success metrics
- an engineering version, focused on architecture, components, data flows, evaluation, and technical tradeoffs
- follow-up questions and scaling decisions
- code on GitHub

The cases include customer support agents, enterprise knowledge assistants, Text-to-SQL, underwriting, security and compliance, coding agents, voice support, SDR agents, and clinical scribes.

If you’re interviewing for an AI PM, AI Engineer, FDE, or AI Strategist role, the goal is to give you a repeatable way to work through the system design round instead of guessing what a strong answer should include.
