😅 5 easy sentences to help you spot AI influencers and call BS. 

⛳ Don’t be left behind. Do X!
Classic fear based marketing. Usually light on substance, heavy on panic.

⛳ The top 0.1% of AI experts do X.
There’s no way on earth you know who the top 0.1% are, what they’re doing, and whether it applies to everyone else.

⛳ I replaced my entire team with agents.
Then when people ask for actual workflows, code, failures, costs, or lessons, there’s suddenly nothing useful to share.

⛳ RAG is dead. Context engineering is dead. You’re dead. I’m dead.
No need to explain this.

⛳ Here’s the roadmap to become a $900K AI engineer.
And then it’s a generic list ChatGPT could’ve written in 20 seconds, followed by “comment AI to get it.”

Please do yourself a favor and ask for better if you see these!
