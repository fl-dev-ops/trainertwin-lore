🤔 How are leading AI teams at AWS, Cursor, Uber, etc. designing evaluations for production agents? Here are the 7 golden rules for agent evaluations, straight from those building them.

Arize AI recently hosted their Observe conference, which the LevelUp Labs team attended. It was an excellent opportunity to stay updated with the latest from some of the strongest products and teams in AI evaluations and to hear directly from them about best practices.

Here are the seven key learnings:

⛳ You cannot test your way to safety; instrument production and keep domain experts close enough to catch what the tests miss.
⛳ An evaluation you do not trust is not an evaluation, and evaluations lose trust when the effort to keep them current is neglected, not when the tools fall short.
⛳ Build evaluations around how the system breaks, not around the model, because a better model does not fix structural failures.
⛳ The most durable fixes are organizational, and they may slow you down before they speed you up.
⛳ None of it works if the agent cannot access the current context.
⛳ The moment agents observe your fleet, you must also monitor those agents.
⛳ The harness, not the model, determines what a fleet remembers and reuses.
