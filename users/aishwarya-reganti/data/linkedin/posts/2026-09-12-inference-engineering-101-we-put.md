Inference Engineering 101

We put together an article on Substack and this quick explainer on how to think about inference engineering for AI agents.

We walk through measuring task cost, deciding how much reasoning to allow, routing work across models, and figuring out where extra compute or overlapping tool calls can help.

A lower price per token can still lead to a more expensive agent run. Thinking tokens, repeated context and extra turns all affect what it costs to finish a task.
