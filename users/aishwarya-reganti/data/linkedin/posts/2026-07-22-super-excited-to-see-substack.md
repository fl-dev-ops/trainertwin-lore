Super excited to see Substack shipping AI detection using Pangram, but I’m also concerned about how easy these systems are to game.

For context, I took an article generated entirely by Claude and asked it to keep revising the piece based on Pangram’s score. Within 4 attempts, I was able to bring the score down significantly.

That makes me wonder how much this actually helps vs. simply adding another step to the AI generation process. 

This has always been the pattern. Detection gets better, then generation adapts to it.

I still think this is a strong initiative, but I’d take the scores with a grain of salt. 

They can be useful signals, but they’re far from definitive, and more so as folks start gaming it!
