😇 If you've always wanted to understand reinforcement learning, but assumed it was too technical, this article and cheat sheet are for you.


Reinforcement learning is going to shape the next wave of AI. It'll influence how systems are built, how self improvement loops are designed, and how models learn from context over time.

The problem is that a lot of RL content is extremely mathematical, which makes it hard for people working at the applied layer to build real intuition around it.

That's why Prahitha Movva hosted a session for our internal Chai and AI community, think of it as an "RL 101 for busy people", and this article is based on that session.


We covered all the concepts in the cheat sheet, but we also drew a mind map so the ideas don't feel like a scattered list of terms. The goal is to help you see how the pieces connect.

Check it out if you've been wanting to learn reinforcement learning for a while, but have felt like it's too much. It can be explained in a way that actually makes sense, even without going deep into the math.
