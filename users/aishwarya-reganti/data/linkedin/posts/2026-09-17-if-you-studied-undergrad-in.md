If you studied undergrad in India, couldn't make it to the IITs, IISc, or other top universities, but still never lost the zeal to keep learning from some of the best professors out there, NPTEL should ring a bell 😅

I've always been such a huge fan, and recently landed on this gem from IISc Bangalore: a lecture on pedagogy in the age of AI. Although the lecture is actually about so much more than just pedagogy.

The professor starts with something that I think most of us are battling right now: we're going through a civilizational crisis because of how much AI can already do. Most of us are probably not sleeping very well 😅

He talks about how execution will matter much less, and how judgment will start to matter much more. 

He talks about how learning itself will change over the next few years, and how we may also go through an identity crisis as intellect starts losing some of the status and value that we've historically attached to it.

He gets into inequality, purpose, status, identity, education, and what happens to humans when intelligence itself becomes much cheaper.

Something I really loved was how candid the whole thing was. The man goes on for almost 50 minutes straight, completely scriptless, just talking and throwing out idea after idea. Can only imagine the amount of knowledge sitting in his head.

Best thing I saw on the internet today!
