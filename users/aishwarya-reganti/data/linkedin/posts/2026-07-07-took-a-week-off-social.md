😇 Took a week off social media and AI to go to Iceland on a camper van trip and celebrate a year of starting my company. Probably the best week I’ve had in a bit.

Building LevelUp Labs from the ground up has been one of the most meaningful journeys of my life. But I also think it sometimes takes away one very underrated joy of being an employee, which is the ability to switch off, forget about the outside world for a bit, and just exist outside your work.

For an entire week, my husband and I drove through Iceland living in a camper van. Every day was basically waking up, driving, stopping at some ridiculously beautiful place, figuring out where to park for the night, cooking, sleeping, and doing it all over again. 

And somehow just that grind (and not having the time to think about anything else) gave me more energy to come back and continue work than any productivity system ever has.

I don’t want to turn this into a “7 lessons from living in a camper van” post 🫠 but, the biggest thing I came back with was this: don’t take yourself so seriously.

The world is beautiful and strange and wonderful, even when you’re not paying attention to it. Nothing massive happened that I absolutely had to witness. Nothing I did was so important that the world couldn’t continue without me.

And just that thought it incredibly freeing! It made me feel like I can keep building, keep creating, keep sharing the things I care about, and keep playing the game without making myself the center of the universe.

I came back with a lot more energy, but also with a little more distance from my own seriousness!
