🤗 What an amazing Sunday with a group of incredible AI builders, good coffee, and conversations!

We had another Bay Area cohort meetup from our Maven community, and this one again was fun, relaxed, and full of great conversation.

We usually host in person meetups in the Bay Area after each Maven cohort ends. It’s a chance for us to connect with our community, learn from them, hear what they’re building, and understand how AI is showing up in their real work.

We do weekly community AI sessions online too, but the in person ones are always my favorite. There’s something very different about the kind of open conversation that happens when people meet face to face. 

You get to really see people, hear their stories, and understand what they’re excited about.
We talked about everything from the latest in AI, the trends people are seeing inside enterprises, how learning is changing in the age of AI, which companies are leading the way, and so much more.

We’ve now taught more than 3,000 people through our courses, and so many of them continue to stay close to the community, whether through meetups, weekly workshops we conduct for them, or our Chai and AI sessions where we talk about what’s changing in AI and what it means for builders and more!

I’m so proud of what this community has become and can't wait to meet many more amazing builders and leaders through the process!
