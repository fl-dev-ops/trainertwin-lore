😅 While everyone’s talking about individual AI model releases, think about it: the last 2 months have pretty much been a coding agent moment for 2 entirely new areas.

Graphics and computer/browser use. 

Both existed before, but suddenly the quality has crossed a threshold.

- With graphics, first Astra and now Opus 5.5, you can go from an idea to an actual explainer, motion graphic, interactive visual, presentation, website, or even a 3D scene without doing nearly as much stitching yourself.
- Computer & browser use has had a similar jump. Muse, Astra, Grok Bot Opus 5.5 and others have gotten so good at navigating browsers, filling forms, moving across apps, handling email, bookings, etc. that I find myself touching the computer less and less for a lot of work.

I’m obviously super excited about how much AI can do, but there’s also a weird second order effect here. 

- If everyone can generate more graphics and videos, we’re probably going to overload our eyes very quickly and a lot of this stuff will become much less interesting to watch.
- And if agents are increasingly the ones finding and using products for us, discoverability changes too. You’re not just building websites and applications for humans to look at anymore. You’re increasingly building them for agents to find, understand, and operate.

Scary place to be living in. 😅
