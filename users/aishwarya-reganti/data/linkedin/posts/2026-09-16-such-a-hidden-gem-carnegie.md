🫡 Such a hidden gem! Carnegie Mellon's AI Agents Fall 2026 course is being published on YouTube for free, and it’s taught by one of my favorite professors Graham Neubig. Definitely check it out.

I took his Neural Networks for NLP course during my time there and thoroughly enjoyed his teaching style!

This course is taught by him and Daniel Fried and they are releasing weekly lectures from the Fall 2026 version of AI Agents at CMU. 

I think this is a great way to understand AI agents from a more academic perspective, while also going a little deeper into LLMs and the foundations behind a lot of what we’re building today.

It’s still being released, with only 4 lectures out so far, and each lecture is around an hour. Pretty nice thing to follow along with every week.

I’m following it too!
