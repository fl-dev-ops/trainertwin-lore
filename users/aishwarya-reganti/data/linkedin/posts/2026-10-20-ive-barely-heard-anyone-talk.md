🫠 I've barely heard anyone talk about this open source model online, but it seems like almost every enterprise I speak to is already deploying it.

NVIDIA Nemotron.

I've spoken to nearly 5 or 6 ELT/SLT folks at large public and private companies over the last few weeks, and I keep hearing the same story.

- Most of them are super worried about token costs and frontier model lock in.
- Companies are looking to increase CapEx over OpEx, given tight budgets. Buying GPUs is CapEx, while API costs are OpEx, and this can make a difference to how companies plan their spending. So Nvdia GPUs FTW
- Chinese models are good, but there are lots of internal policies and concerns around even hosting them in house.
- NVIDIA Nemotron might not be the best model out there (but NVIDIA ecosystem anyway), but it's turning out to be one of the best suited for enterprises exploring open models. They're already buying GPUs from NVIDIA and are part of its ecosystem.
- Gemma and OpenAI's open weight models are being used too, but I'm hearing about them perhaps 10% as often as Nemotron. Some companies are exploring newer offerings from players like Thinking Machines, but that's still a very small percentage.


NVIDIA seems to be capturing so much of the distribution of open models, and its Hugging Face acquisition only helps strengthen that.

A few takeaways for me, and maybe for you if you're thinking about where AI is headed:
- Open models are here to stay, for all sorts of reasons, including cost, control, and flexibility.
- NVIDIA is already ahead of the curve when it comes to distribution, not just compute.
- Fine tuning might just be back all over again. Remember how 6 months ago we all thought it was dead?

Will be monitoring this space!
