😅 In 2026 and beyond, I don’t think the biggest advantage will be access to AI. It’ll be the ability to not get distracted by everything AI makes possible.

Most people will want too many things, thanks to AI and social media, but not want one thing badly enough to bet their sweat, sleep, attention, and obsession on making it happen.

You see social media and suddenly you want to be a content creator, a business owner, a VP, a founder, a speaker, a writer, an investor, and also get promoted at work.

AI makes this feel even more possible because everyone online looks like they’ve automated everything and are casually sitting on top of empires.

But once you actually go deep into any one of these paths, you realize just how hard the last mile still is.

The next decade will belong to people who can look at everything they could do, pick what they really want, and pursue it with unreasonable focus and obsession.
