😅 There’s so much hype about the next decade creating a wave of single person billion dollar companies. I think that will be extremely rare in practice for one simple reason:

You don't know what you don't know and AI can't always help

AI gives one person a ridiculous amount of leverage. I believe that deeply. But leverage is not the same as judgment. AI can help you execute faster, write faster, research faster, build faster, and analyze faster. 

😅 But it can’t magically give you the context you don’t even realize you’re missing.

As I’ve started building LevelUp Labs as an AI-Native company and scaling slowly, I’ve become much more thoughtful about where we actually need people. 

Some roles are not just execution roles. They’re shaped by taste, judgment, past experience, and pattern recognition. Without those people in the room, it’s not that AI can’t help. It’s that I may not even know what to ask AI.

A simple example is assessing a company’s AI readiness and providing them with an actionable report. On the surface, it sounds like a technical exercise. 

But in reality, it depends a lot on the business. You need to understand how large enterprises actually work, where decisions get stuck, which teams need to be involved, what incentives are at play, what data access really looks like, and what questions reveal the real bottlenecks.

Of course I can go to ChatGPT or Claude today and ask for an AI readiness assessment. But if I’m thinking only like a tech lead and not like a business executive that has lived in through situations, I may miss the most important questions entirely.

I do understand that the number of people needed to do the same work will absolutely reduce. But you still need people who bring different kinds of judgment, context, and taste to the table.

That’s also how I’ve started thinking about hiring. The question is not just, “How many people do we need?” 

It’s, “How many people do we need who can help us see what we don’t know, versus how many people are just repeating work we already understand?” 

AI can probably help more in the latter than the former
