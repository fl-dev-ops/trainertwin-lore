🤓 This repo turns technical books into skills you can use with your coding agent. Pretty cool if you want to reference a book while you're working through something in Claude Code.

It's called book-to-skill. You give it a book or a collection of documents, and it organizes the material into a skill with separate chapter files.

A few things:
- It extracts frameworks, decision rules, and anti-patterns from the material, so you can look up how to approach a problem.
- The agent can load the relevant chapter when you ask about a topic, without loading the whole book into context every time.
- It also creates a glossary, patterns, and quick-reference tables you can refer back to.
