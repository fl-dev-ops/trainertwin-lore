🤦‍♀️ I get it, Jev is being overhyped, nothing new there. But more annoying are the know it alls dismissing it with, “oh oh, this is just BERT” or “classification models have existed forever.”

The interesting part isn’t that someone invented classification again. It’s the way they’ve packaged general intelligence, structured decisions, and different degrees of model usage into something that makes people question whether they really need an expensive frontier model for every single task.

Maybe this whole thing is transient. Maybe frontier models eat it eventually. Totally possible.

But it’s always much easier to say “I knew this already” after someone else actually thought about it differently, built it, and got everyone talking about it.
