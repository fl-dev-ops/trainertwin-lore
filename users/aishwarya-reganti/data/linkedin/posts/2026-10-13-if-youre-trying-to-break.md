If you’re trying to break into AI engineering without much experience, build projects that reflect what companies are actually building right now.  Don't just throw keywords like RAG, MCP et.al in you resume and build starter projects.

I've interviewed way too many AI engineers over the past year and I see that so much of what they have written on their resume is outdated and not even relevant to the kind of projects companies are working on right now 

So I put together 6 projects I wish I saw more people build. A lot of them come from the kinds of engagements we’ve worked on and the use cases companies are actually building:

1. Document intelligence
2. Enterprise search
3. Conversation intelligence
4. A sovereign AI engine
5. A multi-step support agent
6. A project built around a company you want to work for

I get into what each project involves, the decisions you’ll need to make, and how to talk about your work in an interview. I also share datasets you can use, so you don’t have to complain that there’s no real-world data. 😅
