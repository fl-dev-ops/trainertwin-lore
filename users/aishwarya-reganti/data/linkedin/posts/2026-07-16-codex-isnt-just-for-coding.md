⛳ Codex isn't just for coding, and boy, it's an absolute beast when you know how to set it up properly and build an operating system around it.

We’ve migrated a huge number of workflows across our company to Codex because the limits are generous, it works well for automations, and it can handle all kinds of knowledge work, not just code.

Most of the content out there is still a rundown of features. It tells you what Codex can do, but not how to combine those features into a useful system, which use cases it’s actually good for, or how to set it up so it becomes part of the way you work.

You'll learn how to:
• Build a knowledge base that gives Codex real context about your work
• Organize documents into a searchable AI wiki instead of scattered files
• Connect Codex to your email, calendar, Canva, Linear, and other tools
• Turn repetitive prompts into reusable AI skills and plugins
• Automate recurring workflows with AI agents and goal-based automation
• Use evaluations (evals) to improve AI quality before outputs reach you
• Build feedback loops that make your AI workflows smarter over time
• Run multiple AI threads in parallel to compare and improve results
• Stay in control while safely connecting AI to your business systems
• Create an AI operating system that works with Codex, and any future AI tool
