😅 Having a "casual" conversation with anyone in SF is so dang hard!

Why? Everyone is in founder-mode all the time.

😅 You: How are you?
🤓 SF bro/sis: I’m doing great! My company just raised X million in funding and we’re changing the way (one of these: insurance, health tech, logistics, legal, finance, and basically human civilization) works with AI agents.

😅You: Wow, nice. I love the weather here, it’s great.
🤓SF bro/sis: Yeah, it’s amazing. One of the reasons so many founders move here. The density of talent plus the weather is kind of an unfair advantage, honestly.

😅You: Anyway, I was thinking of going for a walk.
🤓SF bro/sis: Love that. Founder walks are so useful man! Some of the best startup ideas happen while walking.

😅You: Hmm, I’m not really looking to get an idea, but I’d love to meet more people around here.
🤓SF bro/sis: Pretty smart. You’re already meeting people to build distribution while in stealth. What a move.

😅You: That’s a sad way to describe meetups.
🤓SF bro/sis: Agree, but only if you haven’t found the right community led growth loop.

😅Please live a little and let live a little, SF.
