⛳ This is probably the most nuanced and extensive article you’ll read on the context graphs discourse and what companies should be doing today.

Now that the term has made the rounds ( due to the Foundation Capital piece) and the dust has settled a bit, the more useful question is: what should companies actually do with this?

Rip out what exists? Carve out a new layer? Build something completely new?

We had an internal Chai & AI session curated by Jacob Chacko on exactly this, and wrote this article as a summary piece.

- The piece breaks down three paths startups can take: rip out the incumbent system, carve out the decision layer, or build something completely new.
Rip out is bold but brutal because enterprise switching costs are very real. 
- Carve out feels like the most practical near term path. Build new might be the biggest prize, but the market has to catch up.
- Incumbents have scale but the wrong architecture. Startups may have the right architecture but no scale. The company that gets both is where the real lock in might come from.
- There’s also a wildcard people don’t talk about enough: model providers.
If OpenAI or Anthropic sit in the reasoning loop, they may be closer to the decision traces than both the incumbent system and the startup trying to build on top of it. That doesn’t mean they automatically win. Data sovereignty, neutrality, and vertical depth still matter. But it does make the race much more interesting.
