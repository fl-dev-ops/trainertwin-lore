🫨 Did you know that Stanford has a super dense 9-lecture course on the Economics of the AI Supercycle? It goes deep into the AI and GPU economy, AI factories and data centers, the supply and demand of AI tokens, inference, enterprise AI, and more.

h/t to Madhav Bhatia from our community for telling me about it when we met in SF a few weeks ago for our in-person meetup. All the more reason to meet people IRL. 😅

The course moves through the stack layer by layer:
- The economics of generative AI and the GPU economy.
- How AI factories and data centers get built at gigawatt scale.
- Enterprise AI, internal knowledge, and “service as software.”
- The supply and demand of AI tokens, inference, and the companies building the inference cloud.
- The application layer, including coding tools, the future of software, and AI for life sciences.

Nice resource for anyone who wants to understand why so many different businesses exist around the same underlying models, where the constraints come from, and how each layer captures value.

Watched just the first lecture and was blown at the density of the content.
