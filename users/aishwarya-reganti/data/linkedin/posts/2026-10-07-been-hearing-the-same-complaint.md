🫠 Been hearing the same complaint from a bunch of big tech friends off late: corporate politics has become more painful and nastier in the AI era.

Pre AI, being good at execution was appreciated to some extent, even if you didn't know how to play the politics game very well. There simply weren't enough people who could execute incredibly well and fast, even if they weren't always the loudest ones.

Now, that seems to matter less (and will continue to)

The game has shifted more toward folks who are good at selling, getting leadership's attention, knowing how to position their work, doing scope grabs, getting into the right rooms, getting ahead of the narrative, and all the other corporate tricks we're already aware of.

Looks like one unexpected side effect of execution becoming less expensive is that the builders who weren't naturally political, but were actually really good at delivering, might have fewer chances now.
