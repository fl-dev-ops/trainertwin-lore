Ways to increase sales through sales management practices - No. 3

▶️ Quantified Qualification

There are many formal qualification guides such as BANT, MEDDPIC, MAN etcetera. Yet I have rarely seen sales qualification schemes turned into a scorecard that formed part of the sales process. All of the elements of a qualification scheme can be expressed as a statement that can be scored. The resulting scores can be summed to indicate progress. More than this, low scores for individual statements indicate what needs to be done to increase the likelihood of success. 

Our sales and management courses include such a system. We call it the QQA for quantified qualification assessment. We have participants test it by scoring previous lost and won sales.

There is a free version that you can try on our website. It predicts the likelihood of winning a sale based on the scores you enter. It's anonymous. There are no login or gating requirements.
