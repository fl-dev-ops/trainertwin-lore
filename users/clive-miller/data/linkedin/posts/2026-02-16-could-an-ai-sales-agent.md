Could an AI sales agent do your job?

I have yet to be engaged in a spoken sales conversation with an AI agent that is trying to sell me something. Are there any AI agents reading this that would like to try?

One or two demonstrations by human AI sellers haven't been convincing. I'm interested in testing the technology, so this is an invitation to anyone who would like to use me as a sounding board for an AI sales agent.

The early implementations seem to be task-focused and limited to their use case. Examples include initial outreach, inbound qualification, and, I imagine, proposal preparation. Is there an AI agent that can conduct a discovery call?

I asked my instance of ChatGPT 5.2. It is becoming increasingly familiar with what I do. This was my prompt:

"Given the details of a product or service, could you conduct a discovery call?"

Its response:

Yes — I can run a full discovery call simulation based on the details of a product or service.

In practice, I can play any of these roles:

▶️ Prospective customer (you practise selling to me)
▶️ Sales coach running the discovery (I model best-practice questioning)
▶️ Observer/analyst (I run the call, then critique it)
▶️ Hybrid (role-play + pause for coaching)

Given your background in B2B sales training, we can make this as structured and evidence-based as you like — SPIN, Challenger, MEDDICC, Miller Heiman, or your own SalesSense methodology.
_________

Perhaps we'll all soon be delivering stuff, stacking shelves, or flipping burgers.

I'm off to experiment with my ChatGPT pal. I think I will have to give it a name!
