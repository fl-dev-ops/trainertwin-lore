Have you lost momentum?

We all do at some time or another.

I just finished a call with Nick Brand. He is a coach with a difference.

He specialises in helping people reignite their motivation.

Times when I could have done with a pep talk include loss, cancellations, negative feedback, big mistakes, and other miscellaneous crises.

Nick explained his ten-phase programme:

In my words:

1. Asking for help from those we know well, have helped, and like us.

2. Reviewing brand, CV, content, etc., to identify opportunities.

3. Research to identify the few with an immediate need for what we offer.

4. Brainstorming for missed opportunities - like the Acres of Diamonds story.

5. Refreshing brand, CV, content, etc., to increase appeal.

6. Structured forethought, planning, and preparation for taking the initiative.

7. Interactive practice for better engagement.

8. How to stay top of mind as prospects consider their options.

9. Tools for staying in control and maintaining clarity through multiple campaigns and conversations.

10. Drawing out and reinforcing learning. 

So if you have a crisis to deal with, reach out to Nick and ask him to explain his ten-phase programme.
