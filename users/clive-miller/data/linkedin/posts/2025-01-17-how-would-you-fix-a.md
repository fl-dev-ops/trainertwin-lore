How would you fix a sales crisis provoked by any of the following situations?

▶️ A sales forecast that significantly over-predicts results.

Stakeholders can get very upset when sales fall far short of expectations, and over-provisioning has repercussions throughout the organisation.

▶️ A sales forecast that regularly underpredicts results against the target.

Spending plans reflect the expected sales. Predicting significant underperformance usually means everyone has to cut their spending.

▶️ Sales staff churn.

Salespeople leave or are fired when they can't succeed, don't like the culture, or don't like their manager. These occurrences are usually the result of a hiring mistake. The cost of a hiring mistake can be as much as ten times the remuneration of the salesperson concerned when you include the value of missed opportunities. Ask, if you would like the calculations.

▶️ An emptying pipeline.

When the supply of real sales opportunities runs dry, it heralds an imminent sales shortfall.

▶️ Inappropriate or manipulative sales behaviour.

This can take many forms such as lying to customers, gaming the compensation plan, coercing employees, sandbagging, and poaching.
