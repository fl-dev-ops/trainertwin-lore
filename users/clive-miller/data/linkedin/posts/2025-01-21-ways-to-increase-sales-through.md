Ways to increase sales through sales management practices.

▶️ Deal Reviews

This is something that all sales managers do to find out what is happening with important sales opportunities that have yet to close. Yet without a commonly understood framework it can be a hit and miss process. Salespeople are highly motivated to defend their position, previous declarations, and forecast commitments. There credibility is at stake. Without a commonly understood process that uses stages and stage exit criteria, such reviews tend to be an exchange of opinions. Sales managers who work with a detailed sales process that is aligned with buying stages and defines criteria for progress, can discuss the facts. The less the wriggle room, the more productive a deal review can be.

When deal reviews are authentic and candid, the manager learns the real situation and the salesperson's attention is drawn to opportunities and vulnerabilities that can be acted on to increase the likelihood of success.
