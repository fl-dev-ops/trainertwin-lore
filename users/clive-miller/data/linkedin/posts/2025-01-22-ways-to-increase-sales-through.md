Ways to increase sales through sales management practices - No. 2

▶️ Coaching

Most sales managers are aware of their responsibility for developing team capabilities and often try to serve this purpose through deal reviews. Discussing how to win a sale often presents opportunities for coaching.

Unfortunately, the aim of coaching - the improvement of skills, methods, and knowledge - may conflict with a managers priority of ensuring the sales target is achieved. Most people learn by doing rather than being told what to do. I hear and I forget. I see and I remember. I do and I understand. A coach helps people to try things, ideally in a safe environment. A manager needs to get things done. Coaching requires patience. Management requires action. The time set aside for deal reviews is quickly swallowed up by situation reporting, forecast justification, and the discussion of operational issues.

Sales managers who are revered for their coaching activities are rare.

The press of daily, weekly, monthly, quarterly priorities get in the way.
