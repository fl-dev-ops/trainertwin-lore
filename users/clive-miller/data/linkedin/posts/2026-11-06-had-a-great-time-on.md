Had a great time on the @Consulting Rainmakers Podcast hosted by @Mikulas Zach recently.  

Three things I said on the show.  

1. The number one thing for most organisations is finding new business leads, new opportunities, and filling the pipeline. That's why we get inundated by emails from people offering to do it for us.  

2. The challenge of a complex sale is this. It is like trying to get two herds of cats on the same page at the same time.

3. Sales is about helping people do what they want to do. If that sounds odd. Why don't people do what they want to do anyway? How many of the people you know are doing exactly what they want to do?
