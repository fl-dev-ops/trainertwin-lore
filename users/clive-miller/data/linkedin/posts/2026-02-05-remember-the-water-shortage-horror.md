Remember the water shortage horror stories from a few months ago?

It is nothing new to have a wet winter in the UK and this year's deluge is a reassuring reminder.

Resovoirs are a good analogy for our individual reserves of resources like health, wealth, and interesting opportunities.

Sometimes they are drained and things look bleak. It is wise to replenshish our resovioirs in times of plenty yet this is an easy thing to defer. Times of plenty often seem more like recovering the status quo.

It is so much easier to live a litle higher on the hog rather than put something away for leaner times.

My wife and I are rewatching Game of Thrones. It is old now but still hugely entertaining. We see something we hadn't previously noticed in almost every scene. If you have watched the series you may remember the often repeated phrase, "winter is comming".

There is so much we can do to prepare for the future without spending money.  The world is brimming with guidence on health improvement not to mention the endless opportunities to make our money, whatever we have, work for us rather than allowing it to leak away. Then there is learning.

Buttresses against future hardship or disaster lie in what we know, our physical and mental reserves, as well as in our bank balance.

Unlike money, ability and health cannot easily be stripped from us by events.

Now I am wondering if I wrote this to inspire or to voice a warning. Do you ever have to say things to find out what is on your mind?
