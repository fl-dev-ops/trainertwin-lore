The noise about AI is overwhelming. Could it be just a new way to sell businesses more tools?

Don't get me wrong. I use AI regularly and have been doing so for two years. I pay the ChatGPT fee. It helps me with content creation, tech support, writing software, creating images, and getting more intelligent answers to general queries that I would have previously explored with Google.

Like CRM systems, AI has proved hugely useful and continues to get better, yet like CRM systems, could we be trying to use AI as a crutch?

Shouldn't salespeople be able to find their own leads and start a business conversation by themselves?

I wonder if relying on an AI to do this work is making us weaker.

Does anyone remember the Mekon? The bad guy in the Eagle comic from the 1960's. They had huge heads and wasted bodies. If we lean on AI too much, soon we won't be able to do any sales prospecting for ourselves.

Perhaps I am just showing my age. What do you think?
