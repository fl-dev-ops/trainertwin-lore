Your team checked your mood before they checked their inbox.
You didn't say a word.

Think of a boss whose mood you read before approaching them. You adjusted your tone, your timing, even whether you spoke at all.

Research backs up what you felt. In a controlled study, when leaders were in a positive mood rather than a negative one, group members felt more positive and less negative.

The same study found that groups with positive-mood leaders showed more coordination and expended less effort.

The leader's mood travels faster than the leader's message.

This isn't about forced positivity. It's about not leaving your mood to chance:
➡️ Name it. "I'm tense about a deadline. It's not about you."
➡️ Reset before you walk in. One minute in the corridor changes the room.
➡️ Open with something that's working before you open with what isn't.

What's the first thing you do before walking into a tough meeting?
