They told us it was just a desert. 🇦🇪

In the UAE, we are surrounded by a living masterclass in persistence. We walk among skyscrapers that were once just sketches in the sand.

But here is what people forget: Between the "dream" and the "skyline" was a decade of dust, heat, and relentless struggle.

Ideas are abundant in a city as vibrant as Dubai or Abu Dhabi. But success isn't found in the idea; it’s found in the "Long Middle."

The reality of the grind here:

The Noise: Staying focused when everyone else is chasing the "next big thing."

The Resistance: Hearing "it can't be done" and choosing to believe in the vision anyway.

The Departure: Seeing others leave the field when the summer heat of the journey gets too intense.

In this region, we don't just "hustle" - we steward a vision. The people who win in this market aren't necessarily those with the most funding; they are the ones who stay in the game when the novelty fades. They are the ones who understand that "Impossible" is just a dare.

If you are building something right now in the Emirates - don't stop. The desert doesn't bloom overnight, but when it does, it changes the world.
