I still remember the first time I stood in front of a room full of strangers, tasked with giving a presentation. My hands shook, my voice wavered, and I could feel every pair of eyes on me. By the end of it, I was certain—I had failed.  

But here’s the twist.  

After the session, an older colleague came up to me and said, 'You were nervous, but you were real. That’s what mattered.' That moment sparked a journey.  

I realized that communication isn’t about perfection, it’s about connection. Over the years, I’ve learned and taught how to master soft skills - skills that don’t just build careers but also relationships.  

Today, I work with final-year students stepping into the professional world and working professionals seeking growth.
