I used to feel guilty for "distancing" myself from people.

I thought being a #kind person meant giving everyone unlimited access to my time, my energy, and my headspace. But I wasn't being kind - I was being stretched thin, and my growth was stalling.

Then I heard a perspective that changed everything:

"Accept people the way they are, but place them where they belong. You are the CEO of your life, so hire, fire, and promote accordingly."

This isn’t about being "ruthless." It’s about alignment.

In life, just like in any high-performing organization, not everyone belongs in the boardroom.

The #Promotions: These are the people who fuel your peace, challenge your perspective, and root for your wins. They get the #Executive access.

The #Reassignments: These are people you love deeply, but whose current energy doesn't align with your goals. You can love them from the #lobby while keeping your inner office quiet.

The #Departures: Sometimes, #firing someone from your life is the kindest thing you can do for both parties. It stops the resentment and allows you both to find where you truly belong.

The #Lesson: True kindness is accepting someone exactly as they are without trying to fix them. True growth is realizing you don't have to keep them in your inner circle while they do it.

Growth isn't just about who you add to your life, it's about how much peace you keep in it.

Who is someone that has earned a #promotion in your life recently? Let's celebrate them in the comment below 👇
