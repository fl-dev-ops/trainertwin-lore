How important are you?


Anyone can talk when they have time, but the ones who truly care will make time for you. 

If someone values you, they won’t just fit you in when it’s convenient, they’ll prioritize you, no matter how busy life gets. 

Actions always speak louder than words. If someone never makes time for you, maybe you were never a priority to them. 

Don’t chase, don’t beg, and don’t settle for being an option. Know your worth and surround yourself with people who genuinely appreciate you.
