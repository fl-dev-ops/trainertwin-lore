Your first impression isn’t your résumé. It’s your presence.

In White Collar, Neal Caffrey walks into a room where nobody knows him. No track record, no reputation, no credibility to lean on. Just one shot to prove he belongs.

One conversation. One presentation. One pitch. That’s sometimes all it takes to change the direction of your entire career.
