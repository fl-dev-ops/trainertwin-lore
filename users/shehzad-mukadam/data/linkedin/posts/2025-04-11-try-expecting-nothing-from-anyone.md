“Try expecting nothing from anyone, and you'll see how easy it becomes to maintain relationships.”

It hurts the most when the people we care about don’t show up the way we hoped they would.
That’s the weight of expectations, and it quietly damages even the closest bonds.

With time, you learn that peace sometimes means letting go... not of the people, but of the hope that they’ll be different.
You stop overthinking. You stop needing. And in that silence, you find a strange kind of calm.

This is what emotional healing looks like.
It's the heavy part of self-awareness, when you finally accept that how to maintain relationships is not about holding on tighter, but letting go softer.

You don't stop caring.
You just stop expecting.
And that’s how you survive.
