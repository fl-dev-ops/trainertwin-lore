What if I told you that the more AI we use, the more HUMAN your leadership needs to become?

It sounds like a paradox, but I’m living it every day.

Whether I’m wearing my hat as a Founder in the L&D space or sitting down as a Partner in our industrial equipment manufacturing business, the challenge is exactly the same.

Right now, my teams are leveraging #AI for about 80% of their HEAVY LIFTING. In our L&D projects, the data crunching and curriculum scaffolding that used to take weeks now happen in minutes. At the plant, our supply chain and predictive maintenance are practically on autopilot.

We are faster than we’ve ever been. But as a leader, I’ve realized something:
Efficiency is a commodity. Purpose is the new premium.

When the DOING is automated, the MEANING starts to leak out of the room. If I’m not careful, my team starts to feel like they’re just babysitting algorithms rather than using their expertise.

I’ve had to stop leading the process and start leading the person.

I don't check in to see if the work is DONE, the AI tells me that. I check in to see if the SPARK is still there. In manufacturing, it’s about making sure the team still feels like craftsmen, not just data-checkers. In L&D, it’s about ensuring our consultants are true catalysts for change, not just PROMPT EDITORS.

The AI is the engine, but you are the soul of the business. And in 2026, the soul is the only thing that isn't for sale.

If your tech is doing 80% of the work, what are you doing with the other 20%?
