Nobody tells you leadership gets lonelier as you get better at it. 

In my 18 years of career, I’ve led teams for more than a decade. I felt this loneliness myself. 

You hold the decisions. You absorb the pressure. You can’t vent to the people you lead, and you can’t always tell your boss how heavy it feels.

So most leaders carry it quietly. I did too.

Three things that help, none of them complicated: 
➡️ One peer outside your company who also leads people
➡️ One person who tells you the truth, not what you want to hear
➡️ 20 minutes a week to write down what you handled and what it cost you

Feeling the weight doesn’t mean you’re doing it wrong. It usually means you care how your decisions land on people.

You don’t have to carry it alone, but you do have to choose who you share it with.

Who’s the one person you can be honest with about leading?
