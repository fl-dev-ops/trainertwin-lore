I was in a boardroom in Dubai. The client had a broken team. Late deliverables. Missed targets. Blame flying everywhere.

Their first question to me was: "Shehzad, which process should we fix first?"

I told them something they didn't expect.

"None."

The room went quiet.

Because the real problem wasn't the process. It was that the wrong people were running it.

A brilliant process in the wrong hands is just a document. A simple process in the right hands becomes a result.

That day I wrote something on their whiteboard.

"Fix the people. The process fixes itself."

Three months later, they called me back. Not to fix another process. To thank me.

The team had hit their targets for the first time in two years.

Nothing about the process had changed. Everything about the people had.

If your team is underperforming — before you redesign the workflow, look at who's running it.
That's where the real work begins.
