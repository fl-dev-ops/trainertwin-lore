The hardest pill to swallow in any relationships.

Let’s be honest, reading this stings a little, doesn't it?

We have all been there - staring at a phone, inventing elaborate excuses for someone else’s silence. 
✅ Maybe they are overwhelmed at work 
✅ Maybe they didn't see it.

As a Life Coach, I am here to gently shake you awake to a crucial truth: We make time for what we value.

Yes, life gets chaotic. Genuine emergencies happen. But a pattern of being "too busy" to offer a basic reply isn't a scheduling conflict; it’s a statement of priority.

I want you to practice mindfulness regarding the actions of the people in your life.

Shift your focus away from their words and apologies, and place it squarely on their behavior. When you stop making excuses for the lack of effort others show you, you gain incredible clarity.

Today’s Mindful Action:
Take an honest look at your relationships (romantic, platonic, friendship or professional). Where are you investing high energy and receiving low effort in return? It’s time to stop chasing and start observing. You deserve to be a priority, not an option for when the "busy" dies down.

Respect yourself enough to accept the behavior you are seeing as the truth.
