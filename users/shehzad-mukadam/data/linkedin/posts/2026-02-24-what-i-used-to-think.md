What I used to think killed team performance: Lack of skills or experience.

What I know now kills it faster:

Not giving clear expectations.

When managers fail to define what success looks like, teams struggle with:

✅ Disengaging because they don't see the impact
✅ Second-guessing every decision they make
✅ Burning out from constant course corrections
✅ Feeling anxious about their performance
✅ Redoing work that missed the mark
✅ Wasting time on low-priority tasks
✅ Losing confidence in their abilities

The result?

Your best people start looking for the exit.

Clear expectations mean:

→ Setting measurable outcomes
→ Defining what "done" looks like
→ Communicating priorities regularly
→ Explaining the why behind the work
→ Checking understanding, not just agreement

I've seen talented teams fall apart under unclear direction.

And I've seen average teams outperform expectations when they know exactly what's expected.

Your team wants to succeed. They want to deliver great work.

They want to make you proud.

But they can't hit a target they can't see.

So give them clarity  -  it's one of the most powerful tools you have as a manager.
