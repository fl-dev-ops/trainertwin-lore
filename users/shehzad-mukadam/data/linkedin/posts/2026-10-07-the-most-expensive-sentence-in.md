The most expensive sentence in Dubai sales:

I know how this client thinks.

I heard it, in action, while training a real estate team here.

One of the advisors went into a call completely sure of himself. He had the client figured out before the client said a word.

He hadn't. 

He missed how the client preferred to communicate, how he made decisions, and what he needed to feel safe. He pushed his own style on the call.

The client went quiet. Then he stopped replying.Heres the part that stays with me: the client wasnt a time-waster. He was a real buyer with real money.

He bought a property. Just not from us. He bought it from an advisor he trusted.

The advisor didnt lose a client because of the property, the price or the market. He lost him in the first few minutes, when the client felt assumed instead of understood.

Now I teach three questions that replace assumptions:
1️⃣ What matters most to you in this decision?
2️⃣ Who else would you like involved in this conversation?
3️⃣ Would you like to move fast, or step by step?

Your next buyer could be anyone, #Emirati. #Indian. #Russian. #British. #Egyptian. #Filipino... 

Do not assume, ask questions to understand and build trust. 

Stereotypes close doors. Curiosity opens them.
