There comes a time when you realize not everyone deserves your kindness. 

It’s not about changing who you are, being a good person is a strength, not a weakness. 

But it’s about being intentional with your energy and who you choose to support. 

From now on, my kindness is for those who value it, not those who take it for granted. It’s a small shift, but one that makes all the difference. 

Let’s focus on the people and relationships that truly matter.
