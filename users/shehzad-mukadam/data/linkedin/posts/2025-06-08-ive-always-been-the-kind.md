I’ve always been the kind of person who feels deeply.

When someone I care about is going through a hard time, I don’t always have the right words. I may go quiet.
But that silence doesn’t mean distance... it means I feel it with you.

Sometimes, we don’t need to fix everything.
We just need to be present.
To hold space.
To care - quietly but powerfully.

If you’ve ever felt this way, know this: your gentle heart is not a weakness. It’s a gift. 🫶
