Why the best employees stop caring over time...? 

People don't lose motivation. The structure they work inside removes it. 

Peter lays out two things that have nothing to do with attitude. 

First, no upside. Extra effort ships extra units and none of that reaches him. The gain flows one way, so effort is a donation. 

Second, eight bosses. One mistake means eight separate corrections. Downside is multiplied by eight while upside stays at zero. 

That's the whole equation. When the only motivation left is avoiding hassle, the rational output is exactly enough not to get fired. He isn't failing at the job. He's optimizing correctly for the incentives he was given. 

Fix the payoff or the reporting lines. Nothing else moves the number.

Tell me a job where the efforts and the reward were connected.
