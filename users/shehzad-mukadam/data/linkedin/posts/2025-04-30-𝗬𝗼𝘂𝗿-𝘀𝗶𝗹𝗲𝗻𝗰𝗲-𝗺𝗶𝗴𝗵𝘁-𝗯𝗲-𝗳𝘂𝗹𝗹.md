You might be trying to show you care…
By checking in, doing little things, always being there.

"I care."
"I'm proud of you."
"I'm here for you."

Sometimes, it's not about what you do... it's about what you say.
Because the truth is, people can't read minds… and love and care shouldn't be a guessing game.
