Power-Pleasing Won’t Build Your Career!

In the early years of your career, it’s tempting to impress the big names at the top. But if your success depends on pleasing those in power rather than building real skills and authentic relationships, you might be setting yourself up for a short-term win, long-term loss.

🔹 Does your value depend on who’s watching?
🔹 Are you respected for your work or just liked for compliance?
🔹 What happens when the leadership changes?

Real success comes from competence, credibility, and connections at all levels, not just from keeping the bosses happy.
