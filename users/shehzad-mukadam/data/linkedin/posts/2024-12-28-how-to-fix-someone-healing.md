How to fix someone...


Healing is a journey of self-discovery and growth, where we learn to let go of what hurts and embrace what nurtures us. The book How to Stop Breaking Your Own Heart by Megan Roxanne,  beautifully highlights ways to stop self-sabotage and create space for genuine happiness. 

Here are some key lessons from the book—feel free to reach out if you’re seeking guidance or simply need someone to talk to.

✅ Firstly, you can't. You can't fix anyone. The best you can do is inspire them by fixing yourself.

✅ Healing is a personal journey. Offer guidance, not control.

✅ Your role is not to fix, but to hold space for their growth.

✅ True change comes when someone chooses it for themselves.

✅ Don't mistake over-giving for helping. It often enables more than it heals.

✅ Empathy is powerful but boundaries are essential.

✅ Trying to fix someone often comes from a fear of facing your own wounds.

✅ Offer support, not solutions. They must find their own path.

✅ Healing someone else can never come at the cost of losing yourself.

✅ Sometimes the most loving thing you can do is let go.

✅ You are not responsible for anyone else's healing, only your own.
