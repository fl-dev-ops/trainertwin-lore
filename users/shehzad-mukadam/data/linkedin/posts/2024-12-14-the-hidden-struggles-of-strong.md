The Hidden Struggles of Strong People 💔

Strong people often suffer in silence. At night, they battle their thoughts alone. During the day, they put on smiles to hide the pain. They share only half the story with friends and keep everything from their parents just to avoid worrying them.  

If you’re one of those strong souls, remember it’s okay to lean on someone. You don’t have to carry it all alone.
