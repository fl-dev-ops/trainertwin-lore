Your title is a mask, and your team is waiting for you to take it off.

We’re taught that executive leadership means having all the answers. That showing doubt is a #weakness. That professionalism is a synonym for #robotic.

It’s a lie. And it’s why your best people are leaving.

In 2026, the most expensive mistake you can make is being unreachable. Efficiency doesn't build loyalty, vulnerability does. When you sit at the top, the air is thin - but you don’t have to freeze out your culture to stay there.

I spent years trying to be the perfect leader. It was exhausting, and it was ineffective. The moment I started saying I don't know or I need your help, the room finally started breathing again.

Stop managing tasks. Start leading humans.
