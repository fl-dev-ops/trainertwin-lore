You trained the team. Three weeks later, nothing has changed. The team isn’t the problem.

A widely cited finding says that without reinforcement, people lose about half of new information within an hour, 70% within 24 hours and 90% within a week. 

The same source notes that sales managers are often left out of driving the training initiative, even though they’re the people best placed to reinforce it. 

A reinforcement plan I’d ask for before any training budget is approved:
→ Before: brief the manager on what will be taught and what to look for.
→ Within 48 hours: each person picks one behavior to practice.
→ Weekly: the manager runs a 15-minute coaching conversation on that behavior.
→ Day 30: review the numbers and adjust one thing.

Ask your next training vendor one question: “What happens after day 2?”

Source: Training Industry, “The Weakest Link in Sales Training Reinforcement.”
