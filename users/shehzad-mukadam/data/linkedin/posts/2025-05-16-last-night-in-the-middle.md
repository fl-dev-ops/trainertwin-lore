Last night, in the middle of a quiet conversation with a friend… I had a moment of clarity.

We were talking about life — the messiness, the unpredictability, the constant change — and I said this out loud without even realizing how deeply it hit:

“Just when you find the answer… life changes the question.”

And it’s true, isn’t it?

You think you've figured something out — your purpose, your path, your peace — and then #boom, everything shifts. Again.

But maybe life isn’t a problem to be solved.
Maybe it’s a mystery to be lived.

What if the goal isn’t to have all the answers... but to build the strength to keep showing up — even when the questions change?

So if you're feeling lost, tired, or stuck — you're not alone. You're just in the middle of a plot twist. And plot twists make the story worth telling.

➡️ Breathe. Trust the process. Keep going.
Your next chapter might just be your best one yet. 💫
