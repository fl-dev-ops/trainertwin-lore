Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Raheem

On this blessed month of Ramadan, especially as we seek Laylatul Qadr—the Night of Power—I pray from the depths of my heart for all my loved ones: my family, my friends, and every soul connected to mine.

🤲 Ya Allah... Ya Rabb,

- Bless everyone with good health, inner peace, and hearts full of gratitude.
 
- Protect our relationships, fill them with love, understanding, and barakah.

- Ease every hardship, wipe away worries, and replace them with hope.

- Forgive our mistakes, guide us closer to You, and accept our efforts.

- Shower us with Your mercy in this world and the next. Ameen!

May our bonds grow stronger in faith, our laughter be frequent, and our prayers for each other never cease. Keep us under Your protection always. 🤍  

Ameen, ya Rabb!
