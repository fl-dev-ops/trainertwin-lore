I spent 15+ years thinking my job was to have all the answers. I was wrong. In 2026, the hardest part of leadership isn't managing your AI agents... it’s managing the INVISIBLE TAX on your soul.

71% of leaders are QUIETLY CRACKING right now.
The market is volatile. The tech is moving faster than our biology.
And the pressure to be Always-On is a debt we can no longer pay.

I recently sat with a CEO who told me: "I have 200+ employees, but I've never felt more alone."

We’ve optimized for efficiency, but we’ve starved ourselves of HUMAN-CENTRIC LEADERSHIP. If you want to lead in 2026, stop focusing on PRODUCTIVITY METRICS and start focusing on Emotional Regulation.

Here is what I’ve learned from the leaders who are actually winning (and staying sane):

1️⃣ The 90/10 Rule: Spend 90% of your time on human connection; let AI handle the 10% of operational noise.

2️⃣ Radical Simplification: If a process doesn't directly serve your team's mental health or the customer's value, kill it.

3️⃣ Vulnerability is the New ROI: The most engaged teams today aren't the ones with the best tech; they’re the ones with the most PSYCHOLOGICAL SAFETY.

AI can draft your strategy. It can’t look your team in the eye and say, "I see you, and we’ll figure this out together."

We are entering a LEADERSHIP RESET. What is the one thing you’ve stopped doing this year to protect your team’s (and your own) energy?
