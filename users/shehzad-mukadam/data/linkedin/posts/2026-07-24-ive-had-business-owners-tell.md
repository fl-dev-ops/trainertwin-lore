I've had business owners tell me "we tried training before, nothing changed" — right before they hired me anyway.

Here's why: they weren't paying for a workshop the second time. They were paying for someone who wouldn't leave the room until their team could actually handle a real objection, not a scripted one.

One real estate team I worked with in Dubai had consultants who were great talkers but kept losing deals they should've won. Not because they lacked charisma — because they couldn't read the client in front of them or hold their ground when things got uncomfortable.

Months later, they're still messaging me. Not because the training didn't work. Because it did — and now it's just how they sell.

So is sales training worth the money?

Wrong question. The real one is: are you buying an event, or an upgrade to your team that doesn't wear off?
