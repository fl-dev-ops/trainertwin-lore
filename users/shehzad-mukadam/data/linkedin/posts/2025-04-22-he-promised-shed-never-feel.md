He promised she'd never feel alone.

Said, “We’re a team.” Said, “You matter.”

But as his world grew louder -
She became background noise.

Her messages got “seen.”
Her efforts got “later.”

And one day, the person who once asked how her day was…
Just stopped caring.

No fights. No fallouts. Just slow erasure.

She stayed silent, hoping he’d notice.
He didn’t.

Time didn’t change her.
It just revealed that she was the only one still holding on.

So she let go - not in anger, but in peace.
Because true friendship never makes you feel like a stranger in someone’s life.
