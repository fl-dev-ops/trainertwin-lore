People change. Some for convenience.
Some because of insecurity.
Some simply because that’s who they are.

That’s why your private life deserves protection.
Not everyone needs access to your story,
no matter how close they feel today.

Emotions are powerful.
In the wrong hands, they become leverage.

Share wisely.
Trust slowly.
Protect your peace.
