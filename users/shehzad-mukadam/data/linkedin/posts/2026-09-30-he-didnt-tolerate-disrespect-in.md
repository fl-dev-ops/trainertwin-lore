He didn’t tolerate disrespect.

In this deleted scene from Burnt, Adam Jones notices something most leaders ignore.

A team member is pushed to tears —because of a disrespectful customer.

And he steps in immediately.

Most people say “the customer is always right.”

Strong leaders know that’s not always true.

Because once you tolerate disrespect from the outside,
it weakens everything on the inside.

Real lesson:
Leadership isn’t just about results.

It’s about protecting your people and the standards you expect.
