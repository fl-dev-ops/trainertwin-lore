Today hit different....
.
.
A close friend of mine, more like a brother - is quitting his job. While talking, he said something simple but powerful: "All the talks we had and all the time we spent will now become memories."

That moment froze me.

I started thinking… Have I been giving more of my time to people just around me, and not enough to those truly there for me?

He has always been there - not just present, but listening, understanding, and holding space for me, especially on the days I couldn’t even hold space for myself.

It hurts to see him go, but I know this isn’t the end.

Some bonds don’t break with distance or time.

And I’ll forever be grateful for the quiet strength he brought into my life.

Until we meet again, brother Saddam H. Malik (Syed Malik). Thank you - for everything.
