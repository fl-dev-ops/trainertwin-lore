AI can write your sales email.
  
It can't build the relationship.
  
Over the last few months, I've watched sales teams in the UAE rush to adopt AI tools... and most of them are using AI to do the wrong 20% of the job faster.
  
Here's what AI is genuinely good at in sales:
  
→ Researching a prospect in seconds → Drafting the first version of an email → Summarising a call → Building a proposal template → Spotting patterns in pipeline data
  
Here's what it still can't do:
  
→ Read the hesitation in a buyer's voice → Know when to stop pitching and start listening → Handle an objection that's really about trust, not price → Build the kind of relationship that survives a bad quarter → Close on judgment, not just data
  
I've trained enough sales teams to know the pattern: the reps who struggle most with AI aren't the ones who refuse to use it. They're the ones using it as a replacement for the human skill, instead of a shortcut to free up time for it.
  
The best salespeople I've worked with are doing the opposite. They let AI handle the admin... and they reinvest that saved time into the parts of the job a machine will never do: curiosity, patience, and reading people.
  
That's not a soft skill. That's the actual skill.
  
If your sales team has AI tools but revenue hasn't moved, the tool isn't the problem.
  
The gap is human.
  
What's your team using AI for right now and is it freeing up time for selling, or just replacing the thinking part of selling?
