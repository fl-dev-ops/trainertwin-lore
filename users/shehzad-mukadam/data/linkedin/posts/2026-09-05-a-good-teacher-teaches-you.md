A good teacher teaches you. A great teacher changes you.

Years later, you may forget the chapters, formulas and assignments.

But you remember the teacher who saw something in you when you couldn't see it yourself.

The teacher who encouraged you when you wanted to give up.

The teacher who pushed you when you were capable of more.

The teacher who made you believe, I can do this.

That's the real impact of a teacher.

Not just knowledge.

Confidence. Character. Courage.

To every teacher, mentor, trainer and coach who has helped someone become a better version of themselves...

Happy Teacher's Day. ❤️

Your impact is often much bigger than you will ever know.
