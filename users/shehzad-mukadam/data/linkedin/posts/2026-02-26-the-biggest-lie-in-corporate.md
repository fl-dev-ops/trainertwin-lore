The biggest lie in corporate training is this: if people enjoyed the session, they learned something.

#HR feels it. #Founders question it. L&D professionals wrestle with it.

The room was energetic. People participated. The feedback forms were positive.

But a week later… nothing changed.

After spending more than a decade in training rooms across industries, I’ve learned a simple truth: adults don’t change through information. They change through experience.

Slides don’t create growth. Experience does.

The real shift happens in role plays. When someone struggles through a sales conversation. When a manager fails to handle a difficult team member. When a frontline employee realizes their tone is pushing customers away instead of building trust.

And in that moment, with honest feedback and reflection, awareness turns into ownership.

That’s when behavior changes.

Not when they say, “This was a great session.”
But when their manager says, “Something is different about how they show up now.”

Because real learning doesn’t live in the training room. It shows up in performance.

If you are an HR leader, founder, or business leader looking to upskill your teams in #Sales, #CustomerService, or #LeadershipDevelopment for your mid-level managers, let’s connect. The focus isn’t on delivering training. It’s on creating measurable behavior change where it matters most.

What has created more lasting change in your teams: information or real experience?
