Stop hiring for "Experience." Start hiring for "Adaptability Quotient" (AQ).

I’m seeing a dangerous trend in 2026 leadership. We are still hiring for what people did in 2020.

But the world changed. AI changed. The "expert" playbook is being rewritten every Tuesday.

If your leadership style is still "Command and Control," you aren't leading - you’re just managing a slow exit.

Tomorrow’s leaders don’t need to be the smartest person in the room. They need to be the most adaptable.

Stop looking for "years of experience." Start looking for AQ (Adaptability Quotient).

AQ is the ability to unlearn a habit the moment it stops working. It’s the humility to say, "I don't know the answer yet, let's find it."

In 2026, the best "skill" isn't a degree. It's a high tolerance for being a beginner again.
