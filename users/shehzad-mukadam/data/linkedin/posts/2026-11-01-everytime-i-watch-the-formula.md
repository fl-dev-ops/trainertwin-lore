Everytime I watch the Formula 1 movie with Brad Pitt - I never stop thinking about it. 

Yes, it's about racing. But more than that, it's a reminder of what it really takes to win in business, leadership, and life: 

💡 Clear direction. 
🤝 Strong teams. 
🧠 Resilience under pressure. 
🎯 Purpose-driven performance. 

One thing that stood out to me most? No one wins alone. 

You might see the driver on the podium, but behind every lap is a team - engineers, strategists, pit crew, support staff - all working in sync to deliver that moment.

It reminded me a lot of leading a business and building something with others. 

It's the late nights, the decisions no one sees, the people who show up quietly and consistently that's where the real wins are built. 

If you're in a leadership role, or growing something from the ground up - I genuinely recommend watching it. Even better, take your team. 

3 quotes that stuck with me: 
1⃣ Legends aren't born on the track; they're made in the moments no one sees.
2⃣ Drive with purpose, or don't drive at all.
3⃣ The best leaders know when to accelerate -and when to step back and let others drive.
