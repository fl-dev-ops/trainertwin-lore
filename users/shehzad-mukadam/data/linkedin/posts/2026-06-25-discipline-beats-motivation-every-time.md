Discipline beats motivation every time.

A few months ago, I had a week where everything felt off.

Low energy. Zero drive. Full of excuses.

I remember sitting down and thinking, “I’ll do it when I feel better.”

But then I realized something uncomfortable…
If I only work when I feel like it, I won’t be consistent at all.

So I changed one thing. I stopped negotiating with myself.

No overthinking. No waiting for the “right mood.”
Just action.

And surprisingly, once I started, the motivation came later.

Not before. After.

That’s the part most people miss.

Motivation doesn’t create action. Action creates motivation.
