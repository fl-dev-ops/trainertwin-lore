Your best supervisor just resigned. Not for more money. Because nobody ever showed them what came next.

A logistics firm in Dubai lost three warehouse supervisors in eight months this year. Every exit interview said the same thing: no growth path, no training budget attached to their next role.

This isn't a logistics problem. It's happening in retail stores, hotel front desks, real estate sales floors and FMCG plants across the UAE right now.

Here's what 2026 is teaching us:
→ People don't leave jobs. They leave the absence of a "next."
→ Generic compliance training doesn't retain anyone. Role-specific upskilling does.
→ Managers who coach outperform managers who supervise.
→ AI fluency is now table stakes, not a nice-to-have.

If your L&D strategy still looks like a once-a-year course calendar, you're not building a workforce. You're funding an exit.

The companies winning talent in the UAE right now aren't the ones with the biggest training budgets. They're the ones who tied every dirham of it to a real skills gap, a real career path, and a real manager who checks in.
