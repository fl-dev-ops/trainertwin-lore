When Motivation Meets Tea Break ☕😂
Trainers, tell me I’m not the only one who has had this happen.

They say "be the energy you want to see in the room." So I show up bringing full-on "motivational guru" vibes, ready to change lives…

And the first question I get? “Is there a tea break?” ☕️💀

It’s a humbling reminder that no matter how good the slides are, the coffee break is the real star of the show. 😂😂😂
