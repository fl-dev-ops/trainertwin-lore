The problem isn’t your ability. It’s the weight you’ve been carrying.

Most people aren’t struggling because they lack talent or ambition. They’re struggling because they’ve been carrying pressure silently for too long. Trying to stay strong, meet expectations, and keep moving without ever pausing to ask themselves what they actually need.

Here’s the mindset shift: you don’t have to fix your entire life at once. Start by being honest with yourself about what feels heavy right now. Clarity begins the moment you stop pretending you’re okay and start listening to yourself.
