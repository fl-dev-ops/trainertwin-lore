Stop trying to be perfectly efficient.

In 2026, efficiency is cheap.
AI is efficient. Bots are efficient.

Humans are not.
And that’s the point.

We’re entering the era of the Humanity Premium.

Your real edge isn’t your tech stack or productivity system.
It’s your lived experience.
Especially the parts that still hurt to talk about.

Here’s one of mine.
A year ago, I walked away from the safety of a 9-to-5 to build my own business.
When I announced it, the people I had supported for years were loud with encouragement.
“We’ve got your back.”
“Call us for anything.”

Then the climb began.
Starting a business is isolating. You don’t need applause.
You need emotional support.
You need a lead.
You need someone to answer the phone.
So I reached out.
And hit silence.
“Too busy.”
“In a meeting.”
“Let’s catch up next month.”

Polite responses. Vague excuses.
Professional ghosting.

The hardest part wasn’t doing it alone.
It was watching those same people spend time together online while my messages sat on Read.

I wasn’t just building a business.
I was being quietly abandoned by people I once treated like family.
So I kept going. Alone.

There were nights of total isolation where momentum came from one place only: resolve.
But I made it through.

And when the dust settled, I did something uncomfortable.
I audited my circle.

Here’s what I learned the hard way:
Vulnerability without reciprocity turns you into a free First Aid Kit.
Being human doesn’t mean being available to everyone.
Empathy without boundaries is self-betrayal.
So I chose differently.

I stopped being a utility for people who only show up when they’re bleeding.
I started choosing my inner circle with surgical precision.
Because in an automated world, your circle is your real net worth.

Build your own table.
If someone didn’t help you build it, they don’t get a seat at it.

Guard your energy.
AI can simulate empathy.
Only humans can offer loyalty.

The future of work isn’t about how many people you know.
It’s about who stays in the trenches with you when the efficiency runs out.

Have you ever had to prune your professional circle to find your real tribe?
How did you handle the ghosting when it showed you who was really there?
