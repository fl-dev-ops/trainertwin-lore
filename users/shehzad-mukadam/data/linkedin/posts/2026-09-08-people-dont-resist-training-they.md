PEOPLE DON’T RESIST TRAINING. THEY RESIST FEELING STUPID.

Think about the #employee who stays silent in a workshop because they’re afraid their answer will sound wrong. The #manager who rejects feedback because it feels like criticism. The #salesperson who knows the process but still avoids the difficult customer conversation.

These aren’t always skill gaps. They’re human behaviour.

Great L&D goes beyond transferring knowledge. It creates an environment where people feel safe enough to question, practise, fail, reflect and change.

That’s where real learning begins: when people stop protecting themselves and start developing themselves.

If your training is changing what people KNOW but not how they BEHAVE, it’s time to look deeper.
