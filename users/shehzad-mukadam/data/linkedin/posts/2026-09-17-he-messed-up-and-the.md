He messed up, and the Boss showed him a Trick instead... 

A senior person's smallest reaction sets the standard everyone else copies. 

A junior staffer drops the sodas before a client meeting. Howard Hamlin is the name on the building. He kneels, picks them up with him, gets his name right, and shows him how to stop a shaken can from spraying. 

Total cost, thirty seconds and no status. What he bought is a person who will now tell him when something goes wrong instead of hiding it. 

That's the whole return. In any firm, the expensive failures are the ones people conceal because the reaction to a small one taught them to. 

Nobody remembers the sodas. Everyone remembers who knelt.

Tell me the smallest thing a boss did that you never forgot.
