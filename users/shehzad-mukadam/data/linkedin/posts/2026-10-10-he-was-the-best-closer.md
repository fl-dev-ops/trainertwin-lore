He was the best closer in the company.
So they promoted him.

Months later, after one of my sales trainings, he pulled me aside and admitted he misses being on the floor. His numbers now depend on people who can't all do what he did.

Nobody warned him about that.

He didn't fail as a manager. He was never trained to become one.

We reward results with a title, then judge him on a completely different skill. Closing used to be his job. Now his job is making ten other people close.

That's not a promotion. That's a career change with no onboarding.

Founders and HR leaders: what's your plan for training a top performer before the title changes?
