Had the pleasure of training the Dubai Duty Free team on Armaf Perfume Product & Sales at The Creekside Hotel Dubai as a Dynamo Selling trainer. The room was full of energy, curiosity, and real conversations about fragrance, not just as a product, but as an experience.

What made it special was the way the session came alive.
Role plays. Real customer scenarios. And one simple question that changed the mood instantly:

What memory comes back when you smell a fragrance?

Suddenly, the room wasn’t talking about notes and bottles. People were sharing moments. Occasions. Faces they hadn’t thought about in years. That’s when it clicked for everyone. #Perfume doesn’t live in the bottle. It lives in memory.

Sharing the stage with Ebraheem Al-samadi, Netflix #DubaiBling star and Brand Ambassador for Dubai Bling by Armaf Perfume, was a highlight. His presence, energy, and connection with the team lifted the room even further. We wrapped up with a felicitation ceremony where Ebrahim personally handed certificates to each participant. Smiles everywhere. Exactly how a training day should end.

Huge appreciation to Team Armaf Perfume for the flawless organization, and to The Creekside Hotel Dubai for the warm hospitality and great food.

Because at the end of the day, #ARMAF is not about selling fragrance. It’s about selling memories waiting to happen.

Looking forward to Day 2 on Thursday, 12th Feb 2026 with another batch.
