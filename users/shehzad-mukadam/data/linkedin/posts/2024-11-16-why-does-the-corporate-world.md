Why does the corporate world still see women through a biased lens, focusing on their looks and age before their potential?

It’s unfortunate but true that biases like these still exist in the corporate world.

Judging women by their looks, age, or gender before their abilities is not only unfair but also a huge loss for organizations.

I’ve seen how transformative women leaders can be. In one of my previous teams, a female manager led a project with incredible strategic insight and empathy. Her leadership not only delivered outstanding results but also inspired the entire team to perform better. 

It's high time we stop letting stereotypes hold us back and focus on creating environments where talent and expertise are valued above all else. 

True growth happens when we challenge biases and embrace diversity in leadership.
