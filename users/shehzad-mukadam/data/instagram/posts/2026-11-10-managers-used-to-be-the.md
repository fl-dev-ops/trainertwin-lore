  Managers used to be the most engaged people in the company.
  Not anymore.

  Gallup’s 2026 report finds manager engagement declined from 31% in 2022 to 22% in 2025. Individual contributors sit at 19%. The gap that used to exist has almost closed.

  One summary of the report adds that in South Asia, primarily India, manager engagement dropped eight points in a single year.

  Think about what that means.

  The person who’s meant to hold the team up is running on empty. They absorb pressure from above and questions from below, and almost nobody asks how they are.

  I led teams for more than a decade. I know how that feels.

  If you’re a tired manager, that doesn’t make you a bad one. The data says you’re not alone.
  And it isn’t inevitable. In best-practice organizations, 79% of managers are engaged, nearly four times the global average. Gallup’s advice: prioritize manager selection, training and support.

  Managers: what drains you most, the pressure from above or carrying everyone below?
