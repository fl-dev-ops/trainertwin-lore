Think of the manager who told you something you didn’t want to hear. How long did it take you to say thank you?

At the time, it probably stung. You may have gone home annoyed, or defended yourself, or quietly decided they were wrong.

Then months or years later, you realized they were the only one who was honest with you.
The managers who were easy to like often didn’t change much about you. The one who told you the truth did.

What honest managers gave you, and rarely got credit for:
➡️ They told you early, not at the review
➡️ They criticized the work, not you
➡️ They stayed in the room when it got uncomfortable
