A German manager mentored me for a year. Her advice sounded cold at the time. 👇

In 2008, she gave me three rules:
→ Be familiar with everyone. Don't turn colleagues into family.
→ Be empathetic, but don't be too emotional.
→ Care about people, but don't over-care.

Her reasoning: family at work pushes you to make decisions out of emotion, not judgment. And over-caring makes people expect you'll always be available.

I thought she was wrong. 

I didn't see how right she was until about 15 years later.
