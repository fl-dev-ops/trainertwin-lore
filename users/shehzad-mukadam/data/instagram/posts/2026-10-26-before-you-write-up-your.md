  Before you write up your lowest performer, ask them one question: “What does a great month look like in this role?”

  Then listen to how long they take to answer, and whether the answer matches yours.
  Sometimes the person isn’t failing. They’re guessing.

  In a lot of teams, good lives in the founder’s head, or the previous manager’s, or in a number nobody explained.

  People with unclear targets don’t look lost. They look busy, which is why this goes unnoticed for months.

  Run this test with one team this week:
  ➡️ Ask each person to describe a great month in their own words
  ➡️ Compare the answers with yours
  ➡️ Count the gaps

  If your answers don’t match, you’ve found the problem, and it may not be them.
