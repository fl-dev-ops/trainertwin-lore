She told me this in 2008. I only understood it about 15 years later. 

I forgot her advice.

I cared too much. I was always there, always saying yes.

People didn't see a caring leader. They saw someone they could rely on for anything, at any time. And I was taken advantage of.

That's when I remembered her words. She'd been right the whole time.

Caring for people isn't the same as carrying them.
If you're a new manager:
→ Care about the person, not about being liked
→ Say no without guilt, and explain why
→ Keep one clear line between support and rescue

I wish someone had trained me on this before I needed it.

What's one boundary you wish you'd set earlier? 👇
