speaker_1: People say that time management is a key to success, but here's the truth: time management is a myth. Time doesn't change. It moves at the same speed for everyone. What we actually manage is our personal space, our focus, our energy, our boundaries. Think of this: you have a full day planned— meetings, tasks, deadlines— then one unexpected call comes in. Nothing happened to time. Your space got invaded. High performers don't control the clock, they protect their space. That's where productivity really comes from. So don't try to manage your time, try to manage your personal space. Thank you.
