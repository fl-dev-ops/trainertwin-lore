  1. READING THE REAL CONCERN

  A customer may say, It's too expensive.
  AI can identify the objection.
  A good salesperson discovers what is actually behind it.

  Sometimes it's not price. It's trust.
  Sometimes it's timing.
  Sometimes they simply don't see enough value yet.
- |-
  Shehzad Mukadam

  2. BUILDING GENUINE TRUST

  Customers can tell when they're talking to a template. Personalisation isn't the same as connection.

  You can automate a message.
  You can't automate credibility.
- |-
  Shehzad Mukadam

  3. HANDLING EMOTIONAL OBJECTIONS

  A frustrated customer doesn't always need another perfectly worded response.
  Sometimes they need someone to actually listen.

  Empathy isn't a CRM feature.
- |-
  Shehzad Mukadam

  4. NEGOTIATION

  AI can tell you the market price.
  It can analyse previous deals.
  It can suggest a negotiation strategy.

  But knowing WHEN to push, WHEN to pause and
  WHEN to walk away still requires judgement.
- |-
  Shehzad Mukadam

  5. ASKING THE UNCOMFORTABLE QUESTION

  This is where great salespeople separate themselves.
  What's stopping you from moving forward?
  Is it really the price?
  What would make you comfortable making this decision today?

  AI can suggest the question.
  But courage is still human.
