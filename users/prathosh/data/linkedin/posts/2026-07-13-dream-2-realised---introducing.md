Dream 2, realised - Introducing Vāgbodhini (वाग्बोधिनी)- a personalized tutor for Sanskrit.

1. Input any text (any script) and hear a flawless, metrically precise chant.

2. Chant it back and get instant, syllable-by-syllable phonetic feedback.

Try it out here - https://lnkd.in/g2FFBeMU

As usual, completely open-source. 

Vāgbodhini solves a complex closed-loop audio alignment problem. The pipeline utilizes two custom-tuned frameworks: Vagdhenu, our foundational  TTS engine.  Sushroota,  precision ASR evaluator. 

This is not a replacement for a Guru; it's a pedagogical aid. Please use it only for Non-vedic texts.
