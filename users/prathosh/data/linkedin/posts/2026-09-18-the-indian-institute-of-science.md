The Indian Institute of Science, IIT Goa, IIT Palakkad, and IIIT Bangalore are jointly organizing the 33rd National Conference on Communications (NCC 2027) during 17–20 February 2027 at IISc Bengaluru.

NCC 2027 invites original research contributions in:
🔹 Communications
🔹 Signal Processing
🔹 Networks
🔹 RF and Optical Systems

📝 Paper submission deadline: 16 October 2026
📄 Format: Six pages in the standard IEEE conference format
🔍 Review process: Double-blind peer review

Call for Papers: https://lnkd.in/gr3XwQYq
Paper submission: https://lnkd.in/gCTMPJWS

Researchers are invited to submit their latest work, and request you to share this announcement within your research community.
