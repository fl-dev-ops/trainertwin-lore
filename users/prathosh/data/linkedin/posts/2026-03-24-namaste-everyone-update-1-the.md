10,552 ancient mantras. 4 linguistic scripts. 1 massive data architecture problem.


For centuries, indexing a specific Rig Veda verse by its Rishi, Chandas, or Devata, and mapping that exact text to a specific audio chant, has been incredibly difficult.


As a professor, I wanted to treat our ancient heritage with the engineering rigor it deserves. I didn’t just digitize a book; we built a fully searchable, offline database perfectly mapped 
to cloud-streamed audio.

Meet RigVaaNi (V1) - 

 Under the Hood:
• UI: Native Jetpack Compose for a fluid, state-driven interface.
• Search Engine: Heavily optimized Android Room (SQLite) with Kotlin Flow debouncing for instant, stutter-free querying across 10k+ rows.
• Audio Engine: Media3 (ExoPlayer) handling stream buffering, dynamic speed manipulation, and looping.
• Infra: Cloudflare R2 Edge CDN for globally distributed audio and APK delivery.
It is incredibly fulfilling to use modern mobile architecture to keep this knowledge accessible and organized for the next generation.
