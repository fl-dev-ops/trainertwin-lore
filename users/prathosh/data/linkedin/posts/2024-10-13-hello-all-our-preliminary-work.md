Hello All,

Our (preliminary) work on Unlearning concepts in pre-trained diffusion models is accepted at Neurips Safe Generative AI Workshop 2024.  

This is one of the first papers from our group towards the direction of making AI models safer by imbibing them with the capability of "forgetting" unwanted concepts. This seems to be a very exciting direction of research, especially given the rampant influx of LLMs that hallucinate. 

On the technical front, we use a  Gaussian mean-field approximation on the model parameter space, within a variational inference framework to set up the problem of unlearning in diffusion models. Here is the link for the paper - https://lnkd.in/g7S5NAfw 

Congratulations to my PhD student Subhodip Panda and other interns Varun M S and Shreyans Jain.
