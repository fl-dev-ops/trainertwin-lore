Here is our recent most paper acceptance in the journal Medical Image Analysis (one of the top most ranked journals for AI in healthcare). 

Cycle Consistent Twin Energy-Based Models for Image-to-Image Translation

In this paper, we consider the problem of  domain shift between the training (source) and the testing (target) datasets of a learning task, leading to performance drop.
For tasks involving medical images, domain shift may be caused because of  factors such as change in underlying imaging modalities, measuring devices and staining
mechanisms. We adapt an
alternative class of deep generative models called the Energy-Based Models (EBMs)
for the task of unpaired image-to-image translation of medical images. Specifically, we
propose a novel method called the Cycle Consistent Twin EBMs (CCT-EBM) which
employs a pair of EBMs in the latent space of an Auto-Encoder trained on the source
data. 

This is the work of my students Piyush Tiwary and Kinjawl Bhattacharyya. Congratulations to both. Preprint attached for your perusal.
