Our paper  entitled "Concept Forgetting via Label Annealing" is accepted in the 41st Conference on Uncertainty in Artificial Intelligence (UAI) - 

In this work, we develop techniques for forgetting specific undesired concepts from a pre-trained classification model’s prediction. We present an algorithm called Label ANnealing (LAN) to achieve this goal. This iterative algorithm employs a two-stage method for each iteration. In the first stage, pseudo-labels are assigned to all the samples by annealing or redistributing the original labels based on the model's predictions in the current iteration. During the second stage, the model is fine-tuned on this pseudo-labeled dataset generated from the first stage. We illustrate the effectiveness of the proposed algorithms across various models and datasets. 

This is another effort towards making modern machine learning models free of unwanted biases and making them fair via unlearning and forgetting methods.
