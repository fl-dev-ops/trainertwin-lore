Here we go - the mandatory ICML 26, an A* (premier AIML conference) acceptance post. 

We built a system called Hγ-Net that can automatically discover hidden mathematical symmetries in data, without being told what to look for. 

Think of it like this: if you rotate a snowflake, it looks the same; that's a symmetry. Many physical systems have similar hidden symmetries (rotations, boosts, shears), and knowing them makes predictions far more accurate and efficient. Previous AI models had to be told what symmetry to use upfront. 

Our new framework figures it out on its own, learning both the symmetry and the prediction task simultaneously. It's also fully explainable,  you can point to exactly why the model behaves the way it does, rather than trusting a black box.
