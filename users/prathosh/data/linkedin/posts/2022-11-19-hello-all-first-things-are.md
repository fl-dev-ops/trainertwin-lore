Hello All,

First things are always special - I have opened my account of A* publications as an IISc faculty. 

Our paper on instance-level meta learning for supervised learning with Auxiliary losses has been accepted at AAAI 2023 (one of the oldest and top AI conferences). 

In supervised learning, we often combine multiple loss functions with relative weighting. In this work, we show that meta-learning the loss weights, differently for each data instance, leads to improved performance in multiple tasks. Details can be found here - https://lnkd.in/gpN3WuXb
