Another week. Another exciting announcement. 

We at LatentForce LatentForce (https://lnkd.in/gydsEG2S) are thrilled to announce the closure of our $1.7M seed round, co-led by Ideaspring Capital Ideaspring Capital and Yali Capital Yali Capital both strong believers in deep-tech startups.

This journey has been deeply personal for me. I’ve gone from convincing committees of senior academics about the impact of my work while raising funding for my research lab at IISc, to pitching to a room full of sharks (read: VCs 😄) to invest in our ideas.

The difference is stark:
Metrics shift - from papers and patents to P&L.
Impact measures evolve - from h-index and impact factors to EBITDA.
Strategy changes - from research students to GTM and ICP.
Yet the core remains unchanged: the pursuit of excellence through rigorous innovation to solve real problems.

At LatentForce, we are building an Agentic AI platform for large-scale code migration and software modernisation, powered by task-specific Small Language Models and deterministic, audit-ready pipelines.
Our goal is simple but ambitious: Make high-stakes enterprise transformations faster, safer, and economically viable at a scale that was previously impractical.

Anyone building AI agents knows this pain point well - demos (read: toys) work, but large systems struggle. We are solving this gap precisely.
