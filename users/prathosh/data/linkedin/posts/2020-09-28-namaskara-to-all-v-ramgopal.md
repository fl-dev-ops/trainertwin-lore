Namaskara to All! (V. Ramgopal Rao,MAMIDALA JAGADESH KUMAR, Ramakrishnan A G)


Being a serious student of Sanskrit for several years, I always wanted to do some technical work on it. Finally, started some small work with a couple of interested students. We started with the problem of Sandhi (compound word) creation and Vichhed using Seq-2-Seq models without explicitly coding up Paninian rules. To our surprise, a Deep model could actually learn to create and split Sandhi better than rule-based systems on a few benchmark corpora. This work got accepted in ACM IKDD CODS-COMAD 2021 (preprint attached). I believe that this direction leads to analysis and synthesis of rich textual corpora in Sanskrit literature and one day to a virtual Kalidasa!
