Hope you are all well in this time of crisis. 
I'm posting this to share that one more US patent co-invented by me got granted (Number 10) - https://lnkd.in/g4ynEKs

This work seems relevant in today's scenario. We describe a method to predict the need for a patient to be admitted into an ICU by tracking the progression of body-vitals.

Perhaps a similar approach can be adapted to predict the need for ventilators and other critical care for Covid patients by tracking their vitals. This might lead to effective utilization of the resources.  

Once again, thanks to my previous colleagues at Xerox Research.
