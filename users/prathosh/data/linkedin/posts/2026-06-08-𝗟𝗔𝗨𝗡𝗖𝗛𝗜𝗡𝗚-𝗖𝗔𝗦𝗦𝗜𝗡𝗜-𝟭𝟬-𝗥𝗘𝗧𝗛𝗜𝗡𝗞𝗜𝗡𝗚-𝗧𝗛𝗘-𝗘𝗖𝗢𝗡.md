We (LatentForce) are open-sourcing Cassini-1.0, the first in our family of language-agnostic models designed for agentic context management.

The model can be found on Hugging Face: https://lnkd.in/gQvMJrEv

The current narrative is that agentic reasoning over large code bases is simply a matter of context window expansion. We are seeing that this introduces several trade-offs in latency, context degradation, and inference costs. 

We are exploring a different path to solve this. Rather than feeding raw repositories into a model, Cassini processes complex, polyglot codebases into a structured, highly compressed semantic graph.

The attached evaluation, conducted over a 150-file repository set against a Claude Sonnet 4.6 ground truth, demonstrates that Cassini-1.0 defines the Pareto frontier for codebase context.

By filtering out token noise and mapping structural intent, Cassini-1.0 achieves a 77.6 percent F1 accuracy score at an inference cost of $ 0.17 per task. To match or marginally exceed this accuracy, mainstream long-context models from OpenAI OpenAI and Google Google scale exponentially along a log-cost curve, costing between $ 2.00-$7.00 per evaluation.

As a research-first team with roots in top-tier academic venues like ICLR ICLR and ICML [ICML] Int'l Conference on Machine Learning, our focus is on structural efficiency over token volume. We believe a vendor-agnostic context layer is essential for scalable engineering agents.

Cassini-1.0 is open-source and ready for deployment within your own secure infrastructure. We look forward to your feedback.
