Hi All,

A paper acceptance from our group in the conference Uncertainty in Artificial Intelligence (UAI) 2024. 

"Constructing Bayesian Pseudo-Coresets using Contrastive Divergence"

In this work, we construct a synthetic dataset such that, a model trained independently on this synthetic set, yields the same performance as training on the original training set.

Imagine achieving 95% classification accuracy by only using 10 (synthetically generated) data samples from MNIST. 

Details in the preprint for interested readers - https://lnkd.in/gEZTWXtg

Congratulations to my PhD students - Piyush Tiwari and Shubham Kumar for the work.
