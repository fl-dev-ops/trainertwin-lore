Tenured, with an early promotion at Indian Institute of Science (IISc).

When I heard the news, there was a sense of emptiness. My mind started pondering, “Oh ya, not the destination; it’s the journey.” Then came a Zen saying, “It’s neither the destination nor the journey; it’s the company.”

The Mahabharata is not about five princes ruling a kingdom. It is a tale of self-doubt, denial, struggles, war, and Dharma. The company of magnificent people, from Vedavyasa to Vidura, Bhishma and Drona to the sages, and of course, Yogeshwara Krishna himself, is what makes it an epic. 

Thought of writing a self-note to thank those wonderful souls who built me.

An old man, Sheshagiri Rao, who sculpted me inch by inch. From differential equations to Nyaya/Vedanta, he taught me life for 25 long years. Sadly, he is not with me to witness the sapling he planted and nurtured bearing fruit. 

It was Prof. Yogananda and Ashok Rao who inspired me to take up this profession.

At IISc, Ramakrishnan A G and TV Ananthapadmanabha introduced me to academic research. My teachers, including PS Sastry Chandra Murthy, Chandra Sekhar Seelamantula, instilled mathematical rigour.

I was fortunate to be mentored by some of the best engineering minds - Manish Gupta Geetha Manjunath, Lalit Mestha, Sanjay Bharadwaj, PhD. 

I continue to cherish the mentorship, collaboration, and friendship I developed at Indian Institute of Technology, Delhi- Prof. V Ramgopal Rao, Tapan K.  Gandhi, FNAE, FNASc, Mausam ., Parag, N M Anoop Krishnan Sandeep Kumar Manu Kohli,Ph.D.  

The last five years at Department of ECE, IISc, have been a fabulous adventure. My colleagues, including Dean, Prof. Rajesh, and Chairman Prof. Neelesh, have been incredibly supportive of everything I took up.

Most of my publication credits go to my PhD students, Arnab Mondal, Ayush Tripathi, PhD Amit, Oshin, Piyush Tiwary, Subhodip Panda, Pavan kumar shubham, Karn Tiwari. 

I thank all the students, both in-class and online, with whom I learned every time I walked into the classroom. Special thanks to Chandan Jayaram, my right-hand man. 

I thank all my collaborators from industry Ramya Hebbalaguppe, Amith Singhee Dinesh Garg, Kareem Shaik, Pankaj Dayama, Pradeep Shenoy Manohar Kaul. 

Two of my best students, Aravind Jayendran and Vinay Kyatham, are now my cofounders at LatentForce. Our investors Naganand Doraswamy and Ganapathy Subramaniam trusted me to run a business. 

I deeply acknowledge my mentors: Prof. Ashok Rao, Sudhanva Dhananjaya Ganesh Ramakrishnan, Karthik Raman, Saketha Nath Jagarlapudi,Venugopal Chakravarthy Pawan Goyal, Acharya Veeranarayana Pandurangi, shrinivasa varakhedi, who wish me well selflessly. 

Tenure, they say, is freedom accompanied by increased responsibility. I wish and pray that God gives me enough courage and strength to tread the path of Dharma.

कुर्वन्नेवेह कर्माणि जिजीविषेच्छतं समाः।
