We have a WACV 25, acceptance from the group. 

UnDIVE: Generalized Underwater Video Enhancement Using Generative Priors

We propose a framework for enhancing underwater videos. The first stage uses a denoising diffusion probabilistic model to learn a generative prior from unlabeled data. This prior is incorporated into a physics-based image formulation for spatial enhancement in the second stage. Our method enables real-time and computationally efficient processing of high-resolution underwater videos at lower resolutions and offers efficient enhancement in the presence of diverse water types.

This is a peculiar project that involved an undergrad intern Hemang J Jamadagni, a master's student Aditya C, and a PhD student Suhas Srinath, contributing to the work.
