Hello All,


Excited to announce a new offering from IISc!

We are launching an Online Proficiency Course on Generative AI: Principles and Applications (May–July 2026), designed to bridge the gap between theory and real-world AI systems.

This course dives deep into the foundations and practice of modern generative models — from VAEs and GANs to Diffusion Models and Large Language Models (LLMs) — with a strong emphasis on both mathematical understanding and hands-on implementation.

What you’ll gain:

- A rigorous grounding in probabilistic generative modelling
- Practical experience building models using PyTorch
- Insights into cutting-edge techniques like diffusion, score-based models, and RLHF (PPO/DPO)
- The ability to apply generative AI across diverse domains


Who is this for?

Students, engineers, researchers, and professionals with basic programming knowledge who want to deeply understand and build generative AI systems — not just use them.

This is a course I’m particularly passionate about, as it brings together years of research and teaching into a structured, accessible format.

Duration: May – July 2026

 Mode: Online

 Offered by Centre for Continuing Education, IISc

If you're looking to move beyond surface-level AI and truly understand what powers today’s generative systems, this is for you.
