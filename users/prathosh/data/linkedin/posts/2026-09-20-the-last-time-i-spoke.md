The last time I spoke about this, I did it candidly,  with my students, without a script.
 
That video went unexpectedly viral.
 
This time, I tried to put the same unease into words.
 
AI may not be causing a technology crisis. It may be causing a crisis of identity.
 
We spent centuries making intelligence valuable because it was scarce.
 
What happens when intelligence becomes cheap, abundant, and ubiquitous?
 
What do we value then? My attempt at an answer, in The Times of India:
 
“Why the global panic over AI is not a crisis over technology.”
