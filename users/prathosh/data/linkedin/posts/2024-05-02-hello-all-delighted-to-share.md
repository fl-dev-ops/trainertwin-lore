Hello All,

Delighted to share the acceptance of our paper "WISER: Weak supervISion and supErvised Representation learning to improve drug response prediction in cancer" in ICML 2024 (one of the top most venues for AI/ML). 

In this work, we introduce a novel integration of supervised domain-invariant representation learning with weak supervision and subset selection. This combination is strategically tailored for enhancing drug response predictions in cancer research. 

The approach allows for the effective use of labeled data from one domain (cell lines) to enhance learning in a related but distinct domain (patient-derived data), which is a significant challenge in translational medicine.

This work was done in collaboration with Vaibhav Rajan and Aishwarya Jayagopal  at NUS. 

Congratulations to my PhD student kumar shubham at IISc, Aishwarya Jayagopal from NUS  and my intern Syed Danish.

The paper's preprint (review version) is attached for interested readers.
