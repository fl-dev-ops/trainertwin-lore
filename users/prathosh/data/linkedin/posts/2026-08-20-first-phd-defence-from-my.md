First PhD defence from my lab at Indian Institute of Science (IISc). Even though I had graduated 4 students at Indian Institute of Technology, Delhi, this one is special as it's the first one from the Indian Institute of Science (IISc).

Many Congratulations Piyush Tiwary and wish you well in your career.
