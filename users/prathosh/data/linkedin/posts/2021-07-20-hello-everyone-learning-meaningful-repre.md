Learning meaningful representations lie at the heart of machine learning. This is more so in domains such as healthcare where data is bogged with problems such as sparsity, class imbalance etc. We have been trying to solve some of such problems in our group and here is one outcome of such efforts: 

We propose a method for Clustering Single-cell Gene Expression Data which has multiple applications including cancer gene sequencing and discoveries. Our method, based on learning a flexible prior on the latent space of Regularized Auto Encoders, performs the best on a variety of benchmark datasets.
