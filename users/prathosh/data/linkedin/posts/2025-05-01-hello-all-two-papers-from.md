Hello All,

Two papers from the group are accepted at ICML 25 (the largest and most prestigious international conference for machine learning) 

In the first one, we propose Latent Mamba Operator (LaMO), which integrates the efficiency of state-space models (SSMs) in latent space with the expressive power of kernel integral formulations in neural operators.

In the second, we propose LangDAug, a novel Langevin Data Augmentation for multi-source domain generalization in 2D medical image segmentation. LangDAug leverages Energy-Based Models (EBMs) trained via contrastive divergence to traverse between source domains. 

Both papers have their stories - The first one is the first submission, which got all 4 accepts (an extremely rare occurrence in A* venues). The second one saw the light after multiple rejections. 

Moral of the story - Keep doing/enjoying your work. Outcomes aren't under our control. Anything can happen - life throws both positive and negative surprises. As they say, Karmanyeva AdhikaraH (One can only control/enjoy the process).
