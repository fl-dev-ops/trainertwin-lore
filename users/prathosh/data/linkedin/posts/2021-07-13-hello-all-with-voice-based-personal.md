Hello All,

With voice-based personal agents becoming ubiquitous, it is necessary to detect when a person is speaking for triggering them. 

Recently, we found out, that one can determine the speech activity of a person by observing the breathing pattern - The idea is that the breathing pattern changes significantly during the speech. We have a small paper based on this idea. This is a completely privacy-aware method since we neither hear to a person's voice nor look at their face (for lip-reading). 

This might interest some of you. Check it out.
