Here is our work on Variational Diffusion Unlearning. - https://lnkd.in/gyrBSikt

For a responsible and safe deployment of diffusion models in various domains,
regulating the generated outputs from these models is desirable because such
models could generate undesired, violent, and obscene outputs. To tackle this
problem, recent works use machine unlearning methodology to forget training
data points containing these undesired features from pre-trained generative models.
Our proposed method, termed as Variational
Diffusion Unlearning (VDU), is a computationally efficient method that only
requires access to a subset of training data containing undesired features for Unlearning.
