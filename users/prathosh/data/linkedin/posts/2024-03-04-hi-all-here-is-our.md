Hi All,

Here is our preprint on zero-shot localized multi-object editing via diffusion models. This framework empowers users to perform various operations on objects within an image, such as adding, replacing, or editing *many objects* in a complex scene in *one pass*. Our approach leverages foreground masks and corresponding simple text prompts that exert localized influences on the target regions resulting in high-fidelity image editing.
