Hello All,

Our work (DeGPR: Deep Guided Posterior Regularisation For Multi-Class Cell Detection And Counting) in collaboration with AIIMS and IIT Delhi, on detecting and counting multiple classes (cells and tissues) from histopathological images, got accepted in CVPR 2023  #cvpr2023 (One of the premier conferences on CV/ML). 

The uniqueness of this work lies in the fact that we incorporate the expert interpretations into model buliding as posterior regularizers, to show performance gains. 

At the application level, we are tackling the problem of early detection of Celiac disease which affects wheat eating population of the country. 

This is yet another demonstration for my belief on using problem/data specific priors in deep learning instead of blindly throwing a  deep network to a problem.
