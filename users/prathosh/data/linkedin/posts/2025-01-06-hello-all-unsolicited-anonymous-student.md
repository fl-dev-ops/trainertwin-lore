Here are a few samples -

Prathosh Sir's teaching is extraordinary—his ability to break down complex ideas and connect them to real-world applications is inspiring. I feel lucky and grateful to have taken this course, which has been a life-changing experience for me. Thank you, IISc and Prathosh Sir, for such an unforgettable journey!"
