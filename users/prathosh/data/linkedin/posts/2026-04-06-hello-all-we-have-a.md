Hello All,

We have a paper accepted at ACL 2026, main conference (A premier A* conference for AIML). 

In this work, we present a method called BiMol-Diff, a unified diffusion framework for the paired tasks of text-conditioned molecule generation and molecule captioning. 

Our key contribution is a token-aware noise schedule that assigns position-dependent corruption based on token recovery difficulty, preserving harder-to-recover substructures during the forward process of a text conditioned diffusion model. 

Congratulations to my coauthors - Aditya Shahane Anuj Kumar Sirohi Devansh Arora Nitin Kumar and Sandeep Kumar. 

My association with Indian Institute of Technology, Delhi lives on through these brilliant collaborators. Thank you Sandeep Kumar ji.
