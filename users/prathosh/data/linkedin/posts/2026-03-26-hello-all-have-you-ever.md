Hello All,


Have you ever wondered if LLMs are actually good at navigating code, or did they just memorize GitHub during pre-training? 

We put this to the test at Latentforce AI  LatentForce. 

In our latest study, "Déjà Code," (https://lnkd.in/gjUfyNqK) we measured LLM data contamination by comparing performance on repositories created before and after a model's knowledge cutoff. 

The results highlight a huge challenge for building real-world AI tools: models suffer a 60–80% performance drop on unseen, post-cutoff codebases. They rely heavily on "parametric scaffolding" from training data. So, when you drop an AI agent into a private enterprise repo, it immediately loses its edge.

That’s exactly why we built LatentGraph (https://lnkd.in/gydsEG2S) to move beyond theory and give agents the explicit structural context they need to thrive in completely new, unseen codebases. 

Check out the full study and let me know your thoughts: https://lnkd.in/gjUfyNqK
