Unlearning in Diffusion models under Data Constraints


For a responsible and safe deployment of diffusion models in various domains, regulating
the generated outputs from these models is desirable because such models could generate
undesired, violent, and obscene outputs. To tackle this problem, we  propose a machine unlearning methodology that can prevent
the generation of outputs containing undesired features from a pre-trained diffusion model
in such a data-constrained setting. Our proposed method, termed as Variational Diffusion Unlearning (VDU), is a computationally efficient method that only requires access to a subset of training data containing undesired features. 

Congratulations to all the student Authors Subhodip Panda Sarthak Kumar Maharana Varun M S Shreyans Jain.
