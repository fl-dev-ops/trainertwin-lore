Hello All,

Our work on few-shot Generative Adaptation got accepted in the Journal IEEE Signal Processing Letters (SPL). 

Here, we incorporate a (cute) Bayesian reformulation of GAN objective and use a hyper network to sample in the parameter space for few shot adaptation. 

This paper has an interesting trajectory - Got scooped, rejected multiple times (for reasons such as having student's name in the readme file of our codebase that violated the anonymity), was under review for about 2 years but finally went into a top tier place. We had forgotten about this almost. 

For all the budding researchers and PhD students out there - don't get disheartened by rejections. If *you* think that your idea is novel, finding a set of 3 other folks (aka reviewers) who think the same is an event with a fairly high likelihood, given enough time (with no upper bound guarantees :) 

Keep improving, keep submitting is the mantra that I've realised after playing this game for a decade now. 

*Every paper has its place*
