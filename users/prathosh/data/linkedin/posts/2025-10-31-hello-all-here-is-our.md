Hello All,

Here is our work accepted to Nature portfolio Scientific Reports. 

In this work, we study a weakly supervised
method for Cell segmentation task, where only bounding box supervision is available. We propose BoxCell, a cell segmentation framework that utilizes SAM’s
capability to interpret bounding boxes as prompts, both at train and test times. We experiment on three publicly
available cell segmentation datasets namely, CoNSep, MoNuSeg, and TNBC, and find that BoxCell significantly outperforms
existing box supervised image segmentation models, obtaining 6-10 point Dice gains. 

This is in collaboration with my students Ayush and Vaibhav along with my dear friend Mausam .

Besides, this paper has an incredible story - This got rejected 6 times, all with borderline reviews leading to heartbreaks. But we kept improving, submitting and finally, it is in. 

I have seen a lot of research students getting dejected seeing paper rejections, especially in AI research. Here is a bit of (unsolicited) advice - Keep improving, keep submitting, every paper has a place. I have seen it too many times now. Good papers do get through, eventually. In my 10+ years in this business, there hasn't been a single piece of work that isn't published. So, please protect your mental sanity and keep submitting (it's just a game). 

All the best to all budding researches.
