Latest work from the lab accepted at EMNLP: We propose QK Product Steering, a data-free, training-free, and zero-inference-cost weight edit for reducing object hallucinations in Vision Language Models.  The method directly edits the per-head query-key product by suppressing a small number of dominant singular modes in selected middle layers.

Congratulations to the lead author Karn Tiwari.
