An academic post after quite some time - 

Our work on "Learning Equivariant Functions via Quadratic Forms" is accepted for the Twenty-Ninth Annual Conference on Artificial Intelligence and Statistics (AISTATS 2026). This is a premier conference for theoretical AI-ML. 

In this study, we introduce a method for learning group-equivariant functions by learning the associated quadratic form. Certain groups, known as generalised orthogonal groups, preserve a specific quadratic form, and we leverage this property to uncover the underlying symmetry group under the assumption that it is a generalised orthogonal group.

Applications include the discovery of the underlying data symmetry and efficient learning of the corresponding equivariant function.

This work is led by my PhD student Pavan K Pavan Karjol, with contributions from two brilliant intern brothers Vivek v Kashyap and Rohan V Kashyap. 

Preprint attached for interested readers.
