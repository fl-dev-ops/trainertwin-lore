Namaste All,

I am thrilled to announce that VedaVaaNi: The Voice of the Vedas is officially live on the iOS App Store! 🚀

Bridging the world’s oldest continuous oral tradition with modern engineering has been an incredibly rewarding challenge. What started as an effort to build a digital Guru-Kulam for mastering Vedic chanting has already found a home with thousands of users on Android. Seeing the community use it daily for Parayan, and reading the profound feedback from those who lack a physical Guru but still want to learn—pushed me to bring it to the Apple ecosystem.

(Side note to my fellow developers: battling Xcode provisioning profiles and App Store Connect at 1:00 AM last night was an entirely different kind of tapasya, but seeing it go live today makes it completely worth it!)

For those new to the app, VedaVaaNi is meticulously engineered for highly precise, interactive audio alignment. It features:

✨ Millisecond-Precise Sync: Phrase-by-phrase auto-scrolling that tracks the audio perfectly for hands-free practice. 
✨ Authentic Swaras: Mathematically accurate Vedic tonal marks rendered seamlessly across multiple scripts (Devanagari, Kannada, Telugu, Tamil, and English). 
✨ Massive Library: Access the entirety of the Taittiriya Samhita, Brahmana, Aranyaka, and the Rig Veda Samhita (with dual vocal renditions). 
✨ Dynamic Speed Control: Slow down complex phonetic junctions without distorting the pitch. 
✨ Offline First: Download your Anuvakas once and practice anywhere.

Whether you are an advanced scholar or just beginning your journey into the Śruti, VedaVaaNi is built to help you learn with perfect pronunciation and intonation.

A huge thank you to everyone who supported the initial rollout, left such encouraging 5-star reviews, and pushed for this iOS release.
