The Invaluable ROI of Teaching


They often say academia is a "thankless" profession, but then an email arrives on a Sunday evening that puts everything into perspective. 

I recently received a message from a 3rd-year student at college in Kolkata. He shared a story that is all too common: feeling "disconnected" and "trapped" in a curriculum that prioritized black-box coding over the mathematical beauty he actually loved.

He was nearly ready to give up on his passion until he found our NPTEL course on the Mathematical Foundations of Machine Learning (https://lnkd.in/gJ69bhr5)

What moved me wasn't just his gratitude, but his hunger. He didn't want the shortcuts; he wanted the probabilistic big picture. He wanted the "why" behind the "how." 

In my courses,  I’ve stuck to the "Chalk and Talk" method for a reason. In an era of automated code and instant certificates, we need to build AI Architects, not just model-users. We need to provide the mathematical "muscle memory" that allows a student to see the philosophy behind the function.

Moments like this make every late-night lecture prep and every derivation on the board worth it. It reminds me that our real impact isn't measured in h-indices or funding rounds, but in the ignition of a single mind that was almost lost to the noise.

This is why I teach. This is what makes this profession truly invaluable.
To this person and the 10,000+ others currently on this journey with us: Keep pushing for the first principles. The rigor is the reward.
