Efforts at IITD bearing fruits - One more submission to one of the top conferences in AI/ML - Conference on Uncertainty in Artificial Intelligence (UAI), got accepted. In this work, we deal with an important aspect of latent space optimality in a Generative Auto encoder framework.  It's a common experience that in a VAE/AAE, change in latent dimensionality drastically influences the generation quality. We rigorously study this phenomenon and propose a remedial measure.
