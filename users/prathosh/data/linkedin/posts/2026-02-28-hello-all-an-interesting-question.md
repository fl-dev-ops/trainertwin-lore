Hello All,

An interesting question to ask is - what sort of knowledge tasks do modern day LLMs fail at? 

While we were thinking along those lines, we found one - they are not good at constrained generation. 

Specifically, when you ask them to generate a Shloka in a particular Sanskrit meter (Chandas) corresponding to a particular prose (Not free form), they struggle. 

We tried to address this issue via a constrained decoding approach and show that it improves LLMs poetry generation capabilities for Samskrutam. 

This work is appearing in EACL 2026, entitled "Chandomitram - Towards Generating Structured Sanskrit Poetry from Natural Language Inputs".

This work is a part of the Bhashini project carried within a wonderful consortium involving Indian Institute of Science (IISc) Indian Institute of Technology, Kharagpur Indian Institute of Technology, Kanpur Indian Institute of Technology, Bombay and CDAC Bangalore.
