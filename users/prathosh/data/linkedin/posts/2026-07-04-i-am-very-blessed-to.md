I am very blessed to share Bhāgavata-VāNi, now live on iOS, Android, and the web.

Web app - https:// bhagavatavani.com
Android - https://lnkd.in/ggYVwTCf

IOS - https://lnkd.in/gGxMFGbQ

It is a recitation-first Śrīmad Bhāgavatam app: the complete text (all 12 skandhas, roughly 18000 ślokas, 37 stotras) with synced audio and line-by-line karaoke highlighting, readable in 10 Indic scripts, with all-script
search and four traditional indices (by akṣara, by speaker, by topic, and a word concordance). It works fully offline, and it is free with no ads.

What I am most excited about is under the hood. The recitation is not a recording. It is generated by Vāgdhenu, a vṛtta (meter) aware Sanskrit chant text-to-speech system I built (prathosh.in/vagdhenu)

Text courtesy of the Poornaprajna Saṃśodhana Mandiram edition.

Try it at bhagavatavani.com, or search "Bhāgavata-VāNi" on the App Store and Google Play.
