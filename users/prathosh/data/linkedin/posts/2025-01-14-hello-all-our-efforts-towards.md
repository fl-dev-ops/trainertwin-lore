Hello All,

Our efforts towards "selective Unlearning" of Generative Models bore another fruit today. 

Our paper entitled - "Adapt then Unlearn: Exploring Parameter Space Semantics for Unlearning in Generative Adversarial Networks" is accepted in Transactions on Machine Learning Research (TMRL), one of the top tier journals in ML research. 

In this work, we devise a method to prevent the generation of outputs containing undesired features from a pre-trained Generative Adversarial Network (GAN) where the underlying training data set is inaccessible. We propose parameter-space traversal based regularise to achieve this objective. 

We not only provide theoretical insights into the proposed method but validate it via extensive experimentation. 

Paper draft attached for interested readers.
