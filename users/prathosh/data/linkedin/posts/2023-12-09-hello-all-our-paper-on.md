Hello All,  

Our paper on fusing Generative Models with Weak supervision has been accepted for the main track of AAAI 2024 (A top venue in AIML).

In this work, we complement weak supervision with generative models and vice versa, using conditional submodularity. 
 
A rare feature of this work is that it got through an A* venue in the first round (typically ML paper takes multiple iterations to get accepted in top venues). 

This also is the first work of my PhD student kumar shubham, well supported by my RA Pranav Sastry. Congratulations to both. 

The preprint is attached for interested readers.
