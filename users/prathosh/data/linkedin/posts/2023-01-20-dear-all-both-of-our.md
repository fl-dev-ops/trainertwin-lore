Dear All,

Both of our submissions to AISTATS 2023, a premier conference in AI, got accepted. These papers are very special as they are the first pieces of work that was fully carried from our lab (Representation Learning Lab) at IISc (Indian Institute of Science (IISc),) with my PhD students (Pavan Karjol and Piyush Tiwary) as primary Authors. 

We address two important problems - (a) learning deep representations to discover the sub-groups of permutation invariant groups and (b) class-preserving latent space over-sampling for class imbalance using regularized auto encoders. 

Both methods have sufficient empirical evidence of their performance with solid theoretical backing. Attaching the papers (as a single PDF) for interested readers.  

Congratulations to all the Authors and Rep Lab IISc for opening the account in style. 

Piyush Tiwary Pavan Karjol Arnab Mondal Lakshya Singhal Rohan kashyap Chair ECE Department of ECE, IISc
