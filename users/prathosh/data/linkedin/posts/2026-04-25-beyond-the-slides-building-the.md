Beyond the Slides: Building the Mathematical Foundations of ML.


I am pleased to announce that my NPTEL course on the Mathematical Foundations of Machine Learning has now ended. We had more than 10000+ learners in this first edition. 


In an era of one-click model training and high-level wrappers, we are losing sight of the first principles. To build truly robust, agentic systems, you cannot bypass the rigour.


For this course, I made a deliberate choice: No slides. We went back to the "Chalk and Talk" method—deriving everything from scratch on the board. 

This isn't about memorizing APIs; it’s about understanding the underlying geometry and optimization that make ML work. 

We cover the foundations of ML with the corresponding mathematical rigour. I have taken a probabilistic viewpoint to ML here. There are also accompanying programming tutorials. 

Whether you are a student or a researcher moving into a Staff-level role, this course is designed to give you the mathematical "muscle memory" needed to architect the future of AI.
