Hello All,

Happy to share that our work (with Prashant Pandey, Mrigank Raman, Sumanth V.) on Domain Generalization got accepted in IEEE/CVF CVPR 2021 (One of the premier ML conferences). In this work, we tackle the problem of domain shift in ML - Models trained on a source dataset doesn't generalize well on unseen target distribution.  We capitalize on the fact that there exists a single target example during inference. Our work is the new State-Of-The-Art in the area of Domain Generalization. By the way, this is the 4th A* publication of my first PhD student Prashant Pandey. The preprint attached. #cvpr2021 #iitdelhi #deeplearning #machinelearning #AI
