The job posting is active: We are pleased to announce an opening for a Scientist position at TCS Research within the Deep Learning and Artificial Intelligence Research Group and also Project Scientist at IITD starting June 2026. The ideal candidate should hold a Ph.D. in Mathematics, Computer Science and Engineering (CSE), or Electronics and Communication Engineering (ECE).

The high-level project details are below:

Interpretability of Diffusion Models: We are interested in advancing our understanding of diffusion models in order to provide a more reliable, precise and better generation experience for our clients. In an ongoing project, we are studying the relationship between the weights of the diffusion model and its generation capabilities – i.e., we are studying how concept-specific weight subspaces could be identified and applied to produce high-quality image/video edits. 

What You’ll Do

Build and Deploy Models: Experiment with, refine, and productionize high-quality machine-learning models, services, and platforms to deliver significant technological and business impact.

Advance Interpretability Research: Serve as a primary contributor on ongoing research projects focused on understanding and interpreting diffusion models  and compression of diffusion models

Lead Research Initiatives: Design and spearhead projects that promise both academic publications and real-world application value for TCS Research.

Required Qualifications:

Completed MS with a strong academic background from a reputed institute.
Solid programming skills with Python or other equivalent languages.
Knowledge of machine learning frameworks: PyTorch, TensorFlow
Deep knowledge in Data Structures, Algorithms, Machine Learning, Statistics. 
Proficiency in one of the following domains: Computer Vision, Natural Language Processing, Multimodal Models
Basic familiarity of Diffusion Models for Text to Image Generation.

Preferred Qualifications:

Completed PhD with a strong research background from a reputed institute.
Expert-level knowledge in one of the domains: Computer Vision, Natural Language Processing, Multimodal Models.
Deep experience with Diffusion Models for Text to Image Generation.
Strong publication record (i.e., egCVPR, NeurIPS, ICML, ICLR, AAAI, ECML, ECCV, etc.)
