A Significant New Chapter Begins 

As they say, change is the only constant, and today, my hand, which has long held the lens of the researcher and the chalk of the teacher, now grasps the tools of the builder. I am choosing to change and help software do the same.

The software that runs our world carries decades of accumulated tech debt. For us, modernization is not just rewriting code; it is continuity of intent: carrying forward the wisdom of the past into the architecture of the future without distortion or loss.

With this intention, I am humbled to share that I am co-founding LatentForce (https://lnkd.in/gydsEG2S) along with Aravind Jayendran and Vinay Kyatham, who were once my brilliant students at IIT Delhi Indian Institute of Technology, Delhi

We, at LatentForce are building agentic AI systems that understand and transform software the way engineers do, by inferring intent, reasoning over architecture, and planning safe change, so modernization becomes preservation, not disruption.

We are fortunate to have closed our seed funding round to build this vision, and we will share more details soon.

If this space resonates with you, whether you are a thinker, a builder, or an observer of how the world evolves, I would be happy to connect and learn together. And if you are someone responsible for the evolution of large software systems,  CIO, CTO, architect, or transformation leader, I would be grateful to hear your lived experiences.

As The Isha Upanishad Says, 

कुर्वन्नेवेह कर्माणि जिजीविषेच्छतं समाः । एवं त्वयि नान्यथेतोऽस्ति न कर्म लिप्यते नरे ॥
