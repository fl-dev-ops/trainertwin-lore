Some stars should have aligned for our lab today. Three paper acceptances within 24 hours, all on different topics with different authors and timelines. 

Here is the latest one - 

"FAST: Feature Aware Similarity Thresholding for weak unlearning in black-box generative models"  - accepted at IEEE Transactions on Artificial Intelligence. 

In our efforts towards ensuring Safety of generative models, in this work, we devise a method to effectively suppress undesired outputs  of a black box Generative model, by systematically encoding the representation of unwanted features in the latent space. We employ user-marked positive and negative samples to guide this process, leveraging the latent space's inherent capacity to capture these undesired representations. During inference, we use this identified representation in the latent space to compute projection similarity metrics with newly sampled latent vectors.
