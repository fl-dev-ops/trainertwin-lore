One of the hardest decisions I’ve ever made turned out to be the most rewarding.
And I’m so damn proud of myself for taking the risk.
I left a stable and lucrative career in Capital Markets to pursue my passion for helping ambitious professionals elevate their leadership and grow their careers. It was a decision fueled by years of reflection and a deep desire to make a greater impact - and I couldn’t be happier with the path I’m on.
During my 17 years at the bank, I held 4 different roles on the trading floor, spanning both trading and sales desks. I was fully dedicated to the grind, chasing titles and ‘prestige’ as quickly as I could.
For the longest time, I equated success with promotions and pay raises. I believed that if I kept moving up, all the hard work and long hours would be worth it.
But along the way, I learned that success isn’t just about climbing higher, it’s about climbing smarter. I learned the dos and don’ts of career growth, what it takes to achieve meaningful success, and how to create strategies that actually work.
Now, I use those lessons to help others play their game and take their leadership to the next level.
Why did I pivot to coaching?
Well, it started with mentorship…
Towards the second half of my career at the bank, I threw myself into mentorship. It wasn’t something I did because it was expected, it was something I did because it genuinely brought me joy. Knowing I could pave the way for others by sharing my stories and lessons learned lit a fire in me and it’s what ultimately fueled my decision to go all-in on coaching.
But can I let you in on a little secret?
As excited as I was to make the leap, I was terrified. Fear crept in from every angle:
❌ Fear of the unknown
❌ Fear of money
❌ Fear of success
❌ Fear of what others might think
So. Much. Fear.
How did I overcome all the fear brewing inside of me?
I hired a coach! And through our time together…
🗝️ I exposed the truth behind each self-sabotaging thought that was holding me back from pursuing what I really wanted.
🗝️ I solidified my why, my anchor, that grounds me each time my mind spirals.
🗝️ I developed an action plan I felt both confident in and motivated by.
🗝️ I built a growth mindset that’s given me the confidence and courage to turn my dream into a reality.
Partnering with a coach works. Investing in yourself works. Taking control of your career path by setting goals and creating a step-by-step plan to achieve success works.
And it feels pretty damn good to watch it all come to life!
