No one is coming to save your career.

There’s no:

→ Secret invitation on the way
→ “We’ve been watching you and we’d love to promote you” meeting
→ Perfect moment when management suddenly realizes how valuable you truly are

The people who advance the fastest take action, even when the outcome isn’t guaranteed.

You can’t control every opportunity.

Just as much as you can’t avoid failure.

But you can try, learn, and try again.

What do you have to lose? 

No really, what’s the worst case scenario?

You learn what doesn’t work.

You get clearer on what you want.

You build resilience and strategy with every ask you make.

Because here’s what’s actually at risk:

→ Staying stuck in the same role another year

And I know you don’t want that. 

You’re way more in control of your growth than you give yourself credit for. 

Take that first step. And don’t stop until you get everything you desire.

You’ve got this.
