You're not behind.

But Q4 is a good time to ask yourself a question you may have been avoiding all year:

Has my career actually moved forward this year, or has my workload just gotten bigger?

Because those two things aren't the same.

You can spend an entire year delivering.

Taking on more.
Getting great feedback.
Being told to “keep doing what you're doing.”

And still end the year in the exact same role, without much more clarity on what it's actually going to take to get to the next one.

Not because you're underperforming.

But because at a certain point in your career, being great at your job is only part of what determines how far you'll go.

Senior leaders need to see how you think.
People need to know what you're capable of.
Your manager needs to understand what you want.

And you need to understand what leadership actually needs to see before they'll feel confident giving you more.

Those are very different things from simply working harder.

So before another year comes to an end, I'd ask yourself:

What is actually different about how I'm positioned for my next move today vs. where I was at the beginning of the year?

And if the answer is “not much,” that's useful information.

Because now you can do something about it.
