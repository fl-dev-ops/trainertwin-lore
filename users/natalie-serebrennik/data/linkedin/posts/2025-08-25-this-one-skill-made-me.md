This one skill made me successful on the trading floor.

And I didn’t even realize I was building it at the time.

Emotional control.

Learning to regulate my emotions under pressure became the most valuable leadership skill I had.

Not because someone taught me, but because I had no other choice.

It started with the small moments:

→ Getting snapped at by a senior trader for asking a “stupid” question

→ Watching a million-dollar trade unravel with everyone’s eyes on me

→ Being told to “relax” when I knew damn well the tension was real

I took everything personally.

Created stories in my head about not being good enough.

And little by little, it chipped away at my confidence.

I was only a few years into my ‘dream job’ and I was already suffering.

The stress followed me home.
I picked unnecessary fights with family.
Numbed out with garbage TV just to feel okay.

After a few months of this, I knew something had to shift.

Either I’d change the way I showed up or I’d burn out and leave.

So I focused on the one thing I could control:

→ I slowed my reactions
→ I invested in a mindset until I actually believed it
→ I stopped trying to prove I belonged - and started acting like I did

It wasn’t about pretending I didn’t feel anything.

It was about choosing how I wanted to be perceived.

That’s the skill I carry into every room now.

Because real leadership doesn’t happen when everything’s going smoothly.

It happens when things get messy and you stay grounded anyway.

If you want to grow into bigger rooms, get promoted, lead a team - this IS the work.
