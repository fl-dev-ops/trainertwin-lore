Why every jab in corporate landed like the most intense gut punch.

If someone threw a rude comment my way and I'd spend the next 3 days replaying it.

What did I do wrong?
What should I have said?
Why does this bother me this much?

I made their behavior about me.

And it was exhausting.

For a long time I thought that was just part of the job. 

That if you were going to survive on a trading floor full of big personalities and even bigger egos, you had to develop a thicker skin.

But thicker skin wasn't the answer, even though that’s exactly what I built in the process. 

The answer was simpler and way more powerful:

I stopped giving people's bad behavior more weight than it deserved.

Because someone's rude comment says everything about them, not you.

The moment I actually believed that (not just knew it, believed it) my response changed completely.

Instead of internalizing, I started reflecting it right back:

"That's interesting you'd say that."

And it worked every single time.

Their tone changed. The behavior softened. And those moments became fewer and farther between.

You cannot control how someone speaks to you.

But you can control whether you let it live rent free in your head.

Most people don't realize they have that choice.
