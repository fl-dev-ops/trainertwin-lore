Your talent will only take you as far as your direct manager is willing to advocate for you.

So why leave that to chance?

If you want to grow inside your company, one strategy matters more than almost anything else:

Build strong relationships with decision-makers before you need them.

Not when you're up for promotion.
Not when things start to go sideways.

Now.

Start building relationships beyond your boss. Get to know them. Understand what they care about.

What challenges are they trying to solve?
What opportunities are they excited about?

This isn't sucking up.

It's protecting your own growth with the same intentionality you bring to everything else.

Here are 3 things I'm actively coaching a client to do right now as he prepares for his VP promotion:

1️⃣ Send a monthly wins recap that clearly connects your team's results to your boss's top priorities. Business impact front and center.

2️⃣ Book coffee chats with senior leaders you casually cross paths with. Ask thoughtful questions to better understand what success looks like at their level.

3️⃣ Ask a senior leader to sponsor your growth. Share your career goals and the internal roadblocks you’re facing, not as a complaint, but as a leadership conversation.

None of these require a title change or a perfect moment.

They just require you to stop waiting for someone else to grow your career for you.

What's one relationship you've been meaning to build that you keep putting off?
