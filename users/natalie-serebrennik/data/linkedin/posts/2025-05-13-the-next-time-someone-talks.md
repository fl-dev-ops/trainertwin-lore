The next time someone talks down to you at work…

Try this 👇

1️⃣ Seek clarification

“Could you repeat that?”

This simple question can be a wake-up call, making them hear their tone and message.

2️⃣ Address their intentions

“What were you hoping to achieve with that comment? Did you mean to embarrass me or hurt my feelings?”

By shifting the focus to their intent, you encourage them to reflect on their behavior.

3️⃣ Maintain composure and disengage

Regardless of their response, reply with “Understood” and walk away.

By refusing to engage in conflict, you maintain control of the situation.

Each time I’ve tried this approach my confidence grew…and so did their respect for me.

Remember…their actions and words reflect way more on them than you.
