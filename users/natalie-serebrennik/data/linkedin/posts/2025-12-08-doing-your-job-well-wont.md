Doing your job well won’t get you promoted.

Best case - you keep your job.

Worst case - you become stagnant in ‘their’ eyes, and your pay starts to reflect it.

Who’s they?

The leaders who decide your growth.

The ones who approve or reject your promotion.

Fair?

No.

But it still happens every day.

That’s why, when my clients are preparing for a next-level promotion, we don’t just focus on their 'hard work' or the results they've achieved.

We focus on making sure the right people understand the full scope of their value and why it matters to the business.

Here are a few ways:

1️⃣ Take initiative. Lead without waiting for permission.

2️⃣ Solve real problems. Don’t default to ‘not my lane’.

3️⃣ Speak up. They can’t value what they can’t hear or see.

These are just a few strategies we build together.
