The biggest goal my clients have:

Becoming more strategic.

And it’s intimidating for one reason.

It requires you to outgrow the version of you that got promoted in the first place.

The individual contributor.

If you’re still the person who:

Dives into work before thinking about impact
Gets lost in the details without zooming out first
Or “saves” the team by taking on more yourself…

You’ll stall your growth.

Because you’re still operating in execution mode.

Leadership growth happens when you start connecting your work to the bigger picture.

When your boss hands you a project and your first instinct is “How do I get this done?”

Pause.

Then ask yourself instead:

How does this tie to company priorities?
Who needs to be involved so this is successful?
What’s the longer-term impact if we do this right?

It’s this type of thinking that changes how you’re perceived.

You stop looking like a strong doer.
And start looking like someone ready for promotion.
