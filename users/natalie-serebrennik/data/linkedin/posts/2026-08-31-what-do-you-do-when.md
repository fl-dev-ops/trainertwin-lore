What do you do when your boss takes all the credit and none of the blame?

As in:

Labels your wins as his own (even though he never touched the work).

Gloats about how much support he's giving you (even though you can’t remember the last time you had a 1:1).

And when you make the rare mistake… he’s the first person to throw you under the bus.

That was my client's exact situation.

The first thing she said when we met:

"I love my work, I love my team, and I can't freaking stand my boss. I don't know how much longer I can do this."

Most people come to coaching already knowing how bad it is. 

They've been venting about it for months. To their partner, their friends, anyone who will listen.

What they haven't done is figured out what they're actually going to do about it.

Stick it out or leave and hope it’s better somewhere else.

And she had no clue which one made the most sense for her.

Because leaving felt like letting him win. And staying was slowly killing her.

So we didn't pick one or the other right away.

We worked both sides at the same time.

On one side, we stabilized her present. 

Set boundaries. Built a relationship with her skip level. Got her out of reaction mode so she could think straight again.

On the other side, we built her options. 

Got clear on what she actually wanted next. Built her positioning. Made sure she could walk into any conversation and make the case for herself without hesitation.

And while she didn't end up leaving, she was ready to. 

And that readiness changed everything about how she showed up for herself.

When you stop making decisions from desperation and start making them from a place of clarity, the entire dynamic changes.

Not just with your boss. With yourself.
