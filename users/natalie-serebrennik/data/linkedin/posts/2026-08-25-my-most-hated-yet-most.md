My most hated, yet most useful, lesson in corporate:

Being good at your job isn't enough to get you where you want to go.

It took me years to appreciate how true this is.

Because the system trains you to believe that if you deliver great work, stay reliable, and keep proving yourself… your future opportunities will follow.

But after a few disappointing year-end reviews with the same excuse dressed up in different wording, like:

"You're doing great, keep doing what you're doing."

Followed by:

"Unfortunately, we couldn't promote you this year. There's pressure from the top to keep numbers low."

You start to clue in.

Your performance keeps your current seat.
Your self-advocacy gets you promoted.

I had the experience.
I had the results.
I worked hard.

None of that guaranteed my growth.

This is the part that did:

Learning how to advocate for myself strategically. 

Focusing more energy on how well I communicated my value, and to who, and less on people-pleasing and saying yes to every ask that came my way.

If you’re like most people, you’re probably skipping this part too…

You can't advocate for yourself if you don't actually believe in yourself.

And that belief comes from knowing your story, your value, inside and out.

How you've grown
What you've achieved
What you've overcome
Where you're going next

When you own your story, people don't just buy into what you can do.

They buy into YOU.
