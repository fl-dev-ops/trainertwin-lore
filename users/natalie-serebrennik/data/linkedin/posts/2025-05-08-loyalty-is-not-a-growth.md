Loyalty is not a growth strategy.

(and this is coming from someone who spent 17 years at the same company)

Staying at the same company.
Reporting to the same management.
And doing the same-ish work year after year...

That mindset is keeping way too many talented professionals small.

Because here’s the part we actively turn a blind eye to:

Companies benefit from your loyalty, until it’s no longer convenient for them.

You can be the go-to.
The one who gets it done.
The one who’s always reliable.

But if you’re not actively shaping how your role evolves or how your leadership is seen…

You’re making it easy for people to overlook you.

I’m not saying you need to jump ship.

But I am saying… stop waiting for growth to happen.

Stop thinking your loyalty will get you to the next level. 

You're no longer that junior, grateful for the opportunity.

Over the years, you’ve accomplished enough to write a book.

And at this stage of the game, “waiting” isn’t a strategy, it’s a trap.
