Most careers don’t stall because people lack talent.

They stall because of these 10 career mistakes.

I know because these were the mistakes that kept me stuck for years on the trading floor:

1️⃣ Waiting for your manager to advance your career for you.

Why wait when you can get ahead of the conversation by asking: “What gaps do I need to fill to be promoted this year?”

2️⃣ Avoiding tough conversations because you don’t want to ruffle feathers.

Confronting the hard moments shows leaders you can handle bigger responsibility.

3️⃣ Staying in your comfort zone because risk feels scary.

Careers grow when you step toward challenges others hesitate to take.

4️⃣ Thinking that great work alone is enough to get ahead.

Performance builds credibility, but visibility drives promotion decisions.

5️⃣ Not knowing your market value.

Understanding your value changes how confidently you advocate for yourself.

6️⃣ Staying in a job you’ve outgrown because figuring out what’s next feels hard.

Comfort can quietly become the reason your career stops progressing.

7️⃣ Letting people treat you poorly because you don’t like conflict.

Leadership often begins the moment you start setting clear boundaries.

8️⃣ Trusting the wrong people too easily.

Pay attention to patterns before deciding who deserves your trust.

9️⃣ Keeping your ideas to yourself.

The more your voice is heard, the more your influence grows.

🔟 Failing to document your wins.

If you don’t track your results, others will struggle to see them.

I’ve made every mistake on this list.
Most professionals have.

But careers aren't written in stone.
They’re built in decisions.

And most of the mistakes above come from one belief:
Thinking great work alone is enough.

It rarely is.

Which mistake hits the hardest for you?
