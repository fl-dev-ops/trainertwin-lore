The boldest move I ever made in my career and why I regret waiting so long.

Years into my career I discovered a truth that still stings to think about.

I was significantly underpaid compared to my peers.

I raised it with my manager, expecting action.

Instead, I got a string of empty promises, “Next year, we’ll fix it.”

Embarrassed to admit it, but I believed him.

Year after year, I let those reassurances stall my growth.

It wasn’t until I returned from my second mat leave, under a new manager, that something inside me snapped.

I was done waiting.

I made my intentions very clear:
“I’m not okay with this, and I’m taking it up the chain.”

What followed were the boldest conversations of my career.

One leader at a time, I made my value unmistakable 

The results I delivered, the revenue I drove, the gaps I filled.

And once they understood my true value, that’s when I brought up my pay gap and asked them to make it right.

It wasn’t easy. At the start, self-advocacy rarely is.

But it worked. I secured a six-figure bump by the end of the year.

My only regret is not taking bold action sooner.

Self-advocacy isn’t selfish, it’s your strongest corporate strategy.

If you’re working hard for that pay bump, make your life 100x easier and stop waiting for someone else to notice your worth. 

Be the one who makes it known.

What’s the boldest step you’ve taken to advocate for yourself?
