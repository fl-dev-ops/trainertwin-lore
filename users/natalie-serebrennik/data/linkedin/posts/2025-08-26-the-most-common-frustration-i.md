The most common frustration I hear from my clients:

“My manager says I’m doing great but nothing changes. I’m still at the same title and same pay.”

They’re told:

“You’re doing a fantastic job.”
“No feedback, you’re all good.”
“Just keep doing what you’re doing.”

It sounds positive.
But it’s lazy leadership.
And it’s the reason they feel stuck.

If you’re in the same boat, here’s what’s actually happening:

→ They’re not thinking about your future
→ They don’t have a plan for your growth
→ You’ve become too valuable where you are

When your performance is strong but your progress is stalled - this is your sign.

It’s time to shift the dynamic.

And it starts by having a different type of conversation.

Asking questions like:

“What would you need to see from me between now and year-end to greenlight my promotion?”

“What are new ways I can build my visibility with senior leadership?”

“What gaps would I need to close to be considered for the next level?”

Then pay close attention to how they respond.

Because their answers will tell you everything about how invested they are in your future - or not.

And if they’re not invested?

It’s time to stop confusing appreciation with advancement.

You’re being valued but not developed.

And that’s not enough.
