The people getting promoted aren't working harder than you.

That's the part worth paying attention to.

I learned this the hard way.

For years, I worked hard, delivered results, and assumed my growth would take care of itself.

It didn't.

I watched others get promoted faster. Not because they worked harder - because they owned their growth. They took action while I was still waiting for the right moment.

Here's what I'd do differently if I wanted that promotion this year:

Own your growth before anyone else does. Your manager has a lot on their plate. Get clear on what's missing, share your goals, and take steps to close the gaps yourself.

Lean into the uncomfortable. The opportunities that stretch you are usually the ones that move you forward. If things feel stagnant, that's worth acting on.

Build relationships before you need them. Leadership is about influence. The more visible and connected you are, the more doors open naturally.

None of this is complicated. It's just easy to keep pushing off.

And if you've been working toward that promotion for 3+ years, this is probably why.

I wish I appreciated this sooner:

You don't get promoted for doing your job well, you get to keep your job for that.

You get promoted when decision-makers are convinced you can create impact at the next level.

When you start acting like someone who already knows that, that’s when the game changes for you.
