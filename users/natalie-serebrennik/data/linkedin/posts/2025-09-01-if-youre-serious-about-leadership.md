If you’re serious about leadership growth, waiting won’t get you there.

I learned this the hard way.

For years, I worked hard, delivered results, and assumed my growth would take care of itself.

It didn’t.

I watched others move up faster. Not because they worked harder, but because they owned their growth better than me.

They took action while I waited.

When this clicked, everything made sense.

And if I were staring at that realization today, here’s exactly what I’d do:

1️⃣ Stop waiting for your manager to drive your career.

→ Your growth is your responsibility.
→ Make sure your manager knows your goals.
→ Get clear on what’s missing and then take action to close the gaps.

2️⃣ Get comfortable being uncomfortable.

→ Playing it safe won’t get you to the next level.
→ Trust yourself to step into bigger challenges.
→ If you feel stagnant, that’s your sign to push forward.

3️⃣ Build your network before you need it.

→ Leadership is about influence.
→ The more visible you are, the more doors open.
→ Don’t wait. Start building those alliances now.
