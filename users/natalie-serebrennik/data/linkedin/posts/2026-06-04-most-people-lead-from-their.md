Most people lead from their current title.

And then wonder why nothing is changing.

I did this for years. 

I was excellent at the job I had. 

I delivered at the level I was being paid for. 

I showed up as the person the role described, and then I waited for someone to decide I was ready for the next one.

That's not how it works.

The professionals who advance aren't waiting to be given the next level before they start operating at it. 

They're already in those conversations. 

Already framing their thinking at a higher altitude. 

Already making themselves relevant to the problems that exist one level above them.

By understanding that the gap between where you are and where you want to be is not closed by time or patience.

It's closed by deliberately showing up as the version of yourself the next role is asking for, before anyone has officially said you're ready.

Leadership sees that. 

It's one of the clearest signals that someone is ready to move.

The title follows the behavior. It almost never leads it.

If you've been waiting for the promotion to start acting like you've earned it, that's the part worth flipping.

Start by getting your hands on the actual promotion criteria. 

Find the gaps between where you are and where you want to be. 

Then get specific about how you'd close each one.

This isn’t a long exercise, nor is it painful. 

But still... most never do it.
