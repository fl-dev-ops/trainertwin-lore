The mark of a great leader is this:

Having tough conversations.

And the only way you get better at them?

By having them.

Every strong leader can remember the first feedback conversation they led.

It was clunky.
Super awkward.
Too soft or way too harsh.
And uncomfortable for everyone.

That’s normal.

Because tough conversations aren’t a personality trait.

They’re a skill.

And like any skill, they improve with reps.

Over time, you learn how to:

Be direct without being harsh.
Be clear without overexplaining.
Be constructive instead of personal.
Be honest without getting emotional.

If you’re telling yourself,
“I’m just bad at this.”

Stop.

You’re not bad at it.
You’re just under-practiced.

And tough conversations are one of the most promotable skills you can build.

Your reputation isn’t formed in easy moments.
It’s formed in the uncomfortable ones.

How you handle those moments sticks.

If you’re feeling stuck in your career, start there.

Have the conversation you’ve been avoiding.
