When I worked on the trading floor, I was one of very few women in the room.

Young. 
Ambitious. 
Hungry to prove myself.

But I looked nothing like the leaders around me.

And I felt it.

Some assumed I was their assistant.

Others didn’t speak to me unless there was a drink in their hand.

And more than once, I caught people off guard when I spoke with confidence.

I wasn’t what they expected, and that left me questioning if I belonged.

At 27, I didn’t have the tools to challenge it.

So I followed the unspoken rules:

Stay quiet. Blend in. Don’t stand out.

Because that’s what they rewarded.

Or at least, that’s what it looked like from the outside.

And it got me nowhere.

No real growth.
No new opportunities.
Just a warm seat to sit in each day.

I didn’t ‘make it to the floor’ just to be overlooked, damn it!

Eventually, it clicked:

Those old rules weren’t made for people like me.

And following them would never get me where I wanted to go.

That was the start of my journey of standing up for myself when others expected me to blend in.

Fitting in was never the goal and for the next 10 years I made sure of it.

If you’re on the cusp of your next big move, keep going. The only sign you need that you’re ready is this right here. 

What would you tell your 27 year old self today?
