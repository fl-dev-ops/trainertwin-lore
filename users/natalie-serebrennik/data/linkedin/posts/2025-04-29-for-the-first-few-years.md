For the first few years of my career, I was miserable.

Why?

Because I forced myself into a box that didn’t fit.

Quiet office. 
Slow pace. 
Boring.

And for a spicy child turned adult, I struggled to fit in.

I dimmed my spice and pretended to be someone I wasn’t.

Only two years in... and I was already drained.

Burned out from all the pretending.

Then I moved to the trading floor and finally, I could breathe.

The chaos. 
The fast pace. 
The adrenaline.

I found my people. 

My spice fit right in. It was even celebrated.

When you stop wasting energy trying to be someone you’re not, everything feels easier:

→ Getting out of bed on a Monday morning
→ Walking in with a genuine smile on your face
→ Actually enjoying the work you do and who you do it with

If you’re feeling like that square peg in a round hole - can I please remind you:

There are better-aligned options out there.

Your reality today doesn’t need to be your reality tomorrow.

You get to choose better.

And you get to make that choice today.

What box did you have to break free from?
