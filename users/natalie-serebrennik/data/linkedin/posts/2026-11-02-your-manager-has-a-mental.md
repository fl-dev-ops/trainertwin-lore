Your manager has a mental file on you. And what's in it could be the reason you're not getting promoted.

I'm talking about the little things they notice about how you operate, how you treat people, and how you handle yourself in different situations.

Things that don't necessarily show up in your performance review but absolutely influence whether they see you as ready for the next level.

And if you're working toward your next promotion, I’d be paying attention to HOW you show up in these moments:

1️⃣ How you treat people when there's nothing in it for you. Are you just as respectful of junior colleagues as you are of senior leaders?

2️⃣ Whether people feel comfortable being honest with you. Can someone disagree with you or raise a concern without worrying about how you'll react?

3️⃣ How well you delegate. Can you give people ownership while still providing the support and accountability they need?

4️⃣ How you manage your team's workload. Are you setting realistic expectations, or have long hours and constant urgency become the norm?

5️⃣ How you respond to feedback. Can you hear something you don't agree with without immediately getting defensive?

6️⃣ What your peers think of working with you. Are you collaborative? Do you follow through? Would they want to work with you again?

7️⃣ Whether you're developing the people around you. Are you helping others grow, or are you mostly focused on getting the work done?

8️⃣ How you handle things when they go wrong. Do you take accountability and focus on solutions, or start looking for someone to blame?

9️⃣ Whether you can step back from the details. Can you trust others to deliver, or do you feel the need to be involved in every decision?

🔟 Whether people would actually want you as their leader. Because being great at your job doesn't automatically mean people will want to work for you.

And the tricky part is, you might not even realize which of these things are influencing how leadership perceives you.

So don't wait until your performance review to find out.

Have the conversation with your manager now.

Ask what they're already seeing, where they have concerns, and what they'd need to see you demonstrate before they'd feel comfortable recommending you for that promotion.

Because the earlier you understand what's holding you back, the sooner you can actually do something about it.
