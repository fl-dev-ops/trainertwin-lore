Stop calling it imposter syndrome when it’s really poor management.

Not every confidence dip is a mindset issue.

Sometimes it’s the result of being undervalued, overlooked, or unsupported for too long.

I’ve coached too many brilliant professionals who internalized the wrong problem.

They weren’t too sensitive.
They weren’t lacking confidence.
They were responding to broken systems and lazy leadership.

Before you start fixing yourself, ask:
Am I actually underperforming, or am I being mismanaged?

Is this fear of speaking up, or a lack of psychological safety?

Is this self-doubt, or am I strategically protecting myself?

You don’t need to “fix” imposter syndrome.

You need to stop blaming yourself for things that were never yours to carry in the first place.
