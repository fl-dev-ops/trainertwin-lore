I still remember the way I sat, the black blazer I wore, and the faces I studied.

It was my second week on the trading floor.

I was one of very few women in a sea of men, watching closely to figure out the rules.

Who got the laughs?
Who held the most power?
What kind of behavior got rewarded?

Within days, I had it down.
(Or so I thought.)

→ Say yes before they finish asking
→ Don’t challenge anyone in front of a client
→ Skip bedtime again because the last client hasn’t left yet

Be helpful.
Be available.
Be agreeable.

And I played the game well.

There were countless nights I was the one ordering the final round of drinks, just before the bar closed.

Feet aching from heels I knew I shouldn’t have worn.

Exhausted, but pretending I still wanted to be there.

Calculating how many hours I had left before needing to show up polished, composed, ready to trade like I hadn’t been out all night.

Home by 2am. Up by 6. At my desk by 7.

But I didn’t do it just because.

It was strategic.

→ Be visible. Check.
→ Kick it with the ‘boys’. Check.
→ Be the last one standing. Check.

Because you need to be seen to be remembered, right?

Yep, that’s what I believed.

Not because anyone said it out loud, but because that was the culture.

And when you’re new, ambitious, and one of the only women in the room, you learn to adapt fast.

But here’s what I know now:

Success isn’t about disappearing into the system.

It’s about creating space for yourself inside it.

That doesn’t mean skipping client dinners or rolling in at noon.

It means understanding the parameters and then making decisions within them.

Here are the permissions I eventually gave myself:

1️⃣ I don’t need to be the last one entertaining clients at the bar. I paid my dues - someone else can step in.

2️⃣ I don’t need to say “no problem” every time someone asks me to make a lunch reservation. Office housework isn’t my job just because I’m a woman.

3️⃣ I do get to advocate for my boundaries while still delivering exceptional results. These are not mutually exclusive.

✨Because your brand can’t stand out if it blends in.

I built a successful career because I learned to stop mimicking the room and start owning my value inside it.

Now I help my clients do the same.
