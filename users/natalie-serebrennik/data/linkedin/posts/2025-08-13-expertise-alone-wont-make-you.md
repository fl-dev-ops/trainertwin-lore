Expertise alone won’t make you a great leader.

Yes, knowledge matters, but it’s not what makes people follow you.

Think about the best leader you’ve ever had.

Was it their expertise that made them great?
Or was it how they made you FEEL?

The most effective leaders aren’t 'experts'.

They’re connectors.

→ They motivate you to be and do better
→ They support you and your goals
→ They communicate with care 

Leadership isn’t about knowing everything.

It’s about creating an environment where others can succeed.

So if you’re waiting to “know more” before stepping up, stop.

Instead, focus on what actually moves the needle:

✅ Show up with empathy
✅ Build trust through transparency
✅ Create real connections with every person you lead

What you put into your team is what you’ll get out.
