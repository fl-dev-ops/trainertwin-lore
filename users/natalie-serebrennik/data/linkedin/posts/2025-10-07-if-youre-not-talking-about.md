If you’re not talking about the work you’re doing…

You’ll never be rewarded for it.

I used to fight this reality for many years.

And I paid with my salary.

(Making almost HALF of what my peers were making!)

You need to change this ASAP.

Here’s an easy way to start:

→ Track your wins. Every single week.

My go-to: Fridays at 2pm.

I called it my Work journal - a simple spreadsheet I updated with my progress for the week.

Here’s why this 15 min habit works:

It builds your confidence and keeps your impact top of mind and easy to share.

So when it’s time to advocate for yourself, you’re armed with what to say.

This is just ONE strategy I used to double my salary.
