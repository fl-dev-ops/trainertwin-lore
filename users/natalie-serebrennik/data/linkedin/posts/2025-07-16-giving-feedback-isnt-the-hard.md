Giving feedback isn’t the hard part.

Getting someone to act on it is.

That’s the real leadership work.

One of my clients learned this recently.

She’d raised the same issue with a team member more than once… but nothing changed.

We broke it down together and realized:

She was focused on being polite and it came at the cost of clarity.

The message wasn’t wrong. It just wasn’t landing.

A common paradox for empathetic leaders - wanting to be kind, but losing clarity in the process.

So we reworked her delivery using 4 prompts:

→ Why does this matter?
→ What specific examples can you provide?
→ Is the ask clear and actionable?
→ How will you follow up?

And it worked.

Her direct report took ownership and started to make changes within a week.

That’s the power of clear, confident feedback.

When your team understands the why, they’re more likely to deliver the how.
