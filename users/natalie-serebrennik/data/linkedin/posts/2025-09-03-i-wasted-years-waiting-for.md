I wasted years waiting for a bonus that never came.

Thinking I “deserved” it.

Back on the trading floor, I traded one of the fastest-moving, highest-notional products.

You’d think the pressure from that would keep me up at night.

Wrong.

It was my salary.

Even though I made good money that my friends told me to be grateful for, I knew I deserved more.

I saw my peers (who weren’t necessarily delivering more) start earning more than me.

I was getting more and more frustrated.

How much more work could I take on?
How many late nights did I have to give up?
When will my manager see I deserved more?

That frustration taught me a tough lesson:

👉 Your bonus doesn’t grow just because you’re great at your job.

👉 It grows when you make your impact undeniable.

So, I stopped waiting and started having the hardest conversations of my career.

I embodied my “selfish” energy.

With my direct manager:
→ I pushed for transparency.

With senior leadership:
→ I tied my wins to revenue impact.

With the top of the house:
→ I made the case for WHY I deserved more.

Every level was a challenge, but each conversation brought me closer to securing that six-figure bonus increase. 

It wasn’t easy

→ There were tears 
→ Lots of resentment
→ And plenty of doubt

But I stayed relentless.

The funny thing is, when you fight for it, the six-figure bonus feels a whole lot better!
