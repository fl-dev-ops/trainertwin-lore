Early in my career, I was addicted to being busy.

I thought the harder I worked, the faster I’d move up.

So I said yes to everything. Stayed late every night.

And stacked my calendar with more to-dos than I could handle.

I figured - this is what it takes to prove yourself.

Until an early mentor shared this with me:

“Rookie leaders make excuses. Great leaders make time.”

That one hit me.

Because the truth was, I was making excuses.

I hustled hard with busy work… and then wondered why my promotions weren’t coming.

“I’m working harder than anyone else!”

And that was my problem.

I was prioritizing busyness over strategic work, like building my leadership skills.

Once I saw it, I started changing it:

1️⃣ Cut out the unnecessary meetings 
2️⃣ Delegated tasks that didn’t need me
3️⃣ Calendar blocked time for my development

I trimmed the fluff and finally carved out time to work on my leadership.

That shift was the beginning of everything for me.

And it became my personal mantra:

Make time. Not excuses.


What’s your hack for making time to learn?
