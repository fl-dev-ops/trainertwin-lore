17 years on the trading floor taught me a hard truth:

Results alone won’t guarantee your growth.

Sure, performance matters. 

But in an environment that’s:

→ Cutthroat
→ Fast-paced
→ Results-obsessed

Trust and connections hold just as much weight, if not more.

I learned this the hard way.

You can’t go at it alone.

The leaders who thrived weren’t just delivering results. They were building meaningful alliances:

→ People who had their back in stressed moments
→ People who spoke their name in rooms they weren’t in
→ People who elevated their influence far beyond what performance alone could achieve

Because influence isn’t just tied to what you do.

It’s tied to who you’ve built trust with.

The most strategic leaders know this.

Instead of hoping their results will speak for themselves, they fast-track their growth by asking:

1️⃣ Who can I build relationships with that will advocate for me?

2️⃣ And, who can I advocate for in return?

This is how you stop surviving and start winning.

What’s your greatest win because of the relationships you’ve built?
