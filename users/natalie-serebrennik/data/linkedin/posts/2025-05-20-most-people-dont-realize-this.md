Most people don’t realize this mistake until it’s already slowed their growth:

They don’t pay attention to how they’re perceived at work.

And in corporate, that’s a problem.

Why? Because perception drives decisions.

Decisions about you and your future at the company.

Your boss.
Your peers.
Senior leadership.

They already have a story about you, whether you’ve shaped it or not.

→ Do they see you as leadership material?
→ Do they trust your judgment under pressure?
→ Do they believe you’re someone they can bet on?

When I was chasing my next title on the trading floor, I thought if I worked hard and delivered results, that would be enough.

We all know how that played out.

It clicked when I learned there was a committee deciding on my promotion, and most of them didn’t even know me.

That’s when I realized:

Your performance matters. But your visibility matters just as much.

If you’re not shaping how you’re perceived, someone else is.

And they may not get it right.

If you’re anything like my clients, you know this is too important to leave to chance.

Your future growth depends on it.
