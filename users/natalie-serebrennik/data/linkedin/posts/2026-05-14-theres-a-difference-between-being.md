There's a difference between being valued and being positioned.

Most high performers learn that too late.

I did.

17 years in capital markets.

Strong results. Positive reviews. A reputation for delivering.

And still:
A ceiling I couldn't fully see until I stopped moving.

That's the trap.

A lot of professionals think hard work compounds into opportunity automatically.

It doesn't.
Not at senior levels.

Because eventually the game changes.

It's no longer just about doing exceptional work.

It's about whether leadership knows how to place you inside bigger conversations.

Whether people advocate for you when you're not in the room.

Whether your work translates into strategic visibility.

That's a completely different skill set.

And most people are never taught it.

So they stay stuck in the same exhausting cycle:

Praised. Reliable. High performing.

But somehow never quite moving at the speed they want.

The system isn't fair.
But it is learnable.

That's what I spend most of my time helping clients understand.

Not how to work harder.
How to stop relying on hard work alone.
