I can’t stand seeing leaders get promoted without the support to actually lead well.

It happens all the time.

→ Congrats, you land the big promo.
→ But sorry, you’re on your own to figure it out.

That’s exactly what happened to my client.

He had just been named Group Head.

He was excited but felt a ton of pressure.

→ The added responsibilities. 
→ The bigger expectations. 
→ And zero guidance.

No one told him how to handle challenging team dynamics.

No one offered advice on delivering direct feedback with confidence.

No one helped him navigate the shift from peer to people leader.

Companies love handing out big titles, but rarely invest in helping leaders grow into them.

He could’ve gone with the flow. But he made the call to invest in coaching instead.

Because his vision for growth was clear and he didn’t want to waste time ‘figuring it out.’

We’ve now worked together for over a year.

And he’s grown into a calm, confident leader who leads with clarity.

He doesn’t need support.

He chooses it because it keeps him focused, strategic, and grounded.

When you’re operating at a senior level, chaos is a given.

But carrying the pressure to perform alone is optional.
