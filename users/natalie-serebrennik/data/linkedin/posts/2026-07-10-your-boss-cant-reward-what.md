Your boss can't reward what they don't see.

Smart, driven professionals get stuck here all the time.

They assume their hard work is obvious. 

That their results speak for themselves. 

That leadership knows exactly how much they're doing.

But here's what's actually happening:

You fix problems - check.
You deliver results early - check.
You go above and beyond - check.

And you're still waiting to be noticed.

All because your boss is busy. 

They're not tracking your output the way you think they are.

If you want visibility, you have to create it.

Start here:

1️⃣ Track your wins weekly
2️⃣ Tie each win to a business result
3️⃣ Send a regular summary to your manager

Think of it like this: you're not bragging. 

You're briefing them on progress they need to know in order to do their job better.

It works in your favor on two fronts. 

You stop relying on assumptions and start owning your progress with confidence. 

And your manager's life gets easier because they're no longer chasing you for updates.

While quiet excellence is great for the company, it won't get you promoted any faster.

Visible impact will, 100% of the time.

What's one win you haven't shared with your boss yet?
