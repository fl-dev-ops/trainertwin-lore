You deserve the right to be selfish.

Especially as a woman.

Early in my career on the trading floor, I had to learn what it really meant to go after what you want.

When I joined, I was naive about the game being played.

I believed working hard, keeping my head down, and trusting my boss to advance me would be enough.

Truthfully, I was just grateful to be in the room.

Playing in the same arena as the “boys” already felt like a win.

So asking for more?

That felt needy.

So I waited.

And hoped growth would come.

Then I watched peers, performing at a similar level, get promoted ahead of me.

That’s when it clicked.

If you’re not advocating for yourself, you’re standing on the sidelines.

I stopped thinking about “selfish” through that old lens.

And started owning it.

Here’s what followed:

→ Secured a 6-figure increase in my bonus 
→ Promoted to Director (second-highest title) 
→ Recognized with the “Executive Leader” award 
→ Successfully traded one of the riskiest products → Led mentorship for future leaders in banking

None of that happened because someone finally noticed my hard work.

It happened because I stopped waiting to be chosen.

If you’re not getting the results you want in your career, a new company won’t automatically fix it.

The same ceiling tends to follow you.

Until you decide to break through it.

So here’s the real question:

Where in your career are you still waiting to be picked?
