Most people ask for a raise the wrong way.

They hope their hard work speaks for itself.

They assume their boss will recognize their value.

They wait for the “right time” and end up waiting forever.

Here’s how my clients skip the pause and get right into action:

1️⃣ Build your case before you ask.

Your raise isn’t about what you ‘deserve’ - it’s about the value you bring.

2️⃣ Pick the right timing.

Which is much sooner than you think. Don’t wait until your annual review to start the conversation.

3️⃣ Make the conversation strategic.

Lead with data, not emotion. Show the results your work has delivered.

4️⃣ Have a plan for ‘no.’

Not every ‘no’ is final. Trying to see it as a ‘not yet’ instead.

A no today doesn’t mean no forever. Treat it as a ‘not yet’ and get clarity on the next steps.

Raises don’t happen by accident.

When you approach them strategically, your chances of success increase dramatically.
