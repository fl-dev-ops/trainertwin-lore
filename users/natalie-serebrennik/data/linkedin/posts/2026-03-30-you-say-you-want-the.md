You say you want the next level.

But if you’re honest…

You’re not sure you want your boss’s job.

Not fully.

You see what comes with it.

The politics.
The pressure.
The lack of off-switch.

And part of you pulls back.

Quietly.

So you stay where you are.

Still capable of more.
Still thinking about what’s next.

But not fully going after it.

Because wanting the title is easy.

Wanting the reality of it?

That’s different.

And most people never admit that tension to themselves.

They just label it as “I’m stuck.”

When really…

They haven’t decided what they actually want.

Because here’s the part we don’t talk about enough:

We don’t revisit our plans.

The direction you chose years ago…
You keep following it by default.

Even though you’re not the same person anymore.

Different values.
Different priorities.
Different definition of success.

And yet the goal stays the same.

Out of habit.
Out of expectation.
Out of not stopping long enough to question it.

But you owe it to yourself to pause and ask:

Do I actually want this?

Or have I just been moving toward it for so long that I forgot to reassess?

Because the moment you get honest…

There’s a kind of relief that comes with it.

Clarity replaces pressure.
Direction replaces confusion.

And sometimes, that honesty leads to bigger changes than you expected.

I’ve seen it with my clients:

Sales Trader → Entrepreneur
Finance Director → Wedding Planner
VP of Operations → Boutique Owner

Different paths, same decision:

They stopped chasing what they thought they should want…

And chose what they actually did.

What advice would you give to someone on the cusp of a change?
