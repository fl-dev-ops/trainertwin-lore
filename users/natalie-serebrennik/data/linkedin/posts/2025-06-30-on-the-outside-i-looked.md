On the outside I looked like a confident leader.

On the inside:

→ I thought I wasn’t good enough to be an executive.
→ I was afraid of exposing I wasn’t ready for it.
→ I left every meeting hoping I didn’t fail.

Instead of trusting my skills, I poured all my energy into proving I deserved the seat I’d already earned.

That fear pushed me to:

Over-deliver. Over-work. And over-stress myself.

Sound familiar?

With friends, I cancelled plans more than I wanted.

On vacations, I kept thinking about work.

At home, my laptop was open during movie nights with my husband.

I focused so much on not disappointing my boss, my team, and my peers…

That I didn’t realize the person I was disappointing most was… me.

Take a moment right now to:

→ Trust your strengths.
→ Learn from your challenges.
→ Appreciate how far you’ve come.

If you're doubting yourself right now, please reminder:

Imposter syndrome feeds on self-doubt.

Give it a bite, and it’ll have the whole slice.

You earned your seat. You’re there for a reason.

Don’t waste energy second-guessing it. 

Trust in your abilities and channel that energy into your growth instead.


Right now, there’s a leader doubting themselves. What would you tell them?
