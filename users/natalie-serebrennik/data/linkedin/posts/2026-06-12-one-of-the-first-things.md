One of the first things you learn on the trading floor is KYC.

Know your client.

Officially, it’s a compliance requirement.

Unofficially, it teaches you something far more valuable:

Know who you’re speaking to, what they care about, and adjust your message accordingly before you open your mouth.

I carried that principle into client conversations and senior discussions for 17 years.

And when I moved into coaching, I realized how uncommon this practice was.

The same leaders who would never pitch a client without understanding their priorities first…

Walk into conversations with senior leadership using the exact same communication style they use with their team.

Same level of detail.
Same messaging.
Same delivery.

And then they wonder why certain messages never land.

KYC doesn’t stop at the client relationship.

It applies to every conversation that shapes your career.

With senior leadership: Get to the insight before they ask for it.

They need to hear:

A quick context opener, what your recommendation is, and the potential risks at play. 

With peers: Speak to shared priorities, not just your own.

Buy-in comes faster when people understand how your idea helps them too.

With your team: Clarity matters more than polish.

People need to understand exactly what matters, what’s expected, and why.

Same message.

Three completely different conversations.

Master this one skill and buy-in stops being something you work for.

It becomes something you naturally create, one conversation at a time.
