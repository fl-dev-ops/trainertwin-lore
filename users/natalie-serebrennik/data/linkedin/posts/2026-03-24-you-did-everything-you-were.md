You did everything you were told would get you promoted.

And still…

Someone else got the opportunity.

Again.

It’s frustrating.
It’s painful.
It hurts.

Because it doesn’t feel fair.

But here’s the part most people don’t want to hear.

Promotions aren’t just about performance.

They’re about perception.

And if decision-makers don’t see you as ready…

Your results won’t be enough to convince them.

Too many highly capable workhorses fall into this trap.

They assume their work will speak for itself.

Meanwhile, others are playing a different game entirely:

Positioning their impact.
Speaking to business priorities.
Making their results and thinking visible.

Same capability but different visibility.

So if you’ve been overlooked, don’t just ask:
“What more do I need to do?”

Ask:
“How am I being seen?”

Because that’s the question that actually changes outcomes.

I’ve coached 100s of leaders and this pattern keeps showing up.

And honestly, this is one of my favorite patterns to break with clients.

We convince ourselves that getting promoted requires some massive overhaul.

That it’s going to be long, complicated, and out of reach.

But it’s not.

What actually moves the needle is much simpler.

A clear strategy with focused behaviour changes and consistency in how you show up.

It’s incredible how quickly things shift when you start being seen differently.
