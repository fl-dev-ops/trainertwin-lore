I watched someone less qualified get the promotion I wanted.

And I wasn’t even surprised.

They didn’t put in more hours.
They didn’t have stronger results.
They weren’t the “obvious” choice.

But they knew how to do something I wasn’t doing: 
→ advocating for themselves.

I, on the other hand, spent years in complete denial:

Assuming my work would speak for itself.
Assuming promotions were based on merit.
Assuming leadership would notice my contributions.

I was wrong.

I ignored the reality of how the game is played.

The people who move up the fastest aren’t always the most qualified.

They’re the ones who:

➝ Make their impact seen and heard
➝ Position themselves for bigger opportunities
➝ Ensure leadership knows exactly how they want to grow

My a-ha moment was born out of frustration and anger.

You can choose better for yourself.

The only perfect time to advocate for yourself is right now.

Not when you want something.
Not when you finally feel ready.

Start before you think you need to.

➝ Share more of your ideas
➝ Discuss the growth you crave
➝ Advocate for your wins like never before

Because when self-advocacy becomes a life practice, this is what you can expect:

➝ New opportunities become reality
➝ Promotions and raises become inevitable
➝ Your reputation and visibility become powerful

You’ve worked hard enough.

And if the results aren’t there yet, it’s time to focus on strategic self-advocacy.
