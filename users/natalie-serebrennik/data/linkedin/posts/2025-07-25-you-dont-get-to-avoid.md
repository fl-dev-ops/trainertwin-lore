You don’t get to avoid hard if growth is your goal.

Building my success at the bank was far from ‘easy.’

→ A woman in a sea of men
→ Constantly advocating for my worth
→ Saying yes to roles that terrified me
→ Battling imposter syndrome behind a smile

Starting my own business was equally challenging.

→ Questioning my value every single day
→ Speaking to many but is anyone listening?
→ Worrying if my offer and content are good enough

And I can’t forget:

The gut punch I felt when I realized that to do what I love (coaching)

I also had to wear hats I didn’t sign up for. 

Like digital marketing, accounting, ops, and don't get me started with IT.

And yet, I wouldn’t trade these challenges for anything.

Why?

→ Each hard pushed me out of my comfort zone
→ Each hard opened doors I didn’t know existed
→ Each hard taught me what I’m truly capable of

I now approach new experiences with this mindset:

Hard is unavoidable.

The question isn’t if you’ll face it. 

It’s whether you’ll choose it for yourself or let someone else decide for you.

My advice → choose for yourself.

Choose the hard that stretches you.
Choose the hard that aligns with your goals.
Choose the hard that’s for you and no one else.

Just because the next step forward feels challenging doesn’t mean it won’t come with the exact growth you need.

So, what hard are you choosing right now?
