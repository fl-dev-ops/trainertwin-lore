It's already Q3.

And the promotion you were so sure would happen by year-end still hasn't.

I want to ask you something, not as tough love, just as a real, honest question:

What have you actually done differently this year to make that happen?

Not the work. I know the work has been strong. 

I'm asking about the strategy underneath it, the conversations you've had, the visibility you've built, the case you've made for why you're ready.

If the honest answer is "not much," that's worth sitting with. Not as a failure. As information.

Because hoping the timeline will resolve itself rarely changes anything. 

And waiting for the right moment has a way of turning one quarter into the next, indefinitely.

The leaders I work with who see the most success in their career acceleration aren't the ones who work the hardest in the final stretch.

They're the ones who get honest, early, about what they're actually doing to position themselves - and then build a real strategy around it.

There's still time left this year. Not a lot. But enough.

Especially because promotion conversations will be starting in just a few short months.

What would you need to do differently in the next 90 days to walk into your performance review with momentum instead of regurgitating the same conversation you had in January of this year?
