Stop letting other people's fear dilute your ambition.

You know the lines:

"Be grateful for what you have, others would kill for it."

"Do you really want to face possible rejection?"

"What if asking makes things worse for you?"

It sounds like concern.

But it's projection.

I've had my fair share of lessons over the years. 

This one stuck.

Early in my career, I went to a senior leader I deeply respected for advice on how to phrase my ask for more.

He told me to wait.

The bank wasn't having a good year. The timing wasn't right. He didn't want me to get my hopes up.

I walked away deflated and convinced myself he knew better.

It took me years to realize that this particular leader had never once pushed hard for more himself. 

He had stayed comfortable in the same seat for a long time, never made the uncomfortable ask, and never had to wonder what could have been.

He wasn't protecting me.

He was projecting his own relationship with ambition onto mine.

And I almost let it stop me.

Here's the thing, it's never a good time to ask.

There's always a slow quarter. Always a reorg. 

Always something giving you a reason to wait one more year.

But all that wait does is chip away at that beautiful ambition. 

Because as you wait, you're still watching others move ahead, and you can’t help comparing yourself to their progress and ask, “what are they doing that I’m not?” 

And the answer is simple, they’re not waiting. 

They make their ask, regardless of timing. 

That’s the flex. 

What's the worst thing someone said to you when you expressed your ambition?
