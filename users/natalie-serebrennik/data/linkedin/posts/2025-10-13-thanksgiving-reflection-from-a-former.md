Thanksgiving reflection from a former corporate high-achiever:

There are things I appreciate deeply now…

That I never thought to notice back then.

Not because I was unhappy.

I loved the pace, the challenge, the people.

But I was so focused on the next client meeting, next deadline, next deal…

I didn’t always pause to take it all in.

So this year, I’m giving thanks for the things I didn’t know how to value when I was deep in the game:

1) A clear calendar. Where “white space” isn’t a red flag - it’s breathing room.

2) Work that matches my values, not just my strengths. Because just being good at something isn’t a reason to stay.

3) Conversations that go beneath the surface. I’ll take honest reflection over corporate small talk any day.

4) Decisions made from alignment, not just obligation. There’s power in choosing what you say yes to and what you don’t.

5) Supporting high performers in a different way now. Not just helping them do more - but helping them lead with greater confidence and yes, even joy.

I didn’t have this kind of clarity back then.

But I don’t regret it for a second.

Every 7am morning call. 
Every late-night client dinner. 
Every painful mistake and tear shed. 

It shaped how I coach, what I teach and who I am today.
