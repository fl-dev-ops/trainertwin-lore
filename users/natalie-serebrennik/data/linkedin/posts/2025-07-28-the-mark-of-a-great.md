The mark of a great leader is this:

Having tough conversations.

The better someone is at it, the more times they’ve done it.

Each one a notch in your belt.

Ask any great leader and they’ll cringe at the memory of their first “feedback session”.

→ It was clunky
→ It was awkward
→ It was the wrong energy
→ It left everyone uncomfortable

But quickly, they learned how to:

→ Make it more graceful
→ Make it more comfortable
→ Make it more professional and kind
→ Make it more grounded for everyone involved

If you’re telling yourself “I’m just bad at this” 

Please stop.

It’s not a personality trait. It’s a skill.

And it’s one of the most promotable ones you can build.
