Don’t fall into this trap if you’re working hard for that next promotion.

You don’t get promoted because you want one.

And the trap most fall into is assuming they need to make the ask about them.

But your growth isn’t just about you.

→ It’s about the team
→ It’s about the business
→ It’s about the results you deliver

If you’re eyeing that next promotion, here’s how to stand out:

1️⃣ Show how your promotion will help the team solve current challenges.

2️⃣ Highlight how your leadership will drive the business forward.

3️⃣ Tie your contributions to tangible results.

The goal is to make your growth a strategic advantage for everyone, and not just you.

This is what makes leaders promotable.

When leadership sees how your success elevates the bigger picture, saying yes becomes a no-brainer.
