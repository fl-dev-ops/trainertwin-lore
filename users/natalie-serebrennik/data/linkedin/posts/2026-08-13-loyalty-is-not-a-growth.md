Loyalty is not a growth strategy.

And this is coming from someone who spent 17 years at the same company.

Staying at the same company.
Reporting to the same management.
Doing the same-ish work year after year.

That mindset is keeping way too many talented professionals stuck.

Because companies benefit from your loyalty… until it's no longer convenient for them.

You can be the go-to.
The one who gets it done.
The one who's always reliable.

But if you're not actively shaping how your role evolves or how your leadership is seen…

You're making it very easy for people to overlook you.

I'm not saying you need to jump ship.

But stop waiting for growth to happen for you.

Stop thinking your loyalty will get you to the next level.

You've earned too much to still be playing by the rules you inherited when you were just getting started.

At this stage of the game, waiting isn't humility.

It's a trap.

I know this frustration well. I lived in it longer than I should have.

And the moment I stopped waiting and started advocating, everything changed.
