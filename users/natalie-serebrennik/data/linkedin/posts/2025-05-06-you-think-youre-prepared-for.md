You think you're prepared for that meeting, but have you done this?

Most people prepare like this:

→ Finalize the deck
→ Rehearse the key points
→ Anticipate a few tough questions

Which is all good, but...

If you want to build real influence, that’s not enough.

Here’s what most often skip:
→ Set the stage before the meeting even starts.

Don’t wait for the meeting to make your point. 
Shape the narrative ahead of time.

How?

Like this:

1) A quick team’s message.

“Looking forward to walking through X tomorrow. I’m confident the numbers will shift the conversation.”

2) A 2-minute hallway update.

“Wanted to give you a heads up on what I’ll cover. The early results are compelling and will impact our decision to move forward.”

3) A short pre-read with a summary.

“This gives some helpful context ahead of tomorrow’s meeting. I was particularly surprised to learn X. Let me know if anything stands out to you.”

This isn’t overkill.

It’s strategy.

You’re managing perceptions, setting expectations, and building confidence before anyone enters the room.

By the time the meeting starts, they’re not just listening - they’re already leaning in.

And if you’ve been in leadership for long enough, you know - the sooner you can secure buy-in, the better.
