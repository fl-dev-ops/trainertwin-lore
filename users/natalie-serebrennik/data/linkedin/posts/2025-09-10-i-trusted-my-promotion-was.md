I trusted my promotion was coming.

And then I went on mat leave…

Big mistake.

(Not the mat leave. The mistake was trusting my manager.)

When I came back to work after doing the most miraculous thing of my life…

I learned my promotion readiness had been wiped to zero.

No conversations.
No future plans.
Just confusion.

And the worst part? I let it happen.

I accepted the same excuse:
"Next year, we’ll make it right.”

When it was time to go on my second mat leave, I knew better.

This time, I came back with a strategy:

→ I made my ask. Boldly.
→ I laid out my impact and exactly why I was ready.
→ I refused to accept ‘we’ll see what happens’ as an answer.

That year, I secured my next title.

The lesson is clear:

Your growth won’t happen just because you deserve it.

(If it did, you’d already have the next title.)

It happens because you make it happen.

If you want to move up, start here:

1️⃣ Own each result you’ve created.
2️⃣ Communicate it with urgency.
3️⃣ Ask for what you want - without apology.
4️⃣ Have this conversation with every decision maker.

Don’t waste years like I did.
