I applied for an industry leadership award and like most women, 

I aimed way too low.

Not because I wasn’t qualified.
Not because I wasn’t ready.

But because I convinced myself I wasn’t.

I convinced myself the top award:

❌ Was too competitive
❌ Had too many talented leaders in the running
❌ There was no way I’d be chosen over them

Turns out, the selection committee disagreed.

They saw what I didn’t.

They pushed me to go for the highest tier: 

The Executive Leader Award.

And… I got it.

The only one at BMO to receive it that year.

Getting this award transformed my confidence.
It positioned me as a player to be seen.
And caught the eyes of upper mgmt.

So much so that…

→ I was gifted a bottle of Dom P
→ Got courtside basketball tickets 
→ And sat down 1:1 with the Head of Capital Markets to be personally congratulated

I was blown away.

Never imagined I’d be met with this kind of senior leadership generosity. 

The part that sticks with me the most is… 

I almost didn’t even try.

How many times have I been the one holding myself back?

How many times have I assumed I wasn’t ready, when I actually was?

This experience taught me a lesson I won’t forget:

You don’t need to believe in yourself 100% to take action.

✅ Apply for the higher title
✅ Go for the bigger opportunity
✅ Trust others can see your potential, even before you do

Because the biggest opportunities don’t just go to the most qualified.

They go to the ones willing to go for it.
