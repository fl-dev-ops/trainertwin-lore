Most professionals believe this lie:

“If I know enough, I’ll be ready to lead.”

So they do what seems logical.

They collect more expertise.

More certifications.
More technical depth.
More answers in meetings.

Eventually they get promoted.

Then something strange happens.
Their team doesn’t follow them.

Because leadership isn’t about having the most answers.

It’s about creating the conditions where others perform better.

The strongest leaders I’ve worked with weren’t the smartest person in the room.

They were the most intentional.

They did four things consistently:

• Supported individual goals, not just team targets
• Raised standards without creating fear
• Communicated decisions clearly
• Invested real time building trust

Expertise earns credibility.
Connection earns followership.

That’s the difference.

If you’re waiting until you “know enough” before stepping up…

don’t.

Start practising the behaviours that actually make people want to follow you.
