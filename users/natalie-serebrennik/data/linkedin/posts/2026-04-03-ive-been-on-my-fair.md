I’ve been on my fair share of podcasts, but this one hit different.

Caroline N. and I got into something that doesn’t get talked about enough in corporate, what it actually means to be “selfish.”

Not the version that makes people uncomfortable, especially you.

The version where you stop letting your ambition, your time, and your energy serve everyone else’s agenda before your own.

If you’re in corporate and you’ve been doing everything right but the promotion still isn’t happening, this conversation might be exactly what you need to hear.
