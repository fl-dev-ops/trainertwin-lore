Most people wait for visibility.

Here's how my client created it.

He raised his hand to lead a company-wide fundraising initiative, something outside his day-to-day role, but deeply connected to his values.

At first, it just looked like a chance to contribute.

But together, we turned it into a strategic move:

→ We identified two senior leaders who were involved.

→ Mapped out how to build connection before, during, and after the event.

→ Planned 1:1 debriefs after to reflect, share lessons, and talk about what’s next.

This wasn’t luck.
This was smart visibility in action.

And now those same senior leaders see him differently.

Not just as a strong performer, but as someone worth investing in.

When you’re growing into the next level of leadership, being good at your job isn’t enough.

You need to show up as someone who can lead beyond your lane.

Someone who can connect with people on a shared vision and actively take part in creating it.

This single initiative set up my client for incredible support from the very same decision makers who will be deciding his next step.

This is what growth really looks like.

Small moments, used strategically.

How have you built momentum like this in your own career?
