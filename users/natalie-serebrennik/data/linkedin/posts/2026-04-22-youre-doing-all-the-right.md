You're doing all the right things - except the one that actually gets you promoted.

You've said yes more times than you can count.

You've taken on extra work because the team was under water.

You've mentored talent the way you wished someone had mentored you.

And yet… you’re still in the same seat.

Here’s why: No one can reward the work they don’t see.

I sat with that frustration longer than I should have. 

Watching others move up while I kept delivering, kept contributing, kept waiting for someone to connect the dots on my behalf.

Nobody was connecting them.

The moment I started bringing a voice to my own work, that’s when I actually understood what it meant to be visible. 

First, the conversations changed. 

My skip-level started referencing my work in meetings I wasn't in. I only found out because someone told me after.

Then, the opportunities followed.

I got asked to present at a leadership meeting I had never been included in before.

When the right people see and understand how your contribution is creating value for the rest of the business, that’s when you officially stop leaving your growth to chance. 

What's one way you've made your work more visible at work?
