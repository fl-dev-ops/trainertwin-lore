Not everyone who smiles at you at work has your best interests at heart.

This was a hard lesson I learned early in my career.

I trusted the wrong person.

Someone who seemed friendly, supportive and eager to help me grow.

But when it mattered, they put their agenda first.

And I was the collateral.

I wasn’t just disappointed. 
I was blindsided.

Work relationships aren’t always what they seem.

And no one teaches you to watch your back in corporate.

That work relationships aren’t necessarily built on mutual trust.

And there will be people who straight up don’t want to see you win.

Here are 3 pieces of advice I wish I knew then…

1️⃣ Don’t rely on others to advocate for your growth

2️⃣ Share strategically and avoid oversharing personal struggles

3️⃣ Focus on building relationships with those who’ve proven to have your back, not just those who say they do

If I could go back to younger Natalie, I’d tell her this:

Be smart about who gets a seat at your table.

Build alliances, yes, but don’t forget to ruthlessly advocate for yourself.

Remember, your career is your responsibility.

The less you rely on others to create the growth you desire, the more power you reclaim to create it yourself.


Have you ever experienced this in your career? How did you handle it?
