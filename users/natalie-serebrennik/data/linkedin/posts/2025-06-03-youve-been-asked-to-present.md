You’ve been asked to present your big idea to the leadership team.

You’ve done the work. You know the numbers.

But the moment the attention shifts to you…

Your heart races.
Your mouth goes dry.
You wonder if you’re making any sense.

That spiral of second-guessing is more common than you think.

It happens to the best of us and no, it doesn’t mean you’re not ready.

Many of my clients (smart, seasoned professionals) feel this exact pressure when all eyes are on them.

Here’s what I coach them to do differently:

1️⃣ Narrow your focus

You don’t need to win over the whole room. Just speak to the person whose buy-in matters most.

2️⃣ Anchor to outcomes

What’s the one key message you need them to remember? Build around that and cut the rest.

3️⃣ Use the pause

It’s not awkward. It’s actually powerful. It signals control and helps your message land.

Confidence in moments like these comes from intention and repetition.
