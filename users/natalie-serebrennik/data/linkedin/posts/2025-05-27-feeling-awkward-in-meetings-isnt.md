Feeling awkward in meetings isn’t a personality flaw.

It’s a skill gap and here’s how you can fix it.

High-achieving professionals end up here all the time:

→ Holding back until the “right” moment
→ Overthinking every word before speaking
→ Letting others take the lead in conversations they’re more than qualified to weigh in on

Yep, even high-achievers get in their head.

Whether you’re introverted or extroverted, speaking up in meetings isn’t always easy.

You’ve got great ideas to share.

But no one ever taught you how to show up strategically in a room that wasn’t built for you.

Here’s what I coach clients to do differently:

1️⃣ Prepare your point in advance

One clear, outcome-focused message. Not a brain dump. When you're clear, your delivery follows.

2️⃣ Speak earlier, not later

Waiting for the perfect moment often means you miss it. Add value early, even if it’s just framing the problem.

3️⃣ Use language that signals leadership

Say “Here’s what I recommend” instead of “I think maybe...” Your words shape your confidence… even if you don’t fully feel it yet.

That room might not feel like it was made for you.

But the moment you decide to show up anyway is the moment the room starts to adjust to you.
