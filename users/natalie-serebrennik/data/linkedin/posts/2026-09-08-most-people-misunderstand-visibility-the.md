Most people misunderstand visibility.

They think it's about volume.

More DMs.
More coffees.
More LinkedIn connections.

Real visibility is way more about being remembered than being seen.

The goal isn't to show up everywhere. It's to show up in the right places, for the right people, in a way they actually remember when it counts.

If you're networking just to check a box, you're wasting your time.

What actually builds career leverage:

- Following up when there's no obvious ROI
- Staying consistent after the first conversation
- Sharing relevant wins without bragging about it
- Asking better questions than "can I pick your brain?"

None of these require a bigger network. Successful networking has never been a numbers game.

They require a more intentional one, where the people you invest time in actually invest it back when you need it most.
