Getting up close and personal with your limitations is as real as it gets.

When Priya and I started working together, she was doing everything 'right on paper'.

Senior Director.
Strong performance.
Even new responsibilities.

But she still hit a wall internally.

Her boss labeled her “high performer, low potential.”

No real feedback.
No clear path forward.

It stung and it left her confused.

And the thought of moving forward... sucked.

- The lack of hope
- The built-up frustration
- The discouraging mindset

If you've ever been in her shoes, you know how HARD growth feels in that moment.

So in our first few sessions, we focused on her reset.

Mindset and clarity.

Not just around her goals, but around how she was framing the situation.

No more "waiting to be recognized.”

We mapped out a few subtle ways to start doing that:

- Defined how she wanted to grow 
- Identified new areas she wanted to explore
- Shortlisted contacts to start those conversations

No big LinkedIn post.
No sudden spotlight moment.

Just small, strategic moves, made consistently.

And we’ve only just begun.

We all love to complicate the journey.

Pile on the to-dos, overthink the next step, question whether it’s the right one.

But sometimes, all you really need is a new perspective.

My sweet spot as a coach is helping you simplify the process.

Because when this happens, all you need to focus on is your next step forward.

And that’s exactly what she’s doing now.


P.S. Wild how “high performer” and “no feedback” are allowed to exist in the same sentence. Anyone else over confusing and nonconstructive feedback?
