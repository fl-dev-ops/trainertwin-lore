Being reliable doesn’t make you promotable.

It keeps you adding value exactly where you are.

Let me guess…

You’re the one who jumps in when things fall apart.
Who keeps it all together behind the scenes.
Who everyone trusts to handle the mess.

And that’s the trap.

Because the more you become “the one they can count on,”

The less they imagine you doing anything else.

The skills that make you reliable:

→ responsiveness and attention to detail 

Aren’t the same ones that get you promoted from mid to senior titles.

If you want to grow, you have to shift from being the one who executes to the one who leads.

Start here:

→ Instead of solving the same problem repeatedly, pitch a process that prevents it

→ Instead of being the one who triple-checks, assign it and review only the outcome

→ Instead of waiting to be looped in, proactively flag risks and recommend next steps

You’re not here to prove you can hold it all together.

You’ve already done that.

You’re here to lead at a different level and get recognized for it.
