I trusted my promotion was coming.

And then I went on mat leave.

Big mistake.

(Not the mat leave. The mistake was trusting my manager.)

When I came back after doing the most miraculous thing of my life, I learned my promotion readiness had been wiped to zero.

No conversations.
No future plans.

Just confusion.

And the worst part? I let it happen.

I accepted the same excuse every time it came up.
"Next year, we'll make it right."

Year after year, I kept delivering. Kept assuming someone was tracking my progress. Kept believing that the work would eventually speak for itself.

It didn't.

And I was too focused on proving myself inside my role to realize that nobody was advocating for me outside of it.

When it was time to go on my second mat leave, I knew I couldn't come back to the same situation.

So I didn't leave it to chance.

Before I left, I had the conversation I should have had years earlier. 

I told my manager exactly where I expected to be when I returned. I documented my impact. I made my intentions known.

And when I came back, I didn't wait to be noticed all over again.

I made my ask. Boldly.

I reminded him of my impact and exactly why I was ready for that next title.

I refused to accept "we'll see what happens" as an answer.

That year, I secured my next title.

Because I finally stopped leaving my growth in someone else's hands.

Here's what I know now that I wish I had known then:

You don't get promoted just because you've earned it. 

The promotion comes when you make your value impossible to overlook and your intentions impossible to ignore.

That means owning every result you've created, communicating it with clarity, and having that conversation with every decision-maker who has a say in your future - without waiting for the perfect moment.

Because there's no such thing.

You only have the moments you intentionally create.

Don't wait until after you finish the next project. 
Don't wait until your next performance review. 
Don't wait until you feel ready enough to ask.

Start today.
