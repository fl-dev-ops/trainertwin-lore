Something feels off at work.

You can’t quite explain it.

On paper, nothing’s wrong.

You’re still delivering.
Still doing what’s expected of you.

But internally you don't feel as sharp.

You hesitate more.
You second-guess things you used to say with certainty.

And that gap starts to grow.

Between who you used to be at work…
And who you feel like now.

Most people assume this feeling means something is wrong with them.

It doesn’t.

It usually means you’ve outgrown the version of yourself that got you here.

But you haven’t fully stepped into the next one yet.

And that in-between space can be very uncomfortable.

Because your old ways don’t feel right anymore.
But your new way isn’t fully formed.

So you sit there…

Still capable but lost in the 'meh'.

And that’s where confidence dips.

Not because you’ve lost it.

But because you’re on the cusp of rebuilding it at a new level.

The mistake is thinking you need to “get back” to who you were.

You don’t.

You just need to decide who you’re becoming next.
