Most high-achievers won’t say this out loud...

But every time they land a new project, take on a bigger role, or step into a more senior room…

They feel like they have to prove themselves all over again.

It doesn’t matter:

→ How strong their track record is
→ How many wins they’ve delivered
→ How much respect they’ve earned

The pressure to re-prove themselves creeps back in.

I’ve coached many leaders through this, and I get it, because I still struggle with this reality myself from time to time. 

It’s so easy to look confident from the outside. 

But on the inside - you’re stuck in a cycle of re-earning what you’ve already proven.

Here’s a shift that continues to help my clients (and myself):

→ You’re not starting over. You’re building on what you’ve already earned.

And when you operate from that mindset, your confidence hits different.

Here’s how to apply it:

1️⃣ Pull the receipts. Track wins, impact, and credibility you’ve built in a work journal. 

2️⃣ Lead with your expertise. When stepping into a new room/role, speak from what you already know. Don’t downplay it to sound agreeable.

3️⃣ Stop apologizing for your growth. You’re not “lucky to be here.” You worked for this. Stand in it.

This is your biggest opportunity for growth:

Refusing to waste anymore time re-proving yourself.

Instead, invest that time into positioning yourself strategically with the experience and skills you already have.
