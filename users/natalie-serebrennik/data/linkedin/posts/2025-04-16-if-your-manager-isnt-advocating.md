If your manager isn’t advocating for you, you’ve got two options:

Wait for them to notice.
Or make yourself impossible to ignore.

Most people wait.

They assume:

→ Hard work will speak for itself
→ Their boss will drive their growth
→ Recognition will come… eventually

But we both know better.

Doing more of the same won’t get you different results.

You don’t land that senior title because you’re good at the work.

You land it when the right people see and value it.

Here’s a good place to start:

→ When was the last time you clearly stated what you want next?

→ When was the last time you shared your impact, beyond the day-to-day wins?

→ When was the last time you positioned yourself for a role that doesn’t even exist yet?

The biggest shift in my career came when I stopped waiting.

When I realized my growth wasn’t my manager’s job.

It was mine.

Because when you take full ownership of your growth, everything accelerates.
