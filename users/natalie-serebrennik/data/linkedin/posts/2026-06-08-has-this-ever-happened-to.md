Has this ever happened to you?

The performance review was perfect.

No critical feedback. 

No areas of concern. 

Every box checked, every rating strong, every comment positive.

And I walked out with absolutely no idea what came next.

That used to happen to me more than I'd like to admit. 

A conversation that was technically excellent and completely useless.

Because it confirmed everything was fine without telling me anything about where fine was supposed to lead.

The absence of bad news is not a development plan.

And yet so many high performers treat a clean review as evidence that the system is working for them. 

That if things were really going to change, someone would have said something by now.

The system is not designed to tell you what you need to hear. 

It's designed to document what happened.

The direction has to come from somewhere else.

From understanding what you're building toward, making that explicit to the people who can influence it, and treating your own career trajectory as something you manage actively rather than something that reveals itself over time.

A clean review means you're doing the job well.

It says nothing about where you're going next.
