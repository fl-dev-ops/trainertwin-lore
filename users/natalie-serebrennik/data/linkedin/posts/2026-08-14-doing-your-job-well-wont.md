Doing your job well won't get you promoted.

Best case, you keep your job.

Worst case, you become stagnant in their eyes and your pay starts to reflect it.

Who's they?

The leaders who decide your growth.
The ones who approve or reject your promotion.

Fair? No.

But it still happens every day.

That's why when my clients are preparing for a promotion, we don't just focus on their hard work or the results they've achieved.

We focus on making sure the right people understand the full scope of their value AND why it matters to the business.

Here's where we start:

1️⃣ Take initiative. 

Lead without waiting for permission. The people who get promoted are already operating at the next level before anyone officially says they're ready.

2️⃣ Solve business problems. 

Don't default to "not my lane." The leaders who advance are the ones willing to step into the gaps nobody else is touching.

3️⃣ Make your thinking visible. 

They can't advocate for what they can't see. Stop saving your best ideas for after the meeting.
