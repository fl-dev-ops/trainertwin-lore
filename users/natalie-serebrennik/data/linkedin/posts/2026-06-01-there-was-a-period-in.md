There was a period in my career where I was frustrated with almost everyone around me.

My manager wasn't advocating hard enough. 

My peers were getting opportunities I thought I deserved.

The environment didn’t seem designed to recognize what I was bringing.

And I wasn’t entirely wrong about any of it.

But I was using all of it to avoid the one question that actually mattered:

What was my role in this pattern?

That was the shift.

Because the moment I stopped looking outward for the explanation and started looking inward for the common denominator…

The path forward became much clearer.

I realized I had become passive in my own growth.

Waiting to be recognized.

Waiting to be chosen.

Waiting for someone else to create momentum for me.

That awareness was uncomfortable.

But it was also empowering.

Because once you recognize your role in the pattern, you regain influence over the outcome.

The ones who stay stuck longest are usually the ones still waiting for others to change first.

The ones who move fastest eventually ask themselves a different question:

“What would this situation look like if I started participating in changing it?”

That question changed far more in my career than any promotion ever did.

What advice would you give to someone who’s ready to embark on this self-discovery journey?
