Telling someone to “be more confident” is lazy advice.

It assumes confidence is a mindset issue, not a skill gap.

It blames the individual instead of examining the situation.

It ignores the reality that many high-achievers were never taught how to build it.

Confidence isn’t a light switch.
You don’t just flip it on because you’re told to.

It’s a byproduct of clarity and repetition.

If you want to feel more confident:

1️⃣ Get clear on what you stand for - so you stop shrinking when challenged

2️⃣ Learn how to communicate it - so people actually hear and respect you

3️⃣ Practice in real-time moments - until it becomes second nature 

Confidence is a muscle.

And no one gets stronger by being told to just try harder.

If you’ve been working hard but still feel overlooked or undervalued - confidence isn’t the issue.

You just haven’t been taught how to lead with it yet.
