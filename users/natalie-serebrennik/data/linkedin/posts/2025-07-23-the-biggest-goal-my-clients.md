The biggest goal my clients have:

Becoming more strategic.

It’s one of the most intimidating skills to learn because of 1 reason:

It requires you to kill an old version of yourself.

The individual contributor.

What got you here won’t get you there.

Being the one who:

→ Dives into a project without assessing its impact
→ Gets lost in the weeds of a big initiative
→ “Saves” your team from extra work

Will keep you from growing.

Why?

Because leadership growth comes when you learn to focus on how your work connects to the bigger picture.

You know you’ve achieved it when you’re handed a project from your boss and can immediately start mind mapping how it’ll fit into the big picture.

Your first thoughts become:

→ Who can I involve to amplify the results?
→ How does this align with company goals?
→ What’s the long-term impact of this project?

Do it enough times and your promotions become unlimited.

What do you no longer miss about being an IC?
