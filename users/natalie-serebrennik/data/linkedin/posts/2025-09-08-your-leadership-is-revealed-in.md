Your leadership is revealed in:

→ How you command the room
→ How you carry yourself
→ How you sit

These behaviors tell a story.

And that story = your executive presence.

If the phrase “executive presence” feels intimidating, you’re not alone.

That doubt creeps in, making you wonder:

→ Am I coming across as confident or unsure?
→ Did I lose them halfway through my idea?
→ Do I even belong in this room?

But as a leader, you can’t let insecurity hold you back.

You have too much to achieve. An impact to build.

So, what’s really stopping you?

→ Overthinking how others perceive you
→ Convincing yourself you’re not “good at it”

It’s time to shift that narrative.

Start small, with these intentional behaviors:

1️⃣ Sit with confidence.

Take up space at the table - you’ve earned your seat.

2️⃣ Pause before you speak.

That silence draws the room in and commands attention.

3️⃣ Keep it clear.

Short, impactful points connect with the audience more easily.


The key to your success is progress.

→ Every meeting
→ Every decision
→ Every interaction

Is an opportunity to practice and refine your presence.

When you focus on what you can control and commit to 1% growth each day, the impact you’ll create will speak for itself.
