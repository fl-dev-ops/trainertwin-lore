I see this all the time with high-achievers:

Quietly outgrowing your dream role.

That's exactly what my client felt before he reached out to work with me.

He had the title. He had the comp. He also had the team’s respect.

But something was still off.

He had this quiet, nagging feeling that he was showing up to a job he no longer cared about.

It was hard to admit, out loud, that he had outgrown his role, despite feeling this way for years.

Why?

Because the moment you say it out loud, the question creeps in: so what are you going to do about it?

And that part feels paralyzing.

He couldn't keep pretending to his wife, his colleagues, his boss that this version of success still excited him. 

He'd been in the same role for 15 years. 

He'd built a strong brand and wasn't sure how to navigate change without risking everything he had earned.

I knew that feeling well. It was exactly where I was before I made my own pivot.

It was this phrase I shared that gave him the courage to try:

"The real risk isn't what you'll lose if you make a change. It's what you'll miss out on if you don't."

The same phrase that lives on a sticky in my office as a good reminder.

When you're deep in the day-to-day, it's nearly impossible to zoom out and imagine what could be next. 

Especially when you're already successful.

It's easy to justify the discomfort: 

I'm lucky to have this job and this title.
Maybe I'm just going through a rough patch.
Now's not the right time to make a change.

But deep down, he knew the truth.

So we focused our energy where it mattered: clarity.

We uncovered what still lit him up, what he had outgrown, and what kind of leadership impact he actually wanted next.

Only two sessions in and the shift was undeniable.

He felt re-energized. He had a clear, aligned direction. He started actively shaping his next level of growth instead of quietly enduring the current one.

He got his spark back.

And if you’ve been at this game long enough, you know - that spark is everything.

If you've been quietly questioning your next move, this is your sign.

You don’t have to stay stuck. You just need clarity.
