You can be the smartest person in the room.

But if you don’t own your presence:

Your ideas won’t land and your influence won’t grow.

People will only take you as seriously as you take yourself.

Read that again.

People will only take you as seriously as you take yourself.

After years of feeling invisible in my fast-paced capital markets career, I didn’t want to be the unseen, unheard, and unvalued “hard worker”.

That title started to give me the ick.

What was I working so hard for, if I was miserable by the time dinner rolled around and I couldn’t even enjoy it with my husband and children?

Owning my career presence saved my family life, and it saved me from resentment.

Now, I teach this mindset shift to all of my clients:

“Stop seeking permission. Start owning your authority.”

Curious how to start making this shift?

Here are 4 quick ways to see immediate results:

1️⃣ Drop the “just” and “I think.”

↳ “I just wanted to add…” → weakens your point.
↳ “I think we should try…” → undercuts your credibility.
↳ Say it with certainty. Own your expertise.

2️⃣ Speak with intentional pauses.

↳ Rushing through your point makes it feel less important.
↳ Pause. Let your words land.
↳ If you don’t believe your words deserve space, neither will they.

3️⃣ Hold eye contact when you speak.

↳ Looking away = uncertainty.
↳ Direct, steady eye contact = confidence.
↳ If you can’t hold your own gaze, why should others?

4️⃣ Stop waiting for someone else to validate you.

↳ If you act like you need approval, people will assume you do.
↳ Leaders create authority, they don’t wait for it to be given.

The moment you start carrying yourself like the leader you know you can be…

Others start treating you like it.
