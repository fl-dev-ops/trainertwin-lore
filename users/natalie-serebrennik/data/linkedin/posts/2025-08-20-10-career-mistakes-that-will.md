10 career mistakes that will stall your growth

(and how to stop making them today)

1️⃣ Waiting for your manager to advance your career.

↳ Been there. Done that. Doesn’t work.
↳ Get ahead of the conversation early.
↳ Ask: “What gaps do I need to fill to be promoted this year?”

2️⃣ Avoiding tough conversations because you don’t want to ‘ruffle feathers’.

↳ Don’t ask, don’t get.
↳ If speaking up hurts, start small. 
↳ The more you practice, the easier it gets.

3️⃣ Staying in your comfort zone because risk feels scary.

↳ You don’t grow in the status quo.
↳ Pick one challenge you’ve been avoiding and tackle it this week.
↳ You’ll never grow if you continue to overthink each and every action.

4️⃣ Thinking that great work alone is enough to get ahead.

↳ Visibility is what shines a light on your hard work.
↳ Build strategic alliances with those who’ll advocate for you. 
↳ Identify 3 key decision-makers in your company and start finding ways to add value.

5️⃣ Not knowing your market value.

↳ Know your worth early. Never forget it.
↳ Set a calendar reminder every 6m to check again.

6️⃣ Staying in a job you’ve outgrown because figuring out what’s next feels hard.

↳ Complaining won’t move you forward.
↳ Write down 3 things that excite you, or used to.
↳ Follow that curiosity in your research and networking.

7️⃣ Letting people treat you poorly because you don’t like conflict.

↳ No medals for tolerating toxicity.
↳ Next time someone crosses a line, set a boundary.
↳ Even a simple, “I’m not okay with that” works well.

8️⃣ Trusting the wrong people too easily.

↳ Not everyone has your best interests in mind.
↳ Pay attention to who listens, supports, and who gossips.
↳ Trust is earned - not a given.

9️⃣ Keeping your ideas to yourself.

↳ Your voice deserves to be heard.
↳ Speak up at least once per meeting. 
↳ Even if it’s just reinforcing someone else’s point.

🔟 Failing to document your wins.

↳ No one will care about your growth as much as you do.
↳ Start a brag file. Every win, result, milestone. Write it down.
↳ When it’s time to advocate, you’ll be ready.


Which one stings the most?


I’ve made every single mistake on this list.
You’re not alone if you have too. 

Best part is - your future isn’t written yet. You get to decide better for yourself going forward.
