Let’s be honest, not comparing yourself in corporate is a lot easier said than done.

On paper, it makes complete sense.

Everyone’s journey is different.

Everyone’s starting point, manager, and circumstances are unique.

The only real comparison that matters is who you were last year vs. who you are today.

But that’s the logical part.

What about the human part?

Still scrolls LinkedIn wondering how someone else got promoted faster.

Still sits in meetings thinking, “Why are they leading that project and not me?”

Still questions whether you’re moving fast enough (even when you know you’re doing great).

It’s a paradox we all live in.

We know comparison steals joy, but we do it anyway.

Because we care. 
Because we want more. 
Because we’re… human.

So here’s my advice:

Don’t waste energy trying to eliminate comparison. 

Learn to redirect it.

When you notice yourself comparing, pause and ask:

“What is this showing me that I want more of?”

Then channel that energy inward - into developing, positioning, or advocating for yourself differently.

Comparison can crush you if you let it.

Or it can show you exactly where your next level lives.

The choice is yours.
