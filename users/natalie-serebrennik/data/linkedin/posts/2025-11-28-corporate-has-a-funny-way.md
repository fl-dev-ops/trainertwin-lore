Corporate has a funny way of rewarding the performance of leadership more than the practice of it.

You’ve seen it.

The ones who talk a big game in meetings.
The ones who master optics but ignore outcomes.
The ones who know exactly how to show up when the room is watching.

They’re the ones who get promoted.

Meanwhile, the leaders who are actually building strong teams and delivering real results?

They get told to:
"Be more visible.”
“Be more strategic.”
“Be more confident.”

Raise your hand if you’ve ever heard that. 🙋‍♀️

It feels backwards and honestly, it is.

But it’s also the game.

And here’s the part no one says out loud:

You can be exceptional at your job and still be overlooked if you’re not intentional about how you’re perceived.

That’s not a talent issue.

It’s a visibility issue.
A positioning issue.
A strategy issue.

And you’re not wrong for feeling frustrated by it.

But frustration alone won’t move you forward.

So when I start with a client who’s been battling a similar situation, I ask them to make a choice:

You can fight the system.
Or you can learn how to play it on your terms.

Not by faking it.
Not by becoming someone you’re not.

But by being more deliberate with:

→ How you speak about your impact
→ How you show up in high-value rooms
→ How you shape the story leadership sees

This is my sweet spot.
The part of my program my clients love most.

Because it’s tactical.
It’s high-level.
It works.

(Oh hi, my fellow type-As 👋)

You deserve to be seen for the leader you already are.

The one kicking ass and ready to claim that next reward.

Not someday. Now.
