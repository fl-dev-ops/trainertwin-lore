You missed a deadline. Made a bad call. Said the wrong thing in a big room.

Now what?

Here’s how great leaders rebuild trust after a misstep:

1️⃣ Own it.

Don’t explain it away. Acknowledge it early before it festers.

2️⃣ Name the impact.

“I know this put you in a tough position and created extra work.”

3️⃣ Lead the repair. 

Share what you’re doing to fix it and how you’ll prevent it next time.

Us high-achievers put way too much pressure on ourselves to be this perfect person.

The one who doesn’t mess up.
The one who always knows what to say.
The one who brings solutions, not problems.

And I’m over it.

Over the unrealistic standards.
Over the guilt that doesn’t match the mistake.
Over making growth harder than it needs to be.

The best part is - no one’s thinking about your misstep like you are.

They’ve already moved on to thinking about lunch 😂 

Learn the lesson. Then let it go.


What are you over with?
