For the first few years of my career, I was miserable.

And not because the work was hard.

But because I forced myself into a box that didn’t fit.

Quiet office.
Slow pace.
Predictable.

And for a spicy child turned adult, I felt like I was suffocating.

But I convinced myself to ‘suck it up buttercup’. 

I got to yoga at lunch.
I was making 6-figures.
I never had to take work home.

So I did what so many grateful high performers do:

I adapted.
I ignored the “is this really it?” feeling.
I dimmed the parts of me that felt “too much” for the room.

And pretending who I wasn’t drained me faster than the workload ever could.

Two years in… and I was already burned out.

Then I moved to the trading floor and finally…

I could breathe.

The chaos. 
The fast pace. 
The adrenaline.

I found my people. 

My spice fit right in. It was even celebrated.

When you stop wasting energy trying to be someone you’re not, everything feels easier:

→ Mondays don’t feel like a battle
→ You stop performing and finally start being
→ Work becomes something you can actually enjoy again

If you’re feeling like that square peg in a round hole

The one who’s constantly adjusting, shrinking, or “toning it down” to fit:

Please hear this:

There is nothing wrong with you.
You’re just in the wrong room.

Your reality today doesn’t have to be your reality tomorrow.

You’re allowed to choose the environment that matches your current energy. 

You get to choose better.

And you get to make that choice today.

For anyone who’s been in a similar situation, what box did you have to break free from?
