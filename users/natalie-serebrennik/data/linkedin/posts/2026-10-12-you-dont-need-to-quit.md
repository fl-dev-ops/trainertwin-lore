You don't need to quit your job to figure out whether there's something better out there for you.

I work with professionals who've spent 10, 15, even 20 years building successful careers, only to reach a point where they're no longer sure what they want next.

And because they don't have an answer, they stay exactly where they are.

But you can start figuring this out without making a major career decision.

One approach I like is skill stacking.

Instead of starting over, you build on the experience you already have by exploring skills and opportunities that genuinely interest you.

Maybe you're in finance but want more exposure to AI.

Maybe you're great at execution but curious about strategy.

Maybe you've spent years developing technical expertise and want to explore people leadership.

Start testing those interests inside your current company.

Volunteer for a project outside your usual responsibilities.

Ask to get involved in meetings where different decisions are being made.

Connect with someone whose role interests you and learn what their work actually involves.

Take on an assignment that challenges you in a different way.

Then pay attention to what you learn.

What did you enjoy? What came naturally? What would you want to do more of?

You're collecting information that will help you make a better decision about what's next.

You don't need to have your next five years figured out.

You just need to give yourself more opportunities to discover what you might want.
