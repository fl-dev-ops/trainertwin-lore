There’s a stage in your career no one warns you about.

You’re not struggling.
But you’re not moving either.

You deliver.
You hit expectations.
You do everything “right”.

And yet…

Nothing changes.

No bigger scope.
No real stretch from here.
No conversation about what’s next.

I remember this stage like it was yesterday.

I thought the answer was simple:

Work harder, Nat.
Deliver more, Nat.
Be patient, Nat.

It didn’t work.

Because at this level, performance isn’t what gets you promoted.

It’s assumed.
And that’s the trap.

You get just enough validation to stay.
But never enough visibility to move.

So you keep going.
Waiting for someone to notice.

They won’t.

Because progression at this level is not about output.

It’s about positioning.

Can people see your impact?
Can they connect your work to business priorities?
Can they imagine you operating at the next level?

If the answer is no…

You don’t move.
You plateau.

And the longer you stay there, the harder it is to get unstuck...

Or at least that’s what most people believe.

It’s not actually true.

I’ve worked with people who’ve been stuck for years…

And broken through the ceiling in a matter of months.

Just by changing up a few behaviors and adopting a few new habits.
