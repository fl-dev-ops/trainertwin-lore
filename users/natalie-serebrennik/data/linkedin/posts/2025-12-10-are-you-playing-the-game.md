Are you playing the game or losing by default?

If you want to grow inside your company, one strategy matters more than anything else:

Build strong relationships with decision-makers before you need them.

Not when you’re up for promotion.
Not when things start to go sideways.

Now.

Your talent will only take you as far as your direct manager is willing to advocate for you.

So why not hedge your bets?

Start building relationships beyond your boss.

Get to know them. Understand what they care about.

What challenges are they trying to solve?
What opportunities are they excited about?

Don’t think of it as sucking up.

You’re simply protecting and honoring your growth.

Here are 3 things I’m actively coaching a client to do right now as he prepares for his VP promotion:

1️⃣ Send a monthly wins recap that clearly connects his team’s results to his boss’s top priorities. Make sure the business impact is front and center.

2️⃣ Book coffee chats with senior leaders he’s casually crossed paths with. Ask thoughtful questions to better understand what success looks like at their level.

3️⃣ Ask a senior leader to sponsor his growth. Share his career goals and the internal roadblocks he’s facing (not as a complaint, but as a leadership conversation).

And in case you need a nudge:

You are more powerful than you give yourself credit for.

Don’t underestimate how and when these conversations can shift your trajectory.
