No one prepares high-achieving women for this moment.

The moment you become a mother.

Your whole life you’ve been rewarded for ambition.

Winning.
Climbing.
Working hard.
Becoming more.

Your identity was clear.

You were the one who delivered results.
The one who kept growing.
The one people relied on.

Then suddenly…

You’re responsible for a tiny human being.

And everything changes.

Your priorities.
Your energy.
Your time.

Your perspective on what really matters.

But the part no one talks about?

You’re still that ambitious woman.

You still care about your career.
You still want that big seat.
You still want to grow.

And that creates a tension that’s hard to explain.

Because now the questions creep in:

Can I still want this as much as I used to?
Should I want it as much?
What does success even look like now?

No one tells you how complicated it feels to hold both identities at once.

A devoted mother.
And a driven professional.

You love your child more than anything.

But that doesn’t erase the part of you that spent years building a career you’re proud of.

And learning how to honor both sides of yourself is one of the most challenging leadership moments a woman will face.

Not in the boardroom.

But within herself.

If you’ve felt this tension too, you’re not alone.

More women are navigating this than we openly talk about.

And the conversation deserves to be louder.

Do you agree?
