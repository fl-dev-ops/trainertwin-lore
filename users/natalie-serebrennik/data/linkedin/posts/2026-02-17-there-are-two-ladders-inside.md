There are two ladders inside every company.

The performance ladder.
And the trust ladder.

Most people climb the first one.
Then they wonder why they’re “top talent” but not promoted.

Because promotions happen on the trust ladder.

Here are 6 plays I would be focusing on right now if I was working on that next-level promotion:

1️⃣ Decide who needs to trust you

Not everyone. Just three groups:

- Your manager
- One cross-functional partner
- Two leaders who influence decisions

2️⃣ Make your work land and easy to align

Smart work that no one understands is invisible.
Summarize impact in one sentence.
Repeat it until people can repeat it.

3️⃣ Turn updates into leadership signals

Don’t say, “Here’s what I did.”
Say, “Here’s what changed, what’s at risk, and what I’m doing next.”

4️⃣ Give away small power

Share templates. Introduce people.
Make others look competent.
This is how you build your trust army.

5️⃣ Become easy to back

Advocacy is risky.
Make it safe for someone to vouch for you.
Be consistent. Be clear. But also, reciprocate. 

6️⃣ Create moments of earned reputation

A mistake owned quickly.
A hard conversation handled well.
A problem solved when it mattered.

There's a lot of talk about "playing the game" in corporate.

But not enough about what those lived actions actually are.

These are my favorite underestimated plays, what are yours?
