Success didn’t burn me out. 

Stagnation did.

When I was on the trading floor, I had everything I thought I wanted:

The title.
The comp.
The credibility.

But I felt flat on the inside.

I knew I should be grateful - and believe me, I was.

But the part about gratitude no one really talks about is that sometimes it’s not enough to keep going.

Sometimes, you can be grateful and know you’ve had enough.

It felt like I’d climbed to the top of a ladder I wasn’t sure I wanted to be on anymore.

And I kept thinking, what’s wrong with me?

Why wasn’t it enough?
