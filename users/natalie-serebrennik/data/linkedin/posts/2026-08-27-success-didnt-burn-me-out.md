Success didn't burn me out.

Stagnation did.

When I was on the trading floor, I had everything I thought I wanted:

The title.
The comp.
The credibility.

And instead of feeling like I was on top of my game, I felt empty.

I knew I should be grateful. And I was.

But the part about gratitude no one really talks about is that sometimes it's not enough to keep going.

Sometimes, you can be grateful and still know you've had enough.

It felt like I'd climbed to the top of a ladder I wasn't sure I wanted to be on anymore.

And I kept thinking, what's wrong with me?

Why isn't this enough?

This is what happens when high performers outgrow their environment.

From the outside, it’s easy for people to assume you’re entitled. 

But what do they know. 

We know the truth. 

We feel this way because we've stopped growing and we're no longer willing to make peace with complacency.

And that feeling, the flatness, the quiet "is this really it?" that follows you into the weekend, that's not confusion.

That's the best information you’ll ever get.

And the longer you ignore it, the louder that inner voice screams at you.
