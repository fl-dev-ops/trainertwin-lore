The bravest thing a high performer can do is admit that what got them here won't get them where they're going.

Because that feeling…

The friction, the stagnation, the sense that working harder isn't moving the needle the way it used to…

Is deeply uncomfortable when your entire identity is built around being the person who figures it out.

So most people suppress it.

They add more hours and take on more scope.

They double down on the same strategy hoping this time it lands differently.

It rarely does.

What if instead you chose to recognize the friction for what it actually is:

Not a sign that something's wrong with you. But a signal that you've outgrown the level you're playing at.

Probably the most important career data point you'll ever get.

Next-level growth won’t come from doing more of the same with more intensity.

It will come from getting honest about what's actually in the way and then building a completely different strategy around it.
