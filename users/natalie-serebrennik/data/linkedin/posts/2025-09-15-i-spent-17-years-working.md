I spent 17 years working in an extreme hustle culture.

The trading floor is no joke.

It’s one of the most high-stakes and fast-paced environments you will ever work in.

As a working parent, it’s normal to:

→ Cut your maternity leave short
→ Say yes to roles you didn’t actually want
→ Sacrifice family time for client drinks all the time

So what do you do when a boss doesn’t recognize your value?

I can tell you: don’t do what I did.

Don’t sit around waiting for your hard work to finally speak for itself. It won’t.

I wasted too much time thinking it would.

Instead, do what I eventually realized:

Make your value impossible to ignore.

Here are the exact steps I took as I broke free from the “I don’t want to be too pushy” hustler:

1️⃣ Take the lead on the conversation.

“I’d like to check in regularly to ensure I’m delivering at the highest level.”

Your boss can’t recognize what they don’t see.

2️⃣ Tie your work to business results.

“Since implementing X, I’ve improved Y by Z%. How can I build on this success?”

Visibility increases when management connects your efforts to impact.

3️⃣ Make it a two-way exchange.

“What’s most important for our team right now? I want to ensure my work is driving the right results.”

Your boss is busy. Sometimes, they just need the reminder.

You’re not here to blend in.

You’re here to make an impact.

Stop calling it:

→ Too risky
→ Too pushy
→ Inappropriate

Start showing up as a leader who doesn’t wait.

What’s one way you’ve made your work more visible?
