You left that meeting thinking everyone was on the same page.

They weren't.

And now you're spending the rest of your week cleaning up three different versions of what was supposed to be one clear direction.

This isn't a people problem. It's an alignment problem - and it started before that meeting.

The strongest teams don't just work hard. They work together toward a shared vision.

And that alignment starts with you.

Here's how to actually build it:

1️⃣ Understand the vision before you share it.

Ever seen a boss explain a project they didn't fully understand?

Train wreck.

Check yourself first:
↳ What's the direct impact on the team?
↳ Why is the company headed in this direction?
↳ How does your team fit into the bigger picture?

2️⃣ Communicate the vision clearly.

Your team can't align with what they don't understand.

Start with the why - this gives them purpose. 

Follow with what, where, how, and when - this gives them clarity.

3️⃣ Define expectations upfront.

You can't skip this part. If results are inconsistent, that's on you, not them.

Outline what success looks like for the team. Then break it down for each person so they see their role in it.

4️⃣ Open the door for input.

A team that feels heard is a team that's invested.

The sooner they feel safe to voice ideas and concerns, the faster you gain their buy-in.
Truth is, it's easy to blame others for the direction you failed to set.

And if that stung, just know every great leader has been there.

Now's your turn to do something about it.
