Most high performers get lazy feedback.

It’s either:
→ Surface-level praise, or
→ Vague suggestions that don’t mean anything

Which does nothing for your growth mindset that’s looking to push you to the next level.

Here’s what I tell my clients: 

YOU have to train your leadership to give better feedback.

Try this at your next 1:1 meeting:

1️⃣ What would exceeding expectations in this area look like?

2️⃣ If you had to choose one thing that would accelerate my growth, what would it be?

3️⃣ What do you see leaders at the next level doing differently than me?

And here’s the part most people miss:

Sometimes your leader doesn’t have the answer on the spot.

Not because they don’t care, but because they haven’t actually thought about your growth in that way.

So make it easy for them to form an opinion.

👉 Bring options to the table.

For example, after you’ve asked the first question, follow up with this:

“Which would make a bigger difference in your opinion - driving a new initiative or mentoring junior team members to scale impact?

This or that questions help your manager clarify their view, while giving you the specific insight you need to grow.

It also ensures you avoid lazy feedback that stunts your growth (and I know that's not what you want).
