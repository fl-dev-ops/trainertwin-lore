What does being done look like in corporate?

Done as in when you've been excellent for years and your company keeps acting like it doesn't notice.

That was my client a few months ago.

She had been quietly job searching for three months by the time we started working together. 

Her plan was already in motion. 

She wasn't looking for a reason to stay, she was looking for an exit that felt right.

In our first conversation she said: "I think I've just outgrown this place."

Maybe. But I wasn't buying it. There was more to the story. 

Had she actually made the case for how she wanted her career to grow next?

Or was she more focused on doing exceptional work and waiting for the organization to figure out what to do with her?

When I asked the question, she knew the answer immediately.

So instead of working on her resume, we built a self-advocacy strategy.

The conversations she hadn't had, the visibility she hadn't created, the case she had never made for what she was capable of and where she wanted to go.

Three months later she wasn't job searching anymore. 

She had created new scope internally and new visibility with leadership. 

A real path that hadn't existed before. 

Not because the organization suddenly changed, but because she stopped waiting for it to hand her something and started building it herself.

She told me afterward: "I thought I had to leave to create this kind of growth. I thought it was going to be too hard to change people’s perception of me."

Leaving is sometimes the right answer. 

But if what you're escaping is solvable where you are, is it still?

A lot of people leave opportunities that were never fully explored because they never learned how to navigate them.

What's one thing you've been tolerating at work that you've never actually addressed directly?
