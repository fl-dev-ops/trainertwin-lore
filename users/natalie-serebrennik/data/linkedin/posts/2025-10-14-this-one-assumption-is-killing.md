This one assumption is killing your career growth.

Most of my clients come to me frustrated by their lack of career growth.

They’re working hard.
They’re delivering results.
They’re checking all the boxes.

But they still don’t have the title or pay they desire.

Why?

Because they assume their boss already knows what they want.

I see it all the time:

The high performer waiting for a promotion
↳ but never stating their case.

The employee hoping for bigger opportunities
↳ but never asking for them.

The person underpaid for years
↳ but never made a clear argument for a raise.

Your boss isn’t sitting around analyzing your next move.

Even the most supportive leaders need to hear exactly what you want. 

Instead of assuming, make it impossible for them to overlook you:

✔ Make your goals explicit - Say it out loud. Stop assuming they know.

✔ Ask for what you need - Don't be afraid to call out what's not working.

✔ Show them why it makes sense - Tie your impact to business results.

If you’re not actively managing your career, someone else is.

And their version of your growth might not align with what you actually want.

So why leave it to chance?

The sooner you start positioning yourself for what’s next, the faster you’ll see progress.
