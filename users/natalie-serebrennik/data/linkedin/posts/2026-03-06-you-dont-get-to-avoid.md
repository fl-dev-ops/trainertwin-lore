You don’t get to avoid hard if growth is your goal.

Building my career in banking wasn’t easy.

→ A woman in a sea of men
→ Saying yes to roles that scared me
→ Constantly advocating for my worth
→ Battling imposter syndrome behind a smile

Starting my own business was just as hard.

→ Questioning my value daily
→ Wondering if anyone was really listening
→ Doubting whether my offer was strong enough

And then came the real wake-up call.

To do the work I love, coaching,
I also had to run a business.

Sales.
Marketing.
Accounting.
Operations.
And yes, IT.

None of it glamorous.
All of it necessary.

And I wouldn’t trade any of it.

Because every version of hard did something for me.

It stretched me.
It sharpened my thinking.
It expanded what I believed was possible.

If you want growth, hard is unavoidable.

The real question is:

Will you choose your hard?
Or let circumstances choose it for you?

Just because the next step feels uncomfortable
doesn’t mean it isn’t the exact growth you need.

So tell me, what hard are you choosing right now?

And more importantly,

Is it leading you toward the promotion and life you actually want?
