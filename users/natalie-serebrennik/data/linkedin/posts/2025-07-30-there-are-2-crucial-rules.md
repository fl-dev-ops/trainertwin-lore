There are 2 crucial rules in leadership.

Rule #1:

Stop trying to do it all.

Rule #2:

Master delegation.

When you become a people leader your to-do list multiplies overnight. 

If you’re not leaning on your team, you’re capping your results and sabotaging your success.

Yep, I said it. You and your career growth suffer if you don’t delegate.

When my clients stop questioning if they “should” delegate, we start working on the “how”.

And the results are undeniable. They’re able to:

→ Build trust faster than they thought possible
→ Free up time to focus on what drives results
→ Create growth opportunities for their team

As the saying goes, what got you here won’t get you there.

As an IC, it was all about how YOU win.
As a leader, it’s about how WE win.

Want to become a good delegator? Start here:

→ Delegate a task you’d normally do yourself
→ Provide clear guidance, then step back
→ Let them surprise you

You’re not failing if delegation feels hard.

It’s a skill every leader learns over time.

And when you master it, you’ll wonder why you waited so long to start.
