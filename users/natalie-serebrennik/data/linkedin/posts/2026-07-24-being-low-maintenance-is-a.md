Being low maintenance is a horrible career-growth strategy.

You've been told that being agreeable and flexible makes you a team player. 

That not rocking the boat earns you respect. 

That keeping your head down will eventually pay off.

After building a 17-year career in finance and coaching high performers for years, I know better.

Here's what low maintenance actually looks like in corporate:

→ You avoid pushing back
→ You don't ask for what you want
→ You take on more without ever saying "no"
→ You make everyone's life easier except your own

And then you wonder why leadership overlooks you when the next opportunity opens up.

Here's what actually moves the needle instead:

1️⃣ Make your impact visible

↳ Speak to your wins, don't hide them
↳ Share updates before they're asked from you
↳ Put yourself into as many new conversations as possible

2️⃣ Ask for what you need

↳ Clarity and support
↳ An introduction with someone new
↳ Opportunities for continued growth

3️⃣ Advocate without apologizing

↳ Be clear about your goals
↳ Be direct about your value
↳ Be consistent in how you show up

Being respected and being liked aren't mutually exclusive.

But if you only focus on being agreeable, you become invisible.
