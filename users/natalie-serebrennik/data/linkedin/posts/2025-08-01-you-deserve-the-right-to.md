You deserve the right to be selfish.

Especially as a woman.

On the trading floor, I learned what it means to “go after it”.

When I joined, I was naive about the game being played.

I thought working hard, keeping my head down, and trusting my boss to advance me was all I needed.

Truth is, young Natalie was just grateful for the opportunity to play in the same arena as the “boys”.

So to think about asking for more? That felt too needy. 

So I waited and hoped for growth instead.

Then I saw peers landing promotions ahead of me, performing at a similar capacity, and it hit me:

If you’re not selfish, you’re on the sidelines. 

I stopped thinking of “selfish” from an outdated lens. 
I began to own my authentic selfish.

And here’s what happened:

→ Secured a 6-figure increase in my bonus
→ Promoted to Director (second-highest title)
→ Recognized with the “Executive Leader” award
→ Successfully traded one of the riskiest products
→ Led mentorship circles for future leaders in banking

If you don’t have the results you want in your career, the grass won’t be greener somewhere else.

You will face the same glass ceiling wherever you go.

And that glass ceiling will shatter when you finally embrace being selfish.
