Failure is the best leadership training you’ll ever get.

No one wants to fail.

But the strongest leaders know:

It’s not a setback, it’s a strategy.

Early in my career, I obsessed over getting everything right.

Every mistake felt like proof I wasn’t cut out for the job.

That I wasn’t good enough to sit at the table.

I began studying the leaders I admired.

The ones who seemed to effortlessly climb without challenge.

And I noticed something:

They didn’t avoid failure. They leveraged it.

They saw mistakes as:

→ Data, not disasters
→ Opportunities, not obstacles
→ A chance to grow, not a reason to stop

That’s when it clicked.

I wasn’t failing. I was learning.

Here’s what changed everything for me:

1️⃣ Own your missteps.
↳ Take responsibility, extract the lesson, and move forward.

2️⃣ Normalize failure for your team.
↳ Create a culture where setbacks aren’t feared but studied instead.

3️⃣ Turn mistakes into momentum.
↳ Every failure is a stepping stone to the next breakthrough.

You can’t avoid failure.

But you can decide whether it slows you down or sharpens your leadership.

What’s one failure that made you a stronger leader?
