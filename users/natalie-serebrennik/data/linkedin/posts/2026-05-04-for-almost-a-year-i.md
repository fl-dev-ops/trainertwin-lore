For almost a year, I let one person's opinion of me become my opinion of me.

And he wasn't even my boss.

He was a senior trader on the floor. Someone whose approval I desperately wanted and never felt like I fully had.

Every piece of critical feedback he gave me, I took as confirmation that I wasn't as strong as I thought I was.

So naturally, I got really good at shrinking myself around him.

Because who wants proof they don't cut it?

And I use 'proof' loosely because his opinion was never data. It just felt like it.

I second-guessed calls I would've made confidently with anyone else.

I stopped asking questions because I didn't want to feel stupid around him.

I stayed late way more than I needed to just to prove how dedicated I was.

It took me close to a year to do anything about it.

When I reached my breaking point, I only saw two options in front of me.

Quit. Or confront him.

I finally sat down with him and told him directly how I was feeling and what needed to change. 

I was clear that if his behavior towards me didn't change, I'd quite, and that decision would be because of him.

And for the first time in almost a year, I found my voice with him.

It took me months to start trusting him again.
Months to believe he wasn't the worst person.
Months to realize my active role in the experience.

Yep. I too played a role in how I was being treated.

Probably not what you wanted to hear, right?

Yes, his behavior was toxic. But this was the first time in nearly a year that I spoke up about it.

Why did I wait that long?

Why did I choose to internalize his actions as there was something wrong with me?

Years later I can honestly say the problem wasn't him.

It was that I had handed him the pen and let him write the story about who I was. And I kept reading it like it was fact.

One person's opinion is not your performance data.

It's not your potential. It's not your truth.

The professionals I work with who are the most stuck are almost never stuck because they lack capability. 

They're stuck because somewhere along the way, they outsourced their self-assessment to the wrong person, and never took it back.

If that's sitting somewhere in your chest right now, just know, it does get better. But better only starts with your next move.
