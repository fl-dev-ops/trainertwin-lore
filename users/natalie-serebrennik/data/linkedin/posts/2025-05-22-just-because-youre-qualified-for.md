Just because you’re qualified for X… doesn’t mean you’ll get X.

You know the X’s I’m talking about:

→ That new job
→ The next promotion
→ A well-earned pay bump
→ The leadership role you’ve been eyeing for months

This hits so many of my clients, and it hit me too when I was trying to build my career in banking.

I had the experience.
I had the results.
I worked hard.

But none of that guaranteed my growth.

Harsh reality, I know.

I wish I appreciated this truth earlier:

How well you sell yourself will always matter more than what’s on paper.

Good self-advocacy is what:

→ Sets you apart
→ Gets you noticed
→ Moves you forward faster

Some call it ego. I call it confidence.

But here’s the kicker - you can’t sell yourself if you don’t actually believe in yourself.

That belief comes from knowing your story inside and out.

✔️ How you’ve grown
✔️ What you’ve achieved
✔️ What you’ve overcome
✔️ Where you’re going next

When you own your story, people don’t just buy into what you can do, they buy into you.

Your career growth won’t come from waiting for recognition.

It comes from showing the world why you’re ready.
