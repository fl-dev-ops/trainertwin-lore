You’re trying so hard to look like a leader

That you’ve forgotten how to be one.

Ouch, right? But hear me out...

Any of this ring a bell:
→ Every word is measured.
→ Every decision rehearsed.
→ Every meeting feels like a performance.

If you’re anything like me, you’ve done an excellent job building your career on composure.

But at some point, that composure turns into control.
And that’s the exact thing holding you back.

(Spoken as a recovering control freak.)

The pressure to “get it right” is robbing you of the presence that makes people trust you.

The spark. The ease. 

The energy that makes others want to follow your lead.

When you take yourself too seriously, you stop taking risks.

You stop speaking freely.

You stop showing up with the kind of confidence that can’t be scripted.

Looking back, the best leaders I worked with had one thing in common → they were real.

They laughed at themselves.

They didn’t take themselves so seriously.

They created safety because they didn’t pretend to have it all figured out.

Imagine that:

A reality where your growth depends on finally loosening your grip.

Wouldn’t that be nice? 
Wouldn’t that be easier!

I’m all for bringing more ‘human’ into our corporate homes. 

How about you?
