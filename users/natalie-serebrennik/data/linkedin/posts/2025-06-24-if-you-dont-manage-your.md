If you don’t manage your own narrative, someone else will.

And chances are, they won’t get it right.

Whether you realize it or not, you already have a reputation.

The only question is: are you shaping it with intention… or leaving it to chance?

Here’s how I coach clients to build reputations that open doors:

1️⃣ Decide what you want to be known for
2️⃣ Back it up with results, stories, and proof
3️⃣ Show up consistently - in how you speak, lead, and contribute

Reputations with meaningful impact aren’t built because you're great at your job.

You also need to take bold ownership over your efforts to ensure they're both visible and clear.

The longer you wait for people to notice, the longer you’ll stay stuck.

If someone described you in three words, would they match how you want to be seen?

Don’t forget…
You are your biggest asset. It’s time to act like it.
