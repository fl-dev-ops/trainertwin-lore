Most professionals misunderstand visibility.

They think it’s about volume.

More DMs.
More coffees.
More LinkedIn connections.

But real visibility is way more about being remembered than being seen.

The goal is to be remembered for the right things, by the right people, at the right time.

If you’re networking just to check a box, you’re wasting time.

Here’s what actually builds career leverage:

→ Follow up when there’s no obvious ROI
→ Stay consistent after the first conversation
→ Share relevant wins without trying to impress
→ Ask better questions than “Can I pick your brain?”

If career growth is your goal, investing in your visibility efforts is a must.
