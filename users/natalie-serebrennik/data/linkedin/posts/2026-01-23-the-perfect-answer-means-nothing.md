The “perfect” answer means nothing if no one cares.

Early in my career, I was presenting a market outlook to a room full of senior leaders.

You know the kind.

Heavy oak table.
Twenty people around it.
All watching and judging you.

I had done the work.

Every stat memorized.
Every trend mapped.
Every answer polished.

I delivered it cleanly.

And still… I could feel it.

They weren’t bought in.

Not because the analysis was wrong.

But because I’d missed the only question that mattered:

What does this mean for them?

That moment stuck with me.

Because it shows up everywhere.

In big meetings.
In high-stakes interviews.
In career-defining conversations.

Smart people focus on being right.
Strategic people focus on being relevant.

Influence isn’t about having the best answer.

It’s about answering the question your audience is silently asking.

That’s the difference between:

Speaking well
And being listened to

Between:

Showing competence
And earning buy-in

Most people struggle because they haven’t learned how to translate it into meaning for the room they’re in.
