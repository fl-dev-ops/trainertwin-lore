This year, I’m leaving a few things behind.

Not in a dramatic, burn-it-all-down way.

Just in a “wow, I see it so clearly” kind-of-way:

1️⃣ Over-explaining my decisions.

If something makes sense to me, I don’t need to convince everyone else.

2️⃣ Confusing busy with important.

Working past 9pm doesn’t automatically mean I’m progressing in the way that matters.

3️⃣ Carrying pressure that isn’t mine.

Not every fire needs to be put out by me.

4️⃣ Saying yes when my gut says no.

That hesitation is data and I’m finally listening to it.

5️⃣ Proving myself to the wrong people.

If I need to justify my passions, you're no longer my people.


These are my 2026 boundaries.

Each one helping me make better choices about where my energy goes.

Your turn, what are you leaving behind this year?
