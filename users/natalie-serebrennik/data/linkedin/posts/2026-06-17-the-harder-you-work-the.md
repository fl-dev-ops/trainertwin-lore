The harder you work, the less incentive your organization has to pay you more.

I know how that sounds.

But think about it from the other side of the table.

If you are consistently absorbing more than your role requires, delivering beyond expectations, and never pushing back on what lands on your plate…

You have just told your boss exactly what you're worth to them in your current seat.

Exceptional. Reliable. And not going anywhere.

That's not a promotion profile. That's a retention profile.

I had a client who had been hearing the same feedback for three review cycles running. "You're doing great, but..." 

Different words each time. Same outcome, i.e. no movement.

Each quarter working harder than the last, convincing herself that more effort would finally tip the scales.

Common reality when you're not on the same page with management.

So we stopped focusing on the work and started focusing on building her promotion case.

Here's what we accomplished together:

We made her impact visible beyond her direct manager. 

We reframed her value in the language of business outcomes, not effort. 

We built the internal conversations that shifted her from reliable executor to someone leadership saw as a future investment.

And finally she started to see progress.

She secured a $45k raise and a promotion in the next review cycle.

Her work didn't change.

But the story leadership was telling about her did.

If you've been waiting for effort to speak for itself, it's time to give it a louder voice.
