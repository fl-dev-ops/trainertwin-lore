The only thing you teach by replying right away…

Is that you always will.

I struggled with this one.

Because my entire career encouraged quick responses. Fast action.

If the phone rang, you picked it up on the first ring.

If a client emailed, you responded before they hit refresh.

It was how I learned to prove my value - through speed.

So when I started setting boundaries, it felt wrong.

Like I was dropping the ball. Like I’d be seen as less committed.

And honestly, it stressed me out!

Looking back, I wish I appreciated this more:

📌 Reliability doesn’t require immediacy.

📌 Leadership isn’t about being the fastest responder.

📌 The more you over-deliver on availability, the more people expect it.

For what? A reputation built on reactivity?

Here’s how I coach my clients to shift this habit:

→ Set response hours and communicate them clearly

→ Prioritize what’s important, not just what feels urgent

→ Hold your time with the same respect you give everyone else’s

Because if you’re always available, you’re never fully focused.

And if you want to lead strategically, you need to be intentional with your energy.

To all my high-achievers out there, how did you break free from this habit?
