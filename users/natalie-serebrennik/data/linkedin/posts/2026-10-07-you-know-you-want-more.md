You know you want more.

A bigger role.
More money.
More influence.

And you've probably already consumed a ridiculous amount of advice about how to get there.

Speak up more.
Be more visible.
Build relationships.
Find a sponsor.
Work on your executive presence.

None of that advice is wrong.

But knowing what you could be doing isn't the same as knowing what you need to do next.

Because your problem might not be visibility.

It might be that senior leadership doesn't see you as ready for more.

It might be that your manager supports you but isn't advocating for you.

It might be that you're delivering at a high level but haven't built the relationships with the people influencing your next move.

Or you might not actually know what's standing between you and the title you want because nobody has ever told you.

Advice gives you more things to try.

Strategy helps you figure out which things actually matter in your situation.

That's the work I do with my clients.

We figure out why the results they're already delivering aren't translating into the promotion, pay or growth they expected.

Then we build a strategy around the gaps that are actually getting in their way.

Not everything they could be doing.

What they actually need to do next.
