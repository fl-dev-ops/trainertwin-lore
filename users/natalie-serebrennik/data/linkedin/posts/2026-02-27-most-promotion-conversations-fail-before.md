Most promotion conversations fail before they even start.

Not because you’re not ready.

But because you’re framing it wrong.

You don’t get promoted because you want one.

You get promoted because it makes business sense.

The mistake most people make?

They centre the conversation around themselves.

Their effort.
Their ambition.
Their readiness.

But leadership isn’t evaluating your ambition.

They’re evaluating risk.

Can this person solve bigger problems?

Can they carry more responsibility without creating friction?

Can they elevate performance around them?

If you’re aiming for the next level, shift your positioning.

1️⃣ Show how stepping up solves current team challenges.

2️⃣ Demonstrate how your leadership increases leverage, not workload.

3️⃣ Tie your work directly to measurable outcomes.

Promotions are rarely emotional decisions.

They’re strategic ones.

The moment your growth feels like a business advantage, not a personal reward…

approval becomes that much easier.

My clients experience faster promotions inside my program because of strategic changes like this.

If you’re serious about stepping into your next level, this is the work.
