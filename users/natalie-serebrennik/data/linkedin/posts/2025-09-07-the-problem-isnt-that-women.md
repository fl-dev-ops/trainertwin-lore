The problem isn’t that women don’t advocate for themselves.

It’s that we’ve been taught not to.

Taught to play it safe.
Taught to be grateful for the opportunities we do get.
Taught to trust that if we work hard, someone will notice.

I’ve lived that.

I was the “team player.” The one who didn’t complain.

I stayed late. Took on extra projects. Said yes to everything.

And what did it get me?

Years of being told I was doing “great work”… with nothing to show for it.

Because here’s the truth most people won’t say out loud:

Ambitious women aren’t lacking confidence.

We’re navigating systems that reward silence and punish self-advocacy.

Like:

→ Getting labeled “aggressive” for being direct
→ Receiving lower feedback scores for being “intimidating”
→ Being left out of decisions because you “already have too much on your plate”

So it’s no surprise that so many women stay quiet.

We’re not avoiding the spotlight - we’ve just seen the cost of stepping into it.

But here’s what I proudly preach now:

✨ Silence doesn’t protect your career. It stalls it.

And waiting for someone else to speak up on your behalf?

It’s the fastest way to stay stuck.

That’s why the women I work with learn how to lead their own growth.

→ They learn how to ask for what they want and back it up with strategy.

→ They start sharing their wins without fear of sounding arrogant.

→ They stop minimizing their ambition and start owning it like the boss they are.

Girl, enough minimizing who you are and what you can do just because you’re surrounded by men.

You are way too talented for that.
