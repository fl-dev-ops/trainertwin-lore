I don’t coach people who are lost.

I coach people who are driven, but lacking direction.

There’s a difference most people never articulate.

Lost sounds like:
I have no idea where I’m going.

Lack of direction sounds like:
I know I’m meant for more… I just can’t see the path clearly yet.

The people I work with are rarely underperforming.

They’re usually:
→ Respected internally but not top-of-mind
→ Delivering strong results but not advancing
→ Visible, but not fully seen for their real value

Ambition isn’t the issue.

Capability isn’t the issue.

What’s missing is strategy.

Specifically:
Clarity on where to focus next, how to position themselves, and which moves actually compound in high-performing environments.

Without that clarity, even smart, motivated people stall.

They stay busy.
They keep saying yes.
They wait for recognition instead of shaping it.

And that’s exactly where frustration builds because you can feel your potential outpacing your trajectory.
