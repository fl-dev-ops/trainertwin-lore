How to advocate for yourself without sounding like you’re bragging

Let’s clear something up:
→ Self-advocacy isn’t bragging. It’s a necessity.

But if you keep telling yourself it’s:

→ Exaggerating your achievements
→ Taking credit away from others
→ Making it all about you

Then that’s exactly what you’ll continue to believe.

Instead, consider this mindset shift:
→ You’re not being arrogant, you’re being strategic.

Here are 3 ways to make your efforts visible:

1️⃣ Start with impact.

Instead of listing what you did, focus on the outcome:

❌ I did XYZ.
✅ I led XYZ and it resulted in a 20% increase in revenue.


2️⃣ Use numbers.

Facts are undeniable. Keep it objective:

❌ I did a good job.
✅ I led a project that cut costs by 15%.


3️⃣ Expand your visibility.

❌ Sharing your wins only with your manager.
✅ Sharing your wins with your manager, your skip level, strategic collaborators, etc. Don't limit this effort.

Why?

Because the more people who understand your impact, the easier it becomes to position yourself for those bigger opportunities.

Most people assume speaking up is self-serving.

My 2 cents - who cares what they think.

If they're not deciding your next-level growth their negativity is noise.

Don't let the haters get in your way.

You deserve way better than that!
