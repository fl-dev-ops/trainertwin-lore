I used to chase every opportunity that looked impressive.

Bigger title. Better comp. More visibility.

Not once did I stop to ask - do I even want this?

It’s wild how early beliefs shape entire careers.

Like:

Bigger title = I belong.
Better comp = I’m valued.
More visibility = I’m liked.

And that’s how high performers burn out. 

Not from working hard, but from chasing what never actually felt meaningful.

It wasn’t until I left the trading floor that I saw it clearly:

Just because you can do something doesn’t mean you should.

Your potential doesn’t have to live inside old definitions of success.

Even if you were the one who created them.

These days, I’m all about alignment.

Doing work that I freaking love.

With people I choose to do it with.

Making the impact I always craved and now get to.

How about you - are you chasing old metrics or building a career that actually fits?
