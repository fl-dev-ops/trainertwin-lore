The “perfect” answer means nothing if no one cares.

Early in my career, I was presenting a market outlook to a room full of senior leaders.

You know those heavy oak boardroom tables with 20 people around them?

That was the one.

I prepped every stat. 
Memorized every trend. 
Delivered my analysis with precision.

But I could tell, they weren’t sold.

Why?

Because I forgot to answer the only question that mattered:

What does it mean to them?

It’s a lesson I carry into every client session now.

Whether we’re prepping for:

- A big meeting
- A career-defining interview
- A high-pressure presentation

I always ask:

What’s the so-what?
Why should your audience care?
What do they need to hear in order to care?

You secure their buy-in by understanding what they need from you and speaking directly to that.

No one cares about perfect, polished responses.

Or if you had to look at your notes to get back on track.

If you connect on the message that means something to them - you’ve nailed it.

That moment taught me the difference between being smart and being strategic.

Leading with more influence is easier than you think.

Just because you don’t feel comfortable now, doesn’t mean you won’t with a good strategy in hand.
