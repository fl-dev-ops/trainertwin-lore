You can be the smartest person in the room.

And still get ignored. Let’s change that.

There’s nothing more frustrating than having:

😡 Your ideas constantly questioned
😡 People talking over you in meetings
😡 Teammates stealing the spotlight on a project you’re leading

Meanwhile…

Everyone around you is getting the recognition, awards, and promotions.

You keep thinking it’s a competence problem.

(And wasting time trying to learn more.)

When the winners are playing a different game: 

Assertiveness.

Here’s what I know after working with 100+ leaders:

If you don’t claim your authority, someone else will.

Let me show you the 3 key shifts my clients work on in my program:

1️⃣ Stop waiting for permission.

No more softening your points with “I think we should consider…”

Instead: “We’re moving forward with X. Here’s why.”

2️⃣ Take up space, physically and verbally.

No more shrinking into your chair in meetings.

When you speak, hold eye contact and let your words land.

3️⃣ Shut down idea theft immediately.

Reinforce your idea when it gets repeated: "Yes, that’s exactly what I suggested earlier. Let’s build on it."

If someone tries to take over, set a boundary: “I appreciate the input and I’ll consider it as we focus on next steps.”


If you want people to think you have executive presence, start by owning your authority - without apology.
