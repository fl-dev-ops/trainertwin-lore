You're exhausted from work no one sees.

You keep showing up. You keep saying yes. You keep catching what others drop.

You tell yourself it's temporary.

But it's not. It's the expectation.

And while you're busy holding everything together, you're not getting any closer to your next role.

In fact, you're probably being praised for it.

But praise isn't a promotion, and it doesn't add anything to your bank account.

The work that makes you indispensable at your current level is the same work keeping you there.

Read that again.

At some point, being the team hero stops being noble and starts being expensive.

Protecting your time and energy isn't selfish. It's the most strategic career move you can make right now.

If I made this commitment starting today, here’s what I’d do next:

Audit where your time actually goes. 

If most of it is reactive, like fixing non-stop problems and dropping what you’re doing to help support someone’s ask - that's your signal. 

Carve out one block a week (minimum 1 hour, bonus points for 2) for work that builds your visibility at the next level, and protect it like your life depends on it.

And if you're curious what those visibility actions look like in your role, DM me and let’s chat about it.
