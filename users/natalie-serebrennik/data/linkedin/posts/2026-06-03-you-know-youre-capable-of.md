You know you’re capable of more.

And if you’re honest, that awareness has started turning into resentment.

Because deep down, you already know you’ve outgrown the role you’re still sitting in.

Same seat.
Same responsibilities.
Same conversations about “what’s next.”

And every time you try to push the conversation forward, you hear some variation of the same thing:

“Not yet.”

Different wording each time.

Same delay.

Same hope that if you stay patient long enough, eventually someone will decide you’re ready.

And that’s the trap.

A lot of high performers spend years waiting for recognition to arrive externally instead of realizing the change has to happen internally first.

Because eventually the frustration stops being about the organization.

And starts becoming about the fact that you already know this version of your career is no longer enough for you.

That’s the moment that matters.

The moment where you stop waiting for permission.

Stop waiting for timing.
Stop waiting for certainty.

And recognize something uncomfortable:

Nobody is coming to interrupt the pattern for you.

Your career changes the moment you decide to stop tolerating what you already know no longer fits.

If you didn't worry about the success vs. failure, what would you be excited about doing next?
