In this episode of the Exceptional Sales Leader Podcast, I welcome Suresh Babu, a distinguished sales expert and author, tuning in from Frankfurt, Germany. In an insightful conversation, Suresh shares his unorthodox journey from a DJ in Chennai to an established sales leader in Europe, illuminating the intersections between his musical roots and his sales acumen. Suresh’s unique perspective on the sales process underscores the necessity of human connection and adaptability in sales strategy, emphasising the importance of H2H (Human to Human) interactions. As the discussion unfolds, the conversation shifts towards examining the systemic challenges sales teams face and the common pitfalls in traditional sales forecasting. Suresh debunks myths around sales being a people problem, advocating instead for a strategic overhaul of the sales system to curb revenue leakage. By sharing compelling insights from his book, “Revenue Reality,” Suresh provides a roadmap to enhance forecasting accuracy through evidence-based assessments rather than relying on mere confidence levels. Delve into this episode to discover how a strategic focus on process and empathy can redefine sales success.
