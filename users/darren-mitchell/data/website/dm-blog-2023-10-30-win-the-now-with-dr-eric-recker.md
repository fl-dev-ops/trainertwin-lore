Today, more than ever, we are juggling multiple expectations, multiple tasks, and the sheer volume of information being thrown in our direction never seems to abate. There are so many distractions in front of us, with people and organisations vying for our attention, it is easy to get overwhelmed, lose focus, and in many cases, burnout. This is especially true for sales leaders, where we can fall into a false sense of urgency, especially from other people’s agendas. In today’s episode, I have the pleasure of speaking with Dr. Eric Recker, Dentist, Coach, Speaker, Author and recovering Triathelete. Eric is also the founder and CEO of #WINtheNOW and is on a mission to help 10million win the now. It is a fabulous conversation with loads of ideas and perspectives to help us be ever more present in the moment.
