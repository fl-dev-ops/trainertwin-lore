In this insightful episode of the Exceptional Sales Leader Podcast, I am joined by John Tarnoff, career coach and partner at Mid Career Lab. John shares his diverse journey from the world of entertainment to becoming a career coach, emphasising the importance of resilience and continuous adaptation in today’s ever-changing job landscape. The discussion is particularly poignant for mid-career professionals who are navigating career challenges amidst technological advancements and evolving job market demands. John highlights the necessity of pivoting career mindsets, especially for those in their mid-career stages. He explores the concept of “productising” oneself, urging job seekers to see themselves as products needing to fit the market demands rather than merely employees looking for positions. Throughout the episode, insightful discussions on the role of AI in reshaping career strategies, the pivotal role of networking, and the need for continual learning emerge as critical themes. John offers practical advice on how to leverage one’s experience and adjust strategies to thrive in an AI-influenced job market.
