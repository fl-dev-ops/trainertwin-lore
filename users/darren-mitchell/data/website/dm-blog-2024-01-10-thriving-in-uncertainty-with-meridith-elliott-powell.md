If there is one constant in business, it is change, and with change can come various degrees of uncertainty, both for leaders and members of the team, as well as customers. One of the key challenges, and opportunities for leaders, especially in 2024, is to look at the change that is happening, along with any associated uncertainty, and reframe it, to look more optimistically at the change, thereby providing the opportunity for the team and business to thrive. In this episode, I have the absolute privilege of speaking with Meridith Elliott Powell, business motivational speaker, business strategist, CEO of Motion First, and Chair of the National Speakers Association. Meridith is an expert in thriving in uncertainty, and covers 4 of her 9 key pillars of the THRIVE System in this epsiode. Well worth listening to more than once.
