In this episode of the Exceptional Sales Leader Podcast, I am joined by Mark Mills OBE, a distinguished entrepreneur from the UK, who has founded and sold five successful businesses.  Mark shares his path from a young entrepreneur selling broken biscuits to a business mogul owning Cardpoint, a prominent independent cash machine company. Both lighthearted and informative, the episode dives into the challenges and triumphs Mark faced, offering an abundance of wisdom for aspiring entrepreneurs. He provides an enriching perspective on the importance of perseverance and refining business models, emphasising, “Failure cannot live with persistence.” He discusses various business ventures, including how an ordinary petrol stop and a US trip led to groundbreaking ideas. For Mark, entrepreneurship isn’t just about profit but adaptability and integrity. His insights offer a blueprint for identifying not only profitable ventures but also a deep sense of purpose beyond just financial success.
