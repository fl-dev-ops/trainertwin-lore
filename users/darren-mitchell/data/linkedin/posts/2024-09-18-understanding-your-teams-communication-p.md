Understanding your team's communication preferences is key! 📞💬 Leaders, don't overlook the importance of nonverbal cues—even on video calls. Neil Fleming identified 4 key learning preferences in the mid 1980's, known as V.A.R.K.
- Visual
- Auditory
- Reading & Writing
- Kinaesthetic

It is important that we understand these preferences of our people as it helps us better communicate with them, through whichever medium.
