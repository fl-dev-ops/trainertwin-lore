***What Sales Leaders Can Learn From The Brisbane Lions***

When everyone expects you to falter, that’s when your leadership matters most.

The Brisbane Lions just went back-to-back in 2025 and their journey holds powerful lessons for any sales team chasing excellence.

1️⃣  Culture is built through consistency, not flashes of brilliance

Chris Fagan didn’t engineer one perfect season and call it a day. He’s been laying the foundations since 2017, through lean years, injuries, doubts & building standards that players internalise. 

In sales, that means the daily rituals, the accountability systems, the language you use, those are what carry you through adversity.

2️⃣  High performance means embracing both pressure and feedback

Fagan is lauded for his “factual” approach, rewarding where deserved, but also confronting what needs improving. 

Great sales leaders do that too: catch wins early, give credit, but don’t shy away from hard truths that drive growth.

3️⃣  Overcoming adversity without losing faith

In 2025, the Lions had setbacks including injuries, tough losses, questions about whether they could deliver again.

Yet Fagan and his group stayed the course. They didn’t panic; they controlled what they could, leaned into the next contest. In fact, in the second half of the season, they embraced ‘running into the fire’. The team backed themselves, no matter the outcome.

In sales, when deals fall through or targets are missed, staying composed and tactical is what separates leaders from wishful thinkers.

4️⃣  The power of belief, even when others doubt you

Despite external voices casting them as "underdogs," the Lions kept their focus internally. 

Sales leaders, your team listens: if you believe in them, especially when external pressure is high, they step up beyond what they thought possible. Are you demonstrating unwavering belief in your sales team?

5️⃣  Leadership is about enabling others to lead

Fagan has been recognised not just for results, but for how he builds his coaching group, uplifts assistants, invests in younger players, and refuses to take all the credit.

In sales, exceptional leaders build other leaders. Delegate, mentor, trust, empower. That’s how you scale sustainable success.

The Lions’ success isn’t magic. It’s the outcome of relentless standards, faith when it’s hard, and leadership that elevates everyone around you.

What adversity is your team facing right now? How might leaning into “staying the course” change your outcomes?
