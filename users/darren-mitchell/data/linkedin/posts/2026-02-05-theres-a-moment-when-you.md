There’s a moment when you think, “There has to be something else.”

And sometimes, that thought is what keeps you going.

What if the hard parts aren’t random, but shaping an incredible story you haven’t fully seen yet?
What if what you’re going through right now is making you into the only person who can do what’s next?

You don’t need to see the whole picture.
You just need to accept that today’s challenges are here to make you better, not bitter.

Stop comparing. Don’t read your own press, good or bad.
Reinforce what makes you special, because there’s only one you, and there always will be.

Self-acceptance isn’t comfort.
It’s confidence in becoming who you’re meant to be.
