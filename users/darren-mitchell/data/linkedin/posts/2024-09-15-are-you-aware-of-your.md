***Are You Aware Of Your Conversations?***

The title of this post may seem obvious and you may be thinking what a ridiculous question? 🤷‍♂️

Of course we are aware of conversations - we have them every single day. And most of us focus our attention on the conversations we have with others - especially conversations with our team, our partners and our customers. 

Are you aware though, of the conversations you are having with yourself? As sales leaders, this question is critical as it can have a direct impact on the conversations we have with others. Earl Nightingale often said that we become what we think about most, and this concept has origins back to Solomon “As someone thinks in their heart, so they are.”

As sales leaders, we must remember that our team is and always will be a reflection of us, so not only do we need to be conscious and intentional with the actions and behaviours we demonstrate, we must also be equally conscious of the language we use, especially with our self. Why? Because our team will reflect this.

➤ When you first wake up in the morning, what is the thought process you have and what conversation are you having with yourself? Are you optimistic and looking forward to making a positive impact, or do you suffer from excusitis, especially on a Monday?

➤ Just prior to leading the weekly sales team meeting, what conversation are you having? Are you looking at the opportunities or the issues?

➤ As you are about to enter an important pitch to a prospective customer, what is the conversation? ‘I hope we can influence this customer to come on board?’ or ‘Regardless of the outcome, we will leave this customer with an impression of increase’. 

Whatever situation you are in and dealing with each day, if we are able to be more consciously aware of our self talk, of the conversations we are having with ourself, we can have greater control on what happens next.

One of my earliest mentors always said ‘if you can language it, you can have it’. This was designed to help adopt more of a growth mindset, become more intentional and become more aware of self talk. When we can master these conversations, it enables us to then master the external conversations we all focus on.

As we begin the brand new week, here is the challenge. Spend some quality time over the next 24 hours really considering the conversations you are having with yourself. Are you challenging yourself as well as empowering yourself, or are you continuing to be your number 1 critic? Build some clear awareness around this & understand that whatever the conversation you are having with yourself, at some stage, and in some form, this will come out in the conversations you will have with others. So let’s make sure we are having positive, encouraging & uplifting conversations. After all, this is a choice. 

Here’s to a massive week 👊
