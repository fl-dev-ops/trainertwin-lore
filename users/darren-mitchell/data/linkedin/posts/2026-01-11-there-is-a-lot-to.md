There is a lot to be said for developing patience in sales - it may sound counter intuitive, especially when we have either direct or implied pressure to hit a number, however, the ability to 'detach' from the outcome can in fact become a superpower.

If we have done everything in our power to create an environment where a prospective customer can make an informed purchasing decision, then we should be able to 'let go'. Easier said than done, I know, however, it is amazing at what is possible when we are able to develop patience and detach from the outcome. What do you think?
