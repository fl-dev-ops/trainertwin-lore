***Greatness Doesn’t Care with Jason Dunn***

In this inspiring episode of The Exceptional Sales Leader Podcast, I am joined by Jason Dunn, a high-performance coach, ultra-endurance athlete & author, as we explore the transformative journey from ordinary to limitless. Jason shares anecdotes from his life, describing his early dreams of becoming a professional soccer player, the adversities he faced, and how an unexpected opportunity in a corporate triathlon set him on a path of self-discovery. 

This candid discussion delves into how Jason overcame obstacles and honed his discipline, ultimately crafting a framework for greatness. Throughout the conversation, Jason equips listeners with practical insights into the pillars of success, emphasising that greatness is defined by personal growth and the continuous pursuit of becoming the best version of oneself. 

Jason explains how a well-conceived purpose can navigate individuals through fear and adversity. Further, he discusses key elements such as mindset, courage, and discipline, crucial to achieving long-term goals in life and sales careers. With actionable advice and motivational stories, this episode serves as a manual for those seeking to challenge their limits and redefine their capabilities.
