Hiring great salespeople is one of the hardest jobs in leadership.
And most leaders won’t admit it.

On my recent conversation with Russ Hawkins on The Exceptional Sales Leader Podcast, he nailed a truth we all feel but rarely say out loud:

Great sales candidates can sell themselves better than anyone.
They’re energetic. Convincing. Charismatic.
You walk out of the interview excited.

But then comes the field test.
That’s when you discover the gaps, the habits, the blind spots, the frailties they never pitched.
And that’s where real sales leadership begins.

Because while everyone is technically “in sales,”
not everyone actually knows how to sell.

And it’s our job to coach, sharpen, and build them into the reps they can become.
