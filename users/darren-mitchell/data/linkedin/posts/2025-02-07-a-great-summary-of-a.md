A great summary of a fascinating conversation I enjoyed recently with Joelle Gropper Kaufman 🎗️on The Exceptional Sales Leader Podcast.

As leaders, we can get thrown curveballs that if we are not diligent, they can throw us completely off course. 

We must understand that challenges are part of the role, and whilst we may find challenges difficult, it is our choices around the responses to challenges that can have a huge impact, not only on our current environment, but also the people within that environment. 

Joelle fills the conversation with ideas and insights to help leaders better prepare for the inevitable curveballs, turning adversity into leadership gold.
