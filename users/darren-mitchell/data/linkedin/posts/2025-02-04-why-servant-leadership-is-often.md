**Why Servant Leadership is Often Misunderstood in Corporate Sales**

In sales, leadership is often associated with driving performance, hitting quotas, and pushing teams to close deals. But servant leadership flips that script—focusing on empowering and enabling teams rather than simply directing them. 

And yet, it's often misunderstood.

Many assume servant leadership means being "too soft" or putting people’s happiness above results. But the truth is: servant leaders drive stronger performance, not by demanding more, but by creating an environment where people want to give more.

✅ They remove obstacles so their teams can sell more effectively.

✅ They develop individuals, knowing that better people create better results.

✅ They foster trust, leading to higher engagement and lower turnover.

Servant leadership isn’t about coddling—it’s about unlocking potential. And in sales, that’s how sustainable success is built.
