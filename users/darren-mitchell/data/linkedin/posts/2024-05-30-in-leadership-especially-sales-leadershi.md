In leadership, especially sales leadership, it is critical to understand that your team members are highly likely to be at different stages of their development, and may also have different levels of commitment. So to lead them in the same way will not deliver the results that you are looking for. This is why leadership flexibility is so important, where we change our leadership approach based on where the individual is at. This is what situational leadership is all about.

I often say that the leader who is able to demonstrate the most behavioural and leadership flexibility, is the leader who will maximise the potential of their team, and more often than not, deliver exceptional results.

As you reflect on your team, and the way you lead them, are you guilty of leading all members of the team in the same way, or are you focusing on leading them based what the team requires at that moment? Would love your thoughts.
