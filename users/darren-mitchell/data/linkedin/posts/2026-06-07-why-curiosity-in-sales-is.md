***Why Curiosity in Sales is a Superpower***

AI isn't going to replace great salespeople.

But it will expose the ones who stopped being curious.

For years, sales success was often tied to having the right pitch, the right process, or the right playbook.

Today?

Those things are becoming commodities.

➤ AI can generate emails.
➤ AI can summarise meetings.
➤ AI can build proposals.
➤ AI can even coach conversations.

So what's left?

Curiosity.

The salesperson who asks better questions will always outperform the salesperson who delivers better presentations.

The manager who explores new ideas, tests new tools, and challenges old assumptions will always outpace the manager who relies on "the way we've always done it."

Curiosity is no longer a soft skill.

It's a competitive advantage.

In an AI-powered world, the winners won't be the people with all the answers.

They'll be the people asking questions nobody else thought to ask.

👉 What are our customers really struggling with?
👉 What signals are we missing?
👉 What can AI help us see that we've never seen before?
👉 What beliefs about selling need to be challenged?

The future belongs to the curious.

The rest risk becoming experts in a game that no longer exists.

Sales leaders: are you rewarding curiosity on your team or just compliance?

What's one question you've started asking differently because of AI?
