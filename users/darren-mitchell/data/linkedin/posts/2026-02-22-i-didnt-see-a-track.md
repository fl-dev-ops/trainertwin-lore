“I didn’t see a track in front of me.”

That’s what St John Craner shared with me on The Exceptional Sales Leader Podcast.

He walked away from a skyscraper salary to a bungalow.

Not because he had to.
Because he chose to.

He believes a principle is only a principle when it costs you money.

So he bet on himself.

He started small. Hustled hard. Picked up a few clients.
Then made a decision that changed everything:

👉 Obsess over mastery.

➤ Sales psychology.
➤ Human behaviour.
➤ Heuristics. Biases. Curiosity.

Not the buzzwords, the fundamentals.

“I’m going to get world-class at this.”

And he never stopped.

Here’s the lesson for sales leaders:

✅ If there’s no clear track in front of you, build one.
✅ If you want world-class results, commit to world-class learning.

Comfort doesn’t create mastery. Commitment does.

What’s one principle you believe in enough to let it cost you something?
