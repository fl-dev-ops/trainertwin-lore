Why are you in sales? Is sales the right career for you? This is a question that I have been continuing to ask people over the years, especially over the last 10 as I have dived deep into coaching, mentoring and facilitating.

And since starting The Exceptional Sales Leader Podcast in 2020, it is a question that I have asked many of my guests, especially as it relates to sales leadership. As I was re-listening to a recent episode with my wonderful friend 🏀 Stephen Oommen, I just had to post this little clip.

In it, Stephen suggests that perhaps only 40% of sales people globally should be in sales. As he says in the clip, it may be controversial, and he may get hate mail, however, there may well be some honest truth in this. Which begs the question - why are you in sales?
