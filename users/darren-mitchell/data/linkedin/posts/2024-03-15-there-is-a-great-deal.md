There is a great deal written and spoken about how to be a great leader.  Techniques, processes, methodologies, 'secrets' - all designed to help drive great outcomes, and to create great leaders.   Sometimes though, there is a missing piece to the puzzle, and often, this is the key piece that is often overlooked. The power of self leadership.

If there is one attribute that exceptional leaders have over their colleagues, it is their ability to lead self. To know themselves so well, that there is no need or desire to pretend, to 'keep up appearances', or to have to conform. They have worked so hard on themselves that when it comes to leading others, they can build relationships, drive standards, and create loyalty that is difficult to match. 

They have mastered the 4 pillars of self leadership.

As a leader, how much have you invested in leading yourself, and which of the 4 pillars could you continue to build your leadership muscle around? Would love your thoughts 🙏
