***Become Laser Focused on Your Language***

As a Sales Leader, I’ve learned this the hard way:
𝗟𝗮𝗻𝗴𝘂𝗮𝗴𝗲 𝗶𝘀 𝗻𝗲𝘃𝗲𝗿 𝗻𝗲𝘂𝘁𝗿𝗮𝗹 𝗶𝗻 𝘀𝗮𝗹𝗲𝘀.
It either expands thinking… or it quietly puts a ceiling on it.

That’s why in our world we don’t talk about expectations.
We talk about 𝘀𝘁𝗮𝗻𝗱𝗮𝗿𝗱𝘀.
We talk about 𝗼𝗽𝗽𝗼𝗿𝘁𝘂𝗻𝗶𝘁𝗶𝗲𝘀.

“Expectations” often feel like pressure. Like compliance. Like 𝘥𝘰 𝘵𝘩𝘪𝘴 𝘰𝘳 𝘦𝘭𝘴𝘦.

Standards are different. Standards are chosen. They reflect who we decide to 𝗯𝗲, not what we’re told to do.

And this is where sales leadership really shows up.

Great sales leadership isn’t about pushing people harder. It’s about shaping an environment where higher performance becomes the natural outcome.

Words create that environment.

When you raise standards, you invite ownership. When you speak in opportunity, you unlock creativity. When you focus on being & not just doing, behaviour changes without force.

Exceptional sales professionals don’t “𝘩𝘪𝘵 𝘵𝘢𝘳𝘨𝘦𝘵𝘴.” They operate from an identity where excellence is normal.

Exceptional sales leaders don’t manage activity. They model language, presence and standards that others rise to.

𝗦𝗼 𝗵𝗲𝗿𝗲’𝘀 𝘁𝗵𝗲 𝗰𝗵𝗮𝗹𝗹𝗲𝗻𝗴𝗲:

Pay attention to the words you use with your team this week. Then listen closely to the words they use with customers.

Because long before performance shows up in the numbers, it shows up in the language.

And language always reveals who you believe you are and what you believe is possible.
