***Your Sales Leadership Mission***

As a Sales Leader, have you ever stopped to think and consider what your sales leadership mission is? It might sound like a weird question, perhaps even a little woo woo, however, what I have found, is that Exceptional Sales Leaders are always on mission.

They 100% understand why they are in the role, what defines success, what drives them and what the path to the ultimate outcome looks like. 

And very rarely is it the sales number, or ‘Presidents Club’, ‘Number 1 Club’, or any other extrinsic measure. Whilst those accolades are nice and well worth experiencing, it is not what drives exceptional sales leaders. What drives them are intrinsic motivators, those motivators that are often invisible, but are like rocket fuel that propels them forward, as well as the team.

As a sales leadership coach and mentor, what is interesting is when I ask sales leaders as to what their mission is, many of them find it difficult to answer. Or their answer leans to the extrinsic. 

A highly respected colleague of mine recently spent time with some super successful business people, many of whom had recently exited their businesses, and if a measurement of mission and success was the $millions in their bank account, then they had achieved their mission. 

Why was it then that the vast majority of them were not only miserable, but deeply jealous and envious of my colleague? It was because they had not identified their true mission - they had focused all of their attention on the extrinsic as a source of mission. And now they felt empty and unfulfilled.

As we begin this brand new week, what better time than right now to sit down, free of distractions, and seriously contemplate and begin to define your sales leadership mission. And one key underlying principle to consider - it cannot be about you!!

✅ What is your mission? 
✅ What do you want to create? 
✅ How long do you want your sales leadership shadow to be? 
✅ What will be your legacy? 

My mission is to help sales leaders become exceptional, where they can create more meaning, more satisfaction, fulfilment, and create a leadership legacy so big, their ripple effect will be felt for generations to come.
