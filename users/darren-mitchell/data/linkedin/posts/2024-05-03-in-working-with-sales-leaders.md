In working with sales leaders, I constantly share with them the importance of creating a culture of servitude, underlined by the fact that as leaders, we are there to serve our team, our team are not there to serve us.

For some leaders, they misunderstand the concept of servant leadership, thinking that it is about 'doing' things for the team, liking the concept to a 'servant' that is at the beck & call of others. This is not so.

Servant leadership is a very powerful leadership concept, and when executed well, can deliver exceptional results.  It takes work, it takes courage, and it requires the leader to understand exactly what success looks like, and the standards that are required to underpin that success. Servant leaders are also ruthlessly uncompromising when it comes to maintaining those standards, and they do this through servitude, not through position or title.
