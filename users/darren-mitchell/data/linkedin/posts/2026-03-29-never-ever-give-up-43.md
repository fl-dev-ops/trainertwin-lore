***Never, Ever Give Up***

43 points down… and they still found a way to win.

Because belief beats circumstance every single time.

This afternoon, my Melbourne Demons pulled off one of the greatest AFL comebacks I’ve watched in years.
Down by 43 points  against Carlton.
Momentum gone.
Energy flat.
Crowd starting to think it was over.

But the players didn’t.

They didn’t panic.
They didn’t finger-point.
And they didn’t throw the playbook out the window.

Instead, they doubled down on fundamentals:
➤ Pressure 
➤ Connection 
➤ Trust 
➤ Repeat effort 
➤ Staying present in the contest 

Bit by bit, the game shifted.
Not because of luck, but because of belief, tenacity, and their refusal to break from their system.

And honestly… it’s the perfect metaphor for Sales Leadership.

Every Sales Leader has faced a “43-points-down moment”:
📉 Pipeline drying up
📉 A competitor swooping in
📉 A star performer losing confidence
📉 A quarter trending the wrong way
📉 A team absorbing blow after blow

In those moments, your team looks directly at you.
➤ Not for magic.
➤ Not for heroics.
➤ But for composure.
➤ For clarity.
➤ For belief.

Great Sales Leaders don’t abandon the strategy when things go sideways.
They reinforce it.
They remind their team what they’re capable of.
They create an environment where effort compounds and confidence grows.
They model calm, conviction, and discipline, even when the scoreboard says otherwise.

Because winning cultures aren’t built in the easy quarters.
They’re built in the ones where everything should fall apart… but doesn’t.

If the Demons taught us anything this afternoon, it’s this:
You’re never out of the game unless you decide you are.

Belief isn’t a feeling, it’s a leadership decision you make long before the comeback arrives.

What’s one thing YOU do as a leader to help your team stay in the game when the pressure is on?
