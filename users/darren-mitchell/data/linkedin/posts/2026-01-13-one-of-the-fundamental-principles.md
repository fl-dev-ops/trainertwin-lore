One of the fundamental principles in sales is creating a unique value proposition and messaging for the marketplace, and then making the marketplace aware of that messaging.

There are multiple channels to get this messaging out, and many organisations focus on the latest and greatest tools and apps, whether it be video, audio, LinkedIn messaging, Facebook ads plus a host of other channels. Interestingly, email doesn't appear to be at the top of the messaging priority list for many organisations.🤷‍♂️

I enjoyed a fascinating conversation recently with Adam Rosen on The Exceptional Sales Leader Podcast. Adam specialises in helping businesses grow, and has landed some of the biggest brands on the planet as clients, through cold email outreach.
