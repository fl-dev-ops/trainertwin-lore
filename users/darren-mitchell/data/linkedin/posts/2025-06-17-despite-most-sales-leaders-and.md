Despite most sales leaders and sales people understanding that only approximately 3% of prospective customers are ready to make an immediate buying decision, many of them go for the close anyway. Any wonder sales does not have the 'best' reputation.🤷‍♂️

If you have followed me for a while, you would have heard me talk about sales being the ultimate form of service, and being of service does actually include closing a sales, but the key is to know when. It is highly unlikely that the close will happen on the first call or first interaction. So be of service, deliver value and ensure that you remain top of mind with your prospective customer. This will often take patience.
