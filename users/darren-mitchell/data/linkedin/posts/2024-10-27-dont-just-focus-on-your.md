***Don’t Just Focus On Your To Do List - Focus On Your To Be List***

With under 2 months left until Christmas, the pressure cooker will be ramping up left, right and centre to get as many deals closed off as possible. Senior executives conducting daily cadence reviews to lock in committed forecasts. Sales Executive’s doing whatever they can to bring forward deals into this quarter. To say this time of year can be a stressful time would be an understatement.

For many sales leaders, this pressure can only lengthen the items on the ‘to do list’, as they scramble to fit more items & activities into an already packed agenda. The danger though is that mistakes can be made through trying to take on too much, and these mistakes can create a precedence for the new quarter. This may sound counter intuitive, however the key to ending the year well is to actually slow down, and become more targeted with your attention and more intentional with your actions.

How can this be done? Well, instead of focusing all of your attention on the to do list, how about creating a ‘to be list’. Now this may sound ‘wishy washy’ and a bit ‘woo woo’ but I guarantee it can be the difference that makes a massive difference. As a sales leader, when was the last time you sat down and consciously thought about how to ‘be’? Have you ever created a to be list?

We know that your team is watching your every move and at this time of year especially, role modelling excellence is critical. So what can you put on your to be list? Here are some thought prompters:

🙋‍♂️ Be patient - with your team, with your senior directors, with your customers.
🙋‍♂️ Be optimistic - take an intentional glass half full approach.
🙋‍♂️ Be present - remember that the greatest gift you can give anyone, is the gift of your attention.
🙋‍♂️ Be consistent - it may not be a sexy word, but consistency compounds.
🙋‍♂️ Be intentional - with everything you do.
🙋‍♂️ Be relentless - in the pursuit of excellence, not in the pursuit of the ideal outcome.
🙋‍♂️ Be realistic - if deals are not going to close this calendar year, plant the seed for when they will realistically be closed.
🙋‍♂️ Be authentic - don’t pretend to be someone you are not. Be you.

These will enable you to role model the right behaviours, set a clear example for your team and those around you, and who knows, may actually end up creating some great wins on the scoreboard.

So there’s the challenge for this week. Create your ‘to be list’ - you may actually be surprised at what happens when you do! 👊
