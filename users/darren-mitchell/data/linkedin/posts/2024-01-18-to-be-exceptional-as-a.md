To be exceptional as a Leader, we must be able to identify key metrics for success, measure progress against those, as well as keeping an eye on the future. Unfortunately some leaders, when placed under pressure, gravitate back to 'management' and forget that their core role is to 'lead'.

When this happens, not only does the leader's credibility start to be impacted, so does how the people in the team operate - often times resulting in self preservation and protectionism, rather than looking for opportunities to contribute to team growth. The end result? Lower performance, which more times than not will result in micro management - it becomes a vicious circle.

As a leader, we must stay the course, especially when the pressure begins to be applied. Would you agree? Would love your thoughts👇
