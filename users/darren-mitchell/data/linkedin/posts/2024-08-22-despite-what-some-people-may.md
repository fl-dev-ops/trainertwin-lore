Despite what some people may believe and tell you, sustainable sales success is directly linked to an exceptional sales culture. Whilst some teams and organisations may achieve short term success, it is a strong, servant lead sales culture that will win in the end. 

I have been speaking about the power of servant leadership for years, especially in sales, however, often the term is misunderstood or criticised. Servant leadership does not mean that we do the work for our team, or that we wait for them to give us instructions. Servant leadership is about creating independent thinking, of empowering sales people to take ownership, of demonstrating humility and driving high standards of excellence. It is also often about asking the sales team as to what role they would like the sales leader to play in a sales conversation, which can very very impactful.
