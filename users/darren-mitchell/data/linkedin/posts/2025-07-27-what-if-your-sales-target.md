***What if your sales target is the shackle, not the stretch?***

Every year, leaders gather in boardrooms and slap a number on the whiteboard.

“Here’s your target.”
“Big year ahead.”
“Stretch goal.”

But let me ask you this:

Is it really a stretch?
Or is it just a limitation dressed up as ambition?

Here’s what I’ve learned leading B2B sales teams:

👉 The number we’re chasing is often designed to feel just uncomfortable enough to be motivating — but safe enough to be acceptable.

And that’s where the real danger lies.

👉 We train teams to chase permission-based potential.
👉We hit the number and celebrate like we’ve conquered Everest.

But what if we were only climbing a hill?

I remember a year we were given a “massive” target — the kind that turns stomachs.

The team didn’t flinch.

We shattered it by Q3.

Not because we had better leads.

Not because we got lucky.

Because we decided the target was a floor, not a ceiling.

We stopped asking “how do we get there?”

And started asking:
“What if this isn’t the top?”

And that’s when the game changed.

✅ Pipeline doubled.
✅ Conversion rates surged.
✅ We stopped managing numbers and started unleashing possibility.

Sales leaders — let’s get honest:
👉 Are we driving high performance?
❓ Or are we boxing in brilliance?

Your target might be the real bottleneck.

👉 Not your team.
👉 Not your market.
👉 Not your product.

So, tell me honestly:

Is your target a limitation or a launchpad?
Let’s debate. Would love your thoughts  👇
