Unfortunately there is a pattern that still continues to play out in sales organisations today - a top performing sales individual contributor is promoted to the position of Sales Leader, and for some reason, this individual is not provided the coaching, training & mentoring to make the transition a successful one.

And the organisation wonders why the results that the individual contributor was delivering, falls off a cliff!! 

Organisations must set their sales leaders up for success, period. Don't just expect them to know how to lead. Train them, mentor them, teach them.
