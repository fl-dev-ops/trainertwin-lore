AI can make a proposal faster.

It cannot make it useful.

The useful part is still human work:

- understanding the customer's actual problem
- testing the reasoning, not just accepting the output
- showing the source, method and decision
- making the next action obvious for both people and agents

Otherwise, you have built a meat proxy: someone forwarding AI output they have not read, cannot explain and are not accountable for.

In this week's AI Operators, Darren Mitchell joined David Pengelley, Richard Webbe and Matt Slager to unpack the operator advantage.

Use AI to accelerate the work.
Keep the judgement visible.
Keep responsibility human-owned.

Swipe through the comic, then ask one uncomfortable question:

What can AI accelerate in your business, and what must your people still understand?
