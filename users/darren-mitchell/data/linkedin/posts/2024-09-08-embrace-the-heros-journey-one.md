***Embrace The Hero’s Journey***

One of the mistakes I see sales leaders often make when leading their team, is treating every single sales person in exactly the same way. Whilst it is important to set high standards of excellence and consistent performance metrics, it is also important to demonstrate flexibility in how to lead the team, through understanding where each sales person is on their journey.

The challenge for many sales leaders is driving consistently high performance and quite often, they fall prey to the curse of comparison - comparing each sales person in the team, or even worse, comparing the sales person to themselves when they were carrying the bag.

One of the greatest methods I have seen to lead the team effectively is to embrace The Hero’s Journey. Originally created by Joseph Campbell in the 1940’s, it charts the progression of the ‘Hero’ through 12 steps, and explains that people (& businesses as well) are on their own journey to ‘success’ and therefore, very often will be at different stages.

1. Ordinary World
2. Call to Adventure
3. Refusal of the call
4. Meeting the mentor
5. Crossing the threshold
6. Tests, allies & enemies
7. Approach to inmost cave
8. The Ordeal
9. The Reward
10. The Road back
11. Resurrection
12. Return with the elixir

As a sales leader, if I can understand what stage of the journey each of my team members are at, I can customise my approach and increase the odds of unleashing their fullest potential.

For example, one person may still be sitting in the ordinary world wondering why all the change that is happening around them is necessary, refusing the call to change and hence, they stagnate.

Another person may have accepted the call to adventure and crossed the threshold into a new world, adapting to a new process or method of engaging with customers.

Yet another person may be significantly further down the road, working with mentors and preparing for their greatest ordeal, which will provide them with a huge reward, that ultimately will benefit the rest of the team.

As a sales leader, embrace the Hero’s Journey. Understand where your team are on the journey, and lead them accordingly. Also understand at what stage of the journey you are as well & what guidance & assistance you require.
