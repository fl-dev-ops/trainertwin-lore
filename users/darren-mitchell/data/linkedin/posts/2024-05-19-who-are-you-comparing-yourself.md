***Who Are You Comparing Yourself To?***

In 2024, there is information coming at us at a rate faster than in any other time in history. Whether it be the print media, visual media and especially social media, companies and individuals are screaming ‘look at me’ to advertise their products and services. 

It is difficult to look at any social media platform today without being bombarded by ‘Influencers’ spruiking something and it is equally challenging not to be sucked in to the comparison game. Photographs of ‘successful’ people standing next to their luxury car, or standing in front of their massive house, private jet, motor home or yacht. Sometimes we can look at these people and what they ‘have’ and begin to feel a little envious. (Question- do they really ‘have’ what they show?)

We can also often fall into the comparison game within our own industry or our own company, especially when our organisation promotes internal competition through sales incentives and the classic ‘sales scoreboard’. This only promotes comparison behaviours. Is this healthy and does this promote exceptional performance?

Now, let me be clear - I’m all for social proof when it comes to increasing our influence (Cialdini’s Laws of Influence), but I’m simply not interested in the notion that comparing ourselves to others is healthy or drives exceptional performance. Hence, when working with sales leaders and their teams, I am very particular on this topic - who should you be comparing yourself to? 

I do believe we can learn a lot from others, so rather than playing the comparison game, we should seek to identify & understand the specific strategies successful people run that enables them to be successful, and model that. This is modelling excellence, and it is what exceptional performers do all the time.

If you are going to play the comparison game then simply compare yourself to the person who stares back at you in the mirror each day. How can you incrementally improve today based on yesterday’s efforts, and set a goal to ever so slightly improve tomorrow based on today’s efforts? That is the only comparison game that you need to play. Allow your competitors to tie themselves up in knots comparing themselves to ‘highly successful people’, whilst you steadily improve each and every day.

If you have found yourself playing the comparison game, this week, make some adjustments. Simply compare yourself to you, focus on modelling excellence, increase your targeted discretionary effort, and watch what happens. You may actually find others beginning to compare themselves to you. How ironic would that be?

Here’s to a massive week!! 👊
