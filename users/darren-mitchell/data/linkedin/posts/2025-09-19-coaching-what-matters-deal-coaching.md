***Coaching What Matters***

Deal coaching is important. But if you’re only coaching the deal, you’re missing the point.

Because the real multiplier effect comes when you coach the person, not just the pipeline.

Most Sales Leaders think they’re coaching when they’re asking: “What’s closing this quarter?”

That’s not coaching—that’s inspection.

Yes, deal coaching has its place.

But if all you do is chase numbers, you’ll be coaching the same problems over and over again.

The best leaders go deeper. They coach the core drivers of performance:

1️⃣  Mindset : Confidence, resilience, ownership. Deals are won in the rep’s head before they’re won in the customer’s.

2️⃣  Preparation : Research, planning, and intention. A rep who prepares poorly will always struggle to advance deals, no matter how “coachable” the opportunity looks.

3️⃣  Core Sales Skills : Discovery, questioning, storytelling, objection handling. These aren’t one-off lessons, they need continuous sharpening.

When you coach the person, deal coaching becomes easier and more effective.

When you only coach the deal, you stay stuck firefighting.

Great Sales Leaders don’t just win deals. They build salespeople who can win any deal.

Sales Directors and CROs: Are you coaching your people or just their pipeline?
