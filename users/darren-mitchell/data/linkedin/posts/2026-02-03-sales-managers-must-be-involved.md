Sales managers must be involved in developing their team. Period.

I once stopped a sales training because the sales manager wasn’t in the room.

“Everyone’s here,” they told me.
No they weren’t.

If the team is expected to improve, the manager needs to be there too.
Not “too busy.”
Not “calendar’s full.”
Present.

Sales development isn’t just for reps.
It’s just as important for the people leading them.

We put constant pressure on salespeople to get better, but somehow remove that same expectation from sales managers.🤷‍♂️

That’s a big mistake.

Strong sales teams don’t develop on their own.
They’re developed by managers who show up, constantly & consistently.

I had a great conversation with Walter Crosby recently on The Exceptional Sales Leader Podcast where he shared a very similar experience. Sales Leaders, we do not want to be Bob!
