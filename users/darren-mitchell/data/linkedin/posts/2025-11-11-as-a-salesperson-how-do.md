As a salesperson, how do you see yourself? This can sometimes sound like a weird question, however, the thinking patterns that we experience, will have an impact on our language, both to ourselves, and others, and definitely have an impact on our actions.

And yet, many sales people constantly focus on the external - the tactics, the strategies, the conversations.

Working on ourselves, understanding our current thinking patterns and paradigms, is critical if we are going to make any tangible change or create sustainable growth.
