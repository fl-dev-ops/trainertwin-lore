For years, internal departments within organisations have been in competition with each other, and this has especially been true for Sales & Marketing. It is often quite easy for someone in sales to point the finger at marketing for providing poor leads, as it is for someone in marketing to complain about why sales are unable to convert the leads they are provided!! 🤦‍♂️🤷‍♂️

The reality is,  when sales and marketing are able to work together more effectively, understand each other better, the business can hum and the results can become exceptional. When other departments can also be included on the journey, organisations can literally become unstoppable.

Here is a wonderful summary of an incredible conversation I had recently with Sam Shaper on The Exceptional Sales Leader Podcast. If you are either in sales or marketing, or interested in understanding how internal departments can work more effectively together, may I suggest you give this episode a listen.  Sam Shaper shares a number of wonderful insights that can help develop and drive more exceptional outcomes. The link is in the article.
