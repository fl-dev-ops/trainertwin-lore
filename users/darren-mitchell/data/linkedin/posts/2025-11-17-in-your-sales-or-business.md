In your sales or business career, have you ever been overwhelmed by the challenge ahead? The mountain looks so big, and the target so huge, that you cannot help but begin to plant seeds of doubt?

I've seen countless sales people and teams follow this pattern, where they literally convince themselves that the well is dry, there are no more customers, that the market simply is not ready to buy. And guess what? More often than not, this is exactly what starts to play out.

It doesn't have to be like this, and our role as a sales leader plays a critical part in helping our teams through the inevitable troughs. This is where a growth mindset comes in, together with a very simple principle - just one more. There is one more call you can make, one more connection request to make, take one more form of affirmative action. One step forward.

The power of one more creates momentum. Yes, the mountain may be huge, and the sales target massive, however what we have control over is the one more. Take one step forward, as Larry Weidel talks about in this short snippet from our recent conversation on The Exceptional Sales Leader Podcast. It is a simple concept, but a powerful one.
