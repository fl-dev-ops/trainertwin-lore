Part 1 of 3 (January - June 2024)

For the final post of 2024, I would like to take this opportunity to thank a very special group of people who have contributed to the quest of exceptional sales leadership. 

The Exceptional Sales Leader Podcast started 4 years ago with the aim of helping sales leaders elevate their leadership skillset & focus, to drive exceptional & sustainable results, and in 2024, I have been blessed to speak with 90 exceptional people from around the world, delving into what makes them exceptional, and doing my best to extract nuggets of gold from them to help sales leaders become exceptional. It has been an extraordinary privilege.

If you have listened to any of the episodes, I truly hope that they have been valuable and beneficial to you and your team. If you haven't yet listened to the podcast and you are interested in learning from some extraordinary people, please check out the podcast links in the comments below. 👇
