***Exceptional Sales Leaders Have This One Thing In Common***

When studying history and elite performance, there is one attribute that stands out as being a common thread amongst exceptional leaders. And this especially applies to sales leadership. 

Exceptional Sales Leaders are authentic. First coined in 2003 by Bill George, leaders who are authentic simply extract higher levels of performance over sustained periods of time, they have higher engagement levels, and they have higher levels of trust. They remove their mask.

As a sales leader, do you consider yourself authentic? Are you comfortable being you, irrespective of the pressure from others to ‘comply’ with a certain style? Are you comfortable removing the mask? Do you possess the following 5 qualities of an authentic leader and do you execute against them consistently?

1️⃣ Power of purpose - are you a purpose driven sales leader? Do you know why you became a sales leader and why you continue to be a sales leader? Purpose is often bigger than self.

2️⃣ Power of values - do you know your top 5 sales leadership values and if so, do you adhere to them every single day, without fail? These values are your North Star.

3️⃣ Power of relationships - do you genuinely care about your team and key stakeholders and based on that, do you constantly build quality relationships?

4️⃣ Power of self-discipline - do you take the actions that you need to take as a leader, irrespective of how you may feel? Are you constantly focused on doing the right thing, rather than trying to get things right? Leadership is not easy. Mistakes will be made. Being disciplined to move forward anyway is a big differentiator.

5️⃣ Power of heart - linked to relationships, are you able to capture the hearts and minds of your team and others through sharing a common purpose and allowing your team to also be their authentic self, in the pursuit of excellence?

Many ‘gurus’ will point to sales methodologies or processes as the key to sales success. Whilst these can assist, nothing can surpass a sales leader who has mastered the power of authenticity. A leader who genuinely cares, is never pretending to be anyone other than themselves, and certainly not comparing themselves to others. They are not wearing a mask.

If you are looking outside of yourself for the ‘secret’ to sales leadership success, stop. Look inside - embrace the above 5 key principles, and be you. That is the secret. That is what your team is craving for.
