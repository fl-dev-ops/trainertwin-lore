As we head closer to the end of the Financial Year, many sales teams are under immense pressure to hit numbers, to bring everything in they can to close out the year strongly. It is also a time, when some can take their 'foot off the gas' and cruise into the end of June. This can often lead to a soft July & August, and from there we are playing catch up.

As sales leaders, it is critical that we recognise that time waits for no one, and whilst the pressure may be high to hit the end of June strongly, we also need to ensure we are planting seeds for July and beyond. In studying successful people, there are a number of characteristics they possess and continue to focus on, irrespective of the time of year.  

How do you stack up against these? Would love your thoughts in the comments below. 👇
