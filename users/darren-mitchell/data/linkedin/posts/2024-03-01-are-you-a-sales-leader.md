Are you a sales leader who likes to maintain a level of control? It is fascinating the number of sales leaders I work with who crave control over their environment and literally everything in it.

This concept of being able to let go of control came through a coaching session I was running with a Senior Sales Leader a number of years ago. This sales leader was experiencing a huge amount of pressure to perform and hence had wound himself up so tight, that the slightest challenge presented to him or his team resulted in emotional outbursts, akin to a volcano erupting. Not a great way to oversee an exceptional environment.

Through working with him, we explored what it would look and feel like if he was able to relinquish the need to be in control, and through the process, he realised that by giving up the need for control, he actually gained something very powerful - control.

Would love your thoughts and experience on this. Do you need to feel in control, or have you managed to let go? Please comment below 👇🙏
