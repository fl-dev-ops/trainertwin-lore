***Be Ruthlessly Uncompromising***

A couple of months ago I was facilitating a leadership program for Leaders of an iconic Australian brand. It was an incredible 2 days where we went deep into leadership and what it means to be a leader in 2024 and beyond. Leadership today, more than ever, is not for the faint hearted. It was thrilling though to witness first hand the commitment of these leaders to developing their craft, and more specifically, to serve their team.

Halfway through day 1 we were exploring our leadership legacy and discussing key leadership principles when this came to the surface - leaders today need to be ruthlessly uncompromising. It was a response to a question I had from one of the leaders, and this principle resonated so strongly it ended up being a key theme for the next day and a half. It may sound harsh on the surface, however the more we explored it, the more this principle resonated with the leaders as a key pillar to underpin their leadership legacy.

So what is ruthlessly uncompromising all about? It is this:

✅  Being ruthless in creating a vision for the team, and uncompromising in its pursuit.

✅  Being ruthless in the standards of excellence we set for ourselves as a leader, and uncompromising in the pursuit of excellence. It is about being the model of excellence, always.

✅  Being ruthless in the expectations we set for our team and uncompromising in encouraging them in their pursuit of excellence.

✅  Being ruthless in the care we demonstrate for our team. Remember, the team are not our possessions, we are responsible for them, so we must constantly demonstrate care.

✅  Being ruthless in the service we deliver to our customers and stakeholders and again, uncompromising in the level of excellence by which we do this.

✅  Being ruthless in the standards we are prepared to accept and uncompromising in the communication of those standards, irrespective of who the stakeholder may be.

✅  Being ruthless in coaching and mentoring our team and uncompromising in making it a priority.

As we begin the brand new week, here is the question to ask - are you ruthlessly uncompromising in all you do as a Leader? Remember, this is rooted solidly in care.

Here is to a massive week 👊
