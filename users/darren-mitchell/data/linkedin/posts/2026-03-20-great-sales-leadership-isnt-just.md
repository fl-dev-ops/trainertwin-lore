Great sales leadership isn’t just about hitting the number.

It’s about mastering the 3 Ps: People, Partnership, and Performance.

People ➤ how you care for and develop those above, below, and alongside you.
Partnership ➤ how others experience working with you across the business, from marketing to customer success.
Performance ➤ your ability to deliver results and scale excellence through others.

That’s what separates good leaders from great ones: not just what they do, but how they do it.

On a recent conversation with David Fung on The Exceptional Sales Leader Podcast, this framework really stood out to me.

Which of these 3 Ps do you think leaders most often overlook: People, Partnership, or Performance?
