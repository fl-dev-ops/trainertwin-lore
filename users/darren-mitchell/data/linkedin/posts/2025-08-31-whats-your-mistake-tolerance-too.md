***What’s Your Mistake Tolerance?***

Too many CROs are running sales cultures that look great on paper but are quietly killing performance.

And the culprit is simple: zero tolerance for mistakes.

You know the type of culture I’m talking about:

❌ Miss a quarter? You’re branded.
❌ Lose a deal? You’re dissected.
❌ Try something new and it flops? You’re told to “stick to the playbook.”

On the surface, these leaders think they’re enforcing “excellence.” In reality, they’re building compliance machines. Reps stop experimenting. Managers stop innovating. Everyone optimises for not screwing up instead of winning big.

Here’s the uncomfortable truth: the highest-performing sales leaders tolerate more mistakes, not fewer.

Why? Because mistakes are the currency of growth. Exceptional leaders understand:

✅ Every failed approach is tuition paid for future mastery.
✅ Every misstep builds resilience, grit, and creativity.
✅ Every experiment, even when it bombs, pushes the team closer to breakthroughs.

These leaders don’t hide from mistakes — they weaponise them. And the payoff is clear:

💪 Faster learning curve.
💪 Reps who bounce back instead of burn out.
💪 Teams that consistently outperform competitors who are still obsessed with “perfection.”

The irony? The leaders who embrace mistakes end up with fewer of them. Why? Because their teams are constantly learning, adapting, and levelling up.

Meanwhile, the “zero-mistake” CROs keep churning through talent, clinging to the illusion of control while quietly eroding culture and results.

The bravest sales leaders aren’t the ones who eliminate mistakes — they’re the ones who multiply the lessons inside them.

If you looked honestly at your own culture: is it built for compliance… or for performance?
