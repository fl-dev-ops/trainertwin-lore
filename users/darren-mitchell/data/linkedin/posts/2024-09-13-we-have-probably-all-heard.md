We have probably all heard the phrase that 'we become who we hang around with', especially as children. This is especially true in business, and certainly in sales leadership.

I am constantly challenging and encouraging sales leaders to be very specific and very particular with who they choose to have in their inner sanctum, because we are, and will continue to be, products of our environment.

As a sales leader, who are you surrounding yourself with? Are they challenging you, are they uplifting you, are they enabling you, or are they stifling you?

Be very particular on your inner circle.
