In many of the leadership programs I facilitate, I often share a video from Simon Sinek where he talks about the fact that most people have the capacity to become a leader - it doesn't necessarily mean though that they should become one. 

Leadership is not easy, and it is not for the faint hearted, especially in sales. I'm always curious to understand from sales people looking to become a sales leader as to what drives them to move into sales leadership. Interestingly, many of them are not sure or cannot clearly articulate why, and for these people, I know that the road may be quite challenging, until they can get crystal clear as to why they want to be a sales leader.

Are you looking to become a sales leader? If so, consider the points in this article as you start to determine why? Would love your thoughts on what other principles you would add?
