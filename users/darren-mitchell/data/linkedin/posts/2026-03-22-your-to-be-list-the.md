***Your To Be List***

The best Sales Leaders don’t manage behaviour.
They model it.

Because your team isn’t listening as much as you think.

They’re watching.

➤ Every reaction.
➤ Every standard you walk past.
➤ Every excuse you tolerate.
➤ Every commitment you keep… or break.

And if you don’t believe that, revisit Mehrabian’s work, where a significant majority of communication is non-verbal.

Your presence communicates more than your words ever will.

Your team is constantly asking:
“What does success actually look like around here?”

And they’re not getting that answer from your slide decks.

They’re getting it from you.

This is where The Be List becomes non-negotiable.

Most Sales Leaders are fixated on what their team should do:
➤ Make more calls
➤ Close more deals
➤ Build more pipeline

But exceptional leaders flip the equation.

They get crystal clear on who they must be.
➤ Be disciplined when no one’s watching
➤ Be prepared when it’s inconvenient
➤ Be accountable when it’s uncomfortable
➤ Be consistent when it’s boring
➤ Be the standard, not the exception

Because here’s the part most leaders don’t want to confront:

Your team is not the problem.
Your team is the reflection.

If standards are slipping…
If accountability is low…
If energy is inconsistent…
If results are average…

Take a hard look in the mirror.

Because whether you like it or not, your team is mirroring what you model, consciously or unconsciously.

As John Maxwell says:
“People won’t necessarily do what you tell them to do, but they will do what they see.”

So if you’re asking for urgency…
but showing hesitation…

If you’re demanding discipline…
but showing inconsistency…

If you’re pushing for excellence…
but accepting “good enough”…

You’re not leading.
You’re confusing.

And confusion kills performance.

The truth?
Your Be List is already being executed by your team.

Not the one you wrote down.

The one you live out.

So before your next pipeline review…
Before your next forecast call…
Before your next “performance conversation”…

Ask yourself:

If everyone on my team behaved exactly like me, what would our results look like?

Because that’s already happening.

Are you leading a team or creating a mirror?
