***Is Your Team The Industry’s Best Kept Secret?***

One of my core beliefs around sales is that it is the ultimate form of service, and that if we are able to provide a valuable and tangible solution to a problem for a customer, then a sale is inevitable. Presupposing of course that the customer actually wants and needs to solve the problem.

In working with sales teams over the last 9 years, I have been intrigued by the number of sales people who perhaps do not share the same belief around sales, and rely almost entirely on inbound interest to generate their sales & revenue. In great times, when there is a built up demand for their solution, this can be quite lucrative. However, at some stage the well begins to dry up and sales people need to get proactive. Unfortunately not all of them do.

One of my early sales mentors always said that nobody will be interested unless & until you ask, which was a powerful principle to build a habit around being proactive. The last thing we want to be is the industry’s best kept secret. Strangely though, many sales people today are still reluctant to ask, for fear of rejection, or fear of being labeled a ‘pushy salesperson’. And yet the reality is that a high percentage of the sales that are made are not made based on the customer seeking you out, or walking into your world holding up a ‘can I buy your solution today’ sign. They are made through intentional and regular outbound connections, delivering value and leading a customer through a buying process. 

As we begin the brand new sales week, how can your team no longer be the industry’s best kept secret? 

✅ Get on the front foot and proactively reach out to existing customers with some valuable and tangible insights into their industry and the solution you are providing them. This may well create a new opportunity.

✅ Reach out to the ideal future customer of your business and connect with them, whether that be by phone, email or LinkedIn.

✅ When reaching out, don’t talk about your product, your solution or your strategy, ask genuine curiosity based questions. Remember, ask not tell.
Identify industry partners where there could be synergy to provide a unique solution to the marketplace. Partnerships can be powerful.

✅ Build a habit around building awareness in the market. Remember, approx. 3% of customers are in the buy now zone, so understand that nurture will be the name of the game, but you must stay in the game.

What other ideas can you add to stop being the industry’s best kept secret? Would love your input 🙏
