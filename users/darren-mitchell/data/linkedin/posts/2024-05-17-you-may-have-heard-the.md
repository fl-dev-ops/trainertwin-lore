You may have heard the terminology that 'culture eats strategy for breakfast', and this is especially true in sales. Having worked in sales & sales leadership for over 20 years, and now working with sales leaders and teams as a coach, mentor and facilitator, I have observed countless sales teams miss significant opportunities because they focused all of their attention on the sales strategy, and not nearly enough attention on the sales culture.

Whilst these teams may have possessed a robust strategy, as well as a formidable solution, almost always they struggled to penetrate their marketplace because they did not consider the culture they had created, or needed to create, in order to be successful.

As sales leaders, we must never underestimate the importance of sales culture. Yes, you must have a great product, service or solution, and yes, you must have a robust sales process and strategy, however, creating a great sales culture can be the multiplier that delivers exceptional results.
