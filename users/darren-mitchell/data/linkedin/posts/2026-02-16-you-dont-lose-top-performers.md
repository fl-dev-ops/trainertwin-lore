You don’t lose top performers to the market.
Sometimes, they lose to themselves.

In a recent conversation with Will Steel on The Exceptional Sales Leader Podcast (short snippet below), we explored something uncomfortable:

Self-sabotage.

You’ve seen it.

A rep is about to land their biggest deal…
Step into a bigger role…
Break through to the next level…

And then something slips.

It’s easy to call it poor execution.
But often, it’s deeper than that.

Limiting beliefs don’t announce themselves.
They sit quietly in the background, shaped by moments earlier in life where someone didn’t feel “good enough.”

And when success rises above that internal thermostat?
The subconscious pulls it back to equilibrium.

As sales leaders, this is the real work.

Coaching isn’t just about pipeline reviews and activity metrics.
It’s about helping your people raise their internal standard of what they believe they deserve.

Because performance ceilings are rarely strategic.
They’re psychological.

The best leaders don’t just grow skills.
They expand identity.

Have you ever seen self-sabotage show up in your team, or even in yourself?
