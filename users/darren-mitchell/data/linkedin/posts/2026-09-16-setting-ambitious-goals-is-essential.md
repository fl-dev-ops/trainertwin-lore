Setting ambitious goals is essential, but a vast gap between where you are and where you want to be can be demotivating.

The antidote to fear, setbacks, and self-doubt is purpose. True greatness isn't about comparison, but about overcoming struggle and adversity in a way that brings meaning to your life. It's a perpetual journey, not a destination.

Jason's purpose evolved into proving to himself what he's capable of and how far he could push his limits, rather than being constrained by them.
