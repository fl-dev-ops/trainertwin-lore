***The Abundance Mindset with Charm Moore***

In working with sales leaders and their teams, it is intriguing to observe the language that is used and the behaviours demonstrated, that often leads to results that the salesperson and/or sales leader does not want. 

And when trying to get sales people back on track, the leader or the ‘coach’ often looks directly at the behaviour and seeks to make behavioural changes, that will lead to a different result. 

This can very often only have short term results, because the root cause of the challenge hasn’t been dealt with. Highly successful people, exceptional performers, maintain an abundance mindset, and it is something that they have developed and continue to work on. 

In this episode of The Exceptional Sales Leader Podcast, I’m joined by Charmin Moore, a sales leader, registered nurse, leader in biotech and a specialist in digital wealth, and we explore the abundance mindset and how it can hep fast track performance and result.
