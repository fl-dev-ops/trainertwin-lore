Many organisations and sales leaders focus all of their attention on the number, of hitting the sales quota at all costs. In other words, it very much becomes all about the optics of short term achievement and not necessarily about the sustainability of the results.

Exceptional sales leaders understand the importance of driving financial outcomes, but they also equally understand the critical importance of building quality, authentic relationships with people, because it is through these relationships that long term, sustainable results are possible. 

This article is a great summary of a recent conversation I enjoyed with Markus Neukom on The Exceptional Sales Leader Podcast. To listen to the full episode, please click the links in the article.
