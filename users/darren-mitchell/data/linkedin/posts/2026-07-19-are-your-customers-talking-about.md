***Are Your Customers Talking About You When You’re Not In The Room?***

Are your customers talking about you when you're not in the room?

Not your product.

Not your pricing.

You.

Because that's the question every frontline sales leader and CRO should be asking.

If your customers only hear from you when there's a renewal, a proposal, or a problem to solve, you've probably become a supplier.

Not a partner.

The highest-performing sales teams don't just win deals.

They earn a place in their customers' thinking.

They're the first call when priorities change.

They're invited into conversations before budgets are allocated.

They're trusted to challenge assumptions, not just respond to requirements.

That's the difference between being relevant and being replaceable.

In a market where products are increasingly similar and competitors are only a click away, your greatest differentiator isn't your solution.

It's the value you create when you're not selling.

So ask yourself:

If your customer had an important strategic discussion tomorrow, would your name come up?

If the answer is no, it's time to rethink the conversations your team is having today.

The strongest customer relationships are built long before the next opportunity appears.

What's one thing your team is doing to ensure customers see them as trusted advisors rather than just another vendor?
