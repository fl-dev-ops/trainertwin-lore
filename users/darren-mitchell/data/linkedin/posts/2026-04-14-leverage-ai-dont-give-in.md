***Leverage AI, Don’t Give In To It!*** 

Your AI-generated proposal is costing you deals. 

Not because AI is bad.
But because you’re using it lazily.

I’ve sat across from enterprise sales executives who confidently present a proposal…

…and within minutes, it’s obvious:

They didn’t write it.
They don’t fully understand it.
And they definitely can’t defend it.

The narrative is generic.
The insights are shallow.
The “strategy” falls apart under basic questioning.

That’s not a proposal.
That’s outsourced thinking.

AI should accelerate your thinking, not replace it.

The best sales leaders I work with are using AI very differently:

➤ They use it to pressure-test their ideas, not generate them blindly
➤ They challenge every assumption AI produces
➤ They refine the narrative until it reflects real customer context
➤ And most importantly, they can own every word in the room

Because in enterprise sales, the proposal isn’t the product.

You are.

If you can’t confidently stand behind what’s on the page, your customer knows.

And trust disappears fast.

AI is a powerful tool.
But it doesn’t replace judgment, experience, or conviction.

It amplifies them.

Or exposes the lack of them.

Sales leaders, if your team is submitting AI-written proposals they can’t defend in person, you don’t have an AI problem… you have a capability problem.

Are you seeing this in your teams or in the market?
