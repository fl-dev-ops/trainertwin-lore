One of the key characteristics of exceptional business people, exceptional leaders, and certainly exceptional sales people, is their ability to make decisions, take definitive action, and manage the risks. Whilst others may look at their decisions as being 'risky', exceptional leaders run the numbers, look at the consequences, and make data driven decisions.

Sometimes these decisions pay off, and sometimes they don't. However, because they consider the data, and make calculated decisions, they are better placed to respond to what ever is presented to them. Hence, they do not see the vast majority of their decisions as risky.

This is a great approach that we can incorporate into our daily business and life. Here is a short snippet from a fabulous conversation I enjoyed recently with Dan Norcross on The Exceptional Sales Leader Podcast. What Dan has been able to achieve in such a short period of time is incredible.
