Throughout history, there are numerous examples of companies which became super successful and at the first sign of challenge, or a downturn in a market, downsized and laid off countless employees. The very employees by the way who built the success of the company in the first place.

These have often been the same companies which prophesised that 'their people are the cornerstone of the business'. 🤷‍♂️

Great companies, sustainably successful companies, don't simply talk a good game, they back it up with action. When the inevitable dips and downturns arrive, the companies back their people, they double down on their people investment. They don't do this to buy loyalty. They do this because it is the right thing to do. And through this action, they build genuine loyalty.

Fred Joyal is a great example of this approach. He co-founded 1-800-DENTIST and built a massive organisation through building his people, and looking after the people. It was a phenomenal conversation we enjoyed recently on The Exceptional Sales Leader Podcast, and here is a short snippet.
