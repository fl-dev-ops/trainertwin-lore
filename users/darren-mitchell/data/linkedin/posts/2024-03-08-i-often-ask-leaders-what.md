I often ask leaders 'What do you think your team craves more than anything?' 
The responses are varied, from being inspired, motivated, providing strategic direction, being encouraged. Overwhelmingly, one of the key desires team members crave, especially in sales teams, is the feeling of certainty. Certainty that they are valued, that they have the opportunity to contribute, that they will be rewarded. Certainty that they belong. 

Which means for leaders, we must be super conscious and very specific on the environment we create and the role modelling we display. Why? Because, people do what people see. Especially in times of uncertainty, leaders must step up, and lead and demonstrate the behaviours they want to see play out in their team, their business and their environment. This is a non negotiable.

Are you demonstrating the behaviours that you want to see in your team? Are you a Model of Excellence? Would love your comments & perspectives on what you are seeing in your environment right now. 🙏 Please comment below 👇
