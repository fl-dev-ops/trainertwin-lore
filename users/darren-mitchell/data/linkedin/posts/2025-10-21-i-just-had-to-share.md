I just had to share this simple yet profound snippet from a phenomenal conversation I had recently on The Exceptional Sales Leader Podcast with Cameron Bishop.

Cameron talks about the 'lunch lottery' where literally 5 names are drawn out of a hat and he then sits down for an organised yet casual lunch with them to understand what's working in their part of the business, and what improvements can be made.

It is a simple, yet powerful concept that can build strong, engaged cultures, and in the process, deconstruct much of the 'corporate hierarchy' that often stifles the growth of the business.
