***Sean Phelps on Leadership & Student Empowerment***

In this episode of The Exceptional Sales Leader Podcast, I welcome Sean Phelps, the President of CollegeWorks Painting, to discuss the unique leadership approaches that have propelled the company to become a bastion of entrepreneurial success among college students in the U.S. Sean shares insights from his journey from an intern to the president, emphasising critical factors like accountability, servant leadership, and effective training strategies, particularly in nurturing inexperienced college students into successful salespeople. 

Sean delves deep into the intricacies of recruiting high-caliber college students based on characteristics like resilience and work history rather than just previous job roles. He outlines the importance of maintaining transparency with new recruits about the challenges and realities of sales roles, contributing to improved retention and performance. 

Throughout the conversation, the significance of building a strong culture of accountability and continuous mentorship emerges as a central theme, paralleling teachings from prominent figures like Ted Lasso on leadership and tenacity.
