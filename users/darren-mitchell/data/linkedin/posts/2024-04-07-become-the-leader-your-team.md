***Become The Leader Your Team Needs You To Be***

If there is one thing that has come out of the last 4 years, it is that the world feels a lot smaller.

The sheer volume of news and information being front of mind globally has been almost unlike any other time in history. And with this has come a much sharper focus on leadership.

To say that there is a huge void in quality leadership around the world right now would be a massive understatement. For young people with ambitions of leading others in the future, unfortunately they do not have great examples of leaders to emulate. The role models do not model great leadership behaviours. Even more concerning, there is a fear that these young people observe the current leaders and believe that is leadership. It’s not.

We need to change the narrative.

We need leaders to step up and provide a different and better example of what real leadership is.

And it starts with me and it starts with you.

It’s time to become the Leader that your team needs and deserves.

✅ It is time to lead with immovable integrity.
✅ It is time to double down on building trust with your team and earning the right to lead them - don’t rely on the leadership title.
✅ It is time to speak less, and do more. A Leader knows the way, shows the way, and goes the way.
✅ It is time to invest more time being more visible to your team, and less time being visible to your superiors.
✅ It is time to have the conversations you know you need to have, rather than avoiding them.
✅ It is time to empower, encourage & challenge your team, rather than direct your team.
✅ It is time to step back and create space for others to step up. It is time to create other leaders.
✅ It is time to do the right thing, regardless of how you feel, instead of trying to do things right.

As Dwight Eisenhower said, ‘The supreme quality for leadership is unquestionably integrity. Without it, no real success is possible’

It is time to change the course of history. Are you up for it?

Here’s to a massive week 👊
