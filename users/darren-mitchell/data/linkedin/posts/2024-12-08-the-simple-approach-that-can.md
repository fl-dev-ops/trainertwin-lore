***The Simple Approach That Can Make A Huge Difference***

Leaders the world over continue to search for the difference that can make all the difference to their team’s performance. Developing a new strategy, implementing innovative tactics, driving disruption. Anything that they believe will move the dial and drive results.

Their teams certainly possess talent, and they definitely develop strategies to unleash that talent. However, when analysing these high performing teams and high performing individuals across any industry, and whether in sport or business, there is a simple approach that is employed that makes all the difference. The power of 1 more. Such a simple yet powerful strategy.

➤ It is the professional golfer that practices for 1 more hour after their competitors have stopped.
➤ The professional tennis player who plays 1 more set in practice.
➤ The boxer who trains for 1 more round.
➤ The triathlete who pushes themselves in training for 1 more kilometre.
➤ The professional AFL player who undertakes 1 more training session each week.
➤ The soccer team who conducts 1 more hour of practice per week.

So how does this relate to sales? 

✅ It is the sales person who makes 1 more outbound prospecting call.
✅ The sales person who connects with 1 more ideal future customer on LinkedIn.
✅ The sales leader who commits to 1 more hour of reflection on what is working and not working.
✅ The sales leader who provides 1 more piece of constructive feedback to their team.
✅ The sales leader who commits to 1 more hour of sales coaching for their sales people.
✅ The sales person who creates 1 more sales proposal.

The power of 1 more. Consistency compounds and it is the teams and individuals within teams who are prepared to do 1 more, which are the ones who will reap the rewards.

As we begin the new week, here is the challenge. Whatever you happen to have in your schedule for this week, do 1 more, go 1 step further, and just watch what happens. It is such a simple approach that can deliver significant outcomes.

Here’s to a massive week. 👊
