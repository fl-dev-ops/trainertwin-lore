***Would You Rather Be A Good Sales Leader… Or An Exceptional Sales Leader?***

At first glance, the answer feels obvious. Who wouldn’t want to be exceptional?

But in practice, many sales leaders unintentionally stop at “good.”

A good sales leader…
➤  Hits targets.
➤  Keeps the pipeline full.
➤  Holds people accountable.

Those things matter. But they only scratch the surface.

An exceptional sales leader goes further.

✅  They create belief where doubt exists.
✅  They elevate their people, not just their numbers.
✅  They build teams that thrive long after they’ve moved on.

I’ve worked with many sales directors who didn’t even realise they were operating in “good” mode. Chasing quarterly wins, driving performance through pressure, constantly firefighting.

Their teams performed, yes… but the energy was transactional.

When we made the shift to “exceptional” leadership, everything changed.

👉  Engagement rose.
👉  Confidence grew.
👉  Teams stopped needing constant pressure because they were driven from within.

That’s the real difference: good leaders get results. Exceptional leaders build legacies.

And here’s the truth: exceptional leadership is not about working harder, shouting louder, or pushing more.

It’s about changing the way you lead people so that performance becomes sustainable.

Because in sales, deals close… but culture endures.

So I’ll ask again: Would you rather be a good sales leader… or an exceptional one?
