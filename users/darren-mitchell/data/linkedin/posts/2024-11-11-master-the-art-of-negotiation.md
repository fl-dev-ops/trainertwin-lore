Master the art of negotiation by embracing silence. Let others lean into the discomfort and reveal valuable insights without weakening your position. 🤫💼

This is something that I had to learn the hard way throughout my sales career. the temptation is to want to speak and fill the uncomfortable void of silence.

Who is with me? To become a great negotiator, we must learn to embrace the silence, despite how uncomfortable it may be.
