***Why An Exceptional Sales Leader?***

Last week I posted about the importance of being on mission as a sales leader. Sales leaders who become exceptional create a legacy that often has a generational impact. And they are crystal clear on one thing - their ‘Why’.

Simon Sinek many years ago created the Golden Circle principle, based on Why, How & What. Based on his research of highly successful businesses and exceptional leaders, he found that the vast majority are super clear on ‘What’ they do. We can see this on LinkedIn as well with people stating ‘what’ they do in their profile headline. 

When asking sales leaders what they do, I overwhelmingly find they are able to explain their role in absolute detail.

Companies often are super clear also on their ‘What’ and explain this in their advertising. Sinek also found that companies and leaders can explain the ‘How’ of what they do, which delivers a certain level of success.

However, the difference between the good companies and exceptional companies was that the exceptional companies could identify and clearly articulate their ‘Why’. This is also true of exceptional sales leaders. They are dialled into their ‘Why’, and their every thought and action is directly linked to it. This is what enables them to be on mission.

When working with sales leaders, I always ask them this question -‘Why did you become a sales leader?’ The answers vary greatly and what is intriguing is that many actually start to describe the ‘What’ and ‘How’. Some also are quite stumped by the question because they have been thrust into the sales leadership role based on their performance and have never really given it much thought.

Here is one thing I have learned - exceptional sales leaders invest time and energy into identifying and then articulating their ‘Why’. It may take time to figure it out, but when it gets locked in, the ‘How’ & the ‘What’ become significantly easier.

As a sales leader, are you crystal clear on your ‘Why’? Why an exceptional sales leader?
