True leadership is often demonstrated not by demanding perfection, but by showing unwavering belief.

When the creators of a show insisted on a 3-year contract, despite the uncertainty of early ratings, it was a profound act of trust. The request to remove "number one" from the contract highlighted a focus on the job itself, not just the outcome. This approach fosters an environment where individuals feel valued for their potential and empowered to perform.

Investing in team culture and elevating your people isn't just a nice-to-have; it's a strategic imperative that drives significantly better results.
