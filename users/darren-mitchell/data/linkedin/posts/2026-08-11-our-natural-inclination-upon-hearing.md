Our natural inclination upon hearing something is to quickly jump to judgment – either agreeing or disagreeing.

However, true curiosity involves creating a deliberate space between receiving information and forming an opinion. Instead of immediate judgment, the key is to ask reflective questions like, "I wonder if that's true," or "What makes you think that?"

Cultivating this habit of curiosity and healthy skepticism is crucial for genuinely seeking the truth and fostering deeper understanding. A key skillset for leaders seeking to be exceptional.
