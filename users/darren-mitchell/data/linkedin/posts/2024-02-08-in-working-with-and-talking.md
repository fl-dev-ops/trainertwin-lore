In working with and talking to sales people, it is frightening the number of them who report to a manager who leads by absence. A manager 'busy' running from strategy meeting to strategy meeting, or locked behind closed doors forensically analysing this month's sales numbers and trying to forecast next month's achievement.

Every now and then they may pop their head out of their office and normally when they do it is to ask for information to feed into their forecast or strategy. Unfortunately these type of leaders operate under an invisibility cloak and some of them even have the belief that the cloak makes them powerful. 🤷‍♂️

The sad truth is often they know no better, for they have operated in an environment as a salesperson themselves where their manager behaved exactly the same way. Brings home the saying 'people do what people see'.

The reality is that the invisibility cloak does not make you powerful. It makes you invisible (and dare I say, irrelevant? 🙃)

If we are to be exceptional sales leaders, we must understand that our people need and want leadership, they want encouragement, feedback, coaching and mentoring. They want a leader who they can trust, who can be an authentic human being, and who will back them. And it starts with being visible.

I'm curious - have you been guilty of wearing the invisibility cloak? Have you worked in an environment where there seemed to be an endless supply of invisibility cloaks? Would greatly appreciate your comments. 👇🙏
