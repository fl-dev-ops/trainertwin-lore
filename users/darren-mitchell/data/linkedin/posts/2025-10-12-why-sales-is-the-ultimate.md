***Why Sales Is the Ultimate Form of Service***

I’ll say it; sales isn’t about selling.

If your goal is still to “close deals,” you’ve already lost the plot.

After years in sales, I’ve learned this the hard way:

Sales isn’t a transaction game.

It’s a service discipline.

And I don’t mean the “customer is always right” version of service.

I mean the kind that demands courage, clarity, and conviction.

Because real service isn’t about pleasing people, it’s about helping them see the truth.

Sometimes that means saying,
“No, that won’t solve your problem.”

Or,

“You’re optimising the wrong thing.”

The best salespeople I’ve ever met weren’t the smoothest talkers.

They were the best listeners.

They served through confrontation, by challenging assumptions, exposing blind spots, and holding clients accountable to their own goals.

They didn’t sell to the client.

They sold for the client.

That’s the difference between a rep and a trusted advisor.

Between chasing revenue and creating impact.

And here’s the paradox no one talks about:

When you stop trying to sell, you sell more.

Because people feel it when you’re in it for them, not the commission.

Every quota I’ve hit and every team I’ve led to exceed theirs, came from that mindset shift.

✅ Serve first.

✅ Sell second.

✅ Always.

Sales, at its highest level, isn’t about closing.

It’s about contributing. To your client’s success, your company’s growth, and your own legacy.

Curious where you stand. Is sales still about closing, or has it evolved into serving with conviction?
