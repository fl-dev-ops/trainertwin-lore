***Making Sales More Predictable***

As a sales leader, how predictable are the sales opportunities flowing through your team?

❓ Are they highly qualified?
❓ Are you laser focused on specific customers and specific opportunities, or throwing mud against the wall hoping it sticks?
❓ Are you converting a high percentage of the opportunities?
❓ Are the closed opportunities highly profitable?

In working with sales leaders, what I find fascinating is how unplanned and unprepared the vast majority are when it comes to sales opportunities. 

Instead of focusing on a specific problem and developing a strategic solution to that problem, they are heavily influenced by their product or service, and seek to retrofit the product into every opportunity.

And when I ask the sales leader to share with me a strategic account plan or the opportunity plan, very often I hear crickets. Why? Because there is no plan. And the sales leader wonders why the conversion rate is so low?🤷‍♂️

There is a better way. Sales can be predictable and it starts with planning.

✅ Creating an Account Plan, to develop a deeper understanding of the customer, their challenges, their opportunities, and their customers. This enables the team to be very specific with the opportunities they pursue.

✅ Creating an Opportunity Plan. Very rarely do sales teams create one, especially when it comes to strategic business, and when an RFP is released, they jump straight into response mode. A well thought out and intentional Opportunity Plan will enable the sales team to identify whether there is an opportunity, whether there is a compelling reason for the customer to take action, whether there is budget for the solution, and ultimately whether the opportunity is worth pursuing.

As we begin the new week, it is a perfect time to inject more predictability into your sales efforts. 

And the predictability comes directly from planning. 

If you can double down on your planning focus, specifically around the opportunity plan, I guarantee that your confidence in sales forecasting will significantly improve, and you may actually discover that your conversion rate will increase as well. Why? Because you & the sales team will be laser focused on the right opportunities.

Make sales more predictable. Here’s to a massive week 👊
