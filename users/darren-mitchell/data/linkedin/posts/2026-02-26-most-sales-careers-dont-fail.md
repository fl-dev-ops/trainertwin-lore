Most sales careers don’t fail.

They’re abandoned.

Here’s the truth most people won’t say out loud:

Most businesses don’t close because they run out of money.
They close because the owner gives up.

➤ Burnout.
➤ Shiny object syndrome.
➤ A “new path.”

And listen, changing direction is okay.
You’re not a tree. You can move.

But in sales?

Too many professionals step out of the batter’s box right before they make contact.

➤ Prospecting feels slow.
➤ Deals stall.
➤ Quotas feel heavy.

So they pivot… right when momentum was about to compound.

It reminds me of Three Feet from Gold, the idea that most people quit just three feet before the breakthrough.

If you just stay in the game.
✅ Keep swinging.
✅ Keep refining.
✅ Keep following up.

Eventually, you get on base.

Resilience isn’t sexy. But it wins.

Before you pivot… ask yourself:

Are you truly out of options, or just three feet from gold?

👇 What’s one moment in your sales career where sticking it out paid off?
