Most salespeople think they sell products.

They don’t.
They sell a buying decision that feels good.

I recently spoke with Adam C. on The Exceptional Sales Leader Podcast about something most sales teams never define:

What does it actually mean to sell?

Ask ten salespeople and you’ll hear:
➤ Selling features
➤ Selling benefits
➤ Selling outcomes

But none of these capture the essence of great selling.

At its core, selling is helping someone make a buying decision they feel confident about.

The best sales professionals don’t push products.

They guide decisions.

And when leaders teach this philosophy, everything changes, from discovery to closing.

Sales improves when we define what selling truly is.

But do you have an overarching sales philosophy for your team?

Or are you just teaching techniques?

👇 I’d love to hear your view: How do you define selling?
