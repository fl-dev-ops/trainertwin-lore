***Sales Success Isn’t The Opposite of Failure - It’s Built On It***

Success isn’t the opposite of failure.

It’s built on top of it.

And if you’re leading a sales team that treats failure as something to avoid rather than analyse, you’re already capping your organisation’s potential.

A few years ago, my team lost a deal that we’d spent six months chasing.

It was a marquee logo. The kind that would’ve lit up every quarterly business review and made everyone look good on the board slide.

We thought we had it locked.

But we missed something subtle; a shift in their internal priorities that our Account Team caught too late.

We were outplayed by a competitor who understood their changing needs better.

It stung.

We dissected every stage of that deal, from discovery to proposal to negotiation.

Not to find a scapegoat, but to understand how we missed the signal.

That post-mortem became the foundation for a new qualification framework we rolled out across the group.

Three months later, we won a deal three times the size, with the same kind of buyer, same industry, same playbook… but this time, we read the signals early, repositioned mid-cycle, and closed it in record time.

Failure didn’t slow us down.

It taught us how to win faster.

That’s when I stopped treating losses as setbacks, and started seeing them as part of the architecture of long-term success.

Exceptional sales leaders know this:
Every failed deal is unpaid tuition.

The question is, will you collect the lesson you’ve already paid for?

Success isn’t what happens after failure. It’s what happens because of it.

What’s one deal your team lost that ended up reshaping how you sell, and paid off tenfold later?

Here’s to a massive week 👊
