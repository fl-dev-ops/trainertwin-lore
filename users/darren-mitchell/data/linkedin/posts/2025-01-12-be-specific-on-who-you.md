***Be Specific On Who You Surround Yourself With***

Over 100 years ago, James Allen wrote the incredible book ‘As A Man Thinketh’ and in it he described the principle that we become who we think about. It was perhaps one of the first personal development books that delved into the power of mindset, attitude and emotional intelligence.

The principle is still true today as it was over 100 years ago, and as sales leaders we must be constantly vigilant over the thinking patterns we choose to employ and the actions we take from those patterns. And closely aligned to these thinking patterns are the choices we make around who we surround ourselves with.

It has been said that we are the average of the 5 closest people to us, in terms of attitudes, values, beliefs, performance levels, income and even bank balances. Now there are always exceptions to the rule, however, I have come to believe that there is an element of truth to this, which means, as Leaders, we must choose wisely. We must also choose which group(s) we would like to be associated with.

Is the current group you are part of helping you or hindering you? Are they building you up and challenging you, or are you the smartest & most successful person in the group? If that is the case, time to find a new group.😉

In relation to your team, are they challenging you, forcing you to stay ahead of the curve, being proactive in their pursuit of excellence, and creating new levels of performance that even surprises you? I certainly hope so 🤞

As leaders, we must be conscious of who currently surrounds us and become more specific on the qualities that we want in the people who surround us. We don’t want people who agree with everything we say. We want people who will challenge us and force us to jump out of our comfort zone and fast track our leadership development.

So if you haven’t stopped to consider the people who currently surround you, perhaps now is a great time to do so. Are they helping or are they hindering? And in the process, begin to identify those key principles and qualities you want to see and identify people with those qualities to step into your world. It could just be the difference that makes all the difference.
