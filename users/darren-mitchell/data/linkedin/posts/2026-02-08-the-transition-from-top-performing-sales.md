The transition from top-performing sales rep to sales leader?
It’s one of the hardest jumps in sales and one of the most underestimated.

I was reminded of this in a recent conversation with Wesleyne Whittaker on The Exceptional Sales Leader Podcast. 

Like many high performers, Wesleyne raised her hand for leadership.
She was great at sales, got the opportunity, stepped in confidently… and tried to create “50,000 little Wesleynes.”

It didn’t work.

Because the skills that make you an exceptional individual contributor are not the skills that make you an exceptional sales leader.

Closing deals for your team.
Telling them exactly what to do.
Showing them your way.

That feels helpful but it often holds people back.

Sales leadership demands a whole new skillset:

coaching instead of closing,
mentoring instead of managing,
developing thinking rather than directing activity.

The moment you step into leadership, the job is no longer to be the hero.
It’s to build heroes.

And that shift?

That’s where real sales leaders are made.
