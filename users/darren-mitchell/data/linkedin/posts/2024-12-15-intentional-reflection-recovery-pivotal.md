***Intentional Reflection & Recovery Pivotal To Exceptional Performance***

As we approach the final days of 2024, it is the perfect time to dedicate intentional & quality time to reflection and recovery.

When we study elite performers, whether they be individuals or teams, we will discover that they dedicate specific time to reflection & recovery.

Analysing their performance across 2024 - looking at what they did well so they can identify positive habits & build higher levels of performance around those habits.

They also identify gaps & areas that are not yet elite, but through practice & application, will give themselves an opportunity to get to the elite level.

Recovery is also a critical priority - giving their body & mind time to rest, recover, recuperate & getting ready to re-load.

What’s interesting is that many leaders set New Years resolutions & by January 15th, approx. 95% of them are already discarded or forgotten.

To set a platform for an exceptional 2025, it's important to sit down & analyse 2024 from multiple different dimensions. Whilst we are focused on leadership & elite performance, it’s critical that we analyse various different areas of our life, including:
➤  Leadership
➤  Sales performance
➤  Relationships
➤  Health & Fitness
➤  Mental Health
➤  Contributions 
➤  Learning & Education 

Here’s what Exceptional Leaders do:

✅ They reflect & highlight the positives. What worked well in 2024? What were the achievements? It’s critical that we invest time on this as we are naturally tilted towards the negative. 
✅ They rate their level of satisfaction out of 10 (10=highly satisfied) in each of the above areas. This will provide key insights into areas of focus for 2025.
✅ They reflect on what didn’t work as well as they expected in 2024. Where were the gaps? The key point here is to not get critical - it's simply to identify gaps.
✅ Based on outputs of the points above, they then identify key areas of focus for 2025.

To get ready for 2025, & to provide yourself with a strong platform to take your leadership to the exceptional level, there will be 3 key categories to consider:

1️⃣ Start - what will you start to do in 2025 to take your leadership to the exceptional level? Identify 3 core areas.

2️⃣ Stop - what will you stop doing in 2025? You may need to delegate or stop altogether. Have the discipline to not do them.

3️⃣ Maintain - what will be the key things you need to continue to do? They must be tangible & specific activities that you know will lead you to achieving your leadership outcomes.

Exceptional leaders do not become exceptional by accident. It is intentional. So over the Christmas period, give yourself the gift of specific reflection so that you have the best platform from which to launch into 2025.

May you & your family have a very safe & happy holiday period, & may 2025 be your most exceptional year yet!
