The journey to becoming a thought leader often involves taking a leap of faith, a risk with no guarantee of serious consideration.

Initially, there was a struggle to learn and sell simultaneously, facing organisations that served scientists and doctors. A pivotal moment came with the idea to create and mail opinionated, credible white papers, modeled after a trusted source. These pieces, often about emerging trends like Google's pay-for-performance in 2001, dramatically shifted the quality of conversations.

Instead of cold calls about services, the approach became sharing valuable insights. This transition moved from being overlooked to being the person discussing critical, top-of-mind issues that kept potential clients awake at night. Consistently producing actionable, useful thinking for a growing audience proved to be a game-changing decision.
