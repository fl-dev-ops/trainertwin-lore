***Leveraging AI To Revolutionise How We Sell with Usman Sheikh***

If there is one thing that is certain in 2024 and beyond, it is that AI will continue to play a big role in our daily lives. This is especially true in sales. 

The expectations that customers have continue to increase, and the amount of information sales leaders and their teams need to consider to provide valuable and customised solutions to their customers, can often feel overwhelming. 

This is where AI is helping to revolutionise how we sell. 

In this episode of The Exceptional Sales Leader Podcast, I enjoyed a phenomenal conversation with Usman Sheikh, Founder & CEO of xiQ, Inc. 

Usman & his team have created a personality driven sales & marketing platform that enables sales leaders and their teams to leverage AI to become more relevant to their customers, and to provide more specific & customised solutions. 

It is a fascinating conversation on how we can leverage AI to drive even more exceptional results.
