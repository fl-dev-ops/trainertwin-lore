***Mastering Brand Consistency with Lisa Shorr***

As a sales leader, business professional, or sales professional, how conscious are you about your brand? 

Are you representing yourself and your business in the best possible way, that creates an attractive force that draws potential customers to you? 

We all have a brand, but not all of us are actively developing strategies to leverage that brand to build deeper relationships. 

In this episode of The Exceptional Sales Leader Podcast, I’m joined by Lisa Shorr, Image Consultant and Personal & Corporate Brand Expert, with Lisa providing actionable insights into building and sustaining a credible corporate image. 

This conversation not only highlights the nuances of effective branding but also provides valuable lessons on leadership and business development. Lisa emphasises the importance of emotional intelligence, illustrating how cultivating a strong company culture and understanding personal core values can lead to improved client satisfaction and business growth.
