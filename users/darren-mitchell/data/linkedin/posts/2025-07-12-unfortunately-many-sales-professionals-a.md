Unfortunately many sales professionals are promoted to a sales leadership position because of their sales success, and not as to whether they have the desire or the aptitude to make the transition.

Hence, many fail, and the company suffers a reduction in sales pipeline, sales conversion, and a loss of momentum. It doesn’t have to be that way. There is a solution - dedicate more focus to the genuine development of sales leaders and set them up for success.
