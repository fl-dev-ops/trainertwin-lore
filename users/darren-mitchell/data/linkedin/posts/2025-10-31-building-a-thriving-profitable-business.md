***Building a Thriving & Profitable Business from Scratch with Mark D. Gordon***

In this episode of The Exceptional Sales Leader Podcast, I chat with Mark Gordon, a seasoned sales leader and CEO of INTEGRATED GO-TO-MARKET SOLUTIONS. Hailing from Charleston, South Carolina, Mark shares his journey from being a struggling student to a thriving entrepreneur in sales and mortgage businesses. 

His unique approach to leadership and company culture, emphasising radical candour and transparency, plays a vital role in the success of his ventures. Mark discusses the importance of having a well-structured go-to-market strategy, especially in commodity-based industries, where product differentiation is challenging. 

Listeners will learn about key sales and marketing strategies that Mark uses to establish competitive advantages. He discusses how to create a unique messaging approach to attract top talent and sustain a high-performance culture. Mark also emphasises the importance of transparency, accountability, and continuous improvement in building a self-sustaining organisation. 

This episode provides invaluable insights for VPs of sales, entrepreneurs, and business leaders looking to refine their market approach and drive sustainable growth.
