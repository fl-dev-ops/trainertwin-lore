When it comes to selling, a large percentage of salespeople focus on the features of their product or service, a percentage of salespeople focus on the benefit to the customer, and a very minute percentage of salespeople focus on the pain that a current problem is creating for the customer.

Which salesperson do you think will be more successful over the long term? What's fascinating, especially in big ticket sales, is that the sales person who is able to understand the pain, drill into the pain, and then future pace the pain for the customer, is likely to not only drive better results, the 'stickiness' of the customer is likely to be higher as well. Why? Because customers will do more to avoid pain than to move towards pleasure.
