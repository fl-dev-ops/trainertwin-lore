Too many sales leaders are still asking the wrong question.

“Why can’t my team sell like I sell?” was never the real problem.

On my recent conversation with Carlos Garrido on The Exceptional Sales Leader Podcast, he nailed something every VP and Head of Sales needs to hear:

Founders sell differently.
They break rules.
They live the problem.
They don’t need perfect messaging, mature marketing, or a pipeline full of qualified leads.

They built the thing, so they can sell the thing.

But here’s the trap:
When leaders expect their reps to mirror that same energy, grit, or instinct, they miss the bigger opportunity.

The real question isn’t:
❌ “How do I get my reps to sell like a founder?”

It’s:

✅ “How do I unlock the potential in the team I already have?”

Your job isn’t replication.
Your job is activation.

Exceptional sales leadership isn’t about cloning greatness, it’s about releasing it.

Curious, what’s one thing you do to unlock your team’s true potential?
