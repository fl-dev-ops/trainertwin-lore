***Mistakes Are Rarely Fatal & Failure Rarely Final***

Apologies up front for the dramatic title. 

It’s amazing when talking with sales people, many of them share with me that they are so conditioned by their Sales Manager &/or business to not make mistakes, that they rarely take a chance.

Often the feedback when they get something wrong is so negative and discriminatory, it discourages them from even trying. Not a great environment to operate in, wouldn’t you agree? And unfortunately a byproduct of this environment is lack of progress, and lack of results. Which in many people’s eyes is ‘failure’.

I deeply empathise with people in this environment and actively encourage them to find the courage to seek an alternative environment where there is a completely different philosophy.

A philosophy where the only way to develop & grow is to step outside of the comfort zone, to explore the learning zone, and know that you will be supported and encouraged when a mistake is made, and not criticised and condemned. A philosophy which promotes winning or learning, not winning or losing. And a philosophy where failure is not final if you stay in the game.

It reminds me of a quote Melbourne Storm Coach, Craig Bellamy, shared on a podcast - ‘Know that in the end, everything will be okay. And if it is not okay, it is not the end’ 💪

As we begin this new week, are you encouraging your team to push beyond their comfort zone, to take risks, to take chances, and to stay in the game? As I share with clients all the time, if the action you take and decisions you make doesn’t cost the business millions of dollars or put people’s health & wellbeing in jeopardy, what is the worst that can happen? A lesson.

Encourage mistakes, learn from them, provide feedback, and understand that mistakes are not fatal and failure is not final. Stay the course.

Here’s to a massive week 👊
