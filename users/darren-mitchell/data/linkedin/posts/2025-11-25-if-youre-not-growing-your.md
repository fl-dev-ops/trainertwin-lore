***If You’re Not Growing, Your Sales Team Isn’t Either***

Your team will never outperform the version of you that shows up every day.

If you’re not actively developing yourself, you’re actively limiting them. Full stop.

Let’s get real for a moment.

Frontline managers, sales leaders, sales directors, the pressure on you is heavy.
Targets. Forecasts. Pipelines. Coaching conversations. Firefighting.
It’s easy to convince yourself that your development can “wait.”

I know that mindset well.
Because I lived it.

For years, I avoided investing in myself as a leader.
Not because I wasn’t ambitious.
Not because I didn’t want to get better.
But because I couldn’t see the immediate value.

I told myself things like:
“Once the team is performing, I’ll invest.”
“Once the quarter settles, I’ll look at training.”
“Once I’m in a more senior role, I’ll get a mentor.”

All lies I believed because it felt safer than admitting the truth:
👉 I didn’t know what I didn’t know.
👉 And I was afraid to shine a light on my gaps.

But here’s the part that stings…
My team paid for my hesitation.
Their growth slowed because I wasn’t growing.
Their development stalled because I hadn’t invested in mine.
They hit ceilings I unconsciously built.

The day I finally invested in real coaching, development, and mentorship, everything changed:

✅ My decision-making improved
✅ My coaching got sharper
✅ My confidence deepened
✅ My performance jumped
✅ My team’s results followed

It wasn’t magic.
It was simply removing the bottleneck: me.

Sales leaders love to say they’re “servant leaders.”
But serving your team starts with leading yourself.
And leading yourself requires investment in time, money, energy & humility.

If you’re waiting for permission, a budget, or the “right moment”…
You’ll be waiting forever.

Here’s the direct truth no one wants to say out loud:
👉 If you don’t invest in yourself, you forfeit the right to be frustrated with your team’s development.
They can only rise as high as their leader.

Your growth is not a luxury. It’s a responsibility.

What’s the next development step you’ve been avoiding… and what would change if you finally committed to it?
