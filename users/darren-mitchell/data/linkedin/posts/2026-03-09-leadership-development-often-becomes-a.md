Leadership development often becomes a “nice to have” in smaller companies.

Not because leaders don’t value it.
Because survival comes first.

In a recent conversation with Dalmo Cirne on The Exceptional Sales Leader Podcast, we discussed a reality many organisations face:

Early-stage and smaller companies are focused on staying alive.

Budgets are tight.
Time is limited.
Energy is spent building the product and delivering for customers.

And when that happens, leadership development is often pushed aside.

The challenge?

Great individual contributors get promoted into leadership roles… without the training or support to succeed.

Over time, that gap impacts culture, execution, and sales performance.

The best sales organisations don’t wait until they’re “big enough” to develop leaders.

They start early.
They build it into how the team operates.

Because strong sales teams rarely outperform the leadership guiding them.

🎧 If you’re building or leading a sales team, the conversation with Dalmo Cirne on The Exceptional Sales Leader Podcast dives deeper into this challenge. 

How are you developing future leaders within your sales team?
