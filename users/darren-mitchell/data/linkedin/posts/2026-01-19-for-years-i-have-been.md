For years, I have been sharing with sales leaders and their teams that sales is and should always be the ultimate form of service. It is not about trying to retrofit a feature of your product or service into a perceived need.

It is about creating a solution to a problem - a problem that is either known, or will be revealed through the evolution of a conversation. It is therefore about creating outcomes. Outcomes that benefit the customer, not outcomes that benefit me as the seller. Because we also need to recognise that the sale does not begin until after the sale is made.
