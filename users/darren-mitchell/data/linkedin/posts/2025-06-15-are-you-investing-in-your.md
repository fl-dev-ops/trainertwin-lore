***Are You Investing In Your Sales Team?***

With 2 & a bit working weeks left until the end of the Financial Year (or end of Quarter if your company has a different Financial Year), many companies are scrounging to get the deals closed as quickly as possible - to hit the number by the end of the month.

Often, as a result of this, the focus is ‘all hands to the pump’, meaning that other activities and investments take a back seat, or are discarded altogether. If the activity or investment is not directly related to closing deals by June 30th, it is not a priority. And unfortunately this is often the directive from Senior Management which sets the tone for the entire team, resulting in short term, immediate attention.

With organisations who function like this (which by the way seems to be the majority 🤷‍♂️), what do you think happens come July 1st? That’s right - the conversation turns to what deals are going to be closed in the first month of the quarter in order to get a ‘fast start’ and hence the pattern repeats. What these organisations neglect to focus on and prioritise, is the continual investment in their sales team.

What do exceptional organisations do at this time of year which is different? 

✅  They recognise that if deals are not in the ‘commit’ phase now, it is highly unlikely they will close by end June.

✅  They continue to prioritise forward planning and strategy development, always looking 90 days plus into the future.

✅  A key element of this planning is sales team development, as well as individual development. This may take the form of strategic training masterclasses or group/individual coaching & mentoring.

✅  They recognise that when their competitors are focused entirely on pushing through deals to hit the June 30 deadline, they can create a clear & distinct competitive advantage by prioritising sales team development.

✅  They often realise a significant jump on their competitors in terms of results in the first quarter of the next financial year. 

As we begin the brand new sales week, where do you sit in relation to your sales team focus? 

Are you purely under the pump to do everything in your power to deliver the numbers by June 30, and that is the only thing that matters?

Or are you continuing to focus your attention on investing in your sales team’s development, knowing that this investment will not only assist your team in the short term, but set them up for sustained success in the mid to long term? (Hint - this is where we should be focused!! 😉).
