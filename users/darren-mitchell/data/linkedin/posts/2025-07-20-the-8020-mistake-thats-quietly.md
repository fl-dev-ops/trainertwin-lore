***The 80/20 Mistake That’s Quietly Killing Sales Performance***

Only 20% of your sales team is driving 80% of your results.

And yet—most Heads of Sales spend their time coaching the other 80%.

A client I worked with had 42 sales reps.

7 of them carried 81% of total revenue.

But guess where their leadership team spent most of their time?

You guessed it: ride-alongs, 1:1s, and “improvement plans” with the bottom 80%.

This is the leadership tax no one talks about:
We get addicted to “fixing the bottom,” and forget to amplify the top.

The logic sounds noble:
“If we can just lift the middle 60%, we’ll unlock massive growth.”

But here’s the reality:
➤ The bottom 20% usually need to be managed out, not coached up.
➤ The middle? They follow the cultural tone set by your top 20%.
➤ And the top 20%? They're too often left to “just get on with it.”

But here’s the hard truth:
➤You don’t win big by raising the floor.
➤You win by raising the ceiling.

🔁 Flip the model:
Spend 80% of your time on the 20% of your team who are delivering the goods.
👉 Clone what they’re doing right
👉 Remove every barrier in their way
👉 Build your systems around their behaviors
👉 Give them stretch roles, influence, and strategic voice

This isn’t favouritism.

It’s ROI-focused leadership.

Because when your best reps grow, they:

✅ Close more
✅ Raise the standard
✅ Become internal role models
✅ Shape onboarding, enablement, and team culture

Your A-players aren’t just top billers—they’re the blueprint.

Is your leadership time fuelling your top performers—or firefighting the bottom?
