Over 100 years ago, James Allen wrote a little book titled 'As a Man Thinketh' and in it he talked about the power of our thoughts and that 'as we thinketh, so we become'. 

Fast forward to today and it is incredible how many people, especially people in business & sales, who focus most of their attention on the 'action of selling', and not nearly enough on their thinking or their vision. 

Now, we all know that in order for a sale to be made, certain action needs to be taken - we cannot simply sit there in contemplation and wish for the results to flow in. They won't and frustration will quickly take over.

However, we must also have a vision for what we are wanting to achieve and who it is we are wanting to become. When those two are aligned, magic can happen. And a perfect example of this is Nuttha Goutier, CFE, who has built Sabai Thai Spa from scratch to a hugely successful business throughout Canada.

Here is a short snippet from an enlightening conversation I enjoyed with Nuttha recently on The Exceptional Sales Leader Podcast, where she shares what happened when she acted on her vision to create Sabai Thai Spa.
