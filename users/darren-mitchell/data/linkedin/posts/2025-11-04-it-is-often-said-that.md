It is often said that success leaves clues, as does failure. Both can be a tremendous teacher, if we are willing to observe, identify the lessons and then implement.

Many organisations (and people for that matter) are looking for the short term hit, the short term solution - instant gratification. It is very rare that sustainable 'success' is achieved over night - it comes from consistent and persistent effort. There will be many obstacles, challenges and setbacks along the way, but, for those who have a clear vision, and are prepared to invest patience with a mix of urgency, and consistent & focused effort, can create magic.
