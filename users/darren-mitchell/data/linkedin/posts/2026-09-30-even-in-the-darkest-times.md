Even in the darkest times, perseverance is key. There were moments of profound doubt, where the challenges seemed insurmountable.

During one particularly difficult period, after both daughters had experienced tough days at school, the thought of returning to the UK, where things were stable, crossed my mind. The weight of their struggles and the uncertainty of the venture felt overwhelming. 

It was my wife’s unwavering belief that reignited my resolve. Her simple words, "I believe in you. You're going to find a way through this," were a powerful reminder that self-trust and external support can illuminate the path forward, even when facing immense adversity.
