***Yes, I Am In Sales & Proud Of It***

After more than 20 years in sales and sales leadership, and working with sales leaders & their teams over the last 9 years as a facilitator, coach & mentor, I have observed the full spectrum of personality types, experience levels & success ranges.

One of the most fascinating elements I have observed across this time is the link between sustainable sales success and the sales person fully embracing the sales role. Now this may sound obvious, but what is fascinating is the number of sales people (and sometimes sales leaders) who possess a perception about sales that is not necessarily serving them.

In workshops, I often ask sales people ‘who loves being sold to?’ It is very rare that people put their hand up. When asked ‘who loves to buy?’ it is a very different story. And when I ask how many of them openly & proactively share with their customers that they are in sales & their purpose is to sell, there is also a reluctance. Why?

Why is it that sales people, and often, organisations call their sales people by different titles, as if to insinuate that they are not in pure selling roles, but in customer success, or customer enablement? 🤷‍♂️

Full disclosure - I have been there and done that, coming up with creative titles and explanations to describe my role, avoiding the word ‘sales’. Thinking that this would increase the level of credibility that I have in the customer’s eyes. Wrong. 🤦‍♂️

It is time for sales people to stand up and be super proud of the role they play and the purpose of their role. My firm belief is that sales is the ultimate form of service, and that my role is to identify a problem in the market, or within a customer environment, articulate it, and then invite the customer to come on board if there is a fit for the solution.

We must understand that without sales, there is no business. And people will continue to do business with those who they know, like & trust. 

So whether you are a sales leader or an individual contributor sales person, own the fact that you are in sales, be proud of being in sales, and make no excuses for being in sales.

As we begin the brand new week, reflect on your own perception of sales and the perception of your team. Remember- you are in sales, and should be super proud!!  

Here’s to a massive week 👊
