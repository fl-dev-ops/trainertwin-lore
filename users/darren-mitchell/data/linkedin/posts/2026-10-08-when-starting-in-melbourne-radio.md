When starting in Melbourne radio, the initial reception was a significant challenge. As an unknown English personality, there was considerable backlash, and I had to navigate this while pretending everything was going well.

A personal hurdle emerged when I realised the importance of choosing a local Australian Rules Football team. This decision became a focal point, with expectations from listeners about my allegiance. To address this, I initiated a unique 'speed dating' competition with all the clubs, where each had three minutes to convince me to become their fan.

Ultimately, I chose Melbourne. Despite initial listener amusement and even skepticism, particularly from fans of other clubs like Shane Warne, the journey has been rewarding. Witnessing the team's success, including a premiership win, has been a deeply satisfying experience, especially after such a long period of mediocrity.
