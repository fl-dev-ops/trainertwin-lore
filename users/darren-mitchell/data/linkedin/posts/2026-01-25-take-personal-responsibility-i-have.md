***Take Personal Responsibility***

I have long been a fan of Neale Daniher, from the years he coached my beloved Melbourne Football Club, and especially over the last 13 years as he has fought what he calls ‘The Beast’ - Motor Neurone Disease (MND). Over the last couple of days, I have been diving deeply into his new book “The Power of Choice”. It is brilliant & a must read for any leader, especially sales leaders wanting to become exceptional. There have been many incredible insights in this book, but one in particular has stood out for me - taking personal responsibility. 

Here is what Neale reminds each of us as to what we are responsible for:

➤ The effort we give.
➤ The words we speak.
➤ The legacy we leave.
➤ The way we show up.
➤ The choices we make.
➤ The promises we keep or break.
➤ The energy we bring into a room.
➤ The boundaries we set & respect.
➤ The way we respond when life isn’t fair.
➤ The courage to admit when we’re wrong.
➤ The example we set for those around us.
➤ The way we handle failure without blame.
➤ The strength to forgive ourselves and others.
➤ The impact of our actions, intended or not.
➤ The decision to show up, even when it’s hard.
➤ The way we handle success without arrogance.
➤ The way we respond to criticism, praise, or challenge.
➤ The humility to learn from those who disagree with us.
➤ The contribution we make to something bigger than ourselves.
➤ The way we treat people, especially when no one is watching.
➤ The prejudices we hold & our willingness to confront them.
➤ The excuses we make and our willingness to move beyond them.
➤ The commitment to follow through, even when it’s inconvenient or uncomfortable.
➤ The stories we tell ourselves and whether they hold us back or move us forward.

This list has prompted a serious level of reflection for me. What are you taking personal responsibility for? And where do you have some gaps?
