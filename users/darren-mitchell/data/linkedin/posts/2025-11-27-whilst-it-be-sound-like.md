Whilst it be sound like a cliche, it is still true - people like to buy from those who they know, they like, they trust, and who they remember. So in selling, despite the product or the service, the key still remains - get face to face as often as you can.

It doesn't matter whether you are selling a physical product, a professional service, or even an idea, nothing beats getting nose to nose and toe to toe, often with a little help from video technology such as Zoom, which brings a global market a little closer to you.

Here is a short snippet from a wonderful conversation I enjoyed recently with Matt Raad on The Exceptional Sales Leader Podcast, where we explored this concept, along with many others. It was a powerful conversation and well worth listening to.
