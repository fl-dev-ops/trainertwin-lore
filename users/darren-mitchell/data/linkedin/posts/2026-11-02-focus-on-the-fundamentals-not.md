***Focus On the Fundamentals & Not Just The Outcome***

Too many sales leaders are obsessed with the number. And not nearly obsessed enough with what drives it.

Let’s be honest.

For many CROs and frontline sales leaders, leadership has become an endless cycle of pipeline reviews, forecast calls, target updates and conversations about whether the team is going to hit the number.

The pressure to deliver results is real. Targets matter. Accountability matters.

But here's the key question:

How much time are you actually investing in making your salespeople better at selling?

Because you don't achieve sustainable sales performance by constantly talking about the outcome.

You achieve it by mastering the fundamentals that drive the outcome.

That means spending time coaching, mentoring and developing your people to become exceptional at the things that really matter:

➤ Prospecting with purpose and creating quality opportunities.
➤ Asking better questions and truly understanding customer problems.
➤ Building trust, credibility and meaningful relationships.
➤ Communicating value rather than competing on price.
➤ Running disciplined, compelling sales conversations.
➤ Handling objections with confidence and commercial intelligence.
➤ Managing pipelines with rigour and qualification discipline.
➤ Negotiating effectively and consistently closing business.

These aren't new concepts. They're the fundamentals of great selling.
Yet too many leaders spend 90% of their time inspecting results and a fraction of that time improving the capabilities that produce them.

You cannot manage your way to a world-class sales team. You have to coach your way there.

As a sales leader, your job isn't simply to hold people accountable for the number.

It's to build a team capable of delivering that number consistently, predictably and sustainably.

So, get out of the forecast spreadsheet.

✅ Spend time in the field.
✅ Listen to calls.
✅ Observe customer meetings.
✅ Challenge thinking.
✅ Role-play difficult conversations.
✅ Give meaningful feedback.
✅ Recognise great execution.
✅ Invest in individual development.

And most importantly, coach the behaviours, skills and disciplines that create high performance.

Because when your people become world-class at the fundamentals, the results have a much better chance of taking care of themselves.

Stop obsessing over the scoreboard and start investing in the players.

The best sales leaders don't just deliver results. They develop the people who deliver them.

A challenge to every CRO and frontline sales leader:

Look at your calendar over the past month.

How much time have you spent reviewing the number, and how much time have you spent genuinely improving the capability of your people?

And if you changed that balance, what difference could it make to your team's performance?

I'd love to hear your perspective. 👇
