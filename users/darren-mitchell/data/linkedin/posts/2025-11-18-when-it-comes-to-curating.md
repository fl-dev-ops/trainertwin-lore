When it comes to curating inbound leads, do you and your organisation jump quickly to capturing the prospective customer's details, or do you focus all of your attention on how best to serve them in that moment? To confirm whether you may be in a position to help them?

Now there is nothing wrong with asking questions to capture contact information and further personal details when the time is right. However, asking too early in the conversation can have a dramatic impact on whether that prospective customer is going to want to continue the conversation.

Here is a short snippet from a great conversation I enjoyed recently with Terry Wilson on The Exceptional Sales Leader Podcast. In it, he shares a staggering statistic of what happens when we ask for contact information too early in the process. It is a fantastic example of why the focus on giving without any thought of receiving can become a superpower in selling.
