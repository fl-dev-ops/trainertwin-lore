***Are You Guilty Of Trying To Convince?***

I love the LinkedIn platform as it provides access to such a wonderful range of people to build connections with, as well as being an incredible source of information and inspiration. It also provides the perfect metaphor for sales and sales leadership, both in what to do, and especially in what not to do.

If you have been on the LinkedIn platform for a while, you would have received the general connection request, and based on you accepting it, the follow up message which was highly likely to include a pitch for a product or a service. 

I get stacks of these every day, and it is fascinating at how many of the messages focus on trying to ‘convince’ me to jump on a call, or to download a resource. Almost never does the person seek to build a connection or ask anything about me or my business. 🤷‍♂️

Perhaps they are simply playing the numbers game, an approach that may have worked 15 years ago, but in 2024, it is frustrating and annoying (my frame of reference). 🤦‍♂️

There is a lesson here for sales leaders and their teams - please stop trying to convince your customers that you are interesting, that you have the best product, the best service, the latest technology, the greatest widget. Why? Because your customer doesn’t care! They are not looking for what you are selling, period. ❌

What do your customers care about? Themselves and their current problems or challenges. ✅

What are they looking for? A solution to their current problems or ideas to help them more effectively deal with their challenges. ✅

So, whether you are utilising LinkedIn as a sales development resource, or seeking to build sales opportunities in a designated patch, stop trying to convince. For the vast majority of people, convincing is a repellant, and the end result is frustration and a poor reputation.

Instead, focus your attention on being interested in your customer (as a human) and their business. This will enable you to ask insightful & genuinely curious questions about them, and if you do this well, something incredible is likely to happen - you will become interesting to your customer and they may even ask you for a solution to their problem. Not only will you increase your sales numbers, you will also enhance your reputation which will lead to further business opportunities. 💪

Are you guilty of trying to convince? Change the approach this week and just watch what happens! Here’s to a massive week 👊
