***Sales is Not About Your Product***

Your customers don't care about your product.

Read that again.

They don't wake up thinking about your software.
They don't spend their commute wondering about your service.
They don't sit in meetings hoping someone will pitch them.

They care about one thing:

Their problems.

Yet most sales conversations start with features, capabilities, company history, or the latest product update.

Wrong conversation.

➤ Customers don't buy products.
➤ They buy outcomes.
➤ They buy certainty.
➤ They buy reduced risk.
➤ They buy growth.
➤ They buy time back.
➤ They buy solutions to problems they can't solve alone.

So here's a challenge for every frontline sales leader and CRO:

Can your team go an entire week without proactively talking about your product or service?

Instead...

✅ Ask better questions.
✅ Stay curious longer.
✅ Dig deeper into the commercial, operational and personal impact of the problem.
✅ Help customers quantify the cost of doing nothing.
✅ Share insights they haven't considered.
✅ Challenge assumptions.
✅ Teach them something about their business or market.

Become the person who helps them think differently, not the person trying hardest to sell something.

Because when customers fully understand the problem and the cost of leaving it unsolved, the right solution often becomes obvious.

And that's when your product finally has a place in the conversation.

The best salespeople don't lead with what they sell. They lead with what their customers need to solve.

👇 Could you and your team go an entire week without proactively talking about your product or service? What would your customer conversations sound like instead?
