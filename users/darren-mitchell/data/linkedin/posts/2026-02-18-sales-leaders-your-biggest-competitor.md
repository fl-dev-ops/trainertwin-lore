Sales leaders:

Your biggest competitor isn’t necessarily another vendor.
It’s your customer’s overwhelmed brain.

On a recent episode of The Exceptional Sales Leader Podcast, Chelsea Olsen shared something simple, but powerful:

Your buyers are inundated.

Their brains and nervous systems are overloaded. You’re not just competing with other solutions. You’re competing with:
➤ Slack notifications
➤ Internal politics
➤ Budget pressure
➤ Personal stress
➤ Strategic pivots
➤ And everything else in their day

If you want your team to win more consistently, start here:

1️⃣ Teach them to recognise buying signals and behavioural triggers.
2️⃣ Help them understand what actually makes their customers tick.
3️⃣ Get brutally honest about the problems you can truly solve.

Too many companies claim they can solve everything.

The best sales organisations know exactly what they solve and what they don’t.

Clarity builds trust.
Trust drives action.

If you're leading a sales team right now, ask yourself:

Are you training your reps to compete against other vendors… or against overwhelm?

Because in order to do that effectively, you've got to understand what makes your customers tick.
