***The Fast Track To Exceptional Sales Leadership***

Working with sales leaders, it is amazing how many of them were promoted into the leadership role based on their exceptional performance as an individual contributor, without necessarily considering their desire to be a leader, or their potential to be a leader. 

And very often, they were left to their own devices to make a ‘successful’ transition - in fact one leader shared with me that their ‘leadership training’ was a link to a LinkedIn learning course. 🤷‍♂️ Talk about being thrown to the wolves!!

Is it any wonder that many sales leaders flounder and end up returning to the individual contributor role. This needs to change because far too many high potential sales leaders are not realising that potential. And this can have not only an immediate detrimental impact on the team and business, it can have a generational impact.

This is one of the core reasons why I am so fixated on and committed to exceptional sales leadership and believe that it is within reach for every sales leader who is willing to be teachable and do the work - specific work.

For the last few years, most of the leadership work I have done has been group work, which will continue - just love working with groups of sales leaders who ‘get it’ and are heavily invested in their teams. What is not quite possible in the group environment though is the ability to work closely with an individual sales leader, help them customise their leadership approach, and fast track their development to exceptional sales leadership.

To that end, I am opening up 6 spots for sales leaders or aspiring sales leaders, where I will work with you personally over the next 6 months, creating a customised leadership program that will enable you to fast track to exceptional sales leadership. 

This is not a group program - this is a customised 1:1 program where we will work together over 6 months, develop your sales leadership blueprint, identify the triggers and principles that will help you unleash the potential of your team, and in the process, fast track your development to exceptional sales leadership.

The pre-requisites?
✅  You are teachable and coachable
✅  You are committed to your sales leadership development 
✅  You are prepared to invest in yourself & do the work 
✅  You want to be an exceptional sales leader
