The sales profession is evolving. The shift from transactional selling to a buyer-centric approach is crucial for success and for restoring respect to the field.

This transformation hinges on cultivating genuine curiosity and practicing empathetic listening. It's about moving from a mindset of 'just selling' to one of 'serving' the buyer's needs. By prioritising understanding and offering solutions that truly add value, salespeople can build stronger relationships and foster trust.

What if the key to effective selling lies in asking better questions and truly hearing the answers?
