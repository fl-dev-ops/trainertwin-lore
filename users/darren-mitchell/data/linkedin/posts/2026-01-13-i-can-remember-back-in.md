I can remember back in the 'halcyon' of sales where as a salesperson, we had the opportunity to educate our buyer, where if there was even the slightest element of curiosity, it was relatively simple to gain an audience with a prospective customer.

Fast forward to today, and buyers are significantly more educated (not necessarily more savvy!) and they do the bulk of their research even before speaking with a salesperson or an organisation. This includes researching the sales person and the sales leader. 

As a result, it is critical that as a sales leader and a sales person, we are crystal clear on our values, what messaging we put out there, and especially around who we are. Why? Because there will be opportunities that come to you based on the reputation you have in the market place with people you have never even met yet. And also realise, there will be opportunities that don't come to you for the exact same reason. So be real, be the authentic you.
