***As A Sales Leader, You Must Know Your Numbers***

One of the key areas I focus on when coaching and mentoring Sales Leaders is the behaviours and actions that must be taken in order to deliver exceptional results. Far too many sales leaders revert to driving relentless focus on the numbers often at the expense of the behaviours, which can result in poor outcomes, or at best, short term spikes in performance.

If we are to oversee an exceptional team and become in the process an exceptional sales leader, then we must learn to step back and focus our attention on progress rather than perfection. However, there are 2 key things that we as sales leaders must get straight so that we can do this with confidence:

1️⃣  Define current reality - a key attribute of an exceptional sales leader is the ability to understand current reality, and be able to communicate that to all relevant stakeholders. This is not about painting an unrealistic picture and selling to senior leaders what you think they want to hear. It is about being authentic and factual with where the team is at. This becomes a launching pad.

2️⃣  To define reality, we must know our numbers. Now this may sound counter to what I said above in that we should be focusing on behaviours, however in order to do that we must know our key metrics from which we can begin to measure progress.

What are the key numbers? Much will depend on your industry and your particular business, but here are some key numbers that we as sales leaders must get really clear on:

✅  Average conversion rate
✅  # of calls to convert to meetings per month
✅  Average revenue per customer 
✅  Average profit margin per customer
✅  Rolling 90 day pipeline value as a % of sales target
✅  # of meetings or sales conversations per Account Executive per month
✅  # of coaching sessions sales leaders conduct per month 

This is by no means an exhaustive list, but it does allow us as sales leaders to start to get a better handle on our business, help us to define the current reality and then start to identify the key behaviours and actions we want our sales people to focus on so that we can begin to measure progress.

So, as we begin the new week, ask yourself :
➤  Have you clearly defined your current reality?
➤  How well do you know your numbers?

If there is any doubt as to the answers to these questions, what better time than right now to apply some laser focus, so that we can then drive the key behaviours that will drive progress.

Here’s to a massive week 👊
