When it comes to making any decision, whether it be a purchasing decision, a decision on where to go on holidays, or which restaurant to eat at, do you ever stop to consider how you arrive at the decision? According to Aristotle, who was known as one of the greatest orators and influencers in history, there needs to be 3 key components to influence - Ethos, Pathos & Logos. In other words, Credibility, Emotion & Logic.

This is especially true in sales - we must have credibility, we must create an environment where the customer can feel an emotion that propels them forward, and we also need to incorporate logic as well.
