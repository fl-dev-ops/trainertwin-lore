95% of salespeople are a complete waste of time.

Let that sink in.

In a recent conversation with Philip Squire on The Exceptional Sales Leader Podcast, a simple question revealed something uncomfortable:
“What percentage of people sell to you in a way you actually like?”

One leader said 5%.
Another said less than 10%.

Different voices. Same message.

Most sales teams aren’t losing because of competition.
They’re losing because of how they sell.

➤ Too many pitches.
➤ Not enough perspective.
➤ Too much talking.
➤ Not enough understanding.

The gap isn’t product.

It’s experience.

The best sales leaders know this:

👉 Winning isn’t about pushing harder. It’s about selling in a way buyers actually value.

So here’s the real question:

Are you building a team your customers want to buy from?

Or one they’re trying to avoid?

What percentage would your customers give your team?
