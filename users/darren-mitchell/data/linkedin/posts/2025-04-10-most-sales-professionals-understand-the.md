Most sales professionals understand the concept that customers like to do business with those who they know, who they like, who they trust, and ultimately, who they remember. Whilst this is often considered to be a cliché, it is nonetheless true.

And what is fascinating about this is that many sales professionals are not tapping into a very powerful source to create rememberability (I hope that is a word? 🤣) - video.

Video is powerful, it is authentic, it is memorable, and it is completely under-utilised. If you are looking to supercharge your personal brand and build rememberability in your marketplace, consider adding video to your repertoire.
