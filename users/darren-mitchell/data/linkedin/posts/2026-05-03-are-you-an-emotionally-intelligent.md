***Are You An Emotionally Intelligent Sales Leader?***

Most Sales Managers don’t lose their team because of strategy.

They lose them because of themselves.

I learned this the hard way.

Years ago, I led a high-performing team on paper. Targets were being hit, pipeline looked strong, leadership was satisfied.

But behind the numbers?

Tension. Silence. Disengagement.

I thought I was being “direct.”
I told myself I was “driving standards.”

In reality, I lacked self-awareness, and my team paid the price.

I couldn’t see how my reactions in forecast calls shut people down.
I didn’t notice how my body language discouraged challenge.
I completely missed the emotional undercurrent in the room.

And here’s the truth most seasoned leaders avoid:

If your team doesn’t feel safe, they won’t be honest.
If they’re not honest, your forecast is fiction.
If your forecast is fiction, your leadership is failing, no matter the number.

This is where Emotional Intelligence stops being a “soft skill” and becomes a commercial weapon.

Self-awareness is knowing the impact you have, especially when you think you’re right.
Social awareness is reading what’s not being said, and having the courage to address it.

Without these, you don’t have a performance culture.

You have a compliance culture.

And compliance never outperforms commitment.

The best sales leaders I know aren’t just pipeline-driven.

They’re emotionally calibrated.

They know when to push.
When to listen.
When to challenge.
And when they’re the problem.

Because sometimes… you are.

And if you can’t see it, neither can your team, at least not in a way that helps you.

If you’re leading a team right now, ask yourself:
When was the last time you truly examined your impact on your culture?

Or are you still measuring everything, except yourself?
