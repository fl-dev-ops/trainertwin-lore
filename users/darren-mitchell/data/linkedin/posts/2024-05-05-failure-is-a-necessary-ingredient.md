***Failure Is A Necessary Ingredient To Sales Success***

Working in sales for over 20 years, I was staggered at the number of sales managers and teams that would frown upon failure - to the point where sales people would do whatever they could to avoid making mistakes.

And in working as a coach & mentor to sales leaders and their teams for the last 9 years, unfortunately nothing much has changed. The pressure to hit the number is all consuming and within the sales environment, the expectation is to convert every opportunity because the forensic investigation if we ‘fail’ is more about apportioning blame, not learning so as to improve. Not a recipe for exceptional performance.

The fact is - sales success is not a straight road and it is certainly not a downhill road. It is uphill, with lots of obstacles, challenges and detours. Which means sales leaders and their teams will be tested and they will experience ‘failure’. The difference is that exceptional sales leaders embrace ‘failure’ for the lessons it can teach and lean into it knowing that it is an essential ingredient to sales success.

The Law of Polarity states that everything has an opposite. Night and day, good and evil, yes and no. This is especially true in sales where we will not convert every prospect and we will win some deals and we will lose some deals. It is the lesson we take from the losses (‘failure’) that will establish the platform from which to launch into the next deal, the next opportunity. 

This is why I always ask sales leaders and their teams what has been their biggest failure for the week or the month? Because if there has been no failure I know that one of two things has happened;

1️⃣ The sales leader and sales team have caught lightning in a bottle (extremely rare!), or
2️⃣ The sales leader and sales team have not been extending themselves enough beyond their comfort zone.

Now many will tell you that sales is a ‘numbers game’ and this is true, however, we can improve the numbers if we can embrace the necessary ingredient that failure is essential to success. As long as we learn from the failure, and continue to implement tried and tested strategies, the results will turn and success will be achieved. It just cannot be achieved without experiencing failure.

So as we begin the new week, instead of pressuring your team to deliver exceptional results, how about challenging them to step further outside of their comfort zone and allowing them to experience failure. You may just find that the lesson in that failure will be more significant than any success they achieve.

Remember - failure is a necessary ingredient to success.

Here’s to a massive week 👊
