***Are You A Leader Of Impeccable Character?***

I was listening to a podcast today and a reference was made to Charlie Munger, the long term associate of Warren Buffett. He was quoted as saying that ‘trust is one of the greatest economic forces on earth’. In leadership, trust is the foundation of exceptional performance. 

Without trust, our foundation is brittle, and our performance, potentially unpredictable. In leadership workshops, I often ask leaders whether they are trusted? And the response? Almost always, ‘Yes I am’. But how do they know? What evidence is there to support their belief that their team trusts them?

As a leader, remember that your team are always asking 3 key questions about you -

1️⃣  Do you care about me as a person?
2️⃣  Can you help me become effective in my role?
3️⃣  Can I trust you?

These 3 questions are constantly being asked about you as a leader, and if the answer to each of these questions is ‘yes’, you are in a very strong position. This also points to your character. Are you a leader of impeccable character?

✅  Do you do the things that are required of you, every single day, that will lead to you and the team making progress?

✅  Are you uncompromising in your standards of excellence every single day, without question?

✅  When nobody is looking, do you continue to focus on these standards of excellence, or do you wait for ‘public view’ to be focused on the standards?

As leaders, we must approach our craft with the focus of 100% public attention, as if a camera was fixed on our every move. Here is the key question - ‘With the camera following your every move, would you be happy to watch the replay, and would you be comfortable for your team to watch the replay?’

This points to character. As we begin this week, reflect on your leadership and your character - are you a leader of impeccable character? 

I certainly hope so 🤞

Here’s to a massive week 👊
