***Ethical Sales with Daman Grewal***

In this episode of The Exceptional Sales Leader Podcast I am joined by logistics expert Daman Grewal, coming in from British Columbia, Canada. Daman shares his journey from an unexpected entry into the logistics industry to becoming a significant player in sales education through logisticssales.com. The conversation delves into the challenges and opportunities within the logistics sector and the creation of free access to sales training for aspiring salespeople around the globe. 

Daman’s insights shed light on the importance of ethical sales practices and the role of authentic communication in building lasting customer relationships. The discussion flows into the intricacies of transitioning from a traditional employee role in logistics to entrepreneurship, highlighting the impact of leadership and employee treatment in driving business success. 

The episode wraps up with a look at the misconceptions about sales and how to overcome sales blocks to unlock potential, along with the influence of AI in transforming sales procedures without replacing the human touch.
