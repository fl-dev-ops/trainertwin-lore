***The Sales Whisperer - Wes- Schaeffer***

In this episode of The Exceptional Sales Leader Podcast, I have the privilege of speaking with Wes- Schaeffer, The Sales Whisperer. 

From a career in the military, to then moving into commission sales, then high tech sales, and today, now a highly sought after business coach, sales & sales leadership mentor, and keynote speaker, Wes has rather an unconventional story. 

A story that is filled with challenges, and experiences that helps him unleash the potential of sales leaders and their teams. We discuss the #1 job of a Sales Leader, unpack the concept of ‘make every sale’ and explore the new ABC’s of selling in 2024 and beyond. It is a fascinating conversation, and one that I know you will find valuable.
