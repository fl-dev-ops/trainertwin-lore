Grateful doesn’t quite capture it.

In 2025, I had the privilege of working with close to 150 leaders at AMES Australasia, in partnership with PD Training, and it’s been one of the most rewarding chapters in a collaboration that now spans more than five years.

Over that time, I’ve seen the AMES Australasia leadership community continue to evolve, strengthen, and deepen its commitment to growth. This year was no exception. These leaders didn’t just attend leadership development programs; they fully embraced their development. 

They showed up engaged, curious, and proactive, taking ownership of their learning and challenging themselves to lift their impact.

What stood out most was the shift in capability and confidence. Across the group, there were definitive gains in their ability to influence both internal and external stakeholders, communicating with greater clarity, navigating complexity more effectively, leading with purpose and enhancing their decision making capabilities.

A sincere thank you to Claire Fitzpatrick and the broader AMES Australasia team for the continued trust over the past five years. Partnerships like this are built on shared values, consistency, and a genuine belief in developing people, and I’m incredibly grateful to be part of that journey. Claire, I also particularly enjoyed our conversations over the famous chicken & avocado sandwich. 😉

If the leaders I had the privilege of working with in 2025 are any indication, the future of AMES Australasia is in very good hands. Thank you for allowing me to be part of your leadership journey, and may 2026 be your biggest and most impactful year yet. 🙏
