***Coaching That Builds People, Not Just Pipelines***

Most sales coaching sessions fail because they have no structure.

Without a framework, “coaching” becomes another pipeline inspection.

If you want coaching to drive real performance, it needs to be consistent and focused.

And the data backs it up: reps who receive structured coaching see win rates improve by up to 30%.

Here’s a simple framework I recommend to Sales Leaders:

1️⃣  Mindset Check. Start with the rep, not the deal. How are they showing up? Confidence, resilience, and motivation all impact results.

2️⃣  Preparation Review. What’s their game plan? Are they researching, planning, and bringing intention to customer interactions?

3️⃣  Skill Development. Focus on one core sales skill (discovery, storytelling, objection handling, negotiation). Practice it. Roleplay it. Sharpen it.

4️⃣  Deal Application. Now connect it back to a live opportunity. “How will you use this skill in your next customer conversation?”

5️⃣  Action Commitments . End with clarity: What will they do differently this week? What will you hold them accountable to?

When you run coaching sessions this way, every conversation develops the person and strengthens the pipeline.
Skip the structure and you’re just talking numbers.

Great coaching is intentional. It doesn’t just review deals, it builds capability.
