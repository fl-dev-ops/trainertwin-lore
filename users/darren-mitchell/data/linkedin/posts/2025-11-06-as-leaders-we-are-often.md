As leaders we are often placed in situations that are not necessarily black or white. It might be that a customer has asked for a much deeper discount as an incentive to win a deal, or you have been asked by a colleague or Manager to make a decision that goes against your value set, and often the values of the business.

With leadership comes great responsibility and whilst we won't always make the 'right' decision, our focus should always be on doing the 'right' thing, irrespective of the possible ramifications. Because at the end of the day, the only thing we have is our reputation.
