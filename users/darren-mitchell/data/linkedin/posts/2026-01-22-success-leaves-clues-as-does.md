Success leaves clues, as does failure. It is amazing that the more sales leaders, business thought leaders and entrepreneurs I speak & work with, this concept gets more magnified.

We cannot experience success without failure - as John Maxwell says, they are not opposites, they are integrated. Being able to embrace failure, learn from it, and utilise it as rocket fuel to propel yourself, your team and your business forward, is ultimately what separates the exceptional performers from everyone else.

What is your attitude towards failure? Can you tolerate it? Do you embrace it? The fact is, we all experience failure, have experienced failure and will experience failure. It is the meaning we give it and therefore the focus we apply, that will ultimately determine what happens next.
