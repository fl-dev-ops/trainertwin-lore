The use of AI in everyday life is exponentially growing, and whether you are a fan or a detractor of AI, one thing is for sure - it is not going away. In sales, AI is continuing to change how sales leaders and their teams create value, differentiate themselves, and become more relevant to their marketplace.

The smart sales leaders and teams are using AI ethically, to better understand their ideal customer and then to craft value based messages that specifically target the problem or challenge their solution is designed to solve. This helps to further solidify the know, like and trust factor so important in sales.
