**Why Courage is a Critical Skillset for Sales Leaders***

Courage is the most underrated revenue skill.

➤ Not coaching.
➤ Not forecasting.
➤ Not deal inspection.

Courage.

The highest-performing sales teams aren't built by leaders who avoid discomfort. They're built by leaders who lean into it.

Three types of courage separate exceptional sales leaders from average ones:

1️⃣ Tell Courage

Have the conversation others avoid. Give direct, honest feedback. Challenge poor behaviours. Set clear expectations. Radical clarity is a gift, not a threat.

2️⃣ Trust Courage

Stop trying to control every outcome. Trust your people with ownership, decisions, and accountability. Great salespeople don't grow under micromanagement, they grow through belief and responsibility.

3️⃣ Try Courage

Experiment. Innovate. Take calculated risks. Challenge the "this is how we've always done it" mindset. The market is changing too quickly for leaders who play it safe.

Here's the uncomfortable truth:

Many revenue problems aren't capability problems.

They're courage problems.

If you're a frontline Sales Manager, ask yourself:
Which conversation am I avoiding?
Where am I holding on too tightly?
What experiment have I delayed because it might fail?

If you're a Chief Revenue Officer, ask yourself:
Are you building a culture that rewards courage, or one that quietly punishes it?

Exceptional performance doesn't come from perfect playbooks.

It comes from courageous leadership that creates courageous teams.

The teams that outperform aren't the ones with the best strategy, they're the ones led by people brave enough to tell, trust, and try.
