You’ve been introduced. Take a breath. Be present. Be calm. Be aware. Be connected. A thousand people are staring at you. The lights are down. There is stillness. And quiet. And so much potential in the room. Your job is to inform, inspire, encourage and create an experience that people want to be in the middle of. An experience that might be a beginning to something beautiful and powerful for the audience. You are not the gift. You are the conduit to the gift. To the wisdom. The knowledge. The answers. The inspiration. They’re not there for you. They are there for them. As they should be. Be grateful. Be selfless. Be of service. 🩵
