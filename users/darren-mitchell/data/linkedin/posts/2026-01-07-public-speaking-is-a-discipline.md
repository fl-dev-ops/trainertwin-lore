Public Speaking is a discipline that many sales people and sales leaders need to engage in, and yet many seek to avoid. I am staggered at the number of senior, experienced adults who would rather do anything else, than speak in front of a group.

The good news is that public speaking is a discipline that can be learned, and over time, a discipline that we can learn to enjoy. We may never 'master' it, but it can become a very powerful influencing strategy if we continue to step up, learn, adapt and grow.

Gary Daly is a fine example of someone who has done just that, and in this short snippet, shares a story of an experience that many of us can relate to.
