Technical audiences don’t make decisions through logic alone. Scientists, doctors, and engineers are people, and emotion shapes how every decision moves from understanding to action.

Effective conversations start with the customer’s goals and challenges, not a list of product specifications. Orient the discussion around the problem they’re trying to solve; the details matter, but they can come later. How do you make sure your conversations connect with both the analytical and human sides of a decision?
