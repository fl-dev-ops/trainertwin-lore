***Exceptional Salespeople are Buyer’s Assistants***

Your sales process isn't the problem.

Your obsession with making buyers follow it is.

Too many sales teams are still trying to drag prospects through qualification stages, discovery frameworks, and perfectly orchestrated sales plays.

Meanwhile...

The buyer is already on a journey that doesn't care about your CRM stages.

Exceptional salespeople understand something average salespeople don't:

They stop acting like sellers.

They become the buyer's assistant.

They help buyers make sense of complexity.

They help them build internal consensus.

They anticipate objections before procurement does.

They make it easier to navigate risk, politics, competing priorities, and uncertainty.

The best salespeople don't ask, "How do I move this deal to the next stage?"

They ask, "What does my buyer need to confidently take their next step?"

That's a completely different mindset.

And it changes everything.

Frontline sales leaders and CROs:

If your coaching is centred on following your sales process instead of understanding their buying process...

Don't be surprised when your pipeline stalls.

Because buyers don't buy according to your playbook.

They buy according to theirs.

Question for sales leaders: What would change in your team's results if every deal review focused on the buyer's journey instead of the seller's process?
