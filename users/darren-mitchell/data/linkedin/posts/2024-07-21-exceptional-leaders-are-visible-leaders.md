***Exceptional Leaders Are Visible Leaders***

Back when I was a sales individual contributor, one of my most frustrating elements of the role was the lack of access to my Sales Manager. 

Especially in the early months, I needed guidance, coaching & mentoring but I felt as if I was on my own, to work it out for myself. This was super challenging.

Fast forward 7 years when I was given the opportunity to lead a sales team, I wanted to ensure that I created an environment where I was as visible as possible to my team. For 2 years prior to this opportunity, I was forming my sales leadership philosophy and there was one phrase a mentor shared with me that would be my underlying leadership mantra - ‘The greatest gift you can give your team is the gift of your attention’. 

The best way to do this is to be visible.

When coaching & mentoring sales leaders today, this is my first challenge to them - to be visible. And through being visible, focus on being present.

Many sales leaders spend huge amounts of time behind closed doors, working on strategy, analysing numbers, preparing forecasts and reports. Whilst these things are important & required as part of the role, nothing can replace the effectiveness of being visible and accessible to the team.

Exceptional sales leaders are visible leaders. What makes them different?

✅  They prioritise time with their team. They know this is a gift they can continue to give.

✅  They empower their team to make decisions and take action.

✅  They create an environment of psychological safety which encourages their team to be themselves.

✅  They don’t put pressure on themselves to have to have all the answers.

✅  They prioritise progress over perfection and lead by example.

✅  They lead effectively upwards and are not afraid to say no to senior leaders, especially when it will take time away from being with their team.

As we begin the brand new week, here is one fundamental question to consider - ‘Are you a visible leader?’ If the answer is yes, if we asked your team, would they agree? 🤞

Remember, exceptional leaders are visible leaders. 👊
