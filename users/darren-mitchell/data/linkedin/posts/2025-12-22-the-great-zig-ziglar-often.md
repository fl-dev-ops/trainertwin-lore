The great Zig Ziglar often said 'your attitude, and not your aptitude, will determine your altitude'. And when it comes to sales, I firmly believe that a sales person's attitude is higher on the priority list than their past accomplishments.

Many people have disagreed with me on this, and many probably still will, however, what I have observed over more than 25 years in sales and sales leadership is that exceptional sales leaders and exceptional sales people, have exceptional attitudes. Period. Yes, there are other characteristics to consider, however, attitude is right up there.
