***Passion Fades. Commitment Doesn’t***

Early in my sales leadership career, I made this mistake too often.

I’d meet sales professionals who lit up when they talked about sales.

They had energy, charisma, confidence.

“Sales is my passion,” they’d say.

And I’d buy in.

But six months later?

They were the ones missing targets, ghosting pipeline reviews, looking burnt out.

Meanwhile, the quieter, less flashy sales professional—the one who just showed up every day and put in the work—was climbing the leaderboard.

That’s when it hit me:

➤  Passion is a feeling.
➤  Commitment is a behaviour.

Passion is inconsistent. It comes and goes.

Commitment doesn’t care how you feel. It’s a decision you make every single day—especially when things suck.

The top 1% of salespeople I’ve worked with weren’t always the most passionate.

But they were relentlessly committed.

✅  They follow up when others flake.
✅  They prepare when others wing it.
✅  They get back up after 10 “no’s” and ask for the 11th.

I don’t need sales professionals who love sales.

I need sales professionals who do sales—when it’s boring, brutal, or thankless.

➤ Passion is a spark.
➤ Commitment is the grindstone.

Give me the committed grinder. Every. Single. Time.

👉 What do you value more in a sales professional—passion or commitment? 

I’d love to hear where other sales leaders stand.
