***Stop Chasing 2 Rabbits At A Time!!***

Confucius said “The person who chases 2 rabbits, catches neither”. This is so applicable to sales - are you & your team guilty of chasing too many rabbits at once? Perhaps it's pressure from senior executives to deliver the numbers in the short term that can lead to this behaviour. 

Intuitively we know that focused action & attention on the right customer can deliver results, & yet, we've probably all been guilty of chasing multiple opportunities at once, hoping that we'll hit the jackpot.

As we enter the brand new week, consider the following principles & how they may enable more tangible results or facilitate real progress:

1️⃣ Precision Targeting: Concentrate efforts on a specific market or customer segment. Become laser focused.

2️⃣ Deep Understanding: Develop a thorough understanding of your target customer to the point where you know their needs & preferences almost before they do.

3️⃣ Personalised Approach: Ensure that your sales approach is specific to your customer & not generalised. It is all about them & not about you!

4️⃣ Relationship Building: Invest time in cultivating a strong & genuine connection with the customer. This includes identifying & then leveraging the internal informal influencing structure, not just the org structure.

5️⃣ Problem Solving: Identify & address the specific challenges your target customer is facing. Verify whether this is a problem that must be solved. If not, there's no opportunity.

6️⃣ Effective Communication: Clearly convey the unique value proposition of your solution, demonstrating how it will solve the customer’s problem.

7️⃣ Focused Learning: Continuously refine your knowledge about your target market & customer to stay ahead of the competition. Continue to be curious.

8️⃣ Resource Optimisation: When focusing on just one opportunity at a time, we can better utilise key resources efficiently toward the chosen customer, avoiding the scattergun approach. When this happens, you are more likely to have specialists who want to work on your opportunities over others.

9️⃣ Adaptability: Be flexible to adapt your strategy based on feedback and the evolving market dynamics. Really difficult to do when you are chasing multiple opportunities at once.

🔟 Measurable Results: Set clear, achievable goals for your focused efforts & track performance against them. Remember, this doesn't just mean ‘sales closed’ - it's all the incremental calls to action that are executed as part of the buying process.

There is often an overwhelming temptation to chase multiple opportunities at once, & logically we can justify that it increases our odds of delivering outcomes (the sales version of multi tasking). If you are grappling with this temptation right now, just remember the words of Confucius ‘the person who chases 2 sales opportunities, catches neither’.

Here’s to a massive week 👊
