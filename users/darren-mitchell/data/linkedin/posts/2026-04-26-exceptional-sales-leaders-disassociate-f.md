***Exceptional Sales Leaders Disassociate From The Outcome***

You’re not losing deals because of your strategy.

You’re losing them because you’re obsessed with the outcome.

And it’s killing your team.
➤ Every forecast call.
➤ Every pipeline review.
➤ Every “where is this deal at?” message.

You think you’re driving performance.

What you’re actually doing is training your team to chase outcomes they don’t control.

The best sporting coaches in the world don’t obsess over the scoreboard.

They obsess over:
✅ Structure
✅ Discipline
✅ Execution of a repeatable game plan

Because they know something most sales leaders forget:
👉 Outcomes are a lagging indicator.
👉 Behaviours are the only lever you control.

But in sales?

We’ve normalised micromanagement disguised as leadership.
We’ve turned forecasting into a weekly anxiety ritual.
We’ve convinced ourselves that pressure equals performance.

It doesn’t.

➤ It creates hesitation.
➤ It destroys confidence.
➤ It erodes accountability.

High-performing sales teams don’t need more pressure.

They need leaders who can detach from the outcome long enough to coach what actually drives it.

✅ The process.
✅ The standards.
✅ The non-negotiables.

That’s the difference between teams that scramble and teams that scale.

So here’s the uncomfortable question:

Are you leading a process your team can win with…

Or just reacting to numbers you can’t control?
