***Please Avoid The Dysfunctional Sales Leader***


CROs: Stop creating frontline sales leaders who look good on paper but damage the team.

You don’t have a leadership problem because your managers aren’t tough enough.

You have one when your frontline leaders create an environment where people are afraid to challenge, disagree, or speak the truth.

And the cost is enormous.

Healthy sales teams need productive conflict.

➤ Reps should be able to challenge strategy.
➤ Managers should challenge assumptions.
➤ Leaders should challenge performance.
➤ And everyone should be able to disagree without fear of punishment, politics, or personal retaliation.

That’s not dysfunction.

That’s how high-performing sales organisations get better.

Dysfunction looks different:
➤ The manager who confuses pressure with leadership.
➤ The leader who shuts down dissent.
➤ The rep who stays silent because “speaking up” has consequences.
➤ The team that spends more energy managing internal politics than winning customers.
➤ The CRO who tolerates it because the numbers look acceptable, until they don’t.

Here’s the uncomfortable truth:

You can’t build a predictable revenue engine on dysfunctional leadership.

When frontline leaders lack the skills to coach, challenge, resolve conflict and create psychological safety, problems compound:

➤ Performance becomes inconsistent.
➤ Top performers disengage.
➤ Good people leave.
➤ Bad behaviours spread.
➤ Forecast quality deteriorates.
➤ And accountability becomes theatre.

CROs need to stop asking only:

“Can this person hit the number?”

Start asking:

“What happens to the team when this person is in charge?”

Because your frontline leaders don’t just manage revenue.

They define the environment in which revenue is created.

If you’re a CRO, audit your frontline leadership, not just their results.

➤ Who creates clarity?
➤ Who creates conflict?
➤ Who creates fear?
➤ Who creates growth?

The leadership behaviour you tolerate today becomes the sales culture you inherit tomorrow.

What’s the biggest frontline leadership dysfunction you’ve seen destroy a sales team?
