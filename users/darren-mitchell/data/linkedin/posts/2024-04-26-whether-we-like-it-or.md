Whether we like it or not, artificial intelligence (AI) is going to have more and more of an impact on not only our everyday lives, but also on our professional lives. For sales leaders and their teams, it is incredibly important to understand and embrace AI and not be threatened by it. 

It will be the sales leaders and teams that are able to leverage the power of AI in an ethical way, without losing sight of the importance of building and maintaining genuine, value based relationships with customers, that will ultimately win.

Here is a terrific summary of a conversation I had with Nick Caruso, Chief Revenue Office at KnowledgeNet.ai on artificial intelligence in sales. In fact, the approach is more about augmented intelligence rather than artificial. If you are curious as to how AI is going to impact sales and sales leadership, then this article and the podcast episode is well worth exploring. The link is in the article.
