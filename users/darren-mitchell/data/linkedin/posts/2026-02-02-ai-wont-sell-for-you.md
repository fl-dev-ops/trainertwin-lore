AI won’t sell for you.
But it will make you a far better seller.

Most salespeople think AI is about automation.
It’s not. It’s about amplification.

AI gives you:
• More productivity
• Better intelligence
• Higher-quality leads
• Smarter, more relevant conversations

In short: more firepower.

But here’s the part many miss 👇
AI doesn’t decide what you sell or how you sell.

That’s still on you.

What AI really does is remove the grind.
The research.
The tab-hopping between Salesforce, Zoom, outreach tools, and intent data dashboards.

When the mundane disappears, something powerful happens:
You’re forced to think.
You’re forced to be creative.
You’re forced to actually sell.

And that’s what great sellers want anyway.
Time with customers.
Better conversations.
Real problem-solving.

AI doesn’t replace sellers.
It gives them back their best work.

The sellers who win with AI aren’t the ones who automate everything.
They’re the ones who use AI to spend more time doing what humans do best. And Usman Sheikh knows this better than most. I had a great conversation with him on The Exceptional Sales Leader Podcast, where he goes into detail on how AI can amplify selling. This episode is well worth listening to.
