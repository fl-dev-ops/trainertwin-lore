***Would you rather hire a Brilliant Jerk or a Humble Grinder?***

It’s the eternal dilemma in sales leadership:

👉 Do you bet on competence or character?

We’ve all seen it.

The rep who can close a whale with one call — but poisons the team culture.

Or the one with average numbers — but lifts everyone around them.

In the short term, competence looks like the winner.

Revenue now. Numbers now. Glory now. 

But over time?

👉 Character compounds.

Character coaches.

Character scales.

A sales floor full of high-character people can be trained to close.

But a floor full of skilled egos?

Good luck building a culture that lasts. 🤷‍♂️

Skills can be taught. Character is recruited.

Sales Leaders: Where do you draw the line?

Will a person of high competence and low character get a ‘pass’?

Will a person of threshold competence and high character get a ‘chance’?

As a Sales Leader, how do you weigh competence vs. character when hiring?

Would love your thoughts 👇
