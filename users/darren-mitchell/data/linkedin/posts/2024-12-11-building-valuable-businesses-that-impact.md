***Building Valuable Businesses That Impact with Justin Goodbread***

Whether you are a business owner or hold a leadership or influencing position within an organisation, the key objective for the business is to create value that can have a lasting impact. 

And this is not necessarily just linked to the profitability of the business – it is also linked to the sellability of the business. 

In this episode of The Exceptional Sales Leader Podcast, I’m joined by Justin Goodbread, CEO of Financially Simple, a man with over 30 years experience in founding, growing and exiting businesses, the latest of which he exited in 49 months, selling it for over $10m. 

He shares personal anecdotes, including how defining personal and business goals can lead to significant breakthroughs in success, as well as strategically removing owner dependencies and aligning company operations with client expectations. Justin highlights the role of disciplined time management in ensuring that business owners remain focused on high-impact duties and explains how building systems and processes can drive business value and sustainability.
