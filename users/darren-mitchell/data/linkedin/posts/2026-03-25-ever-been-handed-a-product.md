Ever been handed a product so unsellable it should’ve come with a warning label?

On The Exceptional Sales Leader Podcast, Mark Howley reminded me just how far grit can take you.

He told a story about selling vacuum packaging machines into the processed meat industry. We’re talking bacon and hot dogs, across Philadelphia, New York, Boston… and absolutely zero political correctness.

The machine was basically a ship’s anchor with a price tag. No one wanted it. Doors slammed. F-bombs flew. Rejection was the daily special.

Mark thought he was done for. He was already looking for another job…

Then his boss gave him a raise.

Why?

“Because if everyone got the door slammed in their face as many times as you did, we’d be billionaires.”

That’s the part most leaders forget:

You don’t just reward wins.
You reward the people who keep showing up when the wins aren’t there yet.

Tenacity isn’t just a trait, it’s an asset leaders should invest in.

How do you reward resilience on your team?
