The most critical leadership quality? Humility.

As the saying goes, 'Where there is ego, there is excess.' Humility de-escalates tension, opens the door for honest conversations, and creates space for personal reflection on impact. It's a powerful tool for achieving desired outcomes.

Cultivating intellectual, professional, and social humility is deeply underrated. If there's one takeaway, it's this: don't be full of crap, and humility can help.
