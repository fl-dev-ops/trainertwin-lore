Building trust in sales goes beyond product knowledge; it hinges on genuine curiosity about the other person.

A Harvard study on speed dating revealed a fascinating parallel: successful connections, analogous to sales conversions, were linked to asking more questions. Participants who secured a second date averaged nine questions in a four-minute interaction, significantly more than those who didn't.

The key insight? Prioritising inquiries about the other individual, rather than focusing on oneself, fosters connection and signals authentic interest. This principle holds true in professional selling: asking thoughtful questions about your prospect's needs and challenges is paramount.
