In working with sales leaders and their teams, I often observe sales people (and sometimes sales leaders 🤦‍♂️) trying to be someone who they are not. And sometimes when doing this, it looks like they are trying too hard, which only reduces their credibility in the eyes of their customer. 

My philosophy, especially over the last 10 years, has been to work really hard on who you are, do not pretend to be someone who you are not, and certainly do not play the comparison game. Because at the end of the day, sales is a human game, and people want to do business with people who they know, they like, they trust and who they remember. And Andy Reid is certainly the model of excellence around that philosophy.
