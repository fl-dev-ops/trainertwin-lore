***Exceptional Sales Leadership Doesn’t Come From Staring At The Screen!!***

I used to think great sales leadership meant having every number, every metric, every dashboard at my fingertips.

But the more time I spent behind the screen, the more disconnected I became from what actually drives performance; people.

A number of years ago, I was leading a high-performing sales team.

We had dashboards that glowed green, meetings that ran on time, and processes that looked perfect on paper.

Yet something felt off.

Energy was low. Conversations were surface-level.

Reps were doing their jobs… but not owning them.

One day, out of frustration, I shut my laptop, grabbed my coffee, and just started walking the floor.

I joined a few calls. I listened. I coached. I laughed with the team.

By the end of that day, I felt something I hadn’t in months-connection.

That’s when it hit me:
Exceptional sales leadership doesn’t happen behind a desk. It happens in the trenches.

Here’s what I learned, and what I now coach every sales leader to remember:

1️⃣ Lead from the front.

Get out there with your team. See what they see. Feel what they feel. The best leaders are present, not distant.

2️⃣ Coach in real time.

Don’t wait for the quarterly review. The most powerful coaching happens in the moment, right after the call, right after the objection.

3️⃣ Raise the standard by example.

If you want accountability, model it. If you want energy, bring it. Your team mirrors what you project.

4️⃣ Mentor beyond the metrics.

Numbers show results, but not reasons. Spend time understanding what’s driving your people, not just their performance.

5️⃣ Be visible, even when it’s uncomfortable.

Leadership isn’t always polished. It’s showing up, having tough conversations, and being there when your team needs you most.

Sales teams don’t remember your dashboards, they remember your presence.

✅  So shut the laptop.
✅  Walk the floor.
✅  Coach. Listen. Lead.

Because sales leadership isn’t something you say, it’s something your team feels.

Here’s to a massive week 👊
