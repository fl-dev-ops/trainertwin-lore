When it comes to chasing 'success', especially in sales, the vast majority focus their attention on specific actions that they think and believe will enable them to make progress. 

Sales Leaders also encourage specific behaviours, however, the vast majority do not look beyond the surface of these actions. Often the real change sits beneath the behaviour, in the thought patterns and the mindset that ultimately drives behaviour. This is why 2 sales people in the same team, with the same opportunities, can deliver vastly different results, even though from the outside, they are both taking the same action.

I recently had Noah St John back on The Exceptional Sales Leader Podcast where he spoke in detail about the 'head trash' that many people have without consciously realising it, and he started to unpack the opportunities that can be capitalised on when the head trash is removed.
