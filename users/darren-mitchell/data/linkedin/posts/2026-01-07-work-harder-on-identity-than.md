***Work Harder on Identity Than Your Goals***

Most sales leaders are obsessed with goals.

The best ones are obsessed with identity.

Here’s the uncomfortable truth for frontline sales managers:

Your results will never consistently exceed how you see yourself.
You don’t rise to the level of your targets.
You fall to the level of your self-image.

If you see yourself as:

➤ A firefighter → you’ll manage chaos
➤ A deal rescuer → you’ll stay stuck in late-stage saves
➤ A numbers chaser → you’ll miss behaviour change

But if you see yourself as:

➤ A standards setter
➤ A talent multiplier
➤ A builder of adults, not dependents

Your team will rise to meet that identity.

Why identity beats goals:
Goals are external.
Identity is internal.

Goals rely on motivation.
Identity drives behaviour even when motivation is gone.

That’s why two managers with the same target often achieve wildly different outcomes.

How to uncover (and upgrade) your leadership identity:

Try this: no fluff, no posters on the wall:

1️⃣ Audit your calendar
Your real identity shows up in how you spend time, not what you say matters.

2️⃣ Finish this sentence honestly:
“I am the kind of sales leader who always ________.”
(If the answer stings, good.)

3️⃣ Ask your top sales rep one brutal question:
“What do I tolerate that’s holding the team back?”

4️⃣ Define your “non-negotiables”
Not goals. Behaviours. Standards you protect even when the number is at risk.

5️⃣ Act like the leader you claim to be, before results show up
Identity is proven in advance, not after the win.

Here’s the part most sales managers miss:

Your team will never outperform the way you see yourself.

Change the identity.
The behaviours follow.
The results catch up.

So let me challenge you 👇

Are you working harder on your targets… or on the sales leader you’re becoming?
