It may be a very well worn cliché, however, in sales, people still like to buy from people who they know, who they like, who they trust, and who they remember.

Being memorable, trustworthy and likeable is not a direct reflection of the sales methodology or the sales script that is being used. It is about building a genuine connection with a buyer, deeply understanding their challenge they wish to solve, and creating an opportunity for them to review a possible solution to that challenge.

This is about being real. I often say that the sale does not start until after the sale is made. How many sales people move on to the next sale once a sale is made? Too many. They neglect to be there for the entire buyer's journey, and hence, miss opportunity. What does that say to the buyer? 🤷‍♂️

Be real, be authentic, be you. This is exactly the model that Chelsea Olsen has used in some of the highest pressure sales environments. She has doubled down on being genuine and authentic, and through the process, become a sustainably successful, exceptional sales leader.
