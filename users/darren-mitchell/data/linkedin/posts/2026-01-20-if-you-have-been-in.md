If you have been in sales for a while, you have probably experienced the dreaded "CRM" conversation, where your sales director or manager has been annoying you like a pesky mosquito on a humid night to update the customer opportunity, or insert customer contacts. My experience is that this has often been a command and control approach, and rarely works.

The truth is, is that in 2026, the CRM can become a secret weapon, as long as sales leaders can help change the narrative and utilise the CRM as an enabler and multiplier, not as a governance stick to measure compliance.

As a sales leader, are you leveraging your CRM to its fullest advantage, so it can become that secret weapon? If not, why not?
