***From Zero to Seven Figures – The Art of Reinvention with Tanny Diep***

In this episode of The Exceptional Sales Leader Podcast, Tanny Diep shares her inspiring journey from a corporate sales background to becoming a leading figure in the beauty industry in Los Angeles. The episode explores her unconventional career path, including stints in fashion design and yoga, before discovering her passion for eyebrow microblading. 

Tanny candidly shares the challenges and rewards of reinvention and the pivotal moments that guided her toward building a successful beauty brand, Sway Brows. Her story is a testament to perseverance and resilience, shedding light on the power of creativity and service. Essential themes include navigating immigrant expectations, harnessing sales and marketing prowess, and the transformative impact of finding one’s purpose. 

The conversation highlights Tanny’s dedication to uplifting clients through beauty services, especially those dealing with low self-esteem due to hair loss or medical conditions. Her emphasis on gratitude, continuous self-improvement, and serving others solidify her business’s foundation and personal success.
