Many sales leaders are promoted to a leadership role based on their excellence as an individual contributor.  The business looks at their results and believes that by promoting them to a sales leader, these results can now be multiplied across the team.

Unfortunately this rarely happens, because the transition from an individual contributor to sales leader is not easy, and it takes a different mentality and skill set to successfully transition to sales leadership. Hence why we have some incredible sales people who never quite make the grade in sales leadership.

That's not to say that a successful transition is not possible - it is, as long as the sales leader develops specific characteristics that will enable them to persist through the challenges and obstacles. It also helps to have a coach or mentor by their side to help guide them and keep them on track.

Are you committed to becoming an exceptional sales leader? Here are some key characteristics that will help you.
