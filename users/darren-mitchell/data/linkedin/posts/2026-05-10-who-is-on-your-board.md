***Who is on Your Board of Advisors?***

Who is on your Board of Advisors?

And no, I don’t mean your manager, your HR partner, or the people who tell you what you want to hear.

➤ I mean the people who challenge your thinking.
➤ Push back on your blind spots.
➤ Call out your excuses.
➤ Help you see around corners before your competitors do.

The best frontline Sales Leaders never lead alone.

Yet too many leaders operate in isolation, making high-stakes decisions with no trusted circle to sharpen their thinking.

That’s dangerous.

Because the gap between average and exceptional sales leadership is rarely effort.

It’s perspective.

Exceptional leaders intentionally surround themselves with advisors:
✅ Mentors who’ve been where they want to go
✅ Coaches who elevate their leadership capability
✅ Peers who act as honest sounding boards
✅ Operators who challenge assumptions and strategy

While others are relying on instinct…
The best leaders are pressure-testing decisions before they hit the field.

Here’s the uncomfortable truth:

If nobody is challenging your leadership, you’re probably plateauing.

Your competitors are evolving.
Are you?

So I’ll ask again:
Who is on your Board of Advisors?

And more importantly…
Who have you given permission to tell you the truth?

👇 Curious to hear from other Sales Leaders:
Who has had the biggest impact on your growth as a leader?
