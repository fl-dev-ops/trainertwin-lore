111 episodes. One shared mission.

In 2025, The Exceptional Sales Leader Podcast released 111 episodes, and I’m genuinely humbled by what that represents.

111 conversations with outstanding sales leaders, experts, and entrepreneurs.

111 different perspectives.

One common objective.

A deep, unwavering dedication to:

✅ Helping salespeople and sales leaders improve
✅ Lifting the reputation of sales as a profession
✅Adding real value to the marketplace

Every guest brought generosity, honesty, and lessons learned the hard way. The quality of insight shared across these episodes has been nothing short of inspiring.

I can’t tag everyone (LinkedIn would break 😅), but please know this:

I am truly grateful to every single person who said yes, showed up, and shared their story so others could learn from it.

Sales gets better when leaders are willing to give back, and these 111 episodes are proof of that belief.

Here’s to the conversations that challenge us, the stories that sharpen us, and the people who continue to raise the bar for our profession.

Thank you to those who have plugged into the podcast and continue to provide great feedback on the impact it continues to have on your approach to sales and sales leadership. We are approaching 850 episodes and only getting started.

Very best wishes for the festive season and may 2026 be an exceptional year for you!!
