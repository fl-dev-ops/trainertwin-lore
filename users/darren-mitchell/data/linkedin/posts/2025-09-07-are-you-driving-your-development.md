***Are You Driving Your Development or Outsourcing It?***

If you’re waiting for your company to develop you, you’re already behind.

Exceptional sales leaders don’t wait for permission to grow, they take full responsibility for their own development. Period.

I’ve seen it play out too many times.

One Sales Leader steps into the role and immediately starts coasting. They assume the title itself makes them ready. They lean on their experience as a top performer individual contributor. They wait for HR to roll out a leadership workshop or their CRO to send them to a training.

Meanwhile, another Sales Leader approaches the role differently. They know the leap from great seller to great leader isn’t automatic. 

✅ So they take ownership. 
✅ They hire a coach. 
✅ They devour books on leadership. 
✅ They plug into leadership podcasts. 
✅ They become a voracious learner. 
✅ They surround themselves with mentors who push them. 
✅ They seek feedback that stings. And then they use it.

Fast-forward 12 months:

❌ The first leader is frustrated, stuck managing the same problems, with a disengaged team and plateauing results.

✅ The second is thriving. Their team is performing, their influence is growing, and they’re already being tapped for bigger opportunities.

The difference? Mindset and accountability.

The exceptional ones refuse to outsource their growth. They know that if they don’t actively sharpen their leadership, they’ll be left behind. And in sales, that lag is costly, not only for them, but also for their team, and the business.

Your team can only grow to the level that you do. This is the Law of the Lid.

So ask yourself: are you truly leading your own development ? Or waiting for someone else to?  It is a question that must be seriously considered.
