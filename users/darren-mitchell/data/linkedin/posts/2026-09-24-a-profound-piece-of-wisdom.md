A profound piece of wisdom from "The Little Prince" offers a powerful perspective on leadership and motivation.

Instead of dictating tasks, the advice is to "teach them to yearn for the vast and endless sea." This approach, attributed to Antoine de Saint-Exupéry, suggests that inspiring a shared vision and deep desire is far more effective than mere instruction.

When individuals truly yearn for the goal, they naturally seek out the necessary resources, collaborate more effectively, and innovate to achieve the best possible outcome. It's about fostering intrinsic motivation and a compelling vision that drives action.
