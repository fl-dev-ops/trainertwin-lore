AI won’t replace your sellers. 

But it will expose the ones who refuse to evolve.

I recently spoke with Usman Sheikh on The Exceptional Sales Leader Podcast about the real impact of AI on sales teams.

Here’s the truth:

➤ AI gives you more productivity.
➤ More firepower.
➤ Better intelligence.
➤ Higher-quality leads.

It helps your team engage smarter and more strategically.

But it doesn’t answer the most important question:

What are you going to sell and how are you going to sell it?

That’s where leadership comes in.

When you remove the mundane, the endless research, jumping between Salesforce, Zoom, outreach tools, intent data platforms, you free your sellers to do the work they actually love:

✅ Talking to clients
✅ Building relationships
✅ Solving problems
✅ Closing deals

AI should elevate your team’s thinking, not replace it.

The leaders who win will use AI to eliminate low-value tasks and demand higher-value conversations.

Your sellers don’t need more dashboards.
They need more meaningful selling time.

Are you using AI to increase activity… or to increase impact?
