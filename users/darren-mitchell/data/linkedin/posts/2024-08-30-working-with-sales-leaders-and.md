Working with sales leaders and their teams, I continue to be staggered at how many of them are operating under the mindset or expectation that 'failure' is to be avoided. The expectations placed upon them by their senior leaders are so big that these sales teams will almost do anything to ensure that they are 'winning'.

The reality is that we will be faced with challenges, obstacles, rejection, and sometimes, hardship. It is what we do with those, what lessons we take from those, and then what decisions we make, that will determine what happens next.

As a sales leader it is incredibly important to redefine 'failure', not just for ourselves, but also for our team, and especially for our senior leaders. If we are able to focus on progress over perfection, we can start to identify the lessons in the failures and move closer to the success we are seeking.
