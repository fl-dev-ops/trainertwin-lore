88% of sales professionals agree on one thing: Executive relationships must remain human.

In a world rushing to automate everything, that number is surprisingly high.

On The Exceptional Sales Leader Podcast, John Golden shared a powerful insight from recent research:
While AI is transforming sales, leaders are becoming clearer about where it belongs and where it doesn’t.

AI is incredible at:
➤ Data analysis
➤ Research
➤ Process efficiency
➤ Workflow support

But when it comes to executive relationships in B2B selling, the consensus is clear.

➤ Trust.
➤ Credibility.
➤ Strategic conversations.

These are still human-to-human moments.

The best sales leaders aren’t asking, “How do we automate everything?”
They’re asking, “Where does AI amplify humans and where must humans lead?”

In modern sales, technology should support relationships, not replace them.

How are you balancing AI and human connection in your sales strategy right now?
