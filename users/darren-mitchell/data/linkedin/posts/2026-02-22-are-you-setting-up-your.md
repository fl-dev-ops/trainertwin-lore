***Are You Setting Up Your Sales Leaders To Fail?***

Your best seller will fail as a sales leader.

Not because they lack talent.
But because you promoted them without a transition plan.

In enterprise B2B, the jump from individual contributor to sales leader is one of the most underestimated career shifts in our industry.

Top reps are rewarded for:
➤ Closing deals
➤ Owning relationships
➤ Hitting personal targets

Sales leaders are measured on:
➤ Building repeatable systems
➤ Coaching underperformance
➤ Forecast accuracy
➤ Developing other people’s success

That’s not a promotion.
That’s a completely different job.

And yet many Sales Directors treat it like a title change and a bigger number.

Here’s the hard truth:
If your new leaders are overwhelmed, stuck in the weeds, or still acting like super reps… that’s not their failure.
It’s a leadership design failure.

Enterprise sales is too complex to “learn on the job.”

You need a structured transition plan that includes:
✅ Clear role redefinition
✅ Coaching cadence training
✅ Pipeline inspection vs. pipeline takeover
✅ Executive presence development
✅ Peer support systems
✅ Defined 30-60-90 day expectations

Because when you don’t design the transition, they default to what made them successful before.

And that costs you:
➤ Forecast reliability
➤ Team morale
➤ Rep development
➤ Long-term revenue stability

Sales Directors, promoting top performers is easy.
Building enterprise sales leaders is hard.

But that’s the job.

If you’re honest, how many of your current frontline leaders were truly set up to succeed… and how many were told to “figure it out”?

Let’s have the uncomfortable conversation. 👇
