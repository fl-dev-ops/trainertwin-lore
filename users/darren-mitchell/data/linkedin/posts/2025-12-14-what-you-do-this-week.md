***What You Do This Week Can Have a Big Impact on Q1***

Q1 2026 will be won this week.
Not in January.
Not after the kickoff meeting.
Now.

The highest-performing sales teams don’t wait for the year to start before they start acting.

Here’s what to do this week that will still be paying off in Q1 2026:

➤ Audit reality, not forecasts.
Which deals should have closed this year but didn’t, and why? The patterns you uncover now are the coaching moments that change next year’s number.

➤ Upgrade one capability, not ten.
Pick the single skill that limits your team most (qualification, pricing confidence, deal control) and commit to raising the bar before year-end.

➤ Strengthen your pipeline muscle.
Not by asking for “more activity,” but by fixing how opportunities enter the funnel. Better entry = better exits.

➤ Coach your managers, not just your reps.
Q1 performance is a lagging indicator of today’s leadership habits. What managers inspect, reinforce, and challenge now defines January outcomes.

➤ Make one decision competitors are avoiding.
The uncomfortable call. The hard reset. The investment others are postponing. That’s where separation happens.

The teams that dominate Q1 don’t have better intentions. They take earlier action.

Momentum is built before motivation arrives.

👉 What is one thing you can do this week that your competitors are not?
