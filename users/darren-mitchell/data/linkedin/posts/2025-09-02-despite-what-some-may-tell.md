Despite what some may tell you, and many may show you, leadership is not about 'fire & brimstone', pushing the team to breaking point, and remaining disassociated from the team.

Effective leaders in today's complex and increasingly uncertain world are more often than not, humble, authentic, insightful, and generous. This article is a great summary of a phenomenal conversation I enjoyed recently with Joe Davis, on The Exceptional Sales Leader Podcast. Joe is the author of "The Generous Leader", and former Managing Director of Boston Consulting Group. To listen to the full episode, please click the links in the article.
