***Don’t Wait To Feel Confident***

Despite sales people possessing incredible talent and reporting to a Sales Leader who believes in them, it is staggering to observe the number of sales people who lack confidence. 

A lack of confidence - 
❓ To identify tangible opportunities.
❓ To reach out to Senior Executives within their portfolio.
❓ To make new connections in a target Account.
❓ To challenge the status quo and think outside the square.
❓ To take action outside of their comfort zone.
❓ To ask the difficult questions of their customers, their Sales Leader and the organisation.

Too many of them wait to feel confident. They remain in a comfort zone and are reluctant to take risks, fearing the consequences of mistakes. This often results in stagnation and a further reduction in confidence.

A great sales leader will not allow this to happen. They know that the only way to develop confidence is to take affirmative action. They -

✅  Demand their sales execs get moving. Imperfect action is much better than trying to come up with the perfect plan.
✅  Expect mistakes and don’t criticise. They will provide constant feedback.
✅  Set the example through not waiting to feel confident themselves.
Reward progress rather than reward the outcome. Progress over perfection.

This week, if you have sales people in your team who have been lacking confidence, strongly encourage them to remove the shackles, take a chance, and take affirmative action, without any attachment to the outcome.

When you do, you may just be surprised with the outcome. Here’s to a highly productive week 👊
