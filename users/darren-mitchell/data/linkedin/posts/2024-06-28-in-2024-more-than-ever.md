In 2024, more than ever, sales leaders and their teams must adopt a more customer centric, problem centric approach. 

It is no longer about the quality of your product or service, because quite frankly customers are not interested in the product or service. 

What they are interested in is a solution to a problem that they know they have, or discover they have, after having a conversation with you. It is the sales person who is able to have a genuine, authentic conversation with a prospective customer, and provide them with the opportunity to make an educated buying decision, who is the sales person who will deliver results.

I was joined recently on The Exceptional Sales Leader Podcast by Coach Dan Gordon, a seasoned sales and marketing professional, and we discussed the importance of authentic selling, of building and sometimes breaking rapport in the sales engagement, and how this can lead to being a much better & more credible sales professional. This article is a great summary of the discussion - to listen to the episode, please click the links in the article.
