Having now recorded over 800 Podcasts and interviewed over 650 business people, entrepreneurs, sales leaders, and all round exceptional people from all over the world, I finally have a deeper understanding and appreciation of this dance between passion, strengths and capability.

We often hear 'gurus' talk about 'find your passion and you'll never work a day in your life' - sounds great in theory and on a social media post, but if you are really passionate about something & yet clearly lack the capability and it is not yet a strength, the road can be incredibly long and frustrating.

I'm learning more and more about the importance of self awareness, self evaluation and the power of assessments to identify strength, competency and capability zones. Once we can identify a strength, we can now start to identify the best swimming lane to follow, and funnily enough, through consistent & sustainable discretionary effort, also develop a passion.
