***What Price Are You Willing To Pay?***

In business, it is easy to look at others who are successful, and either feel a level of envy or in some cases, dismiss them as being ‘lucky’. In doing this, there is a very important lesson that is missed - a lesson in persistence, resourcefulness and constant improvement.

I recall listening to a podcast earlier this year where John Maxwell was sharing a story of a conversation he had with a young speaker, after John had delivered a keynote address on leadership. The young speaker shared with John that he was committed to becoming an influential speaker like John, and receiving the accolades that came with it. John, in his authentic, laid back way encouraged the young speaker and then asked him ‘are you prepared to do what I have done, to be able to do what I do?’ 

To be successful takes work, it takes persistence, it takes resourcefulness and it takes time. There is no such thing as an overnight success. There are countless examples of people, teams and organisations who have taken years to become an overnight success.

And so it is in sales - whilst we would love to be able to drive exceptional sales results immediately, the reality is that it will take time, it will require us and our teams to learn to deal with failure & rejection and it will require a price to be paid. The question is ‘Am I prepared to pay the price and is my team prepared to pay the price for success?’

Understand that the price is often different for different people, and the price can change over time. This is why persistence and patience are critical elements of the journey.

As we begin the brand new week, reflect on whether you and your team are currently paying the price required to become exceptional, and remember, it is much more important to be making progress than seeking to be perfect.

Here’s to a massive week!! 👊
