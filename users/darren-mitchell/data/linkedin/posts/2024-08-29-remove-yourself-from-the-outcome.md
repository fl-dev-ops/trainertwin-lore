***Remove Yourself From The Outcome***

In the sales game, the vast majority measure success by the numbers. Either you hit the sales target or you don’t and sales leaders can be very laser focused on the scoreboard and lead according to what the numbers are saying. But this does not always tell the full story.

It has often been said that sales is a numbers game and interestingly, sales leaders who focus entirely on the numbers tend to lead teams which achieve outcomes within plus or minus 10% of the sales target. Each conversation and each review is based on the year to date or month to date position against the target. 

Exceptional sales leaders are fully aware of what success looks like and what the target is. However, they lead in a completely different way. Instead of every conversation being about the month to date or year to date position, they have the ability to remove themselves from the outcome and instead focus their attention on fundamental behaviours and progress against these.

They also focus more of their attention on quality behaviours rather than sheer quantity of actions. They are crystal clear on the specific actions which will move the customer relationship forward, and provide clear, specific and constructive feedback on these actions. They know that mastering the key identified behaviours will create the opportunity for the numbers to be taken care of.

As a sales leader, where are you focusing your attention right now? Are you focused on the numbers and the current achievement levels against the numbers, or have you removed yourself from the outcome, allowing your team to build capabilities around the behaviours and actions that will drive the results?

The challenge this week is to remove yourself from the outcome. The target will always be there, but notice what happens when you take your focus off the outcome and instead focus your attention on making progress. You may be surprised.

Here’s to a massive week 👊
