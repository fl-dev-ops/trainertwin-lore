***Your Leadership Goal Should Be To Become Redundant***

How do you feel when you read the title of this post? Is there a level of anxiety which bubbles to the surface, or does this create some excitement?

It is fascinating the varied responses I get from leaders when I ask them what they consider to be the ultimate objective of an exceptional sales leader. Very few actually respond with ‘to become redundant’ and yet that is exactly what exceptional sales leaders’ ultimate objective is.

Unfortunately the vast majority of sales leaders get stuck on the word ‘redundant’ and quickly assume a negative connotation-in fact, I’ve had quite a few sales leaders share with me that they simply cannot afford to lose their job. And this often shapes their thinking and hence their behaviour, with the impact of this playing out in the team’s results. What they fail to understand is that becoming redundant has nothing to do with losing their job!

Here’s what exceptional sales leaders focus on - identifying potential leaders within the team, and then working their butts off to develop, inspire and to challenge them to step up, to take on more responsibility, so that, at some point in the future, they are able to carry the baton of leadership forward, and the team’s future is secured. Through the process, the sales leader develops a long leadership shadow- a legacy.

How do they do this? By consistently focusing on these key practices-

✅  Identifying likely successors within the team, who possess & demonstrate the values, principles & behaviours of exceptional performance.

✅  Providing opportunities for people to take on more responsibility, and encouraging them to demonstrate initiative.

✅  As a sales leader, stepping back and creating the space for others to step up.

✅  Realising that exceptional sales leadership is not about the leader - it is all about the team.

✅  Creating an environment where independent thinking is encouraged and feedback is a daily discipline, in all directions.

When sales leaders focus their attention on these key practices, momentum begins to build, to the point where the team becomes unstoppable. The end result? The leader is no longer required in that team - they are redundant. However there is always an opportunity in front of them because other teams are craving for this type of leadership - and the cycle continues.

As an exceptional sales leader, how quickly can you become redundant? Something to think about. 🧐
