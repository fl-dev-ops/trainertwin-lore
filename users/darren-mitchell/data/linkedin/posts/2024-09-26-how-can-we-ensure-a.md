How can we ensure a win-win outcome in negotiations? It's all about putting skin in the game and understanding each other's values. 🤝💡

Many people in business have the intention of creating win-win outcomes in negotiations with customers, whilst others see it as a competition to win. If you are working with customers long term, we must create an environment that can facilitate a win-win. The key is to define and understand what that looks like.
