If you have been in sales for a while, you would understand that sales is about connection, as people love to buy from those they know, like, trust and remember. 

Most sales people and sales leaders will agree with that and will openly talk about it, and yet, many of them do not focus their attention on building genuine trust based relationships where their customers know them, like them, trust them and remember them. Why? Because the sales person and sales leader is often so busy talking about their product, service or solution.

I enjoyed a wonderful conversation recently with Katie Nelson on The Exceptional Sales Leader Podcast, and here is a short snippet. We talked a lot about building genuine relationships in sales and how these relationships can create massive returns.
