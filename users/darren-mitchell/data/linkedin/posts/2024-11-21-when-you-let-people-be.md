When you let people be themselves, magic happens. 🌟

It is amazing how many sales leaders I observe who seek to create a team of "mini-me's" only to find that it often creates a level of dysfunction rather than high performance. Exceptional sales leaders are very specific on the characteristics they are looking for in their high performing team, but they encourage and enable the individual sales professionals to be themselves, and to create their own style.
