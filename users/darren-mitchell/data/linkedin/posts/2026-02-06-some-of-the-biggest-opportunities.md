Some of the biggest opportunities arrive without warning.

Gene McNaughton shared this story with me on The Exceptional Sales Leader Podcast. After 12 years in a comfortable role, an unexpected recruiter call asked a simple question: How would you like to move to California?

That one decision, backed by years of self-investment and trust, led Gene to work with Tony Robbins. Within two years, his teams broke every sales record in a 29-year history, across all divisions.

My biggest takeaway from Gene’s story?

Growth doesn’t come from waiting until you feel ready.
It comes from backing yourself when the moment shows up.

If that opportunity knocked today… would you trust yourself enough to answer?
