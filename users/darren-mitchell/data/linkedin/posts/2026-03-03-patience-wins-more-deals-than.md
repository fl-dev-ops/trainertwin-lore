Patience wins more deals than pressure ever will.

And most sales leaders learn that the hard way.

On a recent episode of The Exceptional Sales Leader Podcast, Gary Daly ✔ shared a powerful analogy:

Walking into cold water.

You don’t fight it.
You don’t argue with it.
You surrender to it.

That’s patience.

Early in our sales careers, every deal feels like oxygen.
We obsess. We chase. We attach our identity to the outcome.

But maturity in sales changes that.

You prepare relentlessly.
You qualify properly.
You do the work.

And then?

You let it be.

It echoes the “Let Them” mindset popularised by Mel Robbins and the “Let go and let God” philosophy often discussed by Bob Proctor.

Control the inputs.
Release the outcome.

The best sales leaders don’t cling to deals.
They create the conditions and trust the process.

Because desperation repels.
Calm conviction attracts.

How do you personally detach from outcomes while still driving performance?
