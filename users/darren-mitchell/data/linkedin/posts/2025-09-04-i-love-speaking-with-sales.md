I love speaking with sales leaders and their teams and asking the question "Who loves to be sold to?". The vast majority say 'no', however, almost all of them say they love to buy. And when they buy, not only do they have more control over their actions (or they think they do 😉), they will often respond to the way the person they are interacting with makes them feel.

Whether you are in sales or not, you are still in sales, and one of the keys to selling is the ability to sell yourself. Because people prefer to do business with those who they know, they like, they trust & they remember.
