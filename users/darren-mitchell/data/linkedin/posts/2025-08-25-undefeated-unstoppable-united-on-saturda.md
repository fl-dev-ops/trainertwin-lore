🏆 Undefeated. Unstoppable. United.🏆

On Saturday, I had the privilege of watching something special unfold.

Our local Senior Women’s AFL team, Gisborne-Kyneton United, a group of players who only came together two weeks before the season started - won the Premiership.

And they did it going through the season undefeated.

As Team Manager, I experienced first-hand the journey.

A group of women, many of whom didn’t even know each other at the start of the season, came together with different personalities, different backgrounds, and different experiences.

What made the difference?

💡 They leaned into their differences instead of fighting them.
💡 They encouraged each other through challenges.
💡 They built trust quickly and found strength in a shared goal.

The result? A team that achieved greatness together.

The parallels to sales leadership are powerful:

➤ Success isn’t about identical skills, but about harnessing diverse strengths.
➤ Shared purpose accelerates cohesion.
➤ With trust and encouragement, teams can do what looks impossible.

This Premiership win wasn’t just about football.

It was a reminder of what’s possible when a team truly commits to each other and the goal ahead.

🙌 If a group of near-strangers can come together and achieve this in sport, imagine what’s possible in our sales teams and businesses when we embrace the same mindset.

👉 What’s one lesson from sport you’ve seen transform a business team?
