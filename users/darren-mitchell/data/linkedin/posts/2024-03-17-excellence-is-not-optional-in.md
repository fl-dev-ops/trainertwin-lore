***Excellence Is Not Optional***

In working with sales leaders and their teams for many years now, I’m staggered at the number of individuals, whether it be sales people, or in some cases, sales leaders, who search for the easy road - the shortcut. Many of them expect instant results and will often only apply ‘token efforts’ to achieve results. And when the ideal results are not forthcoming, they will look to external forces to justify the outcome.

No matter what industry you look at, or what role within that industry, both ‘success’ and ‘failure’ leaves clues. And it is the astute individual who will be a student, who will observe and ask curious based questions, who will search for and analyse those clues, and who will tap into the one force which separates exceptional performers from everyone else - excellence.

✅ Excellence is not optional.

✅ Excellence is mandatory for exceptional performance.

✅ Excellence requires high standards, both for yourself and for others.

✅ Excellence requires consistency in the application of those standards.

✅ Excellence involves doing what is right, 100% of the time, irrespective of how you feel.

✅ Excellence involves being the example you would like to see play out in your team.

✅ Excellence involves being uncompromising on the expectations you have for yourself and for others. It is about driving accountability.

✅ Excellence is providing feedback constantly, irrespective of how the feedback may be received.

✅ Excellence is having a very specific and optimistic intention for who you want to be and what you would like your team to be known for.

✅ Excellence is doing what is required, no matter how small, and no matter who is watching, to the highest expectations.

✅ Excellence is leaving every single person you interact with, with an impression of increase. They feel much better about themselves after having experienced an interaction with you.

✅ Excellence is continuing to move forward, continuing to take affirmative action, and never resting until the result is locked in.

✅ Excellence is what makes exceptional people, exceptional.

Are you a Model of Excellence? Do you have and maintain uncompromising standards of excellence in everything you do? Are you a Model of Consistency?

If you are committed to becoming an Exceptional Sales Leader, then Excellence is not optional - it is mandatory and will become part of your leadership DNA. And when that is locked in, just watch what happens to the behaviours, engagement and results in your sales team.

Here’s to a massive week 👊
