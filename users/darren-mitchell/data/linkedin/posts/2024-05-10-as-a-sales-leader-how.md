As a sales leader, how often have you sat down and analysed the environment that has been created for your sales team? If you are like a large number of sales leaders, the true answer to that question is 'not often'. Why? Because the vast majority of sales leaders are focused on the mechanics of sales, the number of calls being made, the number of appointments being booked, the volume of sales being created. 

Whilst these things are important to the 'success' of the sales team, even more critical is the environment that is created for these mechanics to function.

How would you describe the environment that you currently operate in? Is it functional, empowering and set up for sustained high performance? Would love your thoughts and experiences - please comment below. 👇🙏
