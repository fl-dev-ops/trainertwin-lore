Despite what many of the 'gurus' may prophesise, developing sustainable success takes work, it takes facing obstacles and challenges, working through problems, and ultimately staying in the game. 

History is littered with people and businesses that were an "overnight success", even though in the vast majority of cases, this overnight success came after years of effort, often away from the spotlight.

I love the quote from Les Brown - "If life knocks you down, try to land on your back. Because if you can look up, you can get up. And if you get up, you can stand up. And if you stand up, you can fight for your dream once again" 

I enjoyed a powerful conversation recently with DJ Carroll on The Exceptional Sales Leader Podcast where he shared a number of stories and experiences that have helped shape in into the person and business owner he is today.
