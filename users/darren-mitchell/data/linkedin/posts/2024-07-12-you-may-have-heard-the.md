You may have heard the phrase, 'Where you focus goes, your energy flows'. It is so true, especially in leadership. As a sales leader, are you focusing on the opportunities to break into new markets, to increase your existing market share, identifying and edifying the talent of your team?

Or are you focused on the challenges, the limitations, the lack of opportunities?

Either way, you will find evidence to support what you are focused on. Hence, doesn't it make sense to be very clear and intentional on the areas to focus your attention? Would love your thoughts. 👇
