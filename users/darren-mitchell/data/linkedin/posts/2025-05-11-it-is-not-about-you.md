***It Is Not About You!!***

I have been fortunate to have been involved in community sport for a number of years. It is an absolute privilege to watch young people develop their skill set in a team environment, as well as develop critical life skills such as teamwork, resilience and effective communication skills. A key component to this is having great coaches and mentors overseeing the team. When that is not in place though, it stifles growth, opportunity and engagement.

A number of years ago, I learned a very valuable lesson on the importance of having a good coach and mentor in place. The coach appointed to the team had terrific credentials and experience, and in the first few weeks, appeared to have the team engaged and energised. However, little by little, cracks started to appear. Players were not as forthcoming in their contribution to the team, the communication between players became less and less, and little cliques had started to form. What I also noticed was that the coach appeared to have some ‘favourites’ and rather than take responsibility for the culture they had created, they started to blame the club for the lack of support. Unfortunately the coach made it all about themselves.

The end result? The team’s performance began to drop and at the end of the season, players left.

This can also happen in business - when a sales leader makes it all about themselves and not about the team, fractures in culture appear, and results begin to plummet.

Great coaches of sporting teams know it is all about the team, and never about themselves. They create a culture where their players are measured on and encouraged to be better teammates, to help others in the team to get better. It is selflessness versus selfishness. 

So it is in sales - whilst each salesperson carries an individual sales target, the exceptional sales leader encourages selflessness over selfishness, and never seeks the limelight. They know it is not about them.

Want to create an exceptional sales culture? Make it all about the team & their contribution to others & not about you.

Why?

Because it is not about you. 😉

Here’s to a massive week in sales 👊
