Are you a sales leader who focuses on results above all else? What if there is a 'toxic' salesperson within the team who constantly delivers the results, but leaves a trail of destruction everywhere they go? 

Do you make allowances for them?
Do you allow them to continue to get away with toxicity, so long as they deliver the results?
Or do you take a stand and remind them of the behaviours, values and standards that are expected, and put them on notice?

This is something that many sales leaders are faced with in their careers. And how they deal with these people can very often be career defining.
