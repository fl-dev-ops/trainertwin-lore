***3 Essential Focus Areas for Exceptional Performance***

As we launch into the first quarter of 2025, many sales leaders and their teams are already being overwhelmed with the expectations being placed upon them, combined with the seemingly never ending ‘to do list’. It seems that every sales leader I speak with, they have different areas of focus, and for many of them, this is causing confusion.

I’m not about to evangelise ‘the secret’ to a fast start to 2025, but there are 3 specific areas that exceptional sales leaders are concentrating on that is enabling them to wrestle back control, build consistency, develop competency and supercharge their confidence.

1. Awareness - more and more sales leaders need to be emotionally intelligent, and a couple of key pillars of emotional intelligence are self awareness and social awareness. Exceptional sales leaders are very self aware, they can recognise & articulate their emotional state and then consciously behave in a manner which will help and not hinder. They can also ‘read the play’ - understand what may be happening in conversations and in interactions with customers and other stakeholders and again behave with volition & purpose.

2. Intention - exceptional sales leaders are crystal clear on their intention for every interaction, every conversation, every email, each and every day. This clarity of intention enables them to be present throughout the day, because they recognise that their greatest gift to others is the gift of their attention. Will they make mistakes? Absolutely. Will they win every deal? No. However, because their intention is clear, and it is based on servitude, they continue to plant seeds that cannot help but turn into a harvest at some point in the future.

3. Action - awareness and intention are great areas to focus on, but nothing happens unless or until someone does something. Exceptional sales leaders are dedicated to action, consistent and targeted action. They clearly understand their ideal customer profile, they understand the problem this customer experiences, and they have the ability to articulate this problem so well that the customer unconsciously (and often consciously) attributes the sales leader as  having a solution to the problem. The end result? Often a sale, and if not, planting the seed for a future sale.

As we begin the brand new week, where is your focus? Are you attempting to keep multiple balls in the air, knowing that a few of them will hit the ground? Or are you laser focused on a vital few areas that will help facilitate a fast start to 2025? I hope it is the latter 🤞

If there is some uncertainty, consider these 3 key areas, awareness, intention & action as they may just be the rocket fuel you and your team needs for a supercharged start to 2025.

Here’s to a massive week. 👊
