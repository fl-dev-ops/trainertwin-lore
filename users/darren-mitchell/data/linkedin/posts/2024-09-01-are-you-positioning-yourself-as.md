***Are You Positioning Yourself As The Oracle?***

Oftentimes when an individual contributor is promoted to the sales leader role, they focus a huge amount of their attention on establishing credibility as quickly as possible. This is expected and encouraged, especially in the first 90 days. ✅

Whilst there are numerous ways of doing this effectively, there is one behaviour that I see play out over and over again, which, for many sales leaders, can create a challenge long term. This behaviour is seeking to be the Oracle. ❌

What do I mean by the Oracle? A person who possesses all the knowledge and wisdom and who seeks to provide the answers to the team. Whilst a new sales leader can become very comfortable having their team come to them for the answers, and in the process, believe that trust is being built, in reality, what is being created is co-dependency.

How do I know this? I experienced it first hand. I believed that through encouraging my team to come to me with problems, challenges and issues, and through me providing the solution or the answer, I would be building trust, and this would lead to high performance. How wrong I was. 🤦‍♂️

Over 90 days, I had conditioned the team to come to me with problems so I could solve them, instead of encouraging them and challenging them to think for themselves.

Thankfully a mentor assisted in re-shaping my thinking, and adjusting away from embedding a co-dependent relationship to one of developing independent thinking. Instead of giving the answers and literally telling the team what to think, I started to challenge their thinking and teaching them how to think. 

The result? The conversations were of a higher quality, and I noticed that the team were incredibly resourceful and in reality, not needing the answers. They started developing solutions. They didn’t need an Oracle. They needed a leader who trusted them, believed in them, challenged them, and encouraged them. ✅

As we begin the brand new week, a question for you to ponder - are you, or have you, positioned yourself as the Oracle? Have you conditioned your team to come to you with problems so you can provide the solution? Whilst this may deliver short term positive results, consider what the long term ramifications may be….

Here’s to a massive week 👊
