***Kevin Davis – Maxed Out Man***

In this engaging episode of The Exceptional Sales Leader Podcast, I enjoy a fascinating conversation with Kevin Davis, an accomplished author and the force behind Maxed Out Man. We delve into the complexities of balancing entrepreneurship and personal life, with Kevin sharing his personal journey of making marriage a priority amidst a demanding career. This conversation sheds light on the pressures of modern executive life and the importance of personal accountability. 

As the conversation progresses, Kevin discusses how a thriving personal life can significantly impact professional success and offers practical advice for entrepreneurs and leaders to optimise their personal relationships. With insightful anecdotes and applicable strategies, Kevin emphasises the importance of prioritising marriage above all, including children. 

His perspective that “all the worst decisions I’ve made have been made under the influence of fear,” encapsulates the importance of fear versus faith in decision-making. The episode is rich with advice on how to realign priorities and underscores the power of strong relationships in fueling career achievements.
