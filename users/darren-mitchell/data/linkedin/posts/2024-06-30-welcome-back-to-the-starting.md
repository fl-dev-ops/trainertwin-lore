***Welcome Back To The Starting Line***

July 1st represents the start of the brand new Financial Year for many companies in Australia, or at least the start of a brand new quarter. And if you have been in sales for a while, you will understand that we are all now back at the starting line.

If you have been fortunate to enjoy a successful Financial Year, congratulations. Hopefully you have the opportunity to review and reflect on the year and celebrate your achievements. If you have finished the year under target, I trust you can also review the year, identify the lessons and themes, and use these as a strong platform to launch into the new Financial Year.

The good news is that July 1 represents the starting line. The not so good news is that July 1 represents the starting line. One of my biggest frustrations with sales organisations is the relentless focus on the next sale, and the next opportunity. Despite bringing forward every possible opportunity to close before June 30th, the expectation is that the sales team must hit the ground running with new sales opportunities come July 1st - which means in many cases, the performance to June 30th almost becomes irrelevant. 

This approach further highlights the concept that you are only as ‘successful’ as your next sale. In fact, I have seen ‘successful’ sales people go from overachieving sales targets for the June 30 year to being placed under a ‘performance improvement plan’ within the first 60 days of the new Financial Year, all based on the numbers. 

Now I fully appreciate the need to continue to focus on opportunity creation and starting the year strongly, but I am more focused on making progress and setting the entire year up to be a successful one. As Sales Leaders, how can we do this?

Through -

✅  Exercising a level of urgency with a corresponding level of patience.

✅  Identifying the wins from the previous year that can be leveraged and duplicated in the new year.

✅  Placing more focus on intentional planning and strategy development, followed by deliberate implementation.

✅  Educating and influencing senior leaders within the organisation in the importance of playing the long game.

✅  Investing in the professional development of the sales team, through sales skills development as well as soft skills development. This includes training, coaching & mentoring.

As a sales leader, what else would you add? 

Welcome to the starting line - the 12 months ahead of us provides a wonderful opportunity to not only consolidate our capabilities, it is literally a blank slate just waiting for us to fill it up.

Here’s to a massive week 1 of the new Financial Year. 👊
