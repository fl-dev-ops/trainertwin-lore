***Abundantly Scarce or Scarcely Abundant?***

It is amazing how many sales leaders and their teams are impacted by the external environment. Just last week I had a couple of sales leaders share with me that the industry they operate in is in a steep decline and that opportunities are few and far between.

When I challenged them on this belief they presented ‘evidence’ to support their case. On the surface it made logical sense, but I also asked them whether there were organisations and individuals within the industry flourishing. They conceded that this was true. 

So what does this mean? These sales leaders were looking at the environment and accepting the evidence as they saw it - the problem was though that they were looking at the environment through the filter of scarcity. In fact, when I dug a little deeper, it became evident that they were abundantly scarce!! 

Their belief was that there was a finite number of prospects to connect with and as a result this is what they were projecting, which meant their team were also operating from a position of scarcity as well. This explained why some of the sales executives were protecting their ‘patch’ of new business opportunities from their peers.

Henry Ford was quoted as saying ‘If you believe you can, or if you believe you can’t, either way, you are going to be right’. And the same is true in sales leadership. As a sales leader, is your natural thinking pattern one of scarcity? Limited numbers of prospects to speak with? Limited market size to attack? Limited time to close deals? If so, you may be operating from a position of abundant scarcity, or scarcely abundant (in other words - limited).

Exceptional sales leaders always operate from a position of abundant thinking (or a growth mindset). They constantly challenge the notion of scarcity, of accepting things as they are, of buying into the idea that the external environment plays an integral part in the success or not of the team. They focus instead on what they can control, the actions they take and the beliefs they project. 

They believe that there is an abundance of customers in their industry that can directly benefit from their solutions, and as a result, continue to have the conversations, with no fixed attachment to the outcome. And in many cases, they close deals that others think are not possible. 🤷‍♂️

As we begin the new week, here is the key message. Instead of being abundantly scarce or scarcely abundant, how about adopting a mindset of abundantly abundant? Take the lid off completely & recognise that the possibilities are endless. Not only will it invigorate you, it will invigorate your team, and just may invigorate your customers as well!!

Here’s to a massive week 👊
