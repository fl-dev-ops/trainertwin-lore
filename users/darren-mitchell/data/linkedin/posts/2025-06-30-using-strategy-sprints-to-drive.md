***Using Strategy Sprints to Drive Exceptional Results with Simone Severino***

In this episode of The Exceptional Sales Leader Podcast, I am thrilled to be joined by Simone Severino, a man who has worked with some of the biggest and most recognised brands on the planet. Widely recognised for his innovative Strategy Sprints method, Simon shares insights on balancing effective sales strategies while maintaining a fun and sustainable approach to business tasks. 

This conversation explores Simon’s unique perspective on creating systems that energise team performance, encouraging consistency and long-term success in sales and marketing. Delve deeper into the mechanics of the Strategy Sprints methodology, which emphasises reducing complexity and fostering strategic clarity. 

Simon discusses the immense value of focusing on a few impactful activities and creating an environment that makes success inevitable. He highlights his process of defining weekly priorities, establishing a positive momentum, and embracing real-time data to enhance performance and drive results. His approach is particularly insightful for organisations seeking to improve engagement and efficiency, reflecting trends in organisational simplification.
