***Mastering Authentic Communication & Trust with Mike Lane-Evans***

When looking at the most influential people on the planet, irrespective of their vocation, one thing they have in common is their ability to communicate a powerful message. 

Many of them appear to do this naturally, however, there is a huge amount of thought and preparation that goes into a powerful and authentic message. 

When it comes to sales, being able to master the art of authentic communication and build trust in the process, can be the difference that makes all the difference. 

In this episode of The Exceptional Sales Leader Podcast, I’m joined by Mike Lane-Evans , Founder of Forefront Coaching Ltd, a man who has built a successful acting career and has now helped over 1000 senior leaders, business people and entrepreneurs get crystal clear on their message, and deliver it with clarity and impact.

Mike shares gold nugget after gold nugget on how to build trust and master the art of authentic communication. This is worth listening to more than once.
