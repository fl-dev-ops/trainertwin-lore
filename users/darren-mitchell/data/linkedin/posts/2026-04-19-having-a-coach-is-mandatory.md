***Having a Coach is Mandatory for Exceptional Sales Leaders***

If you’re a CRO or Head of Sales without a coach, you’re already behind.

Not because you’re underperforming, but because you’re operating unchallenged.

At the top of sales leadership, something subtle happens.

➤ Your access to honest feedback shrinks.
➤ Fewer people question your thinking.
➤ Fewer still push back on your decisions.

And that’s where performance starts to plateau.

The best revenue leaders don’t rely on experience alone.
They actively seek friction.
Not internally, but externally.

Because they understand a simple truth:

Every elite performer in any field has a coach. Without exception.

➤ Sport.
➤ Business.
➤ Military.
➤ Performance arts.

The higher the stakes, the less acceptable it is to operate without one.

Yet in sales leadership, many still treat coaching as optional, or worse, as a company-funded perk.

It’s neither.

It’s a personal responsibility.

The most effective CROs I work with don’t wait for approval.
✅ They invest in themselves.
✅ They create space to be challenged.
✅ They invite scrutiny into their thinking.

Because they know:

You can’t see your own blind spots.
And your team won’t consistently expose them for you.

If no one is challenging how you think, lead, and make decisions, you’re not leading at your full potential.

So I’ll ask directly: who’s coaching you right now?

If you’re ready for a level of challenge most leaders don’t get access to, reach out. I work with a small number of senior sales leaders who take their growth seriously.
