***The Power of Proximity***

Be ruthless about who has access to you.

Because leadership isn’t shaped in isolation.
It’s shaped by the people you allow into your thinking.

Over a century ago, James Allen wrote As A Man Thinketh, highlighting a timeless truth:
👉 We become what we consistently think about.

As Sales Leaders, that truth hits hard.

Your mindset drives your decisions.
Your decisions drive your actions.
And your actions determine your results.

But here’s the part many leaders underestimate 👇
Your environment heavily influences your thinking.

You’ve probably heard the idea that we’re the average of the five people we spend the most time with. While not a perfect rule, there’s enough truth in it to make this question unavoidable:

Is your circle raising your standards—or lowering them?

If you’re always the sharpest person in the room…
If no one challenges your thinking…
If your ideas go untested and unquestioned…

That’s not comfort.
That’s stagnation.

The same applies to your team.

Are they pushing you to stay ahead of the curve?
Are they proactively chasing excellence?
Are they creating results that force you to level up as a leader?

They should be.

Strong leaders don’t surround themselves with “yes people.”
They surround themselves with people who:
✅ Challenge assumptions
✅ Hold standards high
✅ Create constructive tension
✅ Force growth beyond comfort

If you haven’t intentionally assessed who currently surrounds you, now is the time.

Because the right people won’t just support your journey…
They’ll accelerate it.

So here’s the challenge 👊
Who in your world is helping you grow and who might be holding you back?

Here’s to a massive week.

👇 Curious; how intentional are you about the circle you keep as a leader?
