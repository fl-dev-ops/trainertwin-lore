As a leader, what is your attitude towards mistakes? Do you seek to avoid them, and are you teaching your team to avoid them? Or are you embracing mistakes as part of the leadership journey, and learning from them?

When working with leaders, I often ask them 'what is the biggest mistake you have made in the last 2 weeks?' Interestingly, many leaders are reluctant to own up to mistakes, however, the ones who do, find that there is always a lesson to be learned, which is often known as a 'defining moment'.

Mistakes are okay - they are part of our leadership development, as long as we learn from them and continue to improve. We are not defined by our mistakes. We are defined by who we are and what we learn from those mistakes and continue to move forward with ethics, humility and integrity.
