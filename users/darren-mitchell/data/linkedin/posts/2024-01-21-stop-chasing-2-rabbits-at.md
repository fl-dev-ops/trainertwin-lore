***Stop Chasing 2 Rabbits At A Time!!***

Confucius said “The person who chases 2 rabbits, catches neither”. This is so applicable to sales & sales leadership - are you & your team guilty of chasing too many rabbits at once? Perhaps it's pressure from senior executives to deliver the numbers in the short term that can lead to this behaviour. And we can get caught up in the busyness of chasing that we end up burning energy for not much return.

Intuitively we know that focused action & attention on the right customer can deliver results, & yet, we have probably all been guilty of chasing multiple opportunities at once, hoping that we will hit the jackpot.

As we enter the new week, consider the following principles & how they may just in fact enable more tangible results or at least facilitate real progress:

1️⃣Precision Targeting: Concentrate efforts on a specific market or customer segment. Become laser focused on your niche or customer.
2️⃣Deep Understanding: Develop a thorough understanding of your target customer to the point where you know their needs & preferences before they do.
3️⃣Personalised Approach: Ensure your sales approach is specific to your customer & not generalised as many are. It is all about them & not about you!
4️⃣Relationship Building: Invest time in cultivating a strong & genuine connection with the customer. This includes identifying & leveraging the internal influencing structure, not just the org structure.
5️⃣Problem Solving: Identify & address the specific challenges your target customer is facing. Verify whether this is a problem that must be solved. If not, there's no opportunity.
6️⃣Effective Communication: Clearly convey the unique value proposition of your solution, demonstrating how it will solve the customer’s problem.
7️⃣ Focused Learning: Continuously refine your knowledge about your target market & customer to stay ahead of the competition. Continue to be curious.
8️⃣Resource Optimisation: When focusing on just one opportunity at a time, we can better utilise key resources efficiently toward the chosen customer, avoiding the scattergun approach. When this happens, you're more likely to have specialists want to work on your opportunities over others.
9️⃣Adaptability: Be flexible to adapt your strategy based on feedback &  evolving market dynamics. Hard to do when chasing multiple opportunities at once.
1️⃣0️⃣Measurable Results: Set clear, achievable goals for your focused efforts & track performance against them. Remember, this doesn't mean ‘sales closed’ - it is all the incremental calls to action that are executed as part of the buying process.

Stop chasing 2 rabbits at a time!! If you are grappling with this temptation right now, just remember the words of Confucius ‘the person who chases 2 sales opportunities, catches neither’.

Here’s to a massive week 👊
