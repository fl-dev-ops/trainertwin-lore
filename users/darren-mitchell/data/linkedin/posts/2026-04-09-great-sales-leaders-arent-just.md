Great sales leaders aren’t just great operators, they’re great developers of people.

And the right mentor or coach can change the entire trajectory of a career.

In my recent conversation with Rebecca Grimes on The Exceptional Sales Leader Podcast, she shared how mentoring shaped one of the biggest pivots of her career.

After more than a decade mastering marketing roles, she stepped into an organisation undergoing a complete transformation; new ICP, new motions, new market. She spent two years leading that pivot… but the growth she expected didn’t materialise.

What did make the difference?
The leaders who took a chance on her.
The CEOs who coached her.
The sales leader who opened the door to a new path.

Her story is a powerful reminder: frontline sales teams don’t just need strategy, they need support, belief, and guidance to grow into their potential.

Because the best leaders don’t just drive results; they create them through people.

Who is a mentor or coach who shaped your career? Reflect and give them some gratitude today.
