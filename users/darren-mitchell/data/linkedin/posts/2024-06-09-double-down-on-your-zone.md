***Double Down On Your Zone Of Genius***

In professional golf, there is a well known saying - ‘Drive for show, putt for dough’.

The professional golfer who achieves sustained success is the one who is able to convert the opportunities when they count, especially when the pressure is on. 

And whilst a pro golfer has a high level of skill set across their entire game, the elite performers are exceptional in the art of golf within 100 metres of the hole. They double down on their zone of genius.

Do they practice their driving and longer game? Absolutely-but not to the same extent as they practice their shorter game, because it is the shorter game which separates the exceptional players from the competition. They focus their attention on where their strengths lie and double down on developing these even further.

So it is in sales and sales leadership. Whilst there are many facets of sales that can create results, the sales people and sales leaders who identify their zone of genius and then double down on it, are the ones who separate themselves from the pack.

What is your zone of genius? Especially as a sales leader, this is critical to identify and then stick to it. Too many sales leaders try too hard to be all things to all people and end up not doing any of them to an exceptional level. Focusing on 1-2 key areas and becoming an expert in these areas will deliver exceptional results. 

What are these areas? These can differ across leaders, but can include:

🏌️ Relationship building
🏌️ Emotional Intelligence 
🏌️ Strategy development
🏌️ Prospecting strategies
🏌️ Influencing capabilities 
🏌️ Negotiation techniques 
🏌️ Public speaking
🏌️ Problem solving 
🏌️ Decision making
 
The list goes on. The key is to identify your zone of genius and double down on it, to not only create a higher level of competency in the short term, but to develop mastery over the long term. Because is it mastery which will deliver the exceptional results.

Here’s to a massive week 👊
