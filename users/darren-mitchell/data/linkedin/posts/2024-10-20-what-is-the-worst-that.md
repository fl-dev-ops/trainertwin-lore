***What Is The Worst That Can Happen?***

A couple of weeks ago I was running a group coaching session for a group of talented emerging leaders. Each of them had been handpicked to be part of the program based on their track record of exceptional performance as well as their desire to move into a leadership role. 

It was a fascinating session. They had a high desire to learn and develop their leadership capabilities but what became evident throughout the session was a reluctance to make decisions. In fact, some of them shared that there was an ‘implied pressure’ from senior managers to make the ‘right decisions’. Hence the hesitation. What followed was an enlightening conversation.

The key question I posed to the team was ‘What is the very worst that could happen?’ The purpose behind that question was to encourage the leaders to begin to build their muscle around the study of consequences. And when we utilised a number of different scenarios to unpack, what became evident was that in the vast majority of cases, the very worst case could actually be handled.

There were a number of key lessons to come out of the session, which I hope can also serve you as a leader:

✅  Recognise that we all operate within a comfort zone. The role of the leader is to constantly take action that takes us out of the comfort zone.

✅  Despite the uncertainty of the environment, a leader must be certain in all they do, and especially be certain in who they are. Why? Because the team is craving certainty.

✅  This certainty presupposes a strong belief in ourselves, our ability to lead, and our ability to make decisions.

✅  Prior to any decision, be crystal clear on the intention for the decision. And be open and proactive in sharing the intention with the team.

✅  Sometimes, despite our best intention, the decisions may not deliver the desired outcome. This is where perspective is critical-what is the lesson in the outcome? This is feedback.

As we begin the new week, reflect on your own decision making-are you being bold, stepping outside of your comfort zone and being clear on your intention? Or are you playing it safe?

Ask yourself - ‘what is the very worst that can happen?’ If that worst thing did happen, could you handle it, and what example would you be setting for your team in the process? Something to think about.

Here’s to a massive week 👊
