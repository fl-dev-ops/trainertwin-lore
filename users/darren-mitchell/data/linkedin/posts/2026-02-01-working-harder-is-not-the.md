***Working Harder Is Not The Answer***

Sales leaders: if your team is “working hard” but still stuck… you’re likely managing effort, not friction.

This is the uncomfortable truth most leaders don’t want to hear.

Your people aren’t failing because they lack motivation.
They’re failing because they’re driving with one foot on the accelerator and the other on the brake.

❌ More activity won’t fix that.
❌ More tools won’t fix that.
❌ More pressure definitely won’t fix that.

What will?

Addressing the invisible friction inside your team.

I’ve seen it repeatedly:

Highly capable sales leaders, talented sellers, strong intent, yet results plateau.
Not because they aren’t doing enough…
But because their thinking, language, and internal narratives are quietly working against them.

Here’s the part most leaders miss:

👉 Talent is never enough.
👉 Passion doesn’t remove the need for discipline.
👉 Hard work doesn’t override misaligned beliefs.

Exceptional leaders understand this:

✅ Before you coach behaviour, you must examine mindset.
✅ Before you scale performance, you must remove internal resistance.
✅ Before you push harder, you must ask better questions.

Sales leadership isn’t about motivating people to run faster.
It’s about helping them take their foot off the brake.

That’s the difference between teams that grind…
And teams that break through.

So here is an honest question worth asking:

Where might invisible friction be costing your team results right now, and what are you doing to remove it?
