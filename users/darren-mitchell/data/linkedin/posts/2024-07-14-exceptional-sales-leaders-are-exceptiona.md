***Exceptional Sales Leaders Are Exceptional Coaches***

When given the opportunity to lead the sales team for the first time, sales leaders have a huge level of expectation placed upon them. In a large percentage of cases, they have been an exceptional sales person, a pre-requisite for the step to leadership.

Unfortunately though, the vast majority are not provided the blueprint or playbook to make the transition successful. What they are provided is the sales target to achieve, as well as the expectation to drive the sales team to do whatever it takes to achieve. 

Is it any wonder that many sales leaders end up moving back to an individual contributor role? And the company misses the opportunity to develop sales leaders who can become exceptional. This translates to unrealised sales, unrealised revenue, and unrealised talent. The good news though is that there is a remedy.

Exceptional organisations recognise the critical importance of coaching and mentoring, and instead of letting new sales leaders languish and hope they will be successful, they specifically and intentionally invest in professional development. Sales leaders are provided with sales leadership coaching, whether that be for 3 months, 6 months, or even 12 months.  

And a key element of that coaching is helping the sales leader become an exceptional coach themselves. Creating an environment where professional development of the sales team is a priority and can be measured. This is what underpins exceptional performance and leads to sustainable results.

As we begin the brand new week - here are 2 key questions. 

1️⃣  Are you committed to overseeing an exceptional sales team? 

2️⃣  Are you committed to becoming an exceptional coach? 

My hope is that the answer is yes. 🤞
