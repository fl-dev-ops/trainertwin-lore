When making the transition to sales leadership, very often the leader can get caught up in the daily tactical 'stuff', believing that they are adding value and making progress, however, in reality, what they are really doing, is treading water, and going nowhere fast.

To coin a phrase from baseball, they tend to major in the minors, rather than looking to major in the majors. And the difference is massive. Here are some of my reflections from personal experience. The question I still ask myself today is "Am I majoring in the majors or the minors?" It is a great reminder of where to allocate my focus.

Where does your focus go? Are you majoring in the majors or are you majoring in the minors? Would love your thoughts - please comment below 👇🙏
