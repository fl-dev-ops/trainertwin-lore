I firmly believe that sales is the ultimate form of service, and when there is a clearly defined problem that a customer has, it would be negligent not to provide the customer with a proposed solution. 

The challenge for many sales people and business owners is they often just provide the one solution, removing choice and options.  Here is an excellent summary of a powerful conversation I enjoyed recently on The Exceptional Sales Leader Podcast with America’s Services Sales Coach, Joe Crisara,  and we delve into his career as a home services contractor, to now working with services contractors around the world on how to unleash sales success in an ethical and transformative way. It is a phenomenal conversation, filled with great ideas and insights that can be applied to any sales situation, business and industry.
