Making the transition from sales individual contributor to sales leader is not easy, and just because you have been a successful sales person,  doesn't automatically translate into you being a successful sales leader - despite what you may have been told.

Sales leaders who make the successful transition, who go on to develop a long term satisfying and fulfilling leadership career, do not do this alone. They have help, support and guidance, and they follow a blueprint that enables them to create and then maintain an environment that drives sustainable results.
