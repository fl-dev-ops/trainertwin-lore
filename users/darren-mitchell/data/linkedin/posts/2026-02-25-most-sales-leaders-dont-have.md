Most sales leaders don’t have a decision problem.

They have an alignment problem.

On a recent conversation with Tanya Pluckrose on The Exceptional Sales Leader Podcast, we unpacked something that hit hard:

➤ We’re making decisions all day long.
➤ Too often in sales leadership, we wait.
➤ We gather every data point.
➤ We analyse the pipeline.
➤ We pressure-test the forecast.
➤ We get all our ducks in a row.

Now, don’t get me wrong, informed decisions matter.

But here’s the shift:

Sometimes, when it feels right, that’s the moment to move.

In the podcast, Tanya shared a story about a CEO who felt it was right to leave an event early. It didn’t feel right to stay for the nightcap… but he ignored that instinct.

That small misalignment?
It became the “breadcrumb to his bakery,” so to speak.

In sales, those breadcrumbs show up as:
➤ Hiring someone you know isn’t quite right
➤ Discounting when your gut says hold firm
➤ Pursuing a deal that doesn’t align with strategy
➤ Avoiding a tough conversation with a top performer

High-performing sales leaders don’t just make data-driven decisions.

They make identity-driven decisions.

They ask:
👉 “What would my future self, the highest version of me, choose right now?”

That question changes everything.

Because when you decide from your future self:
✅ You protect standards.
✅ You move faster.
✅ You build culture intentionally.
✅ You lead with conviction, not fear.

The best sales leaders don’t wait for perfect certainty.

They align with who they are becoming and act accordingly.

So here’s the challenge:

Before your next big decision, pause and ask, ‘Am I choosing from comfort… or from my highest level as a leader?’

What’s one decision you’ve been overthinking that your future self already knows the answer to?
