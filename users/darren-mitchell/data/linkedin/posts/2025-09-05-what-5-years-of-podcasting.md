***What 5 Years of Podcasting Taught Me About Leadership & Sales***

5 years. 800+ episodes. Listened to in more than 80 countries.

When I launched The Exceptional Sales Leader Podcast, I had no idea where it would lead. 
What started as a passion project to help sales leaders and their teams become exceptional has turned into one of the most rewarding journeys of my career.

Along the way, I’ve had the privilege of sitting down with remarkable leaders, thinkers, and practitioners from around the world. Every conversation has been a chance to learn, to grow, and to share insights with an incredible community of sales professionals & thought leaders.

But the biggest lessons I’ve learned haven’t just come from the guests. They’ve come from the process itself:

✅  Consistency matters. Showing up week after week, even when it wasn’t easy, has been the foundation of everything.

✅  Stay in the game. Progress is rarely linear, but those who keep moving forward eventually create momentum.

✅  Curiosity unlocks growth. The best questions often lead to the most powerful insights.

✅  Presence is powerful. Whether behind the mic, in a client meeting, or with your team, giving your full attention changes the outcome.

These lessons go far beyond podcasting. They’re the same principles that drive exceptional leadership, exceptional selling, and ultimately, exceptional results.

As I reflect on this 5-year milestone, I’d love to hear from you: what lessons from your own journey continue to propel you forward? 👇
