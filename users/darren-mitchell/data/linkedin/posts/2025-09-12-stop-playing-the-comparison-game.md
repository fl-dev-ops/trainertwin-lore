***Stop Playing The Comparison Game***

There’s a silent killer in sales.

And it’s not missed targets, bad leads, or a tough market.

It’s comparison.

I see it all the time.
Sales leaders scrolling through LinkedIn, comparing their numbers to another leader’s “record-breaking quarter.”

Reps staring across the floor, watching a peer close another big deal, and quietly beating themselves up.

The trap is subtle.

You convince yourself that someone else’s pace, style, or results should be your yardstick.

You start chasing shadows, and the more you do, the more you shrink your own potential.

Here’s the truth:

The only person worth competing with is the one staring back at you in the mirror.

Because when you play the comparison game, you’re playing on someone else’s field.

But when you compete with yourself; yesterday’s skills, last week’s effort, last quarter’s results, you’re playing a game you can actually win.

By all means, model others. Learn from them. Steal good ideas.

But don’t become them.

The sales reps and leaders who win long-term are the ones who double down on their uniqueness.

Comparison breeds envy.

Self-mastery breeds growth.

Sales is not about outpacing everyone else. It’s about becoming the best & boldest version of you.

Sales leaders and sales pros: how do you keep yourself focused on your own path instead of falling into the comparison trap?
