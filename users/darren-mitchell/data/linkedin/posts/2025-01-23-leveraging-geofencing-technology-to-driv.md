***Leveraging Geofencing Technology to Drive Exceptional Results with Barbara Wardell & Ernesto Cullari***

Businesses the world over are constantly searching for more innovative ways to capture the attention of their ideal customer, and often this requires a large financial investment, making it more prohibitive for smaller businesses. 

Geofencing Technology can start to change that and make it more cost effective for smaller businesses to identify and then communicate with their ideal customers. 

In this episode of The Exceptional Sales Leader Podcast, I’m joined by Barbara Wardell & Ernesto Cullari, Co-Founders at Cullari Wardell, an organisation that specialises in geofencing advertising that enables businesses to reach highly targeted customers. 

It is a fascinating conversation, and one that opened my eyes to the potential we have through appropriately leveraging technology to drive better customer awareness and engagement.
