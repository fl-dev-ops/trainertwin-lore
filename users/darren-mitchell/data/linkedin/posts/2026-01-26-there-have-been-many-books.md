There have been many books written about the concept of Servant Leadership and despite it being a very tangible and powerful principle, it is quite rare that sales leaders adopt the approach.

Many sales leaders utilise the traditional stick and carrot method, believing that will be the catalyst to drive high performance. This may work for some, but many sales teams never fulfil their potential because they have a leader who does not serve.

Servant leadership is not weak leadership. Servant leaders balance care and candour, they drive high standards, and create an environment where individuals can unleash their potential without having to pretend to be someone they are not. This type of leadership will be the difference that makes all the difference in the ever increasing complex world.
