***Sales Leaders - Get Out of the Way!!***

Are you getting in the way of your sales team’s success?

It’s an uncomfortable question.

But one every frontline sales manager and CRO should be asking.

Too many sales leaders still want to be the sales hero.

➤ Jumping into the big deal.
➤ Taking over the difficult conversation.
➤ Giving the answer.
➤ Solving the problem.

It feels like leadership.

Sometimes, it’s actually the opposite.

Your job isn’t to be the hero.

It’s to be the Guide. The Shepherd. The Sage.

Because sustainable sales performance isn’t built on how often the leader can save the day.

It’s built on how capable the team becomes without needing to be saved.

The best sales leaders know when to step in.
But they also know when to step back.

Because when the leader constantly steps in, the team learns to step aside.

And when the leader steps back, the team has the opportunity to step up.

That’s the real leadership test:

➤ Can your sales team succeed without you being the most important person in every deal?

If not, perhaps the issue isn’t your team.
Perhaps you’re standing in the way of their growth.

Step back so your sales team can step up.

👉 Ask yourself this week: Where am I solving a problem my team needs to learn how to solve themselves?
What would change if you stopped being the hero and started being the guide? 👇
