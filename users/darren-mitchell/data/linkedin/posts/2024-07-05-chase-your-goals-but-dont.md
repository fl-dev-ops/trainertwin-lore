Chase your goals, but don't forget to celebrate the journey 🎉⚾️

As sales leaders, it is incredibly important to set big audacious (and sometimes hairy!!) goals, but equally as important to celebrate the progress the team makes in pursuit of the goals. To only celebrate the achievement of the goal often loses motivational impact.

A great reminder of this from Eric Recker when he joined me recently on The Exceptional Sales Leader Podcast. Make sure we are celebrating progress, because continual progress will deliver the desired outcomes.
