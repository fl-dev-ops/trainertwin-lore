I remember when I was first promoted to a leadership role, not only was I excited to have finally reached that level, my first inkling was to focus on the 'doing' part of the role. 

Even though I had considered and thought about the culture that I would like to create and had started to formulate a leadership philosophy, it hadn't yet crystallised to the point where I could confidently articulate the philosophy and the standards attached to it. 

At that time, my immediate priority was to get stuff done. Pretty soon though I realised how important it was to firstly establish leadership standards, and then be uncompromising in their implementation. It wasn't easy, I made mistakes, I had pushback, but nonetheless I pushed forward, learning through the process that in order to be an effective leader, I had to maintain strong standards. It was non-negotiable.

If you are new to leadership, please focus attention on establishing standards and be relentless in maintaining them.

If you have been in leadership for a while, review your standards, and reflect on whether you have been ruthless enough in their implementation.

Being ruthlessly uncompromising on leadership standards is essential to exceptional leadership. It will also help you create an environment that underpins exceptional and sustained performance.
