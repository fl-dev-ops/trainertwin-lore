***Leaping Into Uncertainty***

A few weeks ago, I spent a day with a terrific group of leaders & high potential individual contributors, helping them develop pitches that win.

Teaching sales leaders & their teams how to pitch their ideas, present with influence & especially present with confidence has been one of my favourite programs over the last 9 years. To witness people’s transformations over the course of a day is a constant source of inspiration, & one that many find hard to believe is possible.

What I have come to appreciate is that public speaking & presenting in front of a group is not something that comes naturally for many people. In fact, the vast majority of people would rather do anything else than speak in public, or even speak in front of a group of people they know. This includes experienced sales leaders!!

Often it is the uncertainty that leads to nervousness, anxiety or in some extreme cases, meltdowns. People often share that they're afraid of:

❌ making mistakes.
❌ forgetting important information.
❌ forgetting what to say.
❌ being judged.
❌ looking foolish.

Presenting in front of people isn't easy & it isn't always enjoyable. I do believe though that everyone can build capability around presenting & through consistent practice, as well as leaping into uncertainty, you can learn to enjoy it.

Here's some tips that mentors have shared with me over the years which has helped me develop confidence & competence in presenting: 

✅ Leap into uncertainty. Take a chance, take a risk. Be certain of self. 
✅ Nervousness is normal. It is a sign you care. 
✅ If nervous, think of service. It is not about you!
✅ Preparation is key. What are the key messages? What do I want them to think, feel, and do, as a result of my pitch?
✅ Who is my audience? What is their communication style, how do they make decisions? What's important to them? 
✅ Be yourself. Don't pretend to be anyone other than you, & remember you don't have to convince anyone of anything - when we remove ourselves from the pressure of having to achieve a specific outcome, funnily enough, our creativity & authenticity increases.
✅ Engage the audience. Make the presentation or pitch a 2 way conversation. Get them involved & get them talking. This increases engagement & can dramatically decrease uncertainty.

As sales leaders, it is incredibly important that we show the way, especially when presenting & pitching. If we understand that our team is a reflection of us, & that our team are more likely to do what they see, then it is incumbent on us to leap into uncertainty & lead the way, irrespective of how we feel.

As we begin the brand new week, identify areas of uncertainty for you. It may be pitching & presenting, or it may be something else. Either way, take the leap into uncertainty & take note of what happens - you may just be pleasantly surprised.

Here’s to a massive week 👊
