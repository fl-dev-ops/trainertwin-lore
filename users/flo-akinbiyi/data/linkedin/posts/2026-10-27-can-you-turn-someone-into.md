Can you turn someone into Steve Jobs, Oprah Winfrey or Barack Obama in just eight hours?

Of course not!

But can you make a tremendous impact on someone’s communication skills in just eight hours?

Absolutely!

And that’s exactly what we set out to do during my latest communication workshop with DHL.

I’m incredibly proud and grateful that DHL has once again trusted me to develop the communication skills of their leaders.

This time, I had the pleasure of training station managers and operational leaders from across the Middle East, Africa and Türkiye, some responsible for stations and regions, others for entire countries.

What a beautiful, diverse group of people!

Their energy, openness and willingness to participate were incredible. And witnessing their growth in confidence, presence, clarity and audience engagement over just eight hours was a real joy.

And this time, I had something special in store for them!

Because great communication isn’t just about how we speak. Especially in leadership, it’s also about how we listen.

That’s why I invited executive coach and longtime friend Mireia Mujika Irusta to co-facilitate the workshop with me.

Bringing her extensive coaching experience and expertise in leadership development, Mira added another valuable dimension to our programme. Together, we explored how leaders can become more conscious communicators, listen with greater intention and create conversations that are more meaningful, balanced and impactful.
