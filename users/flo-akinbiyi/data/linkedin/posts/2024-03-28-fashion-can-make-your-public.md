Fashion can make your public speaking game stronger!

Fashion allows you to make a statement without saying a word.
In public speaking, presentations and personal brands, your outfit can be chosen to work for you, or you can also decide not to care about it. 

Here's the thing, whether you like it or not, what you wear communicates. If you stand in front of an audience with a three-piece suit, jeans and a washed-out t-shirt, or some funky clothes, you're always making a statement. 

Doesn't it then make more sense to own the statement and communicate a statement that reflects your message, your vision, and who you really are? 

I am truly grateful to my Italian wife Ale Aster Akinbiyi who brought me onto the journey of fashion, and after years of hard training that she had with me, I eventually am okay with fashion, and I love it. 

It has helped me so much. I remember these meetings that we would have with an automotive company, where in every meeting, the marketing manager would deliver the icebreaker by talking again about my outfit today, how much he loves it, how cool it is, how they as a brand always stand for fashion, but they're not that so fashionable, and we always had a good laugh, a bit of a conversation around it and we moved on. 

I didn't even have to say a single word. On stage, I have the same experiences. My funky, creative, sometimes daring outfits have become part of my brand, and they make me recognizable. 

Having a strong fashion game is a plus, and as the Trillion Dollar Man©️ Dan Peña says it, “If you want to have money from the banks, you should dress like the president of the country”.
