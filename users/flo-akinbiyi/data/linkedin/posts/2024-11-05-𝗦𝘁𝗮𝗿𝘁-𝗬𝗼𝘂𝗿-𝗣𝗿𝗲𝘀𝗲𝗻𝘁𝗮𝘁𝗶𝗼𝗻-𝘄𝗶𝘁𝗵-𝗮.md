Starting you presentation with power and purpose is your chance to hook your audience before they even know what hit them.

Too often, presenters begin with the usual, “Hi, my name is…” and “I’m here to talk about… And on top of the content being less than exciting, their delivery just puts you to sleep. 

As you can see in the video, this is one of the biggest mistakes a #presenter can make.

𝗜𝗳 𝘆𝗼𝘂 𝗱𝗼𝗻’𝘁 𝗰𝗮𝗽𝘁𝘂𝗿𝗲 𝘁𝗵𝗲 #𝗮𝘁𝘁𝗲𝗻𝘁𝗶𝗼𝗻 𝗼𝗳 𝘆𝗼𝘂𝗿 #𝗮𝘂𝗱𝗶𝗲𝗻𝗰𝗲 𝗿𝗶𝗴𝗵𝘁 𝗳𝗿𝗼𝗺 𝘁𝗵𝗲 𝘀𝘁𝗮𝗿𝘁, 𝘆𝗼𝘂’𝗿𝗲 𝗺𝗶𝘀𝘀𝗶𝗻𝗴 𝗮 𝗴𝗼𝗹𝗱𝗲𝗻 𝗼𝗽𝗽𝗼𝗿𝘁𝘂𝗻𝗶𝘁𝘆 𝘁𝗼 𝗺𝗮𝗸𝗲 𝗮𝗻 𝗶𝗺𝗽𝗮𝗰𝘁.

So, if your content isn’t thrilling on its own, or if you don’t have an engaging #audience interaction or powerful #visual planned, then at least make sure your 𝗱𝗲𝗹𝗶𝘃𝗲𝗿𝘆 grabs their attention. The start of your presentation should be powerful and set the tone for what’s to come.
