Ramadan Kareem to all my Muslim friends around the world.

From the bottom of my heart, I wish you a peaceful and deeply spiritual holy month.

Ramadan is not only a time of fasting.
It is a time of realignment.

A time to slow down.
To reflect.
To reset intention.
To reconnect with what truly matters.

And although I don’t practice the faith, I appreciate this month as it invites a different spirit into our fast-paced, uncertain world: discipline, gratitude, generosity, and clarity.

May it bring strength, perspective, and inner peace to you and your families.

Ramadan Mubarak.
