How Do You Overcome Language Barriers, Boost Confidence, and Make Your Team Better Communicators?

When Marilyn Chircop received multiple requests from her team to improve their communication skills, she took action. For their annual gathering, she brought me in to deliver an educational and inspirational talk on how to communicate with more impact.

And what an incredible session it was! The team of 60 creative professionals made it an absolute pleasure - fun, engaging, and full of insights.

One of my favorite moments? Learning a new cultural gesture from the Filipinos that I had never heard of before. Did you know that they sometimes point with their mouth? Not the best habit to pick up but a fascinating insight into non-verbal communication!

There’s always something new to learn about communication. Hats off to Marilyn for empowering her team with new tools, fresh ideas, and lasting inspiration to help them communicate with more impact.

Great leaders invest in their team’s communication skills because this is how they can enable their organization to further thrive. Is your organization also investing in their team’s communication?
