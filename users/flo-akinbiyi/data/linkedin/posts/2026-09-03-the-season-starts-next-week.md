The season starts next week. And it starts with one of my favourite events of the year.

I am flying to Greece to host the SOC Weekend Costa Navarino for Supercar Owners Circle.

I have been hosting these weekends since 2018. Andermatt, Gstaad, Croatia, Spain, Puglia, Sardinia, Miami. This one is Greece, and you can probably tell from the photo how much I enjoy the company.

Four days in the Peloponnese, which is not the Greece most people book. No white houses on a cliff. Olive groves, mountain roads, and a bay where a sea battle once decided Greek independence.

Two driving days behind a police escort. North towards Tripoli, then east to Sparta, with lunch at a palace.

Some of the finest cars in the world, the people who own them, and a few automotive partners I am looking forward to bringing on stage. One of them is a name you already know.

And on the last night, dinner and the SOC Awards on the beach.

If I get ten minutes in that water, I will take it.

Can’t wait for another trip of a lifetime!
