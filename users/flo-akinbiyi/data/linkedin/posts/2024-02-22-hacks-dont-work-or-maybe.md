Hacks don’t work!

Or maybe, they can in some situations 🤔

Not all of life’s journeys have a shortcut, quickfix or magic bullet. And that’s okay 👈

In public speaking, the path to excellence is often long and winding. There are times when you feel like you will never improve, that the next step to elevate your speaking or delivery or content is always just out of reach - you can see it but you can’t take it. 

This is where practice and patience and perseverance come into play. 💯 

✅Practice at every speaking opportunity

✅Patience with what feels like slow progress

✅Perseverance to overcome the hurdles 

Embrace your public speaking journey, each twist and turn on the experience path, for they lead to mastery.
