Moments and places like this remind me of my place in the world. 

Looking at the 250-million-year-old Dolomite Mountains makes you wonder what those mountains have witnessed. 

In the last 10,000 years, they’ve seen ancient civilizations, medieval battles and knights, more fighting during the world wars.

They’ve watched humans come and go, be born, grow up, live their lives, and leave the earth again… and they are still here and will still be here long after we are gone. 

Taking in these moments of clarity, while sitting in stillness, is a profound experience that reminds me of what is truly important during these few years gifted to each and every one of us.

Up here, you realize how small your worries are – and how big life really is.
