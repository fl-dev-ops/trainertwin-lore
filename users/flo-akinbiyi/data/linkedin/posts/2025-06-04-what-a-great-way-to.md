What a great way to start the day!

Thank you, Esquire, for publishing my article on how to give a killer presentation at work.

Let’s be honest, we’re all done with boring presentations, aren’t we?

The good news: delivering a presentation that blows the socks off your audience is absolutely doable. 

In my piece for Esquire Middle East, I share seven powerful tips to elevate your game.

Let’s have a look at a few of them:

𝟭. 𝗡𝗲𝘃𝗲𝗿 𝘀𝘁𝗮𝗿𝘁 𝘄𝗶𝘁𝗵 ‘𝗛𝗶, 𝗺𝘆 𝗻𝗮𝗺𝗲 𝗶𝘀...’

If you start your presentation like everyone else does, you’re already sending a signal that this is going to be just another dull script. You’ve practically given the audience permission to stop listening, gaze out the window and think about what they’re going to watch on Netflix that night.


𝟮. 𝗙𝗼𝗰𝘂𝘀 𝗼𝗻 𝘁𝗵𝗲 ‘𝗕𝗶𝗴 𝗣𝗶𝗰𝘁𝘂𝗿𝗲’ 𝗿𝗮𝘁𝗵𝗲𝗿 𝘁𝗵𝗮𝗻 𝗱𝗿𝗼𝘄𝗻𝗶𝗻𝗴 𝗶𝗻 𝗱𝗮𝘁𝗮 𝗽𝗼𝗶𝗻𝘁𝘀.

One of the most common mistakes presenters make is cramming in everything they know, all the data, all the charts, all the things they personally find interesting. But here’s the brutal truth: most of it doesn’t matter.


𝟯. 𝗕𝗲 𝗮𝘂𝘁𝗵𝗲𝗻𝘁𝗶𝗰.

Authenticity builds trust so show something of yourself but don’t overshare and embarrass yourself. Being ‘too real’ without context is risky.
