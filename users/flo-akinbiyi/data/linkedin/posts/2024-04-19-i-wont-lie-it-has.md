I won’t lie, it has been an intense week!

A bit of Deja Vu hit me this week as storms and floods hit Dubai and that meant homeschooling for my son and not being able to leave the house and to add on top of that, I had to try my best to work and hold it all together as my wife had traveled out of the country. 

Life was disrupted and it was a strong trip down memory lane for me, with what happened during the COVID-19 pandemic. At the same time, a powerful reminder that life is fragile and you can’t take anything for certain. However, one thing I noticed is that I felt much more confident and stronger in this situation.

It was not the same severity as the pandemic, nevertheless, the battles we fought during COVID, the challenges we overcame, and the growth that we had is something that nobody can take away from us.

I’m grateful for every day of my life. And it’s weeks like that that remind me to always stay true to this philosophy which I had since I was a child.

How was this week for you?
