I've coached hundreds of workshops, but there's something special about working with students who are THIS hungry to learn.

During Bits and Pretzels, Catharina Dahmen approached me and asked if I could do a free workshop for the TUM Speaker Series. And of course I said yes, giving back to students, young professionals, and other groups is part of our business philosophy.

And I have to say, I was absolutely delighted by the entire workshop. Visiting the campus here at TUM in Munich was a great experience. It's rare to sit with a group that is so hungry, so thirsty for knowledge, that is so engaged, and that also though they don't have decades of experience puts in the effort to make up for what they're missing in years through hard work and thorough preparation. 

The two hours with them passed by in no time, and it was inspiring to see how Annett Wöhrle, Nick Wellen, Catharina Dahmen and Cara Speer were able to get tools, strategies, and approaches to be better presenters, better moderators and better speakers.

Can’t wait to visit one of the TUM Speaker Series events, hopefully next year, and see how all of you will use speaking to entertain and engage audiences, but also to grow your careers.
