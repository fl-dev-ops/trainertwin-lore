Own your body language on stage.

When you have the opportunity to be on stage and address an audience, owning your #bodylanguage is essential. Of course, body language is just one part of the equation. Your #content, #voice, #delivery, and presence all come together to give the audience a complete picture.

But body language does so much more:
	•	✅ It communicates confidence.
	•	✅ It helps illustrate your message.
	•	✅ It shows the audience how comfortable you are.

And here’s the best part—you can adjust your body language at any time!

Instead of pacing back and forth or standing static, use the stage with ease and purpose. Move naturally to emphasize your points, creating a stronger connection with the audience. But like any skill, this takes practice.

Here’s a tip:
Arrive early at the event, before the audience arrives. Walk onto the stage, look at the space, and check out a few different spots. Get comfortable with how it feels and how you’ll move. This preparation will make a huge difference when it’s time to speak.
