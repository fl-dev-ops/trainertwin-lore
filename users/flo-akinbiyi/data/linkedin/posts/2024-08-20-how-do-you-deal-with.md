**How do you deal with stumbling over words?**

It's completely normal to mispronounce a word! It happens to everyone from politicians to TV presenters to professional speakers.

Here's the key: *It's not a big deal to your audience.* The real challenge is how the speaker handles it.

*Here are a few tips to handle Mispronunciation*

✅ *Correct and Move On:* Simply correct the mistake and continue without apologizing.

✅ *Use Humor:* Turn the slip into a joke. For instance, if you say "pengrates" instead of "pancakes," laugh it off with, "Sometimes my stomach thinks faster than my brain!"

✅ *Practice Tricky Words:* Write them phonetically. 
As a German, I used to pronounce Mercedes (Merzedes) but internationally you say Mercedes (Mer-say-dees). So I wrote it phonetically in my script when we launched the all-new G-Class and I didn't mess up.

Mispronouncing words is human, and everyone does it. Check out a fun video in the comments of Benedict Cumberbatch struggling with the word "penguins" —it's a great reminder that we all stumble!
