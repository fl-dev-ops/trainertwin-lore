Yesterday, we went to the Zayed National Museum.

A family day to immerse ourselves in one of in Abu Dhabi’s latest cultural monuments.

Even after living 15 years in the UAE, there was so much to learn about the history and legacy of Sheikh Zayed bin Sultan Al Nahyan, the founding father of the UAE. And the exhibitions and galleries that show showcase the history, vision, and evolution of the nation, while the architecture of the museum itself is truly breathtaking. 

We also indulged in a modern Emirati dining experience at ERTH Restaurant. Highly recommended!!

As I had the opportunity to work with Department of Culture and Tourism – Abu Dhabi (DCT Abu Dhabi) of Culture and Tourism, I want to say this very clearly: The staff were exceptionally welcoming, friendly, knowledgeable, and an absolute pleasure to interact with.

Hats off, compliments, and congratulations to everyone involved for creating such a remarkable experience.

The Akinbiyi family will definitely be back.
