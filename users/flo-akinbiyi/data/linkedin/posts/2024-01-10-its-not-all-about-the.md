Being there, in a room that was buzzing with the intense energy of 15 to 17-year-olds, was a humbling experience. Looking at their faces and listening to them speak, I saw not just teenagers, but future leaders, thinkers, and change-makers. 🌟

This session also made me realize again how tough the job of a teacher is - their dedication deserves our highest respect. 👩‍🏫👨‍🏫
