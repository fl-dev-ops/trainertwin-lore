I’ve shared the stage with celebrities, politicians, CEOs, and change-makers, and here’s what I’ve learned…

When you speak to a professional audience, whether it’s a CEO, a politician, an investor, or an expert in their field, you’re never just speaking to their role. 

You’re also speaking to the human being behind it.

I had the honor of meeting the President of Botswana Mokgweetsi Eric Masisi at Bits & Pretzels. 

Of course, I brought my professional expertise. That’s non-negotiable. But I also came in with genuine curiosity, with the intention to connect, to have a real conversation, human to human.

Because here’s the truth: no matter what the title says, we’re all human, and that’s where the magic happens.

⚠️ Be respectful but not robotic.

⚠️ Be professional but not distant.

⚠️ Speak to their role but connect to the person.

⚠️ Deliver value but build trust.

That’s when a presentation becomes a moment. That’s when a speech turns into a connection. And connection, that’s what lasts.

So next time you’re in the room with someone important, remember this: don’t just address their position, speak to the person standing in front of you.
