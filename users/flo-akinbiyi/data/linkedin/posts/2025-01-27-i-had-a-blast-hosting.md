I had a blast hosting the Porsche Carrera Cup Middle East in Abu Dhabi!

A massive thank you to Porsche Center Abu Dhabi and Al Ain, Ali & Sons, for bringing me back for the second year in a row to host the fourth stop of the Porsche Carrera Cup Middle East at the iconic Yas Marina Circuit.

The weekend was nothing short of extraordinary! Engaging with Porsche enthusiasts, car lovers, and motorsport experts was a thrill, and being at one of the most stunning racetracks in the world always feels special. Experiencing the finest German automotive engineering up close was incredible, but what truly stood out were the people and the stories.

I had the privilege of interviewing Saif Al Amri, the founder of the Emirati racing team Rabdan Motorsports, whose vision and passion are elevating Emirati motorsport to the next level. What an inspiring individual!

Congratulations to Janis Diak and James Wallis from Rabdan Motorsports for their incredible performances, securing first and second place in both races. What a show of skill and determination!

From hanging out with mascots to interviewing the first-ever female race driver for the Porsche Carrera Cup Middle East and celebrating unforgettable moments on and off the track, this weekend left me energized and truly grateful.

Thank you again to Jack Morton for organizing such a fantastic event and to everyone who made this experience so memorable. I can’t wait for next year’s edition!
