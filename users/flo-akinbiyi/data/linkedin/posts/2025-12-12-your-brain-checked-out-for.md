Your brain checked out for the holidays two days ago. 
The board presentation is tomorrow morning. What I'm about to tell you will feel unexpected, but it's why my presentations get approved 40% more often
This is a really tough spot to be in.

It happened to me this week while preparing for an event.

At that point, you don’t want to write, research, prepare, or design anymore.

But here is the thing, the audience shows up and gives you their precious lifetime, so they deserve and EXPECT your finest work.

So you got to do it, but do it right!

Use the OBC Framework: 
Opening
Create a powerful opening that grabs attention and excites you to deliver it.
No pressure but you should blow their minds right from the start.

Body
Keep the body concise. If possible, focus on three core points only.  
Your audience is already in holiday mode, everything else belongs in the appendix. 

Closing
End with something memorable.
Inspiration, a short video, an image, or even a song, choose something unexpected that adds value and creates a moment they’ll remember.

This simple framework that we all know and often forget is a guaranteed route to success and the magic happens for you while you prepare the opening.

You start with creating something that is fun.

If your presentation is tomorrow, don’t panic, structure wins.

Start with the opening, cut the noise, and earn the yes.
