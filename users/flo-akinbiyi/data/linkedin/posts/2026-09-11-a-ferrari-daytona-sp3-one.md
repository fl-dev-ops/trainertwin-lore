A Ferrari Daytona SP3. One of only 599. 

Doors are up at a roadside stop under a handwritten sign for tiropita in the Peloponnese, 

Many legendary battles have been fought in Greece. At Costa Navarino, the last major historic sea battles in the history of Greece took place ending the age of sailing ship battles.

The Supercar Owners Circle week in Greece started with some incredible memories. Costa Navarino, the location and our base for the next days, is already one to remember for a lifetime.

And of course, the lineup of super and hypercars that we get to enjoy here is, once again, unbelievable. From Bugattis to Paganis to Gordon Murrays to Ferraris, Lamborghinis, Aston Martins, you name it, we have it! Whatever desires a petrolhead could have, we have it here.

As we drive along the most incredible roads on the Peloponnese, we can feel the spirit of Greece which we unleashed yesterday at the dinner while smashing plates and doing the traditional dances with the horeftes.

We'll show you a few more highlights with a few more automotive memories that will once again last a lifetime.
