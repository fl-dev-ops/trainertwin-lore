What does skateboarding have in common with public speaking?

Nothing? Everything? Something?

For me, both have that sudden burst of joy you can experience.

When you step onto the board and start rolling, the smile on your face just builds up. And the same goes for public speaking - that rush of excitement when you’re in the spotlight.

This weekend, I felt it all again. I found the time, the fitness, and the passion to hit the pump track, even at 37 degrees.

Yes, there were a lot of breaks. Yes, there was a lot of water. 

But the joy of it all - that feeling of flow, that moment when the fear fades and it’s just you and the ride.

That’s what I keep coming back for.

Public speaking has the same beat for me: the butterflies, the heat, the push to step up even when you’re sweating a little.

Then the pure reward of it, the thrill of sharing something real.

That’s the connection I feel between these two worlds: the courage to start, the presence to stay, and the simple pleasure of doing something that lights you up.

What about you? Did you find any of those joyful bursts this weekend?

Wishing you a week filled with them.
