🤔 Can you remember a moment when you faced a great fear and chose to push through? Aren’t these the moments when magic happens and you find your greatness?


As a presenter, public speaker, and communication coach, I regularly encounter this scenario. Whether it's helping my clients deal with their nervousness and anxiety or managing my own nerves due to rising anxiety, it's a familiar battle.


🌟 One of the best ways to conquer your fears is not just to focus on the negativity but to emphasize your capabilities and skills. Put the fear into perspective and then face it head-on. It's about telling yourself that, despite what your brain might suggest, you're not in mortal danger.


🧠 Our old brain, bless it, is wired to protect us from the unknown or perceived danger. However, in today's world, standing in front of an audience or jumping from a height into water isn't a life-or-death situation. We know our bodies can handle it.


💪 Tackling tough challenges step by step is how we discover our greatness and eventually overcome our fears.
