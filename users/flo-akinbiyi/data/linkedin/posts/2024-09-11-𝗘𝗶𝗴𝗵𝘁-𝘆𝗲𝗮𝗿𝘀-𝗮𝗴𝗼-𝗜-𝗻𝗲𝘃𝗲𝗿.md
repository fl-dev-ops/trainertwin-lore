When I attended my first Seamless Europe conference, I had already been a speaker and #presenter. But it was the first conference I ever got to host and be paid for. Thank you, #Seamless, for the trust you instilled in me, and for continuing to foster such a strong relationship.

Special thanks to Joseph, Head of Seamless, for your #leadership and continued belief in me. Now, years later, I’ve had the opportunity to host Seamless in Dubai, Germany, and soon in Africa. It’s been an incredible journey, not only because of the amazing cars and tech but also the amazing people I’ve met along the way.

One such person is Clemens Baumgärtner, the Head of the Department for Labour and Economic Development for the City of Munich. He’s not only behind major initiatives like #Oktoberfest but also deeply involved in the Munich startup ecosystem. Clemens and I have crossed paths at numerous events, including Bits & Pretzels, and I’m thrilled to soon be on stage with him in Munich.

Another personal highlight has been reconnecting with incredible speakers like Karl Lilgert and John Sanei, both of whom bring such inspiration and expertise to the table. These are individuals who are pushing boundaries in leadership and future thinking, and I’m so excited to share the stage with them again.

At Seamless, we’re constantly discussing the future of payments, fintech, and banking. The conversations we had yesterday left a huge impression on me. As I host the Payments, #FinTech, and Banking Theatre, one thing is clear: the future of finance is here. It’s becoming more open, accessible, customer-centric, and smarter than ever before. 

I can’t wait to see what’s next for the world of finance—and I look forward to many more editions of Seamless!
