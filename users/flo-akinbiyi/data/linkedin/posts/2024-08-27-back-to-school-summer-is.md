Back to School: Summer is Officially Over

I write these words with a little bit of a heavy heart because this summer was nothing short of spectacular. I feel blessed to have had a long vacation with my family, some time in my favorite city, Dubai, learning in Denmark, and to top it off, a nice vacation in Italy with my wife’s family.

I’m very fortunate to often choose where I am, and I’m excited for my son who now officially joins school in year one. It’s an exciting day because the next ten-plus years will shape who he becomes and lay the foundation for his life. This will surely challenge us as parents.

I’m excited about the times ahead and can’t wait to take him out of school for another little father-and-son trip. Yes, we’re allowed to do that in Dubai.

To all the parents out there, I hope you had a great start to the school year. How are you guys feeling today?
