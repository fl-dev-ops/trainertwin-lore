Meeting Robin Sharma on stage was without a doubt one of the most inspiring moments of 2024. #throwbackthursday 

At DP World’s Global Freight Summit, I had the honor of interviewing Robin Sharma for the second time in Dubai. Not only was it a terrific conversation that inspired both me and the audience, but as the author of over 20 books with millions of copies sold worldwide, Robin has such wonderful nuggets of wisdom to share.

Here are three learnings that stuck with me:

✅ The 90-90-1 Rule
For 90 days, spend the first 90 minutes of your day focused on one task that will make the biggest impact in your life. Isn’t that a spectacular way to ensure real progress? While I know I can’t implement this right now, it’s on my list for 2025.

✅ Focus on Mastery
Robin emphasizes that mastery is what makes you outstanding. When I asked him whether perfection is something negative, he explained that all the greats, Michelangelo, Picasso, and others, strived for perfection in their craft. To stand out, you need to fully immerse yourself in your field, live it, breathe it, and always strive to deliver your finest work.

✅ Leaders are in the Business of Service
Whether you’re leading a company, a team, or even guiding customers through a store, leadership is about serving others. This perspective is a humbling reminder to reflect on how we can improve strategies and approaches to become better leaders.

These insights have stayed with me for months, and I’m looking forward to meeting Robin again soon on stage.
