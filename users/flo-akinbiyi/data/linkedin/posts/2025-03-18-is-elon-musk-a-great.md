Is Elon Musk a great speaker? My response might surprise you!

I recently thought of the term The Elon Musk Effect.

And this effect proves that you don’t need to be a highly charismatic or traditionally powerful speaker to make an impact - if, and I repeat if (!), your content is literally out of this world (no pun intended).

If you evaluate Elon Musk’s delivery and vocal charisma, he ranks somewhere between 30 - 40%.

Compare that to Barack Obama, who boasts an outstanding charisma score of 96%.

And yet, every time Musk speaks, the world listens.

Why? Because his content hooks the audience.

✔️ He talks about rockets that land by themselves - sometimes caught mid-air by a giant chopstick mechanism.
✔️ He unveils robotaxis, promising to disrupt entire industries.
✔️ He introduces Neuralink, exploring the potential to transform human life itself.

The lesson?

Great speakers aren’t always the ones with the most compelling stage presence.

I’ve seen this firsthand in many pitch competitions.

The winners are often not the best presenters, but their content is so strong that it simply cannot be ignored.

The Elon Musk Effect teaches us a clear truth:

Either your content must be so revolutionary that the world has to listen, or you must elevate your delivery to make them listen!
