15 years ago I came to the UAE to launch the all new Audi A8 L.

Back in the day I was working as the project manager, actually as a junior project manager, for Avantgard, and I started working in Munich under the premise of being sent to a foreign country whenever a project came about. 
Dubai was really not a conscious choice. It was an opportunity that I was offered on a Wednesday and by that Saturday, I was sitting on the plane to Dubai. 

I didn’t know that I would stay here for a rather long time, that I would find the love of my life, that my son would be born, that I would be able to build a unique and exciting career for myself, and that I would meet such an incredible number of exceptional people and have many exceptional experience with them. 

Dubai, I love you and I’m truly grateful for everything that you have provided me and even that you provide for my family and for my next generation. You will always be in my heart for giving me the opportunity to fulfill my potential, and I can only say shukran to many more!
