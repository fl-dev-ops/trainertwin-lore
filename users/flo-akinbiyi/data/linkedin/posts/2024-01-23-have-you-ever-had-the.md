Have you ever had the honour of shaking hands with a president? 🌍 

Last year at Bits & Pretzels Founders Festival, I had the privilege of meeting the President of Botswana, His Excellency Mokgweetsi Eric Masisi. Reflecting on a photo from that day, I realised how clearly we can distinguish leaders from the crowd. 🤔

Whether it's the magnetic aura of the main act at a concert 🎸, the charisma of international celebrities 🌟, or the powerful presence of politicians 🏛️, the 'A players' always stand out.

Have you ever wondered why you can easily detect A-Players?

There 's a lot of magic found in body language. 
From appearance to grooming, your posture, to walking and how you carry yourself! The way you speak, interact, and shake hands - every detail counts. 👔

Body language can make you 'A-list' material! Adjust yours to be the A-lister version of yourself, and watch how the world and your audiences perceive you. 

🚀 Today, who dares to stand, walk, and talk like an A-lister?
