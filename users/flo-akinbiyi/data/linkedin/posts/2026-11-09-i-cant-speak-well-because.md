“I can’t speak well because English isn’t my first language.”

I hear this all the time as a communication coach.

And it bothers me because I know how easily this belief can stop someone from taking opportunities, speaking up and putting themselves out there. English isn’t my first language either, and I’ve made a career out of speaking.

Guess what? I still make grammar mistakes. I still have moments on stage when I can’t find the perfect word to express my thought. But that doesn’t stop me.

Of course, having a better command of a language makes it easier to express your thoughts. But speaking is a skill on its own, and you can practice it with the English skills that you already have.

Look at Jack Ma, the Co-founder of Alibaba. He uses simple English to tell stories, gets his point across and he speaks with great passion and energy. And therfore people listen when he speaks.

If you struggle with speaking in English, here are three simple things you can do.

1️⃣Improve your English. 
Work on your grammar, build your vocabulary. Get a tutor or a coach and have conversations with them. The more you use it and practice it, the easier it gets.

2️⃣Don’t stop. 
Keep going no matter what. If you can’t find a word, explain what this word means using words you already know. And yes, you can even ask the audience to help you find it. It is an opportunity to interact with the audience and have a bit of fun! 

3️⃣Practice speaking on the spot. 
Pick a topic, set a timer for 60 to 90 seconds and explain it out loud. I use impromptu speaking exercises all the time in my coaching because you get used to working with the words you have under time pressure.

One thing I learned from speaking to international audiences is that simple English often makes it easier for people to follow.

And if your accent bothers you and people can’t understand you, it is a good investment to work on it. If people can understand you, it is not a problem. It is part of your personality. So be okay with it.
