The atmosphere was electric and the DJ - out of this world. 

Seun Ogunleye is a talented Nigerian music professional, known for his vibrant work in the Afro House and electronic dance music scenes.

I had the pleasure of meeting him and was impressed by his warm, engaging energy as people entered.

It’s rare to see someone who can captivate an audience so effortlessly with both his voice and presence.

He was able to read the room and elevate the ambience in a way that made everyone feel part of something bigger.

It felt like a masterclass in connecting with people through both sound and spirit.

Well done Seun Ogunleye, you definitely did a great job at making everyone feel welcome and energized!
