Interview with a legend!
Last week, I had the honor to have a truly eye-opening conversation on stage with Kai Diekmann, the former editor of the German BILD newspaper. 📰

He was responsible for creating powerful and provocative headlines, engaging articles, and exciting interviews for 16 years. ✍️ 

During his time at BILD, Kai interviewed literally everyone who is important, famous, or exciting. 

He had conversations with the Pope 🙏, Lady Gaga 🎤, Donald Trump 🇺🇸, Barack Obama 🌐, Michael Gorbachev 🌍, Boris Yeltsin, Angela Merkel, and the list goes on and on and on.

His insights inspired me to even look at my own brand! We talked about the power of storytelling 📖, how to take your personal brand to the next level, and how to make it more desirable and recognisable. 💡

What is your approach to make your personal brand stand out?

Thanks, Kai, for this engaging conversation.
