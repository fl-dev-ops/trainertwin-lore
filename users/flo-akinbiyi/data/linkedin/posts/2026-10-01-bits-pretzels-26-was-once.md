To host the liquid networking at the Oktoberfest together with Lara Sophie Bothur is every single time a memory that lasts for a lifetime.

But before the session starts, all the table captains meet up at The Teufelsrad - Devil’s Wheel - one of the oldest rides at the Oktoberfest.

Connect, network, and also have a little bit of fun. 

And yes, I did participate in the session.

To kick off the table captain sessions, Lara and I created a human moment.

As Bits & Pretzels theme is human, after all.

We got everybody to participate in the heartbeat of Bits.

Bring everyone together.

A simple moment that makes everybody feel connected and feel the power that 5000 people have when they all come together.

With one mission.

To take the European startup ecosystem to the next level.

Thanks again to everybody from the Bits & Pretzels team for having me.

It's been a blast.
