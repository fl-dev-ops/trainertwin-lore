This is how I refuel for the week. Doing exactly what I enjoy with my family and friends, my favourite way to spend the weekend. 

Three dads, a pack of kids from 4 to 14, and way too many wheels. We spent the weekend at one of those fantastic new skateparks hitting the mini ramps, the bowl, and the rollers. 

For me, being on a board is pure joy. Sharing that with my son and our friends is simply the best feeling in the world. 

But there is a deeper reason why I prioritize this: Joy is my performance fuel. 

In my work, I have to be “on”. But let’s be real, sometimes life gets in the way and I’m not in the best mood. Yet, I still have to step on a stage, deliver a keynote, or lead a high-stakes coaching session. 

When those moments hit, I tap into the joy I felt this weekend. It’s what allows me to shift my energy and become the best version of myself for my audience. 

My heart and soul are filled. I’ve fueled up, and now I’m ready for the week.

What’s one thing you do “just for the fun of it” to fuel your week?
