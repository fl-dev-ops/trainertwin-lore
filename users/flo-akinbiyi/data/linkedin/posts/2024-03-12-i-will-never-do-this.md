I will never do this again! 

Don't allow a bad experience to define your future. 

A bad public speaking experience in front of a live audience can be traumatizing. But you cannot allow such a bad experience to define your future. Unfortunately, I come across too many professionals that shy away from public speaking.

Acknowledging that public speaking can be a daunting task is important. It's one of the most severe anxieties that people have developed. And as Jerry Seinfeld even mentioned, the fear of public speaking is greater than the fear of death. 

But here's the thing. No matter how big the fear is, public speaking offers great opportunities. It gives you exposure, helps you to raise your profile, and win business, creating a better brand for yourself. 

When you're standing on stage, you can motivate people, inspire them, and share stories that create emotional connections and moments people will remember for their entire lives. 

Saying no to these powerful moments is, in my view, simply not an option.

If you're struggling with stage fright, speaking to a professional is a step forward. It can be a public speaking coach, a life coach, or sometimes a psychologist. With the right amount of practice, techniques to calm and manage your nerves, and gradual exposure to more serious engagements, you can overcome this fear. 

This is a shout-out to anyone afraid of public speaking: Don't give up. Roll up your sleeves and make it happen because you deserve it.
