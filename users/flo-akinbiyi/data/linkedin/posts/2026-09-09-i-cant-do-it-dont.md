I can’t do it!

Don’t most of us know this thought and don’t we too often let that thought become our reality?

This summer I got to witness firsthand that our negative thoughts do not need to define us.

I was climbing at the Vertikale in Brixen and while I was training I notice climbers with disabilities.

In front of me was the Italian Paralympic climbing team.

Simone Salvagni is a visually impaired climber who sometimes competes without any visual input, using an eye mask.

David Kammerer climbs with a leg prosthesis.

Florian Mutschlechner climbs with paralysis of the lower body.

And the coach Alberto Mazzoleni makes it happen.

All of them climbing at their limit at world class level on a wall that is already challenging for experienced climbers.

Watching them was such a memorable and powerful moment.

It wasn’t that "look how brave they are", no, watching them made me think about the limits I set for myself, the things I decide are possible or impossible before I have really tried.

We create our own limits. The world gives you challenges and they might look impossible to overcome but with dedication, discipline and determination, we can make it happen.

That's the whole recipe.

What they have is a superpower. But guess what, every single one of them built it.

It's time, dedication, and the willingness to actually want it.

So here's the one line for you today: if these athletes can learn to climb at world-class level, we can all learn how to speak at world-class level.
