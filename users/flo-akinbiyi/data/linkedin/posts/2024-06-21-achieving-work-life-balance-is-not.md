Achieving work-life balance is not easy, but it has to be a priority.

Just to make this clear, I am not talking about 9 - 5 on and 5 - 9 off!

When on business trips, I reserve one or two days just for myself. Whether it's to explore new places, meet with friends, or stay active, these moments are crucial. During a recent trip to Germany for the health.tech conference, I made time to visit my hometown, reconnect with childhood friends, and even take our kids to the mountains where I first learned snowboarding and fell in love with board sports.

These experiences are so rich and powerful that even conducting a webinar on top of a mountain seems perfectly fine. 

I am immensely grateful for the ability to live a life filled with such moments, and it’s what sustains me through intense work periods of 100-plus hours.

What strategies do you use to maintain work-life balance, especially during busy periods?
