Launching the all-new Mercedes-Benz G-Class was a spectacular experience.

The all-new electric Mercedes-Benz G-Class and the Mercedes-AMG G63 are truly iconic cars that, as Mercedes likes to say, are "stronger than time”.  Working with Toni Mäntele, the Product Manager of the G-Class, Michael Stroband, President and Director, CEO of Mercedes-Benz Middle East, Mark Sussner, Chief Instructor, G-Class, Mr. Shehab Gargash, Managing Director and Group CEO of Gargash Group, Thomas Schütz, General Manager, Mercedes-Benz Gargash Enterprises, Ali Gargash and many others, was nothing short of spectacular.

The new G-Class is an outstanding, amazing, emotional car that stays true to its core. The features that the electric G-Class offers are a strong direction for what the automotive industry needs to do: make electric cars cool. Give them features that combustion engine cars won't be able to match. If you haven't seen what the G-Raw, the G-Turn, and the G-Steering can do, then I highly encourage you to check the videos out—because this is just insane to see.

Oh, and congratulations to Gargash Enterprises for their new Mercedes-Benz Off-Road Experience Center, which they are launching soon to the public at Dubai Design District.

Wow, what a spectacular event. Thanks again to the entire team for having me.

Ameen Jaber, Lara Mneimneh, Taniya Chandra
