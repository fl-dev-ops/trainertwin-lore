- What advice would you give to new public speakers on maintaining passion and energy in their presentations, regardless of the topic or their mood?
- Have you ever had a situation where personal challenges affected your performance on stage? How did you handle it?

Passion isn’t just an optional element for public speakers; it’s a must for every memorable speech. You have to be passionate as a public speaker when you’re on stage. I guess we all agree with that statement, but here’s a question for you. Is it easy to be always passionate when you’re on stage? 

Well, it’s easy when I’m at Bitz and Pretzels. However, in other instances, I might not be as excited about the event. It can happen if it’s a dry and technical topic, and maybe I had a bad morning when I had a lengthy discussion with my wife. It doesn’t happen because I am a lucky man and we are a lovey-dovey couple, but let’s just assume that it would happen :) and then my son broke one of my favorite cups in the morning. I shouldn't get angry because he’s five, yet it still upset me. And on top of that, somebody dinged my car or scratched the paint on my car. It’s just materialistic values, but I’m getting more and more upset, so by the time I arrive at the venue, I’m in a bad mood. 

And then you want me to go out on stage and be passionate? I am not going to be at my best when I step onto the stage. Or is there a way to do something else? 

Shall I just fake it? No, faking doesn’t work because eventually, the audience picks up on it. So what I do instead is:

 • Mindset Shift: Either I remind myself that it is an honor to be on a stage in front of an audience, and I park my negativity that I might have brought with me, and as I step onto the stage, I am a new person. 
• Harnessing Energy: And if that doesn’t work, I use energy. And energy is not faking it, because I remind myself of speaking at my best, being energetic, being excited, being on a high, and this is all my personal energy. 

And when I do that, and when I tap into this state of energy, I come across as a passionate speaker. These are some techniques that work well for me. What do you do to be a passionate speaker on stage?
