What is the most effective way to engage an audience when delivering a speech or a presentation? 

Storytelling 💯

Telling stories is one of the most powerful techniques that you can and should use when addressing an audience. 

Yet many professionals struggle to tell stories simply because they are not aware that great stories sit all around them, so they rather go back to stats and data that is very easy to collect instead of looking for the stories that resonate deeply with the audience. 

Well, first of all, one should have a little black book. A little black book is what works so well because as soon as you pass by a great story, a great quote, a great fact, then you want to capture it in your little black book. 

My Little Black Book, for example, is a PowerPoint presentation where I record stories, videos, data, links, great stuff that I can use for my presentations to make them better.

But there are many stories that you can very specifically look out for and then incorporate into your presentations. Stories can be journeys of growth, can be leadership insights, case studies of innovation and creativity, overcoming challenges and failure, talking about the power of connections and collaborations, and the list goes on and on. 

Research shows that stories are what you can and should use to make your presentations more impactful and memorable for the audience. 

Are you using storytelling to keep the audience hooked?
