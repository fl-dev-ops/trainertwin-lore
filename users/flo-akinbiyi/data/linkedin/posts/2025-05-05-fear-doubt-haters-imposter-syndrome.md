Fear. Doubt. Haters. Imposter syndrome. 

Screw it. 

Dare to step out of the box you are keeping yourself in!

Whether it’s in public speaking, your career, or life in general, too often we fall victim to impostor syndrome.

We listen more to the haters than to the ones who offer us honest praise. 

We downplay our abilities. 

We say no to opportunities, not because we can’t, but because we’re afraid.

But real growth starts when you step outside your comfort zone.

Yes, there’s risk. 

Yes, there will be challenges. 

Yes, you might fail.

But if you stay the course and step up your game, growth is guaranteed, and success eventually follows.

As a speaker and coach, I’m constantly pushed out of my comfort zone. 

And years ago, I made a promise to myself: No matter how big the challenge, I will never say no to an opportunity that comes my way.

As Arnold Schwarzenegger said, “Ignore the critics. Work like hell, and trust yourself.”

Are you still in the box… or are you ready to break free?
