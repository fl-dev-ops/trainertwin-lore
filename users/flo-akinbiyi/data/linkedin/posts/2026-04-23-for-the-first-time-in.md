For the first time in five years, I stopped posting.

In a world of uncertainty, fear and horrific communication I chose silence. I truly felt that more important than a business strategy, marketing or a sales activity, is that I am are aligned with my values and feelings.

That’s why I chose in the midst of everything that happens to remain silent.
And believe me there were some communications that I wanted to tell the world what I think of them, but I decided not to added more fuel to fire.
Now I have reached the stage where I am okay to start again post on social media.

The main focus will be on sharing knowledge, tools, observations and frameworks that help professionals to communicate better.
I’m going to double down on my philosophy of human-first communication, because if the world needs one thing, in my opinion, it is a more human way of sharing this planet together.
Get ready for some good stuff!

Sending much love and peace to everyone.
