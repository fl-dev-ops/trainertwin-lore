Over the last 10 days, I have slept in two different countries, three hotels, and have been in three different cities. 

On Tuesday, I'm traveling again. I'm going to two countries, two cities, and out again for another seven days, maybe even eight days. 

Very often I get asked, How do you do that? How do you stay calm, centered, composed? Where do you get the energy from? 

By taking care of myself. 

I try to eat as healthy as possible, which is very difficult when traveling. I try to meditate, which is also very difficult for me to have that routine and that consistency. 

But what I do not compromise is to allow myself and give myself a break from everything. 

After six hours of rehearsal, before I go back to the computer and do some more script writing, I will go climbing. 

Climbing is a fascinating sport, not only because it's super difficult and it takes quite some time to get really good at it, but also when you're on the wall, your brain stops thinking. 

I believe it must be because my body is in a position where it thinks survival is what we need to focus on right now, not thinking about any problems or anything else that is going on. That's why I love this sport, because it gives me the opportunity to switch off while staying fit.

If anyone else has some amazing travel tips for staying fresh and healthy besides vitamins, let me know.
