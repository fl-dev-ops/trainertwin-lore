What an amazing first day of Bits & Pretzels Founders Festival 2026.

Started with hosting the pre-rounds of the Golden Pretzel Startup Competition and excited for this afternoon to see who wins!

They're all great.

I also did some pitch training to give the participants one last elevation to their pitch.

A highlight of the day was meeting Trevor Noah - a great moment and a reminder that the most powerful communicators do more than speak well. 

They help people see a familiar idea in a new way.

One of Trevor Noah’s points that stayed with me was this: value is always evolving.​

What we take from a difficult situation often depends on our disposition.​

A setback can stay a setback, or become a lesson that changes how we move forward.

Looking forward to hosting Oktoberfest tomorrow! Catch you there!
