Sometimes, all it takes is one word to change everything - even in the middle of a freezing surf session.

Two weeks ago, my buddy Andreas Stocker and I decided to go surfing at O₂ SURFTOWN MUC . 

It was already the end of September, which means the water was at a chilling 15°C. I didn’t use my thinner Dubai wetsuit, instead I rented a thicker one so I could comfortably surf for an hour.

I have to admit, I’m in love with surfing and board sports.

But I’m not the best surfer on the planet, so the setup at O2 SURFTOWN makes the experience better for me.

They have a coach who helps you position yourself and gives you instructions on when and how to paddle.

This coach, Michael Berger  though, did a little bit more.

During the briefing, he made us all laugh, instantly lightening up the mood.
Once we were in the water, his strong body language made it easy to trust him.
We knew exactly where to go and when to start.

Everything got better after the first wave I missed.

With very clear and minimal instructions, he corrected the mistakes that had been preventing me from catching waves:

🏄🏽‍♂️Whether it was hectic strokes instead of powerful ones
🏄🏽‍♂️A wrong positioning in the pool
🏄🏽‍♂️Or a slightly incorrect angle

With just one or two words of feedback, he helped me improve on the spot.
Thank you for making this surf session so much better, Michael Mohr and to O2 SURFTOWN for hiring such amazing people.

And a quick reminder to all of you:
The way we speak and the words we use can change everything. Inspiration is everywhere - you just have to find it!
