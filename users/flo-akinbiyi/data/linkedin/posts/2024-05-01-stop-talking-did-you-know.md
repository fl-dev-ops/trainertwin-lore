Stop talking! 
Did you know that the saying from Frankfurt, Germany, "bubblenet" means just that?

I had to smile when I found this sign in the hotel, and I had to take a photo of it. It truly reminded me of how many presentations you listen to where somebody just keeps on rambling.

How often have you sat at a gala dinner, and the speaker went on and on and on, and all you wanted to do was eat?

How often were you listening to a birthday or a wedding speech, and you wished they would just keep it more concise, more consolidated, and not make it all about themselves?

Yes, when you talk about speaking, it's important to speak. But it's also important to be silent, to sometimes be a bit more concise instead of taking up too much space.

Shortening your content, editing it, takes time and effort. But what many underestimate is that when you edit your content, it becomes sharper, it becomes better, and it becomes more effective.

So in the words of the people of Frankfurt, remind yourself to "bubblenet"!
