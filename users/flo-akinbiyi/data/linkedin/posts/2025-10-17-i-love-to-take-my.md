I love to take my son to work.

Being a dad is truly the most amazing gift I’ve ever received from my wife.

What looks here like a professional family photo shoot actually wasn’t. I had a work photo shoot scheduled on the weekend but was still committed to spend time with my son.

So I decided to simply bring him along and tell the photographers, make up and producer that they need to now deal with having a child on the set.

And, of course, took the opportunity to take some photos with him.

It was so much fun to see how he enjoyed the entire experience.

How he connected with the people that were there.

And how he, eventually, was brave enough to set in front of the camera and take a few photos without daddy.

Let’s all be sometimes a little bit less professional, and make work a lot more joyful by bringing our children to work. I think it would be good for the world.
