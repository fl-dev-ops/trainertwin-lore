Celebrating Oscar Badibanga Journey to Becoming a Fashion Designer and Launching His Own Brand!

It does not matter how long your journey is, what matters is that you embark on it and see it through to the end. Today, I want to celebrate my friend, designer Oscar Badibanga. After years of honing his craft at world-renowned brands like Levi’s, Timberland, and Bottega Veneta, Oscar launched his own brand, BADIBANGA, with a vibrant event in Dubai. His first collection sold out quickly online, and just a few months later, he’s making waves at Copenhagen Fashion Week with his second collection.

Oscar’s story is a testament to never giving up on your dreams and sticking with your ambitions, no matter the challenges. As an entrepreneur and public speaker, I am profoundly inspired by his determination and spirit. Oscar, you are a shining example, and I am proud to call you my friend. Congratulations!

Be sure to check out his social media channels and website for more inspiring designs and stories.
