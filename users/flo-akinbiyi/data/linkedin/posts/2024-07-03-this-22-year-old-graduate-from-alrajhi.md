This 22-year-old graduate from alrajhi bank just gave me the best business hack of the year. 
Sounds unbelievable. 

Here’s the story: after our workshop, Rakan Albadeen showed me how he’s mastered Notion. He demonstrated how he captured insights from our sessions, streamlined his academic efforts, and organized his personal life—all in one tool. 

Rakan casually asked if I had a similar system. To my embarrassment, I admitted I didn’t. 

Here’s all the tools that I was working with before:

	•	Dropbox: for storage and filing
	•	Apple Notes: for taking meeting notes, capturing thoughts and ideas
	•	Microsoft Word: for script writing and other collaborative documents
	•	Microsoft Excel: for simple budgets and travel agendas
	•	Miro: for our shared content calendar and video editing
	•	Trello: for project management 

Notion, however, could consolidate all these tasks and more into one platform. I’m just beginning to explore the capabilities of Notion, from integrating AI to setting up automation.

It’s amazing what you can learn when you keep an open mind. Thanks, Rakan, for the introduction to Notion!

If there are any Notion pros out there, I’d love to hear your best use cases and tips & tricks. How do you make Notion work for your business?
