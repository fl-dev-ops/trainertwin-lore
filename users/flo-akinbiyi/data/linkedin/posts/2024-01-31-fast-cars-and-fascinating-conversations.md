Fast Cars and Fascinating Conversations: A Weekend to Remember at the Porsche AG Carrera Cup Middle East!

🏁 What a Weekend!
This weekend was Round 4 of the Porsche Carrera Cup Middle East in Abu Dhabi. I had the opportunity to host the Porsche Hospitality Suite for Porsche Center Abu Dhabi and Al Ain. And I have to say- it was an absolute amazing adventure. 🎉

🌟 The Yas Marina Circuit Experience
Being at Yas Marina Circuit is always a highlight for me as I've worked for so many great brands and did so many amazing events. So that was already the icing on the cake. 🍰

🤝 Meeting the Porsche Community
The drivers I met, the team I spoke with, the Porsche enthusiasts I interacted with all have such a wonderful spirit, which is truly unique during this day and age. 🚗 To name a few - Janne stiek, Colin Boenighausen, Jackson Walls, James Wallis, and Marcel Kiefer.

🏎️ A Spin on the Track
I also had time to go around the racetrack in a Porsche 718 and 911 - oh boy, I enjoyed that experience- it was amazing! Don't worry, all cars are still in one piece, my driving skills are decent. 😄

💡 Insights from Champions
And best of all, from my chats with the drivers, I have insights to share on how to develop a strong mindset, stay focused, and become a champion at whatever you do. I'll share them in the next weeks. 🏆

🙏 Thanks to the Porsche and Jack Morton Team
Thanks again to the entire Porsche team for having me. It was so good seeing old friends and making new ones. 👋
