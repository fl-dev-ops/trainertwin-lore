AI is evolving at a rapid pace and it will soon enough allow everyone to have great content. 

That means that everybody can deliver a good speech, a great presentation, and beautiful visuals. So what makes us then stand out of the crowd?

- It is all about you. 
- It is about you, the presenter. 
- It’s about you, the speaker. 

It’s about you, the person standing in front of the audience. It is how you get this beautifully crafted content across and make it your own. It’s about showing the audience who you really are and delivering it with your personal energy, spirit, and enthusiasm that no one else can replicate. 

Authenticity is a trend that we can see evolve over the last decades. Go back and look at political speeches, and company presentations from the 50s, 60s, 70s, 80s, and beyond and see how speaking becomes more casual, personal, and authentic. 

If you are still hiding behind a corporate facade, it is time to dismantle it and present your authentic self to the world.
