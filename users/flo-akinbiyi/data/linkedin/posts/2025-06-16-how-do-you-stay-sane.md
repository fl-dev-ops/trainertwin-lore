How do you stay sane when everything feels like it’s falling apart?

For me, it’s meditation.

2025 has been one of the most challenging years of my life.

I’ve had to navigate a deep personal crisis.

And like many of you, I’m watching a world that feels more unstable by the day - wars, elections, economic shifts, uncertainty everywhere.

Through all of this, meditation has become a daily grounding ritual.

It took me years to commit. I dabbled. I dropped it. I told myself I was too busy.

But then a coach said something that hit me in the chest:

👉 “You need to learn to take yourself more seriously than anything else in the world.”

That was the shift I needed.

Since then, I’ve been meditating almost every day.

Sometimes 5 minutes, sometimes 60.

And it’s changed everything.

More calm. More clarity. More strength to show up for others.

I’m also grateful to my brother, Leonard Akinbiyi, who first shared this tool with me over 20 years ago.

Sometimes it takes a lifetime to listen.

🧘🏾‍♂️ A few things I remind myself through meditation:
	•	Your brain will produce thoughts. You choose which ones to hold on to.
	•	Breath is not just rhythm, it’s life itself.
	•	See things for what they are, without needing to judge, fix, or resist.

Have you ever tried meditation?

If so, what does it do for you?
