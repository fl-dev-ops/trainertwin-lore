It’s been about four decades since I’ve been to my father’s land, Nigeria. I grew up for most of my life without my dad, so I didn’t have many chances to connect with my Nigerian heritage.

Later on, when we reconnected, he was living in London, so every time we met, it was in the UK, not Nigeria. 

And although I’m close with family in Nigeria and they come to visit me in Dubai, which is always a huge pleasure (thank you, guys!) 

I still haven’t found the courage, the opportunity, and the right moment to visit Nigeria myself.

That’s why this photo means so much more than what it might look like to most people. 

It’s not just a photo with Dr. ‘Bosun Tijani, the Minister of Communications, Innovation and Digital Economy of Nigeria - a man who’s working hard to transform his country. Meeting him is another step closer to coming home.

And the fact that this happened at GITEX EUROPE, here in Germany, where I am also at home, makes it even more magical. 

Both Germany and Nigeria are home to me, and I can’t wait for GITEX NIGERIA.

I truly hope I get the opportunity to bring a bit of the Dubai spirit to my father’s land, share a touch of the German organization, and enjoy reconnecting with my roots while witnessing innovation and progress in the making.
