Right before stepping onto the stage, Enrico Galliorini said two words to me.
I have not stopped thinking about them since.

𝗦𝘁𝗿𝗼𝗻𝗴 𝗮𝗻𝗱 𝗳𝗿𝗲𝗲.

We were about to walk onto the stage of the Human2Human Summit together.
And those words landed deeply.

I used to always say “knock em dead”.
An old verbal high five from the theatre world.
I still like the energy behind it, but if I am honest, it carries a slightly aggressive tone.

Strong and free felt different.
More grounded. More human.

It is something Enrico’s father or grandfather used to say.
And it resonated with me because of what it represents.

Strong to speak with confidence, clarity, passion and power.
Free to enjoy the moment, to be yourself, to not perform but express.

It is a pre-speaking ritual.

Before an important meeting.
Before a keynote.
Before a difficult conversation.

A mantra is there to help you show up as the best version of yourself.

Choose one that is more meaningful than: Good luck!
Use it consistently and teach it to the people around you!

Let these be the words that ground and guide you.

𝗦𝘁𝗿𝗼𝗻𝗴 𝗮𝗻𝗱 𝗳𝗿𝗲𝗲.

Do you have a mantra?
One that brings out the best version of yourself before you speak.
