After long and exciting days I am heading home to Bavaria for work.
 
I always find it interesting how a place you know so well can suddenly feel new when you arrive with a schedule, a purpose, and different people around you.
 
Same city. Different lens.


But first the mountains, then the second event.
Snowboarding tomorrow, because nothing resets the mind quite like cold air, a full day outside, and a bit of speed.
 
I have not seen my friends at home for almost a year, so I am thrilled to see them!
 
Those moments with friends and family have a way of grounding everything else, reminding you what actually matters, and why you do what you do in the first place.
 
Work, movement, nature, people. 
That balance never gets old.

Here’s my question for the comments: If you had 2 hours in Munich to “fall back in love with the city” (no tourist spots, no obvious answers), where would you go, and what would you do?
