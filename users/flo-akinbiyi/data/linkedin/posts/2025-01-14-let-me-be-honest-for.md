Let me be honest.

For the last year, we’ve been struggling with staffing. Finding the right people for our unique team setup has been a challenge. We tried and failed - again and again.

Just yesterday, someone we had high hopes for was supposed to start. I was excited about the possibilities of working with them and how we could elevate the business together. But in the morning, they didn’t show up. Instead, they sent a message saying they wouldn’t join us.

One could feel disappointed, upset, or even angry.

We’ll take a moment to reflect.

Reflect on the lessons, the patterns, and what we can do better. And then, as entrepreneurs do, we move forward. Challenge accepted.

Today, I’m sitting with my business manager, strategizing and finding new approaches to build a superstar team. We’re also embracing new opportunities, which now include AI employees to help us scale up, level up, and grow our business in ways we hadn’t imagined before.

Here’s to 2025, with all its challenges and opportunities. We’re ready for you.
