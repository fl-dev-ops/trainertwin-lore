The European startup ecosystem is on fire 🔥 

Day one of Bits & Pretzels Founders Festival was absolutely amazing. We had an incredible number of speakers from politics, from the startup ecosystem, as well as inspirational speakers that uplifted the audience. 

I truly enjoyed the messages, promises and support from politicians such as Katherina Reiche, Dorothee Bär and Dr. Fabian Mehring, who were all on our inspiration state, and showed not only the opportunities that are there, but also the commitment that it needs from all sides to continue to connect Europe.

Andy Bruckschloegl, Bernd Storm vans Gravesande and Felix Haas delivered, as always, a wonderful opening and the Bits & Pretzels team were as brilliant as they always are, and hosting the stage with Céline Flores Willers was a true blast - and I’m not only saying that because we shot the T-shirt canon quite a few times. 

Can’t wait for day two, let’s go!
