4 days - 2 countries - 2 trainings - 2 industries - 70 plus participants

In the last few days I spent time in Budapest and Riyadh coaching Aesthetic Doctors, and Graduates from the banking industry.

Whether they are just months in their first job or have decades under their belt, to elevate your communication skills is a benefit to anyone. Nothing is more beautiful and magical to observe than how professionals are able within hours to deliver more impactful communication. And it is so inspiring to see how much effort the groups and the individuals put in.

One thing that always humbles me is how everyone's journey is different. Some come into the workshops with really strong skills and others are at the beginning. Tthe feedback that this was not just another educational session or another training but it added value to their professional or private life is just simply delicious.

And another icing on the cake was that I got to hang out with Istvan Busak who I haven’t seen in almost a decade!

Thanks RELIFE Aesthetics, alrajhi bank and Exceed for working with me!
