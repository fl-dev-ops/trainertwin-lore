Millions of people hear announcements every single day that they completely ignore.

But this station manager in the London Underground made everybody listen. 

Ten years ago, during rush hour, I was in London, waiting in line to get into the Tube, and I found myself watching this guy in absolute awe.

He turned a completely forgettable communication into an impactful human moment.

The way he speaks makes people smile, pay attention, and even feel better about themselves.

He turned an announcement, which was a safety briefing and information, into powerful communication.

And that blows my mind.

99% of the people who do the same job as he does are just transporting information, while he's changing the emotional state of the audience.

What fascinated me even more is that, as I told that story over the next decade to many audiences, there were a few people from the UK who had seen him as well. They all remembered him and how he made them feel.

That says everything.

We can use our communication to make people feel something, no matter what the content is.

We can use it as well to transport emotions.

There are several wonderful examples of station managers and train announcers in the UK who do this really brilliantly, and I drop two links in the comments.

A masterclass in communication that is effective, memorable, and human-first.

Have you ever experienced someone like this?
