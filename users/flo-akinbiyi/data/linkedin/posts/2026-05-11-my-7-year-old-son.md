My 7 year old son taught me this weekend a new image generating feature on ChatGPT.

And when I reflected on it he actually taught me something about AI that most professionals still don’t understand.

As adults we use AI to optimize the old world.
Children use AI to imagine new worlds.

For many professionals, AI is mainly A productivity tool. To him as a child it is a creative collaborator. It is also a productivity tool but it is a knowledge base. It is so much more to him and that is the shift.

Most professionals approach AI with efficiency, automation, faster emails, summaries, workflow optimization and cost reduction.
And yes, it is great with that but it is still limited.
I love how my son is drawn to approach AI with imagination, experimentation, play, storytelling, curiosity and that mindset of “what happens if I were to...”
Should we move away from seeing AI just as a revolutionary technology that allows us to optimize the world and more and realize that we can create completely new worlds?

Maybe the AI revolution is not just about being more efficient but about becoming more imaginative, more creative and maybe even more human again.

What have you lately imagined with AI?
