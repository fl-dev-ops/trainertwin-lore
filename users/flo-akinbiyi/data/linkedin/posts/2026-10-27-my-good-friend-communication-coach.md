My good friend, Communication Coach and MC Flo Akinbiyi invited me to join his communication workshop for DHL station managers, to work with them on listening and interpersonal communication.

In the morning, each of them stood up and presented themselves. Then they did it again. And again. By the afternoon, the room was different.

One participant in particular. In the morning, honestly, she didn't catch my attention. In the afternoon, I was in awe. Her presence, her authority, her passion. 

Same person, same day, very different presentation. The three rounds of Flo's coaching made a huge difference.

Her content changed. But above all, what changed was how aware she was of her impact, and so how she used her resources: her voice, her emphasis, her melody. 

Rosemercy Wanjiku. You are inspiration!

I learned a lot that day; from Flo, from all and each of the participants, and from Flo's feedback to me.
Thank you, Flo, thank you DHL and the station managers for such a vibrant day!
