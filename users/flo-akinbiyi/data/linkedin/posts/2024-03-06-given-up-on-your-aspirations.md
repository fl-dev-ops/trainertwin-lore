Given up on your aspirations, dreams and goals? 🥺

It is now the beginning of March, and many have already forgotten about their New Year's resolutions. 

This year, I'm taking my goal-setting game, together with my brother Leonhard  " Leo " Akinbiyi, to a whole new level - we have teamed up and are accountability partners. 

We do a monthly review of our goals, our areas of improvement and our achievements. We celebrate our progress and celebrate ourselves; which is easier said than done - happens so often that one of us diminishes the progress made. 

“Ah yeah good but it’s nothing special...”

It’s about celebrating every little victory! 🎉

And as we do this together, we challenge ourselves. 💪

This one monthly check-in of only an hour makes a huge difference in how I look at my goals, how I've progressed with my goals, and how I am working towards achieving them.

It is simply an incredibly powerful, inspirational and invaluable hour that we spend together. I can only recommend finding yourself an accountability partner. 💯

Don't get on this journey by yourself because with a team, everything is easier. 👈

And your New Year's resolutions can actually turn into reality!
