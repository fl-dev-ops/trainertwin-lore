The biggest risk of AI is not that it will replace your voice. It is that you will hand it over before anyone asks.

AI can help us write faster.

It can shape an email, build a first draft, organise our ideas and even help us prepare for difficult conversations.

But it cannot take responsibility for how a message lands.

It cannot read the room. It cannot notice hesitation. It cannot build trust after a difficult decision. And it cannot replace the human being behind the message.
That is why I am looking forward to delivering training with gate - Garchinger Technologie- und Gründerzentrum on AI and human-first communication.

We will look at how to use AI as a useful thinking and preparation tool, while protecting the things that make communication matter. The question is not whether AI will change the way we communicate.

It already has.

The real question is whether we will use it to become more efficient, or more effective with people.

Looking forward to seeing you there!
