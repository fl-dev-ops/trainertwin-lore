Chef Ian Pengelley knows how to engage the audience like no one else. 

At The Saudi Food Show - The Kingdom’s Largest F&B Sourcing Event's top table, he hosted a masterclass. 

Although he had lost his voice, he engaged the audience like no other. 

He turned the masterclass into a true experience by involving the audience in various ways:
•⁠ ⁠Allowing them to taste ingredients
•⁠ ⁠Inviting them behind the counter to grind some spices
•⁠ ⁠Answering their questions
•⁠ ⁠Making them laugh
•⁠ ⁠⁠and much more.

Ian is such a great example that public speaking skills are a crucial element to being successful in whatever you do. 

As a chef, his public speaking skills allow him to communicate well with his team, suppliers, and with his guests.

You see, when he's in an environment like a masterclass, he shines. And his team loves him, which also says a lot. Hats off, Ian. You're an inspiration.
