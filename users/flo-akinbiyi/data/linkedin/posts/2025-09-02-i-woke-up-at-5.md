I woke up at 5 AM to visit the Roman ruins of Tipaza.
 
At the end of my work trip to Algeria last week, I took the opportunity to explore the remains of Tipaza - I love visiting remains of ancient civilizations.

Standing in an amphitheater that is more than 2000 years old is it truly humbling and inspiring experience. 

Whether you reflect on the speeches delivered there or even sad events that have taken plance, you get inspired by how the ancient Romans understood the power of public speaking and it's ability to create moments that stay with the audience for a lifetime.
