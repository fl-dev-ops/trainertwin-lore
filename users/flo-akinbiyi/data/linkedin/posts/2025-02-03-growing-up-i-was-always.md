Growing up, I was always comfortable with my hands in my pockets. I kept them there because I wanted to be cool. I kept them there because they made me feel safe. I kept them there because I was shy.

But when it comes to speaking to people, having your hands in your pockets is never a good idea.

When you speak with your hands in your pockets, you miss the chance to make your talk more engaging, effective, and impactful.

Worse? You’re not shaping how your audience sees you, and they are seeing you. Ask 10 people, and you’ll get 10 different opinions. One will think you’re shy, another will think you’re nervous. One will say you’re cool, another will see you as arrogant. Someone else might think you don’t care at all.

But when you use gestures, you take control of your message. You show confidence. You help your audience understand you better. You emphasize key points. And if you’re a pro, you might even develop your own signature gesture.

So, what will it be - hands in pockets or hands in action?
