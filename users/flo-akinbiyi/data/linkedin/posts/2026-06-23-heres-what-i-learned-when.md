Here's what I learned when I danced on stage with America's Got Talent's host Terry Crews. 

The lesson wasn't about dancing. The lesson wasn't also about confidence. 

During the One Young World Closing Ceremony in 2025 at the BMW Welt in Munich, I was on stage with Terry Crews, Rebecca King-Crews and the co-founders of One Young World, Kate Robertson and David Jones. Part of the event was the amazing ribbon ceremony and a Bavarian brass band would play music, specifically the song Sweet Caroline. As the ribbon ceremony was happening, the band was playing, many were singing. Terry started dancing on stage. I'm not a dancer. I know that Terry is.

And in this moment I committed.

Terry showed me in that moment a skill that I have seen in many celebrities and A-list professionals. Without any hesitation, he jumped right away in and gave the audience an enjoyable moment. And so did I. 

The lesson is about commitment and focusing on the audience. Many corporate speakers I see are more worried about themselves. How would I feel if I try out something new with the audience? How will I feel if I tell this story? How do I look if I do something cool and memorable with the audience? However, if you commit to do something for the audience, let me repeat that, for the audience, which truly adds value and makes the experience better, then you have an advantage. 

As a communicator, you should look at the audience, observe them, ask questions, walk off the stage and talk to them, do interactions. That is where the real magic happens. Most presenters spend a lot of time in preparing a comfortable presentation, which is a safe monologue. Instead of delivering an experience that the audience enjoys and remembers. 

Great performers like Terry Crews have understood that these moments are not about you, they are about the experience of the audience. So here's my invitation for you all to reflect. Are you asking yourself, how do I look? What will they think about me? Or are you asking yourself, what does this moment need to be a memorable and exceptional experience for the audience?
