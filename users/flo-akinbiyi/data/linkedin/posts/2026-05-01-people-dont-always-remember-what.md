People don't always remember what you said…
…but they remember exactly how they felt about you in the first five seconds after meeting you.

Although the saying is a bit worn out, you never get a second chance to make a first impression.
It is one of the most underutilized elements in communication.

The first impression matters, but I experience every day:
Handshakes with no energy.
A good morning that feels like a task or a chore.
A business interaction that jumps straight into business with zero human connection.

It's all communication that is not effective, not memorable, and not setting you up to be successful.

Being neutral is not safe, it's actually forgettable!

I have been experimenting with the first impression consciously for a couple of years.
The more I tried and tested, the more ways I discovered to change how people interact with me and how they feel about me within seconds.

And the crazy thing is that if they feel good about me, they also feel better about themselves.

To be honest, the techniques are surprisingly simple yet truly game-changing.

And yes, I have even used them on and behind the stage with the former President of Botswana, H.E. Mokgweetsi Eric Masisi at the Bits & Pretzels Founders Festival.

I've built a simple toolbox of techniques that shift the way people feel about you instantly, and I'll start sharing them over the next weeks.

I'm curious, do you actively shape your first impression, or do you leave it up to chance?
