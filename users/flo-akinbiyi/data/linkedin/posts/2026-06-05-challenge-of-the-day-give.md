Challenge of the day: Give as many honest compliments as possible.

My trip to Budapest to the RELIFE Train The Trainer offers me the perfect opportunity. I always interact with people, but today I'm taking it a step further and creating as many positive interactions as I can.

There is one important rule though, I don't want to disturb people, be that annoying overjoyed traveler, and every single compliment has to be genuine, honest, and authentic.

It is fascinating if you read up about the science and studies of giving compliments. We often misinterpret how the might receivers feel and we underestimate how giving compliments makes us feel more positive and how it lightens the load on whatever worries we are carrying.

I already today gave a compliment to:
The lady at the check-in counter who looked really great after a 12-hour shift.

The four immigration officers about the wonderful customer experience they have created at the security check.

I thanked Dharma, the toilet attendant from Nepal, for his service and kindness with a Shubha Prabhat.

And this is just the beginning.

Giving compliments is a skill that is valuable everywhere, whether it's in your private life, professional life, in a meeting, at a network event, or even on a big stage. It trains you to pay attention to the people in front of you, and that is truly how you become a better communicator.

Who wants to join me on my challenge?
