Speaking with a monotone voice can kill your message! 

Too often, professionals underestimate the impact their voice has in a positive and in a negative way. 

When you speak to an audience with a monotone voice, your content better be spectacular, world-class, wonderfully crafted, and relevant for every single member of the audience. 

Because if it isn’t, what naturally will happen is that people switch off.
Goodbye – you lost them!

And not only that, with a monotone voice, you are telling the audience that you actually don’t care! 

You’re just here to deliver the message, but you don’t really give them the feeling that you’re making an effort and that this topic is important to you.
Your voice shows confidence, competence, and passion. 

You can use it to highlight information, to create effects and to convey emotion. When you speak with a monotone voice none of that happens!
All you need to do is use voice modulation, playing around with your voice and mixing it up! 

Then, you have a much better chance to keep the attention of the audience, for them to listen to your messages, and to for them to actually care about what you are saying!
