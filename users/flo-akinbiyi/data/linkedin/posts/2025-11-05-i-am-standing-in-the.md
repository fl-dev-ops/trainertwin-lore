I am standing in the middle of the hall and from the side I see a group of people walking towards me with Anna Wintour in the center.

I step to the side, look at her, smile, and say, Hello.

And the woman, who is referred to as the Devil Wears Prada, turns her head to the side, looks at me, smiles, and says “HELLO”, as she passes by.

About an hour later, I have a moment at the meet-and-greet, and as I approach her, she looks at me and says, “Oh! I remember you, I saw you earlier.”

They may say many things about Anna Wintour, but what I observed was a truly inspiring woman. She travelled to Dubai to inspire women at the  WE Convention.

She left a memorable impression on everybody who engaged with her.

She was kind, she was patient, she was empathetic.
And I can only say, Ms. Wintour, it was a true honor to meet you!
