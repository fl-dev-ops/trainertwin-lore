I’ve been using Slido for the last four years to make workshops and online webinars more engaging, to involve the audience while hosting large events, and to create fun, entertaining, and educational moments at various events. The software is simply fantastic. It’s so easy, fast, and efficient to set up an event. It’s just wonderful how flexible you can be in changing content during an event, which is immediately reflected. And it’s outstanding how simple the handling as a presenter or as an operator is. 

Why Slido stands out: 
• Easy Setup: Quick and efficient, it’s incredibly straightforward to get your event ready. 
• Flexibility: You can change content during the event, which updates instantly. 
• User-Friendly: Simple for both presenters and participants to use. 

Additional benefits include: 
• Increased participation encourages active involvement through polls and Q&A. 
• Improved retention as interactive elements help solidify understanding and learning. 
• Greater satisfaction, as participants feel more connected and valued, enhancing their overall experience. 

Oh, and by the way, if you haven’t yet tried using a quiz at your events, you’re missing out on seeing just how much fun your audience can have. 

Thank you, Slido, for always being there for us and making our life easier and our events and training better. Can’t wait to use it again at the next event after the summer break.
