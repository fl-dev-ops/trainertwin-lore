Happy Birthday to Me! 🎉🎂

This year, I’m working on my birthday - a first in a long time. For as many years as I can remember, I’ve always avoided working on my birthday. I’d take the day off to celebrate, do something fun, and live my best life.

But this year, my birthday falls right in the middle of Abu Dhabi Finance Week, so taking the day off just isn’t an option.

As I stand on stage today, I choose to feel grateful:
	•	Grateful for the job I’ve created for myself
	•	Grateful for the incredible people I meet
	•	Grateful for the audiences I get to connect with and inspire
	•	Grateful for the topics I get to learn about
	•	And grateful for the amazing places I get to travel to

While I hope the boss (yes, myself 😉) lets me take next year’s birthday off again, I’m determined to make this day an epic one - one I’ll remember for a long time. 

And having great people around me from Hub71, ADGM, CPI Business and a brilliant show crew helps!

Because at the end of the day, life is what we make of it.

Happy Birthday to Me! 🎉
