How do you become a World Champion?

Insight from the world champion Coach Gordon Herbert!
Recently, I had the incredible opportunity to meet Gordon, the coach of the World Champion German National basketball team, at the health.tech conference. Our backstage conversation and his on-stage presence and content were both enlightening and inspiring.

🏀 Gordon’s Leadership Lessons:
•⁠  ⁠Open Door Policy: "My players can approach me anytime; it's crucial they feel heard."
•⁠  ⁠Team Building: "We invest heavily in activities that enhance team cohesion and performance."
•⁠  ⁠Vision and Ambition: "Having a clear, bold vision is essential. Dream big, and pursue those dreams relentlessly."

Gordon's approach has led his team to impressive heights: third place at the FIBA European Championship, and FIBA World Cup Champions in 2023, and now they're eyeing a Gold medal at the Olympics in Paris.

The most striking takeaway? Gordon's philosophy: "I'd rather try to tame a lion than teach a cat how to roar."
This statement resonated deeply with me, as it helps to understand whether you have an A-Player or a B-Player in front of you.

Are you a lion or a cat? This is a question I will now ask in every interview to understand the drive and ambition of the individual.
