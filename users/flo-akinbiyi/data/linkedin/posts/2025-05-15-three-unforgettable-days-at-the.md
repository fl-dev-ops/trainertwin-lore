Three unforgettable days at The Saudi Food Show - The Kingdom’s Largest F&B Sourcing Event.

Hosting this year’s TopTable together with Dania Musallam was an absolute blast!

We explored how the Kingdom is evolving its food production from farming organic local fish to diving deep into aquaculture and hydroponics (yes, that’s the term for growing vegetables in water and it’s fascinating).

We discovered coffee trends from Saudi Arabia, the region, and around the globe.

And we shared the stage with international, regional, and Saudi chefs who are reimagining dishes with their own creative twist.

The creativity, passion, and attention to detail always blow me away when I see chefs in action.

Huge thanks to the entire team for making this such a smooth, inspiring, and magical experience on the TopTable stage once again.
