The NIO House Public Speaking Workshop was one of my all-time favorites. 

This collaboration with NIO Deutschland allowed me to get familiar with a spectacular car brand, share my knowledge, experience, and insights with the participants of the workshop, and I got to drive the NIO EP6 around Germany.

The workshop that we hosted in April in Frankfurt was so much fun. 
And we were fully booked!

The participants were super engaged and asked many questions.
* I shared with them my signature framework for professional introductions 
* The power of your speaking voice
* Hacks for confident body language on and off stage
* How to get your message across in a way that the audience understands and remembers
* We had the live pitching session where participants got feedback onsite

And on top of that, four lucky participants that won the raffle get to do an AI Voice Profile with a one-on-one session with me. During the AI Voice Profile session, we look at the voice recording and with the science based AI profile discuss the level of charisma, do exercises, give suggestions, and show them how they can take their voice to new heights.

At the end of the day you can use your voice to sound more competent, confident, and passionate you only need to know how.

I truly love brand collaborations where we get to make an impact for the participants, and when I get to drive a car, it's even better. 

And a huge shout out to my brother, who was not only filming but also delivering a spectacular pitch Leonhard  " Leo " Akinbiyi. You rock!
