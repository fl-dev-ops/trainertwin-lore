After this week I feel inspired and filled with hope.

Hosting the Intercultural Innovation Award in Milan has truly shifted my perspective. 

It changed and I’m not gonna lie about it, from a slightly anxious and fearful mindset onto one that is looking with a strong conviction into the future.

Every day I’m still continuing to reflect on the words of His Excellency Miguel Ángel Moratinos, the High Representative of United Nations Alliance of Civilizations and UN Under-Secretary-General, who said pessimism is the snobism of the 21st century. 

And he is so right. It is so easy to indulge yourself in negativity. To jump on board the bad ship. Or start whining and complaining. 

Being optimistic is very often hard work. But it can be a conscious choice. And I for many years of my life have chosen to be optimistic and will continue to do so. 

And what I find quite funny is that I’ve heard way too often that I shouldn’t be always that optimistic. While I say nowadays, why? Why not? 

Wouldn’t the world be a better place if we all were a little bit more optimistic about overcoming challenges? 

These are my thoughts to wrap up this week. If they resonate with you, let me know how you continue to stay optimistic.
