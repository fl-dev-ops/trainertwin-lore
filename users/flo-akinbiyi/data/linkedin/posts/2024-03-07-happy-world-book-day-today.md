📚 Happy World Book Day! 🎉

Today, I'm celebrating with my son’s British school, a book that has profoundly inspired me: "The ABC of Body Language" by Samy Molcho. 

Despite its size and 111 pages, this book has been a significant source of inspiration. More than 15 years ago, Samy Molcho's insights opened a new world for me, long before my journey as a communication coach began. 💡

Back then, hosting funsports events was my world. Yet, the desire to learn about communication and in that case body language was already wide awake within me. This curiosity eventually found its place. Today, as a communication coach, I understand the immense value of all communication aspects - from body language to tone of voice, to structuring content, storytelling, engaging with the audience, and adding that wow factor. 🗣️

I am truly fascinated about body language. Whether we're conscious of it or not, our bodies are always communicating. 

A never ending THANK YOU to Samy Molcho for the inspiration. And once again, “Happy World Book Day” to everyone! 📖❤️
