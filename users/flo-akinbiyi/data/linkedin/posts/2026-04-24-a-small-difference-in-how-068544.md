A small difference in how you communicate can change how people feel around you.

You can use your daily communication to make your co-workers, your clients, as well as everyone you interact with, feel better, worse or neutral.
I choose positive and I push myself to communicate in a such a way every single day.

And yes, it does take effort.

I communicate very intentionally to make people feel better about me, about a situation and most importantly, about themselves.

And the truth is that most people have no idea that I’m doing this consciously and also what I am doing.

And what’s interesting is this approach works in every environment.
Whether I’m speaking to a CEO, a politician, a celebrity, or the team behind the scenes… the same human communication principles apply.

There are tools, approaches, strategies that allow you to come across as a positive communicator and also appear more charismatic.
We use many of these tools subconsciously, but using them consciously is the real advantage.

Because once you’re aware of them, you can choose the impact you want to have instead of leaving it to chance.

Over the next weeks, I will share them, because it is time to push the message of positive and human-first communication.

I wonder, do you use any strategies for positive communication?
If yes, share them in the comments.
