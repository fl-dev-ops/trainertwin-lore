Set the table for Gulfood - The World’s Largest, Most Trusted F&B Sourcing Event 2024!

The 29th edition of Gulfood, unfolding at the Dubai World Trade Centre from 19 – 23 February 2024, will host a record 5,500+ exhibitors from over 127 countries, unveiling new business opportunities and providing solutions to new and growing global challenges within the food and beverage industry.

We are just a few days away from Gulfood 2024, and the anticipation is palpable. I can't wait to peek into the pots of Michelin-starred chefs and ask them questions to learn more about their philosophy of cooking, favorite dishes, and their journey and leadership philosophy.

The event will feature a range of masterclasses and over 100 Michelin-starred chefs, including culinary experts like @Alain Passard, Giovanni M. Papi, Christian le Squer, Enrico Serea, Akira Back, and many more.

The competitive YouthX Chef Challenge is also returning for its third edition, featuring emerging culinary talents.

I am so excited about this 5-day event that will once again be one of the highlights of my season. I can't wait to see some of the world's accomplished Michelin-star chefs in action.
