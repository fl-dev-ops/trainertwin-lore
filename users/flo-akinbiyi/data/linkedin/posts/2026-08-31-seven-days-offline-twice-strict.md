Seven days offline. Twice. Strict digital detox.

No phone, no laptop, no social media.

Something I have not done in over a decade.

This is me doing a backflip of a balcony at one of my favorite lakes in Bavaria, Walchensee.

The first week right at the start of the summer was almost too easy. I was so happy without devices that I missed most of the World Cup and didn’t watch any games. By day three on the beach in Liguria, I finished Terry Crew's book, which I had for more than six months and only read five pages, and his story totally inspired me.

I went on to read another one and a half books. I have been telling people for years that I don't have the patience to read but that was actually not true. I just filled this time with a fast way of getting information.

The second week at the end of the holidays, I broke my strict digital detox.

But not for social media, not for Instagram, not even for LinkedIn. I broke it because the team needed decisions, and there were emails that could not wait. And Dubai work life and school was getting closer.

I don't feel bad that I failed the second time. I'd rather learn that when doing a digital detox in this world, you also have to plan that a little bit because guess what? Getting around as a family without a phone, exploring new places, taking photos, all of that is practically impossible unless you're really well organized.

And also having your business run without you for a week is absolutely wonderful, but it only works for me in certain periods.

So I really wonder how many of you spent this summer away from phones, media and news, and what pulled you back to the digital world?
