Working with founders Andy Bruckschloegl, Bernd Storm vans Gravesande and Felix Haas was always an absolute pleasure. And a huge shoutout goes to the entire Bits & Pretzels team, led by the fearless Ann-Kathrin Kühnle, for creating such an amazing environment. 🙌 Aline Schmid
