If You’re Scared to Fail, You Might End Up Never Trying.

This is one of the biggest mistakes people make when it comes to public speaking. They say no to public speaking opportunities because they fear it could go wrong.

Dare to fail.

If your presentation is not the best in the world, or if you mess up a little bit, it is not the end of the world.

I’m not advocating that you should not prepare for a presentation or not give it your best shot, quite the opposite:

	•	Preparation and practice
	•	Commitment to delivering to the best of your abilities

Nevertheless, if things go wrong a little bit, it is never the end of the world.

Yes, you might get some negative feedback.

You might have to then prove yourself in the next presentation.

But this is all part of the learning experience.

Not delivering presentations is way more harmful to yourself than:

	•	Accepting that you’re not doing a good job
	•	Finding opportunities to practice and become a better presenter

Eventually mastering the craft of public speaking. Public speaking is a skill that can be learned by anybody, and all you have to do is, start. 

Have you ever thought the world would end if your presentation wasn’t that well received?
