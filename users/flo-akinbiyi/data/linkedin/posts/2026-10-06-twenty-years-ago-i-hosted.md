Twenty years ago, I hosted my first event.

To be quite honest, I didn’t host it because I had an inner calling, or because I thought this was such a cool thing to do.

I hosted it because I had to.

I was organizing an event at Alpamare, the world-class swimming pool in my hometown of Bad Tölz.

They had one of these standing waves, a FlowRider.

It was my second event there, the Endless Peaks Surf Jam, and it was my own initiative.

The first one came with a TV team and a presenter.

The second one didn’t.

So there was a gap to fill because there was no one else up for hosting the event.

So I said, "Okay, then I’ll do it."

As the participants for the competition arrived, I collected some basic information, so I would have something to talk about.

I picked up the microphone and started speaking.

I don’t know if I was good that day, but when I look at that photo, I can already see there was an energy and a commitment to giving it my best shot.

Twenty years later, that’s exactly what I do.

I give every single audience the best version of myself.

And I have to say, I am incredibly blessed and grateful.

The young boy from a small Bavarian town of 16,000 people has come quite a long way.

Hosting events on four continents with audiences of thousands of people.

Being on stage with A-list celebrities such as Arnold Schwarzenegger, Michelle Obama, Pep Guardiola and many others.

All while working for some of the world’s most desirable brands.

I would have never imagined any of this.

And all of it started by stepping up to the occasion, taking responsibility, and saying: Why not? Why not me?

Here’s to twenty years.

Time to celebrate and time to say thank you to everyone who supported me on this journey.

First and foremost, Stefan Anselm and Dr. Anton Höfler, who allowed me to first pick up the microphone back in 2006, and to everyone else who has been supporting me on my journey since then.

Here’s to the next twenty and more.

And get ready, there will also be some gifts coming your way.
