Can you believe that I’m standing next to the future?

The IBM Quantum Computer System 2 is no longer just a concept, a study, or a tool in the making.

It’s here. It’s real. And the team is fully dedicated on track to have them commercially available in 2025.

I’m not sure if you know how quantum computers work.

In simple terms, they harness the unique properties of quantum bits (qubits), like superposition and entanglement, to perform calculations that would take traditional computers millions of years.

They explore countless possibilities at the same time, unlocking unprecedented processing power.

Someone told me recently:
“If you want to understand subjects or research areas that are quantum in nature, it makes more sense to calculate them directly with a quantum computer, rather than translating them back and forth from quantum to zeros and ones.”

That’s the real magic - solving problems that were previously impossible.

During my time at GITEX Europe, I listened to a brilliant talk by Dr. Alessandro Curioni, IBM Fellow, on quantum and AI and how these technologies are shaping our world.

I first met Dr. Curioni at the BMW Future of Work event in 2020, where we discussed (virtually and at the gym!) the future of work and how it’s evolving.

Seeing how much progress has been made in just a few years and what’s coming next, was truly inspiring.

So what are the use cases?

Quantum computers are set to transform:
Drug discovery and materials science simulating molecules, to design new medicines faster.

Climate modeling and sustainability, analyzing complex data to unlock smarter environmental solutions.

Financial modeling and risk analysis, solving optimization challenges in seconds, not days.

Cybersecurity and encryption, creating next level security systems to protect our digital world.

At GITEX, I even met a company already offering quantum computing power as a service, giving clients on demand access to multiple quantum computers to tackle the world’s biggest challenges.

Can you believe that the future is already here?
