While visiting my client, DHL Innovation Middle East & Africa Center, I felt inspired to share five innovative tools and approaches that can revolutionize your presentations and take your public speaking skills to the next level.

It’s #2025, and as tech becomes more embedded in our daily lives, we should recognize the opportunities it presents to enhance our speeches and presentations. But here’s the catch – you can’t be lazy about it. You need to think outside the box, do what others wouldn’t, and use tools that others overlook.

Here’s a list of a few tools I personally use. And yes, I have more – but these are five of my favorites:

1. AI tools for script writing.
Don’t settle for a generic ChatGPT model. Train your own ChatGPT to mimic your style and write the way you speak. You can do this with tools like ChatGPT or Jasper.

2. Use collaboration tools on stage.
Engaging the audience in real time is not only effective – it’s fun. Tools like Miro and Slido are absolutely fantastic for this.

3. Manage stage fright and overall well-being.
With tech, you can improve your physical state – whether through meditation, body temperature regulation, or overall well-being. Ambr Wave, Fitbit, and Calm are terrific tools to help with this.

4. Get creative with AI.
Use AI for video, image, and music creation. Tools like Synthesia, Canva, and AIVA allow you to generate amazing content to elevate your presentations.

5. Practice with VR training tools.
Immersive practice is a game-changer. VR tools let you stand in a closet or cabinet and feel like you’re presenting to a full auditorium. VirtualSpeech, Ovation, and SpeechTrainers are fun and effective ways to sharpen your skills.
