Have you ever been to Sardinia?

I have not, and that is one of the reasons why I am thrilled to be hosting the SOC Weekend Costa Smeralda.

Tomorrow, I am stepping onto the plane and I’m excited for a few days in Italy - we’re going to experience the outstanding hospitality and yes, I mean food.

Today still, I remember the very specific Sardinian tortellini that Michelin Star Chef Giovanni M. Papi at Armani Hotel Dubai once made for us a recipe from his grandma that was incredible.

And of course, there would be a lot more than food and great wine.
Beautiful mountain roads with the most wonderful, super hyper cars in the world and all topped by breathtaking scenery.

And if I’m really lucky, I might even get a little surf session in.

Can’t wait for another trip of a lifetime!
