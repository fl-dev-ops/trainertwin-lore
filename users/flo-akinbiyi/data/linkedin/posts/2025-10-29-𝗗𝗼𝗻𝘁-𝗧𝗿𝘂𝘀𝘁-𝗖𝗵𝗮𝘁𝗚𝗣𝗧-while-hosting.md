𝗗𝗼𝗻’𝘁 𝗧𝗿𝘂𝘀𝘁 𝗖𝗵𝗮𝘁𝗚𝗣𝗧!

While hosting the Next in Beauty Conference, I thought I’d multitask in the car on my way to the venue using AI.

I uploaded my entire script into ChatGPT, ready for a voice chat to brief me on the speakers, the event, and the panels.

And then the AI hallucinations began.

Hallucination #1:
I asked, “How can I share my data with you?”
It said, “Just send me the Google Docs link.”
Perfect, right? Wrong.
Once I did, it replied, “Sorry, I can’t access that. Please send a PDF.”
Thanks for nothing, my digital co-pilot.

Hallucination #2:
I asked which panels I’m hosting on day two and to brief me on the speakers.
There were no panels for me that day.
Yet ChatGPT confidently invented one full panel on Inclusivity in the Beauty Industry.
I then spent 50 minutes discussing a completely fictional panel.

Hallucination #3:
It gave me “industry facts” that sounded polished until I asked for sources.
Turns out they were “strong generalizations”.
Translation: made up but with confidence.

So, what is the takeaway?

AI can absolutely be your ally in preparing for events, briefings, or public speaking but it’s only as good as the clarity of your input and your ability to fact-check its output.

Keep your prompts simple, your data clean, and your brain switched on.
So as wonderful, powerful, and helpful as AI is, it still needs a human touch.
Always read through, fact-check, and sanity-check what it gives you before you hit publish or step on stage.

Because imagine I went up there and started introducing a non-existent panel!
Now that would have been a very inclusive hallucination.
