How do you make a perfumer a better communicator? 

Last week, I had, once again, one of my all-time favorite communication workshops.

I trained the Givaudan Middle East Fine Fragrance team. 

Working with perfumers, sales, marketing, on that one topic: how do we communicate better with our clients, teams, and management?

With perfumers, there’s a fascinating challenge. They create scents that are highly emotional. They take an inspiration, turn it into a chemical formula, and ultimately create something that makes us feel, remember, and imagine.

These creations need to be translated into words that clients understand, remember, and also ultimately buy into.

What made this training so special was really the creativity in the team showcased in their communication, their dedication to learn and grow, and the extraordinary team spirit that I was immersed in throughout the two days.

For me, one of the major reasons why I love this part of my job is that even in a short amount of time, you can witness the growth of every participant, and in this case also the growth of an entire team.

Every single one of them was able, after two days, to communicate and speak in a more effective way, and that was entirely objective.

Congratulations to the winner of the awards, and I have to say it is such an honor to be part of someone’s journey from saying, I can’t do this, I can’t speak like this, I can’t present in such a way, to suddenly just doing it.

To enable professionals with decades of experience under their belt to forget about that old belief of I can’t and replace it with I’m rocking the stage is something that is truly magical.

I’m so proud of every participant, grateful to Givaudan for working with me again, and I can’t wait to head to Dubai Duty Free tomorrow to smell some of the perfumes they have created.
