Whale watching tour guide, Patrick, made this adventurous boat tour, on a very rough sea, a once in a lifetime experience!

And yes, at some point, I was a little bit afraid that we weren’t going to make it back to the shore.

From the moment I stepped into the Special Tours office, I noticed there was something very special about Patrick.

It happens frequently that you see tour guides being great communicators as they speak almost every single day of their life to an audience.

But Patrick is something else.

What makes his communication so special is that he uses:

💯Humor to make everybody laugh, whether it is a safety briefing or a bit of education or just a good old laughing in between.

💯His body language was very expressive with large gestures that always give you a clear indication of what he is talking about.

💯He used his voice very effectively and that includes a technique that is also very specific.

And, of course, had fantastic knowledge of Iceland’s wildlife, the whales, and used incredible comparison to demonstrate important facts.

One of the comparisons that I will remember for my lifetime is this:
When a blue whale farts, it creates air bubbles that are so large that a horse fits inside.
😂😂😂

Thank you, Patrick, for making this trip such a memorable experience and giving us confidence to know that we were safe.
