One of the best communicators I’ve met this year wasn’t on stage. 

A few weeks ago, I took my wife to the Burj Club at the foot of the Burj Khalifa. 
Beautiful setting. Great Energy. 

But what made the experience truly memorable was the welcome. 
Janet, the receptionist, did something most people don’t. 

She looked at me 
Really looked. 

Within moments, she adjusted her communication to how I engage. 
She picked up on how I respond to stories, shared experiences, and humor.
 
And then she delivered exactly that. 

Personal stories.
Relatable moments. 
Humor that felt natural, not scripted. 

We laughed. A lot. 
And after we left, that interaction stayed with us.

Here is why this matters, especially for leaders: 

This is exactly what we coach executives to do.

Great communication isn’t about titles or talking more.
It’s about awareness, adaptation, and choosing the right tool for the human in front of you. 

And more often than not, that tool is storytelling. 

Well done to Emaar and the Burj Club for hiring people like Janet, and for giving them the freedom to use their communication skills fully. 

That freedom is what creates world-class experiences. 
Thank you, Janet, for making our day special. 

Looking forward to coming back very soon.
