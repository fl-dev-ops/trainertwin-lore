Was I able to lift the golden 150kg weight at fithera?

To make it short: Yes!

Okay, I need to elaborate.

When I first tried to pick up this beast of a weight last week at fithera, nothing worked.

Not with one hand.

Not with two.

Not even with proper technique, back straight, knees bent. 

I couldn’t move it a single centimeter.

I was making a bit of a fool of myself… until Astrid Buscher walked over.

She saw me struggling, handed me a pair of red glasses, and said nothing.
I put them on.

Got in the same position.

Back straight. Hands gripping the bar.

And then, the back of the weight lifted off the ground.

🤯 What?!

Same me. Same weight. Different glasses. Different outcome.

Without the glasses, I couldn’t move it.

With the glasses, I could.

This was one of the most mind-blowing experiences I had at fithera.

It turns out, for some people, colour can enhance performance.

That’s not just fascinating, it’s powerful.

I’m now planning a deep-dive conversation with Astrid to learn more about her work in neuroscience, performance, and the impact of visual stimuli on learning and energy.

Thanks Astrid for an experience I’ll never forget. 🙏

And thanks fithera for these unexpected discoveries.

Have a great start to your week, everyone and don’t underestimate the power of color. 🎨💪
