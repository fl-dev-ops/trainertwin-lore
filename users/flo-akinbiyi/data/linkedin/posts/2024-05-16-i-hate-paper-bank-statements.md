I hate paper bank statements.
Yesterday I had the pleasure of introducing the CEO of Wio Bank, Jayesh Patel on stage at #SeamlessDXB.
Believe it or not it was the fourth time!

Each time, I've shared my frustrations about my traditional bank:
•⁠  ⁠paper statements
•⁠  ⁠the need to visit a branch for certain tasks
•⁠  ⁠a fee structure that lacks transparency
•⁠  ⁠and the list goes on

It's time for me to join a digital bank.
But the question is..

Why did it take me over one and a half years of complaining without taking action?

Here's the truth: Changing your bank account, especially a business account, isn't easy. 

It’s a significant investment in time, effort, and resources to submit documents, which is very easy with Wio Bank’s amazing application.

However, transitioning means moving all your data to a new bank. 
This involves setting up every vendor, supplier, and payee again. 

It's definitely an investment but one I believe is worth it.

And once it is done I will enjoy all the benefits of Wio Bank
•⁠  ⁠Quick and easy document submission through their app.
•⁠  ⁠No need to visit branches for routine tasks.
•⁠  ⁠Clear and straightforward fees with no hidden charges.
•⁠  ⁠24/7 customer support designed to assist with any inquiries or issues.
•⁠  ⁠And a lot more and more great features to come in the near future.

Jayesh and the Wio Bank team, please approve my bank account, so I can finally step into the 21st century with a bank account that brings joy, savings, and numerous benefits.
