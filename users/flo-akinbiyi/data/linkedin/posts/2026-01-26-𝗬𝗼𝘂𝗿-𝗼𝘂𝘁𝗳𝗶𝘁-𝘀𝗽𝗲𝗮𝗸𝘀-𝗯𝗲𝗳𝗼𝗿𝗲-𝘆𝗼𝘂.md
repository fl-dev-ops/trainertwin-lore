The question is: do you know what it’s saying?

I love fashion.
And I love fashion for work.

Your appearance has the ability to make a statement without you having to say a single word.

My Italian wife taught me a great deal about fashion.
Her sister, who works in the fashion industry, continued my education.
And with both of them, I still feel like an apprentice with so much left to learn.

Over the years, I’ve received countless compliments on stage.
Not about what I said,
but about how I looked.

People remember the suit.
The colour.
The detail.
The way it all came together.

That changed something for me.

It made me realise that body language doesn’t start with movement.
It starts with appearance.

Before you speak.
Before you gesture.
Before you even take your first step on stage.

That’s why choosing what to wear for Paris and Munich feels different.
It’s no longer about style.
It’s about intention.

What do I want the audience to feel?
Excitement.
Surprise.
Recognition.

Your appearance is more than the suit you put on in the morning.
It’s part of how you communicate.
