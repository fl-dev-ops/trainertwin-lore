𝗛𝗘𝗔𝗟𝗧𝗛-𝗜 𝗔𝗪𝗔𝗥𝗗𝗦 𝟮𝟬𝟮𝟱 – 𝗮 𝗳𝗶𝗿𝗲𝘄𝗼𝗿𝗸 𝗼𝗳 𝗛𝗲𝗮𝗹𝘁𝗵 𝗜𝗻𝗻𝗼𝘃𝗮𝘁𝗶𝗼𝗻𝘀! 🥇

This Thursday was a true firework of Health Innovation in Hamburg and I had the honor to co-moderate this inspiring evening with my dear Flo Akinbiyi.

We saw solutions that can truly save lives:

• 𝗴𝗼𝗼𝗱𝘀𝗰𝗮𝗿𝗲 developed a device that reads and analyzes all common rapid tests with the precision of a medical laboratory.

• 𝗜𝗻𝘇𝗶𝗽𝗶𝗼 𝗠𝗲𝗱𝗶𝗰𝗮𝗹 uses AI to let surgeons plan operations themselves within minutes instead of relying on external services.

• 𝗮𝗱𝗺𝗼𝗿𝗮𝗶 digitally stains tissue samples with AI saving chemicals, time, and costs in pathology.

• 𝗖𝗲𝗹𝗹𝗯𝗿𝗶𝗰𝗸𝘀 led by Alex Leutner, Health Personality 2025, is 3D-printing living human tissue paving the way for lab-grown organs.

• 𝗗𝗶𝗮𝗠𝗼𝗻𝗧𝗲𝗰𝗵 uses laser technology for needle-free blood sugar measurement and was crowned All-Star after winning in 2024.

From 3D-printed organs to AI-driven diagnostics… these founders are not building the healthcare of tomorrow. They’re building it TODAY!!
