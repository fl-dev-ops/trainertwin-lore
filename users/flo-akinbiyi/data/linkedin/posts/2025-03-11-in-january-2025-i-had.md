In January 2025, I had the pleasure of moderating a fireside chat at CopeCard’s Mastermind event, featuring two visionary entrepreneurs: Saygin Yalcin, founder of BuyAnyCar and SellAnyCar, and Michael Klöp, CEO of CopeCart.

Moderating a session like this is an art. It requires a mix of preparation, adaptability, and sharp focus to create an engaging conversation. Here’s what it takes to do it right:

✅ Thorough Preparation - Know your speakers inside out. Research their backgrounds, achievements, and industry insights to craft questions that surprise them and add value for the audience. The best moments come when panelists say, “That’s a great question!”

✅ Active Listening & Quick Thinking - A moderator isn’t just asking questions; they’re weaving a story in real time. Pay close attention to what’s being said, connect ideas on the fly, and smoothly bring both speakers into the discussion. Your role is to guide, not dominate.

✅ Flexibility - Kill Your Darlings - Sometimes, the brilliant question you prepared just doesn’t fit the flow of the conversation. Let it go. If the discussion is heading in a more valuable direction, follow it. The best moderators adapt and make the moment count.

✅ Time Management & Audience Awareness - A great panel isn’t just about content - it’s about flow. Keep track of the clock, ensure a balanced conversation, and wrap up strong. No one enjoys a session that drags on or feels rushed.

✅ Storytelling & Data for Impact - Have powerful industry stats, figures, and real-world stories ready to enhance the discussion. Facts bring credibility, but stories make ideas stick. Knowing when to drop a well-timed insight is what sets great moderators apart.

Moderating isn’t just a skill - it’s a performance. When done well, it creates one of the most rewarding moments on stage.
