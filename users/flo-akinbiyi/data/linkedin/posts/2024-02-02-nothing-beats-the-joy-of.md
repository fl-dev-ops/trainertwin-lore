Nothing beats the joy of riding your snowboard through fresh powder. 

Close your eyes and imagine this: 

It's Friday morning, but instead of heading to work, you're on a mountain with few people around. It's just you, your snowboard, and the mountain-all blanketed in fresh snow. 

After a hike to distance yourself further from the sparse crowd, you find yourself alone with a swath of untouched powder. The sun shines brightly- the air is crisp and clean. You buckle up start riding, and the exhilaration is so overwhelming, you can't help but let out a big "Yeah!" 

Snowboarding has always been a great source of joy in my life. After moving to the UAE, I've replaced it with flowriding, skateboarding, and other exciting activities. However, I've been a bit lax with board sports lately – a realization that struck me as somewhat foolish. Why deny myself this joy? 

So, as part of my 2024 goals, I'm committed to injecting more fun and joy into life. And that means getting back into board sports. Who's up for a fun flowriding session at Atlantis Wave House next week?
