What an incredible ride hosting fithera and Deutscher Gesundheitsball last week!

I’ve hosted hundreds of events and these two stood out in a powerful way.
A huge thank you to fithera, the Deutscher Gesundheitsball, and the BODYMEDIA team for putting together such a spectacular experience.

👏 Special kudos to 𝗡𝗶𝗰𝗼 𝗛𝗲𝘀𝘀𝗲𝗹 the initiator and visionary behind it all and his brother 𝗬𝗮𝗻𝗻𝗶𝗰𝗸 𝗛𝗲𝘀𝘀𝗲𝗹.

Together with their team, they created an environment that was nothing short of exceptional.

For the first time, the fitness and therapy worlds came together - recognized, honored, and unified across two incredible events.

It marked a step toward a more holistic approach to health, performance, and well-being.

From the main stage at fithera, I had the honor of hosting a lineup that delivered education, emotion, and energy.

Keynotes from Jochen Schweizer, 𝗛𝗲𝗻𝗿𝘆 𝗠𝗮𝘀𝗸𝗲, Miriam Höller, Philip Keil, Marc Gassert and many more speakers including myself (also deliver a keynote.. ;)) across five different stages.

It was a masterclass in curating a multidimensional program with fitness, therapy, business, innovation, and inspiration all under one roof.

On the second day I had the pleasure of co-hosting the German Gesundheitsball with the amazingly talented Maren Kristin Kern, where we celebrated game-changing entrepreneurs and honored excellence with the prestigious Heracles Award.

These moments matter.

They’re not just about the stage, they’re about connection, collaboration, and collective growth.

🎯 I can’t wait for the 2026 edition and to dive deeper into the fitness, therapy, and business world with all of you.
