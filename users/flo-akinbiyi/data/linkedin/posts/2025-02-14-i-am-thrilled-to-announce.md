I am thrilled to announce that I will be hosting Top Table at Gulfood 2025, which is just around the corner. Believe it or not, this marks the 30th edition of one of the world’s most significant gatherings in the food and beverage industry. This year, we’re looking forward to a celebration of culinary excellence and innovation as we explore the future of food.

For one full week I will be working alongside some of the world’s best chefs, including the one and only Alain Ducasse.

Last year’s memorable experiences of meeting with Alain Passard, Christian Le Sque and Joan Roca are memories that I treasure.

This year’s lineup features an impressive array of culinary talent. In addition to Chef Ducasse, the event will showcase:

•	Romain Meder – Chef & Co-Founder, Sapid 
•	Ángel León – Chef & Owner, Aponiente 
•	Ana Roš – Chef & Owner, Hiša Franko 
•	Giovanni Pappi – Head Chef, Armani/Ristorante 
•	Hadrien Villedieu - Head Chef & Co-Owner, Chez Wam 
•	Mohamad Orfali – Head Chef & Co-Owner, Orfali Bros 
•	Patricia Roig – Executive Chef - Japanese Seafood Ambassador, BOCA 
•	Takashi Namekata – Executive Chef - Wagyu Master, Takahisa
•	Hisao Ueda – Executive Chef - Sushi Master, Takahisa

And many others…

These chefs will showcase their unique culinary styles and innovative dishes.
For anyone attending Gulfood, do connect with me.

And don’t forget that there will also be the YouthX competition, where up-and-coming chefs will showcase their skills and creativity.

Can’t wait for it! 😋
