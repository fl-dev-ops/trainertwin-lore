PAUSE. SILENCE. STILLNESS.

It’s one of the simplest, and at the same time, one of the most difficult tools in public speaking.

When it’s our turn to speak, we often want to get everything off our chest. 

We want to give the audience maximum value. We want to say everything we prepared. We want to tell just one more story…

And in the middle of all that, speakers often forget:
Pauses are key.

They give you a moment to think. They give the audience a moment to digest. They add impact, and sometimes even drama, to your speech.

Pauses are a must.

If you want to be a more impactful speaker, try this tool consciously.

Figure out: What’s the right length of a pause for you? How often can you use it without overdoing it?

By finding the right rhythm, you will become a more powerful speaker.
