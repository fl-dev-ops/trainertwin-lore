This weekend, I celebrated my birthday with some wakesurfing. 🏄‍♂️

Board sports have been a pure source of joy for me for years - which is quite strange because I don’t do them regularly. Nothing makes me happier than standing on a board, performing a trick, carving turns, doing sprays, or just cruising and enjoying the element.
