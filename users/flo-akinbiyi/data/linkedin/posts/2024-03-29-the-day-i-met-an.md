The day I met an automotive legend, Fabrizio Giugiaro ✨

If you're not familiar with the automotive industries, you might not have heard of Fabrizio Giugiaro and his father, Giorgetto Giugiaro. 

However, this father and son have shaped the automotive industry in a significant way. When I first learned that Fabrizio was attending the Supercar Owners Circle AG Owner's Circle weekend in Gstaad in 2020, I have to be honest, I had no idea who he was. 

But then, when I did my research and got to meet him, listen to his presentations, and hear his story, I was just blown away. The list of projects that he and his father have designed is incredibly long, including concept cars like the BMW NASCAR, Bugatti Chiron, Lamborghini Marco Polo, Maserati Boomerang; and production cars like the Audi 80, Fiat Grande Punto, Maserati Coupe, SEAT Ibiza, Volkswagen Golf. And the projects that Fabrizio does today with his company GFG Style, like the Vision 2030 Desert Raid, the Bandini Dora, or the Vision 2030, are just breathtaking.

It’s always an honor to meet such an influential personality. And what always fascinates me when you meet people like him, who are just so nice and humble, it really shows that to be one of the greatest, you just need one thing: love and passion for what you really do
