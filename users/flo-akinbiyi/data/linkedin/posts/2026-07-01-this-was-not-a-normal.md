This was not a normal sales conversation that Sanjay delivered. 

Yesterday, I had a walk around Galleria Mall in Abu Dhabi, and I stumbled across Bacha Coffee, a Moroccan coffee chain that I've never seen before. And I was intrigued, and I wanted a coffee anyways, so I stopped there and had a conversation with Sanjay Maharjan about the brand. 

And then when I found out that they offered coffee that is 500 dirhams a cup, not only was I shocked, but I wanted to know more. And what followed was nothing else than a spectacular introduction into the brand, the coffee, the value, the production, the taste. Absolutely incredible. I was blown away by the knowledge Sanjay had, the passion that he showcased, and the welcoming and outstanding customer experience he delivered. 

There are so few salespeople and floor assistants that really take care of you. Too many take it for granted that the customers come into their shops and give them money and purchase products, while others, and actually quite a few, like Sanjay, work really hard to give the customer a memorable experience. 

So well done to Sanjay and the entire team at Bacha Coffee, and I will definitely stop by the next time I'm again in Abu Dhabi.
