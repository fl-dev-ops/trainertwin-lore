Have you ever felt that you don’t belong here, that people in the room are judging you, that they might not appreciate or like your work. 

Many of us have to deal with these kind of thoughts and feelings, and it is incredibly difficult to be the best version of yourself when these feelings, thoughts and emotions bubble up.

What has helped me is to push through these feelings is knowing the work that I’ve done up to this point. 

I know the skills that I have learned along the way. 

I know that no matter how I feel, the people in the room feel different because my brain is just indulging itself in some assumptions. 

If you ever have to deal with these kind of thoughts, remind yourself of all the good you’ve done, of the all the strength that you have and right then and there make a decision to make this one of your finest work moment.  

And that’s how you can succeed!
