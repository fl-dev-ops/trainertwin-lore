Three Top Tips for the Easiest Confidence Booster!

Start with a smile 🙂

A smile is more than a nice gesture; it’s a powerful communication tool.

Smile like you mean it 😃

Bring the good old smile into your presentations is a key strategy for any successful presenter.

But when you look at screenshots, photos and videos of, especially a male presenters, you see very often, grumpy, angry, focused faces and not entertaining, exciting faces.

But Flo, are you saying that I should fake a smile?

No, I am not.

It’s not about faking a smile, but it’s only about learning to smile in front of an audience.

And that’s nice company genuine place be your authentic smile representing your personality, and of course, matching your content

❌Three Smiling Don’ts❌

Don’t fake a smile!
Because you can see it literally in your face. Fake smile don’t have wrinkles around the eyes.

Don’t over smile!
Scientifically speaking, you want to smile for 33% of your speaking time.

Make sure your smile matches your content!
Of course, you need to smile when and where it fits your content and when it adds to your delivery and does not distract from your message.

In public speaking, meetings, and networking, your smile is your simplest, fastest confidence booster.
