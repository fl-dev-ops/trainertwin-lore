Isn’t this the worst feeling?

You are delivering a presentation or standing on stage, and suddenly you don’t know what to do with your hands anymore.

It’s fascinating how our hands, which we never think about 99% of the time, become the weirdest things in certain social settings as if they weren’t part of our bodies.

Regarding public speaking and presentation skills, ensuring that your gestures and body language work with you to deliver your message—and not against you—is crucial. 

Research has shown that appropriate hand gestures can significantly increase the retention of spoken information by audiences, emphasizing the importance of using your hands effectively to reinforce your message.
