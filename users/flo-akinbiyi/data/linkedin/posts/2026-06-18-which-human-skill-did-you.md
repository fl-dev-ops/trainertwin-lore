Which human skill did you use this week? 

I know we're all excited about AI, and we certainly should be. But there is a side that comes with AI that we're not talking enough about. 

As humans, we are at a crossroad where we are losing human skills. It's not the first time. Look back at the industrial revolution, we also lost skills. For example, staying fit. Why do we have so many gyms? Because we don't walk enough, we just jump into our cars and drive around. So now, we need to go to a facility to train this human skill. 

The same goes for more intellectual skills. Take navigating through a city. How many drive around without Google Maps or Waze? How many can still read the signs on the road or a map? The same goes for our work. Today, we can just go ahead and prompt our presentation really nicely into Claude and we never have to touch a single design. But if you do that for a while, you will have completely lost the skill of what it means to design a great slide. 

I know we're all excited about AI, and we certainly should be, but don't forget that once in a while, doing it yourself so you don't lose the skill is a solid strategy. Don't forget that we should invest more time and effort into further developing other human skills such as creativity, communication, finding inspiration, human connection, because this is truly where human ingenuity paired with AI will shine. 

Are you using or losing skills?
