7500 participants, 10 stages, countless spins of the beer carousel and an energy that is unmatched!

This year’s Bits & Pretzels Founders Festival was once again a terrific showcase that Europe is filled with possibilities, opportunities, and talent. 

Connecting Europe widely discussed by politicians such as Katherina Reiche, Federal Minister for Economic Affairs and Energy of Germany, Dorothee Bär, German Federal Minister for Research, Technology, and Space, Dr. Markus Söder, Minister President of Bavaria, and Dr. Fabian Mehring, Danish Minister for Digital Affairs.

With whom I had the wonderful opportunity to sit down and have a conversation about what Europe can do already, what it needs what needs to change and also learning from Denmark, where everybody’s happy 🙂 

But that’s not all we had on the Inspiration Stage, which I hosted with Céline Flores Willers and Lara Sophie Bothur, we had founders from unicorns such as Personio, Delivery Hero as well as many other investors that support their journeys, and to the many other important players in the ecosystem. 

Andy Bruckschloegl, Bernd Storm vans Gravesande and Felix Haas congratulations on what do you have achieved! 🎉

And thank you for also launching the Bits Communities, extending the Bits & Pretzels experience and growing their community.
