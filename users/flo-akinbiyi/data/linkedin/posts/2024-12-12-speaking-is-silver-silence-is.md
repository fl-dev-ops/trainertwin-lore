Speaking is silver, silence is gold.

Pauses are one of the most effective techniques when it comes to public speaking.

Having the #audience look at you while you’re not saying anything is not easy. It takes strength, willpower, and determination to sustain pauses. But they are incredibly powerful. Pauses give your audience time to absorb your words, emphasize your points, and create moments of powerful connection.

In this video, I show you a really fun way to practice your pauses and become a more effective #communicator.
