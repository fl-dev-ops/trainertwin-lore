This is how I offended Pep Guardiola.

A few months ago, I had the honor of interviewing Pep live on stage.

The conversation was outstanding, inspiring, honest and yes, there was one huge faux pas.

Halfway through, I looked at him and said:

“So Pep… Manchester United.”

The room gasped.

Pep stared.

And with a VERY high voice I asked myself:

“Did I seriously just say that?”

We laughed it off.

And the conversation only got better.

Because Pep isn’t just a football genius, he understands leadership at the deepest level.

Here are the three messages that stayed with me:

- There is no guaranteed winning formula.

What works once won’t always work again. Reinvention is part of leadership.

- Don’t strive to be liked. Strive to be loved.

His hardest responsibility is telling world-class players they won’t play and still keeping trust and connection strong.

- Never speak when emotions are too high.

The halftime speech is the most impactful one, not before the game, not after the chaos.

And there’s one more insight, one I want to highlight as a communication coach, because it’s something every leader should take seriously.

Positive body language is a must. In every moment. In every situation.

Pep - thank you for the wisdom and the incredible experience on stage.

And thank you for forgiving the Manchester… United… Moment.
