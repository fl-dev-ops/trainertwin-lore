I love workcations! July was our holiday month, and August is all about workcation for me. I’m traveling with and without the family, learning and working from everywhere. You can find me working from hotel lobbies, beds, cafes, restaurants, and even from the beach or mountains. Years before COVID, I embraced this lifestyle of ‘workcations’—working while staying longer away—allowing me the luxury to travel and work simultaneously. It’s a concept that really works for me.

One of my craziest experiences was hosting a webinar on top of a mountain in a little hut. I got the best bedroom, which was very basic. The internet was shaky, and the lighting was horrible, yet I managed and delivered a session that everyone loved. If you want to, you can do it!
