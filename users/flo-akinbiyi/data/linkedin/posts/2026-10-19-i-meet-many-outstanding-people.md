I meet many outstanding people in business who have a great mission. 

But every now and then I meet someone who is truly making the world a better place. 

Michael Fritz is one of those. Together with his co-founder and team, he has built something incredible: 

Viva con Agua

A social enterprise with a very clear mission: water is a human right. 

And theyre doing something about it. Through their projects and social businesses, they're helping bring access to clean water to underserved communities around the world. And what I particularly love is that they have created a business model where doing business means doing good. 

The more successful the business becomes, the more positive impact it can create. Three years ago, I visited the Villa Viva Hotel in Hamburg, and back then it wasn't open yet, and I got only a sneak peek. This time I got the full tour, from the incredible art floor to the innovative camping boxes where you can stay for just 19.90 euros a night, to the Jan Delay suite. Yes, an entire suite designed by the German rapper Jan Delay. 
How cool is that? 

What impressed me the most was the creativity behind the entire concept: the art, the rooms, the spaces, the ideas. Everything has a purpose, and everything feels a little different. 

And behind all of that creativity is a mission that truly matters: water is a human right, and access to clean water should never be a privilege. 

So here's my invitation to all of you: check out Viva con Agua, stay in one of their hotels, and learn about the projects and support their mission. Because this kind of business is what we need more in this world. 

And remember: water is a human right.
