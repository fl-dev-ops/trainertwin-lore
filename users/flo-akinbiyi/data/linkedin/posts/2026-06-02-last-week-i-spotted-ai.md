Last week I spotted AI presentations.

One speaker even admitted that the presentation was done by AI and that he only saw it a couple of minutes ago. That’s why he’s improvising.

If you’re lazy when using AI to create amazing looking presentations, but you do not know what is a content, you won't know what words you need to use.

Using AI to create presentations isn't a problem. But if you only created and you don't review and prepare, you’re wasting your audience's time.
