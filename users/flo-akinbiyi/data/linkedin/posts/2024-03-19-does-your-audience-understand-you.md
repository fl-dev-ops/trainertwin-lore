Does your audience understand you? 🤔

Jennifer Lawrence shares a moment when Meryl Streep gets caught in an abbreviation misunderstanding - thinking "GOAT" meant "old goat" 🐐, not realizing it actually stands for "Greatest Of All Times"! 😂

Have you ever wondered about the real power of abbreviations? 

In a world where abbreviations are everywhere - from tech lingo to internal slang and beyond, it's easy to get lost in translation. 🔄 Jennifer's story is a perfect reminder: clarity is key! 🗝

As funny as this exchange was, it’s important to remember that abbreviations could be misunderstood by the audience and your message could be lost, the impact could fall flat or your audience leaves confused.
