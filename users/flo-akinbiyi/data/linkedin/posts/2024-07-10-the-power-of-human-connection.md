The Power of Human Connection in Public Speaking

The power of a human connection is unbeatable. In public speaking and presentation skills, many professionals focus solely on content, often needing to remember this crucial aspect of communication. I’ve mentioned this many times and I’ll repeat it: your content must be solid, well-researched, engaging, and entertaining. However, the delivery should also focus on building a human connection.

Creating a connection with your audience means they will like you more. If they like you more, they will listen more. If they listen more, they will reflect more on your content. Consequently, you will achieve a better outcome from your communication.

Do not underestimate the power of human connection; it can make or break your presentation.
