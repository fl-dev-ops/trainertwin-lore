The best salesperson in Venice never tried to sell us anything.

This summer we spent a day in Venice and we spotted a really nice optician: Ottica Pantano, Venezia.

We opened the door, walked into the store and were respectfully greeted by Gerlando Barba, the salesman.

First of all, he was so attentive. 

He asked us questions and took the time and effort to understand our style and recommend some sunglasses.

He was also not afraid to be a bit strict with my son as he played around with the decoration when we weren’t watching.

But what followed next amazed us all.

First he cleaned my wife’s sunglasses.

Then he checked my son’s sunglasses to confirm if they offer him solid UV protection.

Then he took it to the next level and showed my son a “magic trick”. He showed him a grey display. Then he handed him polarized sunglasses, and the grey display turned into a colorful picture.

Did we buy anything at the end? No.

But I already know today that the next time I am in Venice I will make time to find the shop and stop by and see Gerlando.

Have you ever met a salesperson that was just too good to be true?
