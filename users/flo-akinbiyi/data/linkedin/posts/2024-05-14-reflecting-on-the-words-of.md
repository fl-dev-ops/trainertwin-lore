Reflecting on the words of Michael J. Fox

Last year, I had the honor of hosting the Bits & Pretzels health.tech conference, a place where innovation and ideas meet. Among the many great events of the day, seeing Michael J. Fox take the stage was a highlight that still resonates with me deeply.

Michael’s journey is a testament to resilience and hope. His speech, filled with personal anecdotes and insights, underscored the power of perseverance against life's adversities. His words, "One's response to challenges could either define or refine", stuck with me. This perspective has inspired me to embrace challenges with a renewed strength and optimism, transforming hurdles into stepping stones.

I’ve taken his message to heart, applying these lessons in both my personal and professional life. I will be back at health.tech on the 5 June 2024 in Munich and I am excited for another great conference. Michael’s message has encouraged me to approach obstacles with grace and determination, constantly reminding me that our reactions to challenges can lead to growth and innovation and I hope this year’s message can inspire you too.

Let's keep pushing the boundaries of what's possible, inspired by those who show us how. 🚀
