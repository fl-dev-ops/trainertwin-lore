After years of presenting around the world, here’s what I’ve learned about showing up sharp, focused, and fully present:

✅ 𝗚𝗲𝘁 𝗲𝗻𝗼𝘂𝗴𝗵 𝘀𝗹𝗲𝗲𝗽: 6 -8 hours sleep, gives your mind and body that rest you need for the day ahead.

🏄‍♂️𝗠𝗼𝘃𝗲 𝘆𝗼𝘂𝗿 𝗯𝗼𝗱𝘆: For me it’s board sports whether, skateboarding, snowboarding or surfing.

🥗 𝗘𝗮𝘁 𝘄𝗲𝗹𝗹: Never go on stage hungry or running on sugar or processed food. Eat healthy and light!

🤝 𝗠𝗮𝗸𝗲 𝗿𝗲𝗮𝗹 𝗰𝗼𝗻𝗻𝗲𝗰𝘁𝗶𝗼𝗻𝘀: Before you go on the stage, connect with people and share real moments of kindness with strangers. It's one of the most powerful ways to change your mood!

⚡ 𝗨𝗽 𝘁𝗵𝗲 𝗲𝗻𝗲𝗿𝗴𝘆: Your energy can be managed and elevated whether it is meditation, music, sports, ice bath etc. - make it a habit.

📄 𝗕𝗲 𝗽𝗿𝗲𝗽𝗮𝗿𝗲𝗱: Thorough preparation in your own way, is a magic potion for your confidence. Also be ready to improvise should things not go according to script.
