Classic meets Electric!

Have you ever imagined a classic car going electric?

In 2023, the The Little Car Company launched a replica of the Bentley Motors Blower, a fully electric classic car. The event was a total blast of a weekend! They've transformed a classic, historic car into a zero-emission, fun ride.🌱✨

This level of innovation is the direction we need to head in, proving that the future of electric cars can still be exciting and fun. 🎉 Would you agree?
