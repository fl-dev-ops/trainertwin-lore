Here’s the truth about how I feel before stepping on stage in front of a few hundred or even a few thousand people.

First of all, I don’t always feel that confident, and sometimes I even feel nervous about it.

But regardless of my initial feelings, fears, or instincts, I love what I do.

And here’s what I’ve learned: once you start, it’s always going to be okay.

If you know a few ways to prepare yourself for speaking on stage, it’s never going to feel that challenging.

Here’s the key: preparation.

And preparation is personal. How you prepare your speech or presentation matters.

Here are a few tips:

👉 Know your content inside out. Don’t write a full script. Instead, prepare with bullet points and, if needed, use elegant, modern notes to support you.

👉 Enhance your delivery. Whether it’s your voice, your body language, or your authenticity, you need to ensure that what you say resonates.

👉 Connect with your audience. Don’t only speak on stage - speak with the audience. Engage, entertain, and build those moments of connection.

Knowing that you’ve prepared in this way, and that you’re ready to deliver at a world-class level, is what turns nervous energy into excitement and makes stepping on stage an experience you can love.

Ever had that feeling?
