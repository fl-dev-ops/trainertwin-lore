The Supercar Owners Circle AG Weekend in Sardinia 2025 was, again, one to remember for a lifetime. 

With a wide range of experiences from beautiful coastal road drives to drag racing and donuts on the track to dinners in outstanding locations to 007’s Lotus Esprit back in Costa Smeralda – and one of my personal highlights is when I got to drive a Dallara!

I’m a big fan of the car, Giampaolo Dallara’s story and the direction the company is going in with the new initiative Motor Valley.

Thanks to the entire SOC team for working with me again – looking forward to the next one!
