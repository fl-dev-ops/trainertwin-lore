Some events remind you exactly why you do what you do.
One Young World is one of those events.

This year, I had the privilege of hosting the entire Closing Ceremony in Munich, guiding the audience through the opening, the key moments, the transitions, and the final celebrations.

An experience I will carry with me for a long time.

One Young World is not just another global summit. It’s a movement built to empower the next generations of leaders to take on humanity’s biggest challenges. And since its inception in 2009, OYW Ambassadors have already impacted more than 13.7 million people around the world.

Sharing the stage with Kate Robertson and David Jones, the founders behind this mission, was a powerful moment. Their vision and commitment to young leaders is something you feel instantly.

The Closing Ceremony brought that same energy to life.

* lka Horstmeier, Member of the Board of BMW AG, People and Real Estate,
  Labor Relations Director, arriving on stage the BMW way.
* Dr. Christian Scharpf, Munich’s “Wiesn Boss,” representing the host city
   with pride
* Geordin Hill-Lewis, Mayor of Cape Town, officially welcoming the world to
   the next One Young World.

And one of my personal highlights: Terry Crews and Rebecca King-Crews.

I’ve followed Terry’s journey for years. Being on stage with him was special, he brings a warmth, honesty, and energy that elevates the entire room whether it is at AGT or at One Young World. He is always a phenomenal human being, both on and off stage.

The audience at BMW Welt was unforgettable. From the opening to the ribbon-bowl ceremony to the final celebration, the atmosphere was electric, purpose, joy, and connection all in one space.

I’m truly grateful that One Young World, BMW, and the City of Munich entrusted me with hosting this moment. It was an honour to guide this global community through such a meaningful evening.

If you haven’t explored the impact of One Young World yet, take a moment.

It’s an organization that’s genuinely shaping the future and one worth supporting.
