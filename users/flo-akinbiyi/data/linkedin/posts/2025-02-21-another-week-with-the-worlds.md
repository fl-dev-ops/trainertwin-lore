Another Week with the World’s Greatest Chefs Comes to an End

Gulfood 2025 Top Table was once again an absolute highlight of my year - and it will stay that way. Having the opportunity to work alongside the best chefs of our generation is an absolutely surreal experience.

Sharing the stage and speaking with Alain Ducasse - with his 21 Michelin stars, 35 restaurants, and an empire he has built - was a moment I will savor for a lifetime.

The passion, love, and dedication of chefs like Vladimir Mukhin, Ángel León, Chef Taka, Chef Hisa, Fredrik Berselius, and so many other incredible talents was truly inspiring. And the energy they brought to the table? Unmatched.

But what’s becoming even clearer is that the next frontier of gastronomy is being shaped by sustainability, healthier food choices, and even AI in the kitchen. Vladimir Mukhin himself used ChatGPT to bring back the culinary essence of Alain Bocuse, crafting a dish inspired by a legendary chef who is no longer with us. Innovation in food is happening right before our eyes.

And let’s not forget Locavore - their work is simply amazing.

To top it off, I had the chance to experience Dubai World Cuisine and a four-hand dinner featuring Dubai’s Michelin-starred Chef Rahul Rana alongside the vegan and Michelin Green Star mastermind Zizi Hattab, with her three restaurants in Switzerland.

I’m wrapping up this week inspired, elevated, and filled with new learnings. A huge thank you to Dubai World Trade Centre for the opportunity to be part of this once again.

And now, it’s time for the weekend.
