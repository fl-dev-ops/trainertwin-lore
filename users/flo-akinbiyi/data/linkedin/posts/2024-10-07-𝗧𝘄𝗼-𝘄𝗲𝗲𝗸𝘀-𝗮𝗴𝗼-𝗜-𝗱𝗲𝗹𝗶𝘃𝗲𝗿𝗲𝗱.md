As a #Presenter, #KeynoteSpeaker, and #CommunicationCoach, I regularly stand on stages and speak to audiences. Nevertheless, this was a truly special experience.

I was invited by the amazing TEDX team at RWA, Harsimar Kaur Chhatwal, to speak to a group of students and parents and inspire them under their theme, “Why Not?”

As a Communication Coach, I was tempted to deliver a big talk on public speaking and all its power and glory. But upon reflection, I knew that this would have been all about me and marketing myself, rather than focusing on how I could bring the most value to the students.

That’s why I chose to talk about the future of work, specifically about the human edge.

We all know that with the rapid technological advancements we are seeing these days, thinking ahead five or ten years into the future, we can only imagine what work and #technology will look like.
