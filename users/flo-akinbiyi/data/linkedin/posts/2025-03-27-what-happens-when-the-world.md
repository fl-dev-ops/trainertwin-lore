What happens when the world comes together?

A powerful change.

Looking back at the 10th UNAOC Global Forum in Cascais, Portugal, I was struck by the incredible energy and purpose that filled the room. It wasn’t only about speeches from global leaders - it was about the real, hands-on work being done to make the world a better place.

I had the honor of hosting the Intercultural Innovation Hub, where we celebrated 10 projects from across the globe. These were not just ideas, but really-world efforts that are actively breaking down barriers, building connections, and fostering understanding between cultures. It was humbling to witness how much can be accomplished when people from different backgrounds come together with a shared goal of peace.

What stood out most to me during those days was how small actions, led by passionate individuals, can create a ripple effect that spreads across the world. It reinforced my belief that no matter where we’re from, we’re all connected by the same desire for a brighter future.

I’m grateful for the opportunity to be a part of such an impactful event and to contribute in my own way. Here's to the work ahead and the change we can all create together!
