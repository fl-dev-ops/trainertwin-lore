This is so cold. Whoever tells you that ice baths are fun, I disagree. I hate cold water

Nevertheless, once in a while, I will do an ice bath, mainly for the reason that I want to overcome myself!

Overcome my limiting beliefs, negative thinking, and false belief that I cannot do it.
⁠Embracing discomfort which you have to face in life and in public speaking.
Of course for the Health Benefits: Reduced inflammation, improves circulation, increases metabolism, and burns fat.

Tony Robbins' statement in Jay Shetty’s podcast really inspired me!
He emphasized the importance of conditioning your brain to act when necessary. 
“When I tell my brain it is go time - it is go time!”
Powerful words.

Lately, I've started incorporating these types of activities in my public speaking workshops —haven’t done the ice bath with participants yet, but other challenging activities that require you to confront your fears. 

To be a great speaker you must do the things that scare you, step out of your comfort zone, and come out on the other side flying.

Have you ever tried an ice bath or recently done something that scares you?
