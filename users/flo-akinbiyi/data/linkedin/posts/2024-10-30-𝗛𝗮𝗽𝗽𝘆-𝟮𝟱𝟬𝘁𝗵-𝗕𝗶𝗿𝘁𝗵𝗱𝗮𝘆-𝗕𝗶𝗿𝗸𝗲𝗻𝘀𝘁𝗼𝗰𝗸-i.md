I had the honor of attending the 250 year celebration of the icon German footwear brand

It brought back #memories of our collaboration back in 2017, when I shot a #Birkenstock story. 📸 Back then, just like today, I spoke about my relationship with the brand and showcased my life—because Birkenstock has always been more than just footwear for me.

These healthy, stylish sandals have been a part of my journey for decades. They always remind me of my home country, Germany. 🇩🇪 I still remember wearing my Birkenstocks as a young boy while working as a dishwasher in our local water park's kitchen. Those are memories I cherish for a lifetime. The shoes have become a symbol that keeps me humble and grounded, while still making me look good. ✅
