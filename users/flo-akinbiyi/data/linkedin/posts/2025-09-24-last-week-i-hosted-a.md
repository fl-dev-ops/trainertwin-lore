Last week I hosted a finance conference and I observed how often the presenter is disconnected from the audience.

In most all cases the presenter has good, relevant content but they make mistakes that leave the audience with no other choice than to zone out.

Here are the 7 most common mistakes I observed:

👉Word heavy, complicated slides with small font size
(No 6 is not a good size for the stage)

👉The presenter is reading their slides
(Thank you, I am capable of reading myself)

👉Speaking with a monotone voice
(It signals to the audience what you are saying is not important)

👉Not engaging with the audience
(Feels sometimes like the presenter is on stage talking to themselves and not to the audience)

👉Starting the presentation with a irrelevant or weak opening for example: I understand it has been a long day
(You could also then say: my presentation is just another one you need to listen to so feel free to close your eyes and take a nap)

👉Saying it’s a complicated slide but what I am trying to say here is…
(The presenter simply did not change a complicated slide into a simple one - if Apple can do it so can you)

👉Finish with "thank you and any questions" instead of a memorable closing
(Why say something powerful, memorable that stays with the audience if you can say: “that’s it from me” and run off the stage, don’t need to say more)

All of these mistakes could have been avoided and the presentations could have been many times more effective!
