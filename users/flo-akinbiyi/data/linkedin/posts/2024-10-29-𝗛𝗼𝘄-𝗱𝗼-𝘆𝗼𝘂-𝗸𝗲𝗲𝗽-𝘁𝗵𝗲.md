𝗛𝗼𝘄 𝗱𝗼 𝘆𝗼𝘂 𝗸𝗲𝗲𝗽 𝘁𝗵𝗲 𝗮𝘂𝗱𝗶𝗲𝗻𝗰𝗲 𝗲𝗻𝗴𝗮𝗴𝗲𝗱 𝗱𝘂𝗿𝗶𝗻𝗴 𝗮 𝗽𝗿𝗲𝘀𝗲𝗻𝘁𝗮𝘁𝗶𝗼𝗻? 

As a #CommunicationCoach, #KeynoteSpeaker, and #presenter, I’ve learned through experience that engaging your audience is often what transforms a good presentation into a great one. ✅

It’s not just about delivering content; it’s about involving your audience and inspiring them to take action. 

𝗛𝗲𝗿𝗲 𝗮𝗿𝗲 𝗳𝗶𝘃𝗲 𝘁𝗲𝗰𝗵𝗻𝗶𝗾𝘂𝗲𝘀 𝘁𝗵𝗮𝘁 𝗰𝗼𝗻𝘀𝗶𝘀𝘁𝗲𝗻𝘁𝗹𝘆 𝘄𝗼𝗿𝗸 𝗳𝗼𝗿 𝗺𝗲: 

𝟭. 𝗦𝘁𝗮𝗿𝘁 𝘄𝗶𝘁𝗵 𝗮 𝗽𝗼𝘄𝗲𝗿𝗳𝘂𝗹 𝗼𝗽𝗲𝗻𝗶𝗻𝗴. Avoid the typical “Welcome, ladies and gentlemen…” Instead, grab attention right from the start with a hook, icebreaker, story, or striking visual❗ A strong opening sets the tone and piques curiosity. 

𝟮. 𝗔𝘀𝗸 𝗾𝘂𝗲𝘀𝘁𝗶𝗼𝗻𝘀. In my communication coaching workshops, I ask plenty of questions to make the experience more of a conversation than a monologue. It helps gauge the audience’s knowledge and keeps them engaged. Did you know that questions can even release dopamine? 

𝟯. 𝗨𝘀𝗲 𝘃𝗶𝘀𝘂𝗮𝗹𝘀. Ditch the text-heavy slides. Research shows that our brains process images much faster than text, so use well-designed visuals or videos to keep the audience engaged and emotionally connected. 

𝟰. 𝗣𝗼𝗹𝗹𝗶𝗻𝗴. I’m a big fan of polling and the classic show of hands. This technique gets everyone to participate, puts their phones down, and keeps them 100% focused. When you engage the audience this way, you’re in control. 

𝟱. 𝗧𝗲𝗹𝗹 𝘀𝘁𝗼𝗿𝗶𝗲𝘀. Storytelling remains one of the most powerful tools in public speaking. When I notice the audience’s energy dipping, a relevant story with a strong delivery never fails to recapture attention. 

These are just a few of the strategies I use to keep my audience engaged, but the list doesn’t end here. Brainstorming ideas with your team, ChatGPT, friends, or colleagues can help you discover even more ways to captivate your audience.
