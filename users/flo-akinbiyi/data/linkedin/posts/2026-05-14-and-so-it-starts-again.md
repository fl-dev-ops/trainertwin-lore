And so it starts again.

After months without travel, the season is back for me.

Over the next few weeks, I have multiple trips scheduled, and I’m kicking things off in Riva del Garda at the European Outdoor Week.

I feel quite emotional about this event because my career actually started outdoors with snowboarding.

More than 20 years ago, I took care of the ski and snowboard fun park in Blomberg in Bad Tölz, which was named the Mojo Park. From there, my journey led me to event management and experiential marketing, picking up the microphone and becoming a presenter and communication coach.

It’s amazing how life works sometimes.

You never really know where the path will take you if you’re open to it.

And yes, I’m also a tiny bit :), excited about climbing, good pasta, and a few grapes.

Most of all, I can’t wait to experience the innovation, the energy of the participants, and to immerse myself in the outdoor industry.
