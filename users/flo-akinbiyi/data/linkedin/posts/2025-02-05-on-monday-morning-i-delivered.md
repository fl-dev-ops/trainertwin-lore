On Monday morning I delivered a keynote for the DHL MEA Sales Team but I felt sh*t inside!

Let me be real here, I didn’t feel great.

I didn’t sleep well - woke up a few times because my Shi-Tzu doggy decided that he is a solid guard dog and he had a go at cats, cars and what not.

Then I got up at 4:45 AM and meditated - good start.

Afterward I spent an hour fine-tuning my slides. And my son joined me on the couch and unfortunately I allowed myself to get into fight with him. With a 6 year old! I know! Happens. At the same time, my PowerPoint crashed, and everything I had worked on was gone.

Even with meditation, this was not the best mindset to step into a keynote.
So, how do you deliver at your best when you feel this bad?

The answer is simple: You focus on the audience.

They didn’t come to see you wallow in misery.

They came to learn.

They came for value, information, and inspiration. So, you park the drama. It’s only an hour. Everyone can park their drama for one hour. And after the keynote, you can come right back to it.

And it was worth it. Quite a few attendees thanked me for the insights and knowledge shared, which was great.

But then one gentleman came to me and said in 7 years with the organization this was the best kick off that he experienced. And he had heard multiple speeches about communication but mine had an impact on him.

🙏Mission achieved!

And worth parking the drama for an hour.

And I did return back to it!

As I felt bad about the fight with my son, I took a break, picked him up from school and we spent some extra quality time.

This story Isn’t about hiding your emotions. It’s about focusing on the job.
And if the moment allowed, I might have shared how I felt that day with the audience. But in this case, for this session, that wasn’t what was not what worked in this scenario.

How do you deal with negative emotions and speaking?
