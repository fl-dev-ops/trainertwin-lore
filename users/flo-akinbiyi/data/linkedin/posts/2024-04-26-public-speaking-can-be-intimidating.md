Public speaking can be intimidating. When you are asked to stand on an international stage, many get stressed out, but you actually don't have to.

If you find your voice and share messages and content that you are proud of, it becomes much easier to perform, even on a global stage. Finding your voice means daring to present yourself as you really are, not just hiding behind a professional image.


When it comes to sharing content and messages, it means embracing the risks— sharing impactful messages and possibly engaging in things that other’s wouldn’t. However, doing this requires knowing your audience, understanding their perspectives, and carefully considering the format to avoid causing offense.

Find your voice and make sure it's heard out there, because you deserve it. Wouldn't you agree?
