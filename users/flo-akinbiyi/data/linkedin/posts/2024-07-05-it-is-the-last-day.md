It is the last day of school! 

Finally, the holidays have arrived. There’s all this mumbo-jumbo about: “If you have a job that you love, you never have to work a day in your life again.” I call this nonsense.

I love my job! I truly do!!! I’m grateful every day that I get to do what I love. But it’s not just about working. Life is also about living—enjoying life to its fullest. Holidays, weekends, family time, and travel… these things make me incredibly happy.

So, as you can tell from the expression on my face in this photo, I am super excited to be off work for a few weeks. Don’t worry, though! We’ll still be posting some content. The team’s got it covered. And while I’m trying to disconnect, I’ll still be available for the really, really important calls. If you don’t have a million-dollar project, maybe hold off. But okay, if it’s crucial, just reach out. I’ll carve out a bit of time—just a bit because the next few weeks are all about family, family, and more family.

Are you as ready as I am for the holidays? And no, it’s not true that we came late to school these last few days. 😉
