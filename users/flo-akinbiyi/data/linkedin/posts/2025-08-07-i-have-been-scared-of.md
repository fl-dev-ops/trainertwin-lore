I have been scared of public speaking many times in my life but scared or not I do it anyways!

Speaking in front of an audience is a privilege and an honor - one of the best ways to create amazing opportunities for your personal and professional life and there’s nothing wrong with being scared.

What you need to do, is find ways to overcome anxiety, calm yourself down so you are able to perform, 

Hence, I can only recommend that before your next important speaking opportunity, you create a tool kit that allows you to speak up and try out meditation. 

Breathing exercises, visualization and knowing your content exceptionally well.

Recording yourself and practicing in front of different audiences or any other techniques.
