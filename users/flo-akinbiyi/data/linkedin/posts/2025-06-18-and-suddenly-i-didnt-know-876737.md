And suddenly, I didn’t know what to do with my own hands.

Does that sound familiar?

As soon as we step on a stage or speak in front of high-status people, something strange happens:

Our body language changes and not always for the better.

Hands, which we never think about 99% of the time, suddenly feel like awkward, dangling appendages.

What do most people do?

	•	Hands in pockets
	•	Locked in front of the body
	•	The “steeple” position - made famous by Angela Merkel

Some communication coaches even recommend using the steeple as a power move.

But here’s what I’ve found:
One of the most open, trustworthy, and human gestures is simply this -
👉 Show the inside of your hands. It says: “I have nothing to hide.”

This kind of body language can be learned.

It takes awareness, practice, and a little discomfort.

But over time, it becomes a powerful part of how you show up.

Body language is more than posture - it’s presence.

And it’s another essential layer in winning communication.
