Unforgettable Experience at the MUSEUM OF ICE CREAM

Manish V. and I met at a conference in Abu Dhabi and hit it off right away as I introduced him on stage.

When my family and I were in New York two years ago, we visited the Museum of Ice Cream, and to this day, I cherish the memories of that visit:

	•	The team was exceptionally lovely and cheerful, making the entire visit delightful.
	•	The ice cream served was not only delicious but integral to the immersive experience.
	•	Every area was designed to be Instagrammable, exciting, and engaging.
	•	Even the giveaways were sustainable and well-considered.

I really wish more places offered experiences like the Museum of Ice Cream that whisk you away from the daily grind and transport you to a world filled with joy and happiness. Thanks to Manish and the entire team for creating such a wonderful experience.

I heard they are opening a branch in Miami, and if I make it there this year, I’ll definitely find time to visit.
