Yesterday, I watched a managing director give their end-of-year speech to the team.

The venue was a nice Japanese restaurant. Everyone was in a great mood. People were laughing and enjoying their lunch. 

But the speech was not as exciting and as impactful as it could have been.

The opening was predictable. The middle was generic.
The powerful closing was missing. 

It wasn’t a bad speech, but it wasn’t one to remember and certainly not one that inspired! 

That’s the missed opportunity! 

These moments aren’t just “routine updates” 

They are moments for the leader to shine. 
(After putting in quite a bit of effort!) 

It’s a moment to: 
* Celebrate and acknowledge the past year.
* To revive the team spirit and reinforce belonging and values.
* It’s a moment to spark energy and passion for the coming year. 

Too many leaders treat these speeches like compliance tasks. 

Bullet points, numbers, acknowledgement, done.
No story. No spark. No direction. 

And in a time where 2025 is closing with uncertainty, pressure, and rapid change, teams don’t need information, they need meaning!
