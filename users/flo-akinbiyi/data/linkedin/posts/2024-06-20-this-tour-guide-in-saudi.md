This tour guide in Saudi Arabia, Nasr al-Nasr, was an inspiration and a delight to observe.

If you know that an advanced communicator should always look out for learning opportunities, then seeing outstanding communicators is always bliss. #EachOneTeachOne

During a recent visit to Riyadh, the capital of Saudi Arabia, I joined a cultural tour organized by Scientist’s Gift. We visited the historical birthplace of the Kingdom, Riyadh’s largest mosque, the Al-Rajhi Mosque, and savored a wonderful traditional meal. Our tour guide, Nasr al-Nasr turned the entire experience into one I will never forget.

What made Nasr so remarkable?

	•	Contagious Smile and Energy: His enthusiasm was infectious.
	•	Refined Storytelling: He expertly used storytelling to bring the culture to life.
	•	Excellent Body Language: His gestures perfectly complemented his narratives.
	•	Interactive Experience: He used props like the traditional Arabic misk, which we got to try, and allowed us to feel and experience traditional outfits.

I had the opportunity to try the Arabic misk, a traditional toothbrush with religious significance, see prayers in the mosque, and ask questions about Islam, culture, and traditions. This immersive experience helped us all understand and appreciate the depth of Saudi culture.

If you visit Saudi Arabia, I highly recommend taking a tour with Scientist’s Gift and requesting Nasr al-Nasr as your guide. His ability to convey deep cultural insights through engaging communication is unparalleled.
