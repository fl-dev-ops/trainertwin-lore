I love traveling, but I hate the unfriendly customer experience at some airports.

Last week, I took an overnight flight from Abu Dhabi to Munich Airport International, and from the moment I stepped out of the plane until I arrived at the passport control, I passed by 12 people from the airport staff.

And what shocks me is that there was only one who greeted all the passengers with a friendly good morning.

Not a single one said: “Welcome to Munich.”

After the overnight flight, I wandered around the airport, tried and eventually stopped information desk to get directions.

**Mohammed Keliel-Bengabou** sat behind the desk and I asked him: “I have two questions. Can you please help?”

He responded: “The first one is for free, but for the second one I might have to charge you.”

That moment my face lit up. I loved it. I loved him and within seconds we were laughing. I loved the humorous and human interaction.

He pointed me in the right direction and even though I did not find anything that I was looking for, he changed my mood!

Being nice and communicating in a friendly and human way with customers, clients, passengers is not rocket science. Mohammed didn’t give me an upgrade or something for free, he simply made me feel welcomed and taken care off.

It only takes training and a customer centric mindset to change how people feel and experience an airport, an airline or any other service.

The truth is that passengers and customers won’t remember most interactions, but they will remember how you made them feel!

Thank you, Mohammed, for making my day!
