- Have you every had a culinary experience you'll never forget?

Working with a culinary legend? Nothing short of extraordinary! 🍴✨

My experience at Gulfood - The World’s Largest, Most Trusted F&B Sourcing Event 2024 at Dubai World Trade Centre with Alain Passard, a three-star Michelin chef, was an experience I’ll always remember. 

Alain has held his Michelin stars for over a staggering 30 years - a rare achievement in the culinary world. 🏆 Being alongside such an accomplished chef was truly special. Although I wasn't chopping onions or cleaning dishes (my cooking skills are admittedly modest!), I had the unique opportunity to taste ingredients and co-host a masterclass with Pearl Yan. 🥘👨‍🍳

What struck me most about Alain, and many other high-end Michelin star chefs, is their palpable love for cooking. It's more than a job; it's their passion, their calling, bringing them joy and happiness. 🌟

Alain's masterclass, charmingly delivered in French with his son, Louis, translating, was a blend of humor, pace, and clarity. His warm personality shone through, and his willingness to share the spotlight with his son and others made the event even more memorable. 🎤👨‍👦

Gulfood 2024 was spectacular, with Top Table events and Dubai World Cuisine dinners. It's an honor to be a part of this amazing event every year. 🎉👏
