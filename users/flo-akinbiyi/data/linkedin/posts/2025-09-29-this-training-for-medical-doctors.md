This training for medical doctors was, without a doubt, one of the most exciting and impactful sessions I have ever conducted.

Doctors are brilliant professionals who dedicate years, often decades, to mastering the human body inside and out. They know everything about what goes wrong and how to fix it. But what struck me during this session was how eager they were to learn something outside their usual expertise: how to make a bigger impact when delivering presentations and speeches.

I had the honor of working with the Nordberg Medical with an international group of professionals who regularly present on stage. Seeing their excitement to explore techniques for using their voice more powerfully and refining their delivery was inspiring. Together, we went through exercises and insights that will stay with us for a lifetime.

And to top it all off, being in Iceland made the experience even more special.
A heartfelt thank you to Nordberg Medical, Daniela-Beatrice Ebenburger, MBA, Jack Yu, and Josine Bolenius for having me - it was truly unforgettable.
