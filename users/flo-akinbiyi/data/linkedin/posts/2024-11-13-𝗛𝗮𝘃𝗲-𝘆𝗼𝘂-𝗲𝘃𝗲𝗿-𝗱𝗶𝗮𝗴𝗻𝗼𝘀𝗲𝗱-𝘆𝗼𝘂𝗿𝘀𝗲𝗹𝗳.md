𝗛𝗮𝘃𝗲 𝘆𝗼𝘂 𝗲𝘃𝗲𝗿 𝗱𝗶𝗮𝗴𝗻𝗼𝘀𝗲𝗱 𝘆𝗼𝘂𝗿𝘀𝗲𝗹𝗳 𝘄𝗶𝘁𝗵 𝗰𝗼𝗿𝗽𝗼𝗿𝗮𝘁𝗲 𝘀𝗰𝗵𝗶𝘇𝗼𝗽𝗵𝗿𝗲𝗻𝗶𝗮?

I define #CorporateSchizophrenia as the state when you show up in #business significantly different than how you show up in your private life. If your #communication, actions, and behavior at work are noticeably different from how you are at home, then there’s a disconnect. At the end of the day, we’re one person. We’re always humans—so why should we change our values, #identity, or authenticity just because we’re in a work setting?

I’ll admit, that I’ve fallen into the trap of corporate schizophrenia multiple times. For example, I once cut off my hair to fit into the corporate world, underestimating how much people actually valued my style and appearance. Even with an afro, I could be professional and well-dressed. Nobody says that just because you have an afro, you have to be sloppy.

𝗕𝗲𝗶𝗻𝗴 𝗮𝘂𝘁𝗵𝗲𝗻𝘁𝗶𝗰 𝗰𝗿𝗲𝗮𝘁𝗲𝘀 𝘁𝗿𝘂𝘀𝘁—and I’ve always been conscious of people who show a different side of themselves at work. Show me who you really are, and I’ll show you who I really am. If we don’t embrace authenticity, we miss out on one of the most important factors in business: creating #trust.

So whether it’s in your communication or work style, remember that staying true to yourself and not falling into the trap of corporate schizophrenia is one of the most powerful #business strategies you can have.
