10 years ago, I quit my job. 

The truth is that being employed was just not part of my journey. 
I was passionate about organizing events, but I had an inner calling. 

And because of that calling and because of my incredible life coach, Kate Walsh, I started to work on my communication and public speaking skills for several years. 

That work gave me the knowledge, the confidence, and the know-how to leave the safety of employment and do my own thing. 

In 2016, I did 2 additional presenter courses, one in London and one in Dubai. 
I hosted my first ever conference, a business conference. I did a Moto GP project. 
I hosted an internal celebration for Docker. 
I spent a fortune on printing cue cards. 
I bought my first stage suits. 
I took a ton of photos with photographers and brands to market myself. 
I had no idea that this could become a sustainable business, but I knew I needed to continue.
Because the day I hosted that first conference in a room of 1,000 people, I heard my own voice through the speakers and I had one thought: 
“You were born for this.”
