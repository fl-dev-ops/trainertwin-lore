I took a train from Paris to Munich.
OMG! 
We got delayed.
I miss my connection. 
But regardless, it was a great ride and here is why!

After last week’s Ultimate Supercar Garage in Paris, I took the train to Munich on Monday morning. I thought it’s a great use of my time because I can work on the journey.

But I did forget how unreliable the German train system is.

I had to change trains in Mannheim, and because my train from Paris was delayed, I missed my connection.

To be honest, it wasn’t until I finally sat down on another train to make my way to Munich that I could breathe again.

But then my mood brightened because of the train conductor.

When Jörg Leiendecker welcomed us through the PA to the train he used:
*  Humor
* Positive tone of voice 
* And his wonderful personality was shining through the speakers

A short moment of human connection that brightened the day and changed how I feel about Deutsche Bahn

I hope that the team DB sees this post and gives him a big tap on the shoulder!
