The recent developments, speeches and statements from leaders around the world reminded me strongly of what many of those that have the opportunity to address an audience forget.

 Communication should always be human to human. 

Yes, it is about your content. 

Yes, it is the words, your message, but simply to remind yourself that you are communicating to another human, can have a strong impact on your message.

It can have a strong impact on your delivery and on the way you approach communication for business and politics as a leader, a junior or senior.

Let’s communicate more as humans.

Because it makes communication better and it can make the world a better place.
