COMMUNICATION IN 2026: HUMAN x AI

How to use AI without losing your voice, credibility, or human impact?

Join the AHK workshop: 
COMMUNICATION IN 2026: HUMAN x AI with Flo Akinbiyi 
🗓️ January 16 
📍 Zabeel House, Dubai 

AI can help you find the words, shape the structure, and move faster.

But the real craft, judgment, intention, tone, credibility, and connection still lives with you.

In this workshop, we’ll explore how to work with AI without handing over your thinking, your voice, or your leadership presence.

Because most professionals are feeling the same push-pull right now: more tools, more speed and somehow less clarity, less authenticity, and messages that sound “good” but don’t sound like anyone.

Using our Human-First Communication Philosophy, we’ll look at where AI genuinely strengthens communication, where it quietly dilutes it, and how leaders stay firmly in control every time.

You’ll bring the real communication challenges you’re dealing with day to day, and you’ll leave knowing how to use AI to communicate faster and more clearly while building authority, credibility, and human impact.

This isn’t human vs. AI.

In a world where everyone has access to the same technology, the advantage is the human edge clarity, confidence, and a voice people actually trust, using AI as a powerful ally.
