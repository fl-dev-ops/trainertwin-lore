Bits & Pretzels Main Stage Hosts 2025: Lara Sophie Bothur, Céline Flores Willers & Flo Akinbiyi! 
We’re beyond excited to welcome back three familiar faces to the Bits & Pretzels stage this year: Flo, Lara, and Celine!

Each of them has lit up the Bits stage before but never all together. After seeing their individual magic, we knew we had to unite them. As a trio, they truly embody the spirit of Bits.

Meet the Hosts:
Lara Sophie Bothur – Deloitte's Voice for Innovation in Europe, Germany’s first full-time Corporate Influencer, and the largest female LinkedIn tech voice in DACH & beyond.
Céline Flores Willers – Founder & CEO of The People Branding Company, empowering brands and thought leaders to shape the future of work.
Flo Akinbiyi – Celebrated stage host, communication expert, and will be back again, guiding through the main stage for the forth year now with his captivating presence and expertise.  
A trio of international voices at the intersection of innovation, communication, and tech-for-good.
