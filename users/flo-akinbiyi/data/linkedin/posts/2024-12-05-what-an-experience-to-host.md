What an experience to host WorlDEF Dubai 24 at Dubai Commerce City! 🌍✨

What an incredible kickoff to WORLDEF Dubai 2024! From thought-provoking sessions to visionary insights, the first day has truly set the tone for this landmark event.

Thank you, Omar NART, for getting the day started on such a high note. 

We were was graced by the presence of His Highness Sheikh Ahmed bin Saeed Al Maktoum, a visionary leader and chairman of many of Dubai’s finest organizations, including Emirates Airlines, Dubai Holding, DS, and more.

We were honored to welcome His Excellency Dr. Thani bin Ahmed Al Zeyoudi, UAE Minister of State for Foreign Trade, whose keynote address highlighted Dubai’s dynamic role as a global hub for innovation, trade, and e-commerce.

We also had the privilege of hearing from His Excellency Mohammad Ali Rashed Lootah, President & CEO of Dubai Chambers, who shared invaluable insights into fostering growth and innovation in the digital commerce ecosystem.

Additionally, His Excellency Mohammad Shael Al Saadi, CEO of the Dubai Corporation for Consumer Protection & Fair Trade, captivated us with his perspectives on safeguarding consumer interests in the digital era, emphasizing the importance of trust, transparency, and ethical practices in the evolving e-commerce landscape.

💡 Key Highlights:
 🔹 Fireside chats on the future of quick commerce and digital payments.
 🔹 Panels discussing sustainable logistics and e-commerce marketplaces.
 🔹Tne MOU signing between Dubai CommerCity, QUIKBOT TECHNOLOGIES and Aramex
 🔹 Exclusive insights from industry leaders like Francis Dufay, CEO of Jumia, Clara Shih, CEO of WorldFirst, Alastair Croker, CEO, Tradeling, Adriano Silva 🌎🙌🏼🏆 of Global Sales for GCC region VTEX, Mohamad ElAnsari of Trendyol, and many others.
 🔹 Vibrant networking and knowledge-sharing moments that sparked new ideas and collaborations.

Being in the heart of such a flourishing industry, surrounded by outstanding content, speakers, and attendees, is always exhilarating. Hosting this event at Dubai CommerceCity was an absolute highlight.

I couldn’t wait for day two, where I had the pleasure of speaking with Mitch Bittermann, and many other distinguished speakers. Plus, we’ll witness the startup competition this afternoon, a segment that is always near and dear to my heart.

What a fantastic way to kick off this new engagement—I’m already looking forward to many more events to come!
