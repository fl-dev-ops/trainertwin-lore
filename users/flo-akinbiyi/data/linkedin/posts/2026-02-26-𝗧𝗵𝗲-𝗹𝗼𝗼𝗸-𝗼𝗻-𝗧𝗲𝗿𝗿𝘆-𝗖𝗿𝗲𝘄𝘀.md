At the One Young World closing ceremony in Munich, Terry Crews was our VIP guest, closing an inspiring week for the entire community.
 
Part of his script was simple:  “Please welcome back to the stage… Flo.”
 
What he didn’t know?
We had something in common.
 
Yes. The skin tone. 
 
So when he read the line, he turned around and saw me standing there in the middle of Germany.
The surprise.  
The smile.  
The joy on his face.
 
Unfiltered. Genuine. Human.
 
And in that split second, the entire arena felt it.
 
I first became a fan of Terry Crews through his legendary Old Spice commercials.  
Then, as an actor. 
An entertainer.  
The host of America’s Got Talent.
 
Always energy. 
Always present. 
Always bold.
 
But what struck me most on that stage at BMW Welt wasn’t the showman.
 
𝗜𝘁 𝘄𝗮𝘀 𝘁𝗵𝗲 𝗺𝗮𝗻.
 
His kindness.  
His soft-hearted nature.  
His dedication to empowering the next generation.  
His love when he spoke about his wife.  
His humility in how he interacted backstage.
 
Big stage. Global audience. Massive presence.
 
And yet, deeply human.
 
That’s the part I’ll remember.
