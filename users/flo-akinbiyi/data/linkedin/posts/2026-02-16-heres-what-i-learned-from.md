Here’s what I learned from France’s best-selling international Artist: Richard Orlinski.

A few weeks ago, I had the opportunity to meet Richard Orlinski on and off the stage at the Ultimate Supercar Garage during Retromobile in Paris. And I was truly excited when I saw his name on the programme because his work is a masterclass in something that is much bigger than his fame.

No matter where I travelled around the world, Miami, Munich, Cyprus, Italy, Dubai, I've come across his artworks. And every single time, my reaction was the same. 

I see the art, I know it's his, and I love it. 

Because the identity is clear, the sharp, faceted forms, the bold presence, and it all comes with an unmistakable signature that you recognize right away. If you look at these aspects, it's actually no surprise that he has become one of the best-selling French contemporary artists internationally. 

And that is the lesson that I take away from Richard Orlinski. Whether you are building a personal brand, working on your presentation skills, or stepping onto a stage to pitch, inform, or inspire. 

The question you have to ask yourself is: 
Can my audience instantly recognize me when they hear my voice, when they look at me, when they read my content? 

Is it 100% clear it is yours, or could it be anyone? That's the question Richard Orlinski sparked in me. And in case you haven't figured out what his art looks like, I'll post his iconic Wild Kong in the comments below.

I'm actually curious, do you have one or multiple signature elements that make your presence instantly recognizable?
