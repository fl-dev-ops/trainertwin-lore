I'm excited to be traveling this week to Switzerland to celebrate the 10th anniversary of the Supercar Owners Circle AG in the beautiful village of Andermatt.

Five years ago, I hosted my first event for the Supercar Owners Circle in Dubai, followed by their SOC weekend in Switzerland. Being part of an event with people who are passionate about automotive masterpieces, love memorable experiences, and are always searching for automotive perfection is always inspiring and very special.

I've had the honor to be on stage and converse with Horacio Pagani, Mate Rimac, Christian von Koenigsegg, and Fabrizio Giugiaro—visionaries who dream of automotive masterpieces like no one else. 

I can't wait for this event and to be back in beautiful Switzerland, creating more automotive memories that will last a lifetime. Are you around Andermatt next week? Let’s connect!
