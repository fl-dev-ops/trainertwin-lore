Yesterday, I was in the hospital. 
Don’t worry! Nothing wrong with me just a quick check up. 
Or that’s what I thought!

If you’ve ever been in an Italian hospital, you know what a visit looks like:  
waiting… waiting… and more waiting. 

Most people were on their phones. 
Disconnected. Isolated. 

And honestly, most of the staff wasn’t particularly friendly either. 
I could feel myself getting annoyed and upset.

You know that feeling, when you start focusing on everything that’s not working. 

But then… two women changed the entire experience!

They made people laugh. 
They made people smile. 
Grown-ups and kids alike. 

One of them played on a ukulele. 

And just when I thought, okay, this is already quite special, she pulled out soap bubbles and started blowing them around the waiting room. 

At me. At others. At people who hadn’t looked up from their phones in ages. 

Something shifted. 
People looked up. 
People smiled. 

For a moment, there was a real human connection in that room. 

Dolcella and Maria Luise presented me with such a strong reminder. 

It takes so little to create a human connection!
A bit of effort. A bit of presence. 

Thank you for turning my mood from being annoyed about waiting into noticing a meaningful human moment instead.
