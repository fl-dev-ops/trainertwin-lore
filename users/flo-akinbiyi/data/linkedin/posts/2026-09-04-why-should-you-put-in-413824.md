Why should you put in an extra effort when you communicate?

The answer is surprisingly simple. When I deliver communication workshops, I like to think about the audience and put myself into their shoes, into their communication scenarios, and show them creative examples of what communication can be. 

When I'm on stage as a presenter, I like to think how I can give the audience a moment to enjoy, to have fun, to connect to a story that I can tell them, because it's a lot of content, and a breath of fresh air is what helps, a moment of lightness. 

When I am traveling and I'm meeting a lot of people, I always motivate myself, push myself to not just walk around and grab tickets and money and receipts from people, rather than to look them in the eye and say, thank you, ask them how their day is going, maybe tell them something, connect with them, make them laugh, make them smile, because it has an impact. 

Whenever you're opening your mouth to communicate, you can go down the easy route, or the one that costs you more energy, but is more rewarding in the long run.

So ask yourself this to end this week: are you going the extra mile?
