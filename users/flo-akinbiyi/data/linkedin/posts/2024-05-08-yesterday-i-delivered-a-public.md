Yesterday I delivered a public speaking workshop and more than half of the participants expressed their challenges with stage fright.
A few of the participants told me stories that broke my heart. 

They would say….

NO to public speaking engagements!
NO to delivering presentations to management! 
NO to a short interview on camera!

All because their anxiety and nervousness were overwhelming.

I have to say kudos to each and every one of the participants dealing with stage fright. 
Despite the fear, they showed incredible dedication, motivation, and inspiration to overcome it.

I left them with strategies, tools, and approaches to work on.
And now, it's all about the grind. 

Overcoming stage fright isn't a short task; it's a journey. 
But if you really want it, you can do it.

And so, I thought what better video could I post today than this little Q&A with my take on stage fright. 

Have you ever had to deal with stage fright?
