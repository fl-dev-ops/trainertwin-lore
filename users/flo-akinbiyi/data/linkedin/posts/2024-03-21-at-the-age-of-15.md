At the age of 15, I had my first professional photo taken.

Well, “professional photo”... It was shot with a professional camera. Therefore, the quality looks quite nice.

I remember when I received that image, I was fascinated by it. Not that I was so full of myself that I looked quite cute, maybe a little bit, but there was something that fascinated me about my image.

When I reflect back, I could not put my finger on what it was exactly that fascinated me, but I knew that there was something about it.

And today, looking back, I can totally see what fascinated me because it was a professional camera, a good photographer, and a good pose or ideally an authentic moment can show the world really who you are and fascinate the audience.

I truly believe that this photo had an impact on who I have become and what my career turned out to be because somehow I knew that I wanted to present myself to the world or that I did not mind presenting myself to the world.

I wish school would have really taught me that there are possibilities for you to follow this calling. However, I'm grateful for the path that I've taken in life because it has made me who I am.

When you look back at your development and your story, can you see how at an early age you already had a feeling of who you could become one day?
