🌟Reunion on Stage: Co-hosting with Ali El-Sayed🌟

What a treat getting to co-host an event with the one and only Ali El-Sayed, my brother from another mother. I haven't seen Ali in years and haven’t not worked with him for just as long, so this was just something amazing.

I had so much fun with him on stage. With very little presentation and a lot of improvisation, we managed to pull off a great event. 🎤 

I have to congratulate him on his professionalism, his humorous skills, for his amazing and outstanding usage of body language, interaction with the crowd, and also his wonderful voice. Ali, you've got to sing more!👏

A great reunion at the BMW AGMC annual staff party with the brands BMW AGMC, BMW Motorrad AGMC, Rolls-Royce Motor Cars AGMC and MINI AGMC all there where we celebrated people that worked for 10 and 25 years for the organization. 🎉

Thanks to BMW AGMC for having me, thanks to Ali for working with me and creating memories that will definitely last a long, long time.🙏
