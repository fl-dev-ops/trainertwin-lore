How do you prepare for presenting to the Prime Minister of the UAE? The Ruler of Dubai?

A client of mine was suddenly asked to present to His Highness Sheikh Mohammed bin Rashid Al Maktoum.

He was ready.

Why? Because months before, he decided to work on his communication skills as he stepped into a new role. He put in the effort. Grew his confidence. Trained how to structure a message that lands. How to deliver it with clarity and presence. When he was suddenly asked to present his project to Sheikh Mohammed, he did not panic. He stepped up.

Afterwards he texted me:
"Today my dream came true. I presented to HH Sheikh Mohammed our project. It went very well." ✨

That message made my week.

Here is the lesson: you do not prepare for a moment like this in the days before. You prepare months or even a year before. The executives who win the biggest moments are the ones who invested in their communication long before the opportunity arrived. That way you will not be surprised, stressed, or frightened by the opportunity ahead. You will be ready.

If you have a moment like this coming, prepare now. Not later. Now.
