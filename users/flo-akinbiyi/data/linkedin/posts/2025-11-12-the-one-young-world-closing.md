The One Young World closing ceremony at the BMW Welt marks for me a very special milestone. 

The 200th presenter project I delivered! 

I could have not asked for a better project to celebrate this one!

With the One Young World, BMW Group and the city of Munich I get to work with incredible organisations and people. And I got to host an event in my home Bavaria and be involved in a project that makes the world a better place! 

"The #OYW25 Closing Ceremony, held at the breath-taking BMW Welt, marked the end of four transformative days that have already created a lasting impact."

Looking back at the last 200 projects I am incredibly grateful for every single client and for every single event I got to be part of! 

! From automotive events and launches to start up conferences. 
! From month long festivals to health tech awards 
! From banking and finance discussion to female empowerment organisations 

The diversity of topics, industries and places I got to work with and in has shaped me and immensely expanded my horizon. 

The fact that I got to host events in the Middle East, Asia, Africa, US and Europe is just unreal for the boy who grew up in a small town with 18000 people! 

Thank you to everyone who believed, supported and trusted me on the journey! 

Here’s to the next 200!
