What a treat to be part of the European Outdoor Week in Riva del Garda!

I had the pleasure of hosting two highlights there, the first ever European Outdoor Awards and the In4Out Innovation Day.

The Awards were a special moment. Celebrating the creativity, innovation and bold thinking happening across the outdoor industry right now from groundbreaking gear to materials pushing the boundaries of what's possible.
Huge congratulations to all the winners and everyone who submitted products. You're shaping the future of this industry. 

The Innovation Day was equally inspiring a room full of start-ups, investors and brands who all genuinely care about where outdoor is heading, exchanging ideas and challenging the status quo. None of this happens without the people behind the scenes.

The biggest thanks and congratulations go to Christian Schneidermeier and the team at European Outdoor Group (EOG) to Benedetto Sironi and MagNet srl SB for the vision and for working with me.

And congratulations to the main partner of the awards: MPM AG and Florian von Stuckrad and for building the Future of Sports Hub and making the Awards a reality. Finally thanks to Sophie Offord for being such a terrific co-host to work with!

This was only the first edition and it felt like something truly special. The energy, the conversations, the community is exactly what the outdoor industry needs.

Can't wait to see what 2027 brings.
