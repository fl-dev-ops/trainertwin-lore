​Ferrari just launched a car that does not look like a Ferrari and the next day the stock dropped by 7%. 

The internet exploded and everyone had an opinion about the new Ferrari Luce. 

“It looks like an Apple store on wheels.” 

“That's not a Ferrari.” 

“It looks like a Honda.”

What I find fascinating is that Ferrari must have expected this to happen. 

They spent years developing it in silence and brought Jony Ive and Marc Newson from LoveFrom, the designers behind some of the most iconic Apple products, on board. Instead of following the trend of giant touchscreens, they focused on physical buttons, and gave it a very different and distinct design and a beautiful Italian name: Luce, which means light. 

And here is the communication lesson. 

Ferrari’s management knew that a fully electric Ferrari will never satisfy the traditional Ferrari petrolhead fans. So instead of trying to please everyone, they went into a completely different direction. And that is what most brands and also speakers are afraid to do. 

Most people and companies try to communicate to everyone, and in the process, nobody really remembers them. Ferrari decided to be bold, and only time will tell whether the Luce becomes a legendary collector's piece or a very expensive experiment. 

But one thing is for certain, people are talking about it, and the good old saying is there is no such thing as bad press.
