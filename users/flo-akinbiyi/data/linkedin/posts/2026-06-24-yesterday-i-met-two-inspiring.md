Yesterday, I met two inspiring women. 

One is from Dubai Police, the other one is an Olympic athlete. 

As I hosted the official opening and media day of Dubai Sports World, which is now in its 16th edition, I had the opportunity to meet and speak with two women that genuinely moved me. 

The first one was Fatima Ahmed Buhajir from Dubai Police, from the Chairman of the Positive Spirit Department, and her mission blew me away. The vision is to contribute to the zero-crime city, but not with tougher laws, but by bringing the community closer together through sports. 

One of the initiatives is Train with the Police, where members of the public can train alongside police officers, whether it's volleyball, football, basketball, running, and other activities. Isn't that incredible? I hope that I will find the time to join one of these sessions and challenge a police officer with my fitness skills. Not sure how that will go. Sounds fun. 

The second woman that truly inspired me was Maryam Al-Farsi, the first female Emirati Olympic sprinter who represented the UAE at the Paris 2024 Olympics, and she already has her eyes set on the next games. During our conversation on stage, she said something that wowed me. But at just 18 years old, she told me that participating in the Olympics for her is also about inspiring the next generation, showing them what is possible. Isn't that incredible? Someone who is not even 20 years old is already thinking about those who come after her. 

We need more inspiring women, and we need to showcase more inspiring women, because you guys truly make the world a better place. 

Congratulations to Dubai Sports World on a successful launch event.
