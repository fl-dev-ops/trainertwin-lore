Throwback to an amazing workshop at the NIO Deutschland House Frankfurt. This season I had the honor to host a public speaking workshop at the NIO House in Frankfurt and I had a blast. 

Not only did we have a full house and I got to travel in a NIO car to the location from Munich, but also the participants were super engaged. 

The audience was eager to learn, to challenge themselves, and to get out of their comfort zone.

 Whether it was the questions they asked, the enthusiasm they had for their activities and exercises, or the terrific pitches they delivered in their open pitching sessions at the end. 

A really fantastic workshop that I’ll remember for a very long time.

 Thanks NIO Deutschland for hosting me in your premises. 

I can’t wait for the next one.
