𝗪𝗵𝗮𝘁 𝗮𝗻 𝘂𝗻𝗳𝗼𝗿𝗴𝗲𝘁𝘁𝗮𝗯𝗹𝗲 𝗲𝘅𝗽𝗲𝗿𝗶𝗲𝗻𝗰𝗲 𝗮𝘁 Supercar Owners Circle AG  𝗪𝗲𝗲𝗸𝗲𝗻𝗱 𝗜𝘁𝗮𝗹𝘆! 🇮🇹

This past weekend was nothing short of extraordinary—touring the picturesque streets of Puglia alongside some of the world’s most impressive #supercars and connecting with passionate, driven individuals from all walks of life. 

From the roaring engines to the breathtaking views and staying at the Hotel Borgo Egnazia where Jessica Biel and Justin Timberlake got married and the G7 summit was hosted was spectacular!

But beyond the speed and beauty of the cars, it was the conversations and camaraderie that truly stood out. 

#SOCWeekend wasn’t just about supercars—it was about bringing together #innovators, #leaders, and #storytellers to share their passions and inspire one another. 

Whether we were on the road or in discussion, it was clear that we all shared a deep commitment to pushing boundaries and embracing the extraordinary.

Meeting the CEO’s of Dallara Andrea Pontremoli and CEO of Alcantara S.p.A.  Eugenio Lolli and having conversations with them on stage was inspiring.

Automotive masterpieces like Ferrari, Koenigsegg Automotive AB, Automobili Lamborghini S.p.A., Porsche AG, and Pagani Automobili came together to showcase the pinnacle of luxury and performance.

Puglia, with its stunning backdrop, served as the perfect setting for this blend of excitement, luxury, and meaningful connections. Looking forward to the next adventure and continuing to build relationships with such inspiring individuals!
