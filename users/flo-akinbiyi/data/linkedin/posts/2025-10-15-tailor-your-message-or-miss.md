Tailor Your Message or Miss the Mark

Ever walked away from a presentation wondering why it didn’t land even though you said everything right?

It’s probably because you were speaking to everyone and connecting with no one.

The size of your audience isn't as important as people think - it’s who is your audience.

Tailoring your message isn’t optional. It’s a communication superpower. When you shift from simply “delivering” to truly connecting, everything changes - engagement, influence, and outcomes.

Here’s what to focus on:

- Know your audience. Their goals, mindset, and pain points.
- Speak their language. Cut the fluff and hit the mark.
- Reframe your message. Make it about them, not you.

Tailoring is about intention. It’s about showing respect by making your message relevant. When your audience feels seen and understood, they’ll remember what you said and they’ll act on it.

Communication is not one-size-fits-all. It’s a craft. And the better you get at tailoring, the further your message travels.
