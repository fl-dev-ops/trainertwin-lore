Happy New Year everyone! 
Welcome to the Year of J.O.Y. 
I’ve chosen to theme this year J.O.Y.
For me, 2025 was a year where I listened deeply. To the music of my inner world and the ones I created. To my themes, my emotions and my body. 

And honestly…
It was heavy!
Dark at times.
Intense.
Necessary. 

That’s why this year, I’m choosing differently. 
I am choosing light. 

I choose J.O.Y. 

Not as a cliché.
But as a daily practice. And there is more to it. 
J.O.Y. stands for: 

J - Joyful flow. To live each day with more lightness, presence, and trust.
 
O - One love. For my family, for the people I meet, for the world around me.
I’d rather spend my life loving than no loving. 

Y - Yes & No, intentionally. Because automatic yeses keep us stuck. And unconscious nos close doors that could change everything. 

This year is about awareness in every decision. 
This is my theme. 
My focus. 
My intention. 

You can love it or hate it, that’s okay. 

But I’m curious: 

Do you have a theme for this year? 

Wishing you all a powerful, conscious, and joy-filled start into 2026. 
One love!
