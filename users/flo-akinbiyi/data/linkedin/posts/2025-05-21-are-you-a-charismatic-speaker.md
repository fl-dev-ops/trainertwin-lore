Are you a charismatic speaker?

What’s the secret to being one?

Is it your fashion?

Your smile?

The gestures you use?

Sure, some of these elements definitely contribute to a charismatic presence.

But one of the biggest factors? It’s your voice.

Charismatic speakers like Oprah Winfrey, Barack Obama, or Jensen Huang all have one thing in common: a powerful voice.

Just listen to any of their speeches and you’ll immediately understand how they captivate the audience often just through how they sound, regardless of the content itself.

Thanks to my partnership with Professor Oliver Niebuhr and AllGoodSpeakers ApS, we’re able to offer every organization, and every speaker we work with, a scientific assessment of your voice.

This assessment tells us how charismatic your voice is and it also breaks down specific areas where you may need improvement:

- Passion
- Competence
- Confidence

Your voice is one of the most powerful tools in public speaking.

If you’re not working on it, you’re missing out on one of the biggest opportunities to truly connect with your audience.
