Don’t fake passion when speaking. Do this instead.

One of the simplest ways to become a more impactful speaker is to speak with energy.

I prefer speaking with energy over speaking with passion because, let’s be real, we’re not always passionate about every topic we need to present.

But energy? That’s your own. You don’t need to fake it. 

All you have to do is remind yourself of what you feel like when you’re in a great state of energy and bring that version of yourself on stage.

Speaking with energy amplifies everything about your presence, your body language, your voice and your entire stage presence. 

It’s a powerful way to be more effective, more credible, and more memorable.

Here’s what many people get wrong: they think energy is all about being loud.
 
Being loud is only one part of it. If you overdo it, you end up like Steve Ballmer - no offense to Steve Ballmer (a billionaire and an impressive leader, for sure!).
 
But is that how you want to be perceived when you speak?

Have you ever tried raising your energy, but not your volume? It’s a game-changing experiment. 

You’ll discover that you don’t need to shout to be more exciting and impactful.

Raise your energy before you even step on stage. Before you walk into a room to deliver a presentation. 

Before you even start shaking someone’s hand.

That’s how you become a better communicator, without faking passion.
