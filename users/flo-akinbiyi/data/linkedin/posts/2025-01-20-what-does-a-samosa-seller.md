What does a samosa seller in Dubai’s Textile Souk know about communication that most professionals miss?

Meet Abubakar. He works at a tiny Indian street food stall, tucked away in the bustling Dubai Textile Souk. His stand sells samosas, pani puri, and other incredible snacks - just like the four other stalls right next to him.
Yet, I chose his stand. Every time.

Why? Because of his exceptional communication skills.

Abubakar doesn’t just sell food. He creates an irresistible moment.
Instead of simply asking, “Would you like to try?” he turns his pitch into an experience:

“Try, try, try, try, try, try, try, try, try! Come on, come on, come on, come, come, come, come! Sit down, sit down, sit down, sit down, sit down!”

He repeats every word with #energy, #rhythm, and a melodic tone. Not loud, but captivating. His words pull you in.

And then comes the magic: he holds one of his delicious snacks right under your nose. At that point, resistance is futile. You taste it, love it, and the deal is done.

It’s a masterclass in persuasion.

This isn’t just about selling snacks; it’s a reminder of what makes great communication whether you’re in a meeting room, a boardroom, or on a big stage.

It’s about: 
✨ Going the extra mile. 
✨ Using your voice, energy, and body language. 
✨ Creating a connection that’s impossible to ignore.

Abubakar stands out in a crowded market, and we can too if we’re willing to communicate with intention and enthusiasm.

So next time you’re in Dubai, visit the Textile Souk. Watch him in action, grab a snack, and let his approach inspire your next professional speaking opportunity.
