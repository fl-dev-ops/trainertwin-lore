How to use AI without losing your voice, credibility, or human impact?

Join our workshop: 
COMMUNICATION IN 2026: HUMAN x AI with Flo Akinbiyi 
🗓️ January 16 
📍 Zabeel House, Dubai 

AI can generate words, structure messages, and accelerate output.
But judgment, intention, tone, credibility, and connection remain true human skills.
 
This workshop explores how to work with AI without outsourcing your thinking, voice, or leadership presence to it.
 
Most professionals today face the same tension:
more tools, more speed, but less clarity, less authenticity, and messages that sound polished yet somehow generic.
 
Using our Human-First Communication Philosophy, we will explore where AI genuinely supports communication, where it quietly undermines it, and how leaders can stay firmly in control.
 
You’ll work on real communication challenges you face daily, learning how to use AI to communicate faster and more clearly while strengthening your authority, credibility, and human impact.
 
This is not about choosing between human or AI.
 
In a world where everyone has access to the same technology, the real advantage lies in communicating with clarity, confidence, and the human edge, while using AI as a powerful ally.
