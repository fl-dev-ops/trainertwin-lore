“Why are you so aggressive?" 

I have heard that comment a few times when people who have never seen this side of me watch me during my Muay Thai training.

Here's the truth: I am NOT aggressive. I don't like to fight, except in sports, and I have never been in a street fight in my entire life.

Martial arts has been one of my main sports to keep me in shape and to keep me centered. I enjoy the training so much, whether it is pad work with a trainer that keeps you focused and if you lose your focus or if you don't put enough energy, you get slapped by them.

Whether it is conditioning, where you just go to your limits and you push through, or if it’s sparring, which I do sometimes because I'm always worried about my face for work and my smile. Sparring is where you have to overcome your fears, challenge yourself and deal with the fact that you're not always the best or the strongest person. And of course, it's very powerful for defending yourself.

I even take my five-year-old son to go kickboxing with me, not so much because he enjoys it but because I want him to learn early the discipline, the determination, and the dedication that martial arts require.

What is your source of discipline and personal growth?
