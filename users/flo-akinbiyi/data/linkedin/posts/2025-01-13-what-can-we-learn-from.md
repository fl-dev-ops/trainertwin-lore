What can we learn from someone who gets 1 billion views per month on Instagram? A lot.

Adley Kinsman, who I had the pleasure of meeting at the #Mastermind by #Cope, delivered a keynote and hosted a roundtable session that left everyone inspired. Adley has spent years mastering the art of creating content that audiences want to see, engage with, and share.

Beyond being an Instagram star, Adley is the founder of Viralish, a company specializing in helping creators and brands build strategies for viral content and grow their online presence. Viralish is a leader in guiding influencers and businesses to connect meaningfully with their audiences while achieving massive reach and engagement.

Her keynote at the Mastermind was both entertaining and insightful, packed with valuable tips and techniques. As a communication coach, here are my three takeaways:

	1.	Authenticity 
Adley represented her background beautifully, wearing a cowboy hat that showcased her unique style and heritage.

	2.	Engagement and Energy
She delivered her speech with passion and energy, constantly interacting with the audience and keeping us engaged throughout.

	3.	Content and Visuals
Her presentation was incredibly well-structured, supported by powerful visuals, and designed to keep the audience focused and inspired.

Hats off to Adley Kinsman, your journey and expertise are truly inspiring. I look forward to crossing paths with you again, on or off the stage, somewhere around the world.
