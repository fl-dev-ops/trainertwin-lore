The Secret to Being the Last Person on a Plane...
and still being liked by everyone! 

What’s the trick?

Communication! Done right.

Let me share a story.

I travel a lot for work,  and yes, I’m too often that guy who arrives at the plane a little late. This time, instead of stressing, I embraced it.

As I walked down the gangway, I looked at the staff member at the door and said:

“Oh my God... am I the last one?”
He laughed: “Yes, you are." 

I stepped inside, looked at the passengers near the entrance and said:

"I'm so sorry. I hope you didn’t have to wait for me. I feel like an idiot being the last person.”
They laughed too. 

I put my luggage away, got ready to sit down... and then the captain walked in.

So I turned around and said:

“No, actually... I’m not the last. He is.”
The whole front of the plane cracked up. 

From that moment, the cabin crew were my best friends.
People around me were smiling, chatting, even reminding me not to forget my suit as I was getting off. 

All because I addressed the situation, owned it, and used a bit of humour to make the moment lighter for everyone. 

The secret to being likeable isn’t perfection.
It’s how you communicate when things get messy. 

Have you ever had a moment like this while travelling?
