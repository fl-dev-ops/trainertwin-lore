Yesterday, I was reminded of a powerful lesson when I coached a group of graduates at alrajhi bank in Riyadh, Saudi Arabia.

I loved to see their enthusiasm, their participation, and also their potential for growth.

Today, I want to do something that I usually wouldn't. I want to celebrate one particular lady from the group. Asma S. Alharbi, ECBA™ speaking skills stood out to me as she had above-average presence, strong voice modulation, paired with exciting storytelling skills.

And she was also one of the most engaged learners.

The reason why I'm shining a light on her is not only because she's a good speaker, but because she consciously made an effort to invest in public speaking skills and has practiced them over the last couple of weeks regularly with speaking clubs.

It is so inspiring to see when somebody puts in the effort, you get results.
Of course, it is important to remind everyone that not everybody's journey is the same. Every journey is different. Some take longer, some take shorter, but at the end, it doesn't matter.

If you want to be a powerful communicator and make an impact through communication, you have to put in the effort, no matter how long it takes.
There are many routes that will lead you to the goal. What is important is that you take one.

Well done, graduates. It was a true pleasure coaching and working with you!
