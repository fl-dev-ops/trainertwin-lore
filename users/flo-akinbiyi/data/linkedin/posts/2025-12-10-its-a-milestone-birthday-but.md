It’s a milestone birthday but there is no big party this year. 

But I am celebrating my birthday here on LinkedIn, not so much to rub it in your face, but to remind you to send me some big presents to my house… 

As I am reflecting on the past year, I want to say thank you! 

Thank you to everyone in this community who has reached out, connected with us, worked with us, supported us, challenged us, and in whatever way supported us, enabling and delivering the work that we do. 

The last year of my life has definitely been one that I do not wish to repeat because of all the curveballs the universe has thrown at me. But from a really challenging year, I have turned it into a year of growth. And we are growing. I am growing. 

If there are two quotes that truly resonated with me this year, they are these: “The universe doesn’t give you any challenges you can’t handle.” 
“Never waste a good crisis.” 

With that in mind, I’m heading out for a paddle, celebrating with Mother Nature, some balloons and the wifey on my side.
