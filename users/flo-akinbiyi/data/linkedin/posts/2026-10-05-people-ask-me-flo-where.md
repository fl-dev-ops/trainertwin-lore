People ask me, "Flo, where do you get your energy from?"

Wrong word.

I don't get. I don't have. I give.

I give energy.

Many professionals get this wrong.

They think speaking is all about them, about their content, their ideas, their messages, and they forget the most important part of the equation: the audience.

As content needs to be tailored to the audience, so should you adjust your energy, so the audience can feel your message.

And no, this is not always easy, especially not if you have other things going on in your life. 

But when you remind yourself that you're not here for yourself, but for the audience, then it's significantly easier to give energy.

Have you ever tried this shift from having energy to giving energy?
