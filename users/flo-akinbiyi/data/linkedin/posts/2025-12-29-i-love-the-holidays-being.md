I love the holidays.

Being back home in Europe.
Time in the mountains.
Snow, family, and space to slow down.

This week we went night skiing together.

He skis.
I snowboard.
We’re figuring it out together.
It’s his first season.
And honestly — the darkness scared him at the beginning.

You could feel it in the first run.
He hesitated. Was going super slow. Stayed silence.

Then something shifted.

Run after run, his confidence grew.
More speed. More laughter.
Less fear.

At some point, he was leading the way.

Earlier that day he asked me:
“What is 10 × 100 × 1,000?”

(It’s a million. I had to check.)

Especially this year, I feel deeply grateful —
for family, for presence, and for the ability to step away from work for a few days.

Wishing you calm, joy, and perspective wherever you are this holiday season.
