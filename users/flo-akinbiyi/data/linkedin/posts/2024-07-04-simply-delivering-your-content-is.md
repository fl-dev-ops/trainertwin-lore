Simply delivering your content is not working anymore.

In this era, we are overloaded with content; we become bored if it’s not engaging. We’re constantly distracted by our phones, emails, messages, and social media. We all know what great content, great delivery, and a great presentation look like. To captivate your audience, you must master the art of interaction. Don’t view your presentation as a monologue. It should be a dialogue, a two-way communication, hence the need for interaction.

Interaction can take many forms. Here are a few techniques you can use:

- Ask Insightful Questions: Questions provoke thought, invite the audience to reflect on their own experiences, and engage them.
- Use Polling: This technique is powerful because it transforms passive listening into active participation. When you ask the audience to raise their hand or stand up, the presentation becomes a physical activity, which greatly enhances engagement.
- Leverage Digital Tools Like Slido: These tools are invaluable for real-time interaction. By integrating quizzes, polls, and Q&A sessions, Slido transforms the audience from passive listeners to active participants. This interaction not only makes the session more dynamic but also significantly increases retention and involvement.

And don’t forget, key approaches like:

- 📖 Storytelling
- 😄 Humor
- 📊 Using powerful dynamic delivery
- 🖼️ Visuals and videos

These strategies engage the audience and transform a standard talk or presentation into a memorable experience.
