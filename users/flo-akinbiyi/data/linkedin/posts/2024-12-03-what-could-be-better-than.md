What could be better than seeing the King of Spain, His Excellency António Guterres, and the Prime Minister of Portugal, among many other high-level dignitaries, speak at the 10th United Nations Alliance of Civilizations Global Forum in Cascais, Portugal?

Well, I can tell you—it was hosting the Intercultural Innovation Hub, which took place during the United Nations Alliance of Civilizations (UNAOC) Global Forum.

For me, this was an absolute highlight. We had the privilege to recognize 10 incredible projects from around the world that foster intercultural dialogue, promote understanding, and bridge divides. These projects are real examples of how innovation can break barriers and bring people closer together.

Standing on that stage, surrounded by changemakers and visionaries, reminded me of the profound impact that collaboration and shared purpose can have on building a better future. Each idea, each effort, is a testament to what we can achieve when we work together across borders and cultures.

I am deeply grateful to the UNAOC and BMW Group for creating this platform to celebrate these incredible initiatives—and for giving me the opportunity to contribute to this mission. Here’s to continuing the dialogue and making meaningful connections that spark change!
