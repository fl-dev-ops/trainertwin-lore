Too many people think speaking is all about content, slides, and delivery.

Yes, all of these elements are important in the boardroom, on stage, or in a meeting. But regardless of who is in front of you, you’re not just talking:

❌ B2B (Business-to-Business)
❌ B2C (Business-to-Consumer)
❌ P2P (Peer-to-Peer)
✅ H2H (Human-to-Human)

I have observed and experienced that connection can, in many cases, be just as important, or even more important, than content, slides, and delivery.

When you have a strong connection with people:

❗ They will pay more attention to what you have to say.
❗ They will be more forgiving when you make a mistake.
❗ They will engage with you more.
❗ Most of all, they will enjoy the communication experience.

Open yourself up. It’s not a sign of weakness. The truth is, connection is a strength that takes your communication to the next level.
