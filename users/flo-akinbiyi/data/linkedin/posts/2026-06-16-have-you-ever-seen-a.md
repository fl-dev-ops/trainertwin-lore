Have you ever seen a doctor shed a tear on stage? 

Last week, just 20 kilometers away from my hometown in Bavaria, Bad Tölz, I had the privilege of coaching aesthetic medical doctors from around the world as a part of the Nordberg Medical Train the Trainer program. 

And yes, one doctor delivered an outstanding TED Talk that was so personal, so open, and so authentic that it moved everyone. 

What excited me most throughout the week was not only the quality of the presentations, it was the willingness to learn. Of course, the Nordberg Medical team around Daniela created one of the most impressive learning experiences I have been part of in a long time. From creative exercises, team-building activities, cultural celebrations, fantastic food, and a truly stunning location, they created the perfect space for growth and development. 

The effort the doctors invested and their willingness to step outside of the comfort zones, experiment with new ways of communicating and embracing feedback from their peers was so incredibly delightful to witness. Every single participant is a highly accomplished professional and their willingness to learn and do things for the first time reminded me that true expertise is often paired with humility. 

One of the biggest highlights for me was, of course, to include my Bavarian culture, put on my lederhosen, and bring the good old Goaßlschnalzen and show the doctors some Bavarian culture. Actually, very few are celebrating the Goaßlschnalzen.  

So to the doctors that truly take the Hippocratic oath seriously, I want to say congratulations to the Nordberg Medical team that brought me in. I want to say I’m grateful that I was part of the job. And to the other facilitators and keynote speakers, I want to say I'm honored to be part of a faculty of such esteemed professionals. 

And if I want to leave you, all of you, with one thing, shouldn't every professional take an oath of lifelong learning?
