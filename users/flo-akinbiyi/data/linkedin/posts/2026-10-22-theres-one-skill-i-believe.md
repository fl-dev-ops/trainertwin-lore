There's one skill I believe we should all invest in, and no, it's not AI. 

Don't get me wrong, AI literacy is incredibly important as we are progressing towards an increasingly AI-influenced and technology-driven future. 

But there is something I have been reflecting a lot about. AI and technology is taking over more and more tasks that we used to do. 

And with that, we have to create a balance and also focus more on the skills and things that make us human. And for that, we need to invest in our human skills. 

And one of the most important and underrated skills is communication. 

Think about it. 

Almost everything we want to achieve in life involves other people, and how we interact with them is always communication. As a presenter, keynote speaker, and communication coach, I've had the privilege to work and to meet with some of the world's most outstanding leaders. 

And one thing I observed over and over again is that many of them are exceptional communicators. And that is no coincidence. 

They all have the ability to explain their ideas clearly, inspire and motivate and engage people. 

They're charismatic; they can build trust and make others feel something. And that is, without a doubt, an important contributor to their success. 

And that's what I love about my work. 

Because when I help a person, a team, or an organization to communicate better, I'm not just helping them to deliver a better presentation or speak more confidently on stage. I'm helping them to become more effective in how they connect with other human beings.

No wonder that, for me, it is all about human-first communication, because we're making a difference far beyond the stage, whether it is improving relationships, making organizations stronger, or having even an impact on entire communities. 

And that's why I believe communication deserves a much bigger place in the conversation about the skills of the future. 

So my question to all of you is: Are you consciously investing in becoming a better communicator?
