Never in my life would I have imagined living in a country where I hear missiles being intercepted in the sky.  

Experiencing the intense sound and feeling the adrenaline rushing through your body. Being 24/7 on countless news and social media channels to get a real sense of what’s going on.

And what matters most is to keep my family safe.
 
And thanks to this country and its leadership, I feel as calm as can be. 
And I feel we are truly being kept safe.
 
The UAE has always called itself a safe haven.  And it continues to prove it.
 
We saw it and felt it during COVID.  
And we see it again now during the Iran–Israel–US conflict.
 
A crisis reveals your personal truth, and the same is true for countries. It shows whether safety is a slogan or a lived reality.
 
Yesterday, seeing His Highness Sheikh Mohammed bin Zayed Al Nahyan, the President of the UAE and Ruler of Abu Dhabi, and His Highness Sheikh Hamdan bin Mohammed bin Rashid Al Maktoum, Crown Prince of Dubai, present among people at Dubai Mall, interacting openly with citizens, residents, and visitors, was powerful and emotional.
 
In this crisis, the leadership is not distant. They are present, they are with us. 
 
And that human connection is incredible.
 
This country has given me love, friendship, opportunity, stability, family, and the space to build something meaningful.
 
I am deeply grateful for the UAE, and as gratitude should move in both directions.
 
I would also like to offer support! If anyone in my network needs perspective, time, advice, support, or simply a calm conversation, you know my skill set, you know what I stand for. 

I am here, and I am ready to contribute in any way I can.
 
May everyone in the UAE, and beyond, remain safe.  May this crisis find a swift end.
 
Thank you to the UAE and thank you to the leadership.
 
Shukran شكراً

Thank you.
