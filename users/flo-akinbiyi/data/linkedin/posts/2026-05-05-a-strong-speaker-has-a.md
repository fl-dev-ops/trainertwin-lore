A strong speaker has a 5x higher chance to win the business. 
That's a pretty big number, and I have the data to prove it.

In 2024, I hosted the Expand North Star Supernova Startup competition, and 150 startups pitched.

Out of these 150, only 10 were able to make it to the final. 
That's a 6.7% chance.

As a communication coach, I analyzed every single speaker based on their performance, not on their content. 

20 out of 150 were strong speakers, which is only 13%.
And here's what happened.

7 out of these 20 strong speakers made it into the final.

And that means that a strong speaker had a 35% chance to reach the final, while everyone else who did not have the same level of skills only had a 6.7% chance.

That shows that a powerful delivery is not just an advantage. It's a 5x higher probability of success.

But here's the twist, and I've seen that over and over again in startup competitions.

The winner wasn't a strong speaker, but the winner had a spectacular product.
Nevertheless, places two to five were all strong speakers without exception.

And it shows, yes, your content, your product, your service matters.
But delivery decides who gets remembered, trusted, backed, and who has an advantage.

So the question is, are you just presenting your idea, or are you actually multiplying your chances?
