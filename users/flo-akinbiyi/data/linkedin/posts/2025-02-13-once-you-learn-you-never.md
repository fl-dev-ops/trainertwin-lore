Once You Learn, You Never Forget.

It’s been a few years since I’ve been on the snow, but stepping back onto my snowboard felt like coming home.

That’s the beauty of a skill you’ve invested time, effort, and money into - it never truly leaves you.

From the moment I strapped in, I wanted to go faster rather than slower (sorry, I mean down the slopes faster rather than slower!). And with every hour, with every run, the confidence grows. The technique sharpens. The enjoyment deepens.

And if you happened to be one of the few who saw me crash into a skier… For the record, I caught him. I caught him. Nobody got hurt. (And yes, for the first time ever, I was wearing a helmet being a responsible dad and all.)

The weather wasn’t always perfect, but let’s be honest, skiing is one of the greatest luxuries to experience with family. Watching my little boy learn how to ski? Absolutely priceless.

Grateful for this time to recharge before diving into upcoming events, trainings, and trips.
