Thousands of people posted the same ChatGPT trend on Instagram this week.

Did you ever ask yourself why trends work so well? 

I use the same technique when I'm on the stage or in a room. 

The explanation is simple. 

It is community. 

We all strive for belonging, being part of a group. A trend allows you to be part of one even if it's just for a few fleeing moments on an Instagram story. 

This weekend, I'm in Greece at Costa Navarreno for the supercar ownership weekend. And I am with a group of people where a large number doesn't know each other. Yet by Sunday, there will be a community that doesn't happen by accident. 

As communication coach, I always love to think about ways I can create experiences that turn a group of strangers into a community. Experiences that go beyond simple words and let us all become one, a question that everyone answers, an interaction, a story that gives us all the same feeling. 

Today, for example, we did the traditional Greek plate smashing, and what I did is I grabbed some plates, and I grabbed the people that did not want to get up and participate and encouraged them to participate and smash a few plates. It's all about creating moments, memories, and emotions that last. 

Communication has a power to turn us into a community. And most people that you communicate with will forget the words that you say, but they will remember the feelings, the emotions you created.
