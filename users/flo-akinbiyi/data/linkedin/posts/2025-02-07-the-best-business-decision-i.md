The Best Business Decision I Ever Made

Some moments in business change everything. This was one of them.
When I first started working with Candice-Lynne Barker (she/her), she was a part-time VA from a UK agency, balancing multiple clients and tasks - bookkeeping, invoicing, project prep, marketing, you name it. From day one, she wasn’t just another assistant; she was a force.

Then came the moment I almost lost her.

Candice decided to leave the agency. She loved parts of her work but not everything that came with it. The agency informed me that she’d have to step away.

It was one of the worst, yet best, days of our professional journey.
Because I wasn’t ready to let her go.

Right then and there, I pitched. To Candice. To the agency. I said, “I’ll take her full-time. We’ll make it work.”

And we did.

Fast forward, she went from VA to Ops Manager to Business Manager - because what she does goes far beyond operations.

Candice, your positive spirit, flexibility, loyalty, and dedication have shaped this business in ways I can’t even put into words.

So here’s to the next… shall we say, 20 years?

Thank you, Candice. Best decision ever.
