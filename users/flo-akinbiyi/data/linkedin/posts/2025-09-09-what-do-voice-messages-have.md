What do voice messages have to do with boring presentations? 

We all know how exhausting it is to listen to monotone speaker - you very quickly lose interest. 

It’s difficult to focus and eventually you just zone out and start thinking about work, life, investments, or whatever floats your boat. 

Speaking with a monotone voice is what unfortunately happens a lot in the corporate world, and it's not something that gives you a great professional presence. It’s not what helps the audience to focus on the content and it’s not what works for you as a professional. 

What do you need to do instead is to speak with a charismatic and positive voice - that is something that you can practice!

And the best way to practice is to use voice messages. 

Learn a few simple techniques like speaking faster and slower, speaking louder and softer, using melody, emphasis, and pauses. Simple techniques that you can practice while sending voice messages. You have to communicate anyway, so why not send voice messages instead and then listen to them, improve them delete them, improve them and become a more effective communicator!
