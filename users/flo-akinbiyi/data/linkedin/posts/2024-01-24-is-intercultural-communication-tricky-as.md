Is intercultural communication tricky? 🌍

As a communication coach, I've seen many professionals struggle with uncertainty and insecurity when communicating across cultures. I'll let you in on a little secret: in most cases, I do not worry too much about speaking to other cultures.

Why?

I'll tell you in a bit. 🤫💬

In the photo, you can see my intercultural background. My dad is from Nigeria, my mom is from Germany, and I was born and raised in Germany. Growing up, my brother Leonhard  " Leo " Akinbiyi and I were the kids who stood out and had to communicate across cultures. 

This has now become my superpower. 💪

Looking different but speaking in a native accent taught me the power of curiosity, empathy and delight in understanding other cultures. 🌐

My guiding principle for intercultural communication: 

It’s about connecting human to human. 

Keeping this in mind ensures respectful and effective communication. ❤️
