I built my career as a presenter on what might seem like a big fat lie. 🤥

Early in my journey, I used a promotional image of myself standing at a lectern for an event I organized but didn’t speak at.

Was it deceitful? Not quite. By that point, I had already honed my public speaking skills through years of practice and event hosting. The image was a strategic move to showcase potential—not a fabrication.

In entrepreneurship and personal branding, creating opportunities to highlight your potential is crucial. But where do we draw the line? Here are some thoughts:

Honesty vs. Opportunity: Balancing the need to showcase your potential by staying truthful can be tricky. It’s important that your promotional content accurately reflects your skills without being misleading.

Delivering on Promises: It’s okay to present your abilities in the best possible light—as long as you can follow through on what you promise.

Ethical Considerations: Upholding ethical standards in self-promotion is key. Creativity should never cross into deception.

Looking back, my advice to fellow entrepreneurs is simple: leverage your potential but stay grounded in truth. This approach has allowed me to build genuine connections and maintain integrity throughout my career.

What do you think? Is a little ‘creative framing’ acceptable in marketing yourself, or should absolute transparency always be the goal? I’d love to hear your thoughts.
