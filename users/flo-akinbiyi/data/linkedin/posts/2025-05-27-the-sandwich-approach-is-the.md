The Sandwich Approach is the best structure for any speaking scenario.

Whether you’re delivering a presentation, a speech, or pitching an idea, this is how you win any audience.

Want to impress your boss? Win over a new client? Leave a lasting impression?

The Sandwich is always here to help.

I’m sure you’ve heard about the Opening - Body - Closing Framework or the Three-Act Story Structure.

Aristotle already used it thousands of years ago, and it’s still one of the most powerful ways to connect with your audience in today’s AI-driven world.

Here’s how it breaks down:

The top layer of bread - that’s your opening.

Like a crispy, crunchy slice, your opening must be fresh and engaging.

Don’t start with “Hi, my name is…”

That’s average.

That’s boring.

Instead, create a powerful opening that makes people sit up and pay attention. 

Whether you engage with the audience, tell a story, use humor, or share an unexpected fact.

Grab their attention from the very first bite.

Next is the middle layer, the meat, the salad, the sauce, the good stuff.

But remember:
Too many layers don’t make your sandwich - or your message - better.

When there’s too much to chew on, the audience can’t digest it all, and your main message gets lost.

So curate your content.

Focus on the three to five strongest points that truly add value for the audience.

Cut the clutter, keep the flavor.

Finally, the bottom layer of bread - your closing.

This holds everything together.

Yet in too many presentations, I still hear the dreaded:

“That’s it.”

Or worse:

“Any questions?”

These are not closings - they’re missed opportunities.

A strong closing gives you the audience’s full attention.

Use it well.

End with a memorable statement, a powerful takeaway, a call to action, something that leaves a lasting taste.

I use this structure on stage, in workshops, and even when I’m talking with my wife.

Because when you get it right, you can turn any conversation into a moment that sticks.
