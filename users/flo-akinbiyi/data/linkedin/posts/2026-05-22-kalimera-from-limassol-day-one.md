Kalimera from Limassol!
Day one of the Doers Summit 2026 is in the books.

Day two is happening right now.
I am thrilled to be experiencing the creative and vibrant energy of the Doers Summit and while hosting the Arcade Main Stage I am taking a lot of inspiring and insightful content in.

Yesterday we heard from brands like Revolut, Amazon, Seedcamp, Skroutch.
And there were so many conversations from investors, inspiring startup founders, government entities, innovators, thought leaders that truly left an impression on me.

Three things that I’m taking away from yesterday.

The keynote of Vladimir Sucha: Better output, weaker people.
We all need to be more aware that AI makes us less intelligent and less skilled!
This means focus on building new human skills and avoid unlearning all of our human skills.
(Yes, you should be able to drive a car without navigation! :))

Capital is still flowing, but you have to be even more strategic, effective, and confident in your approach. You have to raise on the right terms, from the right investors.

The theme of the Doers Summit Limassol 2026: Move fast, do things.
AI is enabling us to do things. So, let’s go out there and make it happen.

Thank you so much, Stylianos Lambrou, Dusan Duffek, and Andria Lambrou for having me.
