Resilience was the key message I personally took away from Hub71's Impact 26 first day.

It is a true honor to be hosting this event and to play a part in the story of Abu Dhabi's technology ecosystem. Having worked with Hub71 over the years, it's incredible to witness the growth of the ecosystem, the rising numbers of startups and founders, the outstanding partners and the event itself.

Besides moderating the stage, I had the opportunity to connect and speak with many participants from around the world. And it was incredible how much resilience, positivity and excitement everyone in the audience showed. What I am delighted to experience as a communication coach is the authenticity of the founders on stage. To publicly share their challenges, failures as well as how they shared their personal success formula is incredible to witness.

And yes, I can share a few secrets:

Consistency, consitency and consitency

Pushing yourself out of the comfort zone

Having a great idea doesn’t mean anything, acting on it is everything

And even with the best tech, a winning start-up needs a winning team I have to say, congrats to the entire team of Hub71.

And that was only day one. I cannot wait for Day 2 today.

If you're around, don't forget to say hi. I'm probably somewhere close to the stage.
