You never get a second chance to make a first impression.

That line stuck with me the first time I heard it.

Because it’s true - by the time you say your first word, someone has already formed an impression.

Research from Princeton shows it takes just a tenth of a second for people to judge your trustworthiness, competence, and likability.

Other studies say seven seconds.

Either way - it’s fast.

And in that blink, you’re already communicating.

Here’s what you can start paying attention to:

⚠️ Your posture and energy when you walk into a room

⚠️ The expression on your face

⚠️ The way you say your name

⚠️ How you introduce yourself

These micro-moments matter.

They’re not just about being polished - they’re about being intentional.

Next time you meet someone - and chances are, it’s today - ask yourself:

Am I shaping the impression I want to leave?

Or am I leaving it up to chance?
