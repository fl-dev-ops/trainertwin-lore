This workshop was better than the best workshop in the world according to Bain & Co. from the participant feedback and the Net Promoter Score Training. 

If I’m really honest, participant feedback means more to me than anything else. The transformation that we were able to see in Algeria during the KSB regional two day finance workshop was incredible.

I took each and every participant out of their comfort zone and allowed them to look at their skills, their superpowers, their professional factors from a different angle and the results were spectacular.

One of the main reasons why this workshop was also so successful was, of course, the program that Victoria and her team had put together an incredible mix of intense workshops and learning. 

I also took the opportunity to set up the dinner with a talent show where everyone had to showcase, perform or tell us about a superpower, and the outcome was nothing else than spectacular - whether we saw the participants sing, dance, tell stories, jokes, teach skills, or even make us cry with these learnings, the transformation that we were able to witness the next day was incredible. 

And thanks to the Algerian KSB sightseeing team as well. 

Truly grateful that I was chosen by Damian Smith ACMA PCC ORSC at Thryvv Leadership & Culture to collaborate on this project and deliver one of my finest workshops.
