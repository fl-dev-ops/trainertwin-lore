Do You Weaken Your Words Without Realizing It?

Have you ever started a sentence with “I think…” or “Maybe we should…”? 
These small phrases may seem harmless, but they can make you sound uncertain - even when you know exactly what you're talking about.

In high-stakes situations like job interviews, leadership roles, or tough conversations, confidence is key. The words you choose can make all the difference in how you're perceived.

What if you could swap out weak language for words that instantly make you sound more confident, in control, and decisive?

That’s exactly what these slides are all about. Scroll through and see how just a few small changes can help you sound more self-assured in any situation!
