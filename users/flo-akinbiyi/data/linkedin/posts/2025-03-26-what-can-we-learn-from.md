What Can We Learn from a Hairdresser About Communication?

The truth is - a lot.

Nestor and I have known each other for more than 13 years, and he is the only person I truly trust in Dubai with my Afro. Over the years, our relationship has evolved massively, from a professional hairdressing connection to a genuine friendship.

Nestor is, without a doubt, someone who can teach you a thing or two about communication. Let me share some of his secrets on how he builds relationships that last for decades with his clients. And I’m not the only one - Nestor’s clients come from a wide range of industries, including finance, fashion, oil and gas, food and beverage, and many more.

So how is Nestor able to have engaging conversations with every single one of his clients?

1. He Stays Informed
Nestor reads a lot- a lot of news - so he can always have conversations about politics, the economy, and beyond. He understands that to connect with people from different industries, he needs to be well-versed in topics that matter to them.

2. He Shares His Own Stories
One of Nestor’s subconscious strategies is that he’s never shy about sharing his personal life. He talks about his family, his professional journey, his happy moments, and even his challenges. This openness builds trust and strengthens deep, personal relationships with his clients.

3. He Adapts to the Situation
Nestor knows how to read the room. If you’re busy, he won’t force a conversation on you. If you’re in the mood to chat, he’ll joke around and engage in lively discussion. And if you just need someone to listen, Nestor will do that too.

Great communication skills, right? And all from a hairdresser.
