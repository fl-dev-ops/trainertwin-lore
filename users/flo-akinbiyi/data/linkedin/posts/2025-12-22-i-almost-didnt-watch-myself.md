I almost didn’t watch myself on TV. 
And I’m so grateful that I did. 

Being interviewed on Dubai One’s DXB Today is the final professional highlight of my year. 

Why is it so important to me? 

Quite honestly, the boy from Bad Tölz would have never dreamed of being on TV for my expertise. 
But the real highlight wasn’t the fact that I was on TV. 
What made it special was watching it. 

As a communication coach, I’m incredibly self-aware when I’m in a setting where I’m sharing my expertise. By my own standards, I want to be brilliant.
Impossible the first time!

After the recording, I felt I didn’t deliver. 

The experience itself was amazing. Dubai One’s entire TV crew, from the producer to the director to the amazing co-hosts, were outstanding. 

And yet, the doubt crept in.
Was I good enough?

I debated for weeks whether I even wanted to watch the episode. 
Then yesterday, on Friday night, I spontaneously decided to sit down and watch it live.     
The coach became the coachee and I decided to celebrate the outcome whatever it would be.

And to my surprise right from the start: I loved myself on screen.
I see areas where I can improve, where I can be even better.

But most importantly I celebrated myself.

A wonderful feeling of a decade of work coming together in one appearance!

I’m filled with gratitude.

And I want to share this moment with my coach who coached, inspired and motivated me to become who I am today.

Thank you Kate Walsh this one is for you! ❤️
