A few weeks ago, my son - little T - found some face paint and decided it would be the best idea ever to paint my face.

It took him a solid 25 minutes (with a dinner break in between), but I let him go for it - full black and red. A true masterpiece.

It reminded me of something important - especially as a presenter:

You can’t always take yourself too seriously on stage.

Even if it feels a bit uncomfortable. Even if it’s not the perfect moment. 

Allowing yourself to be authentic, to be playful, to just be you - that’s where the magic happens.

It takes effort. It takes courage. But just beyond the comfort zone is where the transformation really begins.

So, for the parents out there: Go buy your kids some face paint. Let them paint your full face. And if you dare - post it here on LinkedIn… or at least in the comments below.

Let’s have some fun and step outside our comfort zones.
