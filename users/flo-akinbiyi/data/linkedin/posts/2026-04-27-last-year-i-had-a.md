Last year I had a Business Trip to Algeria and it was truly a humbling experience.
Shortly after sunrise I sit by myself in an amazing little Roman Amphitheater right by the sea, almost 2000 years old and I reflect on the history that brought the Romans to Africa.

I wonder who sat on the seats, and who performed on the stage and what kind of speeches were delivered here.

It’s humbling to sit in a place like this and to realize how short a human’s life really is and how limited our time is to contribute something to this world.

I realized that most likely I am not going to change the world, but human-first communication is my piece. 
My way to make this a more positive place.

I think about this a lot in my work.
Every time I coach someone. Every time I stand on a stage. Every time I sit across from a leader who is ready to have a bigger impact through more human communication, I remind myself: 
Every speech is an opportunity to inspire. 

Make it count!
