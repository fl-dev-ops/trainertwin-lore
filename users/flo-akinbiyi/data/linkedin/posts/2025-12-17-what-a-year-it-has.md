What a year it has been!

As we’re wrapping up the year and delivering the last pieces of work, I’m sitting on the couch reflecting.

Ups and downs… and ups again.

I just want to express genuine gratitude for everything my team and I were able to accomplish.

The first big milestone we need to celebrate:
10 years of marriage
Wow! 10 years. One wife. One son. One dog.
Best achievement ever!

And when it comes to work. I’m blown away by the projects, people, and places I got to experience.

From communication coaching across: Iceland, Algeria, Germany, Saudi Arabia, Dubai and Abu Dhabi.

To hosting events like:
- Bits & Pretzels
- DOERS Summit
- The 10th Anniversary of the Health Awards
- The One Young World Closing Ceremony
- Gulfood
- Expand North Star
- GITEX (Singapore, Germany & Dubai)
- The Supercar Owners Circle & Weekend (Costa Smeralda)

And the list goes on.

Right in the middle of it all, I even got to celebrate a personal milestone:
My 200th hosted event project.

And on top of that, I had the honour to meet some of the most inspiring people on the planet including:
- Pep Guardiola
- Alain Ducasse
- Terry Crews
- Henri Maske
- Ilka Horstmeier
- Katerina Reiche
- Doro Bär
- Anna Wintour
- Kate Robertson & David Jones (Co-founders of One Young World)

Another experience that truly stood out was being invited onto Dubai TV.

Sharing insights on communication, leadership, and the human side of performance with a wider audience was both humbling and energising a reminder of how powerful clear, human-first communication can be.

Now, holidays are close, but I’m not standing still.

I’m already working on the strategy for next year. Planning and shaping a completely new chapter with the team.

And getting excited about what we’re building for 2026.

So let me take a moment to say: Thank you to every single person I had the honour to work with this year.

I can’t wait for 2026!

What was your biggest win this year, personal or professional?
