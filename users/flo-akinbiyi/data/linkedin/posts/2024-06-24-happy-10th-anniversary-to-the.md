Happy 10th Anniversary to the Supercar Owners Circle AG!

This weekend marked a remarkable milestone as we celebrated the 10th anniversary of the Supercar Owners Circle AG in Andermatt. Standing beside the iconic Lamborghini Countach, I felt immense pride to have been part of this success story for the past five years.

We managed to drive up the legendary Furkapass, known from the James Bond movie Goldfinger. Leading a convoy of the world’s rarest classic supercars up an exclusively closed mountain road in rain, fog, and snow was a mind-blowing experience.

It was amazing to celebrate the 10th anniversary of the Supercar Owners Circle and to be back in Andermatt after 5 years. The hospitality of THE CHEDI ANDERMATT was nothing short of spectacular, making our stay even more memorable.

Can’t wait to share some shorts and stories from this extraordinary weekend in Andermatt.
