If your communication sounds like AI, you’re falling behind at a very fast pace.

Based on the conferences I’ve hosted, the speakers and presenters I’ve witnessed, and the coaching I’ve delivered across Europe, Asia, the Middle East, and Africa, one thing has become very clear to me:

Many leaders and professionals have a problem.

They sound like AI.
They love working with AI.
And they are starting to become lazy.

Copy. Paste. Deliver. Done.

I see this happening more and more.
And this is not how we deliver qualitative, relevant, and unique communication.

This is exactly why I believe so strongly in human-first communication.

AI is advancing at a rapid pace and this is no longer a future scenario.

A recent MIT study shows that AI is already advanced and cheap enough to perform work equivalent to nearly 12% of U.S. jobs.
That’s not a projection. That’s current analysis.

And this brings us to an uncomfortable truth.

AI has two downsides.

First, it makes people lazy.
Second, it makes the lazy replaceable.

Is that really a downside?
I don’t know.

But when I reflect on what I’m observing, one thing becomes very clear to me:

As AI scales information, humans must scale connection.

Now is the time to invest in human skills.
To be better at what makes us uniquely human.
To learn it.
To enhance it.

IQ was yesterday.
EQ is today.

Human connection is what the world needs.
Collaboration between young and old.
Different perspectives. Real presence.

These are the elements that will make us successful in the future.

And that’s why, for me, the direction is clear.

Next year, with my business, I’m entering the era of human-first communication.
