𝗜𝗺𝗮𝗴𝗶𝗻𝗲 𝗱𝗲𝗹𝗶𝘃𝗲𝗿𝗶𝗻𝗴 𝗮 𝘀𝗽𝗲𝗲𝗰𝗵 𝗶𝗻 𝘁𝗵𝗲 𝗽𝗹𝗮𝗰𝗲 𝘄𝗵𝗲𝗿𝗲 𝗝𝘂𝘀𝘁𝗶𝗻 𝗧𝗶𝗺𝗯𝗲𝗿𝗹𝗮𝗸𝗲 𝗱𝗲𝗹𝗶𝘃𝗲𝗿𝗲𝗱 𝗵𝗶𝘀 𝘄𝗲𝗱𝗱𝗶𝗻𝗴 𝘀𝗽𝗲𝗲𝗰𝗵. 🌟

At the 10th anniversary of the Supercar Owners Circle AG, we spent a weekend in 𝗣𝘂𝗴𝗹𝗶𝗮 at the stunning 𝗕𝗼𝗿𝗴𝗼 𝗘𝗴𝗻𝗮𝘇𝗶𝗮 𝗥𝗲𝘀𝗼𝗿𝘁. The first evening’s welcome dinner took place in the very square where 𝗝𝘂𝘀𝘁𝗶𝗻 𝗧𝗶𝗺𝗯𝗲𝗿𝗹𝗮𝗸𝗲 𝗮𝗻𝗱 𝗝𝗲𝘀𝘀𝗶𝗰𝗮 𝗕𝗶𝗲𝗹 celebrated their love.

I have to admit, even though I knew the audience well, being in such a special location made me a bit nervous. There’s just something about the atmosphere of a place steeped in history and emotion that adds an extra layer of pressure.

But that’s exactly why it’s so important to always be ready to speak. You never know when the opportunity will come, or where it will take you.

This experience reminded me to embrace every speaking opportunity with humility and to recognize the potential impact that each moment on stage can have. It’s not just about delivering words; it’s about creating memories, inspiring others, and leaving a lasting impression.
