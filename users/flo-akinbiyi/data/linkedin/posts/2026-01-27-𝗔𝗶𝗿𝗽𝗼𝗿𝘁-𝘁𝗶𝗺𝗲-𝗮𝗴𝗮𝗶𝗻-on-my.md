On my way to Paris the first trip of the year. 

I love traveling.
To be quite honest, I see it as a real privilege and that mindset alone puts me in a great mood for most journeys.

But we all travel very differently.

Last year, one of my travel partners showed me a completely new side of his personality the moment we entered the terminal.
The airport resting face came on.
Suddenly, he was more serious, more guarded a slightly heavier version of himself.

And that’s what fascinates me.

Whether you’re the don’t-talk-to-me type, the hyper-efficient professional, or the happy chatter when you enter a terminal, airports are one of the most interesting places to observe people and their communication.

So here are my personal do’s and don’ts when traveling

DO:
👉 Smile, and if someone wears a name tag, address them by their name.
👉 Make the check-in staff laugh. It might not get you an upgrade, but it might get you the seat you want.
👉 Say good morning or good afternoon to immigration officers. They’re human too, asking how they are matters.
👉 Check before you travel that you can actually open your luggage lock. Trust me, you don’t want to land and start guessing.
👉 Slow down and communicate clearly. Calm, precise messages always travel better than rushed ones.

DON’T:
👉 Try small talk with customs officers. It doesn’t make you friendly, it makes you look suspicious. (Tried that.)
👉 Communicate from stress or agitation. Nervous energy doesn’t make staff more helpful, clarity does.
👉 Start conversations with people wearing headphones or clearly watching a movie. (Yes... sometimes that’s me.)
👉 Assume rules become flexible because you’re in a hurry. They don’t.
👉 Forget that every interaction is still human-to-human, even at gate B47.

Traveling isn’t just about getting from A to B.
It’s about how you show up along the way.

What are your do’s and don’ts when traveling?
