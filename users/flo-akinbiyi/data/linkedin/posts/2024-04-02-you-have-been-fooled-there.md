You have been fooled. 😲There is not going to be an AI replacing Flo in every aspect of my work.

👔 - I certainly don’t want to take away my outfit budget.

✈️ - I do not want to stop traveling.

❤️ - I do not want to give up on the wonderful gift of facilitating human connections through better communication.

Let me say this loud and clear:💡 - I am all for AI. I use it in my business backend and frontend, and I am definitely launching and introducing more AI aspects to the business in the near future.

But, there are a few things that should not be replaced by AI.

🚫 - Having an AI presenter at an event in between keynotes, is that really what we want? I say no.
🤝 - When we come together as an event, we should have humans leading through the experience, not just stare at a screen.

When we work on improving our communication skills, we should work with AI to look at certain techniques. But, we shouldn’t forget the importance of learning from one human to another.

So therefore, use AI, enjoy AI, work with AI, but don’t forget that communication is, and should always be, human to human. 

🗣️ - Tell me, did you believe that AI was real?
