Giving back to the startup community is always an honor!

Last week, I had the pleasure of delivering a keynote for my DHL friends, Amadou, Irina, and Lindsey, at the DHL Fast Forward Challenge, in collaboration with PwC. For the second year in a row, they’ve involved me in this fantastic initiative, and every time, it’s an incredible experience.

Why? Because the founders in this challenge aren’t just building businesses, they’re preparing to pitch their products, solutions, and ideas to an audience in just a few weeks. And that means one thing: communication skills matter.

Yes, content is key, but just as important is how the founder delivers it. That’s where I come in to help them elevate their storytelling, sharpen their presence, and make a lasting impact.

One of the best compliments I received after the session came from a client who has known and worked with me for two years. She said:

“I’ve known you for a few years now, but every time I see you speak, you get better.”

That meant the world to me. To be seen evolving, improving, and constantly upping my game - that’s what I strive for.

Thank you again to DHL and PwC for having me, and I can’t wait for the finals of the DHL Fast Forward Challenge!
