As a public speaker striving to be great and even outstanding—you have to be ready to get stung and feel the pain. 

If you play it safe, you will never reach greatness. ❌

To truly stand out, you must dare to do something different, experiment with new ideas, and adapt on the fly. 

That’s where the magic happens—outside your comfort zone, where uncertainty tingles and adrenaline surges.

Taking risks in #PublicSpeaking feels like touching a cactus. You don’t know if it will hurt or not, but you have to try.

Just delivering a prepared speech isn’t enough. ❌

You need to step into the unknown and push boundaries.
✅ Read the room and feel the energy.
✅ Rearrange your material as needed.
✅ React to the audience’s mood in the moment.

I’ve experimented with countless techniques—leading Mexican waves, asking challenging questions, even prompting spontaneous standing ovations. 

Each time, I feel like I’m touching a cactus, unsure if the risk will pay off. 

But it’s in these moments of daring that I discover what truly resonates and creates a lasting impact.
