Why would you make the audience balance on one foot and stretch the other one when starting a conference?

The answer is simple - audience engagement.

Audience engagement set you up for a success and creates a strong bond between you and your audience. As a keynote speaker and presenter, I’m a big fan of all engagement. 

Nobody can just sit and listen a speech. Eventually, you need a break to digest, to switch off or, as we’ve known from any cases, drift onto their phone.
And that is where audience engagement comes in handy. Whether you do the good old show of hands, ask the audience questions that they can answer with digital engagement or anything else appropriate for the engagement, you’ll see their attention focus back to you. 

If you watch the world’s best keynote speakers like Tony Robbins, Robin Sharma or John Sanei, you can see how they interact with the audience and hold their attention.
