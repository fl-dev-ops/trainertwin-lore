Yes, I am standing on real metal nails.

You might ask yourself, why am I standing on nails, and why would I show this to my LinkedIn community? This week marks Men's Health Week, and mental health is a topic that men often don't discuss enough. I would like to take this opportunity to share a tool that I have come across this year.

It was Danilo Schmidt 🇩🇪🇦🇪 who introduced me to this tool, which some might think is a torture method, but in fact, it is an outstanding mental health device. The Sathu boards date back to ancient India, where Sathus and Yogis used them on their journey to enlightenment.

Increase Focus: They help you concentrate better.
Embrace Discomfort: They challenge you to endure and grow.
Build Mental Resilience: They strengthen your mental fortitude.
Release Endorphins: Standing on Sathu boards can trigger the release of 'feel-good' hormones.

These are just a few of the wonderful effects of Sathu boards. I have started to incorporate them into my public speaking workshops, as they are a great tool to get out of your comfort zone and show yourself that you can actually do and endure much more than what we think we can.
