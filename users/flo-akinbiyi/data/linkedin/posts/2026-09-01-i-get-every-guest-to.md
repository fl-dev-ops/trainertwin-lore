I get every guest to connect with me. And to like me.
Even if it takes hours.

Moni Flöss, who works at the 5 star Hotel Alpenhof in Murnau as a tour guide that takes people through the marsh, shows them the city and its history, and gives guests amazing tours and experiences.

As a guide, her aim is to bring every guest back to the hotel happier than when she picked them up. And she does that with a die-hard dedication of no matter how grumpy somebody is, no matter how bad somebody feels, no matter in what state of mind they are. Moni uses humor, interaction and a genuine interest for her audience to build that connection, and I could see that in action while we were chatting. 

While she who them on the tour, she constantly works on building a strong human connection so that they eventually forget about their troubles, worries, or whatever is going on in their lives, can be present in the moment and enjoy the magical Bavarian scenery.

In corporate presentations, in meeting rooms, boardrooms, and on the stage, I see over and over presenters that talk and for the entire time not once worry about if they have a connection with the audience or not.

Add a bit of that skill, that dedication of Moni to your next corporate presentation, and I can guarantee you, your outcome is at another level.

What an amazing communication lesson to find on top of mountain.
