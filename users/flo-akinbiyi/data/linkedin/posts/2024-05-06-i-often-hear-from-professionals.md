I often hear from professionals, "I don't like to deliver presentations that I haven't written." Yes, it's challenging to convey content you didn’t create in a way that resonates with your audience. Using someone else's words, getting excited about messages that don't fully align with your beliefs—it's not easy. But, there are ways to enhance this experience for both you and your audience:

1.⁠ ⁠Research and Personalize: Take the presentation and dive deep into the topic, messages, and content. Find stories, facts, and additional content that excite you and make the material more relatable.

2.⁠ ⁠Engage with Authenticity: Deliver the content in an engaging and exciting manner. Practice and rehearse with energy and authenticity. Over time, you’ll find it possible to present it on stage in a way that makes you proud.

3.⁠ ⁠Focus on Value: Understand the value the presentation offers the audience. Shift the focus from yourself to the content and the audience. You are the facilitator, so use your abilities to deliver the content effectively.

Mastering the art of presenting non-original content is about making it your own through personalization, authentic delivery, and a clear focus on the audience’s needs. Embrace these challenges, and you'll not only improve as a speaker but also deliver presentations that truly impact your listeners.
