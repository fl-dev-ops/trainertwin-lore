Evidence is sexy.

That was the line of the evening in Hamburg, and nobody disagreed. Professor Dr. Ariel Dora Stern said it, and she is right. Health tech has grown up. What used to be a space full of promising ideas is now a space full of proven ones, backed by studies, real data and regulatory approval. 

The industry has matured, and you could feel it all evening.

It was also a truly special night. Full of human interaction, full of celebration, and full of emotional moments that will stay with everyone who was there for a lifetime. For the 11th time, the Health-i Award brought together the people trying to make healthcare better, faster and closer to the patient. An initiative by Handelsblatt and Techniker Krankenkasse that gets stronger with every edition. 

The finalists of the Top Ten pitched on the stage, and the quality was incredibly high with very diverse solutions. Oncology, stroke care, clinical documentation, prevention… Completely different problems, completely different solutions. Any one of them would have been a worthy winner. 

A huge congratulations to the three who took the podium. ida from Pöcking in Bavaria. Their ida-care app supports patients with metastatic breast cancer. Patients log their symptoms weekly, an algorithm watches for serious deterioration, and the treating medical team is informed in real time. Inga Bergen gave the most emotional laudatio of the night, and seeing the tears of joy on that teams faces was something I will not forget. 

CatMedica from Mainz. Their Seal-Protect technology aims to remove blood clots completely on the first attempt in stroke treatment. Fewer lasting injuries, lower follow-up costs. Thank you Dr. Tobias Heimann for a wonderful laudatio. 

myScribe GmbH. Their AI generates doctors letters from existing data at the push of a button, giving physicians back two to three hours every single day. And a very special laudatio from Vanessa Schmoranzer.

One more thing worth saying out loud. This year, Germany's female founders were in the majority on that stage. That is not a footnote. That is a very good night for the industry.

Thank you to Lara Sophie Bothur for being a brilliant partner on stage. To Kirsten Ludowig and Jens Baas. To Professor Dr. Ariel Dora Stern for keeping the bar high. To Andreas von Münchow and Uta von Braunn at Die Techniker. To the jury, to every finalist who pitched, and to the whole Health-i team at Handelsblatt for an evening that was organised beautifully from the first minute to the last.

See you at number twelve.
