🌟 A Big Shout-Out to Sebastian Lita 🌟

Back in 2012, my wife Ale Aster Akinbiyi and Bas Jordan, our then-managing director of AVANTGARDE Group's office in Abu Dhabi, had the pleasure of interviewing Sebastian.

Despite facing initial setbacks, Seb's persistence came through. And thanks to the sharp eyes of Ale and Bass, he was given a chance to join us from Romania for an internship in the U.A.E.

🏜️ In the following months, Seb worked with us on the epic BMW Group M Power Experience launching the new BMW M6.

Right from day one, we presented him with challenging tasks. On his first day, we sent him into the desert with a daunting task, to create a parking spot in the middle of nowhere, with no infrastructure around. And he did it so well that the supplier still remembers his professional attitude 12 years later.

🌟 Fast forward to today, and it's amazing to see Seb's growth. Now an outstanding senior project manager at Jack Morton Worldwide, he's delivering incredible projects for Porsche AG and many other world-class clients. His professional evolution is nothing short of inspiring.

👏 Congratulations, Seb! It's always a pleasure to work with you, and I couldn't be prouder of your achievements.
