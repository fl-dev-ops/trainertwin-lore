I am so excited about this work trip with my little son, Little T.

	•	Destination: Munich, Germany
	•	Purpose: Hosting the health.tech conference
	•	Travel Companion: My son, Little T, is coming along!

Little T and I are off to Germany where I’ll host the health.tech conference, and he’ll join me. On our way, a surprising and heartwarming moment happened—Emirates Airlines was kind enough to upgrade me to business class. But, with a smile, I chose to downgrade back to economy. 

Why? I couldn’t imagine sitting apart from my five-year-old son. Whether he’s five or fifteen, spending quality time together during these moments means more to me than any extra comfort.

I’m thrilled to be hanging out with friends and family with him, showing him what I do for work with the amazing team at health.tech conference. We’re looking forward to creating moments and memories that will last a lifetime. I am particularly excited to see him grow during this trip as I will have him handle passports, pay at restaurants and present passports—all explained and guided by me, step by step.

So, thank you, universe, for allowing me to have created this life for me. Any suggestions on travel with a five-year-old would be greatly appreciated!
