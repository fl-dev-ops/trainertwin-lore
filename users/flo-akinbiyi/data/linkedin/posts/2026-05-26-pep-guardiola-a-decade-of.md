Pep Guardiola, a decade of greatness!

10 years, 20 trophies, and a philosophy that redefined football forever.
Last year, I had the honor of interviewing with the G.O.A.T. in front of a live audience and talk about leadership, pressure, success, and the mindset behind one of the greatest coaching careers the world has ever seen.

And honestly, some of his lessons have stayed with me and will most likely stay with me forever.

“I want my players to love me.”

“Never speak when the emotions are high.”

“Success is never guaranteed.”

Simple, humble words from the man himself. What impressed me most about Pep was not only the success, but his dedication, his intention and the humanity. And some of his secrets of success can be found in his dedication, obsession with details, standards, trust, and culture.

Thank you, Pep, for the lessons and for showing the world what leadership at the highest level can look like.
