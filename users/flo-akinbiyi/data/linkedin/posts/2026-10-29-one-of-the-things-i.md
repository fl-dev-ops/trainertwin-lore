One of the things I love most about my job? 
Being part of conversations that can make the world a better place.

From 16–19 November, I'll be hosting the brand-new MEDICA Main Stage at MEDICA in Düsseldorf, one of the world's leading medical trade fairs.

Four days, around 50 speakers, international leaders, innovators and experts coming together to discuss some of the biggest questions shaping the future of healthcare: 
AI,
resilient health systems,
the hospital of the future,
prevention and new care models.

I'm particularly looking forward to moderating two panels close to my heart:
Resilient Healthcare: Building Future-Ready Hospitals, with leaders from Charité Berlin, Sheba Medical Center and Rigshospitalet Copenhagen and
The Future of Precision Medicine, exploring how we create more personalised treatments and prevention

Added to that a fireside chat on Hospital at Home, keynotes on AI in healthcare, and a personal highlight: the MEDICA Start-up COMPETITION, for the first time on the Main Stage. 

On 17 November, 15 finalists selected from over 320 submissions will pitch ideas at the Innovation World Cup that could transform healthcare, from AI diagnostics and robotics to cybersecurity, in front of an international jury and a live audience vote. 

When I think about it, it's pretty special that my work as an international presenter allows me to facilitate conversations that could genuinely improve people's lives.

And that's something I'm truly grateful for.

Thank you Carmen Berger and the MEDICA team for bringing me on board!

Who will be at MEDICA 2026? I'd love to connect!
