This is Dubai’s number one etiquette and manners coach.

When I met Orchids two years ago, she wasn’t happy with her public speaking skills.

Her introduction was lengthy, unstructured, and didn’t truly convey her story or message in the right way.

Fast forward a few sessions and a few years later, Orchids has become an incredible speaker.

She’s inspiring.

She’s fun.

And she creates deep emotional connections with her audience.

Here are 3 things we can all learn from Orchids as a speaker:

1. Know the power of your story. 
Use the best parts to show the audience your value, your experience, and your expertise.

2. Engage with your audience. 
Speaking is never a monologue. It’s always a dialogue, and Orchids does this beautifully.

3. Dare to be vulnerable. 
Orchids isn’t afraid to shed a tear on stage. That kind of openness takes real confidence and emotional maturity, and it’s incredibly powerful.

As someone who works with audiences ranging from children to leading corporates, Orchids constantly adapts her delivery. Watching her in action is proof that you can grow into a powerful speaker if you put in the time, the effort, and the practice.

Compliments to your journey and transformation, Roy. You continue to inspire me.
