What is the difference between speaking and speaking effectively?

Many years ago when I was an employed event director I had a pitch for a Ford project that changed me forever.

We pitched for an internal training with the sales teams from the dealerships from multiple countries.

The presentation was a phone conference with some clients that dialed in from Dubai and others from the US.

I greeted everyone and started to present our concept. But after the first couple of slides I noticed that I am just speaking and not being an engaging presenter.

This was at a time in my life where I had already spent more than a year with Toastmasters and already delivered many speeches, evaluations and impromptu speaking sessions.

So I upped my game, sat up straight and amplified my delivery.
I used voice modulation…
Added gestures to my delivery…
I smiled and spoke with more energy…
And I interacted with the audience on the phone call…

A colleague that was in the room noticed this rather big shift and commented later, how impressed she was with my presentation…

We won the project!

But what I won that day was an insight that there is a big difference between speaking and elevated speaking.

Have you ever had a moment where you realized how your speaking skills can change everything?
