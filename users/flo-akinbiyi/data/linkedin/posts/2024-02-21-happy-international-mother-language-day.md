🎉 Happy International Mother Language Day! 🌍

Today, I'm not just celebrating one language, but two and a half! My journey with languages has been a rich tapestry of experiences and emotions.

🔸 Raised in German, with a heartfelt connection to English...
🔸 A childhood in London, immersed in English culture...
🔸 The tough transition back to Germany and the shift to monolingual upbringing...
🔸 Navigating numbers in two languages, mixing German and English...
🔸 Embracing the Bavarian dialect, a language of its own...

Living in the UAE for 13 years has further solidified my bond with English. Despite the occasional grammatical hiccup, it's a language I deeply cherish. 

Bavarian dialect, on the other hand, feels like a completely different language, one that I switch on and off, adding another layer to my linguistic identity.

💭 Languages are more than just words; they are the essence of our thoughts, dreams, and heritage.

What about you? Which language do you find yourself most at home with? Let's share and celebrate our unique language stories! 🌐
