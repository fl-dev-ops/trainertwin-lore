I have been thinking about this a lot lately.

Every week I stand on stages, sit in coaching sessions, and work with leaders who run some of the biggest companies in the world.

And I keep seeing the same thing.

The way you walk into a room. 
The way you use your voice. 
The way you hold a pause and let someone actually feel what you just said instead of rushing.

Above all, the human connection that you create in any communication.
The intersection of all of this is what I call Human-first Communication. 
It is not a framework I invented in a boardroom. 

It is a philosophy that I live by every single day. 
Whether I host events on international stages for thousands of people or I am at the supermarket having a chat with the cashier or I am talking to a police officer.

The skill is the same. Human-first Communication.

And I love that you get the chance to observe masters of it everywhere. 
I have documented many examples of everyday people delivering memorable, effective and deeply human communication. #Each1Teach1

And sometimes we see a CEO, a celebrity, and a little bit less often a politician that delivers communication that truly resonates because it is human first.

Do you remember someone who communicated like that?
