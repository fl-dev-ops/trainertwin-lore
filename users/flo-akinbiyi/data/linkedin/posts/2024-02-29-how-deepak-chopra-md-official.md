How Deepak Chopra MD (official) inspired me 🤯 

In 2022, amidst the innovative spirit of ADFinanceWeek (ADFW), I had the honor of meeting the visionary Deepak Chopra. His keynote, a blend of wisdom and insight, left us all in awe. But it was a single sentence that resonated with me profoundly, shaping my perspective on success ever since:

"The greatest measure for success is fun." 👈

This statement, simple yet profound, sparked a journey of reflection. While I cherish the impact we create in our communities, industries, and families, Chopra's words reminded me that joy and fulfillment are the true hallmarks of success. It's a reminder that, without happiness, achievements can feel hollow.

As we navigate our paths, let's think about this - success isn't just about the tangible outcomes but the joy we find in our journey. 💯

But what does this mean for you? How do you measure your success?
