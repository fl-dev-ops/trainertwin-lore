Some projects feel like you are coming home!
Hosting the Global All Hands for Messe Muenchen was one of those moments.

I grew up with events at the Messe Munich that I visited, participated in and later on even hosted.

And guiding the team through their new strategy for 2026 - 2031 was a true honor. 

Being on stage with the CEO’s Reinhard Pfeiffer and Stefan Rummel was fantastic. Their energy, authenticity and passion was outstanding and all other speakers displayed the same level of enthusiasm and professionalism!

But we were not only 600 plus people in the room we also had live with us the teams from China, Singapore, India, Turkey and Brazil! 

What made this event even more special was that this entire spectacle was delivered without the help of an agency. 
Kudos to Tobias Redl Gregor Kuhl and the entire team who had worked tirelessly on this event! 👏🏽

And thanks to the amazing show director Malte Joergens and the entire team of Neumann & Müller for making this complicated project an exciting and enjoyable walk in the park!
