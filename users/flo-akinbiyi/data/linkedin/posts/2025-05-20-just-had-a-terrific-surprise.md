Just had a terrific surprise at Dubai Airport.

I was strolling through the Emirates Business Lounge, looking for an empty seat. Eventually, I found one in a quiet corner, right next to two gentlemen deep in conversation.

I left my luggage there and went off to fix myself a healthy breakfast.
When I came back, I looked at the two men… and one of them looked oddly familiar.

After a moment, I couldn’t help myself. I turned to him and said, “I have a feeling we’ve met before.”

He looked back and said, “Yeah, same.”

I asked, “Do you speak at events?” He nodded and said yes. 

Then he looked at me again and said, “You’re Flo, aren’t you?”

I smiled. “Yes.”

We both remembered each other and started trying to figure out where we’d met. He had been a keynote speaker. I had hosted the event. 

We’re connected here on LinkedIn. And even though we couldn’t recall the exact event…

Here’s what struck me: I remembered him. Not his talk. Not a single word of the content (which I’m sure was great). But him - his presence, his energy, his delivery.

And that’s the point.

Thanks, Matt Twilley, for reminding me again that being a great speaker isn’t just about content. 

It’s also, to a large extent, about your personality, presence, and delivery.

Because sometimes, those are the things that truly leave a lasting impression.
