❗I say 𝗻𝗼 it is a powerful way to build human connections!

What people call “#smalltalk" is actually a powerful opportunity to create real, authentic human connections. 

Moments that seem insignificant can turn into experiences that touch lives and shape perspectives.

One of my favorite example was the cashier that I met at the gas station. 
I complimented his tattoo of a lovely lady, which turned out to be his sister who had passed away.
In that instant, we shared an emotional moment where he opened up, expressing gratitude for someone noticing him and his journey. 
It wasn’t just small talk—it was a human connection. 💬

This practice has served me well as a public speaker. 
Training yourself to connect with a random stranger means you’re learning to look beyond uniforms, titles, or positions. 
You're no longer seeing "roles”, “uniforms” or “titles” you're seeing people.

So when it comes to #PublicSpeaking.
The moment you step onto the stage, you’re not just addressing an anonymous crowd—you’re speaking to individuals with their own stories, emotions, and experiences.
