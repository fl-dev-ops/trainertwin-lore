War. Division. A level of geopolitical complexity that is hard to even look at.

So how do you stay optimistic?

Last night in Milan I met ten answers.

Once again I had the honour of hosting the Intercultural Innovation Hub Awards, the partnership between the United Nations Alliance of Civilizations (UNAOC) and the BMW Group, together with Accenture, at the BMW GROUP ITALIA headquarters in Milan.

Ten organisations were chosen from thousands. Not for the tools they use. For the impact they have.

They sit with people who have stopped speaking to each other and get them talking again. They walk into classrooms, neighbourhoods and refugee camps where the divide is already deep, and they close it. Quietly. Without a hashtag. Year after year.

It was something to have all three patrons on stage with us.

Sergio Solero, President and CEO of BMW Group Italia, opened the evening and welcomed everyone into the house.

Alexander Bilgeri for the BMW Group, who said it clearly: this company is not just about selling cars, it is about having a positive impact on society.

Roland Mayr for Accenture, who reminded the room that intercultural dialogue starts with I. With each one of us.

And Miguel Ángel Moratinos, UN Under-Secretary-General and High Representative for the Alliance of Civilizations, who carried the message of hope into a room that needed it: dialogue is stronger than division.

Renzo Vitale, PhD sat down at the piano and created moments that no speech could have created. The room went completely still.

Congratulations to the ten recipients:

Anna Daniel Wakili, The Girl Child Values Support Initiative, Nigeria
Paul Mensah Amanor, Foundation for Educational Equity and Development (FEED Ghana), Ghana
Jonelle Naudé, no-name initiative (nni), South Africa
Aizhan Alymbai kyzy, OASIS Public Charitable Foundation, Kyrgyzstan
Wajid Zahid, Youth Development Foundation, Pakistan
Marius Dumitrescu, Asociația Suryam (MureșHub), Romania
Marc Vives, Fundació Itinerarium, Spain
Stefanos Batsis, Organization Earth, Greece
Marcelo Trivelli, Fundación Semilla, Chile
Juan Carlos Viloria Doria, Venezolanos en Barranquilla, Colombia

Ten organisations. Ten countries. One room.

That is where the optimism comes from.
