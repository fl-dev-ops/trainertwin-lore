"I can introduce you well to my company, but I can't introduce myself."

Sounds insane? Unfortunately, it's reality.

I see this in every single communication workshop I deliver.

We leave school with many skills. But one of the the most fundamental skills is rarely taught:

**How to introduce yourself.**

In my workshops, the first exercise is always:
"Deliver your professional introduction to the people in the room."

And the reactions I get are:
"I wish I had time to prepare."
"Oh, that was stressful."
"I never know how to introduce myself."

Think about it.

You know yourself better than anyone else. Shouldn't introducing yourself be the easiest thing in the world?

We know it isn't. A memorable and impactful introduction requires reflection, preparation and curation of your own story, expereince and life's journey.

That's why, I created a simple framework that makes it easy for people to introduce themselves with clarity, credibility and personality.

Because a great introduction should make the people remember the person they met, not jsut the company they work for.
