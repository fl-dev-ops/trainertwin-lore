90 minutes. That is all it took to change how a room full of founders talk about their product. 
  
Yesterday I ran a communication session at gate - Garchinger Technologie- und Gründerzentrum in Munich. 
  
And one participant gave me such powerful feedback at the end. 
  
He said, "We've been focusing on our product for years. but never really paid attention to how we present it and how we talk about it."
  
In this session, I took everyone out of their comfort zone. Every single participant had to introduce themselves in front of the group. 
  
Then I shared my framework for introductions, and afterwards, everyone got to pitch.
  
And all of that while I recorded them. 
  
Through the feedback, I was able to touch on storytelling, the power of delivery, voice, body language, being authentic, bringing in the wow factor, and using the right language for the audience. 
  
Every single participant in the room got feedback, and not just from me. 
  
Most of us have communicated in the same way our whole lives. Changing how you communicate is difficult. 
  
And it is very difficult to change how you communicate by reading about it. But when you get feedback, see yourself, reflect, and get a few approaches, strategies and tools that actually work, then 90 minutes becomes a short but powerful investment. 
  
When was the last time you watched yourself pitch, present, or speak?
