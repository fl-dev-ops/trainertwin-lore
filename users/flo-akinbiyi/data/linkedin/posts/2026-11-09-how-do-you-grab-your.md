How do you grab your audience’s attention?

I think you’ve probably noticed that we live in a fast-paced world, surrounded by information coming at us from all directions. And for those of us who deliver workshops or presentations, getting people to stop, listen and stay engaged is becoming increasingly challenging.

So, how do we get our message across? How do we make people actually want to listen?

One key ingredient is charisma. We all know it matters, but can it be developed? And, more importantly, can you be charismatic if you’re an introvert?

To explore these questions, I invited Flo Akinbiyi  to join me for an upcoming episode.

But while we’re getting ready to record, I’d love to hear your thoughts:

What do you do to grab and keep your audience’s attention?
