This Gas station staff’s Introduction Made My Day – Find Out Why!
Yesterday, I met the most outstanding petrol pump agent in 13 years 😮.

Inspiration to become a better communicator is all around you, and you can find it anywhere, anytime 🌟. You just have to keep your eyes open 👀.

I was on my way back from Abu Dhabi to Dubai and needed to refuel my car. I stopped at the gas station between Saadiyat Island and Yas Island, an ADNOC Group station. As I pulled out and opened my window, this petrol pump agent greeted me with, "Hello, salaam alaikum," with a big smile 😊.

He goes on, "My name is Mohammed Shabid," and he continues with this big, contagious smile as we start to have a nice, friendly conversation. I'm from the UK, and I was a bit surprised because, looking at him and listening to him, I didn't see 'UK' there. This isn't about being racist; it's about understanding cultures and how someone, even from a foreign country living in the UK, would sound. I looked at him, very surprised and a bit confused.

He held my attention, waited a moment, and said, "I'm from the UK, United Kerala," and we both burst out laughing 😂. Oh my, what a memorable introduction! I took a photo of him, for which I got his permission, and decided to add it to my presentations. I want to give him a big shout-out, and also to ADNOC Group. Please find him and give him a promotion for being such an outstanding communicator, a true champion of your brand 👏.

For everyone else, this is how you become memorable with your introduction. You don't always need to add on the highlights, successes, and authority. Yes, all of that is needed, but a bit of inspiration, humor, and connection with your audience goes a long way in ensuring you are ultimately memorable. I might forget his name, but I will never forget that he is the guy from the UK, United Kerala 🇬🇧🇮🇳.

Do you have a memorable way to introduce yourself?
