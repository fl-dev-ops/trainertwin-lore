And just like that, Arab Health 2025 comes to an end!

But what a journey it has been! 

Four Days. Countless Innovations. One Mission: Transforming Healthcare

Hosting the Transformation Zone over the past four days has been an absolute honor.

From visionary doctors and professors to bold startups and investors, the energy, passion, and groundbreaking ideas in this space were truly inspiring. The goal? To push healthcare forward. And we did just that.

Key Highlights: 
✅ Witnessing Arab Health evolve into WHX - the World Health Expo - a powerful step towards a global healthcare movement. 
✅ Sharing the stage with astronaut, physician, innovator and adventurer Scott Parazynski - a reminder that the sky (or space) is not the limit. 
✅ Hosting the Innovate Startup Challenge, where the finest healthtech startups from around the world pitched for the grand prize.

These moments, these discussions, this progress, it’s why I love doing what I do. While it’s always bittersweet when an event like this wraps up, I leave grateful for the insights gained and the role I played in providing a platform where experts can change lives.

What’s next for healthcare innovation? Let’s keep the conversation going!
