Why should you care about fashion in today's business world? The common belief is that having strong expertise, solid knowledge of your professional subjects, and a deep understanding of your particular line of business are the most critical aspects of success. While these elements are indeed important, fashion plays a significant role in your overall professional presence. Fashion is an extension of body language, and whether you consciously curate your appearance or not, it communicates volumes about you.

Over the years, I have been fortunate to receive invaluable training from my Italian wife and her sister on how to discover my personal style and leverage fashion to my professional advantage. In numerous meetings, my outfit has often served as an icebreaker, sparking conversations with clients who are deeply involved in fashion projects. As a presenter, I utilize fashion to be more memorable, integrating my attire into my overall appearance and presentation.

As a communication coach, I use fashion to make bold statements, demonstrating that there is always room to push boundaries and embrace a more fashionable approach. I aim to dispel the misconception that certain looks are unattainable. In reality, almost any style can be pulled off with confidence and a willingness to experiment, even in the face of negative comments. Experimenting with fashion guarantees a mix of praise and criticism, but how you respond to these reactions is entirely up to you.

These are my insights on the intersection of fashion and communication. What are your thoughts and experiences?
