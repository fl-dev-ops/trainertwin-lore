Nine years ago, I hosted my first conference in Dubai.

My wife and I had just returned from our honeymoon in Japan, and shortly afterward, I had an amazing opportunity: the chance to host my very first conference.

I still remember every detail of that day. I prepared my script meticulously, packed with enthusiasm and a great amount of detail. I researched every speaker, every topic. I even went out and bought myself an Armani suit to look the part and feel confident. I brought not one but two white shirts, just in case nerves got the better of me.

My wife and even my mom came along to support me. I’ll never forget standing there, delivering my opening speech, and hearing my voice echo through the PA system. 

At that moment, I had this thought: “I’m born to do this.”

But let me be honest, while that was an exciting realization, I also had so much to learn.

Years of public speaking practice through Toastmasters had prepared me well, but becoming a great presenter is a journey. Refinement takes time, experience, and countless hours on stage and in front of the camera.

Today, after nine years of hosting conferences, events, and shows, I can say I’m incredibly proud of how far I’ve come - and I know there’s still more to learn. Every project is an opportunity to grow, to push boundaries, and to deliver even more value to the audience.

I am endlessly grateful: 
•	To my amazing clients for trusting me with their events. 
•	To the teams working tirelessly behind the scenes to create magic. 
•	And most of all, to my wife and family for supporting me on this journey.

Nine years in, and I’m just getting started. Here’s to many more!
