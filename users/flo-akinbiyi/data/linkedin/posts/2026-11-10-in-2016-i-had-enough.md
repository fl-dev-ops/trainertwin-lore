In 2016, I had enough material for a 20-minute workshop. 

Today, I can deliver anything from a 20-minute communication keynote to a 5-day workshops with leaders and teams from international corporations and government entities. 

And I have to say, it was anything other than a straightforward journey. 

In 2016, I did a life coaching certification just to find out that I never want to be a life coach. 

I was passionate about the topic because in 2013, I found a life coach who helped me find direction, clean out the closet, face a few new skeletons, explore my interests, and develop my skills. 

And she truly helped me become who I am today. She also sent me to Toastmasters. 

That's where I found also the roles I loved the most. 

Obviously, the speaking, but also the evaluator, watching the speaker, observing the audience, spotting what works and what doesn't, what are the opportunities to grow, and then present it and share it in a way with the other person so they become better. 

What I love about communication coaching is are the outcomes. Helping people to find out what they are good at and what superpowers they have, amplifying them, building on skills that aren't developed yet, and then empowering them to rise for a specific challenge. 

Whether that is a keynote, a TEDx talk, a town hall, a client pitch, a strategy meeting, hosting an event, you name it. There are many communication scenarios where coaching helps you to have a better outcome. 

And I'm blessed because on the way, I got to observe some of the world's best speakers like Arnold Schwarzenegger, Michelle Obama, Pep Guardiola, on and off the stage and behind the stage. 

And also, I feel blessed because I found that I can find inspiration and learning from everyday people on the street. 

If you ask me after 10 years of coaching what I think most people get wrong, it is that they think it is a soft skill. And they don't invest in it. 

What I see and observe is that the most successful leaders, executives, and entrepreneurs, most of them have world-class communication skills. Have you ever invested in your communication skills?
