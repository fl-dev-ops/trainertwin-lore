It’s an amazing challenge, but I have to admit—the real battle is consistency. Not the workouts, not finding activities, but sticking to it every single day. While I think it’s fair to say I’m in decent shape, there’s always room for improvement. And consistency? That’s tough.

For example:
Yesterday, I left the house at 6 a.m. and didn’t return until 8:30 p.m.
Still had to bring my son to bed, spend quality time with my wife, and squeeze in a bit of me-time. 🕒
And yet, the first thing I had to do was a 30-minute workout. It’s tough, but where there’s a will, there’s a way!
