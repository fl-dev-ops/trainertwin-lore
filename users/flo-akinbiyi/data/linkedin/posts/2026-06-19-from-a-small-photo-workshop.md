From a small photo workshop in 1936 to global technology leader. And next week, I get to tell this story. 

I am thrilled that I will be hosting the Durst Group AG Next Technology Festival as Durst celebrates its 90th anniversary at their headquarters in Brixen, South Tyrol, Italy. 

Durst is a company with a truly fascinating story. 

Founded in 1936 as a photographic imaging business, they have grown into a global leader in digital production, industrial printing, and additive manufacturing systems. 

But that's not enough, they are continuously pushing the boundaries of what is possible through innovation, quality, and technology. 

One of my favorite stories from the company's history goes back when Intelligence agencies, including the CIA, relied on Durst enlargers because of their extraordinary optical precision and mechanical accuracy. 

Think about that for a moment. 

A device from South Tyrol became international standard with the most sophisticated intelligence agencies in the world, because it reveal details you could otherwise just not see.

Today, the company is once again looking ahead and they are all geared up for an exciting next chapter. As the CEO Christoph Gamper always says “The guiding principle remains: Making good things better.” 

The festival will bring together experts from technology, manufacturing, entrepreneurship, academia, policy, agriculture, print, and advanced manufacturing. 

And I am looking forward to immerse myself in conversations, ideas and innovations that shape the next chapter of the industry.

I'm looking forward to work together with Rachel Brill, the festival director, her team, and the entire Durst leadership team, and to spend a few intense and inspiring days in South Tyrol with the spirit of a company that continues to reinvent itself. 

See you in Brixen.
