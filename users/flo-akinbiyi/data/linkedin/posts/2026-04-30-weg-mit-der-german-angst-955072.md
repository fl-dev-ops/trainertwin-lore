Weg mit der German Angst! - No more German Angst!

Germany has a mindset problem, and it's a problem that I see throughout Europe.

Over the past years, I have observed the same pattern on stages, in coaching sessions, and within leadership teams.
The focus is on the challenges.

Negativity is leading, and opportunities come second, or sometimes even third, if at all.
The number of negative conversations that I have with Germans, and to be fair, with many Europeans on various subjects, is truly sad.

From taxi drivers to C-level executives, the tone is often the same.
Cautious, seeing mostly the challenges.
Focusing on what could go wrong and often complaining.

Let me be clear: I'm not naive, and negativity isn't realistic.
It is just conditioned behavior.

Every time I talk about opportunities in a crisis, I know where I'm coming from.
In the past five years, I have been through three serious life crises, and I came out of every single one stronger.

And it is the truth!

Every crisis has an opportunity, and if our communication is dominated by fear, we're not helping ourselves, our teams, our clients, our partners, and everyone around us see it.

So the real question is: 
Are you allowing fear to dominate your communication?
