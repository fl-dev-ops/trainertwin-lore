For the small boy from a small town in the middle of beautiful Bavaria, it was a step that I would have never imagined. 

But when I was presented with the opportunity by AVANTGARDE Group and it was Vera Böttcher, who said, “Flo, I need somebody on ground in five days. Would you like to go?” 

Without one doubt of hesitation, I said yes and that was a yes that changed my life. 

So, thank you to Dubai.
