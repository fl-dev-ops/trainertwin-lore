Dare to add more slides! 🤯

"Why would I do that?"

"For this executive presentation, you can have only three slides."

I  have heard and seen this horrible strategy so many times, and I don't  know who came up with it, but I do know that it just doesn't make  sense.

How do you brief an executive with a professional  presentation with a lot of information in a short amount of time with  only three slides?

In most cases, the executive looks at slides full of text, logos, and information.

As a result, they have no idea what to look at first, even if you animate your slides. ❌

They have no idea what's the most important data on the slides. ❌

And most of all, they have no idea what the main message is. ❌

I  challenge everyone who has to deliver a presentation to the board, the  chairman, or an executive to reduce the content on the slides! 💪🏽

Focus  on what's more important and use five, maybe even 10 slides in a few  moments that support your spoken word rather than distract from it. 👈🏽

And  if your executive wants to see more details, graphs, facts, and  figures, have them ready in the appendix so you can always show them if  somebody asks. ✅

Try this technique, and I can tell you one  thing - if you dare to stand against a corporate culture and stand  against a bad industry practice, you can only win. 💯
