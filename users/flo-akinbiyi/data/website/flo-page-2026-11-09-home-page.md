Get to know Flo

Hi, I’m Flo Akinbiyi, a Dubai-based

presenter, public speaker and communication coach.

Over the last two decades, I’ve worked with many of the world's most exciting brands and leading organizations.

I’ve had the honor of meeting and sharing the stage with international celebrities, industry experts and politicians.

I’ve developed a unique ability to captivate any audience and deliver messages in an exciting and engaging way while hosting conferences, events and digital formats. I’ve used this skillset as a tool to offer effective coaching and training to professionals looking to elevate their communication skills.

I’ve spent the last decade deep-diving into the art of public speaking and have researched, analyzed and harnessed the tools and methodologies of some of the world’s most talented and accomplished communicators.

I’m passionate about mentoring people to grow and achieve their full potential. This passion comes to life in my work as a communication coach.
