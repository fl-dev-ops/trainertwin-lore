Why is it good to be expressive while public speaking and delivering a presentation? 💥

There are multiple research findings that speak about the importance of non-verbal language.

Your voice has the power to communicate competence, passion, and confidence 💪🏽

Your body language can create a powerful human connection, engage the audience and make your communication more effective.

And that is a simple reason why you should not speak with a monotone voice 📣

👉🏽 Why you should smile and not present with a resting face.

👉🏽 Why you should not speak with your hands in your pockets but use expressive gestures.

Many small elements of nonverbal communication make your public speaking and presentation skills more effective 🚀 ✨

Expressive public speaking and presentation skills are an essential part of being a successful speaker and presenter.
