Many presentations, speeches, and talks take a lot more time than required.

Why is that Flo?

Because the presenter is not curating his content enough.

Instead of focusing on the big picture, the presenter goes down to a level of detail that is not relevant for everyone in the audience.

What you want to do is:

Focus on the big picture

Evaluate which details are required

Be a harsh editor and cut content that is not relevant

Jerry Seinfeld says, “I will spend an hour taking an eight-word sentence and editing it down to five.”

If Jerry uses an hour for one sentence, should you spend 30 minutes to trim down your content?
