UNLOCK THE POWER OF

YOUR VOICE

We go beyond the surface level to help you harness the

true potential of your voice.

That's where the

Acoustic Voice Profile (AVP)

comes in – an

innovative

offering that revolutionizes the way you

communicate

and connect with others.

AVP is an AI-generated report that provides valuable insights into

your voice, offering a charisma score based on key criteria such as speaking rate, pitch level, pauses and others. It's a powerful

coaching tool which deepens the learning experience.

CHARISMA

AVP helps you understand how your voice is perceived by others, giving you the tools to enhance your charisma and captivate your audience. By fine-tuning aspects such as speaking rate and pitch, you can create a magnetic presence that leaves a lasting impression.

CONNECTION

Connect on a deeper level by gaining insights into empathic stress, which plays a vital role in conveying emotions and building meaningful connections. By mastering this aspect, you can foster trust and engagement by showing empathy and establishing rapport with your audience.

GUIDANCE

AVP provides a comprehensive analysis of your voice, highlighting areas where you excel and areas that can be further developed. This personalized feedback empowers you to make targeted improvements and tailor your communication style to suit different contexts and audiences.
