What will you learn?

COURSE INTRODUCTION

Welcome to the Course: Speak, Engage, Present - The ABCs of Winning Communication.

Over the next 45 days, you’ll master key communication skills like delivering impactful content, preparing effectively, and excelling in online and offline communication. By the end, you’ll feel confident and equipped to communicate like a pro!

Lesson 1: MINDSET, EVALUATION & SUPERPOWERS

We’ll reflect on where you are in your journey and help you develop the right mindset for success. Get inspired and ready to stay committed to achieving your goals!

Lesson 2: SETTING THE SCENE

We’ll explore world-class speeches from some of the greatest communicators. You’ll come away with a favorite speech or speaker that will inspire you for life!

Lesson 3: CREDIBILITY, DATA, FACTS

We’ll dive into the importance of content, the core of effective communication. You’ll learn how to craft your spoken words to ensure your message is clear, engaging, and impactful.

LESSON 4: STRUCTURE

We’ll explore how structure helps your audience absorb your message. Get ready to learn a fun and incredibly effective approach to structuring your talk!

LESSON 5: VISUAL AIDS

We’ll explore the power of visual aids in enhancing your presentation. You’ll learn how to make your PowerPoint slides more effective and discover other visual tools that can take your message to the next level.

LESSON 6: ELEVATE YOUR SPEECH

We’ll dive into language hacks to make your speech more impactful, using the art of storytelling and adding that extra spark. Get ready to elevate your message!

LESSON 7: IMPROMPTU SPEAKING

We’ll explore impromptu speaking and how to handle those "wing it" moments. You’ll learn how to make your impromptu speeches shine!

LESSON 8: YOUR VOICE IS A FERRARI

We’ll explore how to unlock your voice’s full potential. You’ll learn diaphragmatic breathing to speak with power and confidence.

LESSON 9: BODY LANGUAGE

We’ll dive into body language, one of my favorite topics. We’ll cover appearance, posture, facial expressions, gestures, and eye contact to help you communicate effectively.

LESSON 10: LANGUAGE

We’ll revisit the power of language and why it's crucial to communicate in a way everyone understands. We’ll explore accents, pronunciation, and how to connect with any audience through your words.

LESSON 11: CONNECTION & AUDIENCE ENGAGEMENT

We’ll focus on creating a genuine connection with your audience. You’ll discover strategies to engage them and turn your speech into an interactive conversation.

LESSON 12: PASSION & AUTHENTICITY

We’ll discover what sets great speakers apart—passion and authenticity. You’ll learn how to speak with true passion and embrace your authentic voice.

LESSON 13 - PREPARATION

We’ll dive into the power of preparation. You’ll learn how to overcome stage fright and use the 3D methodology to deliver impactful, well-prepared communication.

BONUS LESSON VIRTUAL COMMUNICATION

We’ll dive into virtual communication and its growing importance. You’ll learn how to keep your audience engaged and make a lasting impact in online meetings.
