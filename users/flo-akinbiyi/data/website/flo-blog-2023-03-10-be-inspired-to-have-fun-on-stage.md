When I was on stage with

Deepak Chopra MD (official)

at

Abu Dhabi Global Market (ADGM)

Finance Week last year, he said something that really inspired me!

“The greatest measure for success is fun.”

As an entrepreneur, this inspired me to not just work, but also not to forget that I can have fun and that I can be in charge of my own schedule if I choose to. 👈🏽

As a public speaker, it inspired me even more because if you prepare yourself mentally, physically and, of course, intellectually to be ready for a speaking engagement, it's not only going to be good and enjoyable for the audience but also fun for you, your public speaking will be elevated. So this is my measure in 2023 and beyond to have fun while being on stage. 💪🏽

And here is the best inside inspiration that I got from this quote, this measure  of fun should be continuously taken, and if you realize during a  speaking engagement or a presentation, you’re not having fun then it’s up to you to change it around, to turn it around and ensure that you’re having fun! 💥
