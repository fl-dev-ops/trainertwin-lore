Being in front of a camera can be challenging. But let me share a secret to help you ease into it.

When looking into the camera lens, imagine you are talking to a friend and start your conversation with a genuine, heartwarming smile – just like you would if you were chatting with them face to face 👥

Talking to a camera, especially in a professional context, can feel intimidating. Once that recording is done, it might find its way onto the internet, where it can exist forever!

Therefore, you've got to be kind to yourself during this journey. Understand that stepping into this new medium is a learning process, but with time, you can master it. Who knows, you might even end up loving it!

Being kind to yourself is a crucial part of this process, so embrace the learning curve, celebrate your progress, and watch your confidence grow in front of a camera!
