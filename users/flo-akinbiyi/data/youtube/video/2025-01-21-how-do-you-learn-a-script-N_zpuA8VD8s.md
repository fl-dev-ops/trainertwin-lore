speaker_1: How do you memorize a script Heres what I do when I have to memorize a script for a presentation for a pitch or any other speech What I do is first I will write a script And of course Ill think about my key message Ill find a great outline and Ill add the right stories so my audience is engaged and it works for the occasion Ill send it over to somebody get some feedback in Then Ill edit and edit and edit until I am happy and satisfied with the result And then comes the magic What I will do is I will record it on an iPhone a simple voice note because that allows me to listen to my script in the car in the shower in the gym wherever and whenever I want And through that Im eventually able to repeat every word Then take it even forward and speak a little bit ahead of the recording Through that Im eventually able to memorize this script so well that I know always what comes next And on stage then I can focus on the delivery So try my process Works well for me Might work as well for you
