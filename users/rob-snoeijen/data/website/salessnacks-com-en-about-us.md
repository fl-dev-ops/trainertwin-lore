About SalesSnacks
SalesSnacks High Impact Microlearning is an initiative of Dutch Sales-Inspirator Rob Snoeijen. From his passion for sales he has now written 4 bestsellers about sales, is a sales trainer and sales coach, speaker and is founder of SalesTopics, Accelerate your Sales!
SalesTopics is a driven, ambitious and above all creative training institute that helps organizations and (sales) professionals improve their commercial returns. With a proven successful methodology, the SalesTopics Academy delivers a demonstrable contribution to the personal growth of the participant and the optimization of the operating results of (International) organizations.
Vision of Learning
SalesSnacks High Impact Microlearning as an answer to modern learning challenges
In a world characterized by rapid technological developments, increasing workloads and an abundance of information, it is crucial to develop a renewed vision of learning. Traditional forms of training, such as long classroom sessions or comprehensive courses, increasingly fail to meet the demands of modern times. Microlearning is an engaging way of learning that fits our current world and the need to be able to “snack” information between and during activities. SalesSnacks offers an innovative microlearning solution to teach sales skills that fit the need for flexibility, efficiency and effectiveness.
Learning as a continuous process
Another important view of learning is that it is an ongoing process that is constantly evolving. In SalesSnacks’ vision, this means that learning is not limited to formal teaching moments or long learning sessions. Learning takes place at any time of the day, whenever the learner needs it. With short bephapable learning modules. The SalesSnacks High Impact Microlearnings focus specifically on developing your sales talent, each focusing on one specific “sales topic. SalesSnacks tailor learning to be accessible and fun. Moreover, the short learning units are often revisited, so that the learnings are remembered longer and better through repetition. In addition, the learnings support the development of learning skills needed to take control of one’s own learning process. And that always tastes like more….
