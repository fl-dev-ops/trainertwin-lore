This is a terrific blog by my friend, partner and Managing Director of Infoteam Phil Kreindler about the importance of coaching in the implementation of any behaviour change (which is really what sales training is all about - getting salespeople to change the way they do the «thing» they do every day, all day for a living). My colleague Peter Trix - salesgenetics once wisely said that 80% of the COST of a sales transformation program is in the training, but 80% of the BENEFIT is in the follow-up, the day-to-day coaching and support that the sales team needs to integrate the learnings from a workshop into their day-to-day working practices. 

Many sales leaders believe - wrongly - that once a salesperson attends a sales training course, or receives an e-mail from them with «proper instructions, methods and tools» that magic will happen, and they will change their day-to-day working practices immediately using the new tools and methods... if only it were that easy! Send-button deployment does not work... so read on.
