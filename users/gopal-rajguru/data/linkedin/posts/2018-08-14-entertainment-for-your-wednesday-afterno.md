Entertainment for your Wednesday afternoon 15th of August! 

The Nordic Business Forum will hold the final competition of its speaker sourcing contest in which, in which I will be taking part as one of the 6 finalists chosen from 3 qualifying events. You can watch the final 6 speeches on-line through the live-stream. It starts at 15:00 CET and if you are there from the start, you can also cast your vote (for me of course!). 

Go to www.nbforum.com/contest on Wednesday 15 August a few minutes before 15:00 CET. (no, that's not me in the photo below) The 15-minute speeches should run in total for about 2 hours with the change-overs and all that. I am on the agenda as the 5th speaker. 

The livestream video player will be shown at the top of the screen. You can also find the livestream broadcast on the Nordic Business Forum  Facebook page and YouTube channel.

To participate in the voting, you must start watching at 15:00 CET before the first speaker's presentation. A link will be shown on screen that allows you to register for the voting, which takes place after the last speaker. The results will be announced after the jury deliberates. 

Fingers crossed that you will vote for me! In any event, it will be a great show with some really talented speakers. Worth the watch.
