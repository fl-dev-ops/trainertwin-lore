What makes a leader?

A leader is someone people want to follow – not for you or the organisation, but for themselves: by following you, they become the people they want to be.

Leadership doesn’t require academic degrees or the power of position. It requires learning and consistently applying simple, pragmatic skills – and Lighthouse Leadership focuses on exactly those.
