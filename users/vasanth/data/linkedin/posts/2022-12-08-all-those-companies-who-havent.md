All those companies who haven't #laidoff associates started calling #employees to #bengaluru 😁. If it's your first time in #bengaluru here are tips from native Bengalurian. 

1. If you're unmarried, first join a PG near to your office where your colleagues/friends are living. Only after you find good company, you can search for flats. Searching for a #flat staying in a #hotel will be very #expensive.

2. Don't rely on #ola / #uber  for every #travel needs. There is #excellent BMTC bus service throughout the city. It is comfortable and saves a lot of #money.

3. Negotiate well with #maids and #cooks and clearly explain your needs if you already got a #flat. Otherwise they will not do the #work which you want or keep asking #money for every #small #work .

4. Every #city has its own #problem and #bengaluru's problem is traffic. Easy solution for this, reach office by 9AM and leave office by 4PM. Thus you can avoid traffic and save time. 

#bengaluru is a true #cosmopolitan city and localities like me are very #warm and #welcoming😎 . I'm sure your new journey in this city will flourish. If you have any doubts add it in the comment section.
