5 reasons why it is hard for me to follow Akshat Shrivastava

After watching Akshat's videos for about 6-8 months and learning a lot about the stock market from him. This is why I think it's hard for me to follow him.

1. He is an aggressive investor (who invests in lakhs or crores on many stocks) and divides the investment among different stocks. If one stock underperforms he can get returns from another. Most of us are mild investors. Investing in thousands, even if you split it among shares, when few underperform, you cannot make good profit from over performing stocks.

2. His risk appetite is more. Stocks which are suggested to buy recently (Definitely after our due diligence) : Zomato, Idfc, India bulls, housing finance etc are risky stocks. They might give great returns in a short period or Even after waiting for a long time things may not turn positive. Ex: India bulls housing finance.

3. Many quality stocks that he suggests to buy have not given good returns in the past few years. Ex: Hindustan Unilever, HDFC. They might be good in the long run but even after waiting for a period, they might not perform well. After paying a good amount for stocks like HDFC, everybody cannot wait for a long duration.

4. Many of his thoughts like not to buy houses but buy commercial property doesn't work for all. To own a rental property, along with money you also need to have good connections and you need to be a tough person. I still prefer buying houses in areas where land prices are increasing at more than 10% a year. 

5. I'm getting biased about the stocks he is saying, due diligence is getting missed. I'm starting to see positive points in stocks like Zomata and IDFC, which are still loss making. Akshay might be right, but I'm not able to think in my free mind. 

That being said, if you like his videos, if they are helping you, please continue to watch. No doubt he is a good teacher.
