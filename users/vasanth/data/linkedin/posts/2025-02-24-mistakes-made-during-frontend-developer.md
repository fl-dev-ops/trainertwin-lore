Mistakes made during frontend developer interview. Ep-10 "Over thinking to solve a problem"

Interviewer: "Can you implement a stopwatch that runs for 1 minute" ? 
Candidate: Writes a useEffect and a for loop inside that and a setTimeout inside that ! and finally the solution works. 

Interviewer (thinking in their mind), Why is a for loop needed ? Why set the state happening inside the for loop ? 

When the interviewer asks the same question to the candidate, some will justify the answer, some don't ! I feel code should be as simple as how 2 people converse, plain - simple - easy to understand. Over complicated by over thinking will un-necessarily make your interview clearance difficult !
