#incometax #savemoney As we are just 5 days away from filing income tax, here is how you can save money while filling ITR ?

1.If you have any additional income other than salary, you can file ITR 3/ ITR 4 instead of ITR 1. Where you have to pay tax for just half of the additional income instead of full. 

2. There is a section called 80TTA where you can add upto 10000 Rs/- as savings bank interest. (if you already have more than that, then don't fill it.)

3. If you're in a state where you need to pay a fine of more than 10000 Rs/- to incom tax india. You can surely  consult a CA, who generally charge 2-3K per filing but they will know some hacks to save your tax. 

4. Always pay advance tax if you know you have received any additional income other than salary. Ex: fixed deposits interest or savings bank interest etc. 

Most important, try using the official income tax website instead of third party tools like #cleartax, because sometimes you wouldn't know your income fully and just by uploading office form 16 your filing will not complete. You end up paying a fine later.
