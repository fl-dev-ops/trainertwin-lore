#freshers #hiring #tips The gold mine that #freshers ignore during their placement are ..
..
Your own  college alumni/seniors 

1. Let's say your college has been operating for 20 years, at least a few who passed in the first batch will be already in top positions in a company.

2. They are most likely to accept your connection request as you're from the same college.

3. From the pre-final year itself you start connecting with as many seniors as possible and try to have a good conversation with them.

Whenever you see openings in their company you can approach them and they should be able to help you.
