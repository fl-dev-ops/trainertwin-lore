#jobchange Don't apply for a job on #career #portal. Most people make this mistake of applying for a job on the career portal of the company. Sadly even today in most companies there is no right sync between the career portal and the hiring team.  All the resumes that get uploaded for a job don't reach the hiring team. 

What should you do instead?

1. Try to look for the similar openings on LinkedIn and connect with the job poster and share resume to them directly. Thus you can follow up with them for status.

2. If not on LinkedIn, try to look for the opening on career portal like naukri where you can find HR's email. You can reach out to HR directly using the email address.

3. Connect with people from that company on LinkedIn and ask for referral. Everybody may not respond but there will be someone who will be interested in referring you.

FYI: Once after you upload you're resume into career portal, you cannot be referred by anyone. So, use that chance cleverly..
