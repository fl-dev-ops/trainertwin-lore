As a hiring manager your certificates are the last thing that I would consider while shortlisting. After at least 2 years of experience I don't value either your personal projects or your certifications. I would only focus on what have you done in the past 2 years ? and is it in alignment with my organisational needs.  
Does that mean having certification is of no value ? I don't say that. But do certification for the sake of learning and not with the intention of getting a job.
Linking it to your #linkedin profile will certainly increase your chance of getting shortlisted in initial rounds of #interview. But, just because you have certificates definitely you will not get a job in a good company.
