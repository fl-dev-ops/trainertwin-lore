Influencers these days 
1. Search a job opening on the career portal for the skill that is trending. ex: python. 
2. Come to LinkedIn and say my team at xyz company is hiring for a python developer.
3. Follow me on LinkedIn and send your resume. 

100+ people would message them. Influencer would refer almost everyone to the opening, without even checking their resume once. Most likely nobody gets the interview call but the influencer gained followers. 

Bulk referral works only if the referrer knows the opening personally, just uploading 100s of resume for an opening is the same as not referring to anyone.
