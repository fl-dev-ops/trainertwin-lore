There was a senior in my team who called JIRA as JAIRA. Everyday in standup, he used to say, I'm working on this JAIRA. As it was my first job, I didn't actually know the difference, I thought it's called as JAIRA and I also practiced it. 
Days passed and there was a new manager, this person told JAIRA in standup and the manager corrected it as JEERA. Just once, everybody in the team started pronouncing it as JEERA. 

At least, in the first few years of your career, your leads and managers matter a lot. How you knowingly/unknowingly became like your parents, same might happen in job. So you need a good boss ! 

what you call it ? jeera or jaira ?
