What is frontEnd system design ?? What questions are asked in the frontend system design ? I get this question very often on LinkedIn and in my Youtube channel. I have made a detailed video that explains it and the link to the video is in the comment section.

For starters below are the things that you need to know about frontend system design. Ex: "Youtube"

1. Requirements:
-> Functional Requirements: Related to functionality, ex: video listing, video details etc.
-> Non Functional Requirements: Security, Caching, Accessibility etc.

2. Scoping: Pick some features from the above requirement.

3. Tech Stack: Which framework you chose and why ?

4. Component Architecture: Build a component UI and solve all the problems that the interviewer asks.
