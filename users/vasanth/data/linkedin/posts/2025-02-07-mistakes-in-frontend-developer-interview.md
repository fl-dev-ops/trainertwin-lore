Mistakes in frontend developer interview - Ep 2 "Don't get into arguments"
Interviewer asks a question "Guess the output of the snippet"
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------
var rate = 10
function getRate(){
    if (rate == undefined){
         var rate = 6
         return rate
    }else{
        return 10
    }
}
console.log("Rate is", getRate())
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------
Right answer to the snippet is 6. By mistake if you say 10 in the interview, and the interviewer says it is 6. Don't get into an argument with the interviewer to prove that the answer is 10. Interviewers will usually research to make sure they know about the questions that they ask in the interview. 

Instead, if you have given the wrong answer and the interviewer corrects you then amend your answer and explain the right answer. Good interviewer gives value to the explanation over the answer.
