#laidoff #layoffs2023 5 mistakes to avoid while asking for the #referrals. After seeing more than 100+ referral requests, here are the mistakes that people make while asking for a referral. Please avoid them.

1. Don't send the message "I'm X refer to me". To refer a candidate somebody needs your resume and relevant job id. 

2. Job id's can be found on the career site of respective companies. Please get the job id and share your resume with the person, who can #refer you. 

3. Don't name your resume as "resume". Name of your resume should tell who you are and your experience. Ex: "Naveen_5Yrs_Exp_Java_Developer". This will make the referral very easy. 

4. Don't just send one job id, find multiple relevant job id's and share. Because by the time they refer some job id's might get closed. 

5. While connecting with someone for referral, add a note (option available on LinkedIn web) and add title as "Looking for referral" and in the body mention your experience, skills, if laid off, highlight it.
