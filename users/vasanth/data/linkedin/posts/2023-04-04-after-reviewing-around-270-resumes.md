After reviewing around 270+ #resumes over the period of last 2 weeks, here are the most common mistakes that candidates make in their resume. 

1. Using Non-ATS (Application tracking system) resumes. Ex: using fancy fonts, multi colour, using dark backgrounds etc. If you're already from a premium company then this is fine, otherwise there is a high chance your resume might get rejected. 

2. Experienced people keeping education on top and skills at the bottom. Reverse this, your skill matters more than your education after certain experience. 

3. Having 3-4 page resumes. Since nobody has time to read 3-4 pages, I suggest sticking to one page template. 

4. Sharing photo, signature, DOB, address in the resume. Your resume is a public document, so avoid keeping all personal details from it.
