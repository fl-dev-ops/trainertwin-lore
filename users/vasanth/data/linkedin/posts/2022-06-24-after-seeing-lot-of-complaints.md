After seeing lot of complaints about my City Bengaluru, because of people recently moving here. I decided to write this post. 

1. Bengaluru in not outer Ring-road, just because it has traffic jam doesn't mean whole Bengaluru has traffic jam. 

2. Outer ring road has lot of traffic jam because of metro construction. Which on the other hand is for the larger good of all of us in the coming years.

3. Yes cab, auto fares are high, as charge is not for the distance but for the duration of travel. I don't think Ola, Uber has some special logic to increase rate for Bengaluru. It would have been same in any other city if situation was similar. (Experts from Ola, Uber can correct me)

4. Definitely it is difficult to find house near office and rent is sky rocketing. Landlords could have been quite liberal about it, but they are trying to fill the loses that they had during the lockdown. Same is happening in every other industry. So it is not just Bengaluru landlords, in every city people might have done the same. 

So, please stop complaining, I'm not saying my city is best. But every city it's own merits and demerits. Similar to job change, if your requirements are met by a new company you would change the job. Similarly move to any city if it meets your needs. If not, we always have alternatives.
