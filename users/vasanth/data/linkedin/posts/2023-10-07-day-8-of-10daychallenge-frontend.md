(Day 8 of #10dayChallenge) Frontend developer interviews answers you give v/s answers you should give. (Simple questions that most don't answer)

Question 1:  Can websites built on #reactjs be slower compared to websites built using HTML,CSS, JS
❌ Answer (Wrong answer given by most people): "No #reactjs always fast"

Question 2: What is reconciliation in #reactjs ?
✅ Answer: When state changes in a component it firstly runs a "diffing" algorithm, which identifies what has changed in the virtual DOM. The second step is reconciliation, where it updates the DOM with the results of diff.

Question 3: Don't you think the reconciliation adds additional delay to rendering ? without reconciliation some websites can load fast ?
❌ Answer: Most get confused as they have just learnt #reactjs assuming that is the best.

✅  Right Answer: "For websites that are static which have no frequent re-rendering then using #reactjs is additional overhead. You can stick to plain HTML, CSS, JS"

📖 Lesson for today: "Don't be biased towards any framework/library you use as the best. Know its pros and cons and why you have decided to use it."
