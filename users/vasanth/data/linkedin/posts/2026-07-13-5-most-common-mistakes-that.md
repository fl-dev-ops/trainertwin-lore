5 most common mistakes that people do in machine coding round. 

1. Without reading the problem completely, straight away get started with solving it. 

2. Not asking any questions to the interviewer regarding the problem, remember your questions showcase your experience.

3. Not modularising the given problem. Straight away try to solve the problem as a whole, which has a higher chance of failure.

4. Get panic in the interview, if something which was supposed to work is not working. Because of the panicness many forget the solution that they know. 

5. Series of logs and constant runs to ensure everything working fine. This proves you're not confident about your own solution.
