Why is choosing an offer hard ? 
Many times I see candidates have no idea what type of companies they want to join. They wait until they get an offer. 

1. If they have a notice period of 45-60 days, candidate thinks they can get a better offer.
2. Sometimes company will be good but not the project.
3. Sometimes both project and team will be good but there is no work life balance.
4. Everything will be good at times but that job requires relocation. 

So, I suggest that candidates make a rule before giving the interview, " Is this the company that you want to join"??. If not, at least reject the offer as soon as you get it, don't make them to wait till last day.
