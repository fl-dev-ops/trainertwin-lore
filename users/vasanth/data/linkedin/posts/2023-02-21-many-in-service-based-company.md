2. There is a flip side to this, where you may have to work late nights or early mornings and there is no extra perk for that.

3. You may get a production call at night and you need to wake up and fix it. 

I feel these companies cannot keep a punch time as they don't know when something can go down and when it happens you have to connect and fix it.
