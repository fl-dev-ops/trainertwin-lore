How I got rid of my addiction of #instagram #reels in 30 days?  I was spending around 3 hours on shorts, reels, videos across #facebook #youtube #instagram etc. I used to check my phone for almost every 10-15 minutes. If you're also someone who is doing so, read till the end.

1. Very first, identify how much time you're spending on social media. If it is less than an hour per day, it should be fine. Anything beyond 2 hours is worry-some. You can use apps like stayfree to track your usage.

2. Uninstalling the app, keeping the phone away etc. Will solve the problem temporarily. You will still have the urge to use it.

3. The solution that worked-out well for me was keeping a short term goal of non-usage. Ex: for the next 3 days, I will reduce my mobile usage to 1 hour.

4. Once you achieve it, increase it by 7 days and then 15 days and finally 30 days. After you finish each milestone, you can take a small break.

By the end of 30 days, you will notice it yourself that you are no longer addicted to it. Then if you wish you can uninstall apps or use it in a limit.
