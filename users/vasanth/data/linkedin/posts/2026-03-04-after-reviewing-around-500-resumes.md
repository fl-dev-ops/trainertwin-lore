After reviewing around 500+ resumes below is the most common mistake.


"Directly start explaining about the project. ex: I built a reusable component / I wrote a scalable API etc."

If you have also made the above mistake the here is the solution. Explain about the project in 1-2 lines. 

ex: I worked on a Swiggy user application which is used for ordering food online across 20+ cities of India..currently we have 1M+ daily active users. 

Then start your contribution. This will completely change perspective about your work from the recruiter/managers.
