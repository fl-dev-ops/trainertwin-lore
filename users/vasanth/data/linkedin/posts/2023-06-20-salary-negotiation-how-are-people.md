#salary #negotiation How are people losing offers in the HR round recently ??
I spoke to 5-6 engineers recently who cleared all the technical rounds but didn't receive the offer because of the reasons below.

1. One has 10 lakh salary and told on the HR round that he is looking for a salary of 20 lakh (100% hike that too without having any offers)

2. Others said we have 2-3 offers and am looking for a hike greater than that. Unfortunately this company was better than previous companies but they didn't receive the offer. 

There is nothing wrong in both of the above things, but current market situation is not so favourable for this. You can do it below instead.

1. Before attempting the interview, ask what is the hike that company can give, give interview only if it matches your demand. 

2. During salary negotiation ask the company standard and ask 20-30% higher than that. Don't staight away ask 100%.

3. If the current company is better than offers you have then don't highlight you have multiple offers, max you can say I have just one offer.
