Top corporate lessons that you should always remember:

1. No bayya-didi, boyfriend - girlfriend in office, keep all your relationships official. 
2. Most of the time the only reward you get for completing your task on-time is getting more tasks.
3. Whatever happens in the office keep it in the office, don't take it home and spoil your personal life. 
4. People praise, when you're leaving the company and scrutinize when you're working. Which should ideally be reversed.
5. No #manager or #teamlead is responsible for your #career. It's you, who is responsible.
6. When you want a salary hike and promotion go ahead and ask. Don't wait for someone to notice your good work and give a salary hike or promotion.
