Interviews are not fair ! they were not fair in the past or going to be fair in future ! same company same interviewer might select someone with less skilled compared to more skilled. It depends on below factors 

1. If it is a scheduled interview you have higher chance of clearing interview when compared to hiring drive. 

2. If the position is not filled for months then interview difficultly might reduce, so skilled people who took interview have lost their chance and relatively less skilled might get the opportunity. 

3. Lot of interviewers also have a bias during the interview. Many select/don't select people from their state, language etc.

No matter how skilled you are in conclusion interview clearing is a sheer luck ! what do you think ? are you been lucky in this journey?
