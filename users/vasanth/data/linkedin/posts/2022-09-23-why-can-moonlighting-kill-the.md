Why can moonlighting kill the IT industry ? After the recent layoff of 300 employees at #wipro due to moonlighting, I decided to share my thoughts on the same.

1. No matter what people say, humans are designed to work on one thing at a time. You can time slice but you cannot work on 2 things with the same focus at a time. So, if you do some moonlighting then your focus on office work will definitely reduce.

2. Humans have a usual tendency of being dissatisfied about something that they already have. Ex: Your salary, relations etc. Due to this many start focusing more on moonlighting work than the work at the company.

3. Almost all jobs in the world where you get paid comes with some amount of accountability. So, if you pick some moonlighting work along with your office work, you become accountable for the other work which will affect your office work.

4. Especially in a service company environment developing a bond with clients and working for them in private is a moral crime. Somebody gave you a job and money and you're stealing their own client, which is unacceptable.

5. Rather than spending time on moonlighting engineers can focus on up-skilling themselves and it can give better results in the long run.
