Never get interviewed by an interviewer who has less experience than you !! This might be strange for many !! but with my experience of giving interviews, if the interviewer is having more experience than you then there is a high chance you will clear the interview. If the interviewer is of the same experience as that of you then the chances would reduce further and if the interviewer is having significantly less experience than you then you're least likely to clear the interview.
This happens because whenever a junior developer gets a chance to interview the senior candidate, they always want to prove their brilliance and in quest of that, they will ask questions that are totally irrelevant to the role. So, I always ask the interviewer regarding the interviewer and if they are less experienced then I will not give the interview.
