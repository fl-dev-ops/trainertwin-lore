Don't follow a script in the interview. Being smiling, charming, less confident, over smart. Interviews are like a first date.

1. Both of you don't know each other. 

2. See what the interviewer is interested in and try to answer in that way.

3. Some interviewers are fine with you answering at surface level. In such interviews don't over explain the concepts even if you know things very well. 

4. Same goes for other cases, if the interviewer wants you to answer in detail then don't explain at a high level.

in summary keep your script dynamic. It should change as per the interviewer. Giving more mock interviews will help you with this.
