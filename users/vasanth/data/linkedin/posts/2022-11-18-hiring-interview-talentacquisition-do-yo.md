#hiring #interview  #talentacquisition  Do you have any questions for me ?? This is the last question which will be asked in most of the interviews and most candidates say "I don't have any". You are missing out on a golden opportunity here to know more about the company. 

So what should be your questions to the interviewer ?

1. If any interesting question was asked in the interview and you were unable to answer it, please ask a solution for that. (Interviewer thinks you have an interest in learning new things.)

2. What project does the team currently work on ? What are its milestones ?  (To know more about the future work)

3. Given a chance, what would you like to change in your current company/team ? (With this question you will identify what problems really exists n the team)
