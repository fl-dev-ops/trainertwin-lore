I strongly feel, recently the conflict between Kannadiga's and non Kannadiga's has increased in Bengaluru. Here is what I think, 

1. Nobody from outside of Karnataka who came here to work should be forced to learn Kannada. If they learn that's wonderful. 

2. Nobody from outside Karnataka should expect Kannadiga's to learn Hindi, If they voluntarily speak, again that's wonderful. 

3. No locals should feel so many companies present in Bengaluru because of them.

4. Nobody from outside of Karnataka, who came here to work should feel Bengaluru is so, because of them.

5. Locals even if they don't speak Hindi, should respect it. The same goes for non Kannadiga's, respect for Kannada and local culture.

If we all do this, I think we all can leave in harmony. Sometimes a lot of problems are exemplified because of politics and we will waste our time debating. 

Let me know what do you think?
