Why interviewers don't give feedback as soon as the interview is over ?

Many candidates ask for feedback immediately after the interview in the call itself. If you're too good or too bad then it's very easy to share the feedback. 

Problem is for those who stand in middle, most of candidates in my experience stand in this level. This requires sometime for interviewer to reconcile and share the result. 

so, it's better to wait for the feedback and make sure you leave a positive ending note.
