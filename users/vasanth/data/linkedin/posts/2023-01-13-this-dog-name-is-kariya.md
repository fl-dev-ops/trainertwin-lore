This dog name is "Kariya" (Blacky in Kannada). He is a street dog in my area. Every-time I feel low, the world is cruel etc, I will look at him and gain motivation. "Kariya" was born on a rainy night and it was a very difficult task to take him out of the gutter and surrender it to his mother. By birth he was an alienated kid and unhealthy. Rest of his brothers and sisters always ignored him while playing. I wondered as per the rule "Survival of fittest" Kariya should be dead soon because of his health issues.
But what happened was different, one day people from the corporation came to collect street dogs and all his brothers and sisters were taken; he somehow survived that day. Me and other neighbours treated his disease and now he rules our road !! I started questioning "Survival of the fittest really true" ??
Whichever state you life your current into, don't lose hope !! Don't worry about the people who you think are smarter than you, beautiful than you etc !! have patience and work on your skills. You will reach your goal soon.
