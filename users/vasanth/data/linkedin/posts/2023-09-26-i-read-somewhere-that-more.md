I read somewhere that more than 70% of #frontenddevelopers are not using #javascript. Do you know which language they are using ?? #typescript !!
I'm a big fan of formal programming. So, when I first started programming with #javascript I never liked it. As it was not having classes, it was not strongly typed, it was not at all behaving the way you think it will.
Then like everyone I also moved to #typescript as it was more formal and gave a feel object oriented programming, everything was structured. Back then also I had a thought that this is something that we are doing forcefully and affecting the natural flow of #javascript by adding an additional layer. 
What most people don't know is #typescript was introduced by #microsoft as their engineers were not so happy with the informal way of coding with #javascript. With their power of access to the developer community, it didn't take a lot of time to make typescript a standard.
Now, I'm hearing the news that a lot of companies are migrating back to #javascript because of the slowness caused by the typescript. So, if you're starting a new project don't have a blind decision to pick typescript. Check your requirement and if it is really mandatory only then you pick it.
