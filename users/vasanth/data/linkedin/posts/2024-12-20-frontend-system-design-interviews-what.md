Frontend system design interviews, what to expect? 

I will divide it into 2 parts. Less than 5 years experience and more than 5 years.

Less than 5 years experienced questions will usually be from a sub topic of what company works. Ex: if you apply for a B2C company then.

1. Given a site how to configure the routing?
2. How do you route so that SEO is taken care. 
3. Some general questions like how to handle git, how to improve performance, caching etc. 

Beyond 5 years, usually for a senior role. 
1. A system is picked and in-depth questions will be asked. 
2. Ex: Auto complete system: how do you efficiently make api call ? how do you cache the results ? which caching library is preferred?

whether your junior / senior role, keep learning system design, not just for interview, it will help you to grow.
