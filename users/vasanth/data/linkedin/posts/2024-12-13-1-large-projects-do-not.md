1. Large projects do not require @redux to manage the state.
2. Large projects do not require nosql to manage the database.
3. Large projects do not require micro frontend/monorepo architecture, they can work well in monolithic. 

The point is, do not have a bias over the technology and use it to solve the problem. Instead know the technology, use it when needed. Just because somebody else is using it, we don't have to use it.
