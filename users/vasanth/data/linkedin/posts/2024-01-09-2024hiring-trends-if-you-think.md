#2024hiring  #trends If you think #itjobs market will be better in 2024 then this post will disappoint you. With my interaction with a lot of industry experts, below are the hiring trends you can expect from 2024.

1. Most popular startups were not profitable last year and it continues this year. Because of that, the hiring in #startup sector will be less.
2. Most big companies are not willing to spend more money onto innovation which is the main segment for mass hiring. So, this segment also suffers in terms of hiring. 
3. Even the companies which actually have the necessary quality engineers are not hiring them because they don't know what #ai can do in the coming days. 

So, my advice would be, if the current job is stable, despite the hike that you get this year, you can stick to the job for another year. Make the switch only if it is inevitable.
