5 things to avoid in frontend developer interview.

1. Jumping to answer without listening question fully. 

2. Trying to deviate interviewer by repeatedly saying I haven't worked on it 

3. Just saying one pointer for a question and stop the answer. Ex: how to improve performance of the website. Instead of saying all valid points, just saying one point. 

4. Not having solid understanding of the current project, which will create immediate negative impression about you. 

5. Not able to convince interviewer with your answer. 

easy solution to all of it is giving as many mock interviews as possible. Try to find someone who you think has more knowledge than you and take mock interviews from them.
