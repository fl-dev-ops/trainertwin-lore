I was watching interview of The BarberShop W. with Aman Gupta ( Boat CEO). Such a good interview, where Aman Gupta spoke about his initial struggles, the growth story of Boat , shark tank days etc. 

One part that intrigued me a lot was when Shantanu asked Aman 

"What is your relationship with money ?"

I was expecting Aman would say all I'm doing is for money, instead Aman justified why money is essential but afterwards he said, I do things that gives me Mazza (I think it's relatable to happiness) and he didn't mind saying when Boat stops giving me that Mazza I might quit Boat too. He also gave examples when he was making good money in other firms and left them because he was not getting that Mazza.

He felt like a philosopher to me. I think if we continue to do what gives us happiness all other things like money, fame etc will follow. 

What do you think ? Is Money eternal or happiness ?
