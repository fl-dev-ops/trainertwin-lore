#stopBlamingIndianGovernment 
January 16th is when vaccination was open in India for people with age group of 60+, from March 1st vaccination was open for 45+, total percentage of people who vaccinated two times in India is 1.7%. Even after so many days vaccination was available, that too for free, very few youths have taken initiation to vaccinate their parents & relatives. 
Now second wave has hit India, remember friends, countries which are developed like USA, France, Germany, Switzerland, Italy etc  have also suffered very badly during second wave. There was oxygen shortage in New York and due to unavailability of beds patients were slept on road.
We are the people who neglected corona and travelled like anything, remember there is no city in the world which has capacity to admit 25000+ patients every day (Average number of cases in Bengaluru everyday).  Production of oxygen is very tedious process, now there is surge for demand, over night no country can set up thousands of plants to generate it. 
Yes government could have prepared well, but there is no point blaming them now, no one can protect us, we have to take care of us and our country. As a nation we have overcome lots of difficult times, we will pass this phase also, be strong, avoid going outside, stay healthy.
