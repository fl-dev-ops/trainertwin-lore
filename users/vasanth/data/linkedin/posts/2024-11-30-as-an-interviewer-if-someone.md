As an interviewer, If someone says, they are always 100% sure at the end of an interview to select or reject a candidate then they are lying. 

1. Saying 100% no is very easy. When a candidate do not meet the requirements, it is easy to reject someone. 
2. When a candidate is extremely good, it's easy to shortlist them.
3. The problem is with people who are somewhere in the middle. Let's say out of rating 5, neither can give them 3 which pushes them for next round; nor 2 which will reject them. 

It is tough to make a decision when the candidate is average. Let me know, how do you proceed with someone whom you can't reject/select ?
