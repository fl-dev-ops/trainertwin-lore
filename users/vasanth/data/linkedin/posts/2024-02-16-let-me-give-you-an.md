Let me give you an important interview concept that is used by the interviewers. That is called "length and depth".

In this concept the interviewer will ask you questions on a bunch of topics and ask things in detail only on some of the concepts. 
Ex: On frontend development, the interviewer will ask basic questions on hoisting, closure, promise etc and ask in-depth questions on hoisting. If you answer it well, they may not dig in-depth of the promise. But if you don't answer well on hoisting, they will ask more about closures and then promise. This happens in a loop for 2-3 times. If you answer well for 2 of 3 loops then the interviewer will have a positive opinion about you, if you finish 3 loops negatively then mostly you're done for the #interviewer.

Remember this cycle in mind and try to answer well in an interview. Let me know in the comments section, have you faced this technique in an interview.
