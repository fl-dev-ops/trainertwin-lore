One of the common mistakes that are made during system design interviews by both #frontend and #backend developers are, trying to clone the system instead of designing it.

Let me give you an example: Let's say your in an #systemdesign interview the interviewer asks

Question 1: "Which protocol does Youtube live streaming use ? "

Many candidates feel sorry for not knowing what protocol does #youtube really uses and they get nervous in the interview.

Instead of feeling sorry, whatever the protocol that you know and right fit for Youtube live streaming, mention that. Ex: HLS or WebRtc.

Some interviewers might not end the conversation just there. They will continue and ask,

Question 2: "#youtube actually uses RTMP as it's protocol and you didn't name it"

In such scenarios be bold and say
"I'm not sure what #youtube is using. As long as they officially don't mention it anywhere, rest all are assumptions and if I design Youtube I will use HLS or SRT and these are my reasons for the same."
