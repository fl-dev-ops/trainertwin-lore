(Day 2 of #10dayChallenge) Frontend developer interviews answers you give v/s answers you should give.

Question 1: Tell me about your last project ?
✅  Answer: I worked on a super app similar to #paytm for the middle east. Where users can recharge their phone, pay bills, scan and pay etc.

Question 2: That's wonderful, which frontend architecture your using for this ?
❌ Answer: (Most will not be able to give an answer to this.) I have not followed any specific architecture, just developed the UI as per the design. (Wrong answer)

✅  Right answer: "Most super apps like paytm will use a famous architecture called micro frontend which is relatable to micro services in backend. Rarely some apps like Swiggy use mono repo. So your answer should be micro frontend or monorepo"

📖 Lesson for today: "UI development is not building screens, get a design mindset. Before starting developing any big module, document it and it will help you to grow and build a scalable application".
