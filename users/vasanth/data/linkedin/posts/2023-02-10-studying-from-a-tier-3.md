Studying from a Tier 3 college is a sin ? I was reading a post from Harshit Srivastava yesterday where he motivated a Tier 3 college graduate over 1:1. Here is my take on it.

If you're from Tier 3/4 college first understand below points

1. Though it feels sad, at the start of your #career you and #engineer from #tier1 college are not the same. Definitely #tier1 #engineer is #superior.

2. To get into #tier1 college, students might have worked really hard in 12th and again worked hard during placements to get into a Premium company.

3. Whenever a #company is #premium it expects premiumness from their #engineers also, that's the reason why top companies hire from top colleges.

But if you're from Tier 3/4 why do you need not to worry in the long run ?

1. Career is not a quick race, it's a marathon. In 5-10 years if you have a #potential you can be paid as #equal as that of #engineer from #iit or #niit.

2. Don't compare yourself to anyone in the beginning, just focus on #upskilling, you will beat everyone who is ahead of you.

3. Be open to work for lesser salary and unbranded companies. You can learn well here and later look out for good opportunities.
