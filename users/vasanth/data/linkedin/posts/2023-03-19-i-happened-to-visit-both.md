I happened to visit both State Bank of India and HDFC Bank yesterday. Due to the same concern of changing the address. It's been years since I ever went to banks, but with my past experience State Bank of India always was "Goto counter 3 and then 2 and then manager then get Aadhar and PAN photocopy, finally this is our lunch time etc". I was afraid the same thing will happen today also.

But the manager from Nagashetti Halli branch, Bengaluru of  State Bank of India was so polite. He welcomed every customer whom he was interacting. He used to help them with paper and pen when any letter was supposed to be written, even the staff had very good hospitality. Not just this branch I see in general the way government banks treat customers has improved a lot.

HDFC Bank treated me usual with customer friendly behaviour. They were even ready to offer coffee or tea. Only problem is, their associates will bombard you with insurance, investment schemes etc. 

Interesting part is my account in State Bank of Indiat transferred within the same day whereas HDFC Bank it might take upto 4 days. It's high time to break the stereotype about public sector banks not treating customers properly.
