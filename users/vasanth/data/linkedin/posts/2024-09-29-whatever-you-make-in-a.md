Whatever you make in a freelancing project, you can make 5-10 times of it just by switching to a good company. So if you're in your initial years of #career don't waste too much of your time on freelancing projects, instead spend your time to up-skill and make a switch to a good company. Because in most freelancing projects

1. You don't learn a lot, as completion is more important that quality.
2. You will not be paid on time in most of the cases.
3. You will be pressured to complete it after your office hours. 

Instead pick a few companies you want to join, start connecting with people from those companies, keep up-skilling yourself and crack those companies. Your life will change fully.
