When I started my #career I had a manager who always used to wear US POLO shirts and pants. I used to admire him and told myself that after I start earning well I will also wear US POLO shirts and pants. With God Grace I started earning quite well where I could afford to wear brands like US POLO.

1. But, the better I started earning, my aspirations towards material life started reducing.

2. The more I earned more simple I become. Also, I realised wearing branded clothes will not make you a brand.

now I'm in a state even if you give me your used shirt which fits me, I don't mind wearing it. The intention of writing this post is, unless you shift your life aspirations from materialistic to non-materialistic, living a happy life becomes difficult.
