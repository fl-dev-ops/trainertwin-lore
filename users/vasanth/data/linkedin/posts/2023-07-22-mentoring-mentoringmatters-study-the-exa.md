#mentoring #mentoringmatters  "Study the exam before studying for the exam" is a common statement among people preparing for competitive exams. Same way "Understand the course before paying lakhs together to enrol to it". 
So many students/professionals reach out to me for mentorship. Mostly I do it for free. Every time it is not possible so I ask them to set up a meeting with me on #topmate. They will be afraid to pay 500/1000 Rs/- for a session with me or other mentors. Finally, some representatives from ed-tech firms reach out to them and these professionals enroll in their courses by paying lakhs. 
My suggestion is, before paying lakhs for a course, consult someone whom you think can advise you well. High paid courses are not required for all and definitely needed for some. Most of the time you cannot asses it by yourself, it is assessed by somebody who has more experience than you. 

"Right mentoring at the right time can save years for you. So, take mentorship from someone who has already passed the state that you're currently in".

FYI: I have not linked my #topmate link anywhere, as it is not a post to promote myself, but a post to highlight the importance of mentoring before enrolling for any course.
