There are times where candidates do not know the answer to any of the questions which I asked. I will make 2 choice in such situations.

1. End the interview there itself ! 
2. Give an option to candidates to say some concepts which they know very well.

I usually opt for the second option, I ask the candidate can you say some concept that you know very well ? and I ask questions around that. But the probability of a candidate getting selected in the 2nd approach is very limited.
