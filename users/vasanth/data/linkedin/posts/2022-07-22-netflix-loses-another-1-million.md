Netflix loses another 1 million subscribers in just three months. 

World's biggest OTT giant Netflix Inc has lost around 1 million subscribers in 3 months. The company currently has 221 million global paid subscribers.
This is the second quarter where Netflix lost a significant number of users since the beginning of the year. I think it is not a big problem for Netflix because of the reasons below. 

1. Out of 221M users losing 1M users is not a significant number.

2. Due to inflation spread all across the world, survival is the first major aspect of human life and not entertainment. So, many might have not continued their subscription of Netflix. When the situation is back to normal, Netflix will roar again. 

3. Most important reason is Netflix is the costliest OTT platform. There are many other platforms who are offering good content at lesser cost. But Netflix is an old player; they know how to run a sustainable business.  All these new players might attract users by giving heavy discounts but they might not be able to continue in the race for long run due to negative cash flow. 

4. At this point Netflix can acquire many new players and can dominate the market once again.
