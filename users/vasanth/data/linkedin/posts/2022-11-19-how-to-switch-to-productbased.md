How to switch to #productbased company from #servicebased company ? This is one of the very common questions that I get on all of my #socialmedia  #platforms.

1. First of all, don't aim at changing jobs to a product based company, have an aim of changing to a good company that has a good #workculture and interesting #work .

2. There are a lot of #productbased companies, where after joining you realise #product  is in maintenance state and there are no other products to work on.

3. There are two types of #product #based companies, one which is heavily dependent on #funding and they burn a lot of cash on each order, another is self sustained one. While joining a #productbased company if it is of #first category then make sure they have good funding in recent rounds but I would prefer the second type.
