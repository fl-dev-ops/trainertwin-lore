Thumb rule for fixing prod bug: Involve only those many people who are required to fix the bug, not anyone beyond that.

1. Many developers get panic and involve all possible leaders in the call.

2. Leaders also want to support the team and they jump onto the call.

3. Instead of fixing the bug a lot of time will be spent on explaining the feature and bug to leaders, because leaders wouldn't know all features by themselves.
