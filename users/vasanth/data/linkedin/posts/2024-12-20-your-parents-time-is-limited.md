Your parents time is limited 😢 realise it before it's too late. 

1. I know you're busy, you have meetings till late night. 

2. I know you're busy on weekends  because of the office/ household work. 

3. I know you have a lot of tension, living your life in a metro city and not getting time to call your parents. 

4. I know your sending money to your home on-time without fail. 

But, most parents do not need your money, their life needs are minimal. They need your time, they need you. Try to spend as much quality time as possible with your parents before it's too late. I know many, who have regrets for life time for not doing it. 

let me know, how many days in a year your actually spend with your parents??
