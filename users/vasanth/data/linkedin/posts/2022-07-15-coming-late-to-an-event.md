Coming late to an event is not always bad.
 
My chemistry teacher used to say this story. A student used to come late to first period all the time. (No his father was not suffering from any disease or his mother was working hard to serve the family. 😂😂) He was coming late because of laziness. Teacher used to write all home work on board everyday. After coming late, whatever on the board, he used to note down and solve them. This was going on for some time. One day after noting down the questions, student tried solving it at night, but it was quite difficult. He couldn't ask teacher as teacher was not happy about him, coming late. He tried hard for many days and one day solved all the problems. One day he was little early to first period and showed it to teacher. Teacher and whole class was shocked to see this, as teacher had written some questions on board, that was never solved in the past. So, there is nothing wrong is breaking stereotype as long as you think you’re in right path.
