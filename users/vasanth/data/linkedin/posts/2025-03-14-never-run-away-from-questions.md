Never run away from questions in the interview. Ex: When people explain about their past work they explain about the UI they built etc. I ask "was there an option to upload the image ? if so, how do you handle a scenario when and 100MB image uploaded? " 

Candidate answers by saying I didn't work on it or I don't fully remember it. In this case, your trying to run away from the problem. Never do this in interview, even if you have not worked, be aware of basics and explain it in the interview.

Don't expect interviewer change the question, because you forgot something? let me know, what do you feel ?
