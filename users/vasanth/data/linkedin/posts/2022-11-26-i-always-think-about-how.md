I always think about how #failure perception changes over the years, below are my child failure personalities.

1. "My landlord": Who had just enough rental income to meet his family needs. He never worked hard, he used to sit on a couch in Veranda and talk to neighbours, roam in the town etc.

2. "Our neighbour at grandfather's house": Compared to my landlord, he was quite rich, he had farming lands, plots etc. He also never worked hard, most of the time, he also used to spend sitting outside the house and talking to people passing by. He always wore the same color shirt. 

3. Senior developer in a service company: When I saw engineers who were 45+ years of age and never worked in MAANG or any product companies, I used to think they had wasted their career.

I don't think like that anymore, "They all found what is peace for them, and did that. They were happy doing what they wanted to do. We are not meant to do hard work, do anything that makes you happy."
"World will forget every great achiever one day, it's ok not to do something great, but make sure you leave a great life with what you're doing.".
