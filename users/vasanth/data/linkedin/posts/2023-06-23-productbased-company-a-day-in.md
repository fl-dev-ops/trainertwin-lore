#productbased #company   A day in the life of a product company #softwareengineer 😁
1. Start the day by standup and mostly you will have no update, just give the same update as of yesterday in a different way.😧
2. Check all the meetings for the day, out of 8 hours you plan to work at least 2 hours you will have meetings. You will plan the rest of the day for your work.
3. As soon as you start working, either your junior or stockholders from different teams will ping you and you need to jump to a call with them as it is your responsibility. 😭
4. Lunch -> Meetings -> Instagram -> Little rest you will reach the end of the day. 🤭
5. Lastly, very important, Just before you fall asleep, prepare a story to tell in tomorrow's standup. 😂
