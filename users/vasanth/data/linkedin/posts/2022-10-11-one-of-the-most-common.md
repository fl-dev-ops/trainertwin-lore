One of the most common interview questions in #reactjs interview is "How will you render a huge list with thousands of elements" Most common answer that the candidate gave to this is "pagination". But it's not that straight forward.

Let's take an example of the Youtube comment section.

1. Let's say you have selected newest to old as a filter in Youtube comments and when you open a video all the newest comments loaded on top followed by old comments.

2. When you scroll to the end of the current loaded comments you will try to fetch the new comments. Technically you're trying to load older comments, but by then a lot of new comments might have been added by other users ? How will you handle this case ?

3. Let's take another example of pagination, how will you handle the scenario when loaded contents are updated. Ex: Loaded Youtube comments might be updated, will you update the comments or just load new comments during pagination ?
