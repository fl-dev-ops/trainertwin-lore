It was the beginning of my #career and there was a production issue. No matter what user was unable to place order. It was just myself and another backend developer. As usual everything worked fine on the development environment. So the backend developer told me to restart the pods, as there was no problem on the frontend I said, "as you wish".
In the process of restarting, unknowingly he deleted the order table itself. We went to fill the whole on the tank instead we ended by blasting the tank itself. 

As it was a startup our CTO himself joined the call and we admitted the mistake. I still remember what he said ''let's show a valid error message on the website and let's create the order table and backup the data". He didn't scolded us, no shouting, not speaking about business loss. Just did what is relevant for the moment. I learnt act relevant that day.
