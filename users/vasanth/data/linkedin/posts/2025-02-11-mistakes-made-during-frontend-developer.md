Mistakes made during frontend developer interview ep-6. "not Giving quantifiable answers"

Interviewer: What is your contribution to the current application that you're working on?

Answer: I improved the performance or the application significantly. 

Here improving the performance is hard to quantify!  so do not give such examples. Always explain pointers that are easily quantifiable. Ex: I am reducing the loading time of the application from 1s to 250ms.

how you answer in the interview? in quantifyable terms or generic terms ?
