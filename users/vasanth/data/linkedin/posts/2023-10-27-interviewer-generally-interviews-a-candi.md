#interviewer generally interviews a candidates for about 60-90 minutes. Here are 5 things that I do in that short duration to influence the #interviewer ! 

1. During your introduction itself, clearly highlight what you know and what you don't !! Otherwise the interviewer can ask questions on everything in the resume.

2. I follow a 60:40 rule while guesstimating an answer. If I'm at least 60% sure about the question then I will take the risk of answering. Anything less than that, I will say "I don't have a complete idea about it".

3. Never create an impression you're poor at basics, there is no hack to overcome this. Read the basics thoroughly, so that you can create a good opinion about you.

4. Avoid blabbering as much as possible. Have a clear communication. If you think you spoke multiple sentences in bits and pieces summarise them in one sentence so that #interviewer is clearly knows what you said. 

5. When the interviewer asks "Do you have any questions" at the end, I will always have some interesting questions related tech, work, culture etc. that will create a positive impression about me.
