If you're trying for a job for a few months and not getting any interview calls below are my advice for you.

1. Have few variants of your resume. Depending on the JD share the relevant one.

2. For the job where you feel your a right fit, just ask ChatGPT to update your resume according to JD and use that resume.

3. Try to reach out to founders of startup in a unique way by recording bugs in their product and sharing video with them. Or make a video or yourself explaining why they should hire you.

4. Make as many connections as possible on LinkedIn with your friends and College seniors who will help you to get referral.
