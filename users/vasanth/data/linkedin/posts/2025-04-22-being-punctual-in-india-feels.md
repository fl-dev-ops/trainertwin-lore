Being punctual in India feels like being the only person clapping at the wrong time.

I’m that person who joins every meeting 2 minutes early. I believe in respecting time — both mine and others’.

But over the years, I’ve realized one thing:
Punctuality is a solo sport here.

You wait in the office cab — others join 10 minutes late, sipping chai, no “sorry,” not even eye contact.
You’re at the bus stop on time — the bus shows up fashionably late like a Bollywood hero in the climax.
You plan a 6 PM meetup — your friends show up at 6:30, say “just started from home” at 6:15.

At this point, I’m convinced that in India, “on time” means “why are you in such a hurry, bro?”

Still, I try to stay disciplined — though sometimes I wonder if I’m just playing a game no one else signed up for.

What’s your take — punctual or proudly late?


Vasanth Bhat
