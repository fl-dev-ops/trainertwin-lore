9/10 who ask for referral don't get it. Do you know why ? 

1. Referal is a mercy and not a right. 
2. Originally it was never meant to refer strangers, it was meant for known contacts and sectors like banking are particular about it. 
3. Not helping the referer to refer you:  If you share your resume and ask someone to refer, what's your expectation? 

They will check your resume →goto career portal →look for relevant opportunities for you then → refer you ? Almost no one does that. 

Instead, write a short message, saying why your good fit for the opportunity, share few relevant job IDs. Have a follow-up after 1-2 days.
