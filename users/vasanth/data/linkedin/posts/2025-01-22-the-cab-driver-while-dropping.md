The cab driver while dropping off asked a question "Sir, how does it feel when flying up in the air ?? " with too much curiosity. I said it feels like "Same as how to sit in a train, just with less noise".  Yesterday, while returning from the airport, I asked the cab driver, "How many airport trips do you do in a month". He said "on an average I do around 15 trips". He continued and told me that "it might be more than 10 years, I'm driving a cab in Bengaluru and have done 1000's of airport pick and drop. But, I never went inside the airport ! I never flew to any place !

Same answer was given by many airport staff who work in the airport cleaning and maintenance department. Even flight crew who help in luggage pickup, drop off, who see 100's of flights in a day might have never travelled in flight. 

Somehow, I feel, I must do a charity this year, where I take a few people who always had a dream to fly at least once in their life and couldn't do it ? What do you think ? 

Vasanth Bhat
