If you're a front-end developer and want to pursue companies like #google ?where DSA is a crucial skill and you never solved serious DSA in your career ? not understanding where to get started ? Here is my advice.

1. Goto MDN documentation, learn about set, map and array functions to splice, slice, reduce, fill and few other, same goes for strings. 

2. Goto leetcode -> create an account -> Problems section

3. Select Array filter with easy difficulty problem. Most easy problems will look difficult for you. But, hang on, try to solve them by Brute force first. 

4. After you solve about 30 easy array problems, your confidence will reach a state where you can solve any easy array problems. 

5. That is when you switch to strings, followed by linked list and trees. 

6. Without touch basing easy problems of LL and Trees, don't attempt medium difficulty problems of array and strings. As many problems there involve recursion, DFS, BFS etc, which will be hard to solve without knowing trees. 

By the time you reach Step 6, you will figure out your own strategy to proceed further. Let me know what your DSA strategy is in the comments section.
