In a forest there was a crow and a rabbit. Rabbit used to live under a hollow space of a tree and a crow used to live on top of the tree. Everyday rabbits used to observe crow and it used to just sit on a branch, shout and shit. On the other hand, the rabbit was trying hard to find food. So, Rabbit decided, let's leave like crows for at least one day. It also came out of its space and sat near the tree and started shouting and didn't do anything. It felt so happy for a while, it increased shouting, in no time a fox came and ate the rabbit.

Moral of the story:
"If you want to just and do shit, you have to be at top of hierarchy"
