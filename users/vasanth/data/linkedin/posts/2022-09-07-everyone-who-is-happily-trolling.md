Everyone who is happily trolling Bengaluru for its rain and poor infrastructure, here is what I need to say. Let's be honest, this is open to question to all!! From whichever metro city you're in India, just think once, is your city capable of handling the rain that Bengaluru faced in last week. (I'm from western ghats of Karnataka, if Bengaluru rain comes to my native, even it cannot tolerate it).
 
1. As far as I read it's the highest rainfall in Bengaluru after 90 years. In a simple way, previously Bengaluru had such rainfall before I and you were born. All constructions might have been planned with the last 10 years track record and not 100 years.
 
2. People who are trolling the need for learning swimming in Bengaluru to go to office, ask yourself, since how many years you're in the city ? Have you faced a similar situation before ? In my 10 year outer ring road journey, it's the first time I've seen such a massive amount of water on the road for a few days continuously.
 
3. If mother nature gets angry, nobody can survive. Even the world's most developed countries are facing floods and droughts. I don't deny the fact that Bengaluru's infrastructure could have been much better, but even if it's the best, there is still a chance to face problems like this, if nature goes beyond all the calculations.
 
4. When Chennai, Mumbai, Kerala were flooded everybody shared posts like save our place and contribute to relief fund. But when Bengaluru gets a problem you're trolling it ? Is this the respect one gives to the place which gave you everything ?
 
5. And I feel sad and surprised when people use this opportunity to market some other city where IT companies need to move. Guys be practical, just Manyata tech park has 150,000+ employees. Every city looks good with fewer people, problems start when it gets crowded. you're thinking, your city is bigger than nature ? If it can happen to Bengaluru, it can happen to any city.
 
6. Finally, I don't deny the fact that Bengaluru MLA's, MP's might have acted well, water clogging in India's silicon valley is a shame for all. But everybody who is trolling, just introspect, are you contributing to the betterment of the city in any way by trolling it ? Are you complaining about the problem anywhere ? At least you know who your MLA and Corporator are ? Did you try calling them ? If you're someone who decided to live in the city, do you have voting rights ? Did you choose your leader ?
 
Now the city is in a difficult phase, let's support it whatever way we can. If possible, share food, medicine, shelter to anyone who is in need. I'm 100% sure, we will pass this tough phase, let's make the right decision in the future to avoid this.
