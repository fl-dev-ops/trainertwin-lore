Don't add an open to work badge in your LinkedIn profile. In the current scenario just by adding that badge will reduce your connection request acceptance by a large percentage.

1. If you add open to work and send a connection request then people would think you're connecting for a job and if they don't know any openings, then no point of accepting connection requests.

2. According to me, open to work badge was useful only during COVID time, where there was shortage of talent and it made your discovery easy for recruiters.

but it's no longer the case, so I suggest not to put open to work badge. What are your thoughts on same ?
