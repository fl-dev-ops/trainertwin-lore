I'm a firm believer of setting up resolutions for the new year. Usually either on Google Keep's check box or I write it physically on paper. I keep checking it and as and when I complete the things I mark tick against it. 
That doesn't mean everything we write happens, nature always has it's own course. But that doesn't stop us from achieving what we want to ! So, I suggest everyone who believes in themselves to open any online editor of your choice (Google Keep is my favourite). Add all entries that you want to achieve in this year. 
If there is good grace during the year you can cross check how many got completed and how many are pending !

Do you have a practice of setting resolutions ? Let me know in the comments section.
