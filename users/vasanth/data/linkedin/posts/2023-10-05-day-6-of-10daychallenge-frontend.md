💡Question for you: undefined and null are deep copied or shallow copied ?

Question 1: Explain the difference between deep copy and shallow copy in #javascript.
✅  Answer: "Shallow Copy stores the references of objects to the original memory address. Deep copy stores copies of the object's value." (Most give right answer to this)

Question 2: Why do you think objects and arrays are shallow copied ? whereas primitive types like number, strings are deep copied ?
❌ Answer: I haven't encountered anyone so far, who gave the right answer to this. 

✅ Right answer: The rationale behind deep copy and shallow copy is, objects and arrays generally take up more space. If we deep copy them then it will require more space in memory. So the default approach is copy be reference, if needed then the developer can make a deep copy of it.
