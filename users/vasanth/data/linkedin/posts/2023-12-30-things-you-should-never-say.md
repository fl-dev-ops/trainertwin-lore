Things you should never say on LinkedIn

1. Commented interested for a job openings post.
2. Sending 'Hi' to people and waiting for their reply. 
3. Sharing all your personal updates on LinkedIn. 

By commenting interested nobody will ask for your resume and refer. After saying your expecting someone reply Hi, instead just say what you want from that person. If you keep sharing personal updates when #hiringmanagers see your profile you may not be projected as professional, so avoid it.
