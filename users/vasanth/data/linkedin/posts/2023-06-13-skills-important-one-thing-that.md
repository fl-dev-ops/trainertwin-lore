"Asking what we want"

Lot of kids play in front of my house  and one day 2 kids came and asked "Uncle give me the racket". As they kept putting it inside the gate and they asked us usually, I asked "where is it ? ". One kid replied "we don't have a racket and we need what you have". (Earlier me and my brother used to play shuttle and we had stopped playing it once and there were many rackets which were unused). As the kids asked we gave it until they asked, it never occurred to me !!

Same happens in career: 

1. We want a good hike but we feel what my boss thinks about me ? and we don't ask 
2.We want a promotion but we feel what my boss thinks about me ? and we don't ask 
3. We want to work on a new and interesting project but we don't say it, until it is given to somebody else. 

Especially if you're a fresher, "You're the boss of your #career, unless you ask what you want, you rarely get it."

What do you think ? should we wait for somebody to notice us and do something for us and we ask what we want ?
