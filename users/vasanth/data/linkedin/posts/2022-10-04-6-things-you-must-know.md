6 things you must know before relocating to Bengaluru. As many companies are becoming offline and asking employees to relocate to Bengaluru, here are some tips from native Bengalurian who lived in the city for more than decade.

1. Rental amounts across the outer ring road have soared up significantly in the last 3-4 months. They are making up for the loss of corona. So, if you don't have any friends, preferably join a P.G and understand the city. Later, I passively look for a new flat after making some friends.

2. If you're a morning person you can also find good houses at comparatively lesser rental at adjacent areas of outer ring road like Ramurthy Nagar, Domlur, Kasturi Nagar etc. You can start early from these places to avoid traffic and save some money.

3. Bengaluru has one of India's best local transport systems, throughout the day you will find good number AC/Non AC buses. Take a monthly pass and you're sorted.

4. Don't worry too much about the traffic, every city has its own pros and cons, if you can start early and leave early from the office, traffic is really not a problem.

5. Don't just stick to your native food, Bengaluru is a hub of all the culture, you can enjoy national and international food here. Give a new try every weekend.

6. Finally it's a city with magic weather. I can confidently say one of the best weather in all the Metro cities of India. Enjoy it, stay positive towards your goal. The city of opportunities will never disappoint you.
