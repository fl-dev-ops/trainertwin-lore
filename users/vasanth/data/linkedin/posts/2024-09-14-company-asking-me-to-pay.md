Company asking me to pay 50000 Rs/- as a deposit, before giving the offer letter ? shall I join the company by paying 50000 Rs/- ? A member from my Telegram Messenger group who is a fresher having this doubt. Some among you also might have got such offers, here is my take on it. 

1. Outright rejection of such offers is easy. 
2. But, I know many small companies who train freshers and after they get trained, they leave the company in less than a year causing loss to the company.
3. So, some genuine small companies also ask for a deposit. Few other companies ask for original marks cards. 
4. I suggest, try to negotiate with the company, can you work for less salary/no salary for a couple of months, instead of paying 50k ? 
5. Same goes with marks cards, can they give a company letterhead signed by the company director, that they have taken the marks card ?

Definitely this is applicable for those who are unable to find any job and trying for years and more. Have you got any such offers ? what is your take on it ?
