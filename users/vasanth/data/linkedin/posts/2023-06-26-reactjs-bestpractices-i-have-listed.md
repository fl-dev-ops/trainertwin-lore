#reactjs #bestpractices I have listed 7 best practices that are generally practiced in most of the projects that I have worked on. 

1. Use React.Memo: Though many are aware it's good to use React.Memo in specific use cases most don't use it. It can really improve the performance of your functional component, if you use it right. 

2. Use Fragments: Instead of adding unnecessary div's just to enclose the JSX, use fragments, unlike div, it will reduce the view hierarchy.  

3. Use enums instead of string/number: Don't compare values directly, have a constant or enums and refer from it. This will help for code reusability and maintainability.
