Be wise before attempting something very hard. I appeared for CAT(MBA entrance exam) 2020 along with 185000+ aspirants. I secured a percentile of 85. As you all know, this is not sufficient to get into any IIM's !! Here is why I decided not to attempt the CAT second time. 

1. Unlike UPSC, MBA is not a destination, it's just a journey. 

2. When I attempted the CAT, I already had around 5 years of experience. The later I clear the exam the greater money I would lose in terms of salary. 

3. With a full time job, preparing for competitive exams like CAT was very difficult. Even if I give it a second time, I am sure I will fall short of time. 

As it was not a main goal of my life, I decided not to pursue MBA. The reason for writing this is, you should know when to quit in life. Working for the same goal with no high prospects will ruine your time and energy.
