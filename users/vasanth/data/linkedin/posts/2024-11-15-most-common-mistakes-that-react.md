Most common mistakes that React and #frontend developers do in the interview. 

1. Low conceptual clarity and try to guess the output. Ex: Why is JS a weakly typed programming language ? Candidate thinks they know the answer to this and try to guess which goes against them in the interview. 

2. Not aware of definition: Though bookish definition is not expected, some basic definition should be known. For ex: In hoisting explanation if you don't include appears to move to top of the block, instead say moves to the top of the block, the meaning is fully different. 

3. Blabbering: In technical interviews give answers to the point. Don't make the interviewer pick an answer from your 10 line explanation. 

4. Don't hesitate to skip a few questions: Skipping a few questions is better when compared to attempting questions and giving wrong answers.
