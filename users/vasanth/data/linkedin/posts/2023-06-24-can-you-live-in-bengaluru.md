Can you live in Bengaluru for 30000 Rs/- ? One of the common questions that I receive especially from #freshers who got their job and planning to move to #bengaluru.
My answer is, if you can live with that much money in any other metro cities then you can surely live in #bengaluru also. But it largely depends on your lifestyle. If you're someone who wants to go out every weekend and wants to experience the royal life of #bengaluru then it's certainly not possible.

1. There are a lot of PG's across outer ring-road(area with a lot of IT companies) which costs between 9K to 15K (for 2 sharing) depending upon the amenities that will take care of food, laundry and wifi and other needs .

2. Next major expense is traveling, if your company provides a free cab that's a plus, if not, buy a monthly pass for BMTC (local bus) and most of your traveling needs will be taken care of.

3. What is life without having fun ? rather going out every weekend you can go 1-2 times a week, there will be options meeting your budget. Go and enjoy.

If you're open to hustle and adjust in the beginning of your career you can certainly save 5-10 thousand Rs/- with your 30000 Rs/- salary in Bengaluru. In fact most people who earn in lakhs in Bengaluru started their career around 30000Rs/- only.
