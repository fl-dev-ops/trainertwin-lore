#interview #tip  One of the most underappreciated skills in interviews is the ability to comprehend questions effectively. For those unfamiliar with this concept, I encourage you to read through this post in its entirety.
Comprehension skills, in simple terms, refer to how swiftly you can grasp the questions posed by the interviewer.
For example, consider a query like: "What is the difference between useEffect with no dependency array and a self-invoking function, during the initial rendering of a component in React JS."
Upon encountering such a question for the first time, it's natural to feel a bit confused. However, it's advisable to control yourself from asking the interviewer to repeat the question multiple times. Continuously seeking clarification may lead the interviewer to perceive a lack of understanding, possibly resulting in frustration.
Therefore, it's crucial to hone your ability to comprehend questions promptly. This entails swiftly mapping out the components of the question in your mind. If you find that you're not fully grasping the question, take a moment to reflect and see if clarity emerges. Only if you're genuinely unable to comprehend the question should you consider asking the interviewer for clarification a second time.
