If you look at the #layoffs keenly most #layoffs have happened on those teams which are not directly generating revenue for the company. Ex: In the first round of #layoffs, #amazon laid off almost the entire team from Amazon Go (Amazon physical store) team, Amazon Alexa and music are the other teams impacted heavily. Least people are affected by the ecommerce and AWS business. Same pattern is followed across other companies too, analytics, ML team which are not directly generating revenue are impacted heavily. 

So it's not just about what company you pick it's also important what teams/projects you pick. Try your best to be in those teams that directly align with the core of the business.
