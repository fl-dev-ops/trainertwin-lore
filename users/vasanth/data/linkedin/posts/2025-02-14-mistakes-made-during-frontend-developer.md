Mistakes made during frontend developer interview. Ep-8 - "Divide and conquer"

Interviewer: Can you build a solution similar youtube comments ? where a person can respond to ones comments, like and share it ? 

candidate: Starts coding, tries to implement like feature in middle switch to share and again in middle to reply flow. 

Instead, divide the problem into modules. Like is one flow, same is share and reply. Try to complete the easiest one first, keep the toughest one at the end. With this approach, even if one flow is not solved, there is still a chance for you to show some progress.

Instead, if you solve everything in bits and pieces, there will be nothing to demo at the end.
