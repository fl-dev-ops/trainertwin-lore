I speak to at least 2-3 people every week who are #laidoff in the last 6 months. Despite the market being very competitive, Below are the common problems I notice which are pulling them back from getting their next job.

1. They start applying for jobs as soon as they got #laidoff !! (Instead prepare yourself for the interview and then apply for the jobs)

2. Most have one resume and use it to apply for all the opportunities. (Instead you can have a few variations of your resume).

3. Most apply for a lot of jobs and when they don't get any calls, they will stop applying for sometime. (Instead be consistent in applying)

4. Very common mistake that I have seen very commonly is that most are not interview ready. They give a few interviews in the beginning where they get rejected and later they don't call. (Instead prepare well and try to clear every interview.)
