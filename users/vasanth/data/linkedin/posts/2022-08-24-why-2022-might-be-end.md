Why 2022 might be end of full stack development ? It's high time full stack developers need to re-evaluate the career option that they have taken.
 
1. Most full stack developers are situational full stack developers. I mean, they became full stack developers based on need and not internally. Even after that most never spend time mastering the other skill. Due to which full stack developers becoming mediocre developers.
 
2. What is right full stack combination ? Java-ReactJS, Java-Angular, Java - VueJS. Same goes with C#, Django, c++ etc. When you apply for full stack developer opening, this combination matters a lot and most end up not getting right combination in right company.
 
3. Premium companies have drastically reduced hiring full stack developers. If you don't believe me, checkout career site of any premium company.
 
4. Industry needs specialists and not generalists. Generalists who can develop, test, deliver product 😂 required only during initial phase of project. After a point of growth, it requires specialists and generalists become absolute. 
 
5. Fresher full stack developer has least value. Many freshers spending money and time to master and openings are very few.
