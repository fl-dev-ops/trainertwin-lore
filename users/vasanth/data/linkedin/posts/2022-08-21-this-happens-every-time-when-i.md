This happens every-time when I go to native. I generally go when there are some functions/events and many of my relatives will gather at that time. Railway station is 10 mins from our house and one of our relative who is free and has car will drop us to railway station when we are returning. As it's a small town train passes in front of most houses thus our house too. All our relatives who has seen us 10-15 mins before, will wait near the house gate and wave at the train. Me and especially my mother make sure, we go near the train door and wave at them. Sometimes due to some reason, we failed to wave and my mother feels deeply sad. I wonder why humans do this ? even in 21st century, where we can make a video call from train as soon as train starts. I feel human does lot of things that gives them happiness it needn't have detailed explanation. 

For Ex: 
I have seen many jumping on used lays/Pepsi packets just because it cause a "tapp" sound. Many put chips across their fingers and eat them, some dance whenever they hear a good music, even without knowing the dance. 

What do you think ? mention in comment, is there something you do  for happiness that doesn't have lot of explanation.
