Is #youtube becoming another breaking news TV channel ? Below are some top titles of so-called popular educators from the software industry.

1. X year old boy earning 56 lakh by becoming full stack developer.
2. JEE fail, 45 lakhs salary in Amazon.
3. How I cleared Google interview in one day.
4. Resume that gave me 1 crore+ salary.

Everybody who follows them, just look at the points below once.

1. Some Youtubers' only popular factor is they are working for #microsoft #google etc. Same thing they say in the intro and mostly what they do is reveal some companies salary.

2. Whereas some other Youtubers' only popular factor is they have left #microsoft after 1 year and rest they do is acting in #youtube channel. Yes, I mean it, teaching some random stuff on Youtube without getting into depth is very easy.

3. Finally they launch a free DSA course and people are in the cloud. Just think once, what DSA is not already on Youtube ? almost everything is present, there is nothing new somebody can make. You're getting fooled because of this.

4. All these Youtubers are misleading an innocent candidate who is seriously preparing for an interview. Somebody will open one video for the interview preparation and they end up watching all the above crap and they feel low or jealous and done with that day's preparation.

My request to all the popular youtubers is, please don't mislead an innocent candidate. Life is not about 50 Lakh CTC and 1 Lakh in hand salary. Motivate them to work hard in the organisation and prepare for the interview, if they really prepare well, they get the salary that they deserve.
