People often ask this to me on LinkedIn and Youtube, "Vasanth I can crack interviews with any premium company but my resume is not getting shortlisted P !! What shall I do ? What is the right resume template ?? "
If you're also someone who wants to crack a premium company and your resume is not getting shortlisted follow below trick. 

1. Premium companies expect some premiumness in your resume. It could be a premium company that you worked in the past or a premium college etc. If you have any such premiumness, highlight it.

2. Highlight all the coding competitions or any other competition that you participated with your ranking. If you have not participated in any, at least participate now and get some ranking. This shows the candidate loves competitiveness. 

3. Don't make your resume very vibrant, plain, white and black resume is preferred by premium companies.  

3. Try to fit all your resume content in one page. Premium company recruiters might get 100's of resumes everyday and they might not have time to go through multiple pages. 

4. Mention your personal details like name, email, contact details at top of the resume. Some keep it in the bottom and recruiters sometimes will not have patience to check it out.
