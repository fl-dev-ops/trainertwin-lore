Is it a good idea for #freshers to move to #bengaluru for searching for a job ? Most of my #followers are #freshers and they have this question in their mind and here is my answer. If you have already tried hard from your place and are unable to find a job for whatever the reason, there is nothing wrong in relocating in #bengaluru for a short duration of time.  Being #siliconvalley of #india there is definitely a high probability that you might find some suitable #job here. I would advise you to follow below tips in the process. 

1. Start socialising yourself as much as possible by attending meetups, hackathons or any technology events where you can meet #founders and senior members of the company. 

2. Stay in PG's which are in the outskirts of the city, where the living cost is comparatively very low. 

3. Keep looking out for off campus placements which happens in a lot of colleges which are open for all. Do attend those #placements.

4. Look for #internship #opprtunity and if the company is near to the place where you live nothing wrong in going physically to the company and enquiring. 

"At last if you do what everybody is doing, you will get what everybody will get" so do something creative to find your first job.
