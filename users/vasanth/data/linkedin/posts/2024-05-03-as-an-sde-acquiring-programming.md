As an SDE, acquiring programming languages can come later, but having solid problem-solving skills is important

I got this insight from my 8-year long career, which made me acquire the following techniques:

➤ Mind Mapping: By visualizing the problem, I am able to identify relationships and dependencies.

➤ Pair Programming: I believe that collaborating with a peer encourages and opens up new ideas.

I even shared these techniques with my mentee, who was preparing for a technical interview. He was able to give optimal solutions and cleared the interview.
