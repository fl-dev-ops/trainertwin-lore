I have 10 years of experience and in the past I have cleared interviews of Microsoft, Tekion, Adobe etc. After being interviewed by most top tier companies, here is what I feel the most important skill, that you should have.

1. Conceptual clarity: they don't mind if you don't know advanced concepts, but your basics should be solid.

2. Can they discuss with you ? One can converse with anyone, but you can discuss with someone who are having similar level knowledge of yours. 

3. Communication: Can you communicate your thoughts clearly ? If your answer is countered can you respond well for that ? 

4. Do you have system thinking: Have you just build APIs / screens or you actually understand the problem and know how you solved it end to end.

Some of these skills are hard to master if you never worked on a big product based companies. In such cases opt for mentorship from a good mentor, who can work with you in your preparation.
