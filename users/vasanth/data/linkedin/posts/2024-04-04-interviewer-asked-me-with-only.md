Interviewer asked me, "With only three cuts allowed, what's the maximum number of pieces you can create from a single cake?" ? I was having around 2 years of experience and my company was not doing well and a job change was a must for me. Cleared other rounds and in the final round the manager asked me this question. 
I never thought I needed to cut the cake after joining this company, I thought about it multiple times and answered "3". Which was definitely the wrong answer. #interviewer was not happy with my answer and as you guessed I didn't clear the #interview. After that I made a decision when I grow to the level of manager where I decide the hiring of a candidate. I should never ask questions like this and till date I follow that.
