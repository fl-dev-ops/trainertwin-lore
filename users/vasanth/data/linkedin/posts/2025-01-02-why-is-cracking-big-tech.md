Why is cracking big tech firms not easy ? First let's discuss the right reasons, yes there are wrong reasons too.

Right reasons:
1. Relatively difficult interview process.
2. Trained interviewers, who can access the candidate very well.
3. Multiple rounds which makes it hard to convince every interviewer.

Wrong reasons:
1. Many interviewers think rejecting a candidate is a right/power which they have. More rejections means a more proud feeling for them.

2. Slow interview process. Sometimes interviews last for a month, by then the relevance of the position itself might have de-prioritised.

3. Unrealistic expectations: Expecting candidates to be exceptional instead of coachable. Sometimes interviewers themselves do not answer questions which they are asking.
