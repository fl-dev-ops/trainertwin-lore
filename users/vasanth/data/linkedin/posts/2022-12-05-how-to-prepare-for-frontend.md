1. Frontend system design series by Chirag Goel (Link is in the comment section). Every video will definitely teach you a lot of new concepts. It is not just helpful for interview preparation, you will grow as a developer after watching his series.

3. Definitely this isn't sufficient if you're giving interviews for premium companies. Look at every system you use keenly, every-time when you see an interesting problem solved in simple ways, search it in #google and read more about it.
