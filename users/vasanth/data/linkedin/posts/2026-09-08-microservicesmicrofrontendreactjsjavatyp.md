Microservices/Microfrontend/ReactJs/Java/Typescript have become default options for an end to end application development, without thinking is it really needed for my use case ? 

1. Your website is dedicated for knowledge sharing with limited dynamic content. You don't need #reactjs in such scenarios. Your website can be built using venilla Js, HTML and CSS.

2. You have small engineering team with few APIs to begin with. Worst thing you can do is to opt for micro service architectures. You do this to isolate the issues and give ownership to developers, who really don't need that.

My suggestion, in genAI era is, it's easy to make more systems on need basis. Till then keep the system simple. As long as it works and meets your customer requirements, it's fine.
