When Bhavish Aggarwal posted this last year everybody laughed at him and cursed him for being against #indian #engineers getting good #salary. I posted it back then and saying it now also, "Indian Engineers should not be paid a very high salary" because of the reasons below.

1. #premium talent exists in every #country if the cost of #softwaredevelopment in #india is the same as their #country then most companies do not open their offices in #india .

2. Though #india has mastered the art of building #products still it is in a very nascent stage. Profit making companies are very less. Even today, most people work for #usa and #europe based product/service firms. 

3. Unlike other countries, #India produces 16 lakh+ #engineers  every year. So the supply is more than demand, for a long period no company will keep paying a high salary. 

4. Until #indian companies build products like #siliconvalley and we have users who can afford these products, I think #indian #engineers should be paid moderately. So that market would sustain for a longer period of time.
