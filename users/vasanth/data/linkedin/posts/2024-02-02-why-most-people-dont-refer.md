Why most people don't refer you in their organisation?

Making referral is not a very easy process as it seems from the outside. Different career portals will have different process and a lot of time internal portals don't work well, as it is not a primary focus for most companies. 

1. A lot of times referee might have already applied on career portal which will prevent employee to refer on the portal.
2. After clearing interview many will not join the organisation.
3. One - two months after successful joining is when an employee receives the referral bonus that too after deducting tax.

as a whole referral is having less monitory incentives for an employee, so most tend to ignore it. Only people who want to help people can refer actively. Let me know what you think in the comments section.
