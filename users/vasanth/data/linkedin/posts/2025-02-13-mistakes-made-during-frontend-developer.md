Mistakes made during frontend developer interview part -8. "Be prepared with non technical things"

1. Join interview 5 mins prior, so that there isn't any last minute zoom/gmeet issue.

2. Keep water bottle next to you, in case if you feel thirsty.

3. If it's a coding interview, then spin up the local editor and be ready with a empty project.

4. Keep laptop in charging mode. 

5. Have an alternative internet connection handy, in case if something goes wrong. 

These simple hacks makes your interview less stressful. Share missing points which you feel are important in the comments section.
