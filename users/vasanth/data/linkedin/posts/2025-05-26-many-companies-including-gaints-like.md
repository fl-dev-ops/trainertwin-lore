Many companies including gaints like Amazon and Flipkart have started their physical interviews again. For Covid batches, this would be you're first offline interviews and here are my tips for the same. 

1. Being honest, offline interviews are better than online, except the commute part.
2. You get to see the expression of the interviewer and modify you're answer accordingly. 
3. As cheating is not possible, you can be honest here. You can surely try to create a positive opinion about you. 
4. Same as online interview, if you have any suggestions for changing the question, modifications etc you can surely ask the interviewer. 

As it is physical, the earlier you understand the interviewer and respond in their language, the better it will be.
