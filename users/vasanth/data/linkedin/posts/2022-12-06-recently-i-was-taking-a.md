Recently I was taking a #reactjs and #javascript interview and I asked what is "temporal dead zone" ? and the candidate answered very quickly. Then I asked a classic setTimeout + for loop question. Even for that question, the candidate answered swiftly. Then I realised he has watched all the Akshay Saini - #namasteJavaScript videos R. 

After this point I stopped asking the questions discussed by Akshay Saini in his #youtubechannel. Then I asked a small variation of  settimeout + for loop and the candidate was unable to answer. Here are my tips if you're preparing for a frontend developer interview.

1. No doubt Akshay Saini is a great teacher but  his videos are not relevant enough to crack an interview today.

2. As his videos are extremely popular most #frontenddeveloper's are aware of questions discussed in his video. Good interviewer will surely not ask those questions as they want to test the candidate's real abilities. 

3. My advice is, though many #youtubechannel's like mine(#uncommongeeks link is in the comment section)are not very popular, we will constantly discuss new questions that are relevant for today's interview. So, please subscribe to these #channels and watch these videos also.
