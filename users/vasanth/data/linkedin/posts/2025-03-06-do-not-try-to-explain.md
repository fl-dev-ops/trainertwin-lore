Do not try to explain everything by an example in the interview.  You must know basic definition of a concept. The reason for that is,

1. If you're trying to explain everything by an example then it creates an impression that you haven't really understood the core of the concept.

2. Because, if you really understood it, you really don't need to give an example to explain the it, unless interview asks for it.

3. Also, it saves interviewer time as they don't have to listen over explanation for each of your answer. 

so, I suggest everyone to learn basic definition of a concept, before the start of the interview.
