Mistakes in frontend developer interview - Ep 1 "Answer to the point"
Let me give you an example: "How to ensure the security of banking web app that built" ?
Candidate's answer:

1. As soon as we decided to build the banking app, the top most priority was security.
2. I worked with my architect to come up with a lot of frontend security aspects. 
3. We did many modifications to our security policy to guarantee it works as expected. 

2 mins over and there is no answer yet ! most interviewers are busy and very few actually try to focus and extract answer from your explanation. If you lose interviewer interest in the beginning then it is very difficult to get it later.  

So, I advise "Just answer to the point and ask the interviewer if they need further information, only if they say 'YES', then add further details."
