(Day 10 of #10dayChallenge) Frontend developer interviews answers you give v/s answers you should give. (Questions on Redux)

Question 1: Explain about your project and do you use redux ? 
✅ Answer:  "Yes I use redux in my application and my project is a basic blogging website."

Question 2: What is the right use case for using redux in #reactjs ?
❌ Answer: (I don't know why candidates say this) Redux is used for state management in big web applications. 

Question 3: Can you define a large application ?
✅ Answer: Vasanth Bhat you know right, large applications ? "Applications that are large like e-commerce, ticket booking, OTT platforms".

✅  Right Answer: "Right use case to use redux is when the application state is shared across multiple screens and there is no other easy way to pass the parameters. It is usually needed in big applications but not necessarily."

📖 Lesson for today: "Don't negate your own answer. If you say you 'use it for something big application' try to stick to that. If you negate your own answer then the interviewer thinks your not confident about what you're saying."
