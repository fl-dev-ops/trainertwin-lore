More than 60% of front-end developers make these common interview mistakes👇🏻

1️⃣ Guessing the answer: Common mistake that people make is guessing the answer. Unless you're 80% sure about the answer don't guess it. 

2️⃣ Not understanding the question clearly: Candidates often start solving the problem first instead of clearly understanding the question. To avoid this, seek clarity from the interviewer and then solve the problem.

3️⃣ Not focusing on System Design: Many candidates don’t focus on System Design questions. If you are appearing for middle and higher-level roles, I suggest you practice the core concepts beforehand.
