(Day 1 of #10dayChallenge) Frontend developer interviews answers you give v/s answers you should give

Question 1: Tell me about your last project ?
( Answer: I worked on an e-commerce application which processed around 10000 orders per day.

Question 2: That's wonderful, how do you solve concurrent booking problems ? Ex: 2 people trying to book an item at the same time ?
D Answer: (Most frontend developers answer) I'm not sure about that part, I just make API call and the backend takes care of it.

a Right Answer: As I'm a frontend developer I'm not completely sure about the approach. But at a high level we use the locking approach. Whenever we have the above situation, we will lock the item for one user for about 10-15 minutes of time and if they don't buy it within that time then we will release the lock and allow others to purchase this item.

Lesson for today: "Though you're a front-end developer, never say, I just make an API call when an approach is asked in the interview. Always have an idea about end to end execution"
