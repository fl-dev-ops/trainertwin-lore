Best advice for frontend developers if you're learning DSA right now.

If you're learning in-depth DSA for the first time by solving problems on leetcode, then don't watch YouTube videos to solve the problem.
Watching YouTube videos will make you understand the problem quickly, but you will get biased about the approach. You start feeling that's the best approach, which may not be the case.

My advice, solve problems on your own and if not, go and read solutions from others and pick the right solution and practice. Even though this takes time, you will understand the problem well.
