Mistakes made during frontend developer interview. Ep-9 - "Over simplifying the answer"

Interviewer: What is server driven UI layouts and what are it's problems ? 
Candidate: One main problem that I can think of is, it will take time to render the UI as it waits for the backend response. 

Candidate answers this and keeps quiet. When I extend and ask ?

Interviewer: "What if the backend does not send any layout" ?
Candidate: Vasanth Bhat that's also another problem we faced ? 
Interviewer: I extend and ask "How about versioning of the responses ? how do you handle it ?"
Candidate: Vasanth Bhat that's another important problem.

Here the candidate is not consolidating all answers and explaining it to the interviewer. Try to consolidate all the points that you're aware of and explain that to the interviewer. Explaining it bits and pieces creates a negative impression in the interview.
