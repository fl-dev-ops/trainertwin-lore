My grandfather never cleared MAANG. "Life is easy. Why do we make it so hard? by Jon Jandai". I was watching this video yesterday night and started connecting it with my grandfather’s life style. He used wake up in the morning between 4-5AM, by 7AM he used finish his breakfast. After that he used take a walk in our field and plot, if he thinks some work needs to be made, he used to do otherwise he used to back home by 10AM and he used to have second round of same breakfast. After that there is no work till noon, he used talk to neighbours or neighbours used to come to our house and they used to ear betel leaf (Pan) and talk about everything. Around 1PM he used have lunch and sleep for 2-3 hours, evening he used to talk to people if they are around, otherwise he used listen to radio for some time and by 7PM he used sleep. In entire year he used to work hard for 2-3 months, rest of the days, he used to leave happily. He never bothered if somebody got government job or built a beautiful house or somebody cleared interview at MAANG (just for sarcasm). He was content and happy with what he had. That's the story with everybody in that village.
Why are we making our lives so complicated ? no matter how much we earn we can't eat after our stomach is full, we cannot sleep once sleep is over. As we are in friendship day week let's try to be friend with ourselves and ask what our soul needs.

What are your thoughts ?
