#reactjs #interview What is the right use case for Higher order components in #reactjs ?
This is one of the most common interview topics and most candidates still make the mistake !! Let's see how.

1. What are higher order components (HOC's) in react ?
Ans: HOC are those components that take component as input and mostly return enhanced components as output. (Correct Answer)

2. Give me an example where you/your team built a HOC for your project ?
Ans: Most people answer we had a requirement to show buttons/other components with different colour, border etc and we used HOC.

3. Do you really need a HOC here or just a component ?
Candidate gets confused and makes the mistake of justifying the above example.
