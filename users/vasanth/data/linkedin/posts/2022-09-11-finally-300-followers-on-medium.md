Finally 300 followers on Medium (I write about the latest frontEnd and mobile tech on medium). Here is my advice for somebody who is planning to enter into some sort of content creation. 

1. Don't ever think that all explanations are already present on the internet. Whatever you teach might appeal to someone and they might follow you. Eventually you will find a niche area which is unexplored. So, start your content creation today itself. 

2. You need to have original content. It generally comes with experience and analysis. You cannot create content for a long run by copying somebody else's content. Eventually you will be caught by your followers and you will lose the trust. 

3. Just compete with yourself. Don't worry about somebody who is getting more followers or subscribers. You're in a race with your own self. Just because somebody becomes popular by sharing some content, you don't have to do it. What you think is right just do that. If you play somebody else's game only they will win, not you. 

4. Have certain ethics while creating content and abide by it. Your followers follow you because of that, the day you leave those ethics your follower count will reduce.  

5. You should have perseverance, it took me a year to get 100 followers on Medium and 2 more weeks to add another 100 and 1 more week to add the remaining 100 followers.
