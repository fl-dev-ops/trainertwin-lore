Whether you like it or not "You're as worth as that of your current salary. doesn't matter if it is 3.5LPA or 30LPA". You might argue "No, I do a lot of stuff and I deserve a hike from 3.5LPA to 30LPA" but industry doesn't work that way. 

The whole point of asking your current salary during salary discussion is that they need to know your current value only after knowing your current value, your future value will be decided. It is the same as buying a stock, the company will claim it will turn 1000Rs/- in next 3 years, but all that we want is, what is your current value and is it worth buying shares at that price.
