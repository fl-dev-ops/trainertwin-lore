The interview is not a casual conversation where you will always feel delighted at the end of it. A lot of candidates say this to me "Vasanth Bhat I felt interview was quite intense" I was tired at the end of it. I answer them politely that it is a "reciprocation". If you know what I ask then it feels very comforting but if I ask things that your unaware then it becomes stressful. My advice is don't expect interview conversation to be very joyful and happy, it doesn't work that way. Have a mindset that this interviewer is going to be a tough guy, so that you're already prepared for the worst.
