In the last episode of #pitchers season 2, a senior developer becomes CTO of #pragati #ai company. This is one of the main reasons why I stay away from #startup #interviews. Irrespective of the pay I avoid as much as possible to attend #startup #interviews.

2 year experienced becomes #seniorsoftwareengineer 
4 year experienced becomes #softwarearchitect 
5-6 year experienced becomes #cto #vicepresident and #director 
(I don't generalise it, all companies may not adhere to this hierarchy, but with my experience most companies are like this).

Very few really deserve these roles, most are promoted just because there are nobody else in the organisation. Problem with this hierarchy is when a real architect with 10 year experience wants to join this #organization mostly they will be interviewed by a 4-5 years experienced #engineer, who wants to prove they are brilliant then seeing the talent of the candidate. 
My ask from all #startup #hrs and #founders is, "When you want to #interview a candidate #interviewer should be of equal or more #experienced. If not, at least close to his #experience. If you don't have someone like that, then outsource the #interview "
