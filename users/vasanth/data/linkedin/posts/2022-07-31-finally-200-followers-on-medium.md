Finally 200 followers on Medium. I think Medium is the most difficult platform to gain followers. As people come there to read your article. After reading, most people don't follow you. As it is a textual platform, no one will shout requesting you to follow them. People will follow you only when you're giving good information consistently. It took me a year to gain 100 followers and 2 months to add another 100. If you're someone who is thinking "why to start something new, there is already lot of information out there", please get rid of that thought. Share the knowledge that you have, if you're doing it right, people will definitely like you. You just need to have perseverance, consistency and Patience.
