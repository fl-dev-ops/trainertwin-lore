No toll plazas on national highways of India| Fast tags might come to an end soon: How will it work?

Despite 5.56 crore FASTags being issued and 96.6 per cent of tolling occurring through the technology, vehicle congestion at toll plazas continues to be a problem. Nitin Gadkari announced the new system would be introduced within six months.

The plan works like this: 

1. The Indian government is planning to remove toll plazas from national highways across the country and instead use cameras to deduct fare.

2. The Ministry of Road Transport and Highways is proceeding with a plan to replace toll plazas with Automatic Number Plate Reader (ANPR) cameras which can read number plates,

3. Cameras will be placed at entry and exit of toll roads. These cameras will scan number plates and automatically deduct toll from the bank accounts of vehicle owners.

4. However, only those number plates that have been made after 2019 will be picked up by cameras.
