This is how feature development happens in a #productbased company.

1. #frontend + #backend + #product (Ghandi's 3 monkeys) sit together and discuss the new feature and lock the requirement.

2. As soon as you start the development, #product comes with a new requirement. Manager says it is a small change, can you accommodate it ? You will say "Yes" and your trapped, as  this will not stop until the last day.

3. #frontend finishes development but #backend doesn't give API's on time.

4. When both #frontend and #backend completes their work, there won't be any time for #testing.

5. #tester says I can't sign off with such less time. Again Trimurti's (#frontend + #backend + #product) sit together to remove all features that are not important.

Finally the user updates the application compared to the last and current release, he won't see any change.
