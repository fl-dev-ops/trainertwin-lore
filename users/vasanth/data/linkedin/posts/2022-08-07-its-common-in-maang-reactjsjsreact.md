It's common in MAANG, ReactJS/JS/React Native interview to ask candidate to write PolyFill (Custom implementation) for built-in methods or built in hooks. Most generally asked questions are build useCounter hook or write polyfill for Array.Map or Array.forEach etc. With my experience of taking interviews, most candidate still don't know basics of writing a polyfill. Somehow they will try to write array methods Polyfill but if I ask them to write Polyfill for "Promise.All" candidates get frightened. They will not know what to do !!! In reality, it is very easy to write custom Implementation / Polyfill for Promise.all. To explain that, I have made simple video of 20 mins, where I explain it step by step.
