Keep you're intro short in the interview. You're current project, challenges you faced, you're hobby, strength, weakness is not important for every interviewer.
So, my suggestion is, give a brief intro that contains your experience, skills, one line description of current project. Then ask interviewer, do they want to know more ? 

By giving 5 mins intro you might loose interviewers attention in the beginning itself.
