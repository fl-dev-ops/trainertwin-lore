If you're 5+ years of experience and not following this ! then you're missing and important growth hack ! 

1. Everyday when you start your work, note down what you need to do for that day.

2. Add an entry based on time for all the meetings that you want to attend. 

3. For each meeting, add sub tasks. ex: in a meeting, if you want to some question, note it down. 

4. Prioritise the tasks must and good to do. Make sure all the must do are taken care.

5. For some reason work is not completed, move it to next day. 

with this, you don't get into a scenario where you say sorry I missed it. This also acts as your work log, if someone questions you're work.
