Every time I write I work hard in my initial career to master the skills. Somebody would strongly disagree by saying "Work life balance is very important".  Let's just go back 30 years, where most of our parents were doing small business, running a Kirana shop, Garage etc.  

1. Were they working from 10AM to 5PM and enjoy life afterwards ?

2. Let's take examples of Marwadi shops which are all across the country now. Have they won the art of running business by working 10AM to 5PM ? 

3. Take any successful person, do you think they achieved it by working 6hr/8hrs a day ?

I'm not saying spend all your time on office work, spend all your time on what you really like. You wanted to be a musician and joined IT by your parents force, now spend all your free time learning music and making a career switch. 

Definitely some happiness comes out of 8 hours work and taking rest and spending time with our family. If that is what you want ? then it is fine. You want a high salary, annual foreign trips, great work, good colleagues; all these do not come without working hard.
