5 rules to follow to create best peer reviews. 

1. Limit the overall line changes to be less than 300.
2. Limit overall file changes to less than 10. 
3. Attach proper before and after video/screenshot to explain what is fixed in the PR.
4. Attach right ticket numbers in the description and explain the fix in detail.
5. Have a rule get reviewed by at least 2 people before merging the PR.

Let me know if I have missed any points above.
