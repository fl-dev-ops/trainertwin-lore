#reactjs #interview let's discuss an interesting sequence of basic #reactjs interview questions that I generally ask during the interview.

Question 1:
Is #reactjs framework is fast or #venilla JS with combination of HTML and CSS ?
Answer:
Most candidate's say #reactjs is fast due to #reconciliation.

Question 2:
Though #reconciliation makes it faster, JSX has to be converted into plain JS during execution. Shouldn't plain JS be much faster?
Answer:
Even here most candidate say DOM re-rendering is slow with plain #javascript where as it's faster in #reactjs. 

Question 3:
Finally I will ask, is there a scenario where #reactjs framework is slower than #venillaJS ?
