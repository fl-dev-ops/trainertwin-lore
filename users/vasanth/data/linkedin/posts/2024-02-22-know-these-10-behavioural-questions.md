Know these 10 behavioural questions and clear any #hr / #hiringmanager round.  Save this post and thank me (Vasanth Bhat) later 

1. Tell me about a time when you had to deal with a difficult coworker or team member. How did you handle the situation?

2. Describe a situation where you had to meet a tight deadline. How did you prioritize your tasks and ensure timely delivery?

3. Can you give an example of a project or task that didn't go as planned? What did you do to overcome obstacles and achieve success?

4. Tell me about a time when you had to adapt to a significant change at work. How did you cope with the change, and what was the outcome?

5. Describe a situation where you had to resolve a conflict within a team or between team members. How did you approach the conflict, and what was the result?

6. Give me an example of a time when you demonstrated leadership skills. What was the situation, and how did you motivate and guide your team?

7. Tell me about a challenging problem you faced and how you solved it. What steps did you take to analyze the problem and come up with a solution?

8. Describe a time when you had to deliver constructive feedback to a colleague or team member. How did you approach the conversation, and what was the outcome?

9. Can you give me an example of a situation where you had to multitask and manage competing priorities effectively? How did you stay organized and focused?

10. Tell me about a time when you had to take initiative to drive a project forward. What steps did you take, and what was the result of your initiative?

Know a scenario drive answer for each of these questions.
