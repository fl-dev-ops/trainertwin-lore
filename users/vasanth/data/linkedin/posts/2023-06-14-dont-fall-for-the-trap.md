Don't fall for the trap of #freelancing at your initial #career. 

It's looks very lucrative to make few thousands extra every month by freelancing at your initial career. But it can impede your career growth at later stages because of below reasons.

1. Most of the time when your #freelancing you don't learn new things you will use existing tech stack knowledge.

2. Most of the time you don't focus for quality, ex: unit testing, integration testing, code coverage etc. 

3. You will burn out yourself by working day and night where payment is not made ontime.

Instead use initial career extra time to master the tech that your working on. Understand the problem in-depth and solve it. If you can switch to a good company the hike you receive will be much higher than what you can earn through freelancing.
