-------------Does MAANG becoming IIT and IIM ??----------------

For those of you who don't know what is MAANG it is a short form of Meta, Apple, Amazon, NetFlix, Google.

As per Wikepedia, in last twenty years the number of people who are applying for IIT-JEE and CAT have doubled (In some years it has been quadrupled). Why most wants to join IIT/IIM ? because they can land you in a good job and you will also get an opportunity to learn from worlds best professors.

As usual when there is a demand there needs to be a supply. Like mushrooms, every city has coaching centers for IIT/IIM. The coaching centers will decode questions from past years and teach students how to crack exam by teaching short cuts. Because of this, the exams are getting tougher and tougher every year. Though examination team doesn't want to do it, there is no go, as coaching institutes preparing candidates so well, they have to invent new type of questions every year. This is badly affecting a talented guy with inability to get good coaching.

Don't you think same is happening w.r.t MAANG, so many online training platforms out there, who will prepare you to crack MAANG interviews. If candidate's are prepared well by these companies MAANG has to increase it's interview difficulty to select the best of best talent. Again, this will affect talented poor guy who cannot afford these courses.

What do think ? shouldn't we just leave MAANG as it is so that their interview process remains unaltered. Otherwise in next 10 years, these companies may have 10 rounds DS/Algo before checking your real skills.
