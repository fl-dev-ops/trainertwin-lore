Know your weakness in the interview and play by your strength. 
Ex: Some know the concept but cannot explain it well, if that's your weakness, then instead of speaking, try to explain your answer with the help of small snippets. 

Ex: Some will know theory very well and might not have practiced the coding problems. In such a case, be damn good about the theory concept and at least average in problem solving. 

Whatever is your strength, try to be expert in it which will give you an edge in the interview. Work on your weakness and overcome it quickly.
