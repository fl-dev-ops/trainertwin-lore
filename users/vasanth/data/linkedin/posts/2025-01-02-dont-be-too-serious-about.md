Don't be too serious about yourself in the interview. I have seen it very often during senior developers interviews. 

1. They start their introduction in the base voice. 
2. Explain so many things that they are proud of.
3. Mention scenarios how they held the ground together during earthquake 🫨😂

Finally I ask them "if javascript is interpreted how hoisting works" ? they struggle to answer and feel very low. My suggestion is, don't go to an interview with a baggage. Don't go to an interview with an attitude you know everything, be open to fail, that's the way to succeed.
