Don't set high expectations if you're someone who is starting a career or in a junior position. I have noticed it very often in my career, juniors when they start their career, take more work and stretch day in day out also on weekends to complete the work. 
After a year or so, they realise they cannot cope up with this. They start feeling weak physically and emotionally and  they will be left with no time for themselves. But, they have already set a benchmark and now they cannot reduce the bar and they struggle with it.
My suggestion is "During the first 2-3 months of you working for a new company, you need to certainly put additional effort to create a positive opinion. But after that make sure you set realistic expectation".
