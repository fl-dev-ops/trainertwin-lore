Are full stack developers becoming mediocre engineers ?? if you're a full stack developer please read till the end.

After working with lot of full stack developers and frontend and backend experts separately, this is what I think. Most full stack developers that I worked with are, previously working in either frontend or backend. Due to some project constraints they learnt the other skills and call themselves a full stack engineer. Even now they have a preference, ex: some are most comfortable at backend and some on frontend. So, the area where they are not so comfortable they are just managing it. Ex: a frontend developer after becoming full stack engineer, is not be so sure, how to tune a DBMS query so that the time required to fetch the data is reduced. They will search it on web and find some tuning and complete the story. If you ask most full stack developers who are comfortable on backend to build a star rating widget on frontend, they will just use some library and complete the story.

Most companies are fine with this as long as the work is completed. But the problem is for the engineers, where neither they are mastering backend or frontend. After 10+ years of experience, you
will not have master over any domain, you will again become mediocre architect and start observing void in yourself.

Whereas persons who have just have master over frontend or backend they become more reliable over the years and they will be able to give distinguished opinion about the problems that juniors face.
This is what I'm thinking, I'm open for criticism, please share your opinion.
