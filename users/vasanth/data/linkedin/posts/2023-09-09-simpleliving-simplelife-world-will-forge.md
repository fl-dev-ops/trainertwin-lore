#simpleliving #simplelife  "World will forget everyone, stop the quest for success and try to live a happy life" Most people I meet are in a rush to prove something and want to make a footprint on this earth. Let me ask you a few questions. 

1. Who won the Nobel Prize for their contribution to chemistry last year ?
2. Who invented laptops ?
3. Who invented mobile phones ?
4. Who was the world's richest 5 years back ?
5. Who was the CEO of the current richest company 5 years back ? 

Very few would know answers for all of these. Point I'm trying to make is that the world will forget everyone !! mark my words everyone. Depending on the contribution, it may take more or less time. People don't know the name of their own great grandfather. So "Stop the quest of success in terms of materialistic life. Have an ultimate goal of life as non-materialistic. Ex: Happiness, content, satisfied life"

That doesn't mean you shouldn't achieve anything. "If you win the Nobel prize or become the world's richest person, that would be really wonderful. Even if you don't achieve it and still live a happy life, that's also an achievement."
