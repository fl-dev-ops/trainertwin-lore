Where do you see yourself is the next 3-5 years could be a most cliche question one would encounter in the interview. But, it's important. I ask it to everyone who seek career advise from me. 

If you don't know what's your plan for atleast next 3 years, it will become very hard for you to take the steps towards it.

Let's say you have 9 years of experience and your current role is senior software engineer, if you don't know whether you want to be manager / staff and not working towards it. Mostly no one will help you to achieve your goals.

So, please ask yourself, where do you want to see yourself in next 3 years ? Do you have the answer ?
