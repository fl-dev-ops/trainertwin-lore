Which segment in frontend development is getting most number of interview calls ? With my analysis of interacting with 10-15 people almost every week, sharing this information. 

1. Junior engineers (1-3 years of exp) are getting the least number of calls. 
2. Mid level engineer (3-6 years of exp) getting moderate calls. 
3. Senior engineer (6-10 years of exp) getting highest number of calls. 
4. Above 10 years (usually for staff role) there are least number of opportunities. 

In general I feel, if you're at entry level and at 10+ years of experience changing the job is really challenging. Mid-senior level job change should be relatively easy.
