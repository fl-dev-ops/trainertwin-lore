Don't guess answers in the #interview it's not a quiz show. 
I have seen most #freshers doing this in the interview, simply say some random answer to the question, even when they are aware what they are saying is wrong.

1. #interviews are not like #engineering exams, where valuation happens in bulk. There is no bounty for attempting some random question.

2. Pick only those questions that you know the answer for sure. If you think you're not aware of some concepts, make that clear in the beginning of the interview.

3. If at all you have to guess the output, guess when you're 60-70% sure about an answer. Don't guess when you don't know the answer at all.

4. Conceptual Interview is not about answering all questions that the interviewer asks (Except DSA). It's about picking the right questions and skipping the one's that you're unsure of. Obviously the number of questions you skip should be less.
