By now I have taken at least 100+ #interview for #freshers and below are the most common mistakes that I have seen. If you're a fresher please don't make these mistakes in #interview .

1. Poor communication: Candidate's struggle a lot to explain even basic things that they are aware of.

2. Too quick: Lots of candidate's don't even wait for #interviewer to complete the question. So, please wait, analyse then take action. 

3. Sticking some youtube channel contents: Including my youtube channel "UncommonGeeks", no channel is sufficient to clear all #interviews. Most still come to the interview by watching popular youtube videos which are already outdated. 

4. Not fundamentally strong: I generally don't ask how to reverse a BST in #interview. I would ask a practical question that would be useful on a day to day basis, but most will not be aware of answers to that. 

If you're making these mistakes, please correct yourself, before the next #interview.
