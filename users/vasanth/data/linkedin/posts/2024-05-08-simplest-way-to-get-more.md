Simplest way to get more money from your savings 

1. Credit your salary to salary account. So that it can be zero balance.
2. Take a cut for your basic expenditure for the month and make a Fixed deposit with the remaining amount.
3. Never keep an extra amount on your savings account.

If you know how to invest in mutual funds, gold, stocks, real estate that is wonderful. If not, at least start your savings this way !! right now most banks are giving 7.1% interest, even if you keep 2 lack in FD by the end of a year,  you will make around 14 thousand which could be your one month PG cost. 

If you need money for an emergency break last month FD, still you earn more money than a savings account.
