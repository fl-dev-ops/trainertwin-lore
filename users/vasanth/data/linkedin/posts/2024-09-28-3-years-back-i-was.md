3 years back I was in Rishikesh and going to a nearby town via government bus. The bus was full of people and the conductor came to me and asked "kaha Jana hai" I told the place name and he asked me to give "dedh sau".
As I was taught Hindi as the third language in school so I knew sau, do sau etc but not dedh sau. 

I asked "dedh sau" ? he said "hah dedh sau, jaldi jaldi de do" I asked  "dedh sau in english"? this time he said with irritation "kaha se aata hai ? dedh sau nahi pata" then I looked at others "as it was a local bus nobody knew what is dedh sau in english". Later the conductor gave a ticket and I gave that much amount.

I didn't feel conductor was wrong as nobody willingly wants to be conductor or auto driver. Looking at the number of people they deal with, it's easy to loose the calm. I have faced a similar situation in UP, Rajasthan, Himachal Pradesh.
