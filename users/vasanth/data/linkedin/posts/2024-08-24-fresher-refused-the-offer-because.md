1. Most stay in their job for a year, so 1 year bond is not a tie breaker.

2. Company has average rating, so I refused the offer. From tier 2/3 college we are also average. So, as long as the company is decent please join.

3. The initial salary is just 3.5 lakhs and the Bengaluru cost of living is high, so I refused the offer. Unless you don't have money crunch and confident to find a better salary this is fine. If not, start your career with some salary.

as a fresher finding job was never easy, that too if your from non premium college. So, stop giving excuses to reject the offer and start your career !
