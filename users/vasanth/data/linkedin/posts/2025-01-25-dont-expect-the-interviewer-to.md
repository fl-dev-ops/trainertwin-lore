Don't expect the interviewer to ask questions the way you have prepared. Many after I took their interview say "Vasanth Bhat you focused too much on fundamentals, you could have asked application level questions". 

1. The I ask "how do you upload a 200MB image to your backend in the most efficient way" ? candidates usually struggle to answer it. 

2.  When I ask practical questions people say, "I am primarily prepared for fundamentals". Then I ask "Is JavaScript compiled or interpreted ? or both?" Again most fail to answer it correctly. 

My suggestion is, the interviewer will ask what job needs and what they are really comfortable with. Not really what you're good at ! So, try to master everything that you can, don't expect the interviewer to ask what you know !
