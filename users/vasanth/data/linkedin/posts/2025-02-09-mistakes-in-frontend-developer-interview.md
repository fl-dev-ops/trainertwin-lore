Mistakes in frontend developer interview - Ep 4 "Know concepts in-depth"

Interviewer: What are memoised components in ReactJS ?

Candidate: Answers in a hurry "components that do not re-render as long as state or prop passed to it are not changed are called memoised components. "

Interviewer: If it is so efficient why can't all components be Memoised components ? 

Candidate: Who never expected this question becomes blank all of a sudden and struggles to answer it and blabber something. 

So, it is not necessary to know all the concepts in interview. But, whatever the concepts that you know, understand it in-depth. Ask multiple "Why" to yourself so that you know in and out of the concept.
