Top 5 Things to do when your joining the new company as a mid/ senior developer.

1. Don't try to start working on requirment immediately. Try to understand the system holistically. Who are upstream/downstream systems to your system etc.

2. Build a rapport with the person whom you think have a good understanding of system and try to spend time with them.

3. Don't try to change anything overnight. Despite you know the system is inefficient. First try to understand, why it is so ?

4. Don't speak negative about anyone. Because you don't know about them and it can back fire quickly.

5. Have a regular one on one with your manager, so that they are aligned on your progress.
