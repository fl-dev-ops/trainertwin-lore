"Please don't ignore this post, I have been struggling to find a job for 3 months. Applied for hundreds of jobs but got calls from very few companies. Even they are not updating the status after the first round."

this is one of the most common posts that is on #linkedin today. Though the job market is extremely competitive here are some mistakes that you might be making. 

1. Openings in career portal doesn't mean openings in the company. There will be few placeholder openings and there won't be any hiring happening for that.

2. So, before applying make sure you connect with some HR's from the company and make sure they are hiring.

3. If connecting with HR Becoming difficult connect with other people from the organisation and get clarity about the opening and ask them to refer you. 

4. Don't upload one resume to all the openings. Have few variations of the resume highlighting different skills sets and use them wisely. 

5. Also, get your resume reviewed by some seniors. Make sure it is ATS friendly..

6. Always be prepared for the #interview, don't push interview dates as the market is competitive, HR might find another suitable candidate.

Not just for job hunting, if anything your doing repetitively but not getting the desired results, then your doing some mistakes, Please correct it.
