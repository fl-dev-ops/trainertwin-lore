Don't just rely on Glassdoor review before joining your next organisation.

1. As soon as you get interview calls from the company, try to connect with 10-20 members from the company. 

2. If you get shortlisted, then reach out to at least 3-4 people from different hierarchies to understand the company well.

Even in the best companies there will be the worst product/teams. So, the more insights you get, it will be better for you to make the right decision.
