(Day 7 of #10dayChallenge) Frontend developer interviews answers you give v/s answers you should give. (Simple questions that most don't answer)

💡Question for you: Can you give me some practical use cases for closures in the comments section ? 

Question 1: What are closures in #javascript ?
✅ Answer (Most say it right): "A closure is the combination of a function and the lexical environment within which that function was declared. This environment consists of any local variables that were in-scope at the time the closure was created."

Question 2: Give me a practical use case for closures. 
✅ Answer: Most common answers are debouncing, throttling and setTimeout. 

Question 3: Other than this can you give a use case where you thought closure is the right thing to use and you used it ? 
❌ Answer: Very few have given the right answer to this question. 

📖 Lesson for today: "After 3-4 years of experience, interviewers don't just expect definition from you. One should be able to give a practical use case for all the problems."
