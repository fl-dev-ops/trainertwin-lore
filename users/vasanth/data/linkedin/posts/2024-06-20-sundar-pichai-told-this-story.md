Sundar pichai told this story " In a good restaurant a flying cockroach came all of a sudden and started flying from one table to another everybody started screaming, especially the ladies stopped eating and shouted for the help. All of a sudden a server came, held the cockroach silently and took it outside, and everybody went back to eating"
