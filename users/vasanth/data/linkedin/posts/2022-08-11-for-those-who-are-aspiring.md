For those who are aspiring to join a premium companies like Microsoft, Google, Amazon, Atlassian etc and not able to get placed there, here is my advice.

1. Every-time you don't a get a good project in these companies, lot of engineers who are freshly hired will do maintenance work for few months to years. Instead if you're working in a start-up you have a chance of learning more.

2. Most of these companies have internal tools which you should learn after getting inside. But they are of no use once you're out.

3. Everything in these companies follows a process, just because you want to improvise something, you cannot do it. Rather if you're working in a start-up or mid-range firm your voice has a greater value. So, if you're someone who want work quickly, then this is not the place for you.

4. It just seems like these companies pay very high, but generally most of it will be in terms of stocks or bonus, which are subjective and you cannot rely on them.

5. Don't fall for the goody posts that are made by Engineers of these companies. After a point you don't need so many t-shirts with company name on it or mugs which you rarely use for drinking coffee.

6. Just because your friend or person who you know on LinkedIn joined these companies doesn't mean you should join. So, stop feeling jealous. You might be doing better work than them.

Last point, there is nothing wrong in aspiring for these companies. But, if you have given interviews and not hired, that doesn't make you any less. You can be happy without MAANG, if your happy internally, otherwise you cannot be happy even after getting into MAANG.
