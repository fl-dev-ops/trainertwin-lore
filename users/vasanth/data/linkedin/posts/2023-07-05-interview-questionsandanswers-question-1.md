#interview #questionsandanswers Question 1: Is HTML a programming language ? I ask this question very often in #freshers / #intern interview.
Answer: Most directly say 'Yes'

Question 2: What is the abbreviation of HTML
Answer: Most give the right answer "Hyper text markup language."

Question 3: What is markup language ?
Answer: With my experience most experienced developers also don't answer this properly. Right answer is "A markup language is a text-encoding system consisting of a set of symbols inserted in a text document to control its structure, formatting, or the relationship between its parts."
