Somewhere I read, "Raise your son in the right way, and all daughters will be safe." Just by changing the  law to give death penalty to rapist will not solve the problem. Because,

1. To give the death penalty first the person should be proved as guilty.
2. For that the court should fast track the case and do proceedings quickly.
3. For that, the police should do their investigation quickly and submit the report.  

But the main problem is the police are under staffed, court is under staffed. Without proper investigation one cannot give the death penalty because, in a country like India it will be mis-used.  

Only thing that comes to my mind as a civilian is, there should be a separate task force and special court/bench to handle such cases so that justice will be given to victim quickly, and other would fear to do such a thing.
