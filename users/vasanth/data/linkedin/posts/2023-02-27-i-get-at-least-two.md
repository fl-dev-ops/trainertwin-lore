I get at least  two messages every day asking "Vasanth Bhat, Currently I'm working in #hcl #tcs #infosys #cognizant etc. My dream company is #google #amazon #microsoft  How to switch to these product based companies ?"

Here is my advice: 

1. Don't dream of joining a product based company. Always aim for joining a good company that meets your needs. It could be a service or product based company.

2. Most products goto a state of #maintainance after a point. So, lot of #engineers in #productbased #company are just fixing bugs and giving production support. 

3. Product companies definitely give more perks than a #service #based #company. But nothing comes for free, most of the time it comes at a cost of work life balance. 

4. There are many instances where #servicebased #company #engineers get a chance to work on various different projects compared to product based companies, whose focus is unidirectional. 

No, I'm not saying don't join #productbased #companies. Just keep in mind that, at the end of the day, everybody are #engineers and do the job that is assigned. Nobody is superior or inferior here.
