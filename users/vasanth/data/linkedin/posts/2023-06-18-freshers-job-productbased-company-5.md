#freshers #job #productbased #company 5 simple steps that every #freshers should follow to get into a product based company. Around 15 lakh engineering graduates pass out of college every year in India. Especially if you're not from #tier1 college, it is very hard to secure a good job in a premium company.

1. Don't push placements to the final/pre-final year. You have to start the placement preparation or up-skilling yourself at least from the second year of #engineering. Pick a domain (Ex: frontend/backend) and start understanding it in detail.

2. If you're from Tier2/Tier3 college then you should build a brand that is beyond your college. You can do that by participating in hackathon, attending online coding challenges consistently.

3. Spend time on building your network on #linkedin By the time you reach final year you need to have a good connection which can potentially get you a job.
