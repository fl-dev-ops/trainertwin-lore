(Day 5 of #10dayChallenge) Frontend developer interviews answers you give v/s answers you should give. (Interview question from Meta)

Question 1: What is the sum of 99999999999999999999999999999 + 1
❌ Answer  (Most common wrong answer): 
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
100000000000000000000000000000
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

✅ Right answer:
"1e+29" stands of 10²⁹ (1 * 10 power 29). 

💡 Question for you: If you have to get the answer as "100000000000000000000000000000" how do you achieve it in #javascript? 

📖 Lesson for today: Whenever you see extreme questions like this, don't jump to a conclusion quickly. Even if you don't know about the answer, take some time  and try to guess the most relevant answer. In this case, even if you say it will overflow the default integer limit still you will have some points.
