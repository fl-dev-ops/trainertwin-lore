For all those who are complaining about Bengaluru Traffic. I've been in the city for the last 10 years and I leave near Hebbal and travel to my office which is in Kadubeesanahalli (near Belandur) every other day. It's around 24KM.  Below are my observations about traffic problems.

1. I login to the office at 9/10/11AM and it takes 40-45 minutes with 9AM login, 10AM and 11AM will take around 1 hour to 1:15 minutes. 

2. I logout at 4/5/6PM and it takes 1 hour at 4PM and 1:30 mins at 5PM and 6PM. 

In my entire journey I got stuck while entering Hebbal and near Marathahalli. Rest of the bus/cab goes at full speed. 

Now please tell me, when you guys are getting traffic jams ?? or on a road where metro construction is happening and close to 30% of the road is occupied because of that. 

What should be the ideal travel time ??
