#linkedin #openforwork I think it's not a good idea to add an "open to work" badge on your LinkedIn profile. 

1. It would have been a good idea when there were plenty jobs and less candidates.

2. Now the situation is reverse and most recruiters easily find resume for almost any openings.

3.Very few would go through one's LinkedIn profile and when they see open to work badge, they would try to reduce the salary as you need a job at the earliest.

Instead of adding ready to work badge try to connect with people from that company and get a reference. That way probability of resume shortlisting and chance of getting better package will be high.
