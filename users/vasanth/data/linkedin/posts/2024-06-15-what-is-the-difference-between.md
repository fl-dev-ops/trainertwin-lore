What is the difference between useEffect and useLayoutEffect ?
********************************************************************************
useLayoutEffect:
1. Executes synchronously
2. Executes before the DOM is painted. 
3. Usually used when screen dimensions needs to be calculated before rendering.

useEffect:
1. Executes asynchronously
2. Executes after the DOM is painted. 
3. Used for most side effects. 
********************************************************************************
