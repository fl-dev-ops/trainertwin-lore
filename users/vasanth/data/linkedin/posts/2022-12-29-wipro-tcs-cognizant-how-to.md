#wipro #tcs #cognizant How to solve 90 day notice period problem ? We were discussing this problem yesterday in our telegram group "UncommonGeeks" (Link is in the comment section, please join if you're not already a part of it). I'm summarising the discussion along with my inputs and hope it will help someone.

1. Deepak Sharma ⚛️ suggested if you have a 90 days notice period, apply for those companies where notice period is again 90 days. Ex: If you're from #tcs then apply for #cognizant and get a job and you can resign and hunt for better opportunities if required.

2. As the #noticeperiod is quite subjective to #managers #decision, maintain a good rapport with your manager which might come for help during the resignation.

3. Sometimes there will be an option to buy out the notice period. If your new company is ready to do that then please utilise it.

4. Last point though I don't recommend it, if you have very strong technical skills and are confident to find a job anytime then resign from your current job and start searching for new jobs.
