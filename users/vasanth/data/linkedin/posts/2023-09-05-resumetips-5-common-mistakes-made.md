#resumetips 5 common mistakes made while preparing the resume. The first barrier between you and your dream company is your resume. After reviewing 100's of resumes, below are the most common mistakes that candidates are doing.

1. Don't write I have designed and developed a website, if you have only developed it. 

2. Stop outsourcing resume preparation to AI tool or to a freelancer, you know what you have done and add that your resume.

3. Don't make spelling/grammatical mistakes in your resume. It will create a very poor impression of yours.

4. Don't have a fancy colorful resume, most ATS (application tracking system) will fail to parse it and thus application gets rejected.

5. Most important of them all, don't have a resume with page size more than two. I'm a big fan of 1 page resume but you can have upto 2, anything beyond that will gain less interest from recruiter/#talentacquisition
