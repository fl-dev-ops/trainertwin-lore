Question 2:
Why is Promise.all used ?

Answer:
Some will know the answer and some will not. Correct answer is, the Promise.all() method takes an iterable of promises as an input, and returns a single Promise. When you have more than one promise and your further processing is dependent on resolving all the promises, then you can use "promise.all".

Question 3:
Can you ensure the order of promise being processed in Promise.all

Answer:
Many will not know the answer to this. Correct answer is "we cannot guarantee", as promises are processed parallel, we cannot guarantee the order.
