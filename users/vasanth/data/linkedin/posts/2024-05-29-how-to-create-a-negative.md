How to create a negative impression in the interview part - 1.
****************************************************************************
Explaining too much about the current project even before the interviewer asks.

It's a tradition in the interview where a candidate is asked to explain about themselves. As a part of it, most will explain their past project. My advice is  just explain the gist of your project. Don't explain everything as #interviewer may not be interested in it. 
Only if the interviewer asks you to explain the remaining.
