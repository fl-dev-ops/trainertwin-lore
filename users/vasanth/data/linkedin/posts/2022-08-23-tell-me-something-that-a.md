Tell me something that "A Financially Rich guy can easily do and the poor can't". 

Many would answer like:

1. Rich can buy a posh car and travel around the city at full speed. 

2. Rich can buy a big mansion and live like a king in there, which will be envied by the entire society.

3. Rich can go to foreign trips all across the world and enjoy the amenities of each place.

If you're rich mostly you will also do similar things. 

But what I think is, even after having a posh car, your destination will not change, a poor in cycle/bike can still reach the same place, he might be slow but he will reach. Even if you have a mansion, finally you need to sleep in an area of 6*3 (7*3 if you're tall 😁). Even if you roam the entire world, it's the same you, if you're happy internally you can be happy anywhere, if not, no matter how much money you earn you cannot be happy. 

I'm not against becoming rich, you all might be financially rich compared to yourself a few years back. Just pause for a moment and think, are you really enjoying the current, which you dreamed 5 years back ? or just pushing hard to reach the next 5 years ? If you're not enjoying it, no matter how much you earn, you won't be happy. 

What do you think ?
