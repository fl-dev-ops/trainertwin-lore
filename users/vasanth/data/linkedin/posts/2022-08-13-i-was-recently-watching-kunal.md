I was recently watching Kunal Shah video on Delta 4 theory. It's an interesting theory to predict the success of the startup. If you have not read the rule here it goes. 

Since human beings are hacking evolution, all inefficient things are in the past and efficient things are in the future. The efficiency score of a business solution or approach can be represented on a scale of 1-10 by the amount of efficiency it creates. 

For example, 

Booking tickets via the IRCTC app(Indian Railways official booking platform) can be given a score of 8 or 9, and going to the railway station can be given a score of 2 or 3.

According to Kunal Shah, if the delta of the efficiencies is greater than 4, the business or the solution will unlock the pot of gold. Once the business or the product achieves the delta > 4, it becomes unstoppable and becomes a wealth creation machine. 

Examples include 

1. Uber (Allowing to book cabs easily and efficiently), Google (Helping to search the world of the web faster), 

2. Truecaller (Helping to identify an unknown call received), 

3. Whatsapp (Making text communication faster, efficient and free), and more.
