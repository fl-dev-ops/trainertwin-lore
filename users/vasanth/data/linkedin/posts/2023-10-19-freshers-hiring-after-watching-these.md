#freshers #hiring After watching these Bhayya/Didi videos most freshers started thinking some company is waiting outside their college with 20Lakh+ CTC kept in a silver bowl. Except for a few premium colleges it will not happen for lakhs of freshers out there. Let's be practical:

1. Including myself , 90% of developers will agree with me that their first job was not their dream job.

2. It didn't pay well, there was no free food, cab or joining bonus or goodies.

3. But most saw some opportunity to learn, doesn't matter #frontend, #backend, #qa, #designer etc. At least somebody is giving you an opportunity. 

4. After a year if you realise this is not what you want to do !! no problem you can change your domain, company etc. But let's start. 

Don't worry about the salary, location, goodies etc. As long as there are opportunities to learn, go for it. Rest will follow you eventually. 

Let me know if your first company was your dream company or not in the comments section.
