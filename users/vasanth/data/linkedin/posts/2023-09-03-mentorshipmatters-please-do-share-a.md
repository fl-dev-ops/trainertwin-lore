#mentorshipmatters  "Please do share a weekly status update about your interview preparation, I will suggest if any modifications are required".
This is something that I will ask every candidate who takes mentorship from me. I usually give a routine for 1 month and hardly anyone shares update with me for 2 weeks. 
Here are the things that you should know, before taking #mentorship from someone.

1. If somebody is taking money for #mentorship then make sure in the designated time, you ask all the questions that are in your mind.

2. Make sure you bother them by asking doubts even after the session is over on #linkedin #telegram etc. In 30/60 minutes session all your doubts will not be clear, use the chat as a means for clarifying further doubts.

3. Ask a proper strategy from the mentor for your #career improvement, interview preparation etc. and have a plan on how to measure the progress. (My way is weekly seeing the status update of the candidate).

In the future I'm planning to create #telegram / #whatsapp group of all the candidates who take mentorship from me and in a similar situation. This would help them to discuss the common questions and prepare well for their future endeavours. 

FYI: Don't pay any mentor who thinks #mentoring is just one meeting. For me it's a goal, I want everyone whom I mentor to get a good job, prosper in their #career etc. In case you want to set up a session with me, then link in the comments section.
