#reactjs #bestpractices  Why are you using JSON in your application ? If your answer is that everybody are using it and it is industry standard, then you have also fallen for the trap of developer #blind #spot #syndrome. JSON is not always fast "inefficient serialisation and deserialisation can impact an application’s overall performance". Below are the alternatives for JSON

1. Protocol Buffers
2. MessagePack
3. BJSON (Binary JSON)
4. Apache Avro and many more. 

Next time before picking JSON as the standard for sending and receiving the data, at least explore the above options. Let me know why you're using JSON in your application in the comments section.
