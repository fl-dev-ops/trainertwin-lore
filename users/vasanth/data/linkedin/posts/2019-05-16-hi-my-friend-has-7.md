Hi, My friend has 7 years of experience into core java, hibernate, Machine learning and AI. From past 40 days he is attending interviews. Highly skilled guy with great academics. He got call from many top MNC's and mid range companies. Every company is taking minimum 5-6 rounds of interview. 1st round in Hackerrank, Second round of telephonic, 2-3 rounds of face to face and finally few Skype and Webex meeting with directory level people. Which is taking approximate 3-7 days, including Saturday and Sunday's.
Even after that, many companies are not ready to roll out the offer. Every time my friend calls them, they say it is being processed. My question to HR's out there , will it really take so much time to roll out the offer even after they attends 6-7 rounds of interview. Definitely, the approximate package is well informed before. 
Now if he starts getting offer from the all the companies that he attended, definitely he will bargain for the best company with best offer. 
Now is it a HR/Company fault or candidate's ? open for debate !!!!
