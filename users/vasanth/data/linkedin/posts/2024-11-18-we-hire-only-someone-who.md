We hire only someone who is brilliant, not someone who are good in nature. I always felt this while taking the interview. 

1. As we spend 1-2 hours in an interview, to some degree we can understand how a person is. 
2. Many times I feel a genuinely good candidate by nature, who is honest in his approach might be less skilled for the role.
3. I as an interviewer cannot select them, because we only hire top skilled people for the role and not the ones who are really good as a person but less skilled.

If I start a company in future, I suggest along with decent technical skills, one needs to give value to someone's good nature and honesty while hiring. What do you think ? Should personal characters also be considered while hiring ?
