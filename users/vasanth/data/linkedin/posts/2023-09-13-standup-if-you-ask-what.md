#standup If you ask what is the most common thing that #softwareengineers think as soon as they wake up would be "What to say in today's #standup" and below are some of the updates when people have not done any work on the previous day. 

1. "Smart one" : I'm working on it, I will continue to work on it today and mostly try to complete it tomorrow. (You can do this in loop for 2-3 days)

2. "Lazy one": Actually I have started with the ticket and analysed it. I still need some clarity from #product. Post that I will do the needful. (In reality, no clarification is needed)

3. "Chalu one": I have completed the story but just waiting for unit testing to complete. (In reality they have not started the story itself).

4. "Intern/Fresher": I was waiting for the #seniorjavadeveloper to check and approve my changes. (This is real update, as #freshers really wait for the #seniors approval)
