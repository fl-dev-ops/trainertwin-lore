Decision that I regret in my career. My first company had #laidoff me in 2017 & I joined a service based company in a few weeks. Meanwhile I got an interview call from RedMart (Online supermarket in Singapore). The salary that RedMart could offer me was almost 3 lacks more than what the service company had offered. I used to do some amount of freelancing at that time and I thought I can make that much money by freelancing and let's not attempt RedMart. Another hidden reason was, the first round assignment given by RedMart was also quite tough. 

1. I had to make a few switches after that service company to get a brand in my resume. 
2. It was pre-covid where premium companies never used to hire anyone from tier 3 college / non-premium companies, unless there is a strong reference. 

In case if I would have joined #redmart, my career progression would have been much better. So, I suggest people not to fall for the trap of freelancing during early career.
