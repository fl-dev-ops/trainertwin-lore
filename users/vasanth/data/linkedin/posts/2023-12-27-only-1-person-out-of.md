Only 1 person out of 10 is selected in top product companies

If you want to be the one, focus on these points👇🏻

1️⃣ Strong communication skills: You should be able to communicate your technical knowledge with ease. Remember that the more you blabber the less the interviewer will understand.

2️⃣ Logical reasoning: In the technical interviews, the “why” matters more. Always explain your approach, the reasoning behind it and how it is practical.

3️⃣ Learning curiosity: At the end of the interview, ask about the team and the project. This will create an impression that you're curious about learning.
