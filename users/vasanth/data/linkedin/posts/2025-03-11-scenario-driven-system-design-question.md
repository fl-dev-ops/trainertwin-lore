Scenario driven system design question are one of the most ignored category in the frontend developer interview. Let me give you an example:

Today if you have to build a video calling application like Google meet, what network protocol you will use ? is it TCP or UDP ? whatever is your answer justify it. 

follow up question: During video call, there is a chance that internet connectivity of individual's might fluctuate, how do you handle that scenario?

the best way to practice this is, have a mentor, let them pick a system and you both discuss it end to end. Watching videos according to me are not the best technique here.
