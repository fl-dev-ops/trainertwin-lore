In every bad school there will be one good guy who will score good marks. In the same way every bad mentor can produce few mentees who can get a good job. So, don't trust those posts and make a big commitment with any mentor. 

1. Have 1-2 sessions with them to understand how what they know will help you in your transition.

2. What are their core skills ? can they be better than suggestions by genAI. 

3. Have they helped many others who are in a similar situation as you ?

if all of that is true, only then opt for long term mentorship.
