#joboffer #mistakes  5 common mistakes that most people do make while accepting an offer ? 

1. Not checking the project milestone.

2. Not understanding the team structure.

3. Not focussing on the designation/role.

4. Understanding the exact reason for hiring.

5. Not understanding the salary breakdown and how much will be in hand.


After joining an #organisation all of the above points will be a pain point if you have not asked them during #interview. Let's consider the 4th point ? What if you're hired as a replacement for the only engineer for a legacy project ? Consider the first question ? What if the project ends in the next 3 months ? Don't ask just salary and pursue an opportunity, the organisation you join should be a great fit for you for at least 2 years.
