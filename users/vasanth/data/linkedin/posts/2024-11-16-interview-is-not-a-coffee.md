Interview is not a coffee conversation, expect some heated moments. I have tried it a lot as an interviewer to stay calm throughout the interview. But,

1. Interview is about testing your core skills. So, there will be multiple follow up to a question, which will be intriguing. 
2. There will be some moments, where the discussion will turn into a short heated conversation, be open for that. 
3. All the time interviewers will not be friendly, because they are such, as an individual. Try to deal with such interviewers. 
4. Staying calm in an interview as a candidate is equally important as that of your technical knowledge. 

This doesn't mean, in every interview you will have such scenarios. But, be open for the worst. Take as many mock interviews as possible. So that your confidence increases.
