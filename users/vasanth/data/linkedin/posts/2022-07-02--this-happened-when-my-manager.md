-This happened when my manager decided to fire a lady employee.-

It was a few years back and I was working in a service company. An employee was poorly performing for a couple of quarters, even  her communication skills were moderate. My manager and many teammates tried helping her. But it was not giving much outcome. One day the org decided to fire her.

So, my manager called her to the meeting room and he explained the problems and said "I will be giving you pink slip" . We didn't know this was happening, suddenly that employee came out of the meeting room (We got to know what happened later) and ran to the the last floor of the building.

It was a 15 floor + building and my manager ran behind her, out of curiosity we all ran to the terrace. She said she would jump from the building, my manager was trying to convince her and she was acting just like a movie. If you come a step closer I will jump from the building. Everyone tried convincing her but she was not coming back.

Finally my manager said, we will not fire you, suddenly she was alert and asked for confirmation in writing and my manager got it and she decided to come back. After this for several months, we used to tease our manager about many things. Like when he was not giving a good hike or not taking us to a team outing we also used to tease him like we will fall from the building. Don't know what happened to that employee after I left the org.

What is your call in this situation ? Who is right ??
