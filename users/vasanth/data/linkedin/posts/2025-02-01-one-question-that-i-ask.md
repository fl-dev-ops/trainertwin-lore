One question that I ask without fail in every interview is, "Can you speak about one of your past projects, where you solved an interesting problem ?" Though this question sounds very simple, below are the common mistakes that candidates make.

1. Some oversell their project and get stuck when an intricate implementation is asked.
2. Some say "we just worked on the JIRA that was assigned to me"

You should very carefully answer this question as it is usually asked in the beginning of the interview and if you don't answer it with the right justification, you will get a slight negative opinion about you.
