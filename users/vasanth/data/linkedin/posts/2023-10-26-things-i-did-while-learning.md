Things I did while learning #frontend #development v/s things I would have done:

What I did: 
1. Watched thenewboston #youtube channel and repeated everything that Bucky Roberts (the author) did. 
2. Never focussed on reading, just watched videos, built screens and thought I mastered the #frontend #development. 
3. Focussed more on UI than logic building because of which I didn't understand a lot of web fundamental topics like hoisting, closure etc. 

Instead What I would have done:
1. Master basics of #javascript by extensively reading on developer.mozilla 
2. Learn basics of HTML, CSS by again reading on developer.mozilla  and practicing on the official website.
3. Learn #reactjs by watching @codevolution youtube channel (Vishwas Gopinath is my all time favourite #youtuber)
4. Learned basics of #frontend system design by reading wherever possible. (I don't think, back then, there were any channels or websites that were dedicated for frontend system design)
