I feel it's high time to go back to in person interviews like how it used to be in the past.

1. It's very hard for an interviewer to judge whether a candidate is cheating or not.

2. Tools like ChatGPT will allow candidates to find answers for almost all output driven questions.

3. The more interviews the candidate gives, the more they become expert in cheating. 

Except, scenario driven questions and deep tech questions, for most other questions one can get answers easily.

I'm not saying everybody will cheat, but they are in the majority. What do you think ? should we get back to in-person interviews?
