Before purchasing a #flat in high raised #apartments in #bengaluru please read my #post. I have actively/passively searched for a house in #Bengaluru for almost 4 years, hope my knowledge will help you #guys .
 
1. Flat rates in high raised apartments of #shobha #brigade #prestige starts from 80 lakh and goes upto 2-3 crore depending on the area and amenities. 
 
2. Most of these apartments will have minimum maintenance of 10K and it can go upto 30k (Equivalent to 2BHK house rent in most areas).
 
3. Also, most apartments are fed from #tanker #water. Have you ever imagined a situation when #city undergoes a drought ? After #rajasthan it's #karnataka which has the most dry lands in #country. It will be extremely difficult to get water in such scenarios. 
 
4. Instead you can #purchase #house with #land (Villa) in areas that are little far from the outer ring road like #hoskote or north #bengaluru for almost the same cost. Land value will increase at least 10% every year in #bengaluru. Which will appreciate your #investment .
 
5. I know most #northindians prefer #apartment because of security. But with modern security #technologies you can easily secure the house with efficient and layered locks. Secondly, don't keep a lot of valuables at house.
