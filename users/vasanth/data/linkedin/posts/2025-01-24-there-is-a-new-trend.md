There is a new trend of putting a percentage against all points in a resume. Ex: I increased the efficiency of the website by 30%, User engagement by 20%, Analysis efficiency by 40% etc. My view is,

1. Do not put percentage against those points which are not measurable.
2. Ex: Increased analysis efficiency by 30% ? How to justify this in an interview 
3. Increased user engagement by 20% ? Unless you know how to track user engagement there is no way you can track the increase in percentage. 

So, my suggestion would be, there is harm in putting a percentage against those points which you can justify. Simply if you put some numbers, you will be caught in the interview and you will get a negative opinion in the beginning of the interview itself. 

What do you think ?
