When the Hiring Manager asks "What is your weakness" please don't give below answers.

1. I'm not a team player, I like to work alone and deliver things.
2. My communication skills are not good.
3. I'm not good at this technology, I know it is required for the job, but I'm not well versed in it. 
4. My PR's get many review comments, I'm working on improving my code quality to fix this problem. 

Though you're being honest, most HMs don't like it. Instead say your positive points as a weakness. Ex:

1. When a task is given to me, I won't relax until it is completed.
2. I ask a lot of questions about products and business to clarify my doubts, which might be annoying at times. 
3. I'm not good at seeking credits for my work. I focus only on completing the task. 

Interviews are certainly about being honest but you need a sugar coat sometimes and be politically correct. Let me know, what do you think ?
