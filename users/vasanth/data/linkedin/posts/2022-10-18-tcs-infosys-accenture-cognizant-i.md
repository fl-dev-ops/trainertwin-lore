#tcs #infosys #accenture #cognizant I have observed some peculiar behaviour while interviewing engineers from service companies. After taking 100+ interviews I thought it's worth mentioning it.
Disclaimer: This post is not to hurt anybody, instead if you fall in this category, you can correct yourself.

1. They know "how" but not "why". Ex: If I ask them to build a UI/API they will build it, but if I ask why you want to use only these items to build this, they will not have an answer.

2. Lack of knowledge on fundamentals: They will be working on tech for years. But if you ask very basic questions they will fail to answer. Ex: Hoisting in JS, will declarations move to the top of the block or appear to move ? they wouldn't know the answer.

3. Not focussing more on DSA: With my experience, product company engineers will be much stronger in DSA as they spend more time learning and using, I have it less in service company engineers.

4. Technical communication and documentation skills: During the Hiring Manager round, when they ask holistic software development related questions, many times service company engineers fail to answer. As they don't follow the right procedure while building the softwares.

5. Less knowledge on unit testing/Integration testing. As product companies emphasise more on testing even engineers will have good knowledge in it. But I have observed that service company engineers do not have much knowledge on writing test cases.

Are product company engineers perfect ? no. Above shortcomings might exist in product company engineers too, but I have observed it more in service company engineers.

Feel free to share your feedback on the same.
