I want to share a horrible experience about the current job market and want my followers to stay away from these scammers, please read till the end. 

1. One of my mentees got a call from a company in Marathahalli, Bengaluru. 
2. They asked him to attend the interview in-person this Monday.
3. The company itself was in a remote region, which was suspicious, still candidates went for the interview and there was no board of the company.
4. As soon as the candidate reached, they took his resume and asked him to sit in a room.
5. Sometime later a lady appeared and told, we will give you interviews and offer letters by today EOD. We have 2 plans, pay 1000 Rs/- and get an offer for 3-4 lakhs. Another plan where you pay 2000 Rs/- for 8-10 lakh offer.

My mentee refused their offer and left the place. He was mentioning he was even scared, what if they lock him in the room and loot him ? As they are asking for 1K and 2K, most freshers might not mind giving it. They are using this weakness to cheat people. 

Be aware of such frauds, always make sure you inform someone when you feel a place is fishy, always keep your dear one's informed, where you're going.

Let me know, if you had similar experience in the comments section.
