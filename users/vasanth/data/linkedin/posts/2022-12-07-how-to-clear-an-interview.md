How to #clear an #interview  where the decision is not clear cut yes ?? 

It happens very often where the interview goes moderate. It is not a clear cut "yes" or "no". Both candidate and interviewer are unsure about the result. In such scenarios if you're a candidate follow below steps to make the result in favour of you. 

1. Use the last part of the #interview , where the #interviewer  will ask you for any questions. Make sure you ask the answer for one of the deciding questions that you didn't answer correctly. This creates a feeling you're really interested in problem solving. 

2. Have the best version of you w.r.t to communicate. If you communicate fluently and grasp everything that the interviewer is saying, there is a high chance the result would turn into "Yes".

3. Possess skills that create a feeling that you're a good fit for the team. Ex: be soft spoken, respect the interviewer's opinion, don't argue after a point even if the interviewer is wrong etc.
