My recent post regarding People complaining about Bengaluru became quite viral. Many supported me and some criticised my post. I welcome both, as long as criticism is constructive we need to have a mindset to accept it.

But everyone who attacked me personally, my basic question to you is this.
How many of you have voting rights in Bengaluru ?
People who shifted 10 years back still don't have voting rights in the city. They are too lazy to get a fresh one or transfer. As per TOI, more than 50% Bengaluru population is of Migrants. If you don't possess voting rights in the city, you cannot elect leaders such as corporator, MP, MLA etc.

When you have not elected any of the leaders who are ruling your area, what right do you have to complain ? If you're stubborn and say whoever comes there won't change. Then you have already accepted there won't be any change, the only thing you can do is, shut up and keep quiet.

PS:
1. This applies to native citizens also, who shifted to the city and have no voting rights.

2. People who have shifted recently and decided to leave here, please get/transfer your voting card.

3. Who already has voting rights in the city, let's take at-least next opportunity to pick the right leaders who can bring the change that we need.
