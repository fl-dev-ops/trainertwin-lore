I often ask this concept in ReactJS interview. 

Question 1:
Are functional components stateful or stateless ? 
Answer :
Most will say they are stateless, hooks were introduced to make them stateful.

Question 2:
What are states in React ? 
Answer :
States are memory of component.

Question 3:
I extend my my question and ask this, let's say there is a functional component without hooks and contains a variable

const x = 10; 

Is it state of component or not ? 

Answer:
Most candidate gets confused and don't say right answer.
