A lot of managers die in the age of 40-50 because of stress and over work. In my #telegram group we were discussing about long term #career in #it domain. Sadanand Pai and others suggested how it became stressful for #managers and higher roles in #it industry.

1. At the age of 40 usually you will be at #manager/#director level and you end up working at least 12 hours with regular late night meeting.

2. There is no work life balance in most companies with almost zero time to spend with kids and family during night time.

3. After 15-20 years of #career usually your #salary is so high that changing jobs becomes almost impossible, so people strech more to secure their job.

If you have just started your #career and in the age group of 21-24 and assuming you will work till 50 years. I think that path is going to be very hard. Save money and plan for alternative income stream and aim for an early retirement. By working 50 years in a stressful environment, you will suffer further years of your life with health issues.
