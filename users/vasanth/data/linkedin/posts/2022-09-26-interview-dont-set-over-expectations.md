#interview  Don't set over expectations in the interview. This is the common mistake done by most of the freshers and junior developers. This can ruin your interview in all ways.

Let's look at an example where you unknowingly set an over expectation in the interview.

1. Hi, "I have 2 years of web application development and I have practiced almost all the popular frontEnd systems, you can ask me any system design questions"

2. Hi, "I have solved more than 400 problems in Leetcode and I have taken active participation in a lot of coding competitions and DSA is one of my biggest strengths, I can solve almost any problem."

3. Hi, "I'm an expert in UI development, you can give me any design and I can build it for you in the interview."

In all the above 3 scenarios candidates might be really strong in the area that they are mentioning. But nobody in the world knows everything right ?

Let's say I was the interviewer for you, I wanted to take your DSA and I had a mindset of asking some "medium question". but you said "You're an expert😉", Interview should be competitive for you, so I will ask a hard question now 😭. Just by saying "You're an expert" you simply elevated the difficulty of the interview.

Instead of doing this, do below.

No matter how brilliant you're in an area, mention like you're practicing in that area and you feel very confident about it. If the interviewer decided to ask an easy or medium question why you simply make it hard by adding you're an expert ? we can anyway show our expertise after getting inside 😁.
