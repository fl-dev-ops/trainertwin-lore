How to get fired in an organisation in the current scenario ? Recently I was reading a post from a META employee explaining this ! I thought it was a good read. 

1. Use all the perks that company provides. Ex: Take complete paternity leaves at a stretch. Take sabbatical leaves and roam around the world.
2. Skip stand ups and important meetings and stay chill. Don't attend late night meetings, even if it is important. 
3. Just do the tasks assigned to you, don't act proactive to find issues, enhancements etc. 
4. All of this will lead to an opinion where the team runs well even without you ! This is the right remedy for getting fired. 

In case you don't want this to happen, then do the reverse ! Though the reverse isn't guaranteed, the chances are high.
