3 things that will get you rejected in the interview.

1. Poor listening: If you don't listen to interviewer and keep answering everything you know. 

2. Poor communication: You know answer but you have not practiced to communicate it fluently. 

3. Poor comprehending: You're taking a lot of time to understand the question. 

Work on these skills and improve them, otherwise it might become a bottleneck in clearing the interviews.
