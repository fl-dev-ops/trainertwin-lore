Never hurt the ego of the interviewer during the interview.

1. You're not achieving anything by proving you're right in the interview.
2. In a matter of few minutes, you wouldn't know whether an interviewer is good or bad, a bad interviewer would always wants to prove they are right. 

so, in interview don't spend too much time justifying you're answers, unless interviewer really wants that !
