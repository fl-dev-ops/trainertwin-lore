One of the most common mistakes in an interview is, every time a question is asked in the interview, you will say "I will explain by writing a code", it's still fine if you're a junior developer, but, if you're mid to senior developer then "this is not acceptable" 

Reason: You need to be able to articulate your thoughts as a senior / staff role to juniors. You will be in forums where everyone don't understand the code.

So, try to explain technical concepts without the help of code. Anyhow when your needed to code, interviewer will ask for that. Till then voluntarily don't ask it. Sometimes you might make a mistake while explaining which might also back fire.
