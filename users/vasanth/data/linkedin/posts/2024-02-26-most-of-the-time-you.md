Most of the time You can determine the company culture by looking at the behaviour of the #interviewer and HR. 

1. When I was interviewed by GE HealthCare I had just cleared one round of interview and the HR/company offered me lunch and sufficient time after that before starting the next round.

2. In another service based company, it was evening and after interview, #interviewer took me to coffee on a hotel near by had a casual discussion.

3. In many companies while giving the #interview, interviewers always encouraged me to answer and helped me wherever required.

On the contrary there were companies where interviewers were interrogating me. They want to show their brilliance over understanding my knowledge.  HR's don't give proper update, HM don't know what is the project road map. Access these signs and reject those companies despite slightly high salary. Because you will mostly suffer after joining the company.
