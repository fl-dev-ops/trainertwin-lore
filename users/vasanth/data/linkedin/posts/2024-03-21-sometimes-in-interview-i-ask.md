Sometimes in #interview I ask the candidate, tell me "What topics are you very comfortable with and I will ask questions on that." Usually I will follow a practice of length and depth, where I ask a variety of questions at a high level and dig deeper with 2-3 concepts. If a candidate answers those 2-3 times then I will most likely shortlist them. 
But there will be times where candidates do not answer any questions in this 2-3 times. Then I will not ask questions on a new topic, I will just ask what topic that candidate is aware of the most. 

Ex: Most people say hooks, memoization in ReactJS and I will start asking questions on them. If they answer well in these topics there is a high chance, I will shortlist candidates. 

Reason for that is "candidates need not know what #interviewer knows. #interviewer  should try to extract what candidate knows and is that relevant for the job opening. ??"

What is your take ? Should the interviewer ask a question that they are aware of ? or try to extract information that the candidate is aware of ?
