Mistakes made in frontend developer interview ep-7. "Exaggerating past work"

Most interviews start with questions around the past project and many try to exaggerate it by bluffing about things that they haven't even worked on. 

Ex: I built video calling mobile application, that was used by millions of people, I lead a team of 5, I spoke to product team and fine tuned many requirements etc.

Then I ask let's say an app is killed, how do you initiate a call ? or let's say phone screen is locked, how to show the ringing screen ?

Most say they don't know.

Instead of doing this, set right expections and know in-depth of project that you want to explain.
