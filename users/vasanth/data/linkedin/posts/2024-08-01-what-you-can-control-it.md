"What you can, control it, if you can't control and influence, then influence it."

1. Market is down and hiring has reduced, not in your control. 

2. Layoffs happening in all companies you can't control. 

3. Your manager is micro managing, you can try to influence by speaking to them.

4. Your fellow team is not responding as expected, influence then by yourself or taking help from your manager.

5. Your co-worker not working well, try to control them by giving right inputs. 

in most scenario's we confuse between what we can control and what we can influence ! which causes a lot of problems!
