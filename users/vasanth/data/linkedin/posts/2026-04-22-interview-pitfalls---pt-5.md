Interview pitfalls - pt 5 
Before writing I worked on scalable frontend applications, know answers to these 5 questions.

1. How do you monitor user's experience of your application. Ex: are tracking how many times user clicked on a specific button etc

2. What all errors you log ? which are different than your backend ? are they actionable?

3. For the alerts your logging, how will you determine the threshold to trigger an alert ? 

4. Your web-vitals score is quite high, still users experiencing delay while using the application, what do you do ? 

5. What all resource optimisations you will do, so that your site loads fast also efficiently.

finally the question in the photo, how some sites maintain infinite session for a user ? 

answer them in the comments section.
