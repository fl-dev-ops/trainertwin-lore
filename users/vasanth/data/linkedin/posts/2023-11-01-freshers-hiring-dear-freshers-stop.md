#freshers #hiring  Dear #freshers stop applying for the jobs and do the below instead. If all the freshers go to #naukri / #monster and other #jobportals and keep applying for all the jobs, how will you get shortlisted ? do below innovative techniques instead.

1. Make a video resume of yours where you explain your skills, interests, achievements in less than 2 minutes and send it to recruiters.

2.  If you want to join a startup, understand what the company is doing and make a video expressing yourself why you want to join the company and how you can help the company and send it to #cofounders of the company. 

3. Another idea that worked for me and a lot of others is, identify a critical bug in the product and make a video/short document around it and propose a fix and share it to #cofounders of the company.

4. After connecting with #startup #cofounders constantly like/comment on their post so that you're in their vicinity. In future when they post any job openings and you reach out to them, they won't consider you as an unknown person. 

If you know what everybody is doing, you will get what everybody is getting. Do something creative so that your chances of shortlisting will increase. Above suggestions are just brain teasers, you can think 100 's of such ideas and land in your dream job.

Mention any other technique that really worked for you to find your dream job !
