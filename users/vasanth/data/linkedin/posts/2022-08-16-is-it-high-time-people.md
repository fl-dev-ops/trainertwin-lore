Is it high time people should stop associating themselves too much with the company ?? You all might be seeing recent YouTubers or LinkedIn influencers, most of them start their intro in YouTube as

"Hello all, I'm X I'm working in Microsoft"
"Hi all, I'm Y I'm working in Atlassian"

Yes you guys work there, is that the reason, why we need to watch your YouTube videos or read LinkedIn posts ?? or you have some content to share because of which we need to listen to you ??

1. Everybody including me, who work in a company, very well aware that, no matter how hard we prepare for interview, there is a pinch of luck which will finally give us the job. We all might have seen more brilliant people than us who are not working in good company. So, working in a company itself is not a status.

2. Definitely being in a good company comes with hard work but is that your identity ? Let me ask this way.
"Are you someone because of which they hired you ?? or after they hired you, suddenly you became somebody so that, people need to follow you ? "

3. If we really want us to associate with the company, then we need to associate ourselves with the work that we are doing.

Ex: I work for a product which handles 1 million+ requests per second. Or if you’re working in companies in Atlassian on frontEnd, you can mention like, "I work for a product/library which gives beautiful animations that you see on JIRA" (of-course, Without disclosing internals). This will definitely motivate people to follow you, if you keep saying company name, it just creates a jealousy.

4. My advice to people who follow them is, if you like the content that they are doing and you think it's worth spending your time, please follow them. If you're following them just because they show what goodies that got in a company or how much salary that they are getting or their peers are getting, it's utter waste for you. That information is freely available on sites like GlassDoor.

Choose your idol wisely, don't fall for goodies or package, nobody gives you anything. If somebody is sharing good knowledge, they are the one who should be celebrated and followed.
