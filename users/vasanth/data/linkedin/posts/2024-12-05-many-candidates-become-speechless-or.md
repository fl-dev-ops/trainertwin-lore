Many candidates become speechless or sort of blank in the interview. I have seen many candidates facing this problem. 

1. When I ask an easy question, they answer with a small joy but when difficult questions are thrown at them, they struggle to answer.

2. If the question is twisted further they become speechless and blank all of a sudden.

3. Usually I change the topic in such cases to help the candidate, but all interviewers are not like that. 

If you're facing such a problem, my suggestion is, be calm during the interview. Don't get excited for easy problems or get tensed for a difficult one's. Consider each problem as unique and don't let your mind carry the problem you faced in the previous problem to affect this problem.
