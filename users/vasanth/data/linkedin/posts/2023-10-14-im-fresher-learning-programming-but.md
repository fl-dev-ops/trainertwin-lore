"I'm #fresher learning #programming but I'm fully distracted, I learn one concept and switch to gaming or Instagram. How to overcome this problem ?"
I was watching one of Hitesh Choudhary Instagram reel where he says one of the most difficult things for youths nowadays is "Undivided attention for 2 hours".

Here is what I do to overcome the distraction problem.

1. I keep my mobile away and have a target of not to touch it for 1 hour and after crossing 1 hour, I will extend it to another hour. Doing it for a week will gradually remove the urge to check the mobile constantly.

2. Take a 30/45/60 day challenge on social media. You will write a post everyday about what you learnt that day. Just to maintain the streak you will start studying new things eventually.

3. Write down your goals on a piece of paper and stick it to a wall in front of you. Trust me, looking at it everyday makes you feel guilty if you waste your time. 

4. In all your social media, heavily follow those who you want to be. Though you open those, you should get only knowledgeable content that you're looking for.
