Hard work seems to be disaster, no, I'm not supporting smart work. I'm supporting 4 day work per week, because:
 
Just think 30-40 years back, my grandfather and other
ancestors were farmers. Farmers means, don't think modern farmers who farm 365 days. They used to farm only during monsoon and used to grow one crop. Whether it is rice, sugar cane etc. Many also had a farm where crops like coconut was grown and it used to give crop onetime a year. Putting farm and field together, there was hardly 6 months’ work in the year. Remaining days were mainly maintenance and grazing cattle. They had so much of free time, if there is a
function in the village everybody used to gather and celebrate. If a person in the village dies, then everybody used to gather and console the family. When somebody died, nobody used to say, let me check my calendar and confirm my availability. They used to spend so many days without doing anything, they used chit-chat and live a long happy life (Most lived at least 75+ years). 
 
I don't know, when this hardworking syndrome started
where we are working hard/smart work throughout the year. Even if I say I will give you a treat in a good hotel for free, can you come today ?? most need to check their calendar and confirm their availability. 
 
What do you think ? do we need hard/smart work or less work ? so that we get time for ourselves where we don't do anything. 

#4dayworkweek #hardwork #smartworking
