Another way to look at system design is that there is no frontend or backend. Every backend is also a frontend. Here is how.

1. Your website calls reverse proxy. For reverse proxy your website is a client and it will hit your backend server.

2. For your backend server reverse proxy is a client and it will in turn hit the database.

3. For the database your server is frontend and the database in turn makes calls to the database engine and finally to hardware.

So every backend is also a frontend/client to the next level application. Content credit: Hussein Nasser
