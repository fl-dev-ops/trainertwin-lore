Most of the time questions asked in interviews are not the things that you will work on after joining the company. For some, it feels a stupid #interview process, who asks a question about setTimeOut inside a for loop? in #javascript interview ?? Including myself, very few I think hardly anyone might use such things on day to day work.
But the essence of asking such questions is,

1. Analyse how your mind works in different scenarios.
2. If not exact but definitely similar scenarios will come after joining the organisation and how will you solve it ??
3. How quickly you can solve a problem that you have not encountered in the past !!

So, I'm partially in favour of asking some fancy questions in the interview. What do you think ? mention it in the comments section.
