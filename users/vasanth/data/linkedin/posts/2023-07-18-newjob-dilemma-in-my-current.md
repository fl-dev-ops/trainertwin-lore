#newjob #dilemma In my current organization they are moving me to a good new project and I have got a new job with higher package ? what to do ??
Many a time people get into similar situations and are unable to take the right decision. If you're also in that state, here is the solution.

1. Have a clear purpose before giving the interview. You're changing job for money, promotion or better work etc. Don't attend interview just like that.
2. When you do the first one correctly, if you get a new offer and it meets your requirements, you will be certain to accept it.

Some also feel the current company is like home we are treated well, how to tell them that we are leaving them for a better package. In a nutshell, no serious family exists in a company there can be good colleagues but the ultimate aim of any business is profitability. They wouldn't mind firing you, if business turns into loss. So, do what is good for your career.
