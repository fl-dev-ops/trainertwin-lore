How to choose a right company by analysing the interviews.

1. Good company interviewers will introduce themselves.
2. They don't laugh if you give wrong answer.
3. They will speak politely with you.
4. They will be ok to listen to your approch which is different than the one that they have come prepared.
5. If you ask, they will explain the areas of improvement.

Usually you feel like you can work with them.
