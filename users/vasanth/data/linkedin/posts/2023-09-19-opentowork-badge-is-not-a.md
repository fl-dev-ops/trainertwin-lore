#opentowork badge is not a pride, don't add it to your #linkedin profile unless it is unavoidable. As an #engineer who fired 2 times in his #career I understand the pain of being #laidoff and desperacy that one will be at that time. But adding #opentowork to your #linkedin profile might cause the below problems.

1. People who have no information about #opening might not accept your connection request, as they think you might ask them for a #jobopening.

2. #recruiters who know job openings might try to bargain a lot regarding the salary as you're #openforwork and ready to join almost immediately. 

Instead of adding #openforwork badge, reach out to #recruiters / #hiringmanagers personally over #linkedin or via #email. This has a higher chance of #shortlisting and comparatively you can demand a higher salary.
