In the world of genAI, instead of learning a new programming language, do this instead.

1. Pick a solid problem, that your team / people are facing.
2. Have a vision about how to solve it. (Don't make ai suggestion as your vision)
3. Ideate the solution and check what all technologies are required to build the tool profitably.

Spend time understanding crux of those technologies become fundamentally strong, not necessarily implementation wise you know everything. Ex: how kafka works ? When to use it ? Not every line of code associated with that.

Then start building the solution. This way, your not learning a language for learning sake, instead your building a solution.
