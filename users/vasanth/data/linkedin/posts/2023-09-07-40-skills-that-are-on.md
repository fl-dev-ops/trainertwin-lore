"40 skills that are on top of the resume ??" If I was a part of the hiring committee, I would have straight away rejected this resume. More skills will not shortlist your resume, it's the right skills that do the job. I happened to see this resume recently which has 40 skills which takes up half of the first page of the resume !! 

1. I'm sure this resume was not prepared by the candidate but outsourced to some agency. 
2. If you're someone who has a similar resume, please remove these many skills and add only what is appropriate for the role you're applying for.
