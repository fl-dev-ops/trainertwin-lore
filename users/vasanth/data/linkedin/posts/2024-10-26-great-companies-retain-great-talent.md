Great companies retain great talent. Interviewer asked Stev jobs (founder of Apple) how you built such a great engineering workforce. He said "Initially I searched for highly talented people, I knew they were expensive. I hired them for the cost they were looking for. After I hired quite a few such people they were all extremely happy working for each other. As they were in search of such people, it was hard to find such people in other organisations. In further hiring they made sure only such people are hired, as they don't want to dilute the culture."
When you work for great companies sometimes you feel how everybody is so friendly, regardless of level, how they hear your thoughts. That will be in their DNA ! People who can't fit into that culture, may not stay there for a longer time. 

What do you think ? Does company culture matter for employee retention ?
