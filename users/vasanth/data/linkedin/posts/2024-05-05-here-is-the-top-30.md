Here is the top 30 curated list of #html and #css questions that are commonly asked in the interview. Save the post and thank Vasanth Bhat me later. 

### Easy:

1. **HTML:**
  a) What does HTML stand for?
  b) What is the purpose of the <div> element?
  c) What is the difference between <div> and <span>?
  d) How do you create a hyperlink in HTML?
  e) Explain the use of the <img> tag in HTML.

2. **CSS:**
  a) What is the purpose of CSS?
  b) What is the difference between padding and margin in CSS?
  c) How do you center a div horizontally and vertically in CSS?
  d) Explain the difference between inline, block, and inline-block elements.
  e) How do you include CSS in your HTML document?

### Medium:

3. **HTML:**
  a) Explain the difference between <section>, <article>, <div>, and <aside>.
  b) What is the purpose of the HTML <meta> tag?
  c) Describe the importance of the HTML5 doctype declaration.
  d) How do you create a table in HTML? Mention some common attributes used with the <table> tag.
  e) What is semantic HTML, and why is it important?

4. **CSS:**
  a) What is the "box model" in CSS?
  b) Describe the difference between "inline" and "block" elements in CSS.
  c) How do you target elements with CSS selectors? Provide examples.
  d) What is the importance of the "float" property in CSS? Give an example of its usage.
  e) Explain the concept of specificity in CSS.

### Hard:

5. **HTML:**
  a) What are HTML data attributes? Provide an example of how they can be used.
  b) Explain the difference between GET and POST methods in HTML forms.
  c) How does the HTML5 Canvas element work? Give an example of its usage.
  d) Describe the purpose of the <figure> and <figcaption> elements in HTML5.
  e) What is the difference between cookies, sessionStorage, and localStorage?

6. **CSS:**
  a) Explain the CSS preprocessors SASS and LESS. What are their advantages?
  b) Describe the difference between "em" and "rem" units in CSS.
  c) What is the CSS Grid Layout, and how does it differ from Flexbox?
  d) What is the "cascading" in Cascading Style Sheets (CSS)?
  e) How can you achieve responsive design in CSS? Explain the use of media queries.
