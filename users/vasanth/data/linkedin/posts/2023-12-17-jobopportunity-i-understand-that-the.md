#jobopportunity I understand that the market is challenging and there aren't a lot of openings and even clearing openings which exist also demand a lot of skills. But Even after applying for 600 jobs if you didn't hear from a single company then you're definitely making some mistakes. If you're also in a similar situation, then follow below steps.

1. Stop selecting multiple openings on naukri, monster and click on apply. Instead pick a few openings, understand the JD, make modifications in your resume and apply.
2. If you're not having a decent connection on #linkedin then send maximum connection requests that you can send every week to people from the potential list of companies that you want to join.
3. Whenever you see openings on your potential list of companies then ask for a referral from these companies, referrals always have a higher chance of getting an opportunity than applying on career portals.
4. Do something unique that I haven't specified in the post using your creativity to reach out to recruiters. Your creativity will definitely make your resume get shortlisted.
