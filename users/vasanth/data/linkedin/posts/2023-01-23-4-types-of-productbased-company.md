4 types of #productbased #company that you shouldn't be joining. Must read if you're from service based company and planning to switch to product based company.
There is a myth among #service #company engineers that product based companies are better than service based companies. Definitely it is true to an extent, but not true w.r.t all product based companies. Below are the points that you need to consider before joining a product based company.

1. Tech driven or tech enabled: Join tech driven
2. Profit making v/s loss making: Join profit making
3. Uni-directional innovation and multi directional innovation: Join Uni-directional innovation
