5 real perks of working for a product based company.
.
.
Definitely a good salary, free food, cab, insurance tops the list. But there are other important perks that give you motivation for everyday work 

1. Brilliant people : Who makes sure you always have something new to learn.

2. Flexibility: Nobody minds if you take a few hours off between your working hours.

3. Leaves : In most companies you just inform your manager, there is no process of taking approval. 

4. Open to listen: If you have a new idea and you're talking to your director or VP they will be open to listen to you and guide you through it.

5. Less office politics: As everybody is busy completing their work.
