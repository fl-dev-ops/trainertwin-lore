Who is more successful ? I ask this question very often to myself. Let's take some examples:

1. Most of us are earning more than our primary school teacher. So, are we more successful ? or our primary school teacher who taught thousands (sometimes lacks) of kids in their career ?

2. Some of you might be physically fit, some might be handicapped, Ex: blind, deaf etc. But handicapped person might win a medal for their country in handicapped olympics. So, who is more successful ? physically very fit or one who wins the medal ?

3. Some of you might be very rich, some might be very poor. But the poor have no tension, he sleeps peacefully and has enough food to eat and small shelter to rest. Rich might be having a lot of tension and struggling to sleep. Now who is more successful ? one who has a mansion or one who barely has some shelter ?

These examples can go on and on but you got the gist. At least for me success doesn't mean richness, after a point, humans don't need so much money to be happy and successful. I think success basically means living your highest potential and competing with ourselves everyday. If you're able to earn huge money and fame in this process it's wonderful. 
On the other hand, if you decided not to work hard and live an easy life with your ancestral income without harming anyone, I don't think you're a failure. You have decided to live a life without much struggle and you're working towards it everyday, that should be fine. 

Please tell me, what does success mean to you in the comment section ?
