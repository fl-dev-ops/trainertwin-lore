Mistakes that candidates do in ReactJS interview. 

Interviewer: What are Pure Components ? 

Candidate: Ah, Ah, Ah, In react Pure Components are the, Ah, Ah, Actually, In react, components are generally not pure, basically, when I create components , uh , uh, actually, they are not pure. But we can create them by  uh, uh, using React.memo or extending PureComponent class. 

Is above answer wrong ? no it's right. Will interviewer give full credit to it ? Yes, if interviewer is like me, who has patience to extract answer from all the above sentences. If interviewer is in hurry or they are quite arrogant, they won't give any credit to this. Even if your communication skills are poor how you should answer this question. 

"Ah, Ah, Ah, In react Pure Components are the, Ah, Ah, Actually, In react, components are generally not sure, basically, when I create components , uh , uh, actually, they are not pure. But we can create them by  uh, uh, using React.memo or extending PureComponent class. " 
By now you have tried explaining everything that you know, now summarise it, without saying uh, actually, basically etc. 

"Pure components are those components, that don't re-render as long as the state or prop passed to it are not changing."
