"To type fast and error free, you need to type slow"
A new typist joined an organisation. He was a fast typist and more importantly he was typing without any errors. Soon he became the best typist in the organisation. One day his manager called him and all the other typists of the organisation and asked him to share his secret of fast error free typing. Typist, with some hesitations said, 

"To type fast and error free you need to type slow". 

Everybody gets confused, what is he saying ? how can we type fast
by typing slow. He continues and says, "if you have to type fast and error free, at first you need to type very slow. So that you won't make any mistakes and learn the things properly." End of story
 
For non-musicians like me, when I see somebody playing an instrument, I wonder how they are playing it so fast. Most of the time answer is same "First day nobody plays that fast, they learn it slow and steady". Most of us are now rush this process "We want to learn quickly without learning everything properly". This leads to losing an important opportunity of becoming an expert in the field. So, Please spend right time to learn things.
