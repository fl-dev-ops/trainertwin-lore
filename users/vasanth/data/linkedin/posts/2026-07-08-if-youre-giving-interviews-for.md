If you're giving interviews for senior/lead roles the most important skill that you need to have is not "technical" but "communication". 

Because of my office and personal work, i interact with good number of interview aspirants, most cannot give a minute explanation of their project that an engineer can easily understand. 

Not that they are bad developers, but poor comminicators. At lead/senior roles you need to interact with multiple stack holders ex: design, product, juniors, seniors, managers, directors etc. You need to know the art of communicating the same thing differently to each of them, because they look at the problem from different lenses. One who mastered this will grow rapidly in Indusry.
