#layoffs cannot be controlled but here are a few things that you can do, which will keep you prepared for uncertain situations.

1. After you attain a certain level of financial stability, never spend more than 50% of your salary.

2. Always keep 6 times your monthly spending in your savings account.

3. Try to build some stream of parallel income. It could be your own family business or anything that you have some expertise in.

4. Build a good network of people. This you can get by helping them when you can, so that they will help you when you're in need.

Last point, Be honest, truthful and kind. Nature itself will take care of you in any situation.
