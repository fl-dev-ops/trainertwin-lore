#tcs #infosys not giving joining letters to candidates who were recruited from campus placement by giving some lame reasons like poor academics.

Currently the situation is not that positive, here are my tips for people who are fired or in the potential list.

1. If you're in the potential list of getting fired, don't relax and wait for your turn. Talk to your manager or director and get the right clarity on it. This will help you to plan your preparation properly. 

2. Don't apply for companies desperately. Sit back and re-evaluate are you really ready for interviews ? If not, prepare well and then apply for companies.  Interview rejections will reduce your confidence and thus mental peace. 

3. Never say in the interview that "you're fired". They will try to negotiate as low a salary as possible in that case. 

4. Connect with as many HR's as possible on LinkedIn and watch out for their posts regularly. Rather than waiting for HR's to call you, feel free to contact them and request an interview.  

5. Just because your job is gone, don't be ready to join any company. You should see your career in it and not just a job. If your finances are strong, wait for the right opportunity and then join.
