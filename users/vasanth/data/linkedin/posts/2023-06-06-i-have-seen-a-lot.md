I have seen a lot of #influencers  saying "changing companies frequently is normal and it is good for your growth". If you're a #freshers or junior developer, please don't follow this blindly.

1. There is a difference between #job  and #career. Every company you can do a job, but only in a few companies you can form a #career.

2. In case if your current company is giving you an opportunity to form a good #career you should spend some good time in that company and get some promotion by proving your abilities. 

3. After 10 years when you want to become director or VP etc and if you have a frequent job switch, your chance of getting selected will surely reduce. 

4. You have to settle for some salary. Getting 30-50% cannot happen after certain experience. So, if you're in a good company and doing good work, stick to that.
