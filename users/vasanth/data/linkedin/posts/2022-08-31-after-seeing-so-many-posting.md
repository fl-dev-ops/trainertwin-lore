After seeing so many posting this pic and criticising Bengaluru, this is what I need to say.
 
1. I have said this multiple times in the past, the outer ring road is not Bengaluru. There are a lot of different areas which are least affected by rainfall. So, just because you see a problem in Marathahalli, don't say the entire Bengaluru has a problem.
 
2. This is the road that I and you have travelled for years and there wasn't a flood like this (Even during the rainy season). This year there has been the highest rainfall in Karnataka after many years. Every road is built to operate some amount of surplus water, which is calculated on previous few years rainfall. When it crosses the limit, problems like this will happen.
 
3. People who troll native citizens, who keep saying "weather is good". Second, after traveling to almost all the metro cities in the country, Bengaluru has great weather. You don't have to stay here to enjoy it, if you enjoy it, you can stay.
 
4. Tell me, which metro city in India can handle the rainfall of Bengaluru ? one day heavy rainfall in Mumbai, Delhi, Kolkata, roads will turn into swimming pools. In fact even after having rains for almost 6 months in the year, Bengaluru is trying to accommodate new people with great aspirations, every day.
 
5. Yes, Bengaluru is not Bengaluru just with native citizens. It is so, because of everybody who leaves here. We all contribute to the city and expect good infrastructure. I too expect that, government needs to act swiftly on all these matters and solve the problems quickly.
 
But I see posts with so much hatredness regarding the city. Nobody needs to live in a place which they hate so much. Please stay in the city where you're getting great infrastructure, good weather and which can give you immense opportunities.
