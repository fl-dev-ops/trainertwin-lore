So there are three reasons why recruiters ask this question. The first one is screening. Recruiters want to know earlier in the process what your salary expectations are because that's a very good way for them to screen out certain employees. For example, if your salary expectation is genuinely $150,000 but this job which they are offering will pay $80,000 at best, there is no point in going through the whole recruitment process and interviews and panels and so on. So the second one is negotiation. This question is a very good way for the recruiter to understand where is the base expectation where the negotiation is going to start from. And the third reason is market research. Recruiters are constantly working to understand the benchmark salaries for various jobs their companies offer. It's the constant job of a good HR professional to understand what are the right salary target ranges so that they can attract and also retain the top talent.
