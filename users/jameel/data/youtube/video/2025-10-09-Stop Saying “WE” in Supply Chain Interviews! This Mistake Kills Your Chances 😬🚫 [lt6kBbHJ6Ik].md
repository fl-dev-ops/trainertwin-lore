# Stop Saying “WE” in Supply Chain Interviews! This Mistake Kills Your Chances 😬🚫


## Transcript

Now the next common mistake I see candidates do is overuse the pronoun we versus I. We did this, we did that. Now, of course, if you are giving an example of a question on collaboration or teamwork, of course, using a lot of we makes a lot of sense in that context. But when the question is being asked about action orientation or problem solving or critical thinking, don't use we a lot because when you are using we a lot, that gives an impression to the recruiter that Okay, this person was probably just a part of the team and he or she is explaining what was being done. So he or she is probably reporting the achievement rather than someone who actually took a leadership role in driving that, right?
