# This One Skill Separates Senior Supply Chain Leaders From Everyone Else - Decision Quality

## Transcript

### 00:00:00 · Speaker 1

The next skill is decision quality. Now why is this so important? Because you will be making some very critical decisions almost on a daily or weekly or monthly basis. And you need to build a track record over a period of time of taking good decisions that led to some hard savings or some significant breakthroughs or transformation for your specific supply chains that you are operating in.

### 00:00:25 · Speaker 1

Now this skill is generally called decision quality but some companies have their own names for it. For example Amazon calls this are right a lot. Leaders are right a lot. They have strong judgment and good instincts. They seek diverse perspectives and work to disconfirm their beliefs.
