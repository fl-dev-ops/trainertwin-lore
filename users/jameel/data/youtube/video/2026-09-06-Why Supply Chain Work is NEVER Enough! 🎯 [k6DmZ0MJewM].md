# Why Supply Chain Work is NEVER Enough! 🎯


## Transcript

In supply chain expectations feel unfair. You are expected to work all day managing operations, then stay up at night learning the latest inventory software, transportation management systems or supply chain trends. You're supposed to solve problems before they happen. Nobody sees the hours of coordination, the hundreds of emails, the phone calls with carriers and suppliers. They just see the end result. It feels like no matter what you do, it's never enough. Ship products faster. Okay, now do it cheaper. Cut costs. Great. Now improve quality too. Improve quality? Fantastic. Now reduce lead times. The goal posts keep moving and moving. There's always another metric to hit, another target to reach, another improvement to make.
