How AI is Transforming Contract Enforcement in Supply Chain 🤖📦

Excited to share a powerful insight from my latest discussion with Aileen Sandoval, Senior Digital Supply Chain Leader at Mars! When asked about real-world applications of AI in supply chain, Aileen highlighted how Sourcing and Procurement Managers are leveraging AI for contract enforcement. 📑🤝

AI algorithms can now:
🧠 Analyze service level agreements (SLAs) across vast volumes of contracts
📊 Continuously compare actual supplier performance data against commitments
🚨 Instantly report deviations or non-compliance, allowing teams to act faster and mitigate risks
What used to be a time-consuming task is now getting automated, helping supply chain professionals focus on strategic value instead of manual data checks.
