Supply Chain Fun Fact ✈️📦
Did you know? Airlines started using barcodes on baggage tags in the late 1980s!

● This innovation was inspired by the supply chain industry, where barcodes had been transforming inventory tracking since the 1970s.
● In 1987, the International Air Transport Association (IATA) made barcode baggage tags the industry standard to speed up sorting and enhance security.
● By the early 1990s, airlines like United had fully automated baggage handling using this tech.

Next time you check your luggage, remember: that little barcode is a supply chain innovation helping your bag fly smoothly! 🚀🎫
