Henry Ford wasn't just an auto innovator, but a supply chain mastermind! His assembly line approach to JIT revolutionized car manufacturing, reducing the average time of vehicle production from 12 hours to 93 minutes  in the mid 1920s long before Toyota took this concept to the next level! Today a Ford-F150 truck comes off the line every 53 seconds at their Dearborn Michigan plant which I had the opportunity to visit this week!
