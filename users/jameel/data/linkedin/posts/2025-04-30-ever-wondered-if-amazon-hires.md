Ever wondered if Amazon hires supply chain professionals from outside the tech or e-commerce world? In my latest podcast short, I ask Umair Awan-an Amazon veteran about Amazon’s approach to hiring talent from different industries.

Amazon values diverse backgrounds and actively seeks candidates with experience from a wide range of industries, not just those with a traditional tech or retail background. This openness is part of what makes Amazon’s supply chain so dynamic and innovative. If you’re coming from manufacturing, consulting, FMCG, or even unrelated sectors, you still have a shot-as long as you can demonstrate Amazon’s Leadership Principles and a passion for problem-solving in supply chain.
