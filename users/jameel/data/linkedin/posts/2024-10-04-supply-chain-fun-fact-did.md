💡Supply Chain Fun Fact💡
Did you know that in the United States, toys, clothing, and juvenile products (such as cribs and car seats) are among the most frequently recalled categories?

The Consumer Product Safety Commission (CPSC) in the United States receives millions of reports of product-related injuries each year. This often leads to thousands of product recalls.

So much so that giant retailers like Costco have Recall Notice Boards in their stores!
