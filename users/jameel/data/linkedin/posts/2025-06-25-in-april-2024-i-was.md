In April 2024, I was close to quitting YouTube after three years of inconsistent content on soft skills. Then I got great advice: “Don’t give up—niche down to your area of expertise.” For me, that’s supply chain! 🚚

Today, I’ve grown my channel 10x—from 1,000 to 10,000 subscribers—and coached over 150 SCM professionals, many securing their dream jobs! 🎉

Here are five lessons I’ve learned:
	1.	🎯 Focus on your niche Every pro has an “unfair advantage.” Messi has soccer; what’s yours? Stick to your strength and build a loyal following. “What’s obvious to you may be fascinating to others.” —Ali Abdaal

	2.	📅 Define and commit to your consistency Algorithms reward regularity. Don’t wait for perfection—just start. I commit to one video a week (plus shorts), balancing work and family. “Start before you are ready.” —Sean Cannell

	3.	📚 Lifelong learning is a must Skills fade fast, especially with AI. I’m always learning—editing, camera work, new tools. Keep upskilling! “Wisdom is not a product of schooling but of the lifelong attempt to acquire it.” —Albert Einstein

	4.	🧑‍💼 Leadership can feel lonely Starting something new invites doubt. If you believe in your mission, keep going. “Leadership feels lonely.” —Kobe Bryant

	5.	🤝 Build your support system Great mentors and friends kept me going: HAMMAD SIDDIQUI  Wali Khan  Bahroz Abbas Hussain Syed Nadeem, ACMA, CFM Dr. Muddassir Ahmed and Yasin Nizami. Surround yourself with people on similar journeys—online or off. “You are the average of the five people you spend the most time with.” —Jim Rohn

Thanks to the internet, your support network can be anywhere—just connect and engage! 🌐
