Ever wondered how a single box of coffee travels across three oceans without getting permanently stuck at customs?

It all comes down to a 6-digit universal language created in 1988 by the World Customs Organization (WCO): The HS Code (Harmonized System). 🗺️
Before 1988, border control was a chaotic mess of translation errors and mismatched tariffs. Today, HS codes give global trading nations a unified way to manage duties, track trade data, and keep compliance tightly regulated.
