I am excited that we announced an agreement to acquire Hotel Chocolat, a leading UK premium chocolate brand. Subject to shareholder and regulatory approvals, this strategic acquisition will develop and grow Mars’ portfolio and will build on our strong track record of nurturing entrepreneurial businesses, including KIND, Nature’s Bakery, Tru Fru, Champion Petfoods and PrettyLitter to mention a few recent additions. We see great long-term potential for a business aligned with our principles and has a strong focus on quality, sustainability and innovation.
Simply a fantastic addition to our family of businesses and it will be exciting to help Hotel Chocolat reach full potential and delight more consumers.
