🌎✨ Supply Chain Fun Fact: California & Canada Edition! 🇨🇦🤝🇺🇸

Did you know? If California were its own country, it would be Canada’s second-largest trading partner, with over $53 billion in two-way trade annually! 🚢📦

Here’s the trade breakdown:
Canada’s top export to California: Energy products like oil and gas, valued at around $5 billion per year ⚡️🛢️
California’s top export to Canada: Computers and electronic products, worth nearly $4.5 billion annually 💻📱

This cross-border exchange supports millions of jobs and keeps both economies buzzing. Next time you charge your phone or fill your tank in California, you might just be part of this powerhouse partnership!
