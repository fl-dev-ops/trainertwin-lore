🚛 Road Trips, Freight, and a Vision from the 1950s

Ever stopped at a roadside diner or gas station on a U.S. road trip and wondered how everything gets there? 🤔🛣️

Here's a fun supply chain fact:

Trucks move over 70% of all U.S. freight 🚚📦, with long-haul drivers clocking over 100,000 miles per year 🛤️✈️ to keep shelves stocked and the country moving. This powerhouse industry employs 1 in every 15 working Americans 👷‍♂️👷‍♀️💪.

And none of this would be possible without Dwight D. Eisenhower’s visionary push in the 1950s 🏗️🛣️ to build the Interstate Highway System—one of the biggest drivers (literally and figuratively) of U.S. economic progress 🚦📈.

Next time you’re cruising the highway, remember:
🚚 That truck next to you is part of a supply chain that never sleeps. 🌙🌎
