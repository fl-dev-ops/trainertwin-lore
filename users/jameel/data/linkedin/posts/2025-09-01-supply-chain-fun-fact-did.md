🔔 Supply Chain Fun Fact! 🔔
Did you know the Pullman Railroad Strike of 1894, which took place in Pullman, a company town near Chicago, was a pivotal moment not only for labor rights but also for supply chains across the U.S.?

This major strike disrupted key rail transportation networks vital for moving raw materials and finished goods nationwide. The strike’s widespread impact on supply chains helped spur the establishment of Labor Day as a national holiday—a day to honor the workers who keep supply chains flowing every day.

It's a powerful reminder of how closely labor movements and supply chain resilience have been intertwined from the very start.
