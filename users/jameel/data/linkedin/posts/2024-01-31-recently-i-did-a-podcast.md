Recently I did a podcast with  Wali Khan  where we covered Personal Finance Skills in details. During the podcast Wali did share the Top 3 Personal Finance Skills for young professionals who are in college or have just started their professional journey. 

Wali did talk about the importance of Tracing and Tracking expenses, keeping a check on your credit card spending and ensuring you're paying the full bill on time. He also covered the importance of diversifying one's investment options and not over investing in one area such as real estate since a lot of people do that mistake and over index in one investment vehicle. Lastly he did talk about the importance of constantly learning about new investment opportunities and allocating a small budget to experiment new investment ideas.
