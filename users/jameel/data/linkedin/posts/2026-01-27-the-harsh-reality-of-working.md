🚨 The Harsh Reality of Working in Supply Chain (no sugarcoating)
Spoiler: it’s not all fancy offices, global travel, and million‑dollar strategy meetings.

Most people only talk about the positives. But that paints a false picture.
Here’s what they don’t tell you before you start:

⚠️ Burnout is real
Urgent shipments, delayed suppliers, warehouse at capacity, rising costs — all at once
One day logistics, next day procurement, inventory, crisis management
Expected to be proactive, reactive, and “psychic” — all simultaneously

⚠️ Supply chains are fragile
One delay in China → quarter off track
One truck breakdown → thousands in extra costs
One supplier bankruptcy → weeks of chaos

⚠️ When things go wrong, everyone looks at you
👉 Sales: “When can we deliver?”
👉 Finance: “Why are we expediting?”
👉 Operations: “Where are our materials?”
I’ve worked 50–60 hour weeks in peak.
Taken calls at 11 PM over customs holds.
Weekend work to keep production lines alive.
