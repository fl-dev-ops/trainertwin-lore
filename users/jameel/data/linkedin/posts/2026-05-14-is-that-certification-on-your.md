Is that certification on your resume helping or hurting you? 🧐

I recently sat down with HR Leader Patrick Dedrick, CPCC, ACC to discuss a "spicy" topic that hits my inbox every single day: Supply Chain Certifications.
Patrick shared a perspective that every job seeker needs to hear. Certifications are powerful tools, but they aren't "magic wands" for your career.

✅ WHEN THEY HELP:
Early Career: They build the "foundational confidence" you might lack without years of experience.
The Career Jump: They act as an "order qualifier" to get your foot in the door during a pivot (like moving from IT to SCM).
Third-Party Proof: They offer instant recognition of your commitment to the craft.

❌ WHEN THEY DON'T:
The Experience Trap: Once you reach a certain leadership level, your track record of results outweighs the letters after your name.
The "Lapse" Factor: If you aren't maintaining them or applying the knowledge, they become background noise.

The Golden Nugget: Don't just chase the title—chase the confidence the knowledge gives you to "punch above your weight" in high-level meetings. 🥊
