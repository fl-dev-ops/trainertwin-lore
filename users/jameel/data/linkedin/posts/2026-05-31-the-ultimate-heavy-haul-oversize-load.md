The ultimate heavy-haul oversize load. ✈️🚀
Taking in this incredible view at the Johnson Space Center Houston today during the SupplyChainPoint  conference: a modified Boeing 747 piggybacking the Space Shuttle Independence.

As a supply chain professional, all I see is a masterclass in transportation logistics. When NASA needed to move a spacecraft across the country, standard freight wasn't an option. They had to rewrite the rules of asset modification, aerodynamics, and weight distribution—with absolutely zero margin for error.
