Did you know Tampa wasn’t just a beach town—it was once the cigar capital of the world? 🌎🌴

In the early 1900s, Tampa, Florida earned the nickname “Cigar City” as it became a global cigar supply hub. 🚢🚂 Thousands of workers in more than 200 factories hand‑rolled hundreds of millions of cigars each year, using the city’s port and rail connections to ship product worldwide.

From a supply chain perspective, Tampa was an early example of:
📍 Strategic location selection (port + rail = global reach)
👷 Labor specialization at scale (hand‑rolled, high‑quality production)
🏭 Vertical integration around a single flagship product (cigars as the anchor industry)

Fast forward to today, and a giant flamingo sculpture 🦩 at Tampa International Airport has become a fun, modern symbol welcoming travelers into this historic cigar capital—a reminder that every city has a supply chain story behind its identity.
