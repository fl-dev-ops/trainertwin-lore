China, once the biggest buyer of US soybeans, slapped tariffs up to 34%, slashing purchases nearly to zero. 🚫🌱

🇧🇷🇦🇷 China shifted demand to Brazil and Argentina, hitting US farmers with major losses. Under pressure, the US negotiated a truce where China agreed to resume significant soybean buys, leading the US to drop some tariffs. 🤝✨
