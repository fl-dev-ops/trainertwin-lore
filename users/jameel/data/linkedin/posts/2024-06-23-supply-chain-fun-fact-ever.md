💡Supply Chain Fun Fact 💡 
Ever wondered why milk is sold in bags in Ontario and not in the US or even rest of Canada? Well it’s another fun SCM story…

Historical Timing: Bagged milk's introduction coincided with Canada's switch to the metric system in the 1970s. Bags were easier and cheaper to adapt to metric sizes compared to existing glass bottles or jugs.

Regional Preference: While bags offered some advantages, consumer habits varied across the country. Western Canada and other areas preferred the convenience and sturdiness of cartons and jugs.

Deposit Regulations: Some provinces, like Ontario, implemented deposit fees on jugs but not bags. This incentivized the use of bags for some consumers.

Cultural Tradition: Over time, bagged milk became ingrained in the cultural identity of some regions, particularly Ontario, where it's seen as a practical and familiar choice.
