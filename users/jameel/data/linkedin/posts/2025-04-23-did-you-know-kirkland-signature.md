🛒 Did you know? Kirkland Signature — Costco private label—now generates an estimated $85 billion in annual sales, rivaling consumer goods giants like Procter & Gamble whose revenue is about $87.5 billion! It’s worth noting that Costco consolidated its private label under this name only in 1995 while P&G was founded in 1837.

🏷️ This is a testament to Costco’s powerful strategy of uniting private label products under one brand, making Kirkland a household name and a major force in the industry.

⚡ For CPG giants, this is a wake-up call: continuous innovation is essential, or private labels could dominate commoditized categories.
