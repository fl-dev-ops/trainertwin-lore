🔍 Why is CPIM more “supply” heavy than “demand”?
While CPIM does cover both demand and supply planning, the core emphasis is on inventory accuracy, supply planning, and resource management. It’s designed to help you master the flow of materials and information within the supply chain-making it especially valuable for supply planners, inventory analysts, and anyone working closely with ERP systems.

If you’re aiming to build a solid foundation in supply chain planning and inventory management, this is the certification to check out!
