✈️ When “Profits Over People” Becomes a Supply Chain Lesson
Southwest Airlines just closed a 50-year chapter — ending open seating and introducing bag fees to chase higher revenue.

💡 2025 by the numbers:
📈 Revenue up ~3% to $28.1B
📉 Net income down ~5% to $441M

The airline gained margin levers but paid the price in customer trust and loyalty — a shift every supply chain leader can relate to.

In supply chain terms:
💸 Short-term profit plays (like surcharges or segmentation) can strain long-term demand.
⚖️ Efficiency isn’t just throughput—it’s sustaining goodwill across the value chain.
🌊 The “bullwhip effect” applies to trust too: sudden policy shifts can ripple into lasting perception damage.
Sometimes, the most strategic inventory isn’t stock—it’s goodwill.
