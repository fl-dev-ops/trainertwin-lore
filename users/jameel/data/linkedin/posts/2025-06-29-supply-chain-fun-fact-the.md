☕️🚢 Supply Chain Fun Fact: The Origin of Mocha Coffee & Yemen’s Historic Trade Monopoly 🌍📦

Did you know the famous Mocha coffee gets its name from Yemen’s port of Al Mokha? From the 16th to 19th centuries, Al Mokha was the world’s coffee export hub—beans from Yemen and Ethiopia were shipped exclusively from here. To protect their monopoly, Yemeni traders and Ottoman rulers sterilized beans by boiling or roasting them, stopping others from growing coffee plants from exported seeds.

The name "Mocha coffee" comes directly from this legendary port, and its legacy lives on in the rich, chocolatey coffee flavors and the popular mocha drinks we enjoy today.

As a supply chain professional, this is a brilliant example of how strategic logistics, trade policy, and product handling can shape global markets and build lasting brand legacies.
