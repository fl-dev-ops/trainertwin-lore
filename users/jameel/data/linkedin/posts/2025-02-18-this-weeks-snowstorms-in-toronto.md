This week’s snowstorms in Toronto and Southern Ontario were among the worst in decades, with some areas receiving up to 40 cm of snow! The City of Toronto alone saw a total accumulation of 63 cm over the week, leading to significant disruptions in transportation and daily life. 

While Canada 🇨🇦 is well-equipped to handle snow, such exceptional storms can severely disrupt supply chains. For instance, even a modest snowfall of 5 cm can cause a 200% surge in demand for snow shovels and a 150% increase in demand for salt in Toronto. This spike often strains supply chains, causing shortages and delays.

Key Figures to Consider:
Average cost of a snow shovel: $30
Average cost of a bag of salt: $15
Estimated cleanup cost for a major snowstorm in Toronto: $10 million!

These numbers underscore the economic and logistical challenges posed by severe winter weather. Retailers and distributors must anticipate these fluctuations to ensure adequate stock and minimize disruptions.
