When you go to school to let's say to study Engineering they will give you a clearly defined curriculum of what you will study in each year. Same thing with Med School and other professions. However Soft Skills or Human Skills which are equally important in the workplace are not taught anywhere. Therefore this model is designed to build your awareness on what the critical soft skills are at each level of your career and how you can prepare for them ahead of time!
