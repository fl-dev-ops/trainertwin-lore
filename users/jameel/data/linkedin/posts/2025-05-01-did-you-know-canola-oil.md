DID YOU KNOW? 🌱
Canola oil is a proud Canadian innovation-developed by Canadian farmers and scientists in the 1970s. The name “Canola” combines “Can” for Canada 🇨🇦 and “ola” for oil.

Today, canola drives an economic impact of nearly $46 billion, nearly matching the GDP of Latvia 🇱🇻, and supports thousands of jobs across the supply chain.

While the USA 🇺🇸 remains the largest importer-taking almost 95% of Canada’s canola exports-there’s significant growth potential in markets like Mexico 🇲🇽, China 🇨🇳, and South Korea 🇰🇷, all major importers of cooking oils.
