💡Supply Chain Fun Fact 💡 
Believe it or not, those juicy raspberries you love might be living the high life in Canada! Forget hopping on a truck, these berries travel first class – by airplane!

Here's the juicy details: since these delicate fruits bruise easily and don't last long (average shelf life around 3-5 days), Canadian stores far from the farms get their stock flown in. That's right, next time you pop a raspberry in your mouth, imagine it cruising at 30,000 feet just a few days earlier.
