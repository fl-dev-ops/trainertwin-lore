🩸🚚 The Supply Chain of Blood Donations is as fascinating as it is lifesaving:

Canadian Blood Services transforms each donation into three vital components that are shipped to 700+ hospitals across the country:

🧊 Red blood cells: stored at 1–10°C
🧬 Platelets: stored at 20–24°C (with only a 7-day shelf life)
❄️ Plasma: stored at -18°C or colder

With platelets expiring so quickly, the system requires ⚡ultra-fast cross-country delivery and 🔄 dynamic inventory balancing—a true example of supply chain agility in action.

But the impact goes beyond logistics. ❤️ Regular blood donation is associated with up to an 88% reduction in heart attack risk and each donation saves up to 🌍3 lives!
