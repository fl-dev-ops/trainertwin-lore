🚨 Why Do So Many AI Projects Fail in Supply Chains? 🚨

AI has the potential to revolutionize supply chains — from demand forecasting to inventory optimization. Yet, the reality is stark: 70-85% of AI initiatives don’t make it to full production or fail to deliver expected value.

Here’s what’s holding us back:

🔍 Poor Data Quality & Silos – Without clean, integrated data, AI is flying blind.
🏢 Legacy Systems – Outdated infrastructure can’t keep up with AI demands.
🎯 Lack of Clear Strategy – AI must align with business goals, not just be a shiny pilot project.
👥 Incompetent Leadership – Many AI projects fail because leaders lack systems thinking and the ability to drive holistic change across people, processes, and technology. Without leadership that understands how to integrate AI strategically, efforts stall or amplify inefficiencies.
🔄 Change Management Gaps – Technology alone isn’t enough; people and processes must evolve too.

If we want AI to truly transform supply chains, we need to tackle these challenges head-on — investing in data, breaking down silos, and committing to long-term change led by capable leaders.
