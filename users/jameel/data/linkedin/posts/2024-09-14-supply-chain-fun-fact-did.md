💡Supply Chain Fun Fact💡
Did you know that Airbus A220 is the first airbus aircraft to be fully designed and manufactured outside Europe? It’s a great example of supply chain collaboration between Canada 🇨🇦 and the EU 🇪🇺. A few weeks ago I had an opportunity to fly on one of these planes and here are some supply chain fun facts about it: 

Opportunity out of a crisis: The Airbus A220 was originally designed and developed by 🇨🇦 Canada’s Bombardier as the CSeries aircraft. When Bombardier faced financial difficulties, Airbus stepped in and acquired a majority stake in the program. 

Highly Fuel Efficient: Airbus claims that the A220 offers a 20% fuel burn advantage compared to similar-sized aircraft, including the 737 MAX. This translates to lower operating costs for airlines and reduced environmental impact.

Just-in-Time Delivery: Airbus employs a just-in-time delivery system for the A220's parts, minimizing inventory costs and ensuring that components arrive precisely when needed to keep the assembly line running smoothly.

As of August 2024, there are approximately 351 Airbus A220 family aircraft in commercial service with 20 airlines including Delta, Air France and Air Canada.
