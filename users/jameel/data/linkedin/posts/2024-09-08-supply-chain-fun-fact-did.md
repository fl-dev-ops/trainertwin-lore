💡Supply Chain Fun Fact💡 Did you know that Iowa and Nebraska are the top two 🌽corn-producing states in the US, accounting for nearly 40% of the total US corn production? Corn is a crucial component of our food system, serving as the primary source of livestock feed, a key ingredient in ethanol blended fuel, and a major contributor to corn syrup used in countless food products. The combined corn production of Iowa and Nebraska exceeds that of Brazil 🇧🇷, the world's third-largest corn producer after USA🇺🇸 & China🇨🇳.
