🚩 Most common resume mistake in supply chain hiring? Making it too long!
After 100+ coaching calls with supply chain professionals, I’ve noticed a trend: candidates with less than 5 years’ experience trying to fit their story into 2-3 pages—and those with decades of experience submitting 5+ pages! This can cost you interviews.

Here’s my golden rule:
➡️ Less than 5 years: 1 page
➡️ 5+ years (even 35!): 2 pages max

Why does resume length matter so much? Recruiters look for clarity, relevance, and impact—NOT endless detail. Keeping it concise ensures your real achievements shine and your application gets noticed.
