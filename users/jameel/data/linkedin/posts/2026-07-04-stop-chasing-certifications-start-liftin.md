Stop chasing certifications. Start lifting boxes. 📦🚛
If you are a fresh supply chain graduate, the biggest trap you can fall into is looking straight for a comfortable desk role or jumping right into a PhD.
In this quick clip from our latest podcast episode, Kevin Lawton, CLTD (host of The New Warehouse Podcast) drops a truth bomb that every ambitious logistics student needs to hear:

👉 Go work on the floor.
Whether it is a bustling warehouse, a high-volume manufacturing facility, or a fast-paced trucking company—getting your hands dirty early on is non-negotiable.

Why does this matter so much? 
🔹 Reality vs. Theory: There is a massive disconnect between boardroom leadership and the physical reality of operations. 
🔹 Future-Proofing Leadership: When you move into a senior, office-based role, your decisions will trickle down to the floor. If you understand the physical constraints, your system implementations and efficiency projects will actually succeed. 
🔹 The Experience Dividend: There is zero parallel to raw, on-the-ground experience in supply chain. It pays massive dividends for the rest of your career.
