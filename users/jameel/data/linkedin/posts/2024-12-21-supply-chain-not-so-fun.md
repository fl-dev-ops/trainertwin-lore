💡Supply Chain (Not so) Fun Fact💡Why Economists Oppose US Tariffs on Canada & Mexico: A Toyota Rav4 Case Study

Most economists are critical of a 25% tariff on goods from Canada and Mexico due to the potential for significant economic disruption.

Let's consider the Toyota Rav4: with its engine made in the US, parts sourced from Mexico, and final assembly in Canada, a 25% tariff could have a cascading effect.

Increased Costs: With the parts and final product crossing borders multiple times the final cost impact to the consumer of a 25% tariff could go upto 30-40%!

Reduced Demand: This price hike could significantly impact demand, hurting both consumers and the automotive industry.

This scenario highlights the interconnected nature of North American trade and the potential negative consequences of protectionist policies. Hopefully this is nothing more than a bargaining tactic!
