Today, Mars publishes a comprehensive Net Zero Roadmap with a more aggressive target to reach a 50% reduction by 2030. I am proud to report that since our 2015 baseline, Mars has reduced our GHG emissions by 8% in absolute terms across our entire value chain in a period where our business grew 60%. You may argue that this is not a lot, but we have shown that we can decouple growth from emissions, and we are now accelerating that journey, investing an additional $1bn just in the next three years to deliver on our promises. 

I realize that it is a challenging economic time for many, but climate action can’t wait. And consumers all over the world expect action from business and government alike. In fact, a new Ipsos survey commissioned by Mars ahead of next week’s New York Climate Week shows that 69% of adults across the world's seven largest economies think businesses should focus more or as much on tackling climate change as economic challenges. That’s a clear signal, and we’re listening. 

Nobody’s perfect, and neither are we, but I am happy to share our Roadmap to Net Zero, so we and others can continue to learn from each other and accelerate the journey to zero. 

Learn more about our Net Zero Roadmap here: www.mars.com/netzero2050
