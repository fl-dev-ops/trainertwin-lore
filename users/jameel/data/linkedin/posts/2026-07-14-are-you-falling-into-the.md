Are you falling into the "organizational bubble" trap? 🫧

If you have been in a supply chain role for 5, 7, or 10+ years, it is incredibly easy to lose your professional curiosity. You master your company’s internal processes, use their specific ERP, and assume you are on the cutting edge. 

But here is the hard truth: 

Companies are adopting technologies—like AI, advanced robotics, and sustainable logistics—faster than universities can write the textbooks, and faster than average organizations can build internal training.

In my podcast episode with Kevin Lawton, CLTD, host of The New Warehouse, he shared a vital piece of advice for experienced professionals looking to futureproof their careers:

💡 You must actively build your own "informal MBA" outside your company walls. 

To stay ahead of the curve, you need to deliberately seek out external sources of knowledge:
🎧 Podcasts – Get raw, real-time insights from global peers.
📺 YouTube – Visualise how other companies are implementing new systems.
🤝 Industry Communities & Events – Step out of your daily routine to network and trade ideas.

If you only know what your current employer teaches you, your skills have an expiration date. Don't let someone else define your value in the market.
