2️⃣ Rigorous Quality Control: Trader Joe's maintains stringent quality standards throughout the supply chain, ensuring product safety and ethical sourcing.
3️⃣ Supplier Performance: They closely monitor supplier performance, evaluating factors like on-time delivery, quality, and sustainability compliance.
