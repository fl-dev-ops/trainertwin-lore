💡Supply Chain Fun Fact💡
The National & International Coffee Day is coming up and they say “America 🇺🇸 runs on Dunkin!” 

But did you know that Dunkin' Donuts' coffee beans are sourced from over 12 countries, including Brazil, Colombia, El Salvador, Guatemala, Honduras, Mexico, Nicaragua, and Peru? This diverse sourcing allows Dunkin' to create their signature blend, which is a combination of Arabica beans known for their smooth flavor and aroma. To ensure quality and sustainability, Dunkin' works with coffee farmers and organizations like the Rainforest Alliance to promote ethical and environmentally friendly practices.

As of 2023, Dunkin' Donuts holds approximately 26% of the U.S. coffee shop market share. This makes it the second largest coffee chain in the country, following Starbucks. While Starbucks maintains a dominant position, Dunkin' has steadily gained ground, especially in the Northeast and Southeast regions of the United States.

National Coffee Day in the US is on 29th Sep while 1st October is International Coffee Day!
