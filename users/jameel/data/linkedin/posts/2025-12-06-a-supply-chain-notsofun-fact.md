A supply chain not‑so‑fun fact: every five seconds, the world loses an area of fertile soil roughly the size of a soccer field due to erosion. That “disappearing ground” quietly contributes to higher food prices, more stockouts, and riskier sourcing decisions across global supply chains.  

As degraded soils reduce yields by 30–50% in severely affected regions, buyers must manage more volatile harvest volumes, pay higher costs for inputs and freight, and constantly reconfigure sourcing just to keep shelves and production lines running.  

World Soil Day, observed on December 5th, aims to raise awareness about the vital role soil health plays in sustaining our food systems and supply chain resilience.
