Did you know that the world's very first documented supply chain dates back to...ancient Egypt? Around 2600 BC, the Egyptians built the Great Pyramids. These massive structures required a complex and well-coordinated supply chain to move millions of tons of stone. Imagine the logistics of planning, quarrying, transporting, and precisely placing those giant blocks – all without modern technology!

In my early career days with P&G and Mars I had the opportunity to travel to Cairo several times as it was the Centre of Excellence (CoE) for a lot of great supply chain practices!

A big shout out to all the Egyptian Supply Chain Professionals, some of whom are my great friends!
