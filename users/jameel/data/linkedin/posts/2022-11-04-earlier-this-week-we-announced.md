Earlier this week we announced that Champion Petfoods will join Mars Petcare. I am personally excited to welcome Champion’s more than 800 talented associates to Mars once the transaction closes. The Champion team and its amazing brands – ORIJEN and ACANA – have been pioneers in developing innovative, high-quality and premium pet food in the natural category that pet lovers around the world trust. We look forward to working together to support pet parents and fulfill our Purpose to make A BETTER WORLD FOR PETS.
