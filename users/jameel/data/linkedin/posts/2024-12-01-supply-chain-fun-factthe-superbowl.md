💡Supply Chain Fun Fact💡The “Superbowl” of Supply Chain is here! 

Cyber Monday: The Digital Shopping Bonanza
Back in 2005, a clever marketing team at the National Retail Federation (NRF) coined the term "Cyber Monday." Little did they know, they were unleashing a digital shopping beast!

A Digital Shopping Spree:
Cyber Monday has evolved into a global shopping extravaganza. In 2023, shoppers splurged a whopping $12.4 billion on Cyber Monday alone! That's more than a billion dollars more than Black Friday! 

Mobile Madness:
In 2023, nearly 60% of Cyber Monday sales were made on smartphones. That's a lot of people scrolling, tapping, and checking out from the comfort of their couches or on the go!
