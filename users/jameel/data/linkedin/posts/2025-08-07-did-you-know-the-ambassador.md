🚛 Did you know? The Ambassador Bridge between Detroit and Windsor handles around 25% of all trade by value between the US and Canada—making it the busiest international border crossing for commerce in North America.

📦 Every day, goods worth $300–$390 million cross this vital link, including:
Automotive parts 🚗
Food 🥪
Medical supplies 🏥
This underscores its critical role in "just-in-time" manufacturing supply chains, especially for the automotive industry.

⚠️ But with the trade war looming and tariffs threatening, the smooth flow of these goods could face disruption—impacting industries, logistics, and communities on both sides of the border.
