Hackettstown in New Jersey is an historic manufacturing site for Mars in the U.S. as the home of M&Ms®. This week, we completed a $70M investment in manufacturing efficiencies and advancements in food safety - with a cherry on the cake being a new Research and Development Innovation Studio including a new test kitchen and packaging lab. The capabilities in the Innovation Studio will support other Mars products produced in the U.S. including M&M’S®, SNICKERS®, TWIX®, MILKY WAY®, SKITTLES®, STARBURST®, EXTRA®, and ALTOIDS® as well as new product innovations. Well done to all the Associates at the Hackettstown site!
