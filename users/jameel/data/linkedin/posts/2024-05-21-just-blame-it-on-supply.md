‘Just blame it on Supply Chain’: When you ask a lot of companies about the cause of price increases in the recent years, most blame it on supply chain transportation costs in the post pandemic context.. Truth be told that’s a convinient excuse because most sea-freight rates have very much stabilized. Even things are beginning to normalize after the recent Red Sea disruptions. The question is will the companies who took massive price increases behind these spikes, take those prices down now or just absorb these cost tailwinds to help their profits? Unfortunately it’s more the latter! Classical example of ‘Just blame it on supply chain’ :)
