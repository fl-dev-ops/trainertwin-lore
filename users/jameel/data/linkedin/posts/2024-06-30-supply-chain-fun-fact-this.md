💡Supply Chain Fun Fact 💡 This cup of coffee has been on an epic adventure! ☕️ The average coffee bean travels a whopping 15,000 to 20,000 kilometers (that's 9,300 to 12,400 miles for our American friends) before it lands in your mug. Talk about a jet-setting bean! ✈️

Coffee may grow in tropical paradises, but we sure do love it everywhere. If you are drinking coffee in Europe or North America, that little bean probably did some serious mileage to get there.  Imagine the stories it could tell!
