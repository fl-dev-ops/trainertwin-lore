🍁 Fun Supply Chain Insight for Thanksgiving! 🚛

Canadian Thanksgiving happens earlier than the U.S. because 🇨🇦’s harvest season ends sooner 🌾 — the northern climate brings an earlier bounty of crops like pumpkins, corn, and cranberries. 🥧

That early harvest kicks off Canada’s agri-supply chain first, with fresh produce and goods moving across the country — and even south to 🇺🇸 for American Thanksgiving in November! 🦃

A true cross-border “farm-to-feast” logistics story connecting two harvest seasons. 🌍
