🔑 Supply chain interview mistake #2: Focusing ONLY on the technical details!

I see many candidates go deep into the “how” but skip the “who.” In interviews, the best responses balance technical achievements (about 60%) with the impact on people and the organization (about 40%).

✅ Why? Hiring managers want to know not only what you accomplished, but how it benefited teams, customers, and the business overall.

In my latest YouTube Short, I break down how to craft answers that cover both sides—and show why this balance will set you apart in supply chain, logistics, planning, and procurement roles.
