💡Supply Chain Fun Fact 💡 : Today is World Chocolate Day but did you know that the majority of the world's cocoa (over 70%) comes from just two countries: Ghana and Côte d'Ivoire, located in West Africa.

This means that the journey your chocolate bar takes likely began on a cocoa farm in one of these countries! The beans are then harvested, processed, shipped across continents, transformed into chocolate, and finally delivered to stores around the world.

It's a fascinating example of how a global supply chain brings a beloved treat from farm to table (or should we say, hand to mouth?)
