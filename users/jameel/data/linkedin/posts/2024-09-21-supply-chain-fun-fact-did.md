💡Supply Chain Fun Fact💡
Did you know that railroads in the USA 🇺🇸 and Canada 🇨🇦 are managed by private companies? This is a significant difference compared to many European 🇪🇺 countries, China 🇨🇳, Japan 🇯🇵 & India 🇮🇳 where railways are state-owned or operated by public entities.

The largest private operator in the US is Union Pacific with a 23% market share. Union Pacific controls a significant portion of the western U.S., serving major markets like Los Angeles, Chicago, and Seattle. I had the opportunity to visit its HQ in Omaha, Nebraska recently. 

Other players are BNSF, CSX, CP and CPKC. The last two primarily operate in Canada. 🍁
