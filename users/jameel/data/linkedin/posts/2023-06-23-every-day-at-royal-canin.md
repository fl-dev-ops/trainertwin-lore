Every day at Royal Canin is an opportunity for us to live our purpose:  A Better World For Pets. And we know that pets make our world better. I was able to witness the power of our purpose this week during a visit to the Jefferson City Correctional Center. Royal Canin is the sole nutrition provider of the Missouri Puppies for Parole program, which pairs shelter dogs with offenders in the prison system across the state who train dogs to become more adoptable. This is Mutuality at its finest. Since the program’s inception in 2010, more than 7,000 dogs have been adopted through Puppies for Parole, and offenders are able to find a purpose and means for giving back to the community.  While our team was thanked profusely for our support of the program, which runs on donations alone, I think we are the ones who owe our thanks – for sharing with us the heart behind this program and the way it transforms the lives of people and pets.
