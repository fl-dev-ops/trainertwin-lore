Did you know that Tesla's Cybertruck boasts a unique, custom-made stainless steel exoskeleton? 🤯

Here's the supply chain breakdown:

Strategic Sourcing: Tesla is sourcing this specialized steel from multiple vendors, including Steel Dynamics.

Localization Advantage: Steel Dynamics has a massive $1.7B facility just 167 miles from Tesla's Gigafactory in Texas. This proximity minimizes transportation costs and lead times, optimizing the inbound logistics for Cybertruck production.

Vertical Integration: By developing a custom steel grade, Tesla is exercising a degree of vertical integration, potentially giving them greater control over quality, cost, and supply chain resilience.

This strategic sourcing move showcases Tesla's commitment to innovation and efficiency in its supply chain.
