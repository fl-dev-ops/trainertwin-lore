🚀 What separates a strong supply chain professional from a senior supply chain leader? It’s not just experience. It’s decision quality.

In supply chain, leaders are constantly making choices with incomplete data, shifting priorities, and real business impact. The leaders who rise are the ones who make better decisions, faster, and with stronger judgment.

At Amazon, this is captured in the leadership principle “Are Right, A Lot” — a powerful reminder that leadership is built on good instincts, sound judgment, and the ability to think clearly under pressure.
