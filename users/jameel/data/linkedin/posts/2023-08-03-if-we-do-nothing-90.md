If we do nothing, 90% of the world’s tropical reefs will be gone by 2043. And we can’t save them alone. 

For the last 15 years, we’ve used our know-how to foster a collective transfer of knowledge to inspire global action. We recently brought 100 people together for the largest coral restoration project in history in Indonesia — we're calling the event The Big Build. Our hope is for these scientists, conservationists and NGO partners to take that knowledge home with them, ensuring the future of reef restoration reaches far and wide. 

Meanwhile, 7,000 miles away, Sheba announced its continued commitment to restoring coral reefs, launching a partnership with Kuleana Coral Restoration, a nonprofit organization helping to restore and protect Hawaii's coral reefs and native ecosystem. 

We’re grateful to our partners in Indonesia and beyond. With them, we’re ensuring these restoration efforts mean a better tomorrow. 

To learn more, visit mars.com/TheBigBuild.
