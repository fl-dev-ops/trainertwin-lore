Building a skillful Pakistan with Hunar Foundation! The word “Hunar” in Urdu language means skill. Over the last 10 years the Hunar Foundation has provided vocational training to 6300 needy youngsters and are now running about 8 institutes in Pakistan. Well done to the organizers of Hunar Foundation Canada for organizing a great event tonight and sponsoring 85 students and two workshops!
