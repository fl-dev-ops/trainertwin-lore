🌍 Supply Chain Fun Fact: Hummus-A Global Journey with Many Homes!
Did you know that hummus, the beloved chickpea spread, is at the center of a culinary rivalry spanning continents? As this nutritious dip travels through a complex supply chain-from chickpea farms in India, Turkey, and the US to factory floors and supermarket shelves worldwide-it also carries the pride of several nations.

Countries that claim hummus as their own:
🇱🇧 Lebanon | 🇮🇱 Israel | 🇵🇸 Palestine | 🇪🇬 Egypt | 🇸🇾 Syria | 🇬🇷 Greece

Each country celebrates hummus as a staple of its cuisine, fueling friendly debates over its true origin.

Next time you enjoy hummus, remember: you’re tasting a product shaped by an international supply chain and claimed by many proud cultures!
