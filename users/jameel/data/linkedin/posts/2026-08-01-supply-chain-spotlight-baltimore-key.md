🌉 Supply Chain Spotlight: Baltimore Key Bridge Update
🚢 Impact: The 2024 Baltimore Bridge collapse disrupted North America’s #1 vehicle port, affecting over 750,000 annual auto shipments.
🚚 Bottleneck: Dangerous hazmat freight was forced onto a 30-mile detour, bypassing forbidden harbor tunnels and raising transit costs.
💰 Replacement Cost & Time: The replacement cable-stayed bridge is projected to cost $5.2 billion, with a target completion of late 2030.
