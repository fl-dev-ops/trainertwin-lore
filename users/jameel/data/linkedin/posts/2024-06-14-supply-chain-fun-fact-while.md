Supply Chain Fun Fact! While taking off from St Louis yesterday I captured this nice view of the Missouri River! Did you know that over 60% of US corn exports travel down the Missouri River system? 

While the Mississippi River gets most of the attention, the Missouri River plays a key role in the US supply chain. It functions as a major artery for agricultural goods, fertilizer, and energy products.
