💡Supply Chain Fun Fact💡
Did you know that 🇨🇦 Canada is the largest exporter of frozen crab 🦀 globally followed by Russia 🇷🇺 China 🇨🇳 and USA 🇺🇸? It almost accounts for 10% of the $10 billion+ global crab market growing at an impressive CAGR of about 5%!

Chilly Cargo: Crab is often shipped in refrigerated containers that can maintain temperatures as low as -40°F (-40°C). It's like a mini-freezer on wheels! 🥶🚚
