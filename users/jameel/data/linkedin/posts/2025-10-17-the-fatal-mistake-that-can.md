🎯 The Fatal Mistake That Can End Your Supply Chain Interview Instantly!

Many supply chain candidates prepare hard for interviews — but a single wrong move can wipe it all out: sharing a fake example. As someone who’s interviewed numerous supply chain professionals over the years, I’ve seen this fatal error firsthand. 🚫

Here’s the truth: experienced interviewers and hiring managers can immediately sense when a story is fabricated. Once that trust breaks, there’s no coming back. No matter how good your technical skills are, integrity outshines talent every time.

✅ Always use real examples.
✅ Be honest about what you contributed.
✅ If there’s a gap in experience, talk about what you learned — not what you “made up.”

Authenticity is your biggest advantage in today’s competitive job market.
