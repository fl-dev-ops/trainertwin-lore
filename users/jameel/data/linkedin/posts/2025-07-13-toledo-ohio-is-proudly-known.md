Toledo, Ohio 🏙️ is proudly known as "The Glass City"—a title earned through its historic and ongoing leadership in glass manufacturing and supply chain innovation. At its peak, Toledo produced an impressive 20% of all glass in the United States 🏭, cementing its role as a national industrial powerhouse and a critical node in the American glass supply chain. The city is also called the "Glass Capital of the World" 🌎, thanks to its global reputation and the concentration of major glass companies headquartered here.

Toledo’s legacy continues to shape the industry today, with organizations like the Glass Industry Supply Chain Council based in the city, underscoring its importance in logistics, transportation, and supply chain excellence for the glass sector4. ✨
