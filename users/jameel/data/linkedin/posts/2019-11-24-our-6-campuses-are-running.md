Our 6 campuses are running successfully. Looking forward to expanding to 9 in 2020! NeighborhoodSchools.org is a project to empower children living in Orangi Town Pakistan. It is the largest slum area in the world which is one-third the size of Paris and is home to 2.5Mio people who live below the poverty line and have limited or no access to regular schools. 
Neighborhood Schools is a project of Friends of Humanity International (a 501-c3 Organization)
