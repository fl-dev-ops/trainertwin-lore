This reflects how severe winter conditions cause significant supply chain disruptions through slower transportation, higher operational expenses, and delivery delays. These impacts are driven by hazardous road conditions, increased congestion, and the need for cautious driving, all contributing to reduced efficiency and increased costs in logistics during heavy snowfall periods.
