🚨 Supply Chain KPIs Are Flashing Red: Tariffs Creating COVID-Like Disruptions 🚨

The latest data shows a dramatic 📉 40% drop in outbound freight from China to the U.S, a collapse reminiscent of the early COVID-19 shockwaves in global trade. As highlighted by the FreightWaves CEO on CNBC, this uncertainty is paralyzing shippers, with major companies halting shipments and investment due to unpredictable tariff policies.

The ripple effects are severe:
	•	⚓ U.S. ports, especially on the West Coast, face the risk of losing up to half a million jobs if these trends persist.

	•	🛒 Over 1 million U.S. small businesses-many built on dropshipping and Amazon FBA-are facing existential threats as sourcing from China becomes unviable due to soaring costs and logistics chaos.

	•	🏬 Retailers warn of potential shortages and empty shelves, especially for consumer goods like apparel and electronics.
If policymakers don’t act quickly to stabilize trade relations and clarify tariff structures, we risk a prolonged crisis across the supply chain-impacting jobs, small business survival, and consumer prices.
