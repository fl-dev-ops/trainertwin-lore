🚀 The simplest career advice for young supply chain professionals? Say YES.
On my podcast, supply chain leader Mark Potts, MBA, PMP shared a lesson every early-career professional needs to hear:
👉 When a new project comes your way, say YES.
Here’s why it works:
✅ Visibility: New projects put you in front of leaders and teams you’d never work with otherwise
✅ Skills: Every project teaches you something your day-to-day role can’t
✅ Reputation: You become known as the person who steps up
✅ Opportunity: Promotions often go to the people decision-makers already know
In supply chain, your network inside the organization matters as much as your technical skills. Saying yes is how you build both. 📈
💡 You don’t need to have all the answers. You just need to raise your hand.
