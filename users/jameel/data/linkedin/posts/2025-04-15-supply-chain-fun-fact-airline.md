🚚✈️ Supply Chain Fun Fact: Airline Tire Retreading & Recycling ♻️🛞

Did you know?

🔄 Airline tires can be retreaded up to 7 times before they’re recycled — maximizing their lifecycle and minimizing waste!

💸 Retreading cuts tire costs by up to 50% compared to buying new tires, making it a smart move for airlines’ bottom lines.

🌱 Plus, airlines like American Airlines recycle 2 million pounds of tires annually, turning old rubber into playground mulch and sports field surfaces.

This is a great example of how supply chain innovation drives both sustainability and cost efficiency in the aviation industry!
