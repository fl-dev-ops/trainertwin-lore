Ever wondered why you’re not standing out in your supply chain job interviews? 🤔 
One of the most common mistakes: using “we” instead of “I” when describing your actions in STAR stories. Recruiters want to know about your unique impact, not just the team’s effort.

When discussing collaboration, “we” makes sense. But for problem-solving or critical thinking questions, highlight your personal achievements—it could make the difference in landing that next role! 🚀
