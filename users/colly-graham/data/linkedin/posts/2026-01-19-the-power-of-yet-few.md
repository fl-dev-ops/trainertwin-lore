The Power of “Yet”
Few words change performance in sales more than this one: yet. “I’m not good at discovery” shuts learning down. “I’m not good at discovery yet” keeps the door open.
That one word turns frustration into curiosity. It signals to your brain that progress is possible and effort matters. It reframes struggle from failure into feedback.
Sales is not about being instantly good. It’s about staying engaged long enough to get better. Add “yet” — and watch how your confidence and consistency shift.
