In every sales conversation you are not speaking to one brain.
You are speaking to three.
The survival brain
Is this person safe?
The emotional brain
Do I trust them?
The thinking brain
Does the logic make sense?
Most sales presentations speak only to the third.
But decisions are made in the first two.
The thinking brain doesn’t decide.
It explains the decision the emotional brain has already made.
