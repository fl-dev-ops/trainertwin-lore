🎙️ New podcast episode of Local Women: The Business Pivots is out now! 

This time I’m joined by Jo Rose, Empowerment Coach & Mentor and founder of Creating Conscious Change.

Jo’s work is all about holistic wellbeing and helping women create a more fulfilling life - reconnecting with who they truly are, letting go of what’s been holding them back, and creating lasting change from the inside out.

We had such an honest and thoughtful conversation about how easy it is to lose ourselves in the demands of work, business and life, and why real change starts with paying attention to what’s happening beneath the surface.

One of the things I loved most about Jo’s approach is that she encourages women to simply start the conversation. You don’t need to have everything figured out before you reach out for support, guidance or a different perspective.

If you’ve been feeling stuck, overwhelmed, or like something needs to shift, this episode is a gentle reminder that change often begins with a single step.
