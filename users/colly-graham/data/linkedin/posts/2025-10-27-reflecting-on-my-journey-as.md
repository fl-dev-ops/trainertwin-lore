Reflecting on my journey as my first book, Winning Mindset for Sales People, goes to the printers on Monday. I learned resilience from my Dad, who was diagnosed with multiple sclerosis when I was just four years old. He was the most positive person I’ve known. Throughout his life, he never complained, even when he lost both his legs due to gangrene in his early forties. He passed away at the young age of fifty-two.

His strength and positivity inspired me to start my own sales training business when I turned fifty-two, which has proven to be one of the best decisions I ever made.
