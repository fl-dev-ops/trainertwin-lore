Most businesses try to improve conversion before they improve clarity.
They tweak messaging.
 They adjust pricing.
 They retrain salespeople.
But they cannot precisely describe their most profitable client.
Not broadly.
Precisely.
AI, used properly, can analyse:
• Your highest-margin accounts
 • Your longest-retention clients
 • The sectors where velocity is fastest
 • The stakeholders who influence decisions
When your Ideal Customer Profile is vague, prospecting becomes expensive.
When it is sharp, conversations accelerate.
Clarity precedes velocity.
If I asked your team to define your ideal customer in one sentence — could they all give the same answer?
