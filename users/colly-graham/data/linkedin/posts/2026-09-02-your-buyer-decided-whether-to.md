Your buyer decided whether to trust you in the first few minutes. Probably in seconds.
That is not a sales opinion. It is how the brain is wired. Long before a buyer weighs your solution, the brain is asking one quiet question: is this person safe enough to stay open to? Get that wrong and they listen politely from behind glass. Get it right and the real conversation begins.

Here is the part most salespeople miss: the brain trusts you when it feels understood, not when it feels impressed. The full piece is out now.
