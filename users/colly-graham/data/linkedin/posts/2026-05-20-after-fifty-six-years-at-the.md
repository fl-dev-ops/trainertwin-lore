After fifty-six years at the frontline of selling, I have arrived at the same conclusion time and again. It is not the tactics that are failing your sales team. It is the thinking behind them. The gap between your average performer and your elite performer is rarely skill. It is almost always mindset.
In the latest edition of the salesxcellence LinkedIn Newsletter, I walk through the five invisible mindset barriers that cost organisations millions every year — and why adding more technique training without addressing them is like adding weight to a structure that cannot support what it already carries. Fear of rejection. Imposter syndrome. Scarcity thinking. These are not character flaws. They are learned patterns. And what has been learned can be changed.
If you lead a sales team, this is worth your time. Read the full article in the salesxcellence Newsletter and ask yourself one question: how much of your current performance challenge is a skills problem, and how much of it is a mindset problem wearing the costume of a skills problem?
