Most sales conversations do not fail in the meeting. They fail in the silence that follows. A good conversation creates attention, trust and possibility — but what happens next decides whether that opportunity moves forward or quietly disappears.
In my latest Salesxcellence LinkedIn newsletter, “What Happens After The Conversation Ends,” I explore the follow-through disciplines that separate consistent performers from those who rely on hope. From the first 24 hours after a meeting, to pipeline honesty, to the dangerous gap between verbal agreement and formal commitment, this is where professional selling is often won or lost.
If you are a salesperson, sales leader, or business owner who wants more trust, cleaner pipeline conversations and fewer opportunities drifting into the fog, this article is for you. The brain-based sale does not end when the meeting does. It continues in every action you take afterwards.
