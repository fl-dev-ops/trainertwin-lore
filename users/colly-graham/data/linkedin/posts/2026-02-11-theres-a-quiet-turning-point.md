There’s a quiet turning point in almost every important conversation. Nothing dramatic happens, yet the outcome begins to shift. People lean in or lean back. Momentum grows or fades. And it’s rarely caused by the words themselves — it’s driven by the emotional tone behind them.

This week’s newsletter explores why emotional control under pressure is one of the most powerful performance multipliers in business and life. Neuroscience shows that emotions are contagious. When we feel rushed, others feel pressured. When we feel calm, others feel safe. And safety is what allows trust, influence and decisions to happen.
If you want conversations to become easier to think inside — for you and the people you work with — this edition is for you. Join the newsletter and next week we’ll explore the companion skill: how curiosity unlocks better conversations.
