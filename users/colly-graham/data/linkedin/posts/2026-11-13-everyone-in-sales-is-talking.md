Everyone in sales is talking about AI agents: agents that research the account, agents that write the emails, and agents that buy on behalf of the customer.

I have been selling for 56 years across eleven countries, and I have watched a great deal of technology arrive with the same promise. Each time, one thing stayed exactly where it was. A human being still has to say yes, and that human being decides with a brain that has not had an upgrade.

When machines handle the research and the shortlisting, the conversation is the one part of the sale left to you. It carries more weight now, not less.

That is why I wrote The Brain-Based Sale: The Science of Selling the Way the Brain Decides. It is built on the BRAIN Selling System, five stages that follow the way a buyer thinks.

Background comes first, because you need to understand their world before you say a word about yours. Results and Challenges shows you what is working for them and what is not. Ambitions tells you where they want to be. Impact is where the buyer sees, in their own terms, what changes if they act and what it costs if they do not. Next Steps is a clear way forward that you agree together.

When a buyer pushes back, CALM keeps the conversation open: Cushion, Ask, Listen, Meet.

AI can prepare you for the meeting, however it cannot make a buyer feel understood. Only a person who listens can do that, and trust has always followed from being understood.

If your team has more tools than ever and the conversations are not getting any better, this book is for you.
