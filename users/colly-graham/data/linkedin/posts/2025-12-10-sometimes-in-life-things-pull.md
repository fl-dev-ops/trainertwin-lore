Sometimes in life things pull at your heart strings, I woke this morning to learn of the death of Raul Malo who passed away at age 60 from cancer. Many of you ask who is Raul Malo.
Raul was the lead singer with a band called the Mavericks, my favourite band. I’ve seen them live five times their music and the voice of Raul never fails to entertain me. 
Raul Malo’s voice was one of those rare instruments that feels both timeless and unmistakably personal. 
It feels as if I’ve lost a little bit of my heart.
Raul rest in peace.
