"The First Time I Hit Bottom"
I was on top of the world.
Deals were closing. Money was flowing. After 20 years in sales, I thought I had it all figured out.
Then the market shifted. And I didn't.
Within six months, everything I'd built collapsed. The deals dried up. The confidence evaporated. The bank account followed.
I remember sitting in my empty office, wondering how someone with two decades of experience could fail so spectacularly.
Here's what I learned lying on that floor:
My skills hadn't failed me. My mindset had.
I'd been operating on autopilot—same techniques, same approaches, same thinking. When the market changed, I couldn't adapt because I was mentally rigid.
The comeback took 18 months. Not because I needed to learn new sales tactics. But because I needed to rebuild my mental foundation.
I had to face my fear of rejection head-on. I had to replace scarcity thinking with abundance. I had to stop seeing myself as a victim and start seeing myself as someone who could rise again.
And I did.
But here's the thing: I wouldn't have needed to hit bottom if someone had taught me about mindset FIRST, instead of just tactics.
That's why I wrote "Winning Mindsets for Salespeople." So you don't have to learn this lesson the hard way.
After 56 years in this game, I can tell you: your skills will get you started. Your mindset will determine how far you go.
Have you ever had to rebuild after a major setback? What did it teach you?
