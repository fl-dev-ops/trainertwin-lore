Most sales conversations do not fail through lack of effort. They fail because they are built in the wrong order. After fifty-six years in sales, across eleven countries, I have watched brilliant salespeople lose good opportunities simply because they explained too early and listened too late.
In this edition of the Brain Based Sales newsletter, I walk through the five stages of the BRAIN framework: Background, Results and Challenges, Ambitions, Impact and Next Steps. Each stage matches the order in which the buyer’s brain becomes ready to decide. You will find practical questions for every stage, a coaching tool for sales leaders, and one simple challenge to try in your next conversation.
It is an eight-minute read, and I hope it changes how you plan your very next meeting. Read “The Order Is the Strategy” via the link below, then tell me in the comments which stage you find hardest to slow down for. I read every reply and would value hearing your experience.
