Today is a day I have worked towards for a long time.
 
The Brain-Based Sale: The Science of Selling the Way the Brain Decides is now live on Amazon worldwide.
 
I have spent 56 years in frontline B2B sales and trained more than 1,000 organisations across eleven countries. In all that time one thing has stayed the same: buyers decide the way their brains are built to decide, however most sales training still pays little attention to how that happens.
 
This book is my attempt to put that right. It brings the science of how people decide together with what I have learned in real sales conversations, from earning the first conversation through to the next steps.
 
My thanks go to Tom Williams in Phoenix, Arizona, who wrote the foreword, and to the sales leaders who read early copies and gave me their honest views.
 
If you lead a sales team, or you sell for a living, I wrote this for you. I would value your thoughts once you have read it.
