Most sales opportunities do not stall because the buyer fails to see the value. They stall because the buyer has not yet felt the cost of the problem.
This issue of the Brain Based Newsletter is about the stage that changes that: Impact, the fourth stage of the BRAIN Selling System. It examines the six kinds of cost hiding inside "doing nothing", the four levels of impact from immediate to personal, and seven questions that surface the real weight of a problem. It also shows why the buyer's own brain treats a possible loss as heavier than an equal gain, and how honest urgency uses that truth without ever manufacturing fear.
If a buyer has ever told you a problem was "manageable" and then quietly done nothing, this one is for you. Read it before your next discovery conversation.  https://lnkd.in/e2C7tvX9
