Another five star review on Amazon for my book Winning Mindset for Sales People 
“I picked up my copy of Winning Mindset for Sales People at Colly Graham’s book launch in Belfast, and after hearing him speak, I suspected the book would be good but it exceeded that by quite a bit.

What struck me most is how clearly Colly separates myth from reality. We often get caught up chasing new techniques or tools, yet this book brings the focus back to the one thing that truly moves the needle, how you think. Colly doesn’t approach mindset in a vague, motivational way; he breaks it down into practical, usable shifts that genuinely change how you show up.

I found myself underlining more sections than I expected as they’re not abstract concepts, they’re explained in a way that makes you pause, reflect, and realise exactly where you’ve been getting in your own way. And the examples he uses throughout make the lessons easy to grasp and apply.

What I appreciated most is how grounded the book feels. It’s honest, experience-driven, and refreshingly free of gimmicks. You can tell it’s written by someone who has spent decades in the real world of sales, not just talking about it but living it.

If you work in sales or run your own business, this book will give you the mental clarity and confidence to approach your role differently. And if mindset is something you’ve overlooked until now, this book will show you why it matters and how to strengthen it.

A genuinely valuable read”
