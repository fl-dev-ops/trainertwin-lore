Most salespeople think the problem is their technique. More calls. Better scripts. Stronger questions. I spent five decades in sales before I understood the real truth. The conversation inside your head begins long before the buyer is in the room. And that conversation is running the show.
I have trained over a thousand organisations across eleven countries. The pattern I see most often is not a skills gap. It is a belief gap. A salesperson knows exactly what to do. And still hesitates. Still softens the question. Still lets the follow-up drift. That is not a discipline problem. That is belief in disguise. And pressure alone will never fix it.
My latest Winning Mindset newsletter goes deep on this. Why limiting beliefs hide inside perfectly reasonable professional language. Why the brain protects you rather than promotes you. And what you can do right now to create real distance between the belief and the behaviour. If you lead a sales team, or if you are in sales yourself, this one is worth eight minutes of your time. The link is in the first comment. Read it. Then ask yourself one honest question before your next sales conversation.
