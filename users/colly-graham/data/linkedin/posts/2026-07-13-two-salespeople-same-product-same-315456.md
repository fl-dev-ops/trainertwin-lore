Two salespeople. Same product. Same territory. Same training. Completely different results. Why? It is a question that has puzzled sales leaders for decades. After fifty-six years in frontline sales and thirty years training salespeople in eleven countries, I am convinced the difference is rarely talent. It is the invisible engine beneath performance: mindset.
In this eight-minute edition of the Winning Mindset newsletter, I explore why technique alone cannot produce consistent success, the six pillars of a winning mindset, and what three very different salespeople can teach us. Resilience, you will discover, is not about staying confident. It is about learning how to return. You will also find three practical exercises you can begin using this week.
For CEOs and sales leaders, this raises an important question. Are you developing only the skills of your salespeople, or the person responsible for applying those skills? For salespeople, the message is just as direct. You may not need another clever technique. You may need to strengthen the beliefs, resilience and discipline that decide whether your existing skills are used consistently. Mindset is not positive thinking with a smile painted on. It is self-leadership under pressure. Read The Invisible Engine: Why One Salesperson Wins and Another Struggles, then ask yourself which of the six pillars needs your attention.
