AI can help you find the right person. It can gather the research, shape the first message, and support the follow-up. But when you are finally in the room — facing a real buyer, with real doubt, real pressure, and real consequences — the responsibility comes back to you.
This week’s newsletter, What Happens in the Room, explores the part of selling no tool can do for you: the quality of your presence, the depth of your listening, the conviction behind your words, and the ability to understand the conversation the buyer is having with themselves.
If you lead salespeople, train salespeople, or sit in front of buyers yourself, this is for you. Read it before your next important conversation — because the human edge is not found in the software. It shows up in the room.
