Your last sales conversation may have felt great. Did your buyer feel the same? Most salespeople talk too early and listen too late. They present before they understand the buyer, and the buyer feels overloaded rather than understood. Objections turn into arguments, and good opportunities stall without anyone quite knowing why.
I have trained over 1,000 organisations across 11 countries, and I believe this is not a talent problem. It is a structure problem, which is why I built the free BRAIN Sales Assessment. In 24 questions and about six minutes, it scores your Background, Results and Challenges, Ambitions, Impact and Next Steps conversations. It also shows how you handle objections under pressure using CALM. There are four versions, from those new to selling through to sales leaders assessing a team.
You start by rating yourself, and your self-rating is then compared with your scored answers. Any gap between your confidence and your competence is often the most useful finding of all. Your results appear on screen straight away, with practical questions to try in your very next sales conversation. Your answers stay on your own device, so be honest and answer for what happens today, not what you would like to happen.
