Sales success isn’t luck—it’s five daily disciplines you can start today.
Selling today isn’t just about being enthusiastic or having a great product. We all know that. What really matters is how you show up — clear-headed, steady, and able to move conversations forward without forcing them.
That’s why I’ve been thinking about the small, daily habits that make the biggest difference. I call them Five Daily Disciplines for Sales Success. They’re simple, repeatable, and intentional. And when you stick with them, they compound into stronger relationships, more predictable results, and a calmer inner state.
The first one? Mental Priming (3–5 minutes) — a quick reset that sets the tone for your day.
