Two salespeople. Same product, same training, same market. One wins quietly. The other struggles and cannot say why.
After more than fifty years on the frontline, I can tell you the gap is almost never the script. It is the mindset behind it. My latest newsletter sets out the six pillars that hold a winning sales mindset in place: belief, resilience, optimism, growth, empathy and discipline. Read them as one structure, not a loose list of tips.
If all six feel weak, you only need to begin with one. The pillar you most want to avoid is usually the one costing you most right now. Which one is it for you? Name it in the comments, then read the full eight minute newsletter below.
