Too many sales conversations are lost for one simple reason. The salesperson talks too soon. CEOs, sales leaders and CROs know what that costs. Missed opportunities. Thin discovery. Weak qualification. And buyers who quietly drift back to doing nothing at all.

This week's salesxcellence newsletter, The Seller Who Asks, Leads, looks at why the strongest salespeople are not the best talkers. They are the best questioners.

A good question does the heavy lifting. It uncovers the real need. It reveals the cost of standing still. It maps the buying group. And it lets the buyer describe value in their own words, which is the only version they truly believe.

If you lead a sales team and want more trust, better conversations and stronger outcomes, this one is worth your time. The future belongs to salespeople who slow down, ask well, listen deeply, and lead with understanding. The full piece breaks down the four questions that do the most work in discovery.
