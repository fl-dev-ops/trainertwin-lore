Most salespeople do not have a performance problem. They have a process problem. When there is no clear structure around prospecting, pipeline management and follow-up, capable salespeople spend their energy making decisions that a good system should already have made for them. The result is a salesperson who is busy every single day but rarely moving in the right direction.
The pipeline is not an administrative task. It is the most honest picture of where your sales year is heading. A pipeline full of wishful thinking does not just distort the forecast — it quietly erodes the confidence of the person working it. In the latest salesxcellence newsletter, I write about what a genuinely healthy pipeline looks like, why most salespeople stop following up far too early, and what consistent prospecting actually does for the quality of every conversation that follows.
If you lead a sales team or you sell for a living, this is a straightforward read about the disciplines that separate consistent performers from the rest. No theory. No complexity. Just thirty years of what actually works in the field. The link is in the comments.
