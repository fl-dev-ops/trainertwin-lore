Most sales conversations do not fail because the salesperson runs out of answers. They fail because pressure enters the room, and the buyer's brain shifts from thinking to protecting.

When that happens, logic stops landing. The buyer is no longer weighing your proposal. They are defending themselves.
My latest newsletter shows how to bring a difficult conversation back to CALM: Cushion, Ask, Listen and Meet. It is a simple discipline that lowers defensiveness, surfaces the real concern, and helps the buyer feel safe enough to think clearly again.

This matters whether you sell, lead a sales team, or plan the kick-off where your people first learn to handle resistance under pressure. Pressure costs more sales than any missing answer ever will.
It is not about pushing harder. It is about lowering pressure so better decisions can happen.
When a buyer pushes back, the instinct is to push harder. It is the wrong move, and the newsletter explains why.
