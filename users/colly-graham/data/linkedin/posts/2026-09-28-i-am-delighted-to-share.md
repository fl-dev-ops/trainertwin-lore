I am delighted to share a short summary of the Preface from my forthcoming book, Brain Based Sales. Every company that sells is under pressure to sell better and faster. Competition now comes from every corner of the world. Buyers know more than ever, and they expect more for less. Even strong companies are admitting that the old way of selling no longer carries them. Margins need protecting, sales cycles need shortening, and relationships need to last beyond a single sale.
I believe change is long overdue for the CEO, the sales manager and the salesperson alike. Many of us repeat selling habits out of routine rather than results. There is a better way, and it begins with the brain. People do not buy simply because the facts are correct. They buy when the conversation feels relevant, safe and worth acting upon. Brain Based Sales works with the way buyers actually think, rather than against it.
This is more than a method for one person. It offers teams and individual salespeople a fresh way to think about the role, and a way to lift performance that lasts. Some principles may feel strange at first, even the opposite of what you were taught. One example is to move off the solution. Stephen Covey put it plainly: "Seek first to understand, then to be understood." When we talk less, ask better questions and listen with real attention, the customer feels valued. That is where trust begins.
