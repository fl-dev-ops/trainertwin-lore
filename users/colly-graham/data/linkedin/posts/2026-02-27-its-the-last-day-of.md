It’s the last day of the month — so here’s a simple (slightly uncomfortable) question: how many businesses actually sit down and assess their KPIs across every strand of the operation… not just sales, but marketing, finance, delivery/ops, and customer experience? Most teams look at a revenue number, glance at the pipeline, then roll straight into next month like nothing happened. That isn’t management — it’s hope with a spreadsheet.
And the bigger issue is this: have you even set KPIs for each strand? Not vague intentions like “grow awareness” or “improve service” — I mean measurable indicators that tell you whether the business is getting healthier or just getting busier. Sales: conversion rates, average deal size, cycle time. Marketing: cost per lead, lead-to-meeting rate, channel performance. Finance: cash runway, gross margin, debtor days. Delivery: on-time performance, rework, customer retention. If you can’t see it clearly, you can’t steer it.
Because here’s the truth every senior operator learns the hard way: you cannot manage what you do not measure. If the only thing you measure is revenue, you’ll miss the silent killers — slipping margins, weak handovers, slow collections, rising churn, declining quality, and a team that’s working harder for less impact. Your business can look “successful” right up until it doesn’t.
So today — before you set next month’s goals — do this: pick one KPI per strand, review the trend for the last 90 days, and decide one adjustment. One. Not a reinvention. A correction. And if you’re thinking, “We don’t really have KPIs beyond sales…” then that’s your answer. What’s one KPI you’ll commit to tracking from next month — in sales, marketing, finance, and delivery?
