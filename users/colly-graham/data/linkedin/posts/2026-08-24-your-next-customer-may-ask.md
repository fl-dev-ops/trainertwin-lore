Your next customer may ask AI about you before they ever ask you.
An independent UK study this month tested 304 realistic B2B searches. AI answered 100 per cent of how-to questions and 96 per cent of pricing questions before the buyer reached a single supplier's website. If a machine now writes the first draft of your reputation, the question is simple. Does it find the right version of you?
That is one of three developments in this week's Monday Morning AI Sales Brief. The second: a survey of 755 leaders found that those who use AI on real work themselves report far stronger returns than those who only watch from the sidelines. The third: a new government deployment shows the role AI plays best in any sales process, triage first, human judgement when the stakes rise. Each item comes with the practical move to make before Friday, and the caveats kept in plain sight.
Here is the through-line I keep coming back to. AI can tell you who to contact and what has changed. Only a human conversation can discover what truly matters to that customer. Read this week's brief for the full three, and the one section I would add to any keynote this month.
