In 1996, I sat at my desk staring at bank statements that would not change. Contracts had ended and income had stopped. I was not stretched. I was broke. The harder I chased money, the tighter everything became. Then one quiet question turned my business around, and I have never forgotten it.
    This edition of the Winning Mindset Newsletter is called “Not Wishing, Wiring.” It takes the Law of Attraction out of the realm of fantasy and puts it where it belongs: in the brain, body and nervous system. Drawing on work from Dr Srinivasan Pillay, Dr Tara Swart, Dr James Doty and Paul Zak, I explore why your expectations shape what you notice in every sales conversation.    
     Buyers hear your inner state long before they weigh your words. Read it before your next call. Try the thirty-second practice at the end and notice what changes. Then tell me in the comments where your inner state has shifted the outcome of a conversation. I will reply to every one
