There’s a quiet misunderstanding happening in sales right now.
People think AI is the advantage.
It isn’t.
The advantage is the human being using it.
In 1996, when my business was on the brink, a mentor asked me three questions that changed everything:
• What do you genuinely know?
 • What do you believe with complete conviction?
 • Who truly needs to hear it?
No algorithms. No automation. Just clarity.
Those same questions matter more today than they did then.
Because here’s what I’m seeing across the UK and Ireland right now:
AI is amplifying performance — not replacing it.
The best salespeople are not hiding behind tools.
 They’re using AI to handle the mechanical… so they can show up more powerfully in the moments that actually decide the outcome.
And those moments?
They are still human.
Still emotional.
 Still built on trust.
 Still decided in conversations — not sequences.
If you believe AI will do the thinking for you, you’ll fall behind.
If you use AI to free up your thinking, you’ll pull ahead.
I’ve just written a new piece on this:
“The Human Edge: Why Winning in an AI World Demands More of You, Not Less.”
It’s about:
 • Why trust still beats technology
 • What neuroscience tells us about real decision-making
 • How to use AI without losing your voice
 • Why mindset is now the ultimate differentiator
If you’re in sales — or leading a team — this is not optional reading.
It’s the shift that’s already happening.
And the question is simple:
Will you let AI replace your effort…
 Or will you let it elevate who you are at your best?
