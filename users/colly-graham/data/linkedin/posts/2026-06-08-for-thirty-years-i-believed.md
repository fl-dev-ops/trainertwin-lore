For thirty years I believed the strong salesperson was the one who felt nothing.
I was wrong.
I have trained more than a thousand organisations across eleven countries, and I have never met a salesperson without fear. The difference is never the absence of emotion. It is what they do with it. The best have learned to work with the fear, the silence and the disappointment, rather than fight a private war they can never win.
My latest newsletter explains what is actually happening inside you on a hard call, why suppression quietly burns people out, and three disciplines you can use on your very next conversation. The neuroscience surprised me. It may surprise you too. Read it, then tell me which of the three you will try first.
