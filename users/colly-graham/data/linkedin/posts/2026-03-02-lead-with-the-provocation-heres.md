Lead with the provocation
Here's something the sales world still hasn't fully accepted — your job in 2026 is no longer to persuade. It's to reduce decision anxiety. Buyers are already researching before you get in the room. They arrive informed and in control. The moment things get complex, though, they still look for one thing — a steady, trustworthy voice that gives them clarity. That's your new competitive edge.
This week's newsletter breaks down exactly how to build that edge in practical terms — a 90-second trust-building opening, three phrases that replace needless follow-up, and a simple two-day message that makes buyers feel genuinely heard. Small shifts, but the kind that change the whole tone of a sales relationship.
We're not being replaced by technology or by better-informed buyers. We're being repositioned. And that repositioning demands a stronger mindset than ever.
