I’ve worked in sales for fifty-six years. I’ve knocked doors in the rain, cold-called from paper directories, and watched fax, email, and CRM completely reshape how we prospect. Every time a new tool arrived, salespeople split into two groups: those who feared replacement and those who learned to use it. The second group always won. AI is the biggest shift I’ve seen — even bigger than the internet — but here’s the truth most people are missing: AI won’t replace the human relationship at the centre of selling. It simply clears the undergrowth so you can reach the right conversation faster.

In my latest Salesxcellence newsletter, I break down what AI actually does in prospecting, where it saves time, where it absolutely cannot help you, and the simple five-minute starting point any salesperson can try today.
