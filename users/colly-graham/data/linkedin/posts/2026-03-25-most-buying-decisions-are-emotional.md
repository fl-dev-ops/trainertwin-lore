Most buying decisions are emotional before they are logical.What if your buyer has already made their decision before you reach slide six of your presentation?
Not logically.
Emotionally.
Neuroscience tells us that emotion drives decision-making long before logic arrives to justify it.
Which means the beautiful slide deck many salespeople prepare is often aimed at the wrong part of the brain.
The sale isn’t won in the final slide.
It’s won in the first few minutes of human connection.
Buying is an emotional act wrapped in rational packaging.
Have you ever experienced this in a real sales conversation?
