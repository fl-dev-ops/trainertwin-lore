Most objections are never truly answered. They simply come back wearing different clothes.
A price concern dealt with too quickly returns as a timing concern. A timing concern pushed aside returns as “we need to speak to the board.” After 56 years of frontline sales across eleven countries, I have learned that the problem is rarely the objection itself. The problem is answering the surface before understanding the source, because every objection comes from one of three places in the buyer’s brain, and each one asks for something different from you.
In this edition of the salesxcellence newsletter, I share how to pre-empt the objections you know are coming, and how to reframe the ones that surface using CALM: Cushion, Ask, Listen, Meet. It is an eight minute read, with words you can use in your very next sales conversation. Which objection do you hear most often at the moment? Tell me in the comments, and I will show you how CALM would handle it.
