I saw a sales trainer online complaining about losing business to AI. I understand the concern. When something new arrives, it can feel like a threat. But after fifty-six years in sales, I have learned this: moaning about change has never built a pipeline, improved a conversation, or won a customer’s trust.
AI is not here to replace good sales leadership. It is here to remove some of the heavy lifting. Research, preparation, follow-up, message testing, customer insight, CRM discipline, prospecting rhythm — AI can help with all of that. But it cannot look a buyer in the eye, read the emotional temperature in the room, ask the brave question, or build genuine trust.
That is where HI comes in. Human Intelligence. The judgement, empathy, experience, listening, courage and commercial sense that separate a good salesperson from someone simply going through the motions. The future of sales is not AI versus HI. It is AI plus HI. Better tools in the hands of better trained people.
Sales leaders have a choice. Resist AI and watch others move faster. Or learn how to use it wisely, teach your salespeople to think better with it, and keep the human part of selling where it belongs — right at the centre. The best sales teams will not be the ones who use AI instead of people. They will be the ones who use AI to make their people sharper, better prepared, and more valuable to the customer.
