There’s a quiet shift happening in sales right now.
 Not the kind driven by a new tool, a new script, or a new presentation technique — but a deeper change in how top performers think.
AI is beginning to automate research, drafting, outreach, and even parts of discovery. The administrative side of the role is being reshaped faster than most organisations expected. But here’s the interesting part: while technology is accelerating the mechanics of selling, it’s also exposing something that has always separated exceptional salespeople from average ones — mindset. The ability to think clearly under pressure, ask better questions, build genuine trust, and guide meaningful business conversations.
In other words, the future of sales will belong to those who combine intelligent use of AI with a winning mindset. I’ll be running a practical workshop soon on how sales teams can use AI to improve prospecting, preparation, and performance without losing the human judgement that actually wins business. The question is simple: are you ready for the next era of sales?
