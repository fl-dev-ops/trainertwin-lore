I am delighted to be presenting a workshop at Martin Gilchrist's Seen and Heard, a pop-up co-working day at the Ulster Bank Accelerator Hub in Belfast on Thursday 27 August. My session, Winning Mindset for High Performance in an AI World, reframes artificial intelligence not as a threat but as a test of the one thing no algorithm will ever replicate, which is human character. Drawing on applied neuroscience and three decades in the field, I give a clear and practical answer to the question every professional is quietly asking right now: do I still matter in this world? The answer is yes, and I will show exactly why.
