The best salespeople in 2026 aren't the most persuasive. They're the ones who make buying feel safe. I've spent years studying what separates top performers from everyone else, and it comes down to something most sales training completely ignores — human biology. When a buyer's brain senses pressure, curiosity shuts down, risk tolerance shrinks, and decisions stall. No amount of clever closing techniques can override that. The old playbook of confidence, urgency and momentum isn't just outdated. It's actively working against you.

In my latest newsletter, I break down the four pillars that are quietly driving the biggest deals right now: Trust, Safety, Discovery and Value. Not as buzzwords. As a connected system grounded in decision science and how people actually make choices.
