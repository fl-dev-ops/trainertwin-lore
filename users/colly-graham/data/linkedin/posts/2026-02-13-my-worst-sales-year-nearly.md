My worst sales year nearly broke me — and it taught me everything.
The pipeline dried up. Deals I was certain about fell apart. I started questioning whether I was still cut out for this.
What saved me was not a new technique. It was a mentor who told me to stop hiding from the numbers and face what was actually happening. Not what I hoped was happening.
Turns out, I had been selling to the wrong people, chasing the wrong problems, and confusing activity with progress. Sound familiar?
Once I got honest about that, everything changed. The recovery was slower than I wanted. But it was solid.
Fifty-six years in sales has taught me that your worst year often carries your best lessons — if you are willing to look at it clearly.
What did your toughest year teach you?
