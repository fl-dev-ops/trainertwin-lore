Every sales leader I have worked with wants a team that sells well. Fewer stop to ask what their team feels when they walk through the door on a Monday morning. After fifty-six years in frontline B2B sales, I have come to believe that feeling shapes every conversation your salespeople have with buyers.
In this issue of the salesxcellence newsletter, I explore why culture is really neurology at scale. I look at the five foundations that separate the strongest sales cultures from the rest, drawing on research from Amy Edmondson, Google’s Project Aristotle and Gallup. I also share why a leader’s own brain is the final lever for lasting performance.
If you lead a sales team, or hope to one day, I would value you giving this eight minute read your time. Then tell me one thing a leader did that made you feel safe enough to tell the truth. I read every comment, and I would love to hear your story.
