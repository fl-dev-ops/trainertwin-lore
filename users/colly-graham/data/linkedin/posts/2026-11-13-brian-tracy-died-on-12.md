Brian Tracy died on 12 November 2026, aged 82. He helped me build a winning sales team, and he never knew it.
 
In the early eighties, I was building my first sales team. I put Brian’s videos at the centre of how we trained. We watched one together at sales induction training and evey sales meetin., then agreed what each of us would do differently that week.
 
Three of his lessons did the heavy lifting:
 
1. Write the goal down. Brian taught that a goal kept in your head is only a wish. Every person on the team wrote theirs and kept it in sight.
2. Do the hardest task first. That is the heart of “Eat That Frog!”. For us, it meant prospecting calls before anything else in the day.
3/ Selling starts in the mind of the seller. “The Psychology of Selling” showed my team that how you see yourself decides how you sell.
 
The result was a winning sales team and no small influence in the launch of my book, Brain Based Sales this week.
 
Fifty-six years in sales have taught me that the right teacher at the right moment changes everything. Brian was that teacher for me, and for millions of others.
 
Thank you, Brian. Rest in peace.
