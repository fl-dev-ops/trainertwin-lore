Nearly all sales organisations now use AI somewhere. Yet 41 per cent of leaders still do not trust what it produces. That gap between adoption and belief is where selling happens right now. Your team has the tools. The question is whether they truly believe in them.
I have spent the last week reading a 2026 industry survey against fifty-six years on the frontline. The data is clear about where AI is earning its keep and where it is quietly failing. More importantly, it is clear about why. The barriers are not technical. They are human and operational. That means they are fixable.
What follows is seven areas of impact ranked by what leaders are actually investing in, five barriers ranked by how badly they slow you down, and a practical order to start if you are somewhere between having the tools and trusting them. This is not theory. It is what works in a working sales week.
