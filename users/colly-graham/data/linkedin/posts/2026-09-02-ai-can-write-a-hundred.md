AI can write a hundred cold openers before you finish your coffee. It still cannot earn a single moment of trust.
 
That is the line most sales teams are getting wrong right now. They are pointing AI at the wrong half of the job. So let me be clear about what these tools actually do well, because it is real, and worth using.
 
Tools like Claude are genuinely good at the groundwork. Building a clean prospect list. Reading the news on an account and pulling out the one thing that changed. Drafting a first version of a message so you are editing rather than staring at a blank screen. Tidying your notes after a call into something your CRM can use. That work used to eat hours. Now it does not. That is a fair gain, and I take it.
 
What it cannot do is the part that actually moves a sale. It cannot sit across from a buyer and make them feel understood. It cannot read the pause after a hard question and know to wait. It cannot decide, honestly, that you are not the right fit and say so. The brain does not trust you because your outreach was fast. It trusts you because it feels understood, and that is still a human job.
 
So use AI for the groundwork, and spend the hours it gives you back on the conversation. That is the whole game. What are you handing to the machine, and what are you keeping for yourself?
