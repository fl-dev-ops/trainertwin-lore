This week, three unrelated stories delivered the same warning. Salesforce launched an AI model that can reason across an entire CRM, deciding what should happen next in a sale, not just drafting the email. That should concern you less than what comes after it. A weak process, run at machine speed, becomes a faster mistake. This week's Monday AI Sales Brief explains why judgement, not execution, is about to become the scarce skill in commercial teams.
   KPMG cut two hundred UK advisory roles this month, several of them in AI itself. Having an AI job title did not save anyone, because holding a capability is not the same as creating demand for it. That distinction should worry every sales leader training their team on tools rather than outcomes. This week's brief sets out the difference, and a short exercise to test which one your team is actually building.
   A UK publisher cut two hundred and twenty editorial jobs this month, after AI summaries took away nearly half its search traffic overnight. Generic content, written for search engines rather than readers, is losing its value fast. The same is becoming true of generic sales messaging.
