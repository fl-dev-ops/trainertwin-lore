Cold outreach is not dead. Cold thinking is.
Most sellers open LinkedIn, pick a stranger, and send a message that could have gone to anyone. Then they wonder why nobody replies.
After fifty-six years of frontline selling, I can tell you the principle has never changed. Talk to the people who are already leaning towards you.
They are easier to find than you think.
People commenting on your competitors' posts. Companies hiring salespeople right now. Visitors to your profile. Readers engaging with your newsletter. Every one of them is showing intent before you write a single word.
What has changed is the research. AI now does in minutes what used to take me an hour with a notepad and a phone book. Claude reads the account, finds the signal, and tells me why this person, why now.
But the message still has to sound like you. A human wrote it, for one human, for one reason. AI earns you the time. It does not earn you the trust.
Warm first. Personal always.
Where do you find your warmest prospects, before you ever send a message?
