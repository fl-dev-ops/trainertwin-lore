Last week I delivered my first in-house AI training session for a sales team — and I’ll be honest, I could feel the quiet scepticism in the room at the start. “Is this going to replace us?” “Is this just another shiny tool?” 
By the end of the session, that mood had completely shifted. In a single working block, the team generated 15 fresh, qualified leads — complete with contact details, clear business pain points, LinkedIn profiles, and a structured follow-up cadence mapped directly into their CRM. Not theory. Not slides. Real prospects, ready for conversations.
Here’s what changed: we stopped treating AI like magic and started treating it like a system. AI is essentially code — structured thinking at speed. When you know how to guide it properly, it helps salespeople save hours on prospecting, planning, and research. 
But — and this is critical — AI does not replace the human touch. It doesn’t build trust. It doesn’t listen between the lines. It doesn’t read the emotional temperature in a room. What it does brilliantly is remove the admin drag, surface insight faster, and free the salesperson to do what only a human can do: connect, understand, and help.
