I have spent fifty-six years in sales. I know a too-good-to-be-true promise when I see one. My feed is full of them. “Connect AI to LinkedIn in two minutes and book seventeen meetings a day.” “Copy our exact system.” These posts are not written to help you. They exist to farm comments, harvest your details, and sell you something on the back of a number nobody can verify.

Here is the truth from someone who uses AI in his sales work every day. AI is a useful assistant. It researches prospects, drafts messages, and saves hours. It cannot replace the conversation. No tool books seventeen meetings a day. Meetings come from trust, and trust is built between people.

So before you comment “SYSTEM” on the next viral post, ask three questions. Who is making this claim, and what are they selling? Where is the evidence beyond a screenshot? Would the promise survive a straight question over a cup of tea? Sales has always rewarded honesty over hype. AI has not changed that.
