Your underperforming salesperson probably does not need more pressure. They need to be seen more clearly.
When results dip, the instinct is to push for more activity, more calls, more technique. Sometimes that helps. More often, the visible problem is only the surface. Weak follow-up can be fear of rejection. Poor price confidence can be low belief in value. Avoiding senior buyers can be a quiet question of self-worth. My latest newsletter unpacks the beliefs running underneath sales behaviour, and why the best coaching question is not only "what did you do?" but "what was happening inside you at that moment?"
Inside, I share the six mindset traps that quietly drain a pipeline, the belief inventory worth taking with your team, and a five-day awareness exercise any salesperson can start this week.
