Most salespeople misunderstand reciprocity. And it’s costing them trust.
Reciprocity isn’t “I gave you something — now you owe me.”
That’s not reciprocity. That’s pressure wearing a friendly smile.
Real reciprocity happens when: You help someone think better You reduce their risk You don’t chase the return
People don’t repay favours.
 They repay how you made them feel about themselves.
Agree — or completely disagree?
