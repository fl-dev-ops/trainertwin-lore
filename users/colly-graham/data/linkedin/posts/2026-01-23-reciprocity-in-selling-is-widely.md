Reciprocity in selling is widely misunderstood.
It’s often treated as a tactic:
 give something first, then expect something back.
That framing creates pressure — and pressure kills trust.
Real reciprocity is a response to how a conversation feels.
 When people feel respected, unhurried, and free to think, something changes.
Clarity improves.
 Defences lower.
 Trust forms.
I’ve been exploring this idea over the last few days, and on Monday’s Winning Mindset newsletter I’ll go deeper — including a simple story that explains why reciprocity works when it’s genuine and collapses when it’s engineered.
If professionalism, credibility, and long-term relationships matter in your work, it’s one to look out for.
