The Invisible Mind at Work

Success in sales often begins with tools, strategies, or processes. However, after years of coaching salespeople—from newcomers to seasoned experts—I've discovered that the true driver of success lies within. 
The unexamined thoughts we carry shape everything about our performance:

How we handle objections
How we interpret rejection
How confidently we discuss value

These beliefs create the foundation for every decision we make. Often, we are unaware of their presence, much like navigating a dark room filled with furniture, constantly bumping into things we refuse to acknowledge. Understanding this internal landscape is crucial for achieving lasting success.
