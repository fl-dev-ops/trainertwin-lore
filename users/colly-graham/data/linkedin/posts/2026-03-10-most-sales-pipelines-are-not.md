Most sales pipelines are not inaccurate.
They are fiction.
Not deliberately.
 Not maliciously.
 But fiction all the same.
Because many of the opportunities sitting inside the CRM are not moving forward at all.
They are simply drifting.
A meeting gets postponed.
 A follow-up email goes unanswered.
 A proposal lands in an inbox and quietly disappears under twenty other priorities.
Yet the deal stays in the system.
Still forecast.
 Still “active”.
 Still giving leadership the impression that revenue is on the way.
Until the end of the quarter arrives and reality politely corrects the numbers.
After more than five decades working in sales across twelve countries, I’ve seen this pattern repeatedly.
The strongest sales leaders do something different.
They run what I call an honest pipeline.
Fewer deals.
 Better qualified.
 Clear next steps.
 Multiple stakeholders engaged.
 Real urgency.
In this week’s salesxcellence newsletter, I share:
• The 7 warning signs a deal is actually a “ghost opportunity”
 • A five-minute pipeline diagnostic every sales leader should run this week
 • And the one discipline that prevents proposals from quietly dying
If you lead a sales team, this may change how you look at your pipeline.
Question for sales leaders:
 What percentage of the deals currently in your pipeline are genuinely progressing?
