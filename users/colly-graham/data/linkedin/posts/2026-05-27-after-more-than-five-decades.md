After more than five decades in front-line sales, I have seen one truth repeat itself across every industry, every country, and every type of buyer. The sale is never won by the best presentation. It is won inside the customer's mind — long before logic enters the room. This week's Brain Based Sales Newsletter explores exactly what is happening inside your buyer's brain, and why the order of your conversation matters far more than the content of it.

In this issue, I share what I have learned — and what neuroscience confirms — about the sequence that turns a polished conversation into a committed one. Read it once and you will never approach a sales conversation the same way again.

I have written it for sales professionals who want to understand the real decision-making process — not the textbook version, but the human one.
