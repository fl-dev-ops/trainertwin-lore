Rejection is the moment most salespeople try to avoid. The best ones? They study it. Because every “no” carries data — about fit, timing, message — and that data is where real performance gains are made.

In this edition of the Winning Mindset Newsletter, I unpack the neuroscience behind rejection — why it feels personal, how it quietly shapes behaviour, and how to regain control in the moment that matters most. This is not about positive thinking. It is about understanding how your brain works under pressure — and using that knowledge to your advantage.

If you want to stop second-guessing, sharpen your conversations, and turn rejection into forward momentum, the Winning Mind Newsletter is worth your time.
