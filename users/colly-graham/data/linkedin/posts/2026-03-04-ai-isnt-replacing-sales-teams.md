AI isn’t replacing sales teams.  It’s exposing outdated prospecting.
A Sales Director told me recently,
 “We don’t use AI. We believe in relationships.”
But their competitors are using AI to spot buying signals and prioritise accounts — before a salesperson even picks up the phone.
They’re not less human.
They’re more prepared.
AI handles identification.
Humans build trust.
If your team is still manually building lists for hours each week, is that really relationship-led… or just habit?
How are you balancing AI efficiency with human connection right now?
