Understanding your patterns does not change your sales. Practising a new response does. Many capable salespeople can name every habit that holds them back, and still sell the same way on Monday morning.
The new issue of my Winning Mindset newsletter is about closing that gap. It sets out one simple equation, Event plus Response equals Outcome, and six practices you can rehearse in an ordinary selling day: belief, resilience, optimism, growth, empathy and discipline. Each one is drawn from real research, from Bandura on self-efficacy, to Dweck on growth, to Frankl on the space between what happens and how we meet it.
Here is the question I keep returning to with the teams I train. When a sale goes quiet or a quarter turns hard, where does your response actually begin? Tell me in the comments, and I will read every one.
