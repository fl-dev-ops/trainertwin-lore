In the latest salesxcellence newsletter, I set out seven human skills sales teams need to succeed with AI, and how to choose which one to develop first. It is an eight minute read. 
69 per cent of employers say the skill they value most is not technical. It is analytical thinking.
That figure comes from the World Economic Forum Future of Jobs Report 2025, with resilience, flexibility and agility close behind at 67 per cent. AI is changing how fast we work, however it has not changed why people buy. Buyers still choose the seller who listens well, thinks clearly and keeps their word.
Which of these skills is your team working on right now?
