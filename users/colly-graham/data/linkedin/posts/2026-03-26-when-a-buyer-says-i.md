When a buyer says:
“I need to think about it.”
Most salespeople assume the buyer needs more information.
Often the opposite is true.
The buyer doesn’t need more logic.
They need more certainty.
Something in the conversation has left the emotional brain unconvinced.
Not pressured.
Not unsafe.
Just… uncertain.
Great salespeople don’t push harder at that moment.
They slow down and rebuild clarity.
