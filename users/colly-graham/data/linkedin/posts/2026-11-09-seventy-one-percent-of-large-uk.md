Seventy-one percent of large UK organisations admit they present themselves as more AI-mature than they really are. That figure comes from Avanade research published last week, and it should give every sales leader pause before the next confident claim about their team’s AI capability. In this week’s Monday AI Brief, I look at three developments that tell one clear story: AI maturity is not the number of tools you have deployed, however it is your ability to explain decisions, improve performance and preserve human judgement.
Inside, you will find what the research really says and where its limits lie, why AI prospecting platforms are now showing their reasoning, and how executive training is shifting from tools towards judgement. I have also included three practical exercises you can use with your team this week, including a simple test I call “Do Not Automate a Conclusion You Cannot Explain”. It is an eight-minute read, and I would welcome your view on one question: is your AI maturity based on what you say, or on what the evidence shows?
