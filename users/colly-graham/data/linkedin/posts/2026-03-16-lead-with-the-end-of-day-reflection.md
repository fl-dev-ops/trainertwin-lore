Lead with the end-of-day reflection
At the end of your next sales day, ask yourself three questions. Where did I stay steady? Where did I get pulled off centre? And how quickly did I recover? Because recovery — not perfection — is the real measure of emotional strength in sales.
This week's Winning Mindset newsletter is all about emotional mastery — what it actually means, why emotional control is not enough, and how your inner state is shaping your results in ways you may not fully see yet.
