Most salespeople lose deals not because of price, timing, or competition. They lose because they speak to the wrong part of the buyer's brain — in the wrong order. After 56 years in frontline sales, I can tell you this is the single most overlooked issue in selling today.
The buyer's brain has three decision layers — Primitive, Emotional, and Rational. Most sales training starts with the third one. That's the mistake. Safety comes first. Trust comes second. Logic comes third. Get the sequence wrong and even the strongest business case stalls.
I've written about this in full in my latest LinkedIn newsletter — The Brain-Based Sale: How to Sell the Way the Brain Buys. If you want to understand why buyers say "let me think about it" — and what to do instead — this one's worth three minutes of your time. Link in the comments.
