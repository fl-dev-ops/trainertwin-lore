Reflecting on my life at 81 and a half, I recently had a dream that resonated with my journey. It was late evening, and I had missed the last bus home. Just then, a luxury coach filled with tourists on an expensive trip arrived. They welcomed me warmly and wished me well on my journey. 

At times, I wonder if I've missed the bus in life, but then I remember my experiences delivering training across various locations: Belfast, Dublin, London, Birmingham, Manchester, Malaysia (four times), Singapore (three times), Dubai (twice), Mauritius (twice for 10 days), Bangkok (twice), Sydney, and Melbourne. I've had the privilege of staying in 4 and 5-star hotels, all expenses paid, along with four-figure fees.

This week, a client has requested training for their salespeople on AI, and another company has purchased five copies of my book, "The Winning Mindset for Salespeople," planning to run weekly training sessions based on it. 

No, I didn't miss the bus. It's been quite a ride for an 81-year-old.
