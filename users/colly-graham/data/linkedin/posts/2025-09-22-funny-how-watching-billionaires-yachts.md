“Funny how watching billionaires’ yachts in the bay can teach you more about sales than a pipeline report.”

🌴 This morning I’m on my hotel balcony at Palms Nova Bay, watching the billionaires’ yachts drift across the water.

It’s a beautiful view — but also a reminder:
achievement is built not just on action,
but on recovery.

I can feel the difference myself.
Away from the endless rush, my mind begins to clear.

Neuroscience explains why.
When we pause, the brain shifts into its default mode network — the space where creativity, insight, and problem-solving come alive.

Too often in sales, we treat downtime as indulgence.
In truth, it’s fuel.

Just as a high-performance car needs to refuel, our brains need rest to perform at their peak. Without it, we’re running on empty.

My takeaway as I look out across the bay:
Schedule recovery with the same intention as prospecting.

It’s not wasted time.
It’s the preparation that makes excellence possible. 

Where do you find the best place to switch off and let your brain refuel?

Do you see downtime as a luxury — or part of your sales strategy
