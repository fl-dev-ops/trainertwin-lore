Let me ask a question to the salespeople here.
Have you ever delivered a presentation where the client agreed with everything…
✔ The problem was clear
✔ The numbers made sense
✔ The solution was logical
And yet the deal still stalled?
For years I believed sales success came down to preparation and explanation. But neuroscience research from people like Daniel Kahneman and Antonio Damasio suggests something far more interesting about how buying decisions actually happen.
In this short 8-minute read, I explore why the brain often decides emotionally before logic even enters the conversation — and how the most effective salespeople structure conversations around that reality.
I'm curious about your experience.
In your sales conversations, what tends to drive the real decision first — logic or feeling?
