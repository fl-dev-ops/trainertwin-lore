The Proposal Drift Problem
A strong discovery call.
 Positive alignment.
 A proposal delivered.
Then silence.
Not rejection.
Drift.
The majority of stalled deals don’t collapse — they lose momentum.
Without structured velocity discipline, optimism replaces visibility.
AI linked to CRM systems can:
• Flag inactivity thresholds
 • Trigger intelligent follow-up
 • Surface at-risk opportunities
 • Remove unrealistic close dates
Forecasting should be operational.
Not emotional.
How many deals in your pipeline right now are technically “alive” but strategically drifting?
That’s not a sales problem.
It’s a system problem.
