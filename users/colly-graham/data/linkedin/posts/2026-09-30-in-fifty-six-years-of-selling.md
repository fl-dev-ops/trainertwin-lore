In fifty-six years of selling across eleven countries, I have seen methods come and go. One thing has never changed. The buyer decides who to trust before deciding what to buy. In the early days of salesxcellence, I walked into a boardroom with a blank sheet of paper while four larger competitors brought slick slideshows. I asked two questions, I listened, and I won the business.
This week's Brain Based newsletter asks one question. When the world will not stand still, who wins the sale? Neuroscience gives a clear answer. Research from Caltech and the work of Paul Zak show that the brain treats uncertainty as a threat, and that pressure works against the chemistry of trust. Your buyers are not being difficult. Their brains are doing what they were built to do.
Inside you will find six practical principles for the trust-based salesperson, each one you can start tomorrow morning without new software or a bigger budget. There is also a simple guide to choosing where to start, depending on whether buyers are going quiet, your calls feel like presentations, or your team is busy but results are flat. It is a seven-minute read. Open the newsletter below, and tell me in the comments which principle would make the biggest difference to your team this quarter.
