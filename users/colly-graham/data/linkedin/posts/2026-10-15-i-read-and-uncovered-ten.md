I read and uncovered ten problems that sales leaders posted about this week, all in their own words. Seven of them turned out to be the same problem. Pipeline that never qualifies. Prospects who enjoy the demonstration and never decide. A forecast that nobody in the room believes. The symptoms are different, however the cause is the same. The salesperson presented before they understood.
In this edition of the Brain Based Newsletter, I take all ten in turn and show where the BRAIN Selling System deals with the cause. I also name the two problems it will not fix, and the three where it does only half the job. A trainer who claims to fix everything has not been listening. After fifty-six years in frontline sales, I would rather give you the honest version.
The article ends with four things any sales leader can do before the quarter ends, and none of them needs a budget. Start with your own forecast. Ask the five BRAIN questions of every opportunity in it and count the yeses. Then tell me in the comments which of the ten problems costs your team the most.
