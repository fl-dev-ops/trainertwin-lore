Your team has too many AI tools slowing sales. I have spent 56 years in frontline B2B sales, and I have never seen salespeople so busy doing so little selling. One tool writes the email. Another scores the lead. A third records the call, and a fourth summarises the recording. Each one promised to save time. Together, they have quietly taken it away. Your people now spend their day feeding systems instead of talking to buyers. The problem is not the technology. I use AI every day and I value it. The problem is that nobody asked the simple question first: does this tool help my salesperson have a better conversation with a customer? If the answer is not a clear yes, it is a distraction with a monthly subscription. More dashboards do not create more trust, and a buyer has never signed an order because your tech stack was impressive. Here is my suggestion. Sit down with your team this week and list every AI tool they touch. Keep the ones that give them more time in front of customers. Question the rest. Then invest what you save in the one thing no software can do for them, which is the human conversation. People still buy from people, and that is where sales are won.
