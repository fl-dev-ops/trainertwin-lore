Most people still think trust in sales is built by confidence and persuasion.
 But in a world where everyone sounds competent — and AI can generate fluent answers instantly — that’s no longer the differentiator.
What actually builds trust now is far quieter.
 It’s the ability to slow the moment, reduce pressure, and make it safe for another person to think clearly — without being pushed.
In the modern sales world, the most trusted professionals won’t be the most impressive.
 They’ll be the ones who leave people calmer, clearer, and more confident in their own decisions.
