AI will not replace great salespeople. It will expose average ones.
That is the uncomfortable truth facing sales today.
Artificial intelligence can research customers, analyse markets, draft emails, prepare questions and update the CRM faster than any human being.
So if your value rests on knowing the product, repeating information and following a process, that value is already shrinking.
My latest salesxcellence newsletter, "The Future of Sales: Why Artificial Intelligence Needs Human Intelligence", explains who wins from here.
Not those who resist AI.
Not those who hand their judgement over to it.
The winners will be the salespeople who use AI to become better prepared, more thoughtful and more relevant, while bringing what no machine can offer. Trust. Empathy. Courage. Insight. The ability to help another human being make a difficult decision.
The future of sales belongs to neither the technophobe nor the AI worshipper. It belongs to the person who combines both forms of intelligence.

Is AI making you more valuable to your customers, or is it quietly making your role easier to replace?
