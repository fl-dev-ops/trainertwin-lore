Your next team member may not be human, and nobody has written its job description yet. In the past week, OpenAI introduced always-on AI agents that work continuously towards defined goals. BT launched an AI receptionist for small businesses, and Ireland appointed its first AI Sectoral Champions. Each announcement carries the same message for sales leaders and CEOs: AI is moving from assistant to participant in the customer journey.
I have trained sales teams in eleven countries, and one pattern repeats whenever a new tool arrives. Activity rises, yet nobody asks what commercially meaningful result actually improved. In Monday's AI Brief I set out three practical exercises for your next leadership meeting: the Digital Sales Role Description, the First-Contact Handover Map and the Sector AI Prospecting Lab. Each helps you decide what AI may do, when a human must step in and who remains accountable.
AI may answer the first call, however a salesperson must still earn the next conversation. The full brief is an eight minute read, and it ends with a simple test to help you decide whether to continue, revise or pause any AI-assisted sales activity. https://lnkd.in/eZcKUZzM
