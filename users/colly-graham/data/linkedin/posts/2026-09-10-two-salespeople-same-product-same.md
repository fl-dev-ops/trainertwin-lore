Two salespeople, same product, same market, wildly different results. One had every qualification a sales manager could want. The other broke almost every rule in the book and still won at nearly one hundred and forty per cent of target. The difference had nothing to do with talent or effort. It came down to which part of the buyer's brain each of them was actually speaking to.
In this week's Brain Based Newsletter, I unpack the three-brain model that decides every sale before logic ever gets a vote. The primitive brain checks for safety first. The emotional brain makes the actual decision. The rational brain simply justifies whatever the emotional brain has already decided. Most sales training aims straight at that rational brain, opening with data and features before safety or emotion have been addressed, which explains why so many strong presentations still lose the sale. The piece also walks through the BRAIN-Based Selling Methodology, five stages built to move a conversation in the order the brain naturally works.
