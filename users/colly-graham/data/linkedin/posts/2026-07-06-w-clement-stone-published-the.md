W. Clement Stone published The Success System That Never Fails in 1962, and more than sixty years later, some of his ideas still deserve our attention. In this week’s Winning Mindset newsletter, I explore the honest connection between Stone’s Positive Mental Attitude, his three ingredients for repeatable success, and the five pillars I now teach as part of a modern winning mindset.
This is not about blind optimism, pretending the numbers look better than they do, or forcing motivation when it has gone quiet. It is about acceptance, certainty, disciplined action, reframing challenges, and guarding the environment that shapes your thinking before the working day even begins. An eight-minute read for salespeople, sales leaders, and anyone who believes success is not accidental — it is practised.
