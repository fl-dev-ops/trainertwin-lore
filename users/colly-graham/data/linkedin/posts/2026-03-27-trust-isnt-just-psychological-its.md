Trust isn’t just psychological.
It’s chemical.
When two people experience genuine human connection, the brain releases oxytocin.
Oxytocin lowers defences and increases openness.
This is why meaningful conversations outperform perfect presentations.
Great questions create connection.
Connection creates trust.
And trust changes how the brain evaluates risk.
