The Frame Shift
Most salespeople think they are influencing a decision.
They’re not.
The decision is already forming before the conversation even begins.
Not logically.
Neurologically.
Because the human brain is not asking:
 “Is this a good solution?”
It’s asking something far older:
“Is this safe?”
And until that question is answered…
No amount of information will move the sale forward. This is the part most people miss. The sale is not won in your presentation.
It’s won in the state of the buyer’s nervous system.
Curious — how often do you feel a sale slipping… even when your logic is strong?
