Most salespeople lose the sale before they even start presenting.
Not because their solution is wrong.
Not because their price is too high.
But because they begin with answers instead of questions.
After 56 years in frontline selling across 12 countries, I’ve learned something simple but powerful: the best salespeople don’t impress buyers with presentations — they unlock conversations with questions. One structural trick used is this: the quality of your questions determines the quality of your sales intelligence — and that determines the outcome of the sale.
In this week’s Salesxcellence Winning Mindset Newsletter, I break down the neuroscience behind sales questions and share the BRAIN Question Framework™ — a practical model that helps salespeople move from presenting to discovering, from talking to truly understanding. If you want buyers leaning forward instead of politely listening, this may change how you run every sales conversation.
