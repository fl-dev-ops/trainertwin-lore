There is a moment every salesperson recognises — even if they’ve never said it out loud.
 The call ends. The email lands. The room goes quiet.
 And the answer is no.
What most people don’t realise is this: in that instant, the outcome is no longer commercial — it’s neurological. Your brain registers threat before it registers context. And in that brief window, your ability to think clearly, respond intelligently, and learn effectively is temporarily reduced. This week’s Winning Mindset Newsletter goes beneath the surface of rejection — not as a mindset cliché, but as a biological event — and shows you how to turn that moment from a setback into a strategic advantage.
Because the difference between those who plateau and those who compound is not talent — it’s recovery. The best don’t avoid rejection. They process it faster. They separate identity from outcome. They reset their nervous system, extract insight, and step forward again with clarity. If you’ve ever felt rejection linger longer than it should, this piece will give you a practical, neuroscience-based way to take control of what happens next.
