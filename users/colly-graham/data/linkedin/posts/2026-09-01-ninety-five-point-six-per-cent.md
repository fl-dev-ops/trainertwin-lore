Ninety-five point six per cent of sales teams now use AI. That number sounds like progress, until you meet the one sitting underneath it. Nearly two-thirds, sixty-three per cent, are still bolting AI onto the old way of selling, so the results stay small. Adoption is easy. Advantage is not. That gap is the whole story.
I read Allego's 2026 AI in Revenue Enablement Report and pulled out six findings that matter for anyone who sells. Onboarding time down for eighty-three per cent of teams. Sales cycles shorter for seventy-three per cent. Each finding comes with one action you can take this week, not a theory for next year. The difference between the teams pulling ahead and the teams standing still comes down to a single word. Integration.
