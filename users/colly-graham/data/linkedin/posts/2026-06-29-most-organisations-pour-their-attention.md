Most organisations pour their attention into strategy, process and activity. All three matter. Yet CEOs, sales leaders and CROs know there is another force at work beneath the numbers.
   It is how people respond when pressure rises, confidence dips, targets stretch, and uncertainty walks into the room.
This week's Winning Mindset newsletter looks at why success is not sustained by effort alone. It is shaped by the meanings we make, the assumptions we carry, and the space between what happens to us and how we choose to respond.
   That space is where resilience, composure and real leadership begin.
If you lead a business or a sales team, this one is worth your time. The strongest teams are not simply the ones who work harder.
   They are the ones who learn to work with their own minds. They recover quickly. They stay steady under pressure. They choose their response before circumstances choose it for them.A question for the leaders here. When pressure rose in your team last quarter, did performance hold or wobble? In my experience the difference is rarely effort. It is what people did with the space between the pressure and their response?
