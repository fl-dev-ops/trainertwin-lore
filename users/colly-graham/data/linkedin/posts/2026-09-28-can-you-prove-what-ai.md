Can you prove what AI adds to your sales results? Last week the Office for National Statistics said that, across the UK economy, nobody can yet separate AI's value from the software, data and people around it. If the national statistician cannot do it, a dashboard of hours saved and messages sent will not do it either.
In the same week, Amazon blocked Meta's Muse agent from shopping on its site. Amazon says the agent did not ask permission and did not identify itself. Banks including NatWest and Bank of America then warned that AI agents are moving faster than the rules. That is retail, yet prospecting, meeting-request and procurement agents will soon face the same questions. Who is this? Who allowed it? Who answers for it?
