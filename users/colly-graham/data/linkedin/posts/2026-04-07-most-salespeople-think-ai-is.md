Most salespeople think AI is the advantage. It isn’t. The advantage is understanding how the human brain decides—because long before logic kicks in, something far older is already at work. In my latest newsletter, AI Prospecting • Neuroscience of Selling, I break down why prospecting hasn’t really changed in 56 years… even though the tools have. From paper directories to AI, the same mistake keeps repeating: we reach for technology before we understand the person reading the message. The result? Activity that looks like progress—but doesn’t create connection.
If you’re using AI in your prospecting (or thinking about it), this will sharpen your thinking immediately. You’ll see how to align with how the brain actually makes decisions—where safety, relevance, and timing quietly determine whether your message lives or dies in seconds. AI can accelerate your preparation, but it cannot replace presence, judgement, or genuine interest. That part is still yours. If you want better conversations—not just more outreach—this is where to start.
