Most sales conversations are lost long before price is ever mentioned.
They change in a quiet moment — when the buyer asks themselves a question you will never hear:
"Can I trust this person?"
They may still smile. Still nod. Still ask questions.
But if they do not feel safe, understood, and in control — the real conversation never fully opens.
In this week's Salesxcellence Newsletter, I explore why trust is not a technique, a clever question, or a polished presentation.
It is built through five things:
→ Intention → Listening → Truth → Consistency → Follow-through
The best salespeople do not rush to prove value.
They create the conditions where the buyer feels safe enough to tell the truth.
And that changes everything.
If you want better sales conversations, start with a better question —
"What does this buyer need to feel safe enough to be honest with me?"
That one shift can change the quality of every conversation that follows.
