After fifty-six years in sales, I’ve learned something most professionals never realise.
The brain does not decide to trust someone logically.
It decides neurologically — and it happens in seconds.
Two professionals can offer similar expertise.
 Two companies can provide almost identical solutions.
Yet one immediately feels credible…
 and the other doesn’t.
Why?
Because long before we analyse price, features, or return on investment, the brain is quietly asking a much older question.
“Is this person safe to trust?”
In my latest Brain-Based Sales newsletter, I explore the neuroscience behind online trust — and why understanding how the brain forms trust may be one of the most important advantages a modern salesperson can have.
