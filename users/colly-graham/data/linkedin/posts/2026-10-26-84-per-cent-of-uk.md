84 per cent of UK business leaders believe the future looks bright. Only 41 per cent of their employees agree. That gap is not a technology problem. It is a leadership problem, and it is quietly shaping how salespeople show up in front of customers every day.
In this week’s Monday Morning AI Sales Brief, I look at new Microsoft-supported UK research alongside Ibec’s 2026 Talent Trends Report. Many people say AI has improved their work, however many also feel less secure because of it. When people cannot see their place in the future, they protect themselves rather than experiment. I share two practical exercises you can run with your team this week: the AI Change Conversation and Stop, Shift, Strengthen.
Having trained salespeople across eleven countries, I remain convinced that relationships come first, with customers and with our own teams. AI does not change that principle. It raises the stakes on it. Have you explained the future to your salespeople yet? Read the full brief and tell me in the comments how your organisation is handling it.
