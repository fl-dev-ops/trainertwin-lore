AI is making sales outreach easier to produce, and much harder to tell apart. That was one of the clearest messages I found while researching this week’s developments in AI selling.
In this edition of the Monday Morning AI Sales Brief, I look at AI moving inside the shared sales workspace, live coaching during calls and new platform ambitions from Japan. I also cover what practitioners are reporting about high-volume outreach, and what the more credible research says about revenue. Every item has been checked against its source, and I have been clear where a claim comes from the vendor alone.
My view, after training sellers across eleven countries, has not changed. AI can carry more of the preparation and paperwork than ever, however buyers still decide whom they trust in a human conversation.
