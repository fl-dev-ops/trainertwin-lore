Short showreel from my presentation On Winning Mindset for High Performance in an AI World In the video I tell the true story of three sales people, here are their stories:-
Jim was a salesperson whose belief had collapsed entirely. He wept on a pavement — not from self-pity, but from the moment he realised he had been performing rather than serving. He rebuilt his belief architecture brick by brick. His conversion rate increased by forty per cent within nine months.
Ken had been passed over for promotion twice. Every performance metric confirmed his ability, yet the moment pressure arrived — a difficult boardroom, a sceptical buyer — he retreated inward. He learned one discipline: four breaths in, eight breaths out, sixty seconds of chosen stillness before every significant meeting. That ritual became his resilience. Ken went on to found his own company.
Martin had five filing cabinets filled with proposals worth over five million pounds. He had followed up on none of them. The question that changed everything was not strategic. It was deeply personal.
What story are you telling yourself?
