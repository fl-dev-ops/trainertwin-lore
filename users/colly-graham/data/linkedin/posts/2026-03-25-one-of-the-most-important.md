One of the most important discoveries in neuroscience should change how every salesperson thinks about selling.
Patients with damage to the emotional centres of the brain could still think logically.
Their reasoning was intact.
But they couldn’t make decisions.
Not big ones.
Not small ones.
Some couldn’t even choose what to eat for lunch.
Without emotion, the brain becomes stuck.
The implication for sales is simple.
Your buyer cannot decide until they feel something.
Logic supports decisions.
Emotion starts them.
