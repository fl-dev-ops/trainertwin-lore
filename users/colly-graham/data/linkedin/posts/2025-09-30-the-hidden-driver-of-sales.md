The Hidden Driver of Sales Performance

What if your next big sales win doesn’t come from a new script, CRM feature, or incentive plan—but from something invisible?

When most leaders think about improving sales performance, they focus on skills, tools, or compensation. However, there’s a deeper, often overlooked driver of success: belonging.

Owen Eastwood, elite performance coach to teams like the Ryder Cup, All Blacks, and England Football, emphasizes that belonging is the foundation of high performance. His book, "Belonging", explores how individuals thrive when they feel part of something bigger than themselves.

So what does this mean for sales?

It means that beyond targets and training, the real competitive edge comes from creating a culture where reps feel:
- Connected to a mission, not just a quota.
- Safe to share mistakes and learn faster.
- Valued for their strengths, not just their numbers.
- Proud to be part of a team that wins together.

Skills make sellers capable. Belonging makes them unstoppable.

How are you building belonging in your sales team?
