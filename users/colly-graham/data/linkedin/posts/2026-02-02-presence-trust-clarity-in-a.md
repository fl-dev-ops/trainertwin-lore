Presence. Trust. Clarity.
 In a world of constant noise, the real competitive edge in sales isn’t another tactic or tool — it’s the ability to be fully present when it matters most. This week’s Salesxcellence newsletter explores why presence has quietly become one of the most powerful differentiators in modern selling.
