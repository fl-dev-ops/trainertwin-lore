"I just trust him completely. I cannot really explain it beyond that." That is what a client said about the adviser she has stayed with for twenty-five years. She could not explain it. Neuroscience can.
My latest Brain Based Newsletter, "The Brain on Trust", looks at what happens in the buyer's brain when trust forms, why it starts within the first five minutes, and why it can fracture in a moment. Trust is not one variable in the sales process. It is the operating environment of the whole process.
An eight minute read. Then tell me in the comments. When did you know a buyer had decided to trust you?
