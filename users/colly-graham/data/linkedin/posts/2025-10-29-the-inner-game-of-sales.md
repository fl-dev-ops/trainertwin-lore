The Inner Game of Sales
In my late teens and early twenties, I struggled with poor mental health. On the outside I looked fine, but inside I was fighting a storm.
What turned things around was discovering I could change how I thought. Building a winning mindset gave me resilience, confidence, and a new future.
Sales tests mindset more than any career. Calls go unanswered. Meetings cancelled. Most often, the answer is “no.” Without the right mindset, confidence slips and even strong salespeople start to doubt themselves.
That’s why I wrote Winning Mindset for Sales People. It’s not about quick tips. It’s about bouncing back, reframing rejection as feedback, and showing up every day with belief.
True success in sales starts with mindset. Win the battle in your mind, and you can win in sales — and in life.
