Most sales conversations don’t fail because of poor solutions. They fail because discovery never went deep enough. When questions stay surface-level, buyers stay polite, unconvinced, and undecided. Discovery isn’t a stage you rush through — it’s the engine that creates clarity, value, and momentum.
In this week’s Salesxcellence newsletter, I break down discovery and probing questions as a thinking methodology, not a checklist. We look at how great salespeople move conversations from context → impact → meaning, and how the right follow-up question can unlock insight buyers didn’t know how to express.
If your conversations feel busy but not decisive, this one’s for you. Read the full piece and sharpen the skill that quietly separates average salespeople from trusted advisors.
