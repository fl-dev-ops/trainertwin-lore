In this week's Winning Newsletter avaiable now The Hidden Reason Sales Performance Breaks Down and what leaders overlook in the process We spend an extraordinary amount of time talking about skills, techniques and strategy. Yet the more I work with commercial teams, the clearer one thing becomes:
Performance rarely breaks down because of capability. It breaks down because of mindset. Not mindset in the fluffy, motivational sense — but mindset in the behavioural sense: how people interpret pressure, what their internal dialogue sounds like, and how quickly they can return to clear thinking. 
The irony? Most organisations respond by increasing the very pressure that caused the problem.
