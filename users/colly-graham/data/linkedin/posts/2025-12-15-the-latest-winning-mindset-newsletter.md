The latest Winning Mindset Newsletter is now live on LinkedIn — and this week we’re digging into one of the biggest silent blockers of progress: self-limiting beliefs.
Most people aren’t stuck because they lack talent. They’re stuck because they’re running an old internal script.
Self-limiting beliefs are those quiet assumptions that whisper, “I’m not good enough,” “I’m not confident enough,” or “people like me don’t succeed at that.” The problem is they don’t show up as facts — they show up as conclusions. And conclusions can be upgraded.
Here’s a simple move to try today: write down one belief that’s been holding you back, then list a few real moments that prove it isn’t the whole truth. Replace it with a statement that keeps the door open, like “I’m learning to…” or “I’m becoming…” — and take one small bold action that matches the new story.
Action creates evidence. Evidence builds belief. Belief becomes identity. Time to update the script.
