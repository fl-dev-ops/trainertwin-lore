Two clickbait offers reached me this week.

Both wore a suit. One was a glossy graphic promising a complete revenue system built from sixty-four AI agents. The other was a warm invitation to earn a precise six-figure sum as a certified trainer, for a minimal contribution from my side. Both looked polished. Neither survived a second read. The tells are the same once you know them:

● It sells the outcome before it understands your situation.
● It quotes an exact figure, then calls the cost to you minimal.
● It flatters your profile with words that would fit ten thousand people.
● It counts features rather than results, and hurries you toward a Zoom call.
Genuine value moves more slowly.

It asks questions first.
One question protects you every time. Ask what it truly costs, all in, before any benefit is discussed. Then watch how quickly the warmth cools when the answer has to be honest. Real partners are comfortable with that question. Their model rewards you rather than charges you. The polish is never the point. The business model underneath it always is.

What has landed in your feed lately that failed the second read?
