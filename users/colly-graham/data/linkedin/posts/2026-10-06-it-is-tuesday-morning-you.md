It is Tuesday morning. You know the follow-up should go out today. You also know exactly why you keep putting it off. Understanding has arrived. The behaviour has not.
That gap is where many thoughtful salespeople get stuck, and it is the subject of my latest Winning Mindset newsletter: 6 Practices That Turn Sales Insight Into Behaviour. Inside you will find the E + R = O equation, six practices for belief, resilience, optimism, growth, empathy and discipline, and my PRAMKU method for choosing a direction that fits your real quarter. I have also included a short guide to help you choose where to start.
It is an eight minute read, and every idea can be used on a normal Tuesday. Read it, choose one practice, and tell me in the comments which one you will begin this week.
