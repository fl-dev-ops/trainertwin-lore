There is a moment every salesperson knows. The call ends. The email lands. The answer is no. And in the seconds that follow, something shifts — not in the market, not in the buyer, but in your own mind. Most people think their career is shaped by results. It isn’t. It’s shaped by what happens in the moment after the result.
What follows rejection is not just emotion — it’s biology. The brain processes social rejection like pain. And unless you understand what happens next, it quietly alters how you show up: the questions you ask, the confidence you carry, the conversations you create. This is where performance is won or lost — not in the outcome, but in the recovery.
In this week’s Brain-Based Sales Newsletter, I break down the hidden sequence that follows every setback — and the four disciplines that shorten the recovery gap. Because resilience is not a personality trait. It is a commercial skill.
