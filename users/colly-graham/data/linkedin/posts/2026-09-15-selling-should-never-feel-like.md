Selling should never feel like a fight. Yet every sales team I have trained across fifty-six years and eleven countries tells me the same thing. It is harder than it needs to be.
The struggle rarely comes from the market, the price or the competition. It comes from a salesperson trying to persuade a brain that was never built to be persuaded, only to be helped to think. My latest newsletter sets out six ways Brain Based Sales removes that struggle, from putting safety before change to discovering value instead of declaring it.
If you lead a team, or sell for a living, this one is worth eight minutes of your time. Read it, then tell me which of the six shows up most often in your own conversations.
