"The Question That Changes Everything in Sales"
After 56 years in sales across 12 countries, I've noticed something striking. The top performers rarely talk about targets. They talk about why they sell. And that one word — why — separates the salespeople who burn bright and fade from those who build something extraordinary over a career.
In my latest newsletter I share the question I ask every salesperson I work with. Not about their pipeline. Not about their close rate. The answer reveals more about future performance than any KPI dashboard ever could. I also share what happened four months after I founded salesxcellence in 1996 — and what the only thing was that kept me going.
