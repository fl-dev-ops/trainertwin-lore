WHY SO MANY SALES CONVERSATIONS GO WRONG
Most sales conversations begin in the wrong place. The salesperson starts with the solution, then explains the features, then demonstrates the benefits, then shows the numbers. That feels efficient to the salesperson. But to the buyer's brain, it can feel premature. The buyer may still be wondering whether you understand their situation, whether you are simply there to sell, whether any of this is relevant to them, and what happens if they open up too much. While the buyer is asking those questions internally, the salesperson is busy explaining the solution externally. That is the disconnect. The salesperson thinks they are being clear. The buyer feels they are being moved too quickly. The salesperson thinks the logic is obvious. The buyer has not yet emotionally engaged.
