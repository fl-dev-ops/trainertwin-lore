One in three small business owners now want AI to handle their sales work.
Forbes reported this week that Anthropic expanded Claude for Small Business to include 43 new workflows for lead follow-up, proposals, and marketing. The plugin has been installed 900,000 times since May.
What this means for your team:
• Automation moves beyond admin into discovery and pipeline management
• A business owner with one employee can now operate like a ten-person sales function
• SMBs are no longer waiting for enterprise tools to become affordable—they are building their own
• Sales conversations have shifted from "do we have capacity" to "what can we sell better"
• The winners are those who train their people to direct the AI, not replace themselves with it
Sales training is no longer about scripts. It is about teaching your team to lead an AI through the conversational moves that actually close deals.
