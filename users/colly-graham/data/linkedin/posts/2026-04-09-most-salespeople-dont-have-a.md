Most salespeople don’t have a prospecting problem…
They have a starting problem.
Let me explain.
I’ve watched this for years — across industries, across generations, across every era of selling.
People don’t avoid prospecting because they’re lazy.
They avoid it because they’re uncertain.
Who exactly should I contact?
What do I say?
Will this even work?
So what happens?
They delay.
They overthink.
They “prepare”…
And the pipeline quietly dries up.
Here’s what’s really going on.
The human brain is wired for safety first.
And when there’s no clear starting point, the brain treats that as risk.
So it chooses the safest option available:
Do nothing
Or stay busy with low-value work that feels productive
That’s not a discipline issue.
It’s a clarity issue.
And clarity changes everything.
Because when the starting point is clear, structured, and repeatable…
Action becomes natural.
Prospecting becomes consistent.
And pipeline becomes predictable.
This is the shift most people miss.
Not more effort.
Better starting.
Curious — what part of prospecting do you find hardest to start?
