Two salespeople. Same product, same territory, same target. Three years later, one is burned out and the other is thriving. I have watched this happen again and again across eleven countries and fifty-six years in sales and sales leadership. . The difference is rarely talent or luck. It is purpose.
Quotas matter. They create urgency and focus. When they become your only fuel, however, rejection cuts deeper and success feels hollow. In this edition, I set out the four hidden costs of quota-only selling. I also share four practical steps to find your deeper why, and three daily habits that keep it alive.
