The future of sales will not be decided by AI.
 It will be decided by who learns to use AI first.
Over the next year we will see Artificial Intelligence move from experimentation to everyday execution inside the sales function. Instead of being a standalone tool, AI will be embedded directly into core workflows — helping sales teams research accounts, prepare for conversations, analyse buying signals, and coordinate follow-up. A new generation of agentic AI systems will even carry out multi-step work such as prospect research, drafting communications, and organising information across the sales cycle. For organisations that understand how to use it well, the productivity gains will be substantial.
But this shift also changes the landscape for sales professionals. As AI automates many routine tasks, the advantage will move to those who combine human judgement, meaningful conversations, and AI-powered insight. Over the coming weeks I’ll be announcing a practical workshop on how to use AI effectively in the sales function — focused on helping salespeople and sales leaders apply AI in the real world.
The future of sales is arriving quickly.
Are you ready?
