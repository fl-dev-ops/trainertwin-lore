The Brain-Based Sale
Our flagship methodology is built on one question: how does the brain actually make buying decisions? Using frameworks from neuroscience — the triune brain model, mirror neurons, RAS activation, loss aversion, and the neurochemistry of trust — we show your team how to work with the buyer's brain, not against it.
These are the same principles studied by Damasio, Kahneman, Rizzolatti, and Doty — made practical, memorable, and immediately applicable on the phone or in the room.
- Triune brain model — why logic alone never closes a sale
- Mirror neurons — how rapport is built (and broken) in seconds
- RAS activation — making your message stick in the buyer's mind
- Loss aversion — ethical framing that accelerates decisions
- Neurochemistry of trust — what oxytocin and cortisol mean for sales
