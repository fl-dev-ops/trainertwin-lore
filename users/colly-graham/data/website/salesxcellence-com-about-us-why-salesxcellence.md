56 Years of Frontline Sales Experience — Not Theory
Colly Graham has not studied sales from a lecture theatre. He has lived it — across 12 countries,

The BRAIN Question Framework Changes How Your Team Discovers
Most salespeople ask questions. The best salespeople ask the right questions in the right
sequence. The BRAIN Question Framework — Background, Results and Challenges, Ambitions,
Impact, Next Steps — is a neuroscience-informed discovery model that surfaces real buying
motivations, eliminates wasted conversations, and positions every salesxcellence-trained
professional as a trusted adviser rather than a vendor.
Mindset and Skills Are Addressed Together — Not Separately
Skills without the right mindset are unreliable under pressure. The most technically proficient
salesperson will still avoid the difficult call, abandon the stalled negotiation, or collapse under
rejection if their belief system is working against them. salesxcellence addresses both
simultaneously — which is why our results persist long after other training has been forgotten.
Every Programme Is Bespoke — Not Off the Shelf
There is no standard salesxcellence programme that gets re-badged and delivered regardless of
context. Every engagement begins with a diagnostic — a rigorous assessment of your team's
current skills, behaviours, beliefs, and pipeline discipline. What follows is designed specifically
for your people, your market, and the outcomes your business needs. This is not common in the
industry. At salesxcellence it is non-negotiable.
