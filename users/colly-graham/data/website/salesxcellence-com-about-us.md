Colly Graham - Bio
Colly Graham — Founder & CEO, salesxcellence
Colly Graham is one of the UK's most decorated and experienced sales trainers, named by the British Excellence in Sales & Marketing Awards (BESMA) as one of the top eight sales trainers in the United Kingdom.
With 56 years of frontline sales experience spanning 11 countries
With 56 years of frontline sales experience spanning 11 countries — United Kingdom, Ireland, UAE, Mauritius, Thailand, Malaysia, Singapore, Spain, Australia, the USA, and Canada — Colly brings a depth of real-world expertise that few trainers anywhere in the world can match. He has worked with over 1,000 companies across three decades, helping B2B sales teams to prospect smarter, sell with integrity, and consistently exceed their targets.
Colly founded salesxcellence in 1996 and has built it into Northern Ireland's leading specialist B2B sales training and consultancy.
