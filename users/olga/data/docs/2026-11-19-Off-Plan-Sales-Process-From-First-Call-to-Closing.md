Price Behaviour & ROI

WE MAKE the Client Calculate the approximate ROI
ROI = (Final Value of Investment + Total Rental Income − Initial Cost of Investment) ÷ Initial Cost of Investment × 100
1. Final Value of Investment: This is the value of the property when you are calculating the ROI. This could be the current market value if you're evaluating performance, or the sale price if you've sold the property.
2. Initial Cost of Investment: This includes the purchase price of the property plus any additional costs incurred to make the property ready for sale or rental, such as renovations or legal fees.
3. Total Rental Income: This is the sum of all rental income received during the period you held the investment.
Example: (1 600 000 + 140 000 − 1 000 000) ÷ 1 000 000 × 100 = 74%. So if this Property was purchased 4 years ago we divide 74% ÷ 4 = 18,5% per year
