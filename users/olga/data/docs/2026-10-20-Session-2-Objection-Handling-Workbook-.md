PART 1 · MINDSET
What is an Objection
Objection is only a concern that your client has, it's not a rejection of you.
It's not the time to react to any objection.
You and your client need to work as a team. First to understand the concern, undercover what is behind the concern and then work together as a team to help the client overcome it.

PART 2 · THE FORMULA
3-Step Objection Handling Formula
1. Clarify → 2. Discuss → 3. Diffuse
Clarify - we never assume, just based on our past experiences, what this means for this exact client.
Clarifying Questions to Ask
* “Now…when you say (repeat back what they said) what do you mean by that?”
* “I'm curious, did you ask me that for a reason?”
* “Can I ask where you got this information from?”
* “Can you tell me more about that?”
* “When you say (repeat back the concern), what's behind that, just so I understand?”
Discuss - When you understand what is the actual concern you can discuss what are the possible ways to overcome it.
Diffuse - Offer a perspective, or a solution, that did work for other clients before.
* “Can I offer a suggestion? The way the other clients did that is…”
* “Do you feel this could be an option for you?”

PART 4 · REAL OBJECTIONS
A. Wait and See Objection
THE MOST COMMON OBJECTION NOW
Client: “I'm not sure if that's the right time. Let's wait and see how this conflict gonna go.”
Sales Agent: “Yeah, of course, you have all of the right to decide whenever it's right time for you to move. But eventually, when you started looking into that, what was about, you know, the market or that kind of triggered you to look into this further? Why even start looking?”
“And let's imagine that the things somehow settle down. When will you consider to go back into this market, when the price is going to hit the previous peak, let's say December 2025 or before?”
“So would that make sense for you, I mean if the offer will be too good to miss, for me still to connect with you and discuss, or you want me to completely forget your number till that point?”

PART 4 · REAL OBJECTIONS
E. Guarantee Objection
FEAR-BASED
Fear-based objection, so we need to uncover what exactly is causing it, so we can refer to the root cause.
Clarify
Client: “That all sounds great but what are the guarantees that the project will be finished and functional, as you are describing?”
Sales Agent: “Yeah we can cover that. Tell me what's behind that question just so I understand.”
After we give them all logical-based answers, and explain how this project will be different - if they still insist on a guarantee.
The project could be delayed or cancelled or it will never be as promising.
Sales Agent: “I can see you are concerned with that. Is there any specific event or project in the past you are referring to?”
“And what in your mind, was the main issue with that project?”
“And in your mind why do you think that happened?”
“And I appreciate that this may be a very valid concern from what you are telling me, what could be a guarantee that could be sufficient for you?”
PART 4 · REAL OBJECTIONS
E. Guarantee — Reframe
REFRAME THE WAY OF THINKING
Sales Agent: “I understand, from what you've told me, certainty is important for you. And I wish I could provide you with 100% certainty. However overall, there is not such a thing as a 100% guarantee for anything in life really.”
“Especially when you are investing in off-plan properties. Now, it really depends on how much you believe in ……. Dubai, based on a last 20 years track records and future plans for 2040”
“And/OR how this opportunity will benefit you in the future.”
“If I may humbly guess that you already have made decisions in your life without having a 100% upfront guarantee, and they've led to great outcomes.”

PART 5 · SMOKE SCREENS
F. Partner Objection
SPOUSE · FAMILY · ADVISERS
Many Clients may need to consult their spouses and family or advisers first, before buying.
Others only use this as an appeal to a third-party authority currently absent to avoid a real objection - so its may Be a smoke screen
Client: “This sounds good. I just need to talk to my spouse as we make these types of decisions together as a family.”
Sales Agent: “That's not a problem. How does your wife/family feel about you getting this new home for them?”
Client: “I think they would like me to do this, but I need to ask them first.”
Sales Agent: “Well, what will you do if you go to them and they don't want you to take part in this project?”
Client: “I understand, I just am not sure what they will say about this.”
Sales Agent: “Would it help you if we set up a call/meeting with your family and for you to discuss and clarify any potential questions that they may have. So you could all be on the same page about this opportunity and make the right decision for you.”

PART 5 · SMOKE SCREENS
G. ‘Think About It’ Objection
THE SMOKE SCREEN
This type of objection can be tricky. For the most part, it can be about money. However, you have to dig deep and identify whether it's about money, value, fear, etc.
Client: “This sounds good, but let me think it over.”
Sales Agent: “That's not a problem. What's your timeframe on getting back to me in the next day or two just to make sure that I would be available and to make proper time for you?”
You are getting the commitment from the client with the exact time and date when he will be reconnecting with you.
Sales Agent: “Now before I go, what do you want to go over in your mind (a better way of saying, “What do you want to think about?”), just so I know what questions you might have when we talk tomorrow?”
Now this is the key at this point, they are going to tell you what their real concern is.
They might say, “Well I'm just concerned about, or I'm just not understanding this part, or I'm not sure if I can get the money for this.”
