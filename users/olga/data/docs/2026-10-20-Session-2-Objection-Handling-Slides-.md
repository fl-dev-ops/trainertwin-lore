PART 1 · MINDSET
Objection is only a concern that your client has, it's not a rejection of you.
4

It's not the time to react to any objection.
You and your client need to work as a team. First to understand the concern, undercover what is behind the concern and then work together as a team to help the client overcome it.
5

“It's too expensive.”
“I want a distress deal.”
“I need to talk to my family.”
“Can you give me some information? Just email me, send WhatsApp, and I get back to you.”

PART 2 · THE FORMULA
3-Step Objection Handling Formula
1
Clarify
2
Discuss
3
Diffuse
+ Agree on the next step
where we lead the client
Clarify - we never assume, just based on our past experiences, what this means for this exact client.
7

STEP 1 · CLARIFY
Clarifying Questions to Ask
“Now…when you say (repeat back what they said) what do you mean by that?”
“I'm curious, did you ask me that for a reason?”
“Can I ask where you got this information from?”
8

STEP 1 · CLARIFY
Clarifying Questions to Ask
“Can you tell me more about that?”
“When you say (repeat back the concern), what's behind that, just so I understand?”
9

STEPS 2 & 3
Discuss & Diffuse
Discuss - When you understand what is the actual concern you can discuss what are the possible ways to overcome it.
Diffuse - Offer a perspective, or a solution, that did work for other clients before.
10

STEP 3 · DIFFUSE
“Can I offer a suggestion? The way the other clients did that is…”
“Do you feel this could be an option for you?”
11

E · FEAR-BASED
Guarantee Objection
Fear-based objection, so we need to uncover what exactly is causing it, so we can refer to the root cause.
30

E · FEAR-BASED
Guarantee Objection
CLIENT
“That all sounds great but what are the guarantees that the project will be finished and functional, as you are describing?”
SALES AGENT
“Yeah we can cover that. Tell me what's behind that question just so I understand.”
31

E · UNCOVER THE ROOT CAUSE
Guarantee Objection
After we give them all logical-based answers, and explain how this project will be different - if they still insist on a guarantee.
The project could be delayed or cancelled or it will never be as promising.
32

E · UNCOVER THE ROOT CAUSE
Guarantee Objection
SALES AGENT
“I can see you are concerned with that. Is there any specific event or project in the past you are referring to?”
“And what in your mind, was the main issue with that project?”
33

E · UNCOVER THE ROOT CAUSE
Guarantee Objection
SALES AGENT
“And in your mind why do you think that happened?”
“And I appreciate that this may be a very valid concern from what you are telling me, what could be a guarantee that could be sufficient for you?”
34

E · REFRAME THE WAY OF THINKING
Guarantee — Reframe
SALES AGENT
“I understand, from what you've told me, certainty is important for you. And I wish I could provide you with 100% certainty. However overall, there is not such a thing as a 100% guarantee for anything in life really.”
35

E · REFRAME THE WAY OF THINKING
Guarantee — Reframe
SALES AGENT
“Especially when you are investing in off-plan properties. Now, it really depends on how much you believe in ……. Dubai, based on a last 20 years track records and future plans for 2040”
“And/OR how this opportunity will benefit you in the future.”
36

E · REFRAME THE WAY OF THINKING
Guarantee — Reframe
SALES AGENT
“If I may humbly guess that you already have made decisions in your life without having a 100% upfront guarantee, and they've led to great outcomes.”

PART 5 · SMOKE SCREENS · F
Partner Objection
Many Clients may need to consult their spouses and family or advisers first, before buying.
Others only use this as an appeal to a third-party authority currently absent to avoid a real objection - so its may Be a smoke screen
38

F · SPOUSE · FAMILY · ADVISERS
Partner Objection
CLIENT
“This sounds good. I just need to talk to my spouse as we make these types of decisions together as a family.”
SALES AGENT
“That's not a problem. How does your wife/family feel about you getting this new home for them?”
39

F · SPOUSE · FAMILY · ADVISERS
Partner Objection
CLIENT
“I think they would like me to do this, but I need to ask them first.”
SALES AGENT
“Well, what will you do if you go to them and they don't want you to take part in this project?”
40

F · SPOUSE · FAMILY · ADVISERS
Partner Objection
CLIENT
“I understand, I just am not sure what they will say about this.”
SALES AGENT
“Would it help you if we set up a call/meeting with your family and for you to discuss and clarify any potential questions that they may have. So you could all be on the same page about this opportunity and make the right decision for you.”
41

PART 5 · SMOKE SCREENS · G
‘Think About It’ Objection
The smoke screen.
This type of objection can be tricky. For the most part, it can be about money. However, you have to dig deep and identify whether it's about money, value, fear, etc.
42

G · THE SMOKE SCREEN
‘Think About It’ Objection
CLIENT
“This sounds good, but let me think it over.”
SALES AGENT
“That's not a problem. What's your timeframe on getting back to me in the next day or two just to make sure that I would be available and to make proper time for you?”
43

G · THE SMOKE SCREEN
‘Think About It’ Objection
You are getting the commitment from the client with the exact time and date when he will be reconnecting with you.
44

G · THE SMOKE SCREEN
‘Think About It’ Objection
SALES AGENT
“Now before I go, what do you want to go over in your mind (a better way of saying, “What do you want to think about?”), just so I know what questions you might have when we talk tomorrow?”
45

G · THE SMOKE SCREEN
‘Think About It’ Objection
Now this is the key at this point, they are going to tell you what their real concern is.
They might say, “Well I'm just concerned about, or I'm just not understanding this part, or I'm not sure if I can get the money for this.”
46

PRACTICE
Two Teams. One List.
Team A
Gives the client an objection from the list — call out the number.
Team B
The agent handles it: Clarify → Discuss → Diffuse → Agree on the next step.
Observer
Checks the agent and gives feedback.
Switch teams after every round.
