When trying to make the right decision in developing your career or business, you are constantly faced with a choice.
 
And I felt I had too many choices... yesterday during a session with my business and life coach, Natasha Lykhenko. 
I had many ideas - three different directions in which I could represent myself as a sales trainer for Real Estate companies, a nutrition coach, or a camera confidence/media coach for entrepreneurs.
My dilemma was 'what I should do next to scale my business and what I should focus on to reach my desired outcomes faster'. 
We know the answers are within us, but how do we find that one answer that will move us forward? 
Natasha Lykhenko has a 'magic touch' and was able to help me find that answer in just a 30-minute session.
I realized that the main qualification for my decision and the main parameters that I could use to select the right action, not just for this decision but for any decision in my life, were:
- it should be simple
- connected with communication and collaboration with other people
- challenging for me, outside of my comfort zone
And unexpectedly...the most important factor in deciding whether an action is right is whether I am genuinely interested in doing it.

Seeking professional advice has been immensely helpful in my self-development journey. In the past six months, I've invested around £12,000 in working with professional mentors and purchasing different courses that have accelerated my growth dramatically.
I'm excited to pursue the decision that meets these parameters and propels me towards success!

Do you have your own strategy? What is that one factor that makes you choose a particular action?
