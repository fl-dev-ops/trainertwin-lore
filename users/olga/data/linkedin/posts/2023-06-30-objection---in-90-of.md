“OBJECTION” - In 90% of cases, this is highlighted as the main reason why the client is withdrawing from the deal.

When we discuss “objections” in my practical sessions with real estate agents it is a consistent theme that people believe is an absolute deal breaker.

- The client was not sure if the market is right for the purchase now.
- The client is saying it’s way too expensive.
- The client is not able to convince their spouse to buy the property.
- The client needs time to think.

The last objection is my favourite indeed.

And hands UP AGENTs are absolutely right - clients will always have objections.
However, the real truth is that the agent does not know how to close the deal because they have not done proper lead qualification to begin with.

We are so afraid to hear the truth about the potential obstacles and objections at the beginning, we end up rushing into: 
- The presentation
- Sending property options
- Trying to convince the client that this property and current market are the best

After this, the client simply can’t be honest with us.

How about instead of asking YES-oriented questions like, "Would you be able to make a decision in one month?" Ask something like, "In a scenario where we find the best property for you, you probably WILL NOT be able to make a decision within 1 week CORRECT?”

Let him convince you - "Why not? I definitely will." Or, come up with the objections and discuss his real circumstances, "Well probably not, as I need to sell my wife’s diamond ring first…"

You are qualifying, by disqualifying.
