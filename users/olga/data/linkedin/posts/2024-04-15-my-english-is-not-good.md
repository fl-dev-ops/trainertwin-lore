My English is ‘not good’. Can I still be successful with selling Dubai properties?

 Nine years ago, I stepped into the Dubai real estate market, not just as a newcomer, but as a non-native English speaker. 

My name might have given it away, but every phone call, every client interaction was a steep challenge. In this industry, where English is spoken in countless accents and styles, mastering the language is not just about communication—it's about confidence.

The journey was daunting. The fear of not just understanding but being understood, of making my place in a market as diverse as Dubai, kept me on my toes. But here’s what I’ve learned: Beyond the essential skills and knowledge of real estate, investing in language skills is pivotal. It's about more than just making deals; it's about making connections, understanding needs, and building trust.
To those just starting out, remember, that developing your language skills is as crucial as learning your trade. It’s a massive part of your growth and confidence in this field.
