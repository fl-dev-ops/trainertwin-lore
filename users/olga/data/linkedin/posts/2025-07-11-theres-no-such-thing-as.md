There’s no such thing as perfect leads.

No magical button that says: “Only the clients who are ready to invest today.”

Real estate doesn’t work like that.
People come in unclear, uncertain, emotionally conflicted.
And most of the time — they don’t even fully understand what they want.

Not yet.

That’s why great sales isn’t about chasing warmer leads.
It’s about asking better questions.

You don’t wait for clarity to appear.
You create it — by helping the client understand themselves through the conversation.

When you ask the right questions,
→ Their priorities rise to the surface
→ Their fears become visible
→ And your offer starts to feel like a reflection of their thinking — not a sales pitch

So if your deals feel stuck —
It might not be the market.
It might just be… that you’re not asking enough.

When was the last time a client said something they didn’t even realise they were thinking — because of the question you asked?
