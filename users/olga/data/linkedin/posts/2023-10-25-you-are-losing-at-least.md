You are losing at least  50% of your commission…

If you do not use a sales script.

What separates TOP salespeople from average reps?

It’s NOT that they’re more charismatic or personable…
It’s NOT the product or service they’re selling…
It’s NOT even the “good leads” they’re getting...

The ONLY thing separating top salespeople from the rest is - 

A.) Their SALES SCRIPT and
B.) Their ability to wield it with SKILL.

Think about it as your Road Map. Imagine you need to get from point A to point B.

You can just start driving - hoping and praying that you’ll figure it out. Or, you can use Google Maps - understand point A, look through the route to point B and follow the instructions. Which one will get you where you need to be faster?

Your sales script is the #1 determinant of how well the sales conversation will go. If your SALES SCRIPT triggers ‘sales resistance’ in prospects… Then you’ll struggle to even stay average - let alone crack the entry point to the Top 1% of salespeople.

Clients will be dismissive, and aggressive, and stop responding to your calls eventually.

Untrained salespeople, after reading the script 2-3 times, start using it with clients, fail 99% of the time, and jump to the decision that the SCRIPT itself is the problem.

The real problem is their lack of dedication and persistence.

You should not only understand and memorise the script you should become the script. This is only possible if you work on it, role-play it with colleagues/friends, and make it feel like your own words.
If you master the tonality and speed, you sound curious and concerned, exactly how you should - You will become your own GOOGLE MAPS.

HOW?
 - Give yourself 3-4 months at least to practise and rehearse every day.
 - Record your conversations and review them, noticing your words, tone, and the prospect’s reaction.
 - Role-play with a colleague or a friend.
 - Record yourself on video and notice your facial expressions and body language.

Do you feel it’s too much? Maybe, and this is exactly why there are only 1% of top salespeople out there - if it was easy, everyone would do it.
