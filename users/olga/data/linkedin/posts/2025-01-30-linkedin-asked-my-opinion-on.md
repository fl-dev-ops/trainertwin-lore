LinkedIn Asked my Opinion on...

"The announcement of Etihad Rail will impact real estate through.."

my thoughts...

With new connectivity across the UAE, we’re looking at previously untapped areas becoming prime real estate hotspots. Think about it—every major city in the world thrives on suburban living, and this is our moment to see that model take shape right here.

Less traffic in central areas? Yes, please.

More affordable housing options? Finally.

New investment opportunities in areas that were once “too far”? About time.

For years, certain parts of the UAE have been off the radar simply because getting there was a logistical nightmare. 

Etihad Rail changes that. It opens doors (and tracks) to new workforce hubs, suburban communities, and long-term real estate appreciation.

So, while some people see just a train, I see a blueprint for the next phase of real estate expansion in the UAE.
