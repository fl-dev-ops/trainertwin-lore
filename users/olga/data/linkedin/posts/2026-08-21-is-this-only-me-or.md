Is this only me… or you are as well bombarded by Sales messages- which have Such a generic text…

Written by ai is a one trouble…

Next - they just list the services that they are providing - and honestly half of them I can’t even understand.

But they claimed - that they can help me

And after I do not respond - they follow up with another random “ I’m just following Up on my previous messages “

Please Stop that!

My basic advice with all respect- start with a Problem Statement in a message and ask a question… “…is there something that you potentially experiencing?”

I guarantee higher response rate from already warm clients!

Ps. Keep thinking that Common Sense is Untrainable…
