I almost gave up on my Dubai real estate career and went back home…



When I started in Dubai 2015 I thought my previous experience in sales and real estate would help me Close Fast.

But in Dubai, it felt like none of that mattered.

1. My English was average. 
2. Nationality with “no demand” - it was almost no Russian speaking clients back then.
3. No fancy international degree

And the sales skills that had worked for me before were 

I was 34 and starting again from zero.

At that time, the market was not easy. Prices were going down slowly .. 

Buyers were waiting - Sellers holding to the price. 

Very similar to what we are seeing now.

And back then, just like now, one skill mattered more than anything else: your ability to speak to Strangers on the phone.

That was my challenge - I couldn’t not “convince “ - Clients were not listening to me!
 
And I was too - proud- I’ve seen others begging, chasing, being desperate 

I could not beg… too proud.


So I had two choices. 

Quit, buy a ticket, and go back home. 

Or stay and figure out a different way…

I stayed.

It did not happen overnight. But when I changed the way I spoke to clients, everything started to change. My script changed. My confidence changed. My sales changed.

That is why I still believe this today:

In a difficult market, your ability to influence people on the phone can change everything.

Not through pressure.
Not through pushing or beginning 
But through the kind of conversation that builds trust and helps people move forward.
