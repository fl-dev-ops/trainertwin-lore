"Dubai agents are scammers..."

A client said that to me this week.

I coach agents. But clients pay for my Coaching too - buyers and tenants who want someone to walk the market with them. 

Why do they pay? 

Because honesty failed everywhere else. 

A man is paying out of his own pocket for what an agent should give him for free.

His story. Unit advertised at 120. He messages the agent - The response form agent -' First come, first serve. and its 130 for one or two cheques. and135 for two to four.'
One number in the ad, three in the chat. 

Guess what the client did?
Closed the conversation!
Ok - He's renting. 
Bur your 4M off-plan buyer thinks exactly the same way. 

Here's the uncomfortable part: most of Dubai agents weren't scamming anyone - IM HERE TO WITNESS.

You are Good people - just using bad ADVICE and doing what you were taught.

In my career I've seen around 50 tactics while selling myself, then coaching agents to sell. 
Some work. 
Some work sometimes. 
Most are old abd whatever your favourite top closer does on stage was never built for your client.
You think you're using a technique. 
The clients reads it - SCAMM. He doesn't argue - he just stops replying.

Top 5 to avoid in 2026:
1. Opening with pressure. First come, first serve. Last unit. Last price. You haven't asked him anything and you're already squeezing.

2. Asking nothing but budget. He gives you a number, you tell him it's too low and he needs to pay more. You learned one thing about him and used it against him - he will never opens up.

3. Pushing an offer below what you just said the owner would take. Usually because you're reviving the deal the previous client walked away from. People aren't stupid. They know when they're solving someone else's problem.

4. Ignoring everything except the money. He tells you size, layout, who's living there, what he's already rejected. You send whatever fits the budget line. With your budget you can only get this. The clients know - you are pushing your STOCK.

People want to be heard. When your manager said open your ears and close your mouth, that's what he meant.

5. Not knowing your numbers. Square footage. Price per square foot. His currency. Go vague and he decides you don't know your own stock. He'll make this decision once or twice in his life. He won't hand it to someone guessing.

None of this makes you dishonest - but most of the times your are Getting ghosted just because of that.
