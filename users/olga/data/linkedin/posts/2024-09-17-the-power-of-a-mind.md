The Power of a Mind Shift in Sales

One of my private coaching clients recently faced a tough situation—like dropping your phone in the pool kind of tough. She had mistakenly entered the wrong price for a unit on a booking form—seven thousand dirhams off. A potential disaster, right?

Before our coaching, she would have been stuck, spiraling in frustration. But this time, she took a different approach.

She had options: blame the developer (the classic “it’s not my fault” play) or own up to the error. With her new mindset, she chose to come clean. Not only did she admit her mistake, but she even sent the client screenshots of the messages she’d missed from the developer that led to the mistake.

The result? The client was so impressed by her honesty that he refused to let her cover the seven thousand dirhams herself. That’s like spilling a drink at dinner and having your guest offer to pay for it—only in this case, it strengthened their relationship even more.

But the story doesn’t end there.

Armed with her newfound confidence from our coaching, she went back to the developer, renegotiated the commission, and even convinced the buyer to pay the full deposit upfront—without seeing the sales purchase agreement. That’s like getting someone to buy a car without even taking it for a test drive. Crazy, right?

This wasn’t just about fixing a mistake. It was about the mind shift she experienced through our coaching. She learned that confidence isn’t about being perfect—it’s about how you handle those tough moments when things go sideways.

By focusing on clear communication, staying consistent, and having the right mindset, she turned what could’ve been a catastrophe into a win. 

That’s the real power of coaching—shifting your mindset so you can not only survive those tricky situations but come out even stronger on the other side.
