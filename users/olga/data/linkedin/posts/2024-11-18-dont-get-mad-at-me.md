Don’t Get Mad at Me!

I Won’t Respond to Generic Messages on LinkedIn

Let’s be honest: LinkedIn is an incredible platform for networking. But it’s also a playground for messages that make you wonder, “Did they even read my name?”

Here’s the deal — I don’t respond to messages like, “Hi, Olga. It’s been a long time since we connected. How have you been?”

Why? Because these messages don’t require the sender to switch on their brain. They feel robotic, and honestly, if you’re not putting in the effort to engage meaningfully, why should I?

If you truly want to connect with someone who can add value to your business, here’s my advice:

1. Take the Time to Read Their Profile
Show me you know who I am beyond my job title.


2. Be Specific and Relevant
“I saw your post on X and loved your perspective” works way better than, “Hi, how are you?”


3. Make It Clear Why You’re Reaching Out
Collaboration? Advice? World domination? Just let me know upfront.



Thoughtful, intentional communication makes all the difference. 

Remember: LinkedIn is not Tinder — quality over quantity always wins.
