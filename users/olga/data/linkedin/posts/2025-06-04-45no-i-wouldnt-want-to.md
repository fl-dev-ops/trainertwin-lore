45…No, I wouldn’t want to be younger. 

Not 35, not 25.
This version of me? She’s built from experience, shaped by challenge, and driven by deep clarity.

This birthday gave me a moment to pause and reflect.
People wished me ease, simplicity, dreams coming true without effort. And while I’m grateful for the sentiment — my reality has always been different.

Growth, for me, has never come wrapped in comfort.
It’s come through resistance, through pressure, through showing up even when the path felt unclear.
That’s how I move faster, go deeper, and build something real — in business and in life.

This year, I gave myself two powerful gifts:
My physical form — because I’ve created a life that supports my health and energy.
And a relationship that supports my mind, heart, and vision like nothing before.

I’m building a life, a business, and a body that reflect who I really am.
Not perfectly. But honestly.

Also — thank you.
To everyone who sent me birthday wishes: over 200 messages, and I’m still reading through them all.

Here’s to building the next chapter — with depth, direction, and just enough challenge to keep it interesting.
