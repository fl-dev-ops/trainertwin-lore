Which problem Do You solve?…. This is my first question to

Anyone who writes me with “I’m a founder and we do ABC and EYZ”


Really check - no one is interested what you Do ?
People do not care … unless there is a benefit for them!

So next time when you are Cold Dming or Introducing yourself on a networking event instead of!

Hi I’m Olga the Top 1 Sales coach in the whole world entire world with this and that certification!!!

(🤪Just used it for example- I’ve never done it this way)

Do this…

Hi I’m Olga… and what I do is…
You know how some agents are feeling stuck with not being able to book a meetings, run them and feel consistently busy with No Closed deals for months?
Usually one of the issues is the clients is say “ just send me some options “ and the conversation dies right after that.

So what I do I help agents by understanding the human behavior move the client from first call to the closing using my Off-plan closing system and Neuro emotional persuasion questions.
So they feel in control of every step and always know what to say….

Is this something that you may possibly will benefit from with your own sales team?

Comment which do you feel is bringing more responses?

And steal this structure from me to use it for own intro.

Have a massive week!
