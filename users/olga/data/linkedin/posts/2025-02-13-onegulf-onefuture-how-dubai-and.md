OneGulf, OneFuture: How Dubai and Saudi Arabia Could Reshape Gulf Real Estate Together.
Could Dubai lose its real estate crown to Saudi Arabia? 

The battle for dominance in the Gulf is heating up, and Saudi’s mega-developments are making global investors take notice. 
But is Dubai really at risk of falling behind?

While Dubai is rolling out the red carpet for global investors, Saudi Arabia is building a fortress for its own people—at least for now. 
With Vision 2030 aiming for 70% homeownership, the Saudi government is focused on empowering local buyers rather than aggressively courting foreign investment.
Having visited Saudi Arabia for the first time in September 2023 to attend Cityscape Global, and since then working closely with clients on the ground, I see a different picture.
Yes, Saudi real estate is booming. Projects like NEOM, The Red Sea Project, and Qiddiya are making global headlines. 
But here’s what many miss: developers don’t even need external agents to sell. Unlike Dubai, where commissions fuel transactions, Saudi demand is already strong enough to move inventory without aggressive international marketing.
Thanks to government-backed financing initiatives and homeownership programs, Saudi nationals have direct access to real estate, reducing the need for external investors to fuel the market. For now, Saudi Arabia is focused on its citizens, building a strong domestic foundation before fully opening up to international buyers.
Meanwhile, Dubai remains the undisputed magnet for global capital. Freehold ownership, tax benefits, and Golden Visas keep it the region’s top destination for international investors.
This isn’t about one market replacing another—it’s about strategic balance. Dubai thrives as the go-to global investment hub, while Saudi Arabia is strengthening its domestic real estate market with a long-term, gradual approach to foreign buyers.
For investors, this isn’t a shift—it’s diversification. Instead of picking sides, the smartest money is balancing between both markets, leveraging the different advantages each offers.
