If you think running a real estate company is all about listings and leads… 


let me flip that for you.

If your business depends on people who sell, then what you really need is a Recruitment & Training machine.

Your listings don’t sell themselves.
And leads? They don’t close deals — your agents do.
Which means your biggest asset isn’t the property stock you hold… it’s the skill, motivation, and staying power of the people you hire.

The two engines that decide whether your company grows or stalls? Recruitment and Training.
Get them wrong, and you’re stuck in a hire–replace–repeat cycle.
Get them right, and suddenly your cost per hire drops, retention improves, and your revenue stops depending on luck.

After 5 years of working closely with real estate companies in Dubai, I can say with confidence — I’ve seen only a handful of companies truly get this right.

Otherwise, it’s like building a rocket but forgetting the astronauts — the launch pad is impressive, but no one’s going to the moon.
