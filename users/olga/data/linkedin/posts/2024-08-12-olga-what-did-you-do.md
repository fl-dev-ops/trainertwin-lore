"Olga, what did you do wrong last year?"
Are you asking yourself those types of questions regularly?

Well, it’s August, the perfect time to pause and reflect on that very question. As I look back on the year, I realize I’m not great at delivering ice-breaking activities… or team-building when it involves building a tower from cardboard boxes.

 I also realize that some of the challenges weren’t just about what I did or didn’t do—they highlighted opportunities to better align training with how things are structured within the companies themselves.

Here’s what I mean:

Management MIA: If management isn’t in the game, agents slip back into old habits. No surprise they end up with the same results, and suddenly, all that shiny new training feels like it didn’t stick.

Skills on Paper, Not in Practice: Learning new stuff is great, but if you’re not using those skills in real client interactions, it’s like learning to swim but never jumping in the pool. Spoiler: You won’t get far.

CRM ≠ Sales Strategy: Sure, you’ve got a CRM, but what about a clear client journey? A solid communication strategy? Without those, even the best training can’t work its magic.
Let’s Get Real About Sales Training: It’s not some magic wand that’ll turn your team into sales superstars overnight. Thinking one week of training will fix everything is like expecting six-pack abs after two trips to the gym. It’s a whole process—consistent practice, management backing, a clear plan, and yes, probably better snacks. Without these, you’re just spinning your wheels.
