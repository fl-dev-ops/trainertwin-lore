For all the PropTech founders out there who are planning to blow up the Dubai real estate market…. first, and then the way tech works in real estate all over the world.

I was actually planning to write this for years!

Because I’ve been approached literally every day by one or another startup. 
And I Noticed that the AI voice solutions have gone quiet lately. 

Not because they’re so successful and companies are actively using them. 

The opposite.

Here’s how it goes every time. 

Someone comes to me convinced he has a brilliant idea, or a product that already worked somewhere else. 
And specifically since 2022 I’ve spoken with a number of agencies founded by people who floated into Dubai after resetting their business because of the situation in the homeland.

They managed to get some clients. 
And despite all the good intentions, they ended up with no money.

The reason isn’t that they don’t know the market at all. 
It’s because they don’t understand it on a deeper level, and that’s why - don’t actually have a product - that solves a problem that people are ready to Pay for.

And what they ask me for my network introductions. 

Help me sell it!

But when I look into the product, nobody is going to buy it, no matter how much marketing budget you push behind it. 

Those differences aren’t visible to someone who hasn’t been in the industry like me since 2015, through the previous buyer’s market, and watched the market cycles in Dubai sitting on both sides, the agent’s and the agency’s.

So let me save you the time and the money, because I can already tell you how this goes.

If you’re B2B focused - You’ll probably push it into a few companies and they’ll pay you for two, three months. Then the guy inside who had your back moves to another company, and the next one has no budget at all. 
You end up with no real case studies, no real sales process, and no real product. 

You can stay in that cycle for years.

If you’re selling to agents directly: you’ll have a lot of free users who open it once or twice. 
Maybe someone pays for one month of membership, then cancels. 

No profit. 

And you’re looking at this thing you built and spent so much money on.

The only thing you haven’t done is proper customer development with actual market insiders. Not a marketing agency that has never sold anything except its own services. 

And the reason nobody does this is they think they’re saving the cost and they’ll learn from their own mistakes.

Which is exactly the same as an agent walking into this industry thinking he’ll listen to a couple of smart top closers, watch a few free YouTube videos, and somehow make a million in his first year. With all my respect, they get nothing out of it.

If you feel you’re doing amazingly and you love the money, go ahead. 
But for everyone else, maybe it’s time to rethink where you’re putting your investment…
