What is the main reason to buy real estate?

Agents will come up with at least 10 examples focusing on pricing and convenience.

The reality is, that clients are not simply buying because the price is low, or the unit has a fantastic view.

We buy to solve problems or emotional needs. Finding those problems and needs with clients - is what distinguishes an average salesperson from a 1% TOP performer.

Understanding the prospect’s problem is a mental shift for sales agents. 
The process is the foundation of all service providers - and selling real estate is the same as providing a medical service.

Does the doctor start to bombard you with treatment options 5 minutes after hello?? And if so…would you trust such a doctor?

When have a client who has a problem or a need:
We discover together what his current situation is, and why he decided to change it in the first place.

What he already tried to fix the problem - did he like the results he was getting? Realistically if you are talking to a client now, his problem has not been fixed yet right?

What does he think about the solution, or what does the ideal solution look like? What is the ideal criteria for his property of choice?

Now you need to start building the internal urgency - his desire to change the situation as an opposite to the OVERUSED external urgency when agents are threatening that this is the last unit and the prices are increasing. You ask him what will happen if he does not buy a property now and the situation remains the same. This process should take more than 50% of your communication with the client.

What is the most common problem your clients are trying to solve with real estate?
