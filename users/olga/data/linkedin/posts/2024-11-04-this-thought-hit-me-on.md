This thought hit me on a Saturday night..

 Scrolling through yet another post about a company hiring agents, I just couldn’t help but think…

Companies want agents who are dedicated, ready to hustle, and passionate about building real client relationships. 

They dream of agents who rise with the sun, eagerly dive into cold calling, and treat CRM updates like it’s their favorite pastime. 

They’re looking for someone who’s committed for the long haul, a true partner in the world of real estate.

And then there are agents—some of whom picture the 'dream job' as one where they can sleep in till 10, stroll to a cafe for coffee, and wait for leads to magically come to them. 

They want a manager to handle deal closings, an assistant to enter every detail into the CRM, pre-qualify clients, and even make those cold calls. 

Ideally, there’s also a marketing team shooting their videos, managing their Reels and stories, and curating the perfect social media image.

 And let’s not forget: they’re hoping for a stable salary, plus 70% commission, of course.

So here we are: companies looking for a real commitment, and agents looking for all the perks with none of the sweat.

If you’re in sales management, or recruitment, you know exactly what I’m talking about. 

So tell me—who’s got the right idea here?
