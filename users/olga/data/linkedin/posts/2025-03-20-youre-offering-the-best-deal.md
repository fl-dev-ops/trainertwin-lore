You’re offering the best deal. The perfect solution. It’s exactly what your client needs.

And yet—they disappear. Stop replying. Suddenly, they’re “not ready” or “need to think about it.”

You start wondering: What happened?

Most agents blame the client: “They weren’t serious.” But here’s the truth—of course they weren’t serious. They were too busy defending themselves.

If you’ve never heard the term Sales Resistance, that doesn’t mean it’s not controlling your sales.

In fact, it’s probably the #1 reason why your clients:
🚩 Stop replying to your messages
🚩 Disappear right before closing the deal
🚩 Suddenly start throwing objections at you

In my training, I dedicate an entire day to understanding how clients think and make decisions. Because the ability to lower—or even completely avoid—Sales Resistance can increase your closing rate up to 90%.

And no, it’s not your fault. It’s just the way most of us were taught to sell. Or maybe you believe that a little extra pressure is what the client really needs to make a decision.

But ask yourself—how do you feel when a salesperson starts pushing you?
