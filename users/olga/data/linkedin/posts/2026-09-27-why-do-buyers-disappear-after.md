Why Do Buyers Disappear After ....

You Send Them Options? 👀

They say they like them.

Then silence.
Familiar?

When you came into Dubai real estate, you probably thought selling would work differently.

Dubai is popular.
People want to invest here.
Developers launch beautiful projects every week.
So the job should be quite simple.

- Find people who already want to buy and show them the right property.

Then you actually started working with leads. And realised it does not work like that at all.

Sometimes you think your problem is follow-up. Or at least your manager told you so.

You do not know what else to write after:
'Hi, just checking if you had a chance to review the options.'

But quite often, the problem happened much earlier.

- You did not qualify the client properly.
- You had what felt like a great Zoom call.
- The client was engaged.
- They liked the options.
You sent everything.
And then...

Gone. 👻

So you tried different approaches.
+More follow-up.
- Less follow-up.
 +More information.
 - Less information.

Sometimes you even decided:
'I’m not sending any options until we meet first.'
Still, no deal.

And this is where it starts getting uncomfortable.
When the market is easy, a lot of sales mistakes are forgiven.

When buyers become more careful and competition gets tougher, they are not.
And if you have never sold in a difficult market before, at some point you start asking yourself:
Do I actually know how to sell, or have I just been working in a market where selling was easier?

I think this question matters not only for agents.
If you manage a sales team and the same thing keeps happening across the team, clients disappearing after Zoom calls, endless follow-ups, agents sending more and more options, this may not be an individual agent problem.

It may be a sales process problem.
And somewhere in the background, many agents are asking themselves another question 
they probably do not say out loud:

Could I be next to leave the industry?

I know this problem well.
My first real estate clients did not want to talk to me either.
That is where my understanding of sales in this market really started.
