I highly respect a real estate recruiter's work! Finding the right talents and operating in a highly competitive, complex, and stressful environment can become simpler by incorporating these questions into the assessment process.

1. Besides making more money, what attracted you to the real estate field? 
2. Can you tell me a bit more… What do you mean by, “I'm good at sales.”?
3. If you will make a 1 million AED commission in the next 6 months what would be your next possible step with the money?
4. How do you feel about rejection? How are you dealing with it in your everyday life?

Use these questions and you will be able to select high-potential candidates even if they have no previous experience in sales or real estate.
