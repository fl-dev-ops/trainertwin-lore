I was Angry for 18 hours straight…




Realising you’ve been building something the wrong way for two years is probably a pretty normal Founder experience.

I’m talking about my YouTube channel.

From the outside, it looked fine.
People in the industry know me.
Agents watch my trainings.
There were wins.

Since I finished my contract with One Broker Group, my whole focus is on my online academy - YouTube is a place where agents can learn for free and the funnel into my academy.

The number of my monthly subscribers went 50% down.
Views are at its lowest 

My marketing manager was saying: “…maybe the audience is not interested at the moment - let’s wait”

For me waiting is not a strategy 

I was not only angry on Myself- as I should anyway… my marketing manager…turned in to a “Yes” woman instead of showing me the indicators-she was just sending me Thumbnails for approval…

Wouldn’t you get upset with it?

But…

Now to the fixing part - I usually do it “same old way” - learning!
I’ve invested in a group coaching by the industry expert to become a YouTube Producer.

For the past week I’m navigating the AVD and Hooks along side with the topic research…

Either way, hidden problems grow exactly like that.
They always been there - Current market situation just accelerated them.

Okay, this happened and for me to move I’m asking myself: -so…what’s good about this?

And the answer is simple.

At least now I know.
At least now I can fix it.
At least now I can build it properly.

Would you be interested if I shared the Rxact mistakes I made with YouTube and what I’d do differently now if I would start again?
