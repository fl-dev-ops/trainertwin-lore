Over 20 candidates applied for my Training Coordinator role.

However… only 4 managed to complete the full submission process correctly.
One of the requirements was to record a short 60-second video about themselves.

As you can imagine — if following the application process is already a challenge, it naturally raises the question:
“Will you be able to handle the actual job tasks?”

(Friendly reminder: The answer should always be YES!)

⸻

Career advice for job seekers:
Stop focusing on “I need a salary” or “Who will pay me?”
Start thinking:
“What are my skills? What value can I bring? How can I move the needle for the company?”

That’s the mindset that opens doors — and keeps them open.
