Just a quick productivity fix after a long weekend.

When you are dealing with a new client, first you should find out just 2 things:

Their MOTIVATION - Why they want to buy or sell, and why now?
Their TIME FRAME - When they need this deal to happen and why this exact time?

 - No presentations
 - No sending the client options
 - No viewing should be arranged unless you have 100% certainty

Sounds way too simple right?
Implement it for a week at least and you will find that you are working with serious clients and receiving way less objections.
