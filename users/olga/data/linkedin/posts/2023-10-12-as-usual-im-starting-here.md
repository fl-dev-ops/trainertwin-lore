As usual, I’m starting here with the mindset portion of the post, followed by the practical piece at the end.

Nowadays, we are dealing with someone who is trying to sell you something 24/7!

Today's consumer is much wiser, more informed, more prepared and far less trustworthy of anything which comes out of a salesperson’s mouth, to begin with.

So if you are pushy, needy or ‘salesy’ - you are making your career in sales hard and painful for your ego.

When the agents forward me the messages from the clients asking, “Olga, how should I respond to this?” 

I always try to shift their focus to what it is that the client wants from this property. In other words, how owning this asset will solve the client’s current problem and/or emotional need?

And if the agent thought their questioning and communication ability was not able to uncover the main reason for a purchase there are only really 2 things that may happen…

1. You can be lucky and get a client who already had a firm decision to buy, and you managed not to spoil the deal with your ‘convincing,’ strategy so you took the order.

You can pitch different projects, and end up bargaining the price, payment plan or commission the client will buy in the end, but that would feel like you were in a boxing match.

It is a difficult and unpredictable way to make a living, and it shouldn't be that way…

2. Or you can be respectful, use neutral language and through your advanced questioning abilities, make the client see the expert and authority in you. Just by discovering what is behind the obvious, you can address the unique needs of this particular client.

Remember: 
- Do not assume a sale with every lead.
- Be curious enough to clarify what investment means to every client.
- Listen, listen, listen and ask clarifying and probing questions.

Here are a couple of examples if you are dealing with an investor:

- Besides the obvious of making money, what is your plan for this property?
- Can you tell me why you decided to own a property in Dubai in the first place?
- How do you see this property benefiting you besides making you money?
- When you say ‘investing’ can you tell me what is your long-term plan, so I have a better understanding?
- Help me to understand when you are saying the word ‘investment’ - what will be your main focus?
