Ladies, today is Women’s Day—a day to celebrate everything that matters to us. 

For some, it’s family. For others, it’s career, passion, or personal growth. For me, it has always been freedom.

And today, skiing in the Alps, I had a moment of clarity: freedom is having choices. True freedom begins when you always have at least three options—where to work, what to do, and how to live.

Success isn’t just financial stability. It’s reaching a place where you choose your work not out of necessity, but because you love it. Where even if money were no longer a factor, you’d still do what you do—just with more balance, wrapping up early to enjoy the things that make life truly fulfilling.

Women have fought hard for the right to make their own choices. And today, we continue to build lives on our own terms.

So today, on Women’s Day, I ask: What does freedom mean to you?
