Dubai: The Future That Never Gets Old

Ten years ago, Dubai was already a masterpiece. Today, it’s something entirely new again. Just when you think the city has reached its peak, it reinvents itself—like a place that refuses to age, always young, always ahead.

And as I watch this city evolve, I feel deeply grateful—not just for being here, but for the risk I took ten years ago. At 34, I made the decision to move to Dubai, stepping into the unknown. It wasn’t easy. It wasn’t safe. But looking back, I know—it was worth it.

This morning, someone asked me: “What would you do if you retired?”
And I realized—I’d do exactly what I’m doing now. Because just like Dubai, I never want to stop growing, evolving, and creating.

Some places make you feel nostalgic. Dubai makes you alive.

When was the last time you took a leap of faith?
