From the outside, Dubai looks perfect.

 Sun. Luxury. Freedom.

Instagram makes it look like every agent is living at a brunch table with a Ferrari parked outside.
But anyone who’s actually built a real estate career here knows:
 The first months are not easy.
If you're based in the US, Canada, or the UK and thinking about relocating —
 here’s what I’d want you to know before you make the leap:
1. Build a financial cushion — at least 6 months. Ideally 10.
 Dubai is fast, but real results take time.
 You’ll thank yourself for not panicking when your first few deals take longer than expected.
2. Start studying the market at least 6 months before the move.
 Understand how off-plan works. Watch every sales video you can. Read project brochures like it’s your job — because soon, it will be.
3. Write yourself a letter.
 Why are you doing this?
 What are you really chasing?
 What are you willing to go through to get it?

Because yes — it will get hard.

 And unless your “why” is strong, you’ll want to give up before the rewards arrive.
But if you come with the right mindset, Dubai has a way of changing lives.
