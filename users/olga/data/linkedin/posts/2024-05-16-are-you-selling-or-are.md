Are you selling or are you being sold?

Have you felt...

That the client is right?

That he should think it over?

Needs to discuss it with his family...

Or the project is too expensive?

So, he will not go ahead.

That's because you yourself have the same excuses when you need to take action.

You believe that your excuses are valid—so do the clients.

The reality is—the client has a problem; he was looking for a solution.

He is not buying from you—so he will stay with his problem, and his situation will not change.

Do you really think that this is better for him?
