How to handle the Objections with These Simple Steps! 🤔✨

We all know the rule: never use "BUT" when overcoming objections.

For example: "Yes, it's not the cheapest unit, BUT..."—or maybe you’ve been taught to use "AND" instead.

But does this really work with the modern consumer? Or do you still end up in a boxing game, even with people you trust, like your mom or wife?

I use a different formula: Clarify, Discuss, Diffuse. Curious how it works? Drop a comment below—tell me, what’s your go-to phrase for handling objections?
