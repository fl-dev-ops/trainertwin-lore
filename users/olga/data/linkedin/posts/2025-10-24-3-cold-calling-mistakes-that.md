3 cold calling mistakes that cost Dubai agents millions in commissions

Mistake #1: Starting with property details instead of pain points

"Are you looking to sell or rent your property"

Stop right there. Nobody cares about your property until they trust you understand their situation.

Start with: "I'm not sure it's a right time... but I was hoping to ask you something really quick... and then you decide should we continue this conversation or no.. Fair enough?"

Mistake #2: Talking to anyone who answers instead of qualifying first

Most agents burn through leads like they're free. They're not.

Before you pitch anything, ask: "What brought you to look at Dubai properties?" and "When are you planning to start making money with this investment"

If they can't answer both clearly, move on. Qualifying saves you hours of wasted conversations.

Mistake #3: Giving up after one "no"

This is where agents lose the most money. 

One objection and they're ready to hang up. But most buyers need 3-5 touchpoints before they're ready to move forward.

"I understand it's not a right time - that's why I'm calling to set the better time for a call"

After training 500+ agents, I see the same pattern. The ones who fix these mistakes double their closing rate within 60 days.

The ones who don't? They keep blaming the market instead of their process.

Your next commission is waiting on the other end of a phone call. Make sure you're doing it right.
