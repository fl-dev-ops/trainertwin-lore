Reality check: Commission sharing isn't about the money. It's about psychology.

When you offer to split your commission with someone who refers you, you're not just sharing money.

You're removing their guilt about "bothering" their friend.
You're showing you value their relationship.
You're proving this is a real business transaction.

50% of something is better than 100% of nothing.

Your ego about commission is keeping you broke.
