How I teach agents to help clients convince THEMSELVES to buy (without feeling sold)

This is the technique I perfected when I was actively selling and now teach in my training programs.

Instead of pitching features, use their exact words back to them.

"Remember how you said you wanted to wake up with coffee overlooking the sea? This unit gives you exactly that 365 days a year."

When I was selling properties in areas like Creek, I learned that most agents think selling means talking. But the real skill is listening.

I teach agents to ask specific questions during qualification. Not just "what do you want" but "describe your perfect morning."

When clients tell you they dream of waking up to sea views, having breakfast on a balcony, or being close to their kids' school - write it down. Word for word.

Then during presentation, don't pitch. Remind.

"You mentioned you want your daughter to walk to school instead of sitting in traffic for 45 minutes..."

"You said the most important thing is having your own space after years of flatmates..."

This technique worked for me when I was doubling my money on property investments, and now I teach it because it removes resistance completely.

The client isn't hearing a sales pitch. They're hearing their own dreams reflected back.

When you use their language, their priorities, their emotional triggers - resistance disappears. They're not being sold to. They're being understood.

People trust themselves more than they trust you.

Stop trying to convince them of what YOU think they need. Help them convince themselves of what THEY already told you they want.
