If Tomorrow Money Disappeared, What Would You Have Left?

Grab a Coffee it's a Long Story....

For many real estate agents, success is often defined by one number: earning that first million dirhams a year. It’s the dream figure, the goal that drives many of us.

But what if we step back for a moment and ask: is money the ultimate measure of success?

Let’s take a quick economics lesson (don’t worry, it’ll be painless). According to Karl Marx—basically the original economist influencer, except his posts were books—money serves only three simple functions:

1. A universal equivalent – simplifying trade.


2. A means of exchange – accelerating the movement of goods in the economy.


3. A store of value – allowing wealth to accumulate.


That’s it. Money is just a tool, not a divine measure of your worth or skill.

The real question we should be asking ourselves is this:
If money disappeared tomorrow, what skills, knowledge, and competencies would you have to rebuild your wealth?

True success isn’t about the number in your bank account; it’s about the abilities that make you valuable in any market, under any economic condition. 

It’s about knowing that no matter what happens, you can earn and thrive because of what you’ve built within yourself.
