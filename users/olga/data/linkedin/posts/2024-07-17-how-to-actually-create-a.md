How to actually create a pipeline?
If you're only hunting for sellers who are ready to sell now, you'll always keep hunting.
To build a sustainable pipeline, focus on nurturing relationships with potential sellers over time.
Think about what value you can provide to property owners to be the first one to find out that they are selling...
