The #1 mistake that kills 90% of real estate agents in their first year:

Everyone talks about leads, marketing, and closing techniques.

But the real killer? Trying to do everything instead of mastering ONE thing.


I just coached an agent who was juggling secondary listings, off-plan properties, AND trying to cold call - all while being too shy to pick up the phone.


If you are reading this, I hope you implemented the strategy we created this month!

If you are one of the agents passing by the corridors next to your company office, talking on the phone... 
This may be important for you

Here's what I told him (and what I tell every new agent):

Pick ONE path. Master it completely. Then expand.

❌ DON'T: Chase 5 different strategies at 20% effort each

✅ DO: Commit 100% to one strategy until you're profitable

The agent I coached? 

He had 25-30 warm contacts from his network in Pakistan.

But instead of activating them systematically, he was buying cold databases that had been sold to 100+ other agents.

The math is simple:
• Cold leads = 90% rejection + high competition
• Warm network = existing trust + zero competition


Most agents fail because they mistake activity for productivity.


They think being busy means being successful.


But busy doesn't pay commissions. 

Results do.

If you're new to real estate, ask yourself:
What's the ONE thing I can dominate in the next 90 days?

Master that. Then build from there.
