Starting your Business…. Before
You ready!

My inbox exploded today — just because Linkedin reminded that 5 years ago I started Live It Up - Real Estate Training Academy 

In Reality… 

Five years ago I had no idea what I was doing. I didn’t know how to run social media — even my Instagram was private. 

I had no clue how to find clients online. 

And I was terrified of the camera — recording myself talking to the camera felt impossible.

It felt like a gap I could never close. But life doesn’t pause while you wait to feel ready — and the world around me was changing too.

In 2020, right after the pandemic, nobody could imagine Dubai real estate would grow and transform the way it did afterwards. 

That year I rented a 1,250 sq ft apartment in Jumeirah Lake Towers for 60,000 AED. 

Today, the same apartment would cost at least double.

And back then, there was no real market for sales-training consultants. 
Companies weren’t inviting trainers, they weren’t looking for external support, and this type of service simply wasn’t seen as necessary. 

Stepping into this space felt like walking into a room that didn’t exist yet.

Many people were leaving Dubai — disappointed, uncertain, unsure about the future.
 I chose the opposite: I stayed, invested, and began to build.

Nobody sees the work behind those words. The money invested into my own education. 
Countless coaching programs and mentorships. The sleepless nights on Zoom coaching calls with UAS at two or three in the morning. 
The moments of doubt that made me question if I could really make it.

And then, slowly, things started to move.

Today LinkedIn reminded me it’s been five years since I started building Live It Up Academy. 

Technically, it also means I’ve renewed my Trade License for the fifth time. 


Looking back, I wasn’t prepared. 
I didn’t have the knowledge. 
I didn’t have the confidence. 
I barely had the skills. 

But I had just enough courage to take one step forward — and that step turned into a path, and that path into a business I love.

And today, I can say this with full certainty: you will never feel ready. Readiness isn’t a starting point — it’s something you build along the way. Your courage will feel one size too small at the beginning, but the moment you step forward, it grows with you.

🪁My 3 lessons after 5 years:
	1.	You don’t need to know everything to begin — you learn by doing.
	2.	Showing up consistently attracts clients more than any “perfect plan.”
	3.	“Readiness” is a myth — action creates clarity.

💬 What about you? How many opportunities will pass if you wait until you feel ready?
