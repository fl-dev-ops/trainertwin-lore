Does your mother want the worst for you?
 Of course not.
And yet — even when people we love try to convince us of something “for our own good”…
 We resist.
We feel pressure.
 We feel pushed.
 Even if what they say makes perfect sense.
Now imagine how your clients feel.
 They don’t know you.
 They assume you’re here to sell.
And when you start “explaining,” “educating,” “convincing”…
 what they really hear is: pressure.
The most successful agents I coach don’t “sell hard.”
 They don’t talk clients into buying.
They understand psychology.
 They ask the right questions
 that help clients see their own motivations,
 their own needs — and make the decision on their own.
That’s real persuasion.
 And it’s a big part of why some of my students are making
 1,050,000 AED in personal commission in just 4 months.
I teach this inside my YouTube training —
 the shift from external pressure to internal clarity.
