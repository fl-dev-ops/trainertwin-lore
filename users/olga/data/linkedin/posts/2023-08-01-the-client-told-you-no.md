The client told you NO.

What would you do?

Common sense is telling us to push further and keep convincing. 
Doing this, we are at risk to be seen as pushy, unprofessional, and even worse, desperate. This may only ensure our potential client that his decision to work with your competitor is right. 
I was with this dilemma myself just 2 months ago when after the initial conversation with a company representative, the conversation with the decision maker did not happen.

 On my follow-up with the CEO's assistant, they told me that they selected another sales trainer for the job. In my head, I was screaming - How is that fair if I never even been given an option to pitch my offer? I start to think, what can be the best strategy to keep this opportunity open for me in the future.

I believe that was a brilliant opportunity to use my elegant script, where instead of ‘I will call you in 3 months’ or ‘Call me if you ever need anything’ - which again for my nose smells like desperation - use ‘Would that make sense for us to connect in 3 months, just to confirm that you are happy with your current service provider…

Follow-up is due.
