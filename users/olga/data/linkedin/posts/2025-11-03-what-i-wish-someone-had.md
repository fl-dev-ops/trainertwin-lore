What I wish someone had told me on Day 1 in Dubai real estate

"You're not a salesperson. You're a lead hunter."
"Companies won't give you enough leads. Ever."

"Your biggest competitor is other agents, not other companies."

I thought I knew what I was getting into when I started in Dubai real estate.

I was wrong. Completely wrong.

Back home, sales meant someone handed you qualified prospects. You'd present, handle objections, close deals.

Dubai real estate? Different game entirely.

Your job isn't to sell. It's to hunt.

Every morning you wake up with zero leads unless you create them yourself. Cold calling. Networking. Referral systems. Lead generation is 80% of the work.

The companies promise leads. "We'll feed you quality prospects daily," they say.

That's the biggest lie in the industry.

Even the best companies can't generate enough quality leads to feed their entire sales force. It's mathematically impossible.

So you're competing with every other agent in your office for scraps.

Here's what changes everything: Stop waiting for leads to be given to you.

Start building your own pipeline from day one.

Because the agents making millions here? They're not better closers.

They're better hunters.

The sooner you accept that reality, the faster you'll start making real money.

Practical result: Agents who focus on lead generation from week one earn 3x more than those who wait for company leads.
