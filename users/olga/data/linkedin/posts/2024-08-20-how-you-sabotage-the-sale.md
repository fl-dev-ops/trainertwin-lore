🚫 How You Sabotage the Sale Before You Even Get Started

A few days ago, I was chatting with an agent who couldn’t understand why her latest call didn’t convert.

She said, "From the moment I started the call, I had this thought... 'He won’t be able to afford it. It's too much for him right now. He's juggling his full-time job and a small business on the side. Plus, he’s got a toddler to take care of.'"
So I asked, "Did he say that?" "No, I just assumed it."
There it was—the silent killer of the sale, creeping in at the very start of the call.
Here’s the thing: when you start assuming what your prospect can or can’t do based on their situation—whether it’s their job, lifestyle, or even the tone of their voice—you’ve already made the decision for them.
You’re no longer in a conversation. You’re in your own head.
My agent had unknowingly given up before the conversation even had a chance to unfold. Those assumptions about his financial situation colored everything she said and did during that call.
But here’s the twist: what if those same "reasons" are exactly why the prospect needs what you're offering?
Maybe the hectic schedule, the side business, and the family life are why your product could be the solution they’ve been looking for.
So here’s my advice:
Challenge your assumptions. Don’t let them dictate the outcome before you’ve even begun.
Because until your prospect says it, you don’t really know.
And here’s the truth—people will surprise you.
Be curious, be open, and most importantly, let the conversation guide the outcome.
 You might just find out that what you thought would be an obstacle is the very reason they say yes.
