She Had 100 Contacts. Zero Deals. Here's What Went Wrong.
Me starting a startegy session:
- Agent has No sales or Real Estate experience
- Just Moved to Dubai in October
- Already signed up with one of the companies - where you pay monthly FEE and have 100% commision.

However 100% of 0 is still...

But jokes aside

My coaching client had already called everyone in her phone book. Twice.

Friends, family, former colleagues — all of them.

The calls went well, people were interested... but no transactions.

If this sounds familiar, here's the missing piece most agents never learn.

The Problem: She was treating sales conversations like social catch-ups.

"Hey, I'm in Dubai now doing real estate. If you ever need to buy or sell, let me know!"

The Result: Polite interest. Zero urgency. No next steps.

What She Missed: Moving from conversation to consultation.

Here's what we fixed in her strategy session:

Instead of: "Let me know if you need anything"
We changed it to: "I'm arranging small workshops for friends to understand Dubai investment opportunities. Can you jump on a quick Zoom?"

Instead of: Waiting for them to be ready
We created: A systematic follow-up plan with specific touchpoints

Instead of: One generic message to everyone

We developed: Tailored approaches for different contact types

The Breakthrough: She wasn't selling Dubai real estate.

She was selling Dubai education and market insights.

People don't buy properties on phone calls.
They buy after understanding the opportunity.

Her new process:

Reconnect personally (not about real estate)
Mention her transition and expertise naturally
Invite to educational workshop/consultation
Provide value first, build trust
Guide them through decision process
The Result After Our Session:
→ Two contacts immediately booked Zoom calls
→ One said "I've been waiting for someone I trust in Dubai"
→ Three agreed to January follow-ups with clear timelines

Same contacts. Different approach. Game-changing results.
