Nine years in real estate, from agent to sales manager—does that qualify me to be a sales trainer? 

Honestly...
I’d say no. 

Mastering the art of ‘selling my personality’ didn’t mean I could automatically teach others. 
Real growth demands more than just repeating successes—it’s about stepping out of your comfort zone and embracing the unknown.

In 2017, after a series of big-ticket sales, I was tempted to indulge—maybe upgrade my car or move into a nicer apartment. 

But I knew lasting success required more than just material upgrades. So instead, I invested $28,000 in a journey of learning at the University of Wollongong in Dubai. 

It felt like a business class ticket for my career—committing not just to move forward but to truly soar.
The program was intense, diving deep into managerial accounting, HR, and marketing. 

Unfortunately, the graduation ceremony got cancelled, but it didn’t dampen the impact that education had on my career.

Natural talent can take you far, but to truly excel, you need to actively expand your knowledge—through formal education, challenging your practices, or stepping into roles that push your limits. That’s where the magic happens.

Looking back, what’s one decision that took you out of your comfort zone but made all the difference?
