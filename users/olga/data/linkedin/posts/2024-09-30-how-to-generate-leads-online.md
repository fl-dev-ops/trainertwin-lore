How to generate leads online with 0 investments, creating a stream of clients who choose to work with you, rather than you CONVINCING them to do so?

These tactics are specifically for Instagram where we have 4 types of content
Reels
Stories
Posts
Lives 
My formula is simple: Reels will pull them in, but Stories will close the deals.

Reels are great for catching attention—like that flashy "new listing" announcement. 
But here’s the thing: followers don’t turn into clients just because they saw one cool video. They stick around when they trust you. Trust is built when they see you showing up every day, doing what you do best - authentically living your life and sharing your personal story, values, ideas and of course the Success stories of your past clients.

So, here’s the game plan: Reels are your hook. 

They’re the quick wins—show off a property, drop a success story, or give a peek into what’s new. But then, take them deeper with Stories DAY by DAY.

This is where you bring people behind the scenes—share how you helped a client close a tricky deal, show your day-to-day grind, or even the occasional blooper (because hey, we’re all human!).

The formula? 

Post 5 Stories a day, 4 days a week, and give people a reason to stick around. Show them your value. 
Give them the real you. 
Reels get them in the door, but Stories are what makes them stay and, eventually, sign that dotted line.

What do you think? Are you sharing your personality through Stories?
