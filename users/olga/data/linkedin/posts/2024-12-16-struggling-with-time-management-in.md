Struggling with Time Management in your business? 

When you're starting out, every minute counts. If you're running from one task to another without focus, you’re missing out on crucial opportunities.

Here’s a simple formula to flip the script:
- Dedicate at least three hours daily to connect with potential clients. 'Olga are you talking about Cold Calling?'
- Pick up the phone, send emails, follow up on old leads—even if it feels awkward.
- Consistency is your secret weapon. Stick to this plan for three months, and you’ll witness a transformation.
