What's the difference between being rich and being wealthy? 🤔

Question for the real estate agent who just received their first commission check and is rushing to buy a Louis Vuitton wallet, while still living in a shared accommodation.

Rich people love showing off their wealth with flashy possessions. 

They think having the latest gadgets, cars, and designer clothes will give them more value and status. It's like, "Look at me, I have money!"

But being wealthy? That's a whole different game. 

Wealthy people have the freedom to do what they want, when they want, how they want. It's about long-term financial health and making smart investments. Wealth isn't about what you can buy today, it's about building a future where you don't have to worry about money.
Being rich is having lots of money. Being wealthy is having lots of time. Or, as I like to say, "Rich is buying a fancy car; wealthy is owning the road!"
Which one sounds better to you? 😎
