Why Dubai’s Real Estate Market Desperately Needs Strong Trainers”

🚨 How much money do real estate agencies burn on agents who quit?

Spoiler: A LOT.

Let’s do some quick math.
1.Recruiting an agent? 15K+ AED (visa, recruiter salary, bonuses). Recruiters are making way more money then agents these days….
2.Leads, CRM, marketing? 35K+ AED per agent (first six months minimum).
3.Retention rate? 90% of them quit before their first real commission.

That’s 50K+ AED per agent, straight into the void.

And why? Because most real estate agencies are playing a very expensive game of “hire and pray.”

❌ No structured onboarding.
❌ No real sales training.
❌ No clue what they’re actually supposed to be doing.

And here’s my favorite part—when they struggle, the usual advice is:

“Just watch the top agents and do what they do.”

Oh, so THAT’S the plan? Magic osmosis? No wonder most new agents run for the exit.

Here’s the thing: training isn’t optional—it’s the difference between agents making money or making excuses.

A solid sales trainer can:
✔️ Cut agent turnover in half (because agents who make money don’t quit).
✔️ Increase closing rates (so agencies stop bleeding out money on wasted leads).
✔️ Create a predictable sales system (instead of “just try harder, bro”).

I want to ask the real experts… the agents.
