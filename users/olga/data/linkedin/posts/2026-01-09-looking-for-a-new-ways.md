Looking for a NEW WAYS to Generate your OWN LEADS?....

If the marketing agency says "Don't worry, we'll handle everything" - RUN

Had an agent pitch me his brilliant idea: hire a cheap marketing team overseas to generate Dubai real estate leads.

His budget? "A couple thousand dollars to start."

Here's the reality check I gave him:

Companies like Emaar, Metropolitan, and Ax Capital are spending 650,000-1 000,000 dirhams per month on digital marketing.

You think your couple thousand dollars is going to compete with that?

When agencies tell you "don't worry, we'll create a campaign for you," they're about to take your money and leave you sorely disappointed.

I know this because I've been there.

I spent 33,000 dirhams on a marketing company that promised the world and delivered absolutely nothing. Generic campaigns that couldn't tell an investor from a tourist.

That expensive lesson taught me to spot the red flags:

🚩 "We'll handle everything" = They have no clue about Dubai's unique market
🚩 Promising results without understanding your niche = Recipe for disaster
🚩 Can't explain their strategy in simple terms = They don't have one

Here's what actually works in Dubai real estate marketing:

You need either massive budgets to compete with the big players, OR you need to get personal.

Your authentic story as someone who's actually invested in Dubai properties? You have background on Law?

That's worth more than any generic ad campaign.

Your expertise helping clients navigate specific areas like Creek or Marina? That's your competitive advantage.

Your direct experience doubled every property investment? That's your credibility.

Stop chasing magic marketing bullets.

Start leveraging what makes you different.

The market doesn't need another agent with a fancy campaign.

It needs someone who's actually walked the path their clients want to walk.

If you're considering hiring a marketing agency, ask them this: "Show me exactly how you'll make my personal experience and expertise the center of the campaign."

If they can't answer that, keep your money.
