I never thought I will say this..

But Sales Skills are not what determine your success in Dubai real estate anymore 

I have a first row seat to what is happening with agents in Dubaj real estate.
Agents who were top closers booking the tickets to go away for a summer… god knows is they will ever go back.
Agents who were closing 2-3 deals a month now spending hours in the office discussing how “ridiculous the buyers discount expectations are”.
Instead of calling…
And I understand the fluctuations and disappointment and anger - that’s what I know for sure - that there is a choice…
To see every situation either from the position - why this is happening to Me…

Or From a position “why I Created this “all” in my life”

Can you imagine-this question and reflection exercise is  Now a part of my Training - How to sell in a war uncertainty market.

It comes after several MindShifting concepts - so it’s almost instant change for agents.

After your ability to Act, Call, Handle abjections-changes as now you are in a different state.

When I walked myself through this process…many things surfaced. 
My own fears, illusions and expectations 
This is what keeps you and me stuck in uncertainty-comforting yourself with - What I can Do… it’s all politics- and back to another round of blaming everyone “outside of yourself”.

But after 2 pages written on - How that was me… not Trump 🤪… who created this in my life.

I knew exactly which unfair competitive advantages this situation is giving me and my business.
And what is my next step.
