My favorite question to the seller:

"You have a great property... Why are you even considering selling?"

And you know what... If he shows that he does not have enough motivation to sell yet and the only reason is that he is dreaming of getting an unrealistic price, ask yourself why you need this listing.

Go back to your main activity as a real estate agent - finding motivated buyers!
Try Next time - when you are talking to your POCKET LISTING OWNER.
