How to follow up with a seller (without looking like a stalker).
Yes — this is a real issue.
 And if you’re in sales, you’ve definitely felt it.
You want the listing.
 You’ve already followed up once.
 Maybe twice.
 Now you’re sitting there, second-guessing everything:
— “Did I sound too eager?”
 — “Is it too soon to follow up again?”
 — “Should I ask about his weekend? His cat? Anything?”
At some point, following up feels less like relationship-building…
 and more like digital lurking.
I’ve been there.
 And honestly?
 It’s not just uncomfortable — it’s ineffective.
Because staying “top of mind” doesn’t mean being constantly in their inbox.
 It means showing up strategically.
 With purpose.
 With clarity.
 With timing that makes sense for them, not just for you.
That’s why I created a follow-up formula I still use to this day.
 It’s built on one simple goal:
 Position yourself as the obvious next step — not the annoying next ping.
No fake small talk.
 No “just checking in.”
 Just relevance, trust, and timing.
Let’s make follow-up feel human again —
 and make listings happen for the right reasons.
