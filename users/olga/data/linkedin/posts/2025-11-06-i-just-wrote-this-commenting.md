I Just wrote this commenting on one the post…

And this will probably be supported by those who are in a leadership roles…
Not necessarily having a management position - more about having a Leadership Mindset.

The post was about necessity of providing Mental Health support to the employees… specifically Sales Agents in Real Estate…

And as much as I love you guys as I for the past 5 years sorely focusing on training agents to succeed… what is see is controversial.

The more you give the more that would be taken for granted, the more people expect to be done for them, the more entitlement I hear in the agents conversation with the clients.

Like agents asking me: “Olga, why they even requested the information if they don’t want to buy?”

So coming back to the Mental health support provided by the company - my answer was this:…..how about Adult human beings finally will take the responsibility for their own: Mental Health, Physical health, Sales skills, Motivation and etc.

It’s feels like a trend recently-just to display “we should be given/provided with XYZ” as if it’s someone else responsibility to make you a successful, healthy and Happy Human.

How about step out of 95% and take ownership without expecting a company or your boss to become “You Parent”…

The Secret of Winning Big is the understanding - No one will come to Save You!

At least it is for me….

And just for the record I’m working with therapist online for 20 months now… once a week, and pay from my own pocket.
