10 years out of 53...

I call Dubai my home—for almost a quarter of my life now.

Yes, it may seem money-driven, artificial, or temporary. But Dubai challenges you to reflect on what truly drives you.

Many of my students ask, "With your skills, why aren’t you still selling real estate? You’d make so much money." My answer is always the same: money isn’t what drives me. I’m looking for something meaningful, something lasting—the impact I can create on people and, through them, on the economy of the country I proudly call home.

Dubai can bring out the best in you—or the worst. It all depends on how you choose to channel your energy.
