Is this Unit really that Amazing?

Be real. Highlight the amazing features, but don't shy away from the imperfections. Here's why:

- Transparency builds trust with your clients.
- Trust is essential for closing deals.
- Think of it like dating; perfection is a myth, and reality is more relatable.

Like someone who claims they've never had a bad day—seems fishy, right? The same goes for real estate. 
How do you balance honesty with showcasing the best of what you offer?
