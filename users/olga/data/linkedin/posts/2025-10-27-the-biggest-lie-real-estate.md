The biggest lie real estate companies tell new agents: "Just follow up with leads and you'll make sales."

This advice keeps agents broke and companies profitable.

Because following up without understanding the psychology behind buyer objections is like throwing darts blindfolded.

Companies don't teach the real sales process because it works in their favour.
• New agents burn through leads without closing
• Companies keep the best prospects in their CRM
• Agents blame themselves for "not working hard enough"

What they don't tell you:

Following up isn't about persistence – it's about positioning.
The client who says "we're still looking" isn't asking for more options.
They're signalling they don't trust your advice yet.

Here's what actually converts leads:
• Map the client's real timeline (not their stated one)
• Address the unspoken objection behind every "maybe"
• Position yourself as the expert who eliminates risk, not just another agent chasing commissions

When agents learn the actual sales psychology behind buyer behaviour, their conversion rate jumps from 2% to 15%+.

But most companies would rather hire 10 new agents than properly train 2 existing ones.

The math works better for them.
