“I’m receiving at least 5 calls a day from different agents in Dubai, but I do not want to deal with them…”

This was literally word for word what I heard from a client I met during my trip to Saudi.
He was very interested in investing in Dubai, has funds available, and visits Dubai quite often.

So, why is he still looking?

I often hear from agents - ‘The client was not serious’ or ‘He is not ready’. Most of the time this means that after the agent did the ‘convincing part’ and sent several options, the client stopped responding.

Sound familiar?

No wonder clients are indecisive - they hear different agents trying to convince them about various investments and often, they are given contradictory information - so naturally, they move away.

Why is this happening?

The majority of agents completely ignore client discovery or make it feel like an interrogation. Because of this, clients hold onto their information and give vague answers.

The result? A loss on both sides.

How can you be different and fix this in your sales process?

- Do not assume. Be curious about this particular client’s situation. 

- Ask soft-opening questions. “Why did you choose this project?” Or start with… “I am curious. What was it about the ad that attracted your attention?”

- Clarify the intentions. “Let’s say, we found a property which matches your criteria XYZ, what would be your next possible steps?”

- Show that you care about their particular situation. “Help me to understand. When you said you want the property for both personal use and as an investment, what exactly do you mean by that?”

The client will only choose to work with you if he has the feeling - that you are the one who understands his needs.

Good luck!
