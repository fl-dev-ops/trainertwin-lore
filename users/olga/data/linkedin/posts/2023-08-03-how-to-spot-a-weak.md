How to spot a weak sales trainer:
I felt nervous while listening to one of my mentor's podcasts. What if I find out it's about me? Give it a listen.

In short:

Focus on RAPPORT building as the number one thing in sales.
Often experts on business speak, but do not provide tangible actionable steps.
Always have a strong opinion on what you should be doing but weak in explaining why you should be doing this. If they cannot tell you WHY, always doubt the HOW?
They have not created their own content and use book-smart concepts with very little practical implementation.
