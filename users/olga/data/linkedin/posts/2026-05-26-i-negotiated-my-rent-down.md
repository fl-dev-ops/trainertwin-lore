I negotiated my rent down by 5%.

But honestly, the discount was expected…

The most interesting part was what  rental agent
Told me - over past 2 months he closed 1 rental deal 20% below market.

Who will Drop the Ball first?

I’m checking the portal’s regularly…
I can’t see any movements in a price.

But what I see a lot here - is that Agencies CEO’s complains a lot about the marketing cost - while the leads numbers dropped 3-4 times…

Why is it still not reflected in so many listings we see online?

Landlords still want yesterday’s prices.

Sellers still expect last year’s demand.  

Agents are stuck somewhere in the middle, trying to keep everyone happy.  

And I get it.

But here is the uncomfortable part.

The problem is not only the number of leads.

The bigger problem is how quickly companies adjust their sales process, pricing conversations, agent training and lead conversion strategy to the new market reality.

Many off-plan focused agencies stoped the marketing completely or narrowed down to GCC 

I would consider it a White Flag…

The companies I work with are still running Marketing campaigns and generating leads.  
Agents?  - They are still closing deals.

But they are not pretending the market is the same.

They are looking at the numbers honestly. They are tightening performance, improving client conversations, training agents properly and becoming much more selective with where they spend their money.

So I am genuinely curious.

What is happening in your company right now?

Are you still getting quality leads?

Are you able to successfully negotiate Price Reduction?
