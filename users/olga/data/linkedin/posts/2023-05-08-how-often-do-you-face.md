How often do you face rejection? I you are in sales, your answer could be anywhere from 99% to 50%.

Taking these Risks is an essential part of being successful. 
But what if I told you that rejection can be transformed into a powerful tool for success?

Did you know that 94% of salespeople quit trying after the fourth call, even though 60% of all sales are made after that point? This means that 94% of salespeople are giving up on 60% of prospective buyers.
Here are five things to keep in mind when facing rejection:
First of all, remember that with every contact with a potential client, you have nothing to lose and everything to gain by asking again.
Ask questions as if you expect to get a positive response. Have the attitude that you already have what you want.
Qualify the person and make sure you speak to every decision-maker at the table.
Be clear and specific, especially when asking about money.
Persistence is key to success. Kids understand this principle better than anyone - they'll ask for the same thing over and over again without hesitation until they get what they want.

Practical exercise that I use in my work with salespeople: make a list of things you want in life but haven't asked for. Write down how you stop yourself from asking and what fears you are escaping from. Then, write down what it's costing you.

I do it myself whenever I feel stuck in my career or personal life.
