It's time to evaluate your 2023 and write yourself a letter for 2024.
I'm still putting together my goals using a smart system but recently I've added something less evident.

Lifestyle planning.

I'm starting backwards, going from how I want to spend my day to be happy, to what I should have in place in terms of resources, which skills I should have and what I should do daily/weekly/yearly to achieve it.

My lifestyle starts something like this description of my new Tuesday.

When I wake up in the morning around 6:30, I go for a 45-minute walk in a green neighbourhood, maybe taking a swim.
Gym or yoga at 7:30, then relaxed breakfast on a terrace with an open sea view.
(I have this vision of a house on a cliff, very Australian style - I believe this is already costing me at least around AED 5,000,000 - not even talking about the yearly maintenance involved.)

Now we can talk about: How we are already getting there?!
