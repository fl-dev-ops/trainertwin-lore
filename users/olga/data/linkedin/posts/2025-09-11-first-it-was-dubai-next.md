First it was Dubai. Next, it’s Saudi Arabia.

Saudi Arabia just passed a law that finally opens the real estate market to non-Saudis.
Starting January 2026, foreigners will be able to fully own property — not just for personal use, but as an investment.

✅ Who can own?
	•	Non-Saudi individuals (residents & non-residents)
	•	Foreign companies
	•	International organizations & funds
	•	Saudi companies with foreign shareholders

💰 How much does it cost?
	•	10% total fee (5% standard tax + up to 5% additional non-Saudi fee).

Even before this law, agents in my coaching program who sold properties in Saudi Arabia in 2024–2025 earned significantly more than on Dubai deals. Why? Scarcity and global demand from Muslims who see ownership in the Kingdom as more than investment — it’s a dream.
