Pulling yourself back after dealing with a rude client is quite a task. Agents share stories of being threatened with fines, sworn at, and badmouthed by clients. They stopped calling for days and weeks, feeling discouraged and hurt. Here is the truth – the client DOES NOT OWE YOU anything! Never just assume they are looking for your services. Always use wording like:

        …You can decide if we should continue this conversation

        …If that would make sense for you

…It's up to you to decide

What this does is eliminate the sales pressure from both the client and you. When there is no sales pressure, there are far fewer reasons for the client to push back and be aggressive.

Choose the time of day when you are most energized and active. You should feel good about yourself and your abilities to help your clients. Change your body position – JUMP or dance – your mood will automatically improve.

Your goal is not to get a listing or a client who will buy from you today. Your goal is JUST TO EXECUTE 100 dials and have productive, pleasant conversations. If your focus shifts from your NEED TO SELL to a CONSISTENT practice, your results will skyrocket.

You need volume – if you've only made 20 dials in 10 days, there is nothing to talk about. You will never master the skills even with the best possible script. And 50% of a successful call is the right script.

That’s why I decided not only to share the latest version of my script but also to teach you how to use it – so you can turn ‘Not interested’ into ‘Can you list my property.’
