How I book meetings from cold calls (actual script included)

"Would it be useful if we booked some time together so I can walk you through the process before you start reviewing offers?"

Notice: I'm not asking for a meeting. I'm offering value. The booking happens naturally.

Most agents sabotage themselves with: "Can we book a meeting?" or "Are you available for a call?"

Wrong. 

You just made yourself the one who needs something from them.

Instead, position yourself as the expert who's offering guidance they can't get anywhere else.

My full script breakdown:

After they mention looking at properties: "Have you had someone sit you down and walk you through how the process works from putting down 10% to getting keys in hand?"

When they say no: "Would it be useful if we booked some time together so You  can see the actual statistics - not just promises - of what the market's been doing for the past three years?"

Close with commitment: "Can I expect something from you? If you need to reschedule, just send me a WhatsApp so I can rebook a new time for you... and put someone else in that slot."

The psychology: I'm the doctor. They need the checkup. I'm doing them a favor by making time.
This approach moved my client's conversion rate from cold call to meeting from 20% to 60%.
