Time and time again....

 I observe that agents who may not be the brightest, the smartest, or the most knowledgeable about the market, and who may even have poor communication skills, are succeeding in closing deals. 

Meanwhile, those who strive for perfection and dig deep into knowledge often aren't there yet. 

The answer to this discrepancy is simple: 
Consistency.

Those who show up every day, put in the effort, and remain dedicated to their goals, despite their imperfections, ultimately achieve success. 

Consistency trumps perfection. It's like my grandma always says, "You don't have to be a genius to bake a good pie, just keep an eye on the oven!"
