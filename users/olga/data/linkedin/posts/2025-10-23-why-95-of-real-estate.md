Why 95% of real estate agents never make it past year one (and how the 5% who do think differently)


Most new agents focus on learning the market, memorizing properties, and perfecting their pitch presentations.

But here's what successful agents do instead: They master the psychology of decision-making before they ever show a property.

The difference? 

They understand that buying real estate isn't a logical decision - it's an emotional one that people justify with logic later.


After training 500+ agents in Dubai over 10 years, I've seen this pattern over and over.


The agents who survive don't just know square footage and ROI calculations.

They know that when a client says "I need to think about it," they're really saying "You haven't helped me understand my own decision-making process yet."

The top 5% ask questions like:

→ "What attracted you to the Dubai real estate market in the first place, rather than your own country?"

→ "Help me understand how the decision-making process looks like for your family when it comes to buying property abroad?"

→ "If you were to look ahead in 3-4 years' timeframe, where do you want to be with your property investment?"

They help clients think through their own motivations and goals first, then provide the logical framework to support that vision.

Because when someone understands their WHY clearly, the HOW becomes much easier.


When they're still confused about their motivations, every detail becomes an obstacle.
The market knowledge comes later. 


Understanding the client's decision-making process comes first.
