Succeeding as a new real estate agent in a market depends 70% on the belief system you have and the environment you are in.

Your first sales team can determine whether or not you will make money in your first year.

The wrong way to make decisions on which company to join is based solely on the commission split.

 There are companies where, on paper, you can keep more of your commission, but in reality, you may not have much left to keep. 

My key point is that you should establish whether or not you can be productive in a company as a new agent.
 
Here's how I would determine it if I were a brand new agent in the current market:

1) What are the character traits of the people who succeed in your team?

2) What is the company's business model, and where do the leads come from? Is it inbound prospecting or outbound prospecting?

3) How do you plan to help me develop my skills? What does that plan look like?

4) What are your team standards or belief system?

5) What is my potential career path in this company? Is there an opportunity for growth, and what does that look like?

My first team in Dubai was led by Guy Last .

 He taught us the foundational pieces, such as prospecting, cold calling, closing techniques, team support, and accountability, which, by 90%, determined my success in the industry.
