Rejection is a myth.

Consider this: You started without a customer at the beginning of your pitch, and at the end of your sales pitch, you still don’t have a customer.

Essentially, everything remains the same for you. Instead of perceiving it as a loss, view every opportunity as a chance to gain.

The reason you feel like you've lost something is because you tie your self-esteem to the outcome of client conversations. You search for validation in a place where it is unlikely to be found.

High performance, whether in an industry or sport, sets individuals apart from the average performer by their ability to maintain a neutral attitude and perceive rejection as a mere response, NOT as a FAILURE. 

How about shifting your attitude and cultivating the superpower of unwavering focus? Instead of fixating on positive responses, or the “yes” from clients, shift your focus to the number of attempts you make each day in your prospecting of clients. 

As a real estate agent, a good number is between 15 to 20 conversations a day with new clients.
