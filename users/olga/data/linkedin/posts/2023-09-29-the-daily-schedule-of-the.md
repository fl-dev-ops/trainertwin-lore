The daily schedule of the effective agent.

Your goal is to have at least 10 conversations with new clients daily.

6:30am: Wake Up
7:00-8:00am: Physical activity - Walking, Jogging, Gym
8:30am: Breakfast
9:00-9:30am: Email and News
9:30-10:00am: Role-play your script and ‘Articulation Gymnastics’ - for a clear and confident voice
10:00-11:30am: First prospecting Call Block (New leads) - No distractions, No colleagues chitchat, No social media During the call blocks you are only doing calls and noting down what will be the next steps with clients - but do not execute the steps
11:30-12:30am: You execute what you agreed during the Call Block
12:30-1:30pm: Lunch Break and Walk
1:30-3:00pm: Second Call Block (Follow-up leads) - same rule - No distractions!
3:00-4:00pm: You execute what you agreed during the Call Block
4:00-7:00pm: Client Meetings
