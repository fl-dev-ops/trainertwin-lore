In the world of real estate, there's a costly mistake that many newcomers make: the fear of asking questions. 

You might think that being too polite will make you more likable, but in reality, it can prevent you from closing a deal. Here's the kicker: not asking the right questions is like trying to solve a puzzle without any pieces. 

- Don't shy away from asking about budgets or personal challenges. 
- Digging deeper helps you uncover your client's real situation. 
- Clients will see you as a pro who’s there to genuinely help. 
- Think of it like a doctor's visit: the thorough one asking all the right questions shows expertise more than charm.
