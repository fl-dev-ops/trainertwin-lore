Monday is a day of new beginnings, but I actually started it yesterday...
Why Im already succeeding? 
This is the name of the exercise I was given on my business education last week. 
The programme is for entrepreneurs in social media marketing. It will not give me any certificate or diploma which I will be able to add to a wall, but still, 670 people paid $ 4 000 each to join.
I challenge you...
TO Start writing an article about yourself with the title 'Why I'm already Succeding' Goal is to add 50 reasons to your list.
And you will see how the world will open the doors of possibilities.
 Those I mine for today
1. I love interacting with people.
2. With every interaction, I intend to make others feel better about themselves.
3. I speak 2 languages, English and Russian
4. I'm now in the best possible place to grow the online business.
5. I'm at peace with myself.
6. I accepted my imperfections.
7. My main goal is to transform the lives of other people.
8. I love learning and implementing my knowledge on a spot.
9. I got rid of my alcohol addiction (I believe I was a social alcoholic). 10. It upsets some of my friends..well, one friend at least.
11. I'm at the peak of my performance, mental and physical.
12. I live in the magic utopia place, where every morning I have SUN.
13. The main reason I'm doing things is to BE FREE, CONFIDENT and have FUN.
14. I'm ok to make mistakes and appear stupid in front of other people
15. I'm happy to share my knowledge without waiting for any compensation in return.
16. I realised I always was a PRODUCER - I've seen what would be the best way for a person to grow and develop, but I was using my super-powers without any request - so I was actually hurting people. 
... I wrote 26 so far...
This exercise is a powerful reminder of our achievements, both big and small. 
It helps us acknowledge our strengths and the areas where we've grown. 
So take a moment to reflect on your own journey and write down 50 reasons why you're already succeeding.
