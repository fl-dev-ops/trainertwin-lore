Your ability to become a great salesperson is connected directly with your ability to tolerate pain.

Looking at the bigger picture, this is the same as with individuals who achieve outstanding results in sports, science, and business.

I became fit at 36.

By this, I mean I had the physique I had never had before, even at 16. I would not call myself ‘out of shape’ prior to this. I had been training and watching my food intake since I was 14. At least, I thought I was. I had average results for a very long time. So, I decided this was the max I could squeeze out of my body.

How wrong I was.

So, what changed at 36? Only two things changed.

The resources I put into it (time and money) and my 'Why'. I hired a personal trainer five times a week, & strictly followed the nutrition plan. My 'Why' - the reason why I needed something so strongly for myself - it was now or never.

I decided that to get out of my average body shape and become a person who is somehow trained, I would be going on stage in a bikini fitness competition in one year.

This first year has taught me more than all the business training and courses I have previously taken.

And probably for the first time, I pushed myself consistently through real pain and discomfort to achieve a goal.

I have got a 6-pack for the first time in my life.

Do you think I will make it to the competition?
