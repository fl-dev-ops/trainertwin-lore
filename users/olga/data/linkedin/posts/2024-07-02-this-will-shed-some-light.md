This will shed some light on why you do not want to make calls in your sales office, running to the corridors and lobbies...

Accountability and visibility are key pillars in progressing any skills. 

I often see real estate agents running in corridors, hiding, or going downstairs in the lobby just to make a call to their client. 

They don't want to be seen, heard, or supervised. What does this mean?

Would you do push-ups with the same effort and quality if you were alone at home or if you were in front of a hundred people watching? 

Ask yourself this question next time you decide to run away from your colleagues and your manager who could be watching and able to correct you. 

Embrace visibility and let accountability elevate your skills and conversations to the next level.
