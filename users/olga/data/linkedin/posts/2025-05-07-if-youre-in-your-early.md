If you're in your early real estate career — negotiating price reductions can feel impossible.
You don’t want to offend the client.
 You don’t want to risk the listing.
 So you stay silent, list high, and hope something clicks.
But here’s the thing:
 Silence doesn’t sell.
 And the longer you wait, the harder the conversation becomes.
So how do you make the shift?
There’s a way to present the data, the viewings, and the buyer's silence —
 but it starts with one key question:
“What do you think the market is trying to communicate to us?”
That question changes everything.
 It stops the conversation from being you vs. the seller,
 and turns it into you and the seller vs. the market.
That’s when they start to think.
 That’s when they start to see.
 And often — that’s when they bring up the price adjustment themselves.
This is how I teach agents to build trust and lead like professionals —
 even if it’s only their 5th or 10th listing.
