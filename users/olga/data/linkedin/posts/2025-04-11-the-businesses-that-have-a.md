The businesses that have a brain — dominate.

And in real estate, you are the business.

Whether you’re an agent, a sales manager, or a sales director — you’re not just working in sales.
You’re running your own system,
your own pipeline,
your own performance engine.

Most people track how many leads they bring in.
They even track how many deals they close.

But what happens in between those two points?

Mystery. Magic.
Bermuda Triangle.

No visibility. No structure. No control.
And that’s where most money gets lost.

Time and time again, when I work with companies and review their sales processes, lead qualification, marketing, or recruitment — I see the same thing:
They know performance is below expectations.
But when I ask:
“What’s the benchmark?”
“What’s the actual conversion rate?”
There’s silence.
Or guesses.
Not numbers. Not structure.

Let’s test something.
If you actually know your conversion rate —
from lead generated to a booked meeting —
drop it in the comments.

I have a feeling there won’t be too many comments under this one.
