How do you overcome the complexes and limitations 


that whisper you’re not good enough, that you can’t close that deal, that you’re not cut out for it?

Like
- I’m bad on calling strangers and asking them for listing
- I’m camera Shy I can’t do videos
- I can’t do proper Follow up as I don’t want to bother people 

The truth is, these complexes are nothing more than stories we tell ourselves — born from unrealistic expectations or past disappointments. They come and go, but they don’t define us. If you keep moving forward, each complex is just another step on your path to growth.

I had an agent told me on a Session he was terrible at cold calling. Instead of debating, I gave him a simple challenge: take 30 minutes to study the script, an hour to role-play with a teammate, and then commit to 300 dials in that day,

Two weeks later, he was more confident and booking meetings like never before.

And remember, these complexes will always take new forms as you grow. They are just a natural response to stepping into new territory. It’s not about being perfect; it’s about recognizing that doubts are part of the journey, reframing them, and keeping your momentum.
