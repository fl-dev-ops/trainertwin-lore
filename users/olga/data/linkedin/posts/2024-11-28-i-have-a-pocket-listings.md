I have a Pocket Listings - So Does everyone else!

Let’s get one thing straight: Pocket Listings won’t magically turn into signed contracts, no matter how many times you call or ask the owner to give you the listing.
For those who don’t know, a Pocket Listing is when you speak with an owner, and they share information about their property. 
Maybe they’re not ready to list it publicly. Maybe they already have three agents working on it. 
But here’s the catch: Pocket Listings often stay pocket listings till Sold by someone else.
What’s your recipe for turning Pocket Listings into signed agreements?
