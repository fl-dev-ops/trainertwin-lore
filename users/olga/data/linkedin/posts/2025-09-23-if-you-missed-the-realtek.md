If you missed the REALTEK Summit  last week, let me share a quick highlight. 

For me it was already the third time attending. My very first RealTeck was back in 2023, when we only had a single session about the basic version of ChatGPT. Fast forward to today and the picture is very different.

Now we are already looking at a full AI solution connected with data from the Dubai Land Department. Imagine a ChatGPT that not only chats with you but also tells you what really happened and what is about to happen, all powered by DataBaked insights. The speed of change in our industry is impressive.

The set-up and organisation were, as always, on a very high level. And it felt genuinely refreshing to step out of the online bubble and meet people face to face. Even Zoom needs a break sometimes.

For me, the biggest takeaway is how quickly innovation must be translated into practice. A course is one thing, but a proper training is what turns knowledge into results. This is exactly what we need to focus on when preparing agents for a market that changes faster than your WiFi password.
