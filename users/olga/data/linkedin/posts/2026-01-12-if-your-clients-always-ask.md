If your Clients Always ask for Discount - use this Frame

Most agents panic when a client asks for a discount.

They stammer: "Let me check with the developer" or "I'll see what I can do."

Wrong move. You just lost control.

Here's what I teach my agents:

When a client asks for a discount, ask them one question:

"If you had 3 buyers for your car - one offering full price, and 2  asking for a discount - which would you choose?"

They always say full price.

Then you add:

"Look, developers DO give discounts sometimes. But the units they discount? Those aren't the best units that will bring you the highest profits later.

Those are exactly the units that won't generate your desired returns.

Think about it - if you're selling your car and three people want to buy it, are you giving a discount to the guy who lowballs you? Or are you taking it to auction and watching the price go UP?

Same with property. Discounted units are the ones sitting on the market. The ones nobody else wants. Facing the parking lot, not the sea. Weird layouts that don't rent well.

Developers aren't charities. They give discounts when they HAVE to, not when they WANT to.

So which unit do you need? One with a discount that screams 'something's wrong here,' or one that will actually make you money?"

When you frame it like this, they choose profit over savings every time.

Stop competing on price.
Start competing on value.

Train your clients to hunt for appreciation, not discounts.

Their portfolio will thank you in 3 years when that "expensive" unit doubled and that "discounted" ones ...hopefully at 25%.
