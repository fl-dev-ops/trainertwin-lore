People buy with emotions and justify it with logic. You might think a perfectly tailored investment pitch with all the numbers is the way to sell properties. But if three properties have the same numbers, how will the client choose? Ask them this before presenting. 

And help me understand, Mr Client … If I potentially will show you 3 different properties which will match your ROI goals, how will you choose which one is for you?

Understand what, besides the numbers, makes a significant difference for them.
