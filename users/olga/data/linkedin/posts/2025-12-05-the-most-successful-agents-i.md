The most successful agents I know all understand this truth about objections.

"I need to think about it" doesn't mean no.
"The timing isn't right" doesn't mean never.
"I've decided to invest in my Country" doesn't mean they won't change their mind.

Every objection is information about their current situation, not their permanent position.

People change jobs, get promotions, inherit money, have life events.

The "no" today might be the "yes" in 6 months.

Keep the relationship warm. Time changes everything.
