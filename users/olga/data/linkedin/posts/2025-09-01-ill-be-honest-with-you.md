I’ll be honest with you — I totally stole this structure 


from one of my business partners (whom I genuinely admire 👀). 

He posts his results regularly, and I thought: why not do the same? At least once a month. A bit of reflection, a bit of accountability… and I recommend you borrow the idea too. It really works.

So here we go — Summer Results 2025 🎣

🥍My private mentorship agents earned over AED 1.5M in personal commissions this summer. Yes, in the middle of Dubai’s “everyone-is-on-vacation” season. That’s money they take home, not company turnover.

🏑I ran 30+ trainings, part of them within my six-month coaching program. Sometimes I feel like I speak more in trainings than in my personal life. (My friends would probably confirm.)

🏒I launched the Live It Up Academy on YouTube — think “Netflix for real estate agents.” Trainings on cold calling, online presentations, closing your first deal if you’re new. Basically: the stuff I wish I had when I started. (This deserves its own separate post.)

🏸At One Broker Group, I updated the structure of training for existing agents and new joiners within the Learning & Development department. The company is heading into aggressive expansion, and we needed a model aligned with the overall company strategy. Translation: turning more people into consistent closers before they run out of savings.

🏹Main insight: strategies are meant to be questioned. Not everything in my six-month coaching program worked perfectly. At OBG, I rebuilt the format to be more company-strategy-dependent, and it really feels more effective.

🏏Biggest struggle? Not booking a one-way ticket to Bali for the whole month. (Still on the bucket list.)

⛸️What’s next:
	•	New training products at OBG, including leadership programs for future managers.
	•	Expanding the YouTube Academy so more agents — or those dreaming of becoming one — get access to tools that can actually make them millionaires.

Because nothing breaks my heart more than watching newcomers come to Dubai, burn through their savings, and leave disappointed.
