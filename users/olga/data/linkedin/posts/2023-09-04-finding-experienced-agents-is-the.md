Finding experienced agents is the real challenge in Dubai.

This happens because of a few reasons I believe. 

Firstly, getting into this industry is pretty easy, so lots of people join. 

Secondly, those who do join and start doing well sometimes get too comfortable and stop pushing themselves. 
They don't get how important it is to keep learning. 
They don't see that now's the time to level up and learn crucial sales and marketing techniques. 

So, here's the thing you have to ask yourself: Do you want to keep struggling in sales for another year, facing rejection and losing money in commission or…? 

Maybe it's time to think about putting money into some training so you can make your sales results way better.
