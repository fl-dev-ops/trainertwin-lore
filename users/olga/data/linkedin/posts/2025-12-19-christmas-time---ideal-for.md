Christmas time - Ideal for Get more Deals. And this is how

Reaching out to your 'warm leads' people who know you already - even if you just met once.

Writing this as an Advise for all of you who wants to put own money in Boosting posts or leagen.

If what you are trying to boost - is not already converting when you reach out to your warm audience - people who know you...How this will convert stranges with 0 trust?

you will just Burn money.

Before you start your outreach campaign, do this...

Most people think lead generation is about quantity.

It's about methodology.

Don't spray and pray. Don't sound desperate. Don't pitch immediately.

Start with re- connection. Move to conversation. Then to offer value.

Example on your instagram:

"Hey [name], how's [specific thing they care about]? I made a career transition into Dubai real estate and I'm arranging a small workshop for friends. 
Its not Like I will be trying to sell you anything.
Would you be potentially interested in understanding how some of my clients are generating returns without paying tax?"

Specific. Personal. Low pressure. Clear next step.

That's how you turn contacts into clients.
