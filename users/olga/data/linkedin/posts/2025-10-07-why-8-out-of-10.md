Why 8 out of 10 new agents leave the company in the first few months and how to avoid it.

Most companies run onboarding as a mix of motivation, inspiration, and a rare dose of legislation or brand storytelling.
But what new agents really need is not another motivational speech.
They need to understand how to make money in real life.

That’s why after launching our New Agents Training at ONE BROKER GROUP, I interviewed agents who completed it a month ago.
Not to collect compliments, but to identify what actually helps them earn and what doesn’t.

Here’s what almost everyone said.

“I still don’t fully understand what I should physically do every day to make money.”
“I don’t know how to measure if I’m doing enough.”
“We need templates for communication with clients and other agents.”
“It would help to have practical Q&A sessions with agents who are already closing deals.”

They don’t lack ambition.
They lack clarity on daily actions, on sales processes, on what enough looks like.

So here’s what we are implementing next.
	1.	The full Off-plan market sales process from project briefing to closing.
	2.	The secondary market path from listings to offers.
	3.	Agent-to-agent relations and how to build scalable B2B collaboration even when everyone already knows your company.

The goal is simple. Move every new agent from learning to earning as fast as possible.

Because retention doesn’t depend on culture posters or coffee mugs.
It depends on how quickly a new agent understands exactly what actions generate income.

If you asked your new agents today what they should do tomorrow morning to make their first commission, would they know the answer?
