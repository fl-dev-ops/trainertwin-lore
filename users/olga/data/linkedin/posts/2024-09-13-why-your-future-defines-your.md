Why Your Future Defines Your Present
There was a time when I thought I was simply "manifesting"—talking about the things I wanted to see in my future. 

But as I grew and learned, I realized something crucial: it’s not just about dreaming; it’s about understanding that the future you envision actually drives your actions in the present.

The clearer your vision of the future, the more it influences the decisions you make today. Your future isn't something distant that will "happen" to you; it's a blueprint that guides your present actions. If you know exactly where you want to be in five years—what kind of life you want to live, who you want to be surrounded by, what kind of work you want to do—then every decision you make now starts to align with that vision.

But here’s the catch: if you don’t have a clear vision of your future, how can you expect your present to be anything but chaotic or directionless? That’s why it’s so important to regularly spend time thinking about the future you want to create.
Try this: Take a moment to imagine your life five years from now. What does your ideal day look like? Where do you wake up? What surrounds you? Where do you go for breakfast? What are you doing throughout your day? Who are the people around you? What thoughts fill your mind? The more detailed your vision, the more powerful it becomes.

And remember, money isn’t the primary focus—it’s a byproduct of the value you bring into the world. Focus on the impact you want to have, and let that vision of the future guide your actions in the present.
I encourage you to give this exercise a try and see how it can help clarify your path forward. Your future creates your present—so make sure that future is something you’re excited to build.
