I'm about to share the follow-up strategy that changed my entire sales career.

Most people follow up once. Maybe twice. Then they give up.

Winners follow up with purpose:
• Immediate: "Thanks for the call"
• 1 week: Relevant market update
• 1 month: Success story from similar client
• 3 months: "How's the baby/project/situation you mentioned?"
• 6 months: New opportunity that fits their timeline

Your follow-up game determines your income game.

The fortune is in the follow-up. Period.
