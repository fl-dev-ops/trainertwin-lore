Looking for the secret to attract million-dirham agents into your team?

Let me save you time:
You can’t.
Not because you’re not good enough —
but because any company with a brain is holding on to them like a golden goose.

They’re not just top performers.
They’re internal motivators.
They raise the standard for the entire team.
And believe me — their commission split is not 50/50.
It’s higher than you think.
Because companies know how valuable they are.

So, instead of chasing unicorns,
here’s what you should focus on:

Hire for potential.
Train for performance.
Build the character — not just the pipeline.

When I say “potential,” I don’t mean past sales volume.
I mean traits like:
• The ability to do hard things consistently
• The willingness to look in the mirror
• The mindset to receive constructive criticism — and actually use it

Top salespeople are not built on ego.
They’re built on grit.

If someone gets defensive every time they hear feedback —
they’ll never become a true top closer.

In today’s market, the companies that will win
are the ones who know how to train and retain the right people.

And let’s be honest:
If your business can only survive with top closers —
you don’t have a real business.

⸻

P.S. If you’re already making a million dirham in personal commission —
don’t comment. You’re good.

But if you believe you’ve got the character and mindset
to become one —
I’d love to hear from you.
