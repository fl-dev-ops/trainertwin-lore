How to become a trusted Adviser for your Clients?

Level 1.
Authority doesn't solely come from highlighting past achievements. Rarely do agents come to a potential client saying, 

"I'm #10 in the area."

Everyone claims to be the best, and it can lead clients to view you as just another salesperson trying to sell me...

Puff💨- the trust is Gone


Level 2.

A more effective approach is a neutral one, avoiding desperation in pitching yourself as #1.

Instead, act as a confident professional, saying, "I've successfully helped clients with similar requests to buy/sell properties with a high premium. 
However, that doesn't mean that what I do will 100% work for you. 

I need to know more about your situation, such as..." Begin with your qualification questions to truly understand the client's needs.

This will allow the clients to Open up! Be honest with the responses - just because they feel you are not Trying To Sell them on a spot!
