I don't consider myself very effective. I feel like I'm still wasting too much time on repetitive tasks and lack automation. One of my new rules that I'm applying religiously every workday is planning the next day ahead. I keep sticky notes and a pencil on my nightstand, and before my nighttime reading, I jot down tomorrow's schedule with the points I have to complete.

In the morning, I stick the note on the board in my kitchen first thing, and this way, I reread the tasks again. My brain does the rest—prioritising the tasks and keeping track of the timings. I believe the process of handwriting and rereading helps position it in long-term memory, at least for a day. Plus, using a sticky note is convenient since my phone doesn't go with me to bed.

Do you have a better system?
