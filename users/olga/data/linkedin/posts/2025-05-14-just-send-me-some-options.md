Just send me some options...

 If you’ve heard this from a client — you know it usually leads nowhere.

 No meeting.
 No commitment.
 No deal.
In off-plan real estate, the real challenge isn’t in sending options —
 It’s in creating clarity and trust before the options even show up.
💡 Here are 3 simple questions I teach agents to use —
 to shift the conversation from casual interest to serious decision-making:
1️⃣ Have you been to Dubai?
 2️⃣ And was that more like a leisure tourism or it was specifically investment-driven trip?
 3️⃣ How confident do you feel that you will be able to select the right area in the project, not based on what the agent is promising you, but the real numbers of sales and rentals in the area?
And then, transition with:
🗓️ “Would it be helpful if we book a little bit more time together on a Zoom where you will be able to see the statistics and numbers that we use as professionals to select the best investment option for our clients?”
✅ This small shift in positioning changes everything:
You lead the conversation.
You become the trusted advisor.
You move from chasing to qualifying.
These are just a few questions from my full off-plan sales script,
 which helps agents book serious Zoom calls — not just “option checkers.”
