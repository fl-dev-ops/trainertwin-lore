As a real estate sales trainer, I often teach experienced sales agents communication skills, objection isolation techniques, and how to leverage networking opportunities to their fullest potential. However, I've noticed that some agents respond to my training by saying they already know these skills, yet they still struggle to close deals. The reality is that they aren't using these skills in their day-to-day work.

In my experience, the single greatest factor that separates successful individuals, from those who are struggling, is execution. Successful people don't do anything differently - they simply execute techniques consistently better than others do.

The most successful real estate agents I know started from the same place as everyone else - at the bottom. But they were persistent in executing their cold calling script, even if they struggled with it in the beginning. They learned from every call, took note of what worked and what didn't, and kept going until they got better.

As Jordan Belfort said in The Wolf of Wall Street, "The only thing standing between you, and your goal, is the bullshit story you keep telling yourself as to why you can't achieve it."

It's time to take a step back and ask yourself: "Am I consistently executing the techniques that I have learned?"
