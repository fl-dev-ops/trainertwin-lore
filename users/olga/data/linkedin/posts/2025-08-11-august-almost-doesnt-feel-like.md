☀️ August almost doesn’t feel like the time to talk about business.


It’s the time when, perhaps, you’re not making money — you’re spending it.
And that depends entirely on how you’ve worked for the past year.

For me, August isn’t the eighth month of the year.
It’s my personal December.
The second year in a row, I use it to wrap up my year and map out the next one.

Why? Because Dubai real estate is cyclical — and August gives you the perfect pause to take stock.

This year it’s especially obvious. Business slows down, people disappear to places where you can sip cocktails in the middle of the day without melting. 🍹

When I was a real estate agent — and later a sales manager — I couldn’t take a proper holiday. Not when your phone has to be on 24/7 and every deal depends on how fast you reply.
But the moment I started earning enough in real estate, I allowed myself to step away.

This summer I spent x weeks away — Bali and Portugal — and next year I’m aiming for three.
Because building a business isn’t just about the deals you close — it’s about creating the life you want to live.
And what if the X00,000 AED you might not earn because of those weeks away actually buys you something more valuable — unforgettable memories you’ll never regret?
