How to Spot a “Not Serious” Client in 3 Seconds 
They do not answer immediately?
- Not serious.
They ask for a cheaper option?
- Not serious.
They say they are “just exploring”?
- Definitely not serious. 😅

Much easier than actually finding out what is going on in their head.
Now, a few more very useful sales tips for Dubai real estate agents.
Please follow carefully. 😇

Bad advice #1: Before you WhatsApp the lead, send an email first.
Especially if they are European or American.
- You need to be polite.
- Very polite.
 - Extremely polite.
Ideally, give them enough time to completely forget they ever clicked on the advertisement.

Because obviously, the more formal you are, the higher your conversion. 😂

Bad advice #2: If you call, speak REALLY fast.
You have approximately four seconds to say:
- Your first name.
- Your surname.
- Your company name.
- Where you got their number.
- Why you are calling.
And obviously (this is my Favorite) :

How are you today?

Then wonder why they immediately say:
Sorry, I’m not interested.

Bad advice #3: Keep looking for the perfect script for every client.
- US market?
Find a US script.
- UK client?
Find a UK script.
- Australian client?
Surely there must be a special Australian script somewhere. 😅

And every time something does not work, you look for another phrase.
- Another opener.
- Another objection handler.
- Another follow-up message.
But the problem is usually not that you have not found the perfect line yet.
The problem is that you still do not know how to lead the conversation when the client says something unexpected.

Bad advice #4: If they say they are not interested, tell them they just wasted your advertising money.
And no, I am not exaggerating.
One of the agents in a recent coaching session told me this is exactly what he says to clients.
The client says:
We’re not interested anymore.
And his response is basically:
Then you shouldn’t have clicked on the advertisement. Ads cost money and you just wasted it.
👏 Congratulations.
You won the argument.
And lost the client.

Then comes the next thought:
I need more leads.

But sometimes the problem is not that you need more leads.
It is what happens when you finally get one.
If several agents in the same team keep losing leads at the same stage, depending on scripts and labelling clients as “not serious”, the issue may not be lead quality.

It may be the way conversations are being handled.
The goal is not to memorise every possible answer.

It is to understand how to lead the conversation.

And if any of these “sales tips” felt slightly too familiar 😂, share this post with someone who might urgently need them.
