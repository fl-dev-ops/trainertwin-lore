Do this...

If clients are not responding to your WhatsApp, consider that video or even audio messages are still underutilized in sales communication.

I got sick yesterday and set aside this time to catch up on all my business development activities.

I've sent 12 messages, 6 of which were video or audio.

The main trick here is not to use phrases like "I'm following up" or "checking in," as these can make you appear needy in the eyes of your clients. Instead, try phrasing it as "I'm just reaching out to you regarding XYZ. Have you found what you've been looking for, or what's the latest update?"

Give it a try! You'll likely receive a response in less than an hour!
