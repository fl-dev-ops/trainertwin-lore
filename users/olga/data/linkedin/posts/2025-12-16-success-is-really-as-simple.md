Success is really as simple as this: Stop trying to sell. Start trying to serve.

My best clients came when I stopped pitching and started asking better questions.

Instead of "Do you want to buy property?"
I ask "Are you potentially considering making money without paying tax?"

The language shift changes everything.

You're not selling. You're sharing opportunities. You're not pushing. You're partnering.

People buy from people who understand them, not from people who need them.

Like this post if you've experienced this shift.
