Sales isn't about harassing people until they say yes.

It's about creating an environment where they feel safe to say no.

I just coached an agent who was drowning in Dubai's toxic real estate culture.

Her company teaches agents to:
→ Steal leads from colleagues
→ Power-call prospects 10times a day
→ Pitch projects before understanding needs
→ "Always be closing" mentality

She was miserable. Making no sales. Ready to quit.

Then we changed one thing:

Instead of asking "What's holding you back from buying?"
She started asking "What would need to be true for this to make sense for you?"

The difference?

The first question triggers defense mechanisms.
The second invites honest conversation.

Within 3 weeks:
→ First qualified prospect in months
→ Building genuine relationships
→ Prospects calling HER back

Old school Dubai real estate is built on aggression.

New school is built on emotional intelligence.

Your prospects aren't hiding from good opportunities.

They're hiding from bad salespeople.

When you create psychological safety, people open up.
When people open up, they buy.

This isn't just about being "nice."
It's about being profitable.

The agents making real money in Dubai?
They're the ones prospects actually want to work with.
