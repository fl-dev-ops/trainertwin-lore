One Deal Away from Your Target? Let’s Talk Price Reduction.

It’s the end of the year, and you’re almost there—just one deal away from hitting your target. But here’s the problem: your sellers won’t lower their prices.

I hear this all the time from the agents I coach. Sellers treat price reduction like a competition—buyer vs. seller, and they’re determined to “win.”

 They see lowering the price as losing, and no one likes to lose. 
But here’s the thing: keeping the price high when the market says otherwise isn’t winning—it’s stalling.

So, how do you approach price reduction in a way that actually works? 

Here’s a simple three-step process that gets results:

1. Start with the right conversation.

From the moment you get the listing, set the tone. Sellers need to understand that buyers and the market determine the price, not the seller’s personal preferences or expectations. Be clear, honest, and firm. 
If this conversation doesn’t happen upfront, it’ll make everything harder later.

2. Keep sellers informed with weekly updates.
Regular communication is key. 

Whether it’s calls, emails, or market reports, make sure the seller knows what’s happening: the number of viewings, buyer feedback, and how their property compares to others in the area. When they see the reality week after week, they’re much more likely to adjust their expectations.

3. Deliver the final push.

This is the make-or-break moment. Use the data you’ve gathered to show the seller why reducing the price isn’t about losing—it’s about making their property stand out in the market and attracting the right buyers. Focus on how different his situation will be when instead of chaising one Buyer he will get a multiple offers...

Price reduction doesn’t have to be a fight—it’s a process. And when done right, it can turn a stagnant listing into a sold property.
