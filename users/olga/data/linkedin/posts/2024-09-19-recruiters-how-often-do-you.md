Recruiters, how often do you receive job requests that make you roll your eyes?"

I’ve been flooded with messages like “Help me get a job in Dubai!” or “I’ll take anything!” And I’m not even in recruiting. So, I can only imagine what your inbox must look like.

When candidates send these kinds of desperate, generic messages, are they hurting their chances? From where I stand, these requests often feel like they’re saying, “I’m not prepared, and I don’t know what I really want.” But maybe I’m missing something.

So, recruiters, I’d love to hear from you: What should candidates be doing to stand out? And just as importantly, what should they stop doing? Both tips and pet peeves are appreciated! Let’s help people get it right.
