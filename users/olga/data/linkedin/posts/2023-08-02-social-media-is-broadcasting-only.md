Social media is broadcasting only the wins of real estate agents.

We are all guilty of that, we have our red, yellow, and green zones in terms of the parts of the life we show on social media.

Red Zone - This may be everything related to your personal life, your family, spouse or your single life if that is so

Yellow Zone - What are your fears when you are receiving ‘no’ from your clients, or when you are not wearing makeup or dressed to impress

Green Zone - Your wins, your MRS or MR Perfect, when you’re always put together and know what to say
