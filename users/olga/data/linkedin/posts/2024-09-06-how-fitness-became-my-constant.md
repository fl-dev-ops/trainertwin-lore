How Fitness Became My Constant Journey

If I could talk to that young girl I used to be, sitting in a small town, staring at Britney Spears' flat stomach and thinking I’d never have one like that, I’d tell her one thing: anything is possible—even if you’re starting at 36.

When I first moved to Dubai, I threw myself into work, invested in an MBA, and hit the gym hard. After months of sweat and dedication, I finally got that elusive six-pack. But more importantly, I learned that fitness isn’t just about the body—it’s about building the mental toughness that spills into everything else you do.

But here’s the thing about life—it’s like juggling: sometimes you drop a ball. For me, that ball was fitness, and as I focused on my business this past year, it rolled away a bit. And believe me, I felt it. My energy, my focus, even my mood—they all took a hit.

Now, it’s time to pick that ball back up. I’m ready to get back to where I was, to regain that balance. Fitness has always been a journey for me, not a destination. 

And this time, I’m inviting you to join me. 
I’ll be sharing more about this new chapter on my Instagram. If you’re curious to see how I balance business with getting back into shape—or if you just want to cheer me on—come join me there.
