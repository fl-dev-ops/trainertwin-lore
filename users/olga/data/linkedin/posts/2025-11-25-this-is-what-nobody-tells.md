This is what nobody tells you about your first 2 years in Dubai Real Estate.

The first two years are going to be hell. Complete hell.
And It was for me - when I landed in 2014 and restarted my career in Dubai from scratch - becoming an Agent again...After being Head of Sales and Business owner.

You'll question everything. Your skills, your choices, your sanity.

But here's what separates winners from quitters: they don't depend on their emotional state to stick to the plan.

Even when you feel like absolute shit - you execute.

Your feelings will lie to you. Your emotions will sabotage you. Your comfort zone will trap you.

The plan doesn't care how you feel. The plan works if you work it.

Tag someone who needs to hear this reality check to KEEP GOING - Even if its feels impossible.
