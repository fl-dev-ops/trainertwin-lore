Your client is not interested. Now what? 👀

My first real estate clients did not want to talk to me either.

I started in Dubai real estate in 2015, right at the beginning of the previous buyer’s market, when prices had already started moving down.

And back then, lead generation looked very different.
Most business came through cold calling or referrals.
But when you are brand new in the market, you do not have referrals.

So you cold call. ☎️

That was basically your lead generation.

The luxury of social media leads, people messaging you on Instagram, or your company running marketing campaigns and sending you warm enquiries was simply not on the table.

So I learned sales by speaking to people who did NOT ask me to call them.

- People who were busy.
- People who were not interested.
- People who wanted to end the conversation as quickly as possible. 😅

And very quickly, I realised something.

Knowing the market and knowing the projects was not enough.

Before I could sell anything, I had to learn how to make someone actually WANT to continue the conversation with me.

Years later, agents come to me with almost the same problem, just in a different form.
“I don’t know how to follow up.”
“Clients disappear after Zoom.”
“I send options and hear nothing.”
“I don’t know how to qualify properly.”
“They are speaking to five other brokers.”
“Why should they choose me?”

Eleven years later, even with social media leads landing directly in your CRM, agents are still facing the same core sales problem.

You still need to know how to lead a conversation with someone who does not yet see a reason to keep talking to you.

How to create enough interest for the next step?
                How to keep them engaged?
                           How to understand what actually matters to them.
And how to stop relying on:

                                                  Hopefully this call went well.
That is a skill.
And it can be learned.
I know because I had to learn it myself.

And later, I had to learn how to teach it to other people.

If every new client still feels like a new experiment, despite all the scripts, follow-ups and sales techniques you have tried, the problem may not be what you write after the call.
It may be that there is no clear system for what should happen before the client gets to that point.
