Why do we ask questions during our sales process? 

Why even if you think you know the client's answer - because you are smart and you understood it from your experience, you still need to ask the questions?
The answer to this question is deeper than many salespeople realize.

This is the concept I was clarifying again in today's training for real estate agents in Ray White Dubai.

The strength of your questions doesn't lie only in uncovering details to qualify the buyer, his budget, location etc.

We ask questions to uncover the current problem/needs and make the client tell HIMSELF first of all why solving this problem is important for him now.

All simple - he believes what he is telling himself way more than any salesperson.

For Example:

'And help me understand why you decided to invest in Dubai rather than investing in your home country?'

This question might sound unusual, and the majority of sales property agents will never ask it, but think for a second about what the client will give you as an answer ...

 Probably the problems he has with his own country with the way the economy is with the way the currency is there. He's telling you, but most importantly, he's telling himself.

Think about what you want to receive as an answer from your client. What do you want your client to tell you instead of you telling this to the client yourself... and put it in a question?

Here we go. 
You went from trying to pitch and push your solution down the throat to the client selling himself, and you're just helping him solve his problem.

Do you see how it can change your sales process?
