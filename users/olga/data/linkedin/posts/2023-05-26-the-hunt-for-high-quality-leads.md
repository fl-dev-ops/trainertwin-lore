The Hunt for High-Quality Leads: Debunking the Stigma

I keep hearing it almost in every conversation I have with agents during my training sessions...
What, in your opinion, will improve your sales performance?

Olga - we need quality leads!

Do you often find yourself frustrated with the leads provided by your company? 
It's a common challenge faced by agents, encountering leads that lack seriousness and necessary budgets and seem to be mere window shoppers. 
But let's challenge the status quo and reshape our perspective!

There's a prevalent notion that somewhere out there lies a company with a secret stash of high-quality leads. 
However, I propose a different outlook. 
In truth, all companies in Dubai have leads of SIMILAR quality, but what is different is the Set of skills that agents are actively using.
 🎯 What sets the most successful agents apart is their ability to leverage their knowledge and perfect communication skills, transforming these leads into golden opportunities.

Let's consider leads sourced from social media. While they may not convert overnight, don't underestimate their potential! 
With the right mindset, a strategic follow-up system, and exceptional communication skills, these leads can convert into deals in a matter of weeks, months, or even a year. 
The key lies in nurturing them and tailoring your approach and follow-up system.


So, dear agents, it's time to redefine success. 

Instead of solely relying on external factors, let's focus on what we can control. Your active participation, unwavering dedication, and remarkable skill set will set you apart in this competitive market.

At the end of the day, it's not What you Say it's HOW you Say that!
