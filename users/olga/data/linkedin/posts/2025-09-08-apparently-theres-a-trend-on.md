Apparently, there’s a trend on LinkedIn: “Ask ChatGPT to roast your profile picture.”
So I gave it a try.

This is already the 10th roast ChatGPT gave me… and honestly, I expected something way more brutal.

Here’s what it came up with:
	•	The hair says: “45 and still making it work.”
	•	The face says: “I’m smiling politely, but deep inside I’m already rewriting your sales pitch — and yes, I trained the muscles to stay calm without Botox, just by listening to the endless excuses agents give about why they can’t close deals.”
	•	The blazer? “I couldn’t decide on corporate colors, so I mixed turquoise and red and convinced myself it’s branding.”
	•	The skirt? Clearly “Dubai Sexy Recruiter.” Couldn’t decide if the statement should be top or bottom… so I went all-in on the bottom.
	•	And the vibe? “Like every CEO who calls me for sales training, swearing the problem is his agents — while I can already tell he’s the real headache in his own company.”

🔥 In short: I look like the first person who actually needs a sales training — but somehow still ends up running the whole training academy.

And honestly? I think I could roast myself better.
