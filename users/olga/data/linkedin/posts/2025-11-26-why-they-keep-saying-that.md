Why they keep saying that Cold Calling does not work anymore...

The biggest mistake I see new agents make? They try to close on the first call.

You will NEVER sell anything meaningful on a cold call. Never.

Your job isn't to sell. Your job is to move them to the next step.

The goal is simple: "Would you be interested in a 15-minute zoom to see if this makes sense for you?"

Not "Do you want to invest 2 million in Dubai real estate - I have this pre-launch offer Hurry up!"

People need to know you, like you, and trust you before they'll even consider your offer.

Stop trying to sprint a marathon.
