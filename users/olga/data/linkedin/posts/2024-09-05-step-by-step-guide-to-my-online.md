Benefits of the Online Academy:
The Flexibility of a Desctop Version + Mobile APP:
Agents can learn at their own pace, whenever it suits them. They can revisit scripts, lectures, and materials anytime, even while stuck in traffic or during short breaks.
Improved Knowledge ‘Retention’:
Simply attending a lecture helps retain only about 10% of the information. By revisiting content, agents can catch details they might have missed before, much like re-reading a book reveals new insights.
Consistency in Training:
New hires can quickly get up to speed with the same materials that seasoned agents use. This ensures everyone is aligned and up to date, regardless of their start date.
Supplement to Offline Training:
The online academy supports continuous learning, but it doesn’t replace the value of in-person sessions where agents can ask questions and dive into more technical strategies.
