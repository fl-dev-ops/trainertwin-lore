“How do I stop the client from working with other agents?”
 I hear this question in almost every training.
And my honest answer usually starts like this:
 Can you stop your girlfriend from cheating?
 No?
 Exactly.
You can’t force loyalty.
 Not in relationships — and definitely not in real estate.
 Especially when the client doesn’t owe you anything (yet).
They will always choose what feels better, easier, more trustworthy — for them.
So the real question is:
 “How do I become the one they choose — over and over again?”
 The one they trust.
 The one they believe gets them.
 The one who’s not just selling, but actually helping them decide.
This is what I teach.
 Not manipulation. Not pressure.
 Just psychology, positioning, and trust-building done right.
