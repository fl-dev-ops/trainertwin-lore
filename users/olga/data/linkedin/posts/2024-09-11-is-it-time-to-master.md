Is It Time to Master Online Sales?

"I just can’t close deals online as effectively as I do in person. When I’m face-to-face with clients, it’s easier to build trust. Online, it’s just not the same." I often hear agents say that.

I get it—I used to feel the same way.

But here’s the real question: where will your income be in five years if you keep telling yourself you can’t sell online? The reality is clear—more and more real estate transactions, including secondary market sales, are shifting to online platforms. So, how confident are you in front of a camera? How effectively are you using digital tools to showcase your properties?
Imagine this: you’ve just closed a deal on a one-bedroom apartment in Dubai worth 1.5 million dirhams. Your commission? A solid 6,000 dirhams. But what if I told you that, with the same effort, you could have earned 24,000 dirhams? Sounds too good to be true? Well, it's not—if you know where to focus your efforts.
Selling online requires more than just mastering technology. It demands an understanding of your clients’ psychology. Reading emotions and identifying true desires through a screen can be challenging. That’s why in my courses, I don’t just teach you how to use the latest tools and platforms, but also how to ask the right questions that lead to honest, meaningful conversations.

Now, I’d love to hear from you:
What is the main challenge of selling online? 

Share your experience in the comments, and let’s discuss how we can overcome these obstacles together.
