Whom do you think you are? And why should I listen to you?



Didn't you ask yourself this question honestly, scrolling through the posts on LinkedIn?

I did! And here's the thing that feeling doesn't come from each and every post. If we go a deeper level, you will understand that everyone who is self-promoting is eventually creating exactly this reaction.

Now turn it around.

You're posting. Videos, stories, articles. You show up, you say something, you are consistent…

And then nothing. 


Two likes. No comments. No DMs. 

Definitely no leads. 

You do it again next week, and the week after, and at some point you start thinking: maybe this whole content thing just doesn't work for me. 

Maybe it works for other people. Maybe I'm not that type.

Before you quit one thing to check.

You might not have a content problem. You might have a self-promotion problem.

Look at what you've been publishing. If it sounds like "hi, I've been in Dubai for four years, here's me flying business class, here's me closing multiple deals" - that is exactly what creates the "who do you think you are" feeling in your reader. You've given them two options: believe your claim about yourself, or scroll.

Most people scroll. Not because they don't like you. Because nothing was offered to them. Something was only claimed about you.

Self-promotion is the asks for attention. 

So what you Should do instead?

Idea promotion!

It creates value the only thing that people would actually like and share.

This occurred to me recently from my own “whom do you think you are….” behavior after reading post with first class flight pictures.

How is my point of view different from others? And how can I prove it?

If you can't answer both, you already know why it's quiet. And it's not the algorithm.

Don't stop posting. Change what you're posting about.

Comment below if you feel? Or I’m the only one who feels that way?

Ps. And I’m not even talking about ai generated content here
