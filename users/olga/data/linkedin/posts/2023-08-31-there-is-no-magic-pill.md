There is no "magic pill" to make someone have the desire to buy. 
 
There's a process through which you can guide someone. Help them identify the problems they face, discover the solutions available, understand the reasons behind their desire, and realise the tangible/emotional consequences of solving those problems.
 
Allow them to experience the feeling of pain they are in a bit more, and then empower them to decide how to solve the problem.
 
We buy through emotion and then justify it with logic (Zig Ziglar), even if it's based purely on a numbers game.
 
If you could simply send an ideal Excel calculation or a formally structured Word document, then we would not need salespeople. 
 
We would probably be limited to sending emails with perfectly logical solutions.
 
So, should you keep calling your client or just message them?
