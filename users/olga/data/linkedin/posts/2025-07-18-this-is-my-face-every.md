This is my face every time someone asks:



“But where’s the guarantee I’ll close my first deal this month?”

⸻

Article: “New office, same results?”

Let’s talk about something that shows up a lot in the first year of real estate:

Switching companies.
Again. And again.
Looking for better leads.
More flexible schedules.
A stronger brand.
More support.
A nicer office.
More “ready” clients.

But here’s the question no one’s asking:

What if it’s not about the company?
What if it’s about the actions you haven’t taken yet?

Before you walk into another interview,
before you scroll another job post,
before you decide “maybe I just need a better platform”…

Try this:

Pick up the phone.
Call every old lead.
Message people you’ve worked with in the past.
Reach out for referrals.
And if you don’t have that list — start cold.

Or simply spend that time doing the one thing most agents avoid:
working on your sales skills.
Sharpening your cold call structure.
Practicing objections.
Training your pitch.

Exactly the kind of work I break down every week on my YouTube channel — https://lnkd.in/dcUzHt3c

⸻

And here’s the thought I’ll leave you with:

Are you chasing change…
or are you training for the result you say you want?
