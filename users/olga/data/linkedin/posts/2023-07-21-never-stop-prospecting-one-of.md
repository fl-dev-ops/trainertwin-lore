Never stop prospecting. 

One of my trainees asked me last week - what do I think of “luck” as a component for real estate success?

It may look like luck for someone who has just joined the real estate sales force… BUT…

As simple as it sounds - the foundation of successful real estate agents, in such a highly changeable market as Dubai, is prospecting. I really mean that - even if your pipeline is full now and you are focusing on closing deals, don’t stop. 

Over the years, I have evaluated the strategies of my successful colleagues and prospecting has been a key component in each of their successful careers.
