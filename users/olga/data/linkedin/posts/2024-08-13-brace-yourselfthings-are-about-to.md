Brace yourself—things are about to get squeaky clean! 🧼

Moving into a new place? Some say it’s as stressful as surviving a fire—honestly, they’re not wrong! But as much as I might complain, I could never leave JLT. 

It’s home, and I’m a JLT girl through and through! 🏙️

As I get settled, here’s what I’ve picked up along the way:
AC: Clean those coils and ducts! Fresh air is a must, especially after realizing your vents have collected half the desert. I went with a company that has a 4.8 Google rating—right up there with my own score! 😉 Breathing easier already.
Bathrooms: Once DIWA is connected, do yourself a favour and check for leaks. An unplanned indoor pool is not the kind of luxury you want on move-in day. Trust me, prevention is worth every minute.

Floors: Those floors can be sneakily filthy. I went all in on cleaning (or let’s be real—hire a pro). Your feet and sanity will thank you later.

Moving is chaotic, sure, but with a few tricks up your sleeve, it doesn’t have to feel like a total disaster. 

Speaking of which, have you experienced a rent increase already?
