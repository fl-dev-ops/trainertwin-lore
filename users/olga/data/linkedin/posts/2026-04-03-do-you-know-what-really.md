Do you know what really happened this March?
It is not only the war, the energy crisis, the sounds of drones and jets, or the real estate slowdown...
The hardest part is that we all LOST OUR FUTURE…

And this kind of loss is real. It feels like grief.
First comes denial. Then anger, bargaining, depression, and finally acceptance.
Before you dismiss this as nonsense, let me explain.
I did not read this in a book. I came to this idea through the conversations I have had over the past three weeks. And every time I explained it, people felt lighter. Not necessarily positive, but able to act.

And that is the goal. Not fake positivity. 
The goal is to get back into a productive state and start seeing your competitive advantage again.

So you are still reading - Lets dive in.
You and I were ‘Already Living in that Future”... there we had everything we wanted, the money, the attention, the wins.
If we look back, we have been doing the work, cutting vacations, restricting our choices, getting really uncomfortable - Because we knew WE will be rewarded in the end.
And now that future has fallen apart, it feels like everything you worked for was wasted.
I know that feeling very well...
At the end of January, I submitted my resignation, and 28 February was my last day as Head of Learning & Development. I knew one thing - my own direction had to change.
My Live It Up - Real Estate Training Academy  needed my full attention. 
A lot of people would look at that decision and say it was the worst possible timing - You left a stable role with salary…
Now the market is harder, agencies come under pressure, no one will even think of spending money for training …
I see it differently.
This is not the first difficult cycle I have lived through in real estate. I have seen what happens when confidence disappears, old strategies stop working, and the market tests people.
