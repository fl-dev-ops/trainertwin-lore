Specifics of Calling Clients from Different Countries
When working with international leads, you need to understand that communication habits are different in every market. The platform that works well in Dubai may not work the same way with clients from other countries.
USA Clients
Clients from the USA usually do not use WhatsApp as their main communication channel.
They mostly use normal SMS free and are usually included in their mobile plans. WhatsApp may work only in specific cases, for example if they have family abroad or they already communicate internationally.
What can work better:
* normal SMS;
* iMessage;
* FaceTime calls;
* regular mobile calls.


Europe Clients
European clients often do not pick up calls from Dubai numbers or unknown international numbers (not used to receiving calls from foreign numbers)
If you work with leads from a specific European country, it is better to have a SIM card or eSIM from that country. This can increase the chances that the client will answer the call.
What can work better:
* local SIM or eSIM from the same country;
* WhatsApp messages;
* WhatsApp calls;
Russia Clients
For Russian clients, WhatsApp and Telegram may not work reliably for calls.
There is a local platform called Max, but to use it properly you usually need to have a Russian number.
What can work:
* regular mobile calls;
* using a Russian number if available.


Ukraine Clients
Ukraine is currently at war, so there are a few important specifics to understand.
Official money transfers abroad from Ukrainian bank accounts can be very limited, and 99% of men have travel restrictions due to military obligations.
A Ukrainian phone number does not automatically mean that the client is physically in Ukraine.
WhatsApp and Telegram are widely used, so you can contact them through:
* WhatsApp messages;
* WhatsApp calls;
* Telegram messages;
* Telegram calls;
* regular mobile calls
The key question is where the client is currently located, especially if the client is male.
If he can travel in and out of Ukraine, he may have special permission or status, which can also indicate stronger financial capacity and better client qualification.
