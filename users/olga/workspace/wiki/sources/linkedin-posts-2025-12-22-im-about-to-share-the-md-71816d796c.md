### follow-up strategy · advice

Winners follow up with purpose: • Immediate: "Thanks for the call" • 1 week: Relevant market update • 1 month: Success story from similar client • 3 months: "How's the baby/project/situation you mentioned?" • 6 months: New opportunity that fits their timeline.

> Most people follow up once. Maybe twice. Then they give up.
