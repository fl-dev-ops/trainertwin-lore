> “In 20 years, you would give everything to be where you are right now. At this age, in this body, living this exact life.”
> 
> Jimmy Carr shared this thought on The Diary of a CEO, and it genuinely stayed with me.
> 
> It’s when you say it like this… it hits.
> 
> You might miss your current age, your health, your body, the people around you and even the problems that now feel so important.
> 
> It immediately brings you back to the present.
> 
> But then I decided to reverse the question.
> 
> How much would I pay to return to my life 20 years ago, when I was 26?
> 
> Or even 10 years ago, when I was 36?
> 
> Honestly? Nothing.
> 
> Not because those years were bad. They were necessary. They shaped me, taught me and brought me here.
> 
> But I do not want my old life back.
> 
> I want this version of my life.
