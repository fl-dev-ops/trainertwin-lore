## Property Buying Strategy

**Product:** knowledge · **Type:** method · **Source support:** not_reviewed
**Publication:** 2026-05-07 · **Author:** olgasi · **Format:** transcript

### Summary

- To successfully buy 'distressed deals' in Dubai, one should not act like a casual buyer but like a 'distress deal hunter,' focusing on preparation and emotional control.
  - “if you're hoping for thirty or even forty percent discount for a really good property, you cannot behave like a casual buyer. You are a distress deal hunter. Your weapon is preparation, your advantage is emotional control.” — [lines 176-176](../../../data/youtube/video/2026-05-07-why-most-distressed-deals-in-dubai-are-fake-05sBEJV5Zho.md) (`youtube-video-2026-05-07-why-most-distressed-deals-in-dubai-are-fake-05sbejv5zho-md-5ae767c007:u000034`)

### Goal

- To obtain a significant discount on good properties by taking advantage of distressed deals in Dubai.
  - “if you're hoping for thirty or even forty percent discount for a really good property, you cannot behave like a casual buyer. You are a distress deal hunter.” — [lines 176-176](../../../data/youtube/video/2026-05-07-why-most-distressed-deals-in-dubai-are-fake-05sBEJV5Zho.md) (`youtube-video-2026-05-07-why-most-distressed-deals-in-dubai-are-fake-05sbejv5zho-md-5ae767c007:u000034`)

### Prerequisites

- Understand the real estate market by doing proper research using property tracking systems to compare past sales and rental transactions.
  - “Do your search, use property finder over you to both have an easy system to track and compare past sales and rental transactions.” — [lines 152-152](../../../data/youtube/video/2026-05-07-why-most-distressed-deals-in-dubai-are-fake-05sBEJV5Zho.md) (`youtube-video-2026-05-07-why-most-distressed-deals-in-dubai-are-fake-05sbejv5zho-md-5ae767c007:u000028`)

### Steps

1. Shortlist your project based on unit type, square footage, developer, layout, and budget.
  - “based on unit type, square footage, developer, layout, budget. Do your search, use property finder over you to both have an easy system to track and compare past sales and rental transactions.” — [lines 152-152](../../../data/youtube/video/2026-05-07-why-most-distressed-deals-in-dubai-are-fake-05sBEJV5Zho.md) (`youtube-video-2026-05-07-why-most-distressed-deals-in-dubai-are-fake-05sbejv5zho-md-5ae767c007:u000028`)

2. Check the building reality and potential issues by reading tenants' and owners' reviews, not just brochures.
  - “Before you fall in love with a discount, check the building reality. You need to understand tenants, complainants, owners, comments, maintenance issue, maybe it has an AC problem, noise, or traffic.” — [lines 156-156](../../../data/youtube/video/2026-05-07-why-most-distressed-deals-in-dubai-are-fake-05sBEJV5Zho.md) (`youtube-video-2026-05-07-why-most-distressed-deals-in-dubai-are-fake-05sbejv5zho-md-5ae767c007:u000029`)

3. Avoid using common negotiation methods like WhatsApp offers; instead, use a real check made payable to the owner.
  - “If you want to buy a distress deal, you must act differently. Not with the WhatsApp, but with the real check made payable to the owner.” — [lines 164-164](../../../data/youtube/video/2026-05-07-why-most-distressed-deals-in-dubai-are-fake-05sBEJV5Zho.md) (`youtube-video-2026-05-07-why-most-distressed-deals-in-dubai-are-fake-05sbejv5zho-md-5ae767c007:u000031`)

4. Prepare early by shortlisting options, checking building conditions, and understanding the real market value.
  - “the best opportunities will not go to people who start searching at the last moment. They will go to buyers who prepare early, shortlisted the options, checked their buildings, and understood the real market value.” — [lines 168-168](../../../data/youtube/video/2026-05-07-why-most-distressed-deals-in-dubai-are-fake-05sBEJV5Zho.md) (`youtube-video-2026-05-07-why-most-distressed-deals-in-dubai-are-fake-05sbejv5zho-md-5ae767c007:u000032`)

5. Negotiate on multiple units simultaneously, stay emotionally detached, and act quickly when a suitable deal arises.
  - “Start negotiating multiple units, stay emotionally detached and move fast when the right deal comes.” — [lines 172-172](../../../data/youtube/video/2026-05-07-why-most-distressed-deals-in-dubai-are-fake-05sBEJV5Zho.md) (`youtube-video-2026-05-07-why-most-distressed-deals-in-dubai-are-fake-05sbejv5zho-md-5ae767c007:u000033`)
