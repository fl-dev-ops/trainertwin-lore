### Original context

**authored text by unknown author** · lines 14-31 · authored_text

> ## Caption
> “In 20 years, you would give everything to be where you are right now. At this age, in this body, living this exact life.”
> 
> Jimmy Carr said this on The Diary of a CEO, and the thought genuinely worked. It immediately brought me back to the present.
> 
> But then I reversed the question.
> 
> How much would I pay to go back to being 26? Or even 36?
> 
> Honestly? Nothing.
> 
> I don’t want my old life back. I want this version of it. My people, my health, my body, my business, where I live and everything I have built.
> 
> Maybe in 66… omg that’s a very scary number now… but I want to build every day … to arrive there feeling that’s it’s the best time… not regretting my 40s.
> 
> Would you go back 20 years if you could?
>
