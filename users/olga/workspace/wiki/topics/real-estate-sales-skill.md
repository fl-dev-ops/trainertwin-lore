## changing client communication

**Product:** knowledge · **Type:** self_report · **Source support:** not_reviewed
**Publication:** 2026-04-20T04:45:01.883000Z · **Author:** olgasi · **Format:** post

### Summary

- When I changed the way I spoke to clients, my script, confidence, and sales improved.
  - “when I changed the way I spoke to clients, everything started to change. My script changed. My confidence changed. My sales changed.” — [lines 10-63 part 2](../../../data/linkedin/posts/2026-04-20-i-almost-gave-up-on.md) (`linkedin-posts-2026-04-20-i-almost-gave-up-on-md-65c086757d:u000002`)

### Original context

**authored text by olgasi** · lines 10-63 · authored_text

> I couldn’t not “convince “ - Clients were not listening to me!
>  
> And I was too - proud- I’ve seen others begging, chasing, being desperate 
> 
> I could not beg… too proud.
> 
> 
> So I had two choices. 
> 
> Quit, buy a ticket, and go back home. 
> 
> Or stay and figure out a different way…
> 
> I stayed.
> 
> It did not happen overnight. But when I changed the way I spoke to clients, everything started to change. My script changed. My confidence changed. My sales changed.
> 
> That is why I still believe this today:
> 
> In a difficult market, your ability to influence people on the phone can change everything.
> 
> Not through pressure.
> Not through pushing or beginning 
> But through the kind of conversation that builds trust and helps people move forward.
> 
> This is exactly what I teach in my Academy and in my private
