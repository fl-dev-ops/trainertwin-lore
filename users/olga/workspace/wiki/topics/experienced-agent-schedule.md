Not something you fit, something that you all week is build around.” — [lines 108-108](../../../data/youtube/video/2026-08-30-time-management-for-real-estate-agents-hour-by-hour-schedule-LT8S6d8aeYY.md) (`youtube-video-2026-08-30-time-management-for-real-estate-agents-hour-by-hour-schedule-e4701583b6:u000018`)

### Steps

1. Dedicate two daily time blocks to dialing prospective clients.
  - “It's two blocks a day, forty-five minutes each. Eight blocks in your week.” — [lines 116-116](../../../data/youtube/video/2026-08-30-time-management-for-real-estate-agents-hour-by-hour-schedule-LT8S6d8aeYY.md) (`youtube-video-2026-08-30-time-management-for-real-estate-agents-hour-by-hour-schedule-e4701583b6:u000020`)

2. Place calls before any other task in the calendar.
  - “Two blocks every morning. And they're going into the empty calendar before anything else exists.” — [lines 128-128](../../../data/youtube/video/2026-08-30-time-management-for-real-estate-agents-hour-by-hour-schedule-LT8S6d8aeYY.md) (`youtube-video-2026-08-30-time-management-for-real-estate-agents-hour-by-hour-schedule-e4701583b6:u000023`)

3. Group client meetings in the afternoon or evening and focus on content review on Wednesday afternoons.
  - “Wednesday afternoon is the only block in this week that isn't a conversation. It's a content and one hour listening back to your own calls.” — [lines 132-132](../../../data/youtube/video/2026-08-30-time-management-for-real-estate-agents-hour-by-hour-schedule-LT8S6d8aeYY.md) (`youtube-video-2026-08-30-time-management-for-real-estate-agents-hour-by-hour-schedule-e4701583b6:u000024`)
