## Flipping properties

**Product:** knowledge · **Type:** method · **Source support:** not_reviewed
**Publication:** 2026-06-24 · **Author:** olgasi · **Format:** transcript

### Summary

- In Dubai, flipping involves buying, renovating, and selling older properties for profit. This differs from buying off-plan and reselling before completion.
  - “buy an older property, you renovate it, you rebuild it, and then you sell it with profit.” — [lines 102-102](../../../data/youtube/video/2026-06-24-the-dubai-areas-that-will-rise-over-the-next-10-years-k4mivSZgOpA.md) (`youtube-video-2026-06-24-the-dubai-areas-that-will-rise-over-the-next-10-years-k4mivs-55e5a76b49:u000016`)
