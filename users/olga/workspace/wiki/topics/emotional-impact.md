## Stages of Grief Analogy

**Product:** knowledge · **Type:** claim · **Source support:** not_reviewed
**Publication:** 2026-04-03T04:15:01.770000Z · **Author:** olgasi · **Format:** post

### Summary

- The loss of our future feels like grief and follows stages similar to the stages of grief: denial, anger, bargaining, depression, and acceptance.
  - “The hardest part is that we all LOST OUR FUTURE… And this kind of loss is real. It feels like grief. First comes denial. Then anger, bargaining, depression, and finally acceptance.” — [lines 10-43](../../../data/linkedin/posts/2026-04-03-do-you-know-what-really.md) (`linkedin-posts-2026-04-03-do-you-know-what-really-md-6f879e816b:u000001`)
