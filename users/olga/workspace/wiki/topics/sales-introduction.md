## Problem-Solving Introduction

**Product:** knowledge · **Type:** advice · **Source support:** not_reviewed
**Publication:** 2026-09-21T08:33:34.796000Z · **Author:** olgasi · **Format:** post

### Summary

- When introducing yourself, focus on the problem you solve rather than your title or qualifications.
  - “Which problem Do You solve?…. This is my first question to Anyone who writes me with “I’m a founder and we do ABC and EYZ” Really check - no one is interested what you Do ? People do not care … unless there is a benefit for them!” — [lines 10-39](../../../data/linkedin/posts/2026-09-21-which-problem-do-you-solve.md) (`linkedin-posts-2026-09-21-which-problem-do-you-solve-md-67b47efeb9:u000001`)

### Steps

1. Begin your introduction with the problem that your work solves.
  - “Which problem Do You solve?…. This is my first question to Anyone who writes me with “I’m a founder and we do ABC and EYZ”” — [lines 10-39](../../../data/linkedin/posts/2026-09-21-which-problem-do-you-solve.md) (`linkedin-posts-2026-09-21-which-problem-do-you-solve-md-67b47efeb9:u000001`)

2. Explain how you address specific challenges faced by your audience.
  - “So what I do I help agents by understanding the human behavior move the client from first call to the closing using my Off-plan closing system and Neuro emotional persuasion questions.” — [lines 10-39 part 2](../../../data/linkedin/posts/2026-09-21-which-problem-do-you-solve.md) (`linkedin-posts-2026-09-21-which-problem-do-you-solve-md-67b47efeb9:u000002`)
