**Publication:** 2026-04-08 · **Author:** olgasi · **Format:** transcript

### Situation

- A majority of agents are unable to convert leads when told 'no' by sellers unless they broaden their strategy beyond immediate selling.
  - “are you currently selling or trying to rent your property? And in ninety five percent of cases the person going to tell you no.” — [lines 799-799](../../../data/youtube/video/2026-04-08-full-sales-course-dubai-real-estate-2026-off-plan-secondary-_0b6EYyDuPQ.md) (`youtube-video-2026-04-08-full-sales-course-dubai-real-estate-2026-off-plan-secondary-e5e68c2962:u000186`)
