## Evaluating Property Investment

**Product:** knowledge · **Type:** advice · **Source support:** not_reviewed
**Publication:** 2026-09-10 · **Author:** olgasi · **Format:** transcript

### Summary

- Before signing anything, answer three questions: what creates the real demand, how many new identical units will be handed over, and who will buy or rent the property.
  - “But before you sign anything, answer three questions. First, what creates the real demand for this specific property? Not to buy, not the brochure. Second, how many new identical units going to be handed over within the year in the same district? Third, who buys it or rents it from you and why yours instead the one on the floor above?” — [lines 204-204](../../../data/youtube/video/2026-09-10-dubai-2008-vs-2026-record-prices-falling-rents-massive-suppl-fpjQdsJSLCM.md) (`youtube-video-2026-09-10-dubai-2008-vs-2026-record-prices-falling-rents-massive-suppl-4fdefd4cca:u000042`)
