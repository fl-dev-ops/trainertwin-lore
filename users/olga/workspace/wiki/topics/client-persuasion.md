### Goal

- To persuade clients who say "I'll wait" to act now.
  - “make those who are telling you wait and see to realize that waiting and seeing gonna cost them more” — [lines 87-87](../../../data/youtube/video/2026-04-29-dubai-agents-if-client-say-i-ll-wait-answer-this-before-you-jdVOWThjPM0.md) (`youtube-video-2026-04-29-dubai-agents-if-client-say-i-ll-wait-answer-this-before-you-4c0ad26e8a:u000015`)
