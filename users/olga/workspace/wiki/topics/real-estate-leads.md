## Leads from off-plan vs secondary markets

**Product:** knowledge · **Type:** claim · **Source support:** not_reviewed
**Publication:** 2026-04-08 · **Author:** olgasi · **Format:** transcript

### Summary

- Leads from off-plan property sales are considered less reliable than those from the secondary market because off-plan buyers are often not actively looking to purchase but are merely curious.
  - “is that the leads that the company is raising for you are not coming from any reliable sources. So that means for you that the conversion rate for you in secondary, for you in a plan, going to be different.
