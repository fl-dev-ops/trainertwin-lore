## Identifying Trustworthy Real Estate Agents

**Product:** knowledge · **Type:** method · **Source support:** not_reviewed
**Publication:** 2026-09-25 · **Author:** olgasi · **Format:** transcript

### Summary

- A method to qualify real estate agents involves asking specific questions about recent transactions, upcoming completions, service charges, and potential cons of the property.
  - “Four questions. Ask them and if the agent hesitates, you already have your answer about whether you should work with this person or no.” — [lines 125-125](../../../data/youtube/video/2026-09-25-what-changed-in-dubai-real-estate-in-2026-falling-resales-ha-3kBUFkV0O9k.md) (`youtube-video-2026-09-25-what-changed-in-dubai-real-estate-in-2026-falling-resales-ha-f4d1591cf2:u000023`)

### Goal

- To assess whether a real estate agent is knowledgeable and trustworthy.
  - “knowing how to qualify the agent in ten minutes.” — [lines 121-121](../../../data/youtube/video/2026-09-25-what-changed-in-dubai-real-estate-in-2026-falling-resales-ha-3kBUFkV0O9k.md) (`youtube-video-2026-09-25-what-changed-in-dubai-real-estate-in-2026-falling-resales-ha-f4d1591cf2:u000022`)

### Prerequisites

Not stated in the cited excerpt.

### Steps

1. Ask the agent to show the last three months of transactions in the building or comparable buildings around the area.
  - “Show me the last three months of transactions in this building. And if it's offline in comparable building around the area.” — [lines 129-129](../../../data/youtube/video/2026-09-25-what-changed-in-dubai-real-estate-in-2026-falling-resales-ha-3kBUFkV0O9k.md) (`youtube-video-2026-09-25-what-changed-in-dubai-real-estate-in-2026-falling-resales-ha-f4d1591cf2:u000024`)

2. Ask about other buildings completing in the same year to assess tenant and buyer options.
  - “what else completes around me in the same year? Because if three other towers handed over the same year, the same month, your tenant has options and your buyer has options.” — [lines 133-133](../../../data/youtube/video/2026-09-25-what-changed-in-dubai-real-estate-in-2026-falling-resales-ha-3kBUFkV0O9k.md) (`youtube-video-2026-09-25-what-changed-in-dubai-real-estate-in-2026-falling-resales-ha-f4d1591cf2:u000025`)

3. Inquire about service charges and compare them to buildings in the same area.
  - “what are the service charges here and what are the incomparable buildings in the same area?” — [lines 133-133](../../../data/youtube/video/2026-09-25-what-changed-in-dubai-real-estate-in-2026-falling-resales-ha-3kBUFkV0O9k.md) (`youtube-video-2026-09-25-what-changed-in-dubai-real-estate-in-2026-falling-resales-ha-f4d1591cf2:u000025`)

4. Ask about the cons of the building and potential issues with the developer or area.
  - “What are the cons of the building? What can go wrong with the developer or with the area?” — [lines 137-137](../../../data/youtube/video/2026-09-25-what-changed-in-dubai-real-estate-in-2026-falling-resales-ha-3kBUFkV0O9k.md) (`youtube-video-2026-09-25-what-changed-in-dubai-real-estate-in-2026-falling-resales-ha-f4d1591cf2:u000026`)

Not stated in the cited excerpt.

### Exceptions

- If an agent claims there are no cons, they may not know the product well or chose not to disclose information.
  - “if the agent says there is no cons, everything is great, leave. There is no such a thing.” — [lines 137-137](../../../data/youtube/video/2026-09-25-what-changed-in-dubai-real-estate-in-2026-falling-resales-ha-3kBUFkV0O9k.md) (`youtube-video-2026-09-25-what-changed-in-dubai-real-estate-in-2026-falling-resales-ha-f4d1591cf2:u000026`)
