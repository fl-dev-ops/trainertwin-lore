> ‘Already Living in that Future”... there we had everything we wanted, the money, the attention, the wins.
> If we look back, we have been doing the work, cutting vacations, restricting our choices, getting really uncomfortable - Because we knew WE will be rewarded in the end.
> And now that future has fallen apart, it feels like everything you worked for was wasted.
> I know that feeling very well...
> At the end of January, I submitted my resignation, and 28 February was my last day as Head of Learning &amp; Development. I knew one thing - my own direction had to change.
> My Live It Up - Real Estate Training Academy  needed my full attention. 
> A lot of people would look at that decision and say it was the worst possible timing - You left a stable role with salary…
> Now the market is harder, agencies come under pressure, no one will even think of spending money for training …
> I see it differently.
> This is not the first difficult cycle I have lived through in real estate. I have seen what happens when confidence disappears, old strategies stop working, and the market tests people.
> 2009 in Kiev, Ukraine - where I was a Head of sales of Real Estate Developer - we had a financial system collapse, accounts were frozen by the government for a whole year.
> 2018 in Dubai - When the market was slowing down, \(many do not know that as they started after 2020\) - where I came up with Rent-to-Own scheme and convinces small private developers in Al Furjan to run it.
> 
> 2020 August - when many were leaving Dubai - I opened my company - Live it up… The name is funny right - its even not about real estate
