> Survival is not created in panic. It's created in usefulness. So instead of asking myself what if everything falls apart, ask yourself, what problem can I solve today that somebody would actually pay for? Because in uncertain times useful people survive faster than emotional people. Of course, we all can be emotional. For you to survive, you don't have to be the smartest one. You just have to be the most useful.
