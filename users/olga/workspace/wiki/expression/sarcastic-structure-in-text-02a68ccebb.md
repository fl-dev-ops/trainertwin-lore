> advice #4: If they say they are not interested, tell them they just wasted your advertising money.
> And no, I am not exaggerating.
> One of the agents in a recent coaching session told me this is exactly what he says to clients.
> The client says:
> We’re not interested anymore.
> And his response is basically:
> Then you shouldn’t have clicked on the advertisement. Ads cost money and you just wasted it.
> 👏 Congratulations.
> You won the argument.
> And lost the client.
> 
> Then comes the next thought:
> I need more leads.
> 
> But sometimes the problem is not that you need more leads.
> It is what happens when you finally get one.
> If several agents in the same team keep losing leads at the same stage, depending on scripts and labelling clients as “not serious”, the issue may not be lead quality.
> 
> It may be the way conversations are being handled.
> The goal is not to memorise every possible answer.
> 
> It is to understand how to lead the conversation.
> 
> And if any of these “sales tips” felt slightly too familiar 😂, share this post with someone who might urgently need them.
