> How do you change your income, your life and yourself?
> 
> By the way !!! This is Exactly the main promise behind Tony Robbins and events.
> 
> After four days at Unleash the Power Within in Germany, I would describe his technology in one simple sentence:
> 
> You do not experience life.
> 
> You experience the life you focus on.
> 
> Here is what this actually means.
> 
> A client not answering your call is simply a fact.
> 
> “They have ghosted me. They will never buy” is not a fact.
> 
> It is the meaning your mind gives to the situation.
> 
> And because this explanation comes from your own mind, you immediately believe it.
> 
> But your mind can deceive you.
> 
> Its primary job is not to make you happy, successful or confident. Its primary job is to protect you and help you survive.
> 
> It is constantly looking for potential threats.
> 
> Thousands of years ago, that threat could have been a wild animal.
> 
> Today, it can be a client not answering your call, someone rejecting your offer, a difficult conversation or an uncertain result.
> 
> Your mind sees uncertainty and immediately creates the worst possible explanation to protect you from disappointment or danger.
> 
> Then you react not to what actually happened, but to the story your mind created about it.
> 
> That story changes your emotional state.
> 
> Your emotional state changes your actions.
> 
> And your actions eventually change your results, your income and your life.
> 
> This is why learning to control your focus and emotional state is not just positive thinking.
> 
> It is a practical life skill.
>
