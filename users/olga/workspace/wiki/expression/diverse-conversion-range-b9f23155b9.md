> 1% - That was the conversion rate from social media leads to a qualified meeting.
> Nobody in the room thought the process was the problem.
> I first heard this sales process in 2015 when I entered this market. Last month I heard it again, word for word, from a sales director I was interviewing for a corporate client. Ten years. Same process. Delivered with full confidence.
> In June my mentorship joined a client who is not your typical agent.  
> He co-owns a real estate company and runs his own social media marketing agency. His job is to generate leads for the same business he co-owns. 
> So when the leads do not convert, he is losing money twice.
> He came to me because something was not working. I asked him to describe the sales process. He did it in thirty seconds.
> Call the client. Find out what they want. Send the brochures. Follow up. Follow up again. Pray.
> Last was a Joke of course...
> Or the premium version: call the client, pitch the newest launch, invite them to the sales centre, push the expression of interest form.
> 
> I have been talking about this for years. 
> 
> And for a long time, agencies could survive it because there were enough leads and enough lucky closings to keep the numbers looking acceptable.
> 
> That is changing. Leads are more expensive. Clients are more cautious. The volume game is getting harder to play. And people are finally starting to ask whether the process is the problem.
> Most off-plan buyers today are not in Dubai. They are in Europe, the UK, the CIS, Asia. And even those who live here are not jumping in a taxi to a developer sales centre after a two-minute call from a stranger.
> 
> These are people making a six, seven, sometimes eight-figure decision in a market they may barely understand yet. 
> And the first thing they get is a PDF on WhatsApp.
> 99% of those conversations end there. The client goes quiet. The agent blames the lead.
> What actually works looks nothing like that. 
> 
> Multiple touchpoints. Video calls. Materials that help the client understand why Dubai, why now, why with you. You are not selling a unit. You are building enough trust for someone to wire a significant amount of money across a border.
> When the process is built around that reality, conversion from social media lead to qualified prospect reaches thirty-five percent or more.
