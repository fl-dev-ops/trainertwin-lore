**Product:** knowledge · **Type:** method · **Source support:** not_reviewed

### Summary

- Reframing the idea that the market has stopped into a recognition of market shifts and intelligent buying.
  - “when you're talking to an investor and you are selling an off-plan and this is an off-plan investor, you have to reframe the general idea that he will bring to the table.” — [lines 44-44](../../../data/youtube/video/2026-03-30-if-client-is-telling-you-i-m-hearing-alot-of-negative-new-ab-l2mO_ptlfk8.md) (`youtube-video-2026-03-30-if-client-is-telling-you-i-m-hearing-alot-of-negative-new-ab-14701f23af:u000004`)

### Goal

- To change the client's perception from believing the market has stopped to recognizing market shifts and intelligent buying.
  - “Yes, Mr. client, I know it may look like the market has stopped. But reality is, the market shifted. Now, people are buying very smart. Demand didn't disappear. Buyers became more selective.” — [lines 60-60](../../../data/youtube/video/2026-03-30-if-client-is-telling-you-i-m-hearing-alot-of-negative-new-ab-l2mO_ptlfk8.md) (`youtube-video-2026-03-30-if-client-is-telling-you-i-m-hearing-alot-of-negative-new-ab-14701f23af:u000008`)

  - “This idea is the market has stopped.” — [lines 44-44](../../../data/youtube/video/2026-03-30-if-client-is-telling-you-i-m-hearing-alot-of-negative-new-ab-l2mO_ptlfk8.md) (`youtube-video-2026-03-30-if-client-is-telling-you-i-m-hearing-alot-of-negative-new-ab-14701f23af:u000004`)

2. Reframe this belief by explaining that the market has shifted, not stopped.
