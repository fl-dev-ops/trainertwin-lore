### Summary

- Before investing, ask yourself six questions to guide your real estate investment decision-making.
  - “Before you buy, ask the six questions. Who is the tenant, buyer, or end user for this property? Why would they choose this location, not somewhere else? ### Steps

1. Determine the tenant, buyer, or end user for the property.
  - “Who is the tenant, buyer, or end user for this property?” — [lines 218-218](../../../data/youtube/video/2026-06-24-the-dubai-areas-that-will-rise-over-the-next-10-years-k4mivSZgOpA.md) (`youtube-video-2026-06-24-the-dubai-areas-that-will-rise-over-the-next-10-years-k4mivs-55e5a76b49:u000045`)

2. Analyze why they would choose this particular location over others.
  - “Why would they choose this location, not somewhere else?” — [lines 218-218](../../../data/youtube/video/2026-06-24-the-dubai-areas-that-will-rise-over-the-next-10-years-k4mivSZgOpA.md) (`youtube-video-2026-06-24-the-dubai-areas-that-will-rise-over-the-next-10-years-k4mivs-55e5a76b49:u000045`)

3. Identify the demand engine for the next five to ten years \(business, metro, airport, family lifestyle, waterfront, infrastructure shift\).
  - “What is the demand engine for the next five to ten years? Is it business? Is it metro, airport, family lifestyle, waterfront, the future infrastructure shift?” — [lines 218-218](../../../data/youtube/video/2026-06-24-the-dubai-areas-that-will-rise-over-the-next-10-years-k4mivSZgOpA.md) (`youtube-video-2026-06-24-the-dubai-areas-that-will-rise-over-the-next-10-years-k4mivs-55e5a76b49:u000045`)

4. Assess factors that can increase property prices and factors that can slow down your exit.
  - “What can push the price up, and what can slow down your exit?” — [lines 218-218](../../../data/youtube/video/2026-06-24-the-dubai-areas-that-will-rise-over-the-next-10-years-k4mivSZgOpA.md) (`youtube-video-2026-06-24-the-dubai-areas-that-will-rise-over-the-next-10-years-k4mivs-55e5a76b49:u000045`)

5. Consider the possibility and implications if the project is delayed.
  - “What happens if the project gets delayed.” — [lines 222-222](../../../data/youtube/video/2026-06-24-the-dubai-areas-that-will-rise-over-the-next-10-years-k4mivSZgOpA.md) (`youtube-video-2026-06-24-the-dubai-areas-that-will-rise-over-the-next-10-years-k4mivs-55e5a76b49:u000046`)
