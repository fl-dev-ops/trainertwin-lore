### Steps

1. Avoid asking standard opening questions such as 'how are you?' on the first call.
  - “Well, I'm not saying never ask how are you. Call two, three, ask about the wife, the kids, the dog, it works there. But on a call one,” — [lines 54-54](../../../data/youtube/video/2026-08-13-4-questions-that-kill-your-dubai-real-estate-deals-in-2026-pQCLpcXSx2s.md) (`youtube-video-2026-08-13-4-questions-that-kill-your-dubai-real-estate-deals-in-2026-p-56bc27347c:u000005`)

2. On the first call, avoid making assumptions about the client's intentions.
  - “You already assume that the client wants to buy.” — [lines 62-62](../../../data/youtube/video/2026-08-13-4-questions-that-kill-your-dubai-real-estate-deals-in-2026-pQCLpcXSx2s.md) (`youtube-video-2026-08-13-4-questions-that-kill-your-dubai-real-estate-deals-in-2026-p-56bc27347c:u000007`)
