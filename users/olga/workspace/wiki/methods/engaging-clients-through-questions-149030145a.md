## Engaging Clients through Questions

**Product:** knowledge · **Type:** method · **Source support:** not_reviewed

### Summary

- Use strategic questioning to create a knowledge gap that encourages clients to schedule meetings.
  - “the right question creates vacuum in the client's mind. Suddenly, he realizes that the thing you're asking about is exactly the thing he doesn't know.” — [lines 88-88](../../../data/youtube/video/2026-07-07-how-to-close-off-plan-secondary-deals-in-dubai-in-2026-KBY6lwvcm9E.md) (`youtube-video-2026-07-07-how-to-close-off-plan-secondary-deals-in-dubai-in-2026-kby6l-7b0008a9c7:u000014`)

### Goal

- Encourage clients to agree to a meeting.
  - “Would that make sense to book a time together on a Zoom so you can see with your own eyes and understand the process before you make any decisions?” — [lines 100-100](../../../data/youtube/video/2026-07-07-how-to-close-off-plan-secondary-deals-in-dubai-in-2026-KBY6lwvcm9E.md) (`youtube-video-2026-07-07-how-to-close-off-plan-secondary-deals-in-dubai-in-2026-kby6l-7b0008a9c7:u000017`)

### Prerequisites

- Clients must perceive a gap in their own understanding.
  - “you invite them to a Zoom, not through the let's go to the Zoom, through the questions.” — [lines 88-88](../../../data/youtube/video/2026-07-07-how-to-close-off-plan-secondary-deals-in-dubai-in-2026-KBY6lwvcm9E.md) (`youtube-video-2026-07-07-how-to-close-off-plan-secondary-deals-in-dubai-in-2026-kby6l-7b0008a9c7:u000014`)

### Steps

1. Identify a gap in the client's knowledge through strategic questions.
  - “the right question creates vacuum in the client's mind.” — [lines 88-88](../../../data/youtube/video/2026-07-07-how-to-close-off-plan-secondary-deals-in-dubai-in-2026-KBY6lwvcm9E.md) (`youtube-video-2026-07-07-how-to-close-off-plan-secondary-deals-in-dubai-in-2026-kby6l-7b0008a9c7:u000014`)

2. Propose a meeting to address these gaps.
  - “Would that make sense to book a time together on a Zoom so you can see with your own eyes and understand the process before you make any decisions?” — [lines 100-100](../../../data/youtube/video/2026-07-07-how-to-close-off-plan-secondary-deals-in-dubai-in-2026-KBY6lwvcm9E.md) (`youtube-video-2026-07-07-how-to-close-off-plan-secondary-deals-in-dubai-in-2026-kby6l-7b0008a9c7:u000017`)
