## Effective Interview Techniques

**Product:** knowledge · **Type:** method · **Source support:** not_reviewed
**Publication:** 2026-05-27T06:43:10.327000Z · **Author:** olgasi · **Format:** post

### Summary

- Asking questions in a non-intimidating way can reveal a candidate's true understanding and competence.
  - “The tone is everything… because I know I could be quite intimidating… I asked questions in a way that made him feel like I genuinely wanted to learn from his experience. That is usually when the truth comes out.” — [lines 10-88 part 2](../../../data/linkedin/posts/2026-05-27-if-you-have-a-wrong.md) (`linkedin-posts-2026-05-27-if-you-have-a-wrong-md-f68cc20701:u000002`)

### Goal

- To uncover the candidate's actual level of understanding and competence in sales management.
  - “That is usually when the truth comes out.” — [lines 10-88 part 2](../../../data/linkedin/posts/2026-05-27-if-you-have-a-wrong.md) (`linkedin-posts-2026-05-27-if-you-have-a-wrong-md-f68cc20701:u000002`)

### Steps

1. Ask questions in a way that makes the interviewee feel like you genuinely want to learn from their experience.
  - “I asked questions in a way that made him feel like I genuinely wanted to learn from his experience.” — [lines 10-88 part 2](../../../data/linkedin/posts/2026-05-27-if-you-have-a-wrong.md) (`linkedin-posts-2026-05-27-if-you-have-a-wrong-md-f68cc20701:u000002`)

2. Observe whether the interviewee can explain their thinking clearly or hides behind generic words.
  - “Because when a person really knows what they are doing, they can explain their thinking clearly. And when they don’t, they usually hide behind generic words.” — [lines 10-88 part 2](../../../data/linkedin/posts/2026-05-27-if-you-have-a-wrong.md) (`linkedin-posts-2026-05-27-if-you-have-a-wrong-md-f68cc20701:u000002`)
