### Prerequisites

- Have a back camera to record yourself.
  - “You have to film yourself on the back camera, not the front one.” — [lines 240-240](../../../data/youtube/video/2026-05-26-the-sales-techniques-nobody-teaches-because-they-actually-wo-MSM6H191dh4.md) (`youtube-video-2026-05-26-the-sales-techniques-nobody-teaches-because-they-actually-wo-b31a69b6e9:u000049`)

### Steps

1. Film yourself on the back camera for approximately one minute.
  - “So, you film it, then you watch the video right away.” — [lines 240-240](../../../data/youtube/video/2026-05-26-the-sales-techniques-nobody-teaches-because-they-actually-wo-MSM6H191dh4.md) (`youtube-video-2026-05-26-the-sales-techniques-nobody-teaches-because-they-actually-wo-b31a69b6e9:u000049`)

2. Review the video and let your brain process the self-feedback.
  - “You don't have to do anything. Your brain will do the work.” — [lines 244-244](../../../data/youtube/video/2026-05-26-the-sales-techniques-nobody-teaches-because-they-actually-wo-MSM6H191dh4.md) (`youtube-video-2026-05-26-the-sales-techniques-nobody-teaches-because-they-actually-wo-b31a69b6e9:u000050`)

### Constraints

- The front camera should not be used as it divides attention.
  - “Because the front camera splits your brain in half.” — [lines 248-248](../../../data/youtube/video/2026-05-26-the-sales-techniques-nobody-teaches-because-they-actually-wo-MSM6H191dh4.md) (`youtube-video-2026-05-26-the-sales-techniques-nobody-teaches-because-they-actually-wo-b31a69b6e9:u000051`)

> how do I look and the other half is trying to find the next word. That crashes you. That sounds like nonsense. This split is exactly the same split that's happening in your head on the real sales call. When you are nervous, half of your brain is monitoring how the client perceives you and the other half is trying to remember what to say. Neither half is actually with the client. The back camera kills the monitoring half. It teaches your brain to occupy the space.
