### Steps

1. Borrowed confidence is when you memorize somebody else's script and you feel confident only when the client follows the conversation exactly as you plan it.
  - “The first is borrowed confidence. This is when when you memorize somebody else's script, objection handler presentation, and you feel confident only when the client follows the conversation exactly as you plan it.” — [lines 106-106](../../../data/youtube/video/2026-07-24-the-real-secret-to-success-in-dubai-real-estate-in-2026-and-ctpRUnC9eg8.md) (`youtube-video-2026-07-24-the-real-secret-to-success-in-dubai-real-estate-in-2026-and-4c881de4ed:u000007`)

2. Knowledge-based confidence is when you know the project, area, developer, and market, and can explain details and answer questions, but may struggle if the client is emotional, resistant, or uncertain.
  - “The second is knowledge based confidence. This is when you know the project, the area, the developer and the market. You can explain the details and answer the questions. But if the client becomes emotional, resistant or uncertain, knowledge” — [lines 110-110](../../../data/youtube/video/2026-07-24-the-real-secret-to-success-in-dubai-real-estate-in-2026-and-ctpRUnC9eg8.md) (`youtube-video-2026-07-24-the-real-secret-to-success-in-dubai-real-estate-in-2026-and-4c881de4ed:u000008`)

3. Skill-based confidence is understanding what should happen in the client's mind and knowing what to say to guide the client towards a yes in the sales process.
  - “The third is a skill based confidence. This is when you understand what should happen in the client mind and what you should do and what you should say to engineer between you and the client the yes in the sales process.” — [lines 114-114](../../../data/youtube/video/2026-07-24-the-real-secret-to-success-in-dubai-real-estate-in-2026-and-ctpRUnC9eg8.md) (`youtube-video-2026-07-24-the-real-secret-to-success-in-dubai-real-estate-in-2026-and-4c881de4ed:u000009`)
