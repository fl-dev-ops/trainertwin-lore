  - “start calling them and texting them again using the right script.” — [lines 92-92](../../../data/youtube/video/2026-07-16-the-best-lead-generation-strategy-for-dubai-real-estate-agen-x9mWjb117AA.md) (`youtube-video-2026-07-16-the-best-lead-generation-strategy-for-dubai-real-estate-agen-92502d4649:u000015`)
