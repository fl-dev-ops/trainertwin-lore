A good idea needs people to understand it before they can get behind it.

That’s why I’m so pleased PitchNinjas  is partnering with Google.org for the second year in a row. 

The Google.org Accelerator: AI for Government Innovation has kicked off in Washington, D.C., bringing together 15 nonprofits, social enterprises and academic institutions selected from thousands of applications worldwide.

Over the next three months, they’ll be developing AI solutions to help public services better serve their communities. 

We’ll be supporting them with how they communicate those ideas and turn them into a pitch. Because explaining what you’ve built is only part of the job. 

💡 Can you help someone understand why it matters?
🌍 What it could change for the people using those services?
🤝 And what you need from them to move it forward?

Those are the conversations we love helping people prepare for at PitchNinjas.

After 16 years at Google, it’s a dream to be working together again with Shiri Sivan, Ariel Calliste and the team, through a business we built to help people get their ideas heard.

Looking forward to meeting this cohort and helping them make every pitch count. Meet the organisations and learn more: goo.gle/ai-gov-innovation
