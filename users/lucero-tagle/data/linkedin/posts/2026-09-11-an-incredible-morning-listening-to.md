An incredible morning listening to our #SPARK 2.0 founders pitching at the beautiful  Glasshouse.

Thank you to our programme partner Innovate Cambridge for hosting us, and to the whole team including Francesca Hodgson, MBA, Sophie Harbour, Petra Scalamandré, Maximilian Ge, Lucy Rogers, S. Hanniya Kamran, and Rosalind Howell. 

A special thanks to this morning's judges: Zuleyka Strasner, Lucero Tagle, Anthony Finbow, Ameen Mirjan, MBA, and Maximilian Ge.

Our #SPARK Incubator is a one-month residential programme in Cambridge supporting early-stage founders to accelerate their ventures. This year, close to 200 applications were received from across the University of Cambridge and Anglia Ruskin University ecosystems, with 30 ventures joining the 2026 cohort. 

The programme culminates in Demo Day in late September, hosted by Balderton.

#SPARK is designed and delivered by E-Lab, with support from King's College, Cambridge and Innovate Cambridge.
