I went to Mallorca to catch up with old Google friends.

I came back wondering how I’d worked at the same company as some of these people and never met them.

Enrique Nalda and Kimberley McLoughlin brought us together for our first EMEA reunion. He decided it should happen, organised it, and people came from across EMEA.

We had Google in common, but plenty had changed since.

I thought I’d spend most of my time with people I already knew. Instead, I met people I now feel slightly ridiculous for not having known sooner.

That’s what I loved about it. Everyone brought their own friendships, stories and introductions. By dinner, I was having conversations I couldn’t have planned if I’d tried.

I also did something I don’t do enough. I closed my laptop and gave myself time to enjoy being there.

Long dinners, loud laughter. Conversations that went places and were exactly the point.

I spend a lot of my time helping people communicate better. This mid week getaway reminded me how much it helps to give each other our full attention, with no rush to get to the next thing.

That's the power of human connection, which we did a little bit of on the first day where people got to tell their personal stories  🎉 

And how much we’d have missed if Enrique’s idea had stayed at “we should do this sometime.”

Enrique, thank you for giving us a reason to book the flight and show up.
Already looking forward to the next one. 👊
