The fastest way to make a great idea sound average?

Deliver it in a monotone voice.

I see it all the time when coaching founders before investor pitches.

They've spent months building the product.
They know every number.
Every feature.
Every risk.

Then they get on stage and all of that passion disappears.
Usually it's not a confidence problem, it's nerves.

When we're nervous, we stop breathing properly, our voice flattens, and we sound less convinced than we actually are.

Here's what we remind founders when we coach them before every pitch:

→ If you're naturally expressive, don't dial it down. Let people see your energy.
→ Breathe. A steady breath creates a steady voice.
→ Vary your pace and tone. If everything sounds the same, everything feels the same.
→ Practice with your co-founders or team members until your delivery feels natural, not rehearsed.

Investors don't just back ideas. They back founders who make them believe the idea matters.

Your voice is part of that pitch.
