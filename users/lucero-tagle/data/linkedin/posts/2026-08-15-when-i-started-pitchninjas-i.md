When I started PitchNinjas, I wasn't just building a coaching company. I was trying to solve a problem I'd seen for 16 years at Google.

Coaching was often reserved for senior leaders, even though the people who needed it most were often earlier in their careers.

That became a big part of why Joanna and I built PitchNinjas: to make world-class communication coaching more accessible, practical, and measurable.

I recently joined Dennis Black on The Principal Podcast to share the lessons we've learned scaling PitchNinjas, winning enterprise clients, and helping professionals turn better communication into better business outcomes.

Here are three lessons I shared from building the business:

1️⃣  Communication is not a soft skill, it's a business skill. It directly affects how people pitch, lead, negotiate, sell, and influence.

2️⃣  Growth gets easier when you understand exactly who holds the budget, shorten the path to the decision maker, and run sales and procurement processes in parallel.

3️⃣  Every market communicates differently. What works in the US doesn't always land in the UK, Canada or Europe. Great communicators adapt their style without changing their message.

We also talked about recurring revenue, expanding internationally, client acquisition, and what it really takes to build a business that can scale beyond its founders.

Thanks, Dennis, for having me on. I really enjoyed the conversation.
