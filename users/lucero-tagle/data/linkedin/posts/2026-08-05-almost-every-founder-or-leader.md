Almost every founder or leader we coach eventually asks us the same question.

"What's the best book to become a better communicator?"

There isn't just one. Different communication challenges need different tools.

These are the 8 books we recommend most often because each one solves a specific problem I've either faced myself or watched hundreds of clients work through.

1️⃣ How to Win Friends and Influence People - Dale Carnegie
↳ Read this when people don't seem to warm up to you quickly, or when your work relationships feel more transactional than real.

2️⃣ Crucial Conversations - Patterson, Grenny, McMillan & Switzler
↳ Read this when you've been putting off a conversation you know you need to have. Or when past attempts at hard talks left the relationship worse than before.

3️⃣ Influence - Robert Cialdini
↳ Read this when your pitches, proposals, or asks keep falling flat and you want to understand the psychology behind why people say yes.

4️⃣ Never Split the Difference - Chris Voss
↳ Read this when you're heading into a negotiation such as a salary, a deal, anything, and you always seem to end up giving more ground than you planned.

5️⃣ Made to Stick - Chip and Dan Heath
↳ Read this when people nod at your ideas in the room and then nothing happens. If no one remembers what you said, it doesn't matter that you were right.

6️⃣ Talk Like TED - Carmine Gallo
↳ Read this when you have a big presentation coming up and you want to hold the room not just get through your slides.

7️⃣ Nonviolent Communication - Marshall Rosenberg
↳ Read this when conversations keep turning into arguments, or when you leave every difficult exchange wondering how it went sideways again.

8️⃣ Say It Well - Terry Szuplat
↳ Read this when you want to communicate with more clarity and purpose. Not just sound polished but truly move people.

🔖 Save this for the next time someone asks what to read.

What book would you add to this list? I'm always looking for the next great recommendation.
