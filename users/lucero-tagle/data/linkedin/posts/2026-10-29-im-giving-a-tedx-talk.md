🎤 I’m giving a TEDx talk!

Still feels a bit strange to say that out loud.

On Thursday, November 12, I’ll be speaking at TEDxNortheasternU London.

At PitchNinjas, we’ve helped people prepare for their TEDx talks. I’ve loved being part of that, but it never crossed my mind that I might give one myself.

Then this opportunity came up, I applied…and they said yes!

The girl who used to hide at the back of the room is going to be standing on that stage. That’s quite something to get my head around.

The theme is “Catalyst: Ideas. Spark. Action.” Learning to speak up changed so much for me, including giving me the confidence to co-found PitchNinjas. I can’t wait to share that story.

There’s been plenty of practising since I found out, and a few calls to our coaches too! 😄 More on that soon.

I’m nervous, of course, but I’m so excited I put myself forward. And going through it myself will give me even more to share with our clients who dream of giving their own TEDx talk.

Have you ever ended up doing something you hadn’t even thought to put on your bucket list?
