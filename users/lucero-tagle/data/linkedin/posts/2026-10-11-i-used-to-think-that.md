I used to think that one day I’d wake up and feel ready.

Ready to speak up, ready to pitch an idea, ready to send the email without reading it another ten times.

I could wait a long time.

Early in my career, I pitched a program to a senior leader. She questioned why we needed it. I lost my footing and started tearing up.

I’d love to say I walked out with some great lesson. Mostly, I just wanted to disappear.

But I did speak again and pitch again. Some attempts went better than others. Over time, I got better at explaining my ideas and answering questions without falling apart.

I still get nervous. 
I just don’t take that as a sign I shouldn’t do it.

This made me smile today. Especially the “you suck” bit. Nobody enjoys that part, do they?

A little Sunday reminder, for me too: you can care about doing something well and still let yourself be a beginner.

What’s something you’d like to try, even if you’re a bit rubbish at first?
