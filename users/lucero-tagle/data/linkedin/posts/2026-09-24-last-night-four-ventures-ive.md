Last night, four ventures I’ve coached for the past five weeks stood in front of a room full of investors and owned it.

They were pitching at Balderton for Demo Day, the finale of the five week E-Lab SPARK 2.0 programme at King's College, Cambridge.

And what a finale it was.

🎉 Italy Now: Alina Trabattoni, PhD, Sabina Castelfranco & William T. Whitaker
🎉 Loquelo: Karol Wapniarski
🎉 Moola: Linda Du & Filippo Corbelli
🎉 Finchex: Elizabeth (Lib) Matthews

You all smashed it.

They were clear, confident and, most importantly, themselves. They didn’t sound like they were reciting a script. They knew what they wanted to say and why their idea mattered.

These five weeks reminded me:

- Confidence doesn’t mean you’re not nervous. It means you know what to do when the nerves show up.
- Investors aren’t only listening to the idea. They’re also watching how you think under pressure.
- The best pitches don’t sound memorised.
- You don’t need a perfect script. You need a clear point of view.
- People won’t remember every slide, but they will remember how you made them feel.

There’s always a point when a founder stops “presenting” and starts speaking as themselves.

I saw that last night, and it’s exactly why I love this work and what we do at PitchNinjas. We help people improve their pitch, but the best part is watching them find their voice.

Thank you for trusting me over these five weeks. I’m so proud of what you achieved and can’t wait to see what you do next.

Francesca Hodgson, MBA, thank you for the opportunity 🙏
