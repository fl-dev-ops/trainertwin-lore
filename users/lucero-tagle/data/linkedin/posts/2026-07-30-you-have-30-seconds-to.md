You have 30 seconds to get a senior leader’s attention.

Don’t waste 25 of them on background.

I see this all the time.

Someone has a great idea.
They’ve done the work.
They have the data.

Then they present it in the order they thought about it:

Background → context → data → more context → finally, the point.

Senior leaders don’t need the journey first.

They need the destination.

Here’s a simple structure we use at PitchNinjas:

1/ BLUF (Bottom Line Up Front) 🔝
↳ What are you recommending? Say it upfront.

2/ Give 2–3 reasons 🏛️
↳ What are the strongest reasons to support it? Keep them clear.

3/ Show the evidence 📊
↳ Use the data that proves your case. Not every piece of data you have.

4/ Address the risks 🔍
↳ Show you’ve thought about what could go wrong.

5/  Make the ask 🎯
↳  What do you need from them? A decision? Budget? Approval? Be specific.

The mistake is thinking senior leaders need more information to say yes.

Usually, they need more clarity.

Don’t make them dig for your point.

Lead with it.
