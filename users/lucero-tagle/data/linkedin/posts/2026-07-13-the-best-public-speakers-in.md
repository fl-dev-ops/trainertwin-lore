The best public speakers in the world still get coached.

That's exactly why we got the call.

There's a myth in this industry: coaching is for people who are new, nervous, or struggling.

The opposite is usually true.

The most experienced speakers know something beginners don't: even a strong skill goes stale without a tune-up. That the room, the stakes, and the audience change every single time. 

Before opening the UK Adobe Summit, Claire-Louise Green spent a week with our coach, Octavia Trenchard.

An exceptional speaker already, this wasn't about the basics. This was about elite-level execution:

➡️  Sharpening a massive opening so it landed in the first ten seconds. 
➡️  Translating her deep leadership experience perfectly for this specific arena. 
➡️  Rebuilding her story to bring her authentic voice onto the stage.

A week later, she walked out and absolutely owned it.

Thank you, Octavia Trenchard, for the care and precision you bring to every session. This is exactly the kind of work that makes the difference.

Working alongside Malin Persson on this was a genuine highlight, a stakeholder who truly understood that investing in an already strong speaker isn't a fix, it's a multiplier.

The best in any field still have coaches. Athletes. Musicians. CEOs.

Public speaking shouldn't be any different.
