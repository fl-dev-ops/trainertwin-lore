A quick acid test for your next pitch or presentation:

If you woke up sick tomorrow, handed your deck to a colleague, and they could deliver your talk word-for-word, what would the audience miss by not having you there?

In this clip from our latest PitchNinjas conversation with Terry Szuplat, we talked about something that’s easy to leave out of a pitch: yourself.

We obsess over features, metrics, roadmap slides, and scripted bullet points. We tell ourselves, "It’s not about me, it’s about the product."

Except in almost every industry, a dozen competitors offer something comparable. 

What sticks is the human element: 

❤️ Why YOU care enough to build it
💡 The hard lessons behind the design
🤝 The culture, values, and friction only YOU/YOUR TEAM lived through

Investors and clients need to understand the business. They also need to trust the people behind it. Sharing what you’ve learned and why you care helps them get to know you.

Help people understand your decisions. 
Why you built it this way
Why you’ve stuck with it. 
Why you think it matters.

The next time you rehearse, look for one moment where a personal experience would help the audience understand what you’re saying.

Tell them what happened and why it matters.
