The stars aligned today. 

I had two completely different conversations, both with the same lesson.

As a founder, my default is action. Always moving. 
Always three ideas ahead of myself. 
My mind doesn't really switch off. 

But I've realised you don't grow by doing more. 
You grow by knowing who to ask.  

Not everyone but the right person.

→ Find the right subject matter expert who has already solved the problem you're facing.
→ Ask for targeted direction. Too much advice and you get analysis paralysis.
→ Know who you trust enough to hand something over to completely.
→ Be honest about what you're not good at. Then either learn it or let someone who's already mastered it take the lead.

For a long time, I thought doing everything was simply part of being a founder.

It isn't.

One of the biggest shifts is learning what to let go of.

Even the Eiffel Tower needs a beam of light pointed at it to be seen at night. Who's pointing the light for you right now?
