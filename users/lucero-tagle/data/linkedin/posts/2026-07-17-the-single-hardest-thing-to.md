The single hardest thing to do as a founder?

Taking the complex, sprawling ecosystem of an idea you live and breathe every day and boiling it down into just a couple of sentences that actually click for someone else.

I spent an incredible weekend with WiBT Women in Blockchain Talks doing exactly that.

The room was full of brilliant founders building incredible things. We put them right on the spot: stand up, give us your core pitch in a few sentences, and get immediate, real-time feedback.

Two massive takeaways stood out from the session:

1. 🤫  You don’t have to change your personality to command a room. 

2. 🌱 Founders need a safe space to fail before they fly. If the first time you say your pitch out loud is in front of an investor, you’ve waited too long. 

You need a room where you can stumble, refine, and figure out how to make your audience feel something before the stakes are high. We provided that room.
