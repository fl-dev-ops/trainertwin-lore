Most people see the finish line photos.

They don’t see the unglamorous, silent miles when nobody is watching.

Running and building a business are two things I constantly draw parallels between.

Every time I come home to San Diego, I make a point to run Torrey Pines before the trip gets away from me. It’s my happy place.

People assume I’m out there strictly for the fitness. But that’s only half the story.

The truth? Business looks glamorous from the outside: 
↳ The record revenue quarters
↳ The client logos on your website
↳ The high-ticket client wins
↳ The signed six-figure deals

What people don’t see is the real work: 
↳ The proposals you rewrote 3 times 
↳ The missed targets 
↳ The doubts around making the right call
↳ The days you wondered how much hustle you had left

Progress happens when you continuously show up.

One mile
One client
One decision
One team
Again and again

That’s how I finished 7 marathons. 
And that’s how PitchNinjas got built.
