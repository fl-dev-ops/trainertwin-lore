The biggest presentation mistake isn't what you say.

It's trying to remember every word.

Most people think memorising a presentation makes them more confident.

Our coach Sarah Easter says it does the opposite.

Because the moment you forget one sentence, everything starts to unravel.

You lose your place.
You lose your train of thought.
You lose your confidence.
And your audience can feel it.

Sarah Easter has spent 5 years coaching senior tech, finance, and global leaders at PitchNinjas. With a background in psychology, her instincts were sharpened at the United Nations where a single unclear sentence can change everything. 

She doesn't just polish delivery. She helps leaders build presence, earn trust, and move a room to act.

One of the biggest shifts she teaches:
 ❌ Don't memorise your presentation.
 ✅ Memorise the structure.
 ✅ Know your opening.
 ✅ Know your key messages.
 ✅ Know how you want people to feel.

Then let the words change. That's not being unprepared. That's being present.

The best communicators aren't delivering a script.
They're having a conversation.

It's one of the reasons clients keep asking for Sarah by name.
