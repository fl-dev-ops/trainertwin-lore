Have you ever left a conversation thinking, “I wish I’d said that differently"?

Maybe you wanted to disagree in a meeting but didn’t know how to jump in. Or someone put you on the spot and you said yes, even though you needed more time to think.

I’ve done both.

Earlier in my career, I worried about sounding stupid, so I often stayed quiet. Then I’d replay the conversation afterwards, thinking about what I could have said.

At PitchNinjas, we’ve delivered more than 10,000 coaching sessions, and this comes up a lot. People know their work and have something valuable to say, but finding the words in the moment can be hard.

One thing that helps is having a few starting points ready:

When you disagree:
↳ “I see it a little differently. Can I share another perspective?”

When you need more time:
↳ “I’d like to think about that. Can I come back to you later/tomorrow?”

When something didn’t sit right:
↳ “Can we talk about what happened in the meeting? I’d like to share how I saw it.”

You can put these in your own words. The point is to have a way to get started, especially when the conversation feels uncomfortable.
