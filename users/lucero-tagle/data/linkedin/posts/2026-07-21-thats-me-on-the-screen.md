That's me on the screen behind me. 15 years ago. Presenting.

300 people in the room and I was absolutely terrified.

I remember standing just off stage, heart hammering against my ribs, I was convinced everyone was about to watch me fall apart.

But I'd prepared. Obsessively.

So, I walked out, opened my mouth, and didn't stop until I had delivered every single point I came to make.

When I finished, the rush hit me like crossing a marathon finish line. That specific, hard earned high. Legs shaking, lungs burning, and one thought on repeat: I actually just did that.

I didn't know it then, but that terrifying moment became the foundation for everything I do now.

Every time a leader walks into a PitchNinjas session convinced they're "not a natural presenter." Every time a founder tells us they'd rather send a deck than pitch live.

I know exactly how that feels.

I was that person.

The shift never came from a burst of confidence. It came from a decision:

📚 Prepare hard enough to outrun the fear.
🚪 Say yes to the opportunities that scare you.
🚀 Show up and put yourself out there, no matter what.

I tell this story every time I present, for the person in the room who feels exactly how I did 15 years ago.

The speaker owning the stage today wasn't born with a microphone in hand.

If that version of me could pull it off, you can too.
