Most founders build first and figure out how to explain it later.

That's backwards.

This weekend I'm speaking at Build. Own. Lead. in London, on a topic I care about deeply: pitching before you build.

Not after you've got a deck. Not after you've got a product. Before.

Here's why order matters:
→ If you can't say what you're building in one clear sentence, you don't understand it well enough yet 
→ If you can't say why it matters without notes, you're not convinced by it yet 
→ If you can't hold a room's attention for two minutes, you'll struggle to hold an investor's for twenty

Plenty of places teach AI. Plenty teach blockchain. Almost none ask the harder question first: what are you actually building, and why?

That's the gap this weekend closes. Not a hackathon. Not another networking event. A room to stress-test ideas, validate real problems, and take the first step from curiosity to creation.
