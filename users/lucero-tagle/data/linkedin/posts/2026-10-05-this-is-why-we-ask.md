This is why we ask clients: “If the teleprompter stopped working, could you still make your point?”

Watching this clip, I felt for Prince Harry. Standing in front of a room when the words you’re expecting suddenly disappear would throw most people.

We spend a lot of time coaching clients for exactly that moment. Of course, you can use a script. But you need to know what you’re trying to say well enough to carry on without it. 

🎯 What’s the one point the audience needs to hear?
📖 What story will help them understand it?
➡️ Where do you want to take them next?

Then we practise saying it in different ways, until the speaker can look up, respond to the room and find their way back if something goes wrong.

A teleprompter can help you remember the words. It can’t do the speaking for you.
