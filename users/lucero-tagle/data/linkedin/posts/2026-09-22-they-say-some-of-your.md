They say some of your best work happens nowhere near a desk.

Yesterday, mine happened on a punt along the River Cam, watching the sun set over King's College, Cambridge.

On board were Elizabeth (Lib) Matthews, who I’ve had the privilege of mentoring and advising as part of the SPARK, E-Lab Incubator programme, and Leo Tyson, who I’ll soon be joining forces with some exciting new projects.

As a founder and Entrepreneur in Residence, much of my week is filled with strategy calls, mentoring, pitching, closing deals and admin.

But sometimes you need to get away from the laptop and spend time with the people behind the work.

Elizabeth gave us a brilliant history lesson along the way, from the Mathematical Bridge at Queens'​ College, Cambridge to the stories hiding behind Cambridge’s old walls.

She then invited me to a formal dinner that evening at her college. Academic gown and all. Such a magical experience!

Elizabeth, thank you for being so generous and making Cambridge feel even more special.

Leo, I’m really looking forward to working together.

Some of the best working relationships grow when you stop talking about work.

They say work hard, play hard. We went with work hard, punt gently 😄

Where have some of your best working relationships been built?
