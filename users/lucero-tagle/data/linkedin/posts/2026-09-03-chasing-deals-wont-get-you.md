Chasing deals won’t get you into Netflix Mexico City.

Doing good work in one room might.

That’s why I’m outside the Netflix office in Mexico City, smiling way too much.

We've been coaching Netflix teams across EMEA for a while now. Different offices, different languages, but the same need to make ideas land clearly.

Now Netflix Mexico City is joining us.

This is the part of building PitchNinjas I never get tired of. You do a good job in one room and someone mentions it in another. An intro happens and months later, you're in CDMX, wearing your own merch and trying not to look too pleased with yourself.

That’s how some of the best opportunities happen. Not through chasing every deal, but by doing work people remember and recommend.

A big thank you to the Netflix team for continuing to trust PitchNinjas and opening doors on both sides of the Atlantic.

Very excited to get to work with the Mexico City team. 👊
