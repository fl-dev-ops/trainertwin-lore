Ever been halfway through a story and realised the other person is looking for someone else to talk to?

You lose your place, speed up and try to finish before you lose them completely.

Then there's the person who remembers something you told them weeks ago and asks, "How did that go?"

You could happily talk to them all evening.

I've noticed good company comes down to small things:

📵 Put your phone away.
👂 Let them finish, even when you've got a story of your own.
💬 Ask about something they just said.
🙋 Share a little of yourself too. Nobody wants to feel interviewed.

Something to catch yourself doing: when someone tells you something, do you ask a follow up question or jump straight to “That happened to me too”?

Sometimes your story helps but sometimes it takes over. Let them finish, then ask a little more before sharing your own.

We made this little guide at PitchNinjas as a reminder. Plenty in here for me to practise too.

It’s what we hope for at our “Make Every Conversation Count” events that are going global: people getting curious about each other, sharing a bit of themselves and leaving wanting to keep talking.

What makes you want to keep talking to someone?
