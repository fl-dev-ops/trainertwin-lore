I watched one of our coaches command a West End stage and it all made sense.

One of our PitchNinjas coaches, Henry Proffit, was cast in a lead role in Who Killed Marilyn, running until 29 October in London's West End.

And he was brilliant  🎉 

Henry spends his days coaching leaders on presence, timing, pauses, and how to make a room feel something when they speak.

Tonight I watched him do all of that for real, under the lights, in front of a packed theatre.
It reminded me why he’s so good at what he does.

The skills we teach at PitchNinjas aren’t abstract. They’re lived, practised and tested in front of actual audiences.

→ Owning silence instead of rushing to fill it
→ Reading the room and adjusting on the spot
→ Making people feel something, not just hear words
→ Walking out of every scene fully owning it

Henry brings that same craft to every client session.
No wonder people love working with him.

If you’re in London before 29 October, go see Who Killed Marilyn. Go support Henry. You’ll understand exactly why we wanted him on the team.

Henry, you were extraordinary tonight. Proud to call you a PitchNinjas coach  ❤️
