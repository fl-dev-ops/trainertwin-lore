I sat in on a coaching session with Sophie, one of PitchNinjas’ first coaches.

She was working in French with five senior leaders from Adobe, getting ready to speak at their October summit in Paris.

I don’t often get to watch our coaches work. Usually, I hear about it afterwards: a note from the client, a good result, a renewal. So I sat at the back observing. 

One leader kept rushing through his opening. The point he cared about most was getting lost. Sophie stopped him. “Again. Slower.” He tried it. This time, he gave the words room to land. 

Another speaker had all the right information, but something was missing. Sophie asked her why the project mattered to her. She answered, tried again, and sounded like a different person.

Sophie didn’t write their words for them. She listened for the bit they were rushing past, then helped them say it properly.

By the end, they sounded like themselves. 

Sophie, thank you for letting me sit in. I loved seeing you at work and a reminder of why we built PitchNinjas in the first place 🎉
