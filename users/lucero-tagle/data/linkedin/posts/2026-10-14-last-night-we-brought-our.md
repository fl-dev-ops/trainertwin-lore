Last night, we brought our L&D community together in London for our PitchNinjas Make Every Conversation Count event. Some familiar faces, some new.

And we found plenty of ways to connect. People chatted, laughed, caught up and found out things you’d never get from a LinkedIn profile:

💃 Who’s been secretly taking salsa classes
🍿 Who has very strong opinions on the latest Netflix series
💬 Who are you beyond “so, what do you do?”

That’s the bit I love and why bringing people together means so much to me. When something someone says makes you curious, you ask another question, and suddenly you’re having a conversation you don’t want to leave.
