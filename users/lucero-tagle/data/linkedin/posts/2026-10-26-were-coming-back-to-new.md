✨ We’re coming back to New York for our second Make Every Conversation Count breakfast, and I’d love you to join us. ✨ 

On November 4th, we’re getting leaders in the people space around the table for breakfast, coffee and a chance to talk properly.

There’s always plenty to discuss: what you’re working on, what’s proving difficult, or something you’d love another person’s perspective on.

What I’ve enjoyed most about these events is seeing how much people share once they get talking. Someone mentions a challenge, someone else has been through it. An introduction is offered, a resource gets passed on, and people make plans to meet again.

We leave plenty of room for that. You don’t need to prepare anything. Just come along, meet a few people and see where the conversation goes.

📅 Wednesday, November 4th, 2026
🕣 8:30–10:30 am
📍 La Pecora Bianca Bryant Park, 20 W 40th St, New York, NY 10018

If you work in the people space in New York, I’d love you to join us. Send me a DM, and feel free to pass this on to someone you’d like to bring along.
