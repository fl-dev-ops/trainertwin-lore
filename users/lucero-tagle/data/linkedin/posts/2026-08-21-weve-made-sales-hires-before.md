We’ve made sales hires before. Some worked, some didn’t and we haven’t always got it right.

For the past couple of months, I’ve been going back and forth on the same question:

Do we hire a generalist who can sell everything PitchNinjas offers or a specialist who already knows how to sell to enterprise Learning & Development? Or does it even matter?

The trade-off looks like this:

→ Generalist: more affordable, quicker to onboard and more flexible. But probably a longer ramp on the enterprise deals that actually move revenue.

→ Specialist: faster corporate traction because they already speak the buyer's language. But a narrower fit if the enterprise pipeline slows down.

On paper, it’s a commercial decision but in reality, it feels far more personal.

Whoever we hire will be talking about PitchNinjas in rooms I’m not in.

They’ll be making promises on our behalf and shaping how people experience the brand we’ve spent years building.

That’s the part keeping making me hesitate, it's not the salary but the trust and making the right decision.

🗣️  An ask for my network: I’m calling on the collective wisdom of founders who’ve been there and done it: generalist or specialist and why? 

🔎 And if you know a kick ass sales rep in the US or UK, please tag in comments below.👇
