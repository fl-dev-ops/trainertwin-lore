Most people don't communicate poorly because they lack confidence.

They struggle because they overlook a few fundamentals.

PitchNinjas has run more than 5,000+ communication coaching sessions, and these are the habits I see making the biggest difference.

1/ Listening matters more than talking.
The best communicators ask better questions. They actually hear the answer.

2/ Silence is not dead air.
A well-placed pause gives people time to think. It shows you're not afraid of the room.

3/ Tone can ruin the right words.
People hear your tone before they process your argument.

4/ Shorter is usually better.
Clear, concise communication is not dumbing it down. It is respecting people's time.

5/ Ego gets in the way.
If you go into a conversation trying to win, connection gets harder fast.

6/ Empathy is practical.
If you haven't tried to understand the person in front of you, your message is mostly guesswork.

7/ Feedback usually stings first.
The feedback that makes you defensive is often worth looking at twice.

8/ Clear beats clever.
Simple language is not a weakness. It usually means you understand what you're saying.

9/ Your body speaks first.
If your body is saying one thing and your words are saying another, people believe the body.

None of these are fixed traits. Every single one can be practiced.

Which one are you still working on?
