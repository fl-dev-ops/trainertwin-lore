I knew what I wanted to say. I just kept waiting for the perfect moment to say it.

Then someone else would say exactly what I’d been thinking and I’d sit there wondering why I hadn’t spoken up first.

That quiet 20 yr old is a big part of why PitchNinjas exists.

Whether we’re coaching an associate, a manager or a VP, the same questions come up again and again, even at companies like Google, Netflix and HSBC.

These are not the polished ones people openly ask but the real ones they are hesitant to ask in the workplace like:

→ How do I stop rambling in meetings?
→ What if I blank out mid presentation?
→ What if someone talks over me?
→ What if I'm afraid my voice doesn't sound authoritative?
→ How do I disagree without sounding defensive?
→ What if I feel nervous before presenting?

These are just a few of the questions that come up in our 1:1 coaching sessions every week.

And honestly? We love them. 

If one person’s asking, a dozen more are silently wondering. 

So we're bringing those questions and our honest answers here to the feed.
