I took this photo in Austin yesterday.

I'd just finished a Barry's class and wandered into a Western store nearby. I loved the Texan vibe and thought why not take a picture.

Then I looked closer and saw my son peeking out from behind. I completely missed him the first time. 

It made me laugh because he's cheeky like that but it also landed like a quiet reality check.

Building a business has me uber focused on the obvious things in front of me like needing to send the next proposal that's late, closing the next hire that's overdue and worrying about the next meeting.

Sometimes I miss what's happening around me like the little moments with the family that won't come around again.

Building a company has taught me to chase big goals.
My kids keep reminding me not to miss the small ones.

Funny how the best lesson from Austin had nothing to do with business.
It was hiding in plain sight.
