Everyone wants to become a more confident speaker.

I think that's the wrong goal.

We've coached people who have raised millions, run billion dollar businesses, and led teams of hundreds.

And what we’ve seen after thousands of sessions is that strongest communicators weren't the ones trying to sound polished.

They were the ones paying attention.

↳ They ask better questions.
↳ They notice when the room is confused, bored, or bought in.
↳ They adjust instead of clinging to the script.

And this is what made them look confident.
Not performing to perfection or trying to dominate the room.

Just being present enough to respond to what's actually happening.

Stop chasing confidence and start noticing your surroundings more.
