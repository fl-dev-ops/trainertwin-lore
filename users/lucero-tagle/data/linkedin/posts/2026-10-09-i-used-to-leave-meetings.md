I used to leave meetings thinking about everything I hadn’t said.

The question I swallowed.
The idea I kept to myself.
The moment I nodded along when I should have pushed back.

It took me years to get comfortable speaking up. My corporate career gave me plenty of practise, even when I would have preferred to stay quiet.

One thing I wish I’d understood sooner:

A good conversation isn’t about saying more.
It’s about choosing what matters enough to say.

Here are four things our PitchNinjas coaches help clients practise: 

🎯 Know what you want from the conversation
Do you need a decision? Help? A chance to explain a concern? Get clear on that before you walk in.

👂 Listen past the first answer
Sometimes people need a little time to tell you what’s really bothering them. Ask another question. Give them room to answer.

⏸️ Pause before you jump in
I know how tempting it is to fill the silence. But a pause gives you a moment to think about what you actually want to say.

🤝 Don't end of good vibes alone
A warm conversation is great, but if nobody knows what happens next, something got missed. Name the next step, even if it's small.

These sound simple. Doing them when you’re nervous, rushed or worried about upsetting someone takes practise.
