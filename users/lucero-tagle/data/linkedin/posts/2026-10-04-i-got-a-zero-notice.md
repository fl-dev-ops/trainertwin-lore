I got a zero notice pitch test in an airport lounge.

My flight had been cancelled early this morning, so I was trying to make the wait useful.
Then a stranger walked over and pointed at my laptop.

"Excuse me, what's PitchNinjas?"
I froze for about half a second.

Elevator pitch, no warning. Slight airport lounge panic as I had my headphones on typing an email. 

Basically the exact thing we coach founders to do: explain what you do in 60 seconds, without rambling or talking jargon.

He smiled. Said he loved the name. Said he'd Google us.

And weirdly, this wasn't the first time someone has stopped me to ask about it.
That's what good branding can do, and thanks to the sticker on my laptop 😁.  It gives people a reason to start the conversation.

→ A name gets attention
→ A clear way of explaining what you do keeps it
→ You never really know who's listening

Proud of what we've built. Proud of the name. And honestly, grateful for every stranger curious enough to ask.
