OpenAI just revealed it acquired NextSlide, and presentation building will be easier than ever.

Which means anyone can generate a polished presentation deck in seconds.

My first thought wasn't, "This changes everything". It was, "Good. Now people have one less excuse."

After thousands of coaching sessions at PitchNinjas, I've learned something.

The slides were rarely the reason someone didn't get buy-in. The problem was usually the person presenting them.

AI can format your slides, clean up your layout, and summarize your data. 

But AI still can’t: 

→ Notice when people in the room have mentally checked out.
→ Change direction when a question exposes the real concern.
→ Stay calm when someone challenges your idea in front of everyone.

That's the work.

Three things we spend a lot of time coaching:

1️⃣ Prepare for their questions, not your script.
↳ The strongest presenters answer the audience's biggest concern before it's even asked.

2️⃣ Don't fight difficult questions.
↳ When someone pushes back, it usually means they're engaged. Treat it as the conversation you came to have.

3️⃣ Be comfortable with silence.
↳ If the room goes quiet, don't panic and click to the next slide.
Sometimes the best thing you can say is: "Can I ask what's giving you hesitation?"

AI will make creating presentations faster.
It won't make you more persuasive.
That's still a human skill.
