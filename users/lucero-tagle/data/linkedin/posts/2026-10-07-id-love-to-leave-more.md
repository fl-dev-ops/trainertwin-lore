I’d love to leave more conversations thinking, “That was fun.”

I see so many that feel transactional. We exchange updates, ask for something, agree the next step. Even when we meet someone new, we quickly get onto work.

Listening to one of my favourite communication experts, Vanessa Van Edwards' Spotify episode, “Why AI Is Making Us Lonelier and How to Truly Connect,” her point about bringing a little play into conversation really resonated with me.

Because we don’t do this enough!

I love those moments when someone says something unexpected, you both laugh, and suddenly the conversation feels easier. 

You stop watching what you say quite so carefully. Sometimes all it takes is a different question: 

“What could you happily talk about for hours?” 
“What are you surprisingly terrible at?”

Or sharing the funny thing that happened to you instead of answering “busy, thanks” when someone asks how you are.

I spend my working life thinking about communication, and I need this reminder too. 

I can get so focused on what I want to cover that I forget to leave room for whatever comes up. 

Some of my favourite conversations have gone completely off track.

What’s a question that’s sparked a fun conversation for you?
