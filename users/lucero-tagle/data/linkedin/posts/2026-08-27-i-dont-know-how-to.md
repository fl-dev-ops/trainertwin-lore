I don't know how to do anything halfway.

Never have, probably never will.

The people closest to me know this. Sometimes, they probably wish I came with an off switch.

When I care about something, I'm all in.

I care about the outcome. I care about doing things properly. And I’ll keep pushing until we reach the version that once felt just out of reach.

I think I’ve been like this since my teens, when I first realised I wanted a life that looked very different from the one in front of me.

More freedom. 
More independence. 
More say in what happened next.

It hasn’t always been easy. There have been some real highs and lows that took longer to recover from than I’d like to admit.

But I’ve built a thick skin, learned to keep going, and held on to the optimism that reminds me life is there to be lived.

And I’m proud of that.

Sessions like this are where that energy really comes out.

A room full of founders working on how they pitch, how they hold themselves, how they speak when the nerves kick in.

I’ve run this session so many times, and somehow it still gets me every time.

You can see it happenning:

→ The shoulders drop
→ The voice settles
→ The eye contact starts to land
→ They stop performing and start being heard

That’s the part I love because I strongly believe that nobody should miss their moment because of how they communicate.

So I keep showing up and saying yes to these opportunities, session after session, founder after founder.

When it comes to this work, I’m all in. 🎉
