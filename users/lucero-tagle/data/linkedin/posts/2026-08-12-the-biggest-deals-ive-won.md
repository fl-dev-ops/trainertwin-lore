The biggest deals I've won didn't start with a pitch.

They started with a conversation.

Back in May, I met Edward Gardiner, MPA, CPTD at a conference in LA.

He was there as an L&D leader exploring new ideas. We were there introducing people to PitchNinjas.

That evening, Eddie and Amy Pilon joined us at our rooftop event. There was no agenda or sales script. Just an honest conversation between people who clicked.

When I got back to London, I followed up. That led to a virtual meeting, an introduction to his boss Wade A. McNair, and the conversation kept moving to what is now a signed contract 🙌

This week, while I was in San Diego, I drove up to Orange County to visit the team.

Eddie spent time showing me around the office, introducing me to leaders across the business, sharing where PitchNinjas could add value, and we continued the conversation over lunch. 

We had a fantastic time talking about ways to collaborate.

It felt very natural and reminded me that enterprise partnerships rarely happen because of one great meeting.

They happen because people follow through.
Because they make introductions.
Because they invest time before there's ever a signed contract.

People like working with people they trust.
And trust is built one conversation at a time.

A huge thank you to Eddie and Amy for the warm welcome. We missed you Wade!

We're incredibly excited to welcome Tricon Homes to the PitchNinjas family and can't wait to get started 🎉
