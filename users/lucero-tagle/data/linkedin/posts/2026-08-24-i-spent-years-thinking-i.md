I spent years thinking I just wasn't a natural talker.

Then PitchNinjas was born and we started coaching people through investor pitches, tough conversations, and interviews that could change their lives.

That's when I realised I had it backwards.

Communication is a skill that you can practice, study and get better at.

Here are 10 people I’ve been following and studying and would recommend you follow too:

→ Jefferson Fisher: teaches you how to argue less and have hard conversations without turning everything into a fight.
→ Simon Sinek: helps leaders explain purpose in a way people can actually connect with.
→ Matt Abrahams: the person to learn from when you have no time to prepare but still need to speak clearly.
→ Chris Voss: takes lessons from FBI hostage negotiation and makes them useful for everyday conversations.
→ Brené Brown: shows why trust often comes from being honest, not polished.
→ Vanessa Van Edwards: breaks charisma into cues you can actually practice.
→ Vinh Giang: makes you think differently about your voice, delivery, and presence.
→ Nancy Duarte: teaches presentation structure so your slides stop sounding like a document read out loud.
→ Julian Treasure: reminds us that listening is part of communication, and most of us are worse at it than we think.
→ Terry Szuplat: brings lessons from writing for the Obama White House into practical advice for clearer speaking and writing.

They all come at communication from different angles.

But the lesson I keep coming back to is this:
How you say something can change whether people hear it at all. 

I don’t want people missing opportunities because they couldn’t get their message across. This is the mission of PitchNinjas. 

Which one of these people do you already follow?
And who would you add to the list?
