Most people confuse facilitation with getting through the material.

Will Van Der Hart shows why it’s really about shifting the room.

Will is one of our senior coaches and facilitators at PitchNinjas. Here, he’s taking a group at Netflix through what we call the communication footprint: confidence, candour, story, energy and humour.

Most of us have a couple we lean on naturally. Others take more practice.
What I love about watching Will is that you see all five as he teaches. 

Watching him teach reminds me why facilitation is such a craft.

💪 Confidence: he’s comfortable on stage, and that helps everyone else relax.
💬 Candour: he says the hard thing clearly, without making people feel small.
📖 Story: he makes an idea feel real through a story people recognise.
⚡ Energy: he knows when to lift the room and when to give people a moment.
😄 Humour: he gets people laughing, making it easier for them to join in.

Great facilitators don't just get through the material. They shift the room.

We're lucky to have him.

If you've ever sat in a room with a facilitator who changed something for you, who was it?
