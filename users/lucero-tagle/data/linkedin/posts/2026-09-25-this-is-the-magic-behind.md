This is the magic behind an incubator programme that actually works.


Kamiar Mohaddes, Petra Scalamandré, Francesca Hodgson, MBA, Sophie Harbour. The team who kept the entire E-Lab  SPARK 2.0 Incubator Demo night running without breaking a sweat.

But programmes like this don’t just happen. There is an entire ecosystem that builds and believes in it together and makes it happen.

Founders came together at very different stages, all needing different things. Some needed to think more commercially. Some needed confidence. Others needed someone to challenge the story they’ve been telling for months.

King's College, Cambridge, E-Lab has created something special. 

Founders get structure, but they also get access to investors like Balderton and TRAMLINES as well as coaches, mentors and other founders who continue to help long after the programme ends.

That’s what people often miss about founder incubator programmes. The content matters, but the people in the room matter more.

Who’s there, whether founders feel safe enough to be honest. And whether the people running it are really paying attention.

Kamiar, Petra, Francesca, Sophie, and the rest of the E-lab team, you’re brilliant at what you do. 

Thank you for helping founders get their ideas seen, heard and taken seriously. It has been an absolute pleasure working alongside you supporting this cohort.

The UK startup ecosystem needs more of this.
