I came to the UK alone at twenty for the first time as an exchange student with one suitcase.

Standing on the lawn in front of King's College, Cambridge it is strange how fast a place can pull you backward. The colleges and that very specific quiet of an old English courtyard.

I spent a year abroad at the University of Oxford as a college exchange student.
No friends waiting for me. Just lectures, a new accent to decode, and a version of me who had to learn fast or feel completely lost.

I learned how to sit in a tutorial and actually speak. How to disagree with a professor twice my age. How to build a life from scratch in a new country miles away from what I was use to.

That year taught me a lot about communication and resourcefulness.

Cambridge wasn't even part of my original plan for this trip. I'm here now advising founders, years later, as part of the #SPARK E-Lab programme, and the second I saw those spires I was twenty again.

That year abroad is one of the quiet reasons PitchNinjasnjas exists. My co-founder and I built it for every founder and professional trying to find their voice in a room that feels unfamiliar.

Walking these courtyards again, I feel grateful for the twenty year old who figured it out on her own.

What's a place that instantly takes you back to a version of yourself you'd almost forgotten?
