I've lived in the UK for most of my adult life.

But I grew up in the US.

Every time I return to grow PitchNinjas stateside, I notice the exact same shift in my mindset.

Last trip, I was at a coffee meeting in LA, half joking to a founder I'd just met that I needed intros to two people on her team.

She didn't blink. Pulled out her phone right there and made the intro before I'd even finished my coffee.

↳ Here, people just say "let's make it happen."
↳ Ambition doesn't get side eyed, it gets celebrated.
↳ Not every conversation turns into something. But enough of them do that you keep asking.

The UK built my career. It's where I started this business and learned to actually think.

The US reminds me of something equally important.

Momentum.

That sometimes the biggest difference between growing your business and standing still isn't having a better product.
 
 → It's asking one more person.
 → Sending one more message.
 → Walking into one more room.

At PitchNinjas we talk a lot about clarity and delivery. But half the battle is just being willing to open your mouth and ask.

What's one ask you've been sitting on because you talked yourself out of it?
