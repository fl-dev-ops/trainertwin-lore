We are raising £27,000,000 in funding for our early-stage ventures.

Congratulations to our #SPARK 2.0 Incubator Cohort by E-Lab for their confident and inspiring pitches this week at Demo Day hosted by Balderton.

Whilst we awarded £45,000 of cheques on the night, everyone is a winner here at #SPARK and our support won't end here! When you are part of SPARK, you become part of E-Lab for life.

Over the past 5 weeks we have taken our teams through:

57 sessions - workshops, pitch-practice, and inspirational talks designed to build lasting foundational knowledge and networks to strengthen their entrepreneurial journeys for life.

We partnered our teams with incredible Entrepreneurs in Residence.

Most importantly we had fun and supported each other. 

Beyond the cheques I want to give thanks to what felt like an army of people supporting our teams. We are building powerful partnerships, industry collaborations, and a network of support that means our founders always have somewhere to turn. To everyone involved I am personally so grateful. Programmes like #SPARK are only possible with the support of a value aligned eco-system. 

Congratulations again to our cohort on their wonderful pitches, we are here to support you every step of the way; from capital to money can't buy support, we are here to help you learn, to help you scale and to help you grow. 

This is certainly not goodbye, this is just the start  🚀
