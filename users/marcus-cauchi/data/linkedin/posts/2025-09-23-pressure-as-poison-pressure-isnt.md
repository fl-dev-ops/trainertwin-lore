Pressure as Poison

Pressure isn’t progress. It’s poison.
👉 Before you read further, ask yourself: were your last three deals won on pressure, or on trust?

One team I worked with cut raw activity by a third and revenue jumped 28% in a single quarter. Competitors flatlined. Gallup shows high-trust teams are 50% more likely to hit targets. HubSpot reports trust-led sellers close 20–30% more deals.

The founder’s trap is thinking pressure creates momentum. It doesn’t. It corrodes trust, drains energy, and makes people check out while you’re still counting their calls.

If you think pressure is progress, you’re already in the trap.
