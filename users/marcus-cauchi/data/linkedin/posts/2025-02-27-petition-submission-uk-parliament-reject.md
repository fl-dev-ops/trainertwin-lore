Petition Submission – UK Parliament
🚨 Reject the Data (Use and Access) Bill – Protect Privacy, Business, and National Security

What do you want the government to do?
* We call on the UK Government to immediately halt and redraft the Data (Use and Access) Bill to prevent its devastating impact on:
* Personal privacy & financial autonomy – Stop the mass data harvesting and real-time financial surveillance.
* Business security & trade secrets – Prevent forced data-sharing that hands proprietary intelligence to AI firms and competitors.
* Cyber & financial risk markets – Protect the integrity of underwriting models and insurance structures that provide risk certainty.
* National security & economic resilience – Stop foreign entities from exploiting UK business and citizen data.
* Consumer protection & ethical AI development – Prevent AI firms from using unregulated access to personal and commercial data for profit.

Parliament must protect UK citizens, businesses, and the insurance & cybersecurity markets from irreversible damage.

Background (Why is this important?)
The Data (Use and Access) Bill enables unrestricted government and corporate surveillance, eroding personal privacy, business security, and fair competition.
💥 Threats to Businesses:
* Trade secrets exposed – Companies forced to share competitive intelligence with AI firms.
* Insurance pricing chaos – Proprietary underwriting models absorbed into public AI-driven risk models.
* Cyber liability crisis – AI-led risk aggregation makes cyber insurance unpredictable and uninsurable.
