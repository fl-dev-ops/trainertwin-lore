The most expensive meeting to skip is the one that checks whether the plan is still true.

The weekly meeting keeps work moving.
The monthly meeting checks whether the work still makes sense.

Suzanne C. nails this.

Most teams do not drift because they make one terrible decision. They drift because something changes and nobody creates a proper space to say it plainly.

A customer changes.
A competitor moves.
A key hire does not land.
Pipeline looks busy but weaker.
The market shifts.

The plan still sounds sensible, so the team keeps executing it.

The question I’d add is:

“What are we still treating as true that we have not checked recently?”

If that question gets answered openly in the meeting, the meeting is working.

If it only gets answered afterwards, the meeting is exposing something important.

The issue is not cadence.

It is trust.
