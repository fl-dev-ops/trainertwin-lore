IF YOU HAVE TO FORCE ALIGNMENT, YOU DON’T HAVE IT.

Bad leaders push. Great leaders align.

If you have to constantly persuade people, argue your case, or enforce rules to keep things moving, the real issue isn’t effort, it’s misalignment.

Teams don’t resist hard work.
 They resist working on things that don’t make sense.

When alignment is clear, execution is automatic.
 When incentives are shared, momentum is unstoppable.

Stop convincing. Start aligning.

Tag someone who leads with alignment, not pressure.
