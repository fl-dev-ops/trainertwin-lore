The Decisions That Matter
Part 6: Should We Move Faster?

Speed has become a badge of honour in many organisations. Decisions are praised for being quick, while caution is often mistaken for hesitation.

The difficulty is that pressure narrows attention. Teams skip difficult questions, assume agreement where none exists and compress evidence into confident conclusions because the business wants momentum more than certainty.

The cost rarely appears immediately. It shows up later as rework, missed stakeholders, weak implementation and projects that have to revisit decisions everyone thought had already been made.

The strongest leaders don’t move slowly. They simply know which parts of a decision can move quickly and which deserve another ten minutes of careful thought.

Before asking how fast you can move, ask what you still don’t know.
