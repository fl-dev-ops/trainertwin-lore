You may know the valuation, understand the documents, trust the investor and believe the plan. You may still not know what happens to your economics, authority and options if reality turns out differently.

What happens if the plan misses? What happens if more capital is needed? Which decisions remain yours? Which promises are you relying on? What becomes difficult to reverse after signing? How much of the proposed deal do you actually need to commit to now?

None of those questions assumes the investor is bad or the deal is wrong. They recognise that taking institutional capital changes more than the bank balance.
Before you take the money, find out what comes with it.

If you have a live term sheet, debt proposal, PE offer, rollover or partial sale in front of you, bring me the transaction and the part that doesn't quite sit right.
