Growth burns out good people when leaders confuse empathy with weakness.
Emma Thompson knows better.

She’s helped scale-ups, agencies, and high-growth tech firms rebuild cultures that lost their way - not with slogans, but with structure.

In this conversation, we go deep on:
- How to make empathy operational, not ornamental
- What “psychological safety” really looks like in targets and reviews
- How to hold people accountable without humiliation
- The early warning signs that culture’s cracking
- Why most “values statements” are theatre, not truth

Emma explains how to design a company that feels safe to perform, not safe to hide.
It’s a masterclass for founders and operators who want scale and sanity.

Marcus: “Emma, you’ve coached leaders through chaos. What breaks companies?”
Emma Thompson: “Fear. The kind that stops people telling you the truth.”
Marcus: “And how do you fix that?”
Emma: “By making empathy part of the system, not a speech.”
