Carole Mahoney, I would add Brownie Wise, Mary Kay Ash, Mary Crowley, and Martha Matilda Harper to this story.

What links them is not just that they were successful in sales. It is that they built models that look much closer to being the buyer's ally than transactional selling.

Brownie Wise made demonstration, trust, and social proof central. Mary Kay Ash and Mary Crowley built repeatable systems around training, confidence, and field enablement. Martha Matilda Harper showed much earlier that service, education, and distribution could be systemised without stripping out the human relationship.

The through-line feels important: reduce buyer risk, build trust before pressure, prepare the seller properly, and make the relationship durable enough to compound.

That is much closer to the ally seller than the old close at all costs model the profession later normalised.
