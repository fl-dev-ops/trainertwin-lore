Most decision-making frameworks are love letters to optimism.

They chase upside.
* They play fantasy football with opportunity.
 * They pretend risk is someone else’s problem.
That is why so many strategic decisions die ugly deaths, buried under assumptions, unspoken fears, and polite silence.

/zag flips your script.

Start by removing what hurts.
* Disqualify the unsafe.
 * Expose every hidden risk, ambiguity, and threat before you even think about opportunity.
 
Because you do not deserve upside until you have created clarity.

/ zag turns decision-making into a practical survival tool.
* Risks made visible
* Stakeholders aligned on reality
* Trust built into every step
* Emotional undercurrents surfaced, not suppressed.

Clear pathways mapped, with red flags, green lights and actionable ambers.
You will make faster, safer, smarter decisions, because you will finally be seeing what is really there, not what you hope is there.

That is what /zag does.

Clarity before certainty. Alignment before action. Trust before speed.
Ready to remove what hurts before you chase what works?

Let’s talk.
