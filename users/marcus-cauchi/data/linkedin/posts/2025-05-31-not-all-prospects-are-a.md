Not all Prospects are a Fit

When a Prospect isn't right for your product or service, you sell them anyway right?  After all they've shown interest.  They've been sniffing around for a while.

If you do, your reputation is going to be in for a battering.  Customer service aren't going to be happy, dealing with the fallout. And negative reviews are going to deter future buyers. Not just yours, but the whole sales team's. You are not going to be popular.

So is a short term win worth it? 

Or is it better to just tell them.  Your door flap is perfect for Delilah the Cat.  But it's just not a good fit for a Labrador.  You might be leaving them out in the cold, but you've acted with integrity and they will appreciate your honesty.

What would happen if you referred them to your competitor?
