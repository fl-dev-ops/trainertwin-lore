For Partnerships, Alliances and Ecosystem Leaders and Managers.

Most partnerships don’t fail at signing.
They fail earlier, while everything still feels positive.
You align on intent.
You agree to move.
The relationship feels solid.

What gets left open are the uncomfortable parts you don’t want to press on yet.
Who owns delivery when things get messy.
Where the risk sits when assumptions break.
What happens when incentives stop lining up.
Who absorbs the cost when one side feels it first.

Those gaps don’t stay theoretical.
Once work starts, they harden.
Optimism turns into obligation.
And ownership concentrates where it was never clearly agreed.
By the time it’s visible, reversing it is expensive.
