What if AI works exactly as promised and investors still lose fortunes?

That question matters more than whether AI is a bubble.

Billions are being committed to data centres, chips, power, leases and debt against future customer cash that hasn’t arrived yet. Different investments can look diversified while relying on the same assumption: customers will use enough AI, pay enough for it, and keep paying for long enough to support the commitments being made today.

That may prove completely sound.

But there’s a harder question than “Do we believe in AI?”
How many financial claims are being built on the same future customer cash, who takes the loss if that cash falls short, and when does their response become somebody else’s problem?

I’ve followed the money through some of the biggest AI financing structures I could find.

It changed the question I was asking.
