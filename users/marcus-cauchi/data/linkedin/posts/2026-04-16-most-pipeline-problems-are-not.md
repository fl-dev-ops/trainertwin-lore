Most pipeline problems are not pipeline problems.
They are judgment problems.
Weak questions let bad motion survive.
Better questions expose it before it spreads.

Ask:
Why is this deal here now?
What would need to be true for it to advance without creating risk later?
What, exactly, was promised, and to whom?
Is this account active, or healthy?
Is this expansion driven by proven customer value, or by our pressure to grow too early?
Which paths create compounding value, and which create drag?
Where is risk building before it shows up in the number?
Which exceptions are isolated, and which are evidence the system is drifting?

These questions protect:
- revenue quality
- decision quality
- handoff quality
- trust
- control

Run your current pipeline through this.
Better questions do not slow growth.
They stop bad motion from entering the system disguised as progress.
