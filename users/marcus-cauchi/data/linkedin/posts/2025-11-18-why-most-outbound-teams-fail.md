Why most outbound teams fail...


It’s never one thing.

Leaders dissect messaging, tactics, training, activity, and tech as if each problem lives in isolation. They chase the single culprit.

The truth is: outbound breaks when the system breaks.

One part slips, the rest wobbles.
Two parts slip, the whole machine seizes.

And when they finally look for help, they get fearful—rightfully so—*of a sales mechanic selling something they don’t need.

If this is your challenge:

- Look for someone to diagnose the problem holistically.

- Avoid simple one-off solutions. They don’t work. Training is often the Johnson Rod** that you might not need (yet).

- Find someone you trust. Look to your network for a resource.

- It’s often better and less risky to choose a 3rd party consultant than to hire a new manager.

Consultants are easier to find, hire, fire and they don’t need benefits. They’ll often help you find the right manager when the time comes, too. 

The right consultant is a specialist in diagnosing holistic problems (as they’ve seen them 100x).

A staged improvement approach is often appropriate. Changing everything at once rarely works.

It’s more typical that your outbound engine needs repairs in a certain order.

Bottom line: If your outbound team is struggling, look at the whole system. Don’t get sold a one-off oversimplified part or you might be buying a Johnson Rod.
