BURNOUT IS NOT PROOF OF STRENGTH, IT’S A BROKEN SYSTEM.

Too many leaders wear exhaustion like a badge of honor.
Too many teams are told that suffering is the price of success.
 This is a lie.

Burnout isn’t about working hard, it’s about working in a system designed to break you.

Leaders who demand more but support less.
Workloads that increase while resources shrink.
A culture that rewards overwork but punishes rest.

The strongest teams aren’t the most exhausted, they’re the most aligned.

The best leaders don’t push their people to the brink, they build systems that don’t require burnout to succeed.
