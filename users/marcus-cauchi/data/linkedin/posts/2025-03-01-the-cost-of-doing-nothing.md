The Cost of Doing Nothing

Here’s the trade-off:
Doing nothing costs nothing today – but everything tomorrow.
When laws are designed to strip your rights quietly, your inaction isn’t neutrality – it’s consent.

Every industry, every creative field, every independent thinker – all about to face the same forced choice:
Adapt to rules designed against them
Fight back before those rules become permanent
By the time the real consequences hit, you won’t be negotiating from strength. You’ll be pleading for scraps.

This is why the time to push back isn’t after the damage is done. It’s before they assume you’ll just accept it.

Most people won’t act. But you aren’t most people.
