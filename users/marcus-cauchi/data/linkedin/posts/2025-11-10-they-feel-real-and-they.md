They feel real.

And they are... to the extent you allow them to be.

Thoughts, like nightmares only have the meaning you give them when you're asleep.

The second you wake up, there's a moment of 'oh thank feck I'm in my own bed' and the fear is over.

With your thinking it's a little sneakier than that.

You can think your way into any stress you imagine. You can build it up, you can tear it down.

But rather than good thoughts and bad thoughts - they are nothing more than thoughts.

You give them the meaning, therefore the power they do/don't have.

From your worth, to stress to anxiety and overwhelm.

From unmet expectations of how others 'should' act (in align with how you think they should act of course)

This is all thinking.

And thinking is only as real as you make it.
