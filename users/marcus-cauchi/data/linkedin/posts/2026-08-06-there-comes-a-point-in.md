There comes a point in some careers where the hardest question isn't whether you can do more.

It's whether you've stayed because you still believe in the work... or because you've grown attached to the people.

You tell yourself the timing isn't right. The team still needs you. There are projects to finish, customers to look after and people you don't want to let down. Those reasons are real. That's what makes them so difficult to challenge.
The strange thing is that the longer you care about people, the harder it becomes to know whether you're acting out of loyalty or simply finding reasons not to make a difficult decision.

Most experienced leaders reach that point.

Very few talk about it.

If you've ever wondered whether you're still in the right place for the right reasons, this article may feel more familiar than you'd expect.
