Every day, we’re giving - ideas, empathy, decisions - to kids, clients, notifications, endless demands… and our own minds are left drained.

Exhaustion isn’t just “feeling tired.” 
It’s cognitive fatigue. 
Digital fragmentation. 
Technostress. 

Your brain is working non-stop and rarely gets a chance to receive.

In my latest newsletter, I unpack what your brain is actually missing and how real restoration (not toxic positivity or another app) can bring your energy, clarity, and authority back.

Your brilliance didn’t go anywhere. It’s just buried under demand - and it’s time to reclaim it.
