The Four Tests That Predict Buyer Motion

Great meetings mean nothing if your champion walks out empty-handed.

Before you leave the room, run these four checks:
* Can they repeat the sentence?
* Is the safety visible?
* Is the sequence small and real?
* Can they defend it without you?

If the answer is no to any, your deal’s already stalling.
These are the real readiness tests.
... And they’re not optional.

Deals don’t move on energy.
... They move on design.
