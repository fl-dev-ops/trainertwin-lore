A £50m valuation can stay exactly the same while your economics, authority and options change.

Two founders can agree the same headline number. One rolls little equity, the other rolls a lot. One takes debt, the other doesn't. One retains broad authority over budgets, hiring and future funding; the other needs approval. The valuation hasn't moved. The decision has.
That is why I'd want three answers before I got too attached to the headline number. If the plan misses, what happens to my economics? Which decisions remain mine? Which options do I still have?

A valuation is an important fact about the deal. It doesn't tell me what I am agreeing to live under.
