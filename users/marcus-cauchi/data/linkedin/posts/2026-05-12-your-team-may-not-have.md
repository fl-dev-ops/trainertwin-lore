Your team may not have a pipeline problem.
They may have a progress-definition problem.

A meeting happens, the buyer is warm, the seller feels good, the manager hears confidence, and the forecast gets a little cleaner.
But nothing may have moved inside the buyer’s business. No new owner.
No clearer consequence. No internal action. No decision work. No risk surfaced.

That is where weak forecasts are born.
Not in bad intent.

In the small moment where a useful conversation gets promoted into progress.
So here is the mirror:
Are we measuring buyer movement, or seller relief?
