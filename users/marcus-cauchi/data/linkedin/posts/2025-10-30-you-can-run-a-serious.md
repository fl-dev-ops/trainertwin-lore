You can run a serious business without taking yourself so seriously.

Intensity doesn’t equal importance.
You can be disciplined and still laugh.
 You can demand excellence without fear.

Pressure kills creativity.
 Play revives it.

The best leaders make hard work look light.

 Where could you swap pressure for play next week?
