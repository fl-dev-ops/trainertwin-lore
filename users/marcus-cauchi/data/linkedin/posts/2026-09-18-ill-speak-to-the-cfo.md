“I’ll speak to the CFO.”

A customer says, “I’ll speak to the CFO.” What gets put in the CRM? Because they might say they’ll speak to the CFO, tell you later that they did, bring the CFO to the next meeting, or get the CFO to approve the spend.

Those aren’t four versions of the same thing. One is an intention. One is something the buyer says happened. One is something you can see happened. The last is evidence of an actual decision.

Yet give that information a few handovers and it can easily become “CFO engaged”. Then someone else inherits it. Then the forecast inherits it.

Nobody has to lie. We just keep shaving uncertainty off the original information until what’s left looks much more certain than what the buyer actually told us.
