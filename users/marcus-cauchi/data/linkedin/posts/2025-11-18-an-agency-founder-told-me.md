An agency founder told me, “I used to think we were losing pitches because we needed better ideas.

Or better slides.
Or better storytelling.
Or another late-night sprint.
But that wasn’t it.

We were losing because the decision didn’t feel safe for the buyer.

That’s the bit no one tells you.
You can have the smartest strategy in the room and still lose to a bigger agency with a weaker idea…
because they felt like the safer bet.
Here’s what it looked like for us:

Great meeting.
Lots of nodding.
Smiles.
Energy.
Hope.

Then silence.
Or:
“We loved it… but we’re going in a different direction.”
Or:
“We’re going to pause for now.”

We told ourselves we came second.
But we didn’t.
There is no second.

There’s “yes” and “no reason given.”
Inside the team it created:
- burnout
- doubt
- rewriting the deck again and again
- chasing noise
- over-performing
- under-winning
- holding our breath every quarter

The work wasn’t the issue.
The talent wasn’t the issue.
The effort definitely wasn’t the issue.
The issue was always safety.

If a buyer doesn’t feel safe choosing you, they don’t move.
They delay.
They stall.
They go with someone bigger.
Someone “safer.”
Someone who feels like less risk.

Once we fixed how we guided the decision - not the pitch - everything changed.
Fewer pitches.
Fewer maybes.
More movement.
Better clients.
Calm decisions.
Real forecasts.
The team breathed again.”

If any of this feels familiar:
Send the deck.
Grab a time.
I’ll show you how to find the moment safety cracked -
and how to fix it.

#BondNotBean
