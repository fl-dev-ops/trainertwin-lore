I was called in to help turn things around. The board pack looked clean.

Forecast on track.
Pipeline healthy.
NRR stable.
Delivery “manageable”.

Six months later, the business was fighting margin pressure, renewal risk, and delivery strain nobody thought was there.

Not because the numbers were fake.

Because the friction never reached the board intact.

Weak-fit customers became “strategic revenue”.

Delivery absorbed the cost.
Customer success rescued the accounts.
Finance blended the margin drag.
Leadership preserved confidence.

The board felt safer while the business became less safe.
The danger no one was admitting is REAL RISK can rise while PERCEIVED RISK falls.

Most value erosion starts long before the numbers break.
