THE BEST IDEAS DIE IN LOW-TRUST ROOMS.
* If people are afraid to speak, innovation dies.
* If every move is second-guessed, execution slows.
* If failure is punished, progress stops.

The highest-performing teams aren’t the ones with the smartest people.

They’re the ones where trust moves faster than fear.
* Where people challenge ideas without challenging egos.
* Where failures are treated as feedback loops, not career killers.
* Where truth gets spoken, early, often, and without fear.

If your culture isn’t safe for truth, it’s not safe for growth.

Tag someone who creates fearless execution environments.
