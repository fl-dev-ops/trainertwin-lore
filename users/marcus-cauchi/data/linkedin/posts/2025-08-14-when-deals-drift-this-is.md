When Deals Drift, This Is Usually Why
Rick didn’t lose the deal in the demo.
He lost it in the drift. Polite interest. Positive signals.
“Really helpful call.” ... Then… nothing.

Finally the penny dropped.
“We’ve seem to have done everything right. But I don’t think we’re giving them what they actually came for.”

He was right.
Most teams don’t fail because of weak products. They fail because their message, motion, and method no longer match the customer’s moment.

Rick’s team shifted from selling features to revealing moments, the ones buyers are really reaching for:

✓ Confidence in the decision
✓ Relief from uncertainty
✓ Trust in the direction

And sales cycles shortened. Discovery deepened. Confidence came back.

If you’re seeing ghosting, stalled deals, or “we’ll think about it”...
👉 You’re likely selling to the right problem with the wrong framing.
This post breaks down what high-trust, high-impact teams do instead.
