A common misconception about trust is that it takes time to build.

In reality, only reliability takes time.

The rest is a rapid judgement about decision safety. Buyers quickly assess whether the decision exposes them to risk.

When revenue systems increase that risk, the safest choice becomes no decision, which is why deals stall in the middle of the funnel long before the numbers change.

The earliest signals of trouble rarely appear in revenue. They appear in behaviour.
