MISTRUST SLOWS EVERYTHING.

Distrust is the most expensive tax in any system.
* Leaders who micromanage slow execution.
* Companies that hoard information kill innovation.
* Teams that don’t trust each other waste time covering their backs.

Every extra approval step, every redundant meeting, every process designed for control instead of clarity, it’s all a tax on speed.

The best teams, leaders, and companies?
* They remove friction.
* They communicate with transparency.
* They trust fast, and execute even faster.

If you don’t trust your team, your team can’t trust your leadership.
