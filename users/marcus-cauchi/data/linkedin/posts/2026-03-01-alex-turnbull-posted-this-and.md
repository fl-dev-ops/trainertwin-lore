Alex Turnbull posted this and I thought it worthy of a reshare and some further analysis. Salesforce is down roughly 43 percent from its 52 week high. That is not scandal. It is repricing.

When large cap SaaS compresses like this, the market is not saying the company is broken. It is saying growth assumptions have reset. AI premiums deflate. Margin expansion narratives get retested. Institutional capital rotates.

A P-E of 26 tells you the story clearly. This is no longer hypergrowth. It is a maturing platform being valued on durability and operating leverage.

That shift matters far beyond one stock.

When public markets stop paying a premium for narrative, boards tighten. Investors demand efficiency. Expansion capital becomes selective. Cost per dollar of growth starts to matter more than ecosystem breadth.

This is where most operators miss the signal.

If capital is getting more disciplined at the market level, your revenue engine will be tested next. Flat win rates. Longer cycles. Expensive growth. Those are not just sales metrics. 

They are capital allocation signals. The market is forcing governance.

The real question is whether leadership forces it first.

Multiples compress slowly. Then discipline becomes mandatory. Compounding is not a story. It is maths. When capital tightens, maths wins.
