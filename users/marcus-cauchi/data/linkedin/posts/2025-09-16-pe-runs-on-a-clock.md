PE runs on a clock. Founders don’t.

Here’s the con they’ll run on you:
 “The window is closing.”
 “Valuations won’t last.”
 “De-risk by selling control.”

Translation?
 Their fund cycle is about to expire.
 They must deploy capital or LP trust collapses.
 So they make their chaos your urgency.

But let’s be clear:
 Selling 50–75% doesn’t de-risk you.
 It RE-RISKS you.

Because now your future rides on:
* Debt-loaded bolt-ons.
* EBITDA theatre.
* A 5–7 year flip you don’t control.

That isn’t freedom.
 It’s dependence.

Real de-risking?
* Cash in the bank.
* Dividends that pay you every quarter.
* Resilience that compounds across cycles.
 
Owning the orchard, not begging for a second bite of the apple.
⚡ PE has to deploy. You don’t have to sell.
