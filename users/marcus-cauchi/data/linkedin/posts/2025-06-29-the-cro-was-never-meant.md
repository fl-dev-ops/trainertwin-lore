The CRO Was Never Meant to Exist

The CRO wasn’t born out of a desire to make things better.
The role is a bolt on.

You’re asked to make the numbers.

You inherited a machine built to protect valuation, not create customers.
 And now you’re the human middleware trying to stitch together sales, marketing, finance, and post-sale to do both.
 While the customer drifts further away.

What part of your machine doesn’t trust the rest?
 And what’s it costing you?

I’m Marcus Cauchi.
 The numbers say you’re fine. The feeling says you’re not.
 I build systems that close that gap, for good.
