IF FEEDBACK ONLY GOES ONE WAY, IT’S NOT A CULTURE, IT’S A CONTROL SYSTEM.

Real feedback flows in every direction, not just top-down.
* If leaders can critique but never be challenged, it’s not feedback, it’s control.
* If ideas only get heard based on title, innovation is being silenced.
* If leadership avoids accountability, they can’t expect it from anyone else.

The best teams operate on mutual respect, not rank-based privilege.
* Leaders invite and act on real feedback.
* Employees feel safe pushing back when things don’t make sense.
* Everyone is held to the same standards, no exceptions.

Respect isn’t given because of a title, it’s earned through accountability.
Tag someone who practices what they preach when it comes to feedback.
