When did you last praise someone for taking £500,000 out of the forecast?

Not because they lost the deal. They found out that the evidence no longer supported having it there, and they found out early enough for you to do something about it.

That’s useful information. But what actually happens when someone brings it to us? Do we thank them for finding out early, or ask what went wrong, why the deal slipped, what they’re going to do to get it back and how they’re going to replace the number?

We tell salespeople we want an accurate forecast. I’m convinced we haven't thought hard enough about what we teach them when they bring us bad news early.
