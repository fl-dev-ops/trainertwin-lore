£8 million went into the acquisition case.
Then the operating plan. Then the budget. Then the sales target. Then the refinancing case.

Eighteen months later, the CRO can only support £5.5 million from what customers are actually doing.

Nobody lied. Nobody hid the uncertainty. The original investment paper even said that scale and timing were assumptions.
The warning signs looked enough like execution problems that fixing execution kept making sense.

Until somebody finally pulled up the original investment paper and asked:
“So when did eight become eight?”
Nobody could answer.

If you’re a CEO, CFO, CRO, Chair or PE operator, think about the numbers currently driving your hiring, spending, financing and board expectations.
What happens when the evidence underneath one of them changes before everything relying on it does?

This is the meeting that follows.
