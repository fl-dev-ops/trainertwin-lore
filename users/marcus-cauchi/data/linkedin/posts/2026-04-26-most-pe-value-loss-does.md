Most PE value loss does not begin with a missed plan.

It begins while the board pack still looks acceptable.
Revenue is growing. EBITDA is explainable. Forecast is intact. Management sounds confident.

Underneath, the asset may already be drifting:
NRR softening
CAC payback stretching
Capacity nearing saturation
Margin leakage expanding
Pipeline quality weakening

What looks manageable internally can become expensive at exit.

A £5m EBITDA gap at a 10x multiple is £50m of enterprise value not captured.
That can flow into weaker DPI, thinner carry, harder LP conversations, and a weaker next-fund story.

The most expensive PE problems rarely arrive loudly.
They arrive as plausible explanations.

I wrote about why years 2–4 of hold decide whether value is built, recovered, or surrendered.
