Pipeline babysitting is not coaching.

You don’t have a pipeline problem.
You have a coaching system that’s quietly rotting your forecast.

Here’s the lie most GTM teams are built on:

“If reps are busy and managers are coaching, we’re good.”

But look closer:

Reps chase deals they should’ve killed
Managers spend 1:1s collecting updates
Forecasts slip, quarter after quarter
Nobody’s thinking, just executing

This is what I call pipeline babysitting.
It creates noise. Dependency. False confidence.
And it burns everyone out while pretending to help.
 
Here’s the flip:

Your best reps don’t need scripts, they need permission to think.
Your best managers don’t chase, they shape judgment.

When GTM teams get this right, everything shifts:

* Pre-mortems before pipeline reviews
* Decision logs over vibe-based coaching
* Second-order thinking baked into every call
* Managers who coach for truth, not just movement

The moment your managers stop refereeing and start teaching judgment,your GTM motion stops leaking trust, and starts scaling without you.
 
This is the core of The Ally Method™.

It’s how enterprise GTM teams across Systems Integrators, MSPs, SaaS, and govtech are:
•	Killing zombie deals
•	Raising forecast believability
•	And finally getting their managers out of “reactive babysitting mode”

Message me if your GTM team looks busy, but the numbers feel off.
