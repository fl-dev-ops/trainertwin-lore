The Hidden Taxes of Bad Measurement
Every business pays them.
Most never see them.

Part 1: The Activity Tax
The Activity Tax starts when leaders mistake motion for progress. Calls, emails, meetings, demos and proposals become the scorecard, so people naturally optimise for being busy instead of helping customers move towards a good decision.
Nothing is wrong with activity. It simply isn’t the outcome. The cost appears elsewhere. Sellers chase numbers instead of judgement. Managers coach dashboards instead of customers. Buyers receive more attention but not necessarily more value, while good opportunities are neglected because everyone is trying to hit today’s activity target.
The better question isn’t, “How busy are we?” It’s, “What helped the customer make meaningful progress today?” Activity supports outcomes. It should never replace them.
