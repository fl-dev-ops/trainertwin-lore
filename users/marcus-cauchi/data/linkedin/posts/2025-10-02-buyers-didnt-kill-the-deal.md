Buyers Didn’t Kill the Deal. You Did.

They didn’t ghost. You lost them the second your rep flinched - and your manager did nothing (useful or helpful).

Why? Because your “coach” is actually a spreadsheet babysitter. 

Your system trains for box-ticking, not behaviour-shifting. So when reps hear, “We’re holding off,” they panic. Default to safe. And your buyer? They disengage silently.

This isn’t a talent gap. It’s a trust leak, engineered by the culture YOU tolerate!

Great Front Line Managers don’t fix reps. They rewire response loops. They decode silence. They teach friction fluency. They don’t “check in.” They intervene, at the moment of stall, not after the forecast explodes.

You think cutting managers saves budget?
No! Cutting them trades institutional memory for dashboard theatre.
Your margin's bleeding not from headcount, but from system noise, frozen reps, and deals that rot before anyone notices.

Your managers aren’t “middle layer.” They’re the first to see decay and the last you should ignore.

Fix them, or prepare to scale delusion at cost.
