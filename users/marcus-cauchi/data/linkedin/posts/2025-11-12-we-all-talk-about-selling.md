We all talk about “selling value,” but pressure makes most of us forget what that means.

This story isn’t about one bad deal, it’s about how easy it is to trade your integrity for relief.

If you’ve ever cut price just to end the pain, this will sting a little. And that’s the point. It’s a lived reminder that fear and ego run more deals than strategy does. It shows what happens when you teach buyers not to trust you, and how to earn that trust back.

If you lead a team that hits target but bleeds margin, read this. 

If you’ve ever looked back on a “win” that turned into a service nightmare, read this twice.

The moment you feel pressure, stop selling. Start listening. That’s where the truth starts to talk.
