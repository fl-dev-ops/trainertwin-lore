Most people talk about leadership like it is confidence or charm. 

It isn’t. It is what you do in the moment you feel pressure hit and your mind wants to rush. Nobody teaches that part. You are meant to work it out on your own. Some do. Most don’t. And almost everyone pays for it at some point.

I’ve spent years talking with leaders about this one moment. Not the big speeches. Not the strategies. The small, sharp moments where you either steady yourself or get pulled off course.

If you have ever felt that shift in your chest and thought, “I should know what to do here, but I don’t” this will make sense to you.

I wrote about that moment, why it matters and how people actually grow through it.
