DRIFT KILLS VALUE: Your Blind Spot is Costing Millions

If your NRR slides 1% at £30m ARR, you’ve just erased £12m in exit value. Still think it’s your team’s fault?

This isn’t bad luck. This is drift.
Drift is what happens when leaders anchor in the wrong place: product, pitch, spreadsheet, instead of the customer.

Drift looks like:
 – Sales logged, revenue flat.
 – Marketing loud, pipeline dead.
 – Retention leaking, cash burning, board confidence gone.

Drift feeds off broken values: vanity metrics, founder-as-bottleneck, spreadsheet theatre, rewarding effort instead of impact.

Inside the buyer, the forces don’t care about your story:
 – Problem Owners scream for relief, you hand them features.
 – Outcome Owners want results, you give activity.
 – Risk Avoiders whisper “too dangerous” and the deal dies.
 – Process Owners defend inertia.
 – Invisible influencers kill you in Slack at 9:17pm: “This feels like risk, not relief.”

The evidence from 5,000+ deals across £10bn of spend. The pattern never changes: if you don’t anchor in your customer, drift erases your margin, valuation, and future.
