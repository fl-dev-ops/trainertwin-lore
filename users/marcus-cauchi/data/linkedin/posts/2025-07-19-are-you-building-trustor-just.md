Are You Building Trust,Or Just Hunting Your Next Meal?

Drop the pretense.

I’ve been the target in your so-called “hunter vs farmer” game for years.
First, I get chased, tracked down by a rep eager to hit quota, promising the moon. It feels transactional from the start. You call it “prospecting.” I call it being stalked.

The second I say yes? Suddenly, you vanish. Someone new pops up, “the farmer”, who knows nothing about what I value, why I bought, or the promises you made. Now I’m a plot of land to be managed, not a partner to be understood.

You call this “best practice.”
I call it #trusterosion by design.

Here’s what you miss when you keep playing this game:

I Don’t Want to Be a Trophy or a Crop
I want to be a customer, a partner, heard, remembered, recognised. When you hunt me, I feel like a prize. When you farm me, I feel like an asset. Either way, I’m not a person in your system. I’m just a number in your pipeline.

Your Handoff Feels Like Abandonment
The moment you “pass me down the funnel,” I lose trust. The context, the relationship, and the intent, gone. The new face has no idea what you promised me. And now I have to repeat my story, or worse, fight to get what was sold.

I See Your Shortcuts, And I Don’t Forget
When you chase quick wins, I see it. When you vanish after the close, I remember. When your “farmer” doesn’t understand me, I don’t complain, I just quietly look for someone else who gets it.

You’re Losing Me Before You Even Notice
You call my departure “churn.” I call it escaping a system that treated me like a transaction. I don’t tell you I’m gone until I already am. I just stop answering, stop renewing, stop referring.
