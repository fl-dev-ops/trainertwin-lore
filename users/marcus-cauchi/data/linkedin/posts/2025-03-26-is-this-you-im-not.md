Is this you?

🧨 I'm not sinking. I just stopped pretending this ship was built to sail.

It’s not a job.
 It’s a submarine.
 And not the stealth kind with purpose and precision.

This one’s patchworked, pressurised, and powered by panic.
 The oxygen’s thin.
 The floor’s wet.
 And I’m expected to save it, sell from it, and smile while I do it.

Leadership’s above deck yelling:
 “FASTER! PIPELINE! TRUST THE SYSTEM!”

But they’re not in the engine room.

They don’t see the misfit metrics.
 The non-buyers we pretend are ICP.
 The trust we rebuild mid-call, after the process fails again.

I’m the one keeping us afloat:
Rewriting strategy in real time
Translating chaos into buyer confidence

Building outcomes from fragments while shielding the client from the churn instinct.

This isn’t burnout.
 It’s erosion.
 Of clarity.
 Of craft.
 Of belief that leadership knows, or cares, what the pressure feels like below deck.

I know I’m not failing.
 I’m just done mistaking endurance for alignment.

I know my value.
 I pay for myself.
 And I’m not here to patch a system that treats intuition as a liability.

What part of your job used to feel like a gift… and now feels like a tax?
And what would it take to make your name mean something again, in a system that deserves you?

Tag the manager who actually gets it. The one who’s been in the engine room, fixed the leak, and still brought biscuits to the post-mortem.

Everyone else? They’re probably on Deck 1, scheduling another stand-up to steal pipeline from next quarter to make up the revenue shortfall!

If you believe sales exists to serve customers before shareholders and you want to sell without burnout, compromising your integrity or doing things you feel are immoral and wrong, DM me or comment "Sales is a force for good"
