Most people aren’t lost.
 They’re just running on someone else’s definition of “success.”

They optimise schedules.
 Chase goals.
 Build faster.

But never stop to ask the one question that would change everything:
“What do I actually want?”

I wrote something for the founders, creatives, and operators who are done drifting toward urgency, and ready to design something more honest, more intentional, and more aligned.

It’s not a mindset post.
 It’s not a to-do list.
 It’s a mirror and a compass.
