AUTHORITY IS RENTED. TRUST IS OWNED.

Titles don’t grant trust. Pay-cheques don’t buy belief.
You can be the CEO, the founder, the boss, and still have ZERO REAL INFLUENCE.

Authority is temporary. Trust is compound interest.
 Control is assigned. Leadership is earned.

The fastest-moving, highest-performing teams don’t operate on hierarchy, they operate on belief.

If your leadership disappears when your title does, you were never leading.

Tag someone who earns trust instead of renting authority.
