You didn’t lose the sale. You ignored the signal.
You didn’t ghost that lead because you lacked time.
 You ghosted because something felt off, and you didn’t trust yourself enough to name it.
That’s not a funnel problem.
 That’s a drift problem.

Suzanne shared something powerful this morning on micro-successes in sales, the small, consistent wins that build trust and momentum.

She’s right.
 But here’s the shadow side no one wants to touch:

You’re not just one big win away.
 You’re one small truth away, from either traction or corrosion.

Because it’s not the big mistakes that kill integrity.
 It’s the quiet micro-abandonments we dress up as discipline:
 * The follow-up you “postponed” that you never meant to send
 * The outreach you skipped because your gut already knew they weren’t aligned
 * The customer insight you buried because it made the quarter’s numbers look bad
 * That whisper that said, “this isn’t us anymore”, and you muted it

Micro-success builds trust.
 Micro-avoidance builds rot, creates subtle insidious drift.
And rot doesn’t show up in dashboards.
 It shows up in doubt. Team drift. Ghosted deals. Energy leaks.

So yes, celebrate the 10 minutes of courageous action.
 But start tracking the 10 seconds of conscious avoidance too.
That’s where the signal lives.
 That’s where the rebuild begins.

DM me “Drift Diagnostic.”
 No funnel. No pitch.

Imagine, a 3-minute tool to surface where small, misaligned choices are compounding, so you can course-correct before the brand, team, or your soul pays the price.
