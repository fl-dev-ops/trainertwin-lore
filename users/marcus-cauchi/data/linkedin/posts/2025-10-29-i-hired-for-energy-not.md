I hired for energy, not judgment.

I wanted momentum - people who could “move fast.”
 And they did. Straight into the wrong fires.

Everything looked busy.
 Nothing was moving forward.

Turns out speed without sense just burns fuel.

I didn’t need more energy.
 I needed better judgment.

The team that changed everything wasn’t the loudest -
 they were the calmest.

Now I hire for clarity over charisma. 
 Trust over experience
 Consistency over hype.

Momentum’s easy.
 Direction’s rare.
