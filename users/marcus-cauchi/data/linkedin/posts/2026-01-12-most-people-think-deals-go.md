Most people think deals go quiet because the buyer lost interest.

Wrong problem.

In complex sales and partnerships, the real danger is not silence. It is exposure. Someone has to walk into a second room and defend what you said to people who were not there. Finance, Risk, Legal, Ops, peers, reputation. If they cannot survive that room, nothing moves.

This is not a sales issue. It is a decision issue.

My latest piece breaks down why good meetings stall, why buyers vanish without rejecting you, and why pushing harder makes things worse. It explains the second room, internal exposure, and the aftermath test, and why training the internal sale matters more than polishing the pitch.

If you work in strategic sales, enterprise partnerships, asset management, or founder-led complex deals, you have seen this pattern for years. Nobody names it. So deals stall, forecasts crumble, and everyone invents reasons.

If you want deals that move without pressure, get good at the part nobody trains for: making the decision survivable.

Read it. Think about your pipeline. Send it to someone who lives in second rooms for a living.
