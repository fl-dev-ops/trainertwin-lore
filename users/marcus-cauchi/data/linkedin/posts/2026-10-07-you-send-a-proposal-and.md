You send a proposal, and then the customer goes quiet. Suddenly the teams asking: when do we follow up? Who should call? Is this still in the forecast? 

Before you do any of that, go back to what was agreed before you sent the proposal. Did the customer know what they wanted to change? Who'd have to implement it? What it would cost? Who could sign it off? If any of that was still unclear, your sponsor might now be stuck. 

They have to sell this to their colleagues, and they cant explain it well yet. 

Heres the thing: silence doesnt tell you which of those is the problem. They might just be busy. They might be looking at another option. They might be struggling to get people on board, or they might have stopped pursuing it altogether. You cant tell from the outside. 

So yes, another email can help, but only if it makes replying easier. If you keep asking for an update, youre just giving them more work. 

Ask them straight: is this still a live decision? Is there something that needs sorting out? Or have you decided to stop? Make it easy to say no, and dont make them book a meeting to do it. 

And until you hear back, dont pretend you know why they've gone quiet. Judge the forecast on the evidence you actually have.
