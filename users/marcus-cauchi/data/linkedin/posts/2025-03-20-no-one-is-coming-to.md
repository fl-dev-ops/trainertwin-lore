NO ONE IS COMING TO PICK YOU.

Waiting for recognition? A promotion? Permission?
 No one is coming to anoint you.

The people winning aren’t waiting to be chosen.
* They show up, deliver, and make their value undeniable.
* They put themselves in the arena, whether they feel ready or not.
* They build, ship, execute, before anyone gives them the title.

You don’t get chosen. You take ground.

Tag someone who moves without waiting.
