I keep running into founders who look fine from a distance but tired when you get a couple of minutes with them. Not burned out. Not failing. Just carrying more of the load than anyone realises.

One of them said, “The business is growing, but it feels like more effort than ever.” He wasn’t complaining. He was just telling the truth.

It’s never one big thing.
It’s the small stuff that starts piling up. A decision that should be light taking more breath than it should. A handoff that needs another look. A week that feels off in a way the numbers don’t explain.

Founders tend to brush it off. They’re good at that. But the weight usually means something. It’s often the first sign the system is stretching past the rhythm it was built on.

I’m going to spend a bit of time on this. Nothing dramatic. Because it’s more common than people admit, and a lot less personal than it feels.
