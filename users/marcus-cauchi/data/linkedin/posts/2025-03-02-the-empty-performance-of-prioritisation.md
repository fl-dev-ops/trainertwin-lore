The Empty Performance of “Prioritisation”
(You’re Not Prioritising - You’re Just Kicking the Can Down the Road)

Leaders love to talk about prioritisation.

But most of what they call “prioritisation” is actually avoidance.
🔹 Pushing “big rocks” to next quarter (because they feel too complex to tackle now).
🔹 Overweighting “urgent” tasks that look productive but are actually stalling momentum.
🔹 Framing inaction as “waiting for the right time” - which never comes.

📌 Where is “prioritisation” just an excuse for non-decision?
📌 What’s sitting in the backlog that’s actually the single biggest leverage point?
📌 What are you afraid to move on, because moving means accountability?

Sellers who achieve stellar performance, leaders who execute excellently, and businesses that scale make hard calls.

If it’s critical, it moves now. No excuses.
