You tell your team to delegate. But you can’t even delegate your own worry.

You’ve built a machine that runs on your adrenaline.
The team looks calm because you never let the panic show.
But inside, every win feels like a delay before the next fire.

That’s not leadership. That’s survival on repeat.

The truth?
You’re addicted to being needed.
Because when they need you, you still matter.
And when they don’t… you’re forced to ask what’s left of you beyond the company.

That’s why it’s so hard to let go.
It’s not about trust in them.
It’s about identity in you.

You don’t have to become “zen” overnight.
Just pick one problem this week - and let it stay unsolved for a day.
See what happens when you don’t rescue it.

You might find the team steps up.
And the world keeps turning without your fingerprints on every decision.
