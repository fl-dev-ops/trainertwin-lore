You can be excellent at your job and still reach the point where you no longer trust your own judgement.

That is rarely because you have suddenly become less capable.

A new CEO arrives. Investors want a different number. Your mandate shifts without being rewritten. You remain accountable while authority moves elsewhere. People start deciding what needs to change before you have even been asked what is actually happening.

Soon the question is no longer only whether you can do the job. It is whether the job still exists in the form you accepted.

Should you stay and reshape it?
Should you prepare to leave?
Or are you about to make a permanent decision based on temporary pressure, politics and missing information?

This article is for senior commercial leaders facing that exact moment.
Read it before someone else makes the decision for you.
