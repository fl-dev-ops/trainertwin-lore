Your pipeline isn’t light. It’s bloated with maybes you rushed onto the calendar.
The “<5-minute cold call, just book the meeting” mantra sounds efficient. More dials. More meetings. More pipeline.

But look closer:
No-shows pile up.
AEs waste hours re-running discovery.
Forecasts swell with false positives.
SDRs never learn commercial judgment, just how to chase a yes.
 
A cold call isn’t about minutes. It’s about meaning. The job isn’t to grab time, it’s to earn commitment that deserves time.

Here’s the sharper test:
* Purpose → What are they actually trying to achieve this quarter?
* Priority → Is it urgent and possible, or just “nice to have”?
* Person → Who else needs to see value before it’s real?
* Path → What’s the natural next step if we’re aligned?

If you don’t have Purpose, Priority, Person, and Path, the meeting isn’t a win, it’s a pipeline poltergeist!

Interest ≠ intent ≠ readiness ≠ commitment. 

Almost anyone can stuff a calendar. Skilled prospectors protect AEs from dead work and build immediate viable and medium-term pipeline, not just this quarter’s spreadsheet.

Because the real job isn’t motion. It’s momentum.
Speed that skips qualification isn’t efficient, it’s deferred waste.

So ask yourself: was your last call truly timely, relevant, and valuable … or just another irrelevant interruption in the book-a-meeting game?

If it’s revenue you want, qualify for relevance and commitment, not calendar filler.
