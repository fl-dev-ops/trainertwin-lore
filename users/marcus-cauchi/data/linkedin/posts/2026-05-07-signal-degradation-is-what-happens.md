Signal degradation is what happens when operating truth loses force as it travels upward.

A customer-fit concern becomes an implementation delay.
A margin issue becomes a temporary resourcing problem.
A weak deal becomes a timing issue.
A churn signal becomes account noise.
A delivery failure becomes a customer expectation problem.

Each translation may look reasonable.
Together, they make board decisions less safe.

The board may still receive data.
But data is not the same as operating truth.
