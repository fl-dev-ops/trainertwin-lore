You don’t have a revenue strategy.
- You have a headcount ritual.

Most companies still treat revenue like a supply-side problem:
- Hire a CRO, pump pipeline, pray the spreadsheet closes.

But when pricing lives in Finance…
- When CS is chasing NPS, not cash…
- When Marketing is “support,” not system design…

Your CRO isn’t leading.
- They’re cleaning up after your org chart.

This post calls time on the theatre.
- It shows why discounting is a confession, not a strategy.
- Why “saves” are a symptom of design failure.
- And why the only GTM that scales is one that earns trust before the negotiation even begins.

If you're still tying growth to bookings, not revenue quality,
- If you're still letting Finance veto price enforcement,
- If you think “Sales owns the number” is a strategy,
- You're not scaling. You're surrendering.

It’s not just provocative.
- It’s painfully accurate.

And if it makes you flinch, it’s probably talking about you.
