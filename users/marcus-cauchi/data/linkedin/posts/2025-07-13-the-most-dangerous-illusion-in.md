The most dangerous illusion in PE-backed companies?
A credible CEO.
Impressive VPs
And no one actually managing the business
No rhythm
No accountability
No grip
Everyone reports up. No one coaches down
What looks like a leadership problem is almost always structural or systemic dysfunction

How it shows up:
Sales teams fixated on base pay. Variable feels like fiction
CRM full of deals that will never close, never even moved
Pipeline reviews that protect theatre, not truth
VPs who performed well in big companies, but never coach a rep or lead a recovery
Marketing busy, brilliant, but invisible at the point of sale
CEO in the dark, watching numbers slide while listening to the usual lies and excuses as everyone plays CYA

This is not drift. It is design failure
Weak middle management. And without it, nothing scales
No amount of talent fixes a system that tolerates theatre

Here is how we fix it
I don't run advisory. I don't send decks.
I rebuild execution from the inside out
Three moves. Forty-five days. Results visible within one quarter

1. Diagnose
We start with live pipeline triage
Every deal interrogated
Real buyer, last signal, blocker, next step
No slides. No excuses
Most forecasts fall apart in the first ten minutes
We test the senior team
Not on what they know. On what they build
Are they setting standards, coaching to truth, and creating forward motion?
Or are they hired muscle from bigger firms, protecting their seat while the business stalls?
We cut through the noise and name what is blocking performance

2. Rebuild
We do not reshuffle titles
We build the layer that never existed
Weekly deal reviews based on buyer behaviour
Commercial standards that cannot be misinterpreted
Coaching loops that force clarity
Messaging grounded in what customers want, not what the company wishes
Promotion based on results, not theatrics
We don't assume 
No heroics. Facts
We identify what is working, promote it, and remove what doesn't help you win
Within weeks:
Pipeline accuracy triples
Managers start managing
Salespeople believe again
The business moves from noise to signal

3. Prevent
You do not need transformation
You need a system that cannot drift
You get:
Truth enforced at the edge
Standards reinforced at every level
Forecasting based on evidence, not narrative
Customer insight collected, shared, and acted on
Commercial accountability built into the rhythm of the business
What this creates is not a motivational shift. It is a structural shift
EBITDA moves not because it was targeted, but because it becomes inevitable

What we actually do
We embed. Together, we rebuild the system. We leave behind a commercial rhythm that performs without us

Not strategy. Not theory
Execution
If your portco has padded pipeline, underpowered leadership and a CEO being bamboozled by people who aren't close to the work
You don't just need a reorg. You need a spine
