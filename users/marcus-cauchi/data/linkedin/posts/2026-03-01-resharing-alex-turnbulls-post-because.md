Resharing Alex Turnbull’s post because this is where 5M to 20M businesses drift without realising.

Revenue milestones feel like progress. Ownership economics determines whether that progress compounds or simply scales complexity.

In this range, most decisions feel reversible. Pricing can be adjusted next quarter. Customer mix can be cleaned up later. Cost discipline can wait until scale.

It rarely does.

By the time founders start thinking about exit value, EBITDA quality, revenue per rep and cash conversion were shaped much earlier.

Scale amplifies the model you built. It does not correct it.

If revenue has grown but margin quality and revenue per rep have not meaningfully improved over the last couple of years, that is not momentum. It is structural drift.

Compounding is designed early or not at all. Looking back over your recent growth phase, what actually improved as you scaled?
