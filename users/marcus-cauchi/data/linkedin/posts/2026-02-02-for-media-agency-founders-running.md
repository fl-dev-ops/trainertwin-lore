For Media Agency Founders

Running an agency means living in the space between what was sold and what has to be delivered.
You feel it when a new client comes in that looks fine on paper but already needs exceptions.
When senior people start getting pulled into accounts they were never meant to babysit.
When margin erosion doesn’t come from bad work, but from work that was never properly bounded.

Most of this doesn’t come from bad intent or weak teams.
It comes from moments where momentum mattered more than precision.
- A pitch where scope stayed flexible to keep things moving.
- A renewal where hard questions were deferred.
- A “we’ll sort that later” that quietly became permanent.
- By the time delivery pushes back, the decision is already embedded.
Saying no now costs more than saying yes did earlier.

My work with agency founders is about intervening upstream, in the conversations where these decisions actually form. Not to slow growth, but to stop growth from quietly taxing the business, the team, and you.

The goal isn’t cleaner process.
It’s fewer situations where your best people are compensating for choices that didn’t need to be made that way.
