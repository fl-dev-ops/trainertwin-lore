Just because a seller uncovers pain, budget, and decision
or runs a clean BANT or MEDDPICC checklist
does not mean a prospect is qualified.

It means answers were available.

Qualification is not about whether information exists.
It is about whether a decision can actually be made.

Pain can be real without being actionable.
Budget can exist without being available.
Decision authority can be named without being usable.

A seller can be perfectly aligned to their process
and still be out of sync with the buyer’s reality.

Nothing is technically wrong.
And yet nothing is ready.

Sales has a process.
Buyers have a moment.

Those two are often mistaken for the same thing.
