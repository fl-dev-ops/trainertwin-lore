A £20m offer does not necessarily require a £20m decision today.

Once a transaction gathers momentum, the choices can start to feel binary: take the deal or lose it. There may be more versions than that. You can accept, accept subject to a condition, change one term, take less money, stage part of the commitment, retain one approval right, delay one irreversible step, wait or decline.

The useful question is not always, "Should we do this deal?" It may be, "How much of this deal does the evidence justify us agreeing to now?"

Opportunity size and decision size are not the same thing. You do not have to make the decision as large as the opportunity.
