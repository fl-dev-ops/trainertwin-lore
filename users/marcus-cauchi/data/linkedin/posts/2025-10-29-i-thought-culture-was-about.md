I thought culture was about perks.

Friday beers. Ping pong. “We’re a family.”

But culture isn’t what you bribe people with.
 It’s what you tolerate when no one’s watching.

We had fun ... but we avoided truth.
 We praised effort over output.
 We confused kindness with comfort.

When standards slipped, I called it “learning.”
 It was actually fear - fear of being the bad guy.

The day I started protecting performance,
 people started respecting the culture.

Perks fade.
... Standards scale.
