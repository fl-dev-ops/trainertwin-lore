IF A COMPANY WON’T LET YOU CANCEL EASILY, IT’S A RED FLAG.

Ever notice how signing up is instant, but cancelling is a nightmare?
* They hide the button.
* They force you to call.
* They guilt-trip you into staying.

Good businesses don’t hold you hostage, they make staying worth it.
* They let you leave because they trust you’ll come back.
* They don’t rely on traps to keep customers, they rely on value.

If canceling is hard, assume they know their product isn’t good enough.
Tag a company that lets you leave on your terms.
