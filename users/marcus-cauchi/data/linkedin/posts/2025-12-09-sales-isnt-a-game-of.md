Sales isn’t a game of shortcuts, it’s a reflection of your values. Every call, every email, every conversation tells your buyer who you are. So, who are you? The salesperson chasing quotas, rushing deals, and burning bridges? Or the one who listens, serves, and solves problems?

These 13 cardinal sins of selling call out the mistakes that repel buyers and destroy trust. They’re not just bad habits, they’re toxic behaviours that keep you stuck in mediocrity. 

But here’s the upside: every sin is an opportunity to recalibrate, reconnect, and rebuild.

This isn’t about pointing fingers,it’s about taking ownership. The customer doesn’t owe you their time, trust, or money. You have to earn it, one honest conversation at a time.

Read the full post to uncover how to ditch the bad habits, show up with integrity, and build partnerships that last. Then ask yourself:

What kind of salesperson do I aspire to be?

Which of these sins will you commit to eliminating this week? Let’s talk about how we can do better, together.
