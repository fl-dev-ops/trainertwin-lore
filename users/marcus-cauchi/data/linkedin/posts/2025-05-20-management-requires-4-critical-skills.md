Management requires 4 critical skills:

1. Hire the best people
2. Create the conditions and systems so they can do their best work every day
3. Protect them from acts of idiocy from above (including and especially you)
4. Empower them to make good decisions well, even under pressure. And to empower others (customers, peers, partners) to make good decisions well.

That is it. The rest is lipstick on a pig ... oink!
