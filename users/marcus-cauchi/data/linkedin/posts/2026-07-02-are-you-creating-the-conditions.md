Are you creating the conditions for your buyer to make the best possible decision for themselves? Decision safety is not a nice to have. It is at the heart of why so many deals stall. 

A buyer may love your product or service at a functional level but if they perceive risk at a political or personal level, you will be ghosted or lose to "do nothing".

Your job is less about discovering pain and more about understanding the better future your buyer is looking to rent. Risk is processed on those 3 levels and assessed as affordable, not affordable and compulsory. These factors define whether your "sponsor" will take the fight internally and how hard they will fight to have you win.

Manipulation, false urgency, pressing the pain, having them talk about what they are dissatisfied with their current solution or provider do have noticeable, measurable short term impact. But are they why a buyer will decide to chaneg or change now. 

Read this article by Suzanne C. and ask yourself, "Are we acting as our buyer's ally or as their accomplice or adversary?"
