Sales shouldn’t feel unsafe - for buyers or for us as sellers.

I’ve been working on something that turns that idea into data.
It’s called the #BuyerSafetyAudit - a 5-minute diagnostic that measures how safe people feel when they buy from you.

It’s built on David Maister Rob Galford Charles H. Green’s #TrustEquation - credibility, reliability, intimacy, and low self-orientation - plus the buyer’s sense of risk and fear based on Darryl Stickel, PhD's excellent work on #Trust.

Before I release it publicly, I want to test it with real humans, in real conversations.

I need 50 volunteers - salespeople, founders, coaches - who believe that trust isn’t “soft,” it’s the system.

Here’s the deal:
You’ll get your own Buyer Safety Dashboard.
You’ll see your personal trust–risk ratio.
You’ll help validate what actually drives buyer confidence.
No gimmicks. No data harvesting. Just real learning, and a chance to make sales feel human again.

If that sounds like something you’d contribute to, comment “I’m in” or DM me “Buyer Safety.”

I’ll send you the details of what's required, a private link and a quick guide. 
Let’s make trust measurable, without losing what makes it matter.
