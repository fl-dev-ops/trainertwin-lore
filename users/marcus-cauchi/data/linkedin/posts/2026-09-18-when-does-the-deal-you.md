When does the deal you signed stop being the deal you approved?

At signing, everyone may agree on the plan. Then another debt facility is added, more capital is needed, the board changes, management changes, your role narrows, the exit moves out or the operating plan is reset. None of those changes automatically means something has gone wrong.

But at some point the arrangement you are living under may be materially different from the one you originally agreed to. If this arrangement came back for approval today, would you still agree to it on the same basis?

An old approval is evidence that a decision once made sense. It isn't permanent permission for every version that follows.
