Your CRM can tell you when a deal was created, when it changed stage, when the close date moved and when it was eventually won or lost.

That’s one history. The other is harder to see: what did the evidence available at each of those moments actually justify believing?

Those two histories can separate. While they’re apart, forecasts, hiring, capacity and cash decisions may rely on a recorded position the available evidence no longer supports.

Pick a deal you know well and reconstruct both histories.
How long were you making decisions in the gap?
