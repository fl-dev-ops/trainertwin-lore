Before you believe the promise, test the consequence.

What does it actually change?
Who benefits?
Who pays?
What has to go right for it to work?
What happens if it succeeds exactly as intended?
What happens if three assumptions fail at once?
And who can stop it if the people in charge refuse to?

Political arguments get easier when you stop arguing about motives and start testing decisions.

Problem. Cause. Remedy. Evidence. Cost. Dependency. Power. Consequence. Reversibility.

Nobody asking for power should get to skip the test.
