I once watched the gap between an average Account Manager and a top 1% operator in real time.

Three weeks from launch, deadlines slipping, procurement stalling, someone muttering curses at a spreadsheet full of red cells. The average AM froze.

Then there was Jenny. She already knew her client’s cast of characters, and their pressure points. Two weeks earlier she’d sent procurement every doc they’d need, locked in the only review slot that worked for all decision-makers, and built a contingency plan with ops.

So when chaos hit, she didn’t flinch. The team launched on time, saving five days and thousands in fees.

The rest wait for storms. The top 1% build the lifeboat while the sun’s still out.
