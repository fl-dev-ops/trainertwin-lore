I agree with a lot of what Jarred Young says in this post, especially the move from summarisation to orchestration and the idea that teams will get smaller and faster.

Where I see things get real is underneath all of that. Speed is no longer the constraint. Accountability is.

As systems become more agentic, actions happen quickly and often correctly. But when operators can't clearly explain why a system acted the way it did on pricing, timing, or targeting, trust starts to weaken. First internally. Then with buyers.

That is where I see teams slow down. 

Not because automation does not work, but because it moves faster than the organisation’s ability to stand behind its decisions.

The teams that really compound next year will not just automate execution. They will be deliberate about where automation is allowed to act and where it has to justify itself.

Volume will keep increasing. Attention will not.
That gap is where most GTM systems will either break or differentiate.
