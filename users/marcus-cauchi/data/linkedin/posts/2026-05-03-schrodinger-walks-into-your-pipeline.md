Schrodinger walks into your pipeline review.

Looks at your commits and says, "Interesting. These deals are both closed and dead at the same time."

The CRM says they are real. The forecast says you are on track. Everyone nods because opening the box before month-end would be terribly inconvenient. And no one has tested what happens when the buyer has to defend the decision without you in the room.

So the deal sits there. Alive in your pipeline. Dead in their organisation. And you don't know which one it is.

Until the quarter ends and reality opens the box for you.

Suddenly, the number you defended doesn't survive scrutiny.

If your forecast only works while your seller is in the room, it is not a forecast. It's a thought experiment.
