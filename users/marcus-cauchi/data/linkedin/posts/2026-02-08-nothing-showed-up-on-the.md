Nothing showed up on the dashboard.
The work was still happening.
Time was still being spent.

Coordination was still increasing.
But none of it appeared where decisions get reviewed.
No number jumped.
No report drew attention.

Nothing crossed a threshold that demanded a conversation.
So nothing looked wrong.

The effort lived between teams.
In follow-ups.
In handoffs.
In the small adjustments that kept everything stable.
It didn’t feel like cost.
It felt like doing the job properly.

As predictability set in, reassurance followed.
If things were holding,
there was no reason to look again.

Not because anyone ignored the work.
Because it never showed up where legitimacy is decided.
What isn’t registered doesn’t escalate.

What doesn’t escalate doesn’t get reconsidered.
So the work continues without review.
Not hidden.
Just uncaptured.

Most organisations don’t dismiss effort.
They simply don’t account for the kind
that exists to keep things from breaking.

And what never forces a second look
silently becomes permanent.
