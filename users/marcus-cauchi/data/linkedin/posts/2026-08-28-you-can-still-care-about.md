You can still care about the people, believe in the business and know the role no longer fits the way it once did.

That’s what makes the decision difficult. There may be people relying on you, work you haven’t finished, decent money, years invested and plenty about the place you still value. Leaving has consequences. So does staying.

And sometimes the problem isn’t the company. It may not even be the work. 

The role's changed. Your authority has changed. Expectations have changed. The conditions that once made it worth doing aren’t there any more. 

Or it may simply be a difficult period that can still be repaired. The trouble is, when you’re inside it, another quarter can become another year while you wait to find out which one it is.

I made the Career Decision Mirror for that point. It won’t tell you to resign. It won’t tell you to stay. It helps you work out what has actually changed, what may still be repairable and what evidence you need before making the next decision.
