Every Function Is Hitting Its Number. That Is the Problem.

If every function is hitting its number while the business gets harder to run, stop calling it an execution problem. It is an incentive problem.

The wrong customer enters as pipeline, becomes a celebrated deal, turns into a difficult implementation, then a rescue plan, then a churn explanation. The label changes at each handoff. The cost does not.

That is why green dashboards can sit beside tired teams, thinner margins, rising concessions, slower time to value, and customers who never become references. Nobody has to be careless for this to happen. People are usually doing exactly what they are paid, praised, and protected to do.

If Delivery, Customer Success, and Finance could challenge every deal before signature, how much of your pipeline would survive unchanged?

Read the article. Apply it. Then send it to the people still calling borrowed revenue growth.
