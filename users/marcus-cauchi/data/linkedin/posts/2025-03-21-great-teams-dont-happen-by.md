GREAT TEAMS DON’T HAPPEN BY ACCIDENT.

Everyone wants high-trust, high-execution, high-impact teams.
 Few are willing to engineer the conditions that make them possible.
 Bad teams aren’t filled with bad people. They’re built on systems and thinking that's past their "Best Before" date.

If communication is unclear, trust erodes. (Can you feel it?)
 If incentives are misaligned, execution stalls. (What evidence do you experience?)
 If leadership is inconsistent, chaos takes over. (How does it feel to live it?)

You don’t “get lucky” with great teams, you build them with intent.*

True leaders who build succession and resilience, build trust at the foundation, not as an afterthought.

* Middle managers are often the #UnsungHeroes. 
Which boss had the greatest positive impact on your life and career? 
How? 
What were the ripple effects of their influence? 
What have you passed on?
