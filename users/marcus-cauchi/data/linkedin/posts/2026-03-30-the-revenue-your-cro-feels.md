The revenue your CRO feels best about is often the revenue your CFO trusts least.

Not because the forecast is wrong.

Because the decision behind the deal was never strong enough to bear the weight put on it.

That's when a good quarter becomes a bad customer, a rescue, and a board conversation nobody wanted.

Question for CROs and CFOs:

What is the earliest sign that a deal was booked before the buyer was truly ready?
