They Call It Innovation. You’ll Call It Theft.

There’s a fundamental lie hidden in most discussions about “progress.”
The lie? That you have a choice in what happens next.

Right now, laws are being crafted not to protect you, but to grant more control to those who already have it. The world’s largest players are embedding legalised extraction into policy – and the consequences will be felt for generations.

If they can force you into arbitration you can’t afford, you’ve lost.
If they can strip your rights with contracts you don’t understand, you’ve lost.
If they can claim ownership over your output without compensation, you’ve lost.

The problem is not what they’re doing. The problem is that you’re being convinced there’s nothing you can do about it.

So let’s break that illusion. Because you are not powerless.
By the end of this, you’ll see exactly how and where to push back.
