The Hidden Taxes of Bad Measurement
Every business pays them.
Most never see them.

Part 2: The Discount Tax
The Discount Tax begins long before anyone changes the price. It starts when revenue matters more than revenue quality and weak qualification is hidden by urgency.
Discounting often looks like a pricing problem, but it usually isn’t. It is the bill for poor discovery, weak value, single-threaded deals or opportunities that should have been ruled out much earlier. Finance loses margin, Customer Success inherits difficult customers and everyone wonders why profitable growth feels so hard.
Instead of measuring revenue alone, measure the percentage of deals won without discounting and the quality of qualification before commercial terms are discussed.
