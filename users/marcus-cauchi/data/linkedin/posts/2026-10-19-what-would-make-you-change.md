What would make you change your mind about an investment you’ve already decided makes sense?

The business case is sound. The numbers stack up. The people responsible know what they’re doing. You’re ready to approve it. Then someone asks a question nobody can answer.

Would the answer change your decision? The timing? The amount you’re prepared to commit?
Or would it confirm you were right all along?
Which questions are worth answering before the money is spent?

That’s the challenge behind the next issue of Commercial Reality Notes.

Two ordinary growth proposals. Six more salespeople. Twenty more partners.
Both might be exactly right.

But what would have to be true for them to deliver the return you’re expecting?
