Most PE pain does not start with a miss.

It starts with a room full of smart people staying calm for just a little too long.

The number looks defensible.
The story sounds coherent.
The caveats feel manageable.
No one wants to be the person who turns mild discomfort into a harder conversation.

That is usually the moment the real risk gets protected.

Not because the truth is missing.

Because it is scattered.
One person sees the cash issue.
Another sees the delivery strain.
Another sees the late-quarter heroics.
Another sees the weak assumptions.

But nobody forces those fragments into one ugly sentence the room cannot escape.

So the quarter gets defended.
The board pack gets trusted.
And the intervention starts after the damage is already more expensive to fix.

I turned that moment into a short video.

Watch it if you want to see what weak revenue sounds like just before the room can no longer pretend it is fine.
