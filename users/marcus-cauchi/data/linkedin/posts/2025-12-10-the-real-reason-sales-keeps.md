The real reason sales keeps breaking

It is not your reps.
It is not the market.
It is not their motivation or your tools.
The structure that taught people how to think disappeared.

Most managers were promoted without ever seeing strong management up close. Not once in the field. Not in the moments that matter.

So they can't teach the job ... judgement drains out ... noise rises ... and your results swing.

This is NOT a talent gap. It's a design fault that shows up everywhere.

Fix the structure. Start with the what you measure, recognise and elevate and redirect the load on your managers' back.

Everything improves from there.
