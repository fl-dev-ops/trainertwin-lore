Money & Incentives: What the plan rewards

You get the behaviour you pay for.

* Plans shape behaviour more than pep talks.
* If you reward volume, you get noise.
* If you reward precision, you get signal.

Map behaviour → reward.
If you hate the behaviour, change the reward.

Change one reward today.

Reward Audit Worksheet
Write 3 behaviours you dislike.
Write what the plan rewarded.
Decide: cut, swap, or re-align.

And double check you aren't beating your reps with your carrots to drive the wrong behaviours!
