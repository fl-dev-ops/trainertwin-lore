“Support” is the most powerful word in business.
It changes tone, posture, and intent.
Scott Aaron reminded me that sales isn’t about convincing — it’s about creating safety.

Automation doesn’t kill jobs; it kills belief.
This week, try one thing:
 Replace every sales verb with a support verb - empower equip, enable, guide, show, teach, share.

Then listen for how people respond.
