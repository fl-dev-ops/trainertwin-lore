Trust doesn't collapse when things fail, it collapses when we stop owning what's true.

I walked into a meeting once thinking I had it nailed.
 Big pitch. Bigger expectations.
It went sideways.
And I could feel the room pull away.
Not because the idea was bad, because I was defending it instead of owning it.

It’s a pattern I see everywhere.
When we expect outcomes we haven't earned responsibility for, we protect and deflect.
 When we own our part without demanding vindication, we connect and adapt.

*************************************************
Trust survives failure.
 It doesn't survive dishonesty about our role in it.
*************************************************

Before your next hard conversation:
"What am I protecting right now, and what should I be owning instead?"

The gap between those answers is where conflict lives.
 Close that gap, and trust grows fast.
