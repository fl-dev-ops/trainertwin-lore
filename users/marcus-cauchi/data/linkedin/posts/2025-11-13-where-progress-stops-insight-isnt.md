Where Progress Stops

Insight isn’t the problem.
 Action is.

Deals stall in the same four places:

1. Pricing decisions
Everyone sees the friction.
 No one owns the trade-off.
 Lift: Run a 30-minute CFO brief. Define the ROI range, agree the give-and-get, and set a decision date before leaving the room.

2. Legal and procurement
NDAs, DPAs, liability caps, vendor onboarding.
 Documents move. Decisions don’t.
 Lift: Hold a 45-minute redline workshop with a 72-hour turnaround and one named owner to finish it.

3. Integration and delivery
The work is known.
 No one owns “done.”
 Lift: Write a one-page Definition of Done checklist and name who owns it today.

4. Sales behaviour
Insight shows up in the data.
 The next step never gets booked.
 Lift: No next step and date means no stage advance. Make that the rule, not the wish.
Default rule:
 If no one owns it by the end of the meeting, I own it until someone does.

Where a Human Makes the Difference

A person who makes truth safe to act on unlocks motion at every choke point.
They turn pricing friction into decisions.
 They get legal and procurement to finish instead of discuss.
 They define what “done” means and hold people to it.
 They create accountability where systems stop.

This isn’t about adding more insight.
 It’s about creating ownership.

The Only Question That Matters
We know what’s true.
 Who owns the next move, and by when?
