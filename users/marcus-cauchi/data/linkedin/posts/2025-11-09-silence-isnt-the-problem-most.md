Silence Isn’t the Problem

Most teams panic when a buyer goes quiet.
 We start chasing, calling, filling the gap with noise.

But silence isn’t rejection.
 It’s information.

When a buyer stops replying, it’s not that they’ve lost interest.
 It’s that something in our process stopped feeling safe.
 That isn’t attitude - it’s design.

Silence usually means one of four things is missing:
* Clarity - the next step isn’t obvious.
* Proof - they can’t see what’s moved forward.
* Control - they don’t feel free to slow down or pause.
* Exit - they don’t have a safe way to say “not now.”

When one of these is gone, silence is the smartest move they can make.
 The pattern’s easy to spot once you know what to look for.
 Teams that fix this cut ghosted deals by half within a month.

It’s calmer than you think.
