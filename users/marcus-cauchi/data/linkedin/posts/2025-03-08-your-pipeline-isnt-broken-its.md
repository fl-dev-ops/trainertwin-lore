Your Pipeline Isn’t Broken. It’s Being Rewritten in Silence.

Your pipeline isn’t broken.
 It’s being rewritten in silence.

The deal was solid, until it wasn’t.
The handoff happened, but something got lost.
The team agreed, but adjusted after the meeting.

If you’re tracking what’s visible but missing the hidden drift shaping decisions, you’re not actually in control of speed.

Speed isn’t the problem.
 Drift is.

By the time you see relational drift, you’ve already lost:

 ❌ Time → Hidden rework, slowdowns, and firefighting.
 ❌ Deals → Silent misalignment before the break.
 ❌ Trust → The first thing to drift. The last thing to recover.

The “Unsaid But Felt” Scan
In your next meeting, ask: "Before we lock this in, what’s one small detail that could quietly slow this down?"
 → If they hesitate, you just caught hidden drift before it spreads

The “3-Minute Future Test”
Before finalising a deal, ask: "Imagine it’s 3 months from now, and this didn’t work. What went wrong?"
 → The first answer often surfaces the real risk.

The “Pause Before the Fix” Rule
 Before you jump in to fix a recurring issue, ask:
 "Am I solving the real problem, or covering for someone else’s drift?"
 → Stop fixing symptoms. Start clearing root drift.

 🔹 Drift happens whether you see it or not.
 🔹 Fix it before you pay for it.
