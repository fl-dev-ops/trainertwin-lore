📍The Complexity Drug
(Bad and Out-of-their-Depth Leaders Use Complexity to Hide the Fact They Don’t Have a Plan and Are Lost)

If you want to spot a leader who doesn’t know what they’re doing - look at how they use complexity.

🔹 They overcomplicate simple decisions to make them seem “strategic.”
🔹 They generate noise to make it harder to challenge their ideas.
🔹 They build process layers that make them look “essential” but slow everything down.

📌 Where is complexity being used to hide the fact there is no actual plan?
📌 Where is process a performance, not a productivity multiplier?
📌 What could you cut today that would speed up results instantly?

The best operators don’t worship complexity - they strip everything down to only what moves the needle.
