I used to chase every lead. Now I count the cost.

The job wasn’t to qualify. It was to demo.

We were taught to act fast, book high, and move fast.
So we did.

We filled the calendar, pitched to everyone, and called it “momentum.”

Then the same pattern, over and over:
	•	Long demos to the wrong person
	•	Discovery skipped
	•	Deals stalled
	•	Pressure rose
But it wasn’t a sales problem. It was a system problem. We rewarded motion, not fit.

The Truth:
We didn’t have a sales engine. We had a demo factory.

If your post-demo win rate is low (below 9/10), you’re not selling. You’re burning time.

Could it be putting the demo at the start and negotiation at the end are killing viable opportunities?
