If your results swing wildly, check the manager’s calendar.

If it's full of:
- forecast calls
- internal reviews
- fire drills
- updates
.... and their own deals

Then they aren't managing.
They're surviving.

A surviving manager cannot build a thinking team.
Without a thinking team, your pipeline is only noise with dates.
