A deal can make complete sense when you sign it and still become a different decision later.

More debt. More capital. A different board. A narrower role. A delayed exit.
None of that proves the original deal was wrong.

But there comes a point where “we already approved this” stops being enough.
If the arrangement came back for approval today, would you still say yes on the same basis?

If you’re not sure, the decision may be worth reopening before more becomes difficult to change.
