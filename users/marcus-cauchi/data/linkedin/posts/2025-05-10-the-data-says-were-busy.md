The data says we’re busy. Reality says we’re stuck.

The CRM is full-ish. The forecast is definitely padded. The deals aren’t real.

Every week:
	•	Deals move from “Discovery” to “Proposal”
	•	The forecast looks healthy
	•	Targets feel within reach
But look closer:
	•	No next steps
	•	No committed buyer
	•	No business case
	•	No truth
It’s a map with no terrain.

You manage blind. 
If the system won’t tell you where trust is, the forecast is fiction.

Busy isn’t the same as progress. And no amount of CRM dashboards will fix broken signal.
