Your CRM says the deal is advancing.

The buyer’s world says it isn’t.

After enough deals, the pattern becomes predictable.

Stage changes don’t mean momentum.
They mean activity.

Most deals stall because the conditions to progress never existed.
Not because someone forgot to follow up.

You can’t stage-gate your way past misalignment.

Every deal that closes has the same five things true at the same time:
	•	There is a reason to act now
	•	One person clearly owns the decision
	•	The budget is clear and available
	•	The problem is agreed internally
	•	Doing nothing feels risky

If even one is missing, the deal stalls.

You log another call.
You move it to Proposal.
You update the forecast.

But you can’t force a combination lock. CRM stages are not tumblers.
They’re labels.

Before advancing the next opportunity, ask:

Did the conditions align?
Stages move easily.
Conditions do not.
