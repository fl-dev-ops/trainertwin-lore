Most organisations don't have a transformation problem.
They have a truth problem.

Bad news travels slowly. Optimism travels quickly. Maybe becomes likely. Likely becomes committed. Committed becomes the forecast.

Then everyone acts surprised when reality arrives.
They weren't surprised. They were unsafe.

Read this before you approve another transformation programme.
