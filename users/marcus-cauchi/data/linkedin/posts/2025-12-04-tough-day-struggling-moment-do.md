Tough day? Struggling moment?

Do this:

Take one moment from this week where you felt the spike.
 Write five short sentences:
 A - what happened
 B - what story your mind added
 C - how that story changed your behaviour
 D - what else could’ve been true
 E - one better move for next time.
 Say it out loud once.

Let me know how you got on.
