Alexander J. Buckles opened more than 70 opportunities at a single conference in Q4 2022.

He closed zero.

Not because the product was wrong.
Not because the market was not there.
Not because partnerships do not work.

Because he was talking to partnership professionals who owned the problem, but not the outcome.

No budget.
No authority.
No influence over the sales organisation.

That failure became the foundation of everything Forecastable does today.

In this episode of The Inquisitor Podcast, Alex and I go after one of the biggest fantasies in B2B:

That partnerships magically create pipeline.

They do not.

Real partner-led growth comes from:

- building co-sell playbooks around one motion, one ICP, one pain
- creating a door opener that leads with value, not a pitch
- bridging the problem owner and the outcome owner internally
- understanding whether the buyer actually feels safe making the decision
- knowing that most deals die in the second room, when you are not there
- treating demos as confirmation, not persuasion

A few lines from the conversation stayed with me:

“Distribution is all that matters.”
“Partnership people often have the problem, but not the power.”
“Demos very rarely push a deal forward.”

And maybe the most uncomfortable:

“The buyer does not care about your quota. They care whether this is a safe decision for them to live with.”

If you are a founder, sales leader, CEO, or partnerships leader trying to make partnerships actually produce, this episode is worth your time.

Question for the room:

Where are your partner motions failing today:
- at partner selection,
- at internal alignment,
- at the door opener,
or 
- in the second room after your champion takes it inside?
