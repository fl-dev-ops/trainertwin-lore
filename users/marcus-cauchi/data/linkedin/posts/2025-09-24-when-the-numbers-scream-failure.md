When the numbers scream failure, remember: the weather isn’t your fault.

Targets missed. Deals stalled. Silence from clients who used to reply instantly. It’s easy to let the storm convince you that it’s personal, that the numbers are shouting a verdict on your worth. But maybe they’re not.

This post isn’t about avoiding the reality of missed goals. It’s about what you do next. Shaun thought the silence meant rejection. Helen reminded him it was something else entirely: fear, chaos, uncertainty, on the client’s side. Not his failure.

What if the storm you’re caught in isn’t your fault? What if the solution isn’t pushing harder but listening closer? Adapting. Showing up, even when it feels messy, tired, and futile.

The real question isn’t about Q3. It’s about the story you’ll tell later. Is this where your reputation begins? Or where you gave up?

Maybe the weather isn’t your fault. Maybe it’s just time to stand tall until the storm passes.
