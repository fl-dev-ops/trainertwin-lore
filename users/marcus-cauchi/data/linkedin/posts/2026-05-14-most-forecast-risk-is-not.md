Most forecast risk is not born in the board pack.

It starts earlier, when a positive conversation gets promoted into real progress.

A buyer sounds interested. A seller feels confident. A manager accepts the story. A stage holds. A date stays in the forecast.

Nothing looks reckless.

But the buyer may not have done enough decision work inside their own business to justify the confidence now travelling upward.

That's where the number starts to become unsafe.

Not because people are lying.

Because confidence is travelling faster than evidence, and that confidence soon starts shaping hiring, spend, targets, board commitments, and value creation plans.

This week’s Commercial Reality Notes looks at the forecast question most teams avoid until the quarter exposes it.

What part of the number is safe to trust, and what part is still running on belief?
