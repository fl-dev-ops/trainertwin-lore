The person being blamed for the failure may be the person who inherited it.
By the time the damage is visible, the people who shaped the original decision have often moved on.

The seller owns the stalled deal, the CRO owns the forecast miss and the new CEO owns a plan agreed before they arrived. Everyone can see who is holding the cost. Far fewer people return to the judgement, assumptions and approvals that created it.

That is how organisations punish the final symptom while preserving the cause.

This article names the mechanism behind that pattern and explains why sensible businesses keep rewarding decisions before their quality is known.
Before you decide who failed, reconstruct who shaped the decision and who was left with its consequence.
