It’s easy to settle on an explanation once it feels like it fits. This is just a quick check. What do you actually know, what are you assuming, and would you change your mind if the evidence changed?

1. What do you believe?
2. How confident are you that it’s true?
3. What evidence is that confidence based on?
4. How do you know that evidence is reliable?
5. What evidence would make you more or less confident?
6. Would you use the same standard if the evidence pointed to a different conclusion?
7. What other explanation fits the evidence?
8. What would we expect to see if your belief were wrong?
9. Given what you actually know now, how confident should you be?

The point isn’t to doubt everything. It’s to know what deserves your confidence. If the evidence holds up, keep the view. If it doesn’t, change the view, not the standard.
