Are you interested in learning more about early detection of hearing loss? 
Join us as we launch World Hearing Forum’s Webinar Series featuring vitally important topics relevant to hearing loss, prevention and rehabilitation.
In this first webinar, learn about Newborn Hearing Screening and Early Intervention programs which have had a profound effect on outcomes for children born with hearing loss. Hear from leading experts from both middle- and low-income countries as they share their firsthand experiences in establishing a newborn hearing screening program, outlining why early detection remains so critically important in enabling a deaf child to get a head start on developing language skills as they begin life.
Speakers:
·        Dr. Carolina Der, Moderator
·        Dr. Katrin Neumann
·        Dr. David Tordrup
·        Dr. Christopher Ndoleriire
·        Dr. Nailya Malikovna
