When Your Playbook Poisons Your Leverage, You Inherit the Chaos You Created

If your own playbook erodes faith in your commitments, then the leverage you hold today is the instability you inherit tomorrow.

Is that the game you really want to win?
Or is that just the lie you’re telling yourself to justify playing it?

Are You Watching in Confusion and Wonder?
The extractors never win clean. They win by confusing, by exhausting, by planting polite fiction and hoping you accept it as real. But their tricks only work if we agree to play by their rules, rules designed to keep us reacting instead of thinking.

We see them now. And once seen, selfish fictions cannot be unseen.

What’s their next move? The same tired feints, again and again. Learn them, name them, and call them out loud enough that even their base hears the rot beneath the pitch.

The 5 Fictions They Hope You’ll Keep Swallowing

The Strength Feint - "Dominate and they will fold."
They confuse fear for respect. They mistake compliance for alignment. But fear corrodes leverage, it builds quiet enemies who wait for the moment to strike back. Strength is not control. Strength is trust. (check them for #AlphaMale-ness. Lol!)
Ask them: If your strength depends on silencing people, is it strength or just borrowed time?

The Anchor Feint – "Set a number high, make the real price seem generous."
They train you to doubt every offer by shifting goalposts in real time. They hope you’ll ignore the pattern. But an anchor that moves is just a rock tied to your ankle.
Ask, "If your numbers keep shifting, why should anyone believe the next one?"

The ‘Jam Tomorrow’ Feint – "Give me X now, I’ll give you Y later."
They deal in IOUs they never plan to pay. Their promises have expiration dates they won’t honour. “Security later.” “Favorus later.” “Support later.” But trust is not a debt market, it is paid forward, or it collapses.

Ask, "If your promises worked the last ten times, why do you still need to make them again?"

The ‘No Choice’ Feint – "This is your only option."
They want you exhausted enough to believe them. But when someone limits your options, it’s never for your benefit. Every squeeze play has an escape hatch, if you’re willing to break their frame.

Ask, "If this is the only option, why are they working so hard to make me believe it?"

The Emotional Feint - "Make it tense, make it personal, make it urgent."
They want reactive minds, not reflective ones. Because if you slow down, if you question, if you breathe, the polite fiction loses its grip.

Ask yourself, "What happens if I don't react?"

The Move That Breaks Their Game
 🚫 Stop arguing inside their frame.
 🚫 Stop proving your worth on their terms.
 🚫 Stop playing their game with their deck of rigged cards.

Watch the geopolitical behaviour filter into business. Who are the winners from all the uncertainty, the rapid forced change to context? How do they affect your thinking? Your emotions? You?
