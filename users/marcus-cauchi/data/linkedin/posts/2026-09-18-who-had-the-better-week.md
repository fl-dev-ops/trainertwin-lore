Who had the better week?

Two salespeople. One makes 100 calls, sends 40 emails, has eight meetings and sends three proposals. The other makes 45 calls, sends 18 emails, has five meetings and sends one proposal. Who had the better week?

You can’t tell, because I’ve only told you what the salespeople did. I haven’t told you what their buyers did. Did anyone bring their CFO into the conversation, get colleagues involved, complete the internal work they’d agreed to do, start procurement or find a problem that could stop the purchase?

Did anyone decide this wasn’t worth pursuing? That matters too. If one salesperson found out quickly that three deals were going nowhere while the other spent another six weeks chasing theirs, who was more productive?

We measure an extraordinary amount of sales activity. I’m increasingly interested in how little that tells us about whether anybody is actually buying.
