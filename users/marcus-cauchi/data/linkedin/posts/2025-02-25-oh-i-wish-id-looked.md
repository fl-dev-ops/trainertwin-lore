Oh I Wish I'd Looked After My Data ...

Oh, I wish I’d looked after my data,
 And kept all me secrets secure,
 But now Big Tech’s got it all open,
 And they’re riflin’ through me front door.

I signed up to some app for convenience,
 Clicked “I agree” without care,
 Now Google knows what’s in me fridge,
 And Alexa is sniffin’ me air!

Oh, I wish I’d looked after me banking,
 But HMRC’s watchin’ me spend,
 They say it’s to “fight fraud and scroungers,”
 But it’s me that gets flagged in the end!

They say I bought too much chocolate,
 They say I withdrew too much cash,
 They say I must prove it’s all legit,
 Or they’ll freeze me account in a flash!

Oh, I wish I’d looked after me business,
 But Amazon’s got all me plans,
 They’ve copied me products and customers too,
 And left me with empty hands!

Oh, I wish I’d looked after me health records,
 But they’ve flogged 'em to AI and more,
 Now insurance has doubled me payments,
 Cos they think I might snore!

So MPs, take heed of this warning,
 Before you all vote this thing through,
 Cos if our data ain't safe from the vultures,
 Then your secrets ain't safe from us too!
