The Lie Your Pipeline Tells You

Your pipeline is lying to you.
Your manager is lying to themselves.
And your board doesn’t believe a word of it.

That’s not opinion. That’s how modern sales runs.

Look at the evidence. Forecasts miss quarter after quarter. “Commit” deals slip into next month, then vanish. The funnel looks full - but half of it is already dead. What you call a pipeline is a morgue.

Reps keep parading corpses in Friday reviews. Managers cheer activity instead of killing bad deals. Execs defend these pipelines in board decks with a straight face. Everyone knows the truth but nobody says it: most deals are zombies.

Zombie deals look alive but are already dead. They have a sponsor, a meeting, even a next call ... but no real buyer movement. They rot silently, inflating forecasts and wasting capital. And like real zombies, they spread: reps waste hours, managers coach, SEs demo, finance models. By the time the board calls it, millions are gone.

This isn’t a rep mistake. It’s a system disease: Pipeline Theatre.

Theatre is when sales is performed instead of enforced. Reps talk about activity, not advancement. Managers run reviews to look busy, not to kill bad deals. Execs tell boards what they want to hear, not what’s true. Theatre is the weekly lie every sales team acts out.

And theatre is expensive. It drives CAC up, win rates down, cycles long, and valuation multiples down.

That’s the enemy The Ally Method™ exists to slay.

Pipeline theatre isn’t a harmless show ... it’s the lie that kills revenue.
