The deal closed.

Three months later, everyone who had to live with it was suffering.

Customer success was carrying work no one priced. Delivery was fixing problems never mentioned in the sale. Finance had booked the revenue but did not trust its quality. The sponsor was already defending the decision internally. Renewal was months away, yet the account already felt heavy.

The contract was real.

The decision underneath it was weak.

This is where many businesses fool themselves.

They count signed revenue and assume it is good revenue.

It's not.

Some revenue creates momentum. Some creates drag.

Some expands, renews, and compounds. Some needs rescuing from day one.

That gap is what I call #DecisionSafety:

Helping a buyer make a decision they can still defend, use, and live with after you are gone.

Not just a deal they will sign.

A decision that survives real life.

The best commercial teams do not just ask:

Can we win it?

They ask:

Will this still look smart six months after signature?

That question can save a fortune.
