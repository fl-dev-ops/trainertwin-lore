The systems you built to scale now slow you down.

Your Notion board hums with rules.
 Your Slack lights up like a control panel.

Every decision has a doc.
 Every doc has a review.
 And every review ends up back on your desk.

You built structure to create speed -
 then drowned in your own precision.

Control was never the goal.
 It was just fear wearing a spreadsheet.

The business didn’t outgrow your systems.
 It outgrew your need to feel in control.

Which process would move faster if you stepped out of it today?
