Saluting Mahan Khalsa

In “Let’s Get Real or Let’s Not Play.”
Mahan Khalsa gave every seller permission to stop pretending.
 To stop faking interest.
 To stop chasing deals that were never real.

But how many companies have the infrastructure to let their teams live that principle?
 I’ve watched good people say “let’s get real”…
 …then get punished for "losing" a deal. (You can't lose what you never had)

We didn’t need new words. We needed new rules of engagement.
 So we built them.
Structural trust.
 Role-level SoPs (mutual Standards of Performance).
And, Adult-to-Adult recovery ladders.
 GTM systems where clarity isn’t a slogan, it’s a scorecard.
Mahan was right.

What are you doing to prepare for the chaos that is coming?

DM me or Suzanne C. to learn to thrive through it 
 without compromising who you are
 your values 
 or by sacrificing your humanity
