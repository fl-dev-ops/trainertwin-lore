The Vague Goal Con
(When Outcomes Are Blurry, Nobody Can Call You Out for Failing)

Ever noticed how some leaders love broad, vague objectives?

There’s a reason.
🔹 If the goal isn’t measurable, you can never be held accountable.
🔹 If the outcome isn’t clear, failure can always be reframed as “progress.”
🔹 If there’s no way to track success, you can always say you’re “on the right track.”

📌 Where are goals so vague that accountability doesn’t exist?
📌 Where is execution stalling because teams don’t know what “done” looks like?
📌 Where would absolute clarity force momentum - now, not later?

Sellers who win consistently and leaders who execute well make outcomes so clear that success or failure is undeniable.
