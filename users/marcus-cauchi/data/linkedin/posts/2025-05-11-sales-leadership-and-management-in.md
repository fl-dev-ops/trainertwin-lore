#Sales #Leadership and #Management in a tough market requires #Trust not control, not coercion. 

You’re not helping. You’re hiding.

Every fix you make steals the shot they never got to take.
You read it. It’s wrong. You fix it.
 Not to control, to protect.
 But they don’t grow. They don’t stretch.
They stay stuck.

You say you’re supporting them.
 But what you’re really doing is protecting yourself from what might happen if they fail without you.

You’re not building a team.
 You’re building dependence.

You become the main bottleneck when you "Rescue" your people defined helping without boundaries or permission, diminishing them, stealing learning opportunities every time you fail to ask them a question, and instead you give them the answer or do their work again for them.

Do you think that might just piss them off too?
