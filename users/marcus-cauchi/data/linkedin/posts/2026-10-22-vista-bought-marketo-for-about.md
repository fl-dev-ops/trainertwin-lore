Vista bought Marketo for about $1.8 billion and sold it to Adobe two years later for $4.75 billion.

Richard Owen asks how much of that gain Vista actually created. 

Over the same period, comparable software companies increased in value by almost as much. But the comments underneath his analysis raise another question.

People who worked at Marketo describe better sales execution and bigger enterprise deals.  Others describe cuts to support, slower product development and declining product quality. Those accounts need testing. And more than one could be true.If a company cuts costs and improves EBITDA, how would you know whether it had removed unnecessary work or simply stopped paying for work that still needed doing?

And if that work later turned up in customer support, account management, renewals or product development, where would the cost appear? The financial gain is documented. What happened to the business underneath it deserves the same scrutiny.
