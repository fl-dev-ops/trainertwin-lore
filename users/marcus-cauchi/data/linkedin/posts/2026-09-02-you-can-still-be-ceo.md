You can still be CEO and no longer have enough authority to deliver the plan.

Can you set the budget you are accountable for delivering? Can you hire or replace the people you need? Can you change pricing, stop an acquisition, raise more money or reject an exit? The answer may quite reasonably be, "not without somebody else agreeing".

That doesn't make the arrangement bad. Institutional capital normally comes with rights, protections and conditions. But there is a big difference between owning shares, holding the CEO title and having enough practical authority to produce the outcome everyone is relying on.

I'd want to know where those lines sit before one of those decisions becomes urgent.
