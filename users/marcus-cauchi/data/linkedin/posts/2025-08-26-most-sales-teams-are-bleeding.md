Most sales teams are bleeding value because they confuse activity with progress. The clearest example? Proposals.

Proposals feel productive. They’re polished, they tick boxes, they give managers something to count. But they don’t survive contact with the boardroom. They get cut up, repurposed, or used to benchmark competitors. The result? Unpaid consulting, fake forecasts, and deals that evaporate under pressure.

This post is a line in the sand: proposals are dead. What wins is co-created business cases. Built with your buyer, not for them. Embedded into their internal logic, not emailed as a PDF. When you co-create, the buyer owns it, defends it, and carries it into the boardroom as their idea. That’s the difference between value leakage and value creation.

If you’re a top performer, you’ll recognise this instantly. If you’re a manager sick of fake pipeline, you’ll feel the relief. And if you’re still clinging to “proposal theatre,” this will sting, but it’s the wake-up call your pipeline needs.

👉 Read it. Share it with your team. And next time you’re tempted to hit “send” on that proposal, ask instead: how do we build this case together so it survives contact with the boardroom?
