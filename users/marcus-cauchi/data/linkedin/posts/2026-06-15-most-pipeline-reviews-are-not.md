Most pipeline reviews are not reviews. They're confidence theatre.

The seller says the deal is strong. The manager asks about next steps. The CRM has a close date. Everyone talks as if the deal exists.

But nobody asks the harder question: “What has the buyer done, without us pushing, that proves this is real inside their business?”

Not what they said. Not whether they liked the demo. Not whether they asked for pricing. What have they done?

Have they involved risk? Have they named the cost of staying as they are? Have they brought in the person who can block it? Have they agreed what happens if they do nothing? Have they changed anything internally?

If not, you may not have pipeline. You may have a polite buyer and an optimistic seller.

That is how false revenue enters the forecast.
