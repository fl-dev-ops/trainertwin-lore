A full pipeline can still be unsafe.

Board-pack phrase: “Pipeline coverage remains strong.”

That may be true. It may also hide the wrong thing.

Coverage tells the board how much pipeline has been reported. It does not prove how much pipeline is defensible. A deal can have meetings, stage movement, a proposal, next steps, and a confident close date, while the buyer has done very little decision work inside their own business.

That matters because pipeline coverage does not stay in the sales meeting. It travels into hiring decisions, spend decisions, target confidence, board commitments, and growth plans.

The board question is not only, “Do we have enough pipeline?”
The better question is, “How much of this pipeline is defensible?”

Defensible means the buyer has a named problem, a consequence owner, a decision path, a funding route, and a reason to act. Without that, coverage may be real in the CRM but weak as a growth signal.

This is Commercial Reality in practice.

Your pipeline is not the problem. What you trust inside it is.

Which board-pack phrase deserves the hardest evidence test?
Pipeline coverage, forecast commit, expansion upside, renewal confidence, or sales productivity?
