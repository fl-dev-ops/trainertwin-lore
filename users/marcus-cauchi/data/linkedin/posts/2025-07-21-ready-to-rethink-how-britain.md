Ready to rethink how Britain plans its future? 

In “Forging a Vision for UK Plc 2025–2035,” we pit the laser‑focus of Dubai’s monarchy‑driven growth against our own messy, brilliant democracy, and then ask: what if we combined the best of both worlds?

You’ll discover how a citizen‑powered “Vision 2035” town‑hall model, borrowed from Denmark’s climate success, could unite Ministers and communities around clear, measurable goals. You’ll see why “Outcome Units” in our public funds can turbocharge green jobs and AI scaling without sacrificing scrutiny. And you’ll learn how “smart taxation” and “Future Skills Dividends” can turn every pound of private wealth into public gain.

We break down eight practical pillars, state‑corporate partnerships, regulatory sandboxes, Growth Councils, Britain Growth Bonds, Data Commons Trusts and more, each designed to spark lively debate and real action.

Whether you’re a policymaker tired of endless manifesto wars, a business leader seeking clarity on public‑private fusion, or simply someone who cares about leaving a stronger, fairer nation for the next generation, this post is your conversation starter. Dive in to challenge assumptions, contribute fresh ideas and help chart a roadmap that’s ambitious, accountable and proudly British.
