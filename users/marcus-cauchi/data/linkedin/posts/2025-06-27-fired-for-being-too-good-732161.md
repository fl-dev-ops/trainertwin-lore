You didn’t fail.
 You didn’t underperform.
 You committed the one unforgivable sin in a metrics-first system:
 You exposed that excellence is possible in a world of mediocrity.

Let’s be clear: you didn’t break trust. You broke the narrative.
The system preaches repeatability.
 You showed them reality.

They talk about enablement.

So they fired you. Or they’re about to.
Not with drama.
 With process.
 With a calendar invite, a Google Doc, and a lot of fake concern about “team dynamics.”
They’ll call it coaching.
 It’s containment.
They’ll say it’s about alignment.
 It’s about erasure.
