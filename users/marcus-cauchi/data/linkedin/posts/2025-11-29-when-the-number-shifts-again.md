When the number shifts again, you feel it.
A date slides.
 A deal sounds a bit stronger.
 A maybe turns into a probably to keep the call smooth.

You hear the update, but you feel the wobble under it.
That happens because someone is scared.
Scared of how you’ll take the truth.
 One moment of tension is enough to bend the story.

So they tidy the words.
 They shave the risk.
 They hope the deal firms up before you check again.

And the forecast starts to drift.
This isn’t about performance.
 It’s about safety.

Raise the safety and the number stops moving.
The room softens.
 People speak plainly.
 Risk shows up early.
 Your week gets quieter.
 The forecast holds its shape.

If the number won’t sit still, your effect on the room is the issue.

Try this next meeting.
 Ask one calming line.
 What feels shaky, and what can I do to make it safer to say that sooner?

Stay open.
 Let them feel safe being honest with you.

The truth will arrive on time.
