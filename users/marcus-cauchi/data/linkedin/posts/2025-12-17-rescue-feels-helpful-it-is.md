Rescue feels helpful. It is not.

Every time a manager gives the answer, the rep loses the lesson.
Every shortcut kills a little bit of judgement.

By the end of the quarter, the rep has not grown.
And the manager has no time left to coach.

Rescue feels good in the moment.
It is expensive in the long run.
Slow guidance now gives you speed later.
