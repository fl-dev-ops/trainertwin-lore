Ethics & Trust: Run the Leak Test
Trust is an operating standard, not a slogan.
* Every doc, slide, or email is a test. If leaked tomorrow, would it build trust or destroy it?

Your words live longer than you think.
* A single reckless line can torch reputation faster than any competitor.
* That’s why high-trust teams run the Leak Test before they hit send.

Open one live doc today. Circle the sentence you’d least want in the hands of a buyer, regulator, or journalist. Edit it until you’d be proud of it, anywhere.

This is not spin, it’s discipline.
* Protecting trust is cheaper than repairing it.

Action: Edit one risky line today.
