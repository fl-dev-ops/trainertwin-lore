“You don’t mean to be a tw*t…
 But that’s how you’re being perceived.”

You’re not evil.
 You’re not trying to intimidate anyone.
 You’re not yelling or swearing or throwing chairs.

But the team’s walking on eggshells.
Sales Manager flinches before speaking.
 No one runs a live call when you’re watching.
 And the system you built to empower people?
 It freezes when you show up.

Because intent doesn’t matter.
 Impact does.

And the impact is this:
People are scared to try
Silence feels safer than truth
Progress stalls unless you push it yourself
And when you push, they brace, they don’t build

You’re not a tw*t. But the effect is indistinguishable.
That’s the problem.

It’s not about how YOU feel.
 It’s about what your presence signals to the room.

And right now?
Your presence says:
“Don’t get it wrong.”
 “Don’t look weak.”
 “Don’t tell me something I don’t want to hear.”

So what do they do?
They perform theatrics.
 They polish.
 They hide.
 They sandbag.

And you lose the 2 most important things in the system: 
1. The Truth.
2. Trust

Want to fix it?
