Moving Goalposts = Silent Sabotage

Every time you move the target, you make winning impossible. And your people know it before you do.
👉 Could everyone on your team write down exactly what “winning” means this week, and trust it’ll be the same next week?

If the answer is no, they’re not playing to win. They’re playing not to lose. That’s how cultures rot. Teams with unstable goals churn talent 2.5× faster, and those who stay often stop fighting long before you notice.

The founder’s trap is thinking high pressure sharpens performance. In reality, moving goalposts is sabotage, and it’s usually self-inflicted.

If you’re changing the rules to chase hope, you’re the one breaking the game.
