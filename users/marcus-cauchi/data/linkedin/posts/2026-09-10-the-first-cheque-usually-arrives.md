The first cheque usually arrives while you’ve still got choices.

The harder question is what happens later, when you need more money, more time or a change of plan and some of those choices have disappeared.
That doesn’t make the original deal bad. It just means the decision isn’t only about what the capital gives you today.

It’s also about what you’ll still be able to decide tomorrow.
