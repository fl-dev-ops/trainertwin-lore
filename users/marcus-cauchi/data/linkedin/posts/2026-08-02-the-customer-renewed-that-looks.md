The customer renewed.

That looks like good news because it is good news. Revenue remained in the business and the relationship survived.

The operating story may still be less comfortable.

Perhaps the CEO had to intervene. Customer success absorbed work the product was meant to remove. Services built another exception. Product promised a fix that is not yet funded.

The revenue can still be counted. The harder question is how it should be interpreted.

You might count it as dependable value, count it with conditions, discount it because rescue remains necessary, reclassify the cost of keeping it or stop treating rescue as proof the operating model works.

Same renewal. Five different decisions.

What is actually keeping this revenue in place?
