What do your customers actually care about?

Usually not what shows up in the survey.
 The pressure behind the decision.
 The risk they’re trying to cut.
 The delay making them look bad internally.
 The constraint they can’t say out loud.
 The trade-off they’re being forced to make.

Most companies don’t get close enough to see it.
 They measure what’s easy to track.
 They build messaging around words customers don’t use.
 They mistake activity for understanding.
Then the campaign gets attention, the dashboard looks healthy, and the deal still stalls.

Because the NPS score makes the company feel informed.
 The CRM “pain point” helps the seller structure the pipeline.
 And the persona from the messaging workshop rarely sounds like anyone the customer actually knows.

This session is for sellers, marketers, and operators who want a more accurate view of customer behaviour than the market usually rewards.

For people who believe ethics and ambition belong in the same room.
We’ll talk about what customers say versus what drives the decision, why “value” often means risk, status, speed, control, or relief,  where customer research turns into performance, how teams train buyers to give polished but useless answers, and  what changes when you understand the job beneath the purchase

 No growth hacks.
 No funnel theatre.
 No fake customer obsession.

Just a blunt conversation about how decisions actually get made.
