The tragedy isn’t that connect rates have collapsed.
It’s that we’re still pretending the fix is another plug-in.

We’ve industrialised curiosity.
Built sales factories full of dashboards that can tell you everything, except whether you’re talking to the right human.

Every tech vendor says, “Do more with less.”
Yet every quarter, we somehow do less with more.

This isn’t a sales efficiency problem, it’s a management imagination problem.
If your growth model burns people and budget faster than your buyer changes their number, you don’t have a system - you’ve got a slot machine with better branding.

Maybe the next breakthrough isn’t more automation.
Maybe it’s the courage to stop worshipping the dashboard, and start thinking again.
