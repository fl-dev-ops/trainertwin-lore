One of my favourite things we've done in 2026. Last month ScaleStation and HubSpot hosted The Profitable Builder, an exec round table and lunch for residential construction leaders in Sydney.

We had a group of business leaders that are navigating one of the toughest  environments the industry has seen.

There was plenty of head nodding, and a lot of "we're dealing with that".

The consensus in the room was that builders who connect their systems, focus on nurturing their prospects, and get AI working in the next 12 months are going to have an advantage over those who don't.

Thanks to Fabio Pancaldi for presenting, Dan Stevens and Danielle Gormley for all your help and Ariane Baker for your support.
