Most leadership teams spend time refining strategy.
Very few examine the mechanism that shapes daily decisions.
If you want to understand what your organisation truly values, don't start with the vision statement.

Start with the compensation plan.

Incentives do more than reward outcomes. They train judgement. They define what is rational. Over time, that logic compounds into behaviour, culture, and financial results.

Many executives sense something is off long before the numbers show it. Margin pressure feels structural. Forecast swings feel familiar. Strong people make short term decisions in long term businesses.

The root cause is rarely motivation. It's design.

The article below examines the one structural lever most boards underestimate, and why it may be shaping your enterprise value more than any strategic initiative currently in flight.

Before you adjust targets, revise strategy, or question your people, look at the system that tells them how to win.

It may be telling you more than you think.
