PROXIMITY TO POWER IS NOT POWER.

Too many people think sitting next to decision-makers makes them powerful.
 It doesn’t.

Power isn’t given, it’s earned through trust, execution, and momentum.
 Power isn’t who you know, it’s what you do when the moment comes.

If your influence depends on borrowed credibility, you don’t have power, you have a short-term seat at someone else’s table.

Build your own table.

Tag someone who empowers others instead of clinging to power.
