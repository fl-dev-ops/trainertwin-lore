Mark Roberge makes a strong case that AI may evolve us back toward the full cycle seller.

I agree with the capability shift. AI will dramatically increase individual leverage. It will compress handoffs, flatten structures, and reduce management dependency.

But AI does not correct structural flaws. It magnifies them.

If your sales system is acquisition first, AI will accelerate volatility. Faster pipeline. Faster closes. Faster expansion pressure. Faster churn when embed is shallow.

If your system is Protect before Expand before Win, AI becomes a compounding lever. It strengthens time to value, stabilises accounts before expansion, and scales only what is already durable.

AI increases leverage. Architecture determines whether that leverage compounds value or compounds fragility.

The debate is not specialisation versus full cycle. It is what the system optimises first. That is the real design decision for 2026.
