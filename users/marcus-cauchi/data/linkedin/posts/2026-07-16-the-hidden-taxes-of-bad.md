The Hidden Taxes of Bad Measurement
Every business pays them.
Most never see them.

Part 3: The Forecast Tax
The Forecast Tax appears when confidence replaces evidence. Forecast meetings become exercises in optimism because seller belief is easier to measure than buyer commitment.
The tax arrives next quarter. Hiring decisions have already been made, inventory has been ordered and investor expectations have been set. Suddenly the business isn’t paying for an inaccurate forecast. It’s paying for every decision that depended on it.
Measure evidence instead of opinion. Ask what the buyer has done, not what the seller believes.
