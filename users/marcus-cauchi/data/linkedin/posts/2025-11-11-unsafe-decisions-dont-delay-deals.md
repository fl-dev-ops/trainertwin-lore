Unsafe decisions don’t delay deals. They kill quarters.

Most deals don’t die because buyers don’t get it.
 They die because the decision doesn’t feel safe.

Once you see it that way, everything changes - coaching, forecasting, closing.
Funny how the habits that once kept us safe
 now make us hard to trust.

We don’t rise above our schemas.
 We just replay them in new clothes.

That’s why even good sellers still sound “salesy” sometimes.
 You can’t fake intent.
 If the goal’s still to close, people feel it - and the trust’s gone.

It’s not about new tricks.
 It’s about intent.
 Stop trying to win.
 Start helping them think.

When someone leaves a call clearer and calmer, that’s a win.
 Even if they don’t buy.

Most of us were taught that persuading means helping.
 That instinct runs deep. It once kept us alive.
 Now it just gets in the way.

Buyers don’t need a pitch.
 They need space to work out what’s true for them.
 You can’t give them that if you’re proving you’re right.

So flip the order.
 Safety first. Exploration second. Decision last.

Selling’s human. So is self-protection.
 The work isn’t to sell less -
 it’s to sell without threat.

How do you catch yourself before the old pattern takes over?
