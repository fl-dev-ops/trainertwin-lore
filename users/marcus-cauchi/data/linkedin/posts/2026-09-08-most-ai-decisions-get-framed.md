Most AI decisions get framed as yes or no. The harder question is how much commitment the evidence actually supports.
 
A strong case on six dimensions does not compensate for the seventh. If nobody holds the authority to withdraw, or the exposure cannot be reversed, that constraint sets the size regardless of how good the business case looks.
 
The largest safe commitment is the smallest size every gate can support.
 
I have written up the full method: seven gates, assessed against the same commitment sizes, and how to identify the binding constraint.
 
How to tell when the answer should be stage, test or reduce rather than a clean yes or no.
 
It is a pre-validation baseline, not a validated finding. The retrospective test comes next.
