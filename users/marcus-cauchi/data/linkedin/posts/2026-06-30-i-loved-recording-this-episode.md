I loved recording this episode with Richard Spanier. We got into the nitty gritty of why, despite a mountain of tech, highly paid salespeople are performing woefully. Last I read 29% of salespeople hit quota in 2025. That means 71% missed quota. Think about that waste.

You pay for salaries (and a fraction of the commission you could be paying - is that what finance means by cutting costs??), tech stack, management, insurance, leads, data, marketing and  sales training, yet 7 out of 10 are below target. Since 2016 when our glorious profession had 56% of so hitting quota (which was pretty shocking) performance has halved.

That begs the question why?

It not as simple answer Rick and I give it a damned good go shining a light on the acts of self harm, self sabotage, destructive business practices and damaging intent that make sales among the least trusted occupations out there.

That is the OPPOSITE of good selling.
