CUSTOMER SERVICE SHOULD SOLVE PROBLEMS, NOT CREATE THEM.

Ever feel like customer support is designed to make you give up?
 That’s because, for some companies, it is.
* They make wait times so long that you drop the issue.
* They train reps to wear you down, not help you.
* They pass you between departments until you lose patience.

Companies that value trust don’t play games.
* They hire support to solve problems, not deflect them.
* They make getting help as easy as buying.

If it’s hard to reach support, assume they don’t want to help you.
Make a #CustomerHero of a brand that delivers real service.

Martin Lindstrom's #Buyology and #SmallData are great starting points.
