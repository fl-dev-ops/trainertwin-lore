What gets called a skills crisis is often what shows up when a team has been under-managed for too long.

Most teams aren’t short of talent. They’re short of managers who can coach in the moment, reps who get proper practice before the pressure hits, and training that still works when a deal gets messy.

A lot of this is trained by what leaders measure. If the system rewards activity, stage movement, and tidy forecasts more than sound judgement, buyer progress, and deal quality, it should not surprise us when teams look busy and still miss.

That’s why win rates drop. Not just because the market changed, but because too many teams are still being run for a market that no longer exists.
