The Hidden Taxes of Bad Measurement
Every business pays them.
Most never see them.

Part 6: The Meeting Tax
The Meeting Tax is rarely questioned because everyone experiences it together. Businesses measure attendance, updates and alignment, but almost never ask whether a decision was made.
Time disappears into calendars. Decisions slow down because responsibility becomes diluted. Twelve people attend, nobody owns the outcome and another meeting is scheduled to discuss the first meeting.
Measure decisions made, actions completed and cycle time between issue and resolution.
