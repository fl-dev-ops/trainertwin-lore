Fat in the Forecast

Muscle at rest turns to fat.
 So does your customer base.

When deals sit still, they don’t hold their value - they rot quietly in the forecast.
 They make you look busy, not healthy.

Every silent customer.
 Every “verbal yes.”
 Every deal that hasn’t moved in weeks.
 That’s fat in the forecast.

RevIntel spots it.
Funnelcast strips it out.
Principled Selling® and #TrustOS makes sure it never comes back.

What’s left?
 A lean, honest forecast built on proof, not hope.

No bloat. No theatre.
 Just revenue that moves.

Before your next forecast review, ask yourself:
 What’s muscle, and what’s just fat?
