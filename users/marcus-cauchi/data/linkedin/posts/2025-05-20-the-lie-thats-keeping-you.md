The Lie That’s Keeping You Stuck

"More pipeline. More leads. More activity."

It sounds logical. Fill the funnel, work the numbers, and revenue will follow. But if that were true, why do so many teams with massive pipelines still struggle with unpredictable revenue, stalled deals, and conversion rates that barely move?

Because you don’t have a pipeline problem. You have a decision problem.

Your pipeline isn’t just a list of names. It’s a series of decisions, made by you, your team, and your buyers.

 Decisions about who to pursue (and who will be a waste of your time and theirs).
 Decisions about when and how to engage (without being ignored).
 Decisions about which deals will close (and which will drain resources for months with no result).

Without precision, clarity, and alignment, more pipeline just means more bad decisions at scale (and mid-funnel CONSTIPATION!)

What if the answer isn’t more, but better?
