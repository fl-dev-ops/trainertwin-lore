The Beer Goggles Forecast

Every quarter, we stumble back into the boardroom like it’s closing time. The lights are low, the numbers look good, and everyone’s just tipsy enough on optimism to believe they’re seeing clearly.

We squint at the pipeline through Excel-tinted lenses and tell ourselves, maybe this time it’s real. Deals that ghosted us suddenly look charming again.  Discounts start to sound romantic.

That one red account? Probably just needs “a quick follow-up,” right?

Over 35 years I’ve poured a few too many pints of pipeline confidence myself.  It’s funny until you realise the hangover isn’t financial ... it’s cultural.

Forecasting under the influence of wishful thinking is a company-wide night out we keep mistaking for strategy. 98 % of forecasts look attractive under fluorescent light and panic.

Then comes the hangover:
 • Deals vanish
 • Confidence crashes
 • Everyone claims they “learned a lot”

The detox isn’t another dashboard, it’s honesty.
* What did we actually sell?
* What did we just imagine through the haze?
* Who’s sober enough to call last orders on the fantasy?

Maybe next quarter we skip the liquid courage, tell the truth before the music stops, and see if reality still looks as pretty under the lights.

What would change if we had to defend every forecast sober, with the lights fully on?
