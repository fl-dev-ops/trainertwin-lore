Why do deals disappear after “commit”?

Not lost.
Not to a competitor.
Just… stop.

Look at the symptoms.

Late stage deals stall.
Commit slips.
Forecasts get rewritten.
End of quarter discounts appear.

So the response is predictable.

More urgency.
More pressure.

But urgency at the moment of decision often does the opposite.

It raises perceived risk.

The buyer starts imagining the meeting where they must explain why they approved it.

If that film looks painful, they stop engaging.

The pipeline usually shows the pattern before anyone admits the cause.
