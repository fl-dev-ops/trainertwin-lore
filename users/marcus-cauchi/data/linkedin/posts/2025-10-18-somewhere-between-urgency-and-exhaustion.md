Somewhere between urgency and exhaustion, a lot of good people lose their edge.

The numbers look fine, the effort’s there, but the signal is gone. You can feel it in the tone of your calls, the weight of your updates, the silence after a “thanks for following up.” That isn’t failure ... it’s fatigue disguised as performance.

Lesson: Trust doesn’t erode all at once; it leaks out through small moments when fear starts making the decisions. Every shortcut to speed eventually costs safety.

When you rebuild coherence, when you let clarity set the pace again, work gets lighter. The edge returns, not because you push harder, but because you stop pretending. That’s when credibility starts compounding again.

If this resonates, read the full post and share it with someone who’s been sprinting on empty.
