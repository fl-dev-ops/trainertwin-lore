I was inspired by Morgan McDonald's post earlier.

The Insanity Tax Checklist: Are You Paying for Broken Sales Tactics?

If you’re still running outdated sales motions (cough ... traditional cold calling ... cough), you might be unknowingly paying an Insanity Tax - wasting time, money, and energy on things that don’t work.

Run this quick self-check. If you check more than 3, it’s time for a new playbook.

❌ Activity Over Outcomes
 ☐ Our team celebrates “calls made” instead of deals closed.
 ☐ We track SDRs by volume, not conversion.
 ☐ Our CRM is full of noise—logged activities that don’t move the needle.

❌ Mass Automation Madness
 ☐ We send 10,000+ cold emails a week with low reply rates.
 ☐ AI is being used to spam, not to research & personalize.
 ☐ We’re getting domain-blocked by key prospects.

❌ SDR Burnout Factory
 ☐ Our SDR turnover is sky-high (because they hate their jobs).
 ☐ They spend more time dialing than actually learning our customers.
 ☐ We’ve accepted that “it’s just a numbers game” (it’s not).

❌ Buyer Experience Nightmare
 ☐ Prospects ignore us, unsubscribe, or block our emails.
 ☐ Our outreach feels transactional, not valuable.
 ☐ We interrupt buyers instead of meeting them where they are.

🛠️ Fix It Before It Tanks Your Pipeline
 ✅ Shift from volume to quality. Track revenue impact over activity.
 ✅ Use AI for intelligence, not spam. ICP-based insights > mass automation.
 ✅ Turn SDRs into brand advocates. Referrals, insights, and warm conversations.
 ✅ Meet buyers on their terms. Less interruption, more relevance.

💡 How many did you check? If it’s more than 3, you’re paying the Insanity Tax. Time to stop throwing money at broken tactics and start playing a smarter game.
