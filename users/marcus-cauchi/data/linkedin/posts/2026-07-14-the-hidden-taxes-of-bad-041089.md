The Hidden Taxes of Bad Measurement
Every business pays them.
Most never see them.

Part 5: The Partner Tax
The Partner Tax appears when businesses measure recruitment instead of activation. Announcing another hundred partners feels like progress, even though only a fraction will ever create meaningful revenue.
The hidden cost is substantial. Enablement, support, management attention and programme investment are spread across partners who never become productive. The channel looks impressive on paper while genuine coverage barely changes.
Measure active, profitable partners instead of total partners. Recruitment is only the beginning of the job.
