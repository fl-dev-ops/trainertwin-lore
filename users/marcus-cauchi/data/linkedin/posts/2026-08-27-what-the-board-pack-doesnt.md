What the Board Pack Doesn’t Tell You

Most weak revenue does not look weak when the board first sees it.
It looks like growth.

The problems usually become obvious later, when cash conversion slips, activation drags, margin weakens or someone gets blamed for a problem that was already there.

The more useful question is what was visible before the P&L made it undeniable.

If that looks familiar, I’ve put the full PE Revenue Fragility Stress Test into a short working PDF for sponsors, Operating Partners, IC members and portfolio CFOs.

It's built for the point before more capital, headcount or reputation is committed, when the numbers still look defensible but something underneath them may not be.
