It’s 08:43. I’ve already broken three promises to myself.
Didn’t finish that planning doc. Skipped my outreach window. Hit snooze on the “strategic” hour I carved out. Again.
Because someone needed me.
Because I couldn’t let the team drop the ball.
 Because it’s faster if I just do it.

I know what that really means:
 I’m the bottleneck. The shortcut. The fixer.
 But not the leader I wanted to become.

By mid-morning, I’ve patched a call, rewritten a message, handled a client wobble. And stopped a fire, yes, but at what cost?

I keep stealing teachable moments from my team.
 Not out of ego. Out of care. And fear.
 But also, I think… habit.

A few of them are getting better. But too many stall, over and over, waiting, stuck.
 Because I’m still their answer. Not their guide.
 Because I don't feel safe fully letting go, delegating, trusting.

Lunch? Skipped. Outreach? Cancelled. Strategy? Still just a tab on my screen.
 And that scary conversation I’ve been putting off?
 Still there. Looming. Avoided.
It’s not the volume. It’s the drag, stretch and slow drain.

I feel the responsibility without feeling like I have agency.
 The urgency without the authority.
 The title without the time to grow into it.

And yet…
 There’s a moment. Just one.

When I imagine what it would feel like…
 To trust my team and myself.
To realise not all conflict is bad, and how to tell the difference.
 To have hard conversations and walk away proud, not raw.
 To grow people, not rescue or parent them.

The best version of you:
 She doesn’t repeat herself.
 She doesn’t stall or scramble or second-guess.
She leads.

If any of this sounds too familiar, it probably is.
If someone you trust shared this with you, they see something in you.
 And they know what’s next can be better than now.
When you’re ready, the door is already cracked open.

You have a real choice. 
 Work harder or 
 Design your GTM and 
Management processes 
 To deliver the results you do want
 Not the ones you don't
