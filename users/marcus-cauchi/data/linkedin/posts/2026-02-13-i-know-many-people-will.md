I know many people will say experience matters. But the data says it adds only one thing. A slightly faster ramp up time. I’ve coached enough top 1%ers who got the job with no experience in their industry, had been rejected for the lack of it and yet became top performers in their company, their industry and worldwide. 

Are you a hiring manager looking for a diligent, intelligent salesperson to apprentice?

Ana has demonstrated resilience, initiative and adaptability all her life. Of course I’m biased. She’s been listening to coaching calls since she was born. Her questions are excellent and despite being deaf, she really listens and reads the room. She is attuned to behavioural cues better than most hearing people. She’s had to be. 

I’ve listened to her on interviews and frankly, she is better on them than many veteran salespeople I’ve interviewed over the past 30 years. Her preparation and research is excellent and her energy is infectious. 

She doesn’t have formal sales experience but she does have the qualities any manager wants. Trustworthiness, curiosity, fast learner, hard working and attention to detail among many other qualities. 

On and off the pitch she is a team player. She’s ambitious, motivated and eager to learn. Nothing has come easy for her because of her hearing and every time she gets knocked back, she gets up and finds a way to make what she wants happen. 

So, if you or someone you know is open to consider giving her a chance to prove her worth with an entry level role in sales or account management, please drop her a line.
