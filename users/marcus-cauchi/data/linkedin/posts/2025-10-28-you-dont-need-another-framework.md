You don’t need another framework.
 You need one real decision.

You’ve read the books.
 Bought the course.
 Filled the whiteboard ... twice.

Every new model feels like momentum.
 But it’s just motion.

Because frameworks let you think about the work
 instead of doing the hard part ... choosing.

You don’t have a strategy problem.
 You have a courage problem.

Burnout isn’t from doing too much.
 It’s from never deciding what to stop.

Clarity isn’t found in frameworks. It’s found in finally choosing.
