The Hidden Taxes of Bad Measurement
Every business pays them.
Most never see them.

Part 8: The Capability Tax
The Capability Tax starts when cost reduction becomes more important than capability building. Training is delayed, coaching is reduced and hiring decisions favour lower salaries over stronger judgement.
The savings appear immediately. The bill arrives later through slower growth, weaker decisions, higher turnover and managers spending their time correcting mistakes that better capability would have prevented.
Measure improvement in judgement and performance, not simply the cost of developing people.
