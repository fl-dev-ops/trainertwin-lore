We’ve quietly been testing something. Now it’s working.
It’s called #SellersAnonymous.

Every two weeks, we gather in small groups (max 12) with one guest storyteller. No coaching. No judgement. Just space to tell the truth.
You share the moment you realised your selling, or leadership, was causing harm.
 To your clients. To your team. To yourself.

And what happened next.

We’ve run a few already. You can move between pods. You can build 1:1 support relationships.
 You don’t have to perform. You just have to show up, listen, and reflect on what’s real for you.

Charles H.,  has been instrumental in shaping the experienc,e and is now helping us build a facilitator’s guide so this can grow far beyond us.

If you don’t know his work, start with #TrustBasedSelling. It’s the most personally impactful book on sales I’ve ever read.

It is up there with #TheTransparencySales by Todd Caponi and David Brock, and #SalesManagerSurvivalGuide, and Matt McDarby's #TheCadenceOfExcellence: Key Habits of Effective Sales Managers and Keith Cunningham's #TheRoadLessStupid and of course Dr. Mark Goulston's #JustListen
