Why I Disappeared

You didn’t do anything terrible.
 I just stopped feeling safe replying.

You sounded sure.
 I wasn’t.

And I didn’t know how to say that without disappointing you.
At first I meant to answer. I even wrote a draft.
 But every line felt like it would open a debate or another pitch I couldn’t escape.
 So I closed the tab and told myself I’d come back to it.

I didn’t.
That’s what ghosting really is.
 A quiet way to buy time, protect face, avoid guilt.

I liked what you offered.
 I liked you, even.
 But I needed space to think without feeling hunted.

If you’d written, “No rush, if it’s not right now that’s fine,”
 I’d have replied in minutes.

Most of us don’t vanish to be rude.
 We vanish to feel safe.

And the moment you make it safe again, we come back.

What could someone have said that would have made it easier for you to reply?

PS: If you’re losing deals to silence, you don’t need more leads. You need safer conversations. That’s what we help people build.
