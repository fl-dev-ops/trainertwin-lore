EXECUTION EATS STRATEGY FOR BREAKFAST.

 The world isn’t short on ideas. It’s short on executors.
* Most people overthink and under-deliver.
* Most teams strategise endlessly but fail to act.
* Most businesses die, not from lack of vision, but from lack of speed.

A mediocre plan executed fast and iterated in real time will beat a perfect plan stuck in a deck every single time.

You don’t need more strategy. You need faster execution.

Tag someone who moves from idea to action instantly.
