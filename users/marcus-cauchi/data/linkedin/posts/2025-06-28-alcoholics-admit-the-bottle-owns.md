Alcoholics admit the bottle owns them.
Most sales teams have no idea their pipeline is the bottle.

They are hooked on malpractice: smile-and-dial, quote-and-hope, sell-and-run.
These habits feel productive. They are actually blood-letting your valuation.

Stop ordering reps to bring home deals like hunters dragging carcasses to the quarterly altar. Your customers are not prey. They are the source of every future renewal, referral and expansion you will ever book.

Unless your buyers enjoy pain (doubtful), forcing urgency, “handling” objections and corner-closing is self-sabotage. Pressure destroys trust. Lost trust kills lifetime value.

Three lies the old playbook keeps telling you

* Selling is a numbers game. Wrong. It is a relevance game. More calls to the wrong people compounds waste, not revenue.
* Always be closing. Translation: always be pushing. Modern buyers shut down at the first hint of coercion.
* Pain equals £££'s. Manufactured pain triggers buyer defences. Coach outcomes, not agony.
