Sales teams don’t have a pipeline problem. They have a trust problem.

They’re:
	•	Rushing discovery instead of building understanding
	•	Pitching to one contact instead of mapping the buying group
	•	Showing up with data instead of insight
	•	Confusing activity with progress

The real issue?

The buyer isn’t avoiding your product, they’re avoiding risk.
Not the technical kind. The political kind.
The cost of choosing something new and being wrong.

We’ve been rebuilding how SDRs and AEs operate around one principle:

Trust is the only asset that compounds in every deal.

That means:
	•	Diagnosing what’s really driving or blocking motion
	•	Showing up with hypotheses that open thinking, not objections
	•	Using tone, timing, and relevance to lower perceived risk
	•	Aligning internal stakeholders around shared outcomes

This doesn’t just improve call quality.
It transforms how reps think, and how buyers respond.

If you’re working on a trust-first sales model, I’d love to connect and swap ideas.
