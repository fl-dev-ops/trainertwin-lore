*************
**First Drill**
************* 
Cut one deal. Right now.

Open your pipeline. Look at your top five. Ask one question:
“What’s the buyer’s next irreversible step?”
If you can’t name it, the deal is dead and emotional attachment has you trapped.

If the answer is a another undefined “meeting,” “check-in,” or “circling back,” it’s a zombie.
Delete it from your forecast.

If you won’t cut one dead deal today, stop reading. You’re not ready for truth.

This is LAZARUS Rule #1: 
No Parking. 
If it’s not advancing, it’s dead.

A deal that can’t advance is already a corpse. Stop carrying it.
