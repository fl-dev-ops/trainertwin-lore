The biggest mistake people make when choosing a CRM is shopping for features instead of outcomes.

They compare checkboxes.

They get lost in demos.

They pick based on what looks impressive, not what solves their revenue gaps.

The fix is simple.

Start with your problems.
Map your workflows.
Define success.

Then choose the platform that supports the strategy.

Technology should support your process, not define it.
