Q3 Numbers Stuck? Here's What They Won't Tell You (And How to Break Free When You Feel Frozen Too).

The whiteboard screams!
Target: £910,319. Actual: £862,773. Delta: -£47,546.
Red ink everywhere. Revenue dipping 10%. 

Deals grinding to a halt at proposal stage. Clients who once buzzed back in hours? Now it's crickets, weeks of eerie silence.

It hits hard. Feels like a personal attack. Like you're the failure, the reason everything's stalled.

But stop. You're not the storm. You're just caught in it.

Clients vanish into silence because of their fears, their chaos, not yours. Deals freeze from uncertainty, not rejection. Storms rage, but they don't last forever. The key? Don't fight the wind, stand firm until it shifts.

How you respond defines you. And how you are remembered.
