This is the right diagnosis.
Correlation funded confidence.
Capital followed activity, not authority.

Where this still fails is after approval.
That is where most value is lost.

A plan gets signed off.
Pressure rises.
Dates slip.
Assumptions stop holding.
No one feels able to reopen the decision, so workarounds take over.

Causality explains variance.
It does not protect the decision itself.

For boards and PE, that distinction matters.
The risk is not poor modelling.
It is funding plans whose authority has already decayed.

Better maps help.
What is missing is knowing when a decision is no longer safe to fund.

That is where value leaks.

Causal AI helps explain performance.
Decision survivability protects enterprise value.

One tells you why results moved.
The other tells you when capital should pause, before outcomes fail.

That is the difference between insight and governance.

If this resonates, the harder question is what happens to decisions after approval.
