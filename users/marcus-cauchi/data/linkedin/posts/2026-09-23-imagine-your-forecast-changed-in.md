Imagine your forecast changed in May. What if the evidence supporting it had already materially weakened in March?

For those two months, Finance may still have planned against the number. People and capacity may have been allocated around it. Management may have made perfectly reasonable decisions using a conclusion the available evidence no longer supported.

Most companies can tell you when the forecast changed. Far fewer can tell you when it should have been reconsidered.

How big is that gap in your business, and what are you deciding inside it?
