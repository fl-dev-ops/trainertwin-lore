Founders, when you set up in business, you bought a sales job. Like it or not, that's reality. If you hate selling, enough to avoid doing it or seeing yourself as a salesperson, you'll probably struggle to make a success of your business.

This session is the opposite of being "salesy", pushy, or manipulative.

Our job is to facilitate buying not force a transaction. The seller's role is to empower the buyer to make the right decision for themselves and recognise if we are the right fit for each other. The pressure comes from starting from the wrong intent. The role is one of service not servitude or selfishness.
