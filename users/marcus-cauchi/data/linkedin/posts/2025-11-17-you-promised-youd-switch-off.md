You promised you’d switch off. Your inbox had other plans.
Most founders know that moment too well.
 You step away, but the work doesn’t. Not really.

Your family thinks you’re resting.
Your team thinks you’re offline.
Your phone’s an itch that needs scratching.

This isn’t about burnout or discipline.
 It isn’t about needing a better handover.
 It’s the nagging doubt that something will slip if you’re not watching.
 And once that doubt is there, you never fully switch off.

You stay half-present.
 On the beach.
 At dinner.
 Everywhere.

This newsletter gets to the truth underneath that pattern.
 Why work slows when you step away.
 Why your team hesitates.
 Why you end up checking email even when you meant not to.

If it sounds like your life, read the full post.
It calls out what you’ve been holding together, and why it’s felt normal for too long.
