Last night I was nervous.

Why?

The physical proof of our book arrived… and I was almost afraid to open the package.

It was supposed to show up Monday, but the storm delayed it. All day UPS said “out for delivery.”

7pm hit. Nothing.

Then I get the email: Delivered.

I check both doors. The mailbox. Nothing.

Now I’m walking the neighborhood, trying not to look like a porch pirate, peeking at stoops. On the fourth house over I found it.

I brought it home, took a breath, and opened the box.

And wow.

It turned out great.

Writing a book is long and complicated. This one is a big collaboration, too.

But beyond the writing itself, the production quality really mattered to Peter Cleary, Eli 5 Stone and me.

We have full-color comics throughout. We wanted it to feel like a graphic novel in your hands—not just a business book with illustrations.

We labored over paper stock. Coatings. Color processing. How the images would print. On PDF it looked fantastic.

But how would it feel physically?

Peter and I are comic fans. Eli is a legendary comic book writer and artist. We wanted that Watchmen / Batman graphic novel feel. Something substantial. Something you’d actually want to hold.

We’re also on a launch timeline… and our publisher (the amazing Kory Kirby) is currently riding his mountain bike across Arizona. So making changes at this stage would have been… complicated.

So when I held it, felt the glossy cover, flipped through the pages, and saw the comics pop the way we hoped they would…
it was a real moment.

Then my son Ted Stearns walked in, picked it up, flipped through it, and said:
“That’s legit.”

At that moment, I knew we got it right.

Now we’re onto launch.

Graphic Sales officially drops April 1st.
