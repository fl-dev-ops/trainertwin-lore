You go on holiday.
You promise your family you’ll rest.
You still check email.

Not because you want to.
Because a part of you isn’t sure the work will run clean without you.

Work doesn’t wait because people are lazy.
It waits because they’re not sure what’s safe without you.

You see it in the tiny moments:
the quick check ins, the pauses, the softened truth.
Work moves, then hesitates, then lands back on your desk.

This isn’t control.
It’s fear.
Quiet, reasonable fear that comes from not knowing if the system will hold without you watching it.

And that fear has a cost:
Decisions land late.
Problems show up only when they’re already hot.
Your inbox stays full because nothing feels finished until you look at it.

People trust you.
That’s why everything circles back.

This doesn’t get fixed with more hires, more tools, or more effort.
It gets fixed when the system can prove it works without you.

That’s the real job.

I help founders remove the fear that keeps work waiting for them.
When that fear drops, decisions land earlier.
Escalations fall away.
Truth shows up before the fire.
You stop being the person everything waits for, and your team stops guessing what’s safe.

You take Tuesday mornings off without checking your phone.

If you want to see where your business still waits for you, we can map it together.
No pressure.
Just clarity on what’s actually keeping you in the loop.
