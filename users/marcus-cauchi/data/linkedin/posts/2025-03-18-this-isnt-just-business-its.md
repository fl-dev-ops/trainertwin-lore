THIS ISN’T JUST BUSINESS. IT’S A NEW ERA.

The old way?
Hoard power.
Gatekeep opportunity.
Demand control instead of earning trust.
 That era is collapsing. Rapid undesired forced changes in quick succession has made the old playbook redundant.

The new way?
* Trust is the currency.
* Execution speed beats outdated authority.
* Leaders don’t extract, they expand.

The game isn’t changing, it’s already changed. The only question is who’s still pretending it hasn’t?
