💡Most GTM teams don’t have a tool problem.  They have a visibility problem.

Deep dive gap analysis used to be a luxury. 
It was slow, expensive, and built for enterprises.

But things are changing.

Thanks to AI, startups and SMBs can now run faster diagnostics on their GTM engines. CRM data. Calendar metadata. Call transcripts. Engagement signals. All available in minutes.

You can plug in AI. You can pull CRM data, calendar metadata, and call transcripts. You’ll get a heatmap. A dashboard. Maybe even a few automated “insights.” 

But real friction?

AI only sees what it’s told to look for.
Gap analysis isn’t just about data.

It’s about patterns. Team dynamics. Repeated friction points no dashboard will ever show.
💡A rep hesitating to book a meeting.
💡 An AE skipping discovery because "they won’t buy anyway."
💡A handoff that consistently breaks, but no one talks about it.

These aren’t database problems.
They’re human problems.
And they require human observation.

The most powerful insights still come from the ground.
From interviews. Shadowing. Listening between the lines.

Then—and only then—AI can help validate the patterns and scale the learning.
