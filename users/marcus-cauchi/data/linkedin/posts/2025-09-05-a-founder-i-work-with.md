A founder I work with said this recently:

“Revenue’s solid. Burn’s under control. But I feel further away from what’s actually working than ever.”

He wasn’t panicking. He was just being honest.
The team’s humming. The dashboards are full. The story makes sense on paper.

But under the surface? The signal’s soft. It takes longer to answer simple questions.
- Which customers are driving margin?
- What’s working in this channel vs. that one?
- Are we retaining for the right reasons?

That’s the drift. It always shows up, usually somewhere between Series A and “Oh shit, we’ve got a real company now.”

Not because the business is broken. Because it’s growing faster than the clarity.
And when you lose that connection, between action and impact, it gets harder to lead. Decisions slow down. Conviction slips.

You still trust the team. You still believe in the product. But you’re guessing more than you used to.

And that feeling? That “I don’t know why this is working” feeling? That’s the red flag. Not for failure. For compounding risk.

The good news: you can fix it. You can get back to real signal. Not post-hoc. In real time. That’s what we help teams do. Quietly. Precisely. Fast.

If you’re in that blurry middle, where everything looks fine, but something feels off, you’re not crazy.

It’s not your gut that’s wrong. It’s your visibility. Your eyes won't see if your imagination is out of focus.
