We measure everything - profit, performance, precision - except the one force holding it all together: #trust. It drives who we hire, who we back, and which deals survive, yet we still treat it as instinct.

The Mathematics of Trust turns that gut feeling into data. I want to develop it further with leading researchers and early-stage investors. It maps how trust and risk shape every decision.

This isn’t theory, it’s evidence, showing how to see what intuition only senses.

You can’t lead what you can’t measure.
Now we can start.
