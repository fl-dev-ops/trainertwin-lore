Most “objection handling” advice is still stuck in the 90s.

Memorise 30 clever comebacks. Hit the buyer fast. Win the point.
It works, if your goal is to win arguments ... and lose people.

The truth? Objections rarely kill deals. What you do with them does.

And right now, most sellers are doing the exact thing that shuts buyers down:
* You trigger a status threat.
* Cortisol goes up, curiosity drops.

The conversation most in need of truth and safety gets flooded with pressure and performance.

The result? Buyers stop telling you what’s really at stake. You chase ghosts for months.

It doesn’t have to be that way.

Objections aren’t walls. They’re flares in the night, noisy, bright, and unbelievably useful if you know how to read them.

In my latest post, I break down:
* The four drivers underneath almost every objection
* The trust/timing/change/value gaps they signal
* How to turn “pushback” into clarity, trust, and progress in minutes

If you’ve ever lost a deal after “handling” the objection perfectly, read this before you go into your next call.

Click. Learn the map. Stop boxing shadows.
