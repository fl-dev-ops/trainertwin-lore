One deal can erase a decade of work. Too many founders take the cheque and lose their company, their team, their sovereignty.

You built your company for years, maybe decades.

One deal can strip it away in twelve months. Too many founders take the cheque, and what looked like a win turns into regret: the team gone, the culture erased, the customers scattered, and your sovereignty reduced to a clause in someone else’s contract.

The theatre of M&A is convincing. Glossy decks. Urgent buyers. Inflated multiples. But behind the curtain the process is often designed to extract, not protect. Valuation headlines make you feel rich. Deal terms make you poor.

Here’s the hard truth:
* Structure beats price.
* Sovereignty beats speed.
* Continuity beats theatre.

This article is not another “valuation playbook.” It is a compass. A way to sell without surrendering what you have built.

Before you step into the process, check yourself against 7 red flags that reveal when you are being nudged into a bad deal. Miss even one, and you will regret it.
