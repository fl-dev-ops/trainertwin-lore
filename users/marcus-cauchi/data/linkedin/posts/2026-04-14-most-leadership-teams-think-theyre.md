Most leadership teams think they’re managing risk.

They’re not. They’re deciding who ends up living with it.

A deal gets signed. A programme goes live. Everything looks fine until it doesn’t. Then the problem shows up somewhere else, usually far away from the people who made the call.

And when it does, it gets labelled execution.

It isn’t. It’s the point where a decision no longer holds. There’s a simple question most teams avoid asking early enough.

If this works less well than promised, who pays first?

The answer tells you everything about how your organisation really operates.
