Every commercial organisation has work that shouldn’t exist.

Not because people are lazy or incapable, but because one team’s decisions become another team’s repairs. The cost rarely stays where it starts. It moves through meetings, reports, escalations, discounts, forecast changes, onboarding issues, customer complaints and management firefighting until it simply becomes “the way things are.”

That’s what makes this difficult to diagnose. Sales sees one problem. Customer Success sees another. Finance sees a third. The board sees a fourth. Everyone is looking at the same underlying condition from a different vantage point.

I’m interested in where you think the repair work is most visible today, not because that’s necessarily where it began, but because it’s usually where the pain is finally felt.

Vote in the poll. Then, if you’re willing, tell me why you chose your answer. I’m especially interested in organisations where the same commercial problems keep returning despite investing in people, technology and process.
