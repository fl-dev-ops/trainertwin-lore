You don’t notice when you’re in a rut.

Work still moves.
Meetings still happen.
Problems still get handled.

Nothing feels blocked.
Nothing feels urgent.

That’s what makes it hard to name.

36 years ago, my first mentor described a rut as a coffin with both ends kicked out.
Not stuck.
Just unable to leave.

Most organisations don’t feel trapped.
They feel busy.

And that’s why the state can last for years without being questioned.
