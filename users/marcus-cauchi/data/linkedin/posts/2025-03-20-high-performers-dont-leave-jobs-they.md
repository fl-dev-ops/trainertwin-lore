HIGH-PERFORMERS DON’T LEAVE JOBS. THEY LEAVE LOW-TRUST ENVIRONMENTS.
Leaders act shocked when their best people walk.
  “But we paid them well!”
  “But they had perks!”
  “But they had the title!”

What they don’t ask? 
👉 Did they trust us to build a place where they could thrive?

People stay where they trust leadership.
 People stay where trust fuels speed, autonomy, and execution.

If you’re losing top talent, it’s not a salary problem, it’s a trust problem.

Look in the mirror. Is there a valuable lesson there? 

Oh, and tag someone who builds teams that people don’t want to leave. Who is/was the best boss you've ever worked with and why?
