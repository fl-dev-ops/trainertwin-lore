Revenue durability is rarely tested where the risk actually sits.

Most diligence validates the product and the market story. The fragility usually sits in the conditions that produced the revenue. If deals were won through discounting, political sponsorship inside the buyer, or service work masking product gaps, the numbers can look healthy for years.

By the time churn shows up in NRR, the drift has already happened.

The real question for boards is not just why customers bought. It is whether the decision conditions that produced that revenue are repeatable and stable.
