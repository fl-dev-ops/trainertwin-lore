Why Your Forecast Is Bullshit (And You Know It)

We’re not here to flatter your funnel.
We’re here to show you why your forecast can’t be trusted, and why that’s your system’s fault, not your people’s.

You might not want to hear this, but:
	•	You’ve built your forecast on CRM stages your buyers don’t believe in.
	•	You’re forecasting revenue from people you’ve never met in rooms you’ve never entered.
	•	You’re basing decisions on urgency that only exists in your pipeline, not in their boardroom.
	•	You’re rewarding activity that creates updates, not outcomes.
	•	You’re optimising for TAM when most of your SOM doesn’t even know they have a problem.

And worst of all?

You’ve turned your CRM into your strategy, and now it’s lying to you, at scale.

Your system:
	•	Mistakes data entry for deal momentum
	•	Measures touchpoints instead of trust
	•	Assumes sequence in what is actually chaos
	•	Accepts rep belief as buyer truth
	•	Believes champions are decision-makers
	•	Tracks intent and ignores inertia
	•	Logs what you did instead of what they did

You don’t have a forecast.
You have a projection of internal hope onto external silence.

The truth?

You can’t forecast what you can’t see.
And you can’t see the buyer’s world unless you stop mapping yours onto it.

So here’s what this is really about:
	•	Replacing CRM compliance with buyer signal.
	•	Replacing ego with evidence.
	•	Replacing linear logic with nonlinear models.
	•	Replacing noise with narrative clarity.
	•	Replacing hustle with heuristics that scale.

You don’t need a better forecast.
You need a system that tells the truth, even when it hurts.

Wake up. Look in the mirror.
