The most dangerous idea in sales is simple:

“We don't need apprenticeship any more.”

Look around.

Managers promoted too early. Reps left to guess. Pipelines full of noise. Coaching done after the fact. Training replaced by tools. AI used as a shortcut instead of a multiplier.

None of this is mysterious.
We removed the thing that made skill possible.
