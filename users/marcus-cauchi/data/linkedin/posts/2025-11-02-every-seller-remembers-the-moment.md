Every seller remembers the moment they snapped.
For Edward Ingham, it came mid-lockdown - five years into boiler-room selling.
 Alone in a Madrid flat, staring at another rejection, he thought: “Is it me?”

It was.
That realisation - and 1,200 hours of TheInquisitor Podcast episodes - led him to rebuild how he sells from the ground up.

He stopped pushing products.
 Started learning buyers.

Built mentors from old prospects.
 Learned procurement’s KPIs before pitching.
 Stopped discounting completely.

And within two years, he’d gone from SDR burnout to board director.

In this conversation, we dissect that shift: from fear to ownership, rejection to trust, scarcity to respect.

If you’ve ever wondered what it actually looks like to go from “product peddler” to “customer ally,” this is the episode you share with your team.

🎧 Stop Product-Peddling. Start Selling Like a Human.
