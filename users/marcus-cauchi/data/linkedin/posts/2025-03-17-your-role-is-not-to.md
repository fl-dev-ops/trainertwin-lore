YOUR ROLE IS NOT TO BE ADMIRED, IT’S TO CREATE MORE LEADERS.

Too many “leaders” are obsessed with looking powerful instead of building power in others.

If your success depends on keeping others small, you’ve already lost.
* Real leaders aren’t the centre of the system, they create systems that thrive without them.
* Real leaders don’t need control, they build trust so strong that people move with them, not for them.
* Real leaders don’t collect followers, they multiply leaders.

Power isn’t what you hold. It’s what you pass on.

Tag someone who builds leaders, not followers.
