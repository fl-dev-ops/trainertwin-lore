Margarita Savitytska raises a problem worth thinking about if you’re responsible for a B2B marketing budget.

You look at your reports, see what’s working and what isn’t, and decide where to spend more, less or nothing at all.

Fair enough. But what if you’re missing something important?

Someone asks ChatGPT to recommend a supplier. Three of your competitors appear. You don’t.
Someone else searches for help with a problem you solve, but finds your competitors instead.
Neither person visits your website. Neither appears in your reports.
Now you’re sitting there wondering why enquiries are down, whether your campaigns need fixing or whether you should increase your spend.

But what if the problem is happening before anyone gets near your website?

Of course, being missing from a search or AI answer doesn’t prove you’ve lost business. You’d need to know whether your buyers are actually looking there and whether it affects their choices.

But wouldn’t you want to find that out before committing more money?

I’m curious how many B2B marketing leaders can see where their business is being recommended, where it’s being overlooked and which competitors are getting attention they never knew was up for grabs.

And more importantly, how that knowledge changes what they decide to do.
