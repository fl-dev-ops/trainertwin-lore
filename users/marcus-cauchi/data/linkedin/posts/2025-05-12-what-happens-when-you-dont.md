Not coaching doesn’t just stall growth.
 It silently builds a system that breaks under pressure.

Here’s what that looks like:

Blindspots
Reps can’t see what’s missing, until the deal is already lost.
Pipeline looks “full” but it’s built on false signals.
Everyone’s guessing what “good” looks like.

Result
You review lagging indicators and wonder why there’s no change.

Bottlenecks
Every key decision routes through you.
Forecasts feel like fiction because trust in the system is gone.
