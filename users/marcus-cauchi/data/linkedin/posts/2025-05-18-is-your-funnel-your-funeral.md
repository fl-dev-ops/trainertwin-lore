 Just like Edward Bear. Bumping down the funnel. Campaign to campaign. 

Forecast to forecast.
 Not because they’re foolish.
 But because no one ever stopped to ask:
 "Is there a better way?”

 Isn’t marketing’s big moment, but your trust suicide note, three quarters from now?

If you keep optimising the wrong model…
 You’ll wake up 6-9 months later with burned-out reps, churned customers, and even deeper boardroom suspicion.
