Some careers don't stall because you've stopped growing.

They stall because you've become the person everyone relies on.

You're trusted with the difficult conversations, the awkward customers, the cross-functional problems and the work that doesn't fit neatly into anyone else's job description. People tell you you're strategic, dependable and invaluable. They ask for your opinion when the stakes are high.

Yet when the next opportunity appears, you find yourself watching someone else step into it.

That creates a difficult question most experienced leaders never say out loud. Is patience still serving me, or have I confused being useful with moving forward?
If that question has crossed your mind, even once, you're not alone.
