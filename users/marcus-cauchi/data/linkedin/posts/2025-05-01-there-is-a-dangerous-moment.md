There is a dangerous moment in every sales organisation, and it rarely shows in a dashboard.

It is when the manager feels the tension, senses the hesitation, notices the rep’s energy slipping, but looks at the dashboard flashing green and convinces themselves they are wrong.

The system says all is well.
 The numbers look healthy.
 So they override their own gut, their heart, their head.

They trust the machine over their human judgement.
Not out of laziness, but out of fear. Fear of raising an alert too early. Fear of sounding alarmist. Fear of being told they are the problem, not the system.

This is not leadership.
 This is abdication dressed as alignment.

The hard truth is this:
 Dashboards report what has already happened.
 The human system senses drift in real time.

The signal is always felt before it is seen.
 The risk is always visible to those close enough to feel it.

The work is to trust those human signals and build systems that do not punish early warning, but reward it.
