The time to understand your dependence on the next cheque is before you take this one.

The first investment may happen while you still have cash, momentum and several choices. You can negotiate, choose between investors and still say no. The next round may happen after growth has slowed, cash is tight, debt is already in the structure and one existing investor is the obvious source of more money.

That doesn't make the first cheque a mistake. But it changes the question I'd ask before taking it: what has to remain true for us to have genuine choices the next time we need capital?

The first cheque changes the bank balance. It may also change who holds the cards when you need the second one.
