Pipeline can grow while fit gets worse.
Retention can look steady while the account is already weakening.
Forecasts can stay clean while confidence outruns evidence.
That is the gap many GTM reviews miss. They inspect the reported performance, but not always the condition underneath it.
By the time the numbers force the conversation, the cheapest intervention window has usually gone.
I wrote about revenue that starts becoming expensive before the board pack says anything is wrong.
When GTM Looks Fine and Value Is Already Leaking
The harder question is not whether GTM is performing.
It is whether the performance can be trusted.
