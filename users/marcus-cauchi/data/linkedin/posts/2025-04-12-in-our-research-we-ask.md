In our research, we ask buyers if they initiated contact with sellers in their buying journey, or if sellers did. You can see what they say, and it's very consistent across the spectrum of purchase cost.

At the larger end, it's notably lower (though still north of 70%). I imagine at the higher end, it is likely that the accounts have more dedicated sales coverage and so sellers have more relationships prior to the start of the buying journey. 

In related research, we found that 75% of buyers were receiving calls and emails prior to that time.

But the message could not be clearer. 

Buyers initiate contact as and when they are ready. The fact of you calling and emailing simply doesn't change the timing of when they will engage. 

How you conduct yourself while trying to contact them surely has an influence on where you sit on the preference list when that first conversation happens.
