I once spent 10 straight days in Las Vegas managing two trade shows at Mandalay Bay.

On day five, I realized I hadn’t been outside once.

No fresh air. No daylight.

Every minute was the exhibit hall, hotel, casino, or a restaurant in the same building. That realization was… unsettling.

If you’re at CES this week, a few reminders:

Pace yourself.
Late nights add up fast. Have fun but stay sharp.

Your company is spending a lot of money to be there.
Treat it like the investment it is.

If you’re working a booth, do the basics right:
 • Face out
 • Smile
 • Ask questions before you pitch

Most trade shows have a plan.
Too few have disciplined reps.
