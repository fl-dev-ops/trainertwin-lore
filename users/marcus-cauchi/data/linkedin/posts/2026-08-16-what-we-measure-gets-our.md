What we measure gets our attention.
Activity is easy to measure.
Pipeline rewards movement.
Movement looks like progress.
Hesitation looks like friction.
Friction attracts pressure.
Pressure can create motion.
Persuasion can create commitment.
Commitment can close the deal.
The seller records the result.
The signature changes who owns the risk.
The buyer owns what the sale sets in motion.
Some consequences hit the numbers.
Some change the decision.
Some only show up when you try to make the change work.
Some hit the job.
Some hit the politics.
Some hit the person.
Some can be fixed.
Some cannot be undone.
Some take the choice away.
The risk of changing needs testing.
The risk of staying put needs testing.
The better future needs testing.
Questions expose assumptions.
Evidence tests what we think we know.
Evidence shows what else is possible.
What's possible makes choice real.
A real choice includes the freedom to stop.
Confidence has to be earned.
Confidence can justify movement.
Sometimes slowing the sale speeds up the buying.
Buying well starts where the buyer actually is.
The buyer pays for the outcome.

Now read it from the bottom up.
