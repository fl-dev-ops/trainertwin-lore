No one sets out to pass the burden on.

Most decisions are made in good faith.

Urgency gets satisfied at the moment of decision.

Things move.

Relief follows.

And something unresolved looks for somewhere to land.

From the decision to implementation.

From alignment into execution.

From one set of hands into another.

No one chooses that moment.

It happens while everyone moves on.

The decision still makes sense.

Nothing failed.

But the work feels heavier than expected.

Months later, people look for what went wrong.

They rarely ask a quieter question.


Where did the pressure go

after the decision felt finished?
