Failure is inevitable. It’s your best teacher if you learn from it. But corporates tend to treat failure as bad thing, even as a personal deficiency. 

That encourages people to hide their failures. That means you institutionalise waste, repeatedly making the same mistakes and people feeling like they are going to get found out and punished. 

The job is hard enough. Don’t be a twat!

Get your ego out of the way and ask, how do we trim failure into feedback? How do encourage our people to surface what’s not working and fix it?
