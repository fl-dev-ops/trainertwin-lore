As a buyer do you like to be convinced, handled, pressured, coerced?
As a buyer do you care about the target the seller has to hit?
As a buyer do you prefer salespeople to tell you the truth or lie to you?

Or do you want to achieve a better future?
And feel safe and certain in your decision?
And confident that the outcome you intended is what is realised?

TRUST = credibility+reliability+INTIMACY over Self-Orientation
Uncertainty x Vulnerability = Perceived Risk

If you make it about you, your quota and your needs, you trigger your buyer's brain to see you as a threat (high self orientation, spike in perceived risk).
Trigger uncertainty in your buyer's brain, and their brain's default setting is THE WORST CASE SCENARIO ... manufacturing anticipatory buyer's remorse in your buyer's brain seems like a shot to both your own feet.

That is going to be the peak emotional experience and hwo they will receive you the next time you try to engage with them.

Ever been ghosted?
Ever ask yourself, "Could it be me?"

Sorry to burst your bubble.

It is. 

Buyers object when salespeople project they are unsafe.
