Sales underperformance is often a system design problem, not a talent problem.

When leadership pressure makes trust uneconomic, pipeline quality collapses into compliance:
- forced timing,
- optimistic commits,
- and CRM data shaped to satisfy the forecast.

The denominator worth tracking is cost per credible opportunity.

How much of your pipeline reflects buyer intent, and how much reflects internal pressure translated into theatre?
