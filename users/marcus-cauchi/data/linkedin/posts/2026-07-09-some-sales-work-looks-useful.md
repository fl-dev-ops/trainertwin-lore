Some sales work looks useful because it creates movement.
 
 A meeting gets booked.
 A proposal gets sent.
 A CRM stage moves.
 A forecast improves.

But the buyer may not be any closer to making a good decision.
That’s dead work.

And dead work doesn’t disappear. It gets exported into Customer Success, Operations, Product, Finance, Renewals and the customer.

This edition is about the hidden cost of commercial work that should have been stopped earlier: wrong-fit customers, rushed decisions, thin business cases, exposed champions, margin leakage, rescue work and trust damage.

It’s not an argument against growth.
It’s an argument against pretending all growth is good growth.

Read it if you want to know where your dead work is hiding.
