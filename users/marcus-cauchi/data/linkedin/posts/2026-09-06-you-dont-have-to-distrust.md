You don't have to distrust an investor to say no to part of a deal.

You can believe they are capable, decent and acting in good faith. You can want them on the board and believe their involvement will improve the company. You can still decide that a particular term, amount of rollover, level of debt, approval right or future obligation asks too much of you.

Trust answers a question about the people you are dealing with. It doesn't answer whether every part of this particular decision is right for you. Founders can get trapped into thinking that pushing back implies mistrust. It doesn't.

You can trust the investor and still ask for a different deal.
