Your business is producing exactly what it was built to produce.

The problem is not bad luck or a few weak people. It is the design. Pressure comes down from the top. The middle is held responsible for outcomes it does not control. So managers stop managing reality and start managing the story.
That creates an optics loop. Targets are set. Pressure is applied. Managers adapt to survive. Reporting gets cleaned up. Leaders then make new decisions based on edited signals. The better the deck looks, the less leadership can actually see.

The costs are predictable. Weak deals stay alive. Customers get pushed too early. Delivery and support inherit promises they cannot keep. Good people burn out managing perception instead of truth.

This is not accidental. It is a rational response to the environment. If you want different outcomes, change the conditions that make distortion necessary.
