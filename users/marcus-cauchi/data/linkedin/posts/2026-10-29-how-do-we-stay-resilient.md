How do we stay resilient through so much change?

That's the question I asked James Elliott MSc, author of the book 'Think yourself resilient'.

James served 14 years in the airborne forces and became second in command of British Army mental resilience training. 

Because the speed of change and uncertainty in the marketing services industry can be de-stabilising for those working in it right now, I asked James for tips on how to move through it.

He said you need to separate what you control and influence from what you can't (attitude and effort are always within our control). 

“There is no such thing as 'it is what it is'. There is only, 'it is what I choose to make it'."

When worry kicks in, James suggests changing the question. 

Instead of “why do I feel this way?”, ask “why is my subconscious choosing for me to feel this way?”

He's also blunt about resting. Having coached elite athletes, his view is that “you can only grow from the training that you recover from”.

James quoted a line from the wall at the RAF parachute training school at Brize Norton: ‘knowledge dispels fear’. 

I really enjoyed this interview, James is so likeable and passionate about this topic.

His story is heartbreaking but inspiring. He's had to overcome huge adversity in his life.
