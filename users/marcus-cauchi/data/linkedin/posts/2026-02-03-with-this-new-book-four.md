With this new book, Four Levers Negotiating, that we annoyed your timelines with during yesterday's launch (that wasn't on purpose, btw...), I tried to write a "long-game" book.

As a sales history nerd, two things I mean by that:

1) I have quite literally hundreds of dusty 100+ year old sales books in my collection. 

Every book on sales has seemingly been written already. Just with different spins, titles, levels of aggression, etc. 

I'd like to believe that none of the three books I've written now had ever been written before.

2) I love books that can serve as a guide for the rest of someone's career. It's not a business card. It's not a bio booster.

And, my nightmares are made up of writing a book, launching it, having a bunch of people say, "Good for you, Todd!", and a month later it has been forgotten.

The Transparency Sale still sells like crazy...years later. (The Transparent Sales Leader? Not as much, although I think it may be more useful and better written than my first...)

Excited to see the opening spike, but hope Four Levers Negotiating is something you can use for an entire career.

(And I'll keep asking...if you've read it, PLEASE leave an honest review wherever you bought it. It has such an impact on future readers, and on retailers deciding what to prioritize)
