Before you let AI update your CRM

Before you let AI update your CRM automatically, give it four statements: “I’ll speak to the CFO.” “I spoke to the CFO.” “The CFO is joining our next meeting.” “The CFO has approved the spend.”

Those four statements don’t tell you the same thing. If they all end up as some version of “CFO engaged”, you’ve got a problem.

Automating it hasn’t solved the problem. You’ve just made it much faster to do properly or badly.
