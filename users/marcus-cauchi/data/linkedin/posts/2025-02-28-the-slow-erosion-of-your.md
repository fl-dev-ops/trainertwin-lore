The Slow Erosion of Your Power

Ever noticed how loss of rights doesn’t happen in one big move?
Instead, it happens in increments. Little by little. One “small” legal shift at a time.

You barely register it. A slight tweak in terms and conditions. A new policy no one reads. A subtle wording change in a bill.

Then one day, you wake up and realise you have nothing left to bargain with.
But here’s the thing: it only works if you stay passive.

If they expect no resistance, they’ll take everything.
If they know you’re watching, engaged, and unwilling to roll over, they’ll calculate the cost of pushing forward.

Every successful rights movement in history began not with permission, but with resistance.

What happens next isn’t up to them. It’s up to you.
