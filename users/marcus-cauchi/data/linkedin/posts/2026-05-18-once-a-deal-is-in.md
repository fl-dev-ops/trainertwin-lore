Once a deal is in "commit" the price paid by those who tell the truth that the deal won't come in by the expected date is often so high, it's safer to say nothing. Boards are then working off the narrative and optics management not the truth. The ripple effects of this are enormous, and felt on the balance sheet, in sales, CS, operations, account management, product, HR, it affects hiring, firing and above all, it affects your customers.
