Which unknown could remove an option you may need later?

You are never going to know exactly what the market will do, when the exit will happen, whether another round will be available or how every future board decision will go. Trying to remove all of that uncertainty before signing is impossible.

But not every unknown matters equally. One may make the commitment unaffordable if it goes against you. Another may leave you without an option you would need to recover. A third may simply be uncertainty that everybody has to live with.

The job isn't to make uncertainty disappear. It is to know which unanswered question should stop you committing and which one you can knowingly accept.

I've put the five questions I would want answered before signing into a one-page check. If you are dealing with a live capital decision, I'm happy to send it.
