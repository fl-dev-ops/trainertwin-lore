About 20 years ago, I realised I was caught in a procurement trap when on the 5th occasion dealing with Halfords, 1000 miles of driving to and fro, I called to tell them I had to postpone because of a family matter, and procurement told me, "That's OK. Could you send us your deck?" and the penny finally dropped. I was column fodder and expert in giving free consulting.

They loved my research and had no intention of buying. That's when I realised I had to get my need to people please under control. Some would say I've swung too much over the other way! Read on and you can decide for yourself.
