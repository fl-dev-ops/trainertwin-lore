When someone goes quiet

You know that moment after a good call
 when the buyer goes silent?

You replay what you said.
 You check your tone, your timing, your follow-up.
 Nothing feels wrong, but nothing moves.

It’s not the price.
 It’s not the deck.
 It’s safety.

Something in that moment made truth feel risky.
 And silence always feels safer than risk.

We ran the numbers across thousands of deals.
 Almost every ghosted opportunity broke at the same point —
 between belief and proof.

The story landed.
 But safety cracked.

You don’t fix that with pressure.
 You fix it with proof, patience, and honesty that costs you something to say.

Because the moment you make it safe to speak again,
 the silence ends on its own.

What part of your process makes honesty feel risky?
