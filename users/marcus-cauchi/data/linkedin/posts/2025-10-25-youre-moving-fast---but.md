You’re moving fast - but everything still pulls back to you.
That’s not a failure of leadership; it’s physics.

You’ve built a company that orbits the wrong centre: you.
Every decision, every question, every fire ends up back in your inbox.
You’ve become the energy source and the bottleneck - the gravity holding it together and the weight holding it down.

The Founder Gravity Trap is what keeps brilliant founders exhausted, reactive, and quietly ashamed of their own success.

You don’t need to work harder, hire more, or find better hacks.
You need to change the physics - to redesign how clarity, trust, and ownership flow through your company.

This is the moment to step out of the pull.
 You’re not broken. Your system just needs new laws.
