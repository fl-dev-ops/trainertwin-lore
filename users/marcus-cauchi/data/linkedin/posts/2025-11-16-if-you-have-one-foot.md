“If you have one foot in tomorrow and one foot in yesterday you are well positioned to piss on today.” - Charlie Green

You know how this goes.
 You sit down and your head drifts.
 One part replaying something old.
 One part bracing for something that hasn’t even happened.

And today pays for it.
Here’s the question no one asks, even though it decides the whole day:
 “What have I pushed to ‘later’ even though I know it’s on me?”

Most call this pressure.
 It’s drift.
 Drift drains judgement.
 It slows pace.
 It makes simple things feel heavy.

“Let go or be dragged.”

Pick one thing you can touch.
 Do that first.
 Everything else can queue.

Sounds simple.
 Here’s the snag:
 You still have to pick the right thing.
 Guessing is how drift wins.
