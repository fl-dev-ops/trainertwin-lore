ALIGNMENT BEATS TALENT.

Raw talent means nothing if it’s pointed in the wrong direction.
  A misaligned genius slows a team down.
  A brilliant but resistant player creates drag.
  A talented but selfish operator kills momentum.

Alignment multiplies results. Misalignment bleeds energy.
* A mid-level player fully aligned with the mission will out-execute a genius who resists direction.
* A team rowing in the same direction will outperform a room full of high-performers working at odds.

Talent is potential. Alignment is execution.

Tag someone who knows alignment is the real unlock.
