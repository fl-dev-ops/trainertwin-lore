When Avner Baruch (B.Eng') asked me to write the foreword to his third book, Renewal Is Not a Date, the timing was obvious.

Everyone is talking about AI.
Everyone is complaining about no pipeline or broken Marketing.
Everyone is suddenly rediscovering Customer Success as the growth engine.

Very few are asking what actually changed underneath.
Avner does.

He connects the dots most leaders prefer to keep separate.
And he does it without the usual theatre. No platitudes. No “customer-centricity” slogans. No advice designed to make executives feel comfortable.

Buckle up ... this book leaves you blissfully unable to hide.

Most leadership teams are still optimising for optics. Numbers that look good in board decks. Growth that can be explained in a sentence. Expansion that sounds impressive. They reward visibility. They ignore effectiveness.

Until the model starts to strain.

Then they look for someone to blame. Headcount becomes the lever.
Execution becomes the excuse. The system remains untouched.

This book does not give you excuses.
It puts Cost-to-Serve next to ARR.
It treats renewal as the outcome of your decisions, not your negotiation skills.
It forces you to confront which revenue is worth keeping.
And what's not.

This book is for those prepared to deal with the mechanics of how their business actually works, not for leaders who want better narratives.

If you are serious about revenue, read it properly.
Congrats Avner Baruch (B.Eng') on the launch.
