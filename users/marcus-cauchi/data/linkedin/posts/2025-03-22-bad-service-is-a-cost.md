BAD SERVICE IS A COST SHIFTED TO THE CUSTOMER.

When companies cut corners, who pays?
  You do.

* When a business saves money by reducing support, you pay with wasted time.
* When they don’t train their staff properly, you pay with frustration.
* When they rush you through a purchase, you pay with regret.

Businesses that prioritise trust don’t shift their costs onto you.
* They build systems that make things faster, easier, and clearer.
* They respect your time as much as your money.

You deserve better. Demand it.
Tell your stories of a company that delivers real service, or one that needs to step up.
