The Hidden Taxes of Bad Measurement
Every business pays them.
Most never see them.

Part 9: The Evidence Tax
The Evidence Tax is paid whenever decisions are made on stories, opinions or confidence instead of proof. It rarely feels dangerous because everyone believes they have enough information.
The cost appears through avoidable surprises, emergency decisions and expensive clean-up. Good people acted with good intentions, but the business confused confidence with evidence.
Measure the strength of the evidence behind important decisions, not just the decisions themselves.
