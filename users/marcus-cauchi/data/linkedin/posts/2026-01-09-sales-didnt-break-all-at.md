Sales didn't break all at once. It drifted. Growth turned into a magic trick, leadership confused volume for value, and trust became a bulletpoint on a slide. Buyers built defences. Sellers learned workarounds. Everyone got busier; nobody felt safer.

Here's the hinge: trust isn't a mood; it's a system. When incentives misalign and people can't say no without a penalty, suspicion is rational. We trained for persuasion and got corrosion. We optimised for the close and lost the relationship.

What's next isn't a shinier script. It's a different operating system: decision safety, shared reality, and accountability that makes both sides braver. It scales trust without theatre. It replaces pressure with clarity. It swaps the transaction for a partnership where the risks are shared.

If your models were built for 2010 and your market lives in 2026, you don't need more hustle; you need different physics.

Next week, swap "commit date" coaching for "clean-exit rights": every deal gets an explicit pause/exit both sides can use without penalty-then watch cycle time shorten and win rate climb.
