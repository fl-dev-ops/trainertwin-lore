Most PE pain does not begin when the quarter misses.

It begins earlier, when the Board still thinks it has time.

The pack looks fine enough.
The narrative still holds.
The caveats sound manageable.
No one wants to be the person who turns mild discomfort into a harder conversation.

So the signal gets softened.

What should feel dangerous gets described as timing.
What should be challenged gets labelled strategic.
What should trigger action gets left as something to watch.

That is how weak revenue survives long enough to become expensive.

Not because nobody saw it.
Because nobody forced it into one truth the Board could no longer explain away.

I turned that moment into a short video.

Not the miss itself.
The conversation just before the Board can no longer pretend the number is sound.

Watch it if you want to hear what late recognition, soft language, and unowned truth sound like before value leakage becomes impossible to hide.
