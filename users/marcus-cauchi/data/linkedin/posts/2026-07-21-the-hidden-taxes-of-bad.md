The Hidden Taxes of Bad Measurement
Every business pays them.
Most never see them.

Part 7: The Hero Tax
The Hero Tax is charged whenever one exceptional person repeatedly rescues the business. Their performance is celebrated, but the dependence they create is rarely measured.
Eventually the organisation discovers it has built success around individuals instead of systems. Burnout rises, knowledge remains trapped and growth slows because everything important depends on the same few people.
Measure how reliably the system performs without its heroes.
