“Can you help me stop guessing what buyers care about and have better conversations with real customers?”

Yes.

Most founders know their product inside out.
What they often know far less about is the decision happening on the other side.
Why did this buyer start looking?
What were they hoping to change or protect?
What made acting difficult?
Why did someone who seemed positive stop moving?

When those answers are missing, it is easy to fill the gaps yourself. Then you change the pitch, chase harder, add features or assume the buyer was never serious.

In this 60-minute working session, bring one real customer, prospect, lost buyer or stalled opportunity.

You will separate:
What you know because the buyer actually said or did it.
What you currently think may be true.
What you still need to ask.
You will leave with one person to contact, one question worth asking and a date to ask it.
You know your product. How well do you know why customers buy?

60-minute working session exclusively for founders
