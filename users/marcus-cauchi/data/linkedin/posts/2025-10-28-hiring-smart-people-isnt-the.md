Hiring smart people isn’t the hard part.
 Letting them actually run things is.

You say you want ownership.
 But when they take it, your chest tightens.

They make calls without you - and it feels wrong.
 Not because they failed.
 Because they didn’t need you.

That’s the quiet fear no founder admits:
 Becoming optional in your own company.

But leadership isn’t about being needed.
 It’s about being trusted.

You hired them to lead.
 Now let them prove you were right.

What’s one decision you could stop approving this week?
