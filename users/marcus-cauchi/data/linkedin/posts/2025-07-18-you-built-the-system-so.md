You Built the System. So Why Are You Still Blaming the Team?

You hired them.
You set the comp plan.
You picked the tech stack.
You approved the VPs.
You signed off the playbook.
So when it all underperforms, why is it still their fault?
The CRM’s a bin fire.
The pipeline’s theatre.
Your middle layer can’t coach.
And trust collapses the second you're out the room.
At some point, it’s not “underperformance.”
It’s design failure.
And you’re the designer.
The truth?
You’re not leading.
You’re just expensive drift in a tailored jacket.

WHAT AI WOULD DO INSTEAD
Flag the forecast fiction
Bin the vanity dashboards
Coach with context, not vibes
Fire managers who can’t build muscle
Surface GTM rot without spinning the story
It wouldn’t wait for a workshop.
It wouldn’t need a slide deck.
And it wouldn’t ask for a bonus to do it.

WHAT YOU STILL HAVE TO DO
Build a layer that coaches, not reports
Make trust the OS, not the side effect
Catch drift before it metastasises
Show up when it breaks, not just when it's easy
Lead the work between the work
You built the rules.
You designed the game.
You hired the players.
If the system keeps failing, you’re not the victim.
You're the architect.
And for god’s sake, stop hiding behind your fucking slide decks.

If you’re ready to rebuild the system you designed, before someone (or something) replaces you, say the word.

If you’ve been carrying this truth quietly, you’re not alone. Forward this to the person who needs to see it.
