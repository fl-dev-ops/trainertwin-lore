The Problem You Feel But Can’t Name

Something feels off. You sense it. A creeping loss of control over your own ideas, creativity, and choices.

It’s subtle. A shift in ownership you never agreed to. The rules are being rewritten - but not for your benefit.

Your work, your thoughts, your expression – suddenly not entirely yours anymore. Corporations and institutions are positioning themselves as the gatekeepers of permission. 

The fine print says it all:
Your data? They control it.
Your creativity? They monetise it.
Your ability to challenge them? Buried in legalese, drowned in delays.
And yet, it’s being framed as progress.
The real question isn’t “Will this affect me?” – but “How much have I already lost?”

If you had complete certainty that your work, your thoughts, and your contributions would remain yours, what would you create? What would you fight for?

Hold that thought. In a few days, you’ll know exactly what’s at stake.
