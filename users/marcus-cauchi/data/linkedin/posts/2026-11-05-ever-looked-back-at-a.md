Ever looked back at a decision and thought, why the hell didn’t we ask that?

The numbers looked right. The people involved knew what they were doing. Nobody thought they were making a mistake.

Then something came to light that changed how you saw the decision.

What interests me is: was there something we could reasonably have known beforehand? A question nobody asked? Another option we never considered?
And what might we have done differently if we’d known?

We explore that in this Commercial Reality Notes newsletter. The decisions we make about money, growth, customers and people, and the things that might change what we decide.

Not every decision needs changing. Sometimes a closer look tells us we’re on the right track.

If you’d rather spot something important before it’s too late to act on it, subscribe.
