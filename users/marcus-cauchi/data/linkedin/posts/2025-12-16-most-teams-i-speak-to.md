Most teams I speak to right now are feeling the same wobble.
Good people look unsure.
Managers are stretched thin.
Numbers swing for reasons no one can explain.
And the board wants answers that the data can’t give.

If any of that feels close to home, you’re not on your own.

I’m sharing this carousel that gets to the heart of it.
It’s about churn and turnover, but not the kind you see in dashboards.
It’s the slow leak that starts inside the team, inside the management layer, long before a customer ever leaves.

If the slides hit a nerve, and you want to sanity-check what you’re seeing in your own org, just message me.
No pitch.
No pressure.
Just the truth about what’s actually going on, and what other leaders are doing to steady the ship.

A lot of founders and CROs tell me the same thing after we speak.
“I knew something was off. I just couldn’t name it.”

If this helps you name it, good.
If it raises questions, even better.
Either way, you don’t have to work it out alone.
