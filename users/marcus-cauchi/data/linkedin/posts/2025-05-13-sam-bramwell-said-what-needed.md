Sam Bramwell said what needed to be said:
 No women at the table.
 No seat. No mic. No future.
Not a single voice representing over half the world’s population was included in a conversation about the most disruptive force in modern history.

This isn’t a glitch.
 It’s the pattern.

And it’s how exclusion at the design stage becomes inequality at scale.
 ❌ Women are most likely to hold the roles first erased by AI
 ❌ VC funding still flows disproportionately to male-led firms
 ❌ AI is being trained on biased, incomplete, and male-informed, male-weighted data

AI isn’t just a tool. It’s a value system in code.
 And right now, it’s being written without the majority having any input into the outcomes.

Sam called it. I’m amplifying it.
 Because without women at the table, there’s no ethical AI.
 No safe AI.
 No real future.

Dr. Lisa Palmer no doubt has real value to add to this debate as she brings range, compassion, intelligence and scientific rigour to the debate which frankly, was more of an exercise in willy waving that advancing humanity.
