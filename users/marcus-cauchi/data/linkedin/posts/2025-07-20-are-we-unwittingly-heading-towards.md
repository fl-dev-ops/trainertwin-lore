Are we unwittingly heading towards a future where a small number of extremely wealthy individuals and powerful corporations exert disproportionate influence over our legislation and institutions?

What kind of society do we aspire to create?

Is there a collective desire to challenge the systems that seem to dominate our democratic processes? As individuals, what actions can we take to foster a more equitable future?

I believe there's potential for meaningful change, but it begins with cultivating critical thinking and a willingness to question both ourselves and our leaders. The dialogue surrounding wealth inequality may only be scratching the surface. Should we consider advocating for reforms that address the monopolistic tendencies within sectors like finance, technology, and media?

As a parent and a concerned citizen, I encourage you to reflect on these questions. Our role as stewards of the planet implies a responsibility to ensure we leave it in a better state for future generations. There is a growing concern that unchecked capitalism prioritises profit over the well-being of our environment and society.

Now may be the time for each of us to clarify our values and take a stand. Will we actively shape our future, or will we allow circumstances to dictate our path?

I invite you to ask questions, share your thoughts. I have questions not answers. Is my critique flawed? Have my emotions got in the way of objectivity?

I'm confused, more than a little scared of what might happen; angry that I have sleepwalked my way into a corner. It feel like we are in a position where human lives and societal collapse are the price we are paying, and calling out what is morally unconscionable is considered by our government as "terrorism". We are gearing up for war, and injustices abound on both sides of the Pond.

Our perception of history is skewed and we are ignoring the warning that those who don't learn from history are doomed to repeat it. In Howe and Strauss's book #TheFourthTurning they describe 1929-1939 and the parallels are chilling. That didn't end well. Empires rise and fall and the battle between Wall St/Big Tech and China is playing out in Europe. The rise of religious fundamentalism, and extremism on Left and Right seems to be influencing geopolitics at such an unhealthy level.

We are guilty of conceit when it comes to Africa and the Global South after centuries of abuse. But I am an optimist that eventually, we will learn, but when?
