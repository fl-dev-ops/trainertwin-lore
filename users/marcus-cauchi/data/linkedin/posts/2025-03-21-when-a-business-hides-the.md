WHEN A BUSINESS HIDES THE TRUTH, YOU ALREADY KNOW THE GAME.

If a company makes it hard to get a straight answer, it’s because they’re hiding something.
If they bury key details in fine print, they don’t respect you.
If they force a call just to tell you basic information, they’re gaming you.
If they make you click through 5 pages to find what you need, it’s manipulation.
If they talk in circles instead of giving a real answer, assume the worst.

Great businesses don’t play hide-and-seek with their customers.
They’re upfront. Transparent. Confident in their value.

If you have to dig for the truth, assume the worst.
Tag a brand that gets transparency right.
