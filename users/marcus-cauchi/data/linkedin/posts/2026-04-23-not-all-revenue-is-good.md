Not all revenue is good revenue. Some revenue flatters the top line while eroding margins, increasing complexity, absorbing support capacity, distracting product teams, and draining leadership attention. Over time, that weakens execution and depresses performance. Investors see that risk in the numbers and price it in. Left unchecked, it can lead to tougher terms, dilution, and loss of control.
