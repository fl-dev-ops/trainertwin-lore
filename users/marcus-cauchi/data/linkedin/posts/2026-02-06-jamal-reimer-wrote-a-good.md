What I keep thinking about with stories like this is what the organisation inherits next.

Urgency reshapes decisions.
 Those decisions create commitments that revenue, marketing, CS, and operations have to live with long after the original insight is forgotten.

Sometimes that shape fits.
 Sometimes it becomes friction later.
