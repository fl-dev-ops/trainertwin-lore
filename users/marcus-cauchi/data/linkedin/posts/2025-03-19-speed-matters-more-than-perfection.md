SPEED MATTERS MORE THAN PERFECTION.

Most people wait too long to move.
* Waiting for more certainty.
* Waiting for permission.
* Waiting for the "perfect" moment.

By the time they act, the opportunity is gone.

The fastest teams win, not the perfect ones.

The leaders who execute, iterate, and adapt outpace those who overthink.

Speed is a competitive advantage. Over-analysis is a death sentence.
Move. Adjust. Move again. That’s how momentum is built.

Tag someone who moves fast and breaks limits.
