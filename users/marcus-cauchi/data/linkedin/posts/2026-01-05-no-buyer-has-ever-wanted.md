No buyer has ever wanted to be closed.

No one wants to be convinced, handled, or pushed.
No one wants to be lied to, rushed, or put under pressure.
No one wants facts hidden, risks softened, numbers stretched, or outcomes spun.

No buyer has ever put a seller's targets above their own decision.
No buyer has ever cared about your quarter, your pipeline, or your forecast call.
No buyer has ever sacrificed their job, reputation, or future so a seller could hit a number.

They never will.

Buyers are not buying features.
They are not renting functions.

They are trying to secure outcomes they can live with, defend, and survive if they are wrong.

When you add pressure, you become the risk they are trying to manage.

Everything else is noise.
