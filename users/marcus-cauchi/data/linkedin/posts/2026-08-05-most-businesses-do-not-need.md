Most businesses do not need another speech about accountability.
They need to stop placing all of it on the person standing closest to the damage.

A decision passes through judgement, authority, action, consequence and learning. When the people who shape and approve it disappear before the consequence becomes visible, demanding more ownership simply increases the pressure at the end of a broken chain.

That is how weak assumptions survive, inherited targets remain untouched and the wrong person gets replaced.

This article is about reconnecting the decision before asking for more accountability. It shows what leadership must preserve, revisit and learn before the same failure appears under a different name.

Approval is not the end of responsibility.
It's where responsibility begins.
