Time to Be Angry. Time to Stand Up. Time to Be Counted.

Because corporations get justice.
Software gets justice.
Soon AI may get justice.

But people?
We wait. We suffer. We’re told to be patient.
And nature? It doesn’t even get a hearing.

Fictions above facts.
Profits above people.
Justice upside down.

Enough.
