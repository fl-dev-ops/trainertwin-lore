Apprenticeship dies, judgement dies.
Judgement dies, managers rescue.
Rescue feels fast, but kills learning.
Reps wait instead of think.
Waiting becomes habit.
Habit becomes culture.
Culture becomes noise.
Noise becomes churn.
This is avoidable with structure.

Give managers back the space to manage.

Managers are not burning out.
They are being burned down by design.

Every team with wild swings shows the same pattern.
Fire drills. Forecast reviews. Internal updates. A bag to carry. Eight people to manage. No time to teach judgement. All the blame when things slip.

This is not a performance issue. It's structural load.

If you want predictable revenue, give managers the conditions to create it.
