AI's job should be to help the business see:

Where seller motion is being mistaken for buyer progress
Where confidence is being accepted before evidence
Where qualification is vague
Where forecasts are over-protected by narrative
Where managers are coaching performance instead of judgment
Where poor-fit revenue will create cost after signature
Where the customer has bought but not really decided

The wrong belief is that AI will make sales better.
