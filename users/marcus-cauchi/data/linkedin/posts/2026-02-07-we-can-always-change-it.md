We can always change it later.

That's what makes a decision feel safe.

Nothing is final.
Nothing feels locked.
If it doesn't work, we'll come back to it.

For a while, that feels true.

Then the work adjusts.

Days organise around it.
Handoffs adapt.
Small compensations make it hold.

By the time revisiting feels relevant,
there's more there than a decision.

There's a way of working.
A rhythm.
An expectation people have learned to live with.

Changing it now wouldn't just mean changing a call.
It would disturb all of that.

So "later" keeps slipping.

Not because anyone avoided it.
Because it stopped feeling simple to touch.

Most decisions don't become permanent suddenly.

They become difficult to undo
long before anyone notices.
