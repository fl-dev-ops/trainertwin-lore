Before you take the money, find out what comes with it

You have a term sheet. The valuation is better than you expected. The investor has a strong name, your advisers seem positive and everyone is telling you the deal is moving in the right direction.

You still have a nagging feeling that you cannot quite explain.

You are probably not imagining it. Everyone involved may be reviewing their part of the transaction, but nobody is responsible for joining all those parts together and showing you what the deal will actually do to you, your company and your options when the plan does not go as expected.

The lawyer reviews the documents. The accountant reviews the numbers. The corporate finance adviser helps get the deal done. The investor is deciding whether your company is safe enough for their capital.

Who is checking whether their capital is safe enough for your company?
