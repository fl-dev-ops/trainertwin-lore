99% failure is not best practice.

It is a sign the whole thing went sideways and nobody stopped to say it out loud. Somehow the industry turned a 1 percent reply rate into a badge of honour. As if that proves effort or grit. But if you step back for even a second, you can see what is really happening. People are working themselves into the ground because the system gave them one lever to pull, and that lever is always “send more.”

Reps are not the issue. Most of them are doing the only thing they can think of to keep their head above water. It's the equation that is broken. If ninety-nine people out of a hundred ignore you, the message is not “push harder.” The message is “you aimed at the wrong hundred.”

The work is not about volume. It is about seeing clearly again. Who matters. Why they might care. What moment they are in.

No amount of hustle fixes a bad target. And no amount of noise turns guesswork into strategy.We keep pretending the failure rate is normal.

It is not.

It is the clearest sign that we lost the plot and need a saner way forward.
