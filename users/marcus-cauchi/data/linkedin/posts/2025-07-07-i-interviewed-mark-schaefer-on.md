I interviewed Mark Schaefer on TheInquisitor Podcast a few years back. One thing he said has stuck with me and I've oft repeated 

"Think AS your customer,
  NOT ABOUT your customer 
  or what you can do or take from them"

Customers are not organic ATMs. 

We exist BECAUSE of the customer
 NOT IN SPITE of them.

Start by checking your ICP.

If you are struggling to fill your pipeline with quality prospects
 Or customers churn
 And referrals are rare

LOOK IN THE MIRROR

Are you selling selfishly?
 Is your marketing ... TIMELY, RELEVANT or VALUABLE to them?

Or are you boring them to death with features, functions and stuff YOU think is important?

When was the last time you spoke to your hero customers, the ones who love what you provide because you make them the hero in their own story?

If the answer to that is "I haven't"
 ... Look in the mirror BEFORE you waste another penny or marketing or another minute on prospecting.

YOU ARE THE PROBLEM!

You will probably have a constipated middle in yoru sales funnel
 A pipeline that needs a LAXATIVE:

L  Look for Sticking Points
 Spot what’s genuinely slowing things down.
A  Ask Real Questions
 Dig for what actually matters to them.
X  eXplore What’s at Stake
 Show the true cost of standing still.
A  Activate Ownership
 Help them take charge of the next step - shift from passenger to driver.
T  Turn Doubts into Drivers
 Work with their concerns so these fuel momentum rather than stall it.
I  Invite Options
 Let them choose possible ways forward.
V  Verify Concerns
 Check what’s still holding them back, openly.
E  Empower the Decision
 Leave them feeling in control, whatever they choose.
