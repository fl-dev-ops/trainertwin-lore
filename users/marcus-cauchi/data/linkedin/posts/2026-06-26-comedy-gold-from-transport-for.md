Comedy gold from Transport for London 🤣 - the fact that it was written by a marketer makes it even funnier!

Great public-service copy does two things:

1: gives you useful information, and 
2: is highly relatable.

“Carry water, wear light clothing” is the advice.

“The oven is angry with you” is the empathy.

“The marketing industry is away in Cannes” is the wink.

London Underground heat is a genuine problem... 

at least the copywriter understood the assignment 🤣👏
