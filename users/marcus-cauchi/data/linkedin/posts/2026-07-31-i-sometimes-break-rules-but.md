I sometimes break rules. But not these.

I don't drop links to my own posts on someone else's.
I don't pitch our services or events in someone else's comments.
I don't promote my sales community in someone else's sales community.

To me, that's bad form. Just as walking into the independent shop on the high street, setting up my stall, and telling every customer who walks in to pop down to my shop would be.

By all means engage. Agree, disagree, share your thoughts.  That will do more for your reputation than self-promotion ever will.
