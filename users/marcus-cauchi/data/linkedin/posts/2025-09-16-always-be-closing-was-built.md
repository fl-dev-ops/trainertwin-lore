“Always Be Closing” was built for a world where buyers had no power. That world is dead.

Today, the fastest way to kill trust is to treat every interaction as a push to close. Buyers see it. They feel it. And they walk.

Closing isn’t a skill. It’s the SYMPTOM OF SAFETY, clarity, and relevance. If you’ve done your job, built trust, removed risk, aligned outcomes, the close happens without tricks.

ABC belongs in the museum next to hard sell scripts and fax machines. The new standard?

Always Be Clearing. Clearing doubt. Clearing noise. Clearing the path to value Always Be Contracting. Little agreement after little agreement after little agreement  ... not one mega-contract at the beginning delivered like a shopping list.

That’s how deals survive contact with reality.

Ask yourself today: what can I clear for my buyer right now? What can we agree on now? And next? And after that?
