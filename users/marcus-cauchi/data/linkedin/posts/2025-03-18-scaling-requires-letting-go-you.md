SCALING REQUIRES LETTING GO.

You can be in full control or you can have full momentum.
You can’t have both.

Leaders who hoard decisions slow everything down.
Leaders who refuse to delegate cap their own impact.
Leaders who demand to be at the center of everything? They build bottlenecks, not businesses.

* If you want to scale, build leaders, not dependencies.
* If you want speed, replace permission-seeking with trust.
* If you want longevity, remove yourself as the single point of failure.

Let go or stay small. Those are the only options.

Tag someone who scales trust instead of hoarding control.
