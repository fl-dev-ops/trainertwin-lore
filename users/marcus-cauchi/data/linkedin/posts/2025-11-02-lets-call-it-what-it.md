Let’s call it what it is.
 Most sales teams aren’t forecasting - they’re guessing.

Every quarter it’s the same pantomime.
 Numbers rolled up. Arguments over “commit”.
 Everyone pretending the data’s solid.
 Then we miss. Again.

It’s not a data problem.
 It’s a behaviour problem that data keeps exposing.

Good sellers waste weeks on deals that were dead a month ago.
 RevOps keeps cleaning up instead of leading.
 Leaders keep hoping effort will turn into progress.

It doesn’t.
That’s why Bill Kantor and I teamed up.
* His company, Funnelcast, shows you - with real data - which deals to advance, which to park, and how that changes your number.
* I work on the behaviour and discipline that makes those choices stick.
Between us, we stop forecast fiction at the source.
