The Decisions That Matter
Part 7: Should We Trust This Dashboard?

Dashboards don’t simply measure performance. They shape it.

People quickly learn what matters by looking at the scorecard. If activity is rewarded, activity increases. If revenue matters more than customer outcomes, short-term wins become more valuable than long-term relationships. If recruitment is celebrated while activation is ignored, the numbers grow but the capability doesn’t.

The dashboard may be completely accurate and still encourage poor decisions because every measure rewards one behaviour while quietly making another less important.

The better question isn’t whether the numbers are correct. It’s whether the behaviour they encourage is the behaviour the business actually needs.

Before adding another metric, ask who will eventually pay for whatever that measure ignores.
