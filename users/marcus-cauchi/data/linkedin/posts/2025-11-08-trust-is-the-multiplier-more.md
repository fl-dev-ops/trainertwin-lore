Trust is the multiplier

More than once, I’ve sat in on a sales meeting that went sideways.
 The founder was angry.
 The team was quiet.
 They’d missed the quarter - not by much, but enough.

Everyone had worked hard.
 Made the calls. Sent the decks. Chased the deals.
 But somewhere along the way, they stopped telling the truth.

They told the founder what he wanted to hear,
 not what he needed to know.

That’s how trust disappears.
 Not in one big moment -
 in small ones, when honesty starts to feel unsafe.

When trust drops, everything slows.
 Decisions drag.
 Forecasts turn into fiction.
 You can still hit the number,
 but it’ll feel like pushing through wet concrete.

Trust isn’t fluffy.
 It’s fuel.
 It shortens the distance between truth and decision.

When people trust you, they move faster.
 They share problems sooner.
 They take ownership instead of waiting for permission.
 That’s what multiplies results - not pressure, not process, trust.

You don’t need another dashboard or another piece of software.
 You need PEOPLE WHO FEEL SAFE to tell you the truth.

So try this:
 ask one person this week what feels unclear or risky -
 and really listen.

That’s where speed starts again.
 Because trust doesn’t make you soft.
 It makes you fast.
 And lighter to lead.
