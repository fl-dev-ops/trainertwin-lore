There are conversations some leaders carry around for months.

Nobody knows they're happening because they never leave your head.

You rehearse the opening on the drive home. You rewrite it in the shower. You convince yourself next week will be a better time, after the restructure, after the board meeting, after the quarter ends. You tell yourself you're waiting for the right moment.

Most of the time, you're waiting for something else.

You're waiting for a way to ask that can't end in disappointment.

If you've ever found yourself rehearsing the same conversation for longer than you've been willing to admit, this might explain why.
