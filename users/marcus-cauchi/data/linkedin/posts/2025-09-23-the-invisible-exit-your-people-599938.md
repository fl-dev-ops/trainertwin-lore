The Invisible Exit

Your people don’t have to quit to leave. They can leave while staring you in the eye.
👉 Look at your producers right now. Are they working, or just present?

Dashboards can look full while belief is empty. Within 90 days of effort not converting, discretionary energy collapses. What you think is retention is often dead weight, people still on payroll, already gone in spirit.

The founder’s trap is mistaking presence for commitment. If you believe “they’re still here, so they’re still in,” you’re already paying for ghosts.

If you haven’t cleared a drag point that gave your team momentum this week, you don’t have a team. You have a slow-motion exit.
