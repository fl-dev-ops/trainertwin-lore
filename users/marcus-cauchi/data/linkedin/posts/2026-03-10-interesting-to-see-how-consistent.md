Interesting to see how consistent the use cases are.

Most teams are using AI for speed:
research, CRM hygiene, outreach, qualification.

All useful.

But buried in the report is the more interesting idea.

AI can reveal signals long before a deal ever appears in pipeline.

Patterns in sales calls.
Language that shows up before deals stall.
Early buyer behaviour across accounts.

The question that follows is uncomfortable.

If AI can see those signals earlier,
how many executive forecasts are being built on incomplete information?
