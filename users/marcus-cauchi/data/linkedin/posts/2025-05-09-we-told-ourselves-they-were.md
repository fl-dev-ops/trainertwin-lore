We told ourselves they were qualified. They weren’t.

They took the call. They liked the pitch. But they could never buy.

It looked like a win.
Nice call, long list of problems, said all the right things.

But when it came time to move? Silence.
They weren’t a buyer.
Not now. Not ever.
We were chasing noise, not signal.

They weren’t lying.

We just never asked the questions that would’ve made that clear.

It’s not the rep’s job to close uncloseable deals.
It’s the system’s job to protect them from wasting their time.

Next time a deal goes quiet, ask: did they trust us, or were they just being polite?
