If you keep calling it professionalism while ignoring the part that doesn't sit right, you're already training yourself to look away

Selling leaves a trace

Not on the CRM
On you

If you're reading this, you're not confused about selling
You're conflicted about how you're being asked to do it

You already know which deal this is about
The one you've explained enough times that the explanation stopped sounding true

Sellers Anonymous exists for people who can hit the number and still feel the cost

Not because they failed
Because they succeeded in ways they don't want to keep repeating

This isn't about being nicer
It's about refusing to outsource judgement to a process that doesn't live with the consequences

You know the moment

When urgency is manufactured
When risk is minimised because momentum matters more
When the buyer asks for time and the forecast says no

Each time you comply, something subtle happens
The deal moves forward
And so does the line of what you're willing to ignore next time

That's the drift

Sellers Anonymous is a peer community for experienced sellers who put buyers first, the sale second

Not as a belief
As a constraint they're willing to operate under even when it makes things harder

People come here because winning at all costs stopped feeling like winning
Because they're buyer-centric by instinct and penalised by the system
Because hitting quota hasn't felt clean for a while

If that description irritates you slightly, pay attention to that

In our meetings, we don't trade tactics or polish narratives
We surface the part of the deal that everyone else told you to move past

We talk about deals that look fine on paper and wrong in practice
Trade-offs that get justified because the number resets next quarter
Decisions that technically worked and personally didn't

We use TheSellerCode.org not as values to agree with, but as constraints that test whether a deal still holds when pressure is real

If your buyer has to hurry for your forecast to work, something's wrong
If the risks can't be said out loud, the deal isn't done
If you need pressure to close, you're not helping them decide

This isn't therapy
It isn't anti-sales
And it isn't a place to complain about the system

It's a room where experienced sellers stop pretending the compromise doesn't matter

Meetings are small and confidential
No recording
No pitching
No performance

Most people listen their first time

What matters is what you notice while you're listening

The deal you've already justified
The explanation you don't fully believe anymore

People don't come here because they're failing
They come because they're succeeding in ways that are costing them more than they want to keep paying

Join a Sellers Anonymous meeting

Not to become more ethical
To stop becoming more numb

If you can read this without thinking about a specific deal you're in right now, this isn't for you
