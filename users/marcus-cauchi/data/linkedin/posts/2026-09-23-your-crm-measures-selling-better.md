Your CRM measures selling better than buying

You probably know how many calls your sales team made yesterday. You can find the emails, meetings, demos, proposals and follow-ups. You can probably get an AI summary of every conversation before you’ve finished your coffee.
Now pick your biggest deal.

What did the customer do yesterday?

That’s harder to answer. And there’s a hell of a difference between a salesperson doing lots of work on a deal and a customer doing the work needed to buy.

We’ve spent years getting better at seeing what sellers do. Then we use that information to decide whether customers are buying.

That’s how a deal can look busy for months while almost nothing is happening on the customer’s side. More calls, more meetings, more follow-ups, more management attention. Plenty happening in the CRM.

But is the customer any closer to buying?

Take your favourite deal and ignore the seller activity for a minute. What has the customer actually done that makes you believe they’re going to buy?
