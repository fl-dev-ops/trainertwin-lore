Not all revenue is good revenue. Booked revenue is not the same as secure revenue.

Not all paying customers are created equal. Some are ideal, lifetime, sweet spot customers ... others are the wrong customers ... and a few are customers from hell. Some customers will be profitable and others will rack up a huge cost to service as they tie up your customer success, operations and product people with complaints, mismatched expectations and edge cases.

And these taxes are paid by your HR, your sales team, and your customer. And they hurt your profits.
