Speed Doesn’t Kill. Dirty Speed Kills.

Speed doesn’t kill.
Dirty speed kills.
The biggest risks aren’t visible failures.
They’re the quiet, invisible shifts that happen before the failure.

The deal looked solid, until a last-minute change broke trust.
The team was aligned in the meeting, but adjusted in private.
The pipeline said one thing, but the numbers told another.
This isn’t a people problem.

This is the invisible cost of misaligned trust conditions.
By the time you see it, you’ve already lost:

 🚫 Time → Workarounds, wasted meetings, hidden rework.
 🚫 Deals → Misalignment creates slow leaks before the break.
 🚫 Trust → The first thing to drift. The last thing to recover.

 * Your competitors are optimising for speed.
 * If you optimise for #cleanspeed, they won’t catch you.
