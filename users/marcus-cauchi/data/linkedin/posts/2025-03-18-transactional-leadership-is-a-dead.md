TRANSACTIONAL LEADERSHIP IS A DEAD END.

The leaders who think they can trade bonuses for loyalty, perks for engagement, and punishment for accountability?
 They’re losing their people.

Transactional leadership gets you bare-minimum effort.
 Trust-based leadership gets you discretionary effort.

People don’t work hard because they’re paid to. They work hard because they believe in the mission.

If your leadership is just an equation of rewards and punishments, you don’t have a team, you have a ticking clock before attrition hits.

Build trust. Build purpose. Build something that makes people stay.

Tag someone who earns loyalty instead of trying to buy it.
