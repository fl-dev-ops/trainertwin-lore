Your pipeline can look healthy while the quality of your revenue is getting worse.

That’s the problem.

A deal can move, forecast, and close while the customer still isn’t really able to defend it internally, implement it properly, or live with it once the seller’s gone.

When that happens, the cost doesn’t stay in sales. It turns up later in onboarding, customer success, support, product, renewals, and eventually in leadership decisions made on a false reading of revenue quality.

This deck is about that gap.

Not whether the deal was sold well.

Whether the decision behind it was strong enough to hold.
