Watch this, pay heed, apply. Outstanding, clear and simple advice anyone can adopt to improve #trustworthiness. Trust is the operating system.

***

Most salespeople don’t fail because they lack skill.

They fail because trust has worn thin and nobody wants to be the one to say it out loud.

Trust isn’t a soft extra. It’s the operating system. It decides whether a buyer leans in, slows things down, or chooses not to decide at all. Long before price, features, or timing come into play.

When trust drops, everything else has to compensate. Scripts tighten. Follow ups stack up. Pressure builds. None of that fixes the underlying problem.

Trustworthiness is the missing superpower because it lowers personal risk. It makes it easier for a buyer to say yes and easier for a seller to stand behind what they’ve said later. That’s why some salespeople move deals forward without force, while others grind themselves down.

They’re not better closers.
They’re safer to trust.

And in a world where buyers are trying to avoid blame, safety wins over persuasion every time.
