Most executive failure is diagnosed too late and too narrowly.

By the time the numbers turn, the person carrying the damage is often blamed for decisions made months earlier. The mandate may have changed. The market may have moved. The exit runway may have shortened. The measures may reward the wrong output while the real cost travels downstream.

That makes “high performance or high exit” sound more rigorous than it is. Before removing a leader, boards and PE sponsors need to separate what they inherited, what changed around them and what their own decisions actually improved or damaged.

This post examines the hidden chain between hiring, timing, authority, measurement, politics and blame.

Read it before the next senior hire, performance review or removal decision.
