High standards don’t break people. Shifting rules do.

When targets move every week, people don’t just feel uncertain, they catastrophise. “If the goalposts keep shifting, maybe my job isn’t safe. Maybe nothing I do counts.”

That spiral kills loyalty fast. Teams with unstable goals churn talent 2.5× faster. Every departure takes revenue, culture, and trust with it.

Managers who protect stability build the only currency that matters right now: predictability. Their people don’t just stay, they buy in.

Ask yourself: could every person on your team write down exactly what “winning” means this week, and will that still be true next week?

If not, the instability isn’t theirs. It’s yours.
