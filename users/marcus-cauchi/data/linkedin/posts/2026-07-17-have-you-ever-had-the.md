Have you ever had the feeling you're already doing the job...
...just without the title, the authority or the recognition?

That's a surprisingly difficult place to be.

You become the person everyone trusts when something matters, yet you're still waiting for permission to fix the things that keep creating the problems you're being asked to solve.

It's a position many experienced leaders find themselves in, and it's far more common than people admit.

This article is about that moment.
Not how to escape it.

How to recognise it before it silently becomes your normal.
