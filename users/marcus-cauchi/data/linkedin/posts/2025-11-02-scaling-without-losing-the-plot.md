Scaling Without Losing the Plot
Robert Israch, President of Tipalti, on how to lead growth that doesn’t rot from the inside.

Most companies celebrate growth right up to the moment it breaks them.
Rob Israch has scaled Tipalti from 25 to 1,000+ people - without losing customer trust, culture, or control.

He explains why middle managers decide your fate, how to detect rot under the floorboards before investors do, and why trust is now the ultimate competitive advantage.

This is how real operators stay sane while scaling fast.

Most scale-ups break for the same reason - they stop listening.
Metrics look good.
 Headcount grows.
 But under the floorboards, rot sets in.

Rob has lived both sides - the chaos of #hypergrowth and the discipline of control.

As President of Tipalti, he’s taken the company from 25 people to over 1,000.
In this conversation, Rob and I dig into what healthy scaling really looks like:
* Why “grey space” is where real leaders are made.
* How to build middle managers who think, not just report.
* Why culture isn’t free - and what happens if you delay defining it.
* How to spot deal theatre before it poisons decision-making.

What balance sheets never show: #TrustErosion.
This isn’t a vanity-growth story.
 It’s a lesson in how to grow without losing the plot, and why trust, not metrics, is the true indicator of resilience.
