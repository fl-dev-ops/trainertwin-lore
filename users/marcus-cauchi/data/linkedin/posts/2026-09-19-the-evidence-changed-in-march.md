The evidence changed in March. The forecast changed in May.

Here’s a number I’d like to see on a sales dashboard. The buyer’s situation changed on 12 March. We had evidence of it on 18 March. The forecast changed on 27 May.

What happened during those ten weeks? How many calls did we make, how many meetings did we have and how much management time went into the deal? Did we bring in a solutions engineer, an executive sponsor or Legal? Did Finance continue relying on the number?

I’m less interested in whether the forecast eventually became right. I want to know how long we kept believing the old story after the evidence supporting it had changed, and what it cost us while we did.
