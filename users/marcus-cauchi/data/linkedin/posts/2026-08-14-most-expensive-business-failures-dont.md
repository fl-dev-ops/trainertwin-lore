Most expensive business failures don’t start with a stupid idea.
They start with a real problem followed by a confident therefore.

 Pipeline is weak, therefore hire more SDRs.
 Margins are tight, therefore automate.
 Growth has stalled, therefore build a partner channel.
 CAC is too high, therefore cut marketing.

The problem may be real.
The weakness is often hiding inside therefore.

Before you back the plan, ask:
 What proves the cause?
 Why does this remedy follow?
 Who inherits the downside?
And what happens if it works exactly as intended?

Don’t defend the plan.
Defend the test.
