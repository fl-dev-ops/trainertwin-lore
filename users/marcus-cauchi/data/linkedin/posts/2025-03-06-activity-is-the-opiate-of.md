Activity is the Opiate of Broken Leadership Decisions

Struggling Leadership teams love movement.

More meetings.
More updates.
More dashboards.
More pipeline reviews.
More deal reviews to review the last review.

The machine must be fed.
But here’s the truth no one wants to admit.

Most of that activity is not progress.
It's the commercial equivalent of getting drunk to forget.

You are not solving problems - you're numbing the symptoms.

The symptoms of:

* Decisions made without clarity.
*Strategies built on hope, not evidence.
* Deals pushed forward by fear, not fit.
* Pipelines padded with fiction, not truth.
* Forecasts treated as prayers, not predictions.

When leaders lose sight of what matters, they turn to activity to make themselves feel better.

 More calls.
 More emails.
 More touchpoints.
 More noise.

Busyness becomes the drug that lets you ignore the real pain , the fact that you are making decsiions blind, disconnected from reality, and terrified to stop long enough to see the wreckage.

Real leadership starts when you *** stop mistaking activity for progress.***

It starts when you have the courage to remove what hurts before you chase what works.

It starts when you surface the risks you would rather not see, disqualify the deals you secretly know are junk, and face reality before it blindsides you.

That is what /zagthinking does.

It forces clarity before certainty.
It makes risk visible before it becomes fatal.
It scrubs away the comfort of activity and leaves you with something better -  #TheTruth.

If you are ready to lead without anaesthetic, let’s talk.
