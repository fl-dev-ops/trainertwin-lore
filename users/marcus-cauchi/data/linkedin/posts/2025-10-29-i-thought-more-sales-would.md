I thought more sales would fix everything.

Every time we hit target, I raised it.
 More ads. More hires. More dials. More pressure.

But growth didn’t solve the chaos.
 It multiplied it.

Cash moved faster.
 But so did mistakes.
 Margins shrank while stress grew.

I wasn’t scaling a business.
 I was scaling the noise.

The fix wasn’t revenue.
 It was restraint.

We stopped chasing “more”
 and started asking “enough?”

Turns out profit isn’t what’s left after expenses.
 It’s what’s left after ego.
