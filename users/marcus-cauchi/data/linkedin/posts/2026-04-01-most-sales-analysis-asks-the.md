Most sales analysis asks the wrong question.

Why did we win?
Why did we lose?

Useful. But too late, and too shallow. The harder question is this:

Which of our won deals became durable revenue,
and which ones started decaying the moment they closed?

A deal can score well in discovery, close cleanly, hit the forecast, and still become bad ARR. Weak adoption. Thin sponsorship. Suppressed expansion. Revenue that looks recurring until scrutiny arrives.

Won vs lost is a sales view.
Won vs durable is a value view.

That is the one the board eventually pays for.

Marcus Cauchi
