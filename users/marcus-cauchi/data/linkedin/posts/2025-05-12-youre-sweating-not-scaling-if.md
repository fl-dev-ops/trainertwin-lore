You’re sweating, not scaling.
If your team can’t run without you,
 you haven’t built a team,
 you’ve built a bottleneck.

You’re rescuing deals.
 Patching gaps.
 Dragging dead weight with good intentions.

 You can’t forecast what you haven’t coached.
 You can’t scale what you still carry.
 And no, it's not your reps failing, it's the system you've trapped them in.

Coaching isn't a soft skill.
 It's the engine.

Start building it.
