Most risk models treat founders like they're running a bakery.
 As if risk is just about rising costs or missed delivery slots.

But if you're scaling fast, just funded, or prepping for exit - you know better.
Risk isn’t an entry in a spreadsheet. It’s what stalks you in the margins:
 – A deal that unravels too quietly
 – A team that burns out before the board meets
 – A partner who goes dark at the worst time

Gallagher’s 2025 Risk Index names cybercrime, inflation, and supply chain issues. Fine.

But it misses what scaling founders actually feel:
Climate’s not a future issue - it’s already shifting insurance, pricing, and ops
AI isn’t just “opportunity” - it’s displacing teams and trust
Institutional fragility, platform dependence, and geopolitics aren’t “out of scope” - they’re already at your door

The risk isn’t the shock. It’s the silence before.
Founders who survive don’t just plan. They scan. They listen to what’s not being said.

And they build relationships that can hold the weight when things wobble.
If your risk map doesn’t name your real fear, it’s not protecting you.
