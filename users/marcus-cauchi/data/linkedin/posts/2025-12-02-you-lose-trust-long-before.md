You Lose Trust Long Before You Lose Performance

1. Trust Them
When trust is missing: People hide mistakes, delay decisions and wait to be told what to do. Everything slows down. The best people leave first.
2. Show Appreciation
When appreciation is missing: People stop trying. They do the minimum because nothing more is ever noticed. Bitterness replaces effort.
3. Create Safety
When safety is missing: People play small. They avoid risk, avoid honesty and avoid the truth. Innovation dies quietly.
4. Value Input
When input is ignored: People shut up. They stop sharing issues early. Leaders get surprised by problems that could have been prevented.
5. Offer Challenges
When challenge is missing: Good people stagnate. Boredom sets in. They leave for somewhere that stretches them.
6. Support Growth
When growth is missing: People feel stuck. They start job hunting in secret. Your top talent becomes someone else’s top hire.
7. Stay Transparent
When transparency is missing: Rumours fill the gaps. Distrust spreads. Teams start building their own versions of the truth.
8. Inspire Purpose
When purpose is missing: Work becomes mechanical. People stop caring. Morale sinks and so does the quality of decisions.
9. Celebrate Wins
When wins go unrecognised: Teams burn out faster. Everything feels like a slog with no payoff. People lose their sense of progress.
10. Pay Fairly
When fairness is missing: Resentment takes root. Politics increases. Your most consistent people feel punished for being steady.

And here's the punchline: These failures don’t add up. They multiply.

One missing petal weakens the others. Two missing petals start a drift. Three missing petals and the culture quietly flips from adult-to-adult to fear-to-fear.
People stop bringing their best selves. They bring their safest selves.

That is how companies lose trust long before they notice it on a dashboard. By the time the dashboards show the damage, the team has already felt it for months. You lose culture in silence before you lose it on paper.
