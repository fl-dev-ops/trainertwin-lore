SHOULD YOU EVER HAVE TO FIGHT FOR A REFUND?

If a company makes it hard to get your money back, they don’t deserve to keep it.
* If you need to call three times, argue, and beg, that’s not a policy, it’s a scam.
* If they say “it’s in the terms and conditions,” that’s a failure of trust.
* If they hide behind a customer service maze, they know they’re wrong.

Great businesses stand behind their product.
* They make refunds easy because they believe in their own value.
* They don’t trick you into keeping something that didn’t work.
A refund policy is a trust test. If they make you fight, they’ve already failed.

Which brand that stands behind their product.
