Leadership: Standards Need Gates + Teeth

If it’s not enforced, it’s optional.

Standards without teeth are wishes.
* If no one checks, it slides.
* If there’s no consequence, it’s theatre.

Pick one standard that matters.
* Name the gate - who checks, when, with what proof.
* Add consequence: fix, coach, or stop.

Take Action: Announce one gate + explain ownership and the consequences of non-performance today.

Comment CARD for the manager checklist.
