“I can always tell when a vendor picked me because I’ve got budget, not because I’m a fit.”

It shows up in the first few minutes.
They talk fast.
They talk big.
But they don’t ask what I’m actually trying to fix.
And they keep going even when it’s obvious I’m not their customer.

It’s not just offensive.
It’s tiring.

When you target me for my wallet instead of my reality, you hand me work I didn’t ask for.

I have to figure out if your thing matters.
I have to do the qualification you skipped.
And I remember that next time your logo lands in my inbox.

I’m not your ICP just because I can pay.
I’m your ICP if the problem I live with is the problem you’re built to solve.

The vendors I buy from get that.

They’re honest about who they’re right for.
They don’t chase everything that moves.
They listen first.
They let the truth decide the fit.

If more teams did that, we’d all get our time back.

****
And if your team is burning hours on people who were never real prospects, message me.

I’ll help you sort who’s worth your time and who isn’t, in one conversation.
