Most say they want partner-sourced revenue yet almost nobody is honest about why it fails.

It usually fails for one of three reasons:
- you are talking to partnership people who do not control budget, authority, or sales execution
- your “co-sell motion” is really just asking for referrals
- you are still running the deal like a direct sales motion, not a buyer-safe motion

One line from my conversation with Alex Buckles has stuck with me:
He opened 70-plus opportunities from one conference and closed zero.
* That is not a bad quarter.
* That is a diagnostic.

Because it exposed a truth most companies miss:
The person who feels the problem is often not the person who owns the outcome.
And until you bridge those two, pipeline is theatre.

What works instead?
- one motion
- one ICP
- one acute pain
- one valuable door opener
- one clear internal bridge between the problem owner and the outcome owner

Not generic partnerships. Not PRM theatre. Not “let’s build relationships.”
Real distribution.

And if you are still leading with a demo, you are probably late to the wrong conversation. I am increasingly convinced that most partner programs fail for the same reason most direct sales motions fail:
They are built around the vendor’s need to move the deal, not the buyer’s need to make a safe decision.

Prove me wrong.

Where do most partner motions really fail:
- partner selection,
- sales leader buy-in,
- internal execution,
 or 
- buyer safety?
