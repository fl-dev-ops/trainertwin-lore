STOP TRYING TO BE THE SMARTEST PERSON IN THE ROOM.
 The best leaders aren’t the most brilliant.
 They’re the most trusted.

They don’t compete for attention, they elevate others.
 They don’t hoard knowledge, they create environments where ideas flow.
 They don’t need to be the answer, they build teams that generate solutions.

Powerful leaders don’t dominate the room, they amplify it.

Who is your #UnsungHero, who builds platforms, not pedestals?
