The Hidden Taxes of Bad Measurement
Every business pays them.
Most never see them.

Part 10: The Trust Tax
The Trust Tax is the most expensive because it compounds quietly. Every broken promise, hidden risk, missed expectation and poorly handled handover makes the next sale harder than the last.
Trust is built slowly and spent quickly. Once it falls, every future customer demands more proof, every negotiation takes longer and every relationship becomes more expensive to maintain.
Measure whether customers would make the same decision again knowing what they know now. That single question reveals more about the health of the business than almost any dashboard.
