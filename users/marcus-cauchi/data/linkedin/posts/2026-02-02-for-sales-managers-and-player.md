For Sales Managers and Player Managers

Most problems don't start when a deal goes wrong.
They start earlier, when decisions move forward under pressure and you are the one who will have to stand behind them later.

Deals progress.
Targets are hit.
Everything looks fine.

Then someone asks what was agreed, why it was agreed, and who owns the outcome.
That moment is predictable.
So is the fallout.

I coach founders, GTM leaders, and sales managers on how decisions actually form in real conversations, and how to stop momentum from turning into commitments that create problems months later.

This is not sales training.
It is judgement work, done on live decisions before they harden and end up in review decks and post-mortems.

The point is not just better performance.
It is fewer escalations, fewer explanations, and less time spent cleaning things up after the fact.
