Most professionals don’t lose money because of clients. They lose it because of their own money scripts.

Lawyers I've coached tell me once: “I’d quote a fee, then hear my mum’s voice in my head saying we can’t afford it, and I’d cut the price before the client even spoke.”

That script is why:
* The least you accept is the most you’ll ever get.
* Credentials don’t matter, buyers don’t buy your CV, they buy your conviction you’ll keep them safe.
* The worst answer to “what do you charge?” is an apology in your tone. The number is secondary. The doubt is fatal.

Stories beat arguments.
Questions like “What happens if you don’t fix this?” surface the hidden cost of inaction.
And remember, money is just the score.
* The real game is trust, safety, and progress.
* Play that game well, and the score looks after itself.

Matthew Dashper-Hughes nails this in #ShowMeTheMoney.
If you’ve ever flinched in a fee conversation, you’ll see yourself in these pages.
