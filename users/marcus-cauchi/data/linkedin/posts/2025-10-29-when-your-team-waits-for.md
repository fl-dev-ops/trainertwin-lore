When your team waits for your approval, you’ve stopped leading.
They call it “checking in.”
 You call it “quality control.”

But what it really means is:
 they don’t trust their own judgment -
 because you trained them not to.

Leadership isn’t control.
 It’s creating confidence in others.

When your team stops asking,
 that’s not loss of power.
 That’s proof of progress.

Sneaky Peek in the Mirror:
 What would break if you let them decide without you?
