Show me the evidence that the customer is buying

Pick the deal you’re most confident about. Take away the CRM stage, the probability and the forecast category. Don’t tell me how many calls you’ve made, how many meetings you’ve had, whether you’ve sent the proposal or how confident the salesperson feels.

Show me what the customer has actually done. What have they completed? Who have they involved? What have they committed? What did they say they’d do that they’ve actually done?
What’s changed inside their business? What have you learned that makes the purchase less likely? What’s still unknown?

Now put your forecast back on the table. Does the evidence support it?
