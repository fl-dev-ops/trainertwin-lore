Most founder-led agencies don’t stall on growth. 

They stall on continuity. Things still work. But only if you do. When you step back: decisions wait, renewals lean on you, pricing softens, escalation finds you. 

Nothing breaks. The system just recentres on you. That’s not failure. It’s a continuity gap. 

Boards and renewals read that gap fast. So do senior hires. 

If you already know where things slow down without you, DM LOS. 5–10 minutes. Private. No follow-up unless you ask.
