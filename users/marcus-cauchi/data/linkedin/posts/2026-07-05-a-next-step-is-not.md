A next step is not progress unless it creates buyer decision work.

While a meeting booked, a proposal sent, or a demo completed can be useful, none of these actions automatically prove that the buyer has moved forward in their decision-making process.

The critical question is not "what did the seller do next?" but rather, "what did the buyer do that makes the decision clearer, safer, or more real?" Before labeling any action as progress, it is essential to test the evidence.
