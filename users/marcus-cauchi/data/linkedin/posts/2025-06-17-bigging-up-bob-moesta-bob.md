Bigging Up Bob Moesta
Bob Moesta changed how I see selling.

He reminded us the product isn’t what we sell. It’s what the buyer becomes.
He taught us that people don’t buy solutions. They hire them to make progress.
That insight rewired how I work with sales and GTM teams.

Most selling still starts with pitch, not purpose. That’s why trust breaks.

I borrowed Bob’s question:
“What happened that made you start looking?”
Then I built a system to surface the moments before that question is even possible.
A system to detect drift.
To map jobs-to-be-done to revenue-critical execution.
And to build structural trust into every stage of the sale.

Bob gave us the compass.
I built the map.
