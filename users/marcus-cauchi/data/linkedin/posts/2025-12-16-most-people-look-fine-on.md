Most people look fine on the outside.
But the ones who keep everything together for everyone else… usually aren’t.
They just get better at hiding it.

If you’re the person everyone leans on, but you’ve got nowhere to put what you’re carrying, this one is for you.

It’s not a post about working harder.
It’s about seeing the truth you’ve been too busy to name.

If the pace, the pressure, or the politics have started to blur your judgement, this piece will help you spot what’s really going on before you burn yourself down to keep the lights on.
