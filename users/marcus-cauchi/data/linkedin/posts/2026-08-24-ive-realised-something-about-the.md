I’ve realised something about the way I like to work.
I genuinely like people disagreeing with me.

Not the LinkedIn kind of disagreement where everyone still somehow agrees 😄

I mean the useful kind.

Someone looks at something I’ve been working on for weeks and says:
“Wojciech, I don’t think this part holds.”

Or:
“What happens one step later?”

Or just:
“No. I think you’re wrong here.”

Perfect.

Because I’m not looking for people to help me protect an idea.
I’m looking for people willing to help me find out whether it deserves to survive.

And lately, some of the best developments in my work have started exactly like that.

A comment became a question.
A question became a test.
A test broke part of the map.
The map got better.

That’s probably why I enjoy doing some of this work in public.
People come from completely different worlds, poke holes in something, bring a case I hadn’t seen, or force me to admit that two things I treated as one are actually different.

That’s fun.
And... to me, that’s science.

So if you see something in my work that doesn’t make sense, push it.

Seriously.

I’d much rather lose an idea than spend six months defending a bad one 🙂
