The Decisions That Matter
Part 1: Should We Pursue This Opportunity?

One of the most expensive decisions a business makes isn’t closing the wrong deal. It’s deciding to keep pursuing it.

Every opportunity consumes something. Sales invests time. Managers invest attention. Technical teams become involved. Finance starts planning around expected revenue. Customer Success begins thinking about delivery. The longer an opportunity stays alive, the more expensive it becomes to admit it was never strong enough.

Most qualification focuses on whether the buyer has budget, authority, need and timing. Those questions matter, but they don’t answer the question that determines whether the opportunity deserves another hour of work. Has the buyer done enough to justify our confidence? What evidence suggests they’ll make a decision? What would convince us to stop?

Strong commercial judgement protects time before it protects revenue. It recognises that every weak opportunity steals attention from a stronger one, distorts the forecast and quietly reduces the quality of every decision that follows.

Before asking, “Can we win?” ask, “Does this opportunity still deserve our time?”
