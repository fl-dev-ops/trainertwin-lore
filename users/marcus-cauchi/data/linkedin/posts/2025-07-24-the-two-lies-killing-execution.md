The Two Lies Killing Execution: TAM and EBITDA

TAM and EBITDA are the PowerPoint gods of delusion.
Worshipped in boardrooms. Revered in pitch decks. Feared by execution teams.

But here’s the mind-boggling, depressing truth:
Together, they create the perfect storm of false confidence and fragile companies.

Lie One: TAM – Total Assumption Market
“We’re going after a £160B space.”
So are 300 other teams with the same Google slide and no idea what the market actually trusts them to solve.
TAM (Total Addressable Market) is pure cosplay fantasy at scale.
SAM (Serviceable Addressable Market) is a polite guess.
SOM (Serviceable OBTAINABLE Market)is where the real battle begins and most teams are wildly unprepared.

What you call "market opportunity" is often a distraction from market reality.
TAM thinking pulls you into markets where you don’t matter.
You chase scale before trust.
You flood go-to-market with noise before fit.
You make yourself a meaningless, unwelcome interruption at scale.

Lie Two: EBITDA – Earnings Before I Deal with the Bad Actuals
“We’re EBITDA positive.”
Translation: “We make pretend money as long as you ignore the things that make us fragile.”
EBITDA Earnings Before Interest Tax Depreciation and Ammortisation) is accounting theatre. 
(Who doesn't pay those things? PE. Who does? You)

It hides operational decay behind a fake clean number.
It celebrates financial illusion while cash flow bleeds out backstage.
Interest. Taxes. Depreciation. Amortisation.

All stripped out, as if the real world doesn’t cost anything.
You’re not profitable.
You’re temporarily unexposed.

Here’s what these lies cost you:
TAM and EBITDA create illusion, not clarity.

GTM and leadership teams chase phantom goals.
Leaders make resource bets on markets they don’t belong to.
Operators drown trying to scale systems that are ALWAYS UNstable.

Execution doesn’t die from chaos.
It dies from delusion.

There’s a better lens:
Trust velocity: How quickly can you turn shared clarity into action?
Fit tier: Who actually needs you right now, not hypothetically next year? 
 - Timely? Relevant? Valuable?
Execution margin: How much pressure can your system take before it snaps?

You don’t scale by projecting size.
You scale by earning #Trust. 
Repeatedly. 
Relentlessly. 
In the raging storm, not the pitch deck.

If you're still leading with TAM and EBITDA, you're not building a company. You're rehearsing a collapse. Pure Onanism (look it up)
