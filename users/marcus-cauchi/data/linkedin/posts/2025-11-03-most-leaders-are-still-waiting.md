Most leaders are still waiting for permission.

Permission to act.
 Permission to innovate.
 Permission to think differently.

Marcus: “What’s the biggest barrier to innovation?”
Ron: “Waiting for permission that no one’s coming to give.”
Marcus: “So where do we start?”
Ron: “With humility, trust, and empathy. Everything else is noise.”

Ron Brumbarger says stop waiting.
The world’s moving too fast for committees, consensus, or corporate caution.

In this conversation, we talk about:
- Why innovation dies when middle management plays “cover your grass”
- How inattentional blindness keeps companies stuck in past cycles
- What “innovation friction” looks like - and how to clear it
- The mindset shift from execution to exploration
- Why humility and empathy are non-negotiable for real innovation

Ron built and exited multiple ventures before founding Stranova, an “innovation-as-a-service” firm that helps organisations see what they’ve been blind to.

His simple truth: the closer you get to your customer, the faster you see what matters.
