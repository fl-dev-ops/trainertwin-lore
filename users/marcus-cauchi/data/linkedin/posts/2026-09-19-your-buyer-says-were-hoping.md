Your buyer says, “We’re hoping to get this sorted before the end of the year.” AI hears a timeline. The CRM gets Q4. The forecast gets 70%. Finance gets a number. The board starts making decisions around it.

Nobody lied. But somewhere along the way, a possibility became a commitment. Now imagine doing that automatically across every sales call and every opportunity.

AI can save your salespeople hours of admin. It can also make weak evidence look certain faster than any sales team ever could.

Before you automate another CRM update, take the deal you trust most and trace one important claim back to what the buyer actually said.

How much confidence did your systems add along the way?
