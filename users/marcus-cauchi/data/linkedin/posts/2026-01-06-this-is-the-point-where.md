This is the point where harm starts.

Not loud harm.
Not illegal harm.
Quiet harm that looks like progress.

Here’s why it matters.

When buyers lose the ability to decide safely, three things happen at once.

First, bad decisions get made.
Not irrational ones. Defensive ones.
Choices optimised to avoid blame, not to create outcomes.

Second, trust gets damaged without anyone noticing. Between teams.
Between leaders and operators. Between buyer and seller. It doesn’t explode. It erodes.

Third, the system learns the wrong lesson. Sales learns to push harder.
Buyers learn to hide hesitation. Leaders learn to reward relief over truth. Next time is worse.

That’s the damage.
It compounds.
