The Alignment Trap
(Most Alignment Meetings Are Just Delayed Accountability Sessions)

Alignment sounds good in theory. What does it mean in practice? Is it an excuse for not making a call.

🔹 The same decision getting “revisited” in every leadership meeting.
🔹 Teams waiting for “full buy-in” when the real issue is fear of making a move.
🔹 Endless rounds of input-gathering that create paralysis, not clarity.

📌 Where is alignment being used as an excuse to avoid ownership?
📌 Where is “collaboration” actually just delayed execution?
📌 Where could one strong decision collapse months of wasted cycles?

High-trust teams and leaders don’t waste time in alignment loops - they move first, validate later.
