PE doesn’t care where you learned the words.
It cares whether things actually move.

But speed on its own isn’t the win.

We’ve all seen fast operators who look great until diligence starts asking questions.

Things shipped.
Dashboards were green.
Activity was high.

Then pressure hit.

This isn’t about values.
It’s about whether the decisions you made still hold when pressure rises.

The test was never “did it move quickly?”
It was “did the decision still hold when the pressure went up?”

That’s where a lot of value quietly leaks out.

The people who really compound value don’t just push things through.
They leave behind decisions that survive scrutiny, scale, and handover.

Momentum feels good.
Enterprise value is what’s still standing at exit.
