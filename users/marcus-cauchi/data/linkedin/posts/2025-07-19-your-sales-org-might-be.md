Your Sales Org Might Be Committing Organisational Self-Harm.
You’ve built a system that trains your people to break trust on contact.
They interrupt strangers, misrepresent value, vanish post-signature, and hand poisoned accounts to CS, and you call that pipeline progress?

You’re not hunting.
You’re bleeding out.

Your team’s forecast isn’t slipping because they need better sequences.
It’s slipping because the buyer no longer trusts anything you say.
They’ve ghosted you, not because they’re “not a fit,”
 but because you’ve trained them to expect pain, pressure, and irrelevance.

And you’re still clinging to the “hunter vs. farmer” model,
 as if separating accountability across functions is anything but
 a coward’s way to avoid systemic truth.

This isn’t a headcount issue.
 It’s a belief collapse engine.

One that’s eroding your product’s credibility, your team’s morale,
 and your buyer’s willingness to engage at all.

If you're brave enough to face the mirror, read this:
“The Hunter Doesn’t Win Anymore. The Hunted Just Stops Responding.”

* It’s not comfortable.
* It’s not polite.

But it might just save your GTM motion from self-destruction.
