Protect the buyer's decision, not the deal.

Deal don't slip because buyers are difficult. They slip because they're not safe enough to defend.

As sellers we're taught to protect the deal. Protect the forecast. Protect the pipeline. Protect the quarter. Protect the number.

Buyers are protecting something else.

Their decision. They have to live with it after the seller has moved on. So when a deal stalls, goes quiet, slips or ends in no decision, the answer is rarely "more pressure", better follow-up or sharper closing.

The best sellers ask, "What still has to be true before this buyer can stand behind this decision?". They don't push the buyer towards a signature.

They help buyers make decisions that are easy to understand, safe to support and defensible under scrutiny.

Protect the buyer's decision.
The deal is the outcome.
The decision is the cause.
