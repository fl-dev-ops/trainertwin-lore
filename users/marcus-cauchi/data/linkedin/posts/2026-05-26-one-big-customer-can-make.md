One big customer can make you feel successful right up until they leave.

Customer concentration is one of those risks people ignore because it feels like good news.

It often looks like this:

👉 Big account
👉 Big revenue
👉 Big logo
👉 Big problem if it walks

10 numbers that should make a CEO sit up:

1. One customer above 10 % of revenue
2. One customer above 20 % of revenue
3. Revenue growing while retention falls
4. EBITDA below 15 % with no clear reason
5. Organic growth below inflation plus pricing
6. Voluntary turnover rising in key roles
7. Sales forecast missed three months in a row
8. Gross margin falling while revenue rises
9. One person owning too many customer relationships
10. DSO moving the wrong way

The best businesses don’t just grow.

They grow with less fragility.

A buyer will not only ask how much revenue you have.

They will ask how durable it is.

That’s where many owners get surprised.
