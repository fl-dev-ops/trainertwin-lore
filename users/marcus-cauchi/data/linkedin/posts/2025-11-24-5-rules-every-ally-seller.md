5 Rules Every Ally Seller Lives By

1. Don’t move until you understand their world

If you don’t know what they’re worried about, you can’t guide them.
Slow down.
Learn what they fear losing.
Then speak.

2. If they go quiet, stop

Silence means they don’t feel safe.
Short answers mean the same.
Fast agreement too.
Pause and bring their voice back before you go on.

3. Protect their pride before you challenge them

People shut down when they feel judged or embarrassed.
Show respect first.
Then raise a hard point.

4. When honesty drops, lower the stakes

If they feel danger, they’ll tell you what you want to hear.
Make it safe to say “no”, “not sure”, or “I disagree”.

5. Empathy means changing something, not just saying “I get it”

Listening is good.
Adjusting is better.
If nothing changes, they won’t trust you.
