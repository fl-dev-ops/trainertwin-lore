Most of us didn’t mean to start selling the way we do now.
 The pressure crept in slowly - targets, tools, dashboards, noise.
 Somewhere along the way, helping turned into chasing.

This week’s session is about stopping for a moment and asking:
 “What would selling look like if we made safety the goal again?”

We’ll talk about:
* Why buyers shut down and how to make conversations feel safe again
* How proof replaces persuasion in real deals
* Stories from people who chose calm over chase - and still hit their number

Our guest speaker is Bryan McGarrigle who will be telling his story and mapping his journey from stressed sales leader to top performer selling ethically and for the long term interests of both customer and employer.

No slides. No pitch. Just a small group of people who want to sell with integrity again.

If you’ve ever felt that twinge after pushing too hard or rescuing one more deal, you’ll fit right in.
Join us.
