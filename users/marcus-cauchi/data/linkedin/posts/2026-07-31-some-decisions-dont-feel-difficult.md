Some decisions don't feel difficult because you don't know what to do.

They feel difficult because you've spent so long believing in the future you've imagined that it's painful to ask whether it's still coming.

You keep showing up. You keep delivering. You tell yourself one more quarter, one more change, one more conversation might be enough. None of those thoughts are irrational. They're often the reason good people stay long enough to build something that matters.

The question that slowly gets louder isn't whether you can leave.

It's whether you're still waiting for something that's actually happening, or something you've been hoping will happen for a very long time.

Most experienced leaders reach that point.
Almost none of them talk about it.

If that question has been sitting in the background for longer than you'd care to admit, you're probably not the only one.
