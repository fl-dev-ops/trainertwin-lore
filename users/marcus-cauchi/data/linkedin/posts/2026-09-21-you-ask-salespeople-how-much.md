You ask salespeople how much pipeline they have, which deals will close and when. Then you use their answers to judge their performance.

Nobody needs to fiddle the numbers for that to affect the data. If removing a weak deal, pushing a close date back or saying “I don’t know yet” makes someone’s position worse, the conditions under which they report have changed.

Before blaming poor CRM discipline, look at what happens when somebody tells you something that makes their number worse.

What is your management system teaching people it’s safe to tell you?
