I built a business that worked.
 But I didn’t.

Revenue was up.
 Team was growing.
 Clients were happy.

And I was done.
 Tired in a way sleep couldn’t fix.

Because I built the company I thought I should -
 not the one I actually wanted.

Success without peace isn’t success.
 It’s survival.

I had to rebuild the business
 around a version of me that could breathe again.

Now the goal’s simpler:
 Build something that scales ... and still leaves you human.
