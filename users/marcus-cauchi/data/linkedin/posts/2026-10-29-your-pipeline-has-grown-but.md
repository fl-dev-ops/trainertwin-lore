Your pipeline has grown. But have more customers actually bought?

If you're about to invest more in sales, would the answer change what you spend it on?
More salespeople might be exactly what you need. But if customers aren't moving towards a decision, adding more sellers may simply add cost.

The same question applies when results are good and you're planning to grow. What's producing those results, and will it still work when you expand?

I've attached a free paper about decisions like these. It starts with a £720,000 hiring decision and examines what you'd need to know before committing more money. You may already have enough evidence to make a sound decision. You may discover something worth checking first.
