If pressure worked, wouldn’t your team be performing by now?

Honestly…
WTF are you thinking?

You push harder.
You tighten the deadlines.
You speak in that “urgent tone” you save for when things are slipping.

And then you act surprised when the room freezes.

You know the look:

People nodding too fast.
People agreeing to things they clearly don’t understand.
People saying “yep, all good” while their eyes say “please stop talking.”

Then you get the usual aftermath:

Updates that don’t update anything.
Forecasts that make no sense.
People avoiding you in the corridor.
Slack messages that suddenly turn into essays or silence.

And you wonder why nothing moves unless you push it.

WTFRUThinking?

Pressure doesn’t create performance.
Pressure creates avoidance.
Avoidance creates drift.
Drift creates mess.
And the mess lands back on your desk every time.

This isn’t a people problem.
It’s a safety problem.

People don’t think clearly when they’re braced for impact.
They don’t speak honestly when they’re worried about your reaction.
They don’t take ownership when they’re scared of being wrong and being punished. 

You didn’t motivate them.
You backed them into a corner.

And now you’re exhausted, they’re exhausted, and the work still isn’t moving.

Here’s the truth and you already know it. 

When people feel safe, they tell you what’s real.
When what’s real is on the table, you can actually fix things.
When things get fixed, performance follows.

We make this far more complicated than it needs to be.
