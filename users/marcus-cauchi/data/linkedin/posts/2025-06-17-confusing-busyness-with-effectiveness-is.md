Confusing busyness with effectiveness is a common act of self-sabotage BD, GTM, Bid and Sales teams regularly inflict on themselves.

Have you ever considered the true hidden cost of pursuits whether you win or lose? I remember chatting to a Business Development Director and they spend around £50,000 pursuing deals. He didn't realise the hidden cost because he just put it down to "the cost of doing business". 

Then I asked about win rates? That's when the penny dropped ...
