Most founders aren’t drowning in tasks.
 They’re drowning in decisions.

Not the big, strategic ones.
 The 1,000 tiny ones you didn’t know you'd be responsible for every day.

Should we hire this person now or wait?
Is this message still on point?
Why is ops dragging?
Is that campaign really working or just loud?

It’s not that you don’t know what you’re doing.
 It’s that every choice feels urgent, and every misstep feels expensive.

You’re not burnt out because of how much you’re doing.
 You’re burnt out because of how often you’re deciding.

And what most founders don’t realise is this:

🔁 The more you scale without leverage, the more you become the bottleneck.
You don’t need more energy.
 You need better architecture.

The Ally Method exists to fix exactly this, helping founders install systems that make high-quality decisions happen without them being the bottleneck every time.

If you’ve ever had a moment where you thought:
“This shouldn’t still depend on me…”

Then maybe we should talk.
