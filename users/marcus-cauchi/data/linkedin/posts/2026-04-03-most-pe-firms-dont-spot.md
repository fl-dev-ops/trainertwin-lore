Most PE firms don't spot weak revenue when it is weak. They spot it when it becomes expensive.

By then it looks like:
churn,
margin drag,
delayed value creation,
a miss in the quarter,
or another leadership change that fixes nothing.

The problem is rarely lack of data.

It's that the board pack is built to report outcomes, not expose fragility.

So the wrong revenue gets trusted.
The wrong assumptions get protected.
And the real leak stays hidden until the P&L makes it undeniable.

I wrote a short boardroom piece that shows what this sounds like when the room finally stops talking in adjectives and starts asking better questions.

It is about:
reported growth versus repeatable growth,
value creation versus value leakage,
and leader failure versus system failure.
