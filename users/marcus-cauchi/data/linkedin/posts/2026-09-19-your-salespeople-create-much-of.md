Your salespeople create much of the data they’re judged by

There’s something odd about sales data that we don’t talk about enough. We set someone a pipeline target, expect enough deals at each stage, want close dates, want next steps and want good coverage.

Then we ask the same person to tell us which stage their deals are at, whether they’re likely to close, what the next step is and when they’re going to close. Then we manage them using those answers.

Nobody needs to fiddle anything for this to become a problem. You just need “I don’t know yet” to be a less comfortable answer than “it’s progressing”, or taking a deal out to create more grief than leaving it in.

That’s not really a salesperson problem. I want to know what our management system is teaching people it’s safe to tell us. You?
