A decade ago roughly half of salespeople hit quota. Today, in many organisations, it is closer to one in three. Most leadership teams treat that as a performance problem. It isn’t. It’s a system signal.

Across many companies the same pattern appears. Forecasts become less reliable. Deals stall in the middle of the funnel. Pipeline grows, but conversion quietly falls.

I wrote about the pattern here:
https://lnkd.in/e5pkEVEy

The instinctive response inside most companies is predictable. Increase activity. Add more outreach. Expand the pipeline. But activity rarely fixes the underlying problem.

The reason is simple. Most boards are looking in the wrong place.

They focus on the numbers. But the numbers sit downstream. The earliest signals appear somewhere else entirely. Once you see where those signals originate, a lot of familiar problems start to make sense.

Stalled deals. Late forecast changes. Pipeline that grows but refuses to convert.

And the question boards keep asking about sales changes completely.
