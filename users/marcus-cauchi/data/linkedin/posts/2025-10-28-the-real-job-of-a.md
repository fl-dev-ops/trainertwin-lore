The real job of a founder isn’t growth - it’s clarity.

Growth hides problems.
 Clarity exposes them.

You don’t scale confusion.
 You multiply it.

Most founders chase momentum because stillness feels like regression.
 But stillness is where the truth shows up.

The companies that scale cleanly aren’t faster -
 they’re clearer.

You don’t need to move more.
 You need to see better.

Take a Peek in the Mirror:
 What problem would disappear tomorrow if you saw it clearly today?
