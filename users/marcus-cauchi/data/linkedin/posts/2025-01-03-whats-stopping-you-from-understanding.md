Leading a sales team is more than managing pipelines and hitting targets, it’s about understanding people. Why they thrive, where they struggle, and how you can bring out their best.

I’m offering 10 DISC licenses at a fraction of the usual cost to help you jumpstart this process. DISC provides insights into your team’s behavioural styles, offering a lens to improve communication, coaching, and collaboration.

But before you grab a license, let’s be real: DISC, or any other profiling tool we use, has its limits.

What DISC Can (and Can’t) Do

What It’s Great For:

✅ Giving your team a shared language for understanding behaviour and communication.
✅ Helping you tailor your coaching to individual strengths and blind spots.
✅ Offering clarity on team dynamics, so you can reduce friction and build trust.

What It Won’t Do:

❌ Capture the full complexity of a person. Humans aren’t just quadrants or graphs.
❌ Predict every behaviour in every situation. Context and stress play huge roles.
❌ Replace real conversations and observation. It’s a tool, not the whole story.

Why It’s Still Worth It

When used intentionally, DISC helps you see patterns, spark meaningful dialogue, and create a foundation for deeper understanding. It’s a starting point, not the finish line. But it’s a powerful one.
