Hiring managers don’t want to gamble. They want certainty.

Most profiles drown in adjectives. “Hardworking, strategic, experienced.”

... But adjectives are opinions. Numbers are proof.

Hiring managers look for three things:
Lower risk – you won’t cost them mistakes.
Fast value – you’ll make an impact quickly.
Future relevance – you’ll still matter when the business changes.

That’s why your profile should read like this:

Before → After → Focus

* Before: Where you/your team started.
* After: What improved.
* Focus: The number that proves it.

Examples:
* Before: 40% opp coverage → After: 95% → Focus: Forecast accuracy +12%.
* Before: No onboarding playbook → After: Playbook installed → Focus: Ramp time cut 30%.
* Before: Cold outreach only → After: 3 Dream clients inbound → Focus: Deal size doubled.

Now imagine showing a hiring manager the same logic in your intro:
* A 90-day plan: how you’ll get up to speed fast.
* A risk map: how you spot problems before they cost the team.
* A value plan: how you’ll move the numbers that matter.

That’s how you go from “another applicant” to the safest hire in the room.
Action: Rewrite one line of your profile today with Before → After → Focus.
