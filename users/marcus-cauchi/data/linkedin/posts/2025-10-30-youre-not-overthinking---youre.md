You’re not overthinking - you’re under-deciding.

You call it analysis.
 But deep down, you know it’s avoidance.

Another tab open.
 Another note file.
 Another “let’s revisit next week.”

You’re not searching for the right path -
 you’re stalling on the hard one.

Clarity doesn’t come from thinking.
 It comes from choosing.

Every delay tells your team one thing:
 you don’t trust your own instinct.

What’s one decision you already know - but keep postponing?
