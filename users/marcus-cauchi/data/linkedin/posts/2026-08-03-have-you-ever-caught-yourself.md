Have you ever caught yourself saying, "Maybe next year"?

Not because anyone asked you to wait. Not because someone made a promise. Just because it felt easier to believe things were moving than to ask whether they really were.

Years have a habit of disappearing like that.

You stay busy. You keep delivering. People trust you. Your reputation grows. From the outside, everything looks as though it's heading in the right direction.
Then one day you realise you've become very good at a version of your life that you never actually chose.

That's a difficult thought to sit with.
Not because you've failed.

Because you've spent so long being responsible that you've stopped asking whether the future you're working towards is still the one you want.

If that thought feels uncomfortably familiar, you're probably not the only one.
