The Hidden Taxes of Bad Measurement
Every business pays them.
Most never see them.

Part 4: The Churn Tax
The Churn Tax is paid months after the celebration. It begins when winning the customer matters more than helping them succeed.
Most churn starts before the contract is signed. Expectations drift. Risks stay hidden. Poor-fit customers are accepted because they help this quarter’s target. Customer Success inherits promises they didn’t make, while the organisation quietly pays through lower renewals, higher support costs and damaged trust.
Measure customer outcomes after the sale with the same discipline used before it.
