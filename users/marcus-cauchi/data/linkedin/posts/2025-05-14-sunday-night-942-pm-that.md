Sunday night. 9:42 p.m.
 That sinking feeling hits. Pipeline’s thin. End of quarter’s five days out.
 I’m at 60% of quota. No room for error.

There’s one deal left.
 The big one.
 The one “circling back internally” for two weeks.

Monday. 8:07 a.m.
 Message from my boss:
“Push it. No excuses. Deadline Friday. Play hardball if you have to.”

The pressure lands sharp.
 Not panic, a weight across my chest, like someone’s leaning on a clipboard just beneath my collarbone.

I’ve done the pressure-close thing before.
 It worked. Once.
 The client churned.
 Then I did, on the inside.

I open the email draft. I know how to write it. Urgency, FOMO, discount expires.
 But something holds me.

Two weeks ago, the client told me:
“We want this to be the right decision, not the fast one.”

That’s when I paused.
 Grabbed the one tool I’ve started using this quarter:
 The Integrity-First Decision Tree.

First question:
 Is this mine to solve?
 Second:
 Would I be proud of this close, or just relieved?

I rewrote the email. Direct, human, no pressure.
 They signed two days later.
 Not because I pushed.
 
Because I didn’t.
 📍Still missed quota.
 📍Still kept the relationship.
 📍Still kept myself.

If you’re staring down a pressure close,
 a bad-fit hire, a misaligned deal, or a quota trap ...

DM me “Decision Tree”
 and I’ll send you the exact tool I used.

It’s not a funnel. It’s a lifeline.
 Because the wrong decision doesn’t just cost numbers.
 It corrodes trust, yours, theirs, and the one you have with yourself.

🔁 Share this with your team if you’re building a culture that plays the long game.
