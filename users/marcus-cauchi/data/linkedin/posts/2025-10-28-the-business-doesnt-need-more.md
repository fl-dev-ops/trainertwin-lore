The business doesn’t need more goals - it needs fewer constraints.

Rules protect mediocrity.
 They give safety to people who stopped thinking.

High performers don’t just need tighter systems.
 They need clearer direction and fewer walls.

When everything’s over-structured,
 initiative dies quietly.

Remove the rules.
 Keep the standards.

Then watch how fast people rise.

 Which rule would you delete if you trusted your team completely?
