The teams that win now aren’t doing more, they’re doing what matters.

When a team I coached cut “activity” by a third, revenue jumped 28% while competitors flatlined. They stopped chasing noise and doubled down on moves buyers trusted.

This isn’t an outlier. Gallup shows high-trust teams are 50% more likely to hit targets. HubSpot reports trust-led sellers close 20–30% more deals. Activity quotas drain energy. Trust quotas build it.

The cost of getting this wrong isn’t just missed targets. It’s burnout, attrition, and mistrust. You don’t just lose deals, you kill careers.

Check your last 10 wins. Were they closed by pressure, or by trust? If you don’t like the answer, the problem isn’t them. It’s you.
