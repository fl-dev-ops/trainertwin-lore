The problem isn’t out there.
 It’s in the mirror.
 And YOUR system’s working perfectly, to deliver the failure you’re pretending is a surprise!

Perfect System. Shit Results. Your Move.

Your system is perfectly designed
 to produce the exact results
 you’re getting right now.

The churn.
 The drift.
 The silence in your all-hands.
 The top-performers ghosting exit interviews.
 The sales theatre masking a dead pipeline.

None of it is a surprise.
 It’s not a glitch.
 It’s not a market wobble.
 It’s not “post-Covid morale issues.”

It’s a system working exactly as YOU built it!
 You just didn’t build it for #trust.
 You built it for #extraction.
 For #optics.
 For #speed.
 For #ShortTerm gain dressed as strategic intent.

And now the RESULTS YOU DON'T WANT?
 They’re the perfect reward for the decisions you made.
 They're what you deserve for what you tolerate.
 They're what you institutionalise in your playbooks, CRM and metrics.

The decisions you made when you ignored the early signals.
 When you called #misalignment “#PeopleProblems.”
 When you celebrated quarterly wins and quietly buried cultural debt.
 When you ran your teams on #fear and called it #accountability.

The real mistake isn’t the results.
The real mistake is blaming the people downstream
 for what was architected upstream (by you, your SLT, Board and investors)
 And refusing - flat-out refusing to look in the mirror and say:

“We designed this.
 We drove this.
 And now we can choose differently, 
 better, 
 fit for today's context.”

Until that happens,
 you’ll keep blaming the symptoms.
 The attrition. The quiet quitting. 
 Top talent leaving
 Those who stay set up to fail
 Everyone's missing numbers.
 Your are accountable
   For making impossible for them to be responsible.

And you’ll keep missing the root cause:
 A leadership belief system that’s delivering failure
 as if it’s a fluke.
But it’s not a fluke.
 It’s a forecast.
 And it’s right on time.

So what are you going to do?
Build a new belief system?
 Or keep dressing the wounds of the one
 that’s still bleeding your company out?

If you want to pinpoint the single points of failure in your business
If you want to measure the cost of #drift and 
 How that leads to a drop in perceived credibility by eroding #TrustedExecution
If you want to calculate the ROI and COI of Trust as your #LeadingIndicator
