Most people don't talk about the emotional cost of leadership.

People assume the hardest part is the workload.

It isn't.

It's lying awake wondering whether you made the right call. It's knowing a decision that protects the business may hurt a good person. It's questioning whether you moved too soon, waited too long or missed something that would have changed everything.

The higher you climb, the fewer people you can share those thoughts with. Everyone sees the decision. Very few see the weight that came with it.
I've spent three decades working with commercial leaders, and one thing has become clear. Leadership isn't about having all the answers. It's about making the best decision you can with incomplete evidence, treating people with dignity and accepting that you'll still be judged by the outcome.

If you've ever sat alone after making a decision that nobody else could make for you, this one's for you.
