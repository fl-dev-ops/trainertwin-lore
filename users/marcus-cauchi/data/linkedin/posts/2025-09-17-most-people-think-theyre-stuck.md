Most people think they’re stuck because they’re not working hard enough.
But here’s the awkward truth: hard work without positioning is a treadmill.

I see it every week with founders, partners, and investors.

 – Brilliant teams grinding late nights.
 – Managers fixing broken handoffs.
 – Leaders carrying risk for decisions they never got to shape.

It’s not effort that fails them. It’s when they’re invited in. Too late. After budgets are locked. After strategy is set. By then, they’re firefighting instead of shaping outcomes.

That’s why this post matters. Because when you shift from vendor to ally, the game changes:
* You stop being replaceable.
* You become relied on.
* You move from carrying mistakes to owning results.

The Ally Method exists to engineer that shift. To help you win earlier, safer, and with more leverage.

So read the post. Reread it. Ask yourself: “Am I positioned where trust begins, or where the scraps land?”

If the answer stings, good. That’s the signal. The next move is simple: lean in. Start the conversation. Because you don’t need more effort. You need better positioning.
