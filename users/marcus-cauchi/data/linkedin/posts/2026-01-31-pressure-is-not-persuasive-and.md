Pressure is not persuasive, and I won’t pretend it ever is.

Most sellers treat urgency like caffeine.
Add it and expect the decision to move faster.

That is not how senior buyers process it.

Pressure isn’t heard as speed.
It is read as risk misplacement.

When you push for a decision, the buyer doesn’t think about momentum. They think about who is still standing when this goes wrong.

And it always goes wrong somewhere.

Not catastrophically. Just enough to require explanation later.

At senior levels, decisions don’t end when contracts are signed.
They echo.

They resurface in budget reviews.
In leadership changes.
In postmortems that pretend to be forward looking.

You move on.
They remain inside the outcome.

So pressure doesn’t feel like help.
It feels like a preview of absence.

Here is the calculation buyers make and never say out loud.

If you need this decision now, your incentives peak before my exposure does.

That does not make you dishonest.
It makes you unsafe.

Unsafe decisions are simply rejected.
They are delayed until they are survivable or until the pressure source disappears.

That is why buyers ghost.
Silence is cheaper than absorbing someone else’s urgency.

Discounts absolutely do not fix this.
They confirm it.

A price drop under pressure signals relief seeking, not partnership.
It tells the buyer you are solving your problem first and hoping they inherit the rest.

From their side, that is not generosity.
It is a liability transfer.

Here is the inversion sellers keep missing.

You think pressure helps people decide.
Buyers experience pressure as evidence of who will not carry consequences.

Once that signal is received, no amount of follow up reverses it.

If you’re selling into senior decisions and seeing stalls, loops, or polite silence, this is not dysfunction.
It is rational self protection playing out on a longer timeline than your forecast.

Pressure does not accelerate decisions.
It exposes who benefits first.

Rule.

If your urgency appears before their decision is safe to live with, you have already slowed the deal.
