Today is School Governor Awareness Day.

School governors are the largest volunteer group in England and yet we still need more! 

We have vacancies in my current school, Andover Church of England Primary School, in case you're interested!

Education is a huge passion of mine. I want every child to have a lifelong love of learning. No matter where they start every child should leave school with new skills and the ability to access the next stage in their lives. 

Making no progress is not acceptable for any child.

Governance is incredibly rewarding. Seeing school in action and spending time with the children is a true joy - knowing you have played a very small part in that process.

If you have some time available I urge you to consider it. I'm always happy to have a chat if you'd like to learn more.

And to my fellow school governors: Cheers to us!
