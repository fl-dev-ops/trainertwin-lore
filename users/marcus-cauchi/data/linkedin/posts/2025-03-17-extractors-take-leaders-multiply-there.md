EXTRACTORS TAKE. LEADERS MULTIPLY.

There are two kinds of power players:
* The Extractors, they take more than they give. They see trust as leverage, people as tools, and relationships as transactions.
* The Expanders, they multiply opportunity, create more leaders, and leave every space better than they found it.

One model is failing. The other is scaling.

Extractors hoard control, then wonder why their people disengage.
Expanders build trust, so execution moves at the speed of belief.

The future doesn’t belong to those who demand loyalty.
 It belongs to those who earn it.

Tag someone who multiplies instead of extracting. What did you learn about what you would accept and your boundaries?
