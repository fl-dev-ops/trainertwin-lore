The multiple usually moves before the churn report explains why.

A surprising amount of ARR is weaker than the board pack suggests.

That is where value leaks.

Not in the churn report.
Not when the board pack lands.
Much earlier.

In weak adoption.
Thin sponsorship.
Suppressed expansion.
Forecast confidence built on revenue that has not earned its place.

By the time it is obvious in diligence, the multiple has already moved.

Very few teams can defend the quality of their revenue.

That is what the Revenue Fragility Audit surfaces, before diligence does.

Marcus Cauchi
Stopping commercial decisions becoming debt
