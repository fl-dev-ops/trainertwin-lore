What broke sales was not people. It was the loss of apprenticeship.

We stopped showing the work.
We stopped riding with managers.
We stopped teaching judgement.
We handed them dashboards instead of guidance.
Then we wondered why everything feels brittle.

Most managers were promoted without ever seeing good management up close.
Not once.
Not properly.
Not in the field.
They are not the problem.
They are the product of a system that removed the craft and kept the pressure.
This series is about giving that craft back.
