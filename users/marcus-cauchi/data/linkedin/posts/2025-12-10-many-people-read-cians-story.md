  Success is a habit formed by design not luck

Many people read Cian’s story and think it is a lucky win. I read it and see a reminder that luck is often just preparation waiting for everyone else to trip first. 

People say luck is preparation meeting opportunity. In enterprise sales, it is often preparation meeting competitor self-harm. You are the best trade off option! 

The seller may think they won because they had a strong day. Woo hoo! 
The buyer later explained they had actually lost three times, but the other vendors managed to disqualify themselves with real enthusiasm. Oooof! 

This is why I push precall planning and post-call debriefs. 

Not as admin. As the only way to stop believing the stories we tell ourselves about deals. Buyers never choose based on the meeting you enjoyed. They choose based on whether the decision is safe to explain to the people who were not in the room and will question it later. 

When you understand the (#JTBD) job your customer is trying to get done, how risk travels through the second and third rooms and the pushes, pulls and fears shaping their path, you shift from guessing to design. 

Preparation creates the conditions for good luck. Debriefing stops you wasting it.
