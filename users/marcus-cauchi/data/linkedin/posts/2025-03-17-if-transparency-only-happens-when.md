IF TRANSPARENCY ONLY HAPPENS WHEN THINGS GO WELL, IT’S NOT TRANSPARENCY.

True transparency means honesty in both success and struggle.
* If leadership only shares wins, they’re hiding the real story.
* If you find out bad news through the rumor mill, trust is already gone.
* If teams don’t know what’s really happening, execution will always be reactive.

The best organisations communicate even when it’s uncomfortable.
* They surface problems early instead of covering them up.
* They trust employees with the truth, good or bad.
* They know that honesty builds buy-in, while silence breeds resentment.

Transparency isn’t just about sharing victories, it’s about sharing reality.

Tag someone who leads with clarity, not selective storytelling.
