This post by Suzanne the sore spot because it touches something most teams avoid saying aloud.

“Qualified” feels precise.
But in many organisations it’s a courtesy label, not a truth claim.

Same CRM stage.
Same framework.
Radically different meanings.

Which means the pipeline isn’t wrong because people are sloppy.
It’s wrong because execution reality never entered the decision.

Here’s the part most teams miss:

Even if every BANT, MEDDPICC or pain. budget and decision box is ticked, a deal can still be unqualified if the buyer can’t realise the value under their real constraints.

Capacity.
Capability.
Internal trust.
Competing priorities.

When those aren’t visible, qualification becomes theatre and forecasting becomes faith.

Serious question for leaders reading this:

If you asked three people in your business what “qualified” means, would you bet your next quarter on the answers matching?
