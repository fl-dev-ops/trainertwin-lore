Trust isn’t soft. It’s the hardest metric in business.

It shows up in speed, retention, and resilience. When trust is high, teams move faster, clients stay longer, and leaders breathe easier. When it’s low, everything drags, until it breaks.

Here’s the uncomfortable truth:
* Burnout isn’t just exhaustion. It’s the collapse of trust.
* Discounts aren’t strategy. They’re a receipt for trust you failed to earn.
* Chaos doesn’t kill leaders. Lack of trust does.

Legacy isn’t built in calm. It’s built in storms.

The post lays it bare: when the next storm hits, will trust make you triumph, or expose you?
