A friendly hint does not constitute evidence of expansion. When a stakeholder mentions, "another team could use this," it can be interpreted in various ways: as politeness, enthusiasm, a personal opinion, or simply a way to conclude a conversation amicably.

Real expansion becomes evident when someone engages in decision-making work by:
- Naming a problem
- Introducing an owner
- Clarifying priority
- Confirming timing
- Accepting responsibility for the next step

Before building the opportunity story, it's essential to test the evidence.
