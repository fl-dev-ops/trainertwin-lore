Strong post. One addition worth making:

Most reps focus on what to add. Most coaches focus on how to push harder. Buyers feel both as pressure.

The real test of a coach is simple:

Can they help you remove things that make buyers tense, confused, or cautious?

If the answer is no, you’ll get more technique, more steps, more “best practices” layered onto an already noisy process. Your manager might like it, but your buyers won’t.

What to ask a coach from the buyer’s side:
	•	Will this make my conversations lighter or heavier?
	•	Will this reduce surprises for buyers or create new ones?
	•	Will this make decisions safer or just faster?
	•	Will this help me walk away clean when we’re not a fit?

If a coach can’t explain how buyers benefit, they’re training you to perform, not to sell.
