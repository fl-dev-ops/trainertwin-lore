First Monday of the month. You can tell the business is working harder than it should.

Marketing thinks it needs more demand. Sales thinks it needs better leads. The website feels behind, the offer is harder to explain, and every fix comes with its own cost and promise.

The hard part is that none of those views is obviously wrong. They may all be signs of the same break.

So the risk isn’t just backing the wrong fix. It’s changing several things while buyers keep reaching the same unclear conclusion about the business.
The problem could sit in who you attract, what you offer, what buyers understand, what they trust or what happens once sales gets involved.
A new website might help. So might better demand, clearer positioning or stronger sales support.

But first, you need to know what the business is teaching buyers now, where that starts to work against you, and which change would alter the outcome.

That’s usually the part everyone can feel, but no one can see clearly enough to call.
