Most founders I meet don’t have a growth problem.
 They have an uncertainty problem.
* Revenue up.
* Revenue down.
* Forecasts that feel like fiction.

Everyone thinks you’ve got it handled.
* But every win hides another sleepless night wondering:
* What happens if payroll hits before cash does?

Everything still runs because of you 
 ... and that’s exactly why it can’t run without you.

You’ve earned your scars.
 ... Now they’re costing you.

That’s founder risk - the quiet leak that compounds every quarter.
 We see it in the data every week:

* £4 million in pipeline, half unqualified.
* £600 000 lost to discounts.
* £1.2 million trapped in stalled deals.
* £5 million a year in preventable waste — and that hits your multiple every quarter.

Not from bad people.
... From a system built on trust in one person.

When that trust gets rebuilt into rhythm and ownership,
* Forecast accuracy moves from ± 40 % to ± 10 %.
* Sales cycles shorten by six weeks.
* And the founder finally stops being the bottleneck.

Investors don’t discount your growth.
* They discount your dependence.

Predictability is the new multiple.

 ... You’ll leave with your own Value Leakage Map - clear, quantified, and built for your business.
