The real cost of unilateral discounting
 Every time you offer a discount without being asked, you’re not closing the deal faster.
 You’re seeding doubt.

 It doesn’t feel like a lie.
 But it tells the buyer: “This wasn’t really worth what you said.”

Over the customer lifetime, that one move can cost:
£10K+ in missed expansion
Renewal delays
Referral silence
Team-wide pricing confidence

 If your team is defaulting to price before value, the system, not the rep, is broken.
