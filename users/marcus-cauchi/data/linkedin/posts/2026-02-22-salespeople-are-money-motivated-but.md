Salespeople are money motivated. But money is not the motivation.

That label is common. And it is incomplete.
Money is the scoreboard. Not the engine.

And yes, remove pay and most people stop showing up.
That's economics, not motivation.

If you want the real driver, ask:

1. What does money unlock that you cannot do today?
2. What number makes you feel safe?
3. If I followed you around on a great week, what would I see you doing really well?
4. If you miss target for 3 months, what do you do on Monday?

Now you are hiring and coaching for the engine:
Security. Growth. Recognition. Autonomy. Fear.

Same comp plan. Different driver. Different leadership.

What is the best question you use to uncover motivation?
