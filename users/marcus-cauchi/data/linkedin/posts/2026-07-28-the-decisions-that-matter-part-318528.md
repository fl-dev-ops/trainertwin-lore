The Decisions That Matter
Part 8: Should We Say Yes?

Every yes commits resources that can no longer be used somewhere else. Time, attention, money, capacity and trust all become less available the moment another commitment enters the system.

Saying yes feels constructive because the cost isn’t paid immediately. It arrives later through overloaded teams, poor priorities, delayed work and opportunities that deserved attention but never received it.

The best commercial leaders understand that every commitment is also a decision about what won’t happen. That makes saying no one of the most valuable judgement skills any leader can develop.

Before saying yes, ask what this decision will force you to say no to later.
