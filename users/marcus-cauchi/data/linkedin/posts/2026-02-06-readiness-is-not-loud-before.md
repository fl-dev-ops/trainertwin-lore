Readiness is not loud.

Before a team is ready, it often looks like this.

People ask questions they cannot answer yet.
Momentum slows.
There is a sense that something still needs working through.

That is not readiness.
That is the work that leads to readiness.

Confidence is louder.

It looks like agreement.
Like momentum.
Like people saying yes without qualifiers.

Most organisations learn to reward confidence.

So when that early work shows up as questions or hesitation, it gets relabelled.

Not as we are not ready yet
but as doubt.
As overthinking.
As resistance.

Once that happens, the work stops.

Questions get worked around.
People learn that slowing things down has a cost.
So they go quiet.

That quiet gets mistaken for consent.

Decisions move forward.

And the organisation only discovers later
what it never actually worked through.
