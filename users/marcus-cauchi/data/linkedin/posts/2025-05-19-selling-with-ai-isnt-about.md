Actionable Insight: Don’t Personalise the Email, Personalise the Moment

Most personalisation means using AI to help you plug names into templates.
 That’s not real personalisation. That’s performance.

Instead, use AI to reflect context:
“Saw you posted about team burnout last week. Made me wonder, how are you protecting momentum without more pressure?”

Now it’s a conversation. Not a pitch.

Prompt: Cross-reference their LinkedIn activity + industry changes. What human question would earn a reply?
