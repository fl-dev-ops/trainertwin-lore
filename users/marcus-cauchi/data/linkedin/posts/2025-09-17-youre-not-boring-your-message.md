You’re not boring. Your message is.

Founders: if people still say
“Interesting, I’ll think about it”
… you don’t have a messaging problem.

You have a belief problem.

What makes you different is buried under slides, product demos, and polite language.

I help founders sell what they built does for people in a way they can’t unhear.

Nobody remembers polite.

They remember the moment you told the truth so sharply …
it cut through the noise.

Be timely. Be relevant. Be personally valuable.
