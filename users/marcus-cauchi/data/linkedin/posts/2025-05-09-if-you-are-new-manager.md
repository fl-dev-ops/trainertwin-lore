If you are new manager this is your real job.

Hire great people. (Your bench is your annuity income)
 Make it easy for them to do great work. (Process that empowers people to execute to the standard intended)
 Block the stupid stuff, especially your own. (Protect them to protect yourself)
 Help them help others make smart decisions. (This is how you can trust your people)

And if I forget:
 My job isn’t control. It’s to serve and support. (Succession depends on the success of others)
When I’ve done it right, they won’t need me anymore. (Make yourself redundant)
