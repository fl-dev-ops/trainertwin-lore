Fear Bends the Truth
Reps don’t overcommit because they’re bold. They do it because they’re scared.
Scared of looking weak.
Scared of slowing the room.
Scared of being the only one unsure.
Fear distorts more numbers than skill ever will.
Where does fear change the story in your team?
