Most leadership assessments are vanity metrics.
They exist to tell you what you already hope is true.

This is different. This isn’t a self-assessment.
It’s a mirror.

The 15 questions below are there to surface what usually gets avoided.
Hiring shortcuts. Single-point failures. The subtle gap between what you think people understand and what they actually do.

The rules are simple.

Don’t total a score.
Notice how fast you answer.
Fast usually means there’s a system.
Hesitation means there’s a blind spot.
Justifying it means there’s a liability.

Blind spots rarely show up where you feel weak.
They show up where things seem fine but haven’t been tested.

If you can get through Q5, Q10, and Q12 without flinching, you’re building a business. If you can’t, you’re holding one together.

Which question makes you pause?
