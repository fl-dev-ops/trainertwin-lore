Strategy is what you’re willing to ignore.
Anyone can add goals.
 The real skill is deleting them.
Focus feels boring when you’re wired for more.
 But that boredom is where strategy lives.
Every “yes” adds friction.
 Every “no” adds speed.
You don’t need a bigger plan.
 You need a smaller one that actually works.
Mirror:
 What’s one thing you keep pretending still deserves your attention?
