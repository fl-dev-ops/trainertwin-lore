The most dangerous board meetings are not the tense ones.
They are the calm ones.

Real risk rises.
Perceived risk falls.

The board feels safer while the business becomes less safe.

That happens when reporting smooths uncertainty instead of reducing it with evidence.

A weak-fit customer becomes “strategic revenue”.
Delivery friction becomes “implementation noise”.
Margin drag gets blended.
Customer silence gets mistaken for stability.
Forecast confidence travels further than buyer evidence.

By the time the number breaks, the operating truth has usually been visible for months.

It just didn't reach the board intact.

Signed revenue is not the same as sound revenue.
A clean board pack is not the same as operating truth.

Most value erosion starts long before the numbers break.
