Are you building strong pipeline, or revenue that churns as fast as it lands?
Are you closing business that hits this quarter while pushing preventable cost into customer success, operations, and account management?
Are you growing revenue, or growing cost to serve because your best people have to stop building and start fixing?

If growth creates clean-up, it is not growth. It is cost disguised as revenue.
