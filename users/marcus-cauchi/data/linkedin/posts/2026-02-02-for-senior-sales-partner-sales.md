For Senior Sales, Partner Sales and Individual Contributors

When you are a senior sales / individual contributor, people lean on you to move things forward.

You get brought in when a deal is complicated, when there is pressure to make progress, or when someone needs the conversation to land safely.

You do that by explaining, reassuring, and filling in gaps so things do not stall.

Most of the time, that works.

The problem is that some of those explanations turn into expectations later. Things you didn't promise start getting treated as commitments. And when something doesn't hold, you're the one people look to.

Not because you were careless.
Because you were the person in the room who made it feel okay to proceed.

My coaching works on those moments while they are still live. The moments where you are deciding how much to say, what to leave open, and when to pause instead of smoothing things over.

We do not rehearse scripts or techniques. We work on real conversations you are already having.

The point is not to slow you down or make you cautious.

It's to let you keep operating at a senior level without ending up responsible for things you never actually agreed to own.
