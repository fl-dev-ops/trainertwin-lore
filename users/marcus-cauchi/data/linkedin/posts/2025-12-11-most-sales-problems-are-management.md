Most sales problems are management problems created by design, not by talent.

Managers were promoted for their numbers.
Not their judgement.
Not their ability to grow people.
Not their coaching.

They were dropped into the job with a quota and hope.
No mentor.
No model.
No apprenticeship.

You cannot build strong teams when every manager is forced to learn the job alone.

That is not leadership development.
That is survival.
