Folks, we have finally found a really powerful format, largely thanks to the fantastic support of Charles H. Green.

It's called ...

#SellersAnonymous

Like AA, it's a bunch of recovering sales addicts who had to learn the hard way that failing to put our customer at the heart of everything we do in sales is much like the road to hell ... paved with good intentions and bad outcomes.

Will you step up and run these locally. We aim to build only by personal introduction and through our personal networks. 

You all stepped forward and said that #TheSellerCode resonates with you.

This is YOUR community. No central leadership. Small groups of up to 12 so everyone has a chance to be heard. No coaching. No judgement.

And we develop close personal relationships during the 121 networking that follows. High challenge, high support.

The last couple have been exceptional, and the impact both storytellers had on the group was profound. Describing their journey through traditional selling and doing everything they could to win at any cost - always be closing, create urgency, "Some will, some won't, so what? Next!" leading to churn through viable relationships, lack of transparency, burnout and constant pressure to produce.

DM or comment "I'm in" and well add you to the invitation list to experience a meeting for yourself
