Why are you paying for your customers to churn?

 You’re not just losing customers.
 You’re funding their exit.

Acquisition spend: gone
Onboarding time: sunk
Expansion potential: vanished

And what did you learn when they left? Usually ... nothing.
Because most churn isn’t a CS issue.

 It’s a trust system failure that started before the contract was signed.

 If your renewals feel like uphill battles, it’s worth asking:
 Are you building loyalty, or just prolonging the loss?
