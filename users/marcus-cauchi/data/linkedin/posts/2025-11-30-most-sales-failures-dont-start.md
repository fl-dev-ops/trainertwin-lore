Most “sales failures” don’t start in sales. They start long before anyone is hired.

They start when a company brings someone in to do a job it hasn’t actually taken the time to understand.

They start when a founder mistakes their own charm and improvisation for something another human can repeat. They start when no one has bothered to watch how real buyers behave in the wild, how they hesitate, get spooked, wander off, or simply go quiet.

They start when product, ops, and marketing aren’t ready for the weight that a real pipeline puts on them.

By the time a rep is missing quota, the ending was already written.
They’re not failing. They’re just the first honest sign that the story underneath wasn’t finished.

Hiring sellers into an unfinished setup doesn’t speed anything up.
It only makes the confusion louder, faster, and far more expensive.

What job are you actually hiring your next seller to do?
