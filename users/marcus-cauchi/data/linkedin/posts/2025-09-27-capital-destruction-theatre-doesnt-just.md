Capital Destruction 

Theatre doesn’t just waste time. It destroys capital.

Every zombie deal in a forecast bleeds money. Marketing paid for the lead. SDRs chased it. AEs burned discovery hours. SEs built demos. Managers reviewed it. Ops updated it. Finance modelled it. All sunk into a deal that was never real.

Now multiply that by 10, 50, 100 deals. That’s not just wasted effort, that’s millions in wasted capital.

Boards spot it instantly. That’s why forecast variance is the fastest way to lose trust. A rep might get away with optimism. A manager might spin. But when the CFO can’t connect pipeline to cash, valuation multiples shrink. Investors don’t price hope. They price truth.

This is the pattern across failing GTM systems:
* Inflated pipelines → CAC bloats.
* Fake commits → variance widens.
* Boards stop trusting → cost of capital rises.

That is why we LAZARUS every deal. To kill theatre. To cut zombies. To restore buyer truth. Because without truth, your revenue system is uninvestable.

Zombie deals don’t just kill your quarter. They kill your valuation.
