Most GTM breakdowns do not start with bad execution.
They start with the wrong explanation.

When growth slows, teams add visibility.
When confidence drops, leaders add alignment.
When things still do not move, RevOps gets pulled in to explain the gap.

That pattern feels responsible.
It is also how systems quietly destabilise themselves.

This post is not about fixing GTM.
It is about recognising when the system is misreading what is happening, and why effort stops working at the exact moment pressure rises.

A few questions worth sitting with before you scroll past:

Are your metrics still settling decisions, or are they just extending the conversation?

When forecasts miss, do you change behaviour, or do you add explanation?

Is RevOps reducing ambiguity, or absorbing it so no one else has to decide?

If results stabilised tomorrow, would your system actually stay stable?

If any of those feel uncomfortably familiar, this will land.

Read it slowly.
It is not asking you to act.
It is asking you to see something you cannot unsee once you do.
