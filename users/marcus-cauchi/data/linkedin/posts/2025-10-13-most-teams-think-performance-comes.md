Most teams think performance comes from speed or pressure. It doesn’t.
It comes from coherence - when Time, Truth, and Progress finally line up.

Time gives pattern and patience.
Truth gives honesty and protection from pretence.
Progress gives motion and proof.

Lose one, and trust starts to leak.
Keep all three, and the system compounds quietly - calm people, clean decisions, steady results.

This is the hidden equation behind every low-drama, high-performing team.
It’s not balance. It’s alignment.
And alignment turns effort into equity.

If you’re leading under pressure, ask yourself:
Which one slips first - Time, Truth, or Progress?
