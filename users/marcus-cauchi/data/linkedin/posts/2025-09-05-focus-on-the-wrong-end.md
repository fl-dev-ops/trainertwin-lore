Focus on the wrong end of your problem, and it will cost you. The real killer of exits isn’t one bad quarter. It’s compounding the wrong things, over time, at scale.

Ignore these 5 signs and it won’t be one big crash, it’ll be death by a thousand cuts. Long nights. Doubling down on the grind. Watching your exit drift further out of reach.

It always starts slow.
 Then you compound what doesn’t work, at scale.

And in today’s economy, you don’t get away with that for long. Debt is expensive. Customers are more selective, slower to move. The margin for error has collapsed.

If you abdicate leadership and clarity to AI, you won’t default tomorrow. But you will.

So take stock. Ask yourself the hard questions, before the market asks them for you. Life’s too short, and the window’s too narrow, to scale in the dark.
