LEADERSHIP THAT DEPENDS ON FEAR HAS ALREADY FAILED.
 Fear-based leadership looks powerful, until it collapses.

Fear creates compliance, not commitment.
 Fear kills creativity, ownership, and innovation.
 Fear-driven teams spend more energy covering themselves than driving results.

The strongest leaders don’t need to intimidate, they inspire.

Tag someone who leads with vision, not fear.
