A deal can look healthy in the CRM and already be dying in the room.

Nobody says no. The meetings keep happening. The forecast stays put. But inside the buyer's world, the decision has started to feel heavier than it looks.

That is where most deals really stall. Not because the value is unclear. Because the cost of being wrong has become too real.

Most sellers make the same mistake at this point. They push harder. More urgency. More ROI. More pressure. But pressure does not make the decision safer. It makes the buyer look harder at their own exposure.

If your late-stage deals keep slipping, the problem may not be your pitch. It may be that your process is helping decisions become real before they become safe.

That is how commercial debt gets created. And that is why it later shows up in slower time to value, weaker renewals, expansion resistance, and forecast slippage.
