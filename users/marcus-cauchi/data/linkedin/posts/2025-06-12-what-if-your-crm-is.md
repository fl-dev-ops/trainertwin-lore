What if your CRM is just a stage, and everyone's acting?

It doesn’t show what the customer’s actually trying to do.
It doesn’t tell you who matters. Or where the risk sits.
It doesn’t capture trust. Or fear. Or doubt. Or hope.

And it definitely doesn’t surface the politics or silence that derail deals.
Because those don’t fit in a dropdown.
So what do you get? A performance.

Reps pretending the numbers mean something.
Managers pretending to believe them.
Boards making decisions off data nobody trusts.
Everyone gaming the system to stay safe.

And behind it all, a quiet, shared fear:
The truth would break the dashboard.
That’s not a sales system.
That’s a culture of self-protection, not progress.

If your “single source of truth” is 80% theatre,
you’re not managing the business.
You’re managing the appearance of it.

And your customer?
They don’t see your CRM.
But they feel every corner you cut to make it look good.
Want to see what clean, truth-led selling looks like?
