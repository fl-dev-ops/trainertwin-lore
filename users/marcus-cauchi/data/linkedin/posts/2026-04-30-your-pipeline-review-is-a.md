Your pipeline review is a séance.

Everyone sits around trying to contact deals that died weeks ago. “Still in play.”
“Good conversation.” “Waiting on feedback.”

Translation: No evidence. No buyer movement. No value proven.

A live deal leaves footprints. New stakeholders. Internal conversations. Priority shifts. Decision work.

If the buyer has done nothing, nothing is happening.

The deal isn't slow. It's dead.
No evidence of value is evidence of death.

Everything else is pipeline theatre.
