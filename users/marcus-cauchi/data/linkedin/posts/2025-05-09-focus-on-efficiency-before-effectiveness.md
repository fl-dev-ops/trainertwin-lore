Focus on efficiency before effectiveness kills culture.
No one’s measuring the damage.

Yes, we’ve mastered doing more with less.
But what happens when “more” is just louder noise, faster?

David Brock nailed the uncomfortable truth:
Efficiency is seductive because it's easy.
But when efficiency outpaces effectiveness, chaos isn't reduced. It's multiplied, quietly, invisibly, fatally.

Being brutally honest:
🧠 Thinking has become heresy.
 George Brontén said, teams aren’t encouraged to solve better. They’re rewarded for doing more of the same, faster.
💰 We’ve monetised speed. But we’ve bankrupted insight.

Charles H. Green called out the insanity: when businesses are run like single-goal machines, everything looks like a cost. Including innovation. 

Including trust.

💣 And AI? It's become a 10x accelerator ... of whatever system you're too lazy to question.
Orrin Broberg warns, if you scale what’s broken, you just professionalise dysfunction.

Here’s the real price of false efficiency:
* Eroded trust
* Burnt-out teams
* Generic experiences
* Blindspots that turn into blow-ups

🚨 You're not optimising. You’re outsourcing judgment.

Want to build real advantage?
Swap efficiency addiction for #TrustVelocity.

🔁 Pause for empathy: What’s actually not working?
 📍 Focus with clarity: What’s the one thing that moves the needle?
 🤝 Lead with alignment: Are we rowing toward the same goal, or just rowing faster?
👉 Trust amplifies everything.

But if you don’t stop to earn it, you’re just facing toward irrelevance.
