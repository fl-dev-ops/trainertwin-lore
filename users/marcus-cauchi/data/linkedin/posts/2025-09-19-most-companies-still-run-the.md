Most companies still run the ABC playbook:

A customers = drain them.
B customers = upsell if easy.
C customers = ignore or squeeze.

It looks tidy in a board pack. But it’s not strategy. It’s extraction.
That’s why so many “perfect” ABC models plateau.

Revenue spikes, then churn accelerates. CAC climbs. Margins shrink. Trust dies.
JTBD flips the frame. Instead of slicing wallets, you map jobs:

What progress are they trying to make?
What risk are they desperate to avoid?
What outcome makes them look like a hero?
When you solve for jobs, not segments, growth compounds because trust compounds.

ABC is extraction. JTBD is equity. Which game are you really playing?
