If everything feels urgent, nothing is important.

Busyness is a drug.
 It numbs you from deciding what actually matters.
 Like a cocktail of crack and ketamine.

Founders don’t burn out from hard work -
 they burn out from half priorities.

Urgency keeps you active.
 Importance keeps you alive.

The goal isn’t to do everything fast.
 It’s to do the right things slow.
