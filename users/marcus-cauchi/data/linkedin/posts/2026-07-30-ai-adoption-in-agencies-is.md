AI adoption in agencies is uneven.

Brett Harned and I will be diving deeper into AI for AMs & PMs at the AM:PM Event in Atlanta 29 & 30 Sept.

After three years speaking at the event, I’m now co-hosting it with Brett with David C. Baker as our featured speaker. 

We’ve built this year’s programme around a problem we both keep seeing.

Project managers are carrying more operational pressure – particularly to create AI workflows. 

Account managers are being pulled into delivery instead of growing client relationships – and some agencies are losing clients unexpectedly. 

Agency owners are still being pulled into the difficult client conversations.

These are connected problems, seen from different angles. 

The idea of this AM:PM event is to bring account management, project management and agency leadership into the same room to work through them together.

The takeaways will be:

- Clearer roles and shared accountability around scope, handovers and growth
- Practical AI workflows that reduce admin and protect human judgement and client relationships 
- A chance to discuss your agency's operational problems 

Early bird tickets end tomorrow, 31 July.
