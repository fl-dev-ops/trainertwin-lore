Your salespeople create much of the data they’re judged by

Try this in your next forecast meeting.

A salesperson tells you a £500,000 deal isn’t as strong as they thought. Watch what happens next.

Why? What changed? Can we save it? Who else can we get involved? What’s the recovery plan? Where’s the replacement £500,000 coming from?

Now imagine they’d said nothing and left the deal where it was.
What would have happened today?
Probably very little.

That’s worth paying attention to. We tell salespeople we want bad news early, then we can make bringing us bad news considerably harder than leaving yesterday’s answer alone.

Nobody needs to fiddle the CRM for that to affect the data. People learn which answers create grief and which ones let them get on with their day.

Then management looks at the pipeline and assumes it’s seeing an accurate picture of what’s happening.

Before blaming CRM hygiene, I’d look at what happens when someone makes the number smaller because the evidence changed.

What does your business teach people to tell you?
