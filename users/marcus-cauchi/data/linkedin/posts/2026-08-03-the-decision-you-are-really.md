The decision you are really making

You are not simply choosing an investor.

You are choosing the operating conditions under which you, your management team and your company will have to perform.
You are choosing who controls the bad case.
You are choosing whose money is protected first, whose approval will be required and what happens if more capital becomes necessary.
You are choosing which promises will remain conversational and which will become safe to rely upon.

That is why the headline valuation is never the whole deal.

Before you take the money, understand what comes with it.

Bring me the proposed transaction and the part that doesn't feel safe. We will find out what you are actually being asked to rely upon before it becomes binding.
