You don’t need to work less. You need to stop working on things that don’t move you.

Burnout isn’t about hours.
 It’s about emptiness.

You can work twelve-hour days and feel alive -
 or four-hour days and feel done.

The difference isn’t time.
 It’s meaning.

Work that moves you gives energy back.
 Work that drains you steals more than time.

Which part of your week actually gives you energy?
