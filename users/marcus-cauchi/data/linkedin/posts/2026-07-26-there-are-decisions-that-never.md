There are decisions that never appear in your calendar.

Nobody asks you to prepare for them. There isn't a meeting invite. There's no deadline. Yet they sit in the back of your mind for weeks, sometimes months, quietly taking more energy than the conversations you're actually having.

You tell yourself you're waiting for the right moment. After the quarter. After the restructure. After they've seen one more result. After things settle down.
Meanwhile, you keep doing the work. You solve the awkward problems. You step in when something important starts drifting. You make things easier for everyone around you and tell yourself that people will eventually connect the dots.

Most experienced leaders have a conversation like this with themselves at some point.

Very few ever say it out loud.

If you've been carrying one around for longer than you'd like to admit, you might recognise more of yourself in this than you expect.
