Most sales teams don’t stall because people stop trying.
They stall because the structure that worked at £2m stops working above £10m.

You add reps.
You add managers.
You add pressure.

The numbers move, but the system is fragile. A few people carry everything. Forecasts wobble. Every quarter costs more effort than the last. That isn’t a performance issue.

It’s a design flaw.

---

Most fixes aim at behaviour. We work on structure.

When learning is pushed into decks, judgement decays. When managers sell and lead, conflict creeps in. When work fragments, accountability disappears. Change the structure and behaviour changes on its own.

---

We work on the architecture of revenue. How judgement forms and compounds. How sales scales without breaking trust. If you want more activity, this isn’t for you.

If you want a system that holds as you grow, it might be.
