If the Number Keeps Moving, Somebody’s Scared

You know that feeling when the number shifts again.
 Nothing huge.
 Just enough to make you think, Here we go.

A date slips.
 A deal gets “a little more likely”.
 A maybe turns into a probably because someone wants the call to go smoothly.

Founders spot this before anyone else.
 You hear the words, but you feel the wobble underneath.

Here’s the truth nobody names.
 When numbers keep moving, somebody is scared.

Scared of letting you down.
 Scared of the look on your face.
 Scared of being the one who brings bad news.
 Scared of saying, I don’t know, when they think they should know.

So they tidy the update.
 They polish the risk.
 They hope the deal will firm up before you ask again.

And the forecast starts drifting like a boat with no anchor.
This isn’t a performance problem.
 It’s a safety problem.

Drop the fear, and the number stops dancing around.
 People get honest.
 You get the truth while you can still act on it.
 Your week gets calmer.
 Your forecast stops acting like guesswork.

If the number won’t stay still, it’s not the deal that’s unstable.
 It’s the room.

Try this in your next meeting.
 Look them in the eye and ask,
 What feels shaky here, and what help would make it easier to say that sooner?

Be sincere. Do react defensively. You’ll hear the truth.
 Not the tidied version.
 The real one.
And the number will finally sit still.
