The investor diligences the company. We diligence the capital.

Founder Capital Decision Safety helps founders, management teams and boards understand what institutional capital will do before the consequences become binding.

We examine the transaction from the position you will be living in after the money arrives. That includes the parts people usually discuss separately: ownership, control, debt, preferences, fees, future dilution, board rights, founder authority, data access, removal, rollover and what you actually receive in a disappointing exit.

A strong valuation does not answer those questions.
Neither does being told the terms are “market standard”.

The real question is whether this capital will do the job you are hiring it to do without creating consequences you cannot afford, reverse or control.
