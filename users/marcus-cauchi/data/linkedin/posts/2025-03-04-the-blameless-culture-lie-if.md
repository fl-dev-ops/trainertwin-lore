📍 The Blameless Culture Lie
(If Nobody Owns the Problem, Nobody Can Solve It)

No one wants toxic blame culture.

But the opposite extreme? It’s just as dangerous.
🔹 If nobody is accountable, failures repeat.
🔹 If nobody owns mistakes, learning never happens.
🔹 If every issue is “nobody’s fault,” the best people leave, because they want to win.

📌 Where is “blameless culture” being used as an excuse for no accountability?
📌 Where is silence on mistakes costing you credibility and trust?
📌 Where would radical ownership fix systemic failures instantly?

Sellers who dominate and leaders who build high-trust teams don’t fear accountability - they demand it.
