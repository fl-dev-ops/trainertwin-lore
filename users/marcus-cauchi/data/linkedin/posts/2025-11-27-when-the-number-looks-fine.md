When the number looks fine but your gut tightens.
Every founder knows that moment.
That happens because people polish the story when the room feels unsafe.

They hold the wobble back.
 They wait.
 They hope it fixes itself.
 They don’t want to trigger another hard conversation.

So you hear good news for days, then the whole gap drops at once.

The real problem is safety, not skill.
 When people feel exposed, the truth gets delayed.

But when they can say this might slip without bracing, the picture sharpens fast.

A 40 person team I worked with stopped having Friday shocks.
 Updates got quiet.
 Risk showed up early.
 Misses went from constant to rare.
 The founder finally felt the number hold its shape.

Try this next week.
 Open the meeting with one line.
 What feels at risk, and what would make it easier?

You’ll hear the truth sooner.
 And the forecast will settle.
