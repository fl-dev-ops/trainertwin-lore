Founders don’t fail because the market beats them.
They fail because they scale the cracks.

Capital doesn’t fix broken systems. It multiplies them. If your engine leaks trust, stability, or clarity, a term sheet won’t save you, it’ll expose you faster.

* Pressure ≠ progress. 
* Retention ≠ loyalty. 
* Shifting targets ≠ high standards. 

These are the founder delusions that kill companies. And funding makes them lethal.

The uncomfortable truth: most unicorns are illusions. For every headline, there are hundreds of corpses nobody writes about. The graveyard is full of founders who confused valuation with value, and investment with progress, scaling for valuation with having a life.

This piece is your mirror. If you can’t answer three simple questions about trust, stability, and progress, you’re not ready for fuel. You’re holding lighter fluid and a match.

👉 Read the article. Stop scaling the cracks. Build something that lasts.
