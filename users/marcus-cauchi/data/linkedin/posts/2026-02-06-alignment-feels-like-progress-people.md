Alignment feels like progress.

People agree.
Meetings end cleanly.
Next steps get written down.

From the outside, it looks like the hard part is over.

Inside, less changes than it appears.

Trade offs are still unresolved.
Risk is still present.
It has just stopped being spoken out loud.

Alignment creates confidence.

That unfinished work usually shows up as friction.

So when everything feels smooth,
no one is quite sure what is still unfinished.

Later, when things feel riskier than expected,
it is described as a surprise.
