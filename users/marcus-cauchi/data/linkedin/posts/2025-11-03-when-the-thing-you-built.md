When the Thing You Built Starts Running You ...
If you ever built something you love that quietly started owning you, this is for you.

I work with founders who feel the way I used to: tired, proud, and quietly wondering if the freedom we chased even exists anymore.

We built companies to give us space. Instead, they took all of it.

Every problem still finds its way back to our phones.
Every decision waits for our thumbs-up. Even when the team’s good, we don’t quite trust the system to run smoothly without us. You feel it at 11 p.m., replying to messages you shouldn’t need to see.

That’s what I help untangle - not the people, the wiring beneath. Because most founders aren’t broken.
We’re just running on the same survival code that got us here. It worked … until it didn’t.

My work’s about unwinding that code ... making the business safe enough to run without our grip on its throat.
Getting back to a place where growth feels light again and leadership feels human.

If that sounds familiar, let’s design the fix together.
No pitch. No pretence.
Just a calm look at how to stop the business from eating the life it was meant to fund.
