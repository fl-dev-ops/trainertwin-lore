Cian Mcloughlin is right. The old SaaS growth model is fading.

Scale no longer signals safety. Feature breadth no longer differentiates. Having AI no longer impresses.

Clarity matters more. But something deeper is happening. Authority is built in public. Deals are decided in private.

In complex B2B, the real sale happens when you are not in the room. Your champion forwards your deck. Finance rebuilds your ROI model. Procurement dissects your pricing. A sceptical stakeholder asks what happens if this fails.

That is where authority is truly tested.

In this market, vendors don't win because they're bigger. They win because they design decision safety.

Your narrative must travel without you.
Your numbers must survive hostile scrutiny.
Risk reduction must be explicit.
Implementation certainty must be credible before signature.

If your case collapses under internal pressure, you were never in the deal. Clarity opens the door. Commercial proof secures the decision. The companies that design for internal conviction will adapt.

The rest will confuse visibility with influence.
