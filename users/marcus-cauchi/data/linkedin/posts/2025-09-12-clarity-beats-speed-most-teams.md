Clarity beats speed.

Most teams chase speed. Then they drown in rework.
One clear “done” rule saves hours of debate.
It stops the endless “is this finished yet?” loop.

* Write the outcome in one line.
* Share it where everyone can see.
* Hold to it, every time.

Action: Write one “done” rule today.
