Tests, Not Objections
Most objections aren’t a “no.” They’re a check.
Buyers want to know if it’s safe to be honest.
They give you a small concern and watch what you do with it.
Pass it, they open up.
Fail it, they stay closed.
Which “objection” is actually a test?
