Some of the most dangerous failures look like progress at first.

The forecast is accepted, the AI pilot is scaled, the value-creation plan is approved and the new executive takes the job.

Nothing appears broken. The numbers move, the board is reassured and execution begins. The problem only becomes visible later, when the assumptions fail and someone else is holding the cost.

By then, the original decision has usually disappeared behind targets, reporting and a familiar explanation: poor execution.

This article shows how the same hidden break appears across four decisions senior leaders are expected to trust.

The examples are different. The mechanism underneath them is the same.
Start the review at the decision, not where the damage finally appeared.
