The most expensive work in your GTM system is usually the work that should never have existed.
 
Bad targeting becomes sales effort. Weak qualification becomes forecast noise. Poor handoffs become customer rescue. GTM waste rarely disappears where it starts. It travels downstream, changes name, and becomes someone else’s workload.
