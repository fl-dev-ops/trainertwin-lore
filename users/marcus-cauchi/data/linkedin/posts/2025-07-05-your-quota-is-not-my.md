"Your quota is not my problem", said every buyer on the receiving end of another terrible cold call, crap email or dreadful Loom video.

Salespeople, please get your act together. The people you say you want to talk to are busy and don't have time for your spray and pray, noisy irrelevant interruptions. I make no bones about it, cold calling done the way you do it is a waste of their time, your time and the results speak volumes.

If you were one of timely, relevant or valuable it would be a bonus but you are misisng the mark on all 3.

Why should your prospect have to pay the price for your failure to do your job 3 months ago?

You could have been building Revenue Generating Relationships #RGR's that last a lifetime. You could have been doing your research and found ways to be valuable to them. You could have identified their latent pain, or identified a problem they weren't aware they had and helped them understand it, find the clues they are missing and empower them to take meaningful action to fix it.

Instead you spam them, pitch them, keep pestering them until they take the meeting and then prove their original resistance was completely justified. Then they are looking for ways to get you off the phone.

And if you are a manager of these people, SHAME ON YOU. 

The role is hard enough without a clown at the helm telling them to "Make more dials. Sales is a numbers game. More at the top, more at the bottom".

That is a BIG FAT LIE.

It fills the top of the funnel with crap. Which then constipates the middle of the funnel, and then the seller is left with a pipeline full of maybes, think it overs, and send me somethings. 

Then they need an exorcist to shove a laxative down the demon pipeline's throat to unblock it ... but that rarely happens,. Instead they pile on the pressure and repeat the same acts of idiocy and abuse of their people by PIPping them or firing them.

Time to aks better questions.

If you're having to make 1000 dials to get 40 conversations and 3 result in a proposal and 1 eventually buys at the end of quarter at a discount, could it be that THE SYSTEM IS BROKEN?

I grant you, the quality of salespeople is not stellar by any stretch of the imagination. But then I have to ask, did you hire them that way or make them that way?

If you want to ask better questions, let's start with, is a 99.9% failure to achieve the intended outcome a problem with your people or you management?

DM me if you realise that you and your business model might be the cause of your woes, and the poor suckers working for you are victims of systemic rot.
