This Le Creuset Tea Pot with home made jumper represents the Checkout E-Comm team at B&Q/ Kingfisher back in 2018.

Whoever arrived at the office first filled it and let it brew. Jug of milk aside. Every so often, someone might bring in some biscuits. A rare treat. A break and a chat would ensue.

We could lure in and bond with other teams. Offering them a cuppa.

We were hugely successful. Adding ~£2.8million/ month in revenue.

None of that happened during tea time. But how much of it was because of it?
