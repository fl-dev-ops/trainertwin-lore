"No one wants to work in Telecomms. Some grow to love it."

That's what she said to me. Two years ago. In an interview.

I'd just told her I didn't want to be a Product Manager. Or work in Telecomms. She didn't try to talk me out of it. She just said: "I don't need a Product Manager. I need a leader and a coach". 

That was the job. Leading a team through a complete change of operating model - old way out, new way in, no shortcuts. To a better future.

I took it.

Twelve months later, the operating model, and the team was transformed. New model. New way of working. New belief in what they were capable of. And the work that was left... was the job I'd said I didn't want on day one... Product Management. In Telecomms.

So I left. Clean break. No drama, no unravelling - just two honest people, being honest again. She'd told me the truth about what she needed. I told her the truth about what I could still give. When that ran out... We both already knew.

Most exits are messy because someone stopped being honest a long time before the leaving.

Where have you stayed quiet - when honesty would've saved so much pain?
