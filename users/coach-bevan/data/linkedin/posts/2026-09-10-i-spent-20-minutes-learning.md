I spent 20 minutes learning how much neck to show if you want to look confident.

Apparently, the gap between your chin and your chest matters. Too little neck and you look closed. Show more and you appear confident. Too much and it's obvious you're trying too hard.

There are a lot of videos like this. Stand taller. Hold eye contact. Lower your voice. Fake it till you make it.

It amused me how long I spent watching them. But it also made me think about how much effort we put into performing confidence instead of becoming confident.

Real confidence doesn't come from learning the right pose. It comes from knowing yourself.

When you understand who you are, what you value and why you do what you do, you become more authentic. Other people's opinions lose some of their power over you.

Someone can say, "I don't like what you do."
And you can say, "Yeah. That's fine."

You can still listen. You can decide the feedback is useful and change something. Or you can decide it isn't useful and do nothing.

The difference is that you get to choose. You aren't changing automatically because you need their approval.

Confidence isn't never caring what people think. It's not needing their validation to know what you think.

How much of your confidence comes from knowing yourself, and how much comes from trying to look the part?
