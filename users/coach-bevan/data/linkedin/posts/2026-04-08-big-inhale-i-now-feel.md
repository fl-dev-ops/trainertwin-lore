Big inhale... I now feel ok calling myself a Life Coach. 
Big exhale.

Personal growth. Taken a while.

I've struggled with the term. Correcting everyone. Don't call me that... "no, I'm a, err... Leadership/ Executive/ whatever - coach".

I feel uncomfortable with the image "life coaching" brings to mind. Someone that did a weekend course. Dishing out advice. Meddling in personal matters. Adding little value. All while maintaining an air of ego and superiority.

Fills me with ick.

But truthfully... I am a life coach. I help transform peoples lives. Powerfully. Whilst giving zero advice. Holding space. Skillfully. Maintaining a partnership of equality and love.
