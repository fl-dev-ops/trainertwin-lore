At 8pm, I was swimming and trying to work out why 2 teams couldn’t work together.

One built the API. The other built the front end. And somewhere between them, communication had broken down.

So while swimming, I was planning the conversation.
How could I bring them together? How could I keep their egos out of it? How could I make sure they continued talking after I walked away?

The swimming pool was where I did a lot of my thinking.

I’d stare at the bottom of the pool. The view is dull. The water blocked out most of the noise, and the boredom gets my brain going.

I aimed for 64 lengths, which is a mile. I’d usually lose count.

Maybe I swam 66. Maybe 70. Probably 56 on the days I thought I’d missed a few and took some off.

I told myself this mental load was what they paid me for. Why I was worth paying a six-figure salary. 

So somewhere along the way, I decided that the extra money bought the company access to my brain after 5pm.

I think a lot of senior leaders make the same deal with themselves.
The salary becomes something they feel they need to justify.

Where do you complete your extra curricular problem solving?
