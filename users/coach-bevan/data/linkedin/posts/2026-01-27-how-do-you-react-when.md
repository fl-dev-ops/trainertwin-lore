How do you react when encountering a stressor?

Focus. Narrow vision. Black and white. I (like most of us) tend to focus on the stressor with singular vision. I want to remove the stressor as quickly as possible.

But I have learnt. With time. If I can process the energy that the stressor triggered within me. Take personal responsibility for that energy. Bring awareness to it. Be heard and validated. Seen by another kind human.

Then I have the opportunity to view the situation with a wider lens. Consider more options. Better options. To show up, how I want to show up. In alignment with my values.

Core energy coaching has taught me to pass the energy. And to widen my lens.
