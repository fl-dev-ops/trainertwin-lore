We avoid anger. But maybe we need to feel it?

Progress isn't always linear.

Core energy coaching covers 7 levels of energy. 1 is low. 7 is high. Most focus on shifting Catabolic energy (L1/ L2) out of the way. Shifting up.

In my experience L3 is the most dangerous type of energy. L3 is about achieving a personal win. At any cost. When a TRUE WIN isn't possible, at L3 a win will be found through self-manipulation.

What does this look like?

You may hear yourself thinking, "well I didn't want to do it that much anyway", "maybe next year" or "this is good enough". Putting aside what you really want and making do with what you've got. Rationalising it.

It makes sense. It's logical. Reasonable. But what is the real cost to you?

Left unchecked, this type of thinking can cost a lifetime of mediocrity, unfulfillment, and dreams that never happened.

Traditional attempts to shift/ raise the energy will have little impact with someone who has settled for good enough. To progress, the energy must actually shift down a gear; towards anger (L2), or despair (L1).

It isn't pleasant. But it creates movement. Fire-in-the-belly.
