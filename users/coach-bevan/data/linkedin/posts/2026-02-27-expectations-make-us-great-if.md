Expectations make us great!

If we use them right!

They don't need to be true. Not right now. So long as we believe they are possible. Repeating them as a mantra gives voice to our song.

Repeat daily. Repetition tells our whole body what is going to happen. A holistic approach.

How can expectations help a transition?

Expectations for you, and whoever you work for are critical. How much will you reasonably contribute in 3 months? 12 months?

I set myself very relaxed 3 month expectations. Just build foundations. I set myself high expectations for the first year.

We MUST believe the expectations we set ourselves. With our whole body: Heat, heart, gut, even toes. If we do, they'll come true. Self fulfilling. 

If we don't. They work against us. The anxiety can prevent our natural qualities from shining. Both are self fulfilling.

What expectations have you set yourself? To what extent do you believe them? Do you give song to them?
