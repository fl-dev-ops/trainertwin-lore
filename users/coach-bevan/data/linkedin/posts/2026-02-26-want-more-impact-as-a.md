Want more impact as a leader?

Create space for your team.

They need space to get s*** (stuff) done. The less you, or anybody else bothers them... the better.

Rules:
1) All meetings must be of value to all attendees.
2) Set an agenda, so people can decide if they want to attend.
3) Reiterate the agenda at the start and encourage people to leave.
4) Achieve the goal of the meeting (set out in the agenda) as quickly as possible.
5) At the end, ask:
     a) what value was gained from the meeting
     b) whether it was worth the time investment
     c) whether that value could be achieved more efficiently in future.
