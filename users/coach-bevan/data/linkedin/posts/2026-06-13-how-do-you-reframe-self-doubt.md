How do you reframe self-doubt when stepping into high-stakes rooms?

Imposter syndrome shifts the burden onto the individual, telling you that you are the problem that needs fixing, rather than the environment around you. Your feelings of self-doubt are valid, but they are often the byproduct of a system designed to make you feel like you don't belong.😞

The next time you feel like an imposter, remember: it’s not a personal defect. It’s a strategy. Hold your ground and keep your seat at the table.📌
