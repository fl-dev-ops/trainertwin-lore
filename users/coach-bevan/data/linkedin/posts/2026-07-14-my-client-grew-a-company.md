My client grew a company to 80 people. She told me she felt like a mouse.

Years earlier she'd taken on regulators and won. People described her as a lioness, and some were a little afraid of her, in the way you're wary of someone who is very good at what they do.

Then she moved into a much larger organisation, into a role surrounded by people she assumed were cleverer than her. Her confidence drained away. She started taking all her work home, saying yes to everything, shrinking in rooms she used to own. She genuinely believed people who knew her before wouldn't recognise her now.

Nothing about her ability had changed. She was exactly as sharp as she'd always been. What had changed was that she'd lost contact with it.
We spent months on this. Slowly the voice telling her everyone else was better got quieter. She started saying no. She made space for her own work. She stepped back into herself.

Then she applied for the chair of the organisation. She got it.
The lioness was never gone. She'd just stopped being able to hear her.
