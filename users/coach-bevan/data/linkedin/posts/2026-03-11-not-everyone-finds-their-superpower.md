Not everyone finds their superpower.

Everyone has one, of that I'm sure. 

Many people don't receive the support needed from family, and they definitely won't get it from the state!

Some are supported in self-exploration whilst young. Some gifts are just plain obvious... For those lucky few. Finding their gift during childhood results in aligned education and profession, effortless motivation and financial reward.

For the rest of us. The search for our gifts goes on into adulthood. Whilst sustaining an income.

This is hard. Many settle. Find good enough. Something they can tolerate. It is understandable.

I felt like this. Finding joy in family and accepting my work. But something stuck with me: "My children will follow my example", is this good enough for them?

That anger fuelled me. For a while. To keep searching. To do things I enjoy. To explore myself. Take risks. Follow my intuition. To be a role model that I can live with.

Now I don't feel angry. I feel passion. Joy. My energy had to go down before it came up. It feels good now. Sustainable.

Get in touch if you too aren't prepared to settle. I'd love to help ❤️😃
