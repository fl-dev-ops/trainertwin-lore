What are toddlers today

Really being subjected to?

The contrast is clear

Cutting every few seconds
Highly saturated colours
Constant background motion
Layers of audio everywhere
Elevated emotional intensity
The sheer speed and pace of keeping up

The difference is stark

Now it’s not a binary old is good, new is bad situation 

There’s individual differences in children. How long they are viewing and are they with parents. The context matters. And there are modern cartoons that are closer to Pooh than Cocomelon in how they play.

And at a systemic level, streaming and platform incentives push this. Autoplay pushes it further. Monetisation drives design. Children’s development pales in comparison.

I watch the below and see a cartoon designed to hold a child’s attention

And then I see one designed to prevent attention ever leaving

That’s why Festival of the Boy will be about slowing down and exploring.

Its much easier to explore when everything stops running.

Male Allies UK is proud to be partnering on creating that space for young boys between 6 and 11 that priorities play and celebration.

I wish we could say the same about everything else
