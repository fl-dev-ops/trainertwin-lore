The most important metric is one you probably aren't tracking.

For each project you're responsible for, when did you last actually intervene - make the call yourself, take the meeting, rewrite the thing?

Try writing it down. Project one, last stepped in on the 25th. 
Project two, not since June. 
Project three, last week. It takes two minutes and it tells you something no delivery metric does.

Because the honest reading of that list isn't about the team. It's about you. The projects you're constantly stepping into aren't necessarily the weakest - they're often the ones you trust least, or the ones you haven't fully let go of.

Early on, being needed on everything felt like doing my job well. It took me a while to see intervention frequency for what it really is: a measure of how much I trusted the people around me, and how much space I was actually willing to give them.

So the number to watch isn't how much you did. It's how often you had to. When did you last step in on something your team could have handled alone?
