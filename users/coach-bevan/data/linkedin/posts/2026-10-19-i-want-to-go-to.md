I want to go to bed early. I’d be so fresh, alive and capable if I do… but I can’t. 

A client said this to me in a coaching session. It’s simple... Right??? 

Set the goal. Set the KPIs. Get to bed by 10pm 80% of the time. Track it. And adjust… 

But it doesn’t work. I know it doesn’t. Because it doesn’t address the inner world. The well-trodden neural pathways of the person sitting in front of me. From her lifetime of experience. 

This isn’t the first time I’ve worked on bedtime. It’s common in leadership performance. It affects energy, concentration, motivation and more. She couldn’t be the person she wanted to be. And this felt important to her. 

What was going on? 

Her belief system and energy was keeping her stuck. She needed to get out of her own way. 

Belief 1: it’s boring to go to bed early. I don’t want to be boring. 

Tied to her self-image. Did she believe this??? No. But deep down inside, part of her did. We spoke to that part. Make it feel heard, seen. Release the energy it had been holding since her teens. Not to belittle it. To understand it. Then… deep breath out, shoulders sank, relaxed. The energy released. 

New belief: An early bedtime charges me for all the cool stuff I’m going to do tomorrow.

This made space for the next energetic blocker to emerge… 

Belief 2: If I get more done today then I’ll have less to do tomorrow. 

Did she believe this??? Somewhere she did. Deep down. But it didn’t sound like her voice. It sounded like her mum. A fear passed down. Well intentioned. Keeping her up. Preventing sleep. And she did those tasks so slowly, inefficiently, when tired. She took 3x longer than she needed to. 

Did she want to keep the belief? It’s a choice. But… “no, thank you, it doesn’t sound or feel like me”. So choose a new one. It’s only possible if the old energy is fully released. And it was. 

New belief: These tasks will be a doddle for well rested me, in the morning. 

That’s it. Freedom to choose her bedtime. Free from her own mind. For some there might be more. But this was what it took. And don’t get me started on bedtime scrolling! 

Do you go to bed when you want to? What gets in your way?
