I've been hoping I'd magically develop restraint! Especially with the chips. Why are the chips sooooo good?

For years I went on like this. With hope.

Hoping I'd wake up one day and just... have the self control... And sometimes I did. For a bit. But when I was hungry. I never did.

Same fridge. Same 9pm cupboard raid. Same promise to "start Manana."

Then it hit me. Hope isn't a strategy. It's just doing the same thing and expecting a different result. That's not optimism. That's the definition of insanity.

So I'm done hoping. I've booked in with a specialist. Someone who actually knows what they're doing - not someone winging it with willpower at 9pm.

Because the thing I needed wasn't more hope. It was a different approach. So thanks May Cheung Lic. Ac., MAcS, MFHT, CNHC Reg for your support. And your extraordinary interest in my tongue!

Where in your life are you still hoping, when what you actually need is help?
