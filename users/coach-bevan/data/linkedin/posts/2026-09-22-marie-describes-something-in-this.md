Marie describes something in this video that I really like.

The problems she brings to coaching have changed.

When we first started working together, she felt small and overwhelmed. She knew she wanted things to be different, but there was a lot going on in her head.

We’d take one of those problems and slow it down.
What actually happened?
What had she assumed?
Why had that particular thing bothered her so much?

Sometimes the first answer was useful. Quite often we had to keep going.
And over time, Marie started doing more of that thinking herself.

She understands herself better now. She trusts her own judgement more. And the things that used to take up a huge amount of space in her head don’t seem to have the same grip.

Her problems haven’t disappeared. They’ve changed as she has.
I like that.

I still get coached myself, and I suspect I always will. There’s usually another layer to understand, another habit you haven’t noticed yet, or simply a new problem created by the next stage of your life.

Marie explains her experience far better than I could.
Here she is.
