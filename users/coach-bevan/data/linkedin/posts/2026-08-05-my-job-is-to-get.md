My job is to get you to stop needing me as fast as possible. 
Most of the coaching industry is built to do the exact opposite. 

The whole model is quietly built the wrong way round. The longer you stay, the more they earn. Which means the incentive is to keep you needing them. Nobody says this out loud but that doesn't make it less true. 
My job is the opposite. My entire aim is to become unnecessary to you as fast as possible. The best outcome I can point to isn't a client who's still with me 2 years on. It's one who did the work, got what they came for, and doesn't need me anymore.

One client I worked with put it perfectly near the end. “I don't think I need you now.” That was the win. That was the whole point.
Be a bit wary of anyone whose success depends on your dependence. In coaching, therapy, consulting, any of it.

Am I wrong to think redundancy is the goal?
