Are you maximising EFFICIENCY or CREATIVITY?

Many businesses maximise efficiency. Systems are lean. Repeatable. No need for independent thought. Efficient. There are many environments perfectly suited for efficiency.

Very few businesses maximise creativity. Creativity requires space. Trust. Bravery. Do less. Focus on what you are great at. Introspection. Self knowledge. Not every bet pays out. But the ones that do are game changers. Real competitive edge.

In my experience, business want both. Nirvana. Efficient creativity...

But achieve neither. Those birds can't be got with one stone. They're on different continents. Innovation is stunted. No one can work out why... "we have a creative culture, but we don't create enough". Efficiency plummets too. Leadership take on the task of innovation.

What have you seen. Have you seen the holy grail of efficient creativity? I'd love to learn more!
