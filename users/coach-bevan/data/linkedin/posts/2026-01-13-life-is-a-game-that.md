Life is a game that cannot be won or lost, only played.

Rick and Morty taught me this principle long before IPEC reiterated it so beautifully. But I often need a reminder. I get stuck. Thinking about what I need to do. Or should do. I forget that it's just a game. We all have choice. We can play the game however we choose to.

How do you play your game? Which rules suit you? Which will you rewrite?
