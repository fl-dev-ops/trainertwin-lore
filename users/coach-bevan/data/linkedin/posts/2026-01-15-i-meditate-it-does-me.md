I meditate. It does me good. It is training. Like lifting at the gym (or down the park) strengthens my body. 

The goal in meditation is not to avoid thinking... But to notice things. Notice thoughts. Notice breath. Notice the body's natural movements. Notice sounds.  Notice physical feelings. Notice emotional feelings. Notice where my energy is most active. How it moves.

Awareness is the goal. The awareness gradually increases. If you train often.

As a by-product of meditation. I learn to calm myself through breath. I learn to be still. I learn to be present.

Meditation is a very cool practice.
