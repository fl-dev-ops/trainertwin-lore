Full of Doom and Gloom?

This planet is filled with wealth. So many forms. I am grateful. Can you see it all?

 - Financial
 - Knowledge 
 - Genetic
 - Love
 - Life

We have so much of each of these. Maybe we could share a little better. But we are so lucky to be here, now, in a world filled with wealth.

I say my gratitude every day to remind me the world is not full of doom and gloom. We are lucky for what we do have.

What wealth are you grateful for?
