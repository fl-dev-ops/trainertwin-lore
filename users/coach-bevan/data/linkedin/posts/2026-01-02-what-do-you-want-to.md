What do you want to do with your life?...

Awful question. Intimidating. Too big. How can I commit to something with such a large timeframe. I’m anxious that I will get it wrong. Waste time doing something that isn’t my purpose.

Rewind: I wanted purpose in my life so badly. I wanted “what I was doing” to have a meaning that resonated deeply within. But this awful question. Made me avoid thinking about purpose. Living without. Drifting.

But some wise person (my wife Hanaa Skalli) told me that purpose doesn't have to be for forever. It can be just for now. This revelation changed everything!

I LOVE THAT!

What do I care about changing right now? I'll pursue that for as long as it feels relevant. Maybe my whole life? Maybe not. It doesn't matter. I'm not trapped by it. It feels manageable. Allows for twists and turns. Unexpected revelations. New directions. It is fluid. Agile. Yet meaningful. I can change my purpose. Find new meaning. 

Back to the present… Now I can pursue purpose. I can live with meaning. I don’t need to worry about finding a purpose that will last my whole life. My picture. My current vision for the future. Is just the right amount BIG.

What excites me right now?
What makes me angry right now?
When do I feel passionate right now?

For now. I’m going to make crumble!

My purpose. For the next few days... A full belly and a well-fed family.

I won’t bore you with my 12 month or 3 year goals.  But feel free to DM me. If you’re interested in mine. Or want to explore yours.

Enjoy
