Flow state works. Why do we ignore it?

In sport the teams that win (over the course of a season) are those that spend the most time in flow state. Disrupting your opposition's flow is a known, if unethical, tactic.

Yet we rarely focus on flow in the world of business.

We allow flow to be disrupted by internal systems. Constant interruptions from Teams, Slack, Emails, Meetings. No space.

I have observed the less management I do. The more leadership I do. The more I act as a doorman. Protecting my team's space. Enabling flow... The better they solve problems. And we beat the competition. Like a sports team!
