Product managers rely too much on data to make decisions. 

Data can help. But what are you missing? 

It is a prevailing view of our time that “logic is king”. You can think your way out of any problem. To rely on your emotions, or what you feel in your guy is silly. 

But then we applaud people who act on instinct. Animalistically. With a deeper knowledge of people and society. 

Katniss Everdeen, Wolverine, Buffy the Vampire Slayer! 

We talk about honed business instinct. But it isn’t instinct at all. It’s intuition. 

Instinct is reactive, uncontrollable. Like my friend’s son vomiting the second a date touches his tongue. 
Intuition is felt slowly, and can be followed rationally. 

I fell into the data trap. I loved logic. I could think my way out of any problem. Think Myself to happiness even. Except I couldn’t! 

It worked professionally at times. Especially in eCommerce. I had so much data. And I understood industry data norms. I could analyse for anomalies. I found problems. Like poor checkout conversion. I could dig deeper and learn which points in the checkout had the biggest dropoff. I could see demographic stats for that point. And I could watch user sessions from that point in the journey. 

I could even AB Test solutions to check if I’d come up with any decent solutions. 

I became dependant on data. It was time for my worldview to be shaken up. To learn something new. 

I moved to an eComm company that used very little data. Didn’t really collect it. The owner had been a market trader. Relied on his charm and intuition. It’d took him far. He stuck with it. He kept getting business decisions right. More and more success came his way. In an industry led by data. 

Curious? 

It was then that I stopped hiding behind the data. I spent more time with customers. Remembered the times in my career before eComm. When intuition had guided me. I tried to feel into my gut. Cultivate creativity in my mind. 

I didn’t forget data. But I stopped relying on it. I became better. A more complete Product professional. 

I’d love to hear. What are views on intuition? And how much do you rely on data?
