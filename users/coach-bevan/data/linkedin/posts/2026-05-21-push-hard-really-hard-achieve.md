Push hard. Really hard. Achieve. Collapse. Rest? Hate.

Sound familiar? This is the pattern one of my high achieving clients has been living. "If there is another way then I don't know it".

So much progress. Tied to discipline. Tied to rejection of self. Rejection of body signals. Then the inevitable collapse. The accompanying self loathing. "Stop being lazy". And worse.

"If I let go of the discipline then I won't achieve anything..."

A core belief! 

Is it true? Is the cost worth it? Is there another way?

"I love what I do. I'd do it for free. My motivation is intrinsic."

So why the discipline? Why the hate? "I suppose it's just habit. It's what made me successful. What if I can't be successful without it?"

Do you still need it? What could replace it?

"A middle path. A sustainable pace. Motivation is intrinsic. My destiny is clear. I don't think pushing will get me there any faster. I want to work at it though. I enjoy it. When it's the right amount. When it's not too much."
