Yesterday, I went to a "Sensory Deprivation Tank". My curiosity knows no limits!

It was a temperate enclosed tub. Filled with Salt Water for floatation. Dark. Silent.

For 1 hour I lay and floated. With me.

Distractionless. The first thing I felt was... every ache in my 40 year old body. Every one I'd ignored. The messages to my brain became so loud. I attempted not to distract myself. I focussed on my breath. And presence. The sensations my body was experiencing. Especially my neck. And shoulders. 

When I didn't try to avoid it. Felt into it. I realised. They weren't so bad. There was some discomfort. But not pain. The pain was emotional. Wrapped up in my avoidance.

Around 30mins in (I'd guess). As my body gradually released the tension. I fell asleep. I awoke a few minutes from the end. Shortly before the expected, gentle "5 mins remaining" music came on.

I showered the salt water off. My hair took that cool surf look I get when I've been in the sea. And I went home.

I can't say specifically what. But I left something heavy in the tank. Emotional. It didn't come home with me. I am lighter. A little more healed.

I think I'll go back and try it again.
