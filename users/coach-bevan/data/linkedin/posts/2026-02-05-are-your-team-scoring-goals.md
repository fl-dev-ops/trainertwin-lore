Are your team scoring goals in the game of business?

Leadership is creating rules for everyone else.

This can be either... Directly, by the processes you put in place. Or indirectly, by the way you behave. The permission you give.

You choose how much autonomy or control to give your team. You choose what goals to aim for. You choose how the employees can try to score those goals.

What rules are working for you? Which aren't?
