Do you know who you are? 

Really? At your core? 🤨

You are a good person. Values drive you. How connected to your values are you? How often do you make a decision that doesn't feel like you?

I know I have. I look back at some of my life choices with confusion. Good ones. And bad ones. Not regret. They all led me to today. But confusion.

I was so disconnected from myself. I don't know why I made the bad choices. I don't know how I made the good ones.

I prefer now. I don't feel confused. I feel certain. I "mostly" make choices aligned with my values 😉 They may not make sense to everyone else. But they make sense to me. 

I love that. I feel a deep sense of confidence. Even if it doesn't work out... I am doing the right thing. By me.

If you've lost confidence, and want to reconnect with your inner strength. Get in touch. Values could be the heart of the issue.
