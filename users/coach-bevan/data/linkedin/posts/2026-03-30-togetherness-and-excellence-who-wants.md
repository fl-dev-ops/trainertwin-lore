Togetherness and excellence. Who wants that?

Why do so many miss out???    

CULTURE feels silly 

Imagine: 
You suggest to your management team that you all try something new. Well. It's Monday. What could go wrong. A cheeky little morning Haka together. To kickstart the meeting.

Ok, we're in Western Europe. It's not our culture. Our historic culture is most lost. Maybe try some team Morris dancing instead? 

Our culture is lost. In favor of business efficiency. If we want culture - and based on the All Black success - I'd suggest we do... we must build a new culture.

What might that look like?

Ceremony? Something Karl could buy into? Time spent that doesn't directly produce owt. Only intangible value.

A Western leader that tries to embed culture into their organisation is a brave leader. They may face laughter. "What is the point in this". People won't get it... At first

But over time. If you sell it well. Make your people understand the value. They buy into it. What power you will hold.

A workforce that intrinsically values excellence and togetherness. Playing at the top of their game. Seems worth it to me.
