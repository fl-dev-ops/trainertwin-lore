One of my clients described coaching with me as "a little bit woo woo". And then recommended it anyway.

That's Ivana Galic. She came to coaching because, in her words, she had "gremlins in my head".

This is the bit she wrote that I like most.

"There wasn't a recipe to how you do it or a clear structure. We just explored those gremlins."

Which is fair. I don't turn up with a recipe. I ask questions, and she did the digging. She put it better than I would. Someone "that will ask you deeper questions than you ask yourself."

She also wrote about personal stuff that seeps into business. I think that's often where it is. The obvious problem sits on top, and the one doing the damage is underneath.

Thanks Ivana. I'll take woo woo.

If you've got a gremlin or two of your own, message me with the problem as it is. It doesn't need to be tidy.
