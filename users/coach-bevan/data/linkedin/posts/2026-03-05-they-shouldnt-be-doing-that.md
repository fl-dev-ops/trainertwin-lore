Self talk takes many forms. Often the voice is on your side. Looking out for you. Concerned.

People with the same information rarely have a different opinion. Disagreements are great... They allow you to learn what set of different information each of you has. 

Curiosity... 
What am I missing? 
What do I know that I haven't shared? 
What else could be at play here?

But that little protective voice can get in the way. Stifle your natural curiosity. Or another fearful voice may prevent you from asking. Admitting you don't yet know something can feel exposing.

If you can quieten those voices. You can ask, discuss and learn. Collaborate. Be the best version of yourself. The real you.
