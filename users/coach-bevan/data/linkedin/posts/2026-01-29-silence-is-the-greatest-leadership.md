Silence is the greatest leadership skill. Fewer words gives greater power to each uttered.

Many people struggle to be silent. Demonstrating their value through insight or action.

Truly great leaders don't need to demonstrate their value. It is obvious through results.

Silence gives others the space to think. To solve. To innovate. It shows respect. It says "I know you will solve this without me".
