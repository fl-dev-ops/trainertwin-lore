I lead a team, and I've got this! Said confidently. At every stakeholder call ever. 

But what if you don't got this? What then? You are paid to "got this". You'd better got it! 😂😭

We talk about leadership being about vulnerability. Showing strength through that. 

At update meetings though. Why does my intuition tingle uncomfortably? Does everyone really "got this"? Do we have honesty?

No one says... "It's not going well. And I don't know what to do about it". Total vulnerability. Too much vulnerability for work? What would happen if they did?
