A few years ago I set a goal to play my sport (Inline Hockey) for my country (Great Britain) at veteran level (over 38s) because I'm old 😁

I trained. Went to trial. Didn't get in.

I was a bit sad. For a short while. Being honest. I didn't put everything I could into preparation. It wasn't that important. 

I was confused.

If it had really mattered to me, wouldn’t I have been crushed? Wouldn’t I have done absolutely everything possible to prepare?

The truth is: I hadn’t.

I trained enough to say I was serious. But not enough to sacrifice for it.

Sometimes we chase goals because they sound meaningful, impressive, or “right” — not because we deeply want them.

Did I train harder and come back next year? Course not - I set better goals - that mattered to me. I grew.
