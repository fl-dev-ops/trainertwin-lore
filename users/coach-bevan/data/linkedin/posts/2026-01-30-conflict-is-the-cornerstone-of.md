Conflict is the cornerstone of alignment.

People only disagree when basing their opinion upon differing sets of information.

Discussion to draw out the information each is missing is super valuable. Once complete, is it rare they will hold different opinions. 

If everyone understood this. We could enjoy the conflict. Without the anger and the uncomfortable heat conflict can bring. We could align through enjoyable conflict. What a world that would be.

I love conflict. I love a challenge. I don't feel anger. I just feel curious. Why do they think that? What am I missing here? What do I know, that I haven't shared?
