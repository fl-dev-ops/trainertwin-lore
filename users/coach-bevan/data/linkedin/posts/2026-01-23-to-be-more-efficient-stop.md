To be more efficient. Stop focusing on efficiency.

Start focussing on clutter!

Clutter gets in your way. It slows you down. It makes you less efficient.

It could be physical clutter. A messy desk. A pile of books that you no longer need. Christmas presents that you don't use but are nervous to throw away.

It could be mental clutter. An unfinished personal project. A todo list that never ends. Emotion that you've bottled up and held onto for too long.

So whatever your clutter. In the name of efficiency. Let's declutter today!
