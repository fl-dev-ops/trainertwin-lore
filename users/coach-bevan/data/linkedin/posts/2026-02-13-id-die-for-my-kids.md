"I'd die for my kids" - but would you live your dreams for them?

This thought stuck with me. Our kids don't do what we say (well mine doesn't). They do what we do. We teach them how to be human in this world as role models.

I want my son to live his dreams. That means I need to live mine!

I don't want my son to settle for ok. So I can't!

No waiting around. A boost of pure unselfish motivation, to (selfishly???) do what I have always wanted to do!

Would you live your dreams... for your kids?
