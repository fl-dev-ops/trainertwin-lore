A few years ago I felt stuck on a pathway that I'd trodden many times, but didn't take me where I wanted to go.

Those neural pathways kept me trapped. I kept me trapped. I had options. Loads of options. But I couldn't see them.

I am a thinker. Sometimes an over-thinker. I'm analytical. At work, I can think my way out of any problem. So I tried to think more. And I got more stuck.

Eventually I thought myself into such a hole that I couldn't see any way out. At that point I heard something new. It wasn't my head. It didn't sound like a voice. Not like thoughts do.

For the first time in a long time. I heard my gut. My intuition. I wasn't thinking. I was sensing. And I sensed what to do next. And because I couldn't continue. I took a new pathway.
