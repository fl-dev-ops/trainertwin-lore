There's one question I'd stop asking in your 11 meetings. 
“Everything okay?”

You already know the answer you'll get. “Yeah, all good.” Nobody unloads their real worries in response to a question that's basically asking for reassurance.

Try a different one. “What are you pretending isn't a problem yet?”

It gets you a completely different conversation. The workload they can technically still handle. The stakeholder slowly becoming impossible. The project everyone quietly knows is slipping. The stuff that's cheap to deal with now and expensive in three months.

The best one-to-ones aren't status updates. They're the place small truths get said early, while they're still small.

What's a question that's changed the way your one-to-ones go? Always looking for better ones.
