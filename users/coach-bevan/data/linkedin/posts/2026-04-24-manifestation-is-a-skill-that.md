Manifestation is a skill that we can develop over time. But not one we are inherently capable. 

This is a gift.

If we could manifest innately. Our every thought. As children, teenagers and young adults (and many older adults). We would not survive the monsters and demons we could create.

But when ready. If we want to. Our skill can grow.
