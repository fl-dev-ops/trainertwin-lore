Working harder is often the most expensive way to stay exactly where you are.

Most stressed leaders are trying to fix an energy problem with more effort. It doesn't work, and it's worth understanding why.

When you're overloaded, every instinct says push harder. Start earlier, stay later, carry more. So you do. And for a while it holds. Then the returns start shrinking, the decisions get slower, and the work you drag home stops actually getting done.

What you're running low on isn't time. It's energy. And energy doesn't come back by spending more of it.

I watched a client live this. She was working every evening and weekend, sure she just needed to grind through the busy patch. The busy patch had lasted two years. When she stopped treating rest as the reward for finishing, and started treating it as the thing that let her finish, her output went up, not down.
Pushing harder feels responsible. Often it's just the most expensive way to stay stuck.

Where in your week are you spending energy you don't actually have?
