Why do innovation departments fail to innovate?

1) Not core to business success

Your innovation department is little more than a side project. A gamble. Not core business. Many ideas come from directors. Both streams (directors and innovation team) can flow into delivery. We all know who's ideas get prioritised.

2) Insufficient passion

You have hired people with a CV filled with innovation successes. These guys know how to innovate!

But you missed something. Any human can innovate. It's a core design feature of homosapiens. Some simple idea validation processes will help. But what really matters is how passionate these innovation people are about your customers problem...

Not how passionate they are about the industry,  tech, or innovation...

Without passionate people, the innovation team will probably be overlooked in favour of HiPPOs.

3) Too much pressure

The money is running out. This innovation team are expensive. Will they really create something viable. Aren't they just clowns. Not real delivery people?

How to motivate them? Same as the delivery guys. Just pile on the pressure and set targets. You'll see them deliver some ideas. Some bad ones. If they are passionate, they won't need you to crack the wip (see problem #2).

Your innovation team get nervous. Job loss is serious. Anyone with innovation skills aligns themselve with the businesses successful departments - the delivery team. Meaning they no longer have time or space for innovation. They are doing "important" work. Delivering minor optimisations. No step change.

What to do? 

Recognise the situation. 
Talk about it openly. 
Form innovation as a core business function.
Hire passionate people.
Ignore innovation people.
Trust them - give time and space.
