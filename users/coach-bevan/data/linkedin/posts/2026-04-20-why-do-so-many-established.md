Why do so many established tech businesses fail to innovate?

Someone had a good idea once. Maybe a founder. Think Netflix or Uber. They structured to deliver it. Built a competent delivery muscle. Actually delivered it. Monetised it. Succeeded!

No new business would, or should worry about success beyond this point. It is a problem every founder dreams of having.

But every business beyond this point should be concerned about it.

Weirdly. Over reliance on a single individual (even a brilliant Founder) doesn't work beyond that first idea. Who would have thought that! But the business model is built around exactly that. Maybe ego gets in the way?

Lightning rarely strikes the same spot twice. For multiple strikes of genius - you need multiple people. Collaboration. Time. Space. Pondering. Diversity. Obsession. Debate. Honing each others ideas.

This is a totally different shape of business to the one that brought initial success.

And most don't realise it. They believe if they copy the initial model it'll work again. It doesn't.

If you want to innovate. Change your business model. Simple 😎
