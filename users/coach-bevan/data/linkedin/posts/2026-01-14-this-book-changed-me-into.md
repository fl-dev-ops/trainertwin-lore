This book changed me into the type of leader I want to be. Trusting.

The depth of research and evidence presented by Rutger is irrefutable.

We have got ourselves so lost in stories of human selfishness, that we've forgotten about our true nature...

At our CORE. We are KIND.

I trust my teams... They are far smarter than me. They can solve problems considered impossible. When the problem is framed well. And they have the resources they believe they need to succeed. 

I use less energy keeping tabs on where they are up to with projects. I don't try to work out if I can help. They will come to me if they need to. When it matters. If I can prove I am worthy of their trust. This is my goal. To be trusted by my team. As much as I trust them.

Great book!
