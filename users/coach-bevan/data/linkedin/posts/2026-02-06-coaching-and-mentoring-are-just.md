Coaching and Mentoring are just the same thing... Right???

Having been both. For me they differ greatly.

Mentoring is about sharing experience with someone more junior. Someone that is going through scenarios and situations that the mentors experience can help with. The situations are never identical so there may be limited value in the mentors experience. But hearing how someone else tackled a similar challenge can be insightful and stir your own cognitive juices.

Coaching is about exploring complex topics through the lens of the client. Partnering with them. Putting them at the centre of the conversation. The coach may have little experience of the clients specific problems. But by asking open, empowering situations, clients find their own solutions and stir their intuitive, deep and nuanced understanding.

Both are great. Both are different.

What is your experience?
