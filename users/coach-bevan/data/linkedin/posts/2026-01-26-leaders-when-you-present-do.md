Leaders, when you present. Do you face a barrage of questions?

This is the only measure of success for public speaking/ team alignment.

Many questions = good. 
It means people understood you. Not only that. Your subject inspired contemplation. Inner conflict. And permission. Permission to question. To challenge. For safe, open debate. Where alignment can happen. Where you too can learn. What did you miss?

No questions = bad. 
The wall of silence. Either...
Your audience didn't understand you
They don't want to challenge you
The subject was already so clear to everyone, it wasn't worth speaking about.

One or two questions = ok. 
But I know you can do better! You know you can do better. I believe in you!

The quantity of questions is my only measure of success.
