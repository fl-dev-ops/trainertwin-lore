They were struggling.

And I couldn't help.

In being a line manager. A leader within an organisation. Sometimes coaching those with the lowest performance. I found this group the hardest to impact.

Trust got in the way. How could we get past the fact that I had implicit power over their pay/ bonus/ livelihood?

How could we go deep into motivations, demons, why this role isn't working for them right now? Maybe they hated their boss? How could we discuss that?

We couldn't. 

As a line manager we never can really. Line manager coaching works for the top performers, but not the bottom. 

I wanted to help. So I left.

I realised the way I could help was from outside the organisation. Where I have no power. Where we may meet as equals. And make real change happen!
