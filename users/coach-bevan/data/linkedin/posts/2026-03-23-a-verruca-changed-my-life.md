A verruca changed my life.

Well curing it did.

It didn't hurt. I rarely saw it. It didn't bother me. It was on my list of things to do. But I didn't solve it. It sat on my todo list for years. Bit gross right?

There was always something more important to do. The important stuff could be tricky. Took time. I slowed. Only had really big stuff left. I forgot the little stuff. Developed clutter. Lost confidence. Stopped achieving. 

One day I did something different - Because doing the same thing and expecting different results is the definition of insanity - Thanks Einstein.

Instead of prioritising based on value. I wanted momentum. I prioritised on ease. First task was that verruca. 

It was deep. It took around 6 sessions of intense freezing, each spaced 4 weeks apart. As well as a daily cream covering. Yuk. But 6 months later that verruca was gone. Completely.

A few days after that. I wanted a new goal. But I felt confident. Like I could achieve things. 

I enrolled onto IPEC's coach training programme. The most transformative experience of my life.
