Coaching has been dying for years.

Some notable 'deaths' include:

- AI replacing human coaches
- L&D budget cuts post-pandemic
- A wealth of free self-help content
- "We'll just do a workshop instead"
- The rise of self-serve learning platforms
- Clampdowns on spend without tangible ROI

Here's the reality:

- No algorithm ever helped a leader find their courage
- Significant gains in retention for coached leaders
- Average ROI of 7x the cost of coaching (PwC)
- Training alone = 22% productivity gain
- Training PLUS coaching = 88% gain
- Market valued at $103.56B in 2025
- Projected to hit $174.53B by 2031

Leadership is a lot more than a static skillset you can download online.

Coaching is only dead for organisations that never understood what it was doing in the first place.
