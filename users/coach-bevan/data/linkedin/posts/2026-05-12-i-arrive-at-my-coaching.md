I arrive at my coaching session with a problem I don't believe I can make progress on:

"Why am I avoidant of anger?"

This has bamboozled me for 10+ years. There is no way we'll solve it in an hour. But I know the power of coaching. My coach is great...

We begin. It's not simple. Many layers. Peel them back. One by one. Unravel the complexity. New insight after insight is revealed. It all makes so much sense.

"I feel unsafe to be angry so I avoid it."

It's so simple. Complexity is created by me. By my mind. My advanced strategies of avoidance. Awareness makes them redundant. They melt away.

I have a new mantra: "My anger is strength when I use it to know and set boundaries".

The power of coaching is unreal with a top class coach.

Thank you Vanessa von Bergen
