What makes you angry? This guy ⬇️?

A great question. It can help to reveal true purpose.

In answering it. I realised. ANXIETY!

Anxiety makes me really angry. All the people who fret. Worry. Feel they aren't good enough. Feel like "what they are doing" doesn't matter enough. I have felt anxiety. Like my work doesn't matter. My inner voice sometimes tells me "what I am doing" isn't good enough. It is my gremlin. I call him Gandalf. I've personified him so I can talk to him when he shows up. I must reintegrate him. Soon. I'm nearly ready (coach if you're reading this).

Purpose is the opposite of anxiety. knowing what you do matters. Kills anxiety. This insight led me to understand... 

I want to rid the world of anxiety!

Targeting that lofty goal leads me to do work that matters to me. I help to defeat some anxiety. I reduce it a little.

This is my purpose. The question worked for me. So what makes you angry?
