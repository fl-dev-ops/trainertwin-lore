Can you deliver faster without a delivery lead? 

“If you don’t keep an eye on them, software engineers will spend forever creating perfect code that has little tangible difference to customers or to the bottom line” ££££

Is this true? 

Early in my career I was paired with a delivery lead. They called them Scrum Masters back then. That tension between 2 binary humans with binary objectives was meant to create a perfect middle. 

Product just wanted it perfect, didn’t care about any customer using it. 
Delivery just wanted it shipped, didn’t care about quality.

It just sounds implausible. Like no one could be trusted to be reasonable. And if the management can’t be trusted. Never mind the engineers! 

One day, I found myself working in a company that didn’t have delivery leads. Just me as Product Manager + Coach + Line Manager to a squad of 10 engineers. I got to try my methods for real. I decided to trust the engineers. 

We delivered faster than I’ve seen before. We went from once every couple of months to 2 per week. Release frequency is a commonly recognised metric for team health and performance. And we were nailing it!

The engineers made that happen. Because they wanted their recent hard worked developments to be used by real humans. Actual customers. Because they cared. And hated the admin of the old release process. They made it leaner without harming quality (bug count in production).

I empowered them. I trusted them. Sometimes I coached/ guided them a little  😜 

Seems they could be trusted. They acted like adults. I wasn’t surprised.
