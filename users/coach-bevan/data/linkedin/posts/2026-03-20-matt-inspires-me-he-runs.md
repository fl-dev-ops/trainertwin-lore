Matt inspires me. 

He runs his business "Strong in the Park" from my local park.

He is out in the park rain or shine. Usually lugging big kettle bells, sandbags or sledges around. Always accompanied by his massive dog Kodo.

When I first met him. He told me his ideal life was to work outdoors with his dog. He wanted a life that he never NEEDED a holiday from. 

That was enough for me. I began training with him right away.

He is living his dream.

He goes on holiday, for adventures. Not because he needs a break, but because something excites him.  His last was to fight in a nomadic wrestling competition in Mongolia. Google "Mongolian Wrestling Outfit". And yes he wore it.

He's also an expert. Passionate about functional fitness. He wants to help people live active lives well into their 80s, 90s and beyond. And he does.

There are many reasons to train with Matt. In groups of 4. Its social.

His knowledge is beyond compare. One week I did an exercise with him I'd never seen before. He told me it was a competition exercise used in "World Strongest Man" around 1900.

Community, Nature, Strength, Expertise

Can't beat it!
