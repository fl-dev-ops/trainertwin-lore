Can you choose your beliefs? Or are you bound to them?

That depends on what you believe!!!

Trapped. Oppressed. No choices. I've worked with a few clients recently that own a progress master lock... "My beliefs can't be changed. My beliefs are my truth". 

No other belief can be challenged, or changed whilst this fundamental belief remains in place. Growth is impossible.

If your "truth" holds you back. Change it. Find a new truth.

We have all changed a belief at some point... "no not me"... Well. Think back further. Did you believe you'd die when the Spice Girls split up? Do you still?

What's your old belief? What's a better belief?
