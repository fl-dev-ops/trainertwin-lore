Coach Beard just held up a sign at the World Cup Final.

It said BELIEBE.

Ted Lasso got a 30 second spot at half time. Took the mickey out of the Americanness of it all. Then introduced Justin Bieber... Along with a classic Ted pep talk.

Daft? Completely. Brilliant? Obviously.

That sign stuck with me. Because Ted's whole thing isn't tactics. It's belief. And belief is what gets you through a transformation.

I once sat in a room where we'd just removed our entire strategic function. The plan: grow our operational team into something bigger. Operational AND strategic. One function. It made sense. Strategy and operations had never really worked side by side. They barely spoke.

But here's what the plan didn't show...

The middle.

We'd lost all sense of strategy. And hadn't yet grown it anywhere else. No safety net. No "all bases covered" feeling. Just a gap. The facts in that room were ugly. If we'd followed the facts, we'd have stopped. Reversed. Allowed fear to grip us.

But the old thing didn't work. We knew that.

So we had to believe the noise would clear. That we'd land somewhere better. That's every transformation I've been in. The middle feels worse than the start.

Belief creates alignment. Alignment creates confidence. Confidence creates the right behaviours. And the right behaviours create the result.

Belief first. Success second.

If you're a leader in the messy middle right now... what are you leading your team to believe?
