I am writing from deep within a game of hide and seek. Don't make a peep. Half term.

Looking after my son. Fun. Chaotic. Wild. Exhausting. Brilliant.

But also... new

Because this time - I am putting career development on pause. For childcare.

And that feels odd... Why does that feel odd?

For a long time we have quietly accepted something. When family needs time... Women's careers bend. Men's don't.

It's become so normal we barely notice it.

I don't deserve applause. This week for me should be normal. Women do it all the time. Have done it for generations. It made me realise how deeply ingrained our behaviours are.

But behaviours can be changed. It can all be changed. If we all want it to (and I do). 

All we need to do is make the change. Even though is feels uncomfortable. Accept the discomfort. Repeat. Allow time to pass. Until one day. We notice. There is no discomfort left.

(maybe it is time for me to be "found"... but maybe a few more minutes)
