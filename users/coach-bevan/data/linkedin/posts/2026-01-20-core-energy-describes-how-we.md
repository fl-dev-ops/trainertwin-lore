Core energy describes how we feel in every moment. What can these shoes mean for my core energy? 

Any yes, they are Spiderman. And yes, they do light up. And no, they do not come in adult sizes!

There are 7 levels of Core energy. With 1 being lowest. 7 being highest. All energy levels are useful. In certain situations.

Our energy level fluctuates throughout the day. Each of us has an average energy level. Most of us have energy that shows up when it isn't useful.

The most common is a trigger point. Angry catabolic energy (L1/ L2) can show up at times when we can't make much use of it. Not great. Maybe when we're trying to resolve a difference of opinion with a loved one. Maybe when we're trying to put a child's Spiderman shoes on before leaving the house!

Core energy coaching (for me) is about bringing awareness to the current energy level. Allowing us to choose which energy type we want for the given situation.

These shoes also bring me a lot of joy. I see how happy my son is when we finally get them on. And that feels like a win-win (L5) for me! With more awareness. I can see more situations with energy that helps the situation. Not hinder it.
