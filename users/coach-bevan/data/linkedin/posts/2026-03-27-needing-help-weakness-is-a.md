Needing Help = Weakness

Is a belief that keeps us small.

We are social creatures. Learning through community. With other humans.

"I did it all on my own" is strength? Celebrated? But privately, how true is it?

Those who peak above the crowd do so on the shoulders of their support network. 

Maybe they hide that network? Maybe they don't promote it enough? Maybe we don't want to hear about it? Maybe we want to believe they did it on their own?

Does help from another mean you are a failure? 

Or does it simply mean you believe you can achieve more? And you are strong enough to build the support network you need?
