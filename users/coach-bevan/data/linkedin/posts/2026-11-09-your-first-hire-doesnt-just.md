Your first hire doesn't just give you another pair of hands. It gives you trust issues you didn't know you had.

One of my favourite clients had been running his business on his own for seven years. Just him. With a dream a lot bigger than where it was.

So he wanted to hire his first employee. Which brought up a ton of trust issues.

That's the bit nobody puts in the job advert. On paper, a first hire is a recruitment decision. In practice, you're handing something you've done your way, every day, for years, to somebody who might do it differently. Maybe better. Which is somehow worse.

He did make the hire. And it's been pretty interesting, and pretty awesome, watching what it brought up.

I don't think the hard part was finding someone. 

It was working out where his trust issues came from. Then letting them do it, even if not quite as well as he would.
