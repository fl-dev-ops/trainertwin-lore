Feedback is important. I struggled with it. I avoided it. Until... I understood that "NOT giving feedback is unkind".

Even when Kim Scott taught me this. I still struggled with it. I was afraid. Of confrontation.

I challenged myself to give 5 pieces of necessary feedback in 3 months. 1 month later. I had given 7.

I discovered that once I started giving feedback that mattered. To people I cared about. With empathy. They responded with grace and gratitude. My fear was irrational.

The response uttered in almost every conversation was a version of: "I've known this for a while now, deep down, but I've been avoiding it. Thank you. Now I have to do something about it. Will you support me?"

I didn't need to think twice before I said "yes". It brought me such joy to help. And maybe. A little relief. That they hadn't become angry.

Those I helped. And supported. Overcame challenges. That had bothered them subconsciously for years. And the work they did to overcome. Was always less than they expected.

Let's all become more compassionate. And avoid the Obnoxious Aggression!
