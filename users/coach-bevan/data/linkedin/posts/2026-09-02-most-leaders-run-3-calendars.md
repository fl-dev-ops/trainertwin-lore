Most leaders run 3 calendars. Only one of them shows up in their diary.

The first is the real one - meetings, one-to-ones and reviews. Everyone can see it.

The second isn't really a calendar, it's a list you carry in your head. 

The employee who's struggling. The stakeholder you're handling carefully. The project you don't quite trust to land. It never gets booked, but it's with you all day.

The third is the one that matters most. The things you keep not doing. The conversation you avoid, the decision you postpone and the thinking time you never protect.

Here's what took me too long to notice. 
What sits on that third list usually isn't random. The tasks you keep pushing aside tend to be the ones you don't enjoy, aren't naturally good at, or - most telling - the ones that no longer need you at all. Letting them go will release you.

So when something keeps sliding to next week, it's worth a harder question than "why am I procrastinating?" Ask what the avoidance is telling you. Sometimes it's a conversation you need to have. Sometimes it's work that was never yours to keep.

What's been on your avoidance list longest - and what's it trying to tell you?
