When the Lord of the Rings films came out, Aragorn was all I wanted to be. The flashy figurehead. The good looking guy.

Somewhere along the way I switched to Gandalf. He knows the way, doesn't want the burden, and disappears for half of the books to go and do whatever else he's doing.

Which, now I say it out loud, is a fairly accurate description of coaching.

In your business at the moment, are you more Aragorn or Gandalf?
