We didn't get worse at agreeing. We got worse at listening.

Every disagreement, underneath, is the same thing - 2 people running on different information. Different evidence. Different stories. Never compared.

Bottoming that out is valuable. Takes communication. Self-awareness. Active listening. Empathy.

Four skills. In short supply right now.

It wasn't always this way...

Tribes didn't vote on direction. They couldn't. One person walks away unconvinced - and the whole group is weaker for it. So they talked. They listened. Properly. Until everyone understood the full picture. And said "yes".

The earliest democracies weren't a show of hands either. They ran on consent - full, mutual, everyone agreed. One holdout = no movement. You stay. Talk. Listen. Understand. Until everyone agrees.

These methods improve your communication skills. We couldn't implement right now. We couldn't agree. We lack these skills. Inverse evolution. Simplification. But if we did implement... Imagine what we could gain?

We replaced that with voting. Majority wins. Minority loses. And the information gap beneath the disagreement goes untouched.

What disagreement in your life have you voted away instead of resolving?
