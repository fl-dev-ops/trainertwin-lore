Women make the best leaders. 

The biggest variance under tests (other than physical) is that women display higher levels of empathy, kindness and cooperation.

To me, these are non-negotiable traits in a leader.

I'm not saying men can't learn these skills, and some men will have have these traits. But I want more leaders with natural ability + spent time honing it.

Based on that skew, to improve society, we need more than 50% of top level leaders to be female. And I want a better society.

I want female leadership.
