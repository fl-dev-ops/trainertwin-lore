If you want your teams to take responsibility - give them autonomy.

They need:
1. GOAL - A smart goal they care about.
2. AGILITY - The resource flexibility they believe they need to score the goal.
3. TRUST - You to trust them, and step out of the way. Let them decide how to score.

Simple and hard, right?
