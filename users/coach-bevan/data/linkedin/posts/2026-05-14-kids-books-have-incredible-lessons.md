Kids books have incredible lessons that we adults need to learn.

Wanda is authentic, sunny, kind, funny and enjoys a stomp in the swamp.

But... she spots some beautiful Macaws and begins the slippery slope of COMPARISON.

"If they are so perfect that must mean... I'm not"

She ends up grumpy and sad. And begins imitating the Macaws. They don't even notice. She worries that they don't like her.

Then her friends step in...

"Some people like you,
Some people don't,
Some will be best friends,
But most people won't,
Don't waste your time being somebody else,
What you should ask is... Do you like yourself?"

Wanda realises and reframes: 

"I'm a diamond, a gem,
And if they don't like me,
Then bad luck for them."
