Most of what holds you back isn't a skill you're missing. It's a belief you've never questioned.

People ask what actually happens in coaching. Here's the honest version, minus the mystique.
The beliefs I mean run quietly in the background. Things like “if I let go of this it'll fall apart,” or “everyone else is more of a natural at this than me,” or “I can't slow down or I'll lose my edge.”

They feel like facts. That's what makes them powerful. You don't question them, you just live as though they're true, and they quietly shape every decision you make.
The work is mostly this. We find them, hold them up to the light, and check whether they actually hold. Almost none of them do. Then we build something truer to stand on instead.

It isn't strategy and it isn't tricks. It's clearing out the beliefs that have you playing smaller than you need to, so you can lead like yourself again.
That's it. No magic. Just the un-examined stuff, examined.

If you're curious what's running in your own background, that curiosity is usually a good sign it's worth a look.
