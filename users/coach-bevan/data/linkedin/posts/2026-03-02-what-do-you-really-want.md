What do you really want? 
From life?

I worked with a client recently that said he wanted to learn French. But he never practiced...

Over our session, we learnt together that he didn't want to learn French. He felt like he should. But he didn't want to. Like a commitment. To no-one. To a belief that good people should learn other languages. That didn't serve him. That created guilt.

New understanding freed him. Forget about French. He didn't want that. This created space. What came into that space... 

The next chapter is his to decide...

We all carry burdens like this. Watch out for things you feel you "should do". Ask yourself - "Is this really what I want?"
