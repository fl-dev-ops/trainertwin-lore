I love the Unalome. It represents Growth!

We start in the middle of the spiral. Not all progress is upwards. Sometimes we regress. Eventually. We move onwards.

The Unalome represents our whole life, or many lives. The lower parts of the spiral can be months or years.

This is reality. How growth actually shows up in our lives. It can be painful. It can be beautiful. All of it is our life. Our story.

The tough times are a necessary part of our journey. They may not feel like progress. But they are.

I love this representation. Sometimes there are short cuts. Sometimes there are dead ends. 

Eventually we become the tree. A symbol of wisdom. That gives me comfort. 

I love the Unalome!
