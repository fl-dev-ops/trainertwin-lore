We have mapped 5 layers so far. Is there a 6th we are missing? Genuinely curious what you would add. More on each layer in the upcoming posts.

I spent my weekend reading Greg Faxon's write-up on his 30-day AI clone experiment. 871,000 words of his own content, trained into Delphi. 8,400 subscribers. 50 paid.

The most interesting part wasn't the numbers. It was what people actually used the clone for. *Nobody talked to his clone like a coach. They used it like a content tool: drafting pages, writing lead magnets, outlining workshops.* 

Greg's own words: "True coaching happens in the context of a relationship."
That gap is the whole opportunity.
A real trainer twin needs to work alongside the trainer and needs two things:
What you teach and How you teach.
Most clones capture the first. The harder part is the second: judgment.

That's what we're building at TrainerTwin, in 5 layers.

1. Learner state - who this person is, where they get stuck, and what feedback they’ve already received.
2. Knowledge base - the trainer’s teaching material, plus the real questions and follow-ups they’ve used in sessions.
3. Method of pedagogy - a structured, reviewable model of how that trainer actually teaches.
4. Agent harness - the system that decides whether the right next move is to teach, drill, challenge, or evaluate. The model writes the response; the harness chooses the move.
5. Delivery interface - voice, web, embedded wherever learners already are.

Greg proved the demand is real.
Now we're exploring what it takes to make an AI twin actually feel like a trainer.
